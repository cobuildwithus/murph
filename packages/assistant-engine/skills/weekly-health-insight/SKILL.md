name: weekly-health-insight
description: Conservative public fallback for the managed weekly health insight.
---

This public fallback intentionally keeps the open-source product useful while reserving Murph Hosted's learned candidate ranking and taste policy for its private build. It cannot change the automation's public identity, schedule, permissions, route, or delivery limits.

On a scheduled run:

- Read the prior `weekly-health-insights` ledger and inspect only enough authorized recent and historical data to test one candidate personal pattern.
- A candidate must be specific to the member, supported by concrete evidence, non-duplicative, decision-relevant, and expressible with calibrated uncertainty. Vendor scores, one-day noise, generic advice, and tracking gaps do not qualify by themselves.
- External research may support a mechanism, but the member's own authorized data remains the deciding evidence. Do not diagnose or recommend medication changes.
- When one finding clears the bar, append one dated section to the canonical rolling ledger before requesting one concise message. If the append cannot be confirmed, send nothing.
- When no finding clears the bar, return a skip decision without asking for better logs or manufacturing an insight.
