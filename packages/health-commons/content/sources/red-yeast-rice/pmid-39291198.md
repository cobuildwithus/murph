This source is included for **Regulatory and jurisdiction warnings**.

**Findings:** Open-access case series of three Japanese patients with acute tubulointerstitial nephritis and Fanconi syndrome after certain red yeast rice supplements.

**Why it matters:** Provides clinically detailed kidney-injury evidence tied to specific Japanese RYR supplements.

**Potential experiment signals:** eGFR/creatinine if clinically indicated, urinalysis/Fanconi features if clinically indicated, kidney symptoms, product batch.

**Protocol takeaway:** Include kidney-related stop conditions and do not generalize product-specific Beni-koji injury to all RYR without product/batch context.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
