This source is included for **Evidence syntheses and reviews**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-35988871-nma-lipid-ranking` — The network meta-analysis found all analyzed nutraceuticals except policosanols lowered LDL-C and total cholesterol versus placebo/no intervention, and described bergamot and RYR as among the most effective approaches for LDL-C and total-cholesterol reduction.
- `finding:pmid-35988871-directness-boundary` — Because the analysis is comparative across many nutraceuticals, RYR-specific applicability should be treated as context and not substituted for RYR-only evidence syntheses.

**Why it matters:** Places RYR in a broader nutraceutical comparative landscape but is not a direct RYR-only synthesis.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, total cholesterol, triglycerides, HDL-C.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent nutraceutical context only; do not use to replace direct RYR meta-analyses.
