This source is included for **Xuezhikang and proprietary Chinese RYR preparations**.

**Findings:** In 591 CCSPS participants with type 2 diabetes and previous myocardial infarction, Xuezhikang (n=306) versus placebo (n=285) was reported to reduce coronary event risk; this remains adjacent proprietary-preparation evidence. The source is useful for safety review because it evaluates Xuezhikang in a diabetic post-MI population; detailed adverse-event rates were not extracted in this batch.

**Limitations:** Important limitation: this is a proprietary Chinese preparation, usually used in supervised clinical contexts; it should not be generalized to variable OTC red yeast rice supplements.
