This source is included for **Lipid measurement and test-plan context**.

**Findings:** The NLA report identifies cholesterol carried by apoB-containing lipoproteins, reflected by non-HDL-C and LDL-C, as central to ASCVD-risk management and monitoring. This supports endpoint selection but does not test red yeast rice.

**Why it matters:** Older NLA patient-centered guidance retained as foundational context even though newer statements exist.

**Potential experiment signals:** Non-HDL-C as an atherogenic cholesterol marker.; LDL-C and apoB-containing lipoproteins as lipid endpoints.; Risk category to contextualize whether self-experimentation is appropriate..

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as legacy NLA context for interpreting LDL-C and non-HDL-C; do not promote it as direct evidence for red yeast rice efficacy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Limitations and directness:** Directness is `measurement_context`. Older 2015 guidance; newer lipid-measurement statements and multisociety guidelines are available. Not a red yeast rice efficacy study. Consensus recommendations may reflect clinical treatment pathways rather than consumer supplement protocols. Population mismatch: Clinical dyslipidemia-management population may differ from self-selected supplement users.
