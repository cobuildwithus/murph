This source is included for **Evidence syntheses and reviews**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-17302963-placebo-lipid-effects` — RYR showed weighted mean differences versus placebo/no intervention of total cholesterol −0.91 mmol/L, triglycerides −0.41 mmol/L, LDL-C −0.73 mmol/L, and HDL-C +0.15 mmol/L.
- `finding:pmid-17302963-quality-safety-limits` — Adverse effects were generally reported as non-serious, but the included trials were mostly low methodological quality and the authors called for more rigorous and longer-term safety trials.

**Why it matters:** This is an early direct synthesis that quantifies placebo-controlled lipid changes for RYR preparations used in primary hyperlipidemia.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, total cholesterol, triglycerides, HDL-C, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as direct synthesis evidence that RYR preparations can lower LDL-C and other lipid markers, while retaining the low-methodological-quality and long-term-safety caveats.
