This source is included for **Safety reviews and pharmacovigilance**.

**Findings:** The case report describes hospitalization for acute renal deficiency, hepatitis, and rhabdomyolysis after daily intake of 315 mg red yeast rice; the authors used the case to highlight limited adverse-effect knowledge and production control for supplements.

**Why it matters:** Provides a severe multi-organ adverse-event case that ties red yeast rice exposure to rhabdomyolysis, kidney injury, and hepatitis signals.

**Potential experiment signals:** rhabdomyolysis symptoms, acute kidney injury, hepatitis/liver injury, hospitalization, supplement dose history.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as case-report safety signal only; severe renal, hepatic, and muscle injury should be explicit stop/seek-care outcomes.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
