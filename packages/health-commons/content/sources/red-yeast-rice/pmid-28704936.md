This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** The active formulation reduced non-HDL-C by about 15.1% versus -1.1% with placebo, LDL-C by about 19% versus 4%, and ApoB by about 12% versus 4%; HDL-C, triglycerides, glucose, HbA1c, waist, and BMI did not materially change.

**Why it matters:** Useful for non-HDL-C/apoB-adjacent evidence mapping, not plain RYR claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** ldl-c, non-hdl-c, triglycerides, hdl-c, apob, endothelial-function, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote this source to a direct plain red yeast rice claim without a separable RYR-only arm. Preserve the boundary: Berberine/RYR/chitosan/CoQ10 combination evidence, not plain RYR.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Comparator/control:** Placebo.

**Duration/follow-up:** 12 weeks.

**Safety/adverse events:** Three serious adverse events/discontinuations occurred in the nutraceutical arm (duodenitis, Epstein-Barr virus myocarditis, and asymptomatic CPK elevation after intense exercise); no placebo adverse events were reported in the accessible article text.

**Limitations:** Short-term, selected primary-prevention dyslipidemic population; no chitosan-only or RYR-only group.
