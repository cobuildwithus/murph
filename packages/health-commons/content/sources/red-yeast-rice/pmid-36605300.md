## Quick read

- **Source type:** Narrative review of LDL-C measurement and calculation methods (2022).
- **People or setting studied:** Clinical laboratory and cardiovascular-risk contexts for LDL-C measurement.
- **Exposure or intervention:** LDL-C measurement methods, including analytical methods, calculation equations, and automated direct assays.
- **Comparator or control:** Different LDL-C measurement and estimation approaches.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Narrative review; no intervention follow-up.
- **Role in Murph:** context-only / measurement_context.

## Findings

This methods review categorizes LDL-C measurement into analytical, calculated, and automated direct approaches, supporting a protocol caveat that LDL-C values are method-dependent.

**Effect estimate or direction extracted:** Review classifies LDL-C methods into non-automated analytical methods, calculation methods, and automated direct measurement; no clinical effect estimate is extracted.

## Why it matters

LDL-C is the headline endpoint for the protocol, and method choice can affect interpretation.

## Potential experiment signals

LDL-C measurement method, calculated LDL-C, direct LDL-C, lab-fidelity

## Protocol takeaway

Record whether LDL-C is calculated, directly measured, or otherwise estimated; prefer comparable methods across baseline and follow-up.
