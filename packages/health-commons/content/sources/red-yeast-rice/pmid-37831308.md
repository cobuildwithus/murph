This source is included for **Safety reviews and pharmacovigilance**.

**Findings:** The review reported that among FAERS rhabdomyolysis cases through March 31, 2023, four were associated with red yeast rice and none were fatal; CAERS included three rhabdomyolysis reports, and the review summarized published cases including a transplant recipient and later hospitalized cases.

**Why it matters:** Directly addresses the serious safety outcomes most relevant to a user-facing stop-rule: rhabdomyolysis and severe hepatitis.

**Potential experiment signals:** rhabdomyolysis, severe acute hepatitis, hospitalization, adverse-event database limitations, published case reports.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for serious adverse-event context and reporting-system caveats; do not infer incidence from FAERS/CAERS counts.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
