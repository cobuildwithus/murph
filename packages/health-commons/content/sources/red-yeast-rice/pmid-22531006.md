This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** In accessible metadata, the combined powdered product was associated with total cholesterol reduction of 19% (46 mg/dL) and LDL-C reduction of 33% (53 mg/dL) after 6 weeks.

**Why it matters:** Combination with plant sterols/stanols; not RYR-alone but relevant to adjacent supplement stacks.

**Potential experiment signals:** ldl-c, total-cholesterol, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote this source to a direct plain red yeast rice claim without a separable RYR-only arm. Preserve the boundary: Combination powdered product, not plain RYR.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Comparator/control:** No placebo comparator extracted from accessible metadata.

**Duration/follow-up:** 6 weeks.

**Safety/adverse events:** Safety details were not extracted from accessible metadata.

**Limitations:** Small sample; combination with plant stanols/sterols; accessible metadata described a case series despite the ledger design label, so verify before using as controlled evidence.
