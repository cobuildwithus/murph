This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** The review reported improvements in lipid and glucose profiles for nutraceutical combinations, while noting effects had been positive in some but not all trials.

**Why it matters:** Combination-product meta-analysis; relevant to prevent attributing multi-ingredient effects to RYR alone.

**Potential experiment signals:** ldl-c, total-cholesterol, triglycerides, hdl-c, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote this source to a direct plain red yeast rice claim without a separable RYR-only arm. Preserve the boundary: Combination-product synthesis, context-only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Comparator/control:** Placebo/control arms from included trials.

**Duration/follow-up:** Varied across included trials.

**Safety/adverse events:** Safety synthesis details were not extracted from accessible metadata.

**Limitations:** Heterogeneous multi-ingredient combinations; RYR contribution not separable.
