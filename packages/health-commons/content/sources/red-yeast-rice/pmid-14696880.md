**Why it matters:** Adds to the safety signal that red-yeast-rice preparations can produce statin-like muscle adverse effects.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- Muscle pain and weakness symptom log
- Creatine kinase if symptoms occur
- Exposure/rechallenge avoidance tracking
- Supplement brand, dose, and ingredient documentation

**Protocol takeaway:** For self-directed cholesterol protocols, muscle symptoms after starting red yeast rice should trigger stopping criteria and clinical evaluation rather than dose escalation.
