This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** Accessible metadata reports statistically significant LDL-C lowering consistent with monacolin K-based dietary supplements; exact effect sizes were not extracted in this batch.

**Why it matters:** Open-label crossover combination trial; useful for adjacent products and study-design boundary.

**Potential experiment signals:** ldl-c, total-cholesterol, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote this source to a direct plain red yeast rice claim without a separable RYR-only arm. Preserve the boundary: Combination supplement with monacolin K, not generic RYR.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Comparator/control:** Control/crossover periods; exact sequence details not extracted.

**Duration/follow-up:** Duration not extracted from accessible metadata.

**Safety/adverse events:** Safety was assessed, but adverse-event details were not extracted.

**Limitations:** Open-label crossover and multi-ingredient supplement; no plain RYR-only attribution.
