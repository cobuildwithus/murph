This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** The SPORT trial compared low-dose rosuvastatin, placebo, and common supplement arms including red yeast rice; rosuvastatin substantially reduced LDL-C, while no supplement arm significantly reduced LDL-C versus placebo over 28 days.

**Why it matters:** This is the primary supervised null comparator context for a commercial red yeast rice supplement arm against placebo and statin in a modern trial.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, total cholesterol, triglycerides, HDL-C, hsCRP, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only negative/null comparator evidence for one short-duration commercial supplement arm; do not generalize to all red yeast rice preparations.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Single-center; single-blind; short 28-day duration; each supplement arm reflects a specific marketed product; monacolin content for the red yeast rice product is not necessarily generalizable. At-risk adults in a supervised randomized trial, not a long-duration personalized protocol or monacolin-standardized formulation.
