This source is included for **Lipid measurement and test-plan context**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Cross-sectional fasting/nonfasting lipid comparison by statin-treatment status (2021).
- **People or setting studied:** Hospitalized patients with chest pain and normal triglyceride levels, including statin-treated and untreated groups.
- **Exposure or intervention:** Fasting and nonfasting lipid measurements, stratified by statin-treatment status.
- **Comparator or control:** Statin-treated versus untreated participants and fasting versus nonfasting measurement status.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Cross-sectional/paired measurement context; fasting defined as 8 to 12 hours in extracted summary.
- **Role in Murph:** context-only / measurement_context.

## Findings

In 686 hospitalized chest-pain patients with normal triglycerides, fasting/nonfasting lipid measurement differences were reported as not related to statin treatment. This is a lower-priority, population-mismatched measurement-context finding.

**Effect estimate or direction extracted:** Search-extracted summaries report fasting and nonfasting lipid differences were not related to statin treatment, with generally similar panel interpretation in the studied population.

## Why it matters

Useful only as a guardrail that statin status is not necessarily the driver of fasting/nonfasting measurement differences.

## Potential experiment signals

fasting status, statin treatment status, LDL-C, triglycerides, lipid panel

## Protocol takeaway

Do not use this population-specific study to generalize RYR efficacy; use it only for fasting-status measurement context.
