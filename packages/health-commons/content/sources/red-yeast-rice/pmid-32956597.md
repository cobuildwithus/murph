This source is included for **Guidelines and external protocol claims**.

**Findings:** The guideline synopsis states that evidence is insufficient to recommend for or against red yeast rice supplements for reducing cardiovascular disease risk.

**Why it matters:** This source is important because it separates biomarker lowering from patient-oriented cardiovascular benefit.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C may be tracked, but cardiovascular events and safety outcomes should not be assumed from short-term lipid changes.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a conservative boundary against overclaiming cardiovascular risk reduction.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
