This source is included for **Safety reviews and pharmacovigilance**.

**Findings:** After several months of use, case 1 developed hypertension and after 6 months eGFR fell to 22.5 mL/min/1.73 m² with urinary protein-to-creatinine ratio 2.03 g/g; Fanconi syndrome and tubular degeneration were diagnosed, and proteinuria resolved with eGFR return to baseline 35 days after stopping.

**Why it matters:** Adds kidney-safety boundary for a product-specific red yeast rice/beni-kōji exposure and highlights population/product mismatch.

**Potential experiment signals:** acute kidney injury, eGFR, proteinuria, Fanconi syndrome, tubular injury, product-specific exposure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as product-specific safety signal, not a general incidence claim for all red yeast rice products.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
