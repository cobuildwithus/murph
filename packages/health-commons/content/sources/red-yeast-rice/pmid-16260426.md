This source is included for **Direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** At 8 weeks, RYR reduced LDL-C by 27.7%, total cholesterol by 21.5%, triglycerides by 15.8%, and ApoB by 26.0%; HDL-C and ApoA-I increases were not significant in accessible extraction. No ALT, AST, or CPK values at least three times the upper limit of normal were reported at weeks 4 or 8 in accessible extraction.

**Why it matters:** Directly matches the protocol’s core question: RYR-only supplementation for cholesterol biomarkers.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, total cholesterol, triglycerides, HDL-C, ApoB, ApoA-I, ALT, AST, CPK.

**Protocol takeaway:** A direct RCT supports LDL-C and total-cholesterol reduction over 8 weeks at 600 mg twice daily, with short-term lab-safety monitoring.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness and boundary:** direct_protocol. Short duration and a specific Monascus purpureus Went product; longer-term safety and product equivalence are not established. Population mismatch: Hyperlipidemic adults; not necessarily applicable to people with liver disease, pregnancy, interacting drugs, or already-treated LDL-C.
