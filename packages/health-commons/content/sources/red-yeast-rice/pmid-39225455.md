This source is included for **Lipid measurement and test-plan context**.

**Findings:** ADLM recommends standardized lipid-panel reporting that includes measured total cholesterol, triglycerides, HDL-C and calculated LDL-C and non-HDL-C, while documenting fasting status and acknowledging LDL-C calculation limitations. This is measurement-plan context only.

**Why it matters:** Most directly aligned source for this shard because it is a laboratory-medicine guidance document focused on lipid measurement and reporting workflows.

**Potential experiment signals:** Report TC, TG, HDL-C, calculated LDL-C, and non-HDL-C in standard panels.; Do not require fasting routinely, but document fasting status and use fasting samples in selected hypertriglyceridemia contexts.; Choose LDL-C estimation methods mindful of triglyceride range and calculation limits..

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a core laboratory-method source for lipid panel composition, fasting status, LDL-C calculation caveats, and apoB/non-HDL-C reporting.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Limitations and directness:** Directness is `measurement_context`. Laboratory guidance rather than protocol efficacy evidence. Article has an associated correction notice; source page should track corrected version. Local lab methods and reporting fields vary. Population mismatch: Applies to laboratory and clinical reporting workflows rather than supplement intervention populations.
