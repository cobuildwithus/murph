This source is included for **Evidence syntheses and reviews**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-41681060-monacolin-k-lipids` — The meta-analysis reported significant lipid lowering with monacolin K-containing supplements, including an LDL-C weighted mean difference of −34.292 mg/dL (95% CI −41.458 to −27.126) in accessible abstracted material.
- `finding:pmid-41681060-formulation-boundary` — The source is relevant to RYR because monacolin K is a key active constituent, but product formulations may not map one-to-one to all over-the-counter RYR supplements; detailed safety findings were not extracted from accessible abstract material.

**Why it matters:** It updates the evidence base around monacolin K, the statin-like constituent that drives many RYR lipid effects.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, total cholesterol, triglycerides, HDL-C.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as direct/same-mechanism support for monacolin K-containing RYR products, with a formulation boundary around non-standardized whole RYR.
