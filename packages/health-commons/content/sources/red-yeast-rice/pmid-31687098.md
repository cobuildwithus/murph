This source is included for **Guidelines and external protocol claims**.

**Findings:** The review summarizes LDL-C reductions, monacolin K mechanism, associated lipid-marker changes, and tolerability caveats. It also includes secondary-prevention material that should remain adjacent evidence.

**Why it matters:** This is one of the most useful high-level RYR clinical-context sources in the batch, but it is still a narrative review and should not replace source-level trial extraction.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C is the primary signal; non-HDL-C, apoB, triglycerides, HDL-C, hs-CRP, myalgias, CK, liver enzymes, and renal markers are relevant follow-up signals.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a review-level context source for mechanism, expected LDL-C direction, and safety monitoring; direct protocol effect sizes need direct RCT or meta-analysis source pages.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
