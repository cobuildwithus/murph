This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** A 12-week double-blind placebo-controlled trial in metabolic syndrome tested a multi-ingredient extractive formula including red yeast rice and reported larger total cholesterol, LDL-C, and triglyceride reductions than placebo.

**Why it matters:** This adds metabolic-syndrome adjacent combination evidence but is not a red yeast rice-only test.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C, triglycerides, metabolic syndrome blood pressure criterion.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent variant evidence only; do not cite as proof that plain red yeast rice alone lowers cholesterol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Participant count was not available in the accessible abstract; combination product; metabolic-syndrome population; no red yeast rice-only arm. Metabolic syndrome population exposed to a combined extract formula, not a plain red yeast rice cholesterol protocol.
