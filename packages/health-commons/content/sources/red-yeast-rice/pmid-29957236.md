**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-29957236-nutraceuticals-statin-intolerance-context` — The expert review addresses nutraceuticals in statin-intolerant patients and situates red yeast rice within a broader clinician-managed lipid-lowering strategy rather than an unsupervised consumer protocol.
- `finding:pmid-29957236-nonstatin-limitations-standardization` — The review context and ACC summary highlight that nutraceutical use in statin-intolerant patients requires careful attention to evidence quality, safety monitoring, and product standardization.

**Why it matters:** Defines the clinical niche where red yeast rice is often discussed: patients with statin-associated symptoms who still need LDL-C risk reduction.

**Potential experiment signals:** statin-intolerance-history, ASCVD-risk-context, LDL-C, clinician-review-required, concomitant-lipid-therapy-screen.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context/safety-only evidence: red yeast rice belongs in a clinician-managed statin-intolerance discussion, not as direct support for a self-directed cholesterol protocol.

**Limitations:** Expert review with permission-limited full text; not a red yeast rice-only trial and not specific to the Murph protocol variant.

**Population mismatch:** Statin-intolerant patients may differ in baseline risk, medication history, and monitoring needs from general cholesterol self-experimenters.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
