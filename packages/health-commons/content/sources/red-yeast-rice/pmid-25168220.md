This source is included for **Product quality, contamination, and dose uncertainty**.

**Findings:** LC-accurate QToF MS and chemometrics source for quantifying and profiling monacolins and citrinin in RYR materials and products.

**Extracted details:**

- **Population / sample:** Authentic RYR samples, commercial raw materials, and dietary supplements; no human participants
- **Intervention or exposure:** LC-accurate QToF MS chemical profiling and quantification of monacolins and citrinin
- **Comparator / control:** Chemometric comparison of authentic and commercial material profiles; no clinical comparator
- **Duration / follow-up:** Laboratory profiling study
- **Endpoints:** monacolin quantification; citrinin quantification; chemical-profile classification/authenticity
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Chemical profiling method/source for monacolins and citrinin in raw materials and supplements; no clinical lipid effect estimate extracted.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Useful for active-dose and citrinin surveillance across raw material and finished supplement supply chain.
- **Limitations:** Analytical profiling; exact representativeness depends on sample frame and market period.
- **Population mismatch:** No human LDL-C or adverse-event outcomes.
- **Directness:** measurement_context / same_mechanism product-quality evidence

**Why it matters:** Supports authenticity and dose-fidelity boundaries across supply-chain inputs, not just finished capsules.

**Potential experiment signals:** monacolin quantification; citrinin quantification; chemical-profile classification/authenticity

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as analytical QC context; not as a clinical protocol result.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
