This source is included for **Product quality, contamination, and dose uncertainty**.

**Findings:** Screening/risk-assessment of 37 RYR supplements reporting universal citrinin detection and poor compliance with stricter EU limits.

**Extracted details:**

- **Population / sample:** Thirty-seven RYR food supplements from online and retail sources in the EU context; no human participants
- **Intervention or exposure:** RYR supplements screened for mycotoxins and natural statins
- **Comparator / control:** Comparison to EU citrinin regulatory limits
- **Duration / follow-up:** Single product-screening/risk-assessment study
- **Endpoints:** citrinin concentration; EU-limit compliance; natural statin content; mycotoxin screening
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Citrinin was found in all monitored products, ranging 100-25,100 µg/kg; only four products were compliant with the EU limit in force until April 2020 and only one with the lower 100 µg/kg limit introduced after.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Strong contamination signal for citrinin in a contemporary product sample.
- **Limitations:** Sample set and EU market context may not generalize; laboratory product data do not quantify human event rates.
- **Population mismatch:** No clinical protocol outcomes.
- **Directness:** same_mechanism product-quality/safety evidence

**Why it matters:** High-priority evidence that contaminant testing must not be assumed from product category or claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** citrinin concentration; EU-limit compliance; natural statin content; mycotoxin screening

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as contamination-boundary evidence; do not extrapolate exact prevalence to all current markets.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
