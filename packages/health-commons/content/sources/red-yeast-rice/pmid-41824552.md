This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** The 2026 ACC/AHA multi-society dyslipidemia guideline includes a no-benefit/not-recommended boundary for dietary supplements used to lower LDL-C or triglycerides, including red yeast rice.

**Why it matters:** This is current clinician-guideline context that constrains how a red yeast rice protocol should frame claims relative to evidence-based lipid management.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, triglycerides, ASCVD prevention, guideline recommendation strength.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only guideline boundary; it does not replace source-specific trial extraction and does not support an unqualified red yeast rice protocol claim.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Guideline-level statement; the accessible extraction was based on official indexed snippets because publisher pages were access-limited; it summarizes evidence rather than providing a new RYR trial. Broad dyslipidemia guideline population, not a red yeast rice intervention cohort.
