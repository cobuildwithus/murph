This source is included for **Direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** Accessible extraction notes report total cholesterol -13% (-33±10 mg/dL) and LDL-C -19% (-31±4 mg/dL), with 56% achieving LDL goal and 92% tolerating treatment. Treatment was described as tolerated by most patients in accessible extraction notes; detailed AE definitions were not extracted.

**Why it matters:** Provides real-world supervised statin-intolerance context while remaining weaker than RCT evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C, LDL goal attainment, tolerability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only unless full design, denominator, and comparator details are verified.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness and boundary:** clinical_supervised. Nonrandomized/unclear design and no clean comparator extracted; participant count not extracted. Population mismatch: Statin-intolerant clinical population; context-only unless design and denominators are verified.
