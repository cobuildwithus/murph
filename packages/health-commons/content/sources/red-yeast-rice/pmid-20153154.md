This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** A pediatric crossover study tested a red yeast rice extract plus policosanol combination in children with inherited lipid disorders and reported lipid-marker reductions and no detected AST/ALT/CK safety signal over short treatment periods.

**Why it matters:** The source informs special-population safety boundaries, especially because pediatric lipid disorders differ sharply from adult OTC cholesterol self-experiments.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, total cholesterol, ApoB, HDL-C, AST/ALT, creatine kinase.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only special-population context; do not promote pediatric combination results into adult plain red yeast rice claims.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Combination product; pediatric inherited dyslipidemia population; small study; short treatment periods; no adult generalizability. Children with familial lipid disorders, not adults pursuing a general cholesterol protocol.
