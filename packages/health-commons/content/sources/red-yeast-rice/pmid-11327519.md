This source is included for **Product quality, contamination, and dose uncertainty**.

**Findings:** Chemical-profile analysis showing variable monacolin content and frequent citrinin detection among nine proprietary Chinese RYR dietary supplements.

**Extracted details:**

- **Population / sample:** Nine proprietary Chinese red yeast rice dietary supplements; no human participants
- **Intervention or exposure:** Commercial RYR supplements analyzed for monacolin profile and citrinin
- **Comparator / control:** No clinical comparator
- **Duration / follow-up:** Single laboratory analysis
- **Endpoints:** total monacolin content; monacolin profile completeness; citrinin detection
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Total monacolin content varied from 0% to 0.58% w/w; only one of nine products reportedly contained the full complement of ten monacolin compounds; citrinin was measurable in seven of nine products.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Citrinin detection in most tested products is a contamination signal; monacolin variability implies statin-like exposure uncertainty.
- **Limitations:** Small early market sample; product availability and manufacturing practices may have changed.
- **Population mismatch:** Product chemistry, not human lipid response.
- **Directness:** same_mechanism product-quality evidence

**Why it matters:** Foundational evidence that nominal RYR supplement dosing may not map reliably to active monacolin exposure.

**Potential experiment signals:** total monacolin content; monacolin profile completeness; citrinin detection

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as historical product-quality evidence; do not assume current products share the same distribution.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
