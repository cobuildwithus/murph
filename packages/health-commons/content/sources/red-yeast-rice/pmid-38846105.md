This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** Case report of severe kidney dysfunction/acute tubular injury after red yeast rice supplement ingestion; it is a safety signal, not an incidence estimate.

**Why it matters:** Peer-reviewed kidney case report connected to red yeast rice supplement exposure; useful for safety-boundary discovery but not incidence or routine-risk claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** serum-creatinine, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote this source to a direct plain red yeast rice claim without a separable RYR-only arm. Preserve the boundary: Safety-only renal adverse-event boundary.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Comparator/control:** Not applicable.

**Duration/follow-up:** Approximately 7 months exposure before kidney injury was reported.

**Safety/adverse events:** Acute kidney tubular injury and severe kidney dysfunction were reported.

**Limitations:** Single case report; causality and confounding cannot be quantified; product-specific.
