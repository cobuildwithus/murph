This source is included for **Direct trial registry and future evidence watchlist**.

**Findings:** Accessible extraction notes report less muscle fatigue symptom with RYR/Xuezhikang than simvastatin and comparable lipid-lowering effects, but exact lipid values were not extracted. Primary relevance is muscle fatigue/tolerability; detailed adverse-event table not extracted.

**Why it matters:** Safety/tolerability boundary for RYR-derived proprietary preparations versus statins.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle fatigue, lipid profile, tolerability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for adjacent tolerability context only, not for direct RYR-only efficacy claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness and boundary:** adjacent_variant. Xuezhikang/proprietary RYR-derived product, active comparator, pilot size, short duration, and exact lipid table not extracted. Population mismatch: Dyslipidemic Chinese clinical population using Xuezhikang, not an RYR-only placebo-controlled supplement trial.
