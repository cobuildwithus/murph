This source is included for **Lipid measurement and test-plan context**.

**Findings:** The consensus statement concludes that fasting is not routinely required for lipid-profile determination and emphasizes abnormal value flagging plus selected fasting repeats. This supports protocol sampling logistics only.

**Why it matters:** Foundational consensus source for nonfasting lipid panels and repeat/flagging logic.

**Potential experiment signals:** Record fasting status with each lipid panel.; Consider a fasting repeat when triglycerides are markedly elevated or LDL-C calculation is unreliable.; Use non-HDL-C as a robust lipid endpoint across fasting states..

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to justify accepting nonfasting lipid panels while documenting fasting status and repeating fasting tests when triglycerides are high.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Limitations and directness:** Directness is `measurement_context`. Consensus and laboratory-practice source, not an RCT. Does not assess red yeast rice. Fasting-repeat thresholds should be applied according to local laboratory and clinician guidance. Population mismatch: Routine lipid-testing populations are broader than red yeast rice users.
