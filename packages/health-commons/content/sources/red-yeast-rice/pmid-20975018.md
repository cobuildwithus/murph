This source is included for **Product quality, contamination, and dose uncertainty**.

**Findings:** Commercial product analysis reporting wide ranges of total monacolins and monacolin K per capsule plus elevated citrinin in four products.

**Extracted details:**

- **Population / sample:** Twelve commercial RYR formulations labeled 600 mg per capsule; no human participants
- **Intervention or exposure:** Commercial red yeast rice products analyzed by HPLC-MS/MS for monacolins and citrinin
- **Comparator / control:** No clinical comparator
- **Duration / follow-up:** Single product testing survey
- **Endpoints:** total monacolin per capsule; monacolin K per capsule; monacolin KA per capsule; citrinin contamination
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Total monacolins ranged 0.31-11.15 mg/capsule, monacolin K 0.10-10.09 mg/capsule, monacolin KA 0.00-2.30 mg/capsule; four products had elevated citrinin.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** One-third of tested products had elevated citrinin; active monacolin exposure varied widely despite identical labeled capsule size.
- **Limitations:** Small 2010 sample; product names/availability may have changed; not a clinical outcome study.
- **Population mismatch:** Product survey only; no participants taking a cholesterol protocol.
- **Directness:** same_mechanism product-quality evidence

**Why it matters:** Directly supports dose-fidelity and contamination cautions for any RYR implementation.

**Potential experiment signals:** total monacolin per capsule; monacolin K per capsule; monacolin KA per capsule; citrinin contamination

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to justify product testing/label skepticism and interaction screening; do not use as LDL-C efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
