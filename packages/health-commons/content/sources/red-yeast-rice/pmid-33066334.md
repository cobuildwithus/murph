This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** Accessible summary data report mean LDL-C change at week 8 of -32.5 ± 30.2 mg/dL (-19.8%) in the combined nutraceutical group versus +2.5 ± 19.4 mg/dL (+2.3%) with placebo.

**Why it matters:** Modern placebo-controlled RYR-containing combination trial; registered and open access.

**Potential experiment signals:** ldl-c, non-hdl-c, total-cholesterol, hdl-c, apob, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote this source to a direct plain red yeast rice claim without a separable RYR-only arm. Preserve the boundary: Modern multi-ingredient combination product rather than plain RYR.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Comparator/control:** Placebo.

**Duration/follow-up:** 8 weeks primary endpoint; accessible metadata also describes cholesterol control over 6 months.

**Safety/adverse events:** Adverse-event details were not extracted from accessible metadata.

**Limitations:** Phytosterol/RYR/niacin/policosanol combination; no RYR-only arm.
