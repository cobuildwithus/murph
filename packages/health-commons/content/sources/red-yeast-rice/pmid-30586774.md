This source is included for **Lipid measurement and test-plan context**.

**Findings:** The guideline frames cholesterol management around ASCVD risk and lipid response monitoring after treatment changes. It supports measurement planning but does not evaluate red yeast rice.

**Why it matters:** Useful older U.S. guideline still commonly referenced for follow-up lipid measurement intervals and risk-enhancing factor context.

**Potential experiment signals:** Repeat lipid panel after lipid-lowering intervention changes.; Track LDL-C and non-HDL-C as main lipid outcomes.; Use risk-enhancing factors to determine whether clinician supervision is needed..

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as historical U.S. guideline context for lipid monitoring; prefer the 2026 update for current U.S. guideline framing where applicable.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Limitations and directness:** Directness is `general_guideline`. Replaced by newer 2026 U.S. guideline in this batch. Does not test red yeast rice. Clinical medication pathways may not apply to supplement-only self-experiments. Population mismatch: Clinical cholesterol-management populations may include medication indications and risk levels outside consumer red yeast rice experimentation.
