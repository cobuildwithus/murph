This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** Accessible metadata reports the combined red yeast rice/fish-oil supplement was well tolerated and significantly improved LDL-C, total cholesterol, ApoB, hs-CRP, and endothelial function.

**Why it matters:** Helps cover hsCRP/endothelial outcomes while preserving the combination-product limitation.

**Potential experiment signals:** ldl-c, total-cholesterol, apob, high-sensitivity-c-reactive-protein, endothelial-function, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote this source to a direct plain red yeast rice claim without a separable RYR-only arm. Preserve the boundary: PUFA/fish-oil co-intervention, context-only for plain RYR.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Comparator/control:** Placebo.

**Duration/follow-up:** 8 weeks in accessible summary data.

**Safety/adverse events:** The supplement was described as very well tolerated; detailed adverse-event counts were not extracted.

**Limitations:** Combination of RYR and fish oil; no RYR-only arm.
