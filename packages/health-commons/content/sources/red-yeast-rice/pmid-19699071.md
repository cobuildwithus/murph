This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** The trial evaluated a berberine/RYR/policosanol combination and reported lipid and endothelial-function effects; this extraction did not verify numeric effects from full text.

**Why it matters:** Useful for vascular-function context but RYR effect is not separable.

**Potential experiment signals:** endothelial-function, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote this source to a direct plain red yeast rice claim without a separable RYR-only arm. Preserve the boundary: Combination stack with vascular endpoint; context-only for plain RYR.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Comparator/control:** Placebo.

**Duration/follow-up:** Duration not extracted from accessible metadata.

**Safety/adverse events:** Adverse-event details were not extracted from accessible metadata.

**Limitations:** Multi-ingredient combination; no RYR-only arm; numeric effects not extracted.
