This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** Accessible metadata reports reduced cholesterol levels and improved endothelial function; flow-mediated dilation reportedly increased by 18.8% after 8 weeks and 39.3% at end of treatment.

**Why it matters:** Useful for endothelial-function gap mapping but not as direct protocol evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** total-cholesterol, triglycerides, hdl-c, endothelial-function, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote this source to a direct plain red yeast rice claim without a separable RYR-only arm. Preserve the boundary: Endothelial-function combination-product context.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Comparator/control:** Placebo plus controlled diet.

**Duration/follow-up:** Duration not fully extracted; accessible metadata reports assessments through the end of treatment.

**Safety/adverse events:** No adverse events or musculoskeletal disorders were reported in either group in accessible summary data.

**Limitations:** Combined nutraceutical and diet context; RYR not separable.
