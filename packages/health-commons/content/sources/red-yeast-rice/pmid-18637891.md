This source is included for **Safety reviews and pharmacovigilance**.

**Findings:** The Italian surveillance analysis identified nine muscle-disorder reports from April 2002 to December 2007; four involved red yeast rice used for hypercholesterolemia, all red yeast rice cases had increased CPK, and onset ranged from about 2 to 6 months.

**Why it matters:** Early pharmacovigilance signal showing red yeast rice-associated myopathy reports in real-world supplement use, including prior statin intolerance.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle pain, CPK/CK elevation, statin-intolerance history, product exposure timing.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a safety signal: red yeast rice myopathy reports exist and statin-intolerance history is a key caution point.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
