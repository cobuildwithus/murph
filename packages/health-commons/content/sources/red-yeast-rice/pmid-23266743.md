This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** Accessible abstract text reports that twice the recommended dose of the dietary supplement reduced LDL-C and appeared safe; numeric effects were not extracted.

**Why it matters:** Combination supplement trial with untreated adults; important not to treat as RYR-only evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** ldl-c, total-cholesterol, triglycerides, hdl-c, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote this source to a direct plain red yeast rice claim without a separable RYR-only arm. Preserve the boundary: Combination plant-extract supplement context.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Comparator/control:** Usual-dose supplement and placebo.

**Duration/follow-up:** 4 weeks of supplementation with withdrawal follow-up in the study design.

**Safety/adverse events:** Safety biomarkers were evaluated; accessible abstract described the supplement as appearing safe.

**Limitations:** Dose/formulation combination trial; no RYR-only arm; short duration.
