This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** The trial compared a low-monacolin RYR/artichoke supplement with Armolipid Plus and placebo; exact lipid results were not extracted from accessible metadata.

**Why it matters:** Useful to classify modern low-monacolin/combination products separately from RYR-only protocol evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** ldl-c, total-cholesterol, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote this source to a direct plain red yeast rice claim without a separable RYR-only arm. Preserve the boundary: Modern low-dose monacolin combination comparator trial.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Comparator/control:** Armolipid Plus® (RYR plus berberine combination) and placebo.

**Duration/follow-up:** Duration not extracted from accessible metadata.

**Safety/adverse events:** Adverse-event details were not extracted from accessible metadata.

**Limitations:** Comparator product is also a combination; low monacolin and artichoke co-intervention prevent plain RYR attribution.
