This source is included for **Safety reviews and pharmacovigilance**.

**Findings:** EFSA considered monacolin K in lactone form chemically identical to lovastatin and noted that supplement exposure could overlap with therapeutic lovastatin dose ranges.

**Why it matters:** Defines the regulatory safety boundary for monacolin-containing red yeast rice products, especially dose uncertainty and vulnerable subgroup risk.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle symptoms, rhabdomyolysis, liver injury, drug-interaction review, product monacolin content.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a safety/regulatory boundary: EFSA could not identify a monacolin intake level from red yeast rice that does not raise health concerns.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
