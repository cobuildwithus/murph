This source is included for **Product quality, contamination, and dose uncertainty**.

**Findings:** Citrinin-focused analysis of 15 Polish fermented-red-rice supplements reporting no samples above LOD.

**Extracted details:**

- **Population / sample:** Fifteen dietary supplements containing fermented red rice extract from the Polish market; no human participants
- **Intervention or exposure:** HPLC-FLD citrinin analysis after immunoaffinity clean-up
- **Comparator / control:** No clinical comparator
- **Duration / follow-up:** Single product-testing survey
- **Endpoints:** citrinin above LOD; supplement formulation details; declared monacolin K context
- **Effect estimates or direction:** No analyzed sample contained citrinin above the reported LOD.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Preserves a null contamination finding: not all contemporary regional samples show detectable citrinin.
- **Limitations:** Small Polish market sample; citrinin only, not comprehensive contamination or active-dose validation.
- **Population mismatch:** No human cholesterol or safety follow-up.
- **Directness:** measurement_context for RYR supplements

**Why it matters:** Important counterbalance to positive citrinin surveys; contamination risk is product- and market-dependent.

**Potential experiment signals:** citrinin above LOD; supplement formulation details; declared monacolin K context

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a null contamination finding while still requiring product-specific QC.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
