This source is included for **Product quality, contamination, and dose uncertainty**.

**Findings:** Method source for simultaneous lovastatin and citrinin determination in RYR supplements, with observed differences in supplement lovastatin-form contents.

**Extracted details:**

- **Population / sample:** Red yeast rice dietary supplements analyzed in a laboratory method study; no human participants
- **Intervention or exposure:** Simultaneous determination of lovastatin lactone/hydroxy acid forms and citrinin
- **Comparator / control:** No clinical comparator
- **Duration / follow-up:** Laboratory method development
- **Endpoints:** lovastatin lactone measurement; lovastatin hydroxy acid measurement; citrinin measurement; standardization need
- **Effect estimates or direction:** The source reports a rapid method and notes considerably different lovastatin-form contents in dietary supplements, implying a standardization need.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Supports simultaneous active-compound and contaminant monitoring.
- **Limitations:** Analytical method source; does not establish market prevalence or clinical outcomes beyond tested samples.
- **Population mismatch:** No human efficacy or adverse-event follow-up.
- **Directness:** measurement_context for RYR supplement QC

**Why it matters:** Shows that dose uncertainty can be measured and that lactone/acid forms matter for product characterization.

**Potential experiment signals:** lovastatin lactone measurement; lovastatin hydroxy acid measurement; citrinin measurement; standardization need

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as QC method context; not a protocol efficacy source.
