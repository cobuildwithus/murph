This source is included for **Evidence syntheses and reviews**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-35111069-high-quality-rct-lipids` — RYR alone reduced LDL-C versus placebo by −35.82 mg/dL and versus other nutraceuticals by −14.40 mg/dL; compared with statins, RYR was reported as comparable for LDL-C/HDL-C, more effective for triglycerides, and less effective for total cholesterol.
- `finding:pmid-35111069-adverse-events` — Adverse events were not significantly increased overall (RR 1.18, 95% CI 0.91 to 1.54), though events such as gastrointestinal discomfort, myalgia, dizziness, and rash appeared in included trials.
- `finding:pmid-35111069-limitations` — The review noted heterogeneity from manufacturers/active ingredients and that the longest trial lasted 24 weeks, limiting long-term safety and durability conclusions.

**Why it matters:** This more recent synthesis filters to higher-quality RCTs and gives comparator-specific lipid estimates.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, total cholesterol, triglycerides, HDL-C, ApoB, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as high-priority direct evidence for lipid biomarker effects, while explicitly separating whole RYR from Xuezhikang and combination-therapy variants.
