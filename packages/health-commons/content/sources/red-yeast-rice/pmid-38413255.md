This source is included for **Safety reviews and pharmacovigilance**.

**Findings:** The umbrella review included 15 meta-analyses and extracted 61 unique adverse-outcome associations; AMSTAR 2 rated 3 meta-analyses low confidence and 12 critically low confidence.

**Why it matters:** Provides the broadest RCT-synthesis safety overview while grading the credibility of adverse-outcome associations.

**Potential experiment signals:** ALT/AST, uric acid, adverse health outcomes, methodological credibility, meta-analysis overlap.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety synthesis context with a credibility caveat: generally reassuring RCT meta-analyses are not high-certainty rare-event proof.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
