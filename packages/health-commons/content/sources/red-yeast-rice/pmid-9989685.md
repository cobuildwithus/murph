This source is included for **Direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** RYR significantly reduced total cholesterol, LDL-C, and triglycerides while HDL-C was unchanged; accessible extraction notes include total cholesterol reduction from 254±36 to 208±31 mg/dL by week 8 and approximate TC -16% and LDL-C -22% by 12 weeks. Detailed adverse-event extraction was not completed in this batch.

**Why it matters:** One of the earliest direct RYR supplement trials in a Western diet/lipid context.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C, triglycerides, HDL-C, safety.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports lipid-lowering direction but must be labeled historical/proprietary-product evidence.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness and boundary:** direct_protocol. Older proprietary Cholestin-era product; composition and regulatory status may differ from current products. Population mismatch: Hyperlipidemic adults not on lipid-lowering medication; product availability/composition mismatch is substantial.
