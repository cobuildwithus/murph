This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** The study evaluated nutraceutical drink formulations with and without RYR; numerical effects were not extracted from accessible metadata.

**Why it matters:** May be useful for consumer-product format and tolerability context.

**Potential experiment signals:** ldl-c, total-cholesterol, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote this source to a direct plain red yeast rice claim without a separable RYR-only arm. Preserve the boundary: Formulation/context evidence only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Comparator/control:** Drink formulations without red yeast rice and/or comparator formulations; exact allocation not fully extracted.

**Duration/follow-up:** Duration not extracted from accessible metadata.

**Safety/adverse events:** Tolerability was evaluated, but adverse-event details were not extracted.

**Limitations:** Drink-format combination/formulation study, not a plain RYR capsule trial.
