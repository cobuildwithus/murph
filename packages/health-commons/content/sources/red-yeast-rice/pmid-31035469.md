This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** Compared with placebo, the nutraceutical reduced total cholesterol by 11.4% and 14.1%, LDL-C by 19.8% and 19.7%, and ApoB by 12.4% and 13.5% at one and three months, respectively; hs-CRP decreased significantly.

**Why it matters:** Open-access RCT of a commercial RYR-containing combination; useful for combination-boundary and inflammation context.

**Potential experiment signals:** ldl-c, total-cholesterol, apob, high-sensitivity-c-reactive-protein, alanine-aminotransferase, serum-creatinine, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote this source to a direct plain red yeast rice claim without a separable RYR-only arm. Preserve the boundary: Commercial multi-ingredient combination evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Comparator/control:** Placebo.

**Duration/follow-up:** 90 days with assessments at 1 and 3 months.

**Safety/adverse events:** No adverse effects were noted in the trial report.

**Limitations:** Combination of phytosterols, RYR, hydroxytyrosol, and vitamin E; no RYR-only arm; longer studies were requested by authors.
