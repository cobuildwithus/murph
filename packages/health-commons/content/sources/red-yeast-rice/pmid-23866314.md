This source is included for **Direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** LDL-C decreased by 36 mg/dL (22%) and total cholesterol by 37 mg/dL (15%) in the RYR group; controls had no LDL-C or total-cholesterol reduction in accessible extraction. Secondary outcomes included side effects and CK; detailed AE extraction was not completed in this batch.

**Why it matters:** Provides a direct, accessible RCT with absolute and percent LDL-C change estimates.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, total cholesterol, side effects, CK, cardiovascular risk estimate.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports an 8-week LDL-C and total-cholesterol signal, with full-text extraction recommended for product details and AEs.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness and boundary:** direct_protocol. Small educated health-professional sample; product and dose details should be verified from full text before implementation. Population mismatch: Physicians/spouses may not represent broader patient populations or higher-risk medication users.
