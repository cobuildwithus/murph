This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** A systematic review of 10 RCTs involving 905 Chinese subjects with dyslipidaemia found no statistically significant differences between red yeast rice and simvastatin for the examined lipid outcomes, while emphasizing small-trial limitations.

**Why it matters:** This offers statin-comparator context but is not direct evidence for OTC red yeast rice, and the review’s included trials were geographically and methodologically limited.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, total cholesterol, HDL-C, triglycerides, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only statin-comparator evidence; do not treat as direct confirmation that an OTC red yeast rice protocol equals statin therapy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Paywalled full text; accessible extraction relies on abstract-level material; included trials were small and Chinese; clinical cardiovascular outcomes were not the central evidence base. Dyslipidaemia trials in Chinese participants comparing RYR with simvastatin, not a general OTC red yeast rice self-experiment.
