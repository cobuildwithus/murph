This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** In MI patients, RYR extract was associated with lower nonfatal MI (RR 0.42), revascularization (RR 0.58), sudden death (RR 0.71), LDL-C (WMD -20.70 mg/dL), total cholesterol (WMD -26.61 mg/dL), triglycerides (WMD -24.69 mg/dL), and higher HDL-C (WMD 2.71 mg/dL) in accessible summary data.

**Why it matters:** Secondary-prevention/CV-event population differs from general cholesterol protocol; useful boundary evidence and likely Xuezhikang-heavy.

**Potential experiment signals:** ldl-c, total-cholesterol, triglycerides, hdl-c, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote this source to a direct plain red yeast rice claim without a separable RYR-only arm. Preserve the boundary: Post-MI/borderline-hypercholesterolemia population, not the target self-experiment protocol population.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Comparator/control:** Control/placebo/usual care across included RCTs.

**Duration/follow-up:** Follow-up ranged from 4 weeks to 4.5 years in accessible summary data.

**Safety/adverse events:** This extraction did not identify detailed adverse-event rates.

**Limitations:** Secondary-prevention MI population and likely proprietary Chinese preparation-heavy evidence; not a general primary-prevention OTC RYR estimate.
