This source is included for **Direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** LDL-C decreased by 43 mg/dL at 12 weeks and 35 mg/dL at 24 weeks in the RYR arm versus 11 and 15 mg/dL in placebo; between-group differences were significant at both time points. Total cholesterol also differed significantly. HDL-C, triglycerides, liver enzymes, CPK, weight, and pain severity did not differ significantly between groups in accessible extraction notes.

**Why it matters:** Clinically relevant direct RYR evidence for people who stopped statins because of muscle symptoms.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, total cholesterol, HDL-C, triglycerides, liver enzymes, CPK, pain severity, weight.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports LDL-C lowering with clinician-supervised monitoring in statin-intolerant adults, not a blanket substitute for statins.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness and boundary:** direct_protocol. Small single-site study, short follow-up for chronic use, and co-intervention with a lifestyle program. Population mismatch: Statin-intolerant population; may not represent general primary-prevention users or people tolerating statins.
