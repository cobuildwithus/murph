This source is included for **Interactions, contraindications, and population boundaries**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-28601545-ryr-not-statin-substitute` — The review argues that red yeast rice preparations should not be treated as acceptable statin substitutes because their therapeutic and adverse-effect profiles are statin-like while product dose and composition are not reliably standardized.
- `finding:pmid-28601545-monacolin-variability-safety` — The review states that large variability in monacolin K content and other ingredients makes dose-related efficacy and side-effect risk difficult to predict for commercial red yeast rice products.

**Why it matters:** Targets a common protocol misuse: treating an unstandardized supplement as interchangeable with a prescription statin.

**Potential experiment signals:** product-monacolin-K-verification, clinician-review-required, LDL-C, ALT/AST, CK, adverse-symptom-log.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a safety and positioning boundary: do not present red yeast rice as a reliable statin substitute, and require product-quality caveats for dose/effect interpretation.

**Limitations:** Narrative review/commentary; not a protocol trial; publisher full text may require permission and extracted detail is limited to accessible abstract/metadata.

**Population mismatch:** Focused on substitution for statins and on patients refusing or unable to tolerate statins, which is narrower than general cholesterol self-experimentation.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
