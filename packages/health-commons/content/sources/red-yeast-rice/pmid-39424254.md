This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** A case series described three patients with acute kidney injury, renal tubular acidosis, and Fanconi syndrome after consuming a red yeast rice-containing supplement implicated in a Japanese public-health incident.

**Why it matters:** This is a high-priority safety boundary for product-specific renal toxicity and adverse-event surveillance.

**Potential experiment signals:** acute kidney injury, renal tubular acidosis, Fanconi syndrome, kidney biopsy findings, renal recovery, product discontinuation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only adverse-event evidence; do not use it to claim all red yeast rice products cause Fanconi syndrome, but include renal warning and product-quality caveats.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Case series without control group; product-specific outbreak context; causality and contaminant mechanism may not generalize to all red yeast rice products. Severe adverse-event cases from one product/outbreak context, not planned cholesterol-lowering use in screened participants.
