This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** A 1-month triple-blind RCT added a red yeast rice-containing Lesstat tablet to statin therapy and found a significant total-cholesterol change but no significant LDL-C, triglyceride, or hsCRP advantage; liver-function safety appeared acceptable over the short follow-up.

**Why it matters:** This is statin-combination context and a safety boundary for stacking monacolin exposure with statins, but not evidence for standalone red yeast rice.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C, HDL-C, triglycerides, hsCRP, AST, ALT, alkaline phosphatase, bilirubin.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only/statin-combination context; do not use as direct efficacy support for standalone red yeast rice.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Very short one-month follow-up; add-on to statin therapy; combination tablet contained other ingredients; baseline imbalance and small sample; no standalone red yeast rice arm. Dyslipidemia patients already on statins, not statin-free OTC red yeast rice users.
