This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** Accessible abstract text reports reduced serum triglyceride and cholesterol levels with no appreciable adverse effects; the intervention is a Monascus-fermented garlic extract rather than plain RYR.

**Why it matters:** Should be kept separate from RYR supplement claims despite Monascus overlap.

**Potential experiment signals:** total-cholesterol, triglycerides, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote this source to a direct plain red yeast rice claim without a separable RYR-only arm. Preserve the boundary: Monascus product context, not protocol evidence for RYR supplements.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Comparator/control:** Placebo.

**Duration/follow-up:** Duration not extracted from accessible metadata.

**Safety/adverse events:** No appreciable adverse effects were reported in accessible abstract text.

**Limitations:** Adjacent Monascus-fermented product, not red yeast rice; RYR contribution not present/separable.
