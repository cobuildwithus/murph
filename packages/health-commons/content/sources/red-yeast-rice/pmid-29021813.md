This source is included for **Direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** RYR-only arm reduced total cholesterol by 16.1%, LDL-C by 20.5%, and ApoB by 14.4%; combination arm reduced total cholesterol by 18.5%, LDL-C by 23.0%, and ApoB by 19.0% and had an additional LDL-C reduction compared with RYR alone in accessible extraction notes. Detailed adverse-event extraction was not completed in this batch.

**Why it matters:** Lets the protocol separate direct RYR-only lipid effects from additive-combination claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C, ApoB, hs-CRP, triglycerides.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use the RYR-only arm as direct evidence; treat phytosterol-plus-RYR results as adjacent combination evidence.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness and boundary:** direct_protocol_with_adjacent_combination_arm. Comparator structure is not a classic RYR-versus-placebo contrast in accessible extraction; combination arm is adjacent evidence. Population mismatch: Moderately hypercholesterolemic adults; not statin-intolerant or high-risk secondary-prevention population.
