This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** Compared with placebo, the nutraceutical combination reduced total cholesterol by about 16.7%, LDL-C by 25.7%, non-HDL-C by 24%, and ApoB by 17% in accessible summary data.

**Why it matters:** A commonly cited RYR/probiotic stack; useful only as adjacent evidence unless RYR-only arm is separable.

**Potential experiment signals:** ldl-c, non-hdl-c, total-cholesterol, hdl-c, apob, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote this source to a direct plain red yeast rice claim without a separable RYR-only arm. Preserve the boundary: RYR-probiotic combination product, not plain RYR.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Comparator/control:** Placebo.

**Duration/follow-up:** 12 weeks.

**Safety/adverse events:** No adverse effects were observed and compliance was reported at 97% in accessible summary data.

**Limitations:** Small trial and probiotic/niacin/CoQ10 combination; RYR contribution cannot be isolated.
