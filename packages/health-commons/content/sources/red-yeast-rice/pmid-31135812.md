This source is included for **Lipid measurement and test-plan context**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Paired fasting/nonfasting lipid outcomes analysis within ASCOT-LLA (2019).
- **People or setting studied:** ASCOT-LLA participants with paired nonfasting and fasting lipid values; predominantly male, older cardiovascular-risk population.
- **Exposure or intervention:** Nonfasting lipid levels measured about four weeks before randomization.
- **Comparator or control:** Fasting lipid levels at randomization, with fasting defined as at least eight hours.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Median follow-up 3.3 years for major coronary events in extracted summary.
- **Role in Murph:** context-only / measurement_context.

## Findings

In 8,270 ASCOT-LLA participants with paired lipid testing, fasting and nonfasting LDL-C and non-HDL-C showed similar associations with major coronary events, with high risk-classification concordance in extracted summaries.

**Effect estimate or direction extracted:** Search-extracted summary reports similar adjusted hazard ratios for major coronary events: LDL-C nonfasting 1.32 (95% CI 1.08 to 1.61) and fasting 1.28 (95% CI 1.07 to 1.55); non-HDL-C nonfasting 1.33 (95% CI 1.09 to 1.61) and fasting 1.23 (95% CI 1.04 to 1.46); risk-classification concordance was high.

## Why it matters

This is useful evidence for interpreting whether a nonfasting panel is still clinically meaningful, while still separating risk prediction from within-person protocol response.

## Potential experiment signals

## Protocol takeaway

Nonfasting lipid values can carry risk information; for protocol response, compare panels collected under similar conditions.
