This source is included for **Lipid measurement and test-plan context**.

**Findings:** The guideline is useful for interpreting LDL-C alongside non-HDL-C and apoB, particularly when triglycerides affect LDL-C interpretation. It is guideline context only.

**Why it matters:** Important North American guideline that explicitly prioritizes non-HDL-C or apoB in selected triglyceride contexts.

**Potential experiment signals:** Non-HDL-C or apoB when triglycerides complicate LDL-C interpretation.; Adult risk-stratified lipid follow-up.; Clinician escalation for high-risk lipid phenotypes..

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for endpoint selection and triglyceride-aware interpretation; not as evidence that red yeast rice works.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Limitations and directness:** Directness is `measurement_context`. Canadian clinical guideline; jurisdiction-specific. Not a red yeast rice trial. Clinical thresholds may not map to self-experiment goals. Population mismatch: Adult Canadian prevention populations and treatment thresholds may differ from consumer red yeast rice users.
