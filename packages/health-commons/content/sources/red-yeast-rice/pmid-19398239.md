This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** Case report of acute hepatitis during use of a multi-ingredient lipid-lowering product containing red yeast rice extract; causality and RYR-specific contribution cannot be isolated.

**Why it matters:** Hepatology case report showing liver injury in a multi-ingredient/product context; useful but should be kept product-specific.

**Potential experiment signals:** alanine-aminotransferase, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote this source to a direct plain red yeast rice claim without a separable RYR-only arm. Preserve the boundary: Safety signal only; not efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Comparator/control:** Not applicable.

**Duration/follow-up:** Approximately 6 months of product use before hepatitis was reported.

**Safety/adverse events:** Acute hepatitis/severe liver enzyme elevation was reported.

**Limitations:** Single case report; multi-ingredient product; cannot estimate incidence or isolate RYR.
