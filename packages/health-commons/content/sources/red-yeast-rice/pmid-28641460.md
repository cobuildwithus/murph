This source is included for **Product quality, contamination, and dose uncertainty**.

**Findings:** Analysis of 28 RYR supplement brands showing absent or highly variable monacolin K content across products.

**Extracted details:**

- **Population / sample:** Twenty-eight brands of red yeast rice supplements purchased from mainstream retailers; no human participants
- **Intervention or exposure:** Commercial RYR supplements analyzed for monacolin K / strength
- **Comparator / control:** No clinical comparator; product-to-product comparison
- **Duration / follow-up:** Single product-testing survey
- **Endpoints:** monacolin K detected/not detected; monacolin K per 1200 mg RYR; manufacturer daily serving monacolin K exposure
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Monacolin K was not detected in 2 of 28 brands; among products containing it, monacolin K ranged more than 60-fold from 0.09 to 5.48 mg per 1200 mg RYR, and manufacturer daily servings could vary by more than 120-fold.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Wide active-dose variability creates both efficacy uncertainty and statin-like exposure uncertainty.
- **Limitations:** Mainstream-retailer sample from one market/time; not a clinical outcome study.
- **Population mismatch:** No human trial cohort.
- **Directness:** same_mechanism product-quality evidence

**Why it matters:** Directly undermines assumptions that a labeled RYR dose reliably predicts active monacolin exposure.

**Potential experiment signals:** monacolin K detected/not detected; monacolin K per 1200 mg RYR; manufacturer daily serving monacolin K exposure

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as dose-fidelity evidence requiring product-specific active-dose verification.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
