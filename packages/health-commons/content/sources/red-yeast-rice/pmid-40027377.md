This source is included for **Safety reviews and pharmacovigilance**.

**Findings:** After reviewing additional analytical, intake, in vitro, nutrivigilance, post-marketing, case-report, and clinical data, EFSA concluded the new data reiterated the 2018 concerns and did not allow identification of a daily intake below 3 mg that does not raise health concerns.

**Why it matters:** Current EFSA boundary source: it updates the regulatory safety assessment after new submitted data and preserves the no-safe-threshold concern.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle symptoms, rhabdomyolysis, liver injury, adverse-event reporting, drug-interaction review.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as the current safety boundary: additional data did not allow EFSA to identify a daily intake of monacolins from red yeast rice that does not raise concerns.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
