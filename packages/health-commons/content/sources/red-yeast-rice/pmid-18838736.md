**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-18838736-symptomatic-hepatitis`: A case report described symptomatic hepatitis associated with herbal red yeast rice use; causality, incidence, and detailed confounders cannot be established from the accessible metadata alone.

**Why it matters:** Red yeast rice protocols often focus on LDL lowering; this source preserves the adverse-event boundary for liver injury.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- Baseline liver disease and alcohol-use screening
- ALT, AST, bilirubin if symptoms or clinician-guided use
- Jaundice, dark urine, pale stool, fever, fatigue symptom alerts
- Stop criteria for hepatitis-like symptoms

**Protocol takeaway:** Users with liver disease, hepatitis symptoms, or unexplained liver enzyme elevations should not self-escalate red yeast rice and should seek clinician guidance.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
