This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** Compared with baseline, the active combination reduced total cholesterol and LDL-C at weeks 4, 12, and 24; accessible metadata reports LDL-C changes around -29.4%, -25.6%, and -23.7% at those timepoints.

**Why it matters:** Backfills a frequently cited nutraceutical combination trial; not RYR-alone evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** ldl-c, total-cholesterol, triglycerides, hdl-c, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote this source to a direct plain red yeast rice claim without a separable RYR-only arm. Preserve the boundary: Combination-product evidence only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Comparator/control:** Placebo added to the same dietary run-in.

**Duration/follow-up:** 24 weeks after a 3-week diet run-in; assessments at 4, 12, and 24 weeks.

**Safety/adverse events:** Tolerability was evaluated; this extraction did not identify a specific serious adverse-event signal from accessible metadata.

**Limitations:** Common Armolipid-like multi-ingredient stack; RYR contribution cannot be separated.
