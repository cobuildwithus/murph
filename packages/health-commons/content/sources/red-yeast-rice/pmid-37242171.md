This source is included for **Guidelines and external protocol claims**.

**Findings:** The review reports an approximate 15-34% LDL-C reduction range, mentions secondary-prevention ASCVD-event reductions, and summarizes low-dose monacolin K tolerability.

**Why it matters:** This review is helpful for effect-direction context, but its secondary-prevention and review-level nature require careful claim boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C is central; total cholesterol, triglycerides, HDL-C, ASCVD events, and adverse events are secondary or adjacent signals.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for background and hypothesis framing, not as the sole source for protocol claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
