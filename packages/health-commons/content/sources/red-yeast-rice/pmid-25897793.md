This source is included for **Evidence syntheses and reviews**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-25897793-ldl-placebo-statin` — RYR lowered LDL-C versus placebo by −1.02 mmol/L (95% CI −1.20 to −0.83) and did not differ significantly from statin therapy for LDL-C (mean difference 0.03 mmol/L, 95% CI −0.36 to 0.41).
- `finding:pmid-25897793-safety-uncertain` — Kidney/liver abnormalities were reported in fewer than 5% of participants and myopathy rates did not differ, but the authors judged safety assessment and adverse-event reporting to be insufficient for firm safety conclusions.

**Why it matters:** This source directly separates LDL-C efficacy from the evidence gap around safety reporting.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, adverse events, liver injury, kidney injury, myopathy.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for the protocol as mixed evidence: LDL-C lowering is supported, but safety remains an explicit uncertainty boundary.
