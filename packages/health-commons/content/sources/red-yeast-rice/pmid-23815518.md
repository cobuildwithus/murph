This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** Accessible metadata reports reductions in LDL-C and apolipoprotein B100 without significant changes in HDL-C, triglycerides, CK, LDH, CoQ10, liver markers, or renal markers.

**Why it matters:** RYR-containing combination trial reporting lipid and safety parameters; boundary evidence only.

**Potential experiment signals:** ldl-c, triglycerides, hdl-c, apob, creatine-kinase, alanine-aminotransferase, serum-creatinine, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote this source to a direct plain red yeast rice claim without a separable RYR-only arm. Preserve the boundary: Combination supplement with safety context, not plain RYR evidence.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Comparator/control:** Placebo.

**Duration/follow-up:** 16 weeks.

**Safety/adverse events:** No significant changes in extracted safety biomarkers were reported in accessible metadata.

**Limitations:** Multi-ingredient supplement; safety-only in this batch because RYR-specific effects are not separable.
