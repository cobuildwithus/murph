This source is included for **Safety reviews and pharmacovigilance**.

**Findings:** The update reported very small proportions of FAERS musculoskeletal and hepatobiliary cases linked to red yeast rice and, in CAERS, 34 adverse muscle events and 10 adverse liver events where red yeast rice was the suspect product, with additional concomitant-product reports.

**Why it matters:** Adds newer reporting-system counts and explicitly focuses on muscle symptoms and liver dysfunction, the main safety endpoints for protocol stop rules.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle symptoms, liver dysfunction, FAERS/CAERS report context, meta-analysis caveats.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a balanced safety context: reporting-system counts appear low among reports but cannot be interpreted as population risk.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
