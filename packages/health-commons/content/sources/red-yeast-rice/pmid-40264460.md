This source is included for **Safety case reports and adverse-event signals**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-40264460-fulminant-rhabdomyolysis-liver-hyperkalemia`: A 68-year-old woman developed life-threatening fulminant rhabdomyolysis with severe hyperkalemia, tetraparesis, impending compartment syndrome, and liver injury after starting an over-the-counter red-yeast-rice-containing supplement; authors note the causal link is implied rather than definitively proven.

**Why it matters:** Shows a severe tail-risk signal and documents explicit causal limits, useful for conservative safety framing.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- Emergency warning signs: severe weakness, paralysis/tetraparesis, dark urine, syncope
- Creatine kinase, myoglobin, potassium, creatinine, ALT/AST in symptomatic cases
- Supplement disclosure and product-dose documentation
- Clinician-guided exclusion for high-risk or medically complex users

**Protocol takeaway:** Even low-dose or short-duration red yeast rice exposures should have clear stop/emergency criteria for severe muscle symptoms, weakness, or dark urine.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction details

**Population:** One 68-year-old female patient with psoriasis treated with apremilast and no prior statin-based lipid-lowering medication reported in the article.

**Exposure:** An over-the-counter supplement containing red yeast rice extracts; symptoms occurred two days after beginning self-treatment, with reported low monacolin K dose.

**Comparator/control:** None; single-patient case report.

**Duration/follow-up:** Symptoms occurred after approximately 2 days of self-treatment; one-year follow-up reportedly showed regained functionality.

**Endpoints:**
- Fulminant rhabdomyolysis
- Severe hyperkalemia with ECG changes
- Tetraparesis
- Impending compartment syndrome
- Acute liver injury / transaminases
- Renal replacement therapy / myoglobin adsorption

**Effect estimates or direction:** Adverse-event direction only: life-threatening rhabdomyolysis with hyperkalemia, tetraparesis, and liver injury occurred after a few tablets of red-yeast-rice-containing product. The authors state causality is implied rather than definitively proven.

**Adverse events / safety notes:** Fulminant rhabdomyolysis, severe hyperkalemia, tetraparesis, impending compartment syndrome, and acute liver injury requiring intensive care and extracorporeal blood purification.

**Limitations:**
- Single case report; authors explicitly state the causal link is implied rather than definitively proven.
- Genetic predisposition or unidentified environmental confounders cannot be ruled out.
- Liver injury interpretation may be complicated by rhabdomyolysis-related transaminase elevations; liver biopsy was not performed.

**Population mismatch:** Older adult with psoriasis/apremilast context and ICU-level presentation; not representative of average protocol users.
