This source is included for **Direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** Accessible extraction notes report significant decreases in total cholesterol, LDL-C, and triglycerides, but exact effect estimates were not extracted. Safety/tolerability was evaluated, but detailed adverse-event results were not extracted.

**Why it matters:** Provides clinical context for Monascus-containing products but is not clean RYR-only evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C, triglycerides, tolerability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for adjacent-product context only until full text confirms product composition, comparator, and effect tables.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness and boundary:** adjacent_variant. Multi-ingredient supplement and non-placebo comparison; exact effects and AE details require full text. Population mismatch: Low-risk Caucasian subjects and multi-ingredient product; adjacent to RYR-only cholesterol protocol.
