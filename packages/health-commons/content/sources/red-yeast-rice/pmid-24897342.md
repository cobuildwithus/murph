This source is included for **Evidence syntheses and reviews**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-24897342-ryr-placebo-lipids` — RYR reduced total cholesterol by −0.97 mmol/L, triglycerides by −0.23 mmol/L, and LDL-C by −0.87 mmol/L versus placebo; HDL-C change was not significant (+0.08 mmol/L, p=0.11).
- `finding:pmid-24897342-short-term-safety` — No serious side effects were reported and ALT, AST, creatinine, creatine kinase, fasting glucose, and muscle pain did not differ significantly versus placebo in the analyzed short-term trials.

**Why it matters:** This synthesis is comparatively close to supplement-style RYR use because it excluded Xuezhikang and Zhibituo.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, total cholesterol, triglycerides, HDL-C, ALT, AST, creatinine, creatine kinase.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as direct RYR supplement efficacy evidence for LDL-C, total cholesterol, and triglycerides; do not overstate HDL-C benefit.
