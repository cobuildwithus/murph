This source is included for **Lipid measurement and test-plan context**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** NHANES III fasting versus nonfasting LDL-C mortality analysis (2014).
- **People or setting studied:** NHANES III participants with fasting or nonfasting LDL-C measurements linked to mortality follow-up.
- **Exposure or intervention:** Fasting LDL-C versus nonfasting LDL-C measurement status.
- **Comparator or control:** Fasting versus nonfasting groups, including propensity-matched analyses in secondary summaries.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Mean/long-term follow-up reported as about 14 years in secondary summaries.
- **Role in Murph:** context-only / measurement_context.

## Findings

In NHANES III, nonfasting LDL-C was reported to have prognostic value similar to fasting LDL-C for long-term mortality. This finding supports nonfasting LDL-C as interpretable risk context, not direct supplement efficacy.

**Effect estimate or direction extracted:** Search-extracted summaries report nonfasting LDL-C had prognostic value similar to fasting LDL-C. Secondary summary reports C-statistics for all-cause mortality were similar, with 0.59 fasting versus 0.58 nonfasting in one summary and both 0.58 in another.

## Why it matters

Supports cautious use of nonfasting LDL-C for risk context, while not removing the need to standardize fasting status for pre/post self-experiment comparison.

## Potential experiment signals

fasting status, LDL-C, all-cause mortality, cardiovascular mortality

## Protocol takeaway

Nonfasting LDL-C can be informative, but matched fasting state remains preferable for within-person protocol assessment.
