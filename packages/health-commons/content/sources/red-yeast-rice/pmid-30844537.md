This source is included for **Safety reviews and pharmacovigilance**.

**Findings:** The meta-analysis pooled 53 RCTs with 112 treatment arms and 8535 subjects; monacolin K administration was not associated with increased musculoskeletal-disorder risk versus control (OR 0.94, 95% CI 0.53 to 1.65).

**Why it matters:** Provides the major randomized-trial tolerability counterweight to case reports, without resolving rare post-marketing safety questions.

**Potential experiment signals:** musculoskeletal adverse events, non-musculoskeletal adverse events, serious adverse events, trial follow-up duration, monacolin K dose subgroup.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety evidence that RCTs did not show increased common musculoskeletal adverse events, while preserving rare-event and product-variability caveats.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
