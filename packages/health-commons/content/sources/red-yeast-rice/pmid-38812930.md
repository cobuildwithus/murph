This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** A 90-day RCT in stable coronary artery disease compared nattokinase plus red yeast rice, nattokinase alone, red yeast rice alone, and placebo; the combination and red yeast rice arms improved lipid markers, with no intervention-attributed adverse effects reported.

**Why it matters:** This is special-population coronary evidence with a separable RYR arm and combination arm, but concomitant medications and CAD population make it adjacent rather than direct.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, total cholesterol, triglycerides, HDL-C, blood pressure, thromboxane B2, antithrombin III, CK, liver enzymes, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent stable-CAD evidence and safety context; do not generalize to healthy or primary-prevention red yeast rice use.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Stable CAD population on routine medications; intervention includes nattokinase combination and a red yeast rice arm; 90-day surrogate outcomes; conducted in one country. Stable coronary artery disease under routine medical care, not general cholesterol self-experimentation.
