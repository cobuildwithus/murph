## Quick read

- **Source type:** Systematic review and meta-analysis of fasting versus nonfasting lipid testing (2024).
- **People or setting studied:** Normal or generally healthy populations included in fasting versus nonfasting lipid-testing studies.
- **Exposure or intervention:** Nonfasting lipid testing.
- **Comparator or control:** Fasting lipid testing.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Systematic review/meta-analysis; included-study durations varied or were not applicable.
- **Role in Murph:** context-only / measurement_context.

## Findings

This 244,665-participant systematic review/meta-analysis concluded that fasting may reduce variability and improve consistency for lipid testing in normal populations. It is a measurement-boundary finding that tempers blanket acceptance of nonfasting panels.

**Effect estimate or direction extracted:** PubMed summary reports the review concluded fasting may be preferred as a conservative model to reduce variability and increase consistency in routine practice, tempering blanket nonfasting recommendations.

## Why it matters

Important counterweight against over-loose nonfasting policies in a self-experiment where small LDL-C changes matter.
