This source is included for **Lipid measurement and test-plan context**.

**Findings:** The NLA statement supports practical lipid-panel interpretation, including nonfasting sampling context and non-HDL-C as a reliable metric across fasting states. It is measurement context only.

**Why it matters:** High-priority lipid society statement for preanalytical, analytical, postanalytical, and clinical aspects of lipid measurements.

**Potential experiment signals:** Document fasting status.; Use non-HDL-C as reliable across fasting and nonfasting states.; Repeat abnormal or baseline lipid tests when needed to improve certainty..

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a core lipid-measurement source for endpoint fidelity and sampling logistics.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Limitations and directness:** Directness is `measurement_context`. Measurement and reporting statement, not a red yeast rice study. Some recommendations may require laboratory-specific implementation. Does not establish supplement-specific monitoring intervals. Population mismatch: Clinical lipid-testing populations are broader than supplement self-experimenters.
