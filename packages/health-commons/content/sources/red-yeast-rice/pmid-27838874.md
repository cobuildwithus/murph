This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** RYR was associated with lipid reductions within treatment, but adding Lactobacillus casei did not provide an additional cholesterol-lowering effect; only diastolic blood pressure differed between groups at week 12 in accessible metadata.

**Why it matters:** Helps prevent over-attributing combination-product results to red yeast rice alone.

**Potential experiment signals:** ldl-c, total-cholesterol, triglycerides, hdl-c, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote this source to a direct plain red yeast rice claim without a separable RYR-only arm. Preserve the boundary: Adjunct/probiotic combination question rather than plain RYR protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Comparator/control:** Red yeast rice plus placebo capsules.

**Duration/follow-up:** 8 weeks of treatment plus 4-week follow-up.

**Safety/adverse events:** Adverse-event details were not extracted from accessible metadata.

**Limitations:** No non-RYR placebo-only arm for isolating RYR in this extraction; probiotic add-on effect was null for lipids.
