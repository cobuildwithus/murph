This source is included for **Lipid measurement and test-plan context**.

**Findings:** The consensus describes apoB as a validated atherogenic particle measure that can improve risk interpretation when apoB, LDL-C, and non-HDL-C are discordant. It is optional measurement context, not red yeast rice evidence.

**Why it matters:** Best candidate for apoB measurement context and interpretation alongside LDL-C and non-HDL-C.

**Potential experiment signals:** Optional apoB at baseline and follow-up when LDL-C/non-HDL-C discordance is suspected.; Use apoB to contextualize atherogenic particle burden.; Avoid replacing LDL-C/non-HDL-C with apoB unless guided by clinician/lab availability..

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to justify apoB as an optional advanced lipid endpoint, especially for discordance or high-triglyceride contexts.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Limitations and directness:** Directness is `measurement_context`. Consensus statement, not a red yeast rice study. ApoB thresholds are less established than LDL-C thresholds in some settings. ApoB availability and insurance coverage vary. Population mismatch: Adult risk-management populations may differ from supplement self-experimenters without formal ASCVD risk assessment.
