This source is included for **Lipid measurement and test-plan context**.

**Findings:** The statement provides harmonized lipid-reporting recommendations aligned with Canadian dyslipidemia guidance, supporting consistent LDL-C, non-HDL-C, apoB, and triglyceride interpretation.

**Why it matters:** Useful lab-reporting companion to the Canadian guideline, focused on consistency of lipid reports.

**Potential experiment signals:** Check whether lab report includes non-HDL-C and apoB where available.; Use harmonized reporting flags to interpret out-of-range lipid values.; Keep laboratory method consistency across baseline and follow-up..

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a reporting-standardization context source, especially for Canadian lab results.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Limitations and directness:** Directness is `measurement_context`. Canadian lab-reporting context. Not an efficacy or safety study. Implementation varies by laboratory information systems. Population mismatch: Laboratory-reporting guidance applies to labs/clinicians, not specifically to red yeast rice users.
