This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** The review examined efficacy and safety of monacolin K doses; accessible secondary summary states doses from 2-10 mg/day up to 12 weeks significantly reduced LDL-C and total cholesterol, but formulation details must be separated.

**Why it matters:** Mechanism-near review centered on monacolin K, useful when translating between RYR products and quantified monacolin exposure.

**Potential experiment signals:** ldl-c, total-cholesterol, apob, creatine-kinase, alanine-aminotransferase, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote this source to a direct plain red yeast rice claim without a separable RYR-only arm. Preserve the boundary: Same-mechanism/dose review; safety-only boundary in this batch.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Comparator/control:** Placebo/control or trial comparators across included clinical trials.

**Duration/follow-up:** Up to 12 weeks in accessible secondary summary data.

**Safety/adverse events:** The review includes safety outcomes relevant to statin-like adverse effects and formulation variability; detailed adverse-event rates were not extracted.

**Limitations:** Monacolin K rather than plain RYR; clinical trials may include combination products and heterogeneous formulations.
