This source is included for **Product quality, contamination, and dose uncertainty**.

**Findings:** Review of analytical approaches to authenticate products containing RYR extract/monacolin K, including markers and fingerprinting methods.

**Extracted details:**

- **Population / sample:** Products containing red yeast rice extract / monacolin K; review of authentication approaches, no human participants
- **Intervention or exposure:** Multi-level authentication approaches for RYR extract products
- **Comparator / control:** Comparison of analytical authentication approaches; no clinical comparator
- **Duration / follow-up:** Review/workflow source; no intervention follow-up
- **Endpoints:** natural vs added/synthetic monacolin K challenge; Monascus pigments as markers; GC-MS undeclared statin screening; 1H-NMR fingerprinting; standardized authentication protocol
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Review/workflow source proposing multi-level authentication because natural monacolin K and added lovastatin have identical structures; no clinical effect estimate.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Addresses labeling non-compliance, undeclared statins, contamination/adulteration, and authentication uncertainty.
- **Limitations:** Review/workflow rather than representative market testing; proposed methods require validation/implementation in specific products.
- **Population mismatch:** No human outcome data.
- **Directness:** measurement_context for same product family

**Why it matters:** Clarifies why simple monacolin K measurement may not prove authentic RYR sourcing or absence of adulteration.

**Potential experiment signals:** natural vs added/synthetic monacolin K challenge; Monascus pigments as markers; GC-MS undeclared statin screening; 1H-NMR fingerprinting; standardized authentication protocol

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for authentication workflow design, not clinical efficacy.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
