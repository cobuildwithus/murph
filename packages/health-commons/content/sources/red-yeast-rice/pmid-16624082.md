This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** Chinese CCSPS subgroup analysis evaluated Xuezhikang, a proprietary red yeast rice extract, versus placebo in post-MI coronary heart disease patients grouped by duration of MI history.

**Why it matters:** The source adds adjacent secondary-prevention context for a red yeast rice-derived pharmaceutical-style preparation in a high-risk coronary population.

**Potential experiment signals:** nonfatal myocardial infarction, coronary heart disease death, total mortality, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as adjacent Xuezhikang/post-MI context; it is not direct evidence for OTC red yeast rice self-use to lower cholesterol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Chinese-language subgroup report; accessible details are limited; proprietary Xuezhikang is not equivalent to heterogeneous OTC red yeast rice; population had prior MI and conventional secondary-prevention care. Post-MI coronary secondary-prevention population, not generally healthy people seeking cholesterol reduction.
