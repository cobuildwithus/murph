This source is included for **Direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** RYR significantly lowered TC/HDL-C, LDL-C/HDL-C, and ApoB/ApoA-I ratios; accessible extraction notes report TC/HDL-C ratio falling from 5.96 to 4.68 and LDL-C/HDL-C ratio by 1.15 units. Safety details were not the focus of the accessible lipid-ratio extraction.

**Why it matters:** Adds ratio endpoints that may help interpret LDL-C and total-cholesterol movement beyond isolated absolute values.

**Potential experiment signals:** TC/HDL-C ratio, LDL-C/HDL-C ratio, ApoB/ApoA-I ratio, LDL-C, total cholesterol, HDL-C.

**Protocol takeaway:** Useful for TC/HDL-C and LDL-C/HDL-C ratio tracking, but should be linked to the overlapping Monascus Went trial cluster.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness and boundary:** direct_protocol. Ratio-focused publication may overlap the Lin 2005 Monascus purpureus Went rice sample; avoid double counting in synthesis. Population mismatch: Hypercholesterolemic adults; ratio endpoints are useful but not a substitute for modern absolute LDL-C/non-HDL-C targets.
