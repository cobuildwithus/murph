This source is included for **Lipid measurement and test-plan context**.

**Findings:** The guideline emphasizes lipid assessment in endocrine disorders and the need to account for endocrine causes of dyslipidemia. This is confounding and safety context for cholesterol experiments.

**Why it matters:** Boundary source for interpreting lipid panels when thyroid disease, diabetes, or other endocrine conditions may confound cholesterol results.

**Potential experiment signals:** Screen for secondary causes before attributing lipid changes to red yeast rice.; Consider thyroid status and diabetes context when interpreting lipid panels.; Repeat lipid panels after endocrine abnormalities are treated when clinically relevant..

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a safety/interpretation boundary: lipid changes can reflect endocrine disease status, not just the supplement.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Limitations and directness:** Directness is `measurement_context`. Population-specific guideline for endocrine disorders. Not an intervention trial. Not directly applicable to people without endocrine conditions. Population mismatch: Relevant mainly when thyroid disease, diabetes, menopause-related treatment questions, or other endocrine disorders affect lipid interpretation.
