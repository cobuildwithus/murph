This source is included for **Product quality, contamination, and dose uncertainty**.

**Findings:** HPTLC-UV/FLD analysis of 19 RYR products for lovastatin forms and citrinin, showing content differences and non-detectable citrinin at the method LOD.

**Extracted details:**

- **Population / sample:** Nineteen RYR products including powders, dietary supplements, and Chinese proprietary medicines; no human participants
- **Intervention or exposure:** HPTLC-UV/FLD analysis of lovastatin lactone/hydroxy acid forms and citrinin
- **Comparator / control:** Product type comparisons; no clinical comparator
- **Duration / follow-up:** Single product-testing study
- **Endpoints:** lovastatin lactone content; lovastatin hydroxy acid content; citrinin LOD/non-detection; method throughput
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Citrinin was not detectable down to the reported LOD; mean lovastatin in 15 RYR powders was reported as 8.7 g/kg with a 1.5-26.2 g/kg range; two dietary supplements had the highest reported values around 40.7-41.4 g/kg.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Shows some products can have very high lovastatin-equivalent content even when citrinin is not detected.
- **Limitations:** Small product set; sample-selection and regional market context limit generalization.
- **Population mismatch:** No human outcomes.
- **Directness:** measurement_context for same product family

**Why it matters:** Demonstrates that negative citrinin tests do not resolve active-dose uncertainty.

**Potential experiment signals:** lovastatin lactone content; lovastatin hydroxy acid content; citrinin LOD/non-detection; method throughput

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as product-QC context for both active-dose and contamination screens.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
