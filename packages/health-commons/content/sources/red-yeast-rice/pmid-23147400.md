This source is included for **Lipid measurement and test-plan context**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Community-based cross-sectional fasting-duration lipid study (2012).
- **People or setting studied:** Community-based individuals undergoing lipid testing.
- **Exposure or intervention:** Self-reported fasting duration before lipid panel.
- **Comparator or control:** Different fasting-time intervals.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Single lipid-test encounter; no intervention follow-up.
- **Role in Murph:** context-only / measurement_context.

## Findings

In 209,180 community-based lipid tests, fasting time had limited association with many lipid levels. The reusable finding supports documenting fasting duration but not automatically discarding nonfasting panels.

**Effect estimate or direction extracted:** Search-extracted summaries report little association between fasting time and lipid subclass levels in this large community sample; triglycerides are the lipid fraction most expected to vary.

## Why it matters

Helps distinguish true pre/post protocol signal from fasting-time noise.

## Potential experiment signals

fasting duration, total cholesterol, LDL-C, HDL-C, triglycerides

## Protocol takeaway

Record fasting duration and match it across test days when possible; do not assume every nonfasting panel is unusable.
