This source is included for **Direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** LDL-C percent change was -30.2% ±10.5 with RYR versus -27.0% ±15.4 with pravastatin in registry-linked extraction notes. Withdrawal due to myalgia/CK >500 was 1/21 with RYR and 2/22 with pravastatin; no serious adverse events were reported in registry-linked extraction notes.

**Why it matters:** Directly informs safety/tolerability boundaries for users considering RYR because of prior statin symptoms.

**Potential experiment signals:** myalgia withdrawal, CK, LDL-C percent change, non-serious adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as supervised statin-intolerance evidence, not as proof that RYR is safer than statins for all users.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness and boundary:** clinical_supervised. Active comparator, no placebo, small sample, statin-intolerant population, and lifestyle co-intervention. Population mismatch: Previous statin intolerance; results are most applicable to supervised intolerance-management contexts.
