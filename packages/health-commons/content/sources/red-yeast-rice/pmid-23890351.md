This source is included for **Direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** Accessible extraction notes report improvements in lipid pattern, hs-CRP, and vascular remodeling; secondary summaries report decreases in total cholesterol, LDL-C, and triglycerides with no HDL-C change, but exact duration/effect table should be verified. Detailed adverse-event extraction was not completed.

**Why it matters:** Shows an RYR-containing product may affect lipids and hs-CRP, but the CoQ10 coingredient prevents direct RYR-only attribution.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C, triglycerides, HDL-C, hs-CRP, vascular remodeling parameters.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent formulation context, not as a clean RYR-only efficacy claim.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness and boundary:** adjacent_variant. Co-formulated with coenzyme Q10 and small sample; duration/effect details need full-text verification before protocol dosing claims. Population mismatch: Moderately hypercholesterolemic Italian adults; combination product is adjacent to RYR-only self-experimentation.
