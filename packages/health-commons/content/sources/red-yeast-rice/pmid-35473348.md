This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** Accessible metadata reports clinically meaningful LDL-C and total cholesterol lowering, with significant non-HDL-C reduction and slight HDL-C improvement; exact group effects were not extracted.

**Why it matters:** Backfills active-form monacolin K formulation evidence; should not be merged unqualified with starter OTC red yeast rice.

**Potential experiment signals:** ldl-c, non-hdl-c, total-cholesterol, triglycerides, hdl-c, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote this source to a direct plain red yeast rice claim without a separable RYR-only arm. Preserve the boundary: Formulation-specific monacolin beta-hydroxy acid product.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Comparator/control:** Lifestyle modification alone/placebo-style control as described in accessible metadata.

**Duration/follow-up:** 60 days.

**Safety/adverse events:** Safety was evaluated; detailed adverse-event rates were not extracted from accessible metadata.

**Limitations:** Active-form monacolin formulation with additional TCM components; not typical OTC RYR and not plain product.
