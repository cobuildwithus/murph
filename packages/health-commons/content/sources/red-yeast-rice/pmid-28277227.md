This source is included for **Safety reviews and pharmacovigilance**.

**Findings:** The review identified six French national pharmacovigilance cases and nine literature cases involving red yeast rice muscular injuries, including nine CK elevations, three rhabdomyolysis cases, and two myalgia cases.

**Why it matters:** Adds a focused muscle-injury pharmacovigilance source with French database and literature cases.

**Potential experiment signals:** myalgia, CK elevation, rhabdomyolysis, time to onset, product formulation variability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a safety signal for muscle symptoms and rhabdomyolysis; do not convert cases into incidence.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
