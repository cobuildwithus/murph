This source is included for **Lipid measurement and test-plan context**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Systematic review of apoB, LDL-C, and non-HDL-C risk-marker discordance (2025).
- **People or setting studied:** Populations in discordance studies comparing apoB or LDL-particle measures with LDL-C and non-HDL-C.
- **Exposure or intervention:** ApoB, LDL-C, non-HDL-C, and LDL-particle metrics as cardiovascular-risk markers.
- **Comparator or control:** Comparative predictive accuracy among apoB, LDL-C, non-HDL-C, and LDL-P in discordance analyses.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Systematic review through PubMed search completed September 30, 2024; individual study follow-up varied.
- **Role in Murph:** context-only / measurement_context.

## Findings

This systematic review identified 15 studies and 593,354 participants; extracted summaries report apoB generally outperformed LDL-C and often outperformed non-HDL-C in discordance comparisons. It supports apoB as a useful optional endpoint for cholesterol protocols.

**Effect estimate or direction extracted:** Search-extracted abstract reports 15 studies with 593,354 participants; apoB outperformed LDL-C in 9 of 9 comparisons, LDL-P outperformed LDL-C in 2 of 3, and apoB was more accurate than non-HDL-C in 7 comparisons, with non-HDL-C superior in 1 and equivalent in 1.

## Why it matters

Helps the protocol explain why apoB can be an optional secondary lab endpoint for particle-burden context.
