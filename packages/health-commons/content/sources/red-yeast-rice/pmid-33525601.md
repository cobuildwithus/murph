**Findings:** The study reported novel relationships between oxysterols and cardiovascular-risk biomarkers and evaluated the BB536/RYR combination; lipid efficacy should be interpreted alongside the parent combination trial.

**Why it matters:** Useful for apoB/non-HDL/oxidative biomarker gap mapping but not direct protocol support.

**Potential experiment signals:** total-cholesterol, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote this source to a direct plain red yeast rice claim without a separable RYR-only arm. Preserve the boundary: Mechanistic/biomarker context only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Comparator/control:** Placebo.

**Duration/follow-up:** Duration follows the parent randomized design; exact duration not separately extracted here.

**Safety/adverse events:** Adverse-event details were not extracted from this secondary biomarker article.

**Limitations:** Secondary biomarker analysis of a combination product; not designed to isolate RYR efficacy.
