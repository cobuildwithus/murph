## Findings

This CDC-NHLBI lipid-standardization program report provides foundational context that lipid measurement accuracy and precision require reference systems and standardization. It supports lab-fidelity caveats for cholesterol self-experiments.

**Effect estimate or direction extracted:** Foundational laboratory-standardization source; no clinical effect estimates extracted.

## Why it matters

Small LDL-C changes in a self-experiment can be confounded by lab imprecision or method differences.

## Potential experiment signals

lab-fidelity, total cholesterol, lipid measurement accuracy

## Protocol takeaway

Use standardized testing and avoid overreading small lipid changes near assay-variability limits.
