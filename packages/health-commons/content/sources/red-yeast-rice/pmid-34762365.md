This source is included for **Xuezhikang and proprietary Chinese RYR preparations**.

**Findings:** The network meta-analysis included 47 trials with 4,824 subjects; Xuezhikang ranked highest by SUCRA for total cholesterol (84.5%) and LDL-C (82.6%), while Zhibituo ranked highest for triglycerides (80.2%) and HDL-C improvement (90.1%). The review noted limitations in the included studies and called for more rigorously designed trials, so the rankings should remain adjacent-context evidence.

**Why it matters:** This source helps map proprietary Chinese red-yeast-rice preparation evidence while keeping it separate from OTC RYR supplement claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** TC, LDL-C, TG, HDL-C, comparative ranking.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent proprietary-preparation context only; do not promote to a direct OTC red yeast rice efficacy claim.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Population/formulation mismatch: Xuezhikang, Zhibitai, or Zhibituo are proprietary Chinese preparations with named dosing and clinical supervision; this is adjacent to, not direct evidence for, an OTC red-yeast-rice supplement protocol.

**Limitations:** Important limitation: this is a proprietary Chinese preparation, usually used in supervised clinical contexts; it should not be generalized to variable OTC red yeast rice supplements.
