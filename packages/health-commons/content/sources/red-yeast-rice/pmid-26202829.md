This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** Accessible metadata reports improvement of lipid profile and blood-pressure/vascular health markers in the nutraceutical group, while control lipid/BP levels did not significantly change; numeric lipid effects were not extracted.

**Why it matters:** Adjacent low-risk population trial of a multi-ingredient nutraceutical containing RYR; should not be cited as RYR-alone evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** alanine-aminotransferase, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote this source to a direct plain red yeast rice claim without a separable RYR-only arm. Preserve the boundary: Hypertensive/hypercholesterolemic special population.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Comparator/control:** Diet/lifestyle advice alone.

**Duration/follow-up:** 6 months.

**Safety/adverse events:** Safety details were not extracted from accessible metadata.

**Limitations:** Multi-ingredient nutraceutical and hypertensive population; no RYR-only arm.
