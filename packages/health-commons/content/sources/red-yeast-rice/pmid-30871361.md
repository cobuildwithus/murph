This source is included for **Direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** Monacolin K-rich RYR reduced total cholesterol and LDL-C versus baseline and other groups in accessible extraction notes; triglycerides declined but were not significant, and HDL-C showed no significant difference. Two subjects, one in the monacolin K-rich group and one in the GABA-rich group, reportedly had elevated liver enzymes.

**Why it matters:** Adds newer direct RYR evidence and a formulation boundary between monacolin-rich and other RYR variants.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C, triglycerides, HDL-C, liver enzymes.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use the monacolin K-rich arm as direct lipid evidence and the GABA-rich arm as adjacent RYR-variant context.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness and boundary:** direct_protocol_with_ryr_variant_arm. Group sizes, duration, and full adverse-event table require full-text verification; multiple RYR preparations complicate attribution. Population mismatch: Hyperlipidemia trial population; product-specific Monascus pilosus preparations may not match commercial RYR products.
