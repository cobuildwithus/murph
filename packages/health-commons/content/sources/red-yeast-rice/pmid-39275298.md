This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** Accessible secondary metadata reports a 19.3% LDL-C reduction and a 29.3% HDL-C increase over three months with combined supplementation; full effect extraction was not independently verified from article text in this batch.

**Why it matters:** Recent monacolin-containing RCT; relevant for boundary marking because non-RYR co-ingredients may drive effects.

**Potential experiment signals:** ldl-c, total-cholesterol, triglycerides, hdl-c, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote this source to a direct plain red yeast rice claim without a separable RYR-only arm. Preserve the boundary: Recent monacolin-containing combination product, context-only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Comparator/control:** Placebo.

**Duration/follow-up:** Three months in accessible secondary summary data.

**Safety/adverse events:** Tolerability was evaluated, but detailed adverse-event rates were not extracted.

**Limitations:** Monacolin plus gamma-oryzanol/GABA combination; not plain RYR and not separable by ingredient.
