This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** A 2022 meta-analysis of 30 randomized-trial articles and 5,440 participants found favorable pooled effects for RYR preparations on mortality, MACE, metabolic markers, and lipids, with no pooled increase in adverse reactions, but most evidence was preparation-, population-, and geography-specific.

**Why it matters:** The source is a broad adjacent evidence map and safety context; the pooled results should not be converted into direct claims for plain OTC red yeast rice.

**Potential experiment signals:** mortality, MACE, LDL-C, total cholesterol, triglycerides, HDL-C, fasting glucose, HbA1c, adverse reactions.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only/adjacent synthesis context because included preparations, comparators, and populations differ from a plain red yeast rice cholesterol protocol.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Limitations and mismatch:** High heterogeneity; mostly China-based studies; broad preparation category; includes cardiovascular and metabolic-syndrome contexts; RYR preparations may not match OTC red yeast rice products. Metabolic-syndrome and cardiovascular-risk trial populations using varied red yeast rice preparations, not a plain consumer supplement protocol.
