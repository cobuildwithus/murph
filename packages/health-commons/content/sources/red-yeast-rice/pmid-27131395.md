This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** The meta-analysis reports that the berberine/policosanol/RYR combination improved lipid profile in hypercholesterolemic patients; pooled numeric effects were not verified from full text in this batch.

**Why it matters:** Common commercial combination source; should be kept separate from RYR-only interventions.

**Potential experiment signals:** ldl-c, total-cholesterol, triglycerides, hdl-c, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote this source to a direct plain red yeast rice claim without a separable RYR-only arm. Preserve the boundary: Commercial combination-stack evidence only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Comparator/control:** Placebo or controls from included randomized trials.

**Duration/follow-up:** Varied across included trials.

**Safety/adverse events:** Safety/tolerability was in scope, but detailed adverse-event rates were not extracted.

**Limitations:** Combination-product meta-analysis; cannot estimate plain RYR effect.
