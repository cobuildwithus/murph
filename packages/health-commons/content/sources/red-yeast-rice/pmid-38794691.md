This source is included for **Evidence syntheses and reviews**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-38794691-hypercholesterolemia-lipids` — RYR extract significantly reduced total cholesterol (summary mean −37.43 mg/dL) and LDL-C (summary mean −35.82 mg/dL); triglycerides, HDL-C, ApoA-I, and ApoB were not consistently reduced in the meta-analysis.
- `finding:pmid-38794691-safety` — The review concluded that RYR extract was not associated with increased life-threatening side effects in included trials, while also calling for further research in specific subpopulations and outcomes.

**Why it matters:** This is a recent direct synthesis focused on RYR extract and explicitly couples efficacy and safety.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, total cholesterol, triglycerides, HDL-C, ApoB, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as direct RYR extract evidence for LDL-C and total cholesterol; do not promote TG/HDL/Apo findings beyond the mixed evidence reported.
