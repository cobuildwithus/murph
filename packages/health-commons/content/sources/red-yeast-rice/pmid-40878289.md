This source is included for **Guidelines and external protocol claims**.

**Findings:** The update provides scope, supplement nonrecommendation language, red yeast rice monacolin regulatory restrictions, and short-term supplement-comparator trial context.

**Why it matters:** It is a current, high-importance boundary source for safety, guideline support, and cardiovascular-risk claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, documented safety, monacolin dose, and regulatory status are key signals.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a safety and claim-boundary source, not as direct efficacy evidence for the Murph protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
