- **Exposure or intervention:** Analytical error allowances for lipid-panel components.

- **Duration or follow-up:** Analytical/modeling context; no intervention follow-up.
- **Role in Murph:** context-only / measurement_context.

## Findings

This analysis reports that lipid-panel analytical error allowances can misclassify cardiovascular risk and that tighter bias/imprecision limits or serial averaging may improve accuracy. It supports cautious interpretation of small pre/post lipid changes.

**Effect estimate or direction extracted:** PubMed summary reports current NCEP analytical-performance recommendations allow an unacceptable degree of misclassification. Secondary abstract snippets report that reducing proportional bias and constraining imprecision could reduce misclassifications, and that biological variability may be addressed by averaging serial samples.

## Why it matters

Reinforces that one lipid panel may be too noisy to declare a protocol effect, especially when the change is small.
