This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** A 12-month single-blind randomized study in elderly statin-intolerant or statin-refusing patients tested a multi-ingredient nutraceutical including red yeast rice and reported LDL-C and total cholesterol reductions with acceptable tolerability.

**Why it matters:** This source contributes elderly/special-population context but cannot isolate red yeast rice from berberine, policosanol, folate, CoQ10, or astaxanthin.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, total cholesterol, HDL-C, insulin resistance, safety labs, tolerability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as adjacent combination evidence in elderly supervised care.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Combination product; single-blind study; elderly selected population; no separable red yeast rice-only arm; no hard cardiovascular outcomes. Patients older than 75 years who were statin intolerant or declined drug therapy, not a general adult self-experiment population.
