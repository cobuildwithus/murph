This source is included for **Safety case reports and adverse-event signals**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-40909457-rhabdomyolysis-aki-respiratory-failure`: A 78-year-old woman developed progressive weakness, dyspnea, tea-colored urine, severe rhabdomyolysis, AKI, and respiratory failure after taking red yeast rice 2 g/day for 1 month; CK peaked at 112,985 U/L and she improved after RYR withdrawal and organ-targeted supportive care.

**Why it matters:** Documents a very severe muscle-kidney-respiratory presentation with detailed biomarkers and recovery timeline; useful for emergency safety criteria.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- Creatine kinase, myoglobin, creatinine/eGFR, potassium, ALT/AST in symptomatic cases
- Progressive proximal weakness, dyspnea, chewing difficulty, tea-colored urine
- Respiratory symptoms as emergency escalation signals
- Co-supplement and prescription statin exposure review

**Protocol takeaway:** Severe weakness, dyspnea, or tea-colored urine during red yeast rice use should be treated as an emergency signal, not as ordinary supplementation discomfort.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction details

**Population:** One 78-year-old woman with hypertension and diet-controlled type 2 diabetes; she also used supplements including coenzyme Q10, omega-3, and Ganoderma lucidum spore powder.

**Exposure:** Red yeast rice 2 g/day for 1 month before symptom onset; she denied prescription statin use during the period.

**Comparator/control:** None; single-patient case report.

**Duration/follow-up:** One month of red yeast rice before symptom onset; hospitalization course extended to stable discharge on day 44, with dialysis stopped around day 35 in the article timeline.

**Endpoints:**
- Severe rhabdomyolysis
- Acute kidney injury
- Respiratory failure / diaphragmatic dysfunction
- Creatine kinase
- Serum myoglobin
- Creatinine
- ALT/AST
- Mechanical ventilation / CRRT

**Effect estimates or direction:** Adverse-event direction only: severe rhabdomyolysis with AKI and respiratory failure occurred after RYR 2 g/day for 1 month; CK peaked at 112,985 U/L and the patient improved after RYR withdrawal and organ-targeted supportive care. No efficacy or risk estimate was reported.

**Adverse events / safety notes:** Severe rhabdomyolysis complicated by AKI and respiratory failure requiring intubation/mechanical ventilation, CRRT, plasma exchange, hemoperfusion, and supportive care.

**Limitations:**
- Single case report; cannot infer incidence.
- Older age, comorbidities, and co-supplements are population mismatches/confounders.
- Authors note diagnostic challenge with immune-mediated necrotizing myopathy, although negative serology and improvement without immunosuppression supported toxic rhabdomyolysis.

**Population mismatch:** Older adult with comorbidities and multiple supplements; not representative of a typical low-risk cholesterol protocol population.
