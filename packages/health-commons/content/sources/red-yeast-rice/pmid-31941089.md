This source is included for **Product quality, contamination, and dose uncertainty**.

**Findings:** Chemical profiling study of 31 RYR dietary supplements showing missing/incorrect label content and wide monacolin dose variability.

**Extracted details:**

- **Population / sample:** Thirty-one RYR dietary supplements purchased mainly online and from local health-food stores; no human participants
- **Intervention or exposure:** 1H-NMR and UHPLC-DAD-MS chemical profiling and monacolin quantification
- **Comparator / control:** No clinical comparator; product-to-product and label-vs-measured comparison
- **Duration / follow-up:** Single product-testing survey
- **Endpoints:** total monacolins per unit; monacolin K per unit; monacolin K + hydroxy acid form; label disclosure/accuracy; daily intake estimates
- **Effect estimates or direction:** 42% of samples lacked label monacolin concentration; only 9 of 18 with label indication actually contained declared monacolins. Total monacolins, MK, and MK+MKA per unit ranged approximately 0.07-23.84 mg, 0.07-23.18 mg, and 0.07-23.80 mg; recommended daily intakes could reach roughly 46-48 mg/day by measured metrics.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Very high possible daily monacolin exposure and label mismatch create statin-like exposure and monitoring concerns.
- **Limitations:** Sampled products from particular sales channels/time; no human clinical outcomes.
- **Population mismatch:** Product chemistry only.
- **Directness:** same_mechanism product-quality evidence

**Why it matters:** Strong evidence that labeled or recommended servings may not predict active exposure.

**Potential experiment signals:** total monacolins per unit; monacolin K per unit; monacolin K + hydroxy acid form; label disclosure/accuracy; daily intake estimates

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as high-priority dose-fidelity and label-accuracy evidence.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
