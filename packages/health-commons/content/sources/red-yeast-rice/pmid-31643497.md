This source is included for **Safety reviews and pharmacovigilance**.

**Findings:** LiverTox describes red yeast rice as associated with very rare instances of acute clinically apparent liver injury and rare myopathy/liver injury reports similar to lovastatin; prospective controlled trials did not show a consistent signal for enzyme elevations or clinically apparent liver injury.

**Why it matters:** Anchors the protocol safety boundary in a pharmacovigilance-oriented liver-injury source rather than efficacy trials alone.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C context, liver enzymes, jaundice or hepatitis symptoms, muscle pain or weakness, drug-interaction context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as safety context: red yeast rice can behave like low-dose lovastatin, with rare but clinically important liver and muscle injury reports.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
