This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** An International Lipid Expert Panel position paper discusses red yeast rice among lipid-lowering nutraceuticals and emphasizes clinician-directed use, evidence limitations, and statin-like monacolin K interaction/myopathy cautions.

**Why it matters:** The source is useful for safety boundaries, contraindication language, and clinician-facing context rather than direct protocol efficacy.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, statin intolerance context, drug interactions, myopathy, rhabdomyolysis.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only guideline context; do not use to make unqualified efficacy claims for plain OTC red yeast rice.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Expert-position source rather than a new trial; addresses multiple nutraceuticals; safety/effectiveness evidence varies by product and formulation. Clinician-managed dyslipidaemia scenarios rather than a specific red yeast rice self-experiment.
