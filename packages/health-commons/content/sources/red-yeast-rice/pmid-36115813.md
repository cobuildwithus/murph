This source is included for **Safety reviews and pharmacovigilance**.

**Findings:** The NLA defines SAMS as all muscle symptoms temporally related to statin use without regard to causality, estimates SAMS prevalence around 10% (range 5-25%), and estimates pharmacologic SAMS around 1-2% (range 0.5-4%).

**Why it matters:** Provides the muscle-symptom vocabulary and management frame relevant to red yeast rice because monacolin K is chemically identical to lovastatin.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle symptoms, CK elevation context, statin intolerance history, rechallenge/dechallenge history, ASCVD-risk therapy continuity.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as statin-muscle-symptom context only; it supports symptom screening and clinician-guided management, not direct RYR efficacy or safety claims.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
