This source is included for **Direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** After 12 weeks, the RYR/folic-acid group had LDL-C -14.8%, total cholesterol -11.2%, and homocysteine -12.5%; 51% reached LDL-C below 4.14 mmol/L in accessible extraction notes. No intolerances or serious adverse events were reported in accessible extraction notes.

**Why it matters:** Directly informs low-monacolin dose expectations, a key implementation question for modern RYR products.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, total cholesterol, homocysteine, tolerability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports measurable LDL-C reduction at 3 mg/day monacolin K from RYR, with folic-acid coingredient caveat.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness and boundary:** direct_protocol_with_ingredient_boundary. Folic acid coingredient affects homocysteine interpretation; product-specific low-dose monacolin K context. Population mismatch: Nonstatin-treated hypercholesterolemia; not high-risk secondary-prevention or polypharmacy population.
