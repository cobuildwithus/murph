This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** The article describes development of a standardized Monascus purpureus extract intended to be high in active monacolin forms and free of citrinin/inactive secondary monacolins; it is not human efficacy evidence.

**Why it matters:** This source is included to preserve adjacent or boundary evidence and avoid over-attributing Development of a new highly standardized and granulated extract from Monascus purpureus with a high content of monacolin K and KA and free of inactive secondary monacolins and citrinin to plain red yeast rice.

**Potential experiment signals:** total-cholesterol, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote this source to a direct plain red yeast rice claim without a separable RYR-only arm. Preserve the boundary: No human cholesterol-treatment population; product-specific extract rather than generic OTC red yeast rice.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Comparator/control:** Analytical/product-quality characterization; no clinical comparator.

**Duration/follow-up:** No human follow-up.

**Safety/adverse events:** No human adverse-event outcomes were reported because this was not a clinical trial.

**Limitations:** Product-quality and formulation evidence only; no LDL-C, adherence, or safety-lab outcomes in protocol participants.
