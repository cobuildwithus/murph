This source is included for **Xuezhikang and proprietary Chinese RYR preparations**.

**Findings:** The XTREME protocol plans to compare Xuezhikang 1,200 mg/day with atorvastatin 20 mg/day for 24 weeks in dyslipidemia plus prediabetes, with HbA1c as a primary glycemic endpoint and lipid/safety monitoring as secondary context.

**Why it matters:** Modern trial design highlights glycemic-safety monitoring and lipid endpoints for Xuezhikang versus a statin comparator.

**Potential experiment signals:** HbA1c, fasting glucose, LDL-C, non-HDL-C, triglycerides, ALT, CK, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Safety-only adjacent source: useful for planned monitoring endpoints, not for OTC RYR efficacy claims.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Population/formulation mismatch: Xuezhikang, Zhibitai, or Zhibituo are proprietary Chinese preparations with named dosing and clinical supervision; this is adjacent to, not direct evidence for, an OTC red-yeast-rice supplement protocol.

**Limitations:** Important limitation: this is a proprietary Chinese preparation, usually used in supervised clinical contexts; it should not be generalized to variable OTC red yeast rice supplements.
