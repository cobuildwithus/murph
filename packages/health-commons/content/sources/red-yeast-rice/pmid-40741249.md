This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** The updated nutrivigilance analysis concluded a very low incidence of suspected adverse events associated with the RYR product line; this is product-line safety context rather than efficacy evidence.

**Why it matters:** Brand/product-line nutrivigilance data can contextualize postmarketing event frequency but should be separated from independent pharmacovigilance.

**Potential experiment signals:** alanine-aminotransferase, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote this source to a direct plain red yeast rice claim without a separable RYR-only arm. Preserve the boundary: Brand-specific nutrivigilance, safety-only.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Comparator/control:** No clinical comparator; spontaneous report denominator/product-use context.

**Duration/follow-up:** Updated nutrivigilance data through the reporting window described by the paper.

**Safety/adverse events:** Spontaneously reported suspected adverse events were analyzed, including muscle and liver-related safety categories.

**Limitations:** Postmarketing spontaneous-report data can under-report and is brand/product-line specific; not independent efficacy evidence.
