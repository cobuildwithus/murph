## Findings

The NCEP laboratory-standardization report provides historical context that cholesterol measurements require accuracy goals and traceability to reference systems. It supports not overreading small pre/post lipid changes.

**Effect estimate or direction extracted:** Secondary CDC/MMWR-derived context reports NCEP goals for total cholesterol measurement included bias and imprecision targets; this source is used only as lab-standardization context.

## Why it matters

Supports protocol caveats around lipid-panel variability and repeat-test interpretation.

## Protocol takeaway

Do not treat small cholesterol differences as meaningful without considering measurement accuracy and repeat-test conditions.
