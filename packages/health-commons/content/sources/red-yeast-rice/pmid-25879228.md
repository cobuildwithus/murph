This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** An 8-week double-blind RCT in metabolic syndrome tested a capsule combining red yeast rice and olive extract and reported substantial LDL-C and other lipid-marker reductions, plus one severe muscle-ache dropout.

**Why it matters:** This is open-access clinical evidence with detailed product composition and monitoring, but the olive extract and metabolic-syndrome population make it adjacent rather than direct plain-RYR evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, total cholesterol, ApoB, triglycerides, oxidized LDL, blood pressure, muscle symptoms, creatine kinase.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent combination evidence and safety context; do not claim the effects apply to plain red yeast rice alone.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Small sample; short duration; combination product; metabolic-syndrome population; one dropout with severe muscle ache complicates tolerability interpretation. Metabolic syndrome and combined red yeast rice plus olive extract, not a generic red yeast rice-only adult cholesterol protocol.
