This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** The review concluded RYR therapy showed efficacy and acceptable safety for carotid atherosclerosis; accessible secondary summary reported significant reductions in carotid/lipid metrics and increased HDL-C.

**Why it matters:** Adjacent vascular-outcome synthesis; lipid endpoints may be secondary and product types require separation.

**Potential experiment signals:** ldl-c, total-cholesterol, triglycerides, hdl-c, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote this source to a direct plain red yeast rice claim without a separable RYR-only arm. Preserve the boundary: Adjacent vascular-disease synthesis, not direct self-experiment cholesterol evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Comparator/control:** Controls from included trials.

**Duration/follow-up:** Varied across included trials.

**Safety/adverse events:** Acceptable safety profile was reported at aggregate level; detailed adverse-event rates were not extracted.

**Limitations:** Carotid atherosclerosis outcome focus, Chinese population/trials, heterogeneous preparations.
