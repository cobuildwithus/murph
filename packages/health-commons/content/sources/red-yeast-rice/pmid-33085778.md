This source is included for **Safety reviews and pharmacovigilance**.

**Findings:** Lareb collected 94 individual reports corresponding to 187 ADRs; the most reported categories were musculoskeletal/connective tissue disorders (n=64), gastrointestinal disorders (n=33), and general disorders/administration-site conditions (n=23).

**Why it matters:** Gives a structured national pharmacovigilance dataset with adverse-reaction categories and explicit caution around monacolin K exposure.

**Potential experiment signals:** musculoskeletal symptoms, gastrointestinal symptoms, general adverse events, report seriousness, concomitant medication.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as spontaneous-report safety signal and not as incidence; red yeast rice should be considered a significant safety concern in unsupervised supplement use.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
