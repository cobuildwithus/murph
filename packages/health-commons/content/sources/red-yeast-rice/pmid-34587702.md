This source is included for **Direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** RYR reduced LDL-C versus control at 4 and 8 weeks; accessible extraction notes report significant total cholesterol and ApoB reductions and blood-pressure decreases. No severe treatment-related adverse events, rash, muscle pain, or adverse muscle/liver/renal laboratory findings were reported in accessible extraction notes.

**Why it matters:** Adds recent low-dose monacolin K RYR evidence and highlights practical lab/symptom safety monitoring.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, total cholesterol, ApoB, blood pressure, liver enzymes, renal labs, muscle symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports low-dose RYR as a measurable LDL-C intervention, but sample size and non-placebo control are major caveats.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness and boundary:** direct_protocol. Very small post-exclusion sample and diet-only control rather than placebo; underpowered for vascular endpoints. Population mismatch: Japanese mild dyslipidemia population; not necessarily generalizable to high-risk or medication-treated users.
