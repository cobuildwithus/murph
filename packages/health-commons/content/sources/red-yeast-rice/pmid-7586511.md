This source is included for **Lipid measurement and test-plan context**.

**Findings:** The NCEP executive summary provides foundational triglyceride measurement guidance relevant to fasting status and LDL-C calculation reliability. It is legacy lab-fidelity context only.

**Why it matters:** Foundational triglyceride measurement guidance relevant to when TG can distort calculated LDL-C or prompt fasting repeats.

**Potential experiment signals:** Triglyceride values can alter LDL-C calculation confidence.; Use fasting status consistently when triglycerides are high.; Repeat abnormal triglyceride results when clinically indicated..

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as legacy background for triglyceride measurement fidelity and LDL-C calculation caveats.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Limitations and directness:** Directness is `measurement_context`. Published in 1995; current triglyceride and LDL-C calculation guidance should be preferred. Not a red yeast rice study. Does not address modern nonfasting lipid-panel practice in full. Population mismatch: Laboratory measurement standards, not supplement users.
