This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** Accessible metadata describes Monascus purpureus Went rice as a safe/effective/economic treatment strategy for nephrotic dyslipidemia, but numeric lipid effects were not extracted in this batch.

**Why it matters:** Population-mismatch/boundary trial; useful for safety and dosing but should not be merged with uncomplicated hypercholesterolemia.

**Potential experiment signals:** total-cholesterol, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote this source to a direct plain red yeast rice claim without a separable RYR-only arm. Preserve the boundary: Secondary hyperlipidemia in nephrotic syndrome; clinician-managed special population.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Comparator/control:** Fluvastatin comparator.

**Duration/follow-up:** Duration not extracted from accessible metadata.

**Safety/adverse events:** Safety was addressed in the trial, but detailed adverse-event rates were not extracted.

**Limitations:** Special kidney/nephrotic population and active-drug comparator; not general OTC RYR evidence.
