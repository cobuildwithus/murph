This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** A 12-week randomized trial compared simvastatin 40 mg with a multidisciplinary lifestyle program plus red yeast rice and fish oil; analyzed participants in both groups had large LDL-C reductions, but the supplement/lifestyle combination cannot isolate red yeast rice.

**Why it matters:** This is clinically supervised comparator evidence for a lifestyle-plus-supplement package, including standardized monacolin-containing red yeast rice, in a primary-prevention population.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, triglycerides, weight, AST/ALT, creatine kinase, muscle symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context only: the intervention was bundled with therapeutic lifestyle coaching and fish oil, and the comparator was simvastatin rather than placebo.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Small single-center trial; not blinded to lifestyle intensity; no cardiovascular outcomes; red yeast rice effect is confounded by fish oil and intensive lifestyle; short follow-up. Primary-prevention hypercholesterolemia patients under physician supervision, not unsupervised supplement use.
