**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-40881894-narrative-lipid-context` — The narrative review reports that RYR with monacolin K up to 10 mg/day can reduce LDL-C up to 34% within 6–8 weeks versus placebo, with associated changes in ApoB, total cholesterol, non-HDL-C, hs-CRP, and blood pressure in summarized literature.
- `finding:pmid-40881894-safety-regulation` — The review notes statin-like mechanism, product/regulatory variation, generally minimal side effects when used appropriately, and potential concerns including myalgia, liver injury case reports, and citrinin contamination.
