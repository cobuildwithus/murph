This source is included for **Direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** Accessible extraction notes report LDL-C -23.0% and total cholesterol -15.5% versus placebo after 16 weeks, with p<0.001. Study described as well tolerated in accessible extraction; detailed AE denominators were not extracted.

**Why it matters:** Adds a mid-duration direct RYR placebo-controlled lipid trial with percent-change estimates.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, total cholesterol, tolerability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports LDL-C and total-cholesterol lowering but requires product-specific and small-sample caveats.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness and boundary:** direct_protocol. Small sample; product-specific HypoCol formulation and limited AE detail available in extracted notes. Population mismatch: LDL-C-selected adults; not enough extracted information for high-risk comorbid groups.
