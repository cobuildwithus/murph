This source is included for **Adjacent combinations and special-population evidence**.

**Findings:** Accessible metadata reports significant improvements in lipid parameters compared with placebo and endothelial reactivity compared with baseline.

**Why it matters:** Important to keep low-monacolin combination products distinct from plain RYR dosing claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** endothelial-function, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote this source to a direct plain red yeast rice claim without a separable RYR-only arm. Preserve the boundary: Combination low-monacolin product, not plain RYR.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Comparator/control:** Placebo under controlled nutritional habits.

**Duration/follow-up:** Duration not extracted from accessible metadata.

**Safety/adverse events:** Adverse-event details were not extracted from accessible metadata.

**Limitations:** Low-monacolin multi-ingredient antioxidant/lipid-lowering product; RYR contribution not separable.
