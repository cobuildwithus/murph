## Extracted findings

- **finding:pmid-24047916-bmd-no-clinically-significant-diet-effect** — Participants lost 1-2% BMD annually at measured sites, but ANCOVA showed no clinically significant effect of diet or diet-by-time interaction on BMD.
- **finding:pmid-24047916-bone-turnover-slowed** — The authors concluded that high dietary protein intake during weight loss slowed bone turnover; the high-normal-protein diet increased C-terminal telopeptide and osteocalcin despite hypercalciuria.
- **finding:pmid-24047916-calcium-replete-weight-loss-boundary** — Because both diets were calcium-replete and the protein prescription was grams/day rather than g/kg/day, the trial is best used as bone-safety context rather than direct protein-floor evidence.

## Protocol appraisal
