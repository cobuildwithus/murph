## Extracted findings

- **finding:pmid-33668058-protein-diet-serum-urate-reduction** — The protein-rich diet reduced serum urate by about −0.16 mg/dL over 6 weeks (95% CI −0.28 to −0.04; P=0.007), while carbohydrate- and unsaturated-fat-rich diets did not show clear reductions.
- **finding:pmid-33668058-dash-pattern-source-mismatch** — Because the protein-rich OmniHeart diet was DASH-style and partly plant-based, the serum-urate result should not be generalized to meat-heavy or purine-heavy high-protein diets.
