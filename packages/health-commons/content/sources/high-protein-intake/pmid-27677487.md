## Extracted findings

- **finding:pmid-27677487-post-sleeve-ped-body-composition-rmr** — Compared with the 1.0 g/kg/day ideal-body-weight normal-protein diet, the protein-enriched diet had greater fat-mass decrease and smaller decreases in fat-free mass and resting metabolic rate; total body weight variation was not significantly different, and the abstract reports p<0.01 for the fat-mass, fat-free-mass, and RMR contrasts without extractable effect sizes.
- **finding:pmid-27677487-ideal-body-weight-denominator-adherence-context** — The intervention used ideal body weight, not actual body weight, as the protein denominator; compliance was assessed with food frequency questionnaires and described as high in both diet groups.
- **finding:pmid-27677487-renal-function-safety-context** — The abstract states that both diets had no negative impact on renal function; no additional adverse-event details were extractable from the accessible abstract.

## Protocol appraisal

- **evidence_appraisal:protein-floor-high-protein-intake:pmid-27677487** — Post-sleeve 2.0 g/kg ideal-body-weight protein favored fat-mass, FFM, and RMR outcomes in a male bariatric population. Implication: Useful as clinical context for body-composition, resting-energy-expenditure, adherence, and renal-function monitoring, but not as direct proof for a free-living general-adult Protein Floor protocol.
