## Extracted findings

- **finding:pmid-30032227-renal-markers-short-term-normal** — Across RCTs, GFR was higher in some higher-protein groups and unchanged in others, while values remained in the normal range; the review found little or no effect on blood renal markers and no adverse blood-pressure signal in healthy adults.
- **finding:pmid-30032227-stone-evidence-limited-inconsistent** — Evidence on kidney stones was limited and inconsistent, and most studies were shorter than six months with moderate to high risk of bias.

## Protocol appraisal

- **evidence_appraisal:protein-floor-high-protein-intake:pmid-30032227** — Short-term healthy-adult renal markers were generally reassuring, but stone and long-term certainty were limited. Implication: Can support cautious renal-monitoring context for healthy users, while keeping stone-prone and CKD populations as exclusions or clinician-supervised cases.
