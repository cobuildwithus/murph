## Extracted findings

- **finding:pmid-15941879-weight-loss-equivalence** — Both diets produced about 7.3 kg weight loss over 12 weeks, with no overall weight-loss superiority for the higher-protein diet.
- **finding:pmid-15941879-triglyceride-fat-mass-subgroup** — In women with higher baseline triglycerides, the higher-protein diet produced greater fat-mass loss and larger triglyceride reduction; overall triglycerides also decreased more with the higher-protein diet.
- **finding:pmid-15941879-nutrient-renal-bone-safety** — Vitamin B12 moved more favorably with the higher-protein diet, while bone-turnover markers and creatinine clearance changed during weight loss; no hard renal or bone adverse-event signal was established from this short trial.
