## Extracted findings

- **finding:pmid-40541063-oxidative-stress-no-major-impact** — Measured oxidative-stress markers changed mainly by time rather than group, and the authors summarized the high-protein diet plus strength training as showing no major impact on oxidative stress in older adults.
- **finding:pmid-40541063-urea-bun-normal-range** — Urea and blood urea nitrogen increased significantly in the high-protein group, plausibly reflecting higher protein intake, but remained within the normal range.
- **finding:pmid-40541063-sex-specific-marker-caution** — Women showed more altered oxidative-stress markers and reduced uric acid in the high-protein group, making sex-specific monitoring a caution despite the overall no-major-impact conclusion.
