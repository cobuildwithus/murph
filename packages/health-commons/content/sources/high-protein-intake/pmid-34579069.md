## Extracted findings

- **finding:pmid-34579069-body-weight-meta-analysis** — The body-weight meta-analysis found that increased-protein interventions reduced body weight by 1.6 kg (95% CI 1.2 to 2.0) versus controls; heterogeneity was moderate (I2=56%).
- **finding:pmid-34579069-heterogeneity-risk-bias** — Included studies were heterogeneous in duration, comparator, and protein definition; many were nonblinded, several lacked detailed allocation concealment or blinded outcome assessment, and protein was often reported as percent energy rather than g/kg/day.
