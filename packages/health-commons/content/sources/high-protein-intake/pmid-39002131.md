## Extracted findings

- **finding:pmid-39002131-muscle-mass-preservation** — Increased protein intake significantly prevented muscle mass decline during weight loss (SMD 0.75; 95% CI 0.41 to 1.10; p<0.001); the review anticipated intake exceeding 1.3 g/kg/day would increase muscle mass, while intake below 1.0 g/kg/day was associated with higher decline risk.
- **finding:pmid-39002131-strength-function-null** — Enhanced protein intake did not significantly prevent decreases in muscle strength or physical function, so body-composition findings should not be generalized to functional benefit without additional evidence.

## Protocol appraisal
