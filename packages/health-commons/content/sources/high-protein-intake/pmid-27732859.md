## Extracted findings

- **finding:pmid-27732859-ffm-preservation-during-weight-loss** — Both weight-loss groups lost about 10% body weight, and the higher-protein diet reduced the contribution of fat-free mass to total weight loss by about 45%, preserving roughly 700 g of fat-free mass versus standard protein.
- **finding:pmid-27732859-insulin-action-improvement-eliminated** — Standard-protein weight loss improved insulin-stimulated glucose disposal, whereas the higher-protein weight-loss arm did not show this improvement, eliminating the weight-loss-induced insulin-action benefit.
- **finding:pmid-27732859-oxidative-stress-pathway-boundary** — The higher-protein arm prevented weight-loss-related reductions in oxidative-stress-related muscle pathways and increased PRDX3 expression, suggesting a possible mechanism for the adverse insulin-action result.

## Protocol appraisal

- **evidence_appraisal:protein-floor-high-protein-intake:pmid-27732859** — Higher protein during weight loss preserved modest lean mass but blunted insulin-action improvement in postmenopausal obesity. Implication: Treat as a population-specific metabolic caution and include glucose/insulin-sensitivity endpoints when applying a protein floor during weight loss.
