## Extracted findings

- **finding:pmid-41260173-overall-mace-association** — Across 256,258.9 person-years and 967 MACE events, high-protein intake was associated with higher MACE risk after adjustment (HR 1.21, 95% CI 1.02-1.44; P=0.027).
- **finding:pmid-41260173-age-stratified-mace-signal** — Among participants older than 55 years, high protein intake was associated with higher MACE risk (HR 1.36, 95% CI 1.13-1.63; P=0.001), while the association was not significant under age 55 (HR 0.75, 95% CI 0.51-1.11; P=0.099; interaction P=0.003).
- **finding:pmid-41260173-mortality-hf-mi-cvd-death-associations** — High-protein intake was also associated with higher adjusted risks of all-cause mortality, heart failure, myocardial infarction, and CVD death; these are observational associations and should not be treated as causal effects.

## Protocol appraisal

- **evidence_appraisal:protein-floor-high-protein-intake:pmid-41260173** — UK Biobank high-protein intake ≥1.8 g/kg/day was associated with higher CVD risk, especially after age 55. Implication: Use as observational cardiovascular safety context and a reason to stratify by age and baseline CVD risk.
