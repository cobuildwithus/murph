## Extracted findings

- **finding:pmid-31794597-lean-mass-stressor-benefit** — Across all comparisons, >RDA protein favored lean-mass change versus RDA protein (WMD 0.32 kg; 95% CI 0.01 to 0.64; P=0.043); subgroup effects favored energy restriction (WMD 0.36 kg; 95% CI 0.06 to 0.67) and resistance training (WMD 0.77 kg; 95% CI 0.23 to 1.31).
- **finding:pmid-31794597-nonstressed-null** — In adults with no energy restriction and no resistance training, >RDA protein did not differentially affect lean mass (WMD 0.08 kg; 95% CI -0.59 to 0.75; P=0.810), supporting a boundary that stress state modifies relevance.

## Protocol appraisal

- **evidence_appraisal:protein-floor-high-protein-intake:pmid-31794597** — Protein above the RDA favored lean mass under energy restriction or resistance training, but not in nonstressed adults. Implication: Supports stress-state boundaries for when a protein floor is likely most relevant.
