## Extracted findings

- **finding:pmid-33572843-high-protein-pregnancy-preterm-mixed** — High dietary protein intake above 100 g/day was associated with a modestly higher preterm delivery odds ratio (OR 1.10, 95% CI 1.01 to 1.19) but was not associated with low birthweight, fetal death, neonatal death, or changed mean birthweight.
- **finding:pmid-33572843-very-low-protein-lbw** — Very low protein intake below 60 g/day was associated with increased low-birthweight risk (OR 1.59, 95% CI 1.25 to 2.03).
- **finding:pmid-33572843-observational-food-not-supplement** — The authors emphasize observational limitations, possible residual confounding, and that food-based intake in well-nourished cohorts differs from the older Harlem high-protein supplementation concern.

## Protocol appraisal
