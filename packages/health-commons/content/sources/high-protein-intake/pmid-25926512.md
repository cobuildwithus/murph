## Extracted findings

- **finding:pmid-25926512-long-term-compliance-boundary** — The review describes short-term tightly controlled studies as showing benefits of higher-protein intake, while longer-term studies are limited and conflicting; compliance with the higher-protein diet is identified as a likely reason for discrepancies.
