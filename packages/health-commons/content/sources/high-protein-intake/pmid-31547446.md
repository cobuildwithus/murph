## Extracted findings

- **finding:pmid-31547446-tmao-increase** — Compared with RDA, the 2RDA diet increased circulating TMAO (p=0.002) and unexpectedly decreased renal excretion of TMAO (p=0.003).
- **finding:pmid-31547446-ldl-increase-other-markers-null** — LDL cholesterol increased in the 2RDA group compared with RDA (p=0.049), while other CVD risk biomarkers and insulin sensitivity did not differ between groups.
- **finding:pmid-31547446-older-men-biomarker-boundary** — This is direct dose evidence for 1.6 g/kg/day in older men, but TMAO and LDL are biomarker signals rather than clinical cardiovascular outcomes.

## Protocol appraisal

- **evidence_appraisal:protein-floor-high-protein-intake:pmid-31547446** — Twice-RDA protein in older men increased TMAO and LDL, without differences in other CVD biomarkers or insulin sensitivity. Implication: Supports including lipid and TMAO/cardiometabolic biomarker monitoring for older users or high animal-protein patterns.
