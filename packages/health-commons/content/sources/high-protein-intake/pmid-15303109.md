## Extracted findings

- **finding:pmid-15303109-six-month-weight-loss** — At 6 months, the high-protein arm lost more weight than the medium-protein arm (about 9.4 kg versus 5.9 kg; P<0.01).
- **finding:pmid-15303109-twelve-month-maintenance-and-dropout** — At 12 months, weight loss was no longer clearly superior for high protein (about 6.2 kg versus 4.3 kg), while dropout was lower in the high-protein arm (about 8% versus 28%, borderline P<0.07); by 24 months more than half of participants were lost to follow-up.
- **finding:pmid-15303109-visceral-fat-signal** — The high-protein arm had a reported signal for greater intra-abdominal adipose-tissue reduction and more participants losing more than 10 kg, but these findings were not framed as definitive direct protocol evidence.

## Protocol appraisal

- **evidence_appraisal:protein-floor-high-protein-intake:pmid-15303109:implementation-adherence-burden** — Higher-protein diet improved short-term weight loss but long-term durability was uncertain.
