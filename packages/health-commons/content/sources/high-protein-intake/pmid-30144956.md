## Extracted findings

- **finding:pmid-30144956-cirrhosis-protein-range** — EASL recommends protein intake of 1.2-1.5 g/kg body weight/day in patients with cirrhosis to prevent or reverse muscle loss, making liver disease a clinician-supervised rather than blanket-restriction context.
- **finding:pmid-30144956-hepatic-encephalopathy-protein-restriction** — The guideline states that normal-to-high protein intake does not precipitate hepatic encephalopathy and may improve mental status, so routine protein restriction is not supported.
- **finding:pmid-30144956-liver-disease-boundary** — Chronic liver disease requires disease-specific assessment and clinician guidance; these recommendations should not be used as direct efficacy evidence for healthy adults.
