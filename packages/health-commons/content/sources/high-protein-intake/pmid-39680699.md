## Extracted findings

- **finding:pmid-39680699-ahrq-scope** — The AHRQ review searched MEDLINE, Embase, AGRICOLA, and Scopus from January 2000 to May 2024 and evaluated protein intake associations with bone disease, kidney disease, and sarcopenia to inform future DRI/CDRR work.
- **finding:pmid-39680699-evidence-unclear** — The review concluded that evidence since 2000 on associations between dietary protein intake and the risks of bone disease, kidney disease, and sarcopenia is unclear, with insufficient evidence driven by few lower-risk studies, diverse interventions, and inconsistent or intermediate outcomes.
- **finding:pmid-39680699-exercise-exclusion-boundary** — Because the review focused on protein intake without exercise and excluded exercise-evaluated protein interventions and elite-athlete contexts, it should not be used to infer training-response efficacy for the protein-floor protocol.

## Protocol appraisal

- **evidence_appraisal:protein-floor-high-protein-intake:pmid-39680699** — AHRQ review finds protein-health evidence unclear for bone, kidney, and sarcopenia outcomes. Implication: Use to keep safety and chronic-disease uncertainty explicit, not to claim broad efficacy or broad harm.
