This source is included for **Sleep, autonomic, wearable, and acute-alcohol measurement context**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries identify disrupted sleep homeostasis as a primary mechanism for sleep disruption after binge drinking.

**Why it matters:** Mechanistic support for why alcohol-free periods may alter sleep pressure and architecture, without direct efficacy claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep homeostasis, REM sleep, slow-wave sleep, sleep fragmentation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mechanistic background; do not cite for quantified abstinence effects.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `same_mechanism`.

**Population mismatch:** Mechanistic/preclinical literature may not map directly to short-term wellness challenges.

**Limitations and safety notes:** Mechanistic review; not an intervention trial; not duration-specific. Review context only; no abstinence adverse events were extracted.
