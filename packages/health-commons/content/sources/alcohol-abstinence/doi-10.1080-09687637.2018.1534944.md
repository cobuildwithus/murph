This source is included for **Dry January and temporary abstinence campaign evidence**.

**Findings:**
- The qualitative analysis frames Dry January as a socially legible route to self-formation and positive regulation rather than merely abstinence as restriction.

**Why it matters:** Explains why a voluntary alcohol-free month may be socially acceptable or motivating, but it does not measure protocol outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- social meaning
- identity framing
- positive regulation

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for messaging and burden framing, not for claims about drinking reduction or health effects.
