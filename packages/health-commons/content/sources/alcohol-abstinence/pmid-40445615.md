This source is included for **Sleep, autonomic, wearable, and acute-alcohol measurement context**.

**Findings:** The trial did not detect significant benefit on total drinks or alcohol consequences versus active controls; all conditions reduced drinking over time, and secondary/exploratory sleep-related impairment and WHO drinking risk-level outcomes favored the full intervention in some comparisons.

**Why it matters:** Important recent adjacent digital/wearable alcohol-sleep intervention with preserved null primary outcome.

**Potential experiment signals:** total drinks, alcohol consequences, sleep-related impairment, sleep disturbance, WHO drinking risk-level reduction, adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent digital-intervention context and null/mixed evidence; do not cite as direct abstinence-challenge support.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `adjacent_variant`.

**Population mismatch:** Young adults with at-risk drinking and sleep concerns differ from broad Murph short abstinence users.

**Limitations and safety notes:** Adjacent digital alcohol/sleep intervention rather than abstinence challenge; phase 2 trial; primary outcome was null. No adverse events were extracted; population had at-risk drinking and sleep concerns.
