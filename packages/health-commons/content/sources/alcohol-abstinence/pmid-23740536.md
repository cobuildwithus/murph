This source is included for **Withdrawal-risk screening and acute safety guidance**.

**Findings:** Cochrane review of magnesium for alcohol withdrawal concluded there was insufficient evidence to determine whether magnesium is beneficial or harmful for treatment or prevention of AWS.

**Why it matters:** Preserves negative/insufficient evidence for non-benzodiazepine adjuncts; useful to avoid unsupported self-treatment claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** withdrawal symptoms, adverse events, medication safety, null or insufficient evidence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-screening or escalation evidence only. It does not support efficacy claims for 7-, 14-, or 30-day alcohol-free variants.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
