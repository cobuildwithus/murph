This source is included for **Registry, external protocol, and miscellaneous context records**.

**Findings:** Brief-intervention participants had greater reduction in alcohol quantity across follow-up; accessible reports also note reduced heavy drinking days, frequency at six months, and association with improved diabetes self-care adherence.

**Why it matters:** Adds sparse diabetes/self-care context for alcohol-reduction interventions, but the intervention is counseling rather than a short alcohol-free challenge.

**Potential experiment signals:** self-reported drinks per drinking day, heavy drinking days, diabetes self-care adherence, glucose-related self-tracking where available.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent behavior-change context for at-risk drinkers with diabetes; do not generalize to healthy users or claim a direct 7-, 14-, or 30-day abstinence effect.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `adjacent_variant`.
