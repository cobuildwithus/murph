This source is included for **Alcohol-reduction comparator trials, digital interventions, and liver-focused protocols**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-17870206-modest-three-month-reduction` — At three months, intervention patients reported about 3.25 fewer US standard drinks per week than controls and a lower maximum drinks-per-occasion difference of roughly three-quarters of a drink in the extracted summary.
- `finding:pmid-17870206-severity-boundary` — The extracted summary reported larger apparent benefit among lower-severity at-risk drinkers than dependent drinkers, with 37% versus 19% below low-risk limits in the intervention versus control subgroup with CAGE score under 2.

**Why it matters:** It gives setting-specific brief-intervention context and highlights that severity and acute-care context affect generalizability.

**Potential experiment signals:** weekly alcohol consumption, maximum drinks per occasion, low-risk drinking status, CAGE severity subgroup, follow-up completion.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent emergency-care reduction evidence only; modest short-term reductions after SBIRT do not equal voluntary abstinence-challenge efficacy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Quasi-experimental sequential design rather than individual randomization; emergency-department population; substantial loss to follow-up; reductions concentrated among lower-severity drinkers; no abstinence arm.
