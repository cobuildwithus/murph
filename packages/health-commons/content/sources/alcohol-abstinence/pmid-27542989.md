This source is included for **Medication, pregnancy, liver disease, and mental-health safety boundaries (part 1)**.

**Findings:** In alcoholic patients followed with transient elastography, liver stiffness fell after short abstinence, indicating that active drinking can confound liver-stiffness interpretation in clinical liver-disease populations.

**Why it matters:** Four-week abstinence/liver-stiffness source with labs, but clinical population is outside wellness-challenge scope.

**Potential experiment signals:** liver stiffness, ALT, AST, GGT, bilirubin, liver enzymes.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as measurement context for liver stiffness after abstinence in clinical populations only.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
