This source is included for **Dry January and temporary abstinence campaign evidence**.

**Findings:**
- Dry January completers had beneficial changes in physical health, mental wellbeing, drinking-refusal self-efficacy, and AUDIT-C that were not also observed among adult drinkers in the general population.

**Why it matters:** Strengthens direct 30-day evidence by showing some benefits were not mirrored in non-abstaining adult drinkers.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- physical health
- mental wellbeing
- drinking-refusal self-efficacy
- AUDIT-C

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports direct 30-day claims about possible short- and medium-term benefits, framed as observational rather than causal.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
