## Extraction notes

- **Study design:** cross_sectional_survey
- **Participant count:** 1022 (reported)
- **Population:** Great Britain adults aged 16+ who had attempted to restrict alcohol consumption in the past year
- **Intervention or exposure:** Use of alcohol-free and low-alcohol drinks during attempts to restrict alcohol consumption
- **Comparator or control:** Survey comparisons by no/low use and restriction-attempt characteristics
- **Duration or follow-up:** Past-year restriction attempt; cross-sectional survey waves in 2023–2024
- **Endpoints:** no/low drink use, self-reported reduced consumption, self-reported success, future-health motivation
- **Effect estimates or direction:** 33% used no/low drinks to support a restriction attempt and 77% reported reduced consumption since the attempt; adjusted association with self-reported success was inconclusive.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No clinical adverse events; substitution/addition and success uncertainty are implementation notes.
- **Limitations:** Cross-sectional and self-reported; cannot infer causality or effectiveness of no/low drinks.
