This source is included for **Withdrawal-risk screening and acute safety guidance**.

**Findings:** Cochrane review of baclofen for alcohol withdrawal concluded that no conclusions could be drawn about efficacy and safety because the evidence was insufficient and very low quality.

**Why it matters:** Preserves mixed/insufficient medication evidence so protocol safety language does not overgeneralize medication options.

**Potential experiment signals:** withdrawal symptoms, adverse events, medication safety, null or insufficient evidence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-screening or escalation evidence only. It does not support efficacy claims for 7-, 14-, or 30-day alcohol-free variants.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
