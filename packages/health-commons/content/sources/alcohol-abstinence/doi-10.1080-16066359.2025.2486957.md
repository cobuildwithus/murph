This source is included for **Dry January and temporary abstinence campaign evidence**.

**Findings:**
- Interviews identified four practical challenge areas: breaking routine, dealing with socialising, avoiding loss of motivation, and dealing with potential failure; official participants reported more use of digital support tools than unofficial participants.

**Why it matters:** Provides direct implementation evidence on routine disruption, socialising, motivation, perceived failure, and differences between official and unofficial participants.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- socialising difficulty
- motivation
- habit/routine disruption
- coping strategies

**Protocol takeaway:** Protocol guidance should include social-event planning, routine replacement, motivation repair, and non-punitive lapse framing.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
