This source is included for **Dry January and temporary abstinence campaign evidence**.

**Findings:**
- The commentary emphasizes uncertainty about campaign effects and flags that unsupervised abstinence may not be appropriate for people at risk of alcohol withdrawal or dependence-related complications.

**Why it matters:** Provides a safety and claims-boundary guardrail, especially around self-selection, evidence gaps, and detox risk in dependent drinkers.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- safety boundary
- unintended consequences
- evidence uncertainty

**Protocol takeaway:** Protocol copy should include a medical caution for dependent/heavy drinkers and avoid unsupported rebound or detox claims.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
