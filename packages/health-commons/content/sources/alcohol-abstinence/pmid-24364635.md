This source is included for **Withdrawal-risk screening and acute safety guidance**.

**Findings:** Outpatient AWS management review states that mild or moderate alcohol withdrawal syndrome can be treated as outpatient care, but this is framed as clinical management and not as unsupervised self-directed cessation.

**Why it matters:** Older practical outpatient review that preserves boundary logic: outpatient only for mild/moderate cases; severe or high-risk cases need inpatient treatment.

**Potential experiment signals:** mild withdrawal, moderate withdrawal, severe symptoms, complications, inpatient referral.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-screening or escalation evidence only. It does not support efficacy claims for 7-, 14-, or 30-day alcohol-free variants.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
