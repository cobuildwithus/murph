This source is included for **Alcohol-reduction comparator reviews and public-health guideline context**.

**Findings:** The scientific statement synthesizes alcohol and cardiovascular disease evidence for professional guidance; it is safety and clinical-context evidence, not an abstinence-challenge efficacy trial.

**Why it matters:** Current cardiovascular-professional context for alcohol exposure and safety endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** morning blood pressure, resting heart rate, palpitations or arrhythmia symptoms, cardiovascular safety flags.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for cardiovascular safety framing and endpoint selection only; do not use as protocol efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness:** `general_guideline`.

**Population mismatch:** Broad cardiovascular guidance is not specific to low-risk self-experimenters.

**Limitations and safety notes:** Guideline/scientific statement; no direct 7-, 14-, or 30-day abstinence-challenge outcomes. Provides cardiovascular safety context for alcohol exposure, including endpoints relevant to blood pressure and arrhythmia risk.
