This source is included for **Withdrawal-risk screening and acute safety guidance**.

**Findings:** Michigan Alcohol Withdrawal Severity protocol describes alcohol withdrawal syndrome and offers recommendations for identification, evaluation, and management of AWS in hospitalized patients.

**Why it matters:** Recent evidence-based institutional protocol with practical screening and escalation structure; useful for onboarding exclusion and urgent-care guidance boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** withdrawal symptoms, severe withdrawal, escalation of care, adverse events, clinician guidance boundary.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-screening or escalation evidence only. It does not support efficacy claims for 7-, 14-, or 30-day alcohol-free variants.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
