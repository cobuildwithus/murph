This source is included for **Dry January and temporary abstinence campaign evidence**.

**Findings:**
- The weighted survey reported substantial complete abstinence among current drinkers during the campaign and additional partial abstinence or reduced quantity, but the extracted source should be treated as adjacent campaign evidence.

**Why it matters:** Adjacent evidence on completion and behavior change during a longer public abstinence campaign.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- complete abstinence
- partial abstinence
- reduced drinking quantity
- campaign participation

**Protocol takeaway:** Useful for campaign-adherence context, but the 3-month Buddhist Lent setting should not be merged with 30-day Dry January claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
