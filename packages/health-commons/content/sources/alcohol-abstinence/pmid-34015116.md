This source is included for **Sleep, autonomic, wearable, and acute-alcohol measurement context**.

**Findings:** Binge alcohol reduced high-frequency and time-domain HRV components including RMSSD across sleep stages and reduced cardiovagal baroreflex sensitivity.

**Why it matters:** Provides stage-specific nocturnal HRV and baroreflex evidence for acute alcohol effects.

**Potential experiment signals:** HRV RMSSD, high-frequency HRV, pNN50, cardiovagal baroreflex sensitivity, sleep stages.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as same-mechanism acute alcohol evidence for HRV/RMSSD and autonomic endpoints, not as abstinence efficacy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `same_mechanism`.

**Population mismatch:** Binge drinkers and acute exposure differ from Murph users avoiding alcohol.

**Limitations and safety notes:** Binge alcohol exposure, small sample, and laboratory setting; not a voluntary abstinence study. Autonomic disruption after binge alcohol is a cardiovascular risk mechanism, not a reported abstinence adverse event.
