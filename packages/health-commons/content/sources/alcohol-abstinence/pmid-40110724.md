This source is included for **Direct protocol and duration evidence**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-40110724-three-change-patterns` — The extracted abstract identified three patterns: mainly clear change, unclear/complicated change, and no change or limited change, with lower T3 alcohol intake among four of six participants.
- `finding:pmid-40110724-ambivalence-backfire` — Some interviews suggested ambivalence and possible backfire effects in which positives of alcohol consumption were highlighted and linked to negligible consumption change.

**Why it matters:** Captures mixed subjective and longer-term behavior-change responses among heavier drinkers, including ambivalence and possible backfire effects.

**Potential experiment signals:** post-challenge drinking, drink-refusal self-efficacy, reflection, ambivalence, subjective burden, qualitative experience.

**Protocol takeaway:** Heavier drinkers had wide-ranging TAC responses, including clear change, complicated change, and limited/no change patterns; protocol off-ramp and reflection steps matter.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** n=6 qualitative sample; not designed for effect-size estimation; heavier-drinker UK context; extracted source includes mixed/ambivalent experiences, not uniform benefit. Population mismatch: UK heavier drinkers may not generalize to lower-risk wellness users; qualitative findings illustrate experience rather than population rates.
