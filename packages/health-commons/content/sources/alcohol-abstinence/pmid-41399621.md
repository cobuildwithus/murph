This source is included for **Registry, external protocol, and miscellaneous context records**.

**Findings:** Hepatic fat fraction increased 2.5-fold from 1.9% to 4.6%; 6 of 15 participants developed steatosis; liver stiffness and triglycerides increased while LDL-C decreased; after 10 days of abstinence, outcome measures normalized in accessible reporting.

**Why it matters:** Closest short-term abstinence recovery context in this batch, but it follows a binge exposure and should be framed as liver-safety/recovery evidence rather than a clean planned challenge.

**Potential experiment signals:** GGT or liver enzymes where clinically ordered, triglycerides, LDL cholesterol, MRI-PDFF in research contexts, liver stiffness in research contexts.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a safety/recovery boundary after binge exposure; do not generalize to normal drinkers beginning a planned 7-, 14-, or 30-day abstinence challenge without binge exposure.

**Directness:** `adjacent_variant`.
