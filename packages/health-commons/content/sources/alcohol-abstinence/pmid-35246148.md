**Findings:**
- The scoping review found participant benefits such as reduced drinking and perceived health improvements, but emphasized limited study numbers, heterogeneity, and largely observational evidence.

**Why it matters:** Synthesizes the early Dry January/Dry July literature and highlights both benefits and evidence limitations.
