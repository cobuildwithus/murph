This source is included for **Alcohol-reduction comparator reviews and public-health guideline context**.

**Findings:** The review concludes that alcohol reduction has evidence for blood-pressure relevance generally, but little evidence identifies any one care model as clearly effective for blood pressure; more established-intervention studies that measure BP are needed.

**Why it matters:** Useful for endpoint rationale and for caution that care-model evidence is thinner than general alcohol/BP associations.

**Potential experiment signals:** morning blood pressure, intervention model, weekly alcohol intake.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as blood-pressure measurement context and uncertainty guardrail; not direct protocol support.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `measurement_context`.

**Population mismatch:** Care-model literature is broader and more clinical than self-directed Murph abstinence challenges.

**Limitations and safety notes:** Narrative review; not a pooled estimate or direct abstinence intervention. Safety details were not extracted as an intervention adverse-event source.
