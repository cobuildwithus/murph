This source is included for **Registry, external protocol, and miscellaneous context records**.

**Findings:** Reducing ethanol intake from roughly 336 to 65 mL/week was associated with significantly lower systolic blood pressure; blood pressure rose when regular drinking resumed.

**Why it matters:** Foundational adjacent evidence that changing alcohol intake can move blood pressure over weeks, an endpoint many users may track.

**Potential experiment signals:** home blood pressure, morning systolic blood pressure, weekly alcohol intake.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as same-mechanism/adjacent BP evidence; do not treat it as a clean 30-day total-abstinence challenge.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `same_mechanism`.
