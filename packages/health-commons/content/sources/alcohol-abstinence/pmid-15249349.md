This source is included for **Withdrawal-risk screening and acute safety guidance**.

**Findings:** Evidence-based guideline/meta-analysis on alcohol withdrawal delirium. It supports treating delirium tremens as a severe medical complication requiring clinical management rather than a self-directed abstinence challenge condition.

**Why it matters:** Foundational guidance on the most severe withdrawal manifestation; relevant to emergency stop conditions and exclusion of anyone with prior DTs.

**Potential experiment signals:** delirium tremens, agitation, mortality, morbidity, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-screening or escalation evidence only. It does not support efficacy claims for 7-, 14-, or 30-day alcohol-free variants.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
