This source is included for **Withdrawal-risk screening and acute safety guidance**.

**Findings:** ASAM clinical practice guideline provides evidence-based strategies for ambulatory and inpatient alcohol withdrawal management and emphasizes that withdrawal management alone is not treatment for alcohol use disorder.

**Why it matters:** Core professional guideline for defining who should not attempt abrupt cessation without medical evaluation and which settings are appropriate for withdrawal management.

**Potential experiment signals:** withdrawal symptoms, seizure risk, delirium tremens, adverse events, clinician guidance boundary.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-screening or escalation evidence only. It does not support efficacy claims for 7-, 14-, or 30-day alcohol-free variants.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
