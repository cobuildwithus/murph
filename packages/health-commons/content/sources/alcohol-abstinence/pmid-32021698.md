This source is included for **Direct protocol and duration evidence**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-32021698-feasible-with-imperfect-adherence` — In the extracted trial report, 25 women were randomized and 24 were retained through the 28-day intervention; median verified abstinent days were 24 in the complete-abstinence arm and 12 in the intermittent arm.
- `finding:pmid-32021698-no-adverse-events-reported` — No adverse events were reported in the extracted feasibility trial summary, but the small selected sample limits safety inference.

**Why it matters:** Direct randomized feasibility evidence for a complete abstinence arm and an intermittent abstinence variant, with objective breathalyser verification.

**Potential experiment signals:** breathalyser-verified abstinent days, adherence, retention, mood, sleep, adverse events, post-challenge alcohol consumption.

**Protocol takeaway:** Four-week complete and intermittent abstinence variants were feasible in a small heavy-drinking female sample, with good but imperfect adherence and no adverse events reported; not powered for efficacy.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Small feasibility trial; women aged 40-60 drinking heavily; no usual-drinking control; efficacy outcomes not powered; support and breathalyser verification may differ from Murph self-guided use. Population mismatch: Heavy-drinking women aged 40-60 may not represent all users, men, younger adults, or low-risk abstinence-challenge participants.
