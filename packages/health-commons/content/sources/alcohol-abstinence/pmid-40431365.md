## Extraction notes

- **Study design:** randomized_investigator_blinded_trial
- **Participant count:** 44 (reported)
- **Population:** Healthy young male adults
- **Intervention or exposure:** 660 mL/day of non-alcoholic pilsener, mixed beer, or wheat beer for 4 weeks
- **Comparator or control:** Water control group
- **Duration or follow-up:** 4 weeks
- **Endpoints:** glucose metabolism, lipid metabolism, body composition, liver enzymes, microbiota
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Different non-alcoholic beer types produced mixed metabolic changes; mixed and wheat beers showed unfavorable glucose/lipid signals in extracted results, while pilsener and water lowered cholesterol/LDL.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Metabolic concerns from sugar/calories in some non-alcoholic beers are a safety/implementation note; no clinical adverse events extracted.
- **Limitations:** Healthy young male-only sample, specific beverage dosing, and metabolic endpoint focus; not an abstinence challenge or behavioral substitution trial.
- **Population mismatch:** Non-alcoholic beer metabolic trial differs from alcohol-free challenge completion and broad adult populations.
- **Directness to protocol:** replacement-beverage safety context
- **Claim-use boundary:** safety-only
