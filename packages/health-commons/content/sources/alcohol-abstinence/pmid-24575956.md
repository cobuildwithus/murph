This source is included for **Clinical supervised abstinence, AUD detox, and liver-disease biomarker context**.

**Findings:** Recently detoxified alcohol-dependent adults had poorer sleep and lower sleep HRV versus controls, supporting HRV/sleep as clinical safety signals rather than direct short-challenge endpoints.

**Why it matters:** Sleep-autonomic dysfunction in recently detoxified alcohol-dependent men and women; useful boundary not wellness evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep HRV, heart rate, SDNN, high-frequency HRV, RMSSD, sleep quality.

**Protocol takeaway:** HRV/sleep changes in detoxified AUD patients remain clinical context only.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
