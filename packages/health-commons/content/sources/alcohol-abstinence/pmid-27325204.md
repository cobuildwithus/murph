This source is included for **Withdrawal-risk screening and acute safety guidance**.

**Findings:** Systematic review of community detoxification reported high completion rates and safety in accessible summaries, with community detoxification examined for effectiveness, safety, acceptability, and feasibility.

**Why it matters:** Systematic review of community detoxification as a supervised care model; useful boundary evidence for what a protocol should not attempt to replace.

**Potential experiment signals:** community detoxification, safety, acceptability, feasibility, withdrawal management.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-screening or escalation evidence only. It does not support efficacy claims for 7-, 14-, or 30-day alcohol-free variants.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
