This source is included for **Dry January and temporary abstinence campaign evidence**.

**Findings:**
- Both groups decreased automaticity, craving, and consumption at 8 months, but the self-help guide showed no clear between-group advantage and did not change drinking-refusal self-efficacy.

**Why it matters:** Important null evidence for an added support component: self-help content did not clearly improve outcomes beyond the challenge itself.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- drinking automaticity
- craving
- consumption
- drinking-refusal self-efficacy

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not assume a static self-help guide improves outcomes; measure use, fidelity, and engagement if included.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
