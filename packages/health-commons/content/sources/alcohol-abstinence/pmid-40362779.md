This source is included for **Sleep, autonomic, wearable, and acute-alcohol measurement context**.

**Findings:** Nocturnal resting heart rate increased from 63.6±9.2 bpm at baseline to 66.6±9.0 bpm during exposure (p<0.001) and 64.9±9.3 bpm after exposure; objective sleep parameters were not significantly changed in accessible summaries.

**Why it matters:** Close wearable-context design with an alcohol-free baseline and objective RHR/sleep endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** nocturnal resting heart rate, objective sleep parameters, smartwatch measurement.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mixed wearable context: RHR changed, but objective sleep parameters did not clearly change in this small study.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `measurement_context`.

**Population mismatch:** Alcohol exposure after baseline is adjacent to, not identical with, voluntary alcohol-free variants.

**Limitations and safety notes:** Small sample; observational design; smartwatch-based sleep outcomes; moderate exposure rather than abstinence challenge. No adverse events were extracted; alcohol exposure increased nocturnal heart rate.
