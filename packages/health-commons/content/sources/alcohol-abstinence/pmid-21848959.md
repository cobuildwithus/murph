This source is included for **Sleep, autonomic, wearable, and acute-alcohol measurement context**.

**Findings:** Alcohol inhibited parasympathetic nerve activity during sleep and produced dose-related sympathetic predominance in accessible abstract summaries.

**Why it matters:** Foundational controlled evidence that alcohol before sleep suppresses parasympathetic sleep physiology.

**Potential experiment signals:** parasympathetic activity, HRV, heart rate during sleep, sleep stages.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as same-mechanism context for why alcohol-free nights might improve nocturnal autonomic recovery signals; do not present as direct abstinence evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `same_mechanism`.

**Population mismatch:** Young male students differ from mixed-age Murph users undertaking voluntary abstinence.

**Limitations and safety notes:** Very small male-only sample; acute alcohol exposure rather than abstinence; laboratory setting. No adverse events were extracted from accessible source text.
