This source is included for **Withdrawal-risk screening and acute safety guidance**.

**Findings:** SAWS development paper describes a 10-item Short Alcohol Withdrawal Scale with five psychological symptom items and five physical symptom items for brief withdrawal severity measurement.

**Why it matters:** Brief withdrawal-symptom scale candidate for symptom monitoring, not a substitute for medical withdrawal-risk screening.

**Potential experiment signals:** withdrawal symptom severity, self-report symptoms, monitoring.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-screening or escalation evidence only. It does not support efficacy claims for 7-, 14-, or 30-day alcohol-free variants.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
