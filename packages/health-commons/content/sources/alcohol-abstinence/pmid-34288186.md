**Findings:** Clinical management review frames AWS as a condition affecting people with long-term heavy alcohol consumption who stop or reduce alcohol and may need clinical recognition and management.

**Why it matters:** Recent clinical management review that helps frame why abstinence challenges require pre-screening for long-term heavy use and dependence.

**Potential experiment signals:** withdrawal symptoms, severe withdrawal, complications, management boundary.
