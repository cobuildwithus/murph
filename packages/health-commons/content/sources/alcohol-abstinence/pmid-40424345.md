This source is included for **Direct protocol and duration evidence**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-40424345-subgroups-predicted-compliance` — The extracted abstract reported that subgroups differed in TAC abstinence and in changes in weekdays drinking frequency and glasses per weekday.
- `finding:pmid-40424345-limited-postchallenge-prediction` — The extracted abstract reported no significant subgroup differences for excessive drinking volumes and weekend glasses per drinking day, so subgroup assignment had limited predictive value for post-challenge drinking.

**Why it matters:** Direct TAC cohort evidence on which participant subgroups comply with abstinence and how far subgroup prediction extends to later drinking behavior.

**Potential experiment signals:** abstinence compliance, post-challenge drinking frequency, weekday drinks, weekend drinks, excessive drinking volume, participant subgroup.

**Protocol takeaway:** Subgroups predicted abstinence compliance and some weekday drinking changes, but had limited predictive value for broader post-challenge drinking behavior.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Self-selected Dutch TAC cohort; subgroup prediction is not a randomized intervention; predictive value for post-challenge drinking was limited; not a biomarker source. Population mismatch: Campaign participants with 8-month follow-up may differ from self-guided Murph users and from users who do not complete follow-up.
