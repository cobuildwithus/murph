This source is included for **Registry, external protocol, and miscellaneous context records**.

**Findings:** Daily alcohol intake fell from about 56 to 26 mL/day; systolic BP was lower by roughly 2.6 to 4.8 mmHg and diastolic BP by roughly 2.2 to 3.0 mmHg during reduced-alcohol periods.

**Why it matters:** Short-term BP endpoint evidence near the 14- to 30-day range, but it tested reduction, not full abstinence, in hypertensive men.

**Potential experiment signals:** home systolic blood pressure, home diastolic blood pressure, baseline alcohol intake.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent alcohol-reduction BP evidence; do not generalize to complete abstinence or normotensive users.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `adjacent_variant`.
