This source is included for **Dry January and temporary abstinence campaign evidence**.

**Findings:**
- The scoping review found that participants tended to be younger, female, university educated, higher income, and heavier baseline drinkers; completers generally showed short- and mid-term reductions and improved outcomes, but rigorous research gaps remain.

**Why it matters:** Summarizes the Dry January evidence base while preserving participant-profile and evidence-quality limitations.
