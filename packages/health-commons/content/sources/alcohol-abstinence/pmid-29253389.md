This source is included for **Registry, external protocol, and miscellaneous context records**.

**Findings:** Among people drinking two or fewer drinks per day, reduction was not associated with significant BP reduction; among heavier drinkers, BP reductions were dose-related, with strongest reductions among those drinking six or more drinks per day reducing intake by about half.

**Why it matters:** High-yield blood-pressure comparator source; supports dose-related BP expectations for heavier drinkers while preserving threshold findings.

**Potential experiment signals:** home systolic blood pressure, home diastolic blood pressure, baseline drinks per day.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent blood-pressure evidence for alcohol reduction, especially higher baseline intake; do not imply everyone will see BP reductions after short abstinence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `adjacent_variant`.
