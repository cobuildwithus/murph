This source is included for **Withdrawal-risk screening and acute safety guidance**.

**Findings:** Study comparing home detoxification with inpatient care investigated safety and short-term effectiveness by contrasting treatment completion and complications among 41 home detoxification patients.

**Why it matters:** Older safety comparison for home detoxification; important not to conflate supervised home detox with unsupervised short-term abstinence challenges.

**Potential experiment signals:** home detoxification, treatment completion, complications, safety.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-screening or escalation evidence only. It does not support efficacy claims for 7-, 14-, or 30-day alcohol-free variants.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
