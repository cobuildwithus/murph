This source is included for **Dry January and temporary abstinence campaign evidence**.

**Findings:**
- Alcoholic drink sales were lower in January and no/low-alcohol sales were higher than in several other months, but evidence for large sustained alcohol sales reductions was limited or uncertain.

**Why it matters:** Objective sales context for January seasonality and substitution, including limited evidence for sustained sales reductions.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- alcohol sales
- no/low-alcohol sales
- seasonality
- sustained sales change

**Protocol takeaway:** Protocol claims should not imply January challenges create sustained market-level alcohol sales declines.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
