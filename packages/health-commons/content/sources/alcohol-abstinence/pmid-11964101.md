This source is included for **Alcohol-reduction comparator reviews and public-health guideline context**.

**Findings:** The review included 34 non-treatment-seeking brief-intervention versus control investigations and 20 treatment-seeking brief-intervention versus extended-treatment investigations. Brief interventions had small-to-medium effects versus control among non-treatment-seeking participants, while brief interventions generally did not differ from extended treatment among treatment-seeking participants.

**Why it matters:** Separates non-treatment-seeking and treatment-seeking contexts, which is useful for Murph eligibility and referral boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** weekly alcohol intake, treatment-seeking status, baseline severity.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for comparator and population-boundary context; do not cite as proof of abstinence-challenge benefit.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `adjacent_variant`.

**Population mismatch:** Brief interventions are counseling/treatment-lite strategies rather than self-directed abstinence challenges.

**Limitations and safety notes:** The source cautions against broad generalization; population and comparator type substantially affect interpretation. Adverse events were not extracted from the accessible abstract.
