## Extraction notes

- **Study design:** systematic_review_meta_analysis
- **Participant count:** 34390 (reported_total_participants)
- **Population:** Community-dwelling adults and adolescents with hazardous or harmful drinking
- **Intervention or exposure:** Personalised digital interventions to reduce alcohol consumption
- **Comparator or control:** No/minimal intervention or face-to-face comparators in included studies
- **Duration or follow-up:** Follow-up varied across trials
- **Endpoints:** weekly alcohol consumption, drinking days, binge episodes, drinks per drinking occasion, adverse events reporting
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Moderate-quality evidence indicated digital interventions reduced alcohol consumption by about 23 g/week versus controls; evidence versus face-to-face comparators was limited and not clearly different.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse events were reported in the extracted review materials.
- **Limitations:** Digital reduction interventions are not abstinence challenges; heterogeneity and population differences limit directness.
- **Population mismatch:** Hazardous/harmful drinkers using digital reduction tools, not necessarily people attempting full temporary abstinence.
- **Directness to protocol:** adjacent digital-support comparator
- **Claim-use boundary:** context-only
