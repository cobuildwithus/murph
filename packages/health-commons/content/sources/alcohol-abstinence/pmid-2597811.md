This source is included for **Withdrawal-risk screening and acute safety guidance**.

**Findings:** CIWA-Ar is a shortened 10-item scale for clinical quantitation of alcohol withdrawal severity that retained clinical usefulness, validity, and reliability for clinical care and withdrawal trials.

**Why it matters:** Foundational withdrawal-severity monitoring instrument; should be distinguished from pre-cessation risk screens like PAWSS.

**Potential experiment signals:** withdrawal symptom severity, monitoring, treatment triage, clinician guidance boundary.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-screening or escalation evidence only. It does not support efficacy claims for 7-, 14-, or 30-day alcohol-free variants.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
