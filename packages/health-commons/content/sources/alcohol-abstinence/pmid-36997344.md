- **Study design:** behavioral_neuroimaging_mechanistic_study
- **Participant count:** 39 (reported)
- **Population:** Moderate to heavy alcohol consumers meeting sex-specific weekly drinking thresholds
- **Intervention or exposure:** Typical drinking and imposed abstinence periods in a crossover measurement protocol
- **Comparator or control:** Within-person alcohol-use state comparisons and desire versus craving measurement
- **Duration or follow-up:** Three-day typical drinking and imposed abstinence periods
- **Endpoints:** desire ratings, craving ratings, EMA temporal patterns, brain network topology
