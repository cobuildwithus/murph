This source is included for **Withdrawal-risk screening and acute safety guidance**.

**Findings:** Cochrane review of benzodiazepines for alcohol withdrawal. Across 64 studies and 4,309 subjects, benzodiazepines showed protective benefit against withdrawal seizures compared with placebo (RR 0.16, 95% CI 0.04 to 0.69; 3 studies, n=324).

**Why it matters:** High-quality review of a first-line withdrawal medication class; helps define why medication-managed withdrawal is separate from an abstinence challenge.

**Potential experiment signals:** withdrawal symptoms, seizures, delirium tremens, adverse events, treatment boundary.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-screening or escalation evidence only. It does not support efficacy claims for 7-, 14-, or 30-day alcohol-free variants.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
