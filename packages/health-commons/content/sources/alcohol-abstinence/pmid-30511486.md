This source is included for **Dry January and temporary abstinence campaign evidence**.

**Findings:**
- Blog analysis found barriers including stress, tiredness, drinking embedded in social life, negative effects of not drinking, and negative reactions from others; strategies included online/non-drinking networks, self-talk, non-alcohol activities, and substitute drinks.

**Why it matters:** Adjacent support-community evidence for coping strategies, social pressure, substitute activities, and self-talk.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- barriers to abstinence
- coping strategies
- online support community

**Protocol takeaway:** Protocol support should include social-pressure scripts, non-alcohol activities, substitution options, and community-support prompts.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
