This source is included for **Sleep, autonomic, wearable, and acute-alcohol measurement context**.

**Findings:** Review summaries note HRV changes with acute alcohol but almost no data supporting HRV response as an alcoholism susceptibility marker.

**Why it matters:** Methodological and mechanism context for interpreting HRV changes after alcohol exposure or abstinence.

**Potential experiment signals:** HRV, heart rate, autonomic function, susceptibility-marker evidence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as HRV interpretation context and caution against overdiagnostic claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `same_mechanism`.

**Population mismatch:** Acute alcohol HRV literature is not direct evidence for short alcohol-free variants.

**Limitations and safety notes:** Narrative review and older evidence base; not an abstinence intervention. No abstinence adverse events were extracted; source is measurement/mechanism context.
