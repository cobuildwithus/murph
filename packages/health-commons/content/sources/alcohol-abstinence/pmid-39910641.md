This source is included for **Dry January and temporary abstinence campaign evidence**.

**Findings:**
- Participants reported decreased weekly consumption and lower risk/binge drinking proportions short- and medium-term, but low baseline drinkers increased while moderate to high baseline drinkers decreased over time.

**Why it matters:** Adjacent one-month campaign outcome evidence that also preserves mixed findings for low drinkers.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- weekly consumption
- risk drinking
- binge drinking
- subgroup moderation

**Protocol takeaway:** One-month campaign participants may reduce drinking over follow-up, but subgroup differences and low-drinker increases should be disclosed.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
