This source is included for **Clinical supervised abstinence, AUD detox, and liver-disease biomarker context**.

**Findings:** In alcohol-dependent adults studied after detoxification, nocturnal autonomic function was impaired early and showed partial recovery across months of abstinence, supporting HRV/sleep as clinical recovery signals but not as direct wellness-challenge efficacy evidence.

**Why it matters:** Direct sleep-autonomic abstinence recovery evidence in alcohol dependence; important boundary not healthy abstinence challenge.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep HRV, cardiac autonomic function, heart rate, NREM sleep autonomic recovery, abstinence recovery.

**Protocol takeaway:** Clinical HRV recovery signals are relevant to safety and measurement context but cannot be promoted to direct protocol efficacy for short voluntary abstinence.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
