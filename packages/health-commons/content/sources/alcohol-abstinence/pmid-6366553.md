This source is included for **Registry, external protocol, and miscellaneous context records**.

**Findings:** After six weeks, HDL cholesterol and HDL3 mass decreased in abstainers but not drinkers; HDL2 mass was unchanged; resumption increased HDL cholesterol and HDL3 mass.

**Why it matters:** Important counterintuitive lipid context: HDL may fall during abstinence, which should not be misread as net harm or used to encourage drinking.

**Potential experiment signals:** HDL cholesterol, lipid panel, non-HDL cholesterol.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as lipid-context evidence only; do not equate HDL subfraction changes with overall cardiovascular benefit or harm.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `measurement_context`.
