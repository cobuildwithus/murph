This source is included for **Sleep, autonomic, wearable, and acute-alcohol measurement context**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries report that moderate beer intake produced immunomodulatory effects in healthy adults, more apparent in women; abstinence-only benefits were not extracted.

**Why it matters:** Adjacent 30-day abstinence run-in with immune endpoints, useful mainly for lab endpoint breadth and population mismatch.

**Potential experiment signals:** immune markers, sex-specific immune response, adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as adjacent immune/lab context; do not claim abstinence improves immune function.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `adjacent_variant`.

**Population mismatch:** Spanish healthy volunteers and immune lab endpoints may not match Murph users or wearable outcomes.

**Limitations and safety notes:** Main effect is beer reintroduction; participant count not extracted from accessible source text here; immune endpoints are not core consumer challenge endpoints. No adverse events were extracted from accessible source text.
