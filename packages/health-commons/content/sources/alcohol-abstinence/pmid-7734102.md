This source is included for **Registry, external protocol, and miscellaneous context records**.

**Findings:** Average 24-hour BP was not different; abstinence altered diurnal systolic BP pattern, and BP variability was increased during the alcohol phase.

**Why it matters:** Directly includes one week of abstinence, but the clinical alcoholic population and null average-BP result make it a safety/context boundary rather than protocol support.

**Potential experiment signals:** 24-hour ambulatory blood pressure, morning blood pressure, blood pressure variability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as population-mismatched one-week abstinence context; average 24-hour BP did not clearly change in this small clinical sample.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness:** `safety_boundary`.
