**Findings:** Among AUD recovery outpatients, time since last drink was positively associated with HRV, supporting HRV as a recovery-context signal but not direct protocol efficacy.

**Why it matters:** AUD recovery source relating abstinence duration to HRV; useful but not healthy short challenge evidence.
