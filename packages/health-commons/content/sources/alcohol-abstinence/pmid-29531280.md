This source is included for **Alcohol-reduction comparator trials, digital interventions, and liver-focused protocols**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-29531280-no-significant-main-effects` — The factorial trial reported no significant main effects of the five tested app components on alcohol consumption or AUDIT outcomes after four weeks.
- `finding:pmid-29531280-exploratory-component-interactions` — Exploratory interactions suggested that some component combinations may be associated with lower consumption or AUDIT scores, but the extracted report cautioned that these interactions were not prespecified.
- `finding:pmid-29531280-attrition-boundary` — Only 179 of 672 randomized participants completed the primary follow-up in the extracted report, creating a major retention and interpretation boundary.

**Why it matters:** It is adjacent digital alcohol-reduction evidence relevant to app engagement and measurement, but it does not test alcohol-free days as the intervention.

**Potential experiment signals:** weekly alcohol consumption, AUDIT score, app engagement, module usability, follow-up retention.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as digital-intervention context only: app components did not show significant main effects, and attrition makes strong efficacy claims inappropriate.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Only 27% completed the primary outcome follow-up in the extracted article; no abstinence challenge; component interactions were exploratory/not prespecified; self-report outcomes.
