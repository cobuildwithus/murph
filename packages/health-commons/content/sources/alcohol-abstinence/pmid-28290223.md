## Extraction notes

- **Study design:** qualitative_interview
- **Participant count:** 16 (reported)
- **Population:** Australian adults aged 25–65 who stopped or significantly reduced alcohol consumption in the previous year
- **Intervention or exposure:** Stopping or reducing alcohol and adapting social rituals
- **Comparator or control:** No formal comparator
- **Duration or follow-up:** Stopped or reduced in the previous year; exact individual duration varied
- **Endpoints:** replacement drinks, alternative activities, ritual meaning, social acceptability
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Qualitative themes described replacing alcohol with other drinks, replacing drinking occasions with activities, changing ritual meanings, and selecting lower-change approaches as more acceptable.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No clinical adverse events; disruption to social rituals is an implementation burden.
- **Limitations:** Small qualitative sample and longer stopped/reduced histories; not a short challenge trial.
- **Population mismatch:** Adaptation after stopping/reducing differs from planned 7-, 14-, or 30-day variants.
