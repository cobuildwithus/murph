This source is included for **Withdrawal-risk screening and acute safety guidance**.

**Findings:** Review of alcohol withdrawal seizures states that abrupt cessation after prolonged heavy drinking may trigger withdrawal seizures, with generalized tonic-clonic seizures described as characteristic and severe in this setting.

**Why it matters:** Focused source for seizure risk from abrupt withdrawal; useful for stop conditions and exclusion questions about prior seizures.

**Potential experiment signals:** withdrawal seizures, neurobiology, kindling, abrupt cessation risk.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-screening or escalation evidence only. It does not support efficacy claims for 7-, 14-, or 30-day alcohol-free variants.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
