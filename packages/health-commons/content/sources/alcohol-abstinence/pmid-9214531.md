This source is included for **Withdrawal-risk screening and acute safety guidance**.

**Findings:** Meta-analysis/evidence-based practice guideline on pharmacological alcohol withdrawal management concluded that benzodiazepines are suitable agents for alcohol withdrawal, with agent choice guided by duration of action, rapidity of onset, and cost.

**Why it matters:** Foundational evidence-based practice guideline/meta-analysis for pharmacologic treatment; relevant to why high-risk abrupt cessation should not be self-managed.

**Potential experiment signals:** withdrawal symptoms, seizure prevention, delirium tremens prevention, mortality, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-screening or escalation evidence only. It does not support efficacy claims for 7-, 14-, or 30-day alcohol-free variants.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
