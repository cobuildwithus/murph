This source is included for **Sleep, autonomic, wearable, and acute-alcohol measurement context**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries report that HRV/respiratory sinus arrhythmia related to brain-network topology changes between normal drinking and abstinence states.

**Why it matters:** Rare direct short-term abstinence source with HRV-related autonomic and brain-network measures.

**Potential experiment signals:** Porges-Bohrer respiratory sinus arrhythmia, HRV, functional brain network topology.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as direct mechanistic context for short abstinence and HRV/ANS endpoints; not sleep or wearable efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `direct_protocol`.

**Population mismatch:** Everyday drinkers in a mechanistic imaging/HRV protocol may not generalize to broader Murph challenge users.

**Limitations and safety notes:** Small sample; mechanistic brain-network endpoint; not sleep-specific and not a consumer-wearable validation study. No adverse events were extracted from accessible source text.
