This source is included for **Alcohol-reduction comparator trials, digital interventions, and liver-focused protocols**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-17283347-no-alcohol-assistance-benefit` — The intervention was not significantly associated with receipt of alcohol assistance by three months; adjusted proportions were 49% intervention versus 44% control, difference 5% with 95% CI from -8% to 19%.
- `finding:pmid-17283347-no-drinks-per-day-benefit` — The intervention did not improve drinks-per-day outcomes at 12 months; extracted adjusted decreases were 1.5 drinks/day intervention versus 3.1 drinks/day usual care, with group difference CI crossing no effect.

**Why it matters:** It preserves a negative/mixed boundary: brief intervention effects in outpatient or lower-severity settings should not be generalized to medically hospitalized or dependent populations.

**Potential experiment signals:** receipt of alcohol assistance, drinks per day, alcohol dependence, hospital inpatient applicability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a cautionary adjacent source: a single brief intervention was insufficient in medical inpatients, especially where dependence was common.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Hospital inpatient population with high alcohol-dependence prevalence; not a voluntary wellness challenge; baseline imbalances and usual-care assessment/advice may have influenced results; no direct abstinence protocol.
