This source is included for **Registry, external protocol, and miscellaneous context records**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries report larger reductions in weekly alcohol units in treated men and women versus controls and a GGT improvement signal in treated men.

**Why it matters:** Foundational primary-care reduction trial with GGT as a biological marker, but not a short self-directed abstinence challenge.

**Potential experiment signals:** self-reported drinks per week, GGT where clinically ordered.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as historical adjacent brief-advice evidence; avoid making direct abstinence claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `adjacent_variant`.
