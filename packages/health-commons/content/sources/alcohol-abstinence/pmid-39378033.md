This source is included for **Withdrawal-risk screening and acute safety guidance**.

**Findings:** Large primary-care cohort study of AWS hospitalizations reported proportional incidence of hospitalizations involving AWS of 2.3% overall, with higher proportions in men aged 30-49 years and patients with high-risk AUDIT-C scores in accessible summaries.

**Why it matters:** Observational context linking primary-care alcohol screening with AWS hospitalization burden; not direct evidence for an abstinence protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** AWS hospitalization, AUDIT-C score, population risk, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-screening or escalation evidence only. It does not support efficacy claims for 7-, 14-, or 30-day alcohol-free variants.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
