This source is included for **Registry, external protocol, and miscellaneous context records**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries report decreased alcohol use and health resource utilization; one accessible source reports 7-day drinks decreased more in the intervention group than controls at 12 months.

**Why it matters:** Foundational brief-advice comparator source; strong for counseling context but not abstinence challenge physiology.

**Potential experiment signals:** weekly alcohol intake, binge drinking frequency, follow-up adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent evidence that clinician-delivered brief advice can reduce problem drinking; do not cite as direct protocol evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `adjacent_variant`.
