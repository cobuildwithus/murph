This source is included for **Withdrawal-risk screening and acute safety guidance**.

**Findings:** Review of alcohol withdrawal complications describing acute withdrawal syndrome and complications including seizures, delirium tremens, Wernicke-Korsakoff syndrome, neuropsychiatric symptoms, and cardiovascular complications.

**Why it matters:** Older foundational safety review preserving the breadth of withdrawal complications beyond mild symptoms.

**Potential experiment signals:** seizures, delirium tremens, Wernicke-Korsakoff syndrome, neuropsychiatric symptoms, complications.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-screening or escalation evidence only. It does not support efficacy claims for 7-, 14-, or 30-day alcohol-free variants.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
