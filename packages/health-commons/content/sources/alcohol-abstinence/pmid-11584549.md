This source is included for **Sleep, autonomic, wearable, and acute-alcohol measurement context**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries report initial sleep facilitation in occasional nonalcoholic drinkers, second-half sleep disruption at higher doses, and tolerance to sedative effects.

**Why it matters:** Historical mechanism source for the tension between alcohol sedation and later sleep disruption.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleepiness, sleep physiology, sleep onset latency, performance, tolerance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as historical context only; newer reviews and direct evidence should carry more weight.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `same_mechanism`.

**Population mismatch:** Broad sleep/alcohol context does not match short voluntary abstinence variants.

**Limitations and safety notes:** Older narrative review; not a direct protocol study. Review notes sleep disruption and impairment context, but no abstinence adverse events were extracted.
