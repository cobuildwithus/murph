This source is included for **Dry January and temporary abstinence campaign evidence**.

**Findings:**
- Participants showed significant reductions in AUDIT scores across hazardous, harmful, and high-risk/dependent baseline groups at 4 months, with reductions maintained at 7 months; engagement and slip-up patterns were associated with lower consumption.

**Why it matters:** Adjacent online-community evidence suggesting engagement can accompany reduced alcohol use, while duration and program differ from Dry January.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- AUDIT score
- program engagement
- slip-ups
- alcohol consumption reduction

**Protocol takeaway:** Supportive online communities may be useful adjuncts, but 3-month HSM data should not be treated as direct 30-day Dry January proof.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
