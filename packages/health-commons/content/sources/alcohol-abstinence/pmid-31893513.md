This source is included for **Sleep, autonomic, wearable, and acute-alcohol measurement context**.

**Findings:** AF recurrence was lower in the abstinence group than control in accessible summaries, and AF burden was lower.

**Why it matters:** Demonstrates that alcohol abstinence can matter for a cardiovascular endpoint in a medically selected population, while highlighting population mismatch.

**Potential experiment signals:** atrial fibrillation recurrence, atrial fibrillation burden, alcohol intake.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as clinical safety-boundary/context for users with known arrhythmia; do not generalize to healthy-user sleep or HRV benefits.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness:** `clinical_supervised`.

**Population mismatch:** Clinical arrhythmia patients differ materially from typical Murph users.

**Limitations and safety notes:** Population had diagnosed atrial fibrillation; not designed for healthy short-term wellness challenges. Clinical cardiac population; findings should trigger clinician-facing caution rather than unsupervised claims for arrhythmia management.
