This source is included for **Withdrawal-risk screening and acute safety guidance**.

**Findings:** Systematic review/meta-analysis of predictors of severe alcohol withdrawal syndrome. It found prior AWS episode course as the most reliable predictor, with thrombocytopenia and hypokalemia also correlated; sex and comorbid liver disease were not predictive in the summarized evidence.

**Why it matters:** Key review for risk factors that may trigger clinician referral before an alcohol-free challenge.

**Potential experiment signals:** severe withdrawal, delirium tremens, withdrawal seizures, risk factors, prediction.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-screening or escalation evidence only. It does not support efficacy claims for 7-, 14-, or 30-day alcohol-free variants.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
