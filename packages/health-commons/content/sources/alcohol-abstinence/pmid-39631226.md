This source is included for **Sleep, autonomic, wearable, and acute-alcohol measurement context**.

**Findings:** Review summaries report delayed REM latency and reduced REM duration; low doses disrupted REM, high doses shortened sleep-onset and N3 latency but worsened REM disruption; evidence for total sleep time, sleep efficiency, and wake after sleep onset was less clear.

**Why it matters:** Most recent synthesis in this batch for sleep architecture endpoints and uncertainty boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** REM sleep duration, REM latency, sleep onset latency, N3 latency, sleep efficiency, total sleep time, wake after sleep onset.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as same-mechanism sleep evidence and uncertainty boundary; avoid claiming all sleep metrics improve with abstinence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `same_mechanism`.

**Population mismatch:** Alcohol exposure studies in healthy adults do not directly test a 7-, 14-, or 30-day abstinence challenge.

**Limitations and safety notes:** Heterogeneity across alcohol dose, timing, study design, and sleep measurement; exposure evidence rather than abstinence intervention. Sleep disruption from alcohol exposure is a mechanism/safety context; no abstinence adverse events were extracted.
