This source is included for **Dry January and temporary abstinence campaign evidence**.

**Findings:**
- In this survey, 9% were familiar with sober curiosity and 7% reported a temporary abstinence challenge in the past year; about half of challenge participants reported drinking less afterward and 15% remained abstinent, but the associations are observational.

**Why it matters:** Shows who may be interested in abstinence challenges and how participation coexists with heavier drinking and readiness to change.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- sober curiosity familiarity
- temporary abstinence challenge participation
- post-participation drinking
- AUDIT and consequences

**Protocol takeaway:** Younger adults who engage with abstinence challenges may include higher-risk drinkers, so screening and safety guidance matter.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
