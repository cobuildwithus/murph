This source is included for **Sleep, autonomic, wearable, and acute-alcohol measurement context**.

**Findings:** Evening alcohol had marked dose- and time-dependent effects on nocturnal autonomic and cardiovascular function in accessible summaries.

**Why it matters:** Provides controlled sleep-lab physiology for interpreting wearable RHR/HRV signals after alcohol nights.

**Potential experiment signals:** heart rate, heart rate variability, blood pressure, baroreflex, sleep.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as acute alcohol same-mechanism context; avoid claiming direct abstinence benefits.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `same_mechanism`.

**Population mismatch:** Controlled lab dosing and middle-aged healthy adults may not generalize to real-world self-directed abstinence.

**Limitations and safety notes:** Acute alcohol dosing; laboratory setting; not an abstinence challenge. No source-specific adverse events were extracted; the physiologic effects were alcohol-exposure effects.
