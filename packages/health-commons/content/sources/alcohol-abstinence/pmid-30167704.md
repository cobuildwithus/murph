This source is included for **Withdrawal-risk screening and acute safety guidance**.

**Findings:** Rational Clinical Examination systematic review of severe alcohol withdrawal prediction in hospitalized patients. PAWSS was the most useful combination measure in the accessible summary, with LR 174 (95% CI 43 to 696) and specificity 0.93.

**Why it matters:** Most important risk-prediction review for why single symptoms are inadequate and combined tools/history are needed.

**Potential experiment signals:** severe withdrawal, withdrawal seizures, delirium tremens, prediction, PAWSS.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-screening or escalation evidence only. It does not support efficacy claims for 7-, 14-, or 30-day alcohol-free variants.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
