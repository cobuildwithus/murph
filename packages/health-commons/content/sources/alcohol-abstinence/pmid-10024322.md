This source is included for **Direct protocol and duration evidence**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-10024322-bp-heart-rate-decreased` — The source reported significant decreases in ambulatory blood pressure and heart rate after one month of alcohol abstinence among analyzed completers.
- `finding:pmid-10024322-heavy-drinker-boundary` — Because the cohort was heavy-drinking and monitored, the direction of BP change is relevant to a 30-day challenge, but magnitude and applicability to lower-risk users are uncertain.

**Why it matters:** Direct 30-day/one-month abstinence evidence for ambulatory blood pressure and heart rate, a plausible Murph cardiometabolic signal.

**Potential experiment signals:** 24-hour ambulatory blood pressure, systolic blood pressure, diastolic blood pressure, heart rate, adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** A one-month abstinence period in heavy drinkers was associated with lower ambulatory blood pressure and heart rate, but the clinical-heavy-drinker population limits generalization.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Heavy-drinking population; relapse exclusions; no randomized non-abstinence control extracted; not designed as a consumer wellness challenge. Population mismatch: Findings may be larger or more clinically relevant in heavy drinkers than in lower-risk challenge participants.
