This source is included for **Registry, external protocol, and miscellaneous context records**.

**Findings:** PRO-C3 increased by 1.2 ng/mL, about 10%, 24 hours after binge drinking; PRO-C8 increased in a liver-disease subgroup with low fibrosis stage.

**Why it matters:** Provides mechanistic liver-risk context: a single binge can acutely raise fibrogenesis markers, so challenge framing should avoid rebound binge messaging.

**Potential experiment signals:** GGT or liver enzymes where clinically ordered, PRO-C3 in research settings, binge-rebound safety monitoring.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-boundary evidence about binge exposure; do not use it to claim abstinence challenge efficacy.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness:** `safety_boundary`.
