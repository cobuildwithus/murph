This source is included for **Alcohol-reduction comparator trials, digital interventions, and liver-focused protocols**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-33067856-protocol-specifies-comparator-and-primary-outcome` — The protocol planned a two-arm individually randomized online trial with n=5562, using change in self-reported weekly alcohol consumption at six months as the primary outcome.
- `finding:pmid-33067856-not-outcome-evidence` — The protocol is useful for endpoint provenance and comparator definition but should not be used for efficacy or safety claims because it does not report trial outcomes.

**Why it matters:** This protocol source clarifies design, planned sample size, comparator, and outcome timing for the later Drink Less RCT.

**Potential experiment signals:** weekly alcohol consumption, extended AUDIT-C, AUDIT hazardous status, alcohol-related problems, health-related quality of life, engagement and acceptability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as protocol and endpoint context only. It should not be used as an efficacy result once the 2024 outcome paper is separately extracted.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Protocol article without outcome results; planned sample and methods may differ from final trial execution; reduction rather than abstinence; no direct challenge variant.
