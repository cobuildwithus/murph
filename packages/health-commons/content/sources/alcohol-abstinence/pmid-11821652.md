This source is included for **Registry, external protocol, and miscellaneous context records**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries report sustained alcohol-use reductions and lower health-care utilization/costs over 48 months in the intervention arm.

**Why it matters:** Long-term comparator evidence for clinician-delivered brief advice and outcome durability, but far outside short self-experiment variants.

**Potential experiment signals:** self-reported alcohol intake, binge drinking frequency, health-care utilization context only.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for adjacent behavior-change durability context; do not cite as direct short-term abstinence evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `adjacent_variant`.
