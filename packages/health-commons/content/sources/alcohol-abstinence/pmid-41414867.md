This source is included for **Registry, external protocol, and miscellaneous context records**.

**Findings:** The study reported six brain states and significant differences in state occupancy, transitions, and duration between typical drinking and abstinence conditions.

**Why it matters:** Adds mechanism context that abstinence can alter brain network dynamics, but it does not establish symptom or performance benefit.

**Potential experiment signals:** subjective mood, craving or urge ratings, sleep quality, cognitive self-rating; fMRI only in research settings.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as same-mechanism neurobiology context only; do not make protocol efficacy claims from fMRI network changes.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `same_mechanism`.
