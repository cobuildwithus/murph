This source is included for **Sleep, autonomic, wearable, and acute-alcohol measurement context**.

**Findings:** Binge alcohol disrupted REM sleep and increased morning heart rate and cardiovascular reactivity during Valsalva; baseline resting blood pressure and MSNA were similar between conditions in accessible summaries.

**Why it matters:** Provides safety/mechanism context for morning-after autonomic and cardiovascular endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** REM sleep, morning heart rate, blood pressure reactivity, MSNA, Valsalva maneuver response.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as acute binge-alcohol safety/mechanism context, not as abstinence efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `same_mechanism`.

**Population mismatch:** Binge-drinker exposure study differs from alcohol-free challenge participants.

**Limitations and safety notes:** Small sample and mechanistic lab setting; acute binge exposure rather than abstinence. Highlights morning-after autonomic/cardiovascular reactivity after binge alcohol; not an abstinence adverse-event study.
