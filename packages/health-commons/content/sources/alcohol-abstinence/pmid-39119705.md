This source is included for **Sleep, autonomic, wearable, and acute-alcohol measurement context**.

**Findings:** Evening binge alcohol augmented morning-after sympathetic transduction of muscle sympathetic nerve activity to blood pressure at rest and during cold pressor testing in accessible summaries.

**Why it matters:** Autonomic safety/mechanism source for blood pressure reactivity after alcohol exposure.

**Potential experiment signals:** sympathetic transduction, mean arterial pressure, diastolic blood pressure, total vascular conductance, cold pressor response.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as acute alcohol safety/mechanism context; not evidence that abstinence improves BP in healthy users.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `same_mechanism`.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy adults in controlled alcohol exposure differ from Murph abstinence users.

**Limitations and safety notes:** Acute binge-alcohol exposure; small laboratory sample; not abstinence intervention. Highlights altered end-organ responsiveness to sympathetic neural outflow after binge alcohol; no abstinence adverse events were extracted.
