This source is included for **Withdrawal-risk screening and acute safety guidance**.

**Findings:** PAWSS development combined a systematic literature review with a pilot study. The source identified 10 risk items and, in 68 pilot subjects, reported 100% sensitivity, specificity, positive predictive value, and negative predictive value at a threshold score of 4 for complicated withdrawal prediction.

**Why it matters:** Key source for pre-cessation/on-admission prediction of complicated withdrawal rather than post-hoc symptom scoring.

**Potential experiment signals:** complicated withdrawal, seizures, hallucinosis, delirium tremens, risk screening.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-screening or escalation evidence only. It does not support efficacy claims for 7-, 14-, or 30-day alcohol-free variants.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
