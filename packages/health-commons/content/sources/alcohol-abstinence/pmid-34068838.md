This source is included for **Clinical supervised abstinence, AUD detox, and liver-disease biomarker context**.

**Findings:** In ALD patients, one week of alcohol withdrawal improved intestinal barrier and liver-health markers, providing 7-day clinical biomarker context with strong population-mismatch caveats.

**Why it matters:** Fills 7-day abstinence biology but in a clinically distinct liver-disease population; should not be generalized to a wellness challenge.

**Potential experiment signals:** intestinal permeability, gut barrier biomarkers, liver health markers, LBP, TLR ligands, FibroScan.

**Protocol takeaway:** Clinical 7-day biomarker improvement is adjacent/safety context, not direct protocol support.
