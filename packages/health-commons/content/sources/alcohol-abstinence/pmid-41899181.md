This source is included for **Registry, external protocol, and miscellaneous context records**.

**Findings:** Accessible summary reports 72.9% sustained abstinence; abstinent patients had significant improvements in liver stiffness, FIB-4, and APRI at 12 months while non-abstinent patients worsened; GGT was associated with baseline fibrosis severity.

**Why it matters:** Clinical liver-marker abstinence context, but much longer and more medicalized than a Murph short-term challenge.

**Potential experiment signals:** GGT where clinically ordered, AST/ALT where clinically ordered, FIB-4 if labs available, liver stiffness only in clinical/research settings.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as clinical supervised/adjacent liver context only; do not extrapolate 12-month metabolic-syndrome findings to 7-, 14-, or 30-day general-user challenges.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `clinical_supervised`.
