This source is included for **Sleep, autonomic, wearable, and acute-alcohol measurement context**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries report middle-aged participants had lower RSA at rest, lower stress and higher affect overall, and a significant stress reduction after drinking not seen in younger adults.

**Why it matters:** Adds age and stress/affect heterogeneity around drinking and abstinence states.

**Potential experiment signals:** momentary stress, affect, respiratory sinus arrhythmia, age subgroup differences.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mixed subgroup context for mood/stress endpoints, not as generalized abstinence efficacy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `measurement_context`.

**Population mismatch:** Moderate-to-heavy drinkers and age-stratified EMA context may not map to broad Murph users.

**Limitations and safety notes:** Secondary analysis; participant count not extracted here; EMA ratings are not the same as participant count; not a randomized abstinence challenge. Stress/affect heterogeneity may signal burden or vulnerability in some users; no adverse events were extracted.
