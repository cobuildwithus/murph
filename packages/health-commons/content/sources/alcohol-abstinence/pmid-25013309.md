This source is included for **Withdrawal-risk screening and acute safety guidance**.

**Findings:** Systematic review of clinical management of alcohol withdrawal. It emphasizes that complicated withdrawal may include hallucinations, withdrawal seizures, or delirium tremens and that benzodiazepines have the strongest evidence base among treatment classes.

**Why it matters:** Systematic review covering complicated withdrawal, seizures, delirium tremens, and treatment classes; useful for safety-only boundary evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** hallucinations, withdrawal seizures, delirium tremens, benzodiazepines, supportive care.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-screening or escalation evidence only. It does not support efficacy claims for 7-, 14-, or 30-day alcohol-free variants.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
