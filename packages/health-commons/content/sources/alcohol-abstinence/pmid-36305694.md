This source is included for **Registry, external protocol, and miscellaneous context records**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries report reduced binge drinking, drinks per occasion, and consequences for selected text-message techniques; exact arm-by-arm estimates were not extracted here.

**Why it matters:** Adjacent digital-behavior evidence for check-in and feedback design; useful for adherence mechanics rather than biological abstinence claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** binge drinking days, drinks per occasion, message-response adherence, alcohol-related consequences.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent digital support evidence; do not use as direct efficacy evidence for abstaining 7, 14, or 30 days.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `adjacent_variant`.
