**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-7811440-design-specifies-clinical-endpoints` — The design paper specified BP and left ventricular mass endpoints plus alcohol-intake diaries and biochemical markers including apolipoproteins, HDL cholesterol subfractions, and carbohydrate-deficient transferrin.
- `finding:pmid-7811440-counseling-dose-specified` — The planned intensive phase consisted of six counseling sessions over three months, with the trial designed to test six-month BP effects and maintenance of reduced intake for up to two years.

**Why it matters:** It verifies the intervention structure and endpoints for PATHS before interpreting the result paper.
