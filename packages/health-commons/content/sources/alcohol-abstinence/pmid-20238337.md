This source is included for **Withdrawal-risk screening and acute safety guidance**.

**Findings:** Cochrane review of anticonvulsants for alcohol withdrawal found limited data versus placebo and no clear differences when anticonvulsants were compared with other drugs in accessible summaries.

**Why it matters:** Medication-treatment boundary source for seizure-related management; not evidence for self-directed abstinence efficacy.

**Potential experiment signals:** withdrawal symptoms, withdrawal seizures, adverse events, medication safety.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-screening or escalation evidence only. It does not support efficacy claims for 7-, 14-, or 30-day alcohol-free variants.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
