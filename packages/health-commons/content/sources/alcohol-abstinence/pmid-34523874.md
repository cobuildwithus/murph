This source is included for **Withdrawal-risk screening and acute safety guidance**.

**Findings:** Outpatient-management review states that patients with mild-to-moderate AWS without additional risk factors may be treated as outpatients, with daily monitoring for up to five days after the last drink to verify improvement and need for further treatment.

**Why it matters:** Directly relevant to distinguishing lower-risk outpatient withdrawal management from situations requiring inpatient or urgent medical care.

**Potential experiment signals:** withdrawal symptoms, seizure risk, delirium tremens, daily monitoring, transfer conditions.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-screening or escalation evidence only. It does not support efficacy claims for 7-, 14-, or 30-day alcohol-free variants.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
