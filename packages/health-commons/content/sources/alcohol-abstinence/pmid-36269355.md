This source is included for **Registry, external protocol, and miscellaneous context records**.

**Findings:** The intervention showed a beneficial effect on heavy drinking days, but no significant effect on the Short Inventory of Problems/alcohol-related consequences.

**Why it matters:** Adds mixed recent intervention evidence: heavy-drinking frequency improved, but consequences did not clearly improve.

**Potential experiment signals:** heavy drinking days, alcohol-related consequence checklist, booster-message adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent mixed intervention evidence for young intoxicated ED patients; not a direct abstinence-challenge result.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `adjacent_variant`.
