This source is included for **Withdrawal-risk screening and acute safety guidance**.

**Findings:** Psychometric validation of a Danish Short Alcohol Withdrawal Scale in an outpatient setting among patients with alcohol dependence and alcohol withdrawal symptoms/syndrome; accessible summary reports 122 patients completed the 10-item scale.

**Why it matters:** Outpatient psychometric validation helps evaluate whether self-report withdrawal-symptom tracking has a role after medical risk screen boundaries are met.

**Potential experiment signals:** withdrawal symptom severity, outpatient monitoring, psychometrics.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-screening or escalation evidence only. It does not support efficacy claims for 7-, 14-, or 30-day alcohol-free variants.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
