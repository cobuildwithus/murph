This source is included for **Registry, external protocol, and miscellaneous context records**.

**Findings:** The assessment-plus-feedback arm showed small reductions in binge drinking days and drinks per drinking day compared with controls; weekend binge drinking and weekend drinks were lower in the feedback arm in accessible reporting.

**Why it matters:** Shows that low-burden digital prompts can reduce some drinking outcomes in an adjacent young-adult ED population.

**Potential experiment signals:** binge drinking days, drinks per drinking day, SMS check-in adherence, weekend drinking.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent digital-support evidence for adherence and drinking-reduction design, not as direct abstinence-challenge efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `adjacent_variant`.
