## Extraction notes

- **Study design:** repeated_cross_sectional_population_study
- **Participant count:** 9397 (reported)
- **Population:** Adults in Great Britain with AUDIT-C ≥5 who attempted to reduce alcohol in the past year
- **Intervention or exposure:** Use of alcohol-free or low-alcohol drinks during alcohol-reduction attempts
- **Comparator or control:** Trends over time from 2020 to 2024 and attempt-type strata
- **Duration or follow-up:** Repeated cross-sectional survey waves, 2020–2024
- **Endpoints:** no/low drink use in reduction attempts, serious attempts, any attempts, evidence-based support use
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Use of alcohol-free/low-alcohol drinks in reduction attempts increased between 2020 and 2024, while evidence-based support use remained low in extracted materials.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No clinical adverse events; rising replacement-product use without effectiveness evidence is an implementation caution.
- **Limitations:** Repeated cross-sectional self-report; trends do not establish effectiveness or abstinence-challenge outcomes.
