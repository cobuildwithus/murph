This source is included for **Clinical supervised abstinence, AUD detox, and liver-disease biomarker context**.

**Findings:** In alcohol-dependent men monitored with 24-hour ambulatory BP, withdrawal was associated with clinically relevant BP decreases in responsive normotensive and hypertensive subgroups; use as clinical BP safety context, not direct challenge efficacy.

**Why it matters:** Boundary evidence for BP effects during alcohol withdrawal; AUD-treatment context limits direct use.

**Potential experiment signals:** 24-hour ambulatory blood pressure, mean blood pressure, hypertension response.

**Protocol takeaway:** BP can change meaningfully during withdrawal in dependent patients; use as safety boundary and measurement context.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
