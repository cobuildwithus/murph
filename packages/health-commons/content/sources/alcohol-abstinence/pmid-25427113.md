**Findings:** Clinical review on recognition and management of withdrawal delirium/delirium tremens. It supports treating DT as a severe complication requiring prompt recognition and medical management.

**Why it matters:** Authoritative clinical review of DT recognition/management; useful for emergency language and prior-DT exclusion.

**Potential experiment signals:** delirium tremens, withdrawal delirium, severe withdrawal, management.
