This source is included for **Registry, external protocol, and miscellaneous context records**.

**Findings:** Alcohol intake decreased from about 66 to 11 mL/day; office diastolic and daytime systolic BP decreased, while nighttime systolic BP rose and 24-hour BP was not clearly reduced.

**Why it matters:** Endpoint-adjacent BP evidence with mixed ambulatory findings; useful for setting expectations by time of day and baseline hypertension.

**Potential experiment signals:** home morning blood pressure, daytime blood pressure, 24-hour ambulatory blood pressure where available.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as alcohol-restriction BP context; effects were not uniformly present across daytime, nighttime, and 24-hour measures.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `adjacent_variant`.
