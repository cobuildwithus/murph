This source is included for **Dry January and temporary abstinence campaign evidence**.

**Findings:**
- Greater reward-related attentional capture before IkPas predicted drinking during the challenge in the primary analysis and multiple-imputation sensitivity analysis.

**Why it matters:** Suggests cognitive reward sensitivity may help identify who needs more support, without proving a support intervention.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- reward-related attentional capture
- drinking during the challenge
- completion prediction

**Protocol takeaway:** Could justify optional craving/temptation check-ins, but not an efficacy claim for abstinence itself.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
