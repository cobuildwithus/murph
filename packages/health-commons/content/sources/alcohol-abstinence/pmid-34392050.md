This source is included for **Dry January and temporary abstinence campaign evidence**.

**Findings:**
- The repeat cross-sectional analysis found no evidence that increased Dry January participation was associated with large population-level declines in monthly drinking or weekly alcohol consumption.

**Why it matters:** Important null/context evidence that individual campaign benefits should not be promoted as large population-level reductions.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- monthly drinking prevalence
- weekly alcohol consumption
- population-level consumption

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not claim Dry January participation growth produces large population-level consumption declines.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
