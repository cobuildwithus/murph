This source is included for **Alcohol-reduction comparator reviews and public-health guideline context**.

**Findings:** This systematic review of reviews summarized 24 reviews and 56 RCTs reported across 80 papers. The accessible review summary reported a mean difference of about 38 g/week lower alcohol consumption at one year and noted stronger consistency for hazardous/harmful drinkers in primary care, particularly middle-aged male drinkers.

**Why it matters:** Helps position brief intervention as an established reduction-comparator category rather than a direct abstinence intervention.

**Potential experiment signals:** weekly alcohol intake, one-year follow-up, hazardous drinking subgroup.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as high-level comparator context; do not cite as direct evidence for 7-, 14-, or 30-day abstinence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `adjacent_variant`.

**Population mismatch:** Brief interventions for hazardous/harmful drinking are adjacent to voluntary abstinence-challenge participation.

**Limitations and safety notes:** Umbrella-review design depends on prior review quality and does not test short abstinence. Adverse events were not extracted from accessible metadata.
