This source is included for **Sleep, autonomic, wearable, and acute-alcohol measurement context**.

**Findings:** In accessible abstract text, HDL cholesterol decreased after abstinence in men, while moderate beer consumption increased several blood/lipid indices in sex-specific ways; no clean abstinence-only benefit was extracted.

**Why it matters:** Contains a measured 30-day abstinence phase in healthy adults, but the paper mainly evaluates subsequent moderate beer exposure.

**Potential experiment signals:** HDL cholesterol, lipid profile, fasting glucose, liver enzymes, hematocrit and MCV.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for endpoint and duration context around a 30-day abstinence washout; do not cite as straightforward evidence that abstinence improves lipids.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `direct_protocol`.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy Spanish volunteers and a beer-reintroduction design may not match self-selected Murph abstinence users.

**Limitations and safety notes:** Not randomized; primary emphasis is moderate beer consumption after abstinence rather than an alcohol-free challenge outcome. No adverse events were extracted from accessible source text.
