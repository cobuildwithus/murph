This source is included for **Registry, external protocol, and miscellaneous context records**.

**Findings:** No evidence of an intervention effect was found at 3 or 12 months; both groups improved, and the trial was described as underpowered with practical flaws.

**Why it matters:** Preserves null/mixed brief-intervention evidence so the protocol landscape does not overstate behavior-change support.

**Potential experiment signals:** AUDIT score, self-reported drinks per week, drinking diary completion.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent null evidence: brief hospital advice did not clearly outperform no advice in this underpowered trial.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `adjacent_variant`.
