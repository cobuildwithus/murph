This source is included for **Withdrawal-risk screening and acute safety guidance**.

**Findings:** Australian guideline overview states most withdrawal can be managed in an ambulatory setting with appropriate support, while guideline context distinguishes supported ambulatory withdrawal from higher-risk care needs.

**Why it matters:** International guideline overview that supports separating low-risk ambulatory withdrawal from higher-risk medically supervised care.

**Potential experiment signals:** ambulatory withdrawal, withdrawal complications, AUD treatment boundary, clinician guidance boundary.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-screening or escalation evidence only. It does not support efficacy claims for 7-, 14-, or 30-day alcohol-free variants.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
