This source is included for **Withdrawal-risk screening and acute safety guidance**.

**Findings:** Open review focused on delirium tremens assessment and management. It describes DT as the most severe spectrum of alcohol withdrawal with potential death unless managed promptly and adequately, and emphasizes assessment of withdrawal severity, delirium, and medical comorbidities.

**Why it matters:** Open review focused on DT assessment and comorbidity screening; helps define hard stop and urgent-care triggers.

**Potential experiment signals:** delirium tremens, withdrawal severity, medical comorbidity screening, mortality risk.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-screening or escalation evidence only. It does not support efficacy claims for 7-, 14-, or 30-day alcohol-free variants.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
