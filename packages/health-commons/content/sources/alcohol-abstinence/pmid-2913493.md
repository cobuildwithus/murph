This source is included for **Withdrawal-risk screening and acute safety guidance**.

**Findings:** Inpatient-versus-outpatient detoxification comparison in mild-to-moderate AWS reported substantially greater inpatient costs ($3,319 to $3,665 per patient) than outpatient costs ($175 to $388) and concluded outpatient medical detoxification can be effective, safe, and lower cost for selected mild-to-moderate symptoms.

**Why it matters:** Foundational outpatient-vs-inpatient detox comparison for mild-to-moderate AWS; adjacent to but not the same as a wellness abstinence challenge.

**Potential experiment signals:** detoxification completion, withdrawal symptoms, cost, outpatient safety boundary.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-screening or escalation evidence only. It does not support efficacy claims for 7-, 14-, or 30-day alcohol-free variants.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
