## Extraction notes

- **Study design:** within_participant_crossover_lab_study
- **Participant count:** 50 (reported)
- **Population:** Young-adult heavy drinkers, including 27 men, in monitored outpatient abstinence and laboratory alcohol self-administration testing
- **Intervention or exposure:** Two weeks of closely monitored voluntary outpatient abstinence before alcohol self-administration testing
- **Comparator or control:** Usual-drinking condition within participant
- **Duration or follow-up:** 2-week abstinence exposure with laboratory testing
- **Endpoints:** motivation to self-administer alcohol, work for alcohol, sex-difference moderation
- **Effect estimates or direction:** After abstinence, women increased work for alcohol while men decreased work for alcohol in extracted abstract-level results; this is a lab motivation endpoint, not a wellness outcome.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No protocol-specific adverse events extracted.
- **Limitations:** Heavy-drinker lab study; intravenous/self-administration motivation endpoint; not a naturalistic self-guided abstinence protocol.
