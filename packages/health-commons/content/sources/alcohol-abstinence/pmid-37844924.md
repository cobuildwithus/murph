This source is included for **Withdrawal-risk screening and acute safety guidance**.

**Findings:** Canadian guideline includes recommendations spanning screening, diagnosis, withdrawal management, and ongoing treatment for high-risk drinking and alcohol use disorder, connecting risk screening to care pathways rather than stand-alone self-experimentation.

**Why it matters:** Recent national guideline explicitly links AUDIT-C-style screening with PAWSS/withdrawal-history risk stratification for withdrawal management pathways.

**Potential experiment signals:** AUD screening, withdrawal risk, delirium tremens, seizures, clinician guidance boundary.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-screening or escalation evidence only. It does not support efficacy claims for 7-, 14-, or 30-day alcohol-free variants.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
