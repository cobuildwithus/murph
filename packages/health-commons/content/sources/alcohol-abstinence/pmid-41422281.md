This source is included for **Dry January and temporary abstinence campaign evidence**.

**Findings:**
- Complete abstinence declined from 32.3% in 2015 and 30.2% in 2018 to 12.9% in 2021; campaign media exposure was associated with higher odds of complete abstinence, while year, age, baseline drinking frequency, and alcohol affordability also mattered.

**Why it matters:** Adjacent adherence-predictor evidence showing media exposure, baseline drinking frequency, age, and affordability correlates.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- complete abstinence
- media exposure
- pre-campaign drinking frequency
- age
- alcohol affordability

**Protocol takeaway:** Adherence may depend on exposure, baseline drinking frequency, age, and external context; do not generalize 3-month Thai results to 30-day Dry January without caveats.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
