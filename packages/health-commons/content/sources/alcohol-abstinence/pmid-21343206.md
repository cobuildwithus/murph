This source is included for **Registry, external protocol, and miscellaneous context records**.

**Findings:** Meta-analysis reported increases in HDL cholesterol, apolipoprotein A1, and adiponectin and a decrease in fibrinogen with alcohol consumption, while several other biomarkers had no clear association.

**Why it matters:** Helps define lipid, fibrinogen, adiponectin, and other biomarker endpoints that may move with alcohol exposure, while avoiding over-claiming abstinence benefit.

**Potential experiment signals:** HDL cholesterol, LDL cholesterol, triglycerides, apolipoprotein A1, adiponectin, fibrinogen.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for biomarker direction and measurement context around alcohol exposure; not direct evidence that abstinence improves cardiometabolic outcomes.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `measurement_context`.
