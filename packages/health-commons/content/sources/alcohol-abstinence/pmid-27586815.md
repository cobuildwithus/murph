This source is included for **Withdrawal-risk screening and acute safety guidance**.

**Findings:** Review of AWS mechanisms, manifestations, and management. It describes AWS after abrupt cessation of heavy/constant drinking and covers clinical manifestations including severe withdrawal complications.

**Why it matters:** Mechanistic and clinical manifestation review for explaining why abrupt cessation can be dangerous in dependent drinkers.

**Potential experiment signals:** withdrawal symptoms, hallucinations, seizures, delirium tremens, management.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-screening or escalation evidence only. It does not support efficacy claims for 7-, 14-, or 30-day alcohol-free variants.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
