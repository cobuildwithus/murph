This source is included for **safety_guidelines_special_populations**.

**Findings:** ACOG frames physical activity in pregnancy as beneficial for most women but requiring modification for anatomic/physiologic changes, fetal requirements, complications, contraindications, and warning signs.

**Why it matters:** Primary pregnancy boundary guideline for exercise contraindications, warning signs, and individualized modification.

**Potential experiment signals:** contraindications, warning signs, obstetric complications, postpartum exercise modification.

**Safety notes:** Stop/seek care for warning signs such as vaginal bleeding, abdominal pain, painful contractions, amniotic-fluid leakage, dyspnea before exertion, dizziness, headache, chest pain, balance-affecting weakness, or calf pain/swelling.

**Limitations:** Guideline; not static-stretching-specific and not a trial of home flexibility.

**Population mismatch:** Pregnancy and postpartum population, not a default healthy-adult protocol population.

**Protocol takeaway:** Add pregnancy/postpartum as a special-population branch with contraindication and warning-sign language.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
