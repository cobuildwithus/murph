This source is included for **home_low_burden_trials**.

**Findings:** A six-week self-administered knee-flexor stretching study reported ROM and pain-sensitivity changes, but without a control group. Available extraction notes report decreased pain sensitivity and a passive knee-extension ROM increase of about 3.6%; without a control group, causality is uncertain.

**Why it matters:** It adds an interesting symptom/pain-sensitivity signal while preserving that single-arm data cannot prove causality.

**Potential experiment signals:** passive knee extension ROM, pain sensitivity, maintenance after cessation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Pain sensitivity may be worth observing, but ROM remains the cleaner primary endpoint for this protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
