This source is included for **mechanisms_upper_body_general_primary**.

**Findings:** Stiffness decreased immediately after 2-, 4-, and 8-minute passive stretching; the 2-minute effect recovered within about 10 minutes and longer durations within about 20 minutes.

**Why it matters:** This gives a time-course boundary for acute passive-stretch stiffness claims.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- musculotendinous stiffness
- stretch duration
- post-stretch recovery time

**Protocol takeaway:** Acute stiffness reductions should be described as transient unless supported by longer-term training evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
