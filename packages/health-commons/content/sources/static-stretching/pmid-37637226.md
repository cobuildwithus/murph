This source is included for **safety_guidelines_special_populations**.

**Findings:** The review characterizes HSD by joint instability, chronic pain, fatigue, and systemic symptoms, and it states that stretching should focus on tight muscles rather than lax joints.

**Why it matters:** Directly relevant to focusing stretching, if used, on tight muscles rather than hypermobile joints.

**Potential experiment signals:** joint instability, chronic pain, stretching tight muscles only, bracing/taping, periarticular strengthening.

**Safety notes:** Hypermobility management often emphasizes periarticular strengthening, stabilization, taping/bracing, and injury prevention rather than pushing more range.

**Limitations:** Narrative review; does not test an at-home static stretching dose.

**Population mismatch:** HSD clinical population, not general flexibility users.

**Protocol takeaway:** Screen for symptomatic hypermobility and avoid stretches that load or chase range in lax joints.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
