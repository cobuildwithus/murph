This source is included for **home_field_test_measurement**.

**Findings:** The Senior Fitness Test showed high to very high test-retest reliability in this population; MDC90 values included chair sit-and-reach 6.0 cm and back scratch 4.6 cm.

**Why it matters:** It gives a conservative measurement-error context for chair sit-and-reach and back scratch, with a clear population mismatch.

**Potential experiment signals:** Chair sit-and-reach MDC, Back scratch MDC, Test-retest interval.

**Protocol takeaway:** Consider MDC values as subgroup-specific context; avoid applying them to all home users without qualification.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Community-dwelling older people with cognitive impairment; sample count not extracted.
- **Comparator/control:** Two test points 24 hours to 1 week apart.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 24 hours to 1 week between test points.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse events extracted.
- **Limitations:** Cognitive-impairment population differs from general home users.; Sample count was not extracted.
- **Population mismatch:** Older adults with cognitive impairment; adjacent clinical subgroup.
