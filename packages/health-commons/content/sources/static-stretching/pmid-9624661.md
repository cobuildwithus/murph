This source is included for **safety_guidelines_special_populations**.

**Findings:** The position stand includes flexibility exercise as part of a rounded fitness program and emphasizes gradual, low-risk exercise tailored to participant needs, goals, and abilities.

**Why it matters:** Historical anchor for stretch frequency, mild-discomfort intensity, and tailoring to participant needs and risk.

**Potential experiment signals:** flexibility frequency, static stretch holds, repetitions, warm-up/cool-down context, tailoring to risk.

**Safety notes:** Use as an older dose anchor only; update with newer ACSM and Delphi sources.

**Limitations:** Older consensus guidance; later recommendations and special-population records should supersede where they differ.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy adult guideline, not symptomatic or special-population evidence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as supporting historical context for basic static-stretch setup, not as the sole dose authority.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
