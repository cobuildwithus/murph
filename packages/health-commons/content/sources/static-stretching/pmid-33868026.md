## Findings
- Fifteen healthy men completed a 5-week high-volume static stretching program followed by 5 weeks of detraining.
- Dorsiflexion ROM and muscle stiffness changed after the stretching intervention.
- After detraining, measured effects returned to baseline in accessible article details.

## Why it matters
Adds retention/detraining context: flexibility and stiffness effects may decay when stretching stops.
