**Findings:** The review cautioned against passive gastrocsoleus stretching until 12 weeks after Achilles tendon repair and warned that post-transition tightness should not prompt aggressive ROM work. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-39246413`.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** Safety boundary for recent tendon repair: aggressive early stretching may be inappropriate.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Postoperative Achilles repair population, not healthy at-home flexibility users. Limitations: Post-surgical rehabilitation context; not general stretching efficacy.; Narrative review rather than comparative trial..

**Why it matters:** It supports exclusion/caution language for recent surgery or tendon repair.

**Potential experiment signals:** return to sport, postoperative ROM, passive gastrocsoleus stretching timing, rehabilitation safety.

**Protocol takeaway:** Recent Achilles repair or tendon surgery should require clinician guidance before stretching.
