This source is included for **mechanisms_upper_body_general_primary**.

**Findings:** Latissimus dorsi shear modulus differed across trunk positions; contralateral rotation and combined contralateral bending/rotation appeared to load multiple regions.

**Why it matters:** This is upper-body stretch-position mechanism evidence, not an outcome study.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- latissimus dorsi shear elastic modulus
- trunk rotation
- contralateral bending
- upper-limb elevation

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as position-selection context for latissimus stretching; do not cite as evidence of improved flexibility or symptoms.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
