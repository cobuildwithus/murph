This source is included for **home_field_test_measurement**.

**Findings:** Reliability ICCs ranged from 0.93 to 0.99; minimal detectable change was reported as 1.0 or 1.5 cm depending on distance metric. Cross-sectional validity correlations were about r=-0.40 to -0.50; longitudinal validity correlations were about r=0.39 to 0.63.

**Why it matters:** It offers practical minimal-detectable-change context for knee-to-wall style ankle measurements, while warning that clinical populations differ.

**Potential experiment signals:** Weight-bearing lunge distance, Left-right ankle dorsiflexion difference, Minimal detectable change threshold.

**Protocol takeaway:** A 1 to 1.5 cm change may be a useful measurement-error reference in ankle clinical contexts, but should not be treated as a universal home threshold.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Patients with ankle injury or dysfunction.
- **Comparator/control:** Repeated 3-day retest and clinical validity comparisons over 4 to 8 weeks.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 3-day reliability retest and 4- to 8-week follow-up for longitudinal validity.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse events extracted.
- **Limitations:** Clinical ankle-injury population may not generalize to healthy home stretchers.; Sign and interpretation of correlations depend on specific metric direction.
- **Population mismatch:** Ankle injury/dysfunction population mismatch for general protocol users.
