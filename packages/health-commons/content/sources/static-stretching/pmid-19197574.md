- **Comparator / control:** See study design; control or comparator condition where specified in findings.
- **Duration / follow-up:** 4 weeks; one leg stretched daily while the opposite leg served as control
- **Directness:** Same-mechanism hamstring stretching evidence, but unilateral within-person design and not a full home protocol.
- **Claim-use boundary:** context-only

## Findings
- Twelve healthy men stretched one leg daily for 4 weeks while the other leg served as a control.
- The stretched leg improved passive straight-leg raise by about 17°, stretch force by 19 N, and first-felt stretch angle by about 15°.
- No changes were detected in force at the same angles, muscle compliance, EMG, or maximal knee-flexion strength.

## Why it matters
Another boundary source showing ROM and tolerance can improve without measured compliance changes.
