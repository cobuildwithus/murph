This source is included for **mechanisms_upper_body_general_primary**.

**Findings:** The shoulder-pain group showed improved function, but pectoralis minor length did not clearly improve in either group and scapular kinematics results were mixed.

**Why it matters:** This preserves mixed upper-body evidence and prevents overclaiming that pectoralis stretching reliably lengthens the muscle.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- DASH function score
- pectoralis minor length
- scapular kinematics

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as upper-body context with mixed findings; do not claim reliable pectoralis minor lengthening from this protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
