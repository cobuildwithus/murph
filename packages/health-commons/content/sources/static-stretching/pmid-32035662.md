This source is included for **mechanisms_upper_body_general_primary**.

**Findings:** A 3-times-per-week schedule increased ROM and reduced stiffness, while a once-weekly schedule with equal total weekly volume did not show significant changes.

**Why it matters:** This is directly relevant to at-home programming because it separates total weekly time from practice frequency.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- weekly frequency
- total weekly volume
- dorsiflexion ROM
- medial gastrocnemius stiffness

**Protocol takeaway:** Prefer several shorter weekly sessions over concentrating the same weekly stretch time into one session.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
