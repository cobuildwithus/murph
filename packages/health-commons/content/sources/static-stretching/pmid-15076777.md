This source is included for **adverse_event_clinical_population_mismatch**.

**Findings:** The review concluded that evidence was insufficient to endorse or discontinue routine stretching before or after exercise for prevention of injury among athletes. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-15076777`.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** No specific adverse-event rate was extracted; this is a safety-claim boundary rather than an adverse-event source.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Athlete injury prevention, not direct at-home stretching for flexibility. Limitations: Few comparative studies met review criteria.; Sports warm-up/cool-down settings differ from a stand-alone home flexibility protocol..

**Why it matters:** It prevents conversion of athlete warm-up evidence into a direct home-protocol promise.

**Potential experiment signals:** sports injury risk, lower-extremity injury, evidence sufficiency.

**Protocol takeaway:** State that injury-prevention evidence is not definitive and is mostly adjacent to the flexibility protocol.
