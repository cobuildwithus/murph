**Findings:**
- The source is a narrative clinical review rather than a trial.
- It discusses stretching in athletic, older-adult, rehabilitation, and general fitness contexts.
- It notes that stretching method selection should depend on the intended outcome and that evidence remains debated for some goals.
- It provides background terminology and mechanism context, not direct hip-flexor efficacy data.
