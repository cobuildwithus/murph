This source is included for **region_specific_remote_measurement**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries report ICC values around 0.73-0.89 and minimal detectable change values around 0.053 for active and 0.036 for passive normalized measurements.

**Why it matters:** A simple hand-behind-back endpoint may be practical for home shoulder flexibility tracking.

**Potential experiment signals:** Shoulder internal rotation, Hand-behind-back ratio, ICC, Minimal detectable change.

**Protocol takeaway:** If hand-behind-back reach is used, normalize or use consistent body-size-aware scoring and disclose MDC.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction boundaries:**

- Population: Participants assessed with normalized hand-behind-back measurement; exact sample not extracted
- Intervention/exposure: Normalized hand-behind-back ratio for shoulder internal rotation
- Comparator/control: Repeated active and passive hand-behind-back measurements
- Duration/follow-up: Measurement reliability design
- Adverse events or safety notes: No adverse events were extracted.
- Population mismatch: Shoulder measurement context rather than at-home stretching intervention.
- Directness: Measurement-context evidence for shoulder internal-rotation endpoint.

**Limitations:**

- Exact sample size not extracted.
- Hand-behind-back is a composite functional movement.
- Normalization may be useful across physiques but needs clear instructions.
