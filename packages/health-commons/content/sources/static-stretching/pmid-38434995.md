This source is included for **region_specific_remote_measurement**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries report MRI thoracic rotation angle around 29.91 ± 6.57 degrees and compare multiple clinical measurement positions; exact validity coefficients were not extracted.

**Why it matters:** Thoracic ROM is an adjacent endpoint where clinical position choice may change validity.

**Potential experiment signals:** Thoracic spine rotation ROM, MRI comparison, Lumbar-locked position, Seated position, Half-kneeling position.

**Protocol takeaway:** If thoracic rotation is tracked at home, specify the position and avoid MRI-level precision claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction boundaries:**

- Population: Healthy participants
- Intervention/exposure: Clinical thoracic spine rotation ROM measurements with an electronic goniometer in multiple positions
- Comparator/control: MRI thoracic rotation measurements
- Duration/follow-up: Cross-sectional validity comparison
- Adverse events or safety notes: No adverse events were extracted.
- Population mismatch: Healthy participants and imaging comparison; not home stretching intervention users.
- Directness: Measurement-validity context for thoracic rotation endpoints.

**Limitations:**

- Healthy participants.
- MRI comparison is not home feasible.
- Exact clinical-method validity results were not extracted in this batch.
