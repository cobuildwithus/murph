This source is included for **mechanisms_syntheses**.

**Findings:** source_artifact:pmid-26347668 categorized 152 articles and found that duration, frequency, and position were commonly discussed, while intensity was reported less consistently and body-position effects remained uncertain.

**Why it matters:** Supports careful protocol wording around intensity and body position: these variables are often less standardized than hold duration and frequency.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- Ask users to log intensity using a simple scale.
- Describe stretch setup/position in enough detail to improve repeatability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a limitation and measurement-fidelity source: do not overclaim an ideal intensity or position from heterogeneous literature.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness and mismatch:** same_mechanism. Broad heterogeneous literature across athletes, clinical groups, elderly adults, and general populations.

**Safety/adverse events:** No adverse-event signal was extracted; the source supports avoiding poorly defined high-intensity stretching.

**Limitations:**
- Many included studies were lower quality in extracted review discussion.
- Intensity and position were often not well defined.
- Not a direct static-stretching efficacy synthesis for home users.
