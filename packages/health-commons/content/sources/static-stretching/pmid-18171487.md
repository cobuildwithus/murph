This source is included for **ankle_calf_dorsiflexion**.

**Findings:** In 20 healthy women aged 76 to 91 years, a 6-week static calf muscle-tendon stretching program increased passive ankle dorsiflexion ROM by a reported mean of 12.3 degrees.

**Why it matters:** This source extends calf-stretching ROM evidence into an older-adult population, but its uncontrolled design limits claim strength.

**Potential experiment signals:** Passive ankle dorsiflexion ROM; Older adult feasibility.

**Protocol takeaway:** Older adults may see ankle ROM gains, but claims should be cautious because the design lacks a control group.

**Limitations:** No control group extracted. Older women only. Passive ROM endpoint only.

**Population mismatch:** Older-women sample may not generalize to all users, but is useful for age-boundary context.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
