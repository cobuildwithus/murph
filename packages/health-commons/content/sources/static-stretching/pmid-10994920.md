This source is included for **home_field_test_measurement**.

**Findings:** Scores from modified back-saver and other sit-and-reach protocols were compared with criterion measures; exact correlation coefficients were not extracted here.

**Why it matters:** It helps choose between household-feasible sit-and-reach variants and warns that variants are not necessarily interchangeable.

**Potential experiment signals:** Modified back-saver sit-and-reach centimeters, Left/right back-saver reach, Testing order and trial count.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use the same sit-and-reach variant consistently within a participant rather than mixing variants across check-ins.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** College students; 96 women and 62 men, mean age about 20.8 years.
- **Comparator/control:** Criterion measures and alternative sit-and-reach protocols.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Single-day testing session.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse events extracted.
- **Limitations:** Young college sample limits generalization to older or symptomatic users.; Exact correlations were not extracted in this batch.
- **Population mismatch:** Healthy students, not an at-home stretching intervention cohort.
