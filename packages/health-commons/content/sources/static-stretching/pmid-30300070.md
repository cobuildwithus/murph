This source is included for **mechanisms_upper_body_general_primary**.

**Findings:** Both stretching intensity groups showed ROM changes, but stiffness and viscoelastic stress-relaxation measures did not show clear intensity effects.

**Why it matters:** The study adds a null/mixed counterpoint to intensity claims.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- ROM at initial sensation
- maximum ROM
- passive stiffness
- stress relaxation
- fascicle length
- pennation angle

**Protocol takeaway:** Avoid presenting higher intensity as universally superior; acute intensity effects may vary by endpoint.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
