This source is included for **adverse_event_clinical_population_mismatch**.

**Findings:** The review reported acute ROM increases after stretching, potential performance impairment particularly with longer static-stretch exposures, and no clear effect on all-cause or overuse injury incidence. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-26642915`.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** No specific adverse-event rate was extracted; performance decrement and injury-prevention uncertainty are the safety-relevant findings.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Healthy active acute warm-up evidence, not direct home-program adherence or long-term outcomes. Limitations: Acute and athletic contexts predominate.; Not designed as a chronic at-home flexibility protocol review..

**Why it matters:** It supports careful timing and intensity/duration boundaries for users who stretch before activity.

**Potential experiment signals:** range of motion, physical performance, injury incidence.

**Protocol takeaway:** For flexibility practice, avoid framing long static holds immediately before performance as universally beneficial.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`. Boundary: Use to separate ROM effects from injury-prevention and pre-performance claims.
