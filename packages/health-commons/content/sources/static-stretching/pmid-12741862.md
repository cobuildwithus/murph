This source is included for **home_low_burden_trials**.

**Findings:** Equal total daily stretch exposure produced similar hip ROM gains whether delivered as shorter or longer repeated holds. Hip flexion ROM increased over six weeks, and no meaningful difference was found between the two protocol structures when total daily stretching time was equal.

**Why it matters:** This helps separate total session dose from individual hold duration when designing a low-burden routine.

**Potential experiment signals:** hip flexion ROM, daily session burden.

**Protocol takeaway:** A short daily routine can be plausible if total stretching exposure is sufficient and repeated consistently.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
