This source is included for **home_field_test_measurement**.

**Findings:** The review included 129 original studies on reliability of adult field-based fitness tests; flexibility-specific estimates were not extracted in this batch.

**Why it matters:** It contextualizes how field-test reliability should be appraised before turning a home flexibility measurement into a protocol outcome.

**Potential experiment signals:** Test-retest reliability, Measurement error, Field-test protocol standardization.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as broad reliability-context evidence; extract specific flexibility-test rows before making test-specific claims.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
