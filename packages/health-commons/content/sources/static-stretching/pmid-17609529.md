This source is included for **region_specific_remote_measurement**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries indicate the modified supine method was more reliable than side-lying assessment across sessions/testers; exact coefficients were not extracted.

**Why it matters:** Shoulder flexibility tracking can be noisy unless assessment method is specified.

**Potential experiment signals:** Posterior shoulder tightness, Shoulder internal rotation, Reliability, Precision, Accuracy, Validity.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not cite generic posterior shoulder tightness changes without naming the test and population.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction boundaries:**

- Population: Overhead athletes, including baseball pitchers and tennis players in accessible summaries
- Intervention/exposure: Posterior shoulder tightness assessment methods
- Comparator/control: Modified supine assessment versus side-lying assessment and tester/session comparisons
- Duration/follow-up: Repeated measurement design; exact interval not extracted
- Adverse events or safety notes: No adverse events were extracted.
- Population mismatch: Competitive overhead athletes; not general home stretching participants.
- Directness: Shoulder measurement context with sport-specific population mismatch.

**Limitations:**

- Overhead-athlete population.
- Measurement source only.
- Exact quantitative estimates were not extracted in this batch.
