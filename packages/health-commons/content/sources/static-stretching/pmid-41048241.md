This source is included for **ankle_calf_dorsiflexion**.

**Findings:** In 51 amateur sports enthusiasts, the combined foam-rolling-plus-static-stretching arm showed significant improvements on several dorsiflexion and jumping measures compared with static stretching alone.

**Why it matters:** This prevents attributing combined foam-rolling benefits to static stretching alone and helps define when a separate protocol variant may be needed.

**Potential experiment signals:** Ankle dorsiflexion ROM; Dorsiflexion angular speed; Countermovement jump.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use combined foam rolling plus stretching results as direct evidence for a static-stretching-only protocol.

**Limitations:** Adjacent stacked-modality intervention. Sports-enthusiast population. Does not isolate static stretching benefit against no intervention.

**Population mismatch:** Amateur sports enthusiasts; intervention includes foam rolling.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
