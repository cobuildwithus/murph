## Findings
- Ten healthy university men were allocated to stretching or control groups.
- The stretching group completed high-intensity assisted stretching for 8 weeks, averaging 3.1 sessions per week.
- Biceps femoris fascicle length increased by about 12.3 mm and maximum ROM by about 14.2° in the stretching group; controls did not show changes in accessible text.

## Why it matters
Shows that very high-intensity/long-duration stretching may affect architecture, but should not define starter protocol expectations.
