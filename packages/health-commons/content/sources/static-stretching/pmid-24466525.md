This source is included for **adverse_event_clinical_population_mismatch**.

**Findings:** A trained dancer developed sciatic neuropathy, mainly involving the tibial division, after stretching; symptoms included dysesthesia and weakness of right foot dorsiflexion and plantarflexion. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-24466525`.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** Serious neurologic injury case report temporally associated with stretching; rarity and causality strength are limited by single-case design.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Rare adverse event in a trained dancer; safety boundary only. Limitations: Single case report cannot estimate incidence or risk in general home stretching.; Trained dancer and possibly extreme range demands differ from typical at-home static stretching..

**Why it matters:** It supports conservative safety language against forcing end-range stretches or ignoring neurologic symptoms.

**Potential experiment signals:** sciatic neuropathy, dysesthesia, foot dorsiflexion and plantarflexion weakness, electrodiagnostic findings.

**Protocol takeaway:** Stop stretching and seek clinical guidance for numbness, weakness, tingling, or radiating pain.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`. Boundary: Use for stop rules around numbness, tingling, weakness, and radiating neurologic symptoms; do not imply common risk.
