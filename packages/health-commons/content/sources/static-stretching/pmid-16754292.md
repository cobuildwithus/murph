**Findings:** A complete semimembranosus rupture was reported following therapeutic stretching in a person with severe traumatic brain injury. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-16754292`.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** Severe muscle/tendon injury temporally associated with therapeutic stretching; high clinical population mismatch.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Severe traumatic brain injury and therapeutic stretching context. Limitations: Single case report in a highly clinical neurologic population.; Assisted therapeutic stretching is not ordinary home self-stretching..

**Why it matters:** It highlights that stretching can be risky in neurologic impairment or assisted high-force contexts.

**Potential experiment signals:** complete semimembranosus rupture, therapeutic stretching injury, neurologic disability context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Users with severe neurologic injury, spasticity, or dependent stretching needs should not follow a generic home protocol without clinician guidance.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`. Boundary: Use to exclude or require clinician guidance for severe neurologic impairment, spasticity, or assisted forceful stretching.
