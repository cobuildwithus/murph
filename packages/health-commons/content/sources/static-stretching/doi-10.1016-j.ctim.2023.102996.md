This source is included for **adverse_event_clinical_population_mismatch**.

**Findings:** The trial compared Hatha yoga against stretching exercise and reported yoga as non-inferior to stretching for safety and pain/psychological and physical outcomes in sedentary office workers with mild or moderate neck/shoulder pain. Source key: `source_artifact:doi-10.1016/j.ctim.2023.102996`.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** The abstracted source specifically includes safety in the non-inferiority framing; no event counts were extracted.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Clinical neck/shoulder pain treatment context rather than general flexibility self-experiment. Limitations: Clinical pain population and supervised intervention comparison differ from self-directed home flexibility.; Stretching served as comparator, so the trial is not a pure static-stretch efficacy test..

**Why it matters:** It flags that clinical pain programs are adjacent and require careful boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** pain reduction, physical performance, flexibility, psychological status, safety.

**Protocol takeaway:** Avoid presenting the at-home static-stretching protocol as a pain treatment; direct pain patients to clinical guidance.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`. Boundary: Use as clinical context only; do not claim the home protocol treats neck or shoulder pain.
