This source is included for **safety_guidelines_special_populations**.

**Findings:** The guideline strongly recommends fall-prevention exercise programs for community-dwelling older adults that include balance-challenging and functional exercises, with person-centered assessment and risk stratification.

**Why it matters:** A flexibility routine can create fall risk if it uses unsupported standing or balance-challenging positions.

**Potential experiment signals:** falls risk, balance-challenging exercise, functional exercise, leg strength, screening/referral, person-centered care.

**Safety notes:** For high fall-risk users, unsupervised balance-challenging stretches may be unsafe; supported/seated variations or referral may be appropriate.

**Limitations:** Falls-prevention guideline, not a stretching efficacy study.

**Population mismatch:** Older adults at fall risk, not general healthy adult flexibility users.

**Protocol takeaway:** Offer supported versions and screen for fall history/balance concerns before standing stretches.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
