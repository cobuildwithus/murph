This source is included for **home_low_burden_trials**.

**Findings:** A classic duration trial suggests a longer 15-second hold outperformed a 5-second hold for active ROM under equal total session stretch time. The 15-second stretch duration produced greater active ROM improvement than the 5-second duration; passive ROM was not significantly affected by duration in the available abstract-level extraction.

**Why it matters:** It supports a minimum-hold boundary and helps avoid very short holds as the default at-home prescription.

**Potential experiment signals:** active ROM, passive ROM, session duration burden.

**Protocol takeaway:** Avoid making the routine so short that hold times fall below the durations tested as effective in classic trials.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
