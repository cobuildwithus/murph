This source is included for **region_specific_remote_measurement**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries report substantial to excellent agreement for many FMS items across expert and novice raters, though item-level results varied.

**Why it matters:** Movement screens may be tempting as global flexibility outcomes; this source helps classify them as adjacent.

**Potential experiment signals:** Functional Movement Screen, Interrater reliability, Expert and novice rater agreement.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use FMS sources only as movement-screen context, not as primary evidence that static stretching improves flexibility.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction boundaries:**

- Population: Healthy subjects
- Intervention/exposure: Functional Movement Screen performance scored from videotape
- Comparator/control: Four independent raters, including expert and novice raters
- Duration/follow-up: Single-session videotaped scoring design
- Adverse events or safety notes: No adverse events were extracted.
- Population mismatch: Healthy subjects and trained raters; not unsupervised home stretchers.
- Directness: Adjacent movement-screen measurement context.

**Limitations:**

- Composite movement screen, not a direct flexibility endpoint.
- Video-scored controlled setup differs from self-administered home tests.
- Healthy sample.
