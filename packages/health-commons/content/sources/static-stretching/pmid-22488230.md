This source is included for **region_specific_remote_measurement**.

**Findings:** Accessible abstracts report good-to-excellent reliability and provide standard-error and minimal-detectable-change values for thoracic rotation measurements.

**Why it matters:** Thoracic rotation is a region-specific mobility signal that may appear in flexibility tracking even when the intervention is broader static stretching.

**Potential experiment signals:** Thoracic spine rotation ROM, Within-tester reliability, Between-tester reliability, SEM/MDC measurement error.

**Protocol takeaway:** If thoracic rotation is used, include clear measurement instructions and a minimum-change threshold rather than overinterpreting small changes.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction boundaries:**

- Population: Healthy adults
- Intervention/exposure: Thoracic spine rotation ROM measured with clinical measurement procedures
- Comparator/control: Within-tester and between-tester reliability comparisons
- Duration/follow-up: Single reliability study with repeated measurements; exact interval not extracted
- Adverse events or safety notes: No adverse events were extracted.
- Population mismatch: Healthy adults; not home self-measurement or intervention participants.
- Directness: Measurement-context evidence for a regional ROM endpoint.

**Limitations:**

- Healthy adults only.
- Measurement context, not a stretching intervention.
- Thoracic rotation is an adjacent flexibility endpoint rather than the core dose of an at-home static-stretching program.
