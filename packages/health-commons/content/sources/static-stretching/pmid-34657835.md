This source is included for **region_specific_remote_measurement**.

**Findings:** Raw-score ICCs were reported in the 0.8-0.99 range and converted-score ICCs in the 0.69-0.99 range; no significant systematic bias was reported for all tests.

**Why it matters:** This supports the feasibility of remote self-assessment in Murph-style home experiments.

**Potential experiment signals:** Home-based fitness assessment reliability, Flexibility, Mobility, Balance, Strength, Cardiorespiratory fitness.

**Protocol takeaway:** Remote flexibility endpoints should use clear app-like instructions, repeat testing, and measurement-error thresholds.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Extraction boundaries:**

- Population: Healthy young adults
- Intervention/exposure: Self-executed home-based fitness assessments using the R Plus Health mobile app
- Comparator/control: Repeat assessment 2-3 days later
- Duration/follow-up: Two assessments separated by 2-3 days
- Adverse events or safety notes: No adverse events were extracted; feasibility and remote administration were the main issues.
- Population mismatch: Healthy young adults using a specific mobile app; not necessarily general home users across ages or conditions.
- Directness: Directly relevant to at-home/remote measurement feasibility, but not stretching efficacy.

**Limitations:**

- Healthy young adult sample.
- Fitness battery, not solely static stretching.
- App-specific workflow may not generalize to all remote assessments.
