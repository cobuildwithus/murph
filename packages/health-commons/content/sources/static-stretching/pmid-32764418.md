This source is included for **adjacent_variants_recovery_modalities**.

**Findings:** The included studies had a total of 213 participants. Stretching exercises significantly reduced arterial stiffness (SMD = -1.00, 95% CI -1.57 to -0.44, p = 0.0004) and improved vascular endothelial function (SMD = 1.15, 95% CI 0.26 to 2.03, p = 0.01) in the accessible source summary.

**Why it matters:** Vascular findings should be preserved but separated from flexibility outcomes and from this protocol's primary endpoint.

**Potential experiment signals:** arterial stiffness, vascular endothelial function, heart rate, diastolic blood pressure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use vascular meta-analysis findings to claim flexibility improvements; consider only for a future vascular stretching protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
