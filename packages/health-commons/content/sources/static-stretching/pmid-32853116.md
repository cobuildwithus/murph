This source is included for **mechanisms_upper_body_general_primary**.

**Findings:** Muscle-directed stretching increased ROM and reduced muscle-related mechanical measures, while nerve-directed stretching produced distinct nerve-related changes.

**Why it matters:** This source distinguishes muscle-directed from nerve-directed stretching mechanisms, making it adjacent rather than direct for a general static-stretching protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- ankle dorsiflexion ROM
- passive torque
- muscle shear-wave velocity
- sciatic nerve shear-wave velocity
- fascicle length

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context that different stretch targets can produce tissue-specific adaptations; do not treat nerve-directed stretching as the same protocol variant.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
