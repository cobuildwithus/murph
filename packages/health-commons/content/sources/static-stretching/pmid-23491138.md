This source is included for **region_specific_remote_measurement**.

**Findings:** Most intra-rater and inter-rater ICCs were above 0.8; inter-rater agreement between digital inclinometer and TiltMeter was reported as ICC 0.96, while concurrent validity ICC was reported as 0.83 in accessible full text.

**Why it matters:** This is relevant to Murph because low-cost phone-based ROM measurements may be used in home flexibility experiments.

**Potential experiment signals:** Ankle dorsiflexion ROM, Weight-bearing lunge test, Intra-rater reliability, Inter-rater reliability, Concurrent validity.

**Protocol takeaway:** A phone app can be considered for ankle dorsiflexion tracking only when device and positioning are kept consistent.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Extraction boundaries:**

- Population: Allied health practitioners recruited from a workplace
- Intervention/exposure: Weight-bearing lunge test measured with TiltMeter on an Apple iPhone
- Comparator/control: Digital inclinometer measurements in bent-knee and straight-leg positions
- Duration/follow-up: Single-session measurement study
- Adverse events or safety notes: No adverse events were extracted.
- Population mismatch: Workplace practitioner participants and device-specific setup; not a general consumer remote validation sample.
- Directness: Adjacent measurement evidence for a simple home-compatible ankle ROM endpoint.

**Limitations:**

- Study used a specific iPhone app/device surface; authors cautioned against assuming generalizability to other smartphone equipment.
- Participants were allied health practitioners, not typical unsupervised home users.
- Ankle dorsiflexion is adjacent to static stretching rather than proof of protocol efficacy.
