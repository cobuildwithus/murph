## Findings
- Twenty healthy men completed passive and active knee-extension stretching conditions.
- Both methods increased maximum ROM and decreased shear elastic modulus.
- No significant difference between active and passive methods was reported in accessible abstract details.

## Why it matters
Supports the mechanism that acute hamstring static stretching can reduce elastic modulus while increasing ROM.
