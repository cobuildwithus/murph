This source is included for **home_low_burden_trials**.

**Findings:** A classic hamstring trial supports 30-second holds as effective and does not show added benefit from 60 seconds in the available extraction notes. Available extraction notes report that 30- and 60-second holds were more effective than 15-second holds or no stretch, with no clear difference between 30 and 60 seconds.

**Why it matters:** It is a foundational source for low-burden hold duration and avoiding unnecessarily long holds.

**Potential experiment signals:** hamstring ROM, hold duration, time burden.

**Protocol takeaway:** A practical 30-second hold is evidence-aligned for hamstrings and keeps session burden low.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
