**Findings:** All warm-up protocols improved ROM and stability, while dynamic warm-up was described as providing more comprehensive acute benefits without static-stretch performance-inhibition concerns. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-41421999`.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse events were extracted; performance/timing boundary is the relevant safety context.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Clinical athlete shoulder impingement context, not general home static stretching. Limitations: Clinical shoulder impingement population and acute warm-up context.; Male athletes only; not a chronic home flexibility intervention..

**Why it matters:** It flags that shoulder pathology and pre-performance timing change how stretching evidence should be used.

**Potential experiment signals:** shoulder function, range of motion, proprioception, strength, stability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not generalize acute shoulder-impingement warm-up findings to all home flexibility users.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`. Boundary: Use for timing and clinical-shoulder caution only.
