This source is included for **region_specific_remote_measurement**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries report very high ICC ranges across children/adolescents and athlete/non-athlete subgroups, with SEM% ranges reported by subgroup.

**Why it matters:** The source helps preserve youth-specific measurement evidence without applying it directly to adult home stretchers.

**Potential experiment signals:** Flexibility tests, Intrasession reliability, Intersession reliability, Children, Adolescents.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not generalize youth reliability estimates to adults without caveats; use only as population-specific measurement context.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction boundaries:**

- Population: Children and adolescents, including athletes and non-athletes
- Intervention/exposure: Flexibility tests during developmental years
- Comparator/control: Intrasession and intersession reliability across sport, age, and sex groups
- Duration/follow-up: Repeated flexibility testing; exact interval not extracted
- Adverse events or safety notes: No adverse events were extracted.
- Population mismatch: Children/adolescents; not adult home stretching population unless the protocol explicitly includes youth.
- Directness: Measurement-context evidence with strong population mismatch for adult protocol pages.

**Limitations:**

- Developmental-years population, not adult users.
- Measurement reliability only.
- Sport, age, and sex may affect measurement properties.
