This source is included for **region_specific_remote_measurement**.

**Findings:** Strong evidence was reported for inter-clinician reliability with ICCs around 0.80-0.99 and intra-clinician reliability with ICCs around 0.65-0.99; average MDC estimates were reported in degrees and centimeters.

**Why it matters:** WBLT is among the most practical home-compatible ankle dorsiflexion endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** Weight-bearing lunge test, Ankle dorsiflexion ROM, Reliability, Minimal detectable change.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use WBLT changes only when they exceed the expected measurement error, and keep wall distance/angle method consistent.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Extraction boundaries:**

- Population: Studies assessing the weight-bearing lunge test across clinical and healthy measurement contexts
- Intervention/exposure: Weight-bearing lunge test ankle dorsiflexion measurement
- Comparator/control: Intra-clinician and inter-clinician reliability estimates across included studies
- Duration/follow-up: Systematic review; original-study intervals varied
- Adverse events or safety notes: No adverse events were extracted; this is a measurement review.
- Population mismatch: Measurement-review populations, not a dedicated home stretching protocol sample.
- Directness: Direct measurement support for a simple ankle ROM endpoint relevant to at-home flexibility tracking.

**Limitations:**

- Review of measurement reliability, not stretching efficacy.
- Included protocols and populations may vary.
- MDC must be applied using the same unit and setup as the original test.
