This source is included for **region_specific_remote_measurement**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries indicate mixed reliability, with good live-versus-video agreement but poorer interrater reliability in at least one estimate.

**Why it matters:** It preserves a negative/mixed measurement finding rather than cherry-picking positive FMS reliability reviews.

**Potential experiment signals:** Functional Movement Screen, Test-retest reliability, Interrater reliability, Live versus video scoring.

**Protocol takeaway:** Avoid treating FMS as a precise home flexibility outcome; if used, require trained scoring and disclose reliability caveats.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction boundaries:**

- Population: Functional Movement Screen participants; exact sample not extracted
- Intervention/exposure: Functional Movement Screen testing and scoring
- Comparator/control: Test-retest, live-versus-video, and interrater comparisons
- Duration/follow-up: Reliability study; accessible summaries mention repeated testing and live/video comparison
- Adverse events or safety notes: No adverse events were extracted.
- Population mismatch: FMS testing context, not at-home static-stretching users.
- Directness: Adjacent movement-screen measurement context with mixed findings.

**Limitations:**

- Composite movement screen, not a pure flexibility measure.
- Mixed reliability findings.
- Exact values should be verified in the full open-access article before strong claims.
