This source is included for **safety_guidelines_special_populations**.

**Findings:** The framework summarizes terminology, types of joint hypermobility, secondary musculoskeletal manifestations, and a spectrum concept for related conditions.

**Why it matters:** Prevents conflating high flexibility with a healthy protocol outcome when symptoms and syndromic features are present.

**Potential experiment signals:** generalized joint hypermobility, localized/peripheral/historical hypermobility, secondary musculoskeletal manifestations, HSD classification.

**Safety notes:** Symptomatic hypermobility should be classified and managed clinically rather than treated as a normal flexibility endpoint.

**Limitations:** Classification source, not a stretching intervention study.

**Population mismatch:** Nosology and clinical classification, not protocol efficacy.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to label hypermobility as a population-boundary issue, not a flexibility goal.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
