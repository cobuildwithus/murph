This source is included for **mechanisms_upper_body_general_primary**.

**Findings:** Rectus femoris Young’s modulus decreased by 2 and 4 weeks, and knee-flexion ROM increased over the 4-week program.

**Why it matters:** This is recent quadriceps chronic stretching evidence, but the uncontrolled before-after design limits causal claims.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- rectus femoris Young’s modulus
- knee-flexion ROM
- weekly adherence

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as supportive context for quadriceps flexibility change, while clearly labeling the absence of a control group.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
