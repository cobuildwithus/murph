This source is included for **region_specific_remote_measurement**.

**Findings:** Reported high interrater reliability for both devices, with interrater ICC values around 0.89-0.92 and high correlation between inclinometer and goniometer values.

**Why it matters:** Murph users may want hip-extension tracking for hip-flexor stretching; this source helps bound measurement reliability for that endpoint.

**Potential experiment signals:** Hip extension range of motion, Modified Thomas test measurement reliability, Goniometer/inclinometer agreement.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use modified-Thomas hip-extension measurements only with consistent setup and as an outcome signal, not as proof that the protocol works.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction boundaries:**

- Population: Healthy participants in a hip-extension measurement reliability study
- Intervention/exposure: Modified Thomas test hip-extension ROM measured with an inclinometer and universal goniometer
- Comparator/control: Repeated measures across raters and sessions; comparison of inclinometer and goniometric measurement approaches
- Duration/follow-up: Repeated measurement sessions; exact interval not extracted from batch materials
- Adverse events or safety notes: No adverse events or safety signals were extracted; this was a measurement-reliability source.
- Population mismatch: Healthy participants; not necessarily symptomatic, older, or home unsupervised stretchers.
- Directness: Measurement-context evidence for a hip-flexor/hip-extension endpoint, not efficacy evidence for static stretching.

**Limitations:**

- Healthy measurement population rather than a stretching intervention trial.
- Does not test at-home stretching efficacy or adherence.
- Exact protocol details should be verified against the full article before publication.
