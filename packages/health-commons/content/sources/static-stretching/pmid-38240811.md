This source is included for **mechanisms_upper_body_general_primary**.

**Findings:** The stretching group improved flexibility over 8 weeks; the study also measured maximal strength and muscle thickness against a resistance-training comparator and control.

**Why it matters:** This is rare upper-body chronic static-stretching evidence, but its supervised high-volume setup differs from a simple at-home routine.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- pectoralis flexibility
- maximal strength
- muscle thickness
- training adherence

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as upper-body chronic stretching evidence with a clear caveat that the stretching dose and supervision were intensive.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
