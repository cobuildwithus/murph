This source is included for **adjacent_variants_recovery_modalities**.

**Findings:** The accessible PubMed snippet indicates the authors interpreted ROM increases after foam rolling in the broader literature as more likely due to a shift in pain threshold. The record is most useful as a controlled comparator of static stretching, dynamic stretching, and myofascial foam rolling in 14 young men.

**Why it matters:** Provides direct head-to-head boundary evidence for dynamic stretching and foam rolling rather than static-only home stretching.

**Potential experiment signals:** hip-flexion ROM, pain threshold or stretch tolerance, modality separation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as a boundary source; dynamic stretching and myofascial foam rolling should be represented as sibling modalities.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
