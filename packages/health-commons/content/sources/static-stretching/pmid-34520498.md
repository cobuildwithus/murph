## Findings
- Fourteen healthy men completed a randomized crossover protocol comparing 100% and 120% pain-onset-dose static hamstring stretching.
- Both intensities increased ROM and passive torque at end ROM for at least 20 minutes.
- MTU stiffness decreased only after the higher-intensity condition.
- Pain ratings were higher during the 120% condition but disappeared after stretching in accessible article details.

## Why it matters
Separates ROM/tolerance changes from stiffness changes and documents discomfort at higher stretch intensity.
