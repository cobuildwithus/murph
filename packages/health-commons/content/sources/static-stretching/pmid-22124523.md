## Findings
- Eighteen healthy men were assigned to stretching or control groups.
- After 4 weeks, the stretching group had increased ROM and muscle-tendon junction displacement and lower passive torque at 30° dorsiflexion.
- Fascicle length did not increase.

## Why it matters
Shows repeated static stretching can alter passive mechanical endpoints in addition to range of motion.

## Potential experiment signals
- ankle dorsiflexion ROM
