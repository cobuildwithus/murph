This source is included for **home_low_burden_trials**.

**Findings:** Self-administered high-volume pectoralis static stretching produced outcomes similar to supervised stretching in available extraction notes, with strength rather than flexibility as the main focus. Available extraction notes report maximal-strength increases in both supervised and self-administered stretching without meaningful between-stretch differences; rapid force development was not improved.

**Why it matters:** It addresses whether self-administered stretching can approximate supervised implementation, but its high volume and strength focus require caution.

**Potential experiment signals:** adherence, self-administered feasibility, strength, ROM if verified.

**Protocol takeaway:** Self-administered stretching is feasible context, but this strength-oriented high-volume pectoralis trial should not drive a low-burden flexibility dose.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
