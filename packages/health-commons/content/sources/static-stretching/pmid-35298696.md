This source is included for **adjacent_variants_recovery_modalities**.

**Findings:** Twenty studies with 38 effect sizes were included. Meta-analyses found no significant differences between single-bout stretching and foam rolling immediately after intervention (ES = 0.079; P = 0.39) or at 10, 15, or 20 minutes post-intervention (ES = -0.051, -0.011, and -0.161 respectively; all nonsignificant).

**Why it matters:** Acute ROM gains from a single bout should not be confused with multi-week flexibility training effects, and foam rolling remains a sibling modality.

**Potential experiment signals:** acute ROM, short-lived ROM carryover, warm-up context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent acute comparator context only; do not use to define chronic at-home static-stretching dose.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
