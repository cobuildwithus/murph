This source is included for **mechanisms_upper_body_general_primary**.

**Findings:** Passive hip extension increased and anterior pelvic tilt decreased after stretching; lumbar lordosis did not change, and changes in hip extension did not meaningfully correlate with postural changes.

**Why it matters:** The study is useful for hip-stretch mechanism context and for avoiding overclaims about posture or low-back alignment.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- passive hip extension
- anterior pelvic tilt
- lumbar lordosis

**Protocol takeaway:** Hip-flexor stretching may acutely change hip extension and pelvic tilt measures, but it should not be promoted as proven to change lumbar posture from this study alone.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
