This source is included for **safety_guidelines_special_populations**.

**Findings:** The paper illustrates two EDS chronic pain cases and reviews management considerations; the discovery ledger notes gentle stretching limits and avoiding subluxation/dislocation in EDS contexts.

**Why it matters:** Useful as lower-level evidence that EDS pain management should be multimodal and cautious about subluxation/dislocation.

**Potential experiment signals:** chronic pain, rehabilitation, gentle stretching limits, subluxation/dislocation avoidance, multimodal management.

**Safety notes:** Avoid interpreting EDS-related pain or tightness as a prompt for aggressive stretching; clinical, multimodal management is more appropriate.

**Limitations:** Two case reports plus review; low certainty and not a direct static-stretching efficacy study.

**Population mismatch:** EDS chronic pain population, not healthy users.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as safety context for EDS/chronic pain, not for general stretching claims.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
