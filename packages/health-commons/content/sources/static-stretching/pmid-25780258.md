This source is included for **home_low_burden_trials**.

**Findings:** A four-week neck/shoulder stretching program improved pain/function outcomes in office workers with chronic symptoms in available extraction notes. Available extraction notes report decreased neck/shoulder pain and improved neck function and quality of life; participants exercising at least three times weekly had greater function and SF-36 physical-quality-of-life improvements.

**Why it matters:** It informs feasible upper-body stretching in office workers but should not become a general flexibility efficacy claim.

**Potential experiment signals:** neck pain, shoulder pain, function, quality of life, adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Clinical pain benefits need separate framing and screening; healthy-user flexibility should cite cleaner ROM trials.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
