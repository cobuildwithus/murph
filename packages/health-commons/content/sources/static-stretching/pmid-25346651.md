This source is included for **safety_guidelines_special_populations**.

**Findings:** The review found most guidelines supported moderate-intensity activity during pregnancy; 10 of 11 provided advice on initiating activity, 6 included absolute/relative contraindications, all ruled out fall/trauma/collision-risk sports, and 6 included indications for stopping exercise.

**Why it matters:** Useful for comparing warning-sign and contraindication language across multiple pregnancy guidelines.

**Potential experiment signals:** contraindications, warning signs, falls/trauma/collision avoidance, exercise initiation advice, activity dose guidance.

**Safety notes:** Pregnancy stretching guidance should be embedded in broader contraindication, fall/trauma avoidance, and stop-sign language.

**Limitations:** Guideline comparison rather than direct stretching evidence; guideline content varied by country and publication date.

**Population mismatch:** Pregnancy guideline context, not healthy nonpregnant adult flexibility training.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a secondary pregnancy boundary source, especially for contraindications and stop signs.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
