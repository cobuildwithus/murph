## Findings
- Accessible records describe a 300-second hamstring static-stretch exposure with reassessments at 10, 20, and 30 minutes.
- Passive stiffness reportedly decreased at 10 and 20 minutes but was not different by 30 minutes.
- ROM and passive torque at onset of pain remained increased across intervals, while isometric force decreased across intervals.

## Why it matters
Adds a time-course boundary: stiffness effects may fade faster than ROM/tolerance, and strength output can be temporarily reduced.

## Potential experiment signals
- hamstring stiffness
- ROM
- stretch tolerance

## Protocol takeaway
Use for things-to-watch: avoid promising long-lasting stiffness change from a single stretch and avoid scheduling intense strength work immediately after long static stretching.
