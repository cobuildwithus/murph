This source is included for **safety_guidelines_special_populations**.

**Findings:** Cervical radiculopathy can present with neck/arm/shoulder/chest/upper-back radiating pain, paresthesia, numbness, weakness, and reflex changes.

**Why it matters:** Helps define stop rules when a stretch produces radiating pain, numbness, tingling, weakness, or progressive neurologic signs.

**Potential experiment signals:** radiating pain, paresthesia, numbness, weakness, reflex changes, myelopathic signs, red flags.

**Safety notes:** Progressive neurologic loss, myelopathic signs, trauma-related bilateral/multiple-root symptoms, fever/night sweats/weight loss, or gait/urinary/hand-clumsiness symptoms should trigger clinical evaluation rather than continued stretching.

**Limitations:** Clinical review, not a stretching trial.

**Population mismatch:** Symptomatic clinical population, not routine flexibility users.

**Protocol takeaway:** Radiating neurologic symptoms are stop-and-seek-care signals, not stretch-through discomfort.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
