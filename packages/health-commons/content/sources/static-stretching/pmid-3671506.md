This source is included for **home_field_test_measurement**.

**Findings:** The toe-touch test was evaluated for validity and reliability; an abstract snippet reported mean finger-floor distance around 3 cm below floor level with SD 8 cm, but exact reliability coefficients were not extracted.

**Why it matters:** It gives historical support for toe-touch/finger-floor tracking but also points to interpretation limits for global reach tests.

**Potential experiment signals:** Toe-touch distance, Finger-floor distance, Repeated test consistency.

**Protocol takeaway:** Toe-touch distance can be logged as a global reach endpoint, but do not treat it as interchangeable with sit-and-reach or lumbar ROM.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
