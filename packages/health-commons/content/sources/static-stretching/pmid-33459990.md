This source is included for **mechanisms_syntheses**.

**Findings:** source_artifact:pmid-33459990 included 11 studies with 14 independent measures and reported moderate non-local passive ROM increases after acute unilateral passive static stretching, with no significant moderation by sex, training status, intensity, or duration in extracted abstract details.

**Why it matters:** Boundary source showing ROM changes can sometimes appear in non-stretched joints, reinforcing that mechanisms may include neural or psychophysiological tolerance rather than only local tissue change.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- Interpret unexpected ROM changes cautiously; non-local effects may occur.
- Local target-joint measurements remain important.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as boundary mechanism context only; non-local acute effects should not substitute for target-area stretching evidence or home-program outcomes.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness and mismatch:** same_mechanism. Acute, unilateral, non-local effects in healthy adults differ from chronic target-area at-home flexibility practice.

**Safety/adverse events:** No adverse-event findings were extracted from the accessible source summary.

**Limitations:**
- Included 11 studies and 14 independent measures in extracted abstract.
- Aggregate participant count was not extracted.
- Non-local endpoint is adjacent to the protocol’s target-stretch outcomes.
