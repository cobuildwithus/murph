This source is included for **shoulder_thoracic_upper_body**.

**Findings:**
- **Dose/population:** 30 male tennis players with internal-rotation deficiency were randomized to modified sleeper or modified cross-body stretching for 4 weeks, 3 to 5 repetitions once daily, 30 seconds per hold.
- **Outcomes:** Both groups improved internal-rotation ROM and DASH upper-limb function. The abstract reports large relative changes, but no postintervention difference between groups.
- **Limits:** Therapist stabilization was used to limit compensation, and the sample was small, male, asymptomatic, and sport-specific.
- **Safety:** No intervention adverse events were identified in accessible text.

**Why it matters:** It provides recent four-week posterior-shoulder stretching evidence and includes both ROM and upper-limb function measures.

**Potential experiment signals:** internal-rotation ROM, DASH-style function, adherence, shoulder symptom check.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent support that daily 30-second posterior-shoulder holds can improve internal-rotation ROM in tennis players; do not claim superiority between modified sleeper and modified cross-body stretch.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
