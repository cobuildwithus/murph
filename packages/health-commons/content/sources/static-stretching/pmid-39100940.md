This source is included for **region_specific_remote_measurement**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries report high intra-rater and inter-rater reliability, especially when pelvic tilt is controlled.

**Why it matters:** Hip-flexor length is a plausible target for at-home static stretching, but measurement quality is critical.

**Potential experiment signals:** Hip flexor length, Modified Thomas test, Goniometric reliability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Hip-flexor measurement instructions should explicitly standardize pelvic position and rater/user setup.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction boundaries:**

- Population: Healthy individuals
- Intervention/exposure: Goniometric techniques for measuring hip-flexor length in the modified Thomas test
- Comparator/control: Intra-rater and inter-rater reliability
- Duration/follow-up: Measurement reliability design; exact retest interval not extracted
- Adverse events or safety notes: No adverse events were extracted.
- Population mismatch: Healthy individuals; not unsupervised home users.
- Directness: Measurement-context support for hip-flexor endpoints.

**Limitations:**

- Healthy participants.
- Measurement study only.
- Requires careful control of pelvic tilt and standardized technique.
