This source is included for **region_specific_remote_measurement**.

**Findings:** The study tested whether an iPad/mobile app video angle method could reliably and accurately measure FMS deep-squat motion; exact coefficients were not extracted from accessible batch materials.

**Why it matters:** This helps separate app-based movement quantification from direct static-stretching outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:** FMS deep squat, Mobile app goniometry, Reliability, Accuracy.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not treat mobile FMS deep-squat angle as a primary static-stretching endpoint unless the protocol specifically includes that screen.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction boundaries:**

- Population: Healthy subjects
- Intervention/exposure: Goniometer mobile device application used on video of the Functional Movement Screen deep squat
- Comparator/control: Three-dimensional motion analysis system
- Duration/follow-up: Single-session video measurement study
- Adverse events or safety notes: No adverse events were extracted.
- Population mismatch: Healthy subjects under recorded testing, not general self-guided home users.
- Directness: Adjacent remote/app measurement context.

**Limitations:**

- Deep squat/FMS is adjacent and composite.
- Requires video, app, and rater workflow.
- Exact reliability/accuracy values not extracted.
