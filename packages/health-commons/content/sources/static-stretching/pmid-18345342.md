This source is included for **ankle_calf_dorsiflexion**.

**Findings:** In 33 healthy volunteers, 3 weeks of gastrocnemius stretching increased weight-bearing and non-weight-bearing dorsiflexion; subtalar pronation versus supination did not differ significantly.

**Why it matters:** This supports dorsiflexion ROM as a realistic calf-stretch endpoint while suggesting that subtalar position may not need to be over-specified for a broad at-home protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** Weight-bearing dorsiflexion; Non-weight-bearing dorsiflexion; Subtalar position.

**Protocol takeaway:** For at-home instructions, basic gastrocnemius stretching may matter more than fine-grained subtalar-position cueing.

**Limitations:** Healthy-volunteer sample. Subtalar position comparison does not define optimal dose. Sex distribution was mostly female.

**Population mismatch:** General healthy sample but mostly women; not selected for a general home-app cohort.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
