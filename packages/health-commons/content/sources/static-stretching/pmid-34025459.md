This source is included for **adjacent_variants_recovery_modalities**.

**Findings:** Eleven RCTs were included qualitatively and 10 in meta-analysis (n = 229). Compared with passive recovery, post-exercise stretching showed no effect on strength recovery (ES = -0.08, 95% CI -0.54 to 0.39, p = 0.750) and no effect on 24-, 48-, or 72-hour DOMS (ES range -0.09 to -0.24; p = 0.187 to 0.629). The authors concluded recommendations for recovery purposes should be avoided because data were scarce, heterogeneous, and very low certainty.

**Why it matters:** Protects the protocol page from claiming recovery, DOMS, or strength benefits from stretching when the recovery literature is null/very low certainty.

**Potential experiment signals:** DOMS, strength recovery, post-exercise ROM, recovery timing.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promise DOMS relief, strength recovery, or post-workout recovery acceleration from this flexibility protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
