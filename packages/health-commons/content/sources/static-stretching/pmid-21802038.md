This source is included for **hamstring_sit_reach**.

**Findings:**
Static stretching and hold-relax PNF had similar immediate effects on active knee-extension ROM in healthy adults. Endpoint: Active knee-extension range of motion. Effect/direction: Static stretching and hold-relax PNF improved AKE ROM, with no significant difference between interventions in accessible reporting.

**Why it matters:** Balances PNF comparator evidence by showing similar immediate effects under quantified conditions.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- Active knee-extension ROM
- Immediate ROM response

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context that static stretching can perform similarly to PNF acutely in some settings, but do not generalize to long-term home outcomes.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction notes:**
- Study design: Randomized comparison of hold-relax PNF versus static stretching, with the contralateral leg serving as control.
- Population: 30 healthy asymptomatic adults.
- Intervention/exposure: Static hamstring stretching.
- Comparator/control: Hold-relax PNF and within-person control leg.
- Duration/follow-up: Single session.
- Endpoints: Active knee-extension range of motion
- Adverse events or safety notes: No conflicts/funding were reported in accessible abstract; adverse events were not reported.
- Limitations: Single-session immediate-effect design; healthy young adults; not home-specific.
- Population mismatch: Adjacent because of PNF comparator and supervised/lab delivery.
