This source is included for **home_low_burden_trials**.

**Findings:** A recent plantar-flexor RCT compared static stretching and resistance training; available extraction notes emphasize no static-stretch increase in strength or thickness and do not isolate detailed ROM effects. Available extraction notes state the static-stretching arm did not increase muscle strength or thickness; detailed ROM and passive-property effect estimates were not extracted.

**Why it matters:** It preserves a null/negative adjacent finding and prevents importing strength claims into a flexibility protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** ankle ROM, passive stiffness/properties, strength, muscle thickness.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not claim low-burden static stretching increases muscle strength or thickness from this source.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
