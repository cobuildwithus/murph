This source is included for **ankle_calf_dorsiflexion**.

**Findings:** In 13 runners with ankle joint equinus, twice-daily Flexeramp calf stretching increased ankle dorsiflexion from about 5 degrees to 16 degrees over 8 weeks and changed dynamic foot-pressure and stance-time parameters.

**Why it matters:** This is adjacent because of the equinus/running/device context, but it preserves foot-pressure and gait-timing signals that may matter for ankle-specific protocol variants.

**Potential experiment signals:** Ankle dorsiflexion; Plantar pressure; Force; Stance timing.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent context only; do not generalize foot-pressure changes to ordinary home stretching users.

**Limitations:** Small uncontrolled sample. Runners with ankle equinus, not general users. Device-based ramp stretching.

**Population mismatch:** Runners with ankle equinus and device use.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
