## Findings
- Twelve participants completed static stretching, contract-relax, and CRAC ankle-dorsiflexion techniques in accessible summaries.
- CRAC produced larger gains than contract-relax, and contract-relax exceeded static stretching in the accessible summary.
- Static stretching did not show the same significant pre-post ROM gain reported for CR/CRAC in accessible summaries.

## Why it matters
Preserves a negative/mixed finding for static stretching relative to PNF variants.
