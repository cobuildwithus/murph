This source is included for **home_field_test_measurement**.

**Findings:** Weight-bearing ankle dorsiflexion measures showed good intrarater reliability, with reported ICCs above 0.85 for the practical measures.

**Why it matters:** It supports ankle dorsiflexion as a plausible home-measured endpoint when trial averaging and setup controls are used.

**Potential experiment signals:** Weight-bearing lunge distance, Dorsiflexion angle, Repeated trial average.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use a repeatable ankle dorsiflexion measurement method and average repeated trials; avoid mixing angle and distance units.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Healthy adults; mean age about 24 years, measured by a novice rater.
- **Comparator/control:** Repeated measurements across methods.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Reliability testing session; follow-up not extracted.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse events extracted.
- **Limitations:** Small healthy sample.; Novice-rater context is useful but still not fully equivalent to unsupervised self-measurement.
- **Population mismatch:** Healthy measurement sample, not a stretching intervention.
- **Artifact rights:** open_access; no copyrighted PDF is included in Git by this draft.
