This source is included for **region_specific_remote_measurement**.

**Findings:** The study evaluated real-time intersession and interrater reliability of the FMS; exact coefficients were not extracted from accessible batch materials.

**Why it matters:** It helps keep FMS evidence separated from direct flexibility measurement.

**Potential experiment signals:** Functional Movement Screen, Real-time scoring reliability, Intersession reliability, Interrater reliability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep FMS reliability sources out of core protocol efficacy claims unless the page explicitly discusses movement-screen measurement.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction boundaries:**

- Population: Functional Movement Screen participants; exact sample not extracted
- Intervention/exposure: Real-time Functional Movement Screen scoring
- Comparator/control: Intersession and interrater comparisons
- Duration/follow-up: Repeated-session FMS reliability design; exact interval not extracted
- Adverse events or safety notes: No adverse events were extracted.
- Population mismatch: FMS-scored participants and raters, not at-home static-stretching users.
- Directness: Adjacent movement-screen measurement context only.

**Limitations:**

- Exact quantitative results not extracted.
- FMS is a composite screen, not a pure flexibility endpoint.
- Trained real-time scoring differs from home self-measurement.
