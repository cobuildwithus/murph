This source is included for **home_field_test_measurement**.

**Findings:** Intra-rater ICCs were reported as 0.72 on the right and 0.82 on the left; inter-rater ICCs were 0.73 on the right and 0.65 on the left. The study described moderate reliability and excellent concurrent validity.

**Why it matters:** It informs app-assisted ankle tracking as an adjacent home option, but moderate inter-rater reliability argues for within-person consistency.

**Potential experiment signals:** Smartphone goniometer angle, Weight-bearing lunge angle, Repeated-session consistency.

**Protocol takeaway:** Smartphone angle measurement may be useful for personal trends, but use the same app, placement, and rater whenever possible.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Young healthy adults.
- **Comparator/control:** Repeated sessions by two examiners and concurrent validity comparison.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Four sessions, repeated no sooner than 24 hours apart.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse events extracted.
- **Limitations:** Smartphone-app measurement is adjacent to simple tape/wall home measurement.; Young healthy sample.
- **Population mismatch:** Healthy young adults and examiner-led app measurement, not general at-home self-testing.
