This source is included for **ankle_calf_dorsiflexion**.

**Findings:** Among 10 completers, the stretching group increased maximal passive dorsiflexion angle by about 7 degrees and passive torque, while passive stiffness was not significantly changed.

**Why it matters:** This is direct chronic calf-stretching evidence that supports passive dorsiflexion ROM gains while preserving mechanistic nuance.

**Potential experiment signals:** Passive dorsiflexion angle; Passive torque; Passive stiffness.

**Protocol takeaway:** A multi-week calf stretching routine may improve passive ankle dorsiflexion in some users, but mechanisms should be described cautiously.

**Limitations:** Very small sample and only younger women. Only 10 participants completed. Passive stiffness did not change significantly.

**Population mismatch:** Sex-specific unconditioned sample; may not generalize to all at-home users.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
