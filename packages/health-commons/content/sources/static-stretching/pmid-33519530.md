This source is included for **mechanisms_upper_body_general_primary**.

**Findings:** Knee-flexion ROM improved after 100% and 120% intensity stretching, but rectus femoris stiffness decreased only after 100% intensity and not after the highest or lowest intensity.

**Why it matters:** This source gives a safety-relevant dose nuance: more intense is not always better.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- knee-flexion ROM
- rectus femoris shear modulus
- vastus lateralis shear modulus
- vastus medialis shear modulus
- pain intensity

**Protocol takeaway:** Recommend tolerable intensity rather than maximum discomfort, especially for quadriceps stretching.
