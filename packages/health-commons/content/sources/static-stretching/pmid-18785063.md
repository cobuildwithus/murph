This source is included for **adverse_event_clinical_population_mismatch**.

**Findings:** The review reported moderate-to-strong evidence that routine static stretching does not reduce overall injury rates, while preliminary evidence suggested possible reduction in musculotendinous injuries. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-18785063`.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** No individual adverse-event rate was extracted; the source is used for mixed/null injury-prevention context.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Warm-up trials in exercise settings, not at-home flexibility practice. Limitations: Overall methodological quality and reporting were poor.; Warm-up injury-prevention trials are adjacent to, not direct evidence for, at-home flexibility routines..

**Why it matters:** It preserves mixed evidence: overall null, but not purely negative for every injury subtype.

**Potential experiment signals:** overall injury rate, musculotendinous injury, study quality.

**Protocol takeaway:** Avoid broad injury-prevention claims; if mentioned, note possible injury-subtype uncertainty.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`. Boundary: Use only for injury-prevention caution and do not cite as direct flexibility efficacy.
