**Findings:** The study reported increased hamstring flexibility with stretching and deterioration in some measures in the control group, including hip-flexor muscle-tendon length and eccentric knee-extensor peak torque. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-21399855`.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse events were extracted.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Older institutionalized population; clinical/gerontology context rather than general home protocol. Limitations: Small sample and institutionalized older-woman population may not generalize to community home users.; Duration and full protocol details were not extracted in this batch..

**Why it matters:** It offers supportive but non-generalizable context for older adults.

**Potential experiment signals:** flexibility, muscle performance, functionality, hamstring flexibility, knee extensor torque.

**Protocol takeaway:** Older users may need gentler dosing and should be evaluated separately from general adult claims.
