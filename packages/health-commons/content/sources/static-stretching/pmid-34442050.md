This source is included for **home_field_test_measurement**.

**Findings:** The review included 101 original studies of criterion-related validity for adult field-based fitness tests; flexibility-test-specific effect estimates were not extracted in this batch.

**Why it matters:** It supports using field-test validity standards when choosing home flexibility outcomes, while avoiding claims about stretching effects.

**Potential experiment signals:** Field-test validity standards, Sit-and-reach category, Criterion comparison availability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as background support for field-test endpoint selection, not as a source of intervention efficacy claims.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Adults aged roughly 19 to 64 years in field-based fitness-test validity studies.
- **Comparator/control:** Criterion validity measures used in original studies.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Systematic review; no intervention follow-up.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse events; measurement review only.
- **Limitations:** Review is broader than static stretching and includes many fitness-test categories.; No flexibility-specific pooled estimate extracted here.
- **Population mismatch:** Measurement-validity review, not intervention efficacy.
- **Artifact rights:** open_access; no copyrighted PDF is included in Git by this draft.
