This source is included for **region_specific_remote_measurement**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries report that the iPhone Compass app was reliable and correlated positively with universal goniometer measures, but the universal goniometer measured higher by about 2.8 degrees.

**Why it matters:** Phone-based thoracic rotation tracking is attractive for home experiments but method consistency matters.

**Potential experiment signals:** Thoracic rotation ROM, iPhone Compass measurement, Universal goniometer comparison, Reliability, Concurrent validity.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use the same thoracic-rotation method across baseline and follow-up; do not combine phone and goniometer values as equivalent.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction boundaries:**

- Population: Healthy participants
- Intervention/exposure: iPhone Compass application measurement of thoracic rotation ROM
- Comparator/control: Universal goniometer measurements
- Duration/follow-up: Measurement reliability and validity design
- Adverse events or safety notes: No adverse events were extracted.
- Population mismatch: Healthy measurement sample; not at-home stretching participants.
- Directness: Remote/app measurement context for thoracic ROM.

**Limitations:**

- Healthy participants.
- Device/app-specific measurement context.
- Systematic difference between app and goniometer means methods should not be mixed casually.
