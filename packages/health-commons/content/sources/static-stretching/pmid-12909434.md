**Findings:** The review reported mixed trial directions: most randomized trials did not show reduced injury, while some controlled trials and one randomized trial favored stretching. The authors judged the evidence insufficient for a definitive injury-prevention conclusion. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-12909434`.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** No specific adverse-event synthesis was extracted; the source is used as an injury-prevention evidence boundary.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Exercise injury-prevention literature, not direct at-home flexibility evidence. Limitations: Few eligible trials and substantial heterogeneity in populations, stretch types, and injury definitions.; Only two included studies were rated above 50/100 for quality in the accessible abstract..

**Why it matters:** It preserves uncertainty across positive, negative, and lower-quality injury-prevention trials.

**Potential experiment signals:** exercise-related injury incidence, study quality, heterogeneity of stretching protocols.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use this review to promise injury prevention; use it as a boundary note for adjacent warm-up literature.
