This source is included for **home_low_burden_trials**.

**Findings:** A simple cross-body posterior-shoulder static stretch improved shoulder ROM more clearly than a sleeper-stretch comparison in available extraction notes. Available extraction notes report larger improvement for the cross-body stretch than control and a smaller, non-significant comparison for sleeper stretch versus control.

**Why it matters:** It is one of the most direct self-administered static-stretch RCTs for upper-body flexibility.

**Potential experiment signals:** shoulder internal rotation ROM, horizontal adduction ROM, session comfort.

**Protocol takeaway:** Self-administered static shoulder stretches can be credible, but joint-specific results should stay joint-specific.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
