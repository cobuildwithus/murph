This source is included for **ankle_calf_dorsiflexion**.

**Findings:** In 68 adults with diabetes and ankle equinus, 8 weeks of calf stretching produced no significant between-group improvements in non-weight-bearing dorsiflexion, weight-bearing dorsiflexion, in-shoe forefoot pressure, or barefoot peak pressure.

**Why it matters:** This is a critical negative boundary source showing that clinical equinus/diabetes populations should not be assumed to respond like healthy volunteers.

**Potential experiment signals:** Dorsiflexion ROM; Plantar pressure; Clinical risk population.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not claim the protocol reduces diabetic plantar pressure or treats ankle equinus; clinical populations require separate guidance.

**Limitations:** Clinical diabetes/equinus population. Loss to follow-up was higher in the stretching group. Not a healthy flexibility or wellness protocol.

**Population mismatch:** Adults with diabetes and ankle equinus.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
