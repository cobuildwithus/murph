This source is included for **region_specific_remote_measurement**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries indicate that lumbopelvic stabilization was evaluated to reduce measurement error during modified Thomas testing; exact coefficients were not extracted.

**Why it matters:** Hip-flexor endpoints can be invalid if lumbar/pelvic compensation is not controlled.

**Potential experiment signals:** Modified Thomas test, Hip flexor flexibility, Lumbopelvic stabilization, Measurement reliability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Home hip-flexor tests should emphasize pelvis/lumbar stabilization or avoid precise claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction boundaries:**

- Population: Participants in a modified-Thomas reliability study; exact sample not extracted
- Intervention/exposure: Modified Thomas test with lumbopelvic stabilization
- Comparator/control: Repeated measures to estimate reliability and measurement error
- Duration/follow-up: Test-retest reliability design; exact interval not extracted
- Adverse events or safety notes: No adverse events were extracted.
- Population mismatch: Controlled measurement setup rather than home self-assessment.
- Directness: Measurement-context support for controlling lumbopelvic position during hip-flexor endpoint testing.

**Limitations:**

- Measurement source only.
- Exact sample size and coefficients not extracted.
- Requires stabilization that may be difficult for unsupervised home users.
