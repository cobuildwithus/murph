This source is included for **safety_guidelines_special_populations**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries report benefits on proprioception and pain in hEDS but emphasize huge heterogeneity and missing robust randomized controlled studies.

**Why it matters:** Shows that hEDS physical therapy evidence is condition-specific and heterogeneous, not a basis for generic stretching claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** pain, proprioception, quality of life, functional exercise capacity, intervention heterogeneity.

**Safety notes:** Use as a reminder that hEDS management is individualized and clinical, not generic stretching.

**Limitations:** Heterogeneous interventions and limited robust RCT evidence; not a healthy-adult stretching protocol.

**Population mismatch:** hEDS clinical population.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for safety/referral boundaries and avoid converting hEDS PT findings into static-stretching claims.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
