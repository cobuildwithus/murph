This source is included for **mechanisms_upper_body_general_primary**.

**Findings:** ROM increased and passive torque/muscle-tendon stiffness decreased immediately and at 5 minutes after three minutes of static stretching; maximal voluntary torque did not change.

**Why it matters:** This helps bound acute effects of a short, realistic static-stretch dose.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- dorsiflexion ROM
- passive resistive torque
- muscle-tendon stiffness
- maximal voluntary torque

**Protocol takeaway:** A few minutes of static stretching can produce short-term ROM and passive-resistance changes, but the evidence is acute and lower-leg specific.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
