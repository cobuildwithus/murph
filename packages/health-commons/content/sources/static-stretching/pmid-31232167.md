This source is included for **mechanisms_upper_body_general_primary**.

**Findings:** Iliacus shear modulus decreased after the first 1-minute stretch and was lower after five stretches than after one stretch.

**Why it matters:** This is hip-region dose and stiffness context, but it is acute and male-only.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- iliacus shear modulus
- hip extension angle
- number of 1-minute stretches

**Protocol takeaway:** Hip flexor/iliacus stiffness changes may require repeated holds; do not infer long-term benefit from this acute study alone.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
