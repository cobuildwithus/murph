This source is included for **home_low_burden_trials**.

**Findings:** Static stretching and full-ROM resistance training both improved sit-and-reach flexibility in available extraction notes, while resistance training had superior strength effects. Available extraction notes report resistance training and static stretching were similarly effective for sit-and-reach flexibility; resistance training improved hip/lower-back extensor strength more.

**Why it matters:** It is important boundary evidence for comparing flexibility methods without overclaiming static stretching.

**Potential experiment signals:** sit-and-reach, strength, variant comparison.

**Protocol takeaway:** Static stretching can improve flexibility, but other modalities may achieve similar ROM with different tradeoffs.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
