This source is included for **mechanisms_upper_body_general_primary**.

**Findings:** Low- and high-discomfort stretching both increased joint maximum ROM, while intensity did not clearly affect stiffness or muscle architecture endpoints.

**Why it matters:** This preserves a chronic intensity counterpoint and suggests that high discomfort may not be necessary for ROM gains.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- maximum ROM
- stiffness
- fascicle angle
- fascicle length
- affective response

**Protocol takeaway:** For home practice, avoid recommending high discomfort as necessary for benefit.
