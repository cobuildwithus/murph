This source is included for **adjacent_variant_boundaries**.

**Findings:** In 118 sedentary older adults, 8 weeks of yoga was reported as just as effective as stretching-strengthening exercises for functional fitness. Both groups improved balance, strength, flexibility, and mobility measures, with reported partial eta-squared values ranging from about 0.05 to 0.47.

**Why it matters:** Helps keep yoga and stretch-strengthening programs separate from static stretching alone while preserving functional-fitness context.

**Potential experiment signals:** balance, strength, flexibility, mobility, sit-and-reach, back scratch.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as adjacent multimodal context; yoga and stretch-strengthening are not the at-home static-stretching protocol.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Supervised older-adult intervention; multimodal comparison; does not isolate static stretching. No at-home static-stretching dose inference. Population mismatch: Older sedentary adults in supervised group exercise; yoga and strengthening are multimodal, not static stretching alone.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. Context-only boundary for yoga and multimodal functional-fitness claims.
