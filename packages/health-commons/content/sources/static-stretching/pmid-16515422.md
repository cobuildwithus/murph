This source is included for **mechanisms_upper_body_general_primary**.

**Findings:** The experimental leg did not show increased hamstring extensibility at a standardized torque, while stretch tolerance increased.

**Why it matters:** The finding cautions that some ROM improvements may reflect increased tolerance to stretch rather than structural lengthening.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- hip flexion angle at standardized torque
- hip flexion angle at maximum tolerated torque
- stretch tolerance

**Protocol takeaway:** Describe ROM gains as user-relevant flexibility changes; do not overstate them as proof of permanent tissue lengthening.
