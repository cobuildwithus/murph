This source is included for **direct_dose_synthesis**.

**Findings:** Across 77 eligible studies and 186 effect sizes representing 3,870 participants, chronic stretch training increased ROM versus control (ES=-1.002; 95% CI -1.165 to -0.840; p<0.001; I²=74.97). Static and PNF stretching produced greater ROM gains than dynamic/ballistic stretching; females showed higher gains than males in subgroup analysis.

**Why it matters:** It is a central chronic-ROM backbone source and separates static/PNF training from dynamic/ballistic variants.

**Potential experiment signals:** range of motion, chronic stretch training, stretch typology, moderator variables

**Protocol takeaway:** Chronic stretch training can increase ROM; static stretching belongs among the stronger long-term ROM variants, but broad dose moderators were not clearly decisive in this synthesis.

**Directness and population fit:** Direct for chronic stretching and ROM, but static-only claims must be separated from PNF and other stretch variants.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event result was extracted from the accessible abstract/search material.

**Limitations:** Substantial heterogeneity; broad populations and joints; moderator analyses did not support a simple volume/frequency/intensity rule.
