This source is included for **region_specific_remote_measurement**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries report excellent intra- and inter-rater ICCs for reactive peak force and active knee extension measures, with low SEM values.

**Why it matters:** Adds hip-flexor measurement context while avoiding overpromotion of specialized tests.

**Potential experiment signals:** Hip flexor flexibility, Reactive hip flexor test, Reactive peak force, Active knee extension, SEM.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as background only; do not recommend specialized reactive hip-flexor testing for general at-home users.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction boundaries:**

- Population: Healthy young participants; 52 hips assessed
- Intervention/exposure: Reactive hip flexor test for hip-flexor muscle flexibility
- Comparator/control: Reliability and validity comparisons including active knee extension in accessible summaries
- Duration/follow-up: Measurement validation design
- Adverse events or safety notes: No adverse events were extracted.
- Population mismatch: Young healthy participants; specialized assessment rather than simple home endpoint.
- Directness: Measurement context for hip-flexor flexibility, with feasibility limitations.

**Limitations:**

- Healthy youth/young adult population.
- Specialized test/tool may not be feasible for ordinary home users.
- Not a static-stretching intervention.
