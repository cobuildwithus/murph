This source is included for **region_specific_remote_measurement**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries report intrarater ICC around 0.76, interrater ICC around 0.74, SEM within one point, and MDC95 about 2.54 points on the 21-point FMS scale.

**Why it matters:** This helps avoid overinterpreting small FMS changes if movement-screen outcomes are mentioned.

**Potential experiment signals:** Functional Movement Screen, Test-retest reliability, Interrater reliability, SEM, MDC95.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use small FMS score changes as evidence of flexibility improvement unless they exceed MDC and the endpoint is clearly labeled adjacent.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction boundaries:**

- Population: Participants completing the Functional Movement Screen; exact participant count not extracted from accessible batch materials
- Intervention/exposure: Functional Movement Screen scoring
- Comparator/control: Intrarater test-retest and interrater scoring comparisons
- Duration/follow-up: Retest interval reported in accessible summaries as approximately 48-72 hours
- Adverse events or safety notes: No adverse events were extracted.
- Population mismatch: FMS testing context, not home static-stretching participants.
- Directness: Adjacent movement-screen measurement context.

**Limitations:**

- Composite movement screen and not a static-stretching intervention.
- Participant count was not extracted from accessible materials in this batch.
- Total score may not isolate flexibility.
