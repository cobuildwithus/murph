This source is included for **safety_guidelines_special_populations**.

**Findings:** The international classification recognizes 13 EDS subtypes and proposes clinical criteria suggestive for diagnosis.

**Why it matters:** EDS populations may have tissue fragility, instability, and subtype-specific risks that are outside the default home stretching protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** EDS subtypes, diagnostic criteria, clinical heterogeneity, heritable connective tissue disorders.

**Safety notes:** A known or suspected EDS diagnosis should trigger clinician-guided exercise choices rather than generic end-range stretching.

**Limitations:** Diagnostic classification; no stretching intervention tested.

**Population mismatch:** EDS diagnostic context, not at-home static stretching efficacy.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use EDS as a special-population boundary and avoid universal stretching claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
