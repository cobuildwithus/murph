This source is included for **ankle_calf_dorsiflexion**.

**Findings:** In 20 healthy men, four 30-second static calf stretches did not significantly reduce passive resistive torque during ankle dorsiflexion.

**Why it matters:** This directly cautions against assuming that a brief calf stretch immediately changes passive mechanical resistance even when the stretch is relevant to dorsiflexion.

**Potential experiment signals:** Resistive torque; Immediate passive dorsiflexion response.

**Protocol takeaway:** Brief static calf stretching should not be framed as reliably lowering passive ankle resistance after a single session.

**Limitations:** Acute single-session exposure. Healthy young men only. Mechanical torque endpoint may not match user-perceived flexibility.

**Population mismatch:** Sex- and age-restricted sample; not a general home population.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
