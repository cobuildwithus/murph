This source is included for **home_low_burden_trials**.

**Findings:** Several static hamstring-stretch schedules improved ROM over four weeks, with no clear frequency superiority among the tested schedules in the extracted notes. All stretching groups improved ROM during the stretching phase; available extraction notes found no significant differences in gain or loss across the compared frequency protocols.

**Why it matters:** It helps support a manageable low-burden schedule without implying that more daily sessions always add benefit.

**Potential experiment signals:** hamstring ROM, adherence burden, detraining/loss.

**Protocol takeaway:** A low-burden schedule can be credible; more frequent stretching is not automatically superior in the available frequency data.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
