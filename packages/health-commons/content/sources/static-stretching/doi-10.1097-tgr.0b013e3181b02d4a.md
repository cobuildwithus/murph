This source is included for **ankle_calf_dorsiflexion**.

**Findings:** Available abstract-level records indicate stretching with ProStretch or incline board was not more effective for active ankle dorsiflexion ROM than no stretching in healthy college students.

**Why it matters:** This is an adjacent device/stretching-versus-strengthening boundary that prevents overclaiming active dorsiflexion gains from stretching devices.

**Potential experiment signals:** Active ankle dorsiflexion ROM; Device-based calf stretch.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use device-based calf stretching as settled evidence of active dorsiflexion benefit; keep it as a boundary/context source.

**Limitations:** Exact participant count and full protocol details were not extracted. Healthy college-student population. Device-based stretching differs from a simple no-equipment home protocol.

**Population mismatch:** College students and device-based stretching; adjacent to general at-home static stretching.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
