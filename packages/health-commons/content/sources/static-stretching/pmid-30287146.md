- Intervention/exposure: Clinical measurement methods for posterior shoulder tightness
- Comparator/control: Reliability, validity, and methodological quality across measurement approaches
- Duration/follow-up: Systematic review; original-study designs varied
- Adverse events or safety notes: No adverse events were extracted.
- Population mismatch: Shoulder-measurement literature, not at-home static-stretching protocol participants.
- Directness: Measurement context for shoulder ROM/tightness endpoints.

**Limitations:**

- Paywalled review; exact quantitative estimates were not extracted in this batch.
- Measurement context, not intervention evidence.
- Shoulder-specific endpoint may not generalize to whole-body flexibility.
