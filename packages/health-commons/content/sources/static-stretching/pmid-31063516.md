This source is included for **mechanisms_upper_body_general_primary**.

**Findings:** Two minutes did not clearly reduce stiffness, five minutes reduced stiffness, and both durations increased blood-flow measures.

**Why it matters:** The study adds duration and imaging context, but the adolescent athlete sample is a population mismatch for general adult home guidance.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- rectus femoris stiffness
- blood flow
- stretch duration

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not generalize adolescent athlete imaging findings into adult at-home dose rules without caveats.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
