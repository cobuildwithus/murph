This source is included for **home_field_test_measurement**.

**Findings:** Intra-rater ICCs were reported as 0.97 to 0.98; inter-rater ICCs were 0.97 for tibial angle and 0.99 for toe-to-wall distance.

**Why it matters:** It gives strong repeatability support for a low-equipment ankle dorsiflexion endpoint when setup and rater instructions are controlled.

**Potential experiment signals:** Toe-to-wall distance, Knee-to-wall distance, Tibial inclination angle, Average of repeated trials.

**Protocol takeaway:** A wall-lunge ankle dorsiflexion test is plausible as a home endpoint, but self-measurement instructions must be standardized.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Healthy subjects assessed by four raters.
- **Comparator/control:** Repeated measurements across raters and methods.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Same measurement session with repeated trials; follow-up not extracted.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse events extracted.
- **Limitations:** Small healthy sample.; Clinician-rater context may not match unsupervised home self-measurement.
- **Population mismatch:** Healthy measurement sample; not an intervention cohort.
