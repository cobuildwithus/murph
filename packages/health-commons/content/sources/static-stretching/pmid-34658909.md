This source is included for **adjacent_variants_recovery_modalities**.

**Findings:** Thirteen studies with 35 effect sizes and 304 participants were assessed. Overall performance effects did not significantly differ between foam rolling and stretching (ES = -0.071, p = 0.08), but subgroup analyses favored foam rolling over static stretching (ES = -0.118, p = 0.02) and in some muscles, durations, vibration-foam-rolling conditions, and strength tasks.

**Why it matters:** Performance evidence should not be converted into a flexibility promise; foam rolling and stretching have different performance contexts.

**Potential experiment signals:** strength, jump height, sprint/speed, acute performance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not imply that a flexibility protocol improves strength or jump performance based on this comparator review.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
