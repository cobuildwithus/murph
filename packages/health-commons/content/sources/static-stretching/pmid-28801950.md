This source is included for **mechanisms_syntheses**.

**Findings:** source_artifact:pmid-28801950 selected 26 longitudinal studies and reported small effects for maximum tolerated passive torque, with trivial effects for resistance to stretch, muscle architecture, muscle stiffness, and tendon stiffness.

**Why it matters:** Balances structural explanations by reporting small tolerance effects and largely trivial mechanical-property changes in short chronic stretching programs.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- Track discomfort tolerance and ROM together.
- Avoid treating short-program ROM change as evidence of tendon or muscle remodeling.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mechanism context: short chronic stretching may increase tolerance more than it changes muscle-tendon structure.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness and mismatch:** same_mechanism. Includes mixed stretching modalities and laboratory measurements, not an unsupervised at-home static-only protocol.

**Safety/adverse events:** No adverse-event data were extracted from the review summary.

**Limitations:**
- Mixed modalities and protocols.
- Review-level synthesis with high heterogeneity.
- No direct at-home safety or adherence evidence extracted.
