This source is included for **adjacent_variants_recovery_modalities**.

**Findings:** The review reported 17 effect sizes from 12 studies. Combined foam rolling plus stretching had no significant ROM difference versus stretching alone (ES = 0.032) or foam rolling alone (ES = -0.225), while it differed versus control (ES = -0.332). Performance did not significantly differ versus stretching alone (ES = -0.029).

**Why it matters:** Directly tests whether combining foam rolling with stretching adds value; it belongs on a stacked-intervention boundary, not the static-only protocol page.

**Potential experiment signals:** range of motion, physical performance, combined-modality adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use combined foam-rolling-plus-stretching findings as static-stretching-only evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
