This source is included for **home_low_burden_trials**.

**Findings:** A small fibromyalgia feasibility study found excellent adherence and some symptom/QoL improvements, but no significant flexibility or pain-sensitivity gains. Available extraction notes report 91% adherence and improvements in quality of life, physical functioning, fatigue, and pain, but no significant flexibility or pain-sensitivity improvements.

**Why it matters:** It provides practical home-adherence information while preserving negative/mixed endpoint findings.

**Potential experiment signals:** adherence, quality of life, fatigue, pain, flexibility, safety.

**Protocol takeaway:** Adherence can be high with support, but symptom improvements in uncontrolled clinical studies do not prove flexibility gains.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Source kind:** journal_article.

- **Participant count:** 12.

- **Intervention or exposure:** Six-week home-based static stretching program supported by an mHealth application and weekly follow-up..
- **Comparator or control:** Pre/post feasibility design without a control group..
- **Duration or follow-up:** Six weeks.
- **Endpoints:** feasibility, acceptability, adherence, quality of life, physical function, fatigue, pain, flexibility, pain sensitivity.
