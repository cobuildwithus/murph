This source is included for **safety_guidelines_special_populations**.

**Findings:** The Delphi panel supports stretching for acute and chronic ROM improvement, while marking recovery, posture, overall injury prevention, and high-dose hypertrophy/strength claims as limited or not the preferred use case.

**Why it matters:** Directly useful for dose framing while preventing claims that stretching reliably improves recovery, posture, injury risk, or hypertrophy.

**Potential experiment signals:** range of motion, muscle stiffness, injury prevention, recovery, posture, strength/hypertrophy, vascular health.

**Safety notes:** Panel-level caution that stretching may not reduce overall injury risk and that injury-type tradeoffs remain uncertain.

**Limitations:** Expert consensus; evidence quality varies by topic and it should not replace direct RCT evidence for specific populations.

**Population mismatch:** Broad stretching consensus, not exclusively unsupervised home static stretching in healthy adults.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for modern practical framing: stretching is mainly a ROM tool; avoid promising broad recovery, posture, injury-prevention, or muscle-growth benefits.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
