This source is included for **mechanisms_upper_body_general_primary**.

**Findings:** All stretching modalities increased ROM and reduced passive resistance/stiffness, while PNF produced an additional decrease in plantar-flexor maximal voluntary contraction.

**Why it matters:** The static-stretch arm is relevant, but the multimodal acute design makes it better as adjacent mechanism context than a direct protocol claim.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- dorsiflexion ROM
- passive resistive torque
- muscle stiffness
- muscle-tendon stiffness
- maximal voluntary contraction

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context that acute static stretching can change ROM and stiffness, while noting that mixed stretching modes and acute performance changes are not direct home-protocol evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
