**Findings:** A published RCT protocol plans a low-dose home static-stretch intervention for fibromyalgia, but it does not report outcomes. Protocol paper only; no efficacy result is available from this source.

**Why it matters:** It is useful for burden and endpoint design while preventing protocol-only evidence from becoming efficacy evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** adherence, FIQ-R symptoms, pain sensitivity, ROM, quality of life, safety.

**Protocol takeaway:** A six-minute daily home stretching dose is being formally tested in fibromyalgia, but outcome claims must wait for results.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
