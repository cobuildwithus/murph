This source is included for **safety_guidelines_special_populations**.

**Findings:** The guideline supports physical activity during pregnancy for those without contraindications and provides contraindication and warning-sign framing.

**Why it matters:** Pregnancy is a common special population in which exercise is often appropriate but requires contraindication and warning-sign screening.

**Potential experiment signals:** contraindications, warning signs, maternal outcomes, fetal/neonatal outcomes, activity safety.

**Safety notes:** Pregnant users should follow obstetric guidance and stop/seek care for warning signs or contraindications; static stretching alone does not override pregnancy precautions.

**Limitations:** Pregnancy physical-activity guideline, not a static-stretching protocol trial.

**Population mismatch:** Pregnant population, not generally healthy nonpregnant adults.

**Protocol takeaway:** Add pregnancy status as an onboarding safety branch and recommend clinician guidance where contraindications or warning signs apply.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
