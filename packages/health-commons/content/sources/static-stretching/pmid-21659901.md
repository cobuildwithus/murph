This source is included for **adverse_event_clinical_population_mismatch**.

**Findings:** The review concluded that performance decrements were most evident with longer static-stretch durations, particularly 60 seconds or more per muscle group, while shorter durations were less consistently detrimental. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-21659901`.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** No injury adverse-event rate was extracted; acute performance decrement is the safety/timing concern.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Pre-performance acute physiology context, not home flexibility training. Limitations: Acute lab/performance outcomes, not chronic home flexibility.; Study heterogeneity in stretch protocols and performance tests..

**Why it matters:** It supports conservative advice around long holds before strength or power tasks.

**Potential experiment signals:** maximal muscle performance, strength, power, duration-dependent effects.

**Protocol takeaway:** Long static holds should be separated from maximal strength/power efforts when performance matters.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. Boundary: Use only for timing and dose cautions near maximal performance.
