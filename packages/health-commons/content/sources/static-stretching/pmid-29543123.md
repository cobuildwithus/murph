This source is included for **adjacent_variants_recovery_modalities**.

**Findings:** Both unilateral static stretching and self-assisted foam rolling significantly increased contralateral hip-flexion passive ROM. Post-ROM was greater for static stretching (73.5 ± 4.7 degrees) than self-assisted foam rolling (70.3 ± 4.5 degrees; p = 0.026) in the accessible abstract-level result.

**Why it matters:** Non-local and contralateral findings are mechanistic/contextual and should not become simple protocol promises.

**Potential experiment signals:** contralateral hip-flexion passive ROM, hamstring strength, non-local response.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mechanism/context only; do not promise contralateral benefits from home static stretching.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
