This source is included for **adjacent_variants_recovery_modalities**.

**Findings:** Forty participants with limited passive hip-flexion ROM were assigned to static stretching, foam rolling, combined foam rolling plus static stretching, or control. The combined foam-rolling-plus-static-stretching condition produced greater passive hip-flexion ROM change than static stretching alone, foam rolling alone, or control in the accessible abstract-level result.

**Why it matters:** A frequently cited stacked-modality study: foam rolling plus static stretching may behave differently from static stretching alone and should not be collapsed into the at-home static-stretching dose claim.

**Potential experiment signals:** passive hip-flexion ROM, hamstring flexibility, stacked-intervention adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only to mark foam rolling plus static stretching as a sibling or stacked-intervention variant, not as evidence that static stretching alone has the same effect.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
