## Findings
- Thirty-four volunteers with tight hamstrings were randomized to control, 9 × 10-second stretches, or 3 × 30-second stretches.
- Both stretching groups completed 90 seconds total per session, 6 days per week for 6 weeks.
- Both stretch groups improved ROM versus control by about 11.6° to 13.4°, with no significant difference between the two repetition schemes.

## Why it matters
Clarifies that, within a 90-second total dose, distributing the dose into more short holds versus fewer longer holds may not materially change ROM gains.
