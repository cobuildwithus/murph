This source is included for **ankle_calf_dorsiflexion**.

**Findings:** In 101 healthy adults, daily standing wall stretches for 6 weeks at 30-second, 1-minute, or 2-minute assigned durations did not produce a treatment effect on active ankle dorsiflexion ROM.

**Why it matters:** This is a direct negative boundary for active dorsiflexion ROM, even though the modality and home-feasible setup match the protocol well.

**Potential experiment signals:** Active ankle dorsiflexion ROM; Daily stretch dose.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not present daily calf wall stretching as guaranteed to improve active ankle dorsiflexion; use this source as a null-result boundary.

**Limitations:** Endpoint was active dorsiflexion ROM, which may differ from passive ROM or knee-to-wall distance. Healthy adults rather than restricted-ROM participants. Details on adherence and exact home supervision were not extracted in this batch.

**Population mismatch:** Generally relevant healthy-adult sample, but not specifically selected for low ankle dorsiflexion.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
