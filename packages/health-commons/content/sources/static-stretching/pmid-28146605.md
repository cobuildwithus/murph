This source is included for **safety_guidelines_special_populations**.

**Findings:** The Cochrane review included 49 randomized trials with 2135 participants and found no clinically important short-term effects of stretch on joint mobility in contracture populations.

**Why it matters:** Important because high-dose clinical stretch for contractures should not be conflated with a light home flexibility routine.

**Potential experiment signals:** joint mobility, pain, spasticity, activity limitation, quality of life, adverse events.

**Safety notes:** Reported adverse events included skin breakdown, pain, numbness, venous thrombosis, wound infection, and other events; event risk could not be confidently calculated.

**Limitations:** Clinical contracture populations, often high-dose or device-assisted stretch; not general healthy at-home static stretching.

**Population mismatch:** Large population and modality mismatch.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a context-only warning against overgeneralizing clinical contracture stretch findings to healthy flexibility protocols.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
