This source is included for **ankle_calf_dorsiflexion**.

**Findings:** In 7 patients with diabetes mellitus, four 5-minute stretch-board bouts increased ankle dorsiflexion ROM with knee extended or bent and reduced peak pressure/pressure-time integral during gait, especially under the forefoot.

**Why it matters:** This is adjacent clinical/device evidence that contrasts with the longer diabetes/equinus RCT and should not be generalized to a healthy home protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** Ankle dorsiflexion ROM; Forefoot plantar pressure; Pressure-time integral; Foot-flat duration.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as adjacent mechanistic context; do not claim diabetic foot-ulcer prevention or treatment from the base static-stretching protocol.

**Limitations:** Only 7 patients. Immediate single-session response. Clinical diabetes population and stretch-board setup. No long-term randomized comparator extracted.

**Population mismatch:** Patients with diabetes mellitus; adjacent clinical and device-specific context.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
