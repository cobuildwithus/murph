This source is included for **home_field_test_measurement**.

**Findings:** Intra- and inter-rater ICCs were reported as at least 0.97; smallest real difference was 13.8 mm. A bias was reported for one rater (p=0.01).

**Why it matters:** It informs change-threshold thinking for ankle lunge distance, but clinical injury values should not be directly applied to healthy home users.

**Potential experiment signals:** Ankle lunge distance, Smallest real difference, Rater consistency.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use this as adjacent MDC/SRD context only; do not convert 13.8 mm into a universal home success threshold.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Patients after ankle fracture, tested in the second week after plaster removal.
- **Comparator/control:** Three measurements by two raters and repeated reliability assessment.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Early post-fracture testing context; repeat schedule not fully extracted.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse events extracted.
- **Limitations:** Very small post-fracture clinical sample.; Clinical impairment context differs from healthy home stretch logging.
- **Population mismatch:** Post-fracture population mismatch.
