**Findings:** Dorsiflexion ROM increased after a 20-second stretch, while strength and shear elastic modulus did not show significant changes.

**Why it matters:** This is useful as a low-dose boundary and null mechanism result.

- dorsiflexion ROM

- shear elastic modulus
- post-stretch time course

**Protocol takeaway:** A very short hold may change ROM briefly without measurable stiffness or strength changes.
