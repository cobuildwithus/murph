This source is included for **adverse_event_clinical_population_mismatch**.

**Findings:** Fifteen dancers had acute hamstring strains during slow-speed stretching; semimembranosus involvement was common and injuries were near the ischial tuberosity. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-17567821`.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** Muscle strain event during stretching; case series cannot estimate frequency in general stretchers.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Dance-related stretching injury series, not general flexibility training. Limitations: Elite/professional dancers and extreme positions differ from ordinary home routines.; No control group and no incidence denominator..

**Why it matters:** It supports instructions not to force hamstring end range and to stop sudden pain promptly.

**Potential experiment signals:** hamstring strain location, MRI findings, recovery characteristics, recurrence or long-term symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Sudden sharp posterior-thigh pain during a stretch should stop the session and prompt reassessment.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`. Boundary: Use for caution against aggressive end-range hamstring stretching and for stop rules with sudden posterior-thigh pain.
