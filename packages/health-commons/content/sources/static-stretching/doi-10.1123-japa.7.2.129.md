This source is included for **home_field_test_measurement**.

**Findings:** Developed and validated practical functional-fitness field tests for community-residing older adults; exact psychometric estimates were not recovered in this extraction.

**Why it matters:** It supports chair sit-and-reach and back scratch as low-equipment flexibility endpoints that can be adapted for home tracking when setup is standardized.

**Potential experiment signals:** Chair sit-and-reach distance, Back scratch distance, Functional test adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Useful for selecting accessible chair-based or shoulder-reach field tests, especially when floor sit-and-reach is impractical.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
