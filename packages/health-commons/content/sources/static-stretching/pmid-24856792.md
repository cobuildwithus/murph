## Findings
- Forty-nine volunteers were assigned to static stretching or control.
- Dorsiflexion ROM increased from about 30.9° to 36.3° in the stretching group.
- Measured muscle and tendon structural/function variables were reported as unaltered, leading the authors to interpret the ROM gain as likely stretch-tolerance/nociceptive adaptation.

## Why it matters
