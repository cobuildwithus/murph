This source is included for **adjacent_variants_recovery_modalities**.

**Findings:** Seventeen papers were included. Dynamic and ballistic stretching together produced a small acute flexibility increase (effect size = 0.372, 95% CI 0.187 to 0.557, p < 0.001, I² = 27%). Subgroup analyses did not find significant differences by age, sex, stretching method, stretched muscle, or flexibility outcome.

**Why it matters:** Prevents importing dynamic or ballistic stretching findings into a static-stretching protocol page.

**Potential experiment signals:** acute flexibility, single-joint ROM, straight-leg raise, sit-and-reach.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use dynamic/ballistic acute effects as direct evidence for static stretching.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
