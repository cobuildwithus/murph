This source is included for **ankle_calf_dorsiflexion**.

**Findings:** In 44 healthy college-student volunteers, heat plus prolonged static stretching produced larger ankle dorsiflexion ROM gains than identical stretching without heat, including after a 6-day rest window.

**Why it matters:** This is an adjacent adjunct-modality source: it supports separating heat/device-assisted stretching from simple at-home static stretching.

**Potential experiment signals:** Ankle dorsiflexion ROM; Adjunct diathermy; Retention after 6 days.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not attribute heat-plus-stretch effects to plain static stretching; keep adjunct modalities separate.

**Limitations:** Adjunct pulsed shortwave diathermy is not a simple at-home static-stretching protocol. College-student sample. Long-duration pulley/weight setup differs from ordinary home stretches.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy college students with device-assisted/prolonged stretching context.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
