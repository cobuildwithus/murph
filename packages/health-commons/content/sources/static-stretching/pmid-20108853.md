This source is included for **ankle_calf_dorsiflexion**.

**Findings:** In 16 volunteers with less than 5 degrees passive ankle dorsiflexion, a 3-week gastrocnemius stretching program improved passive dorsiflexion but did not show significant group-by-time gait angle or gastrocnemius EMG effects.

**Why it matters:** This source separates ROM improvement from gait and muscle-activity outcomes, preventing overextended protocol claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** Passive ankle dorsiflexion; Gait ankle/knee kinematics; Gastrocnemius EMG.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use ankle dorsiflexion ROM as the primary calf signal; gait or EMG changes are not established by this source.

**Limitations:** Small sample. Participants selected for less than 5 degrees passive dorsiflexion. Gait and EMG endpoints may require larger samples or different doses.

**Population mismatch:** Restricted-ROM volunteers, not a general flexibility population.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
