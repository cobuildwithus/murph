This source is included for **mechanisms_syntheses**.

**Findings:** source_artifact:pmid-39066912 included 49 studies with 1304 participants; extracted results showed small chronic muscle-stiffness effects and passive peak-torque changes at end ROM, while tendon, MTU, and fixed-angle outcomes were less supportive or null.

**Why it matters:** Provides a methodological caution against over-reading stiffness claims from prior reviews and distinguishes muscle, tendon, MTU, fixed-angle, and end-ROM outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- Choose stiffness endpoint carefully: muscle, tendon, MTU, fixed-angle, and end-ROM outcomes are not interchangeable.
- Self-stretching versus supervised stretching should be logged if possible.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only critical appraisal: stiffness effects depend on endpoint, analysis model, supervision, and duration.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness and mismatch:** same_mechanism. Mostly lower-extremity controlled studies; not an at-home adherence or safety dataset.

**Safety/adverse events:** No adverse-event findings were extracted from this mechanism-focused reanalysis.

**Limitations:**
- Methodological quality was fair in extracted details.
- Blinding was rare in included studies.
- Most studies were lower-extremity and not all involved self-stretching.
