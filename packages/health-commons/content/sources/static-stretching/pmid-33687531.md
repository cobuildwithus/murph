This source is included for **mechanisms_upper_body_general_primary**.

**Findings:** The stretch-training group increased ROM and passive resistive torque and reduced muscle-tendon and muscle stiffness, with no architecture or maximum-force changes reported.

**Why it matters:** This is direct chronic static-stretching evidence, but the high weekly dose limits translation to a simple home protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- dorsiflexion ROM
- passive resistive torque
- muscle-tendon complex stiffness
- muscle stiffness
- maximum force

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as chronic training context, not as proof that low-dose home routines produce the same mechanical adaptations.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
