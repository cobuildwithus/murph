This source is included for **adjacent_variants_recovery_modalities**.

**Findings:** Accessible abstract snippets report comparable acute and training-phase ROM increases across intervention groups: a 4.0% acute increase at week 6 for all three groups, an 8.4% ROM increase from week 3 to week 7, and an 18.3% increase from week 1 baseline to week 7 for all three groups.

**Why it matters:** A direct comparator against foam rolling at the ankle; useful for separating foam rolling from the static-stretching protocol and for avoiding ankle-specific overgeneralization.

**Potential experiment signals:** ankle dorsiflexion ROM, weight-bearing lunge distance, acute versus training response.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as comparator context only; ankle-specific foam-rolling comparisons should not drive general at-home static-stretching claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
