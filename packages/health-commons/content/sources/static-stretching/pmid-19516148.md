This source is included for **safety_guidelines_special_populations**.

**Findings:** ACSM emphasizes regular physical activity for older adults and adapting participation to abilities and conditions rather than inactivity.

**Why it matters:** Older adults may benefit from movement but may need supported/seated variations, lower intensity, and balance-risk precautions.

**Potential experiment signals:** physical activity participation, flexibility, balance, chronic conditions, fall-risk adaptation.

**Safety notes:** Older adults with chronic conditions, frailty, or balance limitations should avoid unsupported positions that increase fall risk.

**Limitations:** Broad physical-activity guideline; not a direct home static-stretching trial.

**Population mismatch:** Older-adult guideline; directness depends on individual health and functional status.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use lower-burden, supported options and fall-risk screening for older adults.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
