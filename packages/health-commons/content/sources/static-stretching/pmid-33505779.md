This source is included for **home_field_test_measurement**.

**Findings:** Distance intra-rater ICCs were reported as 0.95 for clinician, 0.98 for clinician in training, and 0.89 for parent/carer. Parent-clinician concurrent validity ICC was 0.899; inter-rater distance ICC was 0.948. SEM ranged about 0.70 to 2.05 and MDC about 1.90 to 5.70.

**Why it matters:** It supports the feasibility of supported home-style WBLT measurement but is population-mismatched for adult static stretching.

**Potential experiment signals:** Weight-bearing lunge distance, Parent/carer measurement, MDC and SEM.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent support for home-assisted WBLT measurement, not as direct adult endpoint validation.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Children aged 5 to 10 years with congenital talipes equinovarus.
- **Comparator/control:** Inter-rater, intra-rater, and concurrent validity comparisons across raters and methods.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Measurement reliability/validity study; follow-up not extracted.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse events extracted.
- **Limitations:** Pediatric CTEV population differs strongly from general adult home stretchers.; Parent/carer-supported testing is not identical to adult self-testing.
- **Population mismatch:** Pediatric clinical population mismatch.
- **Artifact rights:** open_access; no copyrighted PDF is included in Git by this draft.
