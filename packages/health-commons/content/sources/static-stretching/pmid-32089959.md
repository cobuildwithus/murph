This source is included for **home_field_test_measurement**.

**Findings:** Single-limb reliability ICC was 0.98 with MDC 1.7 degrees; asymmetry reliability ICC was 0.85 with MDC 2.1 degrees. No dominant/non-dominant difference was reported (P=.862).

**Why it matters:** It provides practical MDC context for ankle dorsiflexion angle and left-right asymmetry when using a lunge-style test.

**Potential experiment signals:** Weight-bearing lunge angle, Left-right ankle dorsiflexion asymmetry, Within-session trial repeatability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Ankle lunge angle can be tracked with high within-session reliability, but use consistent technique and avoid overinterpreting small asymmetry changes.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Healthy recreationally active adults; 28 men and 22 women, mean age about 22 years.
- **Comparator/control:** Repeated within-session measurements and limb-asymmetry calculations.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Within-session repeated testing.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse events extracted.
- **Limitations:** Young recreationally active adults.; Within-session reliability does not establish long-term home self-test reliability.
- **Population mismatch:** Healthy measurement study, not stretching intervention.
- **Artifact rights:** open_access; no copyrighted PDF is included in Git by this draft.
