This source is included for **shoulder_thoracic_upper_body**.

**Findings:**
- **Population/dose:** 37 university-level overhead athletes with internal-rotation deficits were randomized to an intervention group or usual-activity control; the intervention was a daily sleeper-stretch program across 8 weeks.
- **Outcomes:** Accessible abstracts report significant increases in internal rotation and horizontal adduction after the stretching program.
- **Limits:** Exact effect sizes were not available in accessible abstracts; sample was small and sport-specific.
- **Safety:** No intervention adverse events were identified in accessible abstracts.

**Why it matters:** It gives a longer-duration overhead-athlete stretching program, supporting week-scale ROM tracking rather than only acute testing.

**Potential experiment signals:** weekly internal-rotation or cross-body reach, adherence to daily shoulder stretch, shoulder symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent support that a daily posterior-shoulder stretching routine can improve internal rotation and horizontal adduction over 8 weeks in athletes with baseline deficit; avoid generalizing magnitude to nonathletes.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

- **Claim-use boundary:** Use as shoulder/GIRD or overhead-athlete context only; do not generalize to users without shoulder ROM restriction or overhead-sport exposure.
