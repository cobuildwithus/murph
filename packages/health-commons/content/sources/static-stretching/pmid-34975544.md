## Findings
- Forty healthy young men were randomized to high-intensity static stretching, low-intensity static stretching, or control.
- Both stretching programs used 3 × 60 seconds, 3 times per week for 4 weeks.
- High-intensity stretching improved ROM by about 40% and reduced muscle stiffness by about 57%; low-intensity stretching improved ROM by about 15% and reduced stiffness by about 24%.
- No changes were reported for strength, drop jump, or architecture in accessible abstract details.

## Why it matters
