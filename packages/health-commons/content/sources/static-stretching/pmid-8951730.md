This source is included for **mechanisms_upper_body_general_primary**.

**Findings:** After stretching, participants could tolerate greater stretch angles, while passive torque/stiffness measures at the same angle were not clearly altered.

**Why it matters:** It is an important cautionary mechanism source for interpreting flexibility gains.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- stretch tolerance
- passive torque
- stiffness
- EMG activity

**Protocol takeaway:** Avoid describing flexibility improvements as simple muscle lengthening; tolerance and perception can be central mechanisms.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
