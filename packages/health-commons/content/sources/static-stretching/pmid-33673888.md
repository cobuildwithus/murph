This source is included for **adverse_event_clinical_population_mismatch**.

**Findings:** The review notes hip flexor injuries are common in certain athletic populations and that treatment is generally conservative, but specific therapy-regimen evidence is sparse and often anecdotal. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-33673888`.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** Safety context for anterior hip pain or suspected hip flexor injury; no stretching adverse-event rate extracted.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Athletic injury-treatment context, not healthy home flexibility. Limitations: Narrative clinical review with sparse regimen-level evidence.; Athletic injury management does not directly support a general flexibility protocol..

**Why it matters:** It prevents turning injury-management practices into universal home stretching claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** hip flexor injury, clinical management, return to sport, evidence limitations.

**Protocol takeaway:** Sharp anterior hip pain or suspected hip flexor strain should pause the protocol and prompt appropriate care.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`. Boundary: Use to advise clinician guidance for suspected hip flexor injury and to avoid stretching through sharp anterior hip pain.
