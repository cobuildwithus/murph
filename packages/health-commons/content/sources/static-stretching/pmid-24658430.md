This source is included for **region_specific_remote_measurement**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries report high intraday reliability for the functional tests, with ICC values reported above 0.8.

**Why it matters:** The at-home protocol may need practical, low-cost field endpoints that work beyond young healthy samples.

**Potential experiment signals:** Functional ability tests, Chair sit-and-reach or related flexibility tests, Back scratch or upper-body flexibility, Intraday reliability, Sensitivity.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use older-adult field-test endpoints cautiously and avoid assuming they match goniometric ROM.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Extraction boundaries:**

- Population: Older women
- Intervention/exposure: Four functional ability tests including flexibility-related field tests
- Comparator/control: Repeated intraday measurements
- Duration/follow-up: Intraday test-retest design
- Adverse events or safety notes: No adverse events were extracted.
- Population mismatch: Older women; may not generalize to young adults, men, or unsupervised home measurement.
- Directness: Directly relevant to field-test endpoint reliability, but not direct intervention efficacy.

**Limitations:**

- Older women only; not a general adult sample.
- Field-test reliability source, not a stretching intervention.
- Exact test list and coefficients should be verified against the full article.
