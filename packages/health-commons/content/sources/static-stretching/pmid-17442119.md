This source is included for **ankle_calf_dorsiflexion**.

**Findings:** Among 92 adults with plantar heel pain, calf stretching for 2 weeks did not show statistically significant benefit for first-step pain, foot pain, foot function, or general foot health versus sham ultrasound alone. Ten adverse events occurred in the stretching group, mostly mild or moderate and short-lived.

**Why it matters:** This adjacent clinical source prevents overgeneralizing ankle ROM evidence into treatment claims for plantar heel pain and preserves safety notes.

**Potential experiment signals:** Pain; Foot function; Adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not present this flexibility protocol as treatment for plantar heel pain; users with pain need clinical guidance.

**Limitations:** Clinical pain population, not healthy flexibility users. Two-week treatment window. Stretching was paired with sham ultrasound context.

**Population mismatch:** Adults with plantar heel pain; not a general at-home flexibility population.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
