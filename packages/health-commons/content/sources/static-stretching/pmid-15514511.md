## Findings
- Accessible snippets describe a 6-week hamstring stretching program with 3 × 30-second stretches, once daily, 5 days per week.
- Knee-extension ROM improved by about 10.1° in the stretching group.
- Passive resistive force and stiffness increased in the newly gained end range, and controls had no significant ROM change in accessible snippets.

## Why it matters
Preserves a mixed mechanism finding: ROM gains do not always mean lower stiffness across the new end range.
