## Findings
- Eighty-one healthy subjects were randomized to static stretching, ballistic stretching, or control for 6 weeks.
- Dorsiflexion ROM increased significantly in stretching groups.
- Static stretching decreased passive resistive torque but did not change Achilles tendon stiffness; ballistic stretching decreased tendon stiffness but not passive torque.

## Why it matters
Shows that different stretching modalities can produce different passive-tissue responses even when ROM changes occur.

## Potential experiment signals
- ankle dorsiflexion ROM
