This source is included for **mechanisms_upper_body_general_primary**.

**Findings:** At 120% ROM intensity, both 1- and 3-minute conditions increased ROM without reducing rectus femoris stiffness; a 3-minute 110% condition increased ROM and reduced stiffness.

**Why it matters:** This source reinforces that intensity can matter more than duration for stiffness, but also that very high intensity may not be optimal.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- knee-flexion ROM
- rectus femoris shear elastic modulus
- stretch intensity
- stretch duration

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not assume longer duration compensates for an unsuitable intensity; keep the protocol within tolerable bounds.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
