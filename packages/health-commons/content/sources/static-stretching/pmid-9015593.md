## Findings
- Twenty-four healthy subjects completed passive stretching, jogging, and combined conditions in random order.
- Passive stretching used five 30-second stretches with rest intervals.
- Stretching and combined conditions increased dorsiflexion ROM more than jogging, while jogging decreased series elastic stiffness more than stretching.

## Why it matters
Shows static/passive stretching may improve ROM without producing the largest stiffness change compared with jogging.
