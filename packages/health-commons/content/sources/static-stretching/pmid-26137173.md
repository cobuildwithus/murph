This source is included for **region_specific_remote_measurement**.

**Findings:** Accessible materials describe the behind-the-back angular method as reliable; exact coefficients were not extracted in this batch.

**Why it matters:** Hand-behind-back is simple for users but anatomically composite; reliability evidence helps bound its use.

**Potential experiment signals:** Shoulder internal rotation behind the back, Angular measurement reliability, Intraobserver reliability, Interobserver reliability.

**Protocol takeaway:** If hand-behind-back is used, label it as shoulder internal-rotation/functional reach and keep scoring consistent.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction boundaries:**

- Population: Participants assessed for shoulder internal rotation behind the back; exact sample not extracted
- Intervention/exposure: Internal rotation behind-the-back angular measurement
- Comparator/control: Intraobserver and interobserver reliability comparisons
- Duration/follow-up: Measurement reliability design; exact interval not extracted
- Adverse events or safety notes: No adverse events were extracted.
- Population mismatch: Shoulder measurement sample; not necessarily home static-stretching users.
- Directness: Measurement context for a shoulder flexibility endpoint.

**Limitations:**

- Exact sample size and coefficients not extracted.
- Shoulder-specific measurement context.
- Behind-the-back tests may be influenced by shoulder, elbow, wrist, and scapular factors.
