This source is included for **ankle_calf_dorsiflexion**.

**Findings:** In 24 healthy adults, passive ankle dorsiflexion ROM increased significantly during each day’s 3-minute stretch, but no significant cumulative gains were reported over 3 days.

**Why it matters:** This is a direct low-duration boundary: immediate session gains may not equal retained multi-day flexibility change.

**Potential experiment signals:** Passive dorsiflexion ROM; Immediate session response; Short-term retention.

**Protocol takeaway:** Immediate calf-stretch ROM changes should be separated from durable adaptation claims.

**Limitations:** Only 3 consecutive days. Healthy young adults. Comparator details were not fully extracted from available records.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy young adults; acute/short protocol rather than multi-week home plan.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
