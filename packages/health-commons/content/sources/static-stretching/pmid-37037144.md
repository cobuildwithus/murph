This source is included for **hip_flexor_hip_extension**.

**Findings:**
- The review screened 2,581 records and included eight RCTs with 508 patients with low back pain.
- Hip stretching was reported to improve short-term pain (MD -0.55, 95% CI -1.02 to -0.08) and disability (SMD -1.03, 95% CI -1.82 to -0.25), with very low certainty.
- No medium- or long-term benefits were found in the available evidence.
- Certainty was downgraded for risk of bias, heterogeneity, and imprecision.

**Intervention/exposure:** Hip stretching and hip strengthening interventions in low-back-pain RCTs.

**Comparator/control:** Specific low-back interventions/comparator conditions in included RCTs.

**Duration/follow-up:** Short-, medium-, and long-term review windows.

**Effect estimates or direction:** Very-low-certainty short-term hip-stretching benefit for pain (MD -0.55, 95% CI -1.02 to -0.08) and disability (SMD -1.03, 95% CI -1.82 to -0.25); no medium/long-term benefits.

**Adverse events/safety notes:** No protocol-specific adverse-event synthesis was extracted for at-home static stretching.

**Why it matters:** It summarizes clinical hip-intervention evidence but should not be transformed into direct claims for healthy at-home static stretching.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- Low-back pain
- Disability
- Certainty of evidence
- Short-term versus medium/long-term effects

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent clinical context only: hip stretching may have short-term pain/disability signals in low-back-pain trials, but certainty is very low and longer-term effects were not found.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `adjacent_variant`. Context-only; do not use for direct flexibility or general-pain claims.

**Population mismatch:** Low-back-pain patients across included RCTs; not direct to general at-home flexibility training.

**Limitations:**
- Very low certainty for hip-stretching estimates.
- Low-back-pain clinical population, not healthy flexibility users.
- Review included broader hip interventions, not only at-home static stretching.
- Medium- and long-term benefits were not found.
