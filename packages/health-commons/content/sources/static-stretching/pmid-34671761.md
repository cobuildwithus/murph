This source is included for **safety_guidelines_special_populations**.

**Findings:** The review assembled a consensus exercise prescription from recommendations of 12 professional societies for healthy pregnant women without exercise contraindications.

**Why it matters:** Useful for pregnancy onboarding and for noting ambiguity/inconsistency in professional exercise recommendations.

**Potential experiment signals:** FITT prescription, exercise safety ambiguity, clinical guidance, pregnancy recommendations, flexibility-exercise guidance.

**Safety notes:** Pregnancy exercise recommendations apply to healthy pregnant women without contraindications; contraindication and stop-sign checks remain necessary.

**Limitations:** Review of recommendations, not a trial of stretching; healthy pregnancy only.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy pregnant women, not general adult flexibility users.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as secondary support for pregnancy-specific onboarding and FITT-style guardrails, not for static-stretching efficacy.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
