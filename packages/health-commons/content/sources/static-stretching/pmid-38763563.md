This source is included for **region_specific_remote_measurement**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries report negligible-to-moderate correlations and poor discrimination of joint mobility levels; only shoulder angles differed between some FMS score groups.

**Why it matters:** This preserves a negative validity finding that prevents overclaiming from movement screens.

**Potential experiment signals:** FMS deep squat, Convergent validity, Joint mobility discrimination, Shoulder angle, Hip mobility.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use FMS deep squat score as evidence of flexibility gains in this protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction boundaries:**

- Population: Subjects assessed with FMS deep squat and 3D motion analysis
- Intervention/exposure: Functional Movement Screen deep squat test
- Comparator/control: Joint mobility levels and 3D Vicon kinematic measures
- Duration/follow-up: Cross-sectional validity/discrimination design
- Adverse events or safety notes: No adverse events were extracted.
- Population mismatch: Movement-screen sample, not home static-stretching participants.
- Directness: Adjacent movement-screen validity boundary.

**Limitations:**

- Deep squat FMS is composite and not a pure flexibility endpoint.
- Permission-required article; use exact statistics only after full text verification.
- Cross-sectional validity, not intervention evidence.
