This source is included for **mechanisms_syntheses**.

**Findings:** source_artifact:pmid-31442850 included six RCTs with 263 adults and reported that kinesiotaping and PNF showed benefits on pectoralis minor measures, while static stretching alone showed no effect on pectoralis minor length in the extracted abstract.

**Why it matters:** Preserves a null/mixed upper-body finding so the protocol does not imply every static stretch improves every target muscle length endpoint.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- Upper-body stretch outcomes may need target-specific measurement.
- Do not infer pectoralis minor length change from general ROM improvements.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent-variant caution: static stretching alone showed no extracted effect on pectoralis minor length in this review, while other modalities performed differently.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness and mismatch:** same_mechanism. Pectoralis minor and shoulder-specific endpoint; includes symptomatic shoulders and non-stretching comparators.

**Safety/adverse events:** No adverse-event findings were extracted from the accessible abstract.

**Limitations:**
- Small evidence base of six RCTs.
- Shoulder/pectoralis minor endpoint is adjacent to general at-home flexibility.
- Paywalled source; extraction based on accessible abstract details.
