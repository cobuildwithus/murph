**Findings:** Adding pectoralis minor stretching did not provide significant short-term clinical benefit for shoulder function or pain reduction over an exercise program alone. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-31472414`.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse events were extracted.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Clinical subacromial pain treatment context. Limitations: Clinical shoulder-pain population and multimodal exercise program are not direct home flexibility evidence.; Participant count and detailed dosing were not extracted in this batch..

**Why it matters:** It guards against broad pain-treatment claims for shoulder stretching.

**Potential experiment signals:** shoulder function, pain reduction, clinical benefit.

**Protocol takeaway:** Shoulder pain should trigger clinical caution rather than protocol claims of treatment efficacy.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`. Boundary: Use to avoid claiming pectoralis stretching treats shoulder pain in a general home protocol.
