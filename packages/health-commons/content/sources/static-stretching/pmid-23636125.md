This source is included for **safety_guidelines_special_populations**.

**Findings:** The guideline addresses medical screening, differential evaluation, diagnosis of tissue irritability, and planning of care for adhesive capsulitis.

**Why it matters:** Helps prevent users with painful frozen-shoulder-like restrictions from being treated as routine flexibility users.

**Potential experiment signals:** medical screening, differential diagnosis, shoulder pain, range of motion, tissue irritability, function.

**Safety notes:** Painful, progressive, or function-limiting shoulder restriction should be treated as a clinical condition, not a cue to push deeper static stretching.

**Limitations:** Clinical practice guideline; not a healthy-adult at-home stretching efficacy source.

**Population mismatch:** Shoulder pathology population, not asymptomatic flexibility users.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for shoulder pain/mobility-deficit referral boundaries; do not use for general static-stretch dose claims.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
