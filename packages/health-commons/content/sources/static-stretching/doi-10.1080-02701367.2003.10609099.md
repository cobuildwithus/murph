This source is included for **home_field_test_measurement**.

**Findings:** The study evaluated validity of classic and modified sit-and-reach tests in middle-aged to older adults; exact coefficients were not recovered in this batch extraction.

**Why it matters:** It anchors adult sit-and-reach measurement directness for a home flexibility protocol, while reminding editors not to overstate lumbar or hamstring validity without coefficients.

**Potential experiment signals:** Sit-and-reach centimeters, Modified sit-and-reach centimeters, Consistency of foot/box setup.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as support for considering sit-and-reach variants as field endpoints, not as a numeric effect-size source in this extraction.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

- **Comparator/control:** Criterion flexibility measures; exact criterion details not fully extracted from accessible metadata.
