This source is included for **region_specific_remote_measurement**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries report good pooled interrater reliability around ICC 0.843 and intrarater reliability around ICC 0.869.

**Why it matters:** This helps prevent overclaiming from FMS-type movement screens in a flexibility protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** Functional Movement Screen total and component scoring, Interrater reliability, Intrarater reliability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not present FMS total score as a primary flexibility outcome for this protocol; use it only as adjacent context if needed.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction boundaries:**

- Population: Studies of Functional Movement Screen scoring reliability
- Intervention/exposure: Functional Movement Screen scoring
- Comparator/control: Interrater and intrarater scoring comparisons
- Duration/follow-up: Systematic review; original-study retest intervals varied
- Adverse events or safety notes: No adverse events were extracted.
- Population mismatch: Movement-screen reliability populations; not at-home static-stretching participants.
- Directness: Adjacent movement-screen measurement context only.

**Limitations:**

- FMS scoring is a composite movement screen, not a pure flexibility endpoint.
- Included studies may differ in rater training and scoring mode.
- Does not validate FMS as a stretching outcome or causal marker.
