**Findings:** An eight-week calf-stretching study in older women reported improvements in passive-elastic properties and function-related outcomes in available extraction notes. Available extraction notes suggest improved calf passive-elastic properties and function-related outcomes after the stretching program; exact effect estimates were not extracted.

**Why it matters:** It adds lower-leg and older-adult context, but should not overdrive a general at-home protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** ankle dorsiflexion, calf flexibility, function.

**Protocol takeaway:** Calf flexibility and function can be observed, but older-adult calf data should stay subgroup/contextual.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
