This source is included for **home_field_test_measurement**.

**Findings:** SRT reproducibility CV was 8.74% with ICC 0.92; TT CV was 9.86% with ICC 0.89. Criterion association with passive straight-leg raise was R²=0.63 for SRT and R²=0.49 for TT; SRT and TT cutoffs were not interchangeable.

**Why it matters:** It gives direct support for home-usable reach tests and a clear boundary: toe-touch and sit-and-reach values should be tracked separately.

**Potential experiment signals:** Sit-and-reach centimeters, Toe-touch/finger-floor distance, Passive straight-leg raise angle, 4-week retest variability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Track sit-and-reach and toe-touch as separate endpoints; use within-person change, not borrowed cutoffs.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Recreationally active young adults; 156 men and 87 women.
- **Comparator/control:** Passive straight-leg raise criterion test and 4-week retest.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Two test sessions separated by 4 weeks.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse events extracted.
- **Limitations:** Young recreationally active adults only.; The authors warn against transferring sit-and-reach cutoffs to toe-touch testing.
- **Population mismatch:** Measurement study, not intervention efficacy.
