This source is included for **mechanisms_upper_body_general_primary**.

**Findings:** The two stiffness measurement approaches were not significantly correlated, and subjective stiffness ratings did not correspond clearly to objective stiffness symptoms.

**Why it matters:** This source warns that upper-body stiffness endpoints may be method-dependent and subjective reports may not map cleanly to imaging measures.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- neck stiffness
- shoulder stiffness
- shear-wave elastography
- muscle hardness meter
- subjective stiffness

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as measurement context only; it does not test stretching efficacy.
