This source is included for **home_low_burden_trials**.

**Findings:** In older adults, longer static hamstring holds were reported to produce larger ROM gains than shorter holds. Available extraction notes report larger ROM gain rates for 60-second holds than shorter holds in older adults, with residual gains at follow-up; full effect details require full-text verification.

**Why it matters:** It preserves a subgroup signal that older adults may need or benefit from longer hold times, while keeping that inference bounded.

**Potential experiment signals:** hamstring ROM, hold-time tolerability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep older-adult or very tight-user dose notes separate from the default low-burden adult protocol.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
