This source is included for **adjacent_variants_recovery_modalities**.

**Findings:** Five randomized studies were included. One found greater improvement with PNF; the remaining four found no significant difference between PNF and static stretching for muscle extensibility, active or passive knee-extension angle, or hip ROM.

**Why it matters:** Shows why PNF should remain a sibling modality rather than being merged into simple at-home static stretching claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** hip-flexion ROM, knee-extension angle, hamstring extensibility.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not treat PNF findings as direct evidence for static-only at-home stretching; use as comparator context.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
