This source is included for **home_low_burden_trials**.

**Findings:** A recent home-based older-adult trial supports feasibility and flexibility improvements but has mixed attribution because several training conditions improved outcomes. Available extraction notes report improved flexibility in stretching-related groups and improved strength/function outcomes across groups; authors cautioned that some effects may reflect learning or overall activity rather than a specific training effect.

**Why it matters:** It is highly relevant for home burden and older adults, while showing why mechanism and comparator boundaries matter.

**Potential experiment signals:** hamstring flexibility, calf flexibility, adherence, physical function.

**Protocol takeaway:** Home stretching can fit older adults, but functional and strength outcomes should be framed cautiously.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
