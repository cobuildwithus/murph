This source is included for **mechanisms_upper_body_general_primary**.

**Findings:** The resistance session and 1-minute post-exercise static stretch did not significantly affect pectoralis major stiffness; stiffness decreased after rest on both sides.

**Why it matters:** This preserves a null upper-body stiffness result and prevents overstating brief post-exercise stretching effects.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- pectoralis major shear modulus
- bench press context
- post-stretch rest

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not claim that a 1-minute pectoralis stretch after resistance exercise reliably reduces pectoralis stiffness.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
