This source is included for **home_low_burden_trials**.

**Findings:** Hamstring ROM gains appeared to decline when stretching stopped and return when stretching resumed. Knee ROM increased during the initial stretching phase, declined after cessation, and increased again after resumption; the control limb did not show comparable change in the available abstract-level extraction.

**Why it matters:** It is useful for setting expectations that flexibility is a maintained practice rather than a permanent adaptation after one block.

**Potential experiment signals:** hamstring ROM, adherence continuity, maintenance after stopping.

**Protocol takeaway:** Plan continued low-burden practice or periodic maintenance; do not imply gains are permanent after stopping.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
