This source is included for **adjacent_variants_recovery_modalities**.

**Findings:** Accessible snippets describe a 6-week, four-times-per-week program with passive static stretching (n = 10), contract-relax PNF (n = 9), and control (n = 9), measuring ROM by goniometer and drop jump by contact mat. Exact effect estimates were not retrieved.

**Why it matters:** Separates chronic PNF comparator evidence from static-stretch-only claims and reminds the protocol not to promise jump-performance gains.

**Potential experiment signals:** range of motion, drop-jump performance, training adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as boundary and performance-caveat evidence; do not claim jump-performance effects from a home flexibility protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
