This source is included for **mechanisms_upper_body_general_primary**.

**Findings:** Both conditions improved ROM and reduced shear modulus, while the high-intensity short-duration condition showed greater post-ROM and relative stiffness change.

**Why it matters:** This source refines dose guidance by showing that intensity and duration interact.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- stretch intensity
- stretch duration
- dorsiflexion ROM
- passive torque
- shear modulus

**Protocol takeaway:** Dose guidance should describe a tolerable stretch sensation and duration together rather than treating time alone as the dose.
