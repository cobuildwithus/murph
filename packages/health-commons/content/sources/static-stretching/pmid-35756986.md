This source is included for **safety_guidelines_special_populations**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries state that exercise/rehabilitation adverse events in people with EDS were conservatively estimated at less than 1% of participants, and that programs are typically individually tailored.

**Why it matters:** Useful for EDS safety context; not for healthy-adult static-stretching claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** safety/tolerability, adverse events, physical function, rehabilitation outcomes, individualized programs.

**Safety notes:** Low reported adverse-event rate should not be read as permission for unsupervised aggressive stretching; EDS rehabilitation is individualized and clinical.

**Limitations:** Heterogeneous EDS subtypes and interventions; adverse events may be underdetected or inconsistently reported.

**Population mismatch:** EDS rehabilitation literature, not healthy home static stretching.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as condition-specific safety context and to support individualized, clinician-guided modifications.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
