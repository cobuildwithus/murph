This ADA Standards chapter is used for glycemic goal and hypoglycemia context. It is especially important for Murph because glucose is not simply a lower-is-better biomarker. Safe interpretation depends on the person's diagnosis, medications, pregnancy status, comorbidities, and clinician-set goals.
