This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** The guideline addresses lower-extremity PAD across asymptomatic, chronic symptomatic, chronic limb-threatening ischemia, and acute limb ischemia states. For pneumatic compression pants, it is used only to define arterial-disease safety boundaries and referral triggers.

**Why it matters:** PAD is a key contraindication/precaution domain for lower-limb compression. This source keeps arterial disease separate from wellness use.

**Potential experiment signals:** PAD diagnosis, Ischemic rest pain, Nonhealing wound, Acute limb ischemia symptoms, Vascular clinician clearance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Known or suspected PAD/CLTI/acute limb ischemia should trigger avoidance or medical evaluation rather than self-experimentation.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
