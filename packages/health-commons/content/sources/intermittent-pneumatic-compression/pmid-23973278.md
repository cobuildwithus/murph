This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** Prospective registry data for 196 lower-extremity lymphedema patients reported consistent limb-volume reductions and favorable patient-reported outcomes including pain, function, and satisfaction; exact effect sizes were not accessible in this extraction.

**Why it matters:** Relevant lower-limb clinical context but lower causal strength.

**Potential experiment signals:** Limb volume, Pain, Function, Satisfaction, Skin changes.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only observational evidence; do not use for causal protocol claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct unsupervised consumer-recovery evidence.

**Safety and boundary notes:** No adverse-event details were located in accessible extraction.

**Limitations:** Observational registry; do not infer causality.; Medical lymphedema population.; Exact effect sizes/duration not available in accessible extraction..
