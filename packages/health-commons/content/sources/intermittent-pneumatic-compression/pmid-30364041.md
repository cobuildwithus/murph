This source is included for **Prolonged standing/sitting and workday leg swelling sibling variant**.

**Findings:** In 58 healthy workers (116 limbs), volumetry was performed at the start and end of three consecutive days: no stockings, 15-20 mmHg stockings, and 20-30 mmHg stockings. Both pressure ranges reduced occupational edema; 20-30 mmHg produced lower volumetric variation, with a notable seated-posture effect.

**Why it matters:** It helps map pressure-dose context in a sibling occupational-edema variant.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker / lower-limb-volume, dose / stocking-pressure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as pressure comparator context only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
