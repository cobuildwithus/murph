This source is included for **Clinical DVT prophylaxis and hospital boundary**.

**Findings:** Nine studies were included. The indexed abstract reports IPC alone was neither superior nor inferior to chemoprophylaxis in orthopedic and neurosurgical postoperative patients. Safety/adverse-event notes: Safety and bleeding comparisons were not fully extracted; postoperative clinical supervision is required. Limitations: Very short indexed article/abstract and postoperative population; no direct consumer or recovery evidence.

**Why it matters:** It defines the supervised clinical boundary for IPC use and helps prevent over-claiming consumer benefits.

**Potential experiment signals:** Skin irritation or skin breaks; Discomfort/tolerance; Falls or tubing/device hazards; Adherence or wear time; DVT/PE only as clinician-managed safety context.

**Protocol takeaway:** When post-op prophylaxis is relevant, defer to the clinical plan.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
