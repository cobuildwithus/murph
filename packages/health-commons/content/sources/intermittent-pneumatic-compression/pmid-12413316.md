This source is included for **Dose, hemodynamics, and wearable-relevant mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Accessible summary reports slight blood-flow velocity decreases during compression and increases upon release in healthy and patient groups.

**Why it matters:** It cautions that flow may vary by phase of compression rather than simply increasing at all times.

**Potential experiment signals:** Arterial inflow, blood flow velocity during compression and release.

**Protocol takeaway:** Time-stamp flow/oxygenation measurements relative to compression cycle where possible.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were extracted from accessible materials unless noted above. Key limitations: Sample count not recovered.; Older study.; Limited effect detail available.; No user-centered recovery endpoint..
