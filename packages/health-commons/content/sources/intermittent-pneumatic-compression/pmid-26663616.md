This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** The guideline update describes IPC as a clinical option for venous ulcer patients with or without compression dressings, particularly when adequate compression systems cannot or will not be used.

**Why it matters:** This guideline reinforces that venous ulcer compression decisions are clinical and depend on wound-care feasibility and patient tolerance.

**Potential experiment signals:** Open wounds, Venous ulcer healing, Dressing tolerance, Clinician-managed compression plan.

**Protocol takeaway:** Venous ulcers are a clinical boundary; pneumatic pants should not be framed as a self-directed ulcer treatment.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
