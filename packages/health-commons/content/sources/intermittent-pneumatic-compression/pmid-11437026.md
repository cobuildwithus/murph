This source is included for **Travel comfort, flight edema, and VTE boundary**.

**Findings:** Population/count: Low-risk and high-risk long-haul air travelers; LONFLIT2 randomized high-risk travelers to stockings versus no stockings. Participant count: 833 (randomized_LONFLIT2; LONFLIT1_observational_context_744). Intervention/exposure: Elastic below-knee stockings during long-haul flights. Comparator/control: No stockings. Duration/follow-up: Average flight duration about 12.4 hours; ultrasound after travel. Endpoints: flight-related DVT, superficial venous thrombosis, asymptomatic thrombosis. Effect/direction: In the high-risk randomized LONFLIT2 component, DVT incidence was reported as 4.5% in controls and 0.24% with stockings; LONFLIT1 observed events mainly in high-risk travelers. Safety/adverse events: Events were travel-thrombosis outcomes; no consumer-device safety data. Limitations: Elastic stocking intervention, not pneumatic compression pants.; High-risk travel population and asymptomatic ultrasound outcomes.; Open-label travel setting; no comfort endpoint for consumer recovery pants. Population mismatch: Stocking prophylaxis in high-risk travelers, not consumer IPC pants.

**Why it matters:** High-risk travelers are the population most likely to ask about prophylaxis, but the intervention was stockings.

**Potential experiment signals:** flight-related DVT, superficial venous thrombosis, asymptomatic thrombosis.

**Protocol takeaway:** Adjacent variant: stockings may reduce asymptomatic travel thrombosis in high-risk subjects; pneumatic pants remain untested for this indication.
