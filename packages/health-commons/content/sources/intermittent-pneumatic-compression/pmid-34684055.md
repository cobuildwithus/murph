This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** The experimental group improved all assessed outcomes (p<0.05), with between-group improvements in passive hip/knee/ankle range of motion and EQ-VAS/EQ-5D quality-of-life measures at 1 month.

**Why it matters:** Direct lower-limb medical IPC but in a high-risk clinical population requiring supervision.

**Potential experiment signals:** Circumference, Range of motion, EQ-5D/EQ-VAS, Glucose/HbA1c if tracked.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only; useful for safety screening and edema/function endpoint ideas.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct unsupervised consumer-recovery evidence.

**Safety and boundary notes:** No adverse-event signal was located in accessible extraction; clinical diabetes/lymphedema screening was required.

**Limitations:** Pilot RCT with 30 participants.; T2DM plus lower-limb lymphedema population.; Co-intervention with manual lymphatic drainage..
