This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** The review included 14 RCTs with 1,397 patients. IPC reduced post-surgical BCRL incidence, improved affected-limb extension, and showed subgroup signals for shorter lymphedema duration, pressure at or below 40 mmHg, treatment longer than two weeks, and evaluation within two months. Other symptoms and chronic treatment outcomes were not clearly improved.

**Why it matters:** This very recent review separates prevention from treatment and preserves the finding that chronic established lymphedema may not respond strongly to IPC alone.

**Potential experiment signals:** Incident BCRL, Limb volume, Range of motion, Pressure and treatment duration, Adverse responses.

**Protocol takeaway:** IPC has supervised clinical signals in BCRL prevention but should not be generalized to healthy-user recovery or chronic lymphedema treatment claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
