This source is included for **Dose, hemodynamics, and wearable-relevant mechanisms**.

**Findings:** No between-trial heart-rate differences were found. Blood lactate during the second ride was higher after IPC boots than control (3.9 ± 1.0 mmol/L vs 2.7 ± 1.5 mmol/L; p < 0.05).

**Why it matters:** It preserves a counterintuitive lactate finding relevant to wearable recovery claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** Heart rate during cycling, blood lactate at 30 minutes of each ride.

**Protocol takeaway:** Track lactate/metabolic response cautiously; IPC may not always reduce lactate in close repeated exercise.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were extracted from accessible materials unless noted above. Key limitations: Small sample.; Submaximal cycling protocol only.; No direct soreness or performance endpoint.; Between-ride posture differed between intervention and control..

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
