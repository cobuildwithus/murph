This source is included for **Prolonged standing/sitting and workday leg swelling sibling variant**.

**Findings:** Twenty adults completed three randomized conditions, and 19 were analyzed. Uninterrupted sitting produced the largest decrease in localized resistance, while sit-to-stand transitions attenuated that decrease; the authors limited inference to short acute conditions and healthy 18-40-year-old adults.

**Why it matters:** It reinforces that posture and movement can change swelling signals even without any compression device.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker / localized-bioimpedance-resistance, biomarker / lower-leg-swelling, biomarker / blood-pressure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for confounder/endpoints context only.
