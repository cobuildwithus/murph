This source is included for **Dose, hemodynamics, and wearable-relevant mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Conventional IPC and the new protocol increased peak volume flow in accessible summaries, while total volume flow was not significantly changed. The new protocol reduced abrupt velocity changes and maintained forward flow more steadily than conventional compression.

**Why it matters:** It highlights that inflation/deflation timing can affect hemodynamic stability.

**Potential experiment signals:** Venous blood-flow velocity, peak volume flow, total volume flow, compression protocol safety/stability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Cycle timing and flow stability matter; document device mode when possible.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were extracted from accessible materials unless noted above. Key limitations: No symptom/performance endpoint.; Protocol-development purpose.; Healthy volunteers.; Device specifics may not match consumer pants..
