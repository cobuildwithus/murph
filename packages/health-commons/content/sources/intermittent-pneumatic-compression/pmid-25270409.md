This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** The review identified two controlled before-after studies and six case series. One retrospective controlled before-after study reported improved limb salvage and wound healing, while all included studies had high risk of bias and complications included pain, skin abrasion, and contact rash.

**Why it matters:** This review highlights that arterial insufficiency and ischemic wounds are medical contexts where compression-related devices require supervision.

**Potential experiment signals:** Ischemic rest pain, Wound healing, Limb salvage, Claudication distance, Skin irritation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Critical limb ischemia is a safety boundary: do not treat ischemic symptoms or wounds with consumer pneumatic pants outside vascular care.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
