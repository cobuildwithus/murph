This source is included for **Direct post-exercise recovery evidence: completed trials and dose studies**.

**Findings:** IPC produced a large but nonsignificant CK reduction (Δ=-138.0 U/L, p=0.160, d=1.41); neither IPC nor manual massage produced significant quadriceps/hamstring IVC or vertical-jump changes, and ANOVA found no main intervention effect.

**Why it matters:** This recent open soccer trial directly compares pneumatic compression with a common recovery modality.

**Potential experiment signals:** Creatine kinase, Quadriceps IVC, Hamstring IVC, Vertical jump.

**Protocol takeaway:** IPC should not be claimed to improve lower-limb strength or vertical jump in this acute soccer-recovery context.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were identified in accessible extraction. Key limitations: Small male youth soccer sample.; Acute crossover with 7-day washout.; Performance outcomes were null..

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
