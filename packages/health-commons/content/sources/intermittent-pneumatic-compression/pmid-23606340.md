This source is included for **Direct post-exercise recovery evidence: completed trials and dose studies**.

**Findings:** The study concluded that IPC was unable to hasten recovery after strenuous eccentric exercise and failed to attenuate isokinetic force loss versus control.

**Why it matters:** This direct eccentric-exercise recovery study is a clear null comparator to positive soreness-only trials.

**Potential experiment signals:** Isokinetic force recovery, Soreness, Muscle function over post-exercise days.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not claim IPC restores muscle force after eccentric exercise based on this small active-male study.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were recovered in accessible extraction. Key limitations: Small n=10 sample.; Paywalled article; limited dose details recovered.; Diet/inflammatory-marker limitations noted in accessible discussion snippets..
