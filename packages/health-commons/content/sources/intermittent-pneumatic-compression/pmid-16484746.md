This source is included for **Safety, contraindications, adverse events, and device instructions**.

**Findings:** The abstract describes bilateral peroneal nerve palsies after craniotomy in a 63-year-old man, with IPC used for DVT/PE prophylaxis believed to be the primary etiology. It warns physicians to avoid compressing the fibular head when using pneumatic devices.

**Why it matters:** It gives a concrete fit-related mechanism for peroneal-nerve injury prevention.

**Potential experiment signals:** Fibular-head pressure; foot drop; numbness; weakness; postoperative exposure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Avoid focal garment pressure near the fibular head; stop for foot drop, numbness, or weakness.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
