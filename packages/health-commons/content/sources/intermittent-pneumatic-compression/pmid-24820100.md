This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** The review included nine randomized trials with 489 people. One trial found more healing with IPC than dressings alone, five add-on trials had mixed results, replacement comparisons versus compression alone were low-quality and unclear, and one trial found rapid IPC healed more ulcers than slow IPC.

**Why it matters:** This is an open-access high-priority review that shows both potential wound-care benefit and uncertainty when IPC is compared with standard compression approaches.

**Potential experiment signals:** Ulcer healing proportion, Healing time, Skin status, Standard compression use, IPC speed/dose comparison.

**Protocol takeaway:** IPC may help venous ulcer healing in some clinical comparisons, but the evidence is not a direct rationale for healthy consumer use.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
