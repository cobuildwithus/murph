This source is included for **Direct post-exercise recovery evidence: completed trials and dose studies**.

**Findings:** Muscle glycogen resynthesis rates were the same for passive recovery and PCD (6.9±0.8 vs 6.9±0.5 mmol/kg wet weight/hour); glucose, insulin, and lactate changed over time but not between trials.

**Why it matters:** This is a direct physiological study addressing a common recovery claim with muscle biopsy data.

**Potential experiment signals:** Muscle glycogen resynthesis, Glucose, Insulin, Blood lactate.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not claim pneumatic compression speeds glycogen restoration when adequate carbohydrate is consumed.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were recovered in accessible extraction. Key limitations: Small n=10 sample.; Male active participants only.; Permission-required article; extraction relies on accessible indexed details..
