This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** Accessible abstracts/summaries state that HPIPC for 2 hours/day over 16 weeks significantly improved peak walking time, reduced resting pain, and improved healing rates versus standard care; exact effect sizes were not extracted.

**Why it matters:** Important supervised arterial treatment boundary and high-pressure dose example.

**Potential experiment signals:** Peak walking time, Rest pain, Wound healing, ABI, QoL.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only PAD/CLI evidence; do not promote to general recovery.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct unsupervised consumer-recovery evidence.

**Safety and boundary notes:** Severe PAD/CLI population; do not infer consumer safety. Funding/device support and software conflict disclosures were noted in accessible article snippets.

**Limitations:** Small RCT (34 randomized).; High-risk PAD/CLI and mostly diabetes; not healthy users.; Exact endpoint effect sizes not fully accessible in extraction..
