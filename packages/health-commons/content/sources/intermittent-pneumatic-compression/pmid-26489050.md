This source is included for **Dose, hemodynamics, and wearable-relevant mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Pretreatment for 30 minutes with EPC had no effect on subsequent supramaximal exercise performance or blood lactate concentrations during passive recovery compared with sham.

**Why it matters:** It directly constrains claims that compression before exercise improves anaerobic output or lactate handling.

**Potential experiment signals:** Repeated Wingate performance, blood lactate during passive recovery.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote pre-exercise compression as an acute Wingate-performance or lactate-recovery enhancer based on this source.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were extracted from accessible materials unless noted above. Key limitations: Sample count not available from accessible extraction.; Brief communication with limited extractable details.; Preconditioning is an adjacent use case, not post-exercise recovery.; Paywalled/permission-limited full text..
