This source is included for **Direct post-exercise recovery evidence: completed trials and dose studies**.

**Findings:** Blood lactate was significantly lower under EPC at 25 minutes (8.91±3.12 vs 10.66±3.44 mmol/L, p=0.021) and 35 minutes (6.44±2.14 vs 7.89±2.37 mmol/L, p=0.006), while peak power, average power, and fatigue index did not differ.

**Why it matters:** This direct crossover trial separates biomarker change from performance outcome.

**Potential experiment signals:** Blood lactate at 25 and 35 minutes, Wingate peak power, Wingate average power, Fatigue index.

**Protocol takeaway:** IPC can reduce lactate during inactive recovery without improving immediate anaerobic performance.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were recovered in accessible extraction. Key limitations: Small n=14 sample.; Acute lab protocol.; Paywalled article..
