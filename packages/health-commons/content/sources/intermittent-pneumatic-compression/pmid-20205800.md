This source is included for **Safety, contraindications, adverse events, and device instructions**.

**Findings:** The review evaluates complications from DVT prophylaxis modalities in trauma. Extracted source text identifies mechanical compression devices as one modality and reports possible complications such as local soft-tissue injury, bleeding, and non-compliance.

**Why it matters:** It provides review-level clinical context for mechanical-compression complications.

**Potential experiment signals:** Soft-tissue injury; bleeding/bruising; adherence; recent trauma mismatch.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not generalize trauma prophylaxis data to healthy users; use it for safety monitoring and population-mismatch warnings.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
