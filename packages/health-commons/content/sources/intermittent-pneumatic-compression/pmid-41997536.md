This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** Accessible publisher/index snippets identify seven randomized controlled trials in lower-extremity lymphedema; reduced limb volume was reportedly measured in all seven and all seven showed statistically significant reductions. Participant totals, protocols, effect sizes, adverse events, and risk-of-bias details were not extracted from accessible snippets.

**Why it matters:** Lower-extremity lymphedema is anatomically closer to pneumatic pants than upper-limb BCRL, but it remains a clinical disease population and current extraction data are incomplete.

**Potential experiment signals:** Lower-extremity lymphedema, Pressure dose, Limb volume, Adverse events.
