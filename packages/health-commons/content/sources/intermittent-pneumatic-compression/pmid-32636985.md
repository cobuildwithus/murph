This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** Median time to wound closure by 9 months was 141 days with IPC versus 211 days with control (p=0.031). Reported healing rate was higher with IPC (2.1±0.8 mm/day) than control (0.8±0.4 mm/day; p<0.05), with lower pain scores at weeks 1–3 and greater but non-significant edema reduction.

**Why it matters:** Recent RCT for wound-care boundary; useful for edema/pain/wound endpoints only in a medical context.

**Potential experiment signals:** Wound closure time, Healing rate, Pain VAS, Ankle/calf circumference.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only wound-care evidence; do not generalize to non-ulcer recovery claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct unsupervised consumer-recovery evidence.

**Safety and boundary notes:** No adverse-event table was located in accessible extraction; devices were provided/acknowledged by BioCompression.

**Limitations:** Wound-care population with chronic CVI and large, painful ulcers.; Adjunct to multilayer compression; not standalone wellness use.; Open medical/wound-care context only..
