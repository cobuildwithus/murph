This source is included for **Clinical DVT prophylaxis and hospital boundary**.

**Findings:** ASH 2019 provides procedure- and risk-specific recommendations for surgical hospitalized patients, including mechanical prophylaxis questions; it is guideline boundary evidence rather than a direct IPC pants efficacy study. Safety/adverse-event notes: Bleeding risk, procedure type, and certainty of evidence shape recommendations. Limitations: Applies to hospitalized surgical patients and is not a direct consumer or post-exercise recovery source.

**Why it matters:** It defines the supervised clinical boundary for IPC use and helps prevent over-claiming consumer benefits.

**Potential experiment signals:** Skin irritation or skin breaks; Discomfort/tolerance; Falls or tubing/device hazards; Adherence or wear time; DVT/PE only as clinician-managed safety context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Recent or planned surgery should be a clinical-supervision flag.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
