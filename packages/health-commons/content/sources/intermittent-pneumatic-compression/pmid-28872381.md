This source is included for **Direct post-exercise recovery evidence: completed trials and dose studies**.

**Findings:** No significant differences were found in 4-minute TT performance (P=.08), blood lactate clearance (P=.57), or TQR (P=.07); the authors concluded there was little additional benefit.

**Why it matters:** This trial directly addresses next-bout cycling performance after lower-limb pneumatic compression.

**Potential experiment signals:** 4-minute time-trial average power/score, Blood lactate clearance, Total quality recovery.

**Protocol takeaway:** A 30-minute ISPC session should not be expected to improve immediate cycling performance recovery in trained cyclists.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were recovered in accessible extraction. Key limitations: Acute crossover design.; Trained cyclists only.; Permission-required article..
