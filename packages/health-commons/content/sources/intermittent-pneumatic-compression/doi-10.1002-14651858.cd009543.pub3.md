This source is included for **Clinical DVT prophylaxis and hospital boundary**.

**Findings:** The review is primarily a device-comparison boundary source; direct evidence for one IPC device type over another was limited and does not identify consumer pneumatic pants as superior. Safety/adverse-event notes: Safety data were study-specific and not fully extracted; use remains postoperative and clinician-managed. Limitations: Total hip replacement population and supervised postoperative prophylaxis are a major population mismatch for healthy recovery-pants users; evidence was limited for device-type selection.

**Why it matters:** It defines the supervised clinical boundary for IPC use and helps prevent over-claiming consumer benefits.

**Potential experiment signals:** Device fit and coverage; Wear-time adherence; Discomfort or skin effects; Clinician-managed VTE endpoints only.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not claim that pneumatic compression pants match or outperform particular hospital IPC sleeve types for thrombosis prevention.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
