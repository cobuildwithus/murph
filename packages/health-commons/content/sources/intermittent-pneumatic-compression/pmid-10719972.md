This source is included for **Safety, contraindications, adverse events, and device instructions**.

**Findings:** The PubMed-indexed case report title states pulmonary embolism occurred as a consequence of applying sequential compression devices to the legs in a patient asymptomatic of DVT.

**Why it matters:** It reinforces why known or suspected DVT/PE is a hard safety boundary despite IPC being used clinically for prophylaxis.

**Potential experiment signals:** DVT symptoms; PE symptoms; clot history; urgent evaluation triggers.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use pneumatic compression pants with known or suspected DVT/PE or unexplained unilateral leg symptoms.
