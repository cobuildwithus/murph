This source is included for **Clinical DVT prophylaxis and hospital boundary**.

**Findings:** The review found the evidence base was poor quality; IPC appeared to have a superior safety profile but worse compliance in some comparisons. Safety/adverse-event notes: Compliance problems and device-related safety profile are the key extracted signals. Limitations: Poor-quality surgical literature; exact quantitative synthesis not re-extracted; no consumer recovery evidence.

**Why it matters:** It defines the supervised clinical boundary for IPC use and helps prevent over-claiming consumer benefits.

**Potential experiment signals:** Skin irritation or skin breaks; Discomfort/tolerance; Falls or tubing/device hazards; Adherence or wear time; DVT/PE only as clinician-managed safety context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Track compliance/tolerance if experimenting; do not claim surgical prophylaxis equivalence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
