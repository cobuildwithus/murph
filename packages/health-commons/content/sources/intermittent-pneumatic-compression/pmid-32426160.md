This source is included for **Comparators and adjacent modalities**.

**Findings:** Sports massage meta-analysis found no evidence of direct performance improvement but small statistically significant improvements in flexibility and DOMS.

**Why it matters:** Massage is often compared with pneumatic compression in recovery settings and user expectations.

**Potential experiment signals:** strength, jump, sprint, endurance, flexibility, fatigue, DOMS.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as massage comparator context only; performance and DOMS/flexibility effects differ.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse-event details extracted. Key limitations: Massage protocols and populations varied across 29 studies.; Effect sizes small for beneficial domains.; Not a pneumatic-compression intervention..

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
