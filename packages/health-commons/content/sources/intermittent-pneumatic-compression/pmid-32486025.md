This source is included for **Prolonged standing/sitting and workday leg swelling sibling variant**.

**Findings:** Twenty female nursing students were randomized to knee-length or thigh-length 25-32 mmHg stockings during a nine-hour clinical shift. There were no significant between-group differences in pain, edema, or satisfaction; one thigh-length participant reported tightness and itching, and right-leg pain increased in the thigh-length group after the shift.

**Why it matters:** It preserves a null/mixed adjacent finding and side-effect signal instead of assuming longer garments are better.

**Potential experiment signals:** symptom / leg-pain, biomarker / leg-circumference-edema, experience / satisfaction, safety / side-effects.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for comfort, adherence, and null-result context only.
