This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** Complete healing occurred in 10/21 IPC patients versus 1/24 controls, and median ulcer-healing rate was reported as 19.8% area/week with IPC versus 2.1% area/week in controls.

**Why it matters:** Supports the wound-care boundary: IPC can be a prescribed adjunct in venous ulcer management.

**Potential experiment signals:** Ulcer area, Complete healing, Edema/circumference, Pain if reported.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only venous-ulcer evidence; do not use for consumer recovery efficacy claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct unsupervised consumer-recovery evidence.

**Safety and boundary notes:** No adverse-event details were located in accessible extraction.

**Limitations:** Historical paywalled study.; Venous-ulcer wound-care population, not wellness/recovery users.; Exact treatment duration and adverse-event reporting not fully accessible..
