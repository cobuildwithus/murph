This source is included for **Comparators and adjacent modalities**.

**Findings:** Twenty minutes of ECP improved within-day 1.2-km shuttle-run recovery and perceived recovery in accessible results.

**Why it matters:** Shows a positive adjacent mechanical-compression signal for within-day performance recovery, while requiring modality separation.

**Potential experiment signals:** 1.2-km shuttle run test completion time, total quality recovery, perceived recovery benefit.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only: ECP can be a comparator but not evidence for pneumatic compression pants.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse-event details extracted. Key limitations: Small sample of 13 males.; ECP instead of IPC pants.; Within-day repeated-bout setting may not generalize..

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
