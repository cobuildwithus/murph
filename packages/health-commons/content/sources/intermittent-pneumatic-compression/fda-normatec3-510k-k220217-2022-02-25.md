This source is included for **Safety, contraindications, adverse events, and device instructions**.

**Findings:** The 510(k) summary identifies Normatec 3 as a Class II powered inflatable tube massager for temporary relief of minor muscle aches and pains and temporary increase in circulation, used by adults in good health in clinic, hospital, athlete-training, and home environments. Extracted settings include a 0–110 mmHg pressure range, labeled pressure levels, ZoneBoost +10 mmHg, and treatment time of 15–60 minutes.

**Why it matters:** It defines the direct device regulatory boundary, intended population, environments, and labeled pressure/time settings.

**Potential experiment signals:** Pressure level; session duration; labeled intended use; adult healthy-user boundary.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use FDA labeling for device context and dose-boundary language, not as efficacy proof.
