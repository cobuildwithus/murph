This source is included for **Prolonged standing/sitting and workday leg swelling sibling variant**.

**Findings:** Twenty healthy women aged 18-23 wore below-knee graduated compression stockings while seated for 30 minutes. Maximum calf circumference and a proxy of extracellular fluid decreased significantly; total lower-leg volume and medial gastrocnemius stiffness tended to decrease, and correlations among changes were not significant.

**Why it matters:** It highlights which swelling endpoints may move quickly and which may not.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker / calf-circumference, biomarker / lower-leg-volume, biomarker / extracellular-water-resistance, biomarker / muscle-stiffness.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as acute endpoint-selection context only.
