This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** Gradient Pump increased foot oxygenated hemoglobin and popliteal/femoral hemodynamics; the sequential foot-calf device decreased foot oxygenated hemoglobin and did not show comparable hemodynamic improvements. All completed Gradient Pump; 9 completed SFC.

**Why it matters:** Shows that IPC effects vary by device design, pressure, and population.

**Potential experiment signals:** NIRS tissue oxygenation, Popliteal/femoral blood flow, Device completion/tolerability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for mechanism/device-parameter caution, not as direct efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct unsupervised consumer-recovery evidence.

**Safety and boundary notes:** The lower completion/compliance with the SFC device is a tolerability boundary; no broader adverse-event claim was extracted.

**Limitations:** Acute mechanistic study with 12 patients.; Severe PAD clinical population.; Device-specific and not an efficacy trial..
