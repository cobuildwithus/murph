This source is included for **Adjacent post-exercise compression evidence: upper-limb eccentric-exercise model**.

**Findings:** IPC significantly decreased circumference and stiffness, most notably on days 2 and 3 after exercise, while strength loss was not affected.

**Why it matters:** This classic source distinguishes swelling/stiffness effects from strength recovery, with major anatomy mismatch.

**Potential experiment signals:** Circumference/swelling, Passive stiffness, Strength loss.

**Protocol takeaway:** IPC may reduce swelling/stiffness in an exercise-injury model, but this does not prove lower-limb strength recovery.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were recovered in accessible extraction. Key limitations: Upper-limb model, not pneumatic pants.; Older study; exact sample size not recovered in accessible extraction.; Permission-required/copyrighted article..
