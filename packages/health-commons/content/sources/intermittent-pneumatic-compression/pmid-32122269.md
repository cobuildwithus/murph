This source is included for **Safety, contraindications, adverse events, and device instructions**.

**Findings:** The consensus reports that skin irritation, discomfort, and pain are the most frequent non-severe adverse events, while serious events such as soft-tissue injury and nerve injury are rare but recognized. It recommends screening for conditions that increase risk and checking correct fit/application. It also notes modality-specific nuance: some severe peripheral arterial disease contraindications for sustained stockings do not automatically apply to IPC, but low ABI still warrants monitoring.

**Why it matters:** This is a high-value safety boundary source that explains both common minor complaints and rare severe harms, while preserving nuance about IPC versus sustained compression.

**Potential experiment signals:** Skin irritation, discomfort, pain, numbness, pressure injury, fit problems, vascular status, and cardiac status.

**Protocol takeaway:** Screen for risk conditions, check fit and application, monitor symptoms, and avoid treating clinical contraindication nuance as a green light for unsupervised home use.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
