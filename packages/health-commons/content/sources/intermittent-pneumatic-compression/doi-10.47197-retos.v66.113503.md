This source is included for **Direct post-exercise recovery evidence: completed trials and dose studies**.

**Findings:** The IPC group differed significantly from passive recovery for blood lactate (p=0.002) and vertical jump (p=0.006); leg explosive power did not differ significantly (p=0.496).

**Why it matters:** This provides emerging direct trial evidence but should be weighted cautiously.

**Potential experiment signals:** Blood lactate, Vertical jump height, Leg explosive power.

**Protocol takeaway:** High-pressure IPC may improve lactate and vertical-jump recovery versus passive rest in a preliminary setting, but dose details and replication are needed.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were recovered in accessible extraction. Key limitations: Preliminary n=14 study.; Exact IPC pressure/duration not recovered in accessible extraction.; Rights unclear..
