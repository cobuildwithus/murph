This source is included for **Safety, contraindications, adverse events, and device instructions**.

**Findings:** The indexed case report title describes soft-tissue injury to the sole of the foot secondary to a retained AV impulse foot pump.

**Why it matters:** It reinforces skin and soft-tissue inspection and device-removal checks for pneumatic compression systems.

**Potential experiment signals:** Skin/soft-tissue injury; retained device; pressure-point pain.

**Protocol takeaway:** Remove garments after the intended session and inspect pressure areas, especially the feet and soles for adjacent foot devices.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
