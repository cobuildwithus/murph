This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** Complete foot wound healing and limb salvage occurred in 14/24 IPC patients (58%) versus 4/24 controls (17%); below-knee amputation occurred in 10/24 versus 20/24. Limb-loss odds were reported as 7.0 for controls versus IPC, and seated TcPO2 was higher with IPC at study completion.

**Why it matters:** Strong boundary source for high-risk arterial disease and supervised use.

**Potential experiment signals:** Limb salvage, Wound healing, Amputation, TcPO2, Compliance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only PAD/CLI evidence; do not apply to wellness use.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct unsupervised consumer-recovery evidence.

**Safety and boundary notes:** Severe ischemic-wound population; no general safety claim for healthy users. Compliance was closely monitored in the clinical study.

**Limitations:** Retrospective controlled design, not randomized.; Critical ischemia/tissue loss population with surgery history.; High-intensity medical regimen requiring monitoring..
