This source is included for **Clinical DVT prophylaxis and hospital boundary**.

**Findings:** Guideline goal is to provide clinicians an evidence-based framework for appropriate thromboprophylaxis in neurocritical care; it is not direct efficacy evidence. Safety/adverse-event notes: Neurocritical care requires careful bleeding and neurologic-risk management; specific adverse-effect recommendations were not fully extracted. Limitations: Guideline covers very high-risk neurocritical populations and cannot be translated to wellness/recovery use.

**Why it matters:** It defines the supervised clinical boundary for IPC use and helps prevent over-claiming consumer benefits.

**Potential experiment signals:** Skin irritation or skin breaks; Discomfort/tolerance; Falls or tubing/device hazards; Adherence or wear time; DVT/PE only as clinician-managed safety context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Neurologic illness or neurocritical care is outside the Murph self-experiment scope.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
