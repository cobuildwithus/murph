This source is included for **Direct post-exercise recovery evidence: completed trials and dose studies**.

**Findings:** The study found significant time effects after exercise but no significant differences between low-intensity cycling, pneumatic compression boots, and voluntary isocapnic hyperpnoea across biochemical, perceptual, jump, or on-ice recovery outcomes.

**Why it matters:** This direct trial compares IPC with realistic athlete recovery alternatives rather than only passive rest.

**Potential experiment signals:** CK, Cortisol, Blood lactate, Pain, Perceived recovery, CMJ, On-ice split times.

**Protocol takeaway:** In elite speedskaters, short pneumatic-boot recovery was not superior to low-intensity cycling or breathing-based recovery.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were identified in accessible extraction. Key limitations: Small elite junior sample.; Short 10-minute dose.; Sport-specific speedskating context..
