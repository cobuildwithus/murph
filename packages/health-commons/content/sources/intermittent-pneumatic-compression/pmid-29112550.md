This source is included for **Clinical DVT prophylaxis and hospital boundary**.

**Findings:** Guideline-level recommendations favor IPC over GCS in some settings and advise against routine mechanical prophylaxis added to pharmacologic prophylaxis except in selected very-high-risk patients. Safety/adverse-event notes: Mechanical prophylaxis choice is partly shaped by bleeding risk and device tolerance; detailed adverse events not fully extracted. Limitations: Perioperative guideline; use as clinical boundary and device-selection context only.

**Why it matters:** It defines the supervised clinical boundary for IPC use and helps prevent over-claiming consumer benefits.

**Potential experiment signals:** Skin irritation or skin breaks; Discomfort/tolerance; Falls or tubing/device hazards; Adherence or wear time; DVT/PE only as clinician-managed safety context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not imply consumer pants are a universal prophylaxis add-on.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
