This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** The guideline addresses diagnosis and management of chronic limb-threatening ischemia. For this protocol, it is used only to define peripheral arterial disease, ischemic rest pain, and tissue-loss safety boundaries.

**Why it matters:** People with ischemic rest pain, tissue loss, or severe PAD need vascular evaluation before compression-device use.

**Potential experiment signals:** Rest pain, Tissue loss, PAD/CLTI history, Need for vascular referral.

**Protocol takeaway:** Known or suspected chronic limb-threatening ischemia belongs in stop-rule and clinician-referral language.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
