This source is included for **Direct post-exercise recovery evidence: completed trials and dose studies**.

**Findings:** In 17 handball players, the experimental group had slightly higher perceived recovery than controls, lower ankle systolic blood pressure, and improved left biceps femoris contraction delay time; session RPE was not significantly changed.

**Why it matters:** This source provides a rare multi-week direct sports-use dose rather than a single acute session.

**Potential experiment signals:** Perceived recovery status, Session RPE, Agility T-test, Countermovement jump, Ankle systolic blood pressure, Tensiomyography.

**Protocol takeaway:** Daily IPC may modestly improve perceived recovery or selected fatigue-related measures during training, but evidence remains small and mixed.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were identified in accessible extraction. Key limitations: Very small parallel-group sample.; Training load and sport context may influence outcomes.; Daily-use dose may not translate to occasional consumer use..
