This source is included for **Dose, hemodynamics, and wearable-relevant mechanisms**.

**Findings:** rSO2 increased from baseline 75.7% ± 5.8% to 79.5% ± 4.7% in period 1 and 81.2% ± 4.8% in period 4 (p < 0.001). rSO2 was higher in periods 2-4 than period 1, plateauing after about 10 minutes. T2 values decreased post-intervention, consistent with fluid clearance.

**Why it matters:** It provides recent, detailed time-course data for oxygenation and fluid-clearance mechanisms.

**Potential experiment signals:** Regional tissue oxygen saturation at posterior calf, T2-weighted MRI fluid-content proxy, lower-leg circumference.

**Protocol takeaway:** A 30-minute session may be a reasonable mechanistic observation window, but claims should remain mechanism-only.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were extracted from accessible materials unless noted above. Key limitations: No sham or randomized comparator.; Mechanistic endpoints only.; Healthy adults rather than fatigued athletes.; Published after the project cutoff; should be rechecked before final protocol synthesis..

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
