This source is included for **Comparators and adjacent modalities**.

**Findings:** Foundational cold-water immersion comparator meta-analysis; accessible snippets report a beneficial direction for delayed-onset muscle soreness, especially across later recovery windows.

**Why it matters:** Cold-water immersion is a common recovery comparator for pneumatic-compression trials and user expectations.

**Potential experiment signals:** recovery, delayed-onset muscle soreness, performance, muscle damage markers.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only: cold-water immersion evidence should not be used to claim benefits for pneumatic compression pants.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse-event details were extracted from accessible snippets. Key limitations: Participant count and full effect estimates were not extracted from accessible primary text.; Cold-water immersion differs mechanistically from IPC pants.; Older meta-analysis; later cold-water reviews may supersede some estimates..
