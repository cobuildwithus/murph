This source is included for **Direct post-exercise recovery evidence: completed trials and dose studies**.

**Findings:** EPC mitigated reductions in pressure-to-pain threshold and flexibility; sham showed increased poly-ubiquitinated proteins and higher 4-HNE relative to EPC, while peak isokinetic strength did not change in either group.

**Why it matters:** This direct lower-limb study provides mechanistic depth and a mixed functional boundary.

**Potential experiment signals:** Pressure-to-pain threshold, Flexibility/knee joint angle, Peak isokinetic strength, Poly-ubiquitinated proteins, 4-HNE.

**Protocol takeaway:** EPC may help soreness/flexibility and some molecular stress signals during heavy training, but strength recovery was not improved.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were recovered in accessible extraction. Key limitations: Exploratory n=20 male sample.; Biopsy/molecular endpoints are not practical consumer endpoints.; Potential protocol burden from one-hour treatments..

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
