This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** Accessible abstract/summaries state that the pneumatic compression pump did not contribute significantly to lymphedema reduction compared with CDT alone, although both groups improved on within-group lymphedema measures.

**Why it matters:** Preserves a null/no-clear-added-benefit clinical finding, important for avoiding one-sided IPC claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** Limb volume, Dermal thickness, Durability after treatment, Patient burden.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only evidence showing IPC is not consistently additive to good decongestive therapy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct unsupervised consumer-recovery evidence.

**Safety and boundary notes:** No adverse-event details were located in accessible extraction.

**Limitations:** Rights unclear and full text not available in this extraction.; Small upper-extremity postmastectomy population.; Some follow-up details were only visible in secondary summaries..
