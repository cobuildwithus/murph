This source is included for **Dose, hemodynamics, and wearable-relevant mechanisms**.

**Findings:** HRR1 and HRR2 differed between IPC and active recovery (p < 0.001). HRV showed time effects but no group or time-by-group effect. Systolic BP and RPE showed group or interaction effects.

**Why it matters:** This source separates consumer-wearable HRR signals from mixed/null HRV effects after IPC.

**Potential experiment signals:** HRR1, HRR2, HRV, blood pressure, rating of perceived exertion.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use HRR and RPE as exploratory signals; do not claim consistent HRV improvement.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were extracted from accessible materials unless noted above. Key limitations: Small male collegiate sample.; Comparator was active recovery rather than sham/passive rest.; No direct pants performance or soreness endpoint.; Full text appears publisher-controlled..

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
