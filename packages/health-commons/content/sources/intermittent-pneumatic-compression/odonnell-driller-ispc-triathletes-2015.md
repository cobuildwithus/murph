This source is included for **Direct post-exercise recovery evidence: completed trials and dose studies**.

**Findings:** 5-km TT times were not significantly different (P=0.31; ES=0.07), with no significant blood lactate or TQR differences; the authors reported ISPC was not effective for recovery between the cycling and running bouts.

**Why it matters:** This is a direct protocol-like cycling-to-running recovery study using lower-limb ISPC.

**Potential experiment signals:** 5-km treadmill time-trial time, Blood lactate, Total quality recovery.

**Protocol takeaway:** For endurance between-bout performance, 30 minutes of ISPC should not be claimed to outperform passive recovery based on this small crossover trial.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were recovered in accessible extraction. Key limitations: Small male-only sample.; Short between-bout recovery setting.; No adverse-event detail recovered..
