This source is included for **Dose, hemodynamics, and wearable-relevant mechanisms**.

**Findings:** The study tested foot, calf, and combined modes across pressures/frequencies. Accessible summaries indicate higher pressure/frequency and combined foot-calf compression improved venous emptying/blood-flow responses, with specific foot pressures showing lower venous pressures than some lower-pressure settings.

**Why it matters:** It informs dose parameters for lower-limb IPC without overclaiming outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:** Lower-limb venous emptying, venous pressure, blood-flow response.

**Protocol takeaway:** Pressure, frequency, and limb segment matter; dose should be treated as an experimental variable.
