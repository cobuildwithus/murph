**Findings:** Stepwise thigh compression was associated with calf-volume changes up to 80 mmHg, and sudden deflation produced reactive hemodynamic responses including augmented arterial inflow and venous outflow in accessible summaries.

**Why it matters:** It links local compression pressure to calf-volume and venous-return behavior.

**Potential experiment signals:** Calf volume, venous return, arterial inflow after deflation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Calf circumference/volume and posture-controlled venous-return proxies may be useful exploratory endpoints.
