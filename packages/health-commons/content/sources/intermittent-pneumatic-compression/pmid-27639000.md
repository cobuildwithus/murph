This source is included for **Prolonged standing/sitting and workday leg swelling sibling variant**.

**Findings:** Of 46 healthy subjects in the initial bare-leg standing test, 18 had no or few symptoms despite leg-volume increases comparable to more symptomatic subjects. In 24 completing the full study, lower-leg volume increased by about 44 mL after 10 minutes and symptom scores rose, without correlation between volume and symptoms.

**Why it matters:** It prevents over-reliance on a single subjective or volume endpoint in workday swelling interpretation.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker / leg-volume, symptom / venous-leg-symptoms, symptom / tingling, symptom / pressure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for endpoint design and things-to-watch only.
