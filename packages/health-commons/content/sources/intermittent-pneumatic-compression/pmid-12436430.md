This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** In the initial treatment phase, DLT plus IPC produced greater mean volume reduction than DLT alone (45.3% vs 26%; p<0.05). In the maintenance phase, DLT plus IPC was associated with mean volume reduction (89.5±195.5 mL) versus mean volume increase with DLT alone (32.7±115.2 mL; p<0.05).

**Why it matters:** Shows how IPC can affect lymphedema volume when added to standard decongestive therapy, while highlighting population mismatch.

**Potential experiment signals:** Arm volume, Tissue tonometry, Range of motion, Blood pressure if monitored.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only clinical lymphedema evidence; do not generalize to healthy lower-limb recovery.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct unsupervised consumer-recovery evidence.

**Safety and boundary notes:** A later-accessible secondary discussion cited good tolerance but one headache with moderate arterial blood-pressure increase; the primary full adverse-event text was not fully accessible in this extraction.

**Limitations:** Paywalled primary article; extraction relies on accessible abstract/secondary snippets.; Upper-extremity breast-cancer lymphedema is not a pants protocol.; Small phase-specific samples..
