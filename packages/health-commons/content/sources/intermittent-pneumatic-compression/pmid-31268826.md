This source is included for **Dose, hemodynamics, and wearable-relevant mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Compression during walking enhanced vascular conductance and early recovery SFA blood flow; compression during recovery increased recovery blood flow and tissue oxygenation compared with no compression.

**Why it matters:** This bridges IPC with walking-relevant hemodynamic and oxygenation changes.

**Potential experiment signals:** Superficial femoral artery blood flow, vascular conductance, tissue oxygenation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Blood-flow and NIRS signals can be considered exploratory endpoints; do not infer symptom or performance benefit.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were extracted from accessible materials unless noted above. Key limitations: Participant count not recovered in this extraction.; Short acute physiology protocol.; No soreness or performance outcome.; PDF rights require permission..
