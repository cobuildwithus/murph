This source is included for **Dose, hemodynamics, and wearable-relevant mechanisms**.

**Findings:** IPC produced faster return toward pre-exercise values for several cardiovascular parameters during recovery, including mean blood pressure and peripheral vascular resistance. dP/dt recovered more slowly during IPC than sham.

**Why it matters:** It is a recent open-access study of IPC and cardiovascular recovery after high-intensity exercise.

**Potential experiment signals:** Blood pressure, heart rate, stroke volume, cardiac output, dP/dt, peripheral vascular resistance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Track cardiovascular variables as mechanistic signals; keep performance/symptom claims separate.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were extracted from accessible materials unless noted above. Key limitations: Small sample.; Physiology outcomes, not direct recovery benefit outcomes.; Short follow-up.; Some cardiovascular parameters showed mixed behavior..
