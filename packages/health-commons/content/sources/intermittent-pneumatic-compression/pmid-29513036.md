This source is included for **Direct post-exercise recovery evidence: completed trials and dose studies**.

**Findings:** Daily subjective measures and 400-m run times were not improved by IPC or manual therapy; both treatments reduced muscular fatigue acutely after treatment following the race and on post-race day 1.

**Why it matters:** This extended version tests repeated treatments and longer follow-up after prolonged running.

**Potential experiment signals:** Muscular fatigue before/after treatment, Muscle pain and soreness, 400-m run times, Post-race CK.

**Protocol takeaway:** Repeated post-race IPC may improve immediate fatigue perception, but evidence did not show extended or functional recovery benefits.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were recovered in accessible extraction. Key limitations: Extreme endurance event.; Lower CK/stress than earlier study may limit comparability.; Copyrighted/permission-required article..
