This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** The study reported durable limb-circumference decreases and improved tissue elasticity, most pronounced around the calf/ankle, over 3 years of daily high-pressure IPC.

**Why it matters:** Useful for long-term lymphedema and safety-boundary context, especially thigh/genital edema monitoring.

**Potential experiment signals:** Circumference, Tissue elasticity, Genital edema, Thigh ring, Adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only long-term clinical cohort; not direct wellness evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct unsupervised consumer-recovery evidence.

**Safety and boundary notes:** The authors reported no observed complications such as thigh ring or chronic genital edema in the cohort.

**Limitations:** Small uncontrolled cohort.; Very high-pressure medical lymphedema regimen over years.; Cohort design cannot prove causality..
