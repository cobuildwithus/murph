This source is included for **Safety, contraindications, adverse events, and device instructions**.

**Findings:** The case title and citation report bilateral lower-extremity compartment syndromes after prolonged surgery in low lithotomy position with serial compression stockings.

**Why it matters:** The case is relevant to worst-case stop rules for compartment syndrome but not to routine healthy-user efficacy.

**Potential experiment signals:** Compartment-syndrome symptoms; prolonged surgery; lithotomy/positioning risk; severe pain and neurologic change.

**Protocol takeaway:** Treat severe limb pain, swelling, weakness, numbness, or recent prolonged surgery as no-go/urgent-evaluation signals.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
