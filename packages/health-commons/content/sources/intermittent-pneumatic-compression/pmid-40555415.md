This source is included for **Direct post-exercise recovery evidence: completed trials and dose studies**.

**Findings:** The study found time effects for EIMD outcomes but no statistically significant between-intervention differences; IPC showed only a trend/effect-size signal for reduced soreness.

**Why it matters:** This is a direct, modern placebo-controlled EIMD trial and provides an important null/mixed boundary.

**Potential experiment signals:** Knee extension/flexion force, Countermovement jump, Broad jump, Muscle soreness score.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not claim high-pressure IPC restores strength or jump performance after muscle damage; soreness may still be worth tracking.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were recovered in accessible extraction. Key limitations: Young male university sample.; Short 48-hour follow-up.; Paywalled full text in this extraction..
