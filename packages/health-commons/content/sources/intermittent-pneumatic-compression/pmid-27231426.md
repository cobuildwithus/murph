This source is included for **Prolonged standing/sitting and workday leg swelling sibling variant**.

**Findings:** Ten workplace participants were measured before and after six hours without intervention and on days using grade 2 stockings, geko, or Revitive. Control leg volume increased by a median 41 mL; stockings significantly reduced percentage volume increase, while electrical devices were tolerated but less clearly significant in the accessible abstract.

**Why it matters:** It helps keep muscle-pump and stocking mechanisms separate from IPC while preserving a workday-swelling endpoint.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker / leg-volume, biomarker / great-saphenous-vein-diameter, safety / tolerability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for mechanism/comparator context, not direct protocol claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
