This source is included for **Comparators and adjacent modalities**.

**Findings:** EECP did not enhance short-term recovery after HIIT in healthy highly trained junior triathletes.

**Why it matters:** External counterpulsation is a mechanical compression-adjacent modality but not the target protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** 8-minute time-trial power output, RPE, blood lactate, heart-rate variability, countermovement jump.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only null comparator; do not use as direct pants evidence.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse-event details extracted from accessible snippets. Key limitations: Small elite adolescent sample.; EECP device/protocol differs from consumer IPC pants.; Short-term lab performance context..

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
