This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** Amputation-free survival was 98% and 96% at 6 and 12 months among device acquirers versus 90% and 84% among non-acquirers. Limb-salvage association was non-significant (p=0.714), while rest pain (p<0.0001), minor amputation reduction (p=0.023), and amputation-free survival (p=0.01) favored device acquisition/use.

**Why it matters:** Preserves mixed major-amputation findings and reinforces medical-supervision boundary.

**Potential experiment signals:** Amputation-free survival, Major/minor amputation, Rest pain, Toe pressure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only CLI evidence; do not generalize to healthy users.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct unsupervised consumer-recovery evidence.

**Safety and boundary notes:** Some CLI patients may be better served with primary amputation; this population requires specialist vascular decision-making.

**Limitations:** Retrospective non-randomized comparison based on device acquisition/use.; Severe non-reconstructable CLI with major comorbidity burden.; Mechanism remains ambiguous..
