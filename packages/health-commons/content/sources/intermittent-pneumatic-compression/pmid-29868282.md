This source is included for **Dose, hemodynamics, and wearable-relevant mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Unilateral EPC improved skin blood-flow/vascular-reactivity measures bilaterally. Reported skin-temperature changes included larger thigh increases than lower-leg increases soon after treatment, with responses declining over later observation windows.

**Why it matters:** This helps explain why compression may affect vascular reactivity beyond the exact compressed segment.

**Potential experiment signals:** Skin blood flow, skin temperature proxy, vascular reactivity, bilateral response.

**Protocol takeaway:** Consider skin temperature or vascular-reactivity proxies as exploratory signals only.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were extracted from accessible materials unless noted above. Key limitations: Participant count not recovered in this extraction.; Skin-flow and temperature proxies are not clinical or performance outcomes.; No long-term follow-up.; Mechanism-only relevance..

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
