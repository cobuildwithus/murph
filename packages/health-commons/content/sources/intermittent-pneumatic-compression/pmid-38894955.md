This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** The meta-analysis included nine RCTs. Percentage volume reduction did not significantly favor DLT plus IPC, pain and heaviness were comparable, and external rotation improved significantly in the IPC adjunct group.

**Why it matters:** This recent open-access meta-analysis preserves an important no-clear-advantage signal for IPC as an adjunct in clinical lymphedema care.

**Potential experiment signals:** Volume reduction, Pain, Heaviness, Shoulder external rotation, Functional mobility.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not claim IPC reliably adds benefit to clinical lymphedema therapy; in this synthesis, volume and symptom effects were not significant.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
