This source is included for **Direct post-exercise recovery evidence: reviews and evidence maps**.

**Findings:** The review included 17 randomized controlled studies, with 14 included in the meta-analysis. It found at-best moderate effects for pain and soreness, small non-significant effects for muscle mechanical function, and variable or unclear results for creatine kinase, inflammation markers, blood lactate, and heart-rate responses.

**Why it matters:** This source is the strongest direct match for pneumatic compression pants because it focuses on lower-limb IPC devices used after exercise in healthy or athletic populations.

**Potential experiment signals:** Soreness/pain, perceived recovery, jump or time-trial performance, heart rate, blood lactate, CK, and inflammatory markers. Device pressure and session duration should be recorded because protocols varied substantially.

**Protocol takeaway:** A practical protocol page should emphasize uncertainty and frame lower-limb IPC as a possible soreness/perceived-recovery tool rather than a proven performance enhancer.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`, with mixed evidence boundaries.
