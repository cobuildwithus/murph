This source is included for **Dose, hemodynamics, and wearable-relevant mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Systolic and end-diastolic peak velocities changed significantly from pre to during IPC (p < 0.001), with greater effects during high-pressure IPC. Hemodynamic variables returned close to baseline two minutes post-protocol. Arterial diameter differed between pressure protocols; heart rate was unaltered.

**Why it matters:** It directly compares moderate and high-pressure IPC hemodynamic effects in athletes.

**Potential experiment signals:** Systolic peak velocity, end-diastolic peak velocity, arterial diameter, heart rate.

**Protocol takeaway:** Pressure is likely to affect hemodynamic signals; avoid assuming high pressure is automatically better for user outcomes.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were extracted from accessible materials unless noted above. Key limitations: At-rest physiology only.; No symptom or performance endpoint.; Short post-protocol observation.; High pressure may not generalize to consumer pants use..

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
