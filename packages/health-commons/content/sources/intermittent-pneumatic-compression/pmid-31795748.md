This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** Excess volume decreased in both groups; percent volume reduction was better with IPC but not statistically significant. Heaviness and tightness were significantly lower in the IPC group.

**Why it matters:** Another mixed/no-clear-volume-advantage RCT that tempers clinical IPC claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** Volume, Heaviness/tightness, Pain, Functional grip strength.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context that IPC may affect symptoms without reliably outperforming CDT for volume.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct unsupervised consumer-recovery evidence.

**Safety and boundary notes:** No adverse-event details were located in accessible extraction.

**Limitations:** Upper-extremity breast-cancer lymphedema.; No clear volume advantage over CDT.; Clinical supervised rehabilitation setting, not consumer recovery..
