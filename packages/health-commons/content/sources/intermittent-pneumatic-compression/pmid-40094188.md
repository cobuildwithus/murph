This source is included for **Direct post-exercise recovery evidence: completed trials and dose studies**.

**Findings:** The IPC group showed significantly improved recovery in VAS soreness and lower-limb tensiomyography measures versus control, including VAS, Dm, and Tc differences at 48 and 72 hours and Ts at 72 hours.

**Why it matters:** This is a current direct DOMS trial with repeated post-exercise IPC dosing and specific time windows.

**Potential experiment signals:** VAS muscle soreness, Tensiomyography Dm, Tensiomyography Tc, Tensiomyography Ts, 48-72 hour recovery window.

**Protocol takeaway:** Repeated short IPC sessions may reduce lower-limb DOMS after plyometrics, with effects appearing most clearly at 48-72 hours.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were identified in accessible abstract-level extraction. Key limitations: Small sample of untrained male college students.; Paywalled article; extraction relies on abstract/indexed snippets.; No protocol safety detail was recovered..
