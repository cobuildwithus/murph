This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** The review identified eight trials. Accessible abstracts summarize mixed ulcer-treatment results, possible symptom relief in chronic venous insufficiency, few reported adverse events, and insufficient evidence to determine optimal device type or protocol.

**Why it matters:** This early review is commonly cited in venous-disease IPC discussions and helps distinguish medical venous insufficiency treatment from consumer recovery use.

**Potential experiment signals:** Venous symptoms, Ulcer healing or recurrence, Treatment adherence, Adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Clinical venous-disease IPC evidence is mixed and should remain context-only for pneumatic compression pants.
