This source is included for **Safety, contraindications, adverse events, and device instructions**.

**Findings:** The case describes a 32-year-old man who developed unilateral lower-limb compartment syndrome after a long surgical procedure with IPC used for DVT prophylaxis. The article discusses prolonged operative time and IPC as possible contributing risk factors among other perioperative risks.

**Why it matters:** It provides open-access IPC-specific case context for compartment-syndrome stop rules.

**Potential experiment signals:** Severe calf/leg pain; swelling; neurologic change; recent long surgery; immobilization.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use pneumatic compression pants after recent prolonged surgery unless cleared; seek urgent care for severe pain, swelling, or neurologic symptoms.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
