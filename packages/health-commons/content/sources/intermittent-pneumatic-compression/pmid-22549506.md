This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** Among 36 completers, APCD users had an average 29% edema reduction, while SPCD users had a 16% edema increase. Tissue dielectric constant fell 5.8% with APCD and rose 1.9% with SPCD.

**Why it matters:** Useful for understanding device design differences and home clinical lymphedema outcomes, not direct protocol efficacy.

**Potential experiment signals:** Limb volume, Tissue dielectric constant, Girth, Home adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only adjacent-variant evidence; useful for endpoints such as limb volume and tissue water.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct unsupervised consumer-recovery evidence.

**Safety and boundary notes:** No adverse-event signal was located in the accessible abstract; study disclosed Tactile Systems funding and one scientific-advisor relationship.

**Limitations:** Upper-extremity lymphedema, not pants.; Device-company funding/conflict disclosures.; Home clinical population and medical devices, not healthy recovery users..
