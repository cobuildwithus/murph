This source is included for **Safety, contraindications, adverse events, and device instructions**.

**Findings:** In the survey, 61 respondents from 28 countries answered questions on compression therapy. Respondents reported that allergies or skin irritations can occur, strangulation/pressure marks can occur, necrosis can occur, and pressure damage on the peripheral nervous system can occur. The paper notes possible intellectual bias because respondents were experts and enthusiasts in compression therapy.

**Why it matters:** It gives recent expert-survey context for side-effect awareness and uncertainty in compression practice.

**Potential experiment signals:** Skin allergy/irritation; pressure marks; necrosis; nerve pressure symptoms; expert-opinion limitations.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for safety watchpoints and uncertainty language, not efficacy or incidence estimates in pneumatic pants users.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
