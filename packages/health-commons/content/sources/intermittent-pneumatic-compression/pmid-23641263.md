This source is included for **Background terminology, physiology, and boundary context**.

**Findings:** Compression-therapy background review explains pressure, stiffness, venous-flow, and edema mechanisms across compression tools.

**Why it matters:** Defines compression concepts and boundary between clinical compression therapy and consumer recovery devices.

**Potential experiment signals:** venous blood flow, interface pressure, edema reduction, limb volume, ambulatory venous hypertension.

**Protocol takeaway:** Background only: use for terminology/mechanism framing, not protocol efficacy.

**Safety and limitations:** Clinical compression settings involve medical indications/contraindications; this background source does not replace device-specific safety guidance. Key limitations: Narrative review, not a trial/meta-analysis for pneumatic compression pants.; Clinical populations and medical indications differ from consumer recovery.; Mechanism evidence should not be promoted to causal recovery claims..

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
