This source is included for **Safety, contraindications, adverse events, and device instructions**.

**Findings:** The report describes two pediatric common peroneal nerve palsy cases associated with IPC during ICU admission. The authors emphasize proper sizing and fitting to avoid covering the fibular head, appropriate decompression, and awareness of risk factors such as body position and thinness.

**Why it matters:** It gives recent open-access detail on peroneal-nerve risk from poor sizing/placement.

**Potential experiment signals:** Size/fit; fibular-head pressure; foot drop; weakness; numbness; thinness/small body size.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not allow cuffs or pants to create focal pressure at the fibular head; stop for foot drop, weakness, or numbness.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
