This source is included for **Travel comfort, flight edema, and VTE boundary**.

**Findings:** Population/count: Studies and recommendations on air travel and VTE prophylaxis. Participant count: 0 (systematic_review_not_participant_count). Intervention/exposure: Air travel exposure and possible prophylaxis including compression stockings or LMWH. Comparator/control: No travel/prophylaxis comparators across reviewed evidence. Duration/follow-up: Flights shorter or longer than 6 hours discussed. Endpoints: VTE risk, DVT prophylaxis decision boundary, risk factors. Effect/direction: The review concluded that travelers on flights shorter than 6 hours and those with no known VTE risk factors do not need DVT prophylaxis; travelers with at least one risk factor should consider stockings and/or LMWH for flights longer than 6 hours. Safety/adverse events: Risk-factor-dependent prophylaxis was emphasized. Limitations: Systematic review of heterogeneous travel-VTE evidence.; Guidance about stockings/LMWH, not pneumatic pants.; Does not test consumer devices. Population mismatch: Air travel/VTE review; not consumer IPC pants.

**Why it matters:** Helps avoid blanket travel-prophylaxis advice.

**Potential experiment signals:** VTE risk, DVT prophylaxis decision boundary, risk factors.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context only: low-risk travelers should not be told they need pneumatic pants for DVT prevention.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
