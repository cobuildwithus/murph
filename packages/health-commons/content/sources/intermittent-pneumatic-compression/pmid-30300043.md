This source is included for **Dose, hemodynamics, and wearable-relevant mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Sequential pulse compression increased total hemoglobin (p < 0.001, d = 0.505) and oxygenated hemoglobin (p < 0.001, d = 0.745) change scores compared with control; deoxygenated hemoglobin did not differ statistically (p = 0.06).

**Why it matters:** This is a lower-extremity wearable-relevant mechanism study in active people.

**Potential experiment signals:** Total hemoglobin, oxygenated hemoglobin, deoxygenated hemoglobin.

**Protocol takeaway:** NIRS-derived THb and oxygenated hemoglobin can be tracked as mechanism signals.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were extracted from accessible materials unless noted above. Key limitations: Single acute session.; NIRS surrogate endpoints only.; No direct performance or soreness outcome.; Paywalled source..

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
