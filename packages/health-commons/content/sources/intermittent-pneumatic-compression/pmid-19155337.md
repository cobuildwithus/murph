This source is included for **Prolonged standing/sitting and workday leg swelling sibling variant**.

**Findings:** Leg-volume changes were evaluated in 70 hospital employees across shift groups, with a 15-person 12-hour subset. The accessible abstract reports significant volume increases and a higher rate of fluid accumulation in the morning.

**Why it matters:** It flags that swelling can vary by workday timing, so Murph logs should capture time-of-day and occupational exposure.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker / leg-volume.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for things-to-watch and baseline context, not efficacy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
