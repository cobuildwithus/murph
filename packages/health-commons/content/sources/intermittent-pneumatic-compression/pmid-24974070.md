This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** The guideline positions IPC as a fallback when other compression options are unavailable, cannot be used, or have failed after prolonged compression therapy. It also notes arterial-disease boundaries for compression decisions.

**Why it matters:** This guideline directly prevents overgeneralizing venous ulcer evidence and flags arterial disease as a compression-safety boundary.

**Potential experiment signals:** Venous ulcer status, Arterial insufficiency screening, Standard compression intolerance, Clinician-supervised treatment plan.

**Protocol takeaway:** People with active venous ulcers or suspected arterial insufficiency should not use consumer pneumatic pants as a substitute for clinician-guided care.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
