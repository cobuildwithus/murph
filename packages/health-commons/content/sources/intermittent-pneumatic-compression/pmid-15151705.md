This source is included for **Travel comfort, flight edema, and VTE boundary**.

**Findings:** Population/count: Published studies of air travel and DVT risk. Participant count: 0 (systematic_review_not_participant_count). Intervention/exposure: Air travel exposure. Comparator/control: Nontravel or lower-duration travel comparators across studies. Duration/follow-up: Primarily prolonged travel; review discusses flights longer than 3 hours and 8 hours. Endpoints: DVT risk, VTE risk factors, air travel duration. Effect/direction: The review found no definitive evidence that prolonged travel over 3 hours increased DVT risk overall, while suggesting flights of 8 hours or longer may increase risk in people with additional risk factors. Safety/adverse events: Highlights the importance of risk factors when interpreting travel DVT risk. Limitations: Review depends on heterogeneous observational studies.; Not a prevention or IPC study.; Older evidence base. Population mismatch: Air-travel exposure review, not consumer IPC pants.

**Why it matters:** Useful for balancing stronger claims from later studies with uncertainty from earlier reviews.
