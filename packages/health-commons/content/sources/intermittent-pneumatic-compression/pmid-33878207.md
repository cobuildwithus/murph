This source is included for **Travel comfort, flight edema, and VTE boundary**.

**Findings:** Population/count: Airline passengers in randomized trials of compression stockings for flights lasting at least 4 to 5 hours. Participant count: 2918 (participants_in_12_RCTs). Intervention/exposure: Compression stockings worn during flights. Comparator/control: No stockings/control conditions. Duration/follow-up: Flights longer than 5 hours in included trials; review eligibility flights at least 4 hours. Endpoints: symptomless DVT, SVT, edema, death, PE, symptomatic DVT, adverse effects. Effect/direction: Across trials, symptomless DVT occurred in 3 stocking participants versus 47 control participants; OR 0.10. SVT reduction was not statistically clear; edema was lower with stockings. No deaths, PE, or symptomatic DVT occurred in included trials. Safety/adverse events: No significant adverse effects were reported; absence of symptomatic events limited ability to assess death/PE/symptomatic DVT. Limitations: Cochrane review of stockings, not pneumatic pants.; Symptomless DVT and edema dominate; symptomatic event evidence absent.; Edema evidence rated low certainty due measurement limitations. Population mismatch: Static stockings, not intermittent pneumatic compression pants.

**Why it matters:** Best-known contemporary stocking synthesis; critical to keep it in adjacent-variant bucket.

**Potential experiment signals:** symptomless DVT, SVT, edema, death, PE, symptomatic DVT, adverse effects.

**Protocol takeaway:** Adjacent variant only: flight-stocking evidence is not consumer pneumatic-pants efficacy evidence.
