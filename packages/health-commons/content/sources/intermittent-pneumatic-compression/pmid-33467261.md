This source is included for **Comparators and adjacent modalities**.

**Findings:** Combined IPC plus CWI appeared to help maintain some recovery-related measures during championship play, but attribution to IPC alone is not possible.

**Why it matters:** Competition-context source with IPC present, but bundled with cold-water immersion and no clean IPC attribution.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle mechanical function, hydration, pain perception, RPE, sleep hours, sleep quality.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only: do not claim IPC-only benefit from this combined protocol.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse-event details extracted; very high 200 mmHg pressure should not be generalized without device/protocol context. Key limitations: No isolated IPC-only arm was extracted.; Combined cold-water immersion confounds attribution.; Competition context and sex-dependent findings may not generalize..

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
