This source is included for **Travel comfort, flight edema, and VTE boundary**.

**Findings:** Population/count: Passengers arriving by air over a large airport surveillance period; severe PE cases recorded on arrival. Participant count: 135290000 (passenger_arrivals_registry; 56_confirmed_PE_cases). Intervention/exposure: Air travel exposure and flight distance. Comparator/control: Distance strata / travel exposure context. Duration/follow-up: Immediate severe PE events associated with arrival after air travel. Endpoints: severe pulmonary embolism, flight distance, arrival events. Effect/direction: Among 135.29 million passenger arrivals, 56 severe PE cases were confirmed; the source is used as severe-event safety context. Safety/adverse events: Severe PE after travel is rare but potentially life-threatening. Limitations: Registry/case context; only severe events apparent on arrival.; No prevention intervention.; Does not estimate all travel VTE events. Population mismatch: Severe PE surveillance, not consumer IPC pants.

**Why it matters:** Useful for explaining why PE symptoms require urgent medical attention.

**Potential experiment signals:** severe pulmonary embolism, flight distance, arrival events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Safety-only: chest pain, shortness of breath, fainting, or suspected PE is not a self-care/pants situation.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
