This source is included for **Direct post-exercise recovery evidence: reviews and evidence maps**.

**Findings:** The review included 12 randomized or crossover studies with 322 participants. Pressotherapy significantly reduced muscle soreness. It did not significantly affect jump height, and it did not significantly affect creatine kinase 24–96 hours after DOMS induction.

**Why it matters:** This is direct post-exercise recovery evidence for a compression modality that maps closely onto pneumatic compression pants or boots.

**Potential experiment signals:** DOMS rating, pain/soreness score, perceived recovery, jump height, and optional CK testing.

**Protocol takeaway:** Pressotherapy may be useful as an add-on recovery practice for soreness, but not as a stand-alone performance recovery intervention.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`, with explicit mixed/null-result boundaries.
