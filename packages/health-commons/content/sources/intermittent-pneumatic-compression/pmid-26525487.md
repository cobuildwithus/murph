This source is included for **Clinical DVT prophylaxis and hospital boundary**.

**Findings:** Review included 14 RCTs (2,633 patients) and 3 observational studies (1,724 patients). IPCDs were comparable to anticoagulation for VTE (RR 1.39, 95% CI 0.73 to 2.64); limited data suggested combined anticoagulation plus IPC may lower VTE versus anticoagulation alone. IPCD use may lower major bleeding versus anticoagulation. Safety/adverse-event notes: Major bleeding was a key safety comparator; ease-of-use and adherence results were inconsistent. Limitations: Mostly arthroplasty/high-risk surgical evidence; limited data for device selection and combined prophylaxis; no consumer setting.

**Why it matters:** It defines the supervised clinical boundary for IPC use and helps prevent over-claiming consumer benefits.

**Potential experiment signals:** Adherence/wear time; Ease of use; Bleeding-risk boundary; Skin/tolerance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use high-risk surgical prophylaxis reviews to advertise consumer clot prevention.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
