This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** The guideline provides clinical recommendations for diagnosing, treating, and preventing recurrence of venous leg ulcers. It is a wound-care guideline, not a trial of pneumatic compression pants in healthy or athletic users.

**Why it matters:** Venous ulcers and wound-care use of compression are materially different from self-directed recovery use; this source helps prevent overgeneralizing clinical wound evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** Skin integrity, Open wound or ulcer history, Medical compression use, Need for referral before self-directed compression.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not present venous leg ulcer guideline evidence as support for casual pneumatic compression pants use; treat open wounds or suspected ulcers as clinician-supervised territory.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
