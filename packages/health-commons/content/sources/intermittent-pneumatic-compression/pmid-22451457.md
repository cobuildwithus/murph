This source is included for **Prolonged standing/sitting and workday leg swelling sibling variant**.

**Findings:** In 108 randomized hairdressers, 15-20 mmHg medical compression stockings were compared with no compression. Wearing stockings reduced pain/swelling score by 0.22 points (12%, P<0.001) and lower-leg volume by about 19 mL (P<0.001); symptom and volume changes were not correlated.

**Why it matters:** It offers relatively large occupational-standing comparator evidence for symptom and volume endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** symptom / pain-and-swelling-score, biomarker / lower-leg-volume, symptom / sleep-disturbance, symptom / leg-heaviness.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent comparator context only.
