This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** The guideline covers chronic venous disease management of the lower limbs. IPC appears in supervised venous-disease contexts such as post-thrombotic syndrome and venous ulcer management, not as a general recovery device.

**Why it matters:** This source provides contemporary vascular-society context for lower-limb venous disease and compression-device boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** CVD diagnosis, PTS severity, Venous ulcer status, Prescription compression use.

**Protocol takeaway:** Known CVD, PTS, or VLU contexts should be separated from general consumer pneumatic-pants claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
