This source is included for **Prolonged standing/sitting and workday leg swelling sibling variant**.

**Findings:** Four protocols were compared: no stockings/no IPC, stockings during work, IPC after work, and stockings plus IPC. Leg pain rose after work in all protocols and improved after the post-work interval, especially with IPC alone. Stocking protocols prevented or reduced work-induced edema, while IPC-containing protocols informed the post-work recovery context.

**Why it matters:** This is the strongest adjacent comparator source for a workday-swelling sibling variant because it includes IPC, stockings, and combination protocols.

**Potential experiment signals:** symptom / leg-pain, biomarker / leg-volume, biomarker / leg-circumference, biomarker / ecf-tbf, biomarker / ecw-tbw.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for context on post-work swelling outcomes and comparator choice; do not claim direct benefits for the pants protocol.
