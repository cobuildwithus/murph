This source is included for **Comparators and adjacent modalities**.

**Findings:** Older static-compression garment meta-analysis suggested a beneficial direction for recovery after exercise-induced muscle damage, especially beyond 24 hours.

**Why it matters:** Static garment evidence is a common comparator and should not be conflated with pneumatic pants.

**Potential experiment signals:** DOMS, strength, power, creatine kinase.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent static-compression context only.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse-event details were extracted from accessible snippets. Key limitations: Participant count and exact pooled estimates were not extracted.; Static compression differs in dose, pressure pattern, and mechanism from pneumatic pants.; Older synthesis with potentially superseded evidence base..

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
