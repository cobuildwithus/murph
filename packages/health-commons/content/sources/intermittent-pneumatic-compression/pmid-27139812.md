This source is included for **Dose, hemodynamics, and wearable-relevant mechanisms**.

**Findings:** NMES-induced blood flow produced higher calf arterial inflow than blood-flow restriction, passive rest, and placebo, and was the only condition that allowed performance recovery in the accessible abstract. Recovery effect was not linked to heart rate or blood lactate.

**Why it matters:** It tests whether blood-flow enhancement itself can relate to performance recovery.

**Potential experiment signals:** Calf arterial inflow, Wingate performance recovery, heart rate, blood lactate.

**Protocol takeaway:** Blood-flow change may be worth tracking, but IPC must be tested directly rather than inferred from NMES.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were extracted from accessible materials unless noted above. Key limitations: Not an IPC pants intervention.; Mechanistic comparator only for this protocol.; Short laboratory recovery window.; Paywalled source..
