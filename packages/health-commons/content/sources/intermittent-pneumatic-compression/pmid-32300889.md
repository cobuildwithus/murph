This source is included for **Clinical DVT prophylaxis and hospital boundary**.

**Findings:** In 18 trials with 2,474 patients, IPC reduced DVT versus placebo (RR 0.41, 95% credible interval 0.26 to 0.60) and PE versus placebo (RR 0.10, 95% credible interval 0.01 to 0.60). IPC appeared equivalent to chemical prophylaxis in the network comparisons. Safety/adverse-event notes: The authors noted limited safety evidence and called for larger randomized trials. Limitations: Neurosurgical perioperative population; safety evidence limited; not consumer setting.

**Why it matters:** It defines the supervised clinical boundary for IPC use and helps prevent over-claiming consumer benefits.

**Potential experiment signals:** Skin irritation or skin breaks; Discomfort/tolerance; Falls or tubing/device hazards; Adherence or wear time; DVT/PE only as clinician-managed safety context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Neurosurgical VTE prophylaxis must be clinician directed.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
