This source is included for **Direct post-exercise recovery evidence: completed trials and dose studies**.

**Findings:** Accessible extraction indicates EPC used with consecutive HIIT and recovery days reduced skeletal-muscle markers of proteolysis; no specific performance-improvement effect was recovered.

**Why it matters:** This study helps explain possible muscle-recovery mechanisms but should not be over-promoted to performance claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** Proteolysis markers, HIIT recovery symptoms, Performance-related variables if full text is available.

**Protocol takeaway:** EPC may influence muscle-protein breakdown markers during hard training, but this is mechanistic evidence rather than proof of improved performance.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were recovered in accessible extraction. Key limitations: Small male sample.; Paywalled full text; limited effect estimates recovered.; Mechanistic endpoints may not translate to consumer outcomes..
