**Findings:** Recent combat-sport study compared pneumatic compression and cryo-compression; accessible snippets reported improved perfusion/elasticity with active compression conditions, with 100 mmHg pneumatic compression maintaining elasticity up to 48 hours and cold-compression giving more immediate soreness relief.

**Why it matters:** Recent source with pneumatic dose comparisons and cold-compression boundary; useful for adjacent dose and outcome mapping.
