This source is included for **Comparators and adjacent modalities**.

**Findings:** Recent static compression-garment review evaluated muscle strength and power recovery after exercise; accessible summaries report beneficial strength/power recovery signals, especially for lower-limb garments.

**Why it matters:** Recent static-garment synthesis relevant to distinguishing compression apparel from active pneumatic devices.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle strength recovery, muscle power recovery.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only: do not use static-garment meta-analysis as IPC-pants evidence.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse-event details extracted. Key limitations: Participant count and exact pooled effect sizes not extracted.; Lower- vs upper-limb garment effects may differ.; Static garment mechanism and dose differ from IPC pants..
