This source is included for **Safety, contraindications, adverse events, and device instructions**.

**Findings:** The manual describes a prescription IPC device for DVT prophylaxis and related circulation/edema indications. It lists contraindications including limb infection/cellulitis without antibiotic coverage, lymphangiosarcoma, suspected or confirmed DVT, inflammatory phlebitis or pulmonary embolism, CHF, pulmonary edema, severe arteriosclerosis or ischemic vascular disease, dermatitis, recent vein ligature, gangrene, skin grafts, casts or splints, and open wounds. It warns that homecare patients should have a prescription and education, recommends frequent skin assessment in vascular disease or diabetes, gives fit/application checks, and calls special attention to compartment syndrome risk in extended supine lithotomy contexts.

**Why it matters:** It adds detailed same-mechanism device-instruction evidence for home-use monitoring and contraindications.

**Potential experiment signals:** Contraindication checklist; skin inspection; vascular disease/diabetes flag; fit; device error/leak/blockage; compartment-syndrome warning.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use same-mechanism manual language for contraindication, fit, skin-inspection, and medical-supervision boundaries, not for sports-recovery efficacy.
