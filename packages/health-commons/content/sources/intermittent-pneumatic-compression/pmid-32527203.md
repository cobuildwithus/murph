This source is included for **Clinical DVT prophylaxis and hospital boundary**.

**Findings:** Five RCTs with 3,133 patients were included. IPC reduced VTE versus no treatment (OR 0.36, 95% credible interval 0.18 to 0.71) and DVT versus no treatment (OR 0.45, 95% credible interval 0.21 to 0.90). No clear differences were found among IPC, anticoagulation, and combined approaches for VTE or DVT. Safety/adverse-event notes: Safety details were not the main extracted signal; anticoagulation bleeding risk remains clinically relevant. Limitations: Critically ill population, limited number of RCTs, and no consumer relevance.

**Why it matters:** It defines the supervised clinical boundary for IPC use and helps prevent over-claiming consumer benefits.

**Potential experiment signals:** Skin irritation or skin breaks; Discomfort/tolerance; Falls or tubing/device hazards; Adherence or wear time; DVT/PE only as clinician-managed safety context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use ICU VTE prevention to support at-home pants claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
