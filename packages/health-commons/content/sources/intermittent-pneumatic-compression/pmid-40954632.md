This source is included for **Comparators and adjacent modalities**.

**Findings:** In this multi-modality meta-analysis, the accessible article preview reported moderate-certainty evidence that IPC did not reduce muscle soreness at 24 hours post-intervention.

**Why it matters:** Recent synthesis directly mentions IPC but remains adjacent because it is not limited to lower-limb pneumatic compression pants.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle soreness, performance, certainty of evidence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only evidence that IPC may not reduce 24-hour muscle soreness compared with control in a pooled subset; do not promote as a direct pants claim.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse-event details were extracted from the accessible article preview.
