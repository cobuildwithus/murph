This source is included for **Dose, hemodynamics, and wearable-relevant mechanisms**.

**Findings:** PPDC increased forward split flexibility more than control: reported increases were about 25.3% right and 33.3% left under PPDC versus 12.2% right and 1.0% left under control.

**Why it matters:** It is a legging-style compression study with dose details relevant to wearable protocols.

**Potential experiment signals:** Forward split range of motion, flexibility.

**Protocol takeaway:** Flexibility/ROM can be tracked as an exploratory endpoint after compression.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were extracted from accessible materials unless noted above. Key limitations: Very small sample.; Acute flexibility endpoint only.; No soreness/performance recovery endpoint.; Paywalled source..

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
