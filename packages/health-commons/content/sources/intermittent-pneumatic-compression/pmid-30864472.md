This source is included for **Dose, hemodynamics, and wearable-relevant mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Sequential IPC increased muscle StO2 by 3.5% versus no compression; single-compartment IPC increased StO2 by 1.6%; sequential mode exceeded single-compartment by 2.1%.

**Why it matters:** It supports mode-specific differences in oxygenation responses while flagging anatomical mismatch.

**Potential experiment signals:** Thenar muscle tissue oxygen saturation by NIRS, mode comparison.

**Protocol takeaway:** Mode matters; do not assume all compression patterns produce the same oxygenation response.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were extracted from accessible materials unless noted above. Key limitations: Participant count not recovered.; Upper-limb site.; Mechanistic NIRS endpoint only.; No patient-centered outcome..

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
