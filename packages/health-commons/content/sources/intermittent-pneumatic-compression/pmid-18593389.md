This source is included for **Clinical DVT prophylaxis and hospital boundary**.

**Findings:** Narrative review emphasizes IPC as a tool for immobile patients; no new pooled effect estimate was extracted. Safety/adverse-event notes: Safety details not the main extracted signal; immobile patients require clinical assessment. Limitations: Narrative review; clinical immobility population; no consumer or post-exercise outcomes.

**Why it matters:** It defines the supervised clinical boundary for IPC use and helps prevent over-claiming consumer benefits.

**Potential experiment signals:** Skin irritation or skin breaks; Discomfort/tolerance; Falls or tubing/device hazards; Adherence or wear time; DVT/PE only as clinician-managed safety context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use immobile-patient IPC narratives to support healthy-user recovery claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
