This source is included for **Dose, hemodynamics, and wearable-relevant mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries identify 30-40 mmHg interface pressure with 15-second inflation and 10-second deflation as optimal for edema reduction; pressures above 40 mmHg did not substantially improve edema reduction.

**Why it matters:** It helps bound pressure claims: higher pressure is not always better for fluid outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:** Edema reduction, interface pressure, inflation/deflation timing.

**Protocol takeaway:** Treat pressure as a tunable dose and monitor comfort/swelling rather than maximizing pressure.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were extracted from accessible materials unless noted above. Key limitations: Sample and population not recovered.; Clinical edema outcome differs from healthy-user recovery.; Older source.; Full text rights require permission..
