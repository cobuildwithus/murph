This source is included for **Prolonged standing/sitting and workday leg swelling sibling variant**.

**Findings:** Forty male security employees were randomized to regular socks or 15-20 or 20-30 mmHg stockings. Lower-leg muscle twitch force, volume, and discomfort were measured before and after a 12-hour standing shift. Regular socks showed significant fatigue, edema, and discomfort, while neither stocking group showed significant changes.

**Why it matters:** It gives an occupational field comparator with a long standing shift and multiple leg endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker / lower-leg-volume, biomarker / muscle-twitch-force, symptom / discomfort, dose / stocking-pressure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent occupational-stockings context only.
