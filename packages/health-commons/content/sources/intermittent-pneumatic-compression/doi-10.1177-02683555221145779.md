This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** Both groups improved; PCD plus conservative care had larger percent circumference reductions at ankle, calf, knee, and thigh, with a significant between-group knee difference (2.3 cm; 95% CI 0.4 to 4.1; p=0.016). SF-36 pain improved within the PCD group, but between-group pain difference was not significant.

**Why it matters:** This is a lower-body, pant-like medical PCD trial, so it is mechanistically near the protocol but clinically and population-wise mismatched.

**Potential experiment signals:** Limb circumference, Bioimpedance fluid measures, Pain or tenderness, SF-36 or lipedema symptom score.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as clinical boundary/context for edema and lipedema symptoms; do not promote to healthy recovery or performance claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct unsupervised consumer-recovery evidence.

**Safety and boundary notes:** No adverse-event result table was located in accessible extraction; the protocol excluded heart failure, pacemaker/ICD, acute venous disease, severe PAD, active limb infection/inflammatory disease, active cancer, poorly controlled kidney disease, pulmonary hypertension, and other conditions.

**Limitations:** Open-label, industry-sponsored proof-in-principle RCT.; Small sample and attrition: 36 baseline/randomized or assessed, 26 completed follow-up.; Follow-up timing varied because of COVID-era disruption.; Medical lipedema/secondary lymphedema population is not comparable with healthy recovery users..
