This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** Edema decreased in the IPC group across ultrasound/circumference/volume measures (all p<0.0001) and increased in controls. Ankle dorsiflexion and plantar flexion improved in IPC, and SF-36 general health improved (p=0.004).

**Why it matters:** Useful lower-limb edema context with functional and QoL endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** Limb volume/circumference, Subcutaneous thickness, Ankle ROM, SF-36, Inflammatory biomarkers.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only edema evidence; avoid general recovery claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct unsupervised consumer-recovery evidence.

**Safety and boundary notes:** No adverse-event details were located in accessible extraction.

**Limitations:** Pilot clinical trial in low-mobility edema, not healthy users.; One-month follow-up.; Paywalled article; extraction from accessible abstract/landing page..
