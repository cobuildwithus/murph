This source is included for **Comparators and adjacent modalities**.

**Findings:** Upper-body ISPC reduced perceived soreness up to 24 hours, but physical-performance effects were negligible/trivial.

**Why it matters:** Shows body-region-specific ISPC can reduce soreness while leaving performance mostly unchanged.

**Potential experiment signals:** grip strength, single-arm medicine ball throw, preacher bench biceps curls to failure, perceived soreness at four upper-body landmarks.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only: upper-body soreness findings should not be promoted as lower-limb pants efficacy.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse-event details extracted. Key limitations: Upper-body single-arm design.; Trivial or nonsignificant performance effects.; Permission-required article; no copyrighted PDF should be committed..
