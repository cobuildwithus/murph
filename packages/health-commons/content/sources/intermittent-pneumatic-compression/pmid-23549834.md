This source is included for **Safety, contraindications, adverse events, and device instructions**.

**Findings:** The case report describes extensive bullous skin lesions beneath IPC sleeves after hip-fracture surgery.

**Why it matters:** It provides open-access IPC-specific evidence for severe skin injury as a rare but possible adverse event.

**Potential experiment signals:** Blistering; rash; skin breakdown; contact-area inspection; postoperative skin fragility.

**Protocol takeaway:** Stop use for blisters, bullae, rash, skin breakdown, or unusual skin pain/irritation.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
