This source is included for **Safety, contraindications, adverse events, and device instructions**.

**Findings:** Accessible index summaries describe two cases associated with IPCD use during surgery, including peroneal neuropathy and compartment syndrome.

**Why it matters:** It is an early IPC-specific adverse-event report covering both neurologic and compartment-syndrome harms.

**Potential experiment signals:** Numbness; weakness; foot drop; severe pain; surgical exposure; garment pressure points.

**Protocol takeaway:** Build stop rules for new neurologic symptoms and severe limb pain; do not treat these events as common.
