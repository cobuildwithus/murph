This source is included for **Direct post-exercise recovery evidence: completed trials and dose studies**.

**Findings:** In nine men, high-pressure IPC produced a smaller vertical-jump reduction than low-pressure IPC or rest, while both low- and high-pressure treatments reduced mean perceived soreness versus no treatment at +1, +24, and +48 hours.

**Why it matters:** This early direct pilot gives concrete pressure/duration details and lower-limb performance/soreness endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** Vertical jump decrement, Leg soreness at 1, 24, and 48 hours, Heart rate, Blood pressure.

**Protocol takeaway:** A one-hour sequential IPC session may reduce soreness and vertical-jump decrement after shuttle running, but the evidence is pilot-level.

**Safety and limitations:** Heart rate and blood pressure were recorded, but no adverse-event reporting was recovered. Key limitations: Pilot sample of nine healthy males.; Older custom device and analysis methods.; Permissions/copyright status prevents committing PDF..
