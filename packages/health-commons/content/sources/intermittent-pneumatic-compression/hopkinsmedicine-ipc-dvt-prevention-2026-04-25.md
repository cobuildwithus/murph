This source is included for **Safety, contraindications, adverse events, and device instructions**.

**Findings:** The page lists discomfort, warmth, sweating, skin breakdown, rare nerve damage, and rare pressure injury as risks. It notes that people with leg ulcers, burns, or peripheral vascular disease may have higher problem risk, advises correct cuff size and skin care, and says fit should be snug but not painful, with provider alerts for pain, swelling, warmth, redness, or skin breakdown.

**Why it matters:** It offers practical patient-facing same-mechanism safety instructions.

**Potential experiment signals:** Cuff fit; skin checks; pain; swelling; warmth; redness; peripheral vascular disease/ulcer/burn risk.

**Protocol takeaway:** Garments should feel snug but not painful; check skin and stop/contact a provider for pain, swelling, warmth, redness, or skin breakdown.
