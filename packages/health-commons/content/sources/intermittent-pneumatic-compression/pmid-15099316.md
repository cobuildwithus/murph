This source is included for **Prolonged standing/sitting and workday leg swelling sibling variant**.

**Findings:** Twelve volunteers had both lower-leg volumes measured in the morning and seven hours later, with below-knee stockings of different pressure levels worn on one leg in random order. The accessible abstract reports that calf-length stockings around 11-21 mmHg reduced or prevented evening edema.

**Why it matters:** It anchors early occupational-edema stocking evidence while preserving the distinction from pneumatic compression.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker / leg-volume, symptom / leg-heaviness, symptom / leg-tiredness.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as stocking comparator context only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
