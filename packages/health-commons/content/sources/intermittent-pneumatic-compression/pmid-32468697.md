This source is included for **Clinical DVT prophylaxis and hospital boundary**.

**Findings:** The review concluded that adding IPC to pharmacologic prophylaxis conferred a moderate VTE benefit, with benefit confined to surgical patients in the indexed abstract. Safety/adverse-event notes: Safety extraction was not detailed in the accessible abstract; pharmacologic bleeding risk remains comparator context. Limitations: Hospitalized/pharmacologic-prophylaxis population; exact effect sizes and safety details not re-extracted here; not consumer evidence.

**Why it matters:** It defines the supervised clinical boundary for IPC use and helps prevent over-claiming consumer benefits.

**Potential experiment signals:** Skin irritation or skin breaks; Discomfort/tolerance; Falls or tubing/device hazards; Adherence or wear time; DVT/PE only as clinician-managed safety context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Combined prophylaxis evidence belongs under clinician-directed care.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
