This source is included for **Dose, hemodynamics, and wearable-relevant mechanisms**.

**Findings:** IPC-induced vasodilation in upstream/uncompressed muscle was reduced or abolished by nitric-oxide synthase inhibition. eNOS mRNA and protein were upregulated in the model after IPC exposure.

**Why it matters:** It is a mechanistic source for one of the central vascular signaling hypotheses.

**Potential experiment signals:** Arteriolar dilation, eNOS mRNA, eNOS protein, nitric oxide synthase inhibition response.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only in a mechanism/background section and clearly label as preclinical.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were extracted from accessible materials unless noted above. Key limitations: Preclinical model.; No consumer outcome.; Older study.; No protocol efficacy endpoint..
