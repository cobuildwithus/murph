This source is included for **Direct post-exercise recovery evidence: completed trials and dose studies**.

**Findings:** In 16 trained adults, IPC after repeated-sprint cycling produced higher total quality recovery and less post-exercise hemodilution than sham, but average cycling power and RPE in the subsequent performance assessment were not improved.

**Why it matters:** This is a recent, direct lower-limb IPC sports-recovery trial with a clearly mixed signal: subjective and hematologic changes without performance improvement.

**Potential experiment signals:** Total quality recovery score, Cycling power output, Rating of perceived exertion, Hematocrit or plasma-volume proxy if available.

**Protocol takeaway:** Pneumatic compression pants may feel beneficial after intense cycling, but this trial does not support a claim that they improve immediate follow-up cycling power.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were identified in the accessible extraction; do not interpret that as proof of no adverse events. Key limitations: Small laboratory crossover study.; Performance endpoints were null despite perceptual and hematologic differences.; Adverse-event reporting was not prominent in the accessible extraction..
