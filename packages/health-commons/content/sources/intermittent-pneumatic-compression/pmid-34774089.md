This source is included for **Direct post-exercise recovery evidence: completed trials and dose studies**.

**Findings:** No significant intergroup differences were found in biochemical or functional measurements; the authors concluded IPC and INP did not attenuate reductions in muscle function markers or pain perception up to 48 hours.

**Why it matters:** This open RCT is one of the more comprehensive direct DOMS trials and preserves negative findings.

**Potential experiment signals:** CK, LDH, Isokinetic strength, Soreness, Active knee flexion.

**Protocol takeaway:** IPC should not be claimed to improve drop-jump DOMS, strength, or muscle-damage markers based on this RCT.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were recovered in accessible extraction. Key limitations: Healthy males only.; Laboratory drop-jump EIMD model.; No clear adverse-event signal recovered..

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
