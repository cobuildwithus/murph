This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** The review included seven randomized controlled trials with 287 patients. Accessible abstracts report that IPC can alleviate lymphedema, but routine management with an IPC pump did not differ significantly from routine management without a pump.

**Why it matters:** This meta-analysis preserves a negative/mixed finding in a clinical lymphedema population and prevents selectively citing only positive IPC results.

**Potential experiment signals:** Upper-limb lymphedema volume, Circumference, Symptoms and function, Adjunct-to-care effect.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not claim that IPC add-on treatment reliably improves lymphedema outcomes based on this RCT synthesis.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
