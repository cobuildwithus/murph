This source is included for **Prolonged standing/sitting and workday leg swelling sibling variant**.

**Findings:** Twenty healthy standing workers completed natural-rest, sequential-IPC, and circular-IPC visits after work. Pain and leg circumferences improved more with IPC than with natural rest; leg volume fell in all conditions without a clear between-intervention advantage. The detailed safety table reported no serious adverse events but did report mild transient symptoms in a small number of participants.

**Why it matters:** This is the closest workday-swelling sibling source because it uses IPC after prolonged standing, but it should stay context-only for Pneumatic Compression Pants.

**Potential experiment signals:** symptom / leg-pain, biomarker / leg-circumference, biomarker / leg-volume, safety / adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent support for tracking leg pain, circumference, and swelling after long standing days; do not promote it as direct sports-recovery efficacy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
