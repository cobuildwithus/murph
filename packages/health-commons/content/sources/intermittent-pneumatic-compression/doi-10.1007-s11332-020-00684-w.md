This source is included for **Dose, hemodynamics, and wearable-relevant mechanisms**.

**Findings:** HRR1 differed significantly between IPC and placebo-control (p = 0.008); HRR2 was not significant. Frequency-domain HRV variables showed group and interaction effects, while time-domain parameters were dominated by time effects.

**Why it matters:** This trial maps IPC to consumer-trackable recovery physiology, especially HRR and HRV, without proving recovery-performance benefit.

**Potential experiment signals:** Heart rate recovery at 1 minute, Heart rate recovery at 2 minutes, Heart-rate variability frequency-domain metrics.

**Protocol takeaway:** Track HRR and HRV cautiously as exploratory signals; do not frame this source as proof that pants improve performance.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were extracted from accessible materials unless noted above. Key limitations: Small male collegiate sample.; Only short-term submaximal exercise context.; HRR2 was not significantly different from placebo-control.; Full protocol details were not available from an open full text..

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
