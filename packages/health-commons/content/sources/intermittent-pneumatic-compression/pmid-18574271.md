This source is included for **Clinical DVT prophylaxis and hospital boundary**.

**Findings:** Guideline recommends every hospital develop a formal active VTE prevention strategy; specific IPC roles are clinical pathway decisions rather than consumer recommendations. Safety/adverse-event notes: Bleeding and thrombosis risk balancing is part of guideline context; detailed safety extraction not performed in this batch. Limitations: Older guideline superseded or complemented by later ACCP/ASH/NICE guidance; use as historical hospital-boundary context only.

**Why it matters:** It defines the supervised clinical boundary for IPC use and helps prevent over-claiming consumer benefits.

**Potential experiment signals:** Skin irritation or skin breaks; Discomfort/tolerance; Falls or tubing/device hazards; Adherence or wear time; DVT/PE only as clinician-managed safety context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Pneumatic compression pants should not substitute for hospital VTE-prevention pathways.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
