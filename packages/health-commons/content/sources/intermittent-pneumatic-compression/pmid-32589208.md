This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** The guideline found no high-quality stand-alone IPC studies. It did not recommend adding standard IPC in clinical phase I CDT because evidence was conflicting and low quality, while standard or advanced IPC may be considered in phase II home care with low-grade evidence.

**Why it matters:** This guideline explicitly grades IPC evidence and warns against overreading conflicting phase I add-on data.

**Potential experiment signals:** Limb volume, BCRL phase of care, Device type, Adverse events, Compression-garment use.

**Protocol takeaway:** IPC may be considered in selected BCRL home-care phases, but this is not direct protocol support for consumer pneumatic pants.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
