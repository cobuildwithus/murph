This source is included for **Comparators and adjacent modalities**.

**Findings:** Cooling/cryotherapy reduced subjective DOMS at 24, 48, and 96 hours, but accessible extraction found no evidence that cooling affected objective recovery variables over 96 hours.

**Why it matters:** Cold and cryotherapy are common comparators; this source cleanly separates subjective and objective outcome domains.

**Potential experiment signals:** DOMS, rating of perceived exertion, objective recovery variables.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as cryotherapy comparator context and as a caution that subjective soreness and objective performance can diverge.

**Safety and limitations:** No IPC-specific safety data; cryotherapy safety/comfort is a separate boundary. Key limitations: Review included heterogeneous cooling approaches.; High performance and detection bias risk was reported in accessible extraction.; Not an IPC-pants intervention..

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
