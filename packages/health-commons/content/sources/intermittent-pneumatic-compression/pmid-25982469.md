This source is included for **Dose, hemodynamics, and wearable-relevant mechanisms**.

**Findings:** PGC-1α mRNA increased 77% at 1 hour post-EPC; eNOS protein and NOx concentrations were upregulated in biopsy samples. SOD2 protein showed a nonsignificant trend, and PGC-1α protein did not change acutely.

**Why it matters:** It links pneumatic compression to muscle-level signaling pathways that are often invoked in recovery claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** PGC-1α mRNA, eNOS protein, NOx concentration, SOD2 protein trend, muscle biopsy molecular markers.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use this as mechanism-only support for exploratory biomarker rationale, not a user-facing efficacy claim.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were extracted from accessible materials unless noted above. Key limitations: Small sample with invasive biopsy endpoints.; No sham comparator reported in the accessible abstract.; Molecular changes do not establish recovery benefit.; Short follow-up only..

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
