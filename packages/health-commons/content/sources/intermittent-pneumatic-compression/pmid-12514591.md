This source is included for **Dose, hemodynamics, and wearable-relevant mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Head-down position increased venous-flow measures and head-up decreased them. IPC increased velocities and volume flow in all venous segments; effects differed by compression site, with foot compression mainly affecting popliteal flow and calf compression acting through deep veins.

**Why it matters:** It identifies posture as a potential confounder in at-home compression experiments.

**Potential experiment signals:** Venous velocity, volume flow, common femoral/saphenous/profunda/superficial femoral/popliteal venous flow.

**Protocol takeaway:** Standardize posture during sessions and measurements when possible.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were extracted from accessible materials unless noted above. Key limitations: Small healthy-volunteer sample.; Hemodynamic endpoints only.; No symptom or performance outcomes.; Older device protocols may differ from consumer pants..

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
