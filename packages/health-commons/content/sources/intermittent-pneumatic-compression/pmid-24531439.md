This source is included for **Direct post-exercise recovery evidence: completed trials and dose studies**.

**Findings:** The experimental group improved pressure-to-pain threshold immediately after PPDC treatment and the improvement persisted through the remainder of the day; the control group did not show a statistical change.

**Why it matters:** This source directly evaluates athlete lower-extremity compression with a pain-sensitivity endpoint.

**Potential experiment signals:** Pressure-to-pain threshold, Lower-extremity tenderness after training.

**Protocol takeaway:** IPC may reduce tenderness or increase pressure-to-pain threshold after training, but this is not the same as improved performance.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were recovered in accessible extraction. Key limitations: Exploratory design.; Endpoint was pain threshold, not functional performance.; Permission-required article..
