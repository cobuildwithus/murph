This source is included for **Safety, contraindications, adverse events, and device instructions**.

**Findings:** The page repeats core contraindications and adds operational rules: consult a physician when concerned, do not use on sensitive skin or inflamed/infected/wounded areas, do not walk while wearing leg attachments, keep the power disconnect accessible, and stop for severe pain or unusual symptoms.

**Why it matters:** It gives direct device-family language for both pre-use exclusions and in-session termination rules.

**Potential experiment signals:** Pain severity; unusual symptoms; skin irritation or wounds; device-liquid/damage checks; walking/fall risk while wearing garments.

**Protocol takeaway:** Stop the device, remove or unzip attachments, and seek clinical advice for severe pain, unusual symptoms, emergency signs, local inflammation, wounds, bleeding, or unsafe device behavior.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
