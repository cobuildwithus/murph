This source is included for **Direct post-exercise recovery evidence: completed trials and dose studies**.

**Findings:** MISPD during 20-minute recovery improved the ability to perform a subsequent exercise bout by 45%, but blood lactate, pyruvate, ammonia, bicarbonate, and pH did not differ from passive recovery.

**Why it matters:** This is early direct lower-limb IPC performance evidence with a positive but uncertain signal.

**Potential experiment signals:** Time to exhaustion, Blood lactate, Pyruvate, Ammonia, Bicarbonate, pH.

**Protocol takeaway:** This older study suggests possible next-bout performance benefit, but it should be heavily caveated and not generalized to modern pants without corroboration.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were recovered in accessible extraction. Key limitations: n=11 and older technology.; Psychological effects could not be discounted.; Mechanistic blood-metabolite outcomes were null..
