This source is included for **Clinical DVT prophylaxis and hospital boundary**.

**Findings:** The review concluded that IPC adherence for VTE prevention is often inadequate and that reasons for inadequate adherence require attention. Safety/adverse-event notes: Adherence barriers may include comfort, mobility, nursing workflow, device availability, and skin/device tolerance, but exact factor frequencies were not re-extracted here. Limitations: Implementation review, not efficacy evidence; hospital context only.

**Why it matters:** It defines the supervised clinical boundary for IPC use and helps prevent over-claiming consumer benefits.

**Potential experiment signals:** Skin irritation or skin breaks; Discomfort/tolerance; Falls or tubing/device hazards; Adherence or wear time; DVT/PE only as clinician-managed safety context.

**Protocol takeaway:** If included in a self-experiment, wear time, discomfort, and interruptions must be logged.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
