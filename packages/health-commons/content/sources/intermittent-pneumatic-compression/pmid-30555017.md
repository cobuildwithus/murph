This source is included for **Clinical DVT prophylaxis and hospital boundary**.

**Findings:** In 1259 primary TKA cases, groups were chemoprophylaxis (414), IPC (425), and no pharmacological/mechanical prophylaxis (420). Overall DVT incidence was 14.8% in control, 6.3% in chemoprophylaxis, and 11.3% in IPC; chemoprophylaxis was significantly lower than the other two groups (p<0.001). Proximal DVT (1.9%, 0.7%, 0.9%; p>0.05) and symptomatic DVT (0.7%, 0%, 0.7%; p>0.05) did not significantly differ, and no symptomatic PE occurred during hospital stay.

**Why it matters:** This source is explicitly negative for IPC-alone prophylaxis in a surgical population and prevents outcome inflation.

**Potential experiment signals:** Skin/comfort monitoring; Adherence / device use; Bleeding only when clinical prophylaxis is involved; No recovery endpoint measured.

**Protocol takeaway:** IPC-alone thromboprophylaxis was not effective in this TKA population; do not generalize clinical prophylaxis evidence to recovery claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch:** TKA surgery patients in hospital do not match consumer recovery-pants users.

**Limitations:** Level III case-control design, not randomized according to the source abstract.; Single-institute, low-DVT-incidence population.; Postoperative day-6 imaging window.; Surgical thromboprophylaxis endpoints, not recovery outcomes..
