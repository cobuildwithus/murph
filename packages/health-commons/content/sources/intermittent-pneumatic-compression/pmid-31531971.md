This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** The review included four trials with 116 participants. Two IPC/mechanical compression trials reported PTS severity improvement with low-certainty evidence. One study reported adverse events in 9% of participants, including leg swelling, irritation, superficial bleeding, and itching.

**Why it matters:** Post-thrombotic syndrome is an adjacent clinical venous condition that can involve swelling and compression devices, but it is not a consumer recovery indication.

**Potential experiment signals:** PTS severity, Quality of life, Leg swelling, Skin irritation, Superficial bleeding or itching.

**Protocol takeaway:** Post-thrombotic syndrome should be treated as a clinician-supervised venous-disease context, not direct support for pneumatic pants.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
