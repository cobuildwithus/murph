This source is included for **Direct post-exercise recovery evidence: completed trials and dose studies**.

**Findings:** Among 72 ultramarathon finishers, pneumatic compression lowered overall muscular fatigue immediately after treatment versus supine rest, but there were no significant group or interaction effects on 400-m run time and no significant between-group outcomes from days 1-7.

**Why it matters:** This is a direct sports-recovery RCT with real-world endurance runners and a clearly bounded immediate-versus-extended effect.

**Potential experiment signals:** Overall muscular fatigue score, Muscle pain and soreness rating, 400-m run time, Daily recovery ratings.

**Protocol takeaway:** A single short IPC session may reduce immediate perceived fatigue after extreme running but should not be presented as restoring running performance.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were recovered from accessible extraction; safety should be bounded to study reporting. Key limitations: Extreme ultramarathon population.; Single 20-minute dose only.; Permission-required article; extraction relies on indexed abstract and accessible article snippets..
