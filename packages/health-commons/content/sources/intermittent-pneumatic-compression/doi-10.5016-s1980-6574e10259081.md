This source is included for **Direct post-exercise recovery evidence: completed trials and dose studies**.

**Findings:** The only significant treatment interaction was for vastus lateralis DOMS, with lower DOMS versus placebo from 48 hours after downhill running; other variables showed time effects rather than clear treatment effects.

**Why it matters:** This direct running-recovery study provides a clear dose and an important narrow positive outcome boundary.

**Potential experiment signals:** Vastus lateralis DOMS, Rectus femoris/vastus lateralis muscle quality, Concentric/eccentric peak torque.

**Protocol takeaway:** Repeated 30-minute 100 mmHg IPC may reduce localized DOMS after downhill running, but objective torque and muscle-quality recovery were not clearly improved.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were identified in accessible extraction. Key limitations: Small active male sample.; Contralateral-limb design may not match whole-body use.; Most objective recovery indicators were not improved..
