This source is included for **Safety, contraindications, adverse events, and device instructions**.

**Findings:** The page separates precautions from contraindications. Precautions include controlled hypertension, osteopenia/osteoporosis, pregnancy, diabetes, varicose veins, abnormal sensations, pressure sensitivity, recent injury or surgery, and heart disease. Contraindications include skin lesions, bone fracture or myositis ossificans, uncontrolled hypertension, acute or severe cardiac/liver/kidney disease, sensory neurologic conditions, bleeding disorders, blood-thinning or sensation-altering medications, direct surgical-site pressure, extreme pain, pacemaker/ICD or embolism history, significant arterial insufficiency, CHF-related edema, phlebitis, wound infection/cellulitis, severe PAOD, cardiopulmonary edema, known or suspected DVT/PE, decompensated cardiac failure, recent graft or bypass, suspected compartment syndrome, and conditions where increased venous or lymphatic return is undesirable.

**Why it matters:** It provides direct consumer-device safety boundaries for the same general pneumatic compression use case.

**Potential experiment signals:** Contraindication checklist; pain; neurologic sensation; medication flags; wound/infection status; cardiopulmonary and vascular risk.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use a broad no-go screen and medical-clearance rule for the listed high-risk conditions before home use.
