This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** Mean ulcer healing rate was 0.15 cm²/day in the IPC group versus 0.08 cm²/day in controls, with the difference reported as statistically significant.

**Why it matters:** Preserves older wound-care primary evidence with modest dose details.

**Potential experiment signals:** Ulcer area change, Complete healing, Pain/edema if available.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only venous-ulcer evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct unsupervised consumer-recovery evidence.

**Safety and boundary notes:** No adverse-event details were located in accessible extraction.

**Limitations:** Small historical study.; Venous insufficiency ulcer population.; Dose and duration details limited in accessible extraction..
