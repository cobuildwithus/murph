This source is included for **Clinical DVT prophylaxis and hospital boundary**.

**Findings:** Across 15 randomized trials with 2,270 patients, IPC reduced DVT risk versus no prophylaxis (RR 0.40, 95% CI 0.29 to 0.56; p<0.001). Safety/adverse-event notes: Adverse events were not the focus of the indexed abstract; supervised postoperative use remains the safety context. Limitations: Older postoperative trials; the authors called for contemporary randomized trials in hospitalized medical patients and combined prophylaxis settings.

**Why it matters:** It defines the supervised clinical boundary for IPC use and helps prevent over-claiming consumer benefits.

**Potential experiment signals:** No consumer endpoint; DVT only as clinician-managed safety context; Skin comfort/tolerance if using device.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not generalize postoperative DVT risk reduction to unsupervised pneumatic compression pants users.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
