This source is included for **Dose, hemodynamics, and wearable-relevant mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Accessible summary reports IPC increased venous peak velocity by a median 51% but did not significantly improve time-averaged maximum velocity or volume flow; IPC did not improve arterial hemodynamics, and NMES produced stronger hemodynamic effects.

**Why it matters:** It prevents overclaiming hemodynamic superiority and separates IPC from active muscle-pump alternatives.

**Potential experiment signals:** Venous peak velocity, time-averaged maximum velocity, volume flow, arterial hemodynamics, laser Doppler skin flow.

**Protocol takeaway:** Compare against active movement or NMES claims cautiously; IPC may not dominate all hemodynamic endpoints.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were extracted from accessible materials unless noted above. Key limitations: Sample count not recovered.; Comparator/device details incomplete in accessible extraction.; Acute physiology only.; Paywalled source..
