This source is included for **Safety, contraindications, adverse events, and device instructions**.

**Findings:** The manual lists pressure settings from 25 to 100 mmHg in 25 mmHg increments, app-adjustable increments of 5 mmHg, and selectable times of 20, 40, 60, or continuous. It describes sequential negative-gradient compression from foot toward heart and warns against use in people with diagnosed blood clots, DVT, phlebitis, numb or insensitive areas, poor circulation, swollen/inflamed areas, skin eruptions, or unexplained calf pain. Pain, discomfort, bruising, or irritation during or after use should lead to discontinuation and physician consultation.

**Why it matters:** This is direct consumer-device instruction material for a pneumatic-compression-boot variant close to pants use.

**Potential experiment signals:** Pressure; session time; comfort; pain; bruising; skin irritation; numbness; calf pain; clot-risk symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use conservative pressure/time, stop if pain or discomfort occurs, and do not use with blood clots, DVT, phlebitis, swelling/inflammation, skin eruptions, numbness, poor circulation, or unexplained calf pain without medical evaluation.
