This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** During IPC, flow volume increased significantly in popliteal, gastrocnemial, and collateral arteries and skin blood flux increased. Two limbs without flow increase had significant popliteal venous reflux and were amputated shortly after.

**Why it matters:** Highlights vascular physiology and nonresponse factors that matter for safety screening.

**Potential experiment signals:** Skin blood flux, Arterial flow, Venous reflux screening, Toe pressure/ABI.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mechanism/safety-boundary evidence, not recovery efficacy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct unsupervised consumer-recovery evidence.

**Safety and boundary notes:** The two nonresponding limbs with significant venous reflux and subsequent amputation are an important clinical boundary; this was severe CLI, not a safety signal for healthy users.

**Limitations:** Acute mechanistic study, not efficacy trial.; Severe CLI population.; No long-term outcomes from the intervention..
