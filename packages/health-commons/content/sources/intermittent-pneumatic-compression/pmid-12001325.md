This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** Accessible abstract states some improvement in healing rate against very high healing rates in both groups and no benefit for preventing recurrence. A review table reported 87% healed in the IPC group and 92% in controls.

**Why it matters:** Preserves mixed/limited venous-ulcer evidence and prevents overgeneralizing IPC wound-care effects.

**Potential experiment signals:** Healing proportion, Healing rate, Ulcer recurrence, Adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only wound-care evidence with mixed signal.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct unsupervised consumer-recovery evidence.

**Safety and boundary notes:** No adverse-event details were located in accessible extraction.

**Limitations:** Rights unclear; full trial text not available.; Some sample and healing details came from an accessible review table/snippet rather than full primary text.; Venous-ulcer wound-care population only..
