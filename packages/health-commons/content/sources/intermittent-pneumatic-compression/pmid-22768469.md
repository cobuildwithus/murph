This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** Accessible abstracts state that IPC can reduce volume of lymphedematous limbs alone or with decongestive therapy, but there is no consensus about frequency or treatment parameters.

**Why it matters:** This broad therapy review reinforces that IPC dosing remains unresolved in clinical populations, which argues against overconfident consumer dosing claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** Limb volume, Swelling symptoms, Dose parameters, Adherence and comfort.

**Protocol takeaway:** IPC has clinical lymphedema use, but the review does not establish a consumer pneumatic-pants protocol or dose.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
