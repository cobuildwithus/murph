This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** Total arm volume decreased 12.2% in the IPC group and 14.9% in the MLD group; both changes were significant but between-group difference was not significant (p=0.582). ASES improved in both groups; some quality-of-life domains improved only in the MLD group.

**Why it matters:** Shows similar but not superior clinical volume effects, useful for balanced context.

**Potential experiment signals:** Arm volume, ASES shoulder/elbow function, EORTC quality-of-life domains.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only; avoid superiority claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct unsupervised consumer-recovery evidence.

**Safety and boundary notes:** No adverse-event details were located in accessible extraction.

**Limitations:** Small upper-extremity clinical study.; Different co-interventions across arms.; No clear superiority for IPC..
