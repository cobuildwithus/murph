This source is included for **Direct post-exercise recovery evidence: completed trials and dose studies**.

**Findings:** After 100 drop jumps, EPC and static compression were greater than no-treatment control for selected squat-jump propulsive force/power measures at follow-up; many other recovery markers reflected exercise-induced time effects.

**Why it matters:** This controlled trial directly compares pneumatic and static compression after lower-limb muscle-damaging exercise.

**Potential experiment signals:** Squat-jump average relative propulsive force, Squat-jump propulsive power, CMJ metrics, Soreness, Fatigue, Peripheral circulation.

**Protocol takeaway:** A single 30-minute partial lower-leg EPC session may help maintain selected jump-performance metrics, but claims should be limited and compared with static compression.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were recovered in accessible extraction. Key limitations: Male sample only.; Partial lower-leg device rather than full pants.; Permission-required/copyrighted article..
