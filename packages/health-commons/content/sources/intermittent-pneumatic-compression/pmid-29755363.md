This source is included for **Comparators and adjacent modalities**.

**Findings:** Cross-modality recovery meta-analysis found massage, immersion/cold modalities, active recovery, cryotherapy, and compression garments changed recovery markers, with massage strongest for soreness and fatigue.

**Why it matters:** High-recall comparator synthesis helps bound what recovery modalities can plausibly affect and highlights the difference between soreness/fatigue outcomes and objective markers.

**Potential experiment signals:** Delayed-onset muscle soreness, perceived fatigue, muscle damage markers, inflammatory markers.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as adjacent comparator context: recovery modalities may improve soreness or fatigue, but this source cannot support IPC-pants-specific efficacy.

**Safety and limitations:** No IPC-pants-specific adverse-event extraction was available from this comparator review. Key limitations: Multi-modality synthesis rather than IPC-specific evidence.; Included studies were heterogeneous in interventions, populations, and outcomes.; Static compression garments should not be treated as pneumatic compression pants..

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
