This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** Response (≥25% reduction in delta circumference) occurred in 25% of the PC group versus 20% of controls (p=0.59). Mean delta decrease was 1.9±3.7 cm with PC and 0.5±3.3 cm in controls. The authors concluded IPC had a limited clinical role.

**Why it matters:** Important negative/no-clear-benefit evidence to balance positive lymphedema studies.

**Potential experiment signals:** Circumference delta, Responder rate, Compliance/tolerability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only null boundary: IPC is not universally superior in clinical lymphedema.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct unsupervised consumer-recovery evidence.

**Safety and boundary notes:** No adverse-event details were located in accessible extraction.

**Limitations:** Historical upper-extremity lymphedema RCT.; Comparator was no-treatment/hygienic guidance rather than contemporary CDT.; Paywalled/copyrighted article; do not commit PDF..
