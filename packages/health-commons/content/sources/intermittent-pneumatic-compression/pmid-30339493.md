This source is included for **Clinical edema, lymphedema, venous, PAD, and wound-care boundary**.

**Findings:** The review included 16 studies. It found no clear dosage guideline, low-level evidence for significant outcomes with 45–60 minute sessions at 30–60 mmHg in sequential IPC, and adverse events in one trial including swelling, pain, numbness, and possible inflammatory or infection-related events.

**Why it matters:** This review is one of the few sources centered on IPC dosage and therefore is useful for defining uncertainty around pressure/session claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** Pressure, Session length, Limb volume, Pain or numbness, Proximal or contralateral swelling.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not present a single optimal consumer pressure or session length based on lymphedema dosage literature.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
