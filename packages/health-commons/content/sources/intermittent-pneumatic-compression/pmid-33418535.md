This source is included for **Direct post-exercise recovery evidence: reviews and evidence maps**.

**Findings:** The critically appraised topic selected three studies: two randomized controlled trials and one randomized crossover study. The clinical bottom line was that IPC may provide short-term DOMS relief, but does not provide continued relief from exercise-induced muscle damage in endurance athletes.

**Why it matters:** This source keeps the protocol honest for endurance-sport use cases, where users may expect faster recovery from long runs, races, or cycling sessions.

**Potential experiment signals:** DOMS, soreness duration, perceived short-term relief, CK where available, and endurance performance recovery.

**Protocol takeaway:** Pneumatic compression pants can be framed as a possible short-term comfort tool, not as proven EIMD treatment for endurance athletes.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`, specifically as mixed/null direct evidence.
