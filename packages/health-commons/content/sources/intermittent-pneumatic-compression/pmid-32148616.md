This source is included for **Direct post-exercise recovery evidence: completed trials and dose studies**.

**Findings:** There was no significant difference between control and treatment runs for CRP (condition p=0.48; interaction p=0.33) or pain (condition p=0.89; interaction p=0.72); authors concluded no substantial IPC benefit for recovery.

**Why it matters:** This direct running study tests a multi-day consumer-like repeated-use protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** CRP, Pain rating, Run order and environmental conditions.

**Protocol takeaway:** Repeated IPC after a long run did not show substantial recovery benefit for CRP or pain in this small study.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were recovered in accessible extraction. Key limitations: Small sample of 10 runners.; Control and treatment runs differed in running time and seasonal temperature context.; Open article, but no adverse events recovered..
