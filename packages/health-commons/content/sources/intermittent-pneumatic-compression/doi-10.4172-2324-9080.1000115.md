This source is included for **Direct post-exercise recovery evidence: completed trials and dose studies**.

**Findings:** After Wingate testing, blood lactate at 20 minutes was lower with IPC than passive recovery; IPC and active recovery were not significantly different.

**Why it matters:** This direct lower-limb anaerobic study provides device dose and comparator context for passive vs active recovery.

**Potential experiment signals:** Blood lactate at 20 minutes, Comparison with active recovery, Comparison with passive recovery.

**Protocol takeaway:** IPC may lower lactate during inactive recovery compared with passive rest, but active recovery may perform similarly.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse events were recovered in accessible extraction. Key limitations: Small sample.; Single acute lactate endpoint.; Rights unclear; do not commit downloadable PDF until verified..
