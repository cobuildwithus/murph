This source is included for **cardiometabolic association context**. It reported that lower slow-wave sleep percentage was associated with incident hypertension among older men after adjustment for several sleep and health factors.

**Murph implication:** This supports a cautious “associated with blood-pressure risk” claim, not a promise that a protocol or wearable-stage increase will lower blood pressure.
