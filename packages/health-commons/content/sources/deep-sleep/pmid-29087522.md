This source is included for **Sleep Heart Health Study association context**. It quantified the association between polysomnography-measured slow-wave sleep and incident hypertension in a large community cohort.

**Murph implication:** It justifies keeping morning blood pressure visible on experiments that target sleep depth, while the consumer deep-sleep metric remains a secondary trend signal.
