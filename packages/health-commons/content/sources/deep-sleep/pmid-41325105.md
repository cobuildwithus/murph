This source is included for **mechanistic restoration context**. It reviews evidence that brain-clearance processes may contribute to restorative sleep and notes that human measurement methods remain under development.

**Murph implication:** The deep sleep page can mention glymphatic biology as a plausible research direction, but it must not imply that a consumer deep-sleep score measures clearance.
