This source is included for **consumer sleep tracker validation context**. It compares multiple consumer sleep trackers with polysomnography and highlights why device-specific validation matters.

**Murph implication:** Deep sleep minutes should be interpreted as an inferred stage estimate. Murph should require same-device trend windows and should avoid cross-device or cross-person comparisons.
