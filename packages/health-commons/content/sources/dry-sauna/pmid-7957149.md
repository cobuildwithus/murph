This source is included for **safety contraindications**.

## Why it matters

Use as a narrow medication-interaction finding; do not infer all anticholinergic/beta-blocker combinations are safe in sauna.

## Findings captured

- In healthy men, small oral-dose scopolamine alone or with atenolol reportedly produced no marked cardiovascular strain during Finnish sauna baths; this is a narrow null/reassuring finding, not a blanket medication-safety claim.

## Protocol takeaway

Cardiovascular effects of atenolol, scopolamine and their combination on healthy men in Finnish sauna baths — safety-only appraisal

## Important limits

Healthy men only.; Specific drugs and doses; not generalizable to frail, older, cardiovascular-disease, or polypharmacy populations.
