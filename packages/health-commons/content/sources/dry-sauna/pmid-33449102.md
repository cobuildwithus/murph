**Findings:** This exertional-heatstroke source is a safety boundary for ear/aural temperature: aural thermometry should not be treated as a definitive internal-temperature measure when heat illness is possible. Effect/estimate: No numerical estimate extracted in this batch; direction is caution against aural thermometry as a replacement for validated internal measures in EHS.

**Why it matters:** Safety-boundary source for why ear temperature should not be treated as definitive core temperature in high-risk heat illness contexts.

**Potential experiment signals:** core body temperature, measurement caveats

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use aural thermometer readings to rule out dangerous hyperthermia in sauna safety guidance.

**Safety/adverse-event notes:** Safety boundary: heatstroke diagnosis and cooling decisions require valid internal-temperature assessment.
