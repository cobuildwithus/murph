This source is included for **Long-term Finnish cohorts and observational outcome context**.

**Findings:** Both frequent sauna bathing and higher cardiorespiratory fitness were reported as individually associated with lower sudden cardiac death risk, with the combination showing the strongest inverse association.

**Why it matters:** Long-term sudden cardiac death context and important fitness-confounding boundary evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** sudden cardiac death context, fitness confounding.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context that post-workout/fitness status may matter when interpreting sauna evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Study design:** Long-term prospective cohort examining sauna plus cardiorespiratory fitness.
- **Population:** Caucasian middle-aged Finnish men in the KIHD cohort.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Combined sauna bathing frequency and cardiorespiratory fitness categories.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower sauna-frequency and lower-fitness categories.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Long-term sudden-cardiac-death follow-up.
- **Endpoints:** sudden cardiac death, cardiorespiratory fitness interaction.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No acute adverse-event data for daily 93 C post-workout sauna.
- **Limitations:** Observational; fitness and sauna use can proxy broader health behaviors; men-only cohort; not randomized.
- **Population mismatch:** Does not test Bryan Johnson dosing, post-workout timing, or short-term wearable endpoints.
- **Directness to Bryan Johnson Sauna:** same_mechanism; this is not direct evidence for daily 20 minutes at 93 C / 200 F after workouts.
