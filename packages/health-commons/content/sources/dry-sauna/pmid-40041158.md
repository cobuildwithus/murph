This source is included for **Near-direct dry-sauna interventions and acute dry-sauna dose evidence**.

**Findings:** `finding:pmid-40041158-facial-cooling-thermal-comfort-il6` — Facial cooling kept thermal comfort more positive than heat alone (P≤0.002), removed the sex difference in discomfort seen during heat alone, and did not diminish the acute IL-6 increase compared with heat alone (HEAT vs FAN P=1.00), although rectal-temperature rise was slightly lower with cooling.

**Why it matters:** Directly relevant to Bryan-style cooling adjuncts because it tests facial cooling during dry passive heating and includes sex-specific perception data.

**Potential experiment signals:** thermal-comfort, thermal-sensation, rectal-temperature, interleukin-6, sex-differences.

**Protocol takeaway:** Facial cooling appears useful for comfort without eliminating the acute IL-6 response in this lower-temperature dry-heating setup.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
