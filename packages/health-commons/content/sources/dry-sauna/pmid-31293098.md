This source is included for **Cardiovascular, autonomic, blood-pressure, and clinical sauna context**.

**Findings:** In a small randomized crossover study of healthy middle-aged and older adults, typical Finnish sauna bathing did not acutely improve brachial FMD or reactive hyperemia.

**Why it matters:** Randomized crossover acute Finnish sauna study in healthy middle-aged and older adults; did not find acute improvement in brachial FMD or reactive hyperemia.

**Potential experiment signals:** brachial artery flow-mediated dilation; reactive hyperemia; vascular function.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as null/mixed acute vascular-function evidence; do not cherry-pick positive vascular studies only.
