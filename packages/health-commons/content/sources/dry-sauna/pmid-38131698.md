This source is included for **Measurement artifacts: core temperature, wearables, sweat, and hydration**.

**Findings:** The review describes HRV as an autonomic-nervous-system signal and compares HRV monitoring across smart wearables, while emphasizing that HRV tracking remains device- and algorithm-dependent. Effect/estimate: No pooled accuracy estimate extracted; review role is context for wearable HRV interpretation.

**Why it matters:** Current review for interpreting HRV-RMSSD from wearables rather than assuming equivalence to ECG-derived HRV.

**Potential experiment signals:** HRV / RMSSD, measurement caveats

**Protocol takeaway:** Use wearable HRV as a trend/context metric, not as a definitive physiologic endpoint for sauna efficacy.

**Safety/adverse-event notes:** Measurement note: proprietary HRV outputs should not be treated as interchangeable with ECG-derived RMSSD without validation.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Narrative review; not sauna-specific and device algorithms change over time. Population mismatch: General HRV wearable literature, not sauna intervention participants.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
