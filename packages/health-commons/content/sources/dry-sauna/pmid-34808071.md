## Why it matters

## Findings captured

- The systematic review and meta-analysis reported that passive heat therapy may be beneficial in type 2 diabetes, with slight improvement in certain cardiometabolic parameters; exact pooled estimates and participant totals were not extracted from accessible records.

## Protocol takeaway

Type 2 diabetes heat-therapy meta-analysis suggests possible slight cardiometabolic benefit.

## Important limits

Do not translate this into a sauna protocol treatment claim for diabetes.
