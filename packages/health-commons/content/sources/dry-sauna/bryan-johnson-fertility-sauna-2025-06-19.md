This source is included for **Fertility, semen, and groin-cooling safety/context**.

**Findings:** Bryan Johnson reported a 31% fertility-health improvement after 21 days of sauna and explicitly noted that sauna is not known to improve fertility health, suggesting that icing the testes might have contributed.

**Why it matters:** Preserves direct protocol provenance for the 200°F/20-minute daily sauna plus fertility/groin-cooling claim context.

**Potential experiment signals:** routine-provenance, dose, cooling-tactics, external-claims, fertility.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as external protocol provenance and a hypothesis-generating self-report; do not use as evidence that sauna improves fertility.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness and limitations:** Direct owner-reported protocol context, but not independent clinical evidence. Single-person uncontrolled social-media report; endpoint definition and raw data were not provided; not independently verified.
