This source is included for **Cardiovascular, autonomic, blood-pressure, and clinical sauna context**.

**Findings:** In 19 healthy adults, a 25-minute hot sauna session produced BP and HR responses comparable to submaximal dynamic exercise, with post-sauna BP reduction and rising HR during heat exposure.

**Why it matters:** Small acute study comparing BP and HR during a sauna bath with submaximal dynamic exercise in healthy volunteers.

**Potential experiment signals:** blood pressure; heart rate; rate-pressure product; cardiac strain context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to frame sauna as an acute cardiovascular load similar to light/submaximal exercise; not as exercise replacement.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
