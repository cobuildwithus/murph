This source is included for **safety contraindications**.

## Why it matters

Use as reassuring-but-limited renal safety context; do not claim sauna prevents kidney disease or is safe for CKD patients.

## Findings captured

- In 2,071 middle-aged Finnish men with normal kidney function, habitual sauna frequency was not clearly associated with CKD risk over median 25.7 years (188 CKD cases; HR 0.84, 95% CI 0.46-1.53), but this is observational context and not proof of renal safety for clinical populations.

## Protocol takeaway

Sauna bathing, renal function and chronic kidney disease: Cross-sectional and longitudinal findings from the KIHD study — context-only appraisal

## Important limits

Observational cohort; association not causation.; Middle-aged Finnish men; limited generalizability to women, kidney disease patients, medication users, or acute dehydration.
