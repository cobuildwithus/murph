## Why it matters

Useful for feasibility context but not a health efficacy claim.

## Findings captured

- The review discusses post-exercise sauna and hot-water immersion as practical alternatives when barriers to traditional exercise heat acclimation exist, while highlighting gaps in translating heat acclimation from laboratory protocols to real-world elite sport settings.

## Protocol takeaway

Post-exercise sauna may be practical in athlete heat-acclimation contexts.

## Important limits

Elite athlete implementation evidence does not generalize directly to Murph wellness users.
