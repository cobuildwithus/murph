- **Study design:** Finnish cohort analysis of systolic blood pressure and sauna interaction.
- **Population:** Finnish men in a cohort context.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Sauna bathing frequency and systolic blood pressure interaction.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower versus higher sauna frequency across systolic blood pressure categories.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Long-term mortality follow-up.
- **Endpoints:** mortality risk, systolic blood pressure interaction.

- **Directness to Bryan Johnson Sauna:** same_mechanism; this is not direct evidence for daily 20 minutes at 93 C / 200 F after workouts.
