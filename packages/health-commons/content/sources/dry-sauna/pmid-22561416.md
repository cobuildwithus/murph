This source is included for **Post-exercise sauna, heat-acclimation, and performance variants**.

**Findings:** A single sauna session, with or without preceding aerobic exercise, induced acute autonomic cardiovascular changes in untreated hypertension with recovery-period normalization reported.

**Why it matters:** Clinical-supervised autonomic safety/context evidence for sauna in a hypertensive population, not efficacy evidence for Bryan Johnson Sauna.

**Potential experiment signals:** HRV RMSSD, resting heart rate, symptoms during heat exposure

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as clinical safety/context evidence for acute autonomic strain, especially in hypertensive users.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
