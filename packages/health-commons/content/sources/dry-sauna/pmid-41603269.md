This source is included for **reviews meta guidelines**.

## Why it matters

Supports modality-specific endpoint tracking and prevents overgeneralizing from water immersion to sauna.

## Findings captured

- The meta-analysis included 20 studies and 384 participants; single hot-water immersion increased heart rate by 28 bpm (95% CI 19 to 36.2) and lowered diastolic blood pressure by 5 mmHg (95% CI −9 to −1) and mean arterial pressure by 7 mmHg (95% CI −12 to −1), while repeated immersion reduced resting heart rate by 3 bpm (95% CI −6 to −1); other cardiovascular markers were not significantly changed and only one study addressed cardiorespiratory fitness, leaving overall benefits inconclusive.

## Protocol takeaway

Hot-water immersion changes acute cardiovascular markers but long-term benefits are inconclusive.

## Important limits

Adjacent water immersion is not dry sauna; cardiorespiratory evidence was sparse.
