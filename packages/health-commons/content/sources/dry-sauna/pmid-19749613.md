This source is included for **Safety, heat illness, medications, pregnancy, alcohol, older-adult risk**.

**Findings:** In 77 Swedish sauna-related deaths, 82% were men, 84% were found dead in a sauna, and among cases with blood alcohol concentration measured, 49 of 69 (71%) tested positive; identified major explanatory states included alcohol-related and cardiovascular causes. The study concluded that middle-aged men with heavy alcohol consumption were an important risk group and noted that among cases found dead in a sauna, all but two were alone.

**Why it matters:** It identifies alcohol, cardiovascular disease, and bathing alone as recurrent serious-risk themes.

**Potential experiment signals:** alcohol-use screen, cardiovascular history screen, sauna-alone screen, dizziness or altered consciousness.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to support alcohol avoidance, cardiovascular screening, and not using sauna alone when impaired or symptomatic.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
