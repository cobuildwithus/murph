This source is included for **acute mechanistic**.

## Why it matters

Use to justify conservative dose limits and menstrual-cycle safety cautions.

## Findings captured

- Accessible abstract text reports no significant changes in thyroid hormones, TSH, testosterone, FSH, or LH; cortisol and ACTH decreased; urinary catecholamines increased slightly; male growth hormone and prolactin increased markedly, and female prolactin increased more than fourfold.
- The abstract reports transient amenorrhoea in five of seven female subjects during the repeated high-dose sauna exposure, making this a safety-boundary source rather than a protocol-efficacy source.

## Protocol takeaway

Extreme repeated sauna exposure changed endocrine markers and produced transient amenorrhea in five of seven women.

## Important limits

The exposure was one hour twice daily for seven days, far above the Murph default protocol.
