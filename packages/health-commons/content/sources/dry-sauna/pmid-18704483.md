This source is included for **Core-temperature threshold mechanisms and heat-stress biology**.

**Findings:** Serum extracellular Hsp72 increased from pre- to post-exposure, but the increase did not differ between high- and low-heat-storage-rate trials; the authors interpreted the response as more related to the core temperature attained than to heat-storage rate.

**Why it matters:** Separates attained internal temperature from the rate of heat storage, which helps constrain mechanistic interpretation of pill-temperature thresholds.

**Potential experiment signals:** core body temperature, rate of heat storage, extracellular Hsp72, thermal strain.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as same-mechanism context only: extracellular Hsp72 increased across heat exposure but was not different between high and low heat-storage rates when participants reached the same rectal-temperature endpoint.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
