This source is included for **Cardiovascular, autonomic, blood-pressure, and clinical sauna context**.

**Findings:** In 41 adults with stable CAD, 8 weeks of Finnish sauna use did not improve FMD, cf-PWV, systolic BP, or diastolic BP compared with lifestyle control, despite signs of heat acclimation.

**Why it matters:** Eight-week randomized trial in stable CAD found no improvement in key vascular-health markers despite heat-acclimation signals.

**Potential experiment signals:** brachial artery flow-mediated dilation; carotid-femoral pulse-wave velocity; reactive hyperemia; systolic blood pressure; diastolic blood pressure; heat acclimation markers.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mixed/null RCT evidence for vascular endpoints; do not ignore because it is negative.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
