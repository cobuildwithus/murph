This source is included for **reviews meta guidelines**.

## Why it matters

Useful for endpoint selection and mechanistic interpretation, not direct sauna claims.

## Findings captured

- The review describes exercise heat acclimation as improving thermoregulation, attenuating physiological strain, reducing serious heat-illness risk, and improving aerobic performance in warm or hot conditions, with adaptations including improved sweating and skin blood flow, lower body temperatures, reduced cardiovascular strain, fluid-balance changes, and cellular protection.

## Protocol takeaway

Exercise heat acclimation review identifies adaptation mechanisms relevant to heat stress.

## Important limits

Exercise heat acclimation is not equivalent to passive Finnish sauna exposure.
