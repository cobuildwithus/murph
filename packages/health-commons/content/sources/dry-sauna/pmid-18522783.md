This source is included for **safety contraindications**.

## Why it matters

Shows adjacent thermal therapy may affect cardiopulmonary endpoints under clinical supervision, but it is not Finnish dry sauna.

## Findings captured

- Exercise time increased from 360±107 to 392±97 seconds (p=0.032), and St George’s Respiratory Questionnaire total score improved from 59.7±16.9 to 55.3±17.2 (p=0.002).
- The authors concluded repeated Waon therapy improved right ventricular positive dP/dt, pulmonary hypertension during exercise, exercise tolerance, and quality of life.
- No adverse effects were observed in the accessible report.

## Protocol takeaway

Supervised low-temperature Waon therapy improved exercise-related outcomes in severe COPD.

## Important limits

60°C far-infrared Waon plus blanket warming in severe COPD is a population and modality mismatch; do not promote as direct protocol evidence.
