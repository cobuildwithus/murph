This source is included for **safety contraindications**.

## Why it matters

Useful for sauna safety operations and adaptation logic, but not direct sauna efficacy evidence.

## Findings captured

- The consensus recommendation identifies heat acclimatization as the most important intervention to reduce physiological strain and recommends repeated exercise-heat exposures over 1–2 weeks.
- The consensus recommends beginning euhydrated, minimizing dehydration, using cooling strategies where appropriate, and providing opportunities for cooling, rehydration, and emergency management.

## Protocol takeaway

Heat-stress consensus supports gradual acclimatization, hydration, cooling, and emergency planning.

## Important limits

Exercise-heat guidance in athletes differs from passive sauna exposure.
