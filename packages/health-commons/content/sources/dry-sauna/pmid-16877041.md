This source is included for **Post-exercise sauna, heat-acclimation, and performance variants**.

**Findings:** Post-exercise sauna increased treadmill run time to exhaustion by 32% with a 90% confidence limit of 21–43% in six competitive male athletes. The sauna block was associated with plasma-volume expansion (+7.1%; 90% CL 5.6–8.7) and a smaller/inconclusive red-cell volume signal.

**Why it matters:** Classic athlete post-exercise sauna evidence with a high temperature near the Bryan range, but tiny sample and performance-only endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** exercise performance, plasma volume, resting heart rate

**Protocol takeaway:** Treat as adjacent athlete/performance evidence, not general health or longevity evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
