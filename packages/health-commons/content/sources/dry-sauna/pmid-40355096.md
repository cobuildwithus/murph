This source is included for **Near-direct dry-sauna interventions and acute dry-sauna dose evidence**.

**Findings:** `finding:pmid-40355096-cooling-meta-analysis-perception` — The systematic review/meta-analysis found physical-performance effects inconclusive, cognitive data not poolable, improved thermal sensation and comfort, reduced target-site skin temperature, negligible effects on heart rate and core/mean skin temperature, rare adverse events, and no superior cooling subgroup.

**Why it matters:** Most current synthesis for cooling adjunct boundaries: perception effects appear clearer than core-temperature or HR changes.

**Potential experiment signals:** thermal-comfort, thermal-sensation, target-site-skin-temperature, heart-rate, core-temperature, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to keep cooling claims narrow: perception benefits, inconclusive performance effects, negligible core/HR changes.
