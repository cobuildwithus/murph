This source is included for **Fertility, semen, and groin-cooling safety/context**.

**Findings:** A Lancet report described six men with poor semen quality and raised testicular temperature treated with a scrotal hypothermia appliance, with semen-quality improvement attributed to hypothermia in accessible summaries.

**Why it matters:** Historical adjacent evidence for scrotal cooling improving semen quality in selected men.

**Potential experiment signals:** groin-cooling-boundary, semen-analysis.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as historical adjacent context.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness and limitations:** Adjacent chronic cooling intervention, not dry sauna groin icing. Very small older report; infertility context; chronic appliance rather than sauna-session ice; detailed methods/results not extracted.
