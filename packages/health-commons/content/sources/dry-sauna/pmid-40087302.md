This source is included for **Cardiovascular, autonomic, blood-pressure, and clinical sauna context**.

**Findings:** A large meta-analysis of over 400 laboratory heat-exposure studies reports consistent heat-induced cardiac-load changes, useful for safety framing rather than sauna efficacy.

**Why it matters:** Large 2025 meta-analysis of laboratory heat exposure showing heat-induced cardiac-load changes across thousands of participant-exposures; useful as safety/context, not sauna-efficacy evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** core temperature; heart rate; cardiac function; cardiovascular strain.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for cardiac-load and heat-stress safety context only.
