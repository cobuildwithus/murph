This source is included for **Post-exercise sauna, heat-acclimation, and performance variants**.

**Findings:** The first sauna session was extracted as increasing heart rate and reducing HRV acutely; repeated-bout adaptation remained unclear.

**Why it matters:** Useful for acute autonomic strain and measurement interpretation, not for direct Bryan claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** HRV RMSSD, resting heart rate, session strain

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a measurement/safety boundary: HRV can fall acutely after sauna exposure.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
