This source is included for **Cardiovascular, autonomic, blood-pressure, and clinical sauna context**.

**Findings:** In 10 young and 8 middle-aged adults, acute sauna heating produced similar vascular shear/blood-flow responses, with BP reduction reported in middle-aged but not young participants.

**Why it matters:** Acute sauna-heating study comparing young and middle-aged adults; useful for age-related vascular-response context.

**Potential experiment signals:** shear rate; blood flow; blood pressure; vascular response.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as age-response context only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
