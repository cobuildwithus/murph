This source is included for **Near-direct dry-sauna interventions and acute dry-sauna dose evidence**.

**Findings:** `finding:pmid-2759081-finnish-sauna-haemodynamic-hormonal` — The extracted summary shows dose-related strain: oral temperature rose by about 1.1, 1.9, and 3.2°C across the three heat conditions; heart rate rose by about 60%, 90%, and 130%; noradrenaline and prolactin increased most in the hottest or humid-exhaustion condition.

**Why it matters:** Useful mechanistic dose-response context for high-heat sauna strain and endocrine response.

**Potential experiment signals:** heart-rate, oral-temperature, noradrenaline, prolactin, beta-endorphin, cortisol.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for physiological response boundaries, not efficacy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
