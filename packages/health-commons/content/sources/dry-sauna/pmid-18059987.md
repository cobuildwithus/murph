This source is included for **Measurement artifacts: core temperature, wearables, sweat, and hydration**.

**Findings:** Outdoor exercise-heat validation found that peripheral body-temperature devices, including axillary-type assessments, can be invalid against rectal temperature because readings are influenced by ambient temperature, skin temperature, blood flow, and sweating. Effect/estimate: Direction only: several non-rectal/peripheral devices were not valid substitutes for rectal temperature under outdoor heat stress.

**Why it matters:** High-priority validation/caveat source for oral, tympanic, temporal, and ingestible/rectal temperature comparisons under heat stress.

**Potential experiment signals:** core body temperature, measurement caveats

**Protocol takeaway:** Use this as a measurement guardrail: body-temperature site matters, and peripheral readings should not be promoted as direct core-temperature evidence.

**Safety/adverse-event notes:** Safety boundary: peripheral estimates can under- or misrepresent hyperthermia during heat stress.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Outdoor athletic heat exposure, not sauna; detailed device-specific estimates were not extracted in this batch. Population mismatch: Exercise outdoors in heat, not passive dry-sauna bathing.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
