This source is included for **Fertility, semen, and groin-cooling safety/context**.

**Findings:** This human experimental heat study reported decreased sperm count and motility after local testicular hyperthermia created by raising the testes toward the inguinal canal, enhancing artificial-cryptorchidism inhibition.

**Why it matters:** Human model for testicular heat impairing spermatogenesis.

**Potential experiment signals:** semen-analysis, heat-risk.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as heat-risk context only.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness and limitations:** Adjacent heat-dose model, not dry sauna protocol. Older artificial-cryptorchidism model; participant count not extracted; not sauna; no cooling arm.
