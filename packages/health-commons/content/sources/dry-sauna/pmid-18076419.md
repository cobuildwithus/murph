This source is included for **reviews meta guidelines**.

## Why it matters

Use cautious reproductive-health safety wording without asserting confirmed sauna harm.

## Findings captured

- The review reports conflicting evidence for occupational high-heat exposures and notes that available studies do not sufficiently support a postulated negative effect of sauna visits on semen quality, while local genital heat stress remains a plausible fertility concern based on other heat-exposure and varicocele evidence.

## Protocol takeaway

Sauna-specific semen-quality harm was not confirmed, but genital heat remains a fertility boundary.

## Important limits

Not an efficacy source and not specific to the Murph protocol.
