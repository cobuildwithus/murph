This source is included for **Safety, heat illness, medications, pregnancy, alcohol, older-adult risk**.

**Findings:** In this retrospective chart review, 209 sauna-related injury patients had 274 diagnoses; the most common injury causes were slip/fall (157; 57.5%) and dizziness/syncope (82; 30.0%). The study reported fractures, concussions, burns, and brain bleeding among sauna-related injuries; nine patients required surgical treatment, and one patient sustained grade IIB–III burns while unconscious with alcohol intoxication.

**Why it matters:** It identifies practical experiment signals that are often ignored: wet-floor falls, dizziness, syncope, and burns.

**Potential experiment signals:** dizziness or presyncope, falls or near-falls, hydration status, slip-resistant footwear/use environment, burns.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to add practical injury-prevention guidance: hydrate, stop for dizziness/syncope, avoid alcohol, and manage slip/fall hazards.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
