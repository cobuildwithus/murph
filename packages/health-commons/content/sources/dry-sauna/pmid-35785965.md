This source is included for **Post-exercise sauna, heat-acclimation, and performance variants**.

**Findings:** EXS showed greater CRF change than exercise-only (+2.7 mL/kg/min; 95% CI +0.2 to +5.3) in the 8-week randomized trial. EXS had lower systolic blood pressure (−8.0 mmHg; 95% CI −14.6 to −1.4) and lower total cholesterol than exercise-only.

**Why it matters:** This is the strongest modern randomized evidence in this batch for a repeated post-exercise sauna variant, but it is not Bryan Johnson’s daily high-heat protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** estimated VO2max / cardiorespiratory fitness, morning blood pressure, resting heart rate and tolerability notes

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent evidence that post-exercise sauna can change cardiovascular endpoints when layered onto exercise; do not treat it as standalone or Bryan-dose evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
