This source is included for **Core-temperature threshold mechanisms and heat-stress biology**.

**Findings:** Heat and exercise acclimation increased resting intracellular Hsp72 and inhibited or blunted the exercise-induced intracellular and plasma Hsp72 increases observed before acclimation.

**Why it matters:** Repeated heat exposure can change baseline and acute Hsp72 responses, which matters for any repeated sauna protocol where adaptation may alter biomarker response over time.

**Potential experiment signals:** heat acclimation, intracellular Hsp72, plasma Hsp72, resting heart rate, thermal tolerance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as same-mechanism context only: repeated exercise heat can increase resting intracellular Hsp72 while blunting acute exercise-induced Hsp72 signals after acclimation.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
