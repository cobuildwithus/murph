This source is included for **Fertility, semen, and groin-cooling safety/context**.

**Findings:** Nocturnal scrotal cooling and heat-stress behavior changes lowered scrotal temperature by about 1°C and were reported to improve sperm concentration, total sperm output, motility, and morphology in men with oligoasthenoteratozoospermia.

**Why it matters:** Human scrotal-cooling evidence with semen endpoints, relevant to the cooling rationale but not direct protocol proof.

**Potential experiment signals:** groin-cooling-boundary, semen-analysis, heat-risk.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent cooling context only; do not infer that brief sauna ice-pack use improves fertility.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness and limitations:** Adjacent scrotal-cooling intervention rather than groin ice during dry sauna. Infertility population; non-sauna intervention; participant count and full adverse-event reporting were not extracted here.
