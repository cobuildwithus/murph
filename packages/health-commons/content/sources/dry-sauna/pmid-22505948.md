This source is included for **Cardiovascular, autonomic, blood-pressure, and clinical sauna context**.

**Findings:** A systematic review found toxic elements can be measured in sweat, but it does not demonstrate that a sauna protocol detoxifies users or improves outcomes.

**Why it matters:** Systematic review of toxic elements measured in sweat, blood, or urine; useful as a detox-claim boundary, not sauna-protocol efficacy evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** arsenic in sweat; cadmium in sweat; lead in sweat; mercury in sweat; detoxification claim context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only to bound detox narratives; do not claim toxin clearance efficacy.
