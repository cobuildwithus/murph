This source is included for **Fertility, semen, and groin-cooling safety/context**.

**Findings:** In three participants, cyclic cooling underwear reduced median scrotal skin temperature from 35.3°C during control to 34.6°C during cooling; no problems, discomfort, or adverse events were reported in the study period.

**Why it matters:** Shows a cooling device can lower scrotal skin temperature and provides tolerability context.

**Potential experiment signals:** groin-cooling-boundary, heat-risk.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as scrotal-temperature measurement/cooling plausibility, not fertility efficacy.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness and limitations:** Adjacent cooling-temperature validation, not fertility outcome or sauna protocol evidence. Very small proof-of-concept; participants were investigators/urologists; temperature endpoint only; not sauna.
