This source is included for **Modality-boundary and adjacent passive-heat context**.

**Findings:** The study reported greater body-mass loss after dry sauna than steam/wet sauna (-0.72 vs -0.36 kg) while measuring core temperature, heart rate, blood pressure, and strain indices. It supports separating dry and steam sauna exposures rather than treating humidity variants as identical.

**Why it matters:** Useful for modality and humidity boundaries around a dry-sauna protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** body mass change / dehydration, heart rate during sauna, morning blood pressure, thermal comfort, tolerability symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as acute dry-versus-steam sauna context; do not use it as long-term efficacy evidence for Bryan Johnson’s daily protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
