This source is included for **Safety, heat illness, medications, pregnancy, alcohol, older-adult risk**.

**Findings:** The clinical review states that heat-related illnesses range from mild to life-threatening, heat exposure can exacerbate cardiac, respiratory, and kidney diseases, and heat stroke requires prompt recognition, rapid cooling, rehydration, and management of end-organ damage.

**Why it matters:** It anchors the serious end of the heat-illness continuum and clinician risk-counseling role.

**Potential experiment signals:** heat illness symptoms, hydration status, resting heart rate, morning blood pressure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to support stop rules, rapid cooling/rehydration, and medical escalation language, not sauna benefits.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
