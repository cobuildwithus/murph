This source is included for **Cardiovascular, autonomic, blood-pressure, and clinical sauna context**.

**Findings:** An acute crossover comparison in non-naive sauna users evaluated standalone sauna against exercise followed by sauna, making it useful for separating post-workout sequence effects from sauna-only physiology.

**Why it matters:** Crossover comparison of standalone sauna versus exercise followed by sauna in non-naive sauna users; useful for post-exercise confounding and sequence boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** blood pressure; heart rate; arterial stiffness; cardiovascular function; post-exercise confounding.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to distinguish standalone sauna from post-exercise sauna; do not promote exercise-plus-sauna results as standalone sauna effects.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
