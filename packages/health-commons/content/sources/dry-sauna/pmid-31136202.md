This source is included for **Near-direct dry-sauna interventions and acute dry-sauna dose evidence**.

**Findings:** `finding:pmid-31136202-pcos-hot-tub-glucose-insulin` — Repeated hot-tub heat therapy improved fasting glucose (105±3 to 89±5 mg/dL, P=0.001), glucose AUC, insulin AUC, adipocyte insulin-stimulated p-AKT, and serum testosterone in obese women with PCOS, without a BMI change; this is adjacent clinical passive-heating evidence, not dry-sauna evidence.

**Why it matters:** Important adjacent passive-heat mechanism source for metabolic signaling, kept separate from dry-sauna claims.
