This source is included for **Measurement artifacts: core temperature, wearables, sweat, and hydration**.

**Findings:** The 2025 nocturnal validation study compared five consumer wearables with ECG in 13 healthy adults; extracted search-result text reports that Oura devices had the highest agreement for RHR and HRV, WHOOP showed acceptable agreement, and Garmin/Polar were lower. Effect/estimate: Direction only from extracted text: Oura highest agreement, WHOOP acceptable, Garmin and Polar lower for nocturnal RHR/HRV.

**Why it matters:** Recent, endpoint-specific wearable validation for nocturnal RHR/HRV data commonly used in self-experiment baselines.

**Potential experiment signals:** HRV / RMSSD, resting heart rate, measurement caveats

**Protocol takeaway:** Device choice matters for nocturnal RHR/HRV endpoints; use within-device trends and avoid cross-device comparisons.

**Safety/adverse-event notes:** Measurement note: nocturnal HRV device choice affects endpoint fidelity.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Small sample, current consumer devices, and nocturnal validation only; not sauna-specific. Population mismatch: Healthy adults in nocturnal validation, not sauna users.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
