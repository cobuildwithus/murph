This source is included for **Measurement artifacts: core temperature, wearables, sweat, and hydration**.

**Findings:** The systematic review identified 20 studies and 25 distinct algorithms for predicting core body temperature with wearable technology; most algorithms meeting extracted clinical-validity criteria still had limited incorporation of individual and environmental factors. Effect/estimate: Extracted abstract text reported 20 studies, 25 algorithms, and 17 of 18 algorithms meeting clinical validity standards in the authors’ framework.

**Why it matters:** Systematic review for deciding whether wearable predicted-core-temperature outputs should be treated as validated endpoints or caveated estimates.

**Potential experiment signals:** core body temperature, measurement caveats

**Protocol takeaway:** Treat wearable-estimated core temperature as model-dependent context that requires validation before safety-critical sauna use.

**Safety/adverse-event notes:** Measurement-safety boundary: estimated CBT algorithms are not the same as direct core-temperature measurement, especially when individual/environmental factors are omitted.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Algorithm literature is heterogeneous and mostly adjacent to occupational/exercise heat; not dry-sauna efficacy evidence. Population mismatch: Wearable algorithm studies, not sauna users.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
