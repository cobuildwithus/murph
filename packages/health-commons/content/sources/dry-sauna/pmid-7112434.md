This source is included for **Safety, heat illness, medications, pregnancy, alcohol, older-adult risk**.

**Findings:** A Finnish case-referent study reported no difference in sauna habits between congenital-defect case mothers and referents, but its exposure context and age limit direct use for high-heat protocol guidance.

**Why it matters:** Preserves an important null/mixed pregnancy-specific sauna finding rather than assuming all pregnancy sauna evidence shows harm.

**Potential experiment signals:** congenital defects, central nervous system defects, orofacial clefts, pregnancy sauna exposure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as pregnancy-context evidence: it does not remove pregnancy hyperthermia precautions and does not support high-heat sauna use during pregnancy.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Finnish pregnancies involving 100 central nervous system defect cases and 202 orofacial cleft cases, with referent mothers interviewed for sauna habits
- **Exposure/intervention:** Maternal sauna habits during pregnancy
- **Comparator/control:** Referent mothers matched by time and area
- **Duration/follow-up:** Pregnancy exposure history; congenital defects as outcome
- **Endpoints:** congenital defects, central nervous system defects, orofacial clefts, pregnancy sauna exposure
- **Effect/direction:** The abstract reports no differences in sauna habits between case and referent mothers and argues against treating mild temporal sauna hyperthermia as hazardous to the embryo in that setting.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Pregnancy hyperthermia remains a safety boundary even though this source reports a null association for sauna habits in the studied Finnish population.
- **Limitations:** Older observational case-referent evidence; sauna exposure details, temperature, duration, and confounding may not map to modern high-heat daily protocols.
- **Population mismatch:** Pregnancy/congenital-defect population, not general Blueprint users; exposure was habitual Finnish sauna rather than Bryan Johnson Sauna.
- **Directness to Bryan Johnson Sauna:** direct_protocol
