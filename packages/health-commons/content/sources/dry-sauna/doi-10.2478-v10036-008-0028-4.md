This source is included for **Near-direct dry-sauna interventions and acute dry-sauna dose evidence**.

**Findings:** `finding:doi-10.2478-v10036-008-0028-4-exercise-heat-performance` — Repeated sauna bathing increased total work in the exercise-heat test, work to the thermal-discomfort threshold, and exercise duration; Wingate maximal anaerobic power and total work did not significantly change. The extracted batch did not identify a reliable participant count.

**Why it matters:** Adds same-modality performance/acclimation context while remaining an adjacent variant.

**Potential experiment signals:** exercise-performance, time-to-exhaustion, thermoregulation, body-water-loss.

**Protocol takeaway:** Useful only for adjacent heat-acclimation/performance context, not for direct protocol claims.
