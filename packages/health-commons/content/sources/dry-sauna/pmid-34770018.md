This source is included for **acute mechanistic**.

## Why it matters

Useful for acute blood-pressure, heart-rate load, hydration, and conservative dosing context.

## Findings captured

- The abstract reports an average heart rate of 102.5 bpm and peak heart rate of 143.3 bpm, increased pO₂ and lipid markers, decreased systolic blood pressure by 9.7 mmHg and diastolic blood pressure by 6.9 mmHg, decreased pH/bicarbonate/base excess/ctCO₂/glucose/lactate, and no significant changes in many physiological parameters except body mass. Authors characterized the 72-minute heat load as safe for properly prepared males.

## Protocol takeaway

High-temperature repeated-bout sauna acutely lowered blood pressure and changed body mass/biochemistry in prepared young men.

## Important limits

Young prepared male regular sauna users and a 72-minute heat-load design limit generalizability to Murph defaults.
