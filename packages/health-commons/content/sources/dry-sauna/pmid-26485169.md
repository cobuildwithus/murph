This source is included for **Measurement artifacts: core temperature, wearables, sweat, and hydration**.

**Findings:** The methods article describes how to use an ingestible telemetry pill for gastrointestinal temperature during exercise and emphasizes that common alternatives such as external tympanic, mouth, and rectal measures can be invasive, slow to respond, or influenced by the environment. Effect/estimate: No efficacy effect; method paper supplies operational safeguards for using GI pill temperature data.

**Why it matters:** Practical methods paper covering exclusion criteria, calibration/serial handling, and protocol factors for pill-temperature measurements.

**Potential experiment signals:** core body temperature, measurement caveats

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a practical checklist source for GI-pill measurement setup and exclusions, not for sauna benefits.

**Safety/adverse-event notes:** Measurement-safety note: incorrect device use, excluded conditions, or protocol deviations can invalidate heat-strain monitoring.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Methods source and exercise context; not a sauna outcome study. Population mismatch: Exercise protocol context rather than Bryan Johnson sauna.
