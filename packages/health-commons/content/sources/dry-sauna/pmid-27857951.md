This source is included for **Measurement artifacts: core temperature, wearables, sweat, and hydration**.

**Findings:** During steady-state and intermittent exercise in heat, the evaluated ingestible telemetry systems under-reported rectal temperature; the authors recommended calibration at multiple temperatures before use. Effect/estimate: Direction only: telemetry systems under-reported rectal temperature; one system predicted rectal temperature with the least accuracy in the extracted summary.

**Why it matters:** Device-validation source for a named ingestible telemetry system under exercise heat stress.

**Potential experiment signals:** core body temperature, measurement caveats

**Protocol takeaway:** Calibration history matters; do not treat any ingestible device as automatically interchangeable with rectal temperature.

**Safety/adverse-event notes:** Measurement-safety boundary: uncalibrated pill systems may underestimate heat strain.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Exercise-heat setting, not sauna; exact bias estimates were not extracted for this batch. Population mismatch: Exercise-heat participants rather than passive sauna users.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
