## Why it matters

Use as a hard safety boundary: do not sauna with transdermal fentanyl/opioid patches unless explicit medical guidance says otherwise.

## Findings captured

- A case report described accidental fatal fentanyl poisoning associated with increased uptake from a transdermal fentanyl patch during sauna heat exposure; it provides a high-severity safety boundary but no incidence estimate.
