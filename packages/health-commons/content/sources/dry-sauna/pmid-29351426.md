This source is included for **reviews meta guidelines**.

## Why it matters

Use for modality boundary and mechanism language; do not promote cold-exposure evidence into dry-sauna claims.

## Findings captured

- The review discusses beneficial Finnish sauna effects while also covering cold exposure, making it valuable for separating dry sauna from contrast-therapy or cold-immersion claims.
- The review provides mechanism context for cardiovascular responses to acute heat stress during Finnish sauna bathing.

## Protocol takeaway

Heat/cold review helps keep Finnish sauna distinct from contrast-therapy claims.

## Important limits

Narrative physiology review, not a protocol-specific trial.
