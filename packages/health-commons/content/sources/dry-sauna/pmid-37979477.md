This source is included for **Modality-boundary and adjacent passive-heat context**.

**Findings:** Portable steam sauna pods generated reliable but modest heat stress; the highest setting achieved about 44°C/99% RH, with reported peak rectal temperature 38.09±0.30°C, heart rate 124±15 beats/min, and sweat loss 0.73±0.33 L. Tympanic temperature was lower than rectal temperature, highlighting a measurement caveat.

**Why it matters:** Measurement and modality-boundary source for portable steam pods, humidity, and temperature-proxy limitations.

**Potential experiment signals:** core/ear temperature measurement context, body mass change / dehydration, heart rate during heat, thermal comfort, tolerability symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as measurement/tolerability context for steam pods; do not extrapolate to dry sauna efficacy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
