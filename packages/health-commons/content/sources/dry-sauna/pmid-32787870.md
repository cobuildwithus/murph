This source is included for **Fertility, semen, and groin-cooling safety/context**.

**Findings:** Ten healthy subjects underwent 43°C testicular warming for 30 minutes daily for 10 days; iTRAQ analysis identified 61 deregulated sperm proteins, many related to spermatogenesis, fertilization, motility, or energy metabolism.

**Why it matters:** Adds molecular mechanism context for heat-induced sperm impairment.

**Potential experiment signals:** semen-analysis, heat-risk.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mechanistic safety context only.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness and limitations:** Same mechanism, not direct dry-sauna protocol. Small mechanistic study; non-sauna heat exposure; no cooling condition; protein changes are not direct fertility outcomes.
