This source is included for **Core-temperature threshold mechanisms and heat-stress biology**.

**Findings:** Extracellular HSP70 increased from baseline after both passive heating and exercise without a between-condition difference. IL-6 rose about two-fold after passive heating and about four-fold after exercise. Passive heating increased energy expenditure and lowered peak post-meal glucose versus exercise, but 24-hour glucose AUC did not differ.

**Why it matters:** Closest in spirit to passive heating among this batch, but it uses warm-water immersion rather than dry sauna and therefore remains an adjacent variant.

**Potential experiment signals:** extracellular HSP70, interleukin-6, postprandial glucose, energy expenditure, core body temperature.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent-variant context: warm-water passive heating increased extracellular HSP70 and IL-6 and changed peak post-meal glucose, but it does not establish dry-sauna efficacy or long-term metabolic benefit.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
