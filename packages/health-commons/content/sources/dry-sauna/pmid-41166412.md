This source is included for **reviews meta guidelines**.

## Why it matters

Supports endpoint-specific tracking and discourages broad cardiometabolic claims.

## Findings captured

- The meta-analysis included 51 publications and 1055 participants; after outlier removal, diastolic blood pressure was reduced after single-bout heat thermotherapy (n=20 comparisons; −2 mmHg [−4, 0]; I²=76%) and after multiple-bout heat thermotherapy (n=9 comparisons; −3 mmHg [−6, −1]; I²=56%).
- Accessible abstract-level records note no clear changes in inflammatory markers, heat-shock proteins, or arterial stiffness after single or multiple heat-thermotherapy bouts; exact pooled estimates were not extracted.

## Protocol takeaway

Heat thermotherapy meta-analysis shows modest DBP reduction but mixed cardiometabolic markers.

## Important limits

Mixed modalities and abstract-level extraction limit direct protocol use.
