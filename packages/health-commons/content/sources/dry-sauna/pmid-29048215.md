This source is included for **Cardiovascular, autonomic, blood-pressure, and clinical sauna context**.

**Findings:** A single 30-minute Finnish sauna session in 102 asymptomatic adults with cardiovascular risk factors was associated with lower pulse-wave velocity and blood-pressure-related measures immediately after exposure and during short recovery.

**Why it matters:** Non-randomized acute Finnish sauna experiment in 102 asymptomatic adults with cardiovascular risk factors, reporting reduced pulse-wave velocity and blood-pressure measures after one session.

**Potential experiment signals:** pulse-wave velocity; augmentation index; heart rate; blood pressure; mean arterial pressure; pulse pressure; left ventricular ejection time.

**Protocol takeaway:** May contextualize acute BP/arterial-stiffness signals; do not treat as proof of long-term healthspan benefit.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
