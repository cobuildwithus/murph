This source is included for **Safety, heat illness, medications, pregnancy, alcohol, older-adult risk**.

**Findings:** The review reports that alcohol-related minor accidents such as sprains and burns are common in sauna and that more serious accidents, including head contusions and heat stroke, also occur; it also notes that large alcohol intake may affect the ability to maintain blood pressure.

**Why it matters:** It reinforces alcohol as a recurrent sauna-specific risk boundary.

**Potential experiment signals:** alcohol-use screen, dizziness or orthostatic symptoms, falls or burns, morning blood pressure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to reinforce a no-alcohol rule around sauna sessions.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
