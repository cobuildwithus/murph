This source is included for **Cardiovascular, autonomic, blood-pressure, and clinical sauna context**.

**Findings:** In 28 healthy young women, repeated 10-minute Finnish sauna exposures with cold-water immersion produced significant SBP, DBP, and HR responses, including about +9 mmHg SBP and +40 bpm HR after the first heating phase.

**Why it matters:** Open-access acute heat-cold study in 28 healthy young women, showing significant BP and HR responses across repeated sauna and cooling cycles.

**Potential experiment signals:** systolic blood pressure; diastolic blood pressure; heart rate; heating-cycle trend; cooling-cycle response.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for female heat-cold cardiovascular response context; separate from standalone sauna claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
