This source is included for **Fertility, semen, and groin-cooling safety/context**.

**Findings:** Two groups of 10 volunteers received repeated 43°C testicular warming; the study concluded that transient scrotal hyperthermia seriously but reversibly impaired spermatogenesis and that oxidative stress may be involved.

**Why it matters:** Human experimental heat-stress evidence for reversible semen/spermatogenesis risk.

**Potential experiment signals:** semen-analysis, heat-risk, fertility-safety.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety context for why testicular heat avoidance matters; do not treat as direct sauna-dose evidence.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness and limitations:** Same mechanism, but not dry sauna and not Bryan Johnson’s groin-icing variant. Non-sauna heat exposure; small volunteer groups; not a cooling intervention.
