This source is included for **Modality-boundary and adjacent passive-heat context**.

**Findings:** Accessible abstracts report that cardiac output and LVEF significantly increased after 4 weeks in the Waon therapy group but did not change in controls. This is clinical-supervised heart-failure Waon evidence with major population and modality mismatch.

**Why it matters:** Provides HRV/autonomic context but should not be generalized to healthy high-heat sauna users.

**Potential experiment signals:** HRV RMSSD, resting heart rate, morning blood pressure, tolerability symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as clinical/autonomic context and safety boundary only.
