This source is included for **Safety, heat illness, medications, pregnancy, alcohol, older-adult risk**.

**Findings:** This burn-center review reports 154 sauna-related burn cases treated over 1994-2000, supporting direct burn-risk safety language but not incidence estimates for general sauna users.

**Why it matters:** Direct sauna adverse-event series for burn risk.

**Potential experiment signals:** burn injury, sauna-related adverse events, treatment burden.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for burn safety boundaries, especially around falls, contact, immobility, intoxication, or prolonged exposure.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Patients treated for sauna-related burns at Kuopio University Hospital Burn Center from 1994 to 2000
- **Exposure/intervention:** Sauna-related burn mechanisms during sauna bathing
- **Comparator/control:** No protocol comparator; descriptive case series
- **Duration/follow-up:** Cases treated between 1994 and 2000
- **Endpoints:** burn injury, sauna-related adverse events, treatment burden
- **Effect/direction:** Descriptive adverse-event series; title reports 154 sauna-related burn cases.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Burn injuries are a concrete sauna-specific adverse event category and can involve severe injury requiring burn-center care.
- **Limitations:** Retrospective single-center burn-center series; does not estimate incidence among all sauna users.
- **Population mismatch:** Burn-center population, not healthy protocol participants; findings support risk boundaries only.
