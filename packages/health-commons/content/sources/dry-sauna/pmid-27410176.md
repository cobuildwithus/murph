This source is included for **Fertility, semen, and groin-cooling safety/context**.

**Findings:** This human study reported that transient, frequent scrotal hyperthermia affected sperm DNA integrity, sperm apoptosis, and sperm protein expression, with reversible heat-related spermatogenesis damage described in the abstracted source.

**Why it matters:** Mechanistic human evidence connecting scrotal heat to sperm integrity endpoints beyond basic semen counts.

**Potential experiment signals:** semen-analysis, heat-risk, fertility-safety.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for safety boundary and possible endpoints, not for efficacy claims.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness and limitations:** Same mechanism but not the Bryan Johnson dry-sauna-plus-ice variant. Small human heat-exposure study; non-sauna; no groin-cooling arm.
