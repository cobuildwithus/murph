This source is included for **Core-temperature threshold mechanisms and heat-stress biology**.

**Findings:** The lower-intensity longer trial reached a higher rectal temperature and greater area above 38.5 °C, while the higher-intensity shorter trial had a faster temperature rise. Monocyte intracellular Hsp72 increased similarly at exhaustion and rose further at 24 hours; regression did not identify clear predictor variables.

**Why it matters:** Adds a cellular Hsp72 endpoint and shows that different heat-dose patterns can converge on similar Hsp72 changes, limiting simple claims about a single dose variable.

**Potential experiment signals:** core body temperature, time above 38.5 °C, monocyte intracellular Hsp72, 24-hour recovery response.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as same-mechanism context: monocyte Hsp72 can rise after exhaustive exercise heat, but this is not passive dry-sauna evidence and regression did not identify a clear single predictor.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
