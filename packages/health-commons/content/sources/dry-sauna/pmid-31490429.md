This source is included for **Post-exercise sauna, heat-acclimation, and performance variants**.

**Findings:** Exercise-plus-sauna protocols decreased leg press force and rate of force development acutely, and strength plus sauna left some performance measures lower at 24 hours. Sauna alone was extracted as strenuous enough to reduce leg and bench press force acutely and leg press force at 24 hours.

**Why it matters:** Important safety/recovery boundary for scheduling sauna around strength training.

**Potential experiment signals:** next-workout readiness, resting heart rate, symptom/fatigue notes

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not position sauna immediately before key strength-performance demands based on this source.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
