This source is included for **Measurement artifacts: core temperature, wearables, sweat, and hydration**.

**Findings:** The review emphasizes that athletes lose water and electrolytes through sweating, but sweat rate and sweat sodium are highly method-dependent and vary within and between individuals. Effect/estimate: No pooled effect estimate extracted; direction is variability and methods-dependence.

**Why it matters:** Best single methods review for sweat-rate and sweat-sodium variability, especially when sauna self-experiments mention electrolytes.

**Potential experiment signals:** fluid loss, measurement caveats

**Protocol takeaway:** Use sweat-rate/sodium data as individualized hydration context, not as a universal sauna-dose outcome.

**Safety/adverse-event notes:** Hydration context: individualized sweat testing requires standardized collection and cautious interpretation.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Athlete exercise literature rather than passive sauna; review does not prove a sauna outcome. Population mismatch: Athletes and exercise heat context, not Bryan Johnson sauna users.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
