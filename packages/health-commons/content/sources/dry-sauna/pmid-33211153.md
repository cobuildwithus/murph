This source is included for **Post-exercise sauna, heat-acclimation, and performance variants**.

**Findings:** Sauna reduced peak rectal temperature, skin temperature, and heart rate during a heat-tolerance test versus control. Sauna improved extracted markers including VO2max and speed at 4 mmol/L lactate in trained runners.

**Why it matters:** High-temperature post-exercise sauna is close in temperature to Bryan-style heat, but athlete and performance endpoints limit directness.

**Potential experiment signals:** estimated VO2max, heat tolerance, resting heart rate during heat test

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent athlete evidence for heat acclimation and exercise-capacity markers, not direct wellness proof.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
