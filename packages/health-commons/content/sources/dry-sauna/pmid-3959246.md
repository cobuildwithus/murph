This source is included for **Fertility, semen, and groin-cooling safety/context**.

**Findings:** In 90 infertile couples, chronic scrotal hypothermia device use was reported with pregnancy outcomes; summaries indicate higher pregnancies among adherent users than among nonusers/short users, but the design and population are highly indirect.

**Why it matters:** Older adjacent evidence frequently cited for scrotal-cooling fertility hypotheses.

**Potential experiment signals:** groin-cooling-boundary, semen-analysis, fertility-safety.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as historical adjacent context; do not generalize to sauna icing.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness and limitations:** Adjacent chronic cooling intervention, not sauna-session ice packs. Older non-sauna infertility treatment context; device intervention; design details and full effect estimates not extracted.
