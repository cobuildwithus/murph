This source is included for **safety contraindications**.

## Why it matters

Useful as neutral observational context for cancer-risk claims; it should not be framed as causal evidence.

## Findings captured

- During median 24.3-year follow-up with 588 cancer cases, sauna frequency was not associated with all-cause cancer risk after adjustment.
- Adjusted all-cause cancer HRs were 0.92 (95% CI 0.76–1.11) for 2–3 sessions/week and 0.92 (95% CI 0.66–1.27) for ≥4 sessions/week versus ≤1/week.

## Protocol takeaway

Prospective Finnish male cohort found no clear cancer-risk increase or decrease by sauna frequency.

## Important limits

Men only, observational, and not the exact 3x/week Murph protocol.
