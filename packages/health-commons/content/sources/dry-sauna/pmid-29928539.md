This source is included for **Fertility, semen, and groin-cooling safety/context**.

**Findings:** In 30 heat-exposed and 14 unexposed steel workers, workplace heat stress was associated with lower semen volume, sperm count, morphology, and motility in an observational cross-sectional design.

**Why it matters:** Open-access human occupational heat study with semen endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** semen-analysis, heat-risk, fertility-safety.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as observational safety context; do not infer sauna causality.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness and limitations:** Same heat mechanism, occupational exposure rather than protocol exposure. Cross-sectional observational study; workplace confounding; not sauna; not a cooling intervention.
