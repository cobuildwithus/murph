## Why it matters

Use to separate supervised heart-failure heat therapy from wellness Finnish sauna claims.

## Findings captured

- The systematic review evaluates Waon therapy in heart-failure patients, making it a supervised clinical sibling variant rather than direct evidence for a healthy-user Finnish dry-sauna protocol.

## Protocol takeaway

Waon heart-failure review is a clinical sibling-variant guardrail.

## Important limits

Heart-failure participants and Waon dosing do not match the Murph protocol.
