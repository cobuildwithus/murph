This source is included for **Post-exercise sauna, heat-acclimation, and performance variants**.

**Findings:** Exercise plus sauna showed extracted short-term and 24-hour BP effects in untreated hypertension, whereas sauna alone was not extracted as having the same BP effect. Sauna and exercise-plus-sauna were extracted as reducing total vascular resistance during the acute recovery window.

**Why it matters:** Clinical hemodynamic context for hypertensive populations and for separating sauna-alone from exercise-plus-sauna effects.

**Potential experiment signals:** morning blood pressure, resting heart rate, symptom/tolerance notes

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as supervised clinical context; avoid promoting sauna-alone BP effects from this source.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
