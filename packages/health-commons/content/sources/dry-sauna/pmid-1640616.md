This source is included for **safety contraindications**.

## Why it matters

Use only as pregnancy/fetal-risk safety boundary; do not use for general adult efficacy or causality claims.

## Findings captured

- An observational pregnancy study linked maternal heat exposures, including sauna/hot tub/fever, with increased neural-tube-defect risk; sauna-specific estimates were imprecise and should be used as a pregnancy safety boundary, not as causal proof for all sauna use.

## Protocol takeaway

Maternal heat exposure and neural tube defects — safety-only appraisal

## Important limits

Observational exposure data with possible recall/confounding.; Sauna, hot tub, and fever exposures are not interchangeable with a controlled modern protocol.
