## Why it matters

Useful for updated source recall and modality boundaries when writing Finnish dry-sauna protocol context.

## Findings captured

- The review states that Finnish saunas have the most consistent and robust health-benefit evidence among passive heat therapies, while still treating other passive-heat modalities as distinct.
- Because the review covers multiple passive-heat modalities, it should be used to separate Finnish sauna evidence from adjacent variants rather than to transfer all heat-therapy findings into the protocol.

## Protocol takeaway

Recent passive-heat review favors Finnish sauna as the strongest sauna-adjacent evidence base.

## Important limits

Broad passive-heat synthesis requires careful separation of Finnish sauna from adjacent heat interventions.
