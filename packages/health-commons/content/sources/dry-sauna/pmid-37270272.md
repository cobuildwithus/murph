This source is included for **reviews meta guidelines**.

## Why it matters

Use as a confounding and interaction guardrail when interpreting real-world user outcomes.

## Findings captured

- The review summarizes evidence on sauna bathing in combination with factors such as fitness, physical activity, blood pressure, inflammation, and socioeconomic status, making it a guardrail against attributing combined-exposure effects to sauna alone.

## Protocol takeaway

Sauna-plus-lifestyle review protects against over-attributing cohort signals to sauna alone.

## Important limits

Combined lifestyle-factor evidence is not a stand-alone sauna protocol test.
