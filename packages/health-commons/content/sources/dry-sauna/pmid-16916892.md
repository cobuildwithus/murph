This source is included for **Near-direct dry-sauna interventions and acute dry-sauna dose evidence**.

**Findings:** `finding:pmid-16916892-face-cooling-comfort-prolactin` — Face cooling lowered discomfort and improved thermal comfort during moderate passive heating and was interpreted as evidence that a meaningful component of the prolactin response is mediated by facial skin temperature rather than core temperature alone.

**Why it matters:** Helps bound the face-cooling claim: comfort and prolactin responses may be affected by facial skin temperature, not only core temperature.

**Potential experiment signals:** thermal-comfort, thermal-discomfort, prolactin, face-skin-temperature.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mechanistic context for cooling adjuncts, not as a direct protocol claim.
