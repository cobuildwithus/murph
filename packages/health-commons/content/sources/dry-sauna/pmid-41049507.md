This source is included for **reviews meta guidelines**.

## Why it matters

Use to keep protocol claims conservative and endpoint-specific.

## Findings captured

- Across 20 randomized controlled trials, the review reported no statistically significant pooled effects for flow-mediated dilation, pulse-wave velocity, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, fasting glucose, HbA1c, total cholesterol, HDL cholesterol, LDL cholesterol, triglycerides, or C-reactive protein.
- The overall pooled systolic blood-pressure effect was −2.46 mmHg (95% CI −5.02 to 0.10; I²=60.3%), while subgroup analyses reported reductions for systemic heating (−4.11 mmHg) and coronary heart disease/risk populations (−2.52 mmHg); the authors cautioned interpretation because of limitations and heterogeneity.

## Protocol takeaway

RCT-only passive heating synthesis finds mostly null cardiometabolic outcomes with cautious systolic BP signals.

## Important limits

Subgroup blood-pressure signals should not be generalized to all sauna users or all passive heating modes.
