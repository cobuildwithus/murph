This source is included for **Fertility, semen, and groin-cooling safety/context**.

**Findings:** In a 40-person scrotal-cooling device study, questionnaire responders commonly under-used the device; discomfort, work interference, and rapid loss of cooling were reported barriers, while motility and vitality improved modestly in the reported cohort.

**Why it matters:** Important practical/tolerability boundary for groin-cooling tactics.

**Potential experiment signals:** groin-cooling-boundary, fertility-safety.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to flag that cooling devices may be under-used and uncomfortable; do not assume perfect adherence.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness and limitations:** Adjacent cooling device evidence; not direct sauna-session icing. Compliance/tolerability study; partial questionnaire completion; infertility context; cooling device differs from sauna ice packs.
