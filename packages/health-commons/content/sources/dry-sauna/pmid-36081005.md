This source is included for **Measurement artifacts: core temperature, wearables, sweat, and hydration**.

**Findings:** The Polar H10 source supports chest-strap HRV as a stronger measurement option than wrist PPG in stable resting contexts, with extracted snippets reporting high resting agreement/correlation around 0.95; exercise validity is less directly applicable to sauna recovery baselines. Effect/estimate: Extracted snippets reported high resting HRV agreement/correlation values around r/rc/ICC 0.95.

**Why it matters:** Modern chest-strap HRV validation source for selecting or caveating HRV measurement devices in self-experiments.

**Potential experiment signals:** HRV / RMSSD, resting heart rate, measurement caveats

**Protocol takeaway:** Use Polar H10-like chest-strap RR data as a preferred HRV reference when feasible, especially at rest.

**Safety/adverse-event notes:** Measurement note: chest-strap quality can still be affected by fit, moisture, artifacts, and motion.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Rest/exercise validation, not sauna; exact device setup and HRV metric processing matter. Population mismatch: Recreational exercise participants rather than sauna users.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
