This source is included for **Safety, heat illness, medications, pregnancy, alcohol, older-adult risk**.

**Findings:** In this crossover study, sauna increased heart rate with or without alcohol, while sauna plus alcohol decreased systolic blood pressure markedly from 136 ± 4 to 113 ± 3 mmHg in the available abstract summary, supporting a hypotension-risk boundary.

**Why it matters:** Alcohol use is a repeated risk factor across sauna safety sources; this study provides physiologic support via blood-pressure effects.

**Potential experiment signals:** alcohol-use screen, morning blood pressure, resting heart rate, dizziness or hypotension symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Avoid alcohol before/during sauna; use this only as safety evidence.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
