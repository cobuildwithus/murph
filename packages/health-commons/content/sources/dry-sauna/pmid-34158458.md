This source is included for **Long-term Finnish cohorts and observational outcome context**.

**Findings:** Extracted records indicate low socioeconomic status was associated with higher pneumonia risk, while low socioeconomic status combined with high sauna frequency was not clearly associated with excess pneumonia risk in one reported joint comparison.

**Why it matters:** Boundary evidence for socioeconomic status and respiratory outcomes; useful for confounding notes.

**Potential experiment signals:** confounding context, respiratory context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as confounding/effect-modification context only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Study design:** Cohort analysis of socioeconomic status, sauna, and pneumonia.
- **Population:** Men with socioeconomic-status stratification in a Finnish cohort context.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Joint categories of socioeconomic status and sauna bathing frequency.
- **Comparator/control:** Higher socioeconomic status and/or lower sauna-frequency reference categories.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Long-term pneumonia follow-up.
- **Endpoints:** pneumonia, socioeconomic-status interaction.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No acute safety data; socioeconomic-effect modification does not alter medical screening.
- **Limitations:** Observational effect-modification study; extracted record does not support causal claims.
- **Population mismatch:** Socioeconomic-status pneumonia context is far from Bryan Johnson protocol endpoints.
- **Directness to Bryan Johnson Sauna:** same_mechanism; this is not direct evidence for daily 20 minutes at 93 C / 200 F after workouts.
- **Artifact and rights:** `permission_required`. No copyrighted PDF is stored in Git; this draft only references a manifest candidate.
