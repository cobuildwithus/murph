This source is included for **reviews meta guidelines**.

## Why it matters

Can inform tolerance and adaptation endpoints, but not a direct cardiovascular or longevity claim for Finnish sauna.

## Findings captured

- The review identified 16 passive heat acclimation articles and concluded that sauna, hot-water immersion, and environmental chambers can induce heat-acclimation responses; practical recommendations included exercising before passive heating, minimizing delay before heat exposure, exposing participants for at least 30 min, and using at least 6–7 exposures when feasible.

## Protocol takeaway

Passive heating can produce acclimation signals in athlete-focused protocols.

## Important limits

Sport-performance context and mixed heat modalities limit directness.
