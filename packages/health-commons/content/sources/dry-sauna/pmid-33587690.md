This source is included for **reviews meta guidelines**.

## Why it matters

May inform clinical safety boundaries and endpoint selection, not general protocol efficacy.

## Findings captured

- The review included 3 randomized controlled trials and 3 acute interventions; whole-body immersion improved six-minute walk distance (p=0.03; effect size 0.94, 95% CI 0.06–1.82) and ankle-brachial pressure index (p=0.01; effect size 1.10, 95% CI 0.20–1.99), while Waon therapy and water-perfused garments did not show those benefits, and no form improved quality of life or brachial blood pressure.

## Protocol takeaway

Clinical PAD heat therapy has modality-specific mixed findings.

## Important limits

PAD participants and supervised heat therapy are not equivalent to community Finnish sauna use.
