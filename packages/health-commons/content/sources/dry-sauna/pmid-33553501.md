This source is included for **Measurement artifacts: core temperature, wearables, sweat, and hydration**.

**Findings:** This Temperature toolbox source addresses telemetry-pill timing and reported that elapsed time after ingestion did not by itself influence validity during exercise-heat stress, while still reinforcing the need to handle water-ingestion and protocol confounders. Effect/estimate: Direction only: time after ingestion alone did not determine validity in the extracted source summary.

**Why it matters:** Ingestion-timing caveat for pill-sensor sauna self-experiments; should be read alongside water-ingestion interference studies.

**Potential experiment signals:** core body temperature, measurement caveats

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not overapply a simple ingestion-time rule; log both pill timing and fluid intake.

**Safety/adverse-event notes:** Measurement caveat: fluid ingestion and protocol details may still confound pill temperature.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Exercise-heat stress, not sauna; details of device and protocol matter. Population mismatch: Exercise-heat stress participants, not dry-sauna protocol participants.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
