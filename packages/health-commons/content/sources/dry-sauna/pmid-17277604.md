This source is included for **Measurement artifacts: core temperature, wearables, sweat, and hydration**.

**Findings:** The ACSM position stand supplies safety and hydration context for replacing sweat losses around heat exposure, but it is exercise-focused and should not be used as evidence that sauna produces benefits. Effect/estimate: Not efficacy evidence; guideline role is safety/context.

**Why it matters:** Professional guidance for hydration and fluid-loss interpretation when sauna protocols track water/electrolyte replacement.

**Potential experiment signals:** fluid loss, measurement caveats

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for conservative hydration and fluid-replacement guardrails; not for efficacy claims.

**Safety/adverse-event notes:** Safety-only: hydration, fluid replacement, and electrolyte practices can affect heat tolerance and adverse-event risk.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Exercise guideline, not sauna-specific and not a trial of Bryan Johnson Sauna. Population mismatch: Exercise/athlete guidance rather than passive dry-sauna protocol participants.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
