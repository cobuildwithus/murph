This source is included for **Measurement artifacts: core temperature, wearables, sweat, and hydration**.

**Findings:** The study addresses variability in sweat sodium measured with regional absorbent patches; standardized field protocols can be relatively robust, but environmental conditions, sample freezing, and analytical technique may introduce statistically detectable differences. Effect/estimate: Direction only: regional patch sodium is method-dependent and should be standardized before interpretation.

**Why it matters:** Direct variability/caveat source for sweat sodium patch data, preserving possible noise in electrolyte endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** fluid loss, measurement caveats

**Protocol takeaway:** If sweat sodium is logged, standardize collection site, timing, environment, storage, and analysis method.

**Safety/adverse-event notes:** Hydration context: inconsistent methods can misclassify sodium loss and electrolyte replacement needs.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Exercise field-testing context; not passive sauna and not a whole-body sodium-loss validation by itself. Population mismatch: Athlete/exercise testing context, not Bryan Johnson dry-sauna users.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
