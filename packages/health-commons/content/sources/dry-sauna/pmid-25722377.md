This source is included for **Core-temperature threshold mechanisms and heat-stress biology**.

**Findings:** Leukocyte Hsp72 mRNA was higher after downhill than flat running and higher in hot than temperate environments; Hsp90α mRNA showed similar downhill and hot-environment increases. The largest transcript stimuli came from combining downhill and hot-environment stress.

**Why it matters:** Adds gene-transcript evidence that heat and exercise-damage stressors can affect leukocyte heat-shock pathways, while also demonstrating a major population and modality mismatch.

**Potential experiment signals:** HSPA1A transcript, HSPC1 transcript, exercise heat exposure, muscle-damage context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as adjacent mechanistic context: hot running and downhill eccentric exercise increased leukocyte Hsp transcripts, but this is not passive dry-sauna evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
