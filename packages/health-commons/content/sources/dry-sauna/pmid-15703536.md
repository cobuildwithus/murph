This source is included for **safety contraindications**.

## Why it matters

Use as pregnancy safety evidence against routine sauna protocol use during pregnancy or possible early pregnancy without clinician guidance.

## Findings captured

- A systematic review/meta-analysis of 15 studies (1,719 cases; 37,898 noncases) reported an elevated neural-tube-defect risk with maternal hyperthermia exposure (OR 1.92, 95% CI 1.61-2.29), supporting pregnancy overheating cautions; included exposures were not sauna-only.

## Protocol takeaway

Maternal hyperthermia and the risk for neural tube defects in offspring: systematic review and meta-analysis — safety-only appraisal

## Important limits

Hyperthermia sources include fever and other heat exposures, not only sauna.; Observational evidence; cannot establish exact sauna dose-risk threshold.
