This source is included for **Safety, heat illness, medications, pregnancy, alcohol, older-adult risk**.

**Findings:** This clinical review summarizes heat-stroke pathophysiology and severe outcomes, supporting emergency stop-condition language for high-heat exposure.

**Why it matters:** Foundational heat-stroke pathophysiology and clinical boundary source.

**Potential experiment signals:** central nervous system dysfunction, multi-organ injury, heat-stroke pathophysiology, mortality risk.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only for severe heat-illness context and emergency boundary language.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Patients with classic or exertional heat stroke and clinicians managing severe heat illness
- **Exposure/intervention:** Severe heat stress leading to heat stroke
- **Comparator/control:** Not an intervention trial
- **Duration/follow-up:** Clinical review; no participant follow-up window
- **Endpoints:** central nervous system dysfunction, multi-organ injury, heat-stroke pathophysiology, mortality risk
- **Effect/direction:** Foundational clinical review describing heat stroke as severe heat illness involving thermoregulatory failure, systemic inflammatory responses, and organ injury risk.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Heat stroke is life-threatening and can progress to multi-organ injury.
- **Limitations:** General clinical review and older than current heat-stroke guidelines.
- **Population mismatch:** Clinical heat-stroke population; not sauna efficacy evidence.
