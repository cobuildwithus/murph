This source is included for **acute mechanistic**.

## Why it matters

Supports mechanistic stress-response context for session effects.

## Findings captured

- The previewed abstract reports significant sauna-associated increases in prolactin and norepinephrine: prolactin rose 113-1280% with a mean increase of 510%, and norepinephrine rose 18-150% with a mean increase of 86%. Epinephrine, β-endorphin, corticotropin, and cortisol varied individually and did not show a statistically significant heat effect versus control.

## Protocol takeaway

Acute intense sauna raised prolactin and norepinephrine in a small women-only study.

## Important limits

Small sample, acute design, and endocrine outcomes do not support wearable or recovery benefit claims.
