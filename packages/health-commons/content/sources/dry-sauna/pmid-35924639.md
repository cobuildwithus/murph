This source is included for **Fertility, semen, and groin-cooling safety/context**.

**Findings:** In a preconception cohort, sauna use was relatively uncommon and reported associations for personal heat exposures were small and imprecise; hot bath/tub exposure showed a small inverse association with fecundability that included the null in confidence intervals.

**Why it matters:** Modern prospective fertility outcome context that preserves weak/imprecise associations.

**Potential experiment signals:** fertility-safety, heat-risk.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as observational context only; do not make causal claims from it.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness and limitations:** Same safety theme but general heat-exposure epidemiology, not the Bryan Johnson protocol. Observational self-report; exposure misclassification; sauna subgroup uncommon; not semen-marker study.
