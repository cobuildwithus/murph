This source is included for **reviews meta guidelines**.

## Why it matters

Avoid claiming endurance-performance benefits for the Finnish sauna protocol from this evidence base.

## Findings captured

- The meta-analysis included 10 studies with 199 participants; for performance in hot conditions the pooled ratio of means was 1.04 (95% CI 0.94–1.15; P=0.46; prediction interval 0.81–1.33), with low to very-low certainty, indicating no clear advantage over training alone.

## Protocol takeaway

Post-exercise heat exposure does not clearly improve endurance performance beyond training.

## Important limits

This is an exercise-coupled athlete/performance literature, not a sauna-only wellness protocol.
