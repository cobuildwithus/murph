This source is included for **Long-term Finnish cohorts and observational outcome context**.

**Findings:** Frequent sauna bathing was associated with reduced risk of respiratory diseases in extracted records; exact source-specific hazard ratios were not available in the batch metadata used here.

**Why it matters:** Respiratory disease association often appears in sauna benefit reviews; include as adjacent context, not direct protocol evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** respiratory safety context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for broad respiratory context and screening nuance, not efficacy claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Study design:** Long-term prospective cohort.
- **Population:** Middle-aged Caucasian Finnish men.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Frequency of sauna bathing.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower sauna frequency categories.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Long-term follow-up for respiratory disease outcomes.
- **Endpoints:** respiratory diseases, COPD, asthma, pneumonia context.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Does not establish safety for people with active respiratory disease using high-heat sauna; respiratory disease remains a screening context.
- **Limitations:** Observational men-only cohort; possible lifestyle and health-status confounding; effect estimates not fully extracted in this batch.
- **Population mismatch:** Respiratory disease prevention is not a direct Bryan Johnson protocol endpoint.
- **Directness to Bryan Johnson Sauna:** same_mechanism; this is not direct evidence for daily 20 minutes at 93 C / 200 F after workouts.
- **Artifact and rights:** `permission_required`. No copyrighted PDF is stored in Git; this draft only references a manifest candidate.
