## Why it matters

Use as a fertility caution source, especially for people actively trying to conceive; do not infer broad benefit or harm magnitude without the full paper.

- Normal adult men were evaluated with computer-assisted sperm analysis around sauna exposure; the source is relevant to possible sauna effects on sperm movement, but no batch-extractable numeric effect estimate was available.

## Protocol takeaway

Effects of sauna on sperm movement characteristics of normal men measured by computer-assisted sperm analysis — safety-only appraisal
