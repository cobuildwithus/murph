This source is included for **Cardiovascular, autonomic, blood-pressure, and clinical sauna context**.

**Findings:** A single 30-minute Finnish sauna session in 102 adults with cardiovascular risk factors acutely changed blood pressure, arterial stiffness, heart rate, and selected blood biomarkers, with sodium and potassium reported as constant and creatinine slightly increased.

**Why it matters:** Acute Finnish sauna physiology study in 102 adults with cardiovascular risk factors, measuring BP, arterial stiffness, heart rate, and blood biomarkers before, after, and after recovery.

**Potential experiment signals:** arterial stiffness; blood pressure; heart rate; blood biomarkers; plasma creatinine; sodium; potassium.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for acute BP/HR/vascular measurement context only; do not claim durable risk reduction.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
