**Findings:** Interval 100 °C sauna initially stressed HR and temperature responses, but later sessions showed reduced HR/core/skin temperature responses consistent with acclimation.

**Why it matters:** High-temperature passive sauna physiology is close to Bryan’s heat context, but it remains supervised acclimation evidence.
