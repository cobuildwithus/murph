## Why it matters

Use to standardize home BP collection and avoid overreading noisy self-experiment measurements.

## Findings captured

- The AHA statement emphasizes that accurate BP measurement is essential and that home BP monitoring is an alternative when ambulatory monitoring is unavailable or not tolerated.

## Protocol takeaway

AHA BP statement supplies measurement rules for interpreting morning BP.

## Important limits

This is measurement guidance, not sauna efficacy evidence.
