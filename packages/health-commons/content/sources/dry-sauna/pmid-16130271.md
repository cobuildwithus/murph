This source is included for **Fertility, semen, and groin-cooling safety/context**.

**Findings:** This retrospective survey of 402 pregnancies studied male occupational heat exposure and time to pregnancy and has been summarized as suggesting occupational heat is a weak risk factor for male subfertility.

**Why it matters:** Adds clinical fertility-outcome context beyond semen markers.

**Potential experiment signals:** fertility-safety, heat-risk.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as observational safety context only, not causality.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness and limitations:** Same heat mechanism, not sauna or groin cooling. Retrospective observational survey; occupational exposure; detailed effect estimates not extracted here.
