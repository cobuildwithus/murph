This source is included for **reviews meta guidelines**.

## Why it matters

Use it to orient direct Finnish-sauna claims and contraindication language, not as stand-alone proof of Murph outcomes.

## Findings captured

- The review summarized epidemiological, experimental, and interventional evidence linking Finnish sauna bathing with cardiovascular outcomes and other health conditions, based on MEDLINE and EMBASE searches through February 24, 2018.
- The review framed candidate mechanisms including endothelial function, arterial stiffness, autonomic modulation, lipid profiles, and systemic blood pressure lowering.

## Protocol takeaway

Finnish-sauna review supports mechanism-rich but evidence-calibrated protocol framing.

## Important limits

The source mixes observational, experimental, and interventional evidence and should not be read as causal proof.
