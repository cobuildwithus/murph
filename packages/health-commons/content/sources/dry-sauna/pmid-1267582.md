This source is included for **safety contraindications**.

## Why it matters

Supports conservative duration guidance and individualized exit before discomfort in high heat/humidity contexts.

## Findings captured

- Dry sauna produced greater early diastolic blood pressure drop and pulse rise during the first 10 min; after 20 min the dry and wet sauna physiological parameters were not significantly different.
- The authors concluded that heat strain and risk were similar in both saunas and that exposure exceeding 10 min may be dangerous under those severe conditions.

## Protocol takeaway

Severe dry and wet sauna exposures produced comparable heat strain after 20 minutes.

## Important limits

The dry-sauna humidity was high and conditions were described as severe; do not directly translate the 10-min warning to all Finnish sauna use.
