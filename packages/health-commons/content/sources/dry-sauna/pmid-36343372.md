This source is included for **reviews meta guidelines**.

## Why it matters

Use to justify HRV as an exploratory endpoint and to explain why effects may differ by heat protocol.

## Findings captured

- The systematic review included 27 studies; it reported that whole-body heat stress may increase sympathetic and reduce vagal modulation, whereas local heating therapy and sauna bathing seem to increase cardiac vagal modulation and oppose sympathetic modulation, with further acute and chronic studies needed.

## Protocol takeaway

Passive-heating autonomic review supports HRV plausibility but not a guaranteed signal.

## Important limits

Heterogeneous passive-heating protocols and not Finnish dry-sauna-specific.
