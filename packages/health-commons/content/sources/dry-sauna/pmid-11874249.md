This source is included for **Near-direct dry-sauna interventions and acute dry-sauna dose evidence**.

**Findings:** `finding:pmid-11874249-sweat-trace-elements` — Sweat contained major and trace elements during sauna bathing; arm-bag samples tended to show lower concentrations than whole-body collection, with significantly lower calcium, magnesium, iron, and manganese values in the extracted summary.

**Why it matters:** Useful for electrolyte and sweat-loss measurement context, especially when interpreting external claims about minerals.

**Potential experiment signals:** sweat-sodium, sweat-potassium, sweat-magnesium, trace-elements, hydration.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as measurement context for sweat and electrolyte discussions, not as efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
