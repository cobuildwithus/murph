This source is included for **Post-exercise sauna, heat-acclimation, and performance variants**.

**Findings:** Post-exercise sauna was extracted as worsening 4 × 50 m swim performance recovery versus placebo/control recovery, with most participants slower after sauna.

**Why it matters:** Important negative adjacent evidence: post-exercise sauna may impair next-day performance in some athlete contexts.

**Potential experiment signals:** next-workout readiness, performance recovery, fatigue/sleep context

**Protocol takeaway:** Preserve as a negative recovery boundary; sauna after hard training is not uniformly performance-enhancing.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
