This source is included for **Long-term Finnish cohorts and observational outcome context**.

**Findings:** More frequent sauna bathing was associated with lower observed sudden cardiac death, fatal cardiovascular disease, and all-cause mortality. Reported sudden cardiac death hazard ratios versus once weekly were 0.78 for 2-3 sessions/week and 0.37 for 4-7 sessions/week after multivariable adjustment.

**Why it matters:** Foundational Finnish prospective cohort linking sauna frequency/duration with fatal cardiovascular and all-cause mortality outcomes; context only, not a short-term protocol test.

**Potential experiment signals:** cardiovascular mortality context, resting heart rate context, blood pressure context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as long-term observational context that sauna is plausibly cardiovascularly relevant, not as causal evidence for a 21-day wearable change.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Study design:** KIHD prospective cohort.
- **Population:** Middle-aged Eastern Finnish men in the Kuopio Ischemic Heart Disease cohort.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Self-reported habitual Finnish sauna bathing frequency and duration.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower-frequency sauna users, especially once weekly, within the same observational cohort.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Median follow-up about 20.7 years for mortality outcomes.
- **Endpoints:** sudden cardiac death, fatal coronary heart disease, fatal cardiovascular disease, all-cause mortality.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** The cohort paper is not an acute safety trial and does not provide protocol-level adverse-event surveillance for daily high-heat sauna.
- **Limitations:** Observational exposure; possible healthy-user, fitness, socioeconomic, and lifestyle confounding; men-only Finnish cohort; does not test an assigned sauna dose.
- **Population mismatch:** Middle-aged Finnish men with habitual sauna practices, not a daily 20-minute 93 C post-workout Bryan Johnson routine or a short Murph self-experiment.
- **Directness to Bryan Johnson Sauna:** same_mechanism; this is not direct evidence for daily 20 minutes at 93 C / 200 F after workouts.
