This source is included for **Post-exercise sauna, heat-acclimation, and performance variants**.

**Findings:** Peak plasma-volume expansion after four sauna exposures was extracted as +17.8% (90% CL 7.4 to 29.2). Waking HR and RMSSD changed during the sauna block, but extracted HRV/plasma-volume associations were unclear.

**Why it matters:** Supports plausible plasma-volume/heat-acclimation mechanisms but provides weak direct evidence for Bryan Johnson Sauna.

**Potential experiment signals:** plasma volume, resting heart rate, HRV RMSSD

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent athlete mechanism and measurement context; do not claim HRV benefit as established.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
