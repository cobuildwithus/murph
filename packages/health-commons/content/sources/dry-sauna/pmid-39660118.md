This source is included for **Safety, heat illness, medications, pregnancy, alcohol, older-adult risk**.

**Findings:** A 2024 case report describes a 61-year-old woman found unconscious after more than 30 minutes in an 80 C sauna who died after a heat-stroke-associated course with DIC and subarachnoid hemorrhage.

**Why it matters:** Recent direct sauna adverse-event case for severe heatstroke boundary.

**Potential experiment signals:** heat stroke, disseminated intravascular coagulation, subarachnoid hemorrhage, death.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for severe heatstroke/death boundary language only; do not infer incidence.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Cardiopulmonary healthy 61-year-old woman found unconscious after more than 30 minutes in an 80 C domestic sauna
- **Exposure/intervention:** At least 30 minutes in an 80 C sauna preceding heat-stroke-associated complications
- **Comparator/control:** No comparator
- **Duration/follow-up:** Died 38 hours after admission in the reported case
- **Endpoints:** heat stroke, disseminated intravascular coagulation, subarachnoid hemorrhage, death
- **Effect/direction:** Case report links prolonged sauna heat exposure to a fatal heat-stroke course with progressive disseminated intravascular coagulation and subarachnoid hemorrhage in a single patient.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Death, heat stroke, DIC, and hemorrhage.
- **Limitations:** Single case report with complicating factors including asymptomatic COVID-19 infection noted by the authors.
- **Population mismatch:** Single older adult fatal case after a longer 80 C exposure, not Bryan Johnson Sauna efficacy evidence.
- **Directness to Bryan Johnson Sauna:** direct_protocol
