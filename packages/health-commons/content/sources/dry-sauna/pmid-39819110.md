This source is included for **reviews meta guidelines**.

## Why it matters

May inform endpoint selection and safety boundaries for users with CVD history.

## Findings captured

- The review screened 2913 records and included 18 eligible studies; interventions lasted 20–90 min, 3–7 times/week for 2–8 weeks, and were reported to consistently improve ejection fraction, flow-mediated dilation, brain natriuretic peptide, NYHA class, and six-minute walk distance, while blood-pressure and heart-rate effects were infrequent and thermoregulatory responses were scarcely reported.

## Protocol takeaway

Passive heat therapy in CVD shows clinical-marker signals under mixed supervised protocols.

## Important limits

Clinical disease treatment evidence cannot be generalized to unsupervised community Finnish sauna use.
