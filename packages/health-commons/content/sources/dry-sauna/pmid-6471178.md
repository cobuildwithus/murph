This source is included for **Fertility, semen, and groin-cooling safety/context**.

**Findings:** In 50 infertile men with oligospermia or reduced motility, nighttime scrotal ice packs were reported to produce at least two-fold increases in sperm density and similar motility increases in about 65% of men.

**Why it matters:** Most topically similar to ice-pack cooling, but still not sauna-session groin icing.

**Potential experiment signals:** groin-cooling-boundary, semen-analysis.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent cooling context only; do not claim sauna ice packs will improve semen parameters.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness and limitations:** Adjacent ice-pack cooling but different duration, setting, and population. Older infertility population; nighttime cooling, not sauna-session cooling; no randomized control extracted here.
