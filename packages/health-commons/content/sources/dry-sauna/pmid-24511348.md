This source is included for **acute mechanistic**.

## Why it matters

Supports hydration, training-status, and acute immune-cell context.

## Findings captured

- The abstract reports higher body-mass loss and plasma-volume changes in athletes than non-athletes, increased WBC, lymphocyte, neutrophil, and basophil counts after sauna, and larger leukocyte and monocyte increments in athletes. These are acute physiologic responses, not long-term immune outcomes.

## Protocol takeaway

Single Finnish sauna session acutely changed white-blood-cell profile and fluid-related measures.

## Important limits

Male-only, acute design with no long-term immune or recovery outcome.
