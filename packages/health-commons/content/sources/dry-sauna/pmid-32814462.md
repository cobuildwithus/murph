This source is included for **reviews meta guidelines**.

## Why it matters

Useful for plausibility and endpoint selection, not for claiming direct Murph schedule effects.

## Findings captured

- The meta-analysis included 16 articles and reported acute increases in body temperature and heart rate with decreases in systolic and diastolic BP; short-term analyses reported improvements in markers such as LVEF, 6-minute walk distance, flow-mediated dilation, BP, BNP, and NYHA class proportions.

## Protocol takeaway

Sauna-treatment meta-analysis reports positive cardiovascular signals but is adjacent to the protocol.

## Important limits

Pooled sauna-treatment modalities and clinical populations create substantial directness limits.
