This source is included for **Fertility, semen, and groin-cooling safety/context**.

**Findings:** The systematic review found the scrotal-cooling evidence base too scarce and heterogeneous to draw firm conclusions about fertility benefits.

**Why it matters:** Important negative/mixed boundary preventing overclaiming from older scrotal-cooling studies.

**Potential experiment signals:** groin-cooling-boundary, semen-analysis.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as an uncertainty boundary: cooling is plausible but not established as a fertility intervention.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness and limitations:** Adjacent cooling evidence, not dry sauna plus groin ice. Few studies; heterogeneous cooling methods and populations; not sauna-specific.
