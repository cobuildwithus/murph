This source is included for **Cardiovascular, autonomic, blood-pressure, and clinical sauna context**.

**Findings:** In 102 non-naive sauna users with cardiovascular risk factors, a single 30-minute Finnish sauna session was used to evaluate acute blood-based cardiovascular biomarkers, with long-term pathway relevance still unproven.

**Why it matters:** Blood-marker companion to acute Finnish sauna physiology in 102 non-naive users with cardiovascular risk factors.

**Potential experiment signals:** high-sensitivity C-reactive protein; creatine kinase; high-sensitivity troponin I; creatine kinase-MB; NT-proBNP; blood-based cardiovascular markers.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for mechanistic biomarker context only; not for claims of reduced cardiovascular events.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
