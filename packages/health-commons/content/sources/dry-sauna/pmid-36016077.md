This source is included for **Measurement artifacts: core temperature, wearables, sweat, and hydration**.

**Findings:** In 53 healthy adults during one sleep-lab night, six devices showed sleep/wake agreement around 86–89% with low-to-moderate kappa values; HR and HRV agreement depended on device and sampling, with WHOOP raw RR intervals showing near-perfect agreement for HR and HRV in the extracted summary. Effect/estimate: Extracted sleep/wake agreement ranged from 86% to 89%; kappa values were approximately 0.30 to 0.51 across devices.

**Why it matters:** Direct device-validation source for the same endpoint family as the protocol wearable baseline: sleep HR, RHR, and HRV.

**Potential experiment signals:** HRV / RMSSD, resting heart rate, measurement caveats

**Protocol takeaway:** For sauna self-tracking, prioritize raw/validated nocturnal HR and HRV trends over proprietary sleep-stage claims.

**Safety/adverse-event notes:** Measurement note: sleep-stage outputs are weaker than total sleep time or raw HR/HRV signals.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Single-night lab study in healthy adults; device versions may age; not sauna exposure. Population mismatch: Healthy sleep-lab adults, not sauna users.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
