This source is included for **reviews meta guidelines**.

## Why it matters

Supports sleep as a plausible tracking endpoint but not a sauna-specific claim.

## Findings captured

- The review screened 5322 records and included 17 studies, 13 in the meta-analysis; warm shower or bath exposure at 40–42.5 °C about 1–2 h before bedtime and for as little as 10 min was associated with shorter sleep onset latency and improved self-rated sleep quality and sleep efficiency.

## Protocol takeaway

Warm bath/shower passive heating improves sleep outcomes in adjacent evidence.

## Important limits

Water-based heat near bedtime differs from Finnish dry sauna protocol exposure.
