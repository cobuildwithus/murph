This source is included for **Fertility, semen, and groin-cooling safety/context**.

**Findings:** This cohort source links preconception heat exposure windows with sperm epigenetic age, with modest reported effect sizes; it is environmental heat context rather than sauna evidence.

**Why it matters:** Emerging heat-exposure endpoint context for sperm epigenetics, with high indirectness.

**Potential experiment signals:** heat-risk, fertility-safety.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as emerging context, not a protocol claim.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness and limitations:** Same heat theme but major population and exposure mismatch. Infertility-treatment population; environmental exposure modeling; not semen count/motility and not sauna; future work needed.
