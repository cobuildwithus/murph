**Findings:** Sauna bathing was associated with reduced risk of venous thromboembolism in the extracted cohort record; exact effect estimates were not retained in this batch metadata.

**Why it matters:** Vascular endpoint context that may appear in broad cardiovascular summaries; observational boundary applies.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Study design:** Prospective cohort VTE analysis.
- **Population:** Middle-aged Finnish men without baseline venous thromboembolism in extracted cohort metadata.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Sauna bathing frequency.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower sauna-frequency categories.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Long-term follow-up for venous thromboembolism.
- **Endpoints:** venous thromboembolism.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Does not evaluate acute dehydration, thrombosis, or high-temperature post-workout safety; hydration and contraindication screening remain relevant.
- **Limitations:** Observational men-only cohort; exact estimates not extracted in this batch; residual confounding possible.
- **Population mismatch:** VTE risk is not a direct Bryan Johnson protocol endpoint.
- **Directness to Bryan Johnson Sauna:** same_mechanism; this is not direct evidence for daily 20 minutes at 93 C / 200 F after workouts.
