This source is included for **Post-exercise sauna, heat-acclimation, and performance variants**.

**Findings:** The pilot RCT extracted no sauna-added effect on targeted gut bacteria, stool pH, zonulin, sIgA, or hsCRP versus exercise-only.

**Why it matters:** Important null adjacent evidence for gut/inflammation claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** hsCRP, gut microbiota, intestinal barrier markers
