This source is included for **Modality-boundary and adjacent passive-heat context**.

**Findings:** When dry heating and hot-water immersion produced similar rectal-temperature rises, IL-6 was reported as similar between dry heat and HWI and higher than control, while dry heating was less thermally comfortable. This supports distinguishing core-temperature matching from comfort/tolerability.

**Why it matters:** Useful for dose-response and comfort boundaries when comparing dry heat with hot-water immersion.

**Potential experiment signals:** thermal comfort, core/ear temperature measurement context, tolerability symptoms, inflammatory response context.
