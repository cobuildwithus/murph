This source is included for **Measurement artifacts: core temperature, wearables, sweat, and hydration**.

**Findings:** This study evaluated a heart-rate-based algorithm for estimating core temperature in elite athletes exercising in the heat; it is useful as a boundary because algorithm-estimated core temperature should not be confused with directly measured core temperature. Effect/estimate: No specific algorithm-error estimate extracted in this batch; role is adjacent measurement context.

**Why it matters:** Useful boundary source where heart-rate-derived core-temperature estimates may be confused with measured core temperature.

**Potential experiment signals:** core body temperature, resting heart rate, measurement caveats

**Protocol takeaway:** Label HR-derived core temperature as an estimate and keep it separate from measured GI/rectal core-temperature endpoints.

**Safety/adverse-event notes:** Safety boundary: HR-derived core temperature may fail when HR is affected by hydration, fitness, stress, illness, or sauna-specific cardiovascular load.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Elite-athlete exercise heat and algorithm validation; not passive sauna. Population mismatch: Elite athletes exercising in heat, not Bryan Johnson sauna users.
