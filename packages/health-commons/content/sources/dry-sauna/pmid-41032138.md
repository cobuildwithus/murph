This source is included for **reviews meta guidelines**.

## Why it matters

Use this source to caution users who pair sauna with workouts and recovery goals.

## Findings captured

- The review included 14 studies with 194 participants; acute findings were mixed, with no effects in 4 studies, beneficial effects in 4, and adverse effects in 1, while longer-term studies suggested possible running improvements in hot conditions but no clear effects for cycling performance or VO₂max.

## Protocol takeaway

Post-exercise heat exposure has mixed recovery and performance-adaptation evidence.

## Important limits

Heterogeneity prevented meta-analysis and the source is not a sauna-only protocol trial.
