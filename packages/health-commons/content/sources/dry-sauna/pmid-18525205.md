This source is included for **acute mechanistic**.

## Why it matters

Useful for skin tolerability and hydration-context logging.

## Findings captured

- The abstract reports that regular sauna users had a more stable epidermal barrier, increased stratum corneum hydration, faster recovery of elevated transepidermal water loss and pH after sauna, a heart-rate/sweat/perfusion training effect, and decreased forehead sebum. Because the study compares regular users with non-users, selection and habituation remain important limitations.

## Protocol takeaway

Regular sauna users showed skin-barrier differences in an exploratory controlled study.

## Important limits

Regular-user comparisons can be confounded, and skin physiology is not a primary Murph outcome.
