This source is included for **Post-exercise sauna, heat-acclimation, and performance variants**.

**Findings:** Both sexes showed extracted improvements in heat-tolerance physiology and VO2max/speed markers after intermittent post-exercise sauna. Female runners showed a larger active sweat-gland signal while male runners showed a larger forearm blood-flow signal in extracted results.

**Why it matters:** Extends high-heat post-exercise sauna evidence and warns against assuming identical male/female adaptation pathways.

**Potential experiment signals:** estimated VO2max, heat tolerance, sex-specific sweat/skin blood-flow signals

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent athlete evidence and sex-specific context, not direct Bryan evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
