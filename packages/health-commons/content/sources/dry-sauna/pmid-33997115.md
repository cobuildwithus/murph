This source is included for **Measurement artifacts: core temperature, wearables, sweat, and hydration**.

**Findings:** This source reports that common body-temperature sites provide invalid measures of body core temperature in hyperthermic humans wearing American football uniforms, supporting a safety boundary against relying on common/peripheral sites in severe heat strain. Effect/estimate: Direction only: common sites were invalid for core-temperature assessment in this hyperthermic football-uniform context.

**Why it matters:** Boundary source for common-site thermometer limitations in severe heat strain, even though the exposure is football equipment rather than sauna.

**Potential experiment signals:** core body temperature, measurement caveats

**Protocol takeaway:** For sauna safety, peripheral/common temperature sites cannot rule out core hyperthermia.

**Safety/adverse-event notes:** Safety-only boundary: inaccurate site choice in hyperthermia can create false reassurance.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Football-uniform exertional hyperthermia, not sauna; still relevant as severe heat-strain measurement context. Population mismatch: Football-uniform hyperthermia rather than passive dry-sauna exposure.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
