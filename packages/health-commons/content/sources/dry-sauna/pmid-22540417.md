This source is included for **Fertility, semen, and groin-cooling safety/context**.

**Findings:** The SCOP paper is a study protocol for a randomized controlled trial of the FertilMate™ scrotal cooling patch, planned at 20 participants per arm; it does not report outcome results.

**Why it matters:** Prevents treating planned RCTs as completed efficacy evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** groin-cooling-boundary, semen-analysis.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as evidence that scrotal cooling merits controlled testing, not as proof of benefit.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness and limitations:** Adjacent planned clinical cooling study, not a sauna intervention. Protocol only; no results; infertility population; cooling patch differs from sauna ice-pack use.
