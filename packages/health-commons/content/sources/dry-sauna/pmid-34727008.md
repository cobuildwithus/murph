This source is included for **safety contraindications**.

## Why it matters

Use as adjacent-variant boundary: no-fluid post-exercise sauna should not be promoted as superior to training alone.

## Findings captured

- In 13 physical education students, a randomized crossover study of endurance training plus post-exercise sauna with no fluid intake found no additional benefits over endurance training alone in the extracted summary; this is an adjacent no-fluid/post-exercise variant, not the default protocol.

## Protocol takeaway

Hematological Adaptations to Post-Exercise Sauna Bathing With No Fluid Intake: A Randomized Cross-Over Study — safety-only appraisal

## Important limits

Small student sample.; Post-exercise sauna with no fluid intake is intentionally adjacent and not the Murph standard protocol.
