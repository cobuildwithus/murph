This source is included for **safety contraindications**.

## Why it matters

Supports keeping beginners near conventional Finnish-sauna temperatures and avoiding extreme 120°C exposure.

## Findings captured

- A 20-min 80°C session decreased blood pressure and body mass, increased heart rate and forehead temperature, and was associated with improved mood-domain scores in this sample.
- The authors reported that 120°C produced adverse physiological and psychological effects and was not advised for women who sporadically use sauna.
- Vomiting and confusion were reported as predictors of syncope in some women during extreme heat exposure.

## Protocol takeaway

In sporadic female sauna users, 80°C looked tolerable while 120°C created a clear safety boundary.

## Important limits

Single-session female-student study; it does not test repeated 3x/week training or long-term outcomes.
