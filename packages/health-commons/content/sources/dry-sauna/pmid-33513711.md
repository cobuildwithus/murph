## Why it matters

Use for mechanism language and logging stress context, not direct protocol proof.

## Findings captured

- The review details proposed mechanisms and pathways involved in acute and chronic sauna responses and presents sauna as a potential adjunct for cardiometabolic disease prevention in high-stress occupations.
- The review cites evidence that sauna bathing may improve autonomic function through HRV-related pathways, but this remains mechanism context for the Murph protocol.

## Important limits
