**Findings:** Repeated 60°C sauna treatment in chronic heart-failure patients was reported to improve vascular endothelial function, cardiac function, and clinical symptoms. This is supervised clinical heart-failure evidence, not healthy-user dry-sauna efficacy evidence.

**Why it matters:** A key source for why Waon/low-temperature sauna appears in cardiovascular discussions, with strong population mismatch.

**Potential experiment signals:** morning blood pressure, resting heart rate, HRV RMSSD, estimated VO2max/exercise tolerance, tolerability symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as clinical Waon/CHF context and safety boundary only.
