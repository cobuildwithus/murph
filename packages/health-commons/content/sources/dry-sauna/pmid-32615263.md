This source is included for **Cardiovascular, autonomic, blood-pressure, and clinical sauna context**.

**Findings:** In stable CAD, acute Finnish sauna bathing was reported to increase FMD and lower BP while raising heart rate; reactive hyperemia was unchanged, so this remains supervised clinical context.

**Why it matters:** Acute Finnish sauna study in stable CAD reporting increased FMD and acute reductions in BP with higher heart rate; clinical-supervised context only.

**Potential experiment signals:** flow-mediated dilation; post-occlusion reactive hyperemia; core temperature; heart rate; blood pressure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as supervised CAD acute vascular context, with safety caveats; do not convert to direct protocol support.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
