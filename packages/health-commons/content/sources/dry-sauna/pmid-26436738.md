This source is included for **Long-term Finnish cohorts and observational outcome context**.

**Findings:** The commentary argues that the association between sauna bathing and mortality may be noncausal and should not be overinterpreted as proof of benefit.

**Why it matters:** Explicit caution against causal overclaiming from observational mortality associations; important for expectation setting.

**Potential experiment signals:** expectation setting, causal inference boundary.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to explicitly prevent causal overclaiming from the Finnish observational mortality literature.
