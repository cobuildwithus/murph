This source is included for **Fertility, semen, and groin-cooling safety/context**.

**Findings:** Bryan Johnson reported that sauna without ice protection produced large drops in motile count, motility, and normal morphology, and that markers later improved after sessions with groin icing; the post is uncontrolled self-report evidence.

**Why it matters:** Directly links the protocol owner’s sauna heat, fertility-monitoring concern, and groin-icing rationale.

**Potential experiment signals:** groin-cooling-boundary, fertility-safety, semen-analysis.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as hypothesis-generating safety/provenance context; do not claim the ice protocol prevents fertility harms without independent evidence.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness and limitations:** Direct protocol-owner report, but not independent evidence and not sufficient to infer causality. Single-person uncontrolled post; raw lab reports, timing, and confounders not extracted; not peer-reviewed.
