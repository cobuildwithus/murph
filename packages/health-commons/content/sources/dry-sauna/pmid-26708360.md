This source is included for **Measurement artifacts: core temperature, wearables, sweat, and hydration**.

**Findings:** In 20 participants, Polar V800 RR intervals during a resting active orthostatic test showed a small number of errors versus ECG and improved validity compared with previous models, supporting use for resting HRV measurement contexts. Effect/estimate: Direction only in extracted batch: fewer errors, narrower limits of agreement, stronger ICC, and smaller effect sizes versus prior models.

**Why it matters:** Reference-device validation for chest/strap RR data that may be preferable to wrist PPG for HRV-RMSSD experiments.

**Potential experiment signals:** HRV / RMSSD, measurement caveats

**Protocol takeaway:** Chest/band-derived RR data may be preferable for resting HRV baselines, but sauna heat/motion artifacts still need logging.

**Safety/adverse-event notes:** Measurement note: resting RR validation does not guarantee accuracy during movement, heat, or sweating.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Resting orthostatic context; device generation may be dated; not sauna. Population mismatch: Resting validation participants, not sauna users.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
