This source is included for **safety contraindications**.

## Why it matters

Use as hydration/body-mass-loss measurement context, not as efficacy evidence.

## Findings captured

- In 674 sedentary 19-20-year-old students, two 10-minute dry sauna sessions with a 5-minute break produced body-mass loss patterns related to body mass, supporting pre/post weight as a hydration-safety signal rather than an efficacy endpoint.

## Protocol takeaway

Sauna-Induced Body Mass Loss in Young Sedentary Women and Men — safety-only appraisal

## Important limits

Young sedentary students; acute body-mass proxy rather than clinical dehydration endpoint.; Exposure differs from 15-20 min sessions or 3x/week practice.
