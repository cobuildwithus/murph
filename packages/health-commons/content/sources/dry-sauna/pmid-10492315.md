This source is included for **Cardiovascular, autonomic, blood-pressure, and clinical sauna context**.

**Findings:** In a small monitored CAD sample, sauna exposure was associated with a myocardial-ischemia safety signal; preserve as a high-risk cardiovascular boundary rather than protocol efficacy evidence.

**Why it matters:** Acute sauna safety-boundary study in patients with coronary artery disease; useful for ischemia-risk guardrails, not efficacy claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** myocardial ischemia; ECG or scintigraphic ischemia context; blood pressure; heart rate.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for CAD contraindication/supervision boundaries only; do not use as evidence of sauna benefit.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
