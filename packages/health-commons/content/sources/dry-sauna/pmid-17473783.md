## Why it matters

Use only for heat-illness screening and post-exercise caution, not for sauna efficacy.

## Findings captured

- ACSM heat-illness guidance identifies risk factors including lack of heat acclimatization, dehydration, medication use, and recent illness; it is adjacent heat-stress safety context rather than sauna efficacy evidence.
