This source is included for **Core-temperature threshold mechanisms and heat-stress biology**.

**Findings:** Extracellular Hsp72 increased after the very-hot condition by 172.4% but did not increase meaningfully after temperate or hot conditions, and returned to baseline within 24 hours. Stepwise regression identified rate of rectal-temperature increase and change in rectal temperature as predictors of the Hsp72 increase.

**Why it matters:** Often-cited mechanistic threshold source because extracellular Hsp72 increased only in the very-hot condition and was associated with core-temperature dynamics.

**Potential experiment signals:** core body temperature, rate of core-temperature rise, extracellular Hsp72, time above 38.5 °C, time above 39.0 °C.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as same-mechanism context only: very-hot exercise raised extracellular Hsp72 and linked it to core-temperature change/rate, but this is not passive dry-sauna evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
