This source is included for **safety contraindications**.

## Why it matters

Use as nuanced pregnancy heat-limit context; do not promote sauna use during pregnancy from it.

## Findings captured

- A pregnancy heat-stress systematic review/best-evidence synthesis suggested controlled passive heat exposures including a lower-temperature sauna condition may stay below a 39°C threshold, but this remains pregnancy safety context and does not override conservative sauna/hot-tub avoidance guidance.

## Protocol takeaway

Heat stress and fetal risk. Environmental limits for exercise and passive heat stress during pregnancy: a systematic review with best evidence synthesis — safety-only appraisal

## Important limits

Systematic review of environmental limits, not a recommendation to prescribe sauna during pregnancy.; Sauna condition does not match hotter Finnish sauna or at-home behavior.
