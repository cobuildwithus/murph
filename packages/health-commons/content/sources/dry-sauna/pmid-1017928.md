This source is included for **safety contraindications**.

## Why it matters

Supports conservative session duration, stop rules, and avoidance of pushing to discomfort.

## Findings captured

- Acute exposure produced marked cardiovascular and temperature responses; at 20 min reported mean HR was 143±25 bpm, rectal temperature 38.6±0.6°C, skin temperature 40.4±1°C, and sweat loss 457±264 g.
- Three participants experienced syncope, one had an anginal attack, and ECG changes suggesting coronary insufficiency were reported.
- The authors concluded that dangers may appear suddenly and without prodromal warning signs.

## Protocol takeaway

Older acute Finnish-sauna experiment documents strong physiological strain and adverse events at 80–90°C.

## Important limits

Older uncontrolled study with severe fixed exposure conditions; results should not be used to discourage all sauna use in healthy adults.
