This source is included for **Near-direct dry-sauna interventions and acute dry-sauna dose evidence**.

**Findings:** `finding:pmid-25001587-10-sauna-lipid-profile` — The intervention was associated with decreases in total cholesterol and LDL during the 3-week sauna series and a transient triglyceride decline, while HDL increased slightly but not significantly; the extracted summary indicates lipid values returned toward baseline within 1–2 weeks and plasma-volume changes complicate interpretation.

**Why it matters:** Same-modality repeated-sauna source with cardiometabolic surrogate signals at temperatures near Bryan’s routine.

**Potential experiment signals:** total-cholesterol, LDL, HDL, triglycerides, plasma-volume.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote lipid changes as a direct Bryan protocol claim; use as limited repeated-sauna context.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
