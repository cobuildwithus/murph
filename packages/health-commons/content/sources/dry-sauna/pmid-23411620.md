This source is included for **safety contraindications**.

## Why it matters

Use for a male-fertility caution: repeated hot sauna may temporarily impair spermatogenesis, especially relevant for people actively trying to conceive.

## Findings captured

- In 10 normozoospermic men, repeated Finnish sauna exposure (2 sessions/week for 3 months, approximately 80-90°C for 15 minutes) was reported to significantly but reversibly impair spermatogenesis; sex hormones were not the main affected endpoint in the extracted summary.

## Protocol takeaway

Seminal and molecular evidence that sauna exposure affects human spermatogenesis — safety-only appraisal

## Important limits

Very small male-only study.; Exposure was 2x/week for 3 months and not exactly the Murph 3x/week protocol.
