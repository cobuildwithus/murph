This source is included for **Long-term Finnish cohorts and observational outcome context**.

**Findings:** High fitness and frequent sauna bathing were each associated with lower pneumonia risk, and the combination was reported to have a substantially lower pneumonia risk than either modality alone.

**Why it matters:** Fitness interaction paper that helps bound confounding and respiratory extrapolation.

**Potential experiment signals:** fitness confounding, respiratory context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use mainly to show fitness and sauna can be entangled in respiratory-outcome associations.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Study design:** Cohort analysis of fitness, sauna, and pneumonia.
- **Population:** Middle-aged Finnish men in a cohort context.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Joint categories of cardiorespiratory fitness and sauna bathing frequency.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower fitness and lower sauna-frequency groups.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Long-term pneumonia follow-up.
- **Endpoints:** pneumonia, cardiorespiratory fitness interaction.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Does not mean sauna should be used during active respiratory infection or fever.
- **Limitations:** Observational joint-exposure design; COVID-19 implications are speculative; men-only cohort.
- **Population mismatch:** Pneumonia and COVID-19 context is not direct Bryan Johnson protocol evidence.
- **Directness to Bryan Johnson Sauna:** same_mechanism; this is not direct evidence for daily 20 minutes at 93 C / 200 F after workouts.
- **Artifact and rights:** `open_access`. No copyrighted PDF is stored in Git; this draft only references a manifest candidate.
