This source is included for **Post-exercise sauna, heat-acclimation, and performance variants**.

**Findings:** The meta-analysis extracted a significant effect of heat acclimation on HSP70 expression (Hedges g 0.97; 95% CI 0.08 to 1.89), with stronger protein-subgroup effects.

**Why it matters:** Mechanistic synthesis for heat-shock response plausibility, not direct dry-sauna efficacy.

**Potential experiment signals:** heat-shock response, session frequency, core-temperature dose

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mechanism context for HSP/thermal-dose hypotheses, not for direct Bryan protocol claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
