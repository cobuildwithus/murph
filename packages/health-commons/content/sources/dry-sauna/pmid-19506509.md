This source is included for **Safety, heat illness, medications, pregnancy, alcohol, older-adult risk**.

**Findings:** This review found six hot-air sauna burn articles with 42 recorded patients and describes rare but potentially fatal burns with rhabdomyolysis and deep tissue injury.

**Why it matters:** Specific review for rare severe hot-air sauna burn pathology and treatment burden.

**Potential experiment signals:** full-thickness burns, rhabdomyolysis, muscle destruction, amputation, flap coverage.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to warn that immobility, prolonged exposure, or impaired consciousness in hot-air sauna can become catastrophic.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Patients with hot-air sauna burns reported in the literature and treated in burn-care settings
- **Exposure/intervention:** Prolonged hot-air sauna exposure, often involving immobility
- **Comparator/control:** No efficacy comparator; clinical review and case literature
- **Duration/follow-up:** Review of six articles and 42 recorded patients
- **Endpoints:** full-thickness burns, rhabdomyolysis, muscle destruction, amputation, flap coverage
- **Effect/direction:** Hot-air sauna burns are described as rare but potentially fatal, involving prolonged hot-air exposure, full-thickness skin damage, deeper tissue destruction, and rhabdomyolysis.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Potentially fatal burns, rhabdomyolysis, aggressive surgery, amputations, and muscle excision are safety endpoints.
- **Limitations:** Rare-event clinical literature; does not estimate population incidence.
- **Population mismatch:** Severe burn-care population rather than typical sauna users.
