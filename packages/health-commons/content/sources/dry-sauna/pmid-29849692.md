This source is included for **reviews meta guidelines**.

## Why it matters

Useful as a backbone for source recall and cautious claim calibration; primary studies still need separate extraction for protocol-level claims.

## Findings captured

- The review identified 40 clinical studies involving 3855 participants, including 13 randomized controlled trials; most studies were small and outcome measures were heterogeneous.
- The review preserved one adverse health outcome from a small study of 10 men reporting disrupted sperm production, making fertility a safety boundary rather than an efficacy claim.
- The authors concluded that regular dry sauna has potential health benefits but that higher-quality data are needed on extent, frequency, and adverse effects.

## Protocol takeaway

Direct dry-sauna systematic review supports cautious protocol rationale but highlights heterogeneity.

## Important limits

The review is not itself a 3x/week Murph protocol trial and includes small, heterogeneous studies.
