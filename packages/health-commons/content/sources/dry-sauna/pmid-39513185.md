This source is included for **safety contraindications**.

## Why it matters

Use for medication-class safety screening; do not turn heat-stress medication findings into sauna efficacy claims.

## Findings captured

- A 2024 systematic review/meta-analysis of 35 heat-stress medication studies (353 individuals; 16 women) found some medication classes increased core temperature (strong anticholinergics, nonselective beta-blockers, adrenaline, anti-Parkinson medications), while several others did not; it is heat-stress rather than sauna-specific evidence.

## Protocol takeaway

The effect of prescription and over-the-counter medications on core temperature in adults during heat stress: a systematic review and meta-analysis — safety-only appraisal

## Important limits

Not sauna-specific; heat-stress protocols varied.; Only 353 individuals and 16 women in extracted summary.
