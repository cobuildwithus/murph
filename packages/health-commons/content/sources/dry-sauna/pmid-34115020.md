This source is included for **Modality-boundary and adjacent passive-heat context**.

**Findings:** A single Waon therapy session lowered systolic and diastolic blood pressure in accessible summaries (118.5±10.1 to 115.1±9.0 mmHg systolic; 70.5±6.4 to 65.9±5.3 mmHg diastolic), while repeated-therapy reductions were significant especially in non-smokers. This is clinical Waon/hypertension context, not direct dry-sauna evidence.

**Why it matters:** Relevant for blood-pressure endpoint selection and screening, but disease and modality mismatch are substantial.

**Potential experiment signals:** morning blood pressure, resting heart rate, tolerability symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as clinical Waon blood-pressure context with population/modality caveat.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
