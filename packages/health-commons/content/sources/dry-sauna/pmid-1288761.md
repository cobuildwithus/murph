This source is included for **Fertility, semen, and groin-cooling safety/context**.

**Findings:** The study recruited 92 exposed ceramics workers and 87 controls, with semen analysis reported in a subset; exposed workers had higher childlessness/difficulty conceiving, while semen analysis showed no significant differences except sperm velocity.

**Why it matters:** Preserves mixed occupational heat evidence instead of overstating semen effects.

**Potential experiment signals:** semen-analysis, fertility-safety, heat-risk.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context that chronic occupational heat evidence is mixed and observational.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness and limitations:** Same heat mechanism, but occupational exposure and reproductive-history context rather than sauna protocol. Occupational observational study; exposure confounding; semen-analysis subset smaller than total cohort; not sauna.
