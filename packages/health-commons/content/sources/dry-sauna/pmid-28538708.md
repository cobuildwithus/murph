This source is included for **Measurement artifacts: core temperature, wearables, sweat, and hydration**.

**Findings:** This validation study is useful as a wearable-measurement boundary: wrist-worn devices may be more usable for heart-rate trends than for energy-expenditure estimates, and accuracy can differ by device and activity. Effect/estimate: No device-specific estimate extracted in this batch; direction is cautious use of wrist HR and stronger caution for energy expenditure.

**Why it matters:** Foundational consumer-wearable HR-accuracy paper for caveating sauna self-experiment RHR and in-session HR logs.

**Potential experiment signals:** resting heart rate, measurement caveats

**Protocol takeaway:** Use wearable HR as a context signal with device caveats; do not use consumer energy expenditure as a sauna efficacy endpoint.

**Safety/adverse-event notes:** Measurement note: calorie/energy-expenditure estimates should not drive sauna safety or hydration decisions.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Wearable validation, not sauna; detailed device-level estimates were not extracted here. Population mismatch: General adult validation cohort, not sauna intervention participants.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
