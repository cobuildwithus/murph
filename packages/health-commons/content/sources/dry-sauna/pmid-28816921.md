This source is included for **Measurement artifacts: core temperature, wearables, sweat, and hydration**.

**Findings:** Four capsule systems showed device-specific temperature differences; extracted results reported a CorTemp systematic difference of 0.077 ± 0.040 °C, e-Celsius of −0.081 ± 0.055 °C, and sensor inertia of 18–39 seconds that was judged physiologically marginal. Effect/estimate: Systematic differences extracted: CorTemp 0.077 ± 0.040 °C and e-Celsius −0.081 ± 0.055 °C; inertia 18–39 seconds.

**Why it matters:** Four capsule systems showed device-specific temperature differences; extracted results reported a CorTemp systematic difference of 0.077 ± 0.040 °C, e-Celsius of −0.081 ± 0.055 °C, and sensor inertia of 18–39 seconds that was judged physiologically marginal.

**Potential experiment signals:** measurement caveats

**Protocol takeaway:** Small capsule offsets should be logged/handled before comparing sauna core-temperature traces across devices.

**Safety/adverse-event notes:** Measurement caveat: even small device-specific offsets can matter when classifying heat strain near thresholds.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Device validation context; not a dry-sauna outcome study. Population mismatch: Measurement-device evidence, not Bryan Johnson sauna participants.
