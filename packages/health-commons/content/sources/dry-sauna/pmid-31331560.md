This source is included for **Post-exercise sauna, heat-acclimation, and performance variants**.

**Findings:** Recovery from a single sauna session was extracted as decreasing LF power, increasing HF power, and lowering resting HR compared with pre-sauna after recovery.

**Why it matters:** Provides autonomic recovery context, but it is acute and not post-exercise or Bryan-specific.

**Potential experiment signals:** resting heart rate, HRV RMSSD, recovery timing

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as acute autonomic context; avoid treating it as long-term HRV efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
