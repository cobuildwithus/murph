This source is included for **reviews meta guidelines**.

## Why it matters

Supports considering blood pressure and endothelial markers as candidate Murph endpoints.

## Findings captured

- Across 15 studies, heat therapy reduced mean arterial pressure by −5.86 mmHg (95% CI −8.63 to −3.10), systolic blood pressure by −3.94 mmHg, and diastolic blood pressure by −3.88 mmHg, improved flow-mediated dilation by 1.95%, and did not significantly change resting heart rate; certainty was reported as moderate for systolic/diastolic pressure and heart rate, and low for mean arterial pressure and flow-mediated dilation.

## Protocol takeaway

Mixed heat therapy meta-analysis shows blood-pressure and vascular-function signals.

## Important limits

The pooled estimate is not specific to Finnish dry sauna or 3x/week dosing.
