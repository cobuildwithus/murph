**Findings:** The systematic review evaluated commercial wearables for step count, heart rate, and energy expenditure and supports a general measurement boundary: validity varies by device, endpoint, setting, and activity, so a consumer wearable signal is not automatically a clinical endpoint. Effect/estimate: No pooled endpoint estimate extracted in this batch; review role is measurement context.

**Potential experiment signals:** resting heart rate, measurement caveats

**Protocol takeaway:** Use wearables for repeated within-person trends; avoid overclaiming exact energy expenditure or universal HR accuracy.

**Safety/adverse-event notes:** Measurement note: consumer wearable summaries should not be used alone for heat-safety decisions.
