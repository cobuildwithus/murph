This source is included for **Core-temperature threshold mechanisms and heat-stress biology**.

**Findings:** Both exercise-heat intensities produced exhaustion with high heart rate and elevated core temperature; plasma Hsp72 and Hsp27 increased and correlated at exhaustion. Hsp72 was associated with core temperature in the lower-intensity longer trial and with rate of core-temperature rise in the higher-intensity shorter trial.

**Why it matters:** Shows that Hsp responses can depend on both total duration/core temperature attained and exercise intensity/rate of temperature rise, complicating a single dry-sauna temperature-threshold narrative.

**Potential experiment signals:** core body temperature, heart rate, plasma Hsp72, plasma Hsp27, rate of core-temperature rise.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as same-mechanism context only: the source supports the plausibility that heat strain can alter Hsp72/Hsp27, but it is exercise-to-exhaustion evidence, not direct Bryan Johnson Sauna evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
