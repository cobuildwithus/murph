This source is included for **Near-direct dry-sauna interventions and acute dry-sauna dose evidence**.

**Findings:** `finding:pmid-35092517-head-face-neck-cooling-narrative` — The narrative review describes head, face, and neck cooling as practical per-cooling strategies that reduce local skin temperature and improve perceptual responses; it recommends matching modality to sport context and notes that more systematic modality-selection evidence is needed.

**Why it matters:** Supports classification of Bryan’s face/neck cooling as an adjunct for comfort and tolerability, not sauna efficacy.

**Potential experiment signals:** thermal-comfort, thermal-sensation, exercise-performance, perceived-exertion.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as cooling-adjunct context only.
