This source is included for **safety contraindications**.

## Why it matters

Useful for background and safety nuance, especially distinguishing ordinary use from misuse/abuse.

## Findings captured

- The review describes normal sauna with moderate cooling as increasing cardiac workload roughly like brisk walking and not posing circulatory risk to healthy people.
- The review states sauna bathing is generally well tolerated in healthy people and uncomplicated pregnancy, and may be taken by stable/asymptomatic cardiovascular patients without undue risk.

## Protocol takeaway

Review-level context frames ordinary sauna as tolerable for healthy and stable cardiovascular populations.

## Important limits

Narrative review; do not use for quantified efficacy claims or unstable clinical populations.
