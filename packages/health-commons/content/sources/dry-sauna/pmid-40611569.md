This source is included for **Post-exercise sauna, heat-acclimation, and performance variants**.

**Findings:** There were no statistically significant differences between exercise plus sauna and exercise-only for measured HRV indices after 8 weeks.

**Why it matters:** Preserves a null HRV finding from a near-adjacent randomized protocol so HRV claims are not over-promoted.

**Potential experiment signals:** HRV RMSSD, resting heart rate, sleep/recovery interpretation caveat

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not cite this source as evidence that regular post-exercise sauna improves HRV beyond exercise alone.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
