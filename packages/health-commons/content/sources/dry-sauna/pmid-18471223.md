This source is included for **Safety, heat illness, medications, pregnancy, alcohol, older-adult risk**.

**Findings:** The forensic review reported that death in sauna was rare even in Finland, but about half of cases were under the influence of alcohol, and prevention should focus on less alcohol and avoiding leaving intoxicated bathers alone.

**Why it matters:** It gives a rare but serious adverse-event boundary and reinforces alcohol/supervision warnings.

**Potential experiment signals:** alcohol-use screen, sauna-alone screen, dizziness or altered consciousness, emergency stop rules.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to support avoiding alcohol and avoiding leaving intoxicated or symptomatic users alone in sauna.
