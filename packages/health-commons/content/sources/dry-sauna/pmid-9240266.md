This source is included for **Fertility, semen, and groin-cooling safety/context**.

**Findings:** In 21 healthy volunteers, a clothing/support intervention increased scrotal temperature by about 0.8–1.0°C over long follow-up but did not affect mean sperm concentration, motility, morphology, viability, hyperactivation, or sperm function measures.

**Why it matters:** Important null boundary showing not every small scrotal-temperature increase suppresses semen parameters.

**Potential experiment signals:** semen-analysis, heat-risk.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to avoid overclaiming that any scrotal warming necessarily damages semen quality.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness and limitations:** Adjacent heat-threshold/null boundary, not direct protocol evidence. Mild scrotal temperature increase, not sauna; no cooling intervention; clothing model differs from heat spikes.
