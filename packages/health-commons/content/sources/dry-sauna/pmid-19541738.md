This source is included for **Measurement artifacts: core temperature, wearables, sweat, and hydration**.

**Findings:** This study compared regional patch collections with whole-body washdown during 90 minutes of cycling in heat to develop equations for estimating whole-body sweat sodium and potassium from regional patches. Effect/estimate: Direction only: regional patches can support estimation, but the regional-to-whole-body relation requires site-specific equations rather than direct substitution.

**Why it matters:** Core validation/caveat source for sweat-patch electrolyte measures versus whole-body electrolyte loss.

**Potential experiment signals:** fluid loss, measurement caveats

**Protocol takeaway:** Use sweat patches only as method-dependent hydration context; avoid presenting regional sodium as a direct whole-body sauna loss.

**Safety/adverse-event notes:** Hydration context: misinterpreting a single patch as total-body electrolyte loss may mislead replacement decisions.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Exercise-heat chamber study, not sauna; equation performance may not generalize to all environments or passive heat exposure. Population mismatch: Athletes cycling in heat, not passive dry-sauna users.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
