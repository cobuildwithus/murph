This source is included for **Post-exercise sauna, heat-acclimation, and performance variants**.

**Findings:** Endurance plus sauna lowered systolic BP at post, 30-minute, and 24-hour time points in the extracted record; diastolic BP fell acutely across loadings. Plasma volume decreased acutely and white blood cells increased after exercise-plus-sauna conditions, indicating fluid/physiological strain context.

**Why it matters:** Useful hemodynamic context and safety boundary, but acute male-only evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** morning blood pressure, resting heart rate, post-session symptoms

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as acute BP/hemodynamic context; do not infer durable BP lowering from a single-session study.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
