This source is included for **Near-direct dry-sauna interventions and acute dry-sauna dose evidence**.

**Findings:** `finding:pmid-30800676-overweight-men-4x10-physiology` — The repeated 4 x 10-minute dry-sauna exposure significantly increased measured physiological parameters (reported P<0.0001), produced about 0.65 kg body-mass loss, and linked heart rate, energy expenditure, blood pressure, and body-mass loss with anthropometric and body-composition features; the authors highlighted greater deleterious cardiovascular adaptation risk in men with the greatest obesity or body size.

**Why it matters:** Provides same-modality safety context near Bryan’s temperature range and highlights body-size/obesity as a heat-strain modifier.

**Potential experiment signals:** heart-rate, blood-pressure, body-mass-loss, energy-expenditure, body-composition.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for screening and heat-strain guardrails, not for efficacy claims.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
