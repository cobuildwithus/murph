This source is included for **Modality-boundary and adjacent passive-heat context**.

**Findings:** The review found published Bikram-yoga literature suggesting improvements in lower-body strength, range of motion, and balance in healthy adults, while adverse-event reporting was sparse; two trials reported no adverse events and many trials did not report adverse events. This is hot-yoga boundary evidence, not passive sauna evidence.

**Why it matters:** Useful for separating active heated yoga claims from passive sauna claims and for adverse-event-reporting caveats.

**Potential experiment signals:** estimated VO2max/performance context, sleep efficiency, tolerability symptoms, adverse event reporting.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as hot-yoga context and safety-reporting caveat only.
