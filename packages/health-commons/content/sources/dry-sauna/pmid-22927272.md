This source is included for **Modality-boundary and adjacent passive-heat context**.

**Findings:** A case report described severe hyponatraemia (sodium 120 mmol/L in accessible summaries) and tonic-clonic seizure after Bikram yoga and large water intake. It is not dry-sauna efficacy evidence, but it supports a hydration-safety boundary: avoid both dehydration and excessive plain-water intake.

**Why it matters:** Safety-only source for heat-plus-exercise hydration risk.

**Potential experiment signals:** hydration symptoms, body mass change / dehydration, tolerability symptoms, neurologic symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as hydration safety boundary, not protocol support.
