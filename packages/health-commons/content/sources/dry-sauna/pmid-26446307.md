This source is included for **Near-direct dry-sauna interventions and acute dry-sauna dose evidence**.

**Findings:** `finding:pmid-26446307-prophylactic-sauna-doms` — The sauna group showed lower deficits in range-of-motion and function measures such as grip strength and wrist-extension strength than controls (reported P<0.05 in extracted summaries), suggesting a prophylactic effect in this localized DOMS model.

**Why it matters:** Relevant to recovery claims only as an adjacent, narrow exercise model.

**Potential experiment signals:** delayed-onset-muscle-soreness, pain-intensity, range-of-motion, grip-strength, wrist-extension-strength.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not claim post-workout recovery benefit for the Bryan protocol from this prophylactic wrist-extensor model.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
