This source is included for **Modality-boundary and adjacent passive-heat context**.

**Findings:** Local cooling improved perceptions during hot-water immersion without inhibiting the acute IL-6 response; accessible abstracts reported IL-6 increases in HWI and HWI+fan versus control but not a significant HWI-vs-fan difference. This informs cooling-tactic context, not dry-sauna efficacy.

**Why it matters:** Relevant to comfort/cooling adaptations around a high-heat protocol while preserving modality mismatch.

**Potential experiment signals:** thermal comfort, tolerability symptoms, inflammatory response context, core/ear temperature measurement context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent HWI cooling-context evidence; do not infer groin/face/neck cooling efficacy in dry sauna.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
