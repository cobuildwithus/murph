This source is included for **season_latitude_weather_setting_modifiers**.

**Findings:** Latitude, work schedule, and outdoor-work opportunity produced large differences in natural-light dose. Natural-light exposure related to sleep and mood signals, but the observational setting makes causality and protocol translation uncertain. The population and exposure definition are mismatched to a prescribed morning outdoor-light habit. No protocol adverse-event signal was extracted from this source; use only as modifier/context evidence unless a finding explicitly states otherwise.

**Why it matters:** This source directly compares natural-light opportunities across geography and occupational setting, which helps bound how feasible and interpretable morning outdoor light is across users.

**Potential experiment signals:** natural light hours, work schedule, latitude, outdoor work status, insufficient sleep, depression symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context that baseline latitude and work-setting light exposure can dominate the dose achieved by a morning-light habit.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct default-protocol efficacy evidence.
