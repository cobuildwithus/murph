This source is included for **clinical_light_therapy_device_boundaries**.

**Findings:** Population: People with bipolar disorders across chronotherapeutic studies. Intervention/exposure: Chronotherapeutic treatments including bright light therapy, wake/sleep scheduling, and related circadian interventions. Comparator/control: Varied by reviewed intervention and study. Duration/follow-up: Varied by treatment and study Endpoints: bipolar depressive symptoms, mania/hypomania switch, sleep and circadian outcomes, tolerability. Effect/direction: The task force systematically reviewed efficacy and tolerability of major chronotherapeutic treatments in bipolar disorder and provides practice recommendations; accessible extraction confirms it is used to support adjunctive BLT recommendations for acute bipolar depression, but no single pooled effect applies. Safety/adverse events: Bipolar safety and tolerability are core review domains; full-text consultation is needed for specific switch-risk recommendations.

**Why it matters:** High-priority boundary source for bipolar-spectrum caution language.

**Potential experiment signals:** clinical recommendation, tolerability, switch risk, sleep/circadian symptoms

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use as efficacy support for self-directed outdoor-light habits; use as safety and clinical-supervision context.

**Directness and boundaries:** Directness is `clinical_supervised` with protocol-evidence scope `clinical_supervised`. Population mismatch: Bipolar disorder clinical care, not general protocol users.

**Limitations and uncertainty:** Guideline/recommendation synthesis; bipolar clinical population; heterogeneous chronotherapies; not outdoor-light specific.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
