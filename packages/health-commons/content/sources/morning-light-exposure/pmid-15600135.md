This source is included for **care_home_dementia_older_adult_indoor_light**.

**Findings:** This severe Alzheimer disease lighting study reported that increased light exposure, including morning and evening bright-light conditions, consolidated sleep at night and strengthened circadian rhythms by actigraphic measures.

**Why it matters:** Adds a foundational positive same-mechanism clinical signal, while emphasizing that dementia severity and institutional care are not the default protocol population.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep consolidation, nighttime activity, circadian rhythm strength, actigraphy

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for mechanism and outcome context only; do not convert severe-AD institutional findings into healthy-adult outdoor protocol claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
