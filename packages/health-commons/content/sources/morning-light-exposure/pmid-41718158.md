This source is included for **indoor_workplace_classroom_home_daylight**.

**Findings:** The melanopic-enhanced lighting group showed a greater reduction in attention-task line errors, while sleep quality, mood, sleepiness, and stress outcomes were not significantly improved in the accessible abstract. Both groups had natural daylight exposure, limiting isolation of the lighting intervention.

**Why it matters:** It is a current youth/classroom source showing that melanopic enhancement may affect cognition without necessarily improving sleep or mood.

**Potential experiment signals:** FAIR line errors and attention score, RCSQ sleep quality, ESS sleepiness, BDI-II mood, perceived stress, and classroom lighting condition.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent youth/classroom evidence and a null sleep/mood guardrail; do not cite as direct adult outdoor-light evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
