This source is included for **season_latitude_weather_setting_modifiers**.

**Findings:** Photoperiod/latitude effects can appear in sleep timing and quality metrics without changing sleep duration. Mood and fatigue effects may be more heterogeneous than sleep-timing effects in photoperiod comparisons. No protocol adverse-event signal was extracted from this source; use only as modifier/context evidence unless a finding explicitly states otherwise.

**Why it matters:** Strong latitude contrast relevant to generalizing the same outdoor-light advice across photoperiod regimes.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep diary, insomnia symptoms, fatigue, mood, latitude, photoperiod.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context that latitude may influence which outcomes change and when.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct default-protocol efficacy evidence.
