This source is included for **season_latitude_weather_setting_modifiers**.

**Findings:** Latitude and season changed bright-light exposure in everyday life. Winter bright-light scarcity was much larger at the higher-latitude site. Outdoor-light protocols are behavioral dose interventions as much as environmental exposure prescriptions. No protocol adverse-event signal was extracted from this source; use only as modifier/context evidence unless a finding explicitly states otherwise.

**Why it matters:** Foundational latitude-comparison field study aligned with the latitude modifier question.

**Potential experiment signals:** minutes ≥1000 lux, minutes ≥10000 lux, season, latitude, indoor/outdoor behavior.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context that the same behavioral instruction can yield different bright-light exposure by location and season.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct default-protocol efficacy evidence.
