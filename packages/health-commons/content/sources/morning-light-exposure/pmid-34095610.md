This source is included for **care_home_dementia_older_adult_indoor_light**.

**Findings:** This proof-of-concept study randomized 24 cognitively impaired nursing-home residents to dynamic lighting or control. The intervention partially improved one diurnal rhythm variable but did not consistently alter rest-activity rhythm outcomes; staff and mobility patterns were noted as likely stronger determinants.

**Why it matters:** A useful mixed result showing that dynamic lighting may not overcome nursing-home routine and mobility constraints.

**Potential experiment signals:** intradaily variability, rest-activity rhythm, mobility, staff routine, feasibility

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mixed same-mechanism context and a cue to track behavior/routine confounders alongside light exposure.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
