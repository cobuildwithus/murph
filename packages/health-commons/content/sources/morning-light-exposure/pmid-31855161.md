This source is included for **care_home_dementia_older_adult_indoor_light**.

**Findings:** In 46 older adults with ADRD across eight long-term-care facilities, a tailored lighting intervention improved PSQI scores versus baseline/control and showed favorable signals in rest-activity variability, depressive symptoms, and agitation.

**Why it matters:** High-priority open-access RCT for same-mechanism indoor circadian lighting in dementia care.

**Potential experiment signals:** PSQI, rest-activity variability, depressive symptoms, agitation, sleep quality

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as clinical same-mechanism evidence; do not promote to direct support for outdoor morning-light habit formation.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
