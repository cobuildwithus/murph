This source is included for **care_home_dementia_older_adult_indoor_light**.

**Findings:** In 11 care-home residents with dementia, a cohort study compared a static-light baseline with a daylight-simulating dynamic luminaire period. Positive affect, social isolation, and feeling-at-home measures improved with moderate effect sizes, but sleep metrics were nonsignificant and agitation did not show clear improvement.

**Why it matters:** Recent open-access study spanning well-being, sleep, agitation, and rest-activity endpoints in dementia care.

**Potential experiment signals:** positive affect, social isolation, feeling at home, sleep metrics, agitation, rest-activity patterns

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for context about daylight-simulating indoor lighting and mixed outcome domains; not direct outdoor protocol evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
