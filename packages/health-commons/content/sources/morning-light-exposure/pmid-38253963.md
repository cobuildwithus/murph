This source is included for **season_latitude_weather_setting_modifiers**.

**Findings:** Polar evidence is heterogeneous and suggests both beneficial and adverse sleep-relevant light effects depending on season and intensity. Artificial-light findings in polar regions should not be treated as direct support for outdoor morning light. No protocol adverse-event signal was extracted from this source; use only as modifier/context evidence unless a finding explicitly states otherwise.

**Why it matters:** Meta-analytic polar-light synthesis useful for modifier evidence when direct protocol trials are sparse.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep duration, sleep efficiency, sleep timing, light type, season, polar location.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as background that polar light-sleep findings are mixed and context-dependent.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct default-protocol efficacy evidence.
