This source is included for **care_home_dementia_older_adult_indoor_light**.

**Findings:** This ambient bright-light trial in persons with dementia did not support ambient bright light as a general treatment for depressive symptoms, although subgroup variation was reported.

**Why it matters:** Preserves a negative/mixed mood result so downstream synthesis does not overstate psychological benefits from light in dementia care.

**Potential experiment signals:** depressive symptoms, Cornell Scale for Depression in Dementia, subgroup response, light timing

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a cautionary adjacent source for mood claims; outdoor morning-light pages should not imply universal depression benefits from this evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
