This source is included for **season_latitude_weather_setting_modifiers**.

**Findings:** Indoor daylight adequacy in cloudy climates depends on high daylight design and unobstructed window access. Cloudy weather and glare limit indoor daylight as a simple substitute for outdoor exposure. No protocol adverse-event signal was extracted from this source; use only as modifier/context evidence unless a finding explicitly states otherwise.

**Why it matters:** Clear shard-specific paper on cloudy-weather feasibility and indoor daylight targets.

**Potential experiment signals:** weather/cloud tag, melanopic EDI, window orientation, glare, indoor daylight access.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context that cloudy climates may require outdoor exposure or better-designed indoor substitutes.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct default-protocol efficacy evidence.
