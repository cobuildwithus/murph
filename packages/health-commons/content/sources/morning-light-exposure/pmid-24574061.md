This source is included for **care_home_dementia_older_adult_indoor_light**.

**Findings:** This Cochrane review concluded that evidence was insufficient to justify bright-light therapy for dementia outcomes including cognition, activities of daily living, sleep, challenging behavior, and psychiatric disturbance. It called for replication and better evidence.

**Why it matters:** A high-quality boundary source that prevents overclaiming dementia light-therapy benefits.

**Potential experiment signals:** cognition, activities of daily living, sleep, challenging behavior, psychiatric disturbance

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only caution; do not cite dementia light therapy as settled direct support for outdoor morning light.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
