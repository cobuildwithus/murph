This source is included for **free_living_observational_measurement**.

**Findings:** In 39 participants monitored for 30 days, Swiss participants had brighter daytime melanopic exposure and darker evenings than Malaysian participants. Extracted values included Swiss M10m 661 lux versus Malaysian 229 lux, daily melanopic EDI 437 versus 170 lux, and evening melanopic EDI 3.5 versus 17.6 lux. Compliance with daytime, evening, nighttime, and overall healthy-light recommendations differed by site; self-reported LEBA correlated with objective exposure in Switzerland but not Malaysia.

**Why it matters:** It is a high-value modern melanopic measurement source for environmental and behavioral modifiers relevant to outdoor morning light routines.

**Potential experiment signals:** melanopic EDI, daytime bright-light compliance, evening dim-light compliance, nighttime darkness, LEBA self-report, site/geography.

**Population mismatch / directness:** Small cross-country field sample; exposure measurement rather than intervention; not designed to test sleep outcomes. Directness: `same_mechanism`.

**Limitations:** small sample; site/culture/climate confounding; not a sleep-efficacy study; LEBA method differences; limited participant-level adjustment.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context for exposure modifiers and measurement choices, especially melanopic EDI and cultural/geographic differences.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
