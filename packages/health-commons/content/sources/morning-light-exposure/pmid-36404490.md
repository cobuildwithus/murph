This source is included for **season_latitude_weather_setting_modifiers**.

**Findings:** Student sleep and circadian timing shifted later in winter relative to summer. Daytime light and post-dusk light exerted opposite directional associations with circadian timing. No protocol adverse-event signal was extracted from this source; use only as modifier/context evidence unless a finding explicitly states otherwise.

**Why it matters:** This high-yield season-modifier source ties real-world daytime light to sleep/circadian timing and flags post-dusk light as an opposing exposure.

**Potential experiment signals:** daytime light minutes ≥50 lux, post-dusk light exposure, MSFc, sleep onset, wake time, season.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context that daytime/morning light and post-dusk light should be tracked together.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct default-protocol efficacy evidence.
