This source is included for **direct_outdoor_daylight_protocol**.

**Findings:** In 1,762 Brazilian adults, self-reported sunlight exposure was analyzed for before 10 AM, 10 AM–3 PM, and after 3 PM. Each 30-minute increment of morning sunlight before 10 AM was associated with an approximately 23-minute earlier sleep midpoint and a lower PSQI total score. Sunlight exposure was not significantly associated with total sleep time, sleep latency, or sleep efficiency. The source is highly direct for morning sunlight timing but remains cross-sectional and self-reported.

**Why it matters:** Direct adult study of morning sunlight exposure before 10 AM and sleep timing/quality, with duration increments reported.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep midpoint, PSQI total score, sleep duration, sleep latency, sleep efficiency, morning sunlight minutes

**Protocol takeaway:** Morning sunlight before 10 AM was associated with earlier sleep midpoint and lower PSQI total score, but not with total sleep time, latency, or efficiency.
