**Findings:** The review links UV exposure with cataract and macular-degeneration risk and emphasizes UV protection such as sunglasses that block wavelengths below 400 nm and wraparound designs to reduce reflected UV.

**Why it matters:** UV exposure is linked to cataract and macular-degeneration risk in ophthalmology review evidence.
