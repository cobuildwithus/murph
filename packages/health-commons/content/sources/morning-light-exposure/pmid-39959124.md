This source is included for **athlete_exercise_travel_sleep_hygiene_bundles**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries report that sleep hygiene was most effective for sleep quantity, while supplementation and light therapy showed improvements in sleep quality and quantity; heterogeneity limits certainty.

**Why it matters:** Recent overview of where light interventions sit among elite-sport sleep interventions.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep quantity; sleep quality; recovery; performance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only review evidence for athlete sleep interventions.

**Directness and boundaries:** Systematic review across heterogeneous interventions; light therapy is not isolated to outdoor morning exposure.

**Limitations and uncertainty:**

- Review-level evidence.
- Interventions and measures varied.
- Elite-sport population.
- Light therapies not specific to outdoor morning light.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
