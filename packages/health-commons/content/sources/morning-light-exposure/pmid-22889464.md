This source is included for **timing_dose_circadian_metrics**.

**Findings:**
- The source reports that evening ambient light exposure can reduce circadian phase advances to morning light.
- The title specifies this effect was independent of sleep deprivation.
- It is a boundary finding for protocol adherence and evening-light hygiene.

**Intervention or exposure:** Evening ambient light exposure before morning light

**Comparator or control:** Lower evening ambient light or different evening exposure context

**Duration or follow-up:** Evening and morning light protocol; exact duration not extracted

**Endpoints:** Circadian phase advance to morning light

**Effect estimates or direction:** Direction: evening ambient light reduced phase advances to morning light.

**Safety/adverse events:** No adverse events extracted.

**Limitations:**
- Controlled context.
- Participant count/effect estimates not extracted.
- Not outdoor daylight exposure.

**Population mismatch:** Controlled light exposure differs from daily outdoor routines.

**Directness to Morning Outdoor Light Exposure:** `same_mechanism`.

**Why it matters:** It explains why evening light can undermine a morning-light routine.

**Potential experiment signals:** evening ambient light, morning light, phase advance, sleep deprivation.

**Protocol takeaway:** The protocol should not imply morning light alone can fully compensate for high evening light exposure.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
