This source is included for **season_latitude_weather_setting_modifiers**.

**Findings:** Mood and sleep-activity signals may shift with season in office workers, making them potential but confounded protocol outcomes. Work setting can limit outdoor daylight exposure and make indoor lighting a weak substitute for seasonally variable outdoor light. No protocol adverse-event signal was extracted from this source; use only as modifier/context evidence unless a finding explicitly states otherwise.

**Why it matters:** Useful for ordinary indoor-working routines where morning outdoor-light feasibility may change by season.

**Potential experiment signals:** time outdoors, bright light minutes, positive affect, sleep-activity diary, office daylight access.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context that office workers may need deliberate outdoor light, particularly in low-daylight seasons.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct default-protocol efficacy evidence.
