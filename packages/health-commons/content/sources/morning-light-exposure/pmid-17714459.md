This source is included for **care_home_dementia_older_adult_indoor_light**.

**Findings:** In a dementia care-setting trial, nighttime sleep increased significantly in participants exposed to morning or all-day high-intensity environmental light, with effects most prominent in severe dementia. Reported clinical magnitude was modest.

**Why it matters:** Important same-mechanism trial for sleep/activity effects of environmental light in dementia, while preserving the caveat that effect size may be small.

**Potential experiment signals:** nighttime sleep, activity, dementia severity subgroup, environmental light timing

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent clinical context only and avoid presenting small institutional effects as healthy-adult outdoor efficacy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
