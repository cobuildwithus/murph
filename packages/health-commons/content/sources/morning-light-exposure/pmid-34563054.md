This source is included for **care_home_dementia_older_adult_indoor_light**.

**Findings:** In 61 nursing-home residents with severe dementia, a 24-week cluster RCT used ceiling LED panels delivering bright light during the daytime. Acrophase advanced after 16 weeks, but other rest-activity rhythm measures did not significantly improve; the authors concluded ambient bright light did not improve rest-activity rhythms overall.

**Why it matters:** High-quality null/mixed same-mechanism evidence that should remain visible beside positive dementia-lighting studies.

**Potential experiment signals:** acrophase, rest-activity rhythm, actigraphy, interdaily stability, intradaily variability

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a mixed/null adjacent RCT; it does not directly support outdoor morning-light efficacy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
