This source is included for **care_home_dementia_older_adult_indoor_light**.

**Findings:** This PLOS ONE meta-analysis of 15 RCTs involving 598 patients with Alzheimer disease reported that light therapy improved sleep-efficiency and rest-activity rhythm metrics and reduced depression, agitation, and caregiver burden, while emphasizing limited sample sizes and the need for larger studies.

**Why it matters:** Recent open-access meta-analysis with sleep, circadian, and psychobehavioral endpoints in an Alzheimer disease population.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep efficiency, interdaily stability, intradaily variability, depression, agitation, caregiver burden

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as Alzheimer-specific clinical synthesis only; do not present it as direct evidence for outdoor morning light in healthy adults.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
