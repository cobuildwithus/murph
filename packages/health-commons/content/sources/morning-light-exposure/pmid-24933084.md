This source is included for **season_latitude_weather_setting_modifiers**.

**Findings:** Young adults at high latitude may show seasonal changes in sleep timing and chronotype. Mood vulnerability may modify how seasonal photoperiod changes affect sleep/circadian timing. No protocol adverse-event signal was extracted from this source; use only as modifier/context evidence unless a finding explicitly states otherwise.

**Why it matters:** Additional Arctic observational context for adaptation and heterogeneity at high latitude.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep timing, chronotype, fatigue, depressive symptoms, season, behavioral habits.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context that age/population and mood state may modify high-latitude responses.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct default-protocol efficacy evidence.
