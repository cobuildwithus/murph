This source is included for **free_living_observational_measurement**.

**Findings:** In 75 adolescents monitored during school and vacation, higher Sleep Regularity Index was associated with a more regular Light Regularity Index in both periods. Sleep regularity was not significantly associated with MLiT variation or daily photoperiod variation. Light metrics were less variable during vacation than school term in the extracted summary.

**Why it matters:** It adds a regularity endpoint, helping future Murph experiments track consistency rather than only dose.

**Potential experiment signals:** Light Regularity Index, Sleep Regularity Index, social jetlag, daily photoperiod variation, MLiT variation.

**Population mismatch / directness:** Adolescents, not adult protocol users; regularity rather than prescribed outdoor morning-light dose. Directness: `adjacent_variant`.

**Limitations:** adolescent sample; observational; regularity metric needs replication; not morning-outdoor-specific; no causal inference.

**Protocol takeaway:** Consider light regularity as a context endpoint, but do not infer causality or adult protocol efficacy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
