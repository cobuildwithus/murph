This source is included for **indoor_workplace_classroom_home_daylight**.

**Findings:** Blue-enriched white workplace light improved multiple self-reported outcomes including alertness, performance, sleep quality, daytime sleepiness, mood, and visual discomfort compared with standard white light. Because outcomes were subjective and the exposure was indoor electric lighting, the study is adjacent only.

**Why it matters:** This foundational workplace trial is useful for same-mechanism evidence about melanopic/blue-enriched daytime light exposure, while remaining outside the outdoor protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** Alertness, daytime sleepiness, sleep quality, performance, mood, concentration, irritability, headache, and eye discomfort.

**Protocol takeaway:** Daytime spectral quality may influence alertness and sleep quality, but this should be cited only as an indoor electric-light context source.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
