This source is included for **athlete_exercise_travel_sleep_hygiene_bundles**.

**Findings:** Accessible review text reports sleep extension and naps as more representative/effective strategies; mindfulness and light manipulation were promising but required more studies, with evidence quality/heterogeneity limiting certainty.

**Why it matters:** Positions light exposure alongside better-established athlete sleep interventions like sleep extension and naps.

**Potential experiment signals:** athletic performance; sleep quality; recovery.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only systematic-review evidence.

**Directness and boundaries:** Light manipulation studies had different objectives and were not direct outdoor morning protocols.

**Limitations and uncertainty:**

- Review-level evidence.
- High intervention heterogeneity.
- Risk-of-bias concerns in included studies.
- Athlete populations and performance endpoints.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
