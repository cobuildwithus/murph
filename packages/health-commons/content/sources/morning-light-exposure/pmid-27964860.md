This source is included for **free_living_observational_measurement**.

**Findings:** The DSWPD group had delayed bed and wake times, similar sleep duration, poorer PSQI, more activity/light at 02:00-04:00, and less activity/light at 08:00-11:00 than controls. Total 24-hour light and activity were not significantly different. Later light exposure correlated with depressive-symptom and sleep-quality measures in the extracted record.

**Why it matters:** It isolates timing patterns that are mechanistically relevant to morning-light recommendations while preserving the clinical population mismatch.

**Potential experiment signals:** light exposure 02:00-04:00, light exposure 08:00-11:00, PSQI, BDI, sleep midpoint.

**Population mismatch / directness:** Adults with delayed sleep-wake phase disorder, not a general healthy-adult protocol population. Directness: `adjacent_variant`.

**Limitations:** clinical case-control design; observational; possible mood/sleep disorder confounding; not an intervention; no causal inference.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent context that delayed sleepers may have low morning and high late-night light exposure; do not present as general adult protocol evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
