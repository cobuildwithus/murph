This source is included for **season_latitude_weather_setting_modifiers**.

**Findings:** Even extreme photoperiod/isolation settings may not show simple linear worsening in sleep metrics across winter. Sleep and mood can interact under light-constrained extreme conditions, but direction and causality remain complex. No protocol adverse-event signal was extracted from this source; use only as modifier/context evidence unless a finding explicitly states otherwise.

**Why it matters:** Boundary source for natural morning-light cues when they are severely constrained.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep quality, sleep onset, mood domains, isolation/stress context, polar winter.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as polar boundary context; do not treat as efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct default-protocol efficacy evidence.
