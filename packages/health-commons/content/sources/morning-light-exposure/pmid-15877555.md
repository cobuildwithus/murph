This source is included for **athlete_exercise_travel_sleep_hygiene_bundles**.

**Findings:** Daytime sleep decreased more in the intervention group and activity/social engagement increased; nighttime sleep effects were limited, with no clear effect on percent nighttime sleep or number of awakenings in the accessible abstract-level extraction.

**Why it matters:** Influential bundle trial that helps prevent over-attributing nursing-home program effects to sunlight alone.

**Potential experiment signals:** daytime sleep; nighttime awakenings; percent sleep at night; physical activity; social activity.

**Protocol takeaway:** Cite only as context for bundled sleep-wake programs; do not use as direct evidence for standalone morning outdoor light exposure.

**Directness and boundaries:** Light was bundled with activity, out-of-bed time, routines, and nighttime environmental changes in a frail institutional older-adult population.

**Limitations and uncertainty:**

- Multicomponent intervention; sunlight not isolated.
- Nursing-home population is a clinical/specialized mismatch.
- No detailed adverse-event extraction was available.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
