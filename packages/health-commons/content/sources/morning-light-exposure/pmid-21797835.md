This source is included for **athlete_exercise_travel_sleep_hygiene_bundles**.

**Findings:** Walking, light, and combination groups had greater post-test improvements in actigraphic total wake time than controls (effect sizes 0.51-0.63); no significant SDI improvement and no group differences at 6 months. No unexpected or serious adverse events were attributed to interventions.

**Why it matters:** Important RCT with separable walking and light arms, but clinical population and timing/modality mismatch.

**Potential experiment signals:** total wake time; Sleep Disorders Inventory; sleep percent; number of awakenings; total sleep time; adherence; adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as clinical adjacent evidence; do not generalize to morning outdoor light without caveats.

**Directness and boundaries:** Clinical AD population; light was artificial and timed before bedtime, not outdoor morning light.

**Limitations and uncertainty:**

- Clinical Alzheimer’s disease population.
- Caregiver-supervised artificial light box rather than outdoor morning light.
- Adherence declined over time.
- No significant SDI improvement and no 6-month group differences.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
