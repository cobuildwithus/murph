This source is included for **season_latitude_weather_setting_modifiers**.

**Findings:** Sunlight exposure behavior can differ dramatically by season in SAD, making season and baseline mood vulnerability important modifiers. Mood-seasonal clinical status may modify baseline exposure and outcome expectations. No protocol adverse-event signal was extracted from this source; use only as modifier/context evidence unless a finding explicitly states otherwise.

**Why it matters:** Foundational seasonal natural-sunlight paper for behavior and exposure opportunities across seasons.

**Potential experiment signals:** sunlight exposure hours, season, SAD symptoms, outdoor behavior.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context that winter daylight behavior may be markedly lower in seasonally vulnerable users.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct default-protocol efficacy evidence.
