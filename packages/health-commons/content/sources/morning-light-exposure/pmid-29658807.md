This source is included for **athlete_exercise_travel_sleep_hygiene_bundles**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries report delayed sleep/wake timing and improved morning/evening reaction-time measures on later study days.

**Why it matters:** Shows that sports-light protocols may intentionally shift timing away from morningness.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep wake timing; reaction time.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as adjacent athlete circadian-timing evidence.

**Directness and boundaries:** Goal was phase delay for late competition; intervention was artificial bright light in elite athletes.

**Limitations and uncertainty:**

- Sport-specific population.
- Artificial bright light.
- Timing goal differs from a default morning outdoor protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
