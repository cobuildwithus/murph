This source is included for **care_home_dementia_older_adult_indoor_light**.

**Findings:** In 47 residents with dementia across senior-care facilities, 25 weeks of all-day circadian-effective lighting was associated with improvements in PSQI sleep quality, depressive symptoms, agitation/behavior, and sleep-efficiency-related measures.

**Why it matters:** Adds long-duration same-mechanism evidence showing that sustained indoor circadian-effective lighting can affect multiple outcomes in dementia care.

**Potential experiment signals:** PSQI, sleep efficiency, depression, agitation, behavior, long-term adherence

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as long-term indoor circadian-light context only; do not equate all-day facility lighting with brief outdoor morning exposure.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
