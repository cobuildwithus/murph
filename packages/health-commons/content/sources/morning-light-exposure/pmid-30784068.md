This source is included for **athlete_exercise_travel_sleep_hygiene_bundles**.

**Findings:** Accessible extraction identified exercise as a circadian zeitgeber; exact phase-shift estimates were not extracted.

**Why it matters:** Important for interpreting outdoor exercise routines where exercise may be the active zeitgeber.

**Potential experiment signals:** circadian phase; exercise phase-response curve; sleep timing.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mechanism/context evidence for exercise confounding.

**Directness and boundaries:** Mechanistic exercise study, not a light intervention or morning outdoor protocol.

**Limitations and uncertainty:**

- No light intervention.
- Mechanistic lab/controlled protocol.
- Sample size/effect estimates not reliably extracted.
- Not an efficacy study for sleep outcomes.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
