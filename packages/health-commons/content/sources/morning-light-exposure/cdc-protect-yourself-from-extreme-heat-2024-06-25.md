**Findings:** CDC advises staying in air-conditioned locations as much as possible, drinking fluids even without thirst, scheduling outdoor activities carefully, wearing loose/lightweight/light-colored clothing and sunscreen, pacing oneself, cooling down, checking on others, and checking local health/safety updates.
