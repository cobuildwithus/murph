This source is included for **timing_dose_circadian_metrics**.

**Findings:**
- The study compared modern electric-light living with exposure to the natural light-dark cycle.
- Natural light-dark exposure shifted circadian timing toward better alignment with the solar day.
- Because the exposure was full-cycle natural light-dark living, it should not be treated as evidence that a short morning-only walk produces the same effect.

**Intervention or exposure:** Natural light-dark cycle with high daytime outdoor light and low nighttime electric light

**Comparator or control:** Modern electric-light environment

**Duration or follow-up:** Field exposure over a camping/natural light-dark interval

**Endpoints:** Circadian phase timing and sleep timing

**Effect estimates or direction:** Direction: natural light-dark exposure advanced/aligned circadian timing relative to modern electric-light living.

**Safety/adverse events:** No adverse events extracted from batch materials.

**Limitations:**
- Small naturalistic field design; participant count not extracted here.
- Bundled exposure prevents isolating morning light.
- Not a pragmatic daily protocol trial.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy adult field exposure rather than diverse users with brief morning routines.

**Directness to Morning Outdoor Light Exposure:** `same_mechanism`.

**Why it matters:** It is foundational direct natural-daylight evidence for circadian entrainment mechanisms.

**Potential experiment signals:** daytime outdoor light, evening darkness, melatonin phase, sleep timing.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to explain why morning outdoor daylight and evening darkness are mechanistically paired, not to set a short-dose prescription.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
