This source is included for **clinical_light_therapy_device_boundaries**.

**Findings:** Population: Hospitalized patients with severe and refractory depression. Intervention/exposure: Sunny hospital rooms. Comparator/control: Dull hospital rooms. Duration/follow-up: Index hospitalization Endpoints: length of stay/recovery time. Effect/direction: Patients in sunny rooms reportedly stayed 16.9 days versus 19.5 days in dull rooms, a 2.6-day or about 15% shorter stay (p<0.05). Safety/adverse events: No adverse events were extracted.

**Why it matters:** Supports including through-window daylight as clinical context only, not default outdoor protocol evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** hospital length of stay

**Protocol takeaway:** Room daylight exposure is confounded and indirect relative to morning outdoor exposure.

**Directness and boundaries:** Directness is `clinical_supervised` with protocol-evidence scope `clinical_supervised`. Population mismatch: Inpatient severe/refractory depression, not community habit users.

**Limitations and uncertainty:** Observational room-assignment context; sample count not extracted; severe/refractory inpatient depression; through-window daylight rather than outdoor light.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
