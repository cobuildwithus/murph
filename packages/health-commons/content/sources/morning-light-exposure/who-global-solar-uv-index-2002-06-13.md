**Findings:** The guide defines the UVI as a measure of UV radiation at the Earth's surface and an indicator of potential skin damage; it is intended to alert people to adopt protective measures when exposed to UV radiation.

**Why it matters:** The WHO Global Solar UV Index gives a public-health framework for deciding when outdoor light needs sun-protection boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** UV Index, skin-damage potential, sun-protection messaging, public-health prevention.
