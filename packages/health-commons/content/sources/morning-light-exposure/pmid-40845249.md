This source is included for **athlete_exercise_travel_sleep_hygiene_bundles**.

**Findings:** Accessible abstract-level summaries report improvements in mood and some performance parameters but no change in sleep parameters.

**Why it matters:** Recent athlete paper close to the protocol concept but still not outdoor-only or light-only.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep parameters; sleepiness; mood; fatigue; heart rate; performance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context that combined light-hygiene bundles may have mixed endpoint-specific effects.

**Directness and boundaries:** Morning light was artificial bright light and bundled with nighttime device restriction.

**Limitations and uncertainty:**

- Combined intervention prevents isolating morning bright light.
- Elite-athlete population.
- No detailed adverse-event extraction was identified.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
