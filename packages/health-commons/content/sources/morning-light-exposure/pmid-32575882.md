This source is included for **season_latitude_weather_setting_modifiers**.

**Findings:** Photoperiod changed real-world light exposure and activity, reinforcing season as a protocol modifier. The population illustrates that outdoor daylight and night darkness can be a combined lifestyle pattern rather than an isolated morning habit. No protocol adverse-event signal was extracted from this source; use only as modifier/context evidence unless a finding explicitly states otherwise.

**Why it matters:** Shows seasonal natural-light exposure in a population with unusually high daylight and low nocturnal artificial light.

**Potential experiment signals:** daytime lux, nighttime darkness, activity, photoperiod, time outdoors.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context that modern indoor users may not share the same baseline daylight/darkness contrast.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct default-protocol efficacy evidence.
