This source is included for **athlete_exercise_travel_sleep_hygiene_bundles**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries identify irregular sleep/wake patterns among student-athletes with early morning training schedules; quantitative estimates were not extracted.

**Why it matters:** Important early-schedule athlete boundary paper for distinguishing morning timing effects from light itself.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep duration; sleep regularity; sleep quality; sleep timing.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as schedule/population-mismatch context only.

**Directness and boundaries:** Training schedule study, not light exposure.

**Limitations and uncertainty:**

- Observational design.
- No light exposure intervention.
- Student-athlete population.
- Exact sample size/effects not reliably extracted.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
