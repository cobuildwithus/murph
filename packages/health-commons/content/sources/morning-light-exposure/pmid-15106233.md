This source is included for **clinical_light_therapy_device_boundaries**.

**Findings:** Population: Patients with non-seasonal depression. Intervention/exposure: Bright light therapy for non-seasonal depression. Comparator/control: Control/sham or usual treatment comparators in included trials. Duration/follow-up: Mostly short-term treatment in included studies Endpoints: depressive symptoms, response, short-term efficacy. Effect/direction: The review concluded that bright light therapy offered modest though promising antidepressive efficacy for non-seasonal depression, especially short-term; exact pooled estimates and study count were not extracted from the accessible summary. Safety/adverse events: Adverse-event extraction was not captured from accessible summary.

**Why it matters:** Preserves early cautious appraisal before later RCT/meta-analytic updates.

**Potential experiment signals:** short-term depressive symptoms, response

**Protocol takeaway:** Use newer syntheses for estimates; do not convert to outdoor protocol evidence.

**Directness and boundaries:** Directness is `clinical_supervised` with protocol-evidence scope `clinical_supervised`. Population mismatch: Non-seasonal depression patients, not general morning outdoor-light users.

**Limitations and uncertainty:** Older Cochrane evidence base; modest and promising rather than definitive conclusion; study counts/effects not extracted; clinical/device treatment not outdoor exposure.
