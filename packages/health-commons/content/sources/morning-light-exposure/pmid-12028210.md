**Findings:** This landmark dementia nursing-home trial reported no clear improvement in nighttime sleep or daytime alertness across treatment groups, while morning bright light delayed activity rhythm peak and strengthened circadian rhythm robustness.

**Why it matters:** Preserves an early mixed result showing that circadian-rhythm signals may shift without obvious sleep improvement in dementia care settings.

**Potential experiment signals:** nighttime sleep, daytime alertness, activity acrophase, circadian rhythm robustness

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as same-mechanism clinical context and as a warning that rhythm effects may not translate into simple sleep benefits.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
