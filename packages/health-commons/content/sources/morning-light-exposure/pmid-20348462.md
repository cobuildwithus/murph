This source is included for **season_latitude_weather_setting_modifiers**.

**Findings:** Baseline light exposure is not uniform across age groups and may be higher in some older adults. No protocol adverse-event signal was extracted from this source; use only as modifier/context evidence unless a finding explicitly states otherwise.

**Why it matters:** Helps with feasibility assumptions because baseline real-world light exposure differs by age.

**Potential experiment signals:** waking minutes <100 lux, minutes ≥1000 lux, age group, season, sleep timing.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context that age may change baseline light dose and intervention contrast.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct default-protocol efficacy evidence.
