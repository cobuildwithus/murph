This source is included for **clinical_light_therapy_device_boundaries**.

**Findings:** Population: Adults and children with circadian rhythm sleep-wake disorders. Intervention/exposure: Treatment guidance including melatonin, light therapy, and behavioral interventions. Comparator/control: Not applicable for guideline. Duration/follow-up: Varies by treatment and disorder; guideline emphasizes diagnostic monitoring of sleep-wake rhythm. Endpoints: sleep-wake rhythm, diagnosis, treatment selection. Effect/direction: The guideline identifies melatonin and light therapy among the most effective treatment methods for circadian rhythm sleep-wake disorders and also recommends behavioral interventions; no outdoor-specific outcome estimate is provided. Safety/adverse events: Safety rates were not extracted from accessible abstract-level text.

**Why it matters:** Consensus boundary source for clinical light use and behavioral co-interventions.

**Potential experiment signals:** diagnostic sleep-wake monitoring, treatment selection, behavioral co-interventions

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not generalize clinical CRSWD light recommendations to a wellness outdoor-light protocol without caveat.

**Directness and boundaries:** Directness is `clinical_supervised` with protocol-evidence scope `clinical_supervised`. Population mismatch: Diagnosed circadian rhythm sleep-wake disorders, not healthy protocol users.

**Limitations and uncertainty:** Guideline rather than primary evidence; clinical CRSWD population; not outdoor-light specific.
