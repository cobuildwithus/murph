This source is included for **athlete_exercise_travel_sleep_hygiene_bundles**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries indicate morning appointment time, rather than training load, affected sleep during camp; exact effect estimates were not extracted.

**Why it matters:** Strong schedule-boundary athlete study distinguishing morning timing effects from light itself.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep duration; sleep timing; training load; recovery.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as schedule-boundary context only.

**Directness and boundaries:** Schedule exposure, not light exposure.

**Limitations and uncertainty:**

- Observational design.
- No light intervention.
- Young elite rugby population.
- Exact sample size/effects not reliably extracted.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
