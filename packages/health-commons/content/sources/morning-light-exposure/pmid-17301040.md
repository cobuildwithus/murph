This source is included for **athlete_exercise_travel_sleep_hygiene_bundles**.

**Findings:** Active portion duration increased and the rest/activity rhythm rise shifted earlier; adjusted analyses retained active-duration and rhythm-rise findings in accessible extraction.

**Why it matters:** Provides objective rhythm outcomes for a bundle often cited around daytime light while preserving the non-isolated intervention boundary.

**Potential experiment signals:** rest-activity rhythm; active duration; actigraphy; daytime activity.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent rhythm-bundle context only, with explicit population and cointervention caveats.

**Directness and boundaries:** Outdoor bright light was inseparable from activity, out-of-bed, bedtime, and nighttime environmental components.

**Limitations and uncertainty:**

- Light exposure was not isolated.
- Institutional older-adult population limits generalization.
- Physiologic circadian markers such as DLMO were not the central extracted outcome.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
