This source is included for **free_living_observational_measurement**.

**Findings:** In 12 DSWPD cases and 12 controls, DSWPD participants had later clock-time light patterns but, when aligned to DLMO, their light pattern was earlier. They had less daily phase-advancing light exposure, particularly in the late advancing window. Extracted group data showed worse PSQI and BDI, later sleep start/end/midpoint and later DLMO, while sleep duration was not different.

**Why it matters:** It is one of the more mechanistically aligned observational studies because it maps habitual light to DLMO and phase-advancing windows.

**Potential experiment signals:** DLMO, phase-advancing light exposure, PSQI, BDI, sleep midpoint.

**Population mismatch / directness:** Circadian-disorder population and small case-control sample; not a general adult protocol test. Directness: `adjacent_variant`.

**Limitations:** small sample; clinical population; observational; complex circadian alignment; not outdoor-specific.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent mechanistic timing context; do not promote it into direct protocol efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
