**Findings:** Population: Patients with seasonal affective disorder in treatment studies published from January 1989 to March 1995. Intervention/exposure: SAD treatments including bright light therapy and medications. Comparator/control: Varied by included studies. Duration/follow-up: Varied by included study Endpoints: depression symptoms, treatment efficacy. Effect/direction: The review concluded several well-studied effective treatments exist for SAD, including light therapy and medications; accessible text states many studies support fluorescent light-box efficacy, while further research was still needed. Safety/adverse events: Specific adverse-event extraction was not captured.
