This source is included for **season_latitude_weather_setting_modifiers**.

**Findings:** Ground reflectance from snow can materially change outdoor-light dose under winter conditions. Weather-modified light dose may affect acute circadian/alertness endpoints without clearly shifting all sleep or mood outcomes. No protocol adverse-event signal was extracted from this source; use only as modifier/context evidence unless a finding explicitly states otherwise.

**Why it matters:** Rare weather-specific source for how snow cover changes real-world light dose and acute circadian-relevant outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:** snow cover, morning lux/melanopic proxy, morning melatonin, sleepiness, mood, activity.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context that snowy conditions may increase light dose despite winter season.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct default-protocol efficacy evidence.
