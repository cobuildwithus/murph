This source is included for **athlete_exercise_travel_sleep_hygiene_bundles**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries report greater sleep duration during/after travel and improvements in some performance, mood, and motivation measures; intermittent sprint performance did not clearly improve.

**Why it matters:** High-value athlete-adjacent travel bundle paper with performance and sleep outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep duration; jet lag; mood; motivation; performance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context that light is often paired with sleep hygiene in travel protocols; avoid direct causal claims for morning outdoor light.

**Directness and boundaries:** Light exposure was bundled with sleep hygiene and occurred in a travel/athlete context.

**Limitations and uncertainty:**

- Sleep hygiene and light not separable.
- Physically trained male sample.
- Travel-specific setting.
- Some performance endpoints showed no clear effect.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
