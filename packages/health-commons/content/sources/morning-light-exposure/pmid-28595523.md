This source is included for **care_home_dementia_older_adult_indoor_light**.

**Findings:** This care-home study compared blue-enriched overhead lighting with standard lighting in communal rooms and measured activity, actigraphic sleep, mood, alertness, and psychomotor vigilance outcomes. Extracted details emphasize relevance to indoor daytime-light mechanisms, while outcome direction should be treated cautiously because full effect estimates were not extracted here.

**Why it matters:** Directly addresses indoor daytime spectral enrichment in older care-home residents, a close same-mechanism variant but not outdoor morning light.

**Potential experiment signals:** activity, actigraphic sleep, mood, alertness, psychomotor vigilance

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for care-home indoor spectral-light context and measurement ideas; do not use as a direct healthy-adult protocol claim.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
