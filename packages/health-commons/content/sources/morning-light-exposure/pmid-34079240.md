This source is included for **care_home_dementia_older_adult_indoor_light**.

**Findings:** This open-access systematic review included 37 articles on indoor daylight and lighting for older persons with dementia in long-term care. It found no conclusive evidence that indoor light decreased challenging behaviors or improved circadian rhythms, while some studies suggested effects on agitation, depressive symptoms, spatial orientation, and quality of life.

**Why it matters:** Long-term-care-focused synthesis explicitly preserves both promising and inconclusive dementia-lighting evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** challenging behavior, circadian rhythm, depressive symptoms, agitation, spatial orientation, quality of life

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a review-level boundary source for indoor dementia lighting; do not treat it as direct protocol efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
