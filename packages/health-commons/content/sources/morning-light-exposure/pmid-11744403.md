**Findings:** The review emphasizes that outdoor ocular UV exposure varies by time of day and reflection/geometry, and that photoprotection includes appropriate sunglasses and exposure control.

**Why it matters:** Ocular UV risk depends on exposure geometry and photoprotection, not only clock time.

**Potential experiment signals:** UV exposure geometry, sunglasses, ocular photoprotection, acute and chronic eye injury.

**Protocol takeaway:** Morning outdoor-light guidance should allow sunglasses/hats when UV or glare is high, rather than requiring bare-eye exposure.
