This source is included for **athlete_exercise_travel_sleep_hygiene_bundles**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries report significant reductions in time in bed, total sleep time, and sleep efficiency, especially during the first 48 hours after arrival.

**Why it matters:** Recent sport-context background for travel-related sleep disruption.

**Potential experiment signals:** time in bed; total sleep time; sleep efficiency; sleep quality; fatigue.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as observational travel context only.

**Directness and boundaries:** Observational, small sample, no assigned light protocol.

**Limitations and uncertainty:**

- Small cohort.
- No intervention.
- Athlete travel population.
- Cannot attribute changes to light exposure.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
