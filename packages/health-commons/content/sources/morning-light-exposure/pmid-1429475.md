This source is included for **clinical_light_therapy_device_boundaries**.

**Findings:** Population: Patients with winter depression. Intervention/exposure: Gradual dawn simulation. Comparator/control: Rapid dawn simulation, hypothesized as a placebo-like condition. Duration/follow-up: Short-term crossover treatment; exact duration not extracted from accessible text Endpoints: depressive symptoms. Effect/direction: Depression decreased under both gradual and rapid dawn simulations; because improvement was similar, the accessible PubMed conclusion states that a placebo effect rather than efficacy of dawn simulation could explain the results. Safety/adverse events: No adverse-event details were extracted.

**Why it matters:** Important mixed/null boundary for device-parameter claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** depression ratings, dawn ramp speed

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not treat dawn simulation as unequivocally effective based on this study alone.

**Directness and boundaries:** Directness is `clinical_supervised` with protocol-evidence scope `clinical_supervised`. Population mismatch: Winter depression patients, not general outdoor-light users.

**Limitations and uncertainty:** Older small device-parameter study; sample size not extracted; placebo-effect concern; not outdoor light.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
