This source is included for **indoor_workplace_classroom_home_daylight**.

**Findings:** The review found that dynamic light scenarios were usually motivated by circadian support, environmental fascination, or acute support. Effects were subtle and heterogeneous, and improved sleep from increased daytime light dose was described as one of the more promising patterns.

**Why it matters:** It is a useful context synthesis for indoor lighting substitutions and reinforces the need to separate indoor dynamic lighting from outdoor morning daylight.

**Potential experiment signals:** Sleep, alertness, circadian rhythm markers, visual comfort, daytime light dose, and dynamic-light scenario characteristics.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to temper claims about indoor dynamic lighting: evidence is promising but not definitive and not direct outdoor evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
