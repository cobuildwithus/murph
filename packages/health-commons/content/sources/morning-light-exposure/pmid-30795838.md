This source is included for **care_home_dementia_older_adult_indoor_light**.

**Findings:** This systematic review examined nonpharmacological interventions for sleep in nursing-home residents. Light-therapy evidence in this setting was not consistently positive, and the review emphasized small study numbers and cautious interpretation.

**Why it matters:** Prevents overstatement by placing light interventions alongside other nursing-home sleep interventions and preserving null/mixed signals.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep quality, sleep duration, nighttime awakenings, daytime sleepiness, intervention adherence

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to keep adjacent nursing-home light evidence balanced; do not cite it as direct support for outdoor morning light.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
