This source is included for **athlete_exercise_travel_sleep_hygiene_bundles**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries report improved subjective sleep-onset latency and sleep quality under stricter adherence, but actigraphy-based sleep estimates did not significantly differ.

**Why it matters:** Directly adjacent athlete intervention highlighting subjective/objective sleep divergence.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep onset latency; sleep quality; actigraphy sleep; alertness.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context for day-night light hygiene in athletes, not as direct outdoor-light evidence.

**Directness and boundaries:** Morning light was artificial and bundled with evening light restriction and fixed schedule.

**Limitations and uncertainty:**

- Multicomponent light-regulation bundle.
- Low adherence affected strict analyses.
- Artificial morning light rather than outdoor sunlight.
- Athlete population may not generalize.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
