This source is included for **clinical_light_therapy_device_boundaries**.

**Findings:** Population: Community volunteer adults using dawn-simulator devices during winter. Intervention/exposure: Dawn simulator used for 2-week blocks. Comparator/control: Each subject acted as own control in an ABAB design with simulator-off periods. Duration/follow-up: 8 weeks total; 2-week alternating blocks Endpoints: Groningen Sleep Quality Scale recorded each morning. Effect/direction: Seventy-seven subjects completed the trial. Subjective sleep quality improved while using dawn simulators (p=0.001); benefit became apparent after six days and did not last after cessation. Authors described benefits as modest. Safety/adverse events: No adverse-event details were extracted.

**Why it matters:** Useful boundary evidence for dawn-simulation sleep endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** Groningen Sleep Quality Scale, treatment persistence, completion

**Protocol takeaway:** Device dawn effects should not be presented as outdoor daylight effects.

**Directness and boundaries:** Directness is `clinical_supervised` with protocol-evidence scope `clinical_supervised`. Population mismatch: Community adults using devices, not outdoor morning-light exposure.

**Limitations and uncertainty:** Device-based artificial dawn; community volunteers rather than target outdoor protocol users; modest and not sustained effects; 77/100 completed.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
