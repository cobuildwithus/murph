This source is included for **safety_boundaries**.

**Source key:** `source_artifact:pmid-8071268`.

**Findings:** Side effects were limited and mild; one patient developed mild hypomania, three became agitated, and sleep disturbance occurred in 5 of 8 patients using evening light; no discontinuations were reported in the extracted snippets.

**Why it matters:** An older SAD side-effect report found mild hypomania, agitation, and sleep disturbance signals during bright-light treatment.

**Potential experiment signals:** side effects, hypomania, agitation, sleep disturbance, visual side effects.

**Protocol takeaway:** Protocol users should be told to scale back or stop if light exposure causes agitation, insomnia, or elevated mood.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Extraction notes:**

- **Participant count:** 30 (reported).
- **Population:** Adults with seasonal affective disorder treated with bright light.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Bright light treatment for SAD, including evening light in a subset.
- **Comparator or control:** Side-effect assessment after treatment; no outdoor-light comparator.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Treatment duration not fully extracted in this batch.
- **Endpoints:** side effects; hypomania; agitation; sleep disturbance; visual side effects
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Side effects were limited and mild; one patient developed mild hypomania, three became agitated, and sleep disturbance occurred in 5 of 8 patients using evening light; no discontinuations were reported in the extracted snippets.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Even mostly tolerated light treatment can produce activation, agitation, and sleep timing problems.
- **Population mismatch:** SAD treatment sample, not general outdoor-light users.
- **Directness to Morning Outdoor Light Exposure:** Safety boundary. This source does not establish direct efficacy for the protocol variant.

**Directness and boundaries:** Artificial light therapy and SAD treatment context limit directness.

- SAD population and artificial treatment context.
- Details of visual side effects were only partially available from accessible snippets.
