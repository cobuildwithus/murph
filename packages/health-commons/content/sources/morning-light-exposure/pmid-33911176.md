This source is included for **season_latitude_weather_setting_modifiers**.

**Findings:** Sleep timing and duration can change seasonally in real-world sensing data, which can confound protocol outcome interpretation. Weather can act as a non-light or mixed light/weather modifier of sleep outcomes. No protocol adverse-event signal was extracted from this source; use only as modifier/context evidence unless a finding explicitly states otherwise.

**Why it matters:** Weather-specific sleep paper useful when separating light-related from broader meteorological influences on sleep.

**Potential experiment signals:** season, weather, sleep duration, wake time, bedtime, outdoor light/weather logs.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context that weather and season can shift sleep outcomes independently of a protocol checkmark.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct default-protocol efficacy evidence.
