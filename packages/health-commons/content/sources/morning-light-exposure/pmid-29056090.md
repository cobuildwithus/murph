This source is included for **season_latitude_weather_setting_modifiers**.

**Findings:** Seasonal light-dose contrast was large in an older-adult care setting. Large light-exposure differences did not necessarily translate into a clear sleep-quality difference in this small exploratory study. No protocol adverse-event signal was extracted from this source; use only as modifier/context evidence unless a finding explicitly states otherwise.

**Why it matters:** Population-specific feasibility and response assumptions for older adults.

**Potential experiment signals:** morning illuminance, minutes >1000 lux, sleep quality, daytime activity, care setting.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context that care-home winter light exposure can be very low and may require setting-specific support.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct default-protocol efficacy evidence.
