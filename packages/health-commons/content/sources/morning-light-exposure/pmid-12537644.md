**Findings:** Retinal light damage is described through thermal, mechanical, and photochemical mechanisms, with risk depending on wavelength, exposure duration, and energy delivery.

**Why it matters:** Retinal phototoxicity mechanisms support a strict no-sun-gazing boundary.

**Potential experiment signals:** retinal phototoxicity, thermal damage, mechanical damage, photochemical damage, wavelength, exposure duration.

**Protocol takeaway:** Outdoor protocols should emphasize indirect ambient light exposure, not staring at the sun or bright reflections.
