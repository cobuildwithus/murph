This source is included for **indoor_workplace_classroom_home_daylight**.

**Findings:** The review included five studies with 282 participants and reported very-low-quality evidence that high correlated color temperature lighting may improve alertness but not mood, low-quality evidence that direct/indirect lighting proportions did not affect alertness or mood, and very-low-quality evidence that blue-enriched task light may improve alertness and mood. Safety/comfort outcomes were inconsistently reported.

**Why it matters:** It preserves null and mixed evidence and helps avoid overpromoting adjacent indoor lighting studies as direct protocol support.

**Potential experiment signals:** Alertness, mood, irritability, eye discomfort, headache, and other workplace comfort outcomes.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context and quality-control evidence: workplace lighting effects are plausible but not uniformly proven.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
