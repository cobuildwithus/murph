**Findings:** Dose-equivalent indoor lighting requires deliberate design and cannot be assumed from room illumination alone. Spectrum and daylight access both influence indoor circadian-light dose. **Potential experiment signals:** circadian stimulus, daylight/electric-light mix, window size, LED spectrum, shift-work setting.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context for dose-equivalence and indoor-substitution discussions only.
