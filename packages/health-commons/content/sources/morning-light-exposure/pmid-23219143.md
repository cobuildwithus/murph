This source is included for **season_latitude_weather_setting_modifiers**.

**Findings:** High-latitude season did not produce major self-reported sleep-wake changes in this older population. The detected chronotype shift was small, suggesting adaptation or behavioral masking at high latitude. No protocol adverse-event signal was extracted from this source; use only as modifier/context evidence unless a finding explicitly states otherwise.

**Why it matters:** Important mixed/negative high-latitude source to avoid overstating seasonal effects.

**Potential experiment signals:** self-reported sleep timing, chronotype, age, latitude, season.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context that seasonal/latitude effects can be small or masked by behavior and adaptation.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct default-protocol efficacy evidence.
