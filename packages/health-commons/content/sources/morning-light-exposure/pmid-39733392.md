This source is included for **clinical_sleep_insomnia_dementia_light_therapy_boundaries**.

**Findings:** Ten RCTs were analyzed. Reported pooled results favored light therapy for PSQI (WMD -2.89, 95% CI -4.80 to -0.97), ISI (WMD -2.16, 95% CI -4.23 to -0.08), actigraphy total sleep time (+16.78 min, 95% CI 0.67 to 32.89), and actigraphy WASO (-12.91 min, 95% CI -25.62 to -0.20). The authors described the findings as preliminary and needing validation in larger trials.

**Why it matters:** This is the newest insomnia meta-analysis in the batch and updates the clinical light-therapy boundary with pooled subjective and actigraphy endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** PSQI, ISI, actigraphy total sleep time, actigraphy wake after sleep onset, risk of bias

**Protocol takeaway:** This source can update clinical-context statements about insomnia light therapy, but not direct claims for outdoor morning light.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
