This source is included for **clinical_light_therapy_device_boundaries**.

**Findings:** Population: Medication-free patients with seasonal affective disorder. Intervention/exposure: Bright light therapy at 10,000 lux for 30 minutes from 6:00 to 6:30 a.m.; dawn simulation peaking at 250 lux over 1.5 hours from 4:30 to 6:00 a.m. Comparator/control: Dim red-light placebo dawn signal peaking at 0.5 lux over 1.5 hours. Duration/follow-up: 6 weeks Endpoints: SIGH-SAD depression ratings. Effect/direction: The study compared dawn simulation, bright light, and dim red-light placebo in SAD; accessible extraction confirmed dosing and outcome scale, but did not extract numeric group outcomes. Safety/adverse events: No adverse-event details were extracted.

**Why it matters:** Device-parameter boundary source for light boxes and dawn simulators.

**Potential experiment signals:** SIGH-SAD score, device lux, timing

**Protocol takeaway:** Use full text before citing efficacy magnitude; accessible extraction did not capture numeric outcomes.

**Directness and boundaries:** Directness is `clinical_supervised` with protocol-evidence scope `clinical_supervised`. Population mismatch: Medication-free SAD patients, not general outdoor-light users.

**Limitations and uncertainty:** Device-based SAD treatment; sample and numeric results not extracted from accessible text; not outdoor daylight.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
