This source is included for **timing_dose_circadian_metrics**.

**Findings:**
- The study examined natural daylight exposure and sleep timing among Arctic day workers.
- Its title-level conclusion links delayed winter sleep to natural daylight exposure in this setting.
- The source supports implementation caution for high-latitude winters.

**Intervention or exposure:** Natural daylight exposure in a high-latitude Arctic setting

**Comparator or control:** Seasonal daylight contrasts and/or exposure variation

**Duration or follow-up:** Seasonal winter/daylight exposure assessment

**Endpoints:** Sleep timing and daylight exposure patterns

**Effect estimates or direction:** Direction: winter sleep delay was related to natural daylight exposure; no extracted causal effect size.

**Safety/adverse events:** No adverse events extracted.

**Limitations:**
- Observational design.
- Occupational Arctic population.
- Participant count and effect estimate not extracted.

**Population mismatch:** Arctic day workers may not generalize to general adult users in lower-latitude environments.

**Directness to Morning Outdoor Light Exposure:** `same_mechanism`.

**Why it matters:** It illustrates real-world constraints for outdoor morning light when daylight is scarce.

**Potential experiment signals:** season, latitude, natural daylight, sleep timing, work schedule.

**Protocol takeaway:** Morning outdoor light prescriptions may need winter or latitude adaptations.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
