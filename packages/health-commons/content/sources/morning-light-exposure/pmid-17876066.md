This source is included for **season_latitude_weather_setting_modifiers**.

**Findings:** Prior light history may influence circadian light sensitivity, but group-level indoor/outdoor differences were not definitive. Retinal sensitivity and circadian sensitivity may adapt differently to indoor/outdoor light histories. No protocol adverse-event signal was extracted from this source; use only as modifier/context evidence unless a finding explicitly states otherwise.

**Why it matters:** Useful indoor-vs-outdoor worker physiology paper for setting contrast and dose-history interpretation.

**Potential experiment signals:** prior-day light exposure, work setting, melatonin suppression, dark adaptation, sunglasses/indoor access.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context that prior light history and indoor/outdoor work setting may change response to a given light stimulus.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct default-protocol efficacy evidence.
