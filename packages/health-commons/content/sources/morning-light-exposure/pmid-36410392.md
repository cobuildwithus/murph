This source is included for **care_home_dementia_older_adult_indoor_light**.

**Findings:** This review summarized 21 long-term-care light-therapy studies, including nonrandomized pilot studies and RCTs. Interventions varied substantially in timing, intensity, duration, delivery mode, and length; the review concluded that sleep benefits were plausible but that larger, higher-quality trials are needed.

**Why it matters:** Provides recall coverage across older-adult residential light interventions and helps define why care-home evidence is adjacent rather than direct.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep quality, sleep duration, nighttime awakenings, intervention timing, light intensity, light delivery mode

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context for dose uncertainty in supervised or institutional light therapy; do not use as direct outdoor morning-light efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
