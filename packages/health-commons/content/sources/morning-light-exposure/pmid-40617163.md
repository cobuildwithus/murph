This source is included for **care_home_dementia_older_adult_indoor_light**.

**Findings:** This pilot clinical trial in 10 older adults with dementia used a counterbalanced biodynamic-lighting and constant-light active-placebo design. Biodynamic lighting increased total sleep time by about 82 minutes versus baseline; both lighting conditions showed only a trend toward lower depression scores.

**Why it matters:** Recent pilot evidence extends biodynamic indoor-lighting research to sleep, depression, and agitation in dementia care.

**Potential experiment signals:** total sleep time, depression score, agitation, actigraphy, feasibility

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only recent pilot evidence; do not treat as direct outdoor morning-light support.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
