## Evidence buckets

- `direct_outdoor_daylight_protocol`: closest prescribed outdoor or natural-light sources.
- `free_living_observational_measurement`: real-world adult exposure/timing context.
- `timing_dose_circadian_metrics`: dose, timing, melanopic, and measurement guidance.
- `indoor_workplace_classroom_home_daylight`: same-mechanism indoor daylight context.
- `clinical_light_therapy_device_boundaries`: light boxes, dawn simulators, SAD/depression, and circadian-treatment boundaries.
- `care_home_dementia_older_adult_indoor_light`: institutional lighting context.
- `athlete_exercise_travel_sleep_hygiene_bundles`: adjacent bundled routines and population modifiers.
- `season_latitude_weather_setting_modifiers`: environmental dose modifiers.
- `safety_boundaries`: mood, eye, skin, photosensitivity, UV, and heat boundaries.
