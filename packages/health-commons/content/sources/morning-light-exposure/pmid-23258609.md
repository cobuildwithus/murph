This source is included for **athlete_exercise_travel_sleep_hygiene_bundles**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries report a transient temperature increase and small increase in jet-lag rating after indoor light, with negligible effects on functioning, diet, bowel, and sleep symptoms.

**Why it matters:** Preserves negative/mixed jet-lag evidence instead of only positive light studies.

**Potential experiment signals:** jet lag; sleep symptoms; temperature; grip strength; functioning.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only evidence that travel light prescriptions may be hard to implement and not always effective.

**Directness and boundaries:** Small athlete travel study with practical compromises and individual variability.

**Limitations and uncertainty:**

- Small sample.
- Travel and sport context.
- Artificial bright light, not outdoor morning exposure.
- Implementation compromises noted in accessible summaries.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
