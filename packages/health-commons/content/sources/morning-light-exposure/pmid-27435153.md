This source is included for **season_latitude_weather_setting_modifiers**.

**Findings:** Photoperiod strongly changed the timing and amount of light exposure in ordinary office-worker life. Seasonal daylight differences may alter circadian hormone profiles, making biomarker timing sensitive to month and latitude. No protocol adverse-event signal was extracted from this source; use only as modifier/context evidence unless a finding explicitly states otherwise.

**Why it matters:** Directly addresses photoperiod and latitude as modifiers of light exposure and circadian biology.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily light exposure, morning light window, DLMO or melatonin timing if available, season, latitude.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context that winter, spring, summer, and autumn may not be interchangeable for morning-light dose.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct default-protocol efficacy evidence.
