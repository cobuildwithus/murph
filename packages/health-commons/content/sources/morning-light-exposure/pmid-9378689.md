**Source key:** `source_artifact:pmid-9378689`.

**Findings:** Within the first week, two patients attempted suicide and the third developed acute suicidal ideation in the reported cases.

**Why it matters:** Three case reports described acute suicidal behavior or ideation soon after beginning light therapy.

**Potential experiment signals:** suicide attempt, acute suicidal ideation, drive/mood dissociation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Protocol safety guidance should not present light exposure as benign for people with acute suicidality or unstable mood; urgent care pathways are needed.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
