This source is included for **timing_dose_circadian_metrics**.

**Findings:**
- The review screened a large literature and summarized 45 eligible adult studies, including experimental and observational designs.
- It reports broad sleep-related patterns for light amount and timing, but heterogeneity prevents a single outdoor-morning dose claim.
- This source should anchor implementation language and uncertainty, not stand-alone efficacy claims.

**Intervention or exposure:** Light amount and timing, including bright-light and ambient-light exposures

**Comparator or control:** Varied by included study; included lower-light, different timing, or observational contrasts

**Duration or follow-up:** Varied across included studies

**Endpoints:** Sleep outcomes including objective and subjective sleep measures

**Effect estimates or direction:** Broad trends were summarized across studies; no single outdoor-morning effect estimate extracted.

**Safety/adverse events:** No adverse-event extraction was available from the batch materials.

**Limitations:**
- Heterogeneous study designs and light interventions.
- Not restricted to outdoor morning exposure.
- Review-level evidence does not establish a default protocol effect.

**Population mismatch:** Adult sleep evidence, but not necessarily healthy adults doing a brief outdoor morning protocol.

**Directness to Morning Outdoor Light Exposure:** `background`.

**Why it matters:** It is the most on-target synthesis for the batch theme of amount and timing of light relative to sleep.

**Potential experiment signals:** light timing, light amount, sleep timing, sleep quality, objective and subjective sleep measures.

**Protocol takeaway:** Morning outdoor light pages can cite this source only for the broader evidence base and for uncertainty around dose/timing-sleep translation.
