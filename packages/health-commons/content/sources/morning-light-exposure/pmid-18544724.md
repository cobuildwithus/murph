This source is included for **care_home_dementia_older_adult_indoor_light**.

**Findings:** This large group-care facility RCT tested whole-day bright light with or without melatonin. Bright light attenuated deterioration in cognition, depressive symptoms, and functional limitations. Melatonin shortened sleep-onset latency and increased sleep duration, but was associated with adverse affect/withdrawn behavior unless combined with light.

**Why it matters:** Major long-term institutional trial with clinically relevant outcomes and an explicit adverse-effect boundary for bundled melatonin.

**Potential experiment signals:** cognition, depressive symptoms, functional limitations, sleep-onset latency, sleep duration, behavioral adverse effects

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as high-priority clinical context for all-day institutional light and for safety notes around bundled melatonin; not direct outdoor morning-light evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
