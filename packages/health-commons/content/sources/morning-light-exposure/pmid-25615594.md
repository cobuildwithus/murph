This source is included for **season_latitude_weather_setting_modifiers**.

**Findings:** At extreme latitudes, light timing and evening exposure can shape sleep/circadian outcomes as much as simple morning exposure. Polar photoperiod makes protocol timing and dose interpretation population-mismatched to typical users. No protocol adverse-event signal was extracted from this source; use only as modifier/context evidence unless a finding explicitly states otherwise.

**Why it matters:** Polar photoperiod study that helps bound expected response differences under extreme summer and winter daylight.

**Potential experiment signals:** local sunrise/sunset or polar day/night, evening light exposure, sleep quality, DLMO/melatonin.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context for high-latitude users where morning/evening distinction can blur under polar day or night.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct default-protocol efficacy evidence.
