This source is included for **athlete_exercise_travel_sleep_hygiene_bundles**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries report better sleep-pattern indices in sunlight and combined arms versus control; combined exercise plus sunlight had higher melatonin than control, while cortisol/catecholamine differences were not significant.

**Why it matters:** One of the few records with light-only, exercise-only, combined, and control arms.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep onset latency; sleep quality; bedtime; wake time; melatonin; cortisol; catecholamines.

**Protocol takeaway:** Useful context for disentangling sun exposure from exercise, but only as adjacent low-sample evidence.

**Directness and boundaries:** Small all-male college sample; exposure timing and daily-life generalizability need careful caveating.

**Limitations and uncertainty:**

- Very small sample.
- Healthy young men only.
- Exercise and sun conditions may not map exactly to a simple morning outdoor-light protocol.
- No detailed adverse-event extraction was identified.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
