**Findings:** Population: Patients with circadian rhythm sleep disorders and related light-timing problems. Intervention/exposure: Appropriately timed light exposure, typically delivered as bright light in clinical protocols. Comparator/control: Not applicable for narrative review. Duration/follow-up: Varies by disorder and protocol Endpoints: sleep timing, circadian phase, daytime alertness, sleep quality. Effect/direction: The review states that appropriately timed bright light can reset sleep-wake timing and improve sleep quality or daytime alertness, with efficacy depending strongly on circadian timing and light dose. Safety/adverse events: The review discusses safe use guidelines, but source-specific adverse-event rates were not extracted.
