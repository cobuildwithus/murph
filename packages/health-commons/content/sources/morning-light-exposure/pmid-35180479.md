This source is included for **care_home_dementia_older_adult_indoor_light**.

**Findings:** This systematic review/meta-analysis of 18 RCTs involving 1012 older adults with dementia reported small-to-medium effects on nighttime awakenings, sleep quality, and circadian rhythm outcomes, but no discernible effect on sleep duration or sleep efficiency. Meta-regression suggested sample size and regional subgroup patterns may matter.

**Why it matters:** Population-boundary synthesis that separates endpoint-specific benefits from null sleep-duration/efficiency findings.

**Potential experiment signals:** nighttime awakenings, sleep quality, circadian rhythm outcomes, sleep duration, sleep efficiency, meta-regression modifiers

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as dementia-specific context and endpoint boundary; not direct evidence for healthy outdoor morning light.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
