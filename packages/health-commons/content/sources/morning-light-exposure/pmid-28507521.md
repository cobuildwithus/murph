This source is included for **athlete_exercise_travel_sleep_hygiene_bundles**.

**Findings:** Total work was not significantly improved by bright or blue light versus control; melanopic exposure was associated with increased performance and decreased melatonin in accessible summaries.

**Why it matters:** Prevents treating all athlete light findings as uniformly sleep-supportive.

**Potential experiment signals:** cycling performance; end-spurt performance; melatonin.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as adjacent performance/mechanism context.

**Directness and boundaries:** Prime-time artificial light in male athletes; endpoint was performance, not sleep.

**Limitations and uncertainty:**

- Acute artificial light exposure.
- Male elite athletes only.
- Performance endpoint rather than sleep.
- Not a morning outdoor protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
