This source is included for **care_home_dementia_older_adult_indoor_light**.

**Findings:** This pragmatic randomized crossover trial tested circadian-adjusted LED-based lighting in elderly care-home dwellers and reported no significant impact on PSQI sleep quality, Epworth daytime sleepiness, or measured inflammatory biomarkers.

**Why it matters:** Important null same-mechanism trial with objective biomarker endpoints, balancing positive tailored-lighting studies.

**Potential experiment signals:** PSQI, daytime sleepiness, IL-6, TNF-alpha, suPAR, pragmatic feasibility

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a null adjacent trial and a reminder that not all indoor circadian lighting improves sleep or biomarkers.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
