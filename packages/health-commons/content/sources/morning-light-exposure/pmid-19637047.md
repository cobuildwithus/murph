This source is included for **season_latitude_weather_setting_modifiers**.

**Findings:** Season materially changed personal light exposure in everyday life. Season affects not only brightness but also spectral composition, which matters for circadian dose interpretation. No protocol adverse-event signal was extracted from this source; use only as modifier/context evidence unless a finding explicitly states otherwise.

**Why it matters:** Spectral composition is a dose-equivalence issue for circadian light: the same lux duration may not represent the same melanopic/circadian stimulus.

**Potential experiment signals:** ambient lux, spectral composition, blue-light proportion, season, time of day.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context that season and spectrum modify the circadian relevance of a morning outdoor-light dose.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. This is not direct default-protocol efficacy evidence.
