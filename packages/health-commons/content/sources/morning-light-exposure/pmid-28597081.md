This source is included for **athlete_exercise_travel_sleep_hygiene_bundles**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries report no improvement in acoustic reaction time or handgrip strength with bright or blue light compared with control.

**Why it matters:** Keeps adjacent athlete light evidence from being overread as protocol support.

**Potential experiment signals:** acoustic reaction time; handgrip strength; melatonin.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as adjacent null performance evidence.

**Directness and boundaries:** Artificial acute light and performance endpoints, not sleep/outdoor morning exposure.

**Limitations and uncertainty:**

- Acute artificial-light design.
- Male athletes.
- Performance endpoint rather than sleep.
- Not morning/outdoor.
