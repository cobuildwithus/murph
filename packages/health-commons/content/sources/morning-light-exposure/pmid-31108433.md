This source is included for **clinical_light_therapy_device_boundaries**.

**Findings:** Population: Patients with intrinsic circadian rhythm sleep disorders and selected neuropsychiatric illnesses with circadian/sleep dysregulation. Intervention/exposure: Light therapies including bright light, dark therapy, and dawn simulation across clinical indications. Comparator/control: Control or comparison conditions as defined in included studies. Duration/follow-up: Varied by included study Endpoints: sleep outcomes, circadian timing, neuropsychiatric symptoms. Effect/direction: The review included 40 studies with 49 intervention comparisons and concluded that light interventions warrant refinement and further testing for improving sleep in psychiatric and circadian disorders; accessible extract did not provide a single pooled effect for the target protocol. Safety/adverse events: Safety details were not extracted from accessible abstract-level text.

**Why it matters:** Useful as backbone context for clinical light-therapy boundaries and endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep outcomes, phase timing, psychiatric symptom outcomes

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not convert device-based clinical effects into outdoor morning-light efficacy claims.

**Directness and boundaries:** Directness is `clinical_supervised` with protocol-evidence scope `clinical_supervised`. Population mismatch: Clinical sleep and neuropsychiatric disorders rather than healthy users.

**Limitations and uncertainty:** Heterogeneous diagnoses and light protocols; mostly device-based or clinically supervised interventions; not outdoor morning-light habit evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
