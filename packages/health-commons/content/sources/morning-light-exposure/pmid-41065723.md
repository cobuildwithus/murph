This source is included for **athlete_exercise_travel_sleep_hygiene_bundles**.

**Findings:** Participants increased engagement in all four behaviors; sleep consistency, resting heart rate, and heart-rate variability improved versus matched controls, with mediation through sleep consistency reported.

**Why it matters:** Wearable-relevant source including morning sunlight, but bundled with multiple behavior changes and self-selection.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep consistency; resting heart rate; heart rate variability; cardiorespiratory fitness; parasympathetic activity.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as adjacent behavior-bundle context.

**Directness and boundaries:** Pre-post quasi-experimental design with self-selection and bundled behaviors; not a randomized light-only study.

**Limitations and uncertainty:**

- Morning sunlight bundled with three other behaviors.
- Quasi-experimental pre-post design, not randomized.
- Wearable and self-selection context.
- No isolated outdoor morning-light estimate.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
