This source is included for **safety_boundaries**.

**Source key:** `source_artifact:pmid-9464216`.

**Findings:** 32 of 70 subjects (45.7%) reported side effects and 9 of 70 (12.9%) reported two or more; headache and eye/vision problems were most common; almost all effects were mild, transient, and did not interfere with treatment.

**Why it matters:** In a short-term 10,000-lux light-therapy report, side effects were common but mostly mild and transient.

**Potential experiment signals:** side effects, headache, eye/vision problems, treatment interference.

**Protocol takeaway:** Protocol safety language can mention headache and eye/vision symptoms as things to watch when increasing light intensity or duration.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Extraction notes:**

- **Participant count:** 70 (reported).
- **Population:** Subjects with seasonal affective disorder receiving brief 10,000-lux light therapy.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Short-term 10,000-lux light therapy.
- **Comparator or control:** Side-effect reporting during brief treatment; no outdoor-light comparator.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Brief short-term treatment; exact session schedule not fully extracted beyond short-term 10,000-lux exposure.
- **Endpoints:** side effects; headache; eye/vision problems; treatment interference
- **Effect estimates or direction:** 32 of 70 subjects (45.7%) reported side effects and 9 of 70 (12.9%) reported two or more; headache and eye/vision problems were most common; almost all effects were mild, transient, and did not interfere with treatment.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Common acute symptoms may occur even when severe events are not observed.
- **Population mismatch:** SAD treatment sample, not general outdoor-light users.
- **Directness to Morning Outdoor Light Exposure:** Safety boundary. This source does not establish direct efficacy for the protocol variant.

**Directness and boundaries:** The exposure was artificial 10,000-lux treatment, not outdoor morning light.

**Limitations and uncertainty:**

- Short report and short-term exposure.
- Clinical SAD population and artificial-light dose.
- No outdoor sunlight arm.
