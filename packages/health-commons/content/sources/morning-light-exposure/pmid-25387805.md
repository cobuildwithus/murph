This source is included for **athlete_exercise_travel_sleep_hygiene_bundles**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries report greater vigor and reduced subjective jet-lag/mood worsening in the intervention, but negligible effects on several performance, physiological, and perceptual responses.

**Why it matters:** Foundational athlete travel bundle evidence that preserves mixed endpoint results.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep duration; sleep quality; mood; jet lag; performance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent travel-bundle context only.

**Directness and boundaries:** Artificial bright light was bundled with sleep hygiene; simulated travel was not a naturalistic morning outdoor-light protocol.

**Limitations and uncertainty:**

- Small sample.
- Simulation rather than actual travel.
- No isolated light-only arm.
- No circadian marker in accessible summary.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
