This source is included for **care_home_dementia_older_adult_indoor_light**.

**Findings:** This small long-term-care intervention installed a low-level bluish-white tailored lighting system and reported improved PSQI global score, increased total sleep time and sleep efficiency, increased circadian stimulus phasor magnitude, and reduced depression and agitation scores.

**Why it matters:** Provides open-access clinical detail on tailored circadian light in dementia care with multiple outcomes relevant to monitoring.

**Potential experiment signals:** PSQI global score, total sleep time, sleep efficiency, circadian stimulus phasor magnitude, depression, agitation

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as positive same-mechanism context for tailored indoor light, not direct evidence for healthy adults going outdoors in the morning.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
