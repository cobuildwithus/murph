This source is included for **safety-older-pregnancy-pediatric-hypotension**.

**Findings:** PPH participants showed selected gait changes after walking/glucose conditions, but AUC effects were not significant and no adverse effects were reported in supervised visits.

**Why it matters:** Directly connects post-meal BP physiology with gait features that matter for falls when older adults walk soon after eating.

**Potential experiment signals:** post-meal SBP change, gait speed, double-support time variability, adverse effects during supervised walk.

**Protocol takeaway:** For older adults with PPH or post-meal dizziness, walking immediately after meals should be modified, supervised, delayed, or clinician-guided.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Older adults with and without postprandial hypotension.
- **Participant count:** 29 participants; 16 demonstrated PPH and 13 did not.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Glucose-only, water-and-walk, and glucose-and-walk acute study days with gait analysis.
- **Comparator/control:** Within-person study days and PPH versus non-PPH groups.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Three separate acute study days; gait and BP assessed over 0–120 minutes after ingestion.
- **Endpoints:** post-meal SBP change, gait speed, double-support time variability, adverse effects during supervised walk
- **Effect estimates or direction:** PPH group showed significant changes in gait speed on water/walk and double-support time variability on glucose/walk, but gait AUC changes were not significant.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Procedures were well tolerated with no adverse effects reported; nevertheless, PPH-related gait changes may increase falls risk.
- **Limitations:** Small sample; Acute supervised setting; Walking may have mitigated some BP effects; Not a free-living protocol efficacy trial
- **Population mismatch:** Safety-boundary evidence rather than routine adult/general wellness protocol evidence.
- **Directness to Walking After Every Meal:** safety_boundary
