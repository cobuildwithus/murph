## Findings
- Postmeal treadmill walking blunted post-breakfast glucose excursions in metformin-only T2D participants.
- The medication profile is a key boundary for safety language.

## Why it matters
It helps separate postmeal walking response in metformin-only users from higher hypoglycemia-risk medication contexts.

## Potential experiment signals
- 2-hour postprandial glucose peak
- 2-hour glucose AUC
- Metformin/washout interaction

## Protocol takeaway
Use as medication-boundary context; do not generalize hypoglycemia risk to insulin or secretagogue users.

## Claim use
`context-only`.

## Extraction details
- **Source kind:** journal_article
- **Study design:** crossover (Randomized crossover postmeal walking with metformin/washout comparison)
- **Participant count:** 10 (reported)
- **Population:** People with type 2 diabetes on metformin monotherapy.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Five 10-minute treadmill-walking bouts at about 60% VO2max after standardized breakfast.
- **Comparator or control:** Sedentary breakfast conditions, tested with metformin and after metformin washout/no-metformin.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Two-hour post-breakfast outcome window.
- **Endpoints:** Postprandial glucose peak and 2-hour total AUC.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Postmeal exercise reduced 2-hour peak glucose and AUC; lowest extracted peak occurred with metformin plus exercise.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Metformin monotherapy has a different hypoglycemia risk profile than insulin or insulin secretagogues; no adverse-event counts extracted.
- **Population mismatch:** T2D metformin-only subgroup; breakfast only; treadmill protocol uses repeated 10-minute bouts rather than every meal.
- **Directness to Walking After Every Meal:** adjacent_variant
- **Artifact candidates / rights:** Publisher article likely requires permission; do not commit PDF. `pdfRightsStatus=permission_required`.

## Limitations
- Small sample.
- Metformin-only subgroup.
- Breakfast-only test.
- Treadmill repeated-bout design, not free-living after every meal.
