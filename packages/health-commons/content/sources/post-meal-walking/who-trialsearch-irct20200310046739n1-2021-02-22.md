## Extraction details
- **Population:** Adults with functional abdominal bloating or distension meeting Rome IV criteria; registry exclusions included alarm features, major comorbidities or medications affecting GI motility, pregnancy, and BMI around 40.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Short-term walking after meals; later publication indicates 10 to 15 minutes after each meal.
- **Comparator or control:** Registry comparator details were not fully extracted in this run; later publication used medication-based control.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Registry record for a short-term intervention; last updated 2021-02-22; final published intervention lasted 4 weeks.
- **Endpoints:** functional abdominal bloating symptoms; GI symptom change; trial recruitment and eligibility
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Registry record supplies protocol and eligibility context but no outcome results.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event results were available from the registry extraction.
- **Population mismatch:** Functional bloating population, not general walking-after-every-meal users.
- **Directness to Walking After Every Meal:** direct_protocol
- **Claim-use boundary:** context-only
- **Artifact rights status:** unknown
