**Findings:** Authoritative diabetes exercise chapter summarizing benefits and safety considerations, including hypoglycemia, cardiovascular disease, neuropathy, retinopathy, and foot-disease screening concerns.

**Why it matters:** It provides safety guardrails for diabetes populations that may try post-meal walking.

**Potential experiment signals:** Hypoglycemia; Cardiovascular symptoms; Foot problems; Neuropathy; Retinopathy; Insulin sensitivity.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use mainly for safety screening and clinical caution in diabetes, not for post-meal walking efficacy.
