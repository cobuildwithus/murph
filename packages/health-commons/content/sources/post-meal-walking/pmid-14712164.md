This source is included for **secondary-metabolism-lipids-insulin-cgm**.

**Findings:** Exercise before or 90 minutes after a high-fat meal reduced triglyceride AUC in healthy young men, but insulin AUC was unchanged.

**Why it matters:** Useful for lipid timing boundaries and for preserving null insulin findings.

**Potential experiment signals:** postprandial triglyceride AUC, insulin AUC, glucose, glycerol, free fatty acids.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as same-mechanism lipid context; the 90-minute exercise dose does not map to a short walk-after-each-meal protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Ten untrained healthy young men.

- **Participant count:** 10

- **Intervention/exposure:** Ninety minutes of exercise at 50% VO2max either 30 minutes before the meal or initiated 90 minutes after the meal.

- **Comparator/control:** High-fat meal-only control condition.

- **Duration/follow-up:** Eight-hour high-fat meal challenge with repeated blood sampling.

- **Endpoints:** postprandial triglyceride AUC; insulin AUC; glucose; glycerol; free fatty acids

- **Effect estimates or direction:** Triglyceride AUC was lower in both exercise-before-meal and exercise-after-meal conditions versus control (3.16 ± 0.99 and 2.96 ± 0.69 vs 6.18 ± 1.10 mmol/L*8h, p<0.05). Insulin AUC was not different.

- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse-event signal was extracted from the available source record.

- **Limitations:** Ninety-minute exercise dose is much longer than the practical protocol; Healthy young men only; High-fat meal challenge; Not repeated after every meal; Insulin outcome was null

- **Population mismatch:** Same postprandial lipid mechanism but not a short after-every-meal walking protocol.

- **Directness to Walking After Every Meal:** same_mechanism
