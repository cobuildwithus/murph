This source is included for **adjacent-timing-exercise-variants**.

Source key for extracted claims on this page: `source_artifact:pmid-31396757`.

## Findings
- Immediate post-breakfast walking/bodyweight exercise improved mean, CV, and AUC glucose compared with control.
- Pre-breakfast and delayed post-breakfast activity did not show the same extracted benefit.

## Why it matters
It provides same-mechanism timing evidence for immediate post-breakfast activity and helps separate immediate versus delayed post-meal timing.

## Potential experiment signals
- Interstitial glucose mean
- Glucose coefficient of variation
- Glucose AUC
- Timing relative to breakfast

## Protocol takeaway
Use as same-mechanism timing context; do not generalize to every meal or diabetes without caveats.

## Claim use
`context-only`.

## Extraction details
- **Source kind:** journal_article
- **Study design:** crossover (Randomized activity-meal timing crossover study with CGM)
- **Participant count:** 48 (reported)
- **Population:** Adults randomized into standing, walking, or bodyweight-exercise activity cohorts; not a type 2 diabetes target cohort in the extracted abstract.
- **Intervention or exposure:** In separate cohorts, 30 minutes of standing, 30 minutes of walking, or bodyweight exercises timed immediately before breakfast, immediately after breakfast, or 30 minutes after breakfast.
- **Comparator or control:** Control trial in which activity occurred after the 2-hour glucose monitoring period.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Two-hour post-breakfast interstitial glucose monitoring in four trials per activity cohort.
- **Endpoints:** Mean interstitial glucose, coefficient of variation, and glucose AUC.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Walking and bodyweight exercises immediately after the meal improved mean, CV, and AUC glucose versus control in accessible extraction; pre-breakfast activity or delayed post-breakfast activity did not affect the same metrics in secondary summaries.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event counts were extracted from accessible records.
- **Population mismatch:** Adult timing study not focused on T2D; breakfast only; not an every-meal walking trial.
- **Directness to Walking After Every Meal:** same_mechanism
- **Artifact candidates / rights:** PMCID/open-access record available; metadata only here, no PDF committed. `pdfRightsStatus=open_access`.

## Limitations
- Breakfast-only design.
- Activity cohorts rather than a single every-meal walking protocol.
- Not a T2D target population in extracted abstract.
- Two-hour postprandial monitoring window.
