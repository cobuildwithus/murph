Source key for extracted claims on this page: `source_artifact:pmid-25539939`.

## Findings
- Postdinner resistance exercise improved triglyceride and glucose-related postprandial risk factors.
- Because both predinner and postdinner resistance exercise reduced glucose iAUC versus no exercise, the glucose timing advantage was less clean than the triglyceride timing advantage.

## Why it matters
It gives an adjacent timing comparator showing that post-meal exercise can outperform pre-meal exercise for some endpoints, while modality changes attribution.

## Potential experiment signals
- Postprandial glucose iAUC
- Triacylglycerol iAUC
- Insulin iAUC
- GLP-1
- Gastric emptying
- Beta-cell function modeling

## Protocol takeaway
Use for timing and mechanism context; do not treat resistance-exercise effects as direct walking effects.

## Claim use
`context-only`.

## Extraction details
- **Source kind:** journal_article
- **Study design:** crossover (Randomized crossover predinner versus postdinner resistance-exercise study)
- **Participant count:** 13 (reported)
- **Population:** Obese adults with type 2 diabetes.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Postdinner resistance exercise beginning about 45 minutes after dinner.
- **Comparator or control:** Predinner resistance exercise and dinner/no resistance exercise control.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Acute post-dinner test conditions.
- **Endpoints:** Postprandial glucose, triacylglycerol, insulin, GLP-1, gastric emptying, and modeled beta-cell function.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Postdinner resistance exercise lowered triacylglycerol iAUC by about 92% versus no resistance exercise and predinner resistance exercise; glucose iAUC was reduced with both predinner and postdinner resistance exercise versus no exercise, with no extracted difference between resistance timings for glucose.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event counts were extracted from accessible records.
- **Population mismatch:** Obese T2D and resistance training, not walking after every meal.
- **Directness to Walking After Every Meal:** adjacent_variant
- **Artifact candidates / rights:** PMCID is available, but extraction keeps metadata only unless redistribution license is verified. `pdfRightsStatus=open_access`.

## Limitations
- Resistance exercise rather than walking.
- Dinner-only acute test.
- Obese T2D population.
- Not every-meal implementation.
