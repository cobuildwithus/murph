This source is included for **safety-guidelines-medication-boundaries**.

**Findings:** Abstract-level conclusion: improved acute postprandial and 48-hour glycemia without increasing hypoglycemia.

**Why it matters:** Shows that light walking breaks in T1D can be studied with hypoglycemia endpoints, offering a safer evidence model than unsupported advice.

**Potential experiment signals:** Postprandial glycemia, 48-hour glycemia, Hypoglycemia exposure, CGM metrics.

**Protocol takeaway:** Treat as adjacent safety evidence only; do not generalize to all T1D meal walks without monitoring.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Inactive adults with type 1 diabetes.
- **Participant count:** 32 (reported).
- **Intervention/exposure:** Frequent 3-minute self-paced walking/light-intensity activity breaks every 30 minutes during prolonged sitting.
- **Comparator/control:** Uninterrupted sitting control.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 7-hour laboratory visits plus 48-hour free-living glycemia follow-up; washout between conditions.
- **Endpoints:** Postprandial glycemia, 48-hour glycemia, Hypoglycemia exposure, CGM metrics
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Abstract-level conclusion: improved acute postprandial and 48-hour glycemia without increasing hypoglycemia.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Important adjacent evidence that light walking breaks may be feasible in T1D when studied with CGM and controlled conditions.
- **Limitations:** Active-break lab protocol, not walking after each meal; Inactive adult T1D sample; Full insulin/meal safety details require full-text extraction.
