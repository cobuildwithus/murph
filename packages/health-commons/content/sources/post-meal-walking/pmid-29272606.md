## Findings
- Eight women with GDM completed an acute CGM meal study comparing moderate-intensity walking after a high-carbohydrate, low-fat meal with sedentary behavior.
- Accessible abstracts report greater postprandial glucose control with walking than sedentary behavior.
- The source is context-only because it is small, acute, pregnancy-specific, and not a long-term protocol trial.

## Why it matters
This is a direct pregnancy/GDM post-meal walking source, useful for clinical context and boundaries but not for a general adult wellness protocol.

## Potential experiment signals
- CGM postprandial glucose

## Protocol takeaway
Use as GDM-context evidence only: acute post-meal walking improved glucose in a small non-insulin GDM sample, but it is not a broad general-population claim.
