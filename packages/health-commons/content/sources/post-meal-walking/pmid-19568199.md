## Findings
- Pre-meal exercise lowered fasting/postabsorptive glucose, while after-meal exercise did not.
- This is a negative boundary for extrapolating post-meal walking to fasting-glucose endpoints.

## Why it matters
It preserves a negative/contrary finding: after-meal exercise is not always superior when the endpoint is fasting glucose rather than immediate post-meal excursions.

## Potential experiment signals
- Fasting/postabsorptive plasma glucose
- Insulin-glucagon ratio
- Glucagon glycemic threshold
- Vagal tone
- Ketosis

## Protocol takeaway
Use as timing-boundary context: after-meal activity may target postprandial excursions, not necessarily fasting glucose.

## Claim use
`context-only`.

## Extraction details
- **Source kind:** journal_article
- **Study design:** crossover (Randomized crossover pre-meal versus post-meal treadmill exercise study)
- **Participant count:** 9 (reported)
- **Population:** Healthy postmenopausal women.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Two 2-hour treadmill exercise bouts at about 43% maximal effort performed 1 hour before meals.
- **Comparator or control:** Two matched bouts performed 1 hour after meals.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Acute crossover test days; postabsorptive glucose followed across roughly 16 hours.
- **Endpoints:** Fasting/postabsorptive plasma glucose and hormonal/autonomic markers.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Before-meal exercise, but not after-meal exercise, reduced postabsorptive plasma glucose by about 20.2% over 16 hours.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Postexercise ketosis was reported for the pre-meal condition; no adverse-event counts were extracted.
- **Population mismatch:** Healthy postmenopausal women; very long treadmill bouts; endpoint is fasting/postabsorptive glucose rather than immediate postprandial glucose.
- **Directness to Walking After Every Meal:** adjacent_variant
- **Artifact candidates / rights:** Publisher article likely requires permission; do not commit PDF. `pdfRightsStatus=permission_required`.

## Limitations
- Healthy postmenopausal women, not people with diabetes.
- Exercise duration was much longer than a practical after-meal walk.
- Endpoint was fasting/postabsorptive glucose.
- Small acute crossover design.
