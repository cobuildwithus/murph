## Findings
- In six non-obese type 1 diabetes patients on intensive insulin therapy, walking after breakfast was reported to lower blood glucose during and after walking and improve morning glycemic control.
- The study compared timing before versus after breakfast rather than a broad everyday protocol in general adults.
- Because participants were insulin-treated, this is safety-boundary evidence rather than general protocol support.

## Why it matters
This directly tests meal-timed walking but in type 1 diabetes on intensive insulin, where insulin timing and hypoglycemia risk dominate interpretation.

## Potential experiment signals
- Breakfast glucose profile
- Postprandial glucose
- Hypoglycemia symptoms
- Insulin adjustment context

## Protocol takeaway
Do not use as general support. It supports a boundary that insulin-treated users need individualized plans if walking after meals.

## Claim use
`safety-only`.
