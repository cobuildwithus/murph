This source is included for **secondary-metabolism-lipids-insulin-cgm**.

**Findings:** Prior-day walking reduced postprandial lipemia but did not affect oxidative-stress or hemorheology endpoints in a tiny healthy-male study.

**Why it matters:** Adds a recent open-access lipid source while preserving null oxidative-stress boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** postprandial triglyceride AUC, hemorheological properties, oxidative stress.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for lipid context only; do not claim oxidative-stress benefit.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Healthy young men.

- **Participant count:** 8

- **Intervention/exposure:** One hour of walking exercise at 50% VO2max on the day before the high-fat meal challenge.

- **Comparator/control:** Rest/control condition.

- **Duration/follow-up:** Two-day crossover conditions with high-fat meal testing after walking or rest.

- **Endpoints:** postprandial triglyceride AUC; hemorheological properties; oxidative stress

- **Effect estimates or direction:** Walking reduced postprandial lipemia in extracted results, with lower triglyceride AUC in exercise versus control, but did not influence hemorheological properties or oxidative-stress measures.

- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse-event signal was extracted from the source record.

- **Limitations:** Very small sample; Healthy young men only; Walking was prior-day/preprandial rather than after each meal; High-fat meal challenge; Oxidative-stress and hemorheology findings were null

- **Population mismatch:** Adjacent walking-lipemia mechanism source, not after-meal walking protocol evidence.

- **Directness to Walking After Every Meal:** adjacent_variant
