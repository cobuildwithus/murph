Source key for extracted claims on this page: `source_artifact:pmid-9112022`.

## Findings
- Moderate bicycle exercise after breakfast lowered breakfast glucose AUC versus control.
- The effect did not persist to lunch, supporting a meal-specific mechanism boundary.

## Why it matters
It is foundational postprandial exercise physiology but bicycle-only, male-only, and not a walking-after-every-meal protocol.

## Potential experiment signals
- Breakfast glucose AUC
- Lunch glucose AUC
- Insulin
- C-peptide

- Glucose disappearance

## Extraction details
- **Source kind:** journal_article
- **Study design:** acute_physiology (Acute controlled post-breakfast cycling physiology study)
- **Participant count:** 9 (reported)
- **Population:** Diet-treated sedentary male patients with NIDDM/type 2 diabetes-era terminology.
- **Intervention or exposure:** 45 minutes of bicycle exercise at about 53±2% VO2max starting 45 minutes after breakfast.
- **Comparator or control:** Rest control day and a diet-reduction day matched to the exercise energy expenditure.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Post-breakfast and post-lunch observation over about 8 hours.
- **Endpoints:** Glucose AUC after breakfast and lunch; insulin/C-peptide; glucose appearance and disappearance.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Breakfast glucose AUC was lower on exercise and diet days versus control; lunch glucose AUC did not differ. Glucose rate of appearance was unaffected while disappearance increased after exercise in accessible extraction.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event counts were extracted from accessible records.
- **Population mismatch:** Diet-treated sedentary male NIDDM patients; bicycle exercise; single breakfast bout.
- **Directness to Walking After Every Meal:** adjacent_variant
- **Artifact candidates / rights:** Publisher article likely requires permission; do not commit PDF. `pdfRightsStatus=permission_required`.

## Limitations
- Older NIDDM terminology and care context.
- Diet-treated sedentary men only.
- Bicycle exercise, not walking.
- Breakfast effect did not persist to lunch.
