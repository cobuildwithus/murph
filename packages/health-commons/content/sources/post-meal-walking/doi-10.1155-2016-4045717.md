This source is included for **reviews-meta-analyses-mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Narrative review of tactical exercise approaches for postmeal glucose surges, emphasizing post-meal timing and accessible aerobic activity while serving mainly as citation-chasing context.

**Why it matters:** It provides historical context and older source leads for postmeal exercise timing, but it should not be used as definitive evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** Postmeal glucose surge; Exercise timing; Aerobic exercise duration.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as background and citation-chasing support for postmeal exercise timing, not as primary protocol evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** People with and without impaired glycemic control discussed across older postmeal exercise studies.
- **Participant count:** Participant count not applicable or not resolved in the extracted record.
- **Participant count kind:** `not_applicable_review`
- **Intervention/exposure:** Light or moderate aerobic exercise after meals, including walking-like aerobic activity in cited studies.
- **Comparator/control:** No exercise or alternate exercise timing across cited literature.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Narrative synthesis of studies over multiple decades; no pooled follow-up duration.
- **Endpoints:** Postmeal glucose surge; Exercise timing; Aerobic exercise duration
- **Effect estimates or direction:** The review states that light aerobic exercise for about 60 minutes or moderate aerobic exercise for 20-30 minutes beginning about 30 minutes after a meal can blunt postmeal glucose surges; no pooled estimate extracted.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse-event rate was extracted.
- **Limitations:** Narrative review; Older heterogeneous literature; No pooled estimate; Not a fixed walking-after-every-meal protocol
- **Population mismatch:** Narrative across mixed populations and older studies; not a direct every-meal walking test.
