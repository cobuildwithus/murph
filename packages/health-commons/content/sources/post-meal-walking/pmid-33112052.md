## Findings
- Postprandial acid reflux episodes were more common in severe esophagitis/Barrett’s subjects than mild-moderate esophagitis subjects: median 10 (3 to 18) versus 6.5 (0 to 18), p=0.048. [source_artifact:pmid-33112052]
- Postprandial acid clearance time was longer in the severe/Barrett’s group: median 0.71 (0.07 to 2.66) minutes versus 0.17 (0.04 to 2.44), p=0.02. [source_artifact:pmid-33112052]
- Postprandial exercise increased TLESR with acid reflux in both groups; the authors concluded GERD patients should avoid exercise immediately after a meal. [source_artifact:pmid-33112052]

## Why it matters
This is an important reflux safety boundary, but it studied standardized exercise in GERD patients rather than gentle walking in general users. [source_artifact:pmid-33112052]

## Extraction details
- **Population:** Ambulant GERD subjects: 16 with mild-to-moderate esophagitis and 11 with severe esophagitis or Barrett’s esophagus.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Meal, rest, walking, and standardized postprandial exercise during high-resolution impedance manometry and pH monitoring.
- **Comparator or control:** Mild-moderate esophagitis versus severe esophagitis/Barrett’s groups; rest versus exercise periods.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Acute/postprandial HRIM and pH-probe monitoring session.
- **Endpoints:** reflux episode mechanisms; postprandial reflux; ambulant GERD physiology
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Postprandial acid reflux episodes were more common in severe esophagitis/Barrett’s subjects than mild-moderate esophagitis subjects: median 10 (3 to 18) versus 6.5 (0 to 18), p=0.048. Postprandial acid clearance time was longer in the severe/Barrett’s group: median 0.71 (0.07 to 2.66) minutes versus 0.17 (0.04 to 2.44), p=0.02. Postprandial exercise increased TLESR with acid reflux in both groups; the authors concluded GERD patients should avoid exercise immediately after a meal.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Reflux episodes in ambulant GERD subjects are the safety-relevant issue.
- **Population mismatch:** Ambulant GERD mechanism study rather than walking-after-meal trial.
