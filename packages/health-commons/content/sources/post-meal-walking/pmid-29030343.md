## Findings
- Low-carbohydrate diet plus postmeal walks improved extracted endothelial-function signal, but diet confounds walking attribution.
- Some endothelial/inflammatory markers were mixed or unchanged.

## Why it matters
It is relevant for vascular endpoints and combined-lifestyle context, but it cannot isolate walking-after-meal effects.

## Potential experiment signals
- Postprandial hyperglycemia
- Brachial artery flow-mediated dilation
- Circulating microparticles
- Endothelial microparticles
- Monocyte-platelet aggregates
- Adhesion molecules
- CRP

## Protocol takeaway
Use only as combined-intervention context; do not attribute vascular or glycemic effects to walking alone.

## Claim use
`context-only`.

## Extraction details
- **Source kind:** journal_article
- **Study design:** crossover (Randomized crossover combined low-carbohydrate diet plus postmeal walking study)
- **Participant count:** 11 (reported)
- **Population:** Individuals with type 2 diabetes.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Low-carbohydrate diet plus 15-minute postmeal walks across 4 days.
- **Comparator or control:** Low-carbohydrate diet alone and low-fat control diet.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Three 4-day controlled interventions.
- **Endpoints:** Postprandial hyperglycemia and vascular/endothelial markers including flow-mediated dilation.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** The combined low-carbohydrate plus postmeal-walking condition improved flow-mediated dilation (+0.8±1.0%, p=0.02) in accessible extraction; endothelial microparticles fell with low-carbohydrate diet alone, while several inflammatory/adhesion markers were unchanged.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event counts were extracted from accessible records.
- **Population mismatch:** T2D is relevant, but the intervention combines diet and walking, preventing walking-only attribution.
- **Directness to Walking After Every Meal:** adjacent_variant
- **Artifact candidates / rights:** PMCID is available; rights status in ledger is unknown, so do not commit PDF unless license is verified. `pdfRightsStatus=unknown`.

## Limitations
- Walking is combined with carbohydrate restriction.
- Short 4-day conditions.
- Small sample.
- Vascular findings cannot be assigned to walking alone.
