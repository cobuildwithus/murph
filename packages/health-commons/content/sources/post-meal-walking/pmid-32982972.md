This source is included for **safety-guidelines-medication-boundaries**.

**Findings:** Accessible metadata reports a dramatic glucose drop during moderate-intensity exercise and low glucose concentrations below 60 mg/dL in some participants, sometimes without symptoms.

**Why it matters:** It counters the assumption that post-meal exercise only lowers excessive glucose and cannot overshoot.

**Potential experiment signals:** Glucose drop during exercise, Transient hypoglycemia, Symptoms, Insulin and glucagon response.

**Protocol takeaway:** Even for non-insulin users, advise stopping for symptoms and considering glucose monitoring when at risk or prone to lows.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
