## Findings
- In accessible summaries, 216 meals were analyzed from 30 minutes before to 270 minutes after eating during approximately 88 hours of CGM/activity monitoring.
- Reported iAUC was lower with walking than inactivity in healthy controls and in participants with type 1 diabetes.
- Because the source includes insulin-treated type 1 diabetes participants, it should remain a safety-boundary source for protocol synthesis.

## Why it matters
The source is close to the mechanism and timing of post-meal walking, but the mixed T1D/healthy design and insulin-treated subgroup require it to stay in boundary evidence rather than direct protocol support.

## Potential experiment signals
- Postprandial glucose iAUC
- CGM glucose excursion
- Walking/activity after meals
- Hypoglycemia monitoring context

## Protocol takeaway
Use cautiously as boundary/mechanistic context: walking after meals reduced glucose excursions in monitored T1D and healthy participants, but insulin-treated users need individualized safety guidance.

## Extraction details

- **Source kind:** journal_article
- **Study design:** acute physiology (Acute CGM/activity-monitor physiology study)
- **Participant count:** 24 (reported)
- **Population:** Twelve adults with type 1 diabetes and twelve healthy controls.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Walking/light physical activity after selected meals, roughly activities-of-daily-living intensity in accessible summaries.
- **Comparator or control:** Post-meal inactivity.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Approximately 88 hours of CGM and physical-activity monitoring; meals analyzed from 30 minutes before to 270 minutes after eating.
- **Endpoints:** Postprandial glucose excursion iAUC over 270 minutes, CGM-measured glucose.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Accessible summaries report healthy-control iAUC 4.5 vs 9.6 mmol/L/270 min with inactivity (P≈0.022) and T1D iAUC 7.5 vs 18.4 mmol/L/270 min with inactivity (P<0.001).
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No specific adverse-event counts were extracted; insulin-treated T1D context implies hypoglycemia precautions are necessary.
- **Population mismatch:** Type 1 diabetes plus healthy controls; useful for clinical boundary and mechanism, not direct general-protocol evidence.
- **Directness to Walking After Every Meal:** safety_boundary
- **Artifact candidates / rights:** PMCID exists, but redistribution rights should be checked before storing any full text/PDF in Git. `pdfRightsStatus=permission_required`.

## Limitations
- Small groups: 12 T1D and 12 healthy controls.
- Acute/short monitoring window and meal-level analysis.
- Not a general adult RCT of walking after every meal.
- Full rights status for storing full text/PDF is not clearly redistributable.
