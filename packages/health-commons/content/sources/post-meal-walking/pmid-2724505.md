## Findings
- Vigorous exercise induced gastroesophageal reflux in healthy volunteers. [source_artifact:pmid-2724505]
- Running produced the most reflux, bicycling less, and weight exercises induced reflux in some participants. [source_artifact:pmid-2724505]
- The postprandial exercise pattern was similar but with greater reflux burden in extracted summaries. [source_artifact:pmid-2724505]

## Why it matters
This prevents unsafe generalization from gentle walking to all post-meal exercise. [source_artifact:pmid-2724505]

## Potential experiment signals
- heartburn with vigorous movement
- running after meals
- weights after meals
- bicycling intensity

## Protocol takeaway
For digestive comfort and reflux, the walking protocol should remain low intensity and avoid post-meal vigorous exercise as an inferred substitute. [source_artifact:pmid-2724505]

`safety-only`.

## Extraction details
- **Population:** Asymptomatic healthy volunteers, including men and women, in fasting and postprandial exercise testing.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Vigorous exercise exposures including stationary bicycling, running, and weight routines, in fasting and postprandial states.
- **Comparator or control:** Baseline fasting/postprandial monitoring and comparisons among exercise modalities.
- **Duration or follow-up:** One-hour exercise period containing exercise bouts and rest periods, plus baseline monitoring.
- **Endpoints:** esophageal acid reflux; postprandial exercise reflux; exercise modality differences
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Vigorous exercise induced gastroesophageal reflux in healthy volunteers. Running produced the most reflux, bicycling less, and weight exercises induced reflux in some participants. The postprandial exercise pattern was similar but with greater reflux burden in extracted summaries.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Exercise-induced reflux is the safety finding.
- **Population mismatch:** Vigorous exercise after meals differs from walking after meals.
- **Directness to Walking After Every Meal:** safety_boundary
- **Claim-use boundary:** safety-only
- **Artifact rights status:** paywalled

## Limitations
- Vigorous exercise modalities are not gentle walking.
- Older small physiology study.
- Does not establish risk from low-intensity walking.
