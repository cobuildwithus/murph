## Extraction details
- **Population:** Healthy volunteers in a running reflux-mechanism study.
- **Intervention or exposure:** After a meal and rest period, participants ran on a treadmill for 30 minutes at 60% of maximum heart rate followed by 20 minutes at 85% of maximum heart rate.
- **Comparator or control:** Post-meal rest/baseline phases and lower versus higher running intensity.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Acute postprandial treadmill protocol with 30-minute and 20-minute running phases.
- **Endpoints:** gastroesophageal reflux during running; reflux mechanisms
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Exercise led to a significantly higher percentage of time with esophageal pH<4 and higher frequency and duration of reflux episodes. Running also decreased contractility and duration of peristaltic contractions in extracted summaries.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Running-associated reflux is the safety note.
- **Population mismatch:** High-intensity/moderate-to-vigorous running context rather than walking after meals.
