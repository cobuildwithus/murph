## Findings
- Eleven adults with type 1 diabetes using hybrid closed-loop systems completed at-home baseline, pre-dinner-walk, and post-dinner-walk conditions.
- In accessible text, pre-dinner walking increased dinner-window time in range and reduced time above range versus baseline; post-dinner walking did not significantly improve those metrics.
- No severe hypoglycemic events requiring third-party intervention were reported in extracted text.

## Why it matters
This is an important contrary/boundary signal: in adults with type 1 diabetes using automated insulin delivery, pre-dinner walking may outperform post-dinner walking for time in range.

## Potential experiment signals

## Protocol takeaway
Use as insulin-treated safety-only evidence: post-dinner walking did not improve time in range in this HCLS study, and pre-dinner timing may be preferable in that population.

- **Participant count:** 11 (reported)
- **Population:** Adults with type 1 diabetes using hybrid closed-loop insulin delivery systems.
- **Intervention or exposure:** 20-minute self-paced walk within 30 minutes after dinner during the post-dinner condition; separate pre-dinner walk 30 minutes before dinner.
- **Comparator or control:** Two-week no-dinner-walking baseline and pre-dinner walking condition.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Two-week baseline plus two-week pre-dinner-walk and two-week post-dinner-walk conditions.
- **Endpoints:** Dexcom CGM time in range, time above range, time below range, dinner-window glucose metrics, 24-hour metrics, severe hypoglycemia.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Pre-dinner walking increased time in range versus baseline during the dinner window; post-dinner walking did not significantly improve time in range or time above range in extracted results.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No severe hypoglycemic events requiring third-party intervention were reported in extracted text.
- **Population mismatch:** Adults with type 1 diabetes using automated insulin delivery; safety-only and partly contrary for post-meal timing.
