## Protocol takeaway
Do not advise reflux-prone users to intensify post-meal walking into running without monitoring symptoms. [source_artifact:pmid-2178535]

## Claim use
`safety-only`.

## Extraction details
- **Population:** Normal volunteers/runners in an acute running reflux study.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Running exposure during ambulatory esophageal pH monitoring; ranitidine pretreatment was also tested.
- **Comparator or control:** Baseline hour and ranitidine versus placebo conditions.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Acute crossover-style running sessions; extracted summaries describe a baseline hour and a running hour.
- **Endpoints:** esophageal acid reflux during running; belching-associated reflux; acid suppression response
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Running increased reflux compared with baseline (p<0.05 in accessible summaries). Ranitidine pretreatment reduced acid exposure during running in the extracted summary.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Running-associated acid reflux, commonly with belching, is the safety signal.
- **Population mismatch:** Runner physiology/safety source rather than walking-after-meal evidence.
- **Directness to Walking After Every Meal:** safety_boundary
- **Claim-use boundary:** safety-only
- **Artifact rights status:** paywalled

## Limitations
- Running is not gentle walking.
- Older small acute study.
- Treatment arm is not relevant to lifestyle protocol claims.
