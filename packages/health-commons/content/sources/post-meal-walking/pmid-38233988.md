## Findings
- Forty women with GDM were randomized and 26 completed the trial in accessible summaries.
- The intervention prescribed 10-minute walks after three main meals for about seven weeks; the comparator prescribed standard-care 30-minute continuous walking most days per week.
- Extracted results state that postmeal walking did not improve postprandial glucose outcomes versus standard care; PMW participants spent about 57 minutes/day more sedentary and about 11 minutes/day less stepping than controls.

## Why it matters
This longer GDM RCT is important because it preserves a negative/no-clear-advantage finding against a continuous-walking comparator and flags sedentary-time tradeoffs.

## Extraction details

- **Source kind:** journal_article
- **Study design:** rct (Randomized clinical trial)
- **Participant count:** 26 (reported)
- **Population:** Women with gestational diabetes mellitus, randomized around 28–30 weeks gestation.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Daily 10-minute walks after breakfast, lunch, and dinner for approximately 7 weeks.
- **Comparator or control:** Standard-care advice for 30 minutes of continuous walking most days per week.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Approximately seven weeks from diagnosis/randomization to late pregnancy follow-up.
- **Endpoints:** Primary postprandial glucose by CGM; 24-hour, nocturnal, and fasting glucose; ActivPAL sedentary/stepping metrics; adherence; delivery/birth outcomes collected.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Postmeal walking did not improve postprandial glucose versus standard-care continuous walking; dinner 3-hour postprandial glucose was higher in PMW at baseline and across intervention in extracted summary; 24-hour/nocturnal/fasting glucose were similar.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event counts were extracted; sedentary time/less stepping were behavioral tradeoffs rather than clinical adverse events.
- **Population mismatch:** Pregnant women with GDM; context-only clinical boundary.
- **Directness to Walking After Every Meal:** clinical_supervised

## Limitations
- 26 completers out of 40 randomized.
- GDM pregnancy population.
- Active comparator, not no-exercise control.
- Baseline dinner glucose imbalance noted in extracted summary.
- Paywalled full text.
