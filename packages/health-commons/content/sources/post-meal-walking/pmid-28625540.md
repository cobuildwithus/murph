This source is included for **sedentary-breaks-standing-micro-walks**.

**Findings:** In healthy adolescents, activity breaks after meals with varying energy content lowered glucose incremental AUC but not total AUC, making this a pediatric adjacent-boundary source.

**Why it matters:** It preserves pediatric/adolescent boundary context for sitting-break physiology.

**Potential experiment signals:** Postprandial glucose incremental AUC, Total glucose AUC.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as pediatric/context boundary.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Healthy adolescents.
- **Participant count:** 13 healthy adolescents.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Activity breaks interrupting sitting after meals with varying meal energy; extracted notes indicate resistance-type activity rather than walking.
- **Comparator/control:** Uninterrupted sitting under different meal-energy conditions.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Acute crossover postprandial protocol.
- **Endpoints:** Postprandial glucose incremental AUC, Total glucose AUC
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Activity breaks lowered glucose incremental AUC but not total AUC in extracted notes.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse-event signal was extracted from available metadata.
- **Limitations:** Adolescent population; Activity breaks not walking-specific; Acute laboratory setting; Meal-energy manipulation differs from protocol
- **Population mismatch:** Population and intervention mismatch: adolescents and non-walking sitting-break activity, not adult after-meal walking.
