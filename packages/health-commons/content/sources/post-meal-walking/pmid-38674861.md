This source is included for **reviews-meta-analyses-mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Prescription-oriented narrative review summarizing how exercise timing, intensity, and duration may manage postprandial glycemia and highlighting that existing guidelines remain broad.

**Why it matters:** It frames practical dose, timing, and intensity questions while making clear that the literature has not settled a simple universal prescription.

**Potential experiment signals:** Postprandial glucose; Exercise intensity; Exercise duration; GI tolerability; CGM response.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for prescription vocabulary and safety boundaries, not as direct proof of a walking-after-every-meal protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Healthy individuals and people with type 2 diabetes discussed across the postprandial exercise literature.
- **Participant count:** Participant count not applicable or not resolved in the extracted record.
- **Participant count kind:** `not_applicable_review`
- **Intervention/exposure:** Postprandial exercise with varied timing, duration, intensity, and modality.
- **Comparator/control:** No exercise, alternative timing, or alternative intensity across cited studies.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Narrative synthesis across acute and training studies; no pooled follow-up duration.
- **Endpoints:** Postprandial glucose; Exercise intensity; Exercise duration; GI tolerability; CGM response
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Review states moderate-intensity exercise shortly after meals may improve glucose response; durations from 10 to 120 minutes can have positive effects, with 30-60 minutes most consistently linked in cited evidence. No pooled estimate extracted.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** High-intensity exercise soon after meals may be challenging and can raise GI tolerability concerns or hepatic glucose output in some contexts.
- **Limitations:** Narrative review; No pooled estimates; Guideline-level evidence gaps; Heterogeneous cited populations and protocols
- **Population mismatch:** Narrative across many populations and exercise types; not specific to every-meal walking.
