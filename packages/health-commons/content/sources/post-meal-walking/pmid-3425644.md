## Findings
- Accessible summaries report 42 pregnant women with type 1 diabetes and 28 nondiabetic pregnant controls, with exercise participants instructed to walk 20 minutes, approximately one mile, after each meal.
- Glycemic control was described as modestly but not significantly superior in diabetic exercisers versus diabetic nonexercisers.
- Walking was associated with lower fasting cholesterol/triglycerides, including significantly lower fasting triglycerides in the diabetic exercise group, and no adverse maternal or infant effects were reported in accessible summaries.

## Why it matters
This is an early after-each-meal walking source in pregnancy plus insulin-dependent diabetes, with a mixed/negative glycemic finding that must be preserved rather than converted into protocol support.
