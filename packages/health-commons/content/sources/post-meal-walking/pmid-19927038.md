## Findings
- Low-intensity exercise improved 24-hour glycemia more clearly than the high-intensity comparator in this small T2D study.
- Because exercise was not a meal-timed walking protocol, it is dose/intensity context only.

## Why it matters
It supports a dose/intensity boundary: lower-intensity activity can be metabolically meaningful, but this was not meal-timed walking.

## Potential experiment signals
- 24-hour mean glucose
- Time above 10 mmol/L
- Hyperglycemia prevalence

## Protocol takeaway
Use as adjacent low-intensity exercise context only; it does not establish a post-meal walking schedule.

## Claim use
`context-only`.

## Extraction details
- **Source kind:** journal_article
- **Study design:** experimental_low_intensity_exercise_study (Randomized crossover exercise-intensity study with 24-hour CGM)
- **Participant count:** 9 (reported)
- **Population:** Sedentary men with type 2 diabetes.
- **Intervention or exposure:** A 60-minute low-intensity exercise bout at about 35% Wmax or a 30-minute high-intensity bout at about 70% Wmax.
- **Comparator or control:** No-exercise control day.
- **Duration or follow-up:** 24-hour CGM under dietary standardization.
- **Endpoints:** Average 24-hour glucose and prevalence of hyperglycemia above 10 mmol/L.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Low-intensity exercise reduced mean 24-hour glucose to 7.8±0.9 versus 9.4±0.8 mmol/L on control and reduced hyperglycemia prevalence by about 50±4%; high-intensity changes were not statistically significant in accessible extraction.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event counts were extracted from accessible records.
- **Population mismatch:** Sedentary men with T2D, single endurance exercise bout, not specifically timed after meals.
- **Directness to Walking After Every Meal:** adjacent_variant
- **Artifact candidates / rights:** Rights status unknown; do not commit PDF without license verification. `pdfRightsStatus=unknown`.

## Limitations
- Small male-only sample.
- Single exercise bout, not repeated after meals.
- Endurance exercise modality rather than walking protocol.
- High-intensity arm not statistically significant in extracted results.
