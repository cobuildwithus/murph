## Findings
- In older people with postprandial hypotension, intermittent walking at a usual pace was reported to attenuate the fall in systolic blood pressure after a glucose drink.
- The model used a glucose drink rather than an ordinary meal, and the sample was small.
- This source belongs in safety boundaries, not direct general-protocol efficacy.

## Why it matters
Postprandial hypotension is a plausible safety issue for frail or older users; this study suggests walking can attenuate systolic BP decline in a supervised context rather than necessarily worsen it.

## Potential experiment signals
- Systolic blood pressure
- Heart rate
- Postprandial hypotension symptoms
- Fall risk

## Protocol takeaway
Use for safety nuance: hypotension-prone users should not assume after-meal walking is unsafe or safe; they need supervised, symptom-aware pacing.
