## Extraction details
- **Population:** Twenty-five symptomatic GERD patients with abnormal 24-hour esophageal pH studies; 14 upright refluxers and 11 combined refluxers.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Thirty minutes of bending/abdominal-contraction exercise, consisting of five 6-minute sessions with toe-touching from supine, sitting, and standing positions.
- **Comparator or control:** Thirty minutes before exercise and day without exercise during ambulatory pH monitoring.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Two-day 24-hour pH monitoring comparison, with 30-minute exercise exposure on day 2.
- **Endpoints:** gastroesophageal reflux during bending; reflux symptoms
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Median reflux time percentage during 30 minutes of exercise was higher than during 30 minutes before exercise: 6.6 (0 to 60) versus 0.0 (0 to 80), p=0.02. In combined refluxers, exercise versus pre-exercise reflux time percentage was 13 (0 to 53) versus 0.0 (0 to 42), p=0.008; the same contrast was not significant in upright refluxers. Two patients (8%) developed symptoms during exercise.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Two patients (8%) developed symptoms during bending exercise; bending precipitated reflux in moderate-to-severe GERD.
- **Population mismatch:** Bending exercise in symptomatic patients differs from gentle walking after meals.
