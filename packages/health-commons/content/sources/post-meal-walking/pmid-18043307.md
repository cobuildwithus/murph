This source is included for **safety-guidelines-medication-boundaries**.

**Findings:** Postprandial sessions generally produced significant glucose decreases, reported across time windows as roughly 18% to 50% decreases depending on interval.

**Why it matters:** It shows that postprandial exercise can substantially lower glucose in T2D, reinforcing both benefit plausibility and hypoglycemia monitoring needs.

**Potential experiment signals:** Capillary blood glucose change, Meal-state safety, Hypoglycemia boundary.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only meal-state context: glucose drops after post-meal exercise can be large, especially in medicated populations.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Middle-aged obese men with type 2 diabetes; some diet-treated and some using hypoglycemic agents.
- **Participant count:** 43 (reported).
- **Intervention/exposure:** One-hour cycle ergometer exercise at about 60% VO2peak in fasted and multiple postprandial time windows.
- **Comparator/control:** Fasted exercise and different meal-to-exercise intervals; pre/post session glucose.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 1,555 exercise sessions with immediate pre/post glucose measurement.
- **Endpoints:** Capillary blood glucose change, Meal-state safety, Hypoglycemia boundary
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Postprandial sessions generally produced significant glucose decreases, reported across time windows as roughly 18% to 50% decreases depending on interval.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Authors characterized fasted exercise as safe in the studied men; the glucose drop magnitude depended on starting glucose and meal timing.
- **Limitations:** Men only; Cycling rather than walking; Immediate session outcomes, not every-meal free-living walking; Medication details by session require full-text review.
- **Population mismatch:** T2D meal-state exercise safety source, but not a walking protocol.
- **Directness to Walking After Every Meal:** safety_boundary / adjacent_variant.
- **Artifact candidates and rights:** permission_required; no copyrighted PDF is vendored in Git by this extraction.
