This source is included for **safety-older-pregnancy-pediatric-hypotension**.

**Findings:** The review argues that assessed weight-bearing exercise can be appropriate for many people with DPN, but foot and fall-risk screening remain essential.

**Why it matters:** Helps avoid both extremes: excluding all neuropathy patients from walking or ignoring foot/fall risk when recommending post-meal walking.

**Potential experiment signals:** foot-ulcer risk status, protective sensation/foot deformity screen, balance or falls history, footwear adequacy.

**Protocol takeaway:** DPN users should not start an unscreened after-meal walking protocol if they have active ulcers, severe deformity, unsafe footwear, or high fall risk; assessed walking may be reasonable for lower-risk DPN.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** People with diabetic peripheral neuropathy.
- **Participant count:** Not applicable; narrative review/perspective.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Physical training, weight-bearing activity, walking, and movement-based interventions in DPN.
- **Comparator/control:** Historical weight-bearing exercise avoidance and lower-risk assessed activity.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Narrative review; cited intervention durations varied.
- **Endpoints:** foot-ulcer risk status, protective sensation/foot deformity screen, balance or falls history, footwear adequacy
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Evidence supports a paradigm shift toward assessed weight-bearing exercise for many people with DPN, while severe deformity or active foot risk remains a safety boundary.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Foot ulcers, severe foot deformity, loss of protective sensation, footwear, gait impairment, and fall risk should be screened before walking recommendations.
- **Limitations:** Narrative review; Not meal-timed; Does not quantify post-meal walking adverse events
- **Population mismatch:** Safety-boundary evidence rather than routine adult/general wellness protocol evidence.
- **Directness to Walking After Every Meal:** safety_boundary
