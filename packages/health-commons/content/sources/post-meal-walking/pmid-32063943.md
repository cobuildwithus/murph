## Findings
- The most common lifestyle factor associated with GERD in accessible summaries was less exercise time (90.9%; OR 6.47; 95% CI 4.91 to 8.53). [source_artifact:pmid-32063943]
- Accessible summaries report late evening meals, inadequate sleep, smoking, and post-dinner lying as associated lifestyle factors. [source_artifact:pmid-32063943]
- The study remains cross-sectional and does not isolate a controlled post-dinner walking dose. [source_artifact:pmid-32063943]

## Why it matters
It adds observational lifestyle context while keeping post-dinner walking separate from other behaviors. [source_artifact:pmid-32063943]
