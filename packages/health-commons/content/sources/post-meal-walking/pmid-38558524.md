**Findings:** Accessible abstract-level conclusion: light-intensity activity breaks ameliorated postprandial vascular-inflammatory/thrombotic responses, with greatest effects in people with concomitant insulin resistance.

**Why it matters:** Adds mechanistic/cardiometabolic context but should not be promoted into glucose protocol claims.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as adjacent biomarker context if discussing T1D walking breaks; not as direct walking-after-meal efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
