**Findings:** Accessible PubMed metadata states postprandial exercise of all modalities was effective, safe, and feasible when necessary precautions such as prandial insulin reductions were taken.

**Why it matters:** Recent evidence makes the key safety point succinctly: postprandial exercise may be feasible in T1D only with precautions.

**Potential experiment signals:** Acute glucose response, Subacute glucose response, Late glucose response, Hypoglycemia safety.

**Protocol takeaway:** For T1D, use as a safety caveat: “may be feasible with clinician-guided precautions,” not as a broad recommendation.
