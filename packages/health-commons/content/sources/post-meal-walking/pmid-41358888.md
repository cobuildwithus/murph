This source is included for **safety-older-pregnancy-pediatric-hypotension**.

**Findings:** ADA older-adult diabetes standards emphasize hypoglycemia prevention, individualized care, medication burden, frailty, and fall-risk considerations that bound post-meal walking advice.

**Why it matters:** Older adults with diabetes are a key boundary group for post-meal walking because falls and medication-related hypoglycemia can be amplified by activity around meals.

**Potential experiment signals:** hypoglycemia symptoms or CGM time-below-range, falls or near-falls, frailty/function screen, medication class: insulin or secretagogue.

**Protocol takeaway:** Older adults with diabetes should use medication-aware and fall-aware screening before adopting after-each-meal walking.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Older adults with diabetes, especially those with frailty, falls, cognitive impairment, insulin use, or insulin secretagogue use.
- **Participant count:** Not applicable; professional guideline.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Older-adult diabetes care standards, including individualized targets, hypoglycemia prevention, and regimen simplification.
- **Comparator/control:** Not applicable.

- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Medication-related hypoglycemia, falls, frailty, and functional impairment can change the safety of adding post-meal walking.
- **Limitations:** Guideline rather than trial; Not specific to post-meal walking; Applies to diabetes care, not general wellness users
