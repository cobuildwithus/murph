This source is included for **safety-older-pregnancy-pediatric-hypotension**.

**Findings:** ACOG’s GDM bulletin supports exercise as part of GDM management context, but permission-controlled access and guideline design require cautious use.

**Why it matters:** GDM sources can sound like direct support for post-meal walking, but they need to remain separate from routine adult trial evidence and carry pregnancy safety boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** post-meal glucose monitoring, GDM medication escalation, pregnancy clinician-clearance status.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as external GDM guideline context only; pregnant users should follow their obstetric/diabetes care plan.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Pregnant women with gestational diabetes mellitus.
- **Participant count:** Not applicable; professional guideline.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Gestational diabetes lifestyle management guidance including exercise and meal-timed walking context.
- **Comparator/control:** Medical nutrition therapy and pharmacologic management as clinically indicated.
