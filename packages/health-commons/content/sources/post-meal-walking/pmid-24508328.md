Source key for extracted claims on this page: `source_artifact:pmid-24508328`.

## Findings
- Post-dinner treadmill walking lowered postprandial glucose compared with table tennis/rest.
- Mood and sympathovagal-balance measures were null in the accessible extraction.

## Why it matters
It adds tolerability-adjacent endpoints (mood, HRV) while remaining a post-dinner-only lab comparison, not walking after every meal.

## Potential experiment signals
- Postprandial glucose
- Profile of Mood States
- Heart-rate variability/sympathovagal balance
- Heart rate
- Motion/activity

## Protocol takeaway
Use as post-dinner adjacent evidence and as a reminder that non-glycemic endpoints may be null.

## Claim use
`context-only`.

## Extraction details
- **Source kind:** journal_article
- **Study design:** acute_physiology (Randomized acute post-dinner activity crossover study)
- **Participant count:** 12 (reported)
- **Population:** Adults with uncomplicated type 2 diabetes, on diet/oral medications and not insulin or beta-blockers.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Thirty minutes of self-paced treadmill walking starting about 30 minutes after the start of a standardized dinner.
- **Comparator or control:** Thirty minutes of table tennis and a resting condition.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Acute post-dinner trials with postprandial glucose measured out to about 180 minutes.
- **Endpoints:** Postprandial glycemia, mood, and heart-rate variability/sympathovagal balance.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Treadmill walking significantly lowered postprandial glucose versus table tennis/rest in accessible extraction; mood and HRV outcomes were not different between conditions.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event counts were extracted; population excluded insulin and beta-blocker users.
- **Population mismatch:** Uncomplicated T2D and post-dinner-only lab setting; not after every meal.
- **Directness to Walking After Every Meal:** adjacent_variant
- **Artifact candidates / rights:** Paywalled article; do not commit PDF. `pdfRightsStatus=paywalled`.

## Limitations
- Small sample.
- Post-dinner only.
- Treadmill walking/table-tennis lab setting.
- Excluded insulin and beta-blocker users.
