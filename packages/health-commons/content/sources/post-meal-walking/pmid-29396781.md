This source is included for **reviews-meta-analyses-mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Systematic review of postprandial exercise in adults with type 2 diabetes, reporting glucose-control benefits across aerobic and resistance protocols while emphasizing protocol heterogeneity and limited prescription certainty.

**Why it matters:** It is a core review for the same post-meal exercise mechanism in type 2 diabetes, but still sits above primary trials and should not be double-counted with them.

**Potential experiment signals:** Short-term glucose AUC; 24-hour hyperglycemia; CGM time above range; Exercise duration and intensity.

**Protocol takeaway:** Postprandial exercise can reduce glucose outcomes in T2D studies, especially with longer moderate aerobic sessions, but this review should remain context for claims rather than direct protocol evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Adults with type 2 diabetes not treated with insulin in randomized crossover studies.
- **Participant count:** 135
- **Participant count kind:** `reported_total_across_included_studies`
- **Intervention/exposure:** Aerobic or resistance exercise performed within 3 hours after a standardized meal.
- **Comparator/control:** No postprandial exercise control conditions in randomized crossover studies.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Acute postprandial exercise studies, with exercise performed within 3 hours after a standardized meal.
- **Endpoints:** Short-term glucose AUC; 24-hour hyperglycemia; CGM time above range; Exercise duration and intensity
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Aerobic exercise reduced short-term glucose AUC by 3.4-26.6% and 24-hour hyperglycemia by 11.9-65%; resistance exercise studies reported 30% short-term glucose AUC and 35% 24-hour hyperglycemia reductions.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Included studies excluded insulin-treated T2D; no batch-level adverse-event rate was extracted.
- **Limitations:** Small total sample across included studies; T2D without insulin treatment limits generalizability; Heterogeneous timing, modality, duration, and glucose metrics; Not specifically walking after every meal
- **Population mismatch:** Type 2 diabetes without insulin treatment; not a general-population every-meal walking test.
