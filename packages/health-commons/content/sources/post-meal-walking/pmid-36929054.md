This source is included for **reviews-meta-analyses-mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Systematic review of clinical practice guidelines for physical activity and exercise in pregnant women with gestational diabetes, including after-meal and walking recommendations but also inconsistent safety guidance.

**Why it matters:** It maps pregnancy/GDM-specific recommendations and safety-warning gaps, which should stay separate from general adult protocol claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** Exercise after meals; Walking; Moderate intensity; Insulin-related warning signs; Pregnancy safety.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only for GDM guideline context and safety boundaries; do not generalize after-meal exercise recommendations to all adults.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Pregnant women with gestational diabetes mellitus addressed by clinical practice guidelines.
- **Participant count:** Participant count not applicable or not resolved in the extracted record.
- **Participant count kind:** `not_applicable_guideline_review`
- **Intervention/exposure:** Physical activity and exercise recommendations, including walking and activity after meals.
- **Comparator/control:** Different guideline recommendations and usual care norms.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Guideline recommendations, not an intervention follow-up study.
- **Endpoints:** Exercise after meals; Walking; Moderate intensity; Insulin-related warning signs; Pregnancy safety
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Guidelines commonly recommended 30 minutes per session on 5 days per week or every day after meals, moderate intensity, and aerobic/resistance exercise including walking; safety warning guidance for women on insulin was inconsistent.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Pregnancy and insulin-use warning signs were inconsistently addressed across guidelines.
- **Limitations:** Guideline review rather than intervention evidence; Pregnancy/GDM population mismatch; Guidelines varied in quality and consistency; No effect estimates
- **Population mismatch:** Pregnant women with GDM; guideline recommendations do not equal direct intervention evidence for non-pregnant adults.
