This source is included for **secondary-metabolism-lipids-insulin-cgm**.

**Findings:** This protocol describes a pregnancy-hyperglycemia crossover trial of morning versus evening walking/stepping with CGM, but no results are available.

**Why it matters:** Useful for planned CGM, adherence, diet, and sleep measurement design in pregnancy, but not for efficacy claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** 24-hour CGM glucose, post-breakfast/lunch/dinner glucose, nighttime and pre-breakfast glucose, activity adherence, food intake, mood and sleep.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as implementation/protocol context only; do not cite for outcomes.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Protocol aims to enroll pregnant individuals with gestational glucose intolerance or gestational diabetes.

- **Participant count:** 39

- **Intervention/exposure:** Thirty minutes of moderate-intensity walking or stepping within 30–40 minutes of breakfast or dinner starts.

- **Comparator/control:** Morning activity, evening activity, and no physical activity conditions.

- **Duration/follow-up:** Planned crossover protocol: morning, evening, and no-activity days with washout.

- **Endpoints:** 24-hour CGM glucose; post-breakfast/lunch/dinner glucose; nighttime and pre-breakfast glucose; activity adherence; food intake; mood and sleep

- **Effect estimates or direction:** No efficacy results were available because this is a rationale and protocol paper.

- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No trial adverse-event results were available in the protocol-only source.

- **Limitations:** Protocol-only; no outcomes yet; Pregnancy hyperglycemia population; Walking or stepping combined; Morning versus evening timing rather than after every meal; Results pending

- **Population mismatch:** Adjacent protocol-only source in pregnancy hyperglycemia; not completed walking-after-every-meal evidence.
