This source is included for **reviews-meta-analyses-mechanisms**.

**Findings:** CGM-based meta-analysis in type 2 diabetes separating postprandial and fasting glucose effects, with exercise lowering postprandial or daily hyperglycemia measures but not fasting glucose.

**Why it matters:** It helps maintain a protocol boundary: post-meal walking should be evaluated on postprandial/CGM outcomes, not fasting glucose alone.

**Potential experiment signals:** Postprandial glucose; Fasting glucose; CGM hyperglycemia; Hypoglycemia.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context that exercise effects may be more visible on postprandial CGM signals than fasting glucose in T2D.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
