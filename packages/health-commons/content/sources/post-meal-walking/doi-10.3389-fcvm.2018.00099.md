This source is included for **reviews-meta-analyses-mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Narrative cardiovascular-risk review arguing that physical activity targeted to the postprandial period may reduce postprandial hyperglycemia and sedentary-time risk, while emphasizing unanswered prescription questions.

**Why it matters:** It explains why postprandial glucose excursions may matter for cardiovascular risk and why sitting interruptions are considered in this family.

**Potential experiment signals:** Postprandial hyperglycemia; Cardiovascular risk context; Sitting interruption timing; Light activity breaks.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mechanistic/risk context and boundary-setting for unanswered timing questions, not as direct support for a specific protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
