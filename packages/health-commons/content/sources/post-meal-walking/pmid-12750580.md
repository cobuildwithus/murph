## Findings
- Ten subjects from each sport with a history of exercise-induced heartburn were enrolled. [source_artifact:pmid-12750580]
- Weightlifters experienced the most heartburn and reflux: 18.51 ± 17.34% time with esophageal pH ≤4.0 fasted and 35.81 ± 34.33% fed. [source_artifact:pmid-12750580]
- Runners had moderate reflux and cyclists milder reflux; the authors concluded strenuous exercise induced significant reflux and symptoms in conditioned athletes. [source_artifact:pmid-12750580]

## Why it matters
This is a clear intensity/modality boundary source: strenuous sport exercise is not equivalent to gentle post-meal walking. [source_artifact:pmid-12750580]

## Potential experiment signals
- heartburn during exertion
- lifting/straining after meals

## Protocol takeaway
Keep the protocol as gentle walking; do not generalize it to post-meal weightlifting, running, or strenuous exercise. [source_artifact:pmid-12750580]

## Extraction details
- **Population:** Conditioned athletes with exercise-induced heartburn: 10 runners, 10 cyclists, and 10 weightlifters.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Standardized sport-specific exercise routines at 65% for 60 minutes and 85% for 20 minutes of maximal capability, assessed fasting and fed.
- **Comparator or control:** Exercise modality and fed versus fasted conditions.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Acute exercise monitoring with 60-minute lower-intensity and 20-minute higher-intensity routines.
- **Endpoints:** esophageal acid exposure; heartburn/reflux during exercise
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Ten subjects from each sport with a history of exercise-induced heartburn were enrolled. Weightlifters experienced the most heartburn and reflux: 18.51 ± 17.34% time with esophageal pH ≤4.0 fasted and 35.81 ± 34.33% fed. Runners had moderate reflux and cyclists milder reflux; the authors concluded strenuous exercise induced significant reflux and symptoms in conditioned athletes.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Exercise-induced heartburn and reflux occurred, especially during strenuous fed-state weightlifting.
- **Population mismatch:** Conditioned athletes and high-strain activities differ from routine walking after meals.
