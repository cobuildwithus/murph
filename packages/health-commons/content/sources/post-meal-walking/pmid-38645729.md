## Findings
- Higher daily steps were associated with lower incident GERD risk in older adults, independent of BMI. [source_artifact:pmid-38645729]
- Accessible summaries report HR 0.44 (95% CI 0.20 to 0.96) for the middle step tertile and HR 0.17 (95% CI 0.05 to 0.54) for the upper tertile versus the lower tertile. [source_artifact:pmid-38645729]
- Obesity was associated with higher incident GERD risk compared with normal weight in accessible summaries: HR 3.00 (95% CI 1.19 to 7.55). [source_artifact:pmid-38645729]

## Why it matters
It supplies walking-adjacent GERD context while preserving the non-meal-timed boundary. [source_artifact:pmid-38645729]
