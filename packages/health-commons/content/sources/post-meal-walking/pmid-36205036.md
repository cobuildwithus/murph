This source is included for **sedentary-breaks-standing-micro-walks**.

**Findings:** In children, interrupting daily sedentary time with short moderate activity bouts for six days improved insulin/C-peptide-related glucose metabolism markers, but the population and protocol are mismatched.

**Why it matters:** Useful for pediatric feasibility and glucose-metabolism context while preserving mismatch.

**Potential experiment signals:** Insulin AUC, C-peptide AUC, Glucose metabolism, Energy intake, Energy expenditure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as pediatric/context evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Healthy normoglycemic children aged about 7 to 11 years.
- **Participant count:** 109 randomized healthy normoglycemic children; extracted notes indicate 93 retained for analysis.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Three-minute moderate-intensity walking/activity breaks every 30 minutes for 3 hours per day across six days.
- **Comparator/control:** Uninterrupted sitting control condition.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Six-day intervention.
- **Endpoints:** Insulin AUC, C-peptide AUC, Glucose metabolism, Energy intake, Energy expenditure
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Extracted notes report lower insulin AUC and C-peptide AUC plus improved model-based glucose metabolism markers, with no group difference in energy intake or expenditure.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse-event signal was extracted from available metadata.
- **Limitations:** Children rather than adults; Moderate intensity and fixed daily sitting sessions; Not a meal-specific every-meal walking habit
- **Population mismatch:** Population mismatch: pediatric sedentary-interruption RCT, not adult post-meal walking.
