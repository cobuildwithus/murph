This source is included for **direct-core-glycemic-dose-timing**.

**Findings:** The post-meal intervention used approximately 1500-1600 steps for 15 minutes starting 15 minutes after each meal, compared with a one-time 45-minute pre-breakfast walk of approximately 4500-4800 steps. The post-meal condition improved blood glucose profile and HbA1c versus the one-time daily condition, with reported p values below 0.001.

**Why it matters:** It offers a longer-duration repeated-meal comparator trial with clinical glucose and HbA1c outcomes plus compliance and safety reporting.

**Potential experiment signals:** Five-point blood glucose profile, HbA1c, Compliance/adherence, Hypoglycemia or adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** For type 2 diabetes, 15 minutes of brisk walking after each meal may outperform concentrating the same total walking time into one morning bout.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Adults with type 2 diabetes in India who completed the evaluable crossover sample after screening and exclusions.
- **Intervention/exposure:** moderate-intensity brisk walking after each meal
- **Comparator/control:** One-time 45-minute pre-breakfast brisk walk.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Two 60-day walking periods; post-meal walking and one-time daily walking were compared without a clear washout period.
- **Endpoints:** Five-point blood glucose profile, HbA1c, Compliance/adherence, Hypoglycemia or adverse events
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** The study reported no hypoglycemic events or other adverse events in the extracted record.
- **Limitations:** No CGM; no carbohydrate counting; no clear washout; telephonic reminders and wristband monitoring; clinical T2D sample may not generalize to healthy adults.
- **Population mismatch:** Direct for type 2 diabetes; less direct for healthy adults and casual wellness users.
