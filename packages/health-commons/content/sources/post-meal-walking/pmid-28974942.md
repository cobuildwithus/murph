This source is included for **reviews-meta-analyses-mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Narrative review arguing that exercise timed after eating can target postprandial glucose excursions, with a focus on mechanisms, CGM-guided timing, and gaps in prescription-level evidence.

**Why it matters:** It explains the biological rationale for placing movement in the post-meal window without converting the review into direct protocol efficacy evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** Postprandial glucose; CGM glucose peak timing; Time above range; Hypoglycemia watch-outs.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mechanism and prescription-gap context for why post-meal walking might be tested; do not cite as proof that walking after every meal works.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Adults with type 2 diabetes or postprandial hyperglycemia risk discussed across cited exercise studies.
- **Participant count:** Participant count not applicable or not resolved in the extracted record.
- **Participant count kind:** `not_applicable_review`
- **Intervention/exposure:** Exercise performed after eating, potentially timed to postprandial glucose excursions.
- **Comparator/control:** Pre-meal exercise, no exercise, or usual-care timing as discussed across cited studies.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Narrative synthesis of acute and short-term post-meal exercise evidence; no pooled follow-up duration.
- **Endpoints:** Postprandial glucose; CGM glucose peak timing; Time above range; Hypoglycemia watch-outs
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Directionally favorable mechanistic rationale for post-meal exercise; no pooled quantitative estimate extracted.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Review discusses the medication-plus-exercise paradox and the need to account for hypoglycemia risk in diabetes care.
- **Limitations:** Narrative review rather than systematic review; No pooled sample size or effect estimate; Protocol heterogeneity; Not a walking-after-every-meal intervention
- **Population mismatch:** Population and protocols are heterogeneous; this is not a direct repeated walking-after-every-meal trial.
