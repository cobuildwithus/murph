This source is included for **sedentary-breaks-standing-micro-walks**.

**Findings:** This crossover study compared sitting, standing, and walking for postprandial lipemia in healthy normolipidaemic adults, making it adjacent lipid-context evidence.

**Why it matters:** It helps distinguish sitting, standing, and walking for postprandial lipemia rather than glucose alone.

**Potential experiment signals:** Postprandial triglycerides, Postprandial lipemia.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as secondary lipid context only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Healthy normolipidaemic adults; extracted metadata specifies Japanese men.
- **Participant count:** 15 healthy normolipidaemic Japanese men.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Sitting, standing, and walking conditions around postprandial lipemia assessment.
- **Comparator/control:** Alternative posture/activity conditions including sitting and standing.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Three randomized two-day trials with postprandial lipemia assessment.
- **Endpoints:** Postprandial triglycerides, Postprandial lipemia
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Retained as a direct sitting-standing-walking lipid comparator; exact extracted direction and effect sizes were not resolved here.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse-event signal was extracted from available metadata.
- **Limitations:** Small healthy male sample; Lipid endpoint rather than glucose-primary protocol; Two-day laboratory conditions rather than repeated after-meal walking behavior
- **Population mismatch:** Adjacent variant: posture/walking comparison for lipemia, not walking after every meal.
