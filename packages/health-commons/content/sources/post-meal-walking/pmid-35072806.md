This source is included for **reviews-meta-analyses-mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Systematic review and meta-analysis comparing accumulated versus continuous exercise for postprandial glycemia, insulin, and triglycerides in adults with or without diabetes.

**Why it matters:** It helps contextualize several short bouts across the day versus one continuous exercise bout, adjacent to walking after meals.

**Potential experiment signals:** Postprandial glycemia; Insulin; Triglycerides; Accumulated activity; Same-day versus next-morning response.

**Protocol takeaway:** Accumulated activity may better control same-day postprandial glycemia than a single continuous bout, but secondary markers and next-morning outcomes were not clearly better.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Adults with or without diabetes in accumulated and continuous exercise intervention studies.
- **Participant count:** 635
- **Participant count kind:** `reported_total_across_included_studies`
- **Intervention/exposure:** Accumulated exercise bouts, including physical-activity breaks or repeated bouts across the day.
- **Comparator/control:** Energy-matched continuous exercise in one bout.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Same-day postprandial outcomes and second-morning outcomes after accumulated or continuous exercise interventions.
- **Endpoints:** Postprandial glycemia; Insulin; Triglycerides; Accumulated activity; Same-day versus next-morning response
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Accumulated exercise reduced same-day postprandial glycemia versus continuous exercise (SMD=-0.36, 95% CI -0.56 to -0.17, p=0.0002, I2=1%); no differences were found for same-day insulin or triglycerides or second-morning markers in extracted notes.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse-event rate was extracted.
- **Limitations:** Not necessarily meal-timed; Exercise modes varied; Secondary outcomes null; Not walking-only
- **Population mismatch:** Accumulated exercise broadly, not necessarily immediately after every meal or walking-only.
- **Directness to Walking After Every Meal:** `same_mechanism`.
