This source is included for **direct-core-glycemic-dose-timing**.

**Findings:** Walking was prescribed at about 40% maximum heart-rate reserve for 15 minutes starting 30 minutes after each meal. The exercise day had smaller 24-hour glucose incremental AUC and reduced glycemic variability versus the no-exercise day in the extracted abstract-level record.

**Why it matters:** It is a very close design match to walking after every meal: 15-minute walks after breakfast, lunch, and dinner with 24-hour glycemic monitoring.

**Potential experiment signals:** 24-hour glucose iAUC, Glucose variability, CGM standard deviation, Medication-context safety.

**Protocol takeaway:** Fifteen-minute moderate walks after each meal reduced CGM-derived 24-hour glycemic exposure and variability compared with the no-exercise day, but the small fixed-sequence design limits certainty.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Adults with type 2 diabetes; medication mix included oral agents and insulin regimens in the extracted record.
- **Intervention/exposure:** moderate-intensity walking after each meal
- **Comparator/control:** No-exercise day.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Two acute inpatient days: a no-exercise day followed by a postmeal walking day; CGM over the 24-hour window.
- **Endpoints:** 24-hour glucose iAUC, Glucose variability, CGM standard deviation, Medication-context safety
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Because the sample included people using insulin, hypoglycemia monitoring is important; the extracted abstract-level record did not provide a clear adverse-event count.
- **Limitations:** N=11; fixed order in the extracted record; acute inpatient two-day comparison; mixed diabetes medications; limited safety details accessible in extraction.
- **Population mismatch:** Direct for T2D under clinical observation; less direct for healthy adults or unsupervised medication changes.
- **Artifact candidates and rights:** Open-access/PMCID article; verify license before redistributing full text/PDF.
