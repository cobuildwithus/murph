## Findings
- Accessible summaries report 41 randomized women with GDM and 32 completers, assigned to standard care/30-minute continuous exercise or standard care plus 10-minute postmeal walks after breakfast, lunch, and dinner.
- Postprandial and overnight glucose were similar between groups, with both groups improving from baseline.
- No adherence difference was reported, but the postmeal-walk group completed more prescribed physical-activity minutes across baseline and intervention days.

## Why it matters
This directly compares a multiple-postmeal-walk pattern with a continuous-walking pregnancy standard-care comparator and reports feasibility/adherence signals, but it is short and GDM-specific.
