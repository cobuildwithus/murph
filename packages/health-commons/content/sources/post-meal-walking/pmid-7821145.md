This source is included for **safety-guidelines-medication-boundaries**.

**Findings:** Exercise reduced the postprandial glucose response area by about one-third in the accessible abstract-level result.

**Why it matters:** Mechanistically relevant to meal-timed activity with insulin on board.

**Potential experiment signals:** Postprandial glucose response area, Peak blood glucose, Constant insulin context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as older mechanism/safety context; do not infer a walking dose or insulin rule.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** IDDM subjects studied at constant insulinemia using an artificial pancreas/Biostator context.
- **Participant count:** 7 (reported).
- **Intervention/exposure:** 30 minutes of cycling starting 15 minutes after a carbohydrate-containing test meal.
- **Comparator/control:** Identical test meal without exercise on a separate occasion.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 180-minute postprandial response window.
- **Endpoints:** Postprandial glucose response area, Peak blood glucose, Constant insulin context
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Exercise reduced the postprandial glucose response area by about one-third in the accessible abstract-level result.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Shows glucose lowering at constant insulinemia, implying potential low-glucose risk if insulin is not adjusted in real-world use.
- **Limitations:** Very small older study; Cycling, not walking; Artificial pancreas/constant insulin setting; Not after every meal.
- **Population mismatch:** Historical IDDM physiology; adjacent safety/mechanism only.
- **Directness to Walking After Every Meal:** safety_boundary / same_mechanism.
