## Findings
- Accessible indexed summaries favor 45-minute postmeal exercise over 90-minute timing for T2D glucose lowering/fluctuation outcomes.
- Because detailed effect estimates and modality specifics were not extracted, confidence remains limited.

## Why it matters
It is current timing context in T2D, but the exercise mode is not walking-specific and the extraction is limited by accessible metadata/abstract snippets.

- **Intervention or exposure:** Moderate-intensity aerobic exercise started about 45 minutes after meals.
