This source is included for **sedentary-breaks-standing-micro-walks**.

**Findings:** This trial compared prolonged sitting, prolonged standing, and walking/activity breaks and found mixed vascular/metabolic patterns rather than a uniform glucose benefit.

**Why it matters:** Key mixed/null comparator evidence for vascular and metabolic outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:** Vascular function, Flow-mediated dilation, Postprandial glucose, Insulin.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to avoid overgeneralizing standing or activity-break findings.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Healthy normal-weight adults.
- **Participant count:** 18 healthy normal-weight adults.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Prolonged standing or activity breaks involving 2-minute walking bouts every 30 minutes.
- **Comparator/control:** Prolonged sitting.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Six-hour acute postprandial trial.
- **Endpoints:** Vascular function, Flow-mediated dilation, Postprandial glucose, Insulin
- **Effect estimates or direction:** The study separated prolonged standing from walking/activity breaks and included mixed metabolic/vascular outcomes; extracted notes emphasize that glucose findings were not uniformly positive.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse-event signal was extracted from available metadata.
- **Limitations:** Healthy normal-weight sample; Acute laboratory setting; Standing and activity-break arms are not equivalent to after-meal walking
- **Population mismatch:** Adjacent variant: posture and activity-break comparator, not every-meal walking.
