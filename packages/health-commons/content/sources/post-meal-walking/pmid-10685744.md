## Findings
- Walking counteracted delayed gastric emptying in 7 of 50 participants (14%); gastric emptying was not influenced in 11 participants (22%). [source_artifact:pmid-10685744]
- Baseline gastric emptying was normal in 28 participants (56%) and accelerated in 4 participants (8%). [source_artifact:pmid-10685744]
- Clinical symptoms and secondary manifestations were reported as not indicating the gastric-emptying changes. [source_artifact:pmid-10685744]

## Why it matters
This is direct postprandial walking evidence for gastric emptying, but it mainly supports a cautious mechanism claim in a population with diabetic delayed-emptying risk. [source_artifact:pmid-10685744]

## Extraction details
- **Population:** Consecutive patients with longstanding insulin-dependent diabetes mellitus; digestive physiology population with clinically relevant delayed gastric emptying risk.
- **Intervention or exposure:** A standardized 30-minute postprandial walk after a semisolid radiolabeled meal during scintigraphic gastric-emptying assessment.
- **Comparator or control:** Within-person meal-emptying assessment before/after walking and subgroups by baseline gastric-emptying status.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Single postprandial testing session with 90 minutes of supine scintigraphy before walking and 20 minutes after a 30-minute walk.
- **Endpoints:** scintigraphic gastric emptying; intragastric meal distribution; upper gastrointestinal symptom/clinical indicators
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Walking counteracted delayed gastric emptying in 7 of 50 participants (14%); gastric emptying was not influenced in 11 participants (22%). Baseline gastric emptying was normal in 28 participants (56%) and accelerated in 4 participants (8%). Clinical symptoms and secondary manifestations were reported as not indicating the gastric-emptying changes.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No explicit adverse events were extracted; the abstracted report emphasized absence of symptom indicators rather than a formal harms assessment.
- **Population mismatch:** Disease physiology population; findings should not be generalized to people without diabetic gastroparesis risk.
- **Directness to Walking After Every Meal:** direct_protocol
- **Claim-use boundary:** supports-protocol
- **Artifact rights status:** paywalled

## Limitations
- Population is longstanding insulin-dependent diabetes rather than generally healthy adults.
- Acute physiology study; not a long-term every-meal behavior trial.
- Only a small delayed-emptying subgroup appeared to respond.
- Symptom benefit was not established.
