This source is included for **sedentary-breaks-standing-micro-walks**.

**Findings:** In a small postmenopausal pilot, overall glucose and insulin iAUC were not significantly different from control, while exploratory second-meal glucose and vascular findings varied by interruption strategy.

**Why it matters:** It preserves mixed/null pilot evidence and vascular-function context for standing and walking interruption strategies.

**Potential experiment signals:** Postprandial glucose, Insulin, Flow-mediated dilation, Blood pressure, Feasibility.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mixed comparator context only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Sedentary, overweight or obese postmenopausal women.
- **Participant count:** 10 enrolled; 9 completed all four conditions in the published pilot.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Three sitting-interruption strategies: 2-minute standing every 20 minutes, 2-minute walking every hour, and 10-minute standing every hour.
- **Comparator/control:** Prolonged sitting control.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Four 5-hour laboratory conditions with standardized meals.
- **Endpoints:** Postprandial glucose, Insulin, Flow-mediated dilation, Blood pressure, Feasibility
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Net glucose and insulin iAUC were not significantly different from control overall; exploratory analyses suggested lower second-meal glucose for 2-minute standing every 20 minutes and hourly walking, and 10-minute standing improved flow-mediated dilation.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Extracted notes did not identify serious adverse events; an exploratory diastolic blood-pressure signal for walking was noted in the source context and should be treated cautiously.
- **Limitations:** Pilot sample; Postmenopausal high-risk population; Exploratory endpoints; Sitting interruptions rather than every-meal walking
- **Population mismatch:** Adjacent variant: modality comparison for sitting breaks in postmenopausal women, not direct protocol evidence.
