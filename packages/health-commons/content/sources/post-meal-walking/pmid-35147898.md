This source is included for **reviews-meta-analyses-mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Systematic review and meta-analysis comparing standing and light-intensity walking interruptions to prolonged sitting, finding walking produced stronger acute glucose and insulin benefits than sitting and standing comparators.

**Why it matters:** It directly separates light walking breaks from standing, a key comparator issue for post-meal walking claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** Postprandial glucose; Postprandial insulin; Triglycerides; Blood pressure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Light-intensity walking interruptions appear more metabolically active than standing, but the evidence is an acute sitting-break variant rather than walking after each meal.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Adults in acute prolonged-sitting interruption studies using standing or light-intensity walking.
- **Participant count:** Participant count not applicable or not resolved in the extracted record.
- **Participant count kind:** `not_resolved_in_extraction`
- **Intervention/exposure:** Standing or light-intensity walking breaks interrupting prolonged sitting.
- **Comparator/control:** Prolonged sitting and standing comparator conditions.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Acute laboratory prolonged-sitting interruption protocols.
- **Endpoints:** Postprandial glucose; Postprandial insulin; Triglycerides; Blood pressure
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Standing lowered glucose modestly (SMD about -0.31); light-intensity walking lowered glucose more strongly (SMD about -0.72) and insulin (SMD about -0.83); walking also outperformed standing for glucose and insulin in available comparisons. Triglyceride findings were limited or mixed.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse-event rate was extracted.
- **Limitations:** Only seven acute studies in extracted notes; Not meal-timed; Participant count not resolved; Triglyceride evidence limited
- **Population mismatch:** Acute sitting-break studies with mixed adults, not meal-specific walking after every meal.
- **Directness to Walking After Every Meal:** `same_mechanism`.
