This source is included for **safety-guidelines-medication-boundaries**.

**Findings:** Full premeal insulin increased hypoglycemia risk at all tested intensities; large insulin reductions reduced exercise-induced hypoglycemia by about 75% in the abstract-level result.

**Why it matters:** This is a core medication-boundary study showing why post-meal exercise advice cannot ignore prandial insulin.

**Potential experiment signals:** Exercise-associated hypoglycemia, End-exercise plasma glucose change, Insulin dose adjustment.

**Protocol takeaway:** Add a bright-line warning: people using mealtime insulin should not independently change dose for after-meal walking; they need individualized clinician guidance.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Eight male adults with type 1 diabetes treated with intensive basal-bolus insulin.
- **Participant count:** 8 (reported).
- **Intervention/exposure:** Postprandial exercise starting about 90 minutes after standardized breakfast under different premeal rapid-acting insulin reductions.
- **Comparator/control:** Full premeal insulin and alternate insulin-reduction conditions across exercise intensities/durations.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Single post-breakfast exercise sessions of different intensity/duration.
- **Endpoints:** Exercise-associated hypoglycemia, End-exercise plasma glucose change, Insulin dose adjustment
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Full premeal insulin increased hypoglycemia risk at all tested intensities; large insulin reductions reduced exercise-induced hypoglycemia by about 75% in the abstract-level result.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Foundational evidence that postprandial exercise in type 1 diabetes often requires premeal insulin reduction; not a user-facing medication algorithm.
- **Limitations:** Very small all-male sample; Lab exercise, not walking after every meal; Basal-bolus regimen and insulin types may not match current devices or analogs.
- **Population mismatch:** Direct for postprandial exercise safety in type 1 diabetes, but not direct for casual short walking after every meal.
- **Directness to Walking After Every Meal:** safety_boundary / adjacent_variant.
