## Findings
- Seven young adults with type 1 diabetes completed an acute breakfast beverage protocol in accessible summaries.
- A 15-minute light postprandial walk was reported to blunt the blood-glucose spike and overall glycemic response compared with sitting.
- The study is safety-only for Murph because it is small, acute, and insulin-treated.

## Why it matters
The dose is practically relevant, but the study is tiny and type-1-diabetes-specific, so it should shape hypoglycemia and insulin caveats instead of the main walking-after-meals claim.

## Potential experiment signals
- Postprandial blood glucose
- Glucose spike
- Overall glycemic response
- Hypoglycemia symptoms

## Protocol takeaway
Use as a safety-boundary example that short post-meal walks can materially affect glucose in type 1 diabetes; do not generalize to all users.

## Claim use
`safety-only`.
