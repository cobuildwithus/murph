This source is included for **reviews-meta-analyses-mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Review/context article on continuous glucose monitoring in people without diabetes, focused on variation in postprandial glycemic responses and interpretation of CGM-visible spikes.

**Why it matters:** It helps interpret CGM-visible postprandial variation in people without diabetes without implying that walking caused any change.

**Potential experiment signals:** CGM postprandial spikes; Glycemic variability; Time above range; Meal-response heterogeneity.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only for CGM interpretation in non-diabetic populations; it is not evidence that post-meal walking changes glucose.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
