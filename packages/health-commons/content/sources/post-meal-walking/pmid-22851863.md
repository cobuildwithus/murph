## Findings
- GERD was identified in 472 participants (10%); high-frequency physical activity was reported by 2463 participants (50%); obesity was present in 680 participants (14%). [source_artifact:pmid-22851863]
- In normal-weight and overweight participants, adjusted analyses found no decreased GERD risk with intermediate or high physical activity compared with low activity. [source_artifact:pmid-22851863]
- In obese individuals, intermediate physical activity was associated with decreased GERD occurrence versus low activity (adjusted OR 0.41; 95% CI 0.22 to 0.77). [source_artifact:pmid-22851863]

## Extraction details
- **Population:** Swedish population-based cross-sectional sample aged 40–79 years; 4910 of 6969 eligible randomly selected individuals participated.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Frequency of habitual physical activity categorized as high, intermediate, or low, stratified by body mass index.
- **Comparator or control:** Low physical activity frequency within normal-weight, overweight, and obese strata.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Cross-sectional population survey; no prospective intervention follow-up.
- **Endpoints:** GERD occurrence or symptoms; physical activity; obesity
- **Effect estimates or direction:** GERD was identified in 472 participants (10%); high-frequency physical activity was reported by 2463 participants (50%); obesity was present in 680 participants (14%). In normal-weight and overweight participants, adjusted analyses found no decreased GERD risk with intermediate or high physical activity compared with low activity. In obese individuals, intermediate physical activity was associated with decreased GERD occurrence versus low activity (adjusted OR 0.41; 95% CI 0.22 to 0.77).
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No walking-specific safety notes extracted.
- **Population mismatch:** General physical activity/obesity context, not a protocol intervention.
- **Directness to Walking After Every Meal:** background
- **Claim-use boundary:** context-only
- **Artifact rights status:** open_access
