Source key for extracted claims on this page: `source_artifact:pmid-30295296`.

## Findings
- Twenty minutes of moderate post-dinner treadmill walking reduced post-dinner glucose response and some variability metrics.
- MAGE was not significant and the study was dinner-only.

## Why it matters
It is close to post-meal walking but limited to dinner and a supervised/treadmill-style setting, so it remains adjacent rather than every-meal evidence.

## Potential experiment signals
- 2-hour postprandial glucose spike
- Mean glucose
- Peak glucose
- Glucose AUC
- 12-hour glucose variability
- MAGE
- Nocturnal hypoglycemia

## Protocol takeaway
Use as post-dinner adjacent evidence; avoid generalizing to every meal or all populations.

## Claim use
`context-only`.

## Extraction details
- **Source kind:** journal_article
- **Study design:** crossover (Self-controlled randomized crossover post-dinner treadmill-walking pilot study)
- **Participant count:** 29 (reported)
- **Population:** Chinese patients with type 2 diabetes.
- **Intervention or exposure:** 20 minutes of post-dinner treadmill walking at moderate intensity, around 40% heart-rate reserve.
- **Comparator or control:** Control days with normal activity but no unusual strenuous activity.
- **Duration or follow-up:** CGM over exercise and control days; post-dinner and overnight windows extracted.
- **Endpoints:** Post-dinner glucose spike, mean and peak glucose, cumulative glucose AUC, glucose variability, MAGE, nocturnal hypoglycemia.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Post-dinner exercise reduced 2-hour glucose spike, mean glucose, peak glucose, and 1-hour post-exercise cumulative glucose AUC; 12-hour SD/CV were lower, MAGE was not significant.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No nocturnal hypoglycemia was extracted on the exercise day.
- **Population mismatch:** Chinese T2D patients; post-dinner only; treadmill protocol rather than all meals/free-living walking.
- **Directness to Walking After Every Meal:** adjacent_variant
- **Artifact candidates / rights:** PMCID/open-access record available; metadata only here, no PDF committed. `pdfRightsStatus=open_access`.

## Limitations
- Post-dinner only.
- Pilot/self-controlled crossover design.
- Chinese T2D population.
- Not walking after every meal.
