This source is included for **secondary-metabolism-lipids-insulin-cgm**.

**Findings:** In men with type 2 diabetes, structured exercise improved 24-hour hyperglycemia more clearly than postmeal ADL bouts, though ADL reduced meal glucose and insulin iAUC.

**Why it matters:** Provides a key boundary: whole-day CGM improvement may need more than light postmeal ADL even when meal iAUC improves.

**Potential experiment signals:** 24-hour CGM glucose homeostasis, time in hyperglycemia above 10 mmol/L, postmeal glucose iAUC, plasma insulin incremental AUC.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent 24-hour CGM/insulin context; do not equate ADL bouts with the walking-after-every-meal protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Male patients with type 2 diabetes.

- **Participant count:** 20

- **Intervention/exposure:** Either one 45-minute moderate-intensity endurance exercise session or three 15-minute postmeal activities-of-daily-living bouts.

- **Comparator/control:** Sedentary control condition.

- **Duration/follow-up:** Three 3-day crossover periods with 24-hour CGM assessment.

- **Endpoints:** 24-hour CGM glucose homeostasis; time in hyperglycemia above 10 mmol/L; postmeal glucose iAUC; plasma insulin incremental AUC

- **Effect estimates or direction:** Time in hyperglycemia was reduced by moderate exercise (6h51 ± 1h04 control to 4h47 ± 1h02, p<0.001) but not significantly by ADL bouts (6h02 ± 1h16, p=0.67). Cumulative meal glucose iAUC was 35 ± 5% lower with exercise and 17 ± 6% lower with ADL; insulin iAUC was 33 ± 4% and 17 ± 5% lower, respectively.

- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse-event signal was extracted from the source record.

- **Limitations:** Male T2D-only sample; ADL bouts/strolling are not identical to a prescribed walking protocol; Short controlled crossover periods; Moderate exercise and ADL effects differed

- **Population mismatch:** Adjacent 24-hour CGM and insulin study in T2D; not direct after-every-meal walking evidence.

- **Directness to Walking After Every Meal:** adjacent_variant
