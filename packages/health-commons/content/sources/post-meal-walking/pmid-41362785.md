This source is included for **secondary-metabolism-lipids-insulin-cgm**.

**Findings:** A 10-week pre/post-meal timing trial found no group-by-time superiority on 24-hour CGM variability or biomarkers, with mixed timing-specific signals.

**Why it matters:** Recent direct timing comparator that preserves null/mixed 24-hour CGM and lipid/insulin findings.

**Potential experiment signals:** 24-hour CGM time in range, glucose variability, fasting glucose, insulin, triglycerides, blood pressure, time below range.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to avoid overclaiming that postmeal timing is always superior to premeal timing.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Young adults aged 18–35 years with overweight or obesity.

- **Participant count:** 34

- **Intervention/exposure:** At least 30 minutes of moderate aerobic exercise within 60 minutes before meals or within 0–90 minutes after meals, five times weekly.

- **Comparator/control:** Premeal exercise compared with postmeal exercise.

- **Duration/follow-up:** Ten-week exercise-timing intervention.

- **Endpoints:** 24-hour CGM time in range; glucose variability; fasting glucose; insulin; triglycerides; blood pressure; time below range

- **Effect estimates or direction:** There were no group-by-time interactions for glucose variability metrics, biomarkers, or cardiovascular parameters. Time effects improved time in range, fasting glucose, insulin, triglycerides, and blood pressure. Premeal exercise had lower hypoglycemic exposure and an exclusive triglyceride reduction; postmeal exercise showed a marginal insulin reduction in extracted records.

- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Lower time-below-range with premeal versus postmeal exercise suggests a hypoglycemia-related signal to monitor, but no clinical adverse-event rate was extracted.

- **Limitations:** Young overweight/obese adults, not broad population; Postmeal exercise was compared with premeal exercise rather than rest; Five-times-weekly prescription, not every meal; Exercise modality was aerobic and not necessarily walking only; No clear postmeal superiority on group-by-time outcomes

- **Population mismatch:** Adjacent timing trial; includes postmeal exercise but not a walking-after-every-meal protocol.

- **Directness to Walking After Every Meal:** adjacent_variant
