This source is included for **safety-guidelines-medication-boundaries**.

**Findings:** Protocol-only; no outcomes reported.

**Why it matters:** Useful for implementation design and safety measurement before extrapolating active breaks to post-meal walking.

**Potential experiment signals:** Time in range 3.9-10.0 mmol/L, CGM metrics, Activity levels, Insulin dose, Carbohydrate intake.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Sedentary adults with type 1 diabetes, planned n=118.
- **Participant count:** 118 (reported).
- **Intervention/exposure:** Active breaks: 3 minutes of self-paced walking every 30 minutes between 09:00 and 17:00, Monday through Friday, for 4 weeks.
- **Comparator/control:** Maintenance of habitual lifestyle.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 7-day baseline plus 4-week randomized intervention.
- **Endpoints:** Time in range 3.9-10.0 mmol/L, CGM metrics, Activity levels, Insulin dose, Carbohydrate intake
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Protocol-only; no outcomes reported.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Shows that serious T1D active-break studies monitor insulin dose and carbohydrate intake alongside CGM.
- **Limitations:** Protocol only; No results; Active breaks are not meal-triggered after every meal.
- **Population mismatch:** Adjacent active-break protocol, not direct protocol evidence.
