## Findings
- All post-breakfast exercise types improved glycemic response versus control.
- Walking alone or walking before resistance was better for peak reduction than resistance before walking in accessible extraction.

## Why it matters
It separates post-meal walking-like aerobic activity from other exercise sequences in T2D, while still being breakfast-only and small.

## Potential experiment signals
- Post-meal glycemic excursion
- Glucose iAUC 0-120 minutes
- Glucose iAUC 0-180 minutes
- Glycemic peak
- Exercise sequence

## Protocol takeaway
Use as same-mechanism context for post-breakfast exercise type and sequencing, not as every-meal protocol evidence.

## Claim use
`context-only`.

## Extraction details
- **Source kind:** journal_article
- **Study design:** crossover (Randomized repeated-measures crossover comparison of post-breakfast exercise types)
- **Participant count:** 8 (completed; 9 recruited)
- **Population:** Sedentary/inactive individuals with type 2 diabetes.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Five randomized post-breakfast conditions: sitting control, 30 minutes aerobic walking, 30 minutes aerobic+resistance, 30 minutes resistance+aerobic, or 15 minutes resistance exercise, all starting about 30 minutes after breakfast start.
- **Comparator or control:** Sitting control and alternate exercise types/sequences.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Acute post-breakfast monitoring, including 0-120 and 0-180 minute AUC windows.
- **Endpoints:** Post-meal glycemic excursion, glucose iAUC, and peak glucose reduction.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** All exercise conditions attenuated the post-meal glycemic excursion and iAUC versus control; greater peak reduction occurred with aerobic walking and aerobic-before-resistance compared with resistance-before-aerobic in accessible extraction.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event counts were extracted from accessible records.
- **Population mismatch:** T2D population is relevant, but study is breakfast-only, small, and compares exercise types rather than every-meal walking.
- **Directness to Walking After Every Meal:** same_mechanism
- **Artifact candidates / rights:** PMCID/open-access record available; metadata only here, no PDF committed. `pdfRightsStatus=open_access`.

## Limitations
- Small sample: 8 completed.
- Breakfast-only.
- Controlled lab setting.
- Multiple exercise sequences rather than a free-living every-meal walk.
