This source is included for **reviews-meta-analyses-mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Systematic review and meta-analysis showing that physical-activity breaks interrupting prolonged sitting lower glucose, insulin, and triacylglycerol outcomes, with a small glucose advantage over energy-matched continuous exercise.

**Why it matters:** It is a broad metabolic synthesis for intermittent activity that helps distinguish activity-break evidence from meal-specific walking evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** Glucose; Insulin; Triacylglycerol; BMI subgroup response.

**Protocol takeaway:** Intermittent activity breaks can improve acute metabolic markers, but this is not a meal-timed every-meal walking protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Adults aged 18 years or older, including metabolically healthy, overweight/obese, prediabetes, and type 2 diabetes populations.
- **Participant count:** 620
- **Participant count kind:** `outcome_specific_glucose_meta_analysis_total`
- **Intervention/exposure:** Physical-activity breaks interrupting prolonged sitting.
- **Comparator/control:** Prolonged sitting, and in some analyses energy-matched continuous exercise.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Acute prolonged-sitting interruption protocols; 37 studies included in meta-analysis.
- **Endpoints:** Glucose; Insulin; Triacylglycerol; BMI subgroup response
- **Effect estimates or direction:** PA breaks reduced glucose (SMD=-0.54, 95% CI -0.70 to -0.37), insulin (SMD=-0.56, 95% CI -0.74 to -0.38), and triacylglycerol (SMD=-0.26, 95% CI -0.44 to -0.09) versus sitting; glucose was also lower versus energy-matched continuous exercise (SMD=-0.26, 95% CI -0.50 to -0.02).
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No batch-level adverse-event rate was extracted.
- **Limitations:** Acute lab sitting-break protocols; Mixed populations; Not meal-timed; Possible publication bias for triacylglycerol outcome
- **Population mismatch:** Mixed adult populations and sitting-break interventions, not walking after each meal.
- **Directness to Walking After Every Meal:** `same_mechanism`.
