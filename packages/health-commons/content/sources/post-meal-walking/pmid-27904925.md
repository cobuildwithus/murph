This source is included for **sedentary-breaks-standing-micro-walks**.

**Findings:** In adults with type 2 diabetes, a sit-less/light-activity pattern involving standing and walking improved glycemic control and insulin sensitivity versus prolonged sitting.

**Why it matters:** It provides lower-intensity daily activity context in type 2 diabetes and separates structured exercise from sitting reduction.

**Potential experiment signals:** 24-hour glucose, Insulin sensitivity, Postprandial glycemia.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for mechanistic and burden context only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Adults with type 2 diabetes.
- **Participant count:** Participant count not resolved in the extracted record.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Sit-less/light-activity condition using standing and light walking across the day.
- **Comparator/control:** Structured exercise and usual sitting control conditions.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Multi-day free-living or semi-free-living crossover periods with glycemic and insulin-sensitivity assessment.
- **Endpoints:** 24-hour glucose, Insulin sensitivity, Postprandial glycemia
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Breaking sitting with light activities improved glycemic control and insulin sensitivity relative to prolonged sitting in extracted notes; comparison to structured exercise is contextual.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse-event signal was extracted from available metadata.
- **Limitations:** Type 2 diabetes clinical population; Composite sit-less condition includes standing and walking; Not specific to walking after every meal; Sample count not resolved here
- **Population mismatch:** Adjacent variant: light-activity sitting reduction, not every-meal walking.
