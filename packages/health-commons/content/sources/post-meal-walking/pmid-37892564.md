This source is included for **reviews-meta-analyses-mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Systematic review and meta-analysis of postprandial exercise in adults with overweight/obesity and type 2 diabetes, reporting lower glucose AUC and 24-hour mean glucose versus no-exercise controls.

**Why it matters:** It is a recent population-specific synthesis but should remain context because many included trials and doses differ from a low-burden every-meal walk.

**Potential experiment signals:** Glucose AUC; 24-hour mean glucose; Exercise duration; Timing after meal; T2D subgroup.

**Protocol takeaway:** Postprandial exercise lowered glycemic outcomes in overweight/obesity and T2D studies, especially with sufficient duration, but general-population and brief-walk claims need primary evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Adults with overweight/obesity or type 2 diabetes; average BMI about 30 kg/m2, age range 21-69 years, majority male in included studies.
- **Participant count:** 516
- **Participant count kind:** `reported_total_across_included_studies`
- **Intervention/exposure:** Postprandial exercise, predominantly aerobic, performed after meals.
- **Comparator/control:** No-exercise control.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Mostly acute postprandial exercise trials with exercise durations from 3 to 60 minutes and timing 20 to 150 minutes after meals.
- **Endpoints:** Glucose AUC; 24-hour mean glucose; Exercise duration; Timing after meal; T2D subgroup
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Postprandial exercise reduced glucose AUC (Hedges g=-0.317, SE 0.057, p<0.05) and 24-hour mean glucose (Hedges g=-0.328, SE 0.062, p<0.05); effects varied by duration, timing, and T2D status.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse-event rate was extracted.
- **Limitations:** Heterogeneous protocols; Mostly acute studies; Population limited to overweight/obesity and T2D; Not a fixed every-meal walking rule
- **Population mismatch:** Overweight/obesity and T2D populations; not healthy normal-weight adults or pregnant/T1D groups; not always walking or every meal.
