This source is included for **secondary-metabolism-lipids-insulin-cgm**.

**Findings:** A direct randomized crossover postmeal walking trial in older CVD-risk adults found no beneficial effects on lipemia, glycemia, insulinemia, inflammation, or oxidative-stress markers.

**Why it matters:** Direct null protocol evidence supporting the guardrail that after-meal walking should not be claimed to improve postprandial lipemia, insulin, inflammatory, oxidative-stress, or broader cardiometabolic endpoints by default.

**Potential experiment signals:** postprandial lipemia, glycemia, insulinemia, inflammatory markers, oxidative stress markers, endothelial adhesion markers.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not claim secondary lipid, insulin, inflammation, or oxidative-stress benefits as established for walking after meals.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Older adults with a cardiovascular disease risk phenotype (about 70 years old; BMI about 30).

- **Participant count:** 26

- **Intervention/exposure:** Thirty minutes of moderate postmeal walking at about 4.6 km/h after Western high-fat or Mediterranean-type meals.

- **Comparator/control:** The same meals followed by rest.

- **Duration/follow-up:** Acute postprandial blood sampling after standardized meals.

- **Endpoints:** postprandial lipemia; glycemia; insulinemia; inflammatory markers; oxidative stress markers; endothelial adhesion markers

- **Effect estimates or direction:** Moderate postmeal walking had no relevant beneficial effects versus rest on postprandial lipemia, glycemia, insulinemia, or selected markers of inflammation and oxidative stress. Meal type affected triglycerides, glucose, and insulin; IL-6 was higher after walking.

- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No serious walking adverse-event signal was extracted; IL-6 was higher after walking and should be treated as a biomarker finding rather than a clinical adverse event.

- **Limitations:** Older CVD-risk phenotype may not generalize to healthier adults; Acute laboratory design; Meal-type interaction and high-fat meal context; Thirty-minute moderate walking may not match all practical walking-after-meal variants; Direct protocol-adjacent null result should temper claims

- **Population mismatch:** Direct postmeal walking trial in older CVD-risk adults; protocol-supporting guardrail evidence because the null/mixed result directly tempers secondary-metabolism claims; the older CVD-risk phenotype remains a generalizability caveat.

- **Directness to Walking After Every Meal:** direct_protocol
