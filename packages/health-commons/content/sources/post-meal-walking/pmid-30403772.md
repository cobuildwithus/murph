This source is included for **sedentary-breaks-standing-micro-walks**.

**Findings:** This acute study compared standing and light-walking breaks during prolonged sitting in older South Asian and White European adults, making it an important population-boundary comparator source.

**Why it matters:** It adds ethnicity and older-adult boundary context for standing-versus-walking interruptions.

**Potential experiment signals:** Postprandial glucose, Insulin, Triglycerides, Blood pressure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for comparator and population-boundary context only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Older South Asian and White European adults.
- **Participant count:** 60 older adults; 30 South Asian and 30 White European participants.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Standing or self-paced light walking for 5 minutes every 30 minutes during a prolonged-sitting day.
- **Comparator/control:** Prolonged sitting.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Acute 7.5-hour laboratory postprandial protocol.
- **Endpoints:** Postprandial glucose, Insulin, Triglycerides, Blood pressure
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Standing and light walking were compared with prolonged sitting for metabolic outcomes; extracted notes retain it as a direct standing-versus-walking comparator with population-boundary value. Exact estimates were not restated here.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse-event signal was extracted from available metadata.
- **Limitations:** Older ethnic-subgroup sample; Acute laboratory sitting-break protocol; Not an every-meal walking prescription
- **Population mismatch:** Adjacent variant: older adult sitting-break comparator, not direct protocol evidence.
