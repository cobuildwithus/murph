This source is included for **secondary-metabolism-lipids-insulin-cgm**.

**Findings:** A real-life T2D crossover study found walking after meals lowered mean CGM glucose versus control in a subgroup, with effects varying by phenotype.

**Why it matters:** Recent high-relevance CGM evidence with an explicit walking-after-each-meal condition and personalization context.

**Potential experiment signals:** CGM mean glucose, other CGM metrics, phenotype-specific intervention response.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only until synthesis; avoid generalizing subgroup T2D results to all users.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Persons with type 2 diabetes.

- **Participant count:** 40

- **Intervention/exposure:** Lifestyle interventions including low-carbohydrate diet, Mediterranean diet, walking after each meal, and active-day hourly exercise bouts.

- **Comparator/control:** Repeated control CGM periods and other lifestyle-intervention periods.

- **Duration/follow-up:** Eleven CGM periods of 4 days each, including repeated control and lifestyle-intervention periods.

- **Endpoints:** CGM mean glucose; other CGM metrics; phenotype-specific intervention response

- **Effect estimates or direction:** Among participants not restricting carbohydrates at baseline, low-carbohydrate diet, walking after meals, and active-day exercise lowered mean glucose versus control; extracted estimates included walking after meals at -0.28 mmol/L (95% CI -0.46 to -0.10) versus control. Effects varied by phenotype/diabetype.

- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse-event signal was extracted from the source record.

- **Limitations:** T2D sample; Short 4-day intervention periods; Multiple lifestyle conditions complicate simple protocol interpretation; Phenotype subgroup/personalization analyses; Canonical ledger classifies as adjacent/context-only despite an explicit walking-after-meals condition

- **Population mismatch:** Explicit walking-after-each-meal condition in T2D, but canonical batch boundary keeps this source context-only pending protocol synthesis.

- **Directness to Walking After Every Meal:** adjacent_variant
