This source is included for **refeeding, electrolytes, and thiamine safety**.

**Findings:** In a hunger-strike hospital cohort, available abstract data described 37 hospital episodes and 30 refeeding cases; gradual refeeding, fluid restriction, and micronutrient substitution were used, and serious complication rates were reported as low when recommendations were followed.

**Why it matters:** Closest cohort-style evidence for prolonged voluntary fasting/refeeding management, preserving electrolyte and edema outcomes in a hunger-strike population.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:serum-phosphate, biomarker:serum-potassium, biomarker:serum-magnesium, biomarker:fluid-balance, biomarker:heart-rhythm, biomarker:refeeding-syndrome-symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent support for gradual, supervised refeeding after prolonged voluntary starvation, not for routine short-fast advice.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
