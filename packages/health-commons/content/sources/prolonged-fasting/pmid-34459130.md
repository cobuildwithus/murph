This source is included for **safety and adverse events**.

**Findings:** A single-arm clinical trial reported reductions in metabolic-syndrome risk markers and increased anti-aging biomarkers during a 5-day water-only fast, with no obvious toxicity in extracted text.

**Why it matters:** Adjacent 5-day water-only fasting trial reporting no obvious toxicity and extensive biomarker shifts.

**Potential experiment signals:** body weight, waist circumference, blood pressure, insulin, IGF-1, immune populations, metabolomics, toxicity

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent safety/biomarker context only; do not generalize “without toxicity” to unsupervised or higher-risk users.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** adjacent_variant.

**Population and exposure:** Normal-weight individuals; extracted text reports 45 enrolled and 41 completed specified repeated serum/urine sampling. Exposure/intervention: Five-day water-only fasting followed by refeeding/follow-up. Comparator/control: Within-person pre/post measurements. Duration/follow-up: 5-day water-only fast, with measurements during fasting and after refeeding/follow-up..

**Effect direction or estimates:** Extracted text reports metabolic-syndrome and age-related marker reductions and states no obvious toxicity; 41 participants completed the core repeated sampling.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** No obvious toxicity reported in extracted source text; immune/hormone shifts require cautious interpretation.

**Limitations:** Single-arm, normal-weight sample, 5-day duration beyond 72 hours, limited adverse-event reporting extraction.

**Population mismatch:** Adjacent because it is a 5-day water-only fast and normal-weight clinical-trial cohort rather than a 24-72 hour community protocol.
