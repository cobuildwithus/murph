This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** The study reported insulin resistance after a 72-hour fast associated with impaired AS160 phosphorylation and accumulation of lipid and glycogen in human skeletal muscle.

**Why it matters:** This source preserves a direct 72-hour negative/mixed insulin-action finding for the upper end of the protocol dose.

**Potential experiment signals:** insulin sensitivity, blood glucose, fasting insulin, ketones, skeletal muscle lipid, glycogen.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not present the 72-hour fast as acutely improving insulin sensitivity; fasting-associated peripheral insulin resistance is a documented direct finding.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
