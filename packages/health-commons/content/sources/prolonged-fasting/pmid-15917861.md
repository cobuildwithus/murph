This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** During 72 hours of fasting, glucose, insulin, and leptin decreased, while serum adiponectin did not show a significant overall time-course change.

**Why it matters:** This preserves an important null finding: not every metabolic hormone or adipokine improves or rises during a 72-hour fast.

**Potential experiment signals:** blood glucose, fasting insulin, leptin, adiponectin, body weight.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not claim that 72-hour fasting acutely raises adiponectin; this source found no significant adiponectin time-course change despite other fasting-marker changes.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
