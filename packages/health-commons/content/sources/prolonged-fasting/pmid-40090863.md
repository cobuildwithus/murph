This source is included for **refeeding, electrolytes, and thiamine safety**.

**Findings:** The AuSPEN consensus recommends thiamin/multivitamin support and regular electrolyte monitoring for people at risk; available abstract information also indicates a more liberal feeding-start approach with close electrolyte monitoring, showing guideline differences from older conservative starts.

**Why it matters:** Recent consensus source to compare with NICE/ASPEN and preserve guideline differences for refeeding safety boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:thiamine-status, biomarker:refeeding-syndrome-symptoms, biomarker:electrolyte-panel, biomarker:refeeding-risk.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to preserve that guidelines differ on calorie initiation, while agreement remains strong around thiamine/multivitamins and electrolyte monitoring for at-risk patients.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
