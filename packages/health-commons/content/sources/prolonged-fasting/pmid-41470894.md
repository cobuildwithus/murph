**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-41470894-endocrine-uncertainty-and-risks` — The review concludes that prolonged fasting activates conserved endocrine mechanisms with plausible cardiometabolic relevance but clinical translation remains speculative; it flags potential risks including transient hypogonadism, relative hypothyroidism, hypercortisolemia, orthostatic intolerance, electrolyte imbalances, catabolic lean-mass loss, and refeeding challenges.

**Why it matters:** This review is useful for balancing mechanistic enthusiasm with clinical uncertainty and safety cautions.

**Potential experiment signals:** cortisol, thyroid markers, sex hormones, orthostatic symptoms, electrolytes, lean mass, refeeding tolerance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context and safety-boundary evidence; avoid translating endocrine mechanisms into clinical efficacy claims.
