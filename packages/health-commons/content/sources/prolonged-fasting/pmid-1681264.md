This source is included for **Clinical/residential supervised fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** In 53 RA patients, the fasting-plus-vegetarian-diet arm reported improvements in joint symptoms and inflammatory markers, but the intervention was a disease-specific diet sequence.

**Why it matters:** A classic RA trial showing why disease-specific fasting-plus-diet evidence must be separated from general protocol evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** joint symptoms, CRP, ESR, function, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent RA evidence only; do not cite for standalone wellness fasting claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Patients with rheumatoid arthritis.
- **Intervention/exposure:** 7–10 day subtotal fast followed by gluten-free vegan diet then lactovegetarian diet.
- **Comparator/control:** Control diet/usual diet group.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Dietary intervention and follow-up over one year.
- **Endpoints:** tender joints, swollen joints, pain, morning stiffness, ESR, CRP, function
- **Effect estimate or direction:** Reported improvements in several RA clinical and inflammatory endpoints in the diet group over follow-up.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Adverse-event extraction not available in this batch.
- **Limitations:** RA disease-treatment study; fasting bundled with major long-term dietary changes; not isolated 24–72 hour fasting.
- **Population mismatch:** Autoimmune disease population and year-long diet sequence.
- **Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** adjacent_variant
