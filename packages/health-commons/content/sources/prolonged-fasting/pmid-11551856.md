This source is included for **metabolic timecourse and mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Accessible abstract text states that indirect calorimetry and tracer methods measured fatty acid oxidation, systemic and regional FFA/glycerol kinetics, and splanchnic triglyceride release after 60 hours fasting; specific numeric outcome estimates were not extracted.

**Why it matters:** A direct 60-hour human fasting lipid-flux study helps interpret fat oxidation and FFA signals in the protocol window.

**Potential experiment signals:** free fatty acids, glycerol, fat oxidation, triglyceride release, regional lipid flux

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for mechanistic lipid-flux context during 60-hour fasting, not as evidence of benefit.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Extraction boundaries:** Directness: `direct_protocol`. Population mismatch: Healthy/adult men only; does not directly generalize to women, older adults, obesity, or clinical-risk groups. Limitations: Very small sample; male-only; mechanistic lipid flux rather than clinical outcomes. Safety notes: No adverse-event signal extracted from accessible abstract text.
