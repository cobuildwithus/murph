This source is included for **eating-disorder and restriction-risk boundary**.

**Findings:** This 5-year population-based cohort evaluated predictors of disordered eating among overweight adolescents.

**Why it matters:** Important for population mismatch: adolescents with overweight can still have disordered-eating risk, so BMI-based eligibility alone is not enough.

**Potential experiment signals:** adolescent safety boundaries, disordered eating behaviors, prevention and treatment.

**Protocol takeaway:** BMI category alone should not clear a person for fasting experiments.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness and caveat:** Overweight adolescent disordered-eating predictors, not direct adult prolonged-fasting evidence. Not fasting-specific and no causal protocol claim.
