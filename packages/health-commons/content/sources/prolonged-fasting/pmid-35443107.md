This source is included for **adjacent fasting variants and terminology**.

**Findings:** At 12 months, weight loss was -8.0 kg with TRE plus calorie restriction and -6.3 kg with calorie restriction alone; the net difference was not statistically significant, and adverse events did not substantially differ between groups.

**Why it matters:** Large TRE RCT showing the importance of separating timing restriction from calorie restriction and prolonged fasting.

**Potential experiment signals:** Body weight; Waist circumference; Body composition; Blood pressure; Metabolic risk factors; Adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a no-clear-advantage adjacent TRE comparator source.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
