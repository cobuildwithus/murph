This source is included for **refeeding, electrolytes, and thiamine safety**.

**Findings:** The review describes multidisciplinary monitoring and refeeding-risk management for hunger strikers after prolonged starvation, including electrolyte/thiamine/cardiac safety concerns.

**Why it matters:** Human prolonged voluntary fasting analogue; useful for monitoring/referral boundaries and ethical/medical context, not routine 24-72 hour fasting efficacy.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:serum-phosphate, biomarker:serum-potassium, biomarker:serum-magnesium, biomarker:thiamine-status, biomarker:heart-rhythm, biomarker:refeeding-syndrome-symptoms, biomarker:refeeding-risk.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent prolonged-voluntary-starvation safety evidence, especially for escalation when a fast extends beyond planned duration.
