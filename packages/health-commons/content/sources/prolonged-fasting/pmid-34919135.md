This source is included for **adjacent fasting variants and terminology**.

**Findings:** The umbrella review included 11 meta-analyses comprising 130 randomized clinical trials and found six statistically significant associations with moderate to high quality of evidence, especially for modified alternate-day fasting and anthropometric/cardiometabolic outcomes in adults with overweight or obesity.

**Why it matters:** Earlier umbrella review for certainty grading and older foundational IF meta-analysis coverage.

**Potential experiment signals:** Body weight; BMI; Cardiometabolic risk factors; Adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as high-level adjacent IF landscape evidence; preserve the modified-ADF specificity.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
