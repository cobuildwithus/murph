This source is included for **eating-disorder and restriction-risk boundary**.

**Findings:** In a 52-week supervised adolescent obesity RCT (N=141), depression, eating-disorder, and binge-eating symptoms improved overall, while some participants required extra mental-health or disordered-eating support.

**Why it matters:** Adolescent randomized evidence with eating-disorder, binge-eating, and depression endpoints; adjacent because it is obesity treatment, not wellness prolonged fasting.

**Potential experiment signals:** mood and anxiety, eating disorder symptoms, binge eating, adolescent safety boundaries.

**Protocol takeaway:** Clinical screening and active monitoring are essential when restriction is used in vulnerable groups.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness and caveat:** Adjacent pediatric obesity treatment with VLED/IER/CER, not unsupervised 24–72-hour prolonged fasting in adults. Adjacent supervised obesity treatment; not direct evidence for a consumer prolonged-fast protocol.
