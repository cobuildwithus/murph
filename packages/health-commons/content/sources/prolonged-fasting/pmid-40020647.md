This source is included for **FMD/VLCD and modified-fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** The trial reported reduced liver fat and improved MRI-derived fibrosis/inflammation markers in patients with type 2 diabetes and hepatic steatosis; detailed effect extraction and participant count were not verified here.

**Why it matters:** Clinical organ-imaging context for FMD, with strong population mismatch to general fasting.

**Potential experiment signals:** imaging:liver-fat, imaging:liver-fibrosis-inflammation, safety:type-2-diabetes-monitoring.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only/clinical boundary; not direct support for zero-calorie 24–72 hour fasting.

**Directness and limitations:** Directness is `adjacent_variant`. Permission-required source; diabetes/steatosis population; FMD program; sample and effect details not fully extracted. Population mismatch: T2D with hepatic steatosis under clinical trial conditions.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
