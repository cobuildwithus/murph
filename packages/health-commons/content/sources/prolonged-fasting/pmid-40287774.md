This source is included for **FMD/VLCD and modified-fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** The FMD RCT meta-analysis reported favorable effects on several cardiovascular risk factors including HbA1c, IGF-1, and blood pressure, while calling for more long-term and safety research.

**Why it matters:** Aggregate FMD evidence belongs in the FMD boundary group, not in direct prolonged-fasting claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:hba1c, biomarker:igf-1, vitals:systolic-blood-pressure, vitals:diastolic-blood-pressure, biomarker:lipids.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only FMD synthesis; do not apply to 24–72 hour zero-calorie fasting without direct evidence.

**Directness and limitations:** Directness is `adjacent_variant`. FMD-specific pooled evidence; trial heterogeneity and long-term safety limitations. Population mismatch: FMD RCTs rather than target protocol exposure.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
