This source is included for **FMD/VLCD and modified-fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** In adults with type 2 diabetes, intermittent and continuous energy restriction produced comparable glycemic-control effects; medication adjustment was needed to manage hypoglycemia risk, especially around restricted days.

**Why it matters:** Directly relevant safety boundary for people using insulin or sulfonylureas who consider fasting-like restriction.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:hba1c, biomarker:body-weight, safety:hypoglycemia, medication:glucose-lowering-medication-adjustment.

**Protocol takeaway:** Safety-only source for diabetes/medication screening; do not use as general prolonged-fasting efficacy evidence.

**Directness and limitations:** Directness is `adjacent_variant`. Clinical diabetes population, calorie restriction not zero-calorie fasting, supervised medication adjustments. Population mismatch: Type 2 diabetes treatment context, not healthy-adult self-directed fasting.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
