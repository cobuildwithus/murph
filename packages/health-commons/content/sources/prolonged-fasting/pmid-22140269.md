This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** During a 48-hour fast in healthy women and men, the study directly measured lean-tissue triglyceride content and whole-body/hepatic carbohydrate, lipid, and energy metabolism, with reported sex-specific lipid-handling responses.

**Why it matters:** Direct 48-hour human metabolism source with sex-stratified lean-tissue lipid endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** liver triglyceride, skeletal muscle triglyceride, plasma fatty acids, glucose metabolism, energy metabolism, sex differences.

**Protocol takeaway:** A 48-hour fast can produce sex-differentiated lipid-mobilization and lean-tissue triglyceride responses; use for biomarker/context claims, not clinical benefit claims.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Study design and directness:** Acute human fasting physiology study using spectroscopy/tracer/calorimetry-style metabolic endpoints.. Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours): `direct_protocol`.

**Comparator/control:** Baseline/postabsorptive sex-stratified measurements during early fasting

**Duration/follow-up:** 48-hour fast

**Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event data extracted.

**Limitations:** Physiology endpoints, not clinical outcomes; detailed effect estimates were not re-extracted from the full article in this batch.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy participants only; sex-specific findings may not generalize to obesity, diabetes, older age, or medication use.
