This source is included for **diabetes, medication, and hypoglycemia safety**.

**Findings:** This small comparative study evaluated glycemic effects of glimepiride, repaglinide, and insulin glargine during Ramadan fasting, with a non-fasting control group, making it medication-regimen safety context.

**Why it matters:** Older medication-comparison trial covering sulfonylurea, meglitinide, and insulin during Ramadan fasting.

**Potential experiment signals:** hypoglycemia, glycemic control, insulin or secretagogue exposure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for the boundary that insulin and secretagogue regimens require individualized medical adjustment around fasting.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
