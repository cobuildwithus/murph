This source is included for **Clinical/residential supervised fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** In a 13-patient randomized pilot, 48-hour fasting around chemotherapy showed hematologic-toxicity signals, but the population and chemotherapy co-intervention make it adjacent evidence only.

**Why it matters:** Duration overlaps 24–72 hours, but the clinical oncology setting prevents direct wellness extrapolation.

**Potential experiment signals:** hematologic toxicity, DNA damage, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as oncology safety/tolerability boundary, not direct protocol efficacy.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** HER2-negative breast cancer patients receiving neoadjuvant/adjuvant chemotherapy.
- **Intervention/exposure:** 48-hour water-only fasting around chemotherapy.
- **Comparator/control:** Usual diet/control arm during chemotherapy.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 24 hours before and 24 hours after chemotherapy; within 48-hour fasting range but oncology/co-intervention context.
- **Endpoints:** hematologic toxicity, DNA damage, chemotherapy tolerance, adverse events
- **Effect estimate or direction:** Pilot reported signals suggesting mitigation of hematologic toxicity; small sample and oncology context.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Safety/tolerance monitored; no protocol-general adverse-event rate extracted.
- **Limitations:** Very small pilot RCT; chemotherapy co-intervention; disease population.
- **Population mismatch:** Cancer treatment setting despite duration overlap.
- **Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** adjacent_variant
