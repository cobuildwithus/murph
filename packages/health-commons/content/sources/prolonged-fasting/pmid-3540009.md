This source is included for **metabolic timecourse and mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Accessible article abstract text reported greater palmitate flux on day 4 than day 1 despite controlled hormones/glucose, and a greater lipolytic response to epinephrine after fasting; authors concluded fasting lipolysis is less suppressed by insulin and more stimulated by epinephrine.

**Why it matters:** Provides direct mechanistic evidence for increased lipolysis and altered insulin/epinephrine control during fasting.

**Potential experiment signals:** palmitate flux, free fatty acids, epinephrine response, insulin suppression

**Protocol takeaway:** Fasting can make lipolysis less insulin-suppressed and more epinephrine-responsive; use as mechanism, not benefit.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Extraction boundaries:** Directness: `partly_direct_protocol`. Population mismatch: Healthy physiology under clamp/infusion conditions may not represent unsupervised fasting. Limitations: Small mechanistic study; 84-hour endpoint exceeds the 72-hour target; infusion/clamp setting. Safety notes: No adverse-event signal extracted.
