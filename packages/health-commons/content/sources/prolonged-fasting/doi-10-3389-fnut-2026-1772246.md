This source is included for **metabolic timecourse and mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Across 32 studies, the review reported duration-dependent lipid effects: LDL and total cholesterol increased overall; HDL decreased overall but not in the <=3-day subgroup; triglycerides were mixed, with reductions reported in shorter fasts and increases in longer fasts. Publication-bias concerns were noted for LDL and total cholesterol.

**Why it matters:** Helps set boundaries around transient lipid changes and duration thresholds during fasting.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL cholesterol, HDL cholesterol, total cholesterol, triglycerides, fasting duration

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to contextualize lipid biomarker direction and duration thresholds; do not use as the main direct efficacy backbone.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction boundaries:** Directness: `same_mechanism`. Population mismatch: Population and protocols vary across studies; should not override primary 24-72 hour direct studies. Limitations: Aggregate review; included study heterogeneity and publication-bias signals limit direct protocol claims; not a single 24-72 hour intervention trial. Safety notes: No acute adverse-event extraction; lipid changes are biomarker context rather than safety-event reporting.
