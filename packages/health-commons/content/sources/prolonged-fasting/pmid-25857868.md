**Findings:** The review grouped alternate-day fasting, modified fasting, time-restricted feeding, and religious fasting as distinct intermittent-fasting variants and emphasized that human evidence was heterogeneous and often extrapolated from small studies or non-protocol-specific contexts.

**Why it matters:** Foundational human IF review with definitions and early evidence by variant class.
