This source is included for **safety and adverse events**.

**Findings:** In 768 qualifying visits, the chart review reported that most adverse events were mild or moderate and commonly recognized fasting reactions. The chart review recorded grade 3 events, one grade 4 event, and serious adverse events, while reporting no grade 5 events/deaths in the extracted source text. The evidence is adjacent to community 24-72 hour fasting because it reflects residential medical supervision and often longer fast durations.

**Why it matters:** Core adverse-event chart-review source for medically supervised water-only fasting.

**Potential experiment signals:** adverse events, CTCAE grade, serious adverse events, headache, nausea, fatigue, presyncope, hypertension

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as high-priority safety-boundary evidence: supervision matters and adverse events can occur even when most events are mild.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** adjacent_variant.

**Population and exposure:** 768 qualifying visits by adults in a residential medically supervised water-only fasting program. Exposure/intervention: Medically supervised water-only fasting for at least 2 consecutive days followed by supervised refeeding. Comparator/control: No external comparator. Duration/follow-up: Fasts of at least 2 days; many durations exceeded 72 hours; refeeding period typically followed..

**Effect direction or estimates:** Among 768 qualifying visits, most recorded adverse events were mild and known fasting reactions, but grade 3, grade 4, and serious events were also recorded; no grade 5/deaths were reported in the extracted abstract/source text.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** Headache, nausea, fatigue, musculoskeletal pain, dyspepsia, presyncope, hypertension, and graded adverse events including rare serious events.

**Limitations:** Retrospective chart review in a residential medical program; visits rather than unique participants; duration and supervision differ from community use.

**Population mismatch:** Adjacent variant because clinical supervision and longer fasts differ from an unsupervised 24-72 hour protocol.
