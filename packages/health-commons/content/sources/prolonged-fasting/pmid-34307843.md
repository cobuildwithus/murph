This source is included for **safety and adverse events**.

**Findings:** The case report describes acute hyponatremia after fasting, with water intake, hypovolemia-mediated AVP, postpartum/lactation context, and NSAID exposure discussed as contributors.

**Why it matters:** Concrete case for hyponatremia and overhydration/solute cautions.

**Potential experiment signals:** acute hyponatremia, serum sodium, water intake, NSAID exposure, postpartum/lactation risk

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a safety boundary for excessive water intake, low solute intake, lactation/postpartum status, and NSAID context.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** adjacent_variant.

**Population and exposure:** One 24-year-old postpartum woman after a religious fast. Exposure/intervention: Religious fasting with high water intake and NSAID exposure in the postpartum context. Comparator/control: None. Duration/follow-up: Religious fast; exact fasting duration not extracted in this batch..

**Effect direction or estimates:** The case describes acute hyponatremia after religious fasting, with extracted text noting rapid sodium drop to 124 mEq/L after about 4 L water intake and contextual postpartum/NSAID factors.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** Acute symptomatic hyponatremia risk in a susceptible context.

**Limitations:** Single case; specific postpartum and NSAID/hydration factors; not a general fasting incidence estimate.

**Population mismatch:** Religious fast and postpartum physiology differ from general wellness fasting.
