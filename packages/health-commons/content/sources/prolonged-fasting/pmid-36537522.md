This source is included for **diabetes, medication, and hypoglycemia safety**.

**Findings:** The ISPAD guideline addresses Ramadan and other religious fasting by young people with diabetes, emphasizing pediatric risk, monitoring, and insulin/diabetes-management boundaries.

**Why it matters:** Useful pediatric/high-risk boundary source; mostly population-mismatched to adult wellness fasting but important for exclusions.

**Potential experiment signals:** pediatric hypoglycemia, CGM glucose, ketoacidosis, insulin management.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only context supporting exclusion or specialist-supervision language for minors with diabetes.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
