This source is included for **adjacent fasting variants and terminology**.

**Findings:** The meta-analysis concluded that TRE has moderate-to-high evidence as a fat-loss strategy, with subgroup signals suggesting 6–8-hour eating windows may be effective; it also noted potential lean-mass reduction and LDL-C increase, though not judged to be major safety concerns in that source.

**Why it matters:** TRE-specific synthesis for distinguishing daily eating-window restriction from prolonged 24–72 h fasts.

**Potential experiment signals:** Fat mass; Body weight; Lean mass; LDL-C; Eating-window duration.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent TRE body-composition context, not as direct prolonged-fasting evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
