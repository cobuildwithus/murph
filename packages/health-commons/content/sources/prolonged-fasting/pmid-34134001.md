This source is included for **refeeding, electrolytes, and thiamine safety**.

**Findings:** Across 35 observational studies, reported RFS incidence ranged widely (0–62%) and refeeding hypophosphatemia ranged widely as well, reflecting heterogeneous definitions, settings, and bias; this should prevent confident single-number risk estimates.

**Why it matters:** Systematic review/meta-analysis documenting variable incidence and definition problems; important for avoiding overconfident risk estimates.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:serum-phosphate, biomarker:mortality, biomarker:refeeding-syndrome-symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to explicitly caveat any incidence discussion and avoid applying pooled clinical rates to wellness fasting.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
