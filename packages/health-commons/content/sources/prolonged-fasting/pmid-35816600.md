This source is included for **diabetes, medication, and hypoglycemia safety**.

**Findings:** The Endotext chapter summarizes Ramadan diabetes risks and management, including pre-fast education, risk stratification, glucose monitoring frequency, hydration/exercise cautions, medication review, and break-fast thresholds for low or very high glucose or symptoms.

**Why it matters:** Open clinical review summarizing Ramadan risk categories and key observational datasets such as EPIDIAR and CREED.

**Potential experiment signals:** blood glucose monitoring, hypoglycemia, hyperglycemia, dehydration symptoms, ketoacidosis.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a high-priority safety-only source for practical monitoring and stop-fast boundaries in diabetes.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
