This source is included for **refeeding, electrolytes, and thiamine safety**.

**Findings:** The ICU guideline recommends careful glucose and electrolyte monitoring during clinical nutrition and restricting energy temporarily when refeeding hypophosphatemia is present, with supplementation and frequent reassessment.

**Why it matters:** High-profile ICU guideline; not a wellness fasting source, but relevant to electrolyte monitoring and refeeding hypophosphatemia in high-risk clinical populations.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:serum-phosphate, biomarker:serum-potassium, biomarker:serum-magnesium, biomarker:blood-glucose, biomarker:refeeding-syndrome-symptoms, biomarker:refeeding-risk.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as clinical-supervised boundary for severe illness and abnormal labs.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
