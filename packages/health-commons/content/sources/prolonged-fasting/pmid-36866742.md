This source is included for **metabolic timecourse and mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Accessible abstract text reported both fasting trials increased ketones, with larger effects after 6 days; core temperature and epinephrine increased only after the 2-day fast; the authors suggested the 2-day fast elicited residual impaired glucose tolerance whereas the 6-day fast maintained glucose tolerance with improved insulin release.

**Why it matters:** Provides direct 48-hour fasting comparison and a boundary showing longer fasts may differ from 2-day fasts.

**Potential experiment signals:** ketones, core temperature, epinephrine, glucose tolerance, insulin release

**Protocol takeaway:** The 2-day arm is relevant to 24-72 hours and showed residual impaired glucose tolerance; the 6-day superiority claim is outside protocol and should be separated.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction boundaries:** Directness: `direct_protocol_for_2_day_arm_adjacent_variant_for_6_day_arm`. Population mismatch: Healthy young males only; 6-day findings should not be promoted into 24-72 hour claims. Limitations: Male-only young sample; 6-day arm is outside protocol; endpoint is acute OGTT response, not long-term metabolic health. Safety notes: No adverse-event signal extracted from accessible abstract snippets.
