This source is included for **adjacent fasting variants and terminology**.

**Findings:** The meta-analysis concluded that intermittent fasting and continuous calorie restriction are comparably effective for reducing body weight/adiposity and improving cardiometabolic markers in adults with overweight or obesity, with IF mode and duration acting as moderators.

**Why it matters:** Longer-term evidence helps distinguish repeated IF schedules from acute or one-off 24–72 h fasts.

**Potential experiment signals:** Body weight; Adiposity; Cardiometabolic markers; Duration/modality moderators.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent evidence that IF may not outperform continuous calorie restriction over longer periods.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
