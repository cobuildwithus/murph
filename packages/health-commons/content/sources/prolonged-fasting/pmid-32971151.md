This source is included for **diabetes, medication, and hypoglycemia safety**.

**Findings:** The expert panel statement considers SGLT2 inhibitors during Ramadan but emphasizes caution for ketoacidosis/dehydration risk, older age, diuretic use, renal impairment, and hypotension, with attention to fluids in non-fasting periods.

**Why it matters:** Focused expert statement on SGLT2 use during fasting; important for ketoacidosis/dehydration medication boundary.

**Potential experiment signals:** ketoacidosis, dehydration, renal impairment, SGLT2 inhibitor exposure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as SGLT2 safety-only guidance; do not imply that SGLT2 users can self-clear for prolonged fasting.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
