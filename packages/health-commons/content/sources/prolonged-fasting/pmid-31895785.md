This source is included for **refeeding, electrolytes, and thiamine safety**.

**Findings:** Among 967 malnourished medical inpatients, refeeding syndrome was confirmed in 141 (14.6%); 180-day mortality was higher in confirmed RFS than no-RFS patients (42/141 [29.8%] versus 181/826 [21.9%], adjusted OR 1.53, 95% CI 1.02–2.29).

**Why it matters:** Important adverse-outcome signal in medical inpatients; should be contrasted with ICU meta-analysis uncertainty rather than used alone.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:serum-phosphate, biomarker:serum-potassium, biomarker:serum-magnesium, biomarker:mortality, biomarker:refeeding-syndrome-symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a serious safety signal for malnutrition/clinical risk, with observational-association caveat.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
