This source is included for **FMD/VLCD and modified-fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** Intermittent and continuous energy restriction were both studied over 6 months in 107 overweight/obese premenopausal women; reported weight-loss effects were broadly similar rather than clearly superior for intermittent restriction.

**Why it matters:** Shows that intermittent low-energy restriction may be feasible but should not be equated with zero-calorie prolonged fasting.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:body-weight, biomarker:insulin-sensitivity, biomarker:lipids, biomarker:leptin, behavior:adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent comparator evidence and not as direct support for 24–72 hour water-only fasting.

**Directness and limitations:** Directness is `adjacent_variant`. Female overweight/obesity population; not zero-calorie fasting; longer 6-month diet program rather than discrete fast. Population mismatch: Two-day weekly low-energy diet in overweight/obese women, not a standalone 24–72 hour zero-calorie fast.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
