This source is included for **diabetes, medication, and hypoglycemia safety**.

**Findings:** The 2026 ADA hospital standards state that SGLT2 inhibitors should be avoided in severe illness, ketonemia/ketonuria, prolonged fasting, and surgical procedures, and stopped several days before scheduled surgery.

**Why it matters:** Current procedural/perioperative diabetes standard; relevant for SGLT2 and insulin-related fasting boundaries, not wellness efficacy.

**Potential experiment signals:** ketoacidosis, ketonemia, SGLT2 inhibitor exposure, prolonged fasting.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as high-priority safety-only support for SGLT2 medication exclusion/supervision around prolonged fasting.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
