This source is included for **adjacent fasting variants and terminology**.

**Findings:** The meta-analysis is useful for Ramadan-related body-weight context but should be treated as religious diurnal fasting evidence rather than direct prolonged-fasting evidence; external evidence reviews noted no RCTs were included.

**Why it matters:** Older Ramadan weight synthesis; useful for preserving mixed body-weight findings by religious-fasting variant.

**Potential experiment signals:** Body weight; Ramadan fasting context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent Ramadan body-weight context only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
