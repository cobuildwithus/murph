This source is included for **FMD/VLCD and modified-fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** The trial reported reduced myocardial triglyceride content in type 2 diabetes after an FMD program; participant count and detailed effect estimates were not verified in this extraction run.

**Why it matters:** Shows FMD may affect organ-fat imaging endpoints, but within a clinical T2D program.

**Potential experiment signals:** imaging:myocardial-triglyceride-content, biomarker:cardiac-metabolism, safety:type-2-diabetes-monitoring.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context/safety boundary only; do not generalize to healthy 24–72 hour fasts.

**Directness and limitations:** Directness is `adjacent_variant`. Paywalled/permission-required source; full sample and effect extraction incomplete; diabetes population and FMD cycles. Population mismatch: Type 2 diabetes clinical FMD trial, not a discrete water fast.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
