This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** Fasting reduced leptin and increased urinary norepinephrine, dopamine, and heart rate while decreasing cardiac vagal modulation; replacement-dose leptin did not alter the fasting-associated autonomic changes.

**Why it matters:** Directly supports autonomic/sympathetic monitoring boundaries at the upper end of the protocol window.

**Potential experiment signals:** resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, leptin, urinary catecholamines, sleep quality, palpitations.

**Protocol takeaway:** Frame 72-hour fasting as potentially adrenergic: resting heart rate and HRV/sleep may be useful safety/tolerability signals.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
