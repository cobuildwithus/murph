This source is included for **eating-disorder and restriction-risk boundary**.

**Findings:** In adults with binge-spectrum eating disorders, the study treats fasting as a non-purging compensatory behavior relevant to severity and treatment outcomes.

**Why it matters:** Useful for defining fasting as a possible compensatory behavior in binge-spectrum disorders and for differentiating wellness fasting from ED maintenance behaviors.

**Potential experiment signals:** binge/restrict cycles, restrictive behavior, treatment boundaries.

**Protocol takeaway:** Screen for binge/restrict cycling and compensatory motives before a fasting protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness and caveat:** Adults with binge-spectrum EDs, not healthy fasting participants. Clinical ED population; not a wellness-fasting intervention.
