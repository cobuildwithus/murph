This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** The pilot intervention studied 48-hour fasting followed by mixed-meal refeeding in adults with and without obesity and reported phenotype-dependent metabolic responses, including speculation that obesity may confer a paradoxical advantage in restoring carbohydrate metabolism after short-term fasting.

**Why it matters:** Very recent direct 48-hour fasting/refeeding evidence with population-stratified metabolic response.

**Potential experiment signals:** glucose and insulin, ketones, free fatty acids, refeeding response, metabolic flexibility, carbohydrate metabolism restoration.

**Protocol takeaway:** 48-hour fasting and refeeding responses differ by obesity status; carbohydrate-metabolism restoration may not be uniform across phenotypes.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Study design and directness:** Pilot intervention with fasting and refeeding phases.. Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours): `direct_protocol`.

**Comparator/control:** Lean versus obesity groups and fasting-versus-refeeding time points

**Duration/follow-up:** 48-hour fast plus refeeding period

**Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event data extracted; obesity/phenotype differences are relevant to fasting and refeeding safety interpretation.

**Limitations:** Very recent pilot intervention; full effect estimates and adverse-event reporting should be rechecked before synthesis.

**Population mismatch:** Adults with and without obesity; findings should remain phenotype-stratified rather than generalized to all healthy adults.
