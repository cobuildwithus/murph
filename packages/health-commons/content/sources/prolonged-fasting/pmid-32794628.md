This source is included for **refeeding, electrolytes, and thiamine safety**.

**Findings:** The review evaluates energy initiation rates and refeeding-related outcomes and should be used to preserve that evidence for very low versus higher initial calories is limited/mixed rather than settled across populations.

**Why it matters:** Important mixed/uncertainty source because cautious low-calorie refeeding is commonly recommended but evidence quality and definitions are limited.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:serum-phosphate, biomarker:refeeding-syndrome-symptoms, biomarker:adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to avoid presenting one calorie-start rule as universally evidence-proven.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
