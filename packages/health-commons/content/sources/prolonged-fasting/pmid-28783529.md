This source is included for **diabetes, medication, and hypoglycemia safety**.

**Findings:** The CREED hypoglycemia analysis included 3,250 people with type 2 diabetes from three continents and evaluated hypoglycemia during Ramadan in real-world treatment settings.

**Why it matters:** CREED hypoglycemia analysis; useful for pre-fast hypoglycemia history and insulin therapy as risk markers.

**Potential experiment signals:** hypoglycemia, diabetes therapy pattern, Ramadan fasting exposure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as contextual evidence that pre-existing diabetes treatment context changes fasting hypoglycemia risk.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
