This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** The study reported a 42% reduction in whole-body insulin sensitivity after 48 hours of starvation, downregulation of SREBP1c and hexokinase II, upregulation of PDK4, and incomplete recovery of insulin sensitivity after 24 hours of carbohydrate refeeding.

**Why it matters:** Direct 48-hour human fast/refeed mechanism evidence, but it should not be promoted as clinical benefit evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** whole-body insulin sensitivity, skeletal muscle SREBP1c, hexokinase II, PDK4, free fatty acids, carbohydrate regulatory genes.

**Protocol takeaway:** Fasting/refeeding may transiently downshift oxidative glucose disposal and insulin sensitivity despite rapid gene-expression reversal after carbohydrate refeeding.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Study design and directness:** Acute mechanistic study with muscle outcomes during 48-hour starvation and 24-hour refeeding.. Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours): `direct_protocol`.

**Comparator/control:** Pre-fast baseline and post-refeeding measurements

**Duration/follow-up:** 48-hour fast plus 24-hour carbohydrate-rich refeeding

**Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event table was extracted; insulin-resistance physiology is safety-relevant for refeeding and glycemic monitoring.

**Limitations:** Mechanistic physiology study; small sample; not designed to test clinical outcomes or unsupervised fasting safety.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy adults under laboratory conditions; does not directly apply to diabetes medication users or people with insulin-treated diabetes.
