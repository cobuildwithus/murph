This source is included for **eating-disorder and restriction-risk boundary**.

**Findings:** USPSTF concluded evidence was insufficient for routine ED screening in asymptomatic adolescents/adults without signs, symptoms, or underweight status.

**Why it matters:** Important screening-evidence boundary; should be extracted carefully because USPSTF addresses asymptomatic screening, not fasting-specific contraindication checks.

**Potential experiment signals:** screening and contraindications, population mismatch, referral boundaries.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use screening as a safety gate, but do not claim a brief screen fully rules out ED risk.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness and caveat:** Preventive screening recommendation, not fasting intervention evidence. Preventive-screening evidence, not fasting-protocol evidence.
