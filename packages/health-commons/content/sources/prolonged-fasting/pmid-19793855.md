This source is included for **FMD/VLCD and modified-fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** The pilot modified alternate-day fasting program reported weight loss, reductions in body fat, total cholesterol, LDL cholesterol, triglycerides, and systolic blood pressure, with HDL unchanged.

**Why it matters:** Early human modified-ADF evidence helps define what a low-calorie fast-day program can and cannot say about prolonged fasting.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:body-weight, biomarker:body-fat, biomarker:lipids, vitals:systolic-blood-pressure, behavior:adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Adjacent modified-ADF efficacy context only; it does not support direct claims for a one-off 24–72 hour zero-calorie fast.

**Directness and limitations:** Directness is `adjacent_variant`. Small uncontrolled pilot with obesity population and repeated low-calorie fast days. Population mismatch: Obesity treatment program, not a single zero-calorie 24–72 hour protocol in general users.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
