This source is included for **FMD/VLCD and modified-fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** In 30 healthy participants, FMD formulations changed body weight, fasting glucose, beta-hydroxybutyrate, HOMA-IR, and autophagic-flux measures versus control over the acute study window; larger studies are needed.

**Why it matters:** Directly relevant to mechanism endpoints, but it is a sponsored FMD formulation trial and not water-only fasting.

**Potential experiment signals:** mechanism:autophagic-flux, biomarker:fasting-glucose, biomarker:insulin, biomarker:igf-1, biomarker:beta-hydroxybutyrate, biomarker:homa-ir, biomarker:body-weight.

**Protocol takeaway:** Mechanistic FMD context only; do not cite as direct evidence for 24–72 hour prolonged fasting.

**Directness and limitations:** Directness is `adjacent_variant`. Small pilot sample, sponsored by L-Nutra, FMD formulation rather than water-only fasting, short follow-up. Population mismatch: FMD product/formulation trial in healthy participants.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
