This source is included for **safety and adverse events**.

**Findings:** A small acute study measured autonomic function, resting frontal brain activity, cognition, and mood before and after a 48-hour fast in nine participants.

**Why it matters:** Direct 48-hour exposure inside the protocol window; useful for autonomic/cognition/mood monitoring context.

**Potential experiment signals:** autonomic function, frontal brain activity, cognition, mood, tolerability

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as direct-duration context for signals to monitor, not as robust safety proof.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** direct_protocol.

**Population and exposure:** Amateur weight lifters / healthy active adults. Exposure/intervention: 48-hour zero-calorie fast. Comparator/control: Within-person pre/post fasting measurements. Duration/follow-up: 48 hours..

**Effect direction or estimates:** Nine participants completed a 48-hour zero-calorie fast with measurements before and after fasting.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** Safety-adjacent autonomic, cognition, and mood measures; specific serious adverse events were not extracted.

**Limitations:** Very small active-adult sample; acute mechanistic outcomes; not powered for adverse events.

**Population mismatch:** Direct duration but narrow athletic population and lab-style outcome set.
