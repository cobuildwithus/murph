This source is included for **eating-disorder and restriction-risk boundary**.

**Findings:** A small non-randomized controlled pilot of 7-day fasting in adults with type 1 diabetes (20 T1D, 10 controls) was supervised and medically monitored, underscoring supervision and exclusion boundaries.

**Why it matters:** Direct prolonged-fasting safety source with a reported eating-disorder-history signal; important for history-of-anorexia exclusion and medical-supervision boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** adverse events, eating-disorder history, screening and contraindications, medical supervision.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not generalize medically supervised diabetic fasting to consumer use; require medical contraindication screening.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness and caveat:** Medically supervised 7-day fasting in adults with type 1 diabetes, not a general unsupervised 24–72-hour protocol. Small pilot, 7-day duration, and T1D population differ from the protocol target.
