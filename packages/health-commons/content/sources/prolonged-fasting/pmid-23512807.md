This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** The study fasted 12 lean and 14 non-diabetic obese participants for 48 hours and examined how fasting affected energy/nutrient-sensing pathways, muscle metabolic gene expression, mitochondrial respiratory-chain content, and insulin sensitivity across phenotypes.

**Why it matters:** Direct 48-hour mechanistic source that preserves obesity-related population differences and metabolic-flexibility limits.

**Potential experiment signals:** AMPK signaling, skeletal-muscle gene expression, mitochondrial respiratory chain content, insulin sensitivity, fuel oxidation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Fasting adaptation may differ by obesity status; mechanistic muscle changes should not be generalized to all adults.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Study design and directness:** Acute mechanistic physiology study with lean/obese comparison.. Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours): `direct_protocol`.

**Comparator/control:** Lean versus obese participants and pre-/post-fasting muscle/metabolic measurements

**Duration/follow-up:** 48-hour fast

**Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event data extracted.

**Limitations:** Mechanistic endpoints and phenotype comparison; not a clinical efficacy trial.

**Population mismatch:** Includes non-diabetic obesity; lean-versus-obese differences should not be collapsed into a single “healthy adult” claim.
