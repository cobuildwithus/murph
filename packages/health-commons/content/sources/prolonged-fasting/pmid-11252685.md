This source is included for **Clinical/residential supervised fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** Systematic review identified controlled RA studies with favorable pooled findings, but the evidence base was small/methodologically limited and confounded by the vegetarian diet phase.

**Why it matters:** Important adjacent evidence where fasting is embedded in disease treatment and diet change rather than a standalone 24–72 hour protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** arthritis symptoms, inflammatory markers, adherence, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as RA/diet-sequence context; do not promote into a general fasting efficacy claim.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Patients with rheumatoid arthritis in controlled studies of fasting followed by vegetarian diet.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Fasting phase followed by vegetarian diet.
- **Comparator/control:** Control diets or usual diet across controlled studies.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Controlled studies with at least 3 months of follow-up in the review inclusion set.
- **Endpoints:** rheumatoid arthritis symptoms, pain, joint measures, inflammatory markers
- **Effect estimate or direction:** Review found statistically favorable pooled results in controlled studies, but emphasized small and methodologically limited evidence.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Safety details were not extracted as a primary adverse-event finding in this batch.
- **Limitations:** Disease-specific dietary sequence; search and study-quality limitations; fasting was followed by long-term vegetarian diet, not isolated 24–72 hour fasting.
- **Population mismatch:** Rheumatoid arthritis treatment population and post-fast vegetarian diet are not direct wellness protocol evidence.
- **Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** adjacent_variant
