This source is included for **metabolic timecourse and mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Accessible search snippets reported that a 36-hour fast had a profound effect on substrate utilization in older women and men, with fat oxidation averaging about 52-55% of resting energy expenditure in the postabsorptive state; related snippets report decreased RER with longer fasting.

**Why it matters:** Adds older-adult substrate-utilization data for the 36-hour fasting window.

**Potential experiment signals:** fat oxidation, RER, protein mobilization, glucose output, lipolysis

**Protocol takeaway:** Older adults may have distinct substrate-use patterns during a 36-hour fast; avoid assuming young-adult kinetics.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Extraction boundaries:** Directness: `direct_protocol`. Population mismatch: Older adult sample; may not generalize to younger adults or clinical-risk groups. Limitations: Very recent source; sample size and complete numeric estimates not extracted; pilot/metabolic physiology context. Safety notes: No adverse-event signal extracted.
