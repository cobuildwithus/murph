This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** DRKS00037060 describes a crossover trial with 48-hour water fasting and 48-hour dry fasting cycles in healthy adults; the registry record supports protocol-context and hydration-boundary mapping but does not provide extracted results.

**Why it matters:** Directly includes a 48-hour water-fast arm but also includes dry fasting, which is an adjacent and higher-risk fluid-restriction variant.

**Potential experiment signals:** physiological processes, molecular biological processes, dry-versus-water fasting differences, safety-screened fasting physiology.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only for registry context and for separating water-only protocol claims from dry-fasting claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Study design and directness:** controlled crossover trial registry. Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours): `direct_protocol`.

**Comparator/control:** Within-participant dry fasting versus water fasting with washout

**Duration/follow-up:** Two 48-hour fasting cycles with washout

**Adverse events or safety notes:** Dry fasting is not equivalent to the water-only protocol and should be treated as a separate safety boundary; no adverse-event rates were extracted.

**Limitations:** Registry-only extraction; outcome results and adverse-event details were not available in the extracted record.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy screened adults; dry-fasting arm is population/intervention mismatch for a water-only 24–72-hour fast.
