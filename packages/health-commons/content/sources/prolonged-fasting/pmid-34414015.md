This source is included for **safety and adverse events**.

**Findings:** The study reports dehydration, increased ketogenesis, hyperuricemia, decreased serum glucose, and hyponatremia after an 8-day water-only fast. The study reports decreased serum calcium and magnesium and more acidic, concentrated urine, indicating physiologic safety-relevant shifts.

**Why it matters:** Directly safety-focused water-only fasting study with electrolyte and dehydration signals.

**Potential experiment signals:** dehydration, ketogenesis, hyperuricemia, serum glucose, hyponatremia, calcium, magnesium, urine specific gravity

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as high-priority adjacent safety source; it supports monitoring/caution rather than blanket safety reassurance.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** adjacent_variant.

**Population and exposure:** Twelve middle-aged men, apparently healthy and with prior fasting experience in extracted secondary text. Exposure/intervention: Eight-day water-only fast with mineral water. Comparator/control: Within-person pre/post measurements. Duration/follow-up: 8 days..

**Effect direction or estimates:** Extracted text reports dehydration, increased ketogenesis, hyperuricemia, decreased serum glucose, hyponatremia, and decreased serum calcium and magnesium during/after 8-day water-only fasting.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** Physiological perturbations relevant to dehydration, glucose, sodium, uric acid, calcium, magnesium, and urine concentration.

**Limitations:** Small all-male sample, experienced/selected participants, 8-day duration, no control group.

**Population mismatch:** Adjacent duration and population; longer than 72 hours and not representative of broad community users.
