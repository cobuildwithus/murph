**Findings:** Early time-restricted feeding improved insulin sensitivity, beta-cell responsiveness, blood pressure, oxidative-stress markers, and appetite-related measures even without intended weight loss in men with prediabetes.

**Why it matters:** Circadian-aligned TRE mechanistic trial; useful as adjacent mechanism, not direct prolonged-fasting evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** Insulin sensitivity; Beta-cell responsiveness; Blood pressure; Oxidative stress; Appetite/adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent circadian/TRE mechanism context, not as proof for prolonged-fasting outcomes.
