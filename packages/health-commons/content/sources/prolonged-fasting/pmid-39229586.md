This source is included for **adjacent fasting variants and terminology**.

**Findings:** The RCT found 5:2 IF improved hepatic steatosis and fibrosis measures versus daily calorie restriction, while between-group differences in body weight, BMI, waist/hip measures, lipids, glycemic indices, and adverse events were not statistically significant.

**Why it matters:** Recent 5:2 RCT in MAFLD; adjacent disease-specific evidence and not direct prolonged fasting.

**Potential experiment signals:** Hepatic steatosis; Fibrosis score; Body weight; Lipids; Glycemic indices; Adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent 5:2 MAFLD context with mixed endpoint findings.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
