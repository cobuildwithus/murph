This source is included for **refeeding, electrolytes, and thiamine safety**.

**Findings:** In 339 critically ill adults with refeeding syndrome, caloric restriction did not significantly improve the primary outcome of mean days alive after ICU discharge at 60 days, but extracted abstract data reported improved day-60 survival and no safety concerns; this is ICU treatment evidence, not fasting guidance.

**Why it matters:** One of the few randomized sources in this area; adjacent ICU treatment evidence, not healthy fasting guidance.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:serum-phosphate, biomarker:heart-rhythm, biomarker:mortality, biomarker:refeeding-syndrome-symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as clinical-supervised treatment context; do not translate to home post-fast calorie prescriptions.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
