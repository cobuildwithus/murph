This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** In FEELGOOD stored samples, 24-hour water-only fasting reduced mean TMAO from baseline 27.1 ng to 14.3 ng (p=0.019), returned toward baseline on refeeding, and altered proline, tyrosine, galactitol, urea, and other metabolites.

**Why it matters:** Open-access direct 24-hour water-only metabolomics source with extractable effect estimates.

**Potential experiment signals:** TMAO, metabolomics, proline, tyrosine, galactitol, urea, metabolic analytes.

**Protocol takeaway:** A one-day water-only fast can acutely alter TMAO and metabolomic analytes, but this remains mechanistic biomarker evidence.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Study design and directness:** Pilot secondary metabolomics analysis of randomized fasting/fed-day trial.. Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours): `direct_protocol`.

**Comparator/control:** 24-hour ad libitum fed day in Latin-square randomized design; baseline and refeeding measurements

**Duration/follow-up:** 24-hour fast with fed-day and refeeding measurements

**Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event findings extracted; secondary analysis focused on stored biomarker samples.

**Limitations:** Pilot secondary analysis; metabolite changes are not direct clinical outcomes and may not persist after refeeding.

**Population mismatch:** Apparently healthy participants; one-day protocol only; research-grade assay endpoints.
