This source is included for **diabetes, medication, and hypoglycemia safety**.

**Findings:** The ADA/Endocrine Society workgroup report summarizes hypoglycemia definitions, clinical consequences, prevention, and treatment context for people with diabetes, especially those using glucose-lowering medications.

**Why it matters:** Older foundational workgroup report on hypoglycemia definitions and treatment context, including medication-related risk.

**Potential experiment signals:** hypoglycemia threshold, hypoglycemia symptoms, rescue carbohydrate.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to anchor definitions and seriousness of medication-related hypoglycemia, not protocol efficacy.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
