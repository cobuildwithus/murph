This source is included for **Clinical/residential supervised fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** In 44 randomized breast cancer patients, FMD during chemotherapy was reported as well tolerated with reduced toxicity and some metabolic benefits; it is adjacent oncology FMD evidence.

**Why it matters:** Recent oncology FMD RCT; useful for current adjacent boundary but must not become direct water-only fasting evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** toxicity, metabolic markers, response measures, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as FMD oncology boundary only.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** HER2-negative breast cancer patients receiving neoadjuvant chemotherapy.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Fasting-mimicking diet during neoadjuvant chemotherapy.
- **Comparator/control:** Control/usual diet during neoadjuvant chemotherapy.
- **Duration/follow-up:** FMD periods around chemotherapy; fasting-mimicking diet rather than water-only fasting.
- **Endpoints:** chemotherapy toxicity, metabolic parameters, radiologic/pathologic response
- **Effect estimate or direction:** Reported FMD was well tolerated, reduced chemotherapy toxicity, and improved some metabolic parameters; response signals favored FMD in extracted summary.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Tolerability was reported, but oncology/FMD setting limits general safety translation.
- **Limitations:** FMD not water-only; small RCT; cancer chemotherapy setting.
- **Population mismatch:** Breast cancer neoadjuvant chemotherapy population.
- **Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** adjacent_variant
