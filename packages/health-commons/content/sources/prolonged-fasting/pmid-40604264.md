This source is included for **FMD/VLCD and modified-fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** Both FMD plus exercise and Mediterranean diet plus exercise improved several cardiometabolic measures; no endpoint was clearly superior between arms, and vascular RHI interpretation was uncertain.

**Why it matters:** Comparator trial helps avoid overstating FMD beyond an active diet-plus-exercise control.

**Potential experiment signals:** vitals:blood-pressure, biomarker:body-weight, anthropometric:waist-circumference, biomarker:hba1c, biomarker:igf-1, biomarker:lipids, vascular:reactive-hyperemia-index.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only FMD evidence; not direct prolonged-fasting support.

**Directness and limitations:** Directness is `adjacent_variant`. Hypertension/overweight population, FMD plus exercise package, participant count not verified in this extraction, not zero-calorie fasting. Population mismatch: Hypertension management trial, not general 24–72 hour fasting.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
