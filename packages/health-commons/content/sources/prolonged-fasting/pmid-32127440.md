This source is included for **eating-disorder and restriction-risk boundary**.

**Findings:** This review highlights restrictive eating disorders in previously overweight adolescents/young adults, warning that medical risk can be present despite non-classic weight history.

**Why it matters:** Highly relevant to underweight-boundary nuance: restrictive ED risk and medical instability can occur without classic low-weight appearance.

**Potential experiment signals:** restrictive behavior, medical instability, population mismatch, referral boundaries.

**Protocol takeaway:** Weight history and rate of weight loss must be screened, not just current BMI.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness and caveat:** Previously overweight adolescents/young adults with restrictive EDs, not healthy adult fasters. Clinical review, not fasting intervention evidence.
