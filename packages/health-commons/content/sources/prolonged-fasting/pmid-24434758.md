This source is included for **Clinical/residential supervised fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** Consensus guideline lists contraindications for fasting therapy, including cachexia, anorexia/eating disorders, uncontrolled hyperthyroidism, advanced cerebrovascular insufficiency/dementia, advanced liver or kidney insufficiency, pregnancy, and nursing.; Consensus guideline emphasizes clinician guidance and medication adjustment/monitoring for drug classes including antihypertensives, antidiabetics, anticoagulants, lithium/psychotropics, anticonvulsants, and other medications.; Consensus guideline describes possible fasting reactions such as circulatory dysregulation, mild hypoglycemia, electrolyte disturbance, headache, and cramps, with severe electrolyte disturbances or arrhythmias as discontinuation criteria.

**Why it matters:** Core safety-boundary source for contraindications, medication adjustment, monitoring, and stop rules.

**Potential experiment signals:** electrolytes, blood pressure, pulse, ECG, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only guidance; not as efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

- **Intervention/exposure:** Clinical fasting therapy practice standards.

- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Describes potential circulatory dysregulation, hypoglycemia, electrolyte disturbances, headache, cramps, and severe electrolyte thresholds as stop criteria.
