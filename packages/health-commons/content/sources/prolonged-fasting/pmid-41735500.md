This source is included for **refeeding, electrolytes, and thiamine safety**.

**Findings:** The critical-care meta-analysis found that RFS was not statistically associated with mortality in pooled ICU data (reported OR 1.51, 95% CI 0.79–2.88), with low certainty and high heterogeneity; this preserves mixed/negative mortality evidence.

**Why it matters:** Very recent systematic review preserving mixed/negative mortality findings and highlighting low-certainty evidence and heterogeneity in ICU populations.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:serum-phosphate, biomarker:heart-rhythm, biomarker:mortality, biomarker:refeeding-syndrome-symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to balance mortality claims: clinical RFS outcome associations are not uniform across settings.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
