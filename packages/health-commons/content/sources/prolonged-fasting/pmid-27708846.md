This source is included for **FMD/VLCD and modified-fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** The meta-analysis found alternate-day restriction was not a clean substitute for very-low-calorie or daily restriction programs; reported comparisons included smaller body-weight and fat-free-mass reductions versus VLCD studies and no clear fat-mass difference.

**Why it matters:** Useful aggregate context for keeping ADF/VLCD boundary claims modest.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:body-weight, biomarker:fat-mass, biomarker:fat-free-mass.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only synthesis; do not use as direct evidence for 24–72 hour zero-calorie fasting.

**Directness and limitations:** Directness is `adjacent_variant`. Study heterogeneity and indirect comparison across diet programs. Population mismatch: Diet-program synthesis rather than direct protocol exposure.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
