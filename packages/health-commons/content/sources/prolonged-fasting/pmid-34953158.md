This source is included for **diabetes, medication, and hypoglycemia safety**.

**Findings:** This systematic review/meta-analysis evaluated SGLT2 inhibitor use during Ramadan in T2D and reported favorable metabolic signals while remaining medication- and population-specific safety evidence.

**Why it matters:** Systematic review/meta-analysis specifically addressing SGLT2 inhibitors during Ramadan; relevant for both reassuring and cautionary safety synthesis.

**Potential experiment signals:** HbA1c, hypoglycemia, ketoacidosis, SGLT2 inhibitor exposure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as cautious safety context only; do not use to clear SGLT2 users for prolonged fasting.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
