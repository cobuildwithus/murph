This source is included for **safety and adverse events**.

**Findings:** The RCT found no LDL-C or cognitive improvement but significant improvements in HOMA-IR and metabolic syndrome score. The trial randomized 103 participants and reported an evaluable sample of 71 after dropouts/losses, limiting power and safety interpretation.

**Why it matters:** Direct 24-hour water-only RCT with mixed metabolic and null LDL/cognition outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, HOMA-IR, metabolic syndrome score, BDNF, cognition, dropout

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as direct 24-hour efficacy/context evidence with mixed outcomes; do not use as 72-hour safety proof.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** direct_protocol.

**Population and exposure:** Adults aged 21-70 with modest LDL-C elevation and at least one metabolic-syndrome feature or diet-controlled type 2 diabetes without anti-diabetic medication. Exposure/intervention: Water-only 24-hour fasting twice weekly for 4 weeks then once weekly for 22 weeks. Comparator/control: Ad libitum control diet. Duration/follow-up: 26 weeks of intermittent 24-hour fasts..

**Effect direction or estimates:** The RCT did not reduce LDL-C or improve cognitive function, but improved HOMA-IR and metabolic syndrome score; evaluable sample was 71 after dropouts.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** Not primarily an AE study; dropout was higher than anticipated and included 12 fasting-arm withdrawals/losses and 20 control-arm withdrawals/losses in extracted text.

**Limitations:** Low-frequency repeated 24-hour fasts, not a single 24-72 hour fast; powered for LDL-C, not safety; attrition reduced power.

**Population mismatch:** Direct 24-hour water-only exposures but repeated over 26 weeks and cardiometabolic eligibility differs from broad wellness users.
