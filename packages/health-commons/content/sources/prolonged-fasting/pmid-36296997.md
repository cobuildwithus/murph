This source is included for **Clinical/residential supervised fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** In a 40-person single-arm follow-up study, prolonged water-only fasting/refeeding reported sustained cardiometabolic improvements, while some markers fluctuated during refeeding; duration exceeds protocol.

**Why it matters:** Captures refeeding/follow-up dynamics after prolonged water-only fasting and supports cautious monitoring language.

**Potential experiment signals:** blood pressure, lipids, HOMA-IR, hsCRP, refeeding markers.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for adjacent refeeding/follow-up context only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Participants in a prolonged water-only fasting and refeeding program.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Prolonged water-only fasting followed by whole-plant-food refeeding.
- **Comparator/control:** Within-person baseline, end fasting/refeeding, and follow-up comparisons.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Prolonged fasting/refeeding with six-week follow-up; exceeds 72 hours.
- **Endpoints:** body weight, BMI, abdominal circumference, blood pressure, lipids, fatty liver index, hsCRP, HOMA-IR
- **Effect estimate or direction:** Reported sustained improvements in several cardiometabolic markers at six weeks; triglycerides/HOMA-IR rose at refeeding then returned toward baseline in extracted summary.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Refeeding dynamics are important; no detailed adverse-event rate extracted.
- **Limitations:** Single-arm, clinical long-duration program; potential refeeding biomarker fluctuations.
- **Population mismatch:** Long supervised water-only fasting with refeeding, not 24–72 hour protocol.
- **Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** clinical_supervised
