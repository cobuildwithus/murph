This source is included for **refeeding, electrolytes, and thiamine safety**.

**Findings:** The case-report synthesis captures severe refeeding-syndrome presentations, including electrolyte derangements, cardiac outcomes, and mortality; it is useful for adverse-event recall but not for incidence estimation.

**Why it matters:** Case-report synthesis useful for treatment/adverse-event context and for avoiding omission of severe outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:serum-phosphate, biomarker:serum-potassium, biomarker:serum-magnesium, biomarker:heart-rhythm, biomarker:mortality, biomarker:refeeding-syndrome-symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for adverse-event seriousness and clinician-referral boundaries, not risk percentages.
