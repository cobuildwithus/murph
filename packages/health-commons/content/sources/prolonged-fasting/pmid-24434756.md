This source is included for **Clinical/residential supervised fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** In 25 inpatients, therapeutic fasting decreased HOMA-IR during the fast, with partial or complete rebound by follow-up; no serious side effects were reported in the extracted summary.

**Why it matters:** Shows reversible insulin-resistance changes in longer inpatient fasting and highlights diabetes/medication boundary issues.

**Potential experiment signals:** fasting insulin, glucose, HOMA-IR, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for metabolic/supervision boundary and rebound caveat.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Inpatients with metabolic syndrome and impaired insulin resistance, including people with type 2 diabetes.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Therapeutic fasting for 7–18 days, mean about 11.5 days.
- **Comparator/control:** Within-person before/after comparison.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 7–18 days fasting with follow-up to about day 80; exceeds 72 hours.
- **Endpoints:** HOMA-IR, insulin resistance, body weight, adverse events
- **Effect estimate or direction:** Reported HOMA-IR decrease during fasting with partial/complete rebound by follow-up.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No serious side effects were reported in the abstract-level extraction.
- **Limitations:** Small open-label inpatient cohort, disease population, long fast, rebound after follow-up.
- **Population mismatch:** Metabolic syndrome/diabetes population under inpatient care.
- **Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** clinical_supervised
