This source is included for **safety and adverse events**.

**Findings:** The systematic review found that human evidence was sparse and largely surrogate-outcome based, with inadequate clinical-outcome RCT evidence for broad recommendations.

**Why it matters:** Historical systematic-review context for benefits-versus-harms uncertainty.

**Potential experiment signals:** adverse events, safety, metabolic markers, cardiovascular markers

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as background uncertainty; do not promote as direct support for 24-72 hour fasting efficacy or safety.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** adjacent_variant.

**Population and exposure:** Human intermittent-fasting literature available through early 2015. Exposure/intervention: Intermittent fasting variants including short-term complete fasts and modified fasts. Comparator/control: Varied across included studies. Duration/follow-up: Varied intermittent-fasting durations and follow-up..

**Effect direction or estimates:** The review found limited randomized evidence, mainly surrogate outcomes, and no randomized clinical-outcome evidence sufficient for firm recommendations.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** Safety discussed at review level; no protocol-specific adverse-event incidence extracted.

**Limitations:** Older review; heterogeneous intermittent-fasting definitions; limited human RCT evidence and no direct 72-hour protocol trial synthesis.

**Population mismatch:** Adjacent intermittent-fasting variants, not specifically a single 24-72 hour fast.
