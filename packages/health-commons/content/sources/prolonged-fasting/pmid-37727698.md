This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** The secondary analysis reported that intermittent fasting increased a 1-year IMRS mortality risk score while decreasing 10-year chronic disease risk scores and reducing insulin resistance more in some higher-risk subjects.

**Why it matters:** Preserves mixed/nonlinear risk-score evidence from the FEELGOOD/WONDERFUL cluster without turning it into direct efficacy proof.

**Potential experiment signals:** Intermountain Mortality Risk Score, Pooled Cohort Risk Equations, Intermountain Chronic Disease score, HOMA-IR, clinical risk scores.

**Protocol takeaway:** Risk-score results are mixed and not a direct one-off 24–72-hour fast outcome.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Study design and directness:** Secondary analysis of randomized trial.. Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours): `direct_protocol`.

**Comparator/control:** Control arm or baseline risk-score change in the underlying trial

**Duration/follow-up:** Repeated intermittent-fasting trial; single 24-hour exposure not fully separable in extracted candidate

**Adverse events or safety notes:** Increased IMRS mortality risk score is a risk-interpretation boundary; no adverse-event counts were extracted.

**Limitations:** Risk scores are surrogate algorithms; repeated fasting intervention and secondary analysis, not one-off protocol outcome evidence.

**Population mismatch:** Trial population and repeated fasting schedule may not match a single 24–72-hour fast.
