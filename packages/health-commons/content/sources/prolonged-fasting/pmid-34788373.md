This source is included for **adjacent fasting variants and terminology**.

**Findings:** The review treats fasting around chemotherapy as a clinical-supervised intervention in a medically vulnerable population, so it is primarily a safety and population-boundary source for Murph rather than general protocol support.

**Why it matters:** Periodic/short-term fasting in oncology is a boundary case; useful to avoid importing chemotherapy-context claims into wellness prolonged fasting.

**Potential experiment signals:** Adverse events; Chemotherapy tolerability; Adherence; Clinical supervision.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only/clinical-supervision boundary; do not use for general efficacy claims.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
