This source is included for **diabetes, medication, and hypoglycemia safety**.

**Findings:** This case series identified euglycemic diabetic ketoacidosis associated with SGLT2 inhibitor therapy during colonoscopy preparation, where bowel prep, clear fluids, and fasting can interact with medication risk.

**Why it matters:** Procedure-associated fasting case series/adverse-event signal for SGLT2-related euglycemic DKA.

**Potential experiment signals:** euglycemic diabetic ketoacidosis, ketones, SGLT2 inhibitor exposure, procedural fasting.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a high-priority SGLT2 safety boundary for fasting or procedure-related restriction.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
