This source is included for **FMD/VLCD and modified-fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** The human pilot component of this translational FMD paper reported reductions in multiple cardiometabolic and aging-related risk markers and no major adverse side effects in the pilot context.

**Why it matters:** Foundational FMD source for the adjacent 5-day low-calorie fasting-mimic boundary.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:body-weight, biomarker:glucose, biomarker:insulin, biomarker:igf-1, biomarker:lipids, safety:adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as FMD context only; a 5-day formulated low-calorie diet is not the 24–72 hour zero-calorie protocol.

**Directness and limitations:** Directness is `adjacent_variant`. Pilot human sample and translational paper with animal/mechanistic components; not a direct prolonged-fasting RCT. Population mismatch: Formulated FMD cycles rather than zero-calorie 24–72 hour fasting.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
