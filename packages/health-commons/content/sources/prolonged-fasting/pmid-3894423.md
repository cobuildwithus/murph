This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** During euglycemic insulin clamp testing, total glucose disposal was reduced by about 45% after a 60-hour fast, while splanchnic glucose uptake during hyperinsulinemia/hyperglycemia remained unchanged; the source concluded peripheral tissues predominantly accounted for starvation-associated insulin resistance.

**Why it matters:** Direct 60-hour evidence for the peripheral insulin-resistance boundary at the upper end of the protocol window.

**Potential experiment signals:** insulin sensitivity, glucose disposal, blood glucose, fasting insulin, splanchnic glucose uptake.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as direct mechanistic evidence that 60-hour fasting can reduce peripheral insulin-mediated glucose disposal.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
