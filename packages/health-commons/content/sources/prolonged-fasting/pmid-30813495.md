This source is included for **adjacent fasting variants and terminology**.

**Findings:** The meta-analysis pooled Ramadan fasting studies and provides weight/body-composition context for a month-long religious dry-fasting pattern, not for water-only or calorie-free 24–72-hour fasting.

**Why it matters:** Ramadan-specific body composition synthesis; important because dry daytime fasting and nocturnal eating differ from target protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** Body weight; Body composition; Religious fasting adherence context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as adjacent Ramadan context for variant boundaries and population mismatch.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
