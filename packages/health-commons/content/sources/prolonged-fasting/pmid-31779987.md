This source is included for **FMD/VLCD and modified-fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** Modified ADF reduced body weight, waist circumference, systolic blood pressure, and fasting glucose more than calorie restriction, while several lipid and insulin-resistance markers did not differ between groups.

**Why it matters:** Metabolic-syndrome RCT with mixed endpoint results helps prevent single-endpoint overclaiming.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:body-weight, anthropometric:waist-circumference, vitals:systolic-blood-pressure, biomarker:fasting-glucose, biomarker:lipids, biomarker:homa-ir.

**Protocol takeaway:** Adjacent modified-ADF evidence only; not direct 24–72 hour prolonged-fasting proof.

**Directness and limitations:** Directness is `adjacent_variant`. Metabolic-syndrome population, modified fast days, short 8-week duration. Population mismatch: Clinical metabolic-syndrome diet trial, not general prolonged fasting.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
