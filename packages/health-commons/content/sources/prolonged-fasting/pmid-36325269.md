This source is included for **Clinical/residential supervised fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** In a 52-participant RCT, 10-day fasting therapy improved vitamin D/vitality/QOL versus normal diet, while weight/BMI did not differ between groups in the extracted summary.

**Why it matters:** Preserves positive and null findings together: vitamin D/QOL signal but no weight/BMI difference.

**Potential experiment signals:** vitamin D, B12, quality of life, body weight/BMI.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent long-fast RCT context; keep null weight/BMI finding.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Young adults/women in a medically supervised fasting therapy study.
- **Intervention/exposure:** 10-day prolonged fasting therapy.
- **Comparator/control:** Normal diet group.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 10 days; exceeds 72 hours.
- **Endpoints:** vitamin D, vitamin B12, body weight, BMI, vitality, quality of life
- **Effect estimate or direction:** Reported improved vitamin D, vitality, and quality of life compared with normal diet; fasting group did not differ from normal diet on weight/BMI in extracted summary.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Safety details not extracted as rates.
- **Limitations:** Long fasting duration, specific population, RCT but outcomes not directly aligned with protocol endpoint claims.
- **Population mismatch:** 10-day supervised therapy, not 24–72 hour protocol.
- **Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** clinical_supervised
