This source is included for **eating-disorder and restriction-risk boundary**.

**Findings:** An 8-year prospective study of adolescent girls identified body dissatisfaction and other risk pathways for eating-disorder onset.

**Why it matters:** Foundational longitudinal risk-factor source for disordered-eating prevention language; adjacent but useful for identifying risk domains beyond fasting exposure.

**Potential experiment signals:** eating disorder onset, restrictive behavior, mood and anxiety.

**Protocol takeaway:** Screen body dissatisfaction and related vulnerability, not just active diagnosis.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness and caveat:** Risk-prediction study in adolescent girls, not prolonged-fasting intervention evidence. Not a fasting intervention.
