This source is included for **adjacent fasting variants and terminology**.

**Findings:** The review included six studies with varied intermittent fasting or intermittent energy-restriction regimens, making it useful as early adjacent weight-loss evidence but not a direct estimate for one 24–72-hour fast.

**Why it matters:** Older foundational systematic review that helped establish the IF evidence base and limitations.

**Potential experiment signals:** Body weight; Adherence; Adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for adjacent IF weight-management context and to avoid treating all IF variants as equivalent.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
