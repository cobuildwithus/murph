This source is included for **FMD/VLCD and modified-fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** In this pilot RCT, zero-calorie ADF and daily caloric restriction produced no clear group differences in weight, body composition, lipids, or insulin sensitivity during the intervention; no ADF adverse effects were reported.

**Why it matters:** Closest boundary source for repeated zero-calorie 24-hour fasts, but still an alternate-day weight-loss program.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:body-weight, biomarker:body-composition, biomarker:lipids, biomarker:insulin-sensitivity, safety:tolerability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Adjacent ADF evidence only; do not treat repeated alternate-day fasting as equivalent to one 24–72 hour fast.

**Directness and limitations:** Directness is `adjacent_variant`. Pilot sample, adults with obesity, behavioral weight-loss context, not a single prolonged-fast study. Population mismatch: Repeated zero-calorie ADF over weeks, not a discrete 24–72 hour protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
