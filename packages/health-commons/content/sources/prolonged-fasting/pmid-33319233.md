This source is included for **diabetes, medication, and hypoglycemia safety**.

**Findings:** This systematic review/meta-analysis found intermittent fasting produced greater weight loss than standard diet while glycemic-control effects were similar, with safety reporting relevant but not direct to prolonged fasting.

**Why it matters:** Meta-analysis of interventional IF studies in T2D; useful to preserve mixed/null safety reporting and adverse-event gaps.

**Potential experiment signals:** body weight, HbA1c, hypoglycemia reporting.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only adjacent IF evidence; do not promote to direct protocol benefits.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
