This source is included for **diabetes, medication, and hypoglycemia safety**.

**Findings:** This meta-analytic Ramadan source compares hypoglycemia incidence with gliclazide versus DPP-4 inhibitors and reports similarly low symptomatic hypoglycemia risk in the included diabetes fasting studies.

**Why it matters:** Medication-class comparison focused on sulfonylurea-related hypoglycemia risk during Ramadan fasting.

**Potential experiment signals:** symptomatic hypoglycemia, medication class, DPP-4 inhibitor exposure, sulfonylurea exposure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only for diabetes medication-class safety context and not for protocol efficacy.
