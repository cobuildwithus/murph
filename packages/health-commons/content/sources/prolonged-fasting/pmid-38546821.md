This source is included for **FMD/VLCD and modified-fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** In 100 primary-care patients with type 2 diabetes, FMD adjunct care reduced medication-effect score, HbA1c, and body weight versus regular care and was described as appearing safe in routine practice.

**Why it matters:** Strong clinical FMD boundary evidence, but only for supervised/primary-care type 2 diabetes context.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:hba1c, biomarker:body-weight, medication:medication-effect-score, safety:clinical-practice-safety.

**Protocol takeaway:** Adjacent FMD/diabetes evidence; use safety and population caveats, not direct protocol claims.

**Directness and limitations:** Directness is `adjacent_variant`. T2D population, medication changes, 5-day FMD cycles, clinical supervision; not zero-calorie 24–72 hour fasting. Population mismatch: Primary-care diabetes management, not healthy-adult prolonged fasting.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
