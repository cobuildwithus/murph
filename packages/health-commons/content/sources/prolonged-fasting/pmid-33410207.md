This source is included for **eating-disorder and restriction-risk boundary**.

**Findings:** This review emphasizes eating-disorder risk among adolescents with obesity and supports careful screening rather than BMI-based clearance.

**Why it matters:** Helps prevent BMI-based false reassurance: adolescents with obesity may still have restrictive or disordered-eating risk requiring careful screening.

**Potential experiment signals:** adolescent safety boundaries, screening and contraindications, population mismatch.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use BMI alone to clear users for fasting experiments.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness and caveat:** Adolescents with obesity, not adult prolonged fasting. Adolescent obesity context, not fasting-specific.
