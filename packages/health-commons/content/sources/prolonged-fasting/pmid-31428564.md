This source is included for **safety and adverse events**.

**Findings:** A small pilot found measured low glucose in symptomatic non-diabetic young women and linked lower glucose with symptoms and time since the previous meal.

**Why it matters:** Background for hypoglycemia-like symptoms in non-diabetic people and for CGM/symptom endpoint design.

**Potential experiment signals:** hypoglycemic symptoms, CGM glucose ≤70 mg/dL, clinically significant hypoglycemia, time since previous meal

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as susceptibility and measurement context for symptoms; do not infer fasting risk rates.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** measurement_context.

**Population and exposure:** Young women without diabetes reporting regular hypoglycemic symptoms. Exposure/intervention: Usual-life symptom reporting with blinded continuous glucose monitoring; not a fasting intervention. Comparator/control: Within-person symptom/glucose concordance. Duration/follow-up: 7 days of blinded continuous glucose monitoring..

**Effect direction or estimates:** All participants had measured hypoglycemia during CGM in extracted source text, and lower glucose was associated with symptoms and time since previous meal.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** Hypoglycemic symptoms in non-diabetic symptomatic individuals.

**Limitations:** Small pilot, all young women, selected for symptoms; not a fasting trial.

**Population mismatch:** Measurement/susceptibility context only; no 24-72 hour fasting exposure.
