This source is included for **eating-disorder and restriction-risk boundary**.

**Findings:** This National Task Force review assessed dieting and ED development in adults with overweight/obesity and is retained as mixed adult dieting context.

**Why it matters:** Older foundational review with mixed/null adult context; helps avoid overextending adolescent fasting findings to all adult weight-loss dieting.

**Potential experiment signals:** eating disorder development, population mismatch, mixed evidence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not infer every adult caloric-restriction context has the same ED-risk profile as adolescent fasting.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness and caveat:** Adult dieting for weight control, not direct prolonged fasting. Older narrative review and not protocol-specific.
