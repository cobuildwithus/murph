This source is included for **eating-disorder and restriction-risk boundary**.

**Findings:** The USPSTF evidence review found no direct studies of screening benefits/harms and 17 accuracy studies (N=6,804) for eating-disorder screening tests.

**Why it matters:** Systematic evidence review underpinning USPSTF; useful for choosing cautious screen language and avoiding claims that brief screens rule out ED risk.

**Potential experiment signals:** screening and contraindications, diagnostic accuracy, referral boundaries.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use screening plus human review/referral, not automated clearance.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness and caveat:** General screening evidence, not fasting participants. Accuracy evidence does not establish fasting safety.
