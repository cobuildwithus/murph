This source is included for **eating-disorder and restriction-risk boundary**.

**Findings:** In a 5-year prospective study of 496 adolescent girls, fasting as a weight-control behavior predicted later binge-eating and bulimic pathology risk.

**Why it matters:** Foundational prospective evidence directly naming fasting and later binge/bulimic outcomes; central for adolescent exclusion and referral boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** binge/restrict cycles, bulimic pathology, restrictive behavior.

**Protocol takeaway:** Adolescents and people using fasting for weight-control/compensatory motives should be treated as high-risk for protocol participation.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness and caveat:** Youth weight-control fasting, not adult protocolized 24–72-hour fasting. Exposure was self-reported weight-control fasting, not a supervised adult prolonged-fast intervention.
