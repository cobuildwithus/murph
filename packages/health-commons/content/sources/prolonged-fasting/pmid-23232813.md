This source is included for **safety and adverse events**.

**Findings:** The case report describes hyponatremia at presentation after a 40-day acaloric water-only fast. During refeeding, the patient developed hypokalemia, hypophosphatemia, and hypomagnesemia, consistent with refeeding-risk physiology.

**Why it matters:** Extreme-duration boundary case for electrolyte and refeeding complications.

**Potential experiment signals:** hyponatremia, hypokalemia, hypophosphatemia, hypomagnesemia, refeeding syndrome

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only for hard-stop and refeeding warnings around prolonged or unsupervised extension beyond the protocol.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** adjacent_variant.

**Population and exposure:** One adult woman after a 40-day water-only religious fast. Exposure/intervention: Forty-day acaloric water-only fast followed by hospitalization and refeeding. Comparator/control: None. Duration/follow-up: 40-day fast plus refeeding period..

**Effect direction or estimates:** The case presented with hyponatremia and developed hypokalemia, hypophosphatemia, and hypomagnesemia during refeeding.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** Hyponatremia on admission and electrolyte derangements during refeeding.

**Limitations:** Single extreme-duration religious-fast case; no incidence estimate; not applicable to expected 24-72 hour risk.

**Population mismatch:** Duration far exceeds protocol and includes hospitalization/refeeding after an extreme fast.
