This source is included for **eating-disorder and restriction-risk boundary**.

**Findings:** The AAP clinical report warns that adolescent weight-loss efforts can escalate into severe restriction, skipped meals, prolonged starvation, purging, diet pills, or laxative use.

**Why it matters:** Professional pediatric guidance for avoiding harmful weight-control messaging and recognizing eating-disorder risk while addressing weight.

**Potential experiment signals:** adolescent safety boundaries, screening and contraindications, referral boundaries.

**Protocol takeaway:** Adolescents should be excluded or handled only under clinical care; do not frame prolonged fasting as routine youth self-experimentation.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness and caveat:** Adolescent clinical prevention guidance, not adult prolonged-fasting efficacy evidence. Guideline evidence, not protocol efficacy evidence.
