This source is included for **Clinical/residential supervised fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** ESPEN cancer nutrition guideline emphasizes nutrition intervention for malnourished or at-risk cancer patients and cautions against energy-restricting approaches when malnutrition risk is present.

**Why it matters:** Major counterweight to oncology fasting enthusiasm: cancer/malnutrition risk changes the safety calculus.

**Potential experiment signals:** weight loss, malnutrition risk, treatment tolerance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety boundary against fasting in cancer/malnutrition contexts without clinical direction.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Patients with cancer, especially those malnourished or at risk of malnutrition.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Clinical nutrition recommendations; caution against energy-restricting provisions in vulnerable cancer patients.
- **Comparator/control:** Not applicable.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Guideline rather than fasting-duration study.
- **Endpoints:** malnutrition risk, nutrition intervention, fasting during chemotherapy, safety
- **Effect estimate or direction:** Guideline recommends nutrition support for malnourished/at-risk cancer patients and does not support energy-restricting/fasting approaches as routine chemotherapy adjuncts.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Malnutrition and treatment tolerance risk are central safety concerns.
- **Limitations:** Guideline source; not a fasting trial.
- **Population mismatch:** Cancer nutrition safety boundary, not healthy-protocol evidence.
- **Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** general_guideline
