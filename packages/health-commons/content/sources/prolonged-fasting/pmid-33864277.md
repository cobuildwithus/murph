This source is included for **eating-disorder and restriction-risk boundary**.

**Findings:** This systematic review supports that restrictive eating disorders can occur in higher-weight persons and should not be dismissed by BMI.

**Why it matters:** Systematic review supporting the boundary that normal or higher BMI does not rule out restrictive ED pathology or need for referral.

**Potential experiment signals:** restrictive behavior, population mismatch, referral boundaries.

**Protocol takeaway:** Fasting-screening boundaries must include atypical anorexia/restrictive ED risk.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness and caveat:** Higher-weight restrictive ED populations, not fasting-protocol participants. Population-safety evidence, not protocol efficacy evidence.
