This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** During a 40-hour fast in six healthy adults, selected myogenic and atrogenic gene-expression markers, plasma myostatin, and IGF-1 were not significantly altered at 15 or 40 hours compared with 3 hours post-meal.

**Why it matters:** Direct duration-window human evidence that counters overbroad claims that a 40-hour fast necessarily activates muscle atrophy or growth-remodeling pathways.

**Potential experiment signals:** myogenic gene expression, atrogenic gene expression, plasma myostatin, IGF-1, skeletal muscle biopsy.

**Protocol takeaway:** A 40-hour fast did not show marked alteration in these muscle mass regulatory markers in this small healthy-adult sample.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Study design and directness:** Small mechanistic biopsy study during a 40-hour fast.. Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours): `direct_protocol`.

**Comparator/control:** Serial biopsies and blood samples at 3, 15, and 40 hours into the fast

**Duration/follow-up:** 40-hour fast

**Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event data extracted.

**Limitations:** Very small sample; molecular markers only; no functional muscle, performance, or long-term body-composition outcomes.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy young adults; not directly generalizable to sarcopenia risk, older adults, athletes under training load, or chronic undernutrition.
