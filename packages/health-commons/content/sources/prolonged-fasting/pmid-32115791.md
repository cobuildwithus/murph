This source is included for **refeeding, electrolytes, and thiamine safety**.

**Findings:** ASPEN defines refeeding syndrome severity by percentage decreases in serum phosphorus, potassium, and/or magnesium, and/or organ dysfunction due to those changes or thiamine deficiency, occurring within 5 days of calorie reintroduction.

**Why it matters:** Consensus definition and management framework for electrolyte shifts after restarting calories; critical boundary source after longer fasts.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:serum-phosphate, biomarker:serum-potassium, biomarker:serum-magnesium, biomarker:thiamine-status, biomarker:fluid-balance, biomarker:refeeding-syndrome-symptoms, biomarker:refeeding-risk.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as the key modern consensus framework for electrolyte/thiamine safety definitions and monitoring boundaries.
