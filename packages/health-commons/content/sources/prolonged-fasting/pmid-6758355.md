This source is included for **implementation, hydration, and refeed context**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-6758355-fasting-complications-review` — The review reports fasting complications including gout, urate nephrolithiasis, postural hypotension, and cardiac arrhythmias; it also describes rapid early weight loss during fasting that slows over time.

**Why it matters:** This source preserves older safety observations that should not be lost when modern fasting protocols emphasize benefits.

**Potential experiment signals:** uric acid, orthostatic symptoms, heart rhythm symptoms, body weight, hydration symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only context for complications and historical pathophysiology; do not use as efficacy evidence.

**Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** `clinical_supervised`.

**Population mismatch:** Historical/prolonged fasting cases and clinical contexts differ from modern 24–72 hour protocols.

**Limitations:** Older narrative review with heterogeneous source material and prolonged-fasting contexts; complication data may not quantify risk for 24–72 hour healthy fasting.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
