This source is included for **FMD/VLCD and modified-fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** In 32 normal-weight/overweight participants, modified ADF lowered body weight and fat mass, with triglyceride, LDL particle size, CRP, adiponectin, and leptin signals reported; several lipid/inflammatory markers were unchanged.

**Why it matters:** Provides randomized modified-ADF context but includes calories on fast days.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:body-weight, biomarker:fat-mass, biomarker:triglycerides, biomarker:ldl-particle-size, biomarker:crp, biomarker:leptin.

**Protocol takeaway:** Adjacent variant only; it cannot establish outcomes for zero-calorie 24–72 hour fasting.

**Directness and limitations:** Directness is `adjacent_variant`. Small sample, 12-week dietary program, modified fast days not water-only, larger trials needed. Population mismatch: Repeated modified ADF in normal/overweight adults, not a single 24–72 hour fast.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
