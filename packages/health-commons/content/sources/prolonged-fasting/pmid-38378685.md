This source is included for **FMD/VLCD and modified-fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** Exploratory analyses associated three FMD cycles with lower hepatic/abdominal fat markers, reduced insulin-resistance/prediabetes markers, immune-profile changes, and a biological-age estimate reduction; not all participants improved and clinical outcomes were not observed.

**Why it matters:** Recent FMD biomarker source useful for mechanistic context and for caveating biological-age claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:hepatic-fat, biomarker:insulin-resistance, immune:lymphoid-myeloid-ratio, biomarker:biological-age, biomarker:body-weight.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only; do not cite as direct evidence that 24–72 hour water-only fasting reduces biological age.

**Directness and limitations:** Directness is `adjacent_variant`. Secondary/exploratory biomarker analyses, estimated age metrics, FMD rather than water-only fast; population and responder heterogeneity. Population mismatch: FMD cycles in trial cohorts, not a standalone 24–72 hour fast.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
