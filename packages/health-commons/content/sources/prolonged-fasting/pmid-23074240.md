**Findings:** Accessible snippets describe coordinated adaptation in which increased adipose lipolytic flux supports liver and muscle lipid use, fatty acid oxidation supports ketone production, and lipid-induced effects on glucose oxidation and uptake are strong.

**Why it matters:** Integrates lipid and glucose substrate shifts relevant to the 24-72 hour fasting timecourse.

**Potential experiment signals:** lipolysis, glucose uptake, ketogenesis, acylcarnitines

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to explain why fasting can both increase fat-derived fuels and transiently reduce glucose utilization.
