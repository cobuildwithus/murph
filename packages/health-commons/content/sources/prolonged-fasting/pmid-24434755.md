This source is included for **Clinical/residential supervised fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** In 30 women with obesity/metabolic syndrome strata, 7-day modified fasting was associated with cardiometabolic and psychological changes; the design was small, clinical, and longer than 72 hours.

**Why it matters:** Useful for metabolic-clinic context and for separating modified/longer fasting from 24–72 hour water-only protocols.

**Potential experiment signals:** weight, lipids/glucose, psychological measures, inflammation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent clinical metabolic evidence only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Obese female subjects with and without metabolic syndrome.
- **Intervention/exposure:** 7-day modified fasting program.
- **Comparator/control:** Metabolic syndrome versus non-metabolic syndrome subgroups; no randomized nonfasting control.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 7 days; exceeds 72 hours.
- **Endpoints:** cardiometabolic markers, psychological measures, inflammatory markers, weight
- **Effect estimate or direction:** Reported cardiometabolic and psychological responses during 7-day modified fasting; direct effect estimates not extracted beyond source-level direction.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Safety/adverse-event details not extracted.
- **Limitations:** Small female clinical cohort; modified fasting; longer than protocol and disease/obesity-adjacent population.
- **Population mismatch:** Obesity/metabolic syndrome clinical setting and 7-day modified fast.
- **Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** clinical_supervised
