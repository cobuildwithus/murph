This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** The source examined splanchnic metabolism after a near-three-day fast and reported fasting-duration and ethanol-challenge effects on free fatty acid transport/storage and triglyceride secretion; ethanol-related results should remain separated from fasting-only protocol claims.

**Why it matters:** This is near-72-hour direct physiology evidence, but the ethanol component is an adjacent challenge that should be separated from fasting-only claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** free fatty acids, splanchnic glucose balance, amino acid flux, triglyceride secretion.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only for splanchnic fuel-metabolism context at about 69 hours; do not use ethanol-challenge findings as a fasting protocol benefit.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
