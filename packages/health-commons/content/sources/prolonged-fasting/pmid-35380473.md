This source is included for **safety and adverse events**.

**Findings:** An acute 24-hour fast was reported to reduce ambulatory arterial pressure and enhance vagal-cardiac modulation in young normotensive volunteers.

**Why it matters:** Direct 24-hour fasting physiology for blood pressure/autonomic monitoring context.

**Potential experiment signals:** ambulatory blood pressure, autonomic cardiovascular control, MSNA, vagal-cardiac modulation

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as cardiovascular physiology context, not as general safety proof.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** same_mechanism.

**Population and exposure:** Young normotensive volunteers. Exposure/intervention: 24-hour fast compared with fed state. Comparator/control: Fed state / within-person comparison. Duration/follow-up: 24 hours..

**Effect direction or estimates:** Extracted text reports that acute fasting reduced ambulatory arterial pressure and enhanced vagal-cardiac modulation.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** Safety-adjacent cardiovascular physiology; no adverse-event incidence extracted.

**Limitations:** Young normotensive volunteers; mechanistic endpoint study; not powered for harms.

**Population mismatch:** Direct 24-hour duration but narrow healthy population and not a 72-hour trial.
