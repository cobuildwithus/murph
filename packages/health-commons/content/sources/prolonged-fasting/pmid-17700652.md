This source is included for **refeeding, electrolytes, and thiamine safety**.

**Findings:** The case-based review highlights that refeeding syndrome can involve low phosphate, potassium, magnesium, thiamine deficiency, sodium/water retention, and cardiac complications; it presents prevention and treatment guidance for high-risk clinical cases.

**Why it matters:** Foundational case-based review from clinical nutrition experts, explicitly covering low phosphate/potassium/magnesium, thiamine, and salt-water retention features.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:serum-phosphate, biomarker:serum-potassium, biomarker:serum-magnesium, biomarker:thiamine-status, biomarker:fluid-balance, biomarker:heart-rhythm.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for symptom/electrolyte safety boundaries and to avoid treating refeeding as merely a calorie issue.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
