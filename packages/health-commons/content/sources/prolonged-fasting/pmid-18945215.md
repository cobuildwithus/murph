This source is included for **metabolic timecourse and mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Accessible snippets report that after 62 hours fasting, insulin-mediated suppression of muscle long-chain acylcarnitines was attenuated compared with 14 hours fasting.

**Why it matters:** Muscle acylcarnitines provide mechanistic context for fasting-induced insulin resistance and lipid flux.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle acylcarnitines, insulin-stimulated suppression, lipid intermediates

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as muscle-metabolite context for the 48-72 hour window, not as direct benefit evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction boundaries:** Directness: `direct_protocol`. Population mismatch: Male lean sample only; does not generalize to women, obesity, or older adults. Limitations: Sample size not extracted from accessible snippets; lean healthy men only; muscle biopsy/metabolite context rather than clinical outcome. Safety notes: No adverse-event signal extracted.
