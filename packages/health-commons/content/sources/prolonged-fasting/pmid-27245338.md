This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** The study directly compared obese and lean subjects before and after a 72-hour fast and reported differential regulation of lipid and protein metabolism across groups.

**Why it matters:** This direct 72-hour source helps avoid assuming lean-person physiology applies unchanged to people with obesity.

**Potential experiment signals:** body weight, blood glucose, fasting insulin, free fatty acids, lipolysis, protein metabolism.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as direct evidence for population-difference language: lean and obese adults may regulate lipid and protein metabolism differently during a 72-hour fast.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
