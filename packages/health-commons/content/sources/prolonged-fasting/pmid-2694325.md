This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** The study reported that 72 hours of fasting decreased responses of counterregulatory hormones to insulin-induced hypoglycemia in normal men.

**Why it matters:** This is a direct safety-boundary source for hypoglycemia physiology, especially when translating to users on glucose-lowering medications or with hypoglycemia risk.

**Potential experiment signals:** blood glucose, hypoglycemia symptoms, glucagon, epinephrine, cortisol, growth hormone.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only evidence: after a 72-hour fast, experimentally induced hypoglycemia may trigger altered counterregulatory responses.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
