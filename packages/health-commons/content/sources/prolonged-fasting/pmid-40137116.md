This source is included for **FMD/VLCD and modified-fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** The narrative review summarizes human FMD studies as showing reductions in BMI/waist and metabolic-profile improvements, while emphasizing the FMD evidence base rather than zero-calorie fasting.

**Why it matters:** Useful background synthesis but not a primary efficacy source.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:bmi, anthropometric:waist-circumference, biomarker:lipids, biomarker:glucose, biomarker:lean-mass.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only; do not use narrative review as direct protocol proof.

**Directness and limitations:** Directness is `adjacent_variant`. Narrative review design and FMD-specific scope; no causal protocol claim for prolonged fasting. Population mismatch: FMD human-study literature, not direct 24–72 hour water-only fasting.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
