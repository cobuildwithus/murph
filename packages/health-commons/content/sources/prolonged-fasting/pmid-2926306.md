This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** The study measured broad hormone changes during and after a 72-hour fast; pancreatic glucagon rose modestly during fasting and changed during refeeding, while multiple endocrine axes were tracked.

**Why it matters:** Foundational direct 72-hour hormone physiology source; relevant to endocrine adaptation but not evidence of durable clinical benefit.

**Potential experiment signals:** blood glucose, fasting insulin, glucagon, cortisol, thyroid hormones, gastrointestinal hormones.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as endocrine context and avoid overstating individual hormone changes as wellness benefits.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
