This source is included for **Clinical/residential supervised fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** Scoping review of 14 human trials found inflammatory-biomarker evidence limited and inconsistent; many studies reported no change or increases during fasting, including frequent CRP increases.

**Why it matters:** Directly guards against overclaiming anti-inflammatory effects from prolonged fasting.

**Potential experiment signals:** CRP, IL-6, TNF-alpha.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to state inflammation evidence is mixed/does not confirm a simple anti-inflammatory claim.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Human clinical trials of prolonged fasting lasting at least 48 hours.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Prolonged fasting of at least 48 hours across eligible human trials.
- **Comparator/control:** Varies across included trials.
- **Duration/follow-up:** At least 48 hours; study durations varied.
- **Endpoints:** CRP, TNF-alpha, IL-6, inflammatory biomarkers
- **Effect estimate or direction:** Review found inflammatory biomarker results limited/inconsistent, with most studies showing no change or increases during fasting and CRP often rising.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Safety events were not the primary extracted outcome here.
- **Limitations:** Scoping review; heterogeneous trials; inflammatory claims remain uncertain.
- **Population mismatch:** Includes varied prolonged fasting protocols/populations, not only 24–72 hour healthy protocols.
- **Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** measurement_context
