This source is included for **diabetes, medication, and hypoglycemia safety**.

**Findings:** The International Hypoglycaemia Study Group position statement recommends reporting glucose concentrations below 3.0 mmol/L (54 mg/dL) as clinically significant biochemical hypoglycemia in clinical trials.

**Why it matters:** Foundational threshold source for clinically significant hypoglycemia; useful for monitoring stop/rescue boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** blood glucose below 54 mg/dL, clinically significant hypoglycemia, CGM or glucose meter reporting.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as measurement context for hypoglycemia thresholds when discussing diabetes medication safety.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
