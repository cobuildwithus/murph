This source is included for **FMD/VLCD and modified-fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** The randomized parallel study compared low- and high-protein 7-day FMD formulations against control in 46 adults, assessing cardiometabolic, autophagy, and microbiome-related endpoints.

**Why it matters:** Shows FMD composition and duration materially differ from the target fasting protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:cardiometabolic-health, mechanism:autophagy, biomarker:glucose, microbiome:gut-microbiome.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only; do not use as direct support for 24–72 hour zero-calorie fasting.

**Directness and limitations:** Directness is `adjacent_variant`. Seven-day low-calorie formulated diet, not target duration/dose; full comparative effect extraction not completed. Population mismatch: FMD formulation study in healthy adults, not water-only prolonged fasting.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
