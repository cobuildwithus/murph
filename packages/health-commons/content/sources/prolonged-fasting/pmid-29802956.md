This source is included for **diabetes, medication, and hypoglycemia safety**.

**Findings:** This observational Ramadan study evaluated people with T2D continuing SGLT2 inhibitors and focused on ketonemia, blood pressure, renal function, and hypoglycemia/dehydration safety signals.

**Why it matters:** Primary Ramadan SGLT2 safety study measuring ketonemia-related boundaries rather than wellness efficacy.

**Potential experiment signals:** ketonemia, eGFR, blood pressure, hypoglycemia.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as SGLT2 safety context with caution; do not use to clear SGLT2 users for prolonged fasting.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
