This source is included for **snowball/gap-fill candidates**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-38429390-seven-day-water-only-proteome` — The study reported average weight loss of 5.7 ± 0.8 kg and measured roughly 3,000 plasma proteins. Systemic changes were reported as evident only after about three days, with more than 1,000 responding proteins and putative beneficial as well as adverse health-consequence estimates.

**Why it matters:** This source is strong mechanistic context for when systemic fasting biology may become detectable, but its seven-day exposure exceeds the 24–72 hour protocol target.

**Potential experiment signals:** body weight, ketosis timing, plasma proteins, lean mass, fat mass, adverse-effect estimates.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent mechanistic context: do not claim that protein-level changes observed after seven days necessarily occur within 24–72 hours.

**Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** `adjacent_variant`.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy volunteers on seven-day water-only fasting are adjacent but longer than the target protocol.

**Limitations:** Small sample, seven-day duration, mechanistic outcomes, no randomized comparator, and estimated health consequences are not clinical outcomes.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
