**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-40596030-metabolic-hormonal-eight-day-water-only-exercise` — The study reported that fasting had a stronger impact than exercise on glucose, uric acid, IGF-1, IGF-2, growth hormone, leptin, and cortisol, improved total antioxidant status, and reduced lipid peroxidation; exercise enhanced fasting effects for triglycerides, insulin, growth hormone, total antioxidant status, peroxide markers, and IL-6, while total protein and lactate changes were attributed to exercise.

**Why it matters:** This study provides adjacent water-only fasting physiology in humans but is longer than the protocol target and confounded by exercise.

**Potential experiment signals:** glucose, uric acid, insulin, HOMA-IR, IGF-1, IGF-2, growth hormone, leptin, cortisol, triglycerides, total antioxidant status, lipid peroxidation, IL-6.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent mechanism/safety context only; do not promote the 8-day exercise-combined findings as direct evidence for a 24–72 hour fasting protocol.

**Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** `adjacent_variant`.

**Population mismatch:** Middle-aged men undergoing eight-day fast plus aerobic exercise differ from general 24–72 hour fasters.

**Limitations:** Small male-only sample, no non-fasting control, eight-day duration, and exercise co-intervention prevents isolating fasting effects.
