This source is included for **metabolic timecourse and mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Accessible snippets indicate FGF21 and GH were associated with insulin resistance in anorectic and GH-deficient subjects; this is population-mismatched endocrine context rather than direct healthy 24-72 hour fasting evidence.

**Why it matters:** Helps prevent overclaiming FGF21 as a simple early 24-72 hour fasting marker in humans.

**Potential experiment signals:** FGF21, growth hormone, insulin resistance

**Protocol takeaway:** Treat FGF21 claims cautiously and separate clinical endocrine-population findings from general fasting claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction boundaries:** Directness: `same_mechanism`. Population mismatch: Anorectic and GH-deficient subjects are not representative of general self-experimentation fasting populations. Limitations: Clinical population mismatch; sample size and specific fasting-duration effects not extracted; not a direct protocol-efficacy study. Safety notes: No adverse-event signal extracted.
