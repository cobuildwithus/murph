This source is included for **adjacent fasting variants and terminology**.

**Findings:** The TREAT trial tested 16:8 TRE against consistent meal timing and did not establish a clear between-group advantage for key weight/metabolic outcomes, with body-composition concerns relevant to interpretation.

**Why it matters:** Sentinel TRE RCT with null/mixed implications for weight/metabolic claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** Body weight; Lean mass/body composition; Glucose; Lipids; Adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a negative/no-clear-advantage TRE boundary source, not as direct prolonged-fasting evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
