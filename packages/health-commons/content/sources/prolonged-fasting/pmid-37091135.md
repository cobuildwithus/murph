This source is included for **safety and adverse events**.

**Findings:** The case report describes lower-limb DVT at the end of a two-week fast, serving as a thrombosis safety boundary rather than incidence evidence.

**Why it matters:** Boundary case for thrombosis risk discussions during prolonged fasting.

**Potential experiment signals:** deep vein thrombosis, dehydration, thrombosis, immobility/hypercoagulability

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a safety-boundary case only, especially for dehydration/immobility/thrombosis warnings.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** adjacent_variant.

**Effect direction or estimates:** The case reports lower-limb DVT at the end of a two-week water-only fast.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** Deep vein thrombosis after prolonged fasting; causality/incidence cannot be inferred.

**Limitations:** Single case, extreme duration, possible dry-fasting/dehydration and other confounders.

**Population mismatch:** Far longer than protocol and includes case-report confounding.
