This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** Adipose-tissue norepinephrine spillover increased after 72 hours of fasting, and arterial norepinephrine increased; total-body norepinephrine spillover, forearm norepinephrine spillover, epinephrine, and energy expenditure did not show the same increase.

**Why it matters:** This directly informs mechanistic claims about sympathetic activation and lipolysis during a 72-hour fast while preserving the null whole-body energy-expenditure finding.

**Potential experiment signals:** resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, free fatty acids, energy expenditure, subjective palpitations or stress.

**Protocol takeaway:** Regional adipose sympathetic activity can rise by 72 hours, but this should be framed as mechanistic context, not as proof of a user-facing benefit.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
