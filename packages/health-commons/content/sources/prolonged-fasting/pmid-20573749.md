This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** The study identifies fasting-induced insulin resistance during a 60-hour fast and frames skeletal-muscle mitochondrial dysfunction as a consequence rather than the cause of that insulin resistance.

**Why it matters:** Directly inside the protocol window and preserves an important mixed/negative endpoint: fasting-induced insulin resistance.

**Potential experiment signals:** insulin sensitivity, blood glucose, fasting insulin, free fatty acids, fat oxidation, mitochondrial function.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as direct 60-hour mechanistic evidence that insulin resistance during fasting can occur and should not be framed as a simple metabolic-health benefit.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
