This source is included for **snowball/gap-fill candidates**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-17263085-mood-leptin-cortisol-modified-fast` — Compared with controls, the fasting group lost more weight (4.8 ± 1.2 kg versus 1.6 ± 0.9 kg), had larger leptin decreases (58% versus 20%; P < 0.001), increased cortisol (17%; P < 0.001), and showed late-phase mood improvement (P < 0.01) that was not related to weight loss, leptin, or cortisol.

**Why it matters:** This source has a comparator and mechanistic mood/endocrine endpoints, but the intervention is modified fasting in chronic-pain inpatients for longer than 72 hours.

**Potential experiment signals:** mood, body weight, leptin, cortisol.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent mechanistic/clinical context for mood, leptin, and cortisol, not as direct support for 24–72 hour fasting efficacy.

**Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** `adjacent_variant`.

**Population mismatch:** Patients with chronic pain under inpatient care differ materially from typical healthy 24–72 hour self-experimenters.

**Limitations:** Chronic-pain inpatient population, non-water modified fast, eight-day duration, and likely non-randomized control structure; not direct protocol evidence.
