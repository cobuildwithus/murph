This source is included for **refeeding, electrolytes, and thiamine safety**.

**Findings:** The case report describes Wernicke’s encephalopathy after a prolonged water-only fasting diet causing malnutrition/thiamine deficiency, with clinical emphasis on immediate parenteral thiamine when suspected and follow-up oral thiamine therapy.

**Why it matters:** Extreme-duration thiamine-deficiency case; important for hard boundaries around prolonged/repeated fasting and neurologic stop signs.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:thiamine-status, biomarker:neurologic-symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for hard neurologic stop signs and clinician referral, not expected-event rates.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
