This source is included for **FMD/VLCD and modified-fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** Across 99 RCTs involving 6582 adults, intermittent fasting strategies generally produced weight and cardiometabolic effects similar to continuous energy restriction, with only minor differences and some short-duration ADF signals.

**Why it matters:** High-level context that prevents overstating intermittent fasting superiority over calorie restriction.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:body-weight, biomarker:bmi, biomarker:lipids, vitals:blood-pressure, biomarker:glucose.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only; not direct evidence for one 24–72 hour water-only fast.

**Directness and limitations:** Directness is `adjacent_variant`. Aggregates heterogeneous fasting strategies and populations; many interventions are not prolonged zero-calorie fasts. Population mismatch: IF diet-strategy evidence, not target protocol directness.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
