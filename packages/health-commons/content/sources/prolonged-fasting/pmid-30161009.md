This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** The study reported that skeletal-muscle autophagy was only modestly affected by 36 hours of fasting in humans, and that training state influenced autophagy signaling and mediator responses.

**Why it matters:** Direct 36-hour human evidence that constrains broad “autophagy boost” claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** skeletal muscle autophagy markers, training state, AMPK/autophagy signaling mediators.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not claim a robust, uniform autophagy response from a 36-hour fast based on this study; effects were modest and training-state dependent.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Study design and directness:** Acute mechanistic skeletal-muscle study.. Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours): `direct_protocol`.

**Comparator/control:** Training-state comparison and pre-/post-fasting muscle markers

**Duration/follow-up:** 36-hour fast

**Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event data extracted.

**Limitations:** Mechanistic muscle-marker study; does not prove whole-body autophagy benefits or clinical outcomes.

**Population mismatch:** Training-state sample; not directly generalizable to sedentary, older, or metabolically impaired populations.
