This source is included for **diabetes, medication, and hypoglycemia safety**.

**Findings:** The 2026 ADA pharmacologic treatment standards provide current context for diabetes medication classes and safety considerations relevant to fasting risk, including therapies that increase hypoglycemia risk and therapies with lower hypoglycemia risk.

**Why it matters:** Current medication-class standard to contextualize insulin, sulfonylureas, meglitinides, metformin, and SGLT2 inhibitors.

**Potential experiment signals:** hypoglycemia risk, insulin therapy, sulfonylurea exposure, medication selection.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to support medication-screening language rather than medication instructions.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
