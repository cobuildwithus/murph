This source is included for **metabolic timecourse and mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Accessible article text reported the low-carbohydrate/high-fat condition produced 44% greater beta-hydroxybutyrate AUC than the high-carbohydrate/low-fat condition, reached ketosis around 12 hours on average, while the high-carbohydrate/low-fat condition did not reach the ketosis threshold by 24 hours; hormone differences appeared at 1 hour but not at 24 hours.

**Why it matters:** Directly shows that the meal/shake before a 24-hour fast can change ketosis timecourse.

**Potential experiment signals:** beta-hydroxybutyrate AUC, time to ketosis, CGM glucose, insulin, glucagon, GLP-1, GIP

**Protocol takeaway:** The pre-fast meal can materially alter ketone readouts during a 24-hour fast; document pre-fast macronutrients.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Extraction boundaries:** Directness: `direct_protocol`. Population mismatch: Older sedentary adults with elevated BMI; not lean young adults or clinical metabolic disease patients. Limitations: Pre-fast shake is an experimental co-exposure; older sedentary overweight/obesity sample; 24-hour fast only. Safety notes: One participant quit due to low tolerance for blood draws; no fasting-specific serious adverse event signal extracted.
