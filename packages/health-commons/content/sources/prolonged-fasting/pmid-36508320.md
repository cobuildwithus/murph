This source is included for **diabetes, medication, and hypoglycemia safety**.

**Findings:** INTERFAST-2 randomized 46 insulin-treated people with T2D to intermittent fasting or control for 12 weeks with CGM and counseling and reported IF as feasible/safe while improving glycemic control signals and reducing insulin dose/body weight.

**Why it matters:** Important insulin-treated T2D IF trial; strong adjacent evidence for CGM-monitored hypoglycemia risk management.

**Potential experiment signals:** CGM glucose, insulin dose, body weight, hypoglycemia.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent clinical evidence; preserve the need for supervision/CGM and do not generalize to unsupervised prolonged fasting.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
