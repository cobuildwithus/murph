This source is included for **Clinical/residential supervised fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** In 40 volunteers, 14-day Buchinger fasting reported decreases in several lipid measures while ApoB, Lp(a), fibrinogen, and hsCRP were unchanged; the program exceeded 72 hours.

**Why it matters:** Useful for lipid/lipoprotein signal selection and for caveating longer supervised modified fasting.

**Potential experiment signals:** lipids, hsCRP, fibrinogen.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent lipid biomarker context, not direct protocol proof.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Adult volunteers undergoing a 14-day Buchinger fasting program.
- **Intervention/exposure:** 14-day Buchinger fasting, about 250 kcal/day.
- **Comparator/control:** Within-person baseline/day 7/day 14 comparison.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 14 days; exceeds 72 hours.
- **Endpoints:** triglycerides, VLDL-TG, LDL-C, HDL-C, ApoA1, ApoB, Lp(a), fibrinogen, hsCRP
- **Effect estimate or direction:** Reported reductions in triglycerides, VLDL-TG, LDL-C, HDL-C/ApoA1; ApoB, Lp(a), fibrinogen and hsCRP unchanged in extracted summary.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Safety/adverse events not extracted as a primary finding.
- **Limitations:** Small cohort, long modified fast, no randomized comparator.
- **Population mismatch:** 14-day Buchinger program exceeds protocol duration.
- **Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** clinical_supervised
