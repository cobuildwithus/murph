This source is included for **Clinical/residential supervised fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** In 131 breast cancer patients, FMD around chemotherapy reported no toxicity difference despite dexamethasone omission and response signals in some analyses; intervention and population are adjacent.

**Why it matters:** Large adjacent FMD oncology RCT that helps separate FMD/clinical-oncology findings from direct water-only protocol claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** toxicity, quality of life, glucose/insulin, adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for FMD oncology boundary, not for direct prolonged fasting claims.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** HER2-negative stage II/III breast cancer patients receiving neoadjuvant chemotherapy, without diabetes and BMI over 18 kg/m².
- **Intervention/exposure:** Fasting-mimicking diet before and during chemotherapy.
- **Comparator/control:** Regular diet during chemotherapy.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Three days prior to and during neoadjuvant chemotherapy cycles; FMD rather than water-only fasting.
- **Endpoints:** toxicity, radiologic response, pathologic response, treatment adherence
- **Effect estimate or direction:** Trial reported no toxicity difference while dexamethasone was omitted in FMD group, with response signals favoring FMD in some analyses.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No overall toxicity difference extracted; oncology safety context only.
- **Limitations:** FMD not water-only; chemotherapy co-intervention; cancer population.
- **Population mismatch:** Breast cancer chemotherapy setting.
- **Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** adjacent_variant
