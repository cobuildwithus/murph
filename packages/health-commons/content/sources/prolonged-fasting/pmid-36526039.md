This source is included for **eating-disorder and restriction-risk boundary**.

**Findings:** In an undergraduate sample (N=298), current/past IF groups differed from never-fasters on binge eating, intuitive eating, and impulsivity-related measures.

**Why it matters:** Directly targets binge eating and self-regulation constructs that are relevant to contraindication and check-in language for fasting experiments.

**Potential experiment signals:** binge/restrict cycles, impulsivity, intuitive eating, mindful eating.

**Protocol takeaway:** Include binge-eating history and intuitive-eating disruption in safety screening.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness and caveat:** College intermittent-fasting status, not a prolonged-fast intervention. Cross-sectional and demographically limited sample.
