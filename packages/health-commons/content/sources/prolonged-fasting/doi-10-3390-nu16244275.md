This source is included for **eating-disorder and restriction-risk boundary**.

**Findings:** In a Polish online survey (N=214), fasting behavior was associated with higher orthorexia and eating-disorder symptom scores, with orthorexia mediating the fasting–ED symptom association in the reported path model.

**Why it matters:** Captures orthorexia and fasting-behavior overlap, an important restrictive-behavior boundary for wellness protocols that may normalize rule-bound eating.

**Potential experiment signals:** orthorexia, restrictive behavior, eating disorder symptomatology.

**Protocol takeaway:** Add orthorexia and rule-bound-eating checks to pre-fast screening.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness and caveat:** Fasting behavior broadly defined in a Polish survey, not a supervised 24–72-hour protocol. Cross-sectional mediation is not causal and does not establish fasting as the cause of ED symptoms.
