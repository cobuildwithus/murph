This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** After 72 hours fasting, lean men showed reduced insulin-stimulated glucose uptake to levels similar to obese men after 12 hours, increased lipid oxidation, large increases in skeletal-muscle ketone-related metabolites, and reduced muscle glycogen; the fasting-induced insulin resistance was interpreted as substrate competition rather than impaired insulin signaling.

**Why it matters:** Direct open-access 72-hour source preserving lean/obese differences and the mixed insulin-resistance interpretation.

**Potential experiment signals:** blood glucose, fasting insulin, ketones, fat oxidation, insulin sensitivity, muscle glycogen, skeletal muscle metabolites.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as direct 72-hour mechanistic evidence for ketone/lipid metabolic signatures, while caveating acute insulin resistance and population differences.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
