This source is included for **diabetes, medication, and hypoglycemia safety**.

**Findings:** The EPIDIAR population-based study enrolled 12,243 people with diabetes across 13 countries and reported increased severe hypoglycemia during Ramadan, making it major observational context for diabetes fasting risk.

**Why it matters:** Foundational large observational dataset for severe hypoglycemia/hyperglycemia during Ramadan fasting; important not to omit despite age.

**Potential experiment signals:** severe hypoglycemia, hospitalization context, diabetes treatment pattern.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as population-mismatched safety context: diabetes and medication exposure materially change fasting risk.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
