This source is included for **Clinical/residential supervised fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** Observational body-composition analysis reported sustained fat-mass and visceral-adipose-tissue loss with fat-free-mass recovery after prolonged water-only fasting/refeeding; median fast length exceeded 72 hours.

**Why it matters:** Useful for body-composition expectations and for distinguishing acute lean-mass loss from follow-up recovery in long supervised fasts.

**Potential experiment signals:** body composition, fat mass, fat-free mass, visceral adipose tissue.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as long-duration body-composition context only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Adults with overweight or obesity undergoing prolonged water-only fasting and whole-plant-food refeeding/maintenance.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Median 14-day water-only fast followed by refeeding and whole-plant-food maintenance.
- **Comparator/control:** Within-person body-composition time points.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Median 14-day water-only fast, about 6-day refeed and follow-up/maintenance; exceeds 72 hours.
- **Endpoints:** fat mass, fat-free mass, visceral adipose tissue, bone mineral content, body weight
- **Effect estimate or direction:** Reported sustained fat-mass/visceral-adipose-tissue loss and fat-free-mass recovery over follow-up; fat-free mass accounted for a large fraction of acute weight loss at end-fasting.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse-event rate extracted in this body-composition source draft.
- **Limitations:** Observational/secondary analysis; overweight/obesity clinical program; long fast and whole-plant diet sequence.
- **Population mismatch:** Overweight/obesity treatment and median 14-day fast, not 24–72 hour protocol.
- **Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** clinical_supervised
