This source is included for **refeeding, electrolytes, and thiamine safety**.

**Findings:** The review explains refeeding physiology as a shift from catabolic to anabolic metabolism with insulin-mediated intracellular electrolyte shifts and thiamine-related carbohydrate metabolism demands, supporting why phosphate, potassium, magnesium, and thiamine matter.

**Why it matters:** Open-access literature review useful for mechanism and older management context, especially electrolyte shifts.
