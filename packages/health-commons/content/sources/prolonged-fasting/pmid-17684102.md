This source is included for **metabolic timecourse and mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Accessible abstract text concluded that 48 hours of fasting produced a marked decline in peripheral insulin action while suppression of hepatic glucose production was maintained.

**Why it matters:** Directly demonstrates that 48-hour fasting can create peripheral insulin resistance while hepatic glucose suppression remains regulated.

**Potential experiment signals:** insulin sensitivity, hepatic glucose production, glucose kinetics

**Protocol takeaway:** Short-term fasting can transiently reduce peripheral insulin action; this should be framed as physiology, not necessarily harm or benefit.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Extraction boundaries:** Directness: `direct_protocol`. Population mismatch: Nondiabetic lean/obese groups only; does not cover diabetes medication or frailty risk. Limitations: Mechanistic physiology study; small groups; nondiabetic lean/obese sample; not designed for clinical outcomes. Safety notes: No adverse-event signal extracted from the abstract text.
