This source is included for **eating-disorder and restriction-risk boundary**.

**Findings:** This prospective high-risk adolescent-female study identified fasting and other risk factors as predictors for future DSM-5 eating-disorder outcomes.

**Why it matters:** Prospective adolescent risk-factor study that can contextualize fasting within broader ED risk screening and referral thresholds.

**Potential experiment signals:** eating disorder onset, restrictive behavior, screening and contraindications.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use fasting history and motivation as screening variables, especially in adolescents.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness and caveat:** Eating-disorder risk prediction, not direct prolonged-fasting evidence. Not a protocol trial and not adult prolonged-fasting evidence.
