This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** Fasting progressively increased fat oxidation and reduced carbohydrate oxidation to minimal quantities, while 24-hour energy expenditure was lower during fasting than during the fed condition and day-night energy-expenditure rhythmicity was not abolished.

**Why it matters:** This is high-directness, recent, controlled human evidence inside the protocol window for energy expenditure and substrate oxidation.

**Potential experiment signals:** body weight, blood glucose, fasting insulin, beta-hydroxybutyrate, fat oxidation, carbohydrate oxidation, energy expenditure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a direct 60-hour anchor for fuel-switching: fat oxidation rises and carbohydrate oxidation falls, but energy expenditure may be lower, not higher.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
