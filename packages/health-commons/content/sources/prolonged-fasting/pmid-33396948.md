This source is included for **safety and adverse events**.

**Findings:** In a small single-arm study, eight days of water-only fasting was reported to improve lower urinary tract function without negative health effects in extracted text.

**Why it matters:** Adjacent water-only fasting safety/tolerability context in a small healthy male sample.

**Potential experiment signals:** lower urinary tract symptoms, urogenital markers, sex hormones, tolerability

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as small adjacent safety context only; not a general safety proof.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** adjacent_variant.

**Population and exposure:** Healthy middle-aged men. Exposure/intervention: Eight-day water-only fasting. Comparator/control: Within-person pre/post measurements. Duration/follow-up: 8 days..

**Effect direction or estimates:** The study reported favorable lower urinary tract changes and stated no negative health effects in extracted text.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** No negative health effects reported in extracted abstract/snippet, but detailed AE extraction was limited.

**Limitations:** Small homogeneous male sample; 8-day duration exceeds protocol; no control group.

**Population mismatch:** Adjacent duration and population; not 24-72 hours and not mixed-sex community use.
