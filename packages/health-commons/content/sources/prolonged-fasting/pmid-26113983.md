This source is included for **diabetes, medication, and hypoglycemia safety**.

**Findings:** The 2015 Ramadan diabetes update emphasizes education, glucose monitoring, and treatment-regimen adjustment before fasting, and advises people at high hypoglycemia risk or with multiple complications against prolonged fasting.

**Why it matters:** Open-access Ramadan update with medication class considerations and practical hypoglycemia risk management.

**Potential experiment signals:** hypoglycemia risk, blood glucose monitoring, medication adjustment, complications.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a strong safety boundary for high-risk diabetes and medication-treated users.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
