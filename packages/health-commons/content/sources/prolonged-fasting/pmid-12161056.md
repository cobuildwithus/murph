This source is included for **diabetes, medication, and hypoglycemia safety**.

**Findings:** The study compared repaglinide with glibenclamide during Ramadan fasting and reported lower glucose-defined hypoglycemic events with repaglinide, making it medication-class safety context for fasting with secretagogues.

**Why it matters:** Older but query-relevant meglitinide/sulfonylurea comparison; useful for preserving medication-specific adjacent evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** hypoglycemic events, midday blood glucose, secretagogue medication exposure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not generalize to healthy fasters; use only to show that fasting safety in diabetes depends on medication class and adjustment.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
