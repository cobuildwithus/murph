This source is included for **refeeding, electrolytes, and thiamine safety**.

**Findings:** The case report describes a 36-year-old man with Wernicke’s encephalopathy after 40 days of water-only fasting, presenting with neurologic symptoms such as diplopia, gait instability/ataxia, and confusion.

**Why it matters:** Recent extreme-duration case report reinforcing thiamine-deficiency neurologic boundaries; not expected-event evidence for 24-72h fasting.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:thiamine-status, biomarker:neurologic-symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as extreme-duration warning and neurologic stop/referral evidence, not expected-event evidence for 24–72 hours.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
