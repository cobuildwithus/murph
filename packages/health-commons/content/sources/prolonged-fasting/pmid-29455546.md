This source is included for **safety and adverse events**.

**Findings:** In a small 11-day water-fasting cohort, uric acid and creatinine increased, highlighting renal and hyperuricemia monitoring concerns.

**Why it matters:** Boundary evidence for uric-acid and renal-marker shifts during complete water fasting.

**Potential experiment signals:** kidney function, uric acid, creatinine, glomerular filtration, blood pressure

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as renal/uric-acid safety context, not as direct protocol evidence.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** adjacent_variant.

**Population and exposure:** Ten volunteers undergoing complete water fasting and regeneration diet. Exposure/intervention: Eleven-day complete water fasting followed by regeneration diet. Comparator/control: Within-person pre/post measurements. Duration/follow-up: 11-day complete water fast plus regeneration diet..

**Effect direction or estimates:** The study reported increased uric acid and creatinine during water fasting, with renal-function markers as safety outcomes.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** Hyperuricemia and kidney-function changes are the main safety signals extracted.

**Limitations:** Small sample; duration exceeds 72 hours; no control group.

**Population mismatch:** Adjacent/safety-boundary duration beyond the target protocol.
