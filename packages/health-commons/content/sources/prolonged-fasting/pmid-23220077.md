This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** The FEELGOOD randomized crossover trial evaluated whether a 24-hour water-only fast initiates acute changes in metabolic, cardiovascular, and general-health biomarkers compared with a fed day.

**Why it matters:** Core direct 24-hour water-only fasting trial for adult human metabolic/cardiovascular endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** adherence and tolerability, body weight, glucose and insulin, ketones, blood pressure, metabolic and cardiovascular biomarkers.

**Protocol takeaway:** A 24-hour water-only fast produces acute biomarker changes, but the extracted evidence does not establish long-term benefit.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Study design and directness:** Randomized crossover trial.. Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours): `direct_protocol`.

**Comparator/control:** Ad libitum fed/usual eating day in randomized crossover order

**Duration/follow-up:** 24-hour fast with follow-up measurements across the crossover period

**Adverse events or safety notes:** Candidate row includes adherence/tolerability endpoints; detailed adverse-event counts were not extracted in this batch.

**Limitations:** Acute biomarker trial; participant count and detailed estimates should be verified from full article before synthesis.

**Population mismatch:** Broader BMI range; one-day intervention only, so it informs the protocol floor more than 48–72-hour claims.
