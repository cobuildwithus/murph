This source is included for **diabetes, medication, and hypoglycemia safety**.

**Findings:** The 2025 update recommends prioritizing therapies with low hypoglycemia risk, leveraging diabetes technology such as CGM/pumps when appropriate, counselling 6–8 weeks before Ramadan, and avoiding fasting in some high-ASCVD-risk contexts until evidence is clearer.

**Why it matters:** Newest located Ramadan medication-management update; useful for checking whether older drug-specific fasting guidance remains current.

**Potential experiment signals:** hypoglycemia, CGM technology, medication selection, ASCVD risk.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as current safety-only clinical guidance for medication-risk minimization and pre-fast counselling.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
