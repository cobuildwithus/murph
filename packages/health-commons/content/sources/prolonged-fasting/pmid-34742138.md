This source is included for **refeeding, electrolytes, and thiamine safety**.

**Findings:** The guideline situates refeeding risk within hospital nutrition screening, organization, and nutrition support services, reinforcing that high-risk refeeding is managed as part of clinical nutrition care.

**Why it matters:** General hospital nutrition guideline that references refeeding risk and prior expert algorithms; useful for clinical-context boundaries rather than fasting efficacy claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:serum-phosphate, biomarker:serum-potassium, biomarker:serum-magnesium, biomarker:fluid-balance, biomarker:refeeding-syndrome-symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as general clinical-context boundary, secondary to refeeding-specific guidelines.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
