This source is included for **diabetes, medication, and hypoglycemia safety**.

**Findings:** The AHRQ PSNet/NCBI Bookshelf case study teaches the SSTOP rule: stop SGLT2 inhibitors three days before procedures, highlighting euglycemic ketoacidosis risk around procedure-related fasting or reduced intake.

**Why it matters:** Safety-focused SGLT2 protocol source for fasting/procedure-related euglycemic ketoacidosis risk boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** euglycemic ketoacidosis, SGLT2 medication hold, procedure fasting, ketones.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as high-priority SGLT2 safety boundary for prolonged-fasting protocols.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
