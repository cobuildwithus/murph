This source is included for **FMD/VLCD and modified-fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** This randomized trial tested intermittent energy/carbohydrate restriction against daily energy restriction in 115 overweight women; it is an energy/carbohydrate-restriction program rather than fasting.

**Why it matters:** Helps define the boundary between intermittent diet programs and fasting protocols.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:body-weight, biomarker:insulin-resistance, biomarker:lipids, behavior:adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as adjacent diet-comparator context for weight and metabolic endpoints.

**Directness and limitations:** Directness is `adjacent_variant`. Women with family history of breast cancer; dietary restriction rather than zero-calorie fasting; not designed for 24–72 hour protocol directness. Population mismatch: Overweight women in a 3-month diet trial, not a general adult prolonged-fasting experiment.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
