This source is included for **safety and adverse events**.

**Findings:** The cohort reported decreased SBP, abdominal circumference, LDL, and hsCRP at end of fasting, while triglycerides and HOMA-IR increased after refeeding in extracted text. The authors flagged increased TG and HOMA-IR after refeeding as requiring further inquiry.

**Why it matters:** Important supervised prolonged water-only fasting/refeeding study with mixed cardiometabolic/refeeding signals.

**Potential experiment signals:** HOMA-IR, blood pressure, abdominal circumference, LDL cholesterol, triglycerides, hsCRP, refeeding

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent refeeding/cardiometabolic safety context, especially for mixed findings after refeeding.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** adjacent_variant.

**Population and exposure:** Overweight/obese non-diabetic adults; 48 enrolled and 26 completed the full protocol in extracted text. Exposure/intervention: Medically supervised prolonged water-only fasting followed by whole-plant-food refeeding. Comparator/control: Within-person baseline/end-of-fast/end-of-refeed observations. Duration/follow-up: Median fasting length 17 days and median refeed length 8 days in extracted text..

**Effect direction or estimates:** At end of fasting, extracted text reports decreased median systolic blood pressure, abdominal circumference, LDL, and hsCRP, but increased triglycerides and HOMA-IR after refeeding.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** Increased triglycerides and HOMA-IR after refeeding were flagged as requiring further inquiry; explicit AE extraction limited.

**Limitations:** Single-arm, residential medical center, high attrition/completion gap, much longer than protocol.

**Population mismatch:** Adjacent clinical-supervised prolonged fast, not 24-72 hours.
