This source is included for **metabolic timecourse and mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Multi-fluid proteomic and multiplex immunoassay profiling identified fasting-associated markers including PBMC RhoGDI2/CLIC1 and plasma ApoA4, leptin, and MMP-3; authors noted high between-subject variability.

**Why it matters:** Direct 36-hour human fasting biology helps explain the protein/hormonal measurement window inside a 24-72 hour fast.

**Potential experiment signals:** plasma leptin, MMP-3, ApoA4, PBMC RhoGDI2, PBMC CLIC1, between-subject variability

**Protocol takeaway:** A 36-hour fast can measurably shift proteomic and endocrine markers, but this is mechanistic measurement evidence rather than clinical efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Extraction boundaries:** Directness: `direct_protocol`. Population mismatch: Generally healthy volunteers, but BMI range included normal to obese; findings are molecular-marker context rather than direct user-facing efficacy. Limitations: Small exploratory mechanistic study; high interindividual variability; not designed to test clinical outcomes. Safety notes: No protocol-relevant adverse event signal extracted from the available source text.
