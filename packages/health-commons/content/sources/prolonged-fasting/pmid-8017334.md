This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** The study reported that plasma glucose and hepatic glucose production decreased during a 60-hour fast while fuel selection shifted toward fasting-adapted substrate use; exact full effect extraction was not completed in this batch.

**Why it matters:** Foundational direct 60-hour physiology source for fuel switching, hepatic glucose production, and substrate oxidation.

**Potential experiment signals:** blood glucose, hepatic glucose production, free fatty acids, ketones, fat oxidation, protein metabolism, energy expenditure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as direct 60-hour fuel-metabolism context, not as clinical-outcome evidence.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
