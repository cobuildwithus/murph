This source is included for **metabolic timecourse and mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Accessible abstract text reported 24-hour energy expenditure decreased with fasting and increased with overfeeding (P < 0.01), 24-hour respiratory quotient decreased with fasting and increased with overfeeding (P < 0.01), and no significant group differences between Caucasian and Pima Indian participants.

**Why it matters:** Clarifies respiratory quotient and energy-expenditure direction during a 48-hour fast under controlled conditions.

**Potential experiment signals:** 24-hour energy expenditure, respiratory quotient, substrate oxidation

**Protocol takeaway:** A 48-hour fast can lower chamber-measured energy expenditure and respiratory quotient, but this is physiology context, not a clinical outcome claim.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction boundaries:** Directness: `direct_protocol`. Population mismatch: Pima Indian obesity-risk context and normal-weight male sample limit generalization to the broader protocol audience. Limitations: Small all-male study; specialized chamber setting; includes overfeeding as well as fasting; population comparison may not generalize. Safety notes: No adverse-event signal extracted from the abstract text.
