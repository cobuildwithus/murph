This source is included for **refeeding, electrolytes, and thiamine safety**.

**Findings:** The review addresses thiamine supplementation in refeeding-syndrome prevention/treatment and should be used to preserve that the thiamine evidence base is limited, not to invent a precise universal dose for healthy fast refeeding.

**Why it matters:** Focused systematic review for thiamine; useful for preserving limited evidence base and avoiding invented dosing certainty.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:thiamine-status, biomarker:heart-rhythm, biomarker:refeeding-syndrome-symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for safety context and uncertainty language around thiamine; avoid protocol claims that over-specify dose certainty.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
