This source is included for **metabolic timecourse and mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Accessible article text reported that obesity blunted adipose tissue insulin-resistance dynamics and reduced lipolytic activity/FFA flux per fat mass during fasting, resulting in lower ketosis in women with obesity; fasting reduced glucose but worsened OGTT glucose AUC in both groups.

**Why it matters:** Directly studies adiposity as a modifier of fasting ketones, lipolysis, and glucose tolerance during a 60-hour fast.

**Potential experiment signals:** 3-hydroxybutyrate, FFA flux, OGTT glucose AUC, insulin, respiratory quotient, adipokines

**Protocol takeaway:** Adiposity can change ketosis and adipose-tissue response during a 60-hour fast; post-fast glucose tolerance may worsen acutely.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Extraction boundaries:** Directness: `direct_protocol`. Population mismatch: Premenopausal women only; obesity subgroup results may not generalize to men or older adults. Limitations: Women-only, lean/obesity comparison; includes refeeding phase and OGTT; not a long-term outcome study. Safety notes: Hospital monitoring was used during fasting; no adverse-event signal was extracted in the captured text.
