This source is included for **eating-disorder and restriction-risk boundary**.

**Findings:** This narrative review of human prolonged water-fasting trials is included for adverse-event, medical-supervision, and exclusion context rather than ED-specific efficacy claims.

**Why it matters:** Direct prolonged-water-fasting safety review; likely central for extraction of exclusions/adverse events while keeping ED-specific claims separate.

**Potential experiment signals:** adverse events, medical supervision, screening and contraindications.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to frame adverse-event and supervision boundaries without treating it as ED-risk efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness and caveat:** Human prolonged water fasting generally, not exclusively 24–72-hour self-experimentation or ED-risk populations. Narrative review; duration/settings vary and may exceed the protocol target.
