This source is included for **Clinical/residential supervised fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** In 145 metabolic-syndrome patients, 5-day fasting plus lifestyle modification reported body-weight/BMI and metabolic-syndrome improvements versus lifestyle context; fasting was longer than 72 hours and bundled.

**Why it matters:** RCT-level evidence for a clinical metabolic program while preserving the longer/bundled intervention boundary.

**Potential experiment signals:** weight, BMI, waist, blood pressure, lipids/glucose, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent clinical-program evidence only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Patients with metabolic syndrome.
- **Intervention/exposure:** 5-day fasting plus 10-week lifestyle modification.
- **Comparator/control:** Lifestyle modification without the fasting module.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 5-day fasting module followed by 10-week lifestyle intervention; fasting exceeds 72 hours and is bundled with lifestyle program.
- **Endpoints:** body weight, BMI, metabolic syndrome criteria, safety
- **Effect estimate or direction:** Reported lower body weight/BMI at follow-ups and improvement in metabolic syndrome criteria after fasting; both arms described as safe.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Both interventions were described as safe in extracted summary.
- **Limitations:** Metabolic syndrome population; fasting bundled with lifestyle modification; 5-day fast exceeds protocol.
- **Population mismatch:** Clinical metabolic syndrome intervention, not healthy 24–72 hour fasting.
- **Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** clinical_supervised
