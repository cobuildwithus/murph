This source is included for **FMD/VLCD and modified-fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** In 15 women completing the intervention, body weight, waist circumference, systolic pressure, and diastolic pressure decreased; lipid and fasting-glucose changes were not clearly significant.

**Why it matters:** Captures both positive and null cardiometabolic signals from a small modified-ADF program.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:body-weight, anthropometric:waist-circumference, vitals:blood-pressure, biomarker:lipids, biomarker:fasting-glucose.

**Protocol takeaway:** Adjacent modified-fasting evidence only; do not turn into direct prolonged-fasting claims.

**Directness and limitations:** Directness is `adjacent_variant`. Small uncontrolled study, women only, short duration, modified fast days not zero-calorie. Population mismatch: Weight-loss program in overweight/obese women, not a 24–72 hour water-only protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
