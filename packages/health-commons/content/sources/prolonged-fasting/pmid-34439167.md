This source is included for **Clinical/residential supervised fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** In 101 selected cancer patients, periodic FMD cycles were reported feasible/safe under active-treatment supervision and nutritional-risk screening; this is adjacent to water-only fasting.

**Why it matters:** Important for oncology nutritional-risk boundary and differentiating FMD from water-only fasting.

**Potential experiment signals:** adverse events, nutritional status, inflammation, glucose/insulin.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as selected oncology FMD safety boundary only.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Cancer patients undergoing active treatment and assessed for nutritional risk.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Periodic 5-day fasting-mimicking diet cycles.
- **Comparator/control:** No randomized comparator in this safety/feasibility extraction.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Repeated 5-day FMD cycles; FMD rather than water-only fasting.
- **Endpoints:** safety, feasibility, nutritional status, metabolic markers, inflammatory factors
- **Effect estimate or direction:** Reported FMD cycles as feasible/safe in selected low nutritional-risk patients, with metabolic/inflammatory factor assessments.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Safety/feasibility assessed; low nutritional risk selection is key.
- **Limitations:** Oncology population, FMD not water-only, selected patients, no direct wellness comparison.
- **Population mismatch:** Cancer active-treatment setting and nutritional-risk screening.
- **Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** adjacent_variant
