This source is included for **safety and adverse events**.

**Findings:** The review found very uncertain evidence that IF may have little to no effect on adverse events versus regular dietary advice (RR 1.45, 95% CI 0.64 to 3.28; 7 studies, 619 participants). The review found very uncertain evidence that IF may have little to no effect on adverse events versus no intervention or waiting list (RR 1.84, 95% CI 0.88 to 3.85; 2 studies, 189 participants). The Cochrane review concluded IF may result in little to no difference in weight loss or quality of life compared with regular dietary advice, with limited applicability beyond short-term follow-up.

**Why it matters:** Most current Cochrane synthesis with adverse-event certainty judgments for IF variants.

**Potential experiment signals:** weight loss, quality of life, adverse events, diabetes status, participant satisfaction

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent null/uncertain safety context; do not overstate absence or presence of harms.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** adjacent_variant.

**Population and exposure:** Adults with overweight or obesity in randomized or cluster-randomized trials. Exposure/intervention: Intermittent fasting variants including time-restricted feeding, periodic fasting, alternate-day fasting, and modified alternate-day fasting. Comparator/control: Regular dietary advice, no intervention, or waiting list. Duration/follow-up: Minimum intervention duration 4 weeks; follow-up at least 6 months in included trials..

**Effect direction or estimates:** The review included 22 studies with 1995 participants and found little to no difference in weight loss or quality of life versus regular dietary advice; adverse-event evidence was very uncertain.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** Adverse events: RR 1.45 versus regular dietary advice across 7 studies/619 participants and RR 1.84 versus no intervention/waiting list across 2 studies/189 participants, both very low-certainty in extracted text.

**Limitations:** Adjacent IF variants; overweight/obesity trials; very low-certainty adverse-event evidence; not water-only 24-72 hour fasting.

**Population mismatch:** Adjacent intermittent-fasting and weight-loss interventions, not the target fast.
