This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** In a 20-participant young-adult sample, a single 36-hour water-only fast vastly remodeled the plasma lipidome and altered concentrations of several lipid species across fed, fasted, and re-fed states.

**Why it matters:** Direct 36-hour water-only biomarker source with fed/fasted/refed states inside the protocol window.

**Potential experiment signals:** plasma lipidome, free fatty acids, cardiometabolic biomarkers, refeeding lipidome.

**Protocol takeaway:** A 36-hour water-only fast can substantially alter lipidomic biomarkers, but validation in larger and different populations is needed.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Study design and directness:** Pilot intervention/secondary lipidomics analysis.. Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours): `direct_protocol`.

**Comparator/control:** Fed, overnight-fasted, fasted, and re-fed sampling states

**Duration/follow-up:** 36-hour fast with refeeding sampling

**Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event data extracted.

**Limitations:** Small pilot sample; lipidomic endpoints require larger-cohort validation and are not clinical outcomes.

**Population mismatch:** Young adult sample; may not generalize to older adults, cardiometabolic disease, diabetes, pregnancy, or medication use.
