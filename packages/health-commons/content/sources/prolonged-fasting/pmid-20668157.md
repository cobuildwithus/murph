This source is included for **diabetes, medication, and hypoglycemia safety**.

**Findings:** The 2010 Diabetes Care update recommends risk stratification, pre-Ramadan education, blood glucose monitoring, individualized medication adjustment, and clear break-fast thresholds for diabetes safety.

**Why it matters:** Older but still foundational consensus update; useful for preserving historical stop-fast and medication-risk language.

**Potential experiment signals:** hypoglycemia, hyperglycemia, ketoacidosis, blood glucose monitoring.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a safety-only source for diabetes supervision, monitoring, medication adjustment, and stop-fast criteria.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
