This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** After 72 hours fasting, mood scores increased and multiple brain metabolite concentrations changed, including Glx decreases in all nine measured regions, with frontal regions showing prominent changes.

**Why it matters:** Direct 72-hour evidence in women, but the endpoints are neuroimaging and mood/cognition context rather than proof of cognitive benefit.

**Potential experiment signals:** mood, cognition, headache, sleep, brain metabolites, subjective mental clarity.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as neuro-metabolic context only; do not claim that 72-hour fasting improves cognition based on this source.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
