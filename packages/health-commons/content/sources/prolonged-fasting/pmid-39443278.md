This source is included for **metabolic timecourse and mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Accessible abstract/search text reported hepatic fat content increased after fasting in lean women, while no statistically significant hepatic fat content change was observed in women with obesity; liver volume decreased during fasting and increased during refeeding in an accessible conference synopsis.

**Why it matters:** Direct 48-hour fasting source for liver fat response, but refeeding design requires careful boundary language.

**Potential experiment signals:** hepatic fat content, liver volume, refeeding carbohydrate load

**Protocol takeaway:** Liver fat can respond differently by adiposity after a 2-day fast; high-carbohydrate refeeding is a separate exposure.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Extraction boundaries:** Directness: `direct_protocol_with_refeeding_boundary`. Population mismatch: Lean/obese women only; refeeding composition limits direct generalization to ordinary refeeding. Limitations: Sample size and full numeric estimates not extracted; women-only; high-carbohydrate refeeding phase may drive effects not attributable to fasting alone. Safety notes: No adverse-event signal extracted.
