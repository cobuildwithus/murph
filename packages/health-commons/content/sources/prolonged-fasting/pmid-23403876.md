This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** In 16 young healthy female volunteers, a medically surveilled 48-hour total fast was used to assess heart rate variability, diurnal cortisol profiles, and subjective hunger.

**Why it matters:** Direct 48-hour source for autonomic/stress and hunger endpoints in a supervised setting.

**Potential experiment signals:** heart rate variability, diurnal cortisol profile, subjective hunger, autonomic stress physiology.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for measurement and tolerability context around HRV, cortisol, and hunger; do not generalize from young women to all users.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Study design and directness:** Controlled/supervised short-term fasting physiology study.. Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours): `direct_protocol`.

**Comparator/control:** Within-person time course during/around fasting

**Duration/follow-up:** 48-hour fast

**Adverse events or safety notes:** Medical surveillance was part of the protocol; detailed adverse-event counts were not extracted.

**Limitations:** Female-only young healthy sample; extracted snippets identify endpoints and supervision but not all effect estimates.

**Population mismatch:** Young healthy women only; supervised total-fast setting may differ from unsupervised water-only fasting.
