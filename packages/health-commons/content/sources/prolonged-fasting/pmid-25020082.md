This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** Fasting increased net phenylalanine release from forearm muscle and decreased mTOR pathway signaling; insulin-stimulated mTOR/rpS6 signaling was also reduced, while autophagy markers were difficult to interpret.

**Why it matters:** Directly informs muscle/protein-metabolism cautions for upper-window fasts, including a non-benefit signal for anabolic signaling.

**Potential experiment signals:** lean mass, muscle soreness, strength, protein intake on refeed, mTOR signaling, phenylalanine balance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Upper-window fasting should not be framed as muscle-anabolic; this source supports a protein-turnover caution.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
