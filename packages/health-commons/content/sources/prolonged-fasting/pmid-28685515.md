This source is included for **diabetes, medication, and hypoglycemia safety**.

**Findings:** This prospective observational open-label study followed 301 people with diabetes using predetermined antidiabetic drug-regimen adjustments during Ramadan and monitored hypoglycemia and acute metabolic events.

**Why it matters:** Direct medication-adjustment safety study during fasting; useful for separating adjusted from unadjusted medication risk.

**Potential experiment signals:** hypoglycemia, drug regimen adjustment, acute metabolic events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as support for clinician-directed medication adjustment, not self-directed prolonged fasting advice.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
