This source is included for **refeeding, electrolytes, and thiamine safety**.

**Findings:** The review describes refeeding syndrome after starvation or poor intake as involving electrolyte shifts, thiamine issues, and fluid balance problems, and emphasizes prevention/treatment through risk recognition, cautious refeeding, supplementation, and monitoring.

**Why it matters:** Foundational, widely cited clinical review with practical prevention/treatment framing and open full text.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a high-recall safety source for risk recognition, electrolyte/thiamine monitoring, and referral language.
