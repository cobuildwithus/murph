This source is included for **adjacent fasting variants and terminology**.

**Findings:** The Cochrane review found insufficient evidence for clinical CVD prevention outcomes and suggested that short-term weight/cardiometabolic changes were small or uncertain, with IF often similar to continuous energy restriction.

**Why it matters:** Cochrane source with explicit IF category separation and cardiovascular-risk endpoint framing.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as cautious adjacent CVD/weight evidence and safety uncertainty source.
