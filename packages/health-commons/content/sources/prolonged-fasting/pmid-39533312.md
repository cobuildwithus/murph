This source is included for **adjacent fasting variants and terminology**.

**Findings:** The network meta-analysis found that IF forms may improve metabolic health compared with usual diets, with ADF appearing to rank highest across investigated outcomes; comparisons with continuous energy restriction were more nuanced.

**Why it matters:** Explicitly compares different IF forms, supporting variant separation rather than lumped claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** IF type ranking; Body weight; Blood pressure; Glucose; Lipids.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent IF-regimen comparison evidence, especially to avoid lumping TRE, ADF, and 5:2 together.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
