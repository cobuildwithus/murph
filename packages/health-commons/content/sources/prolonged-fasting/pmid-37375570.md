This source is included for **Clinical/residential supervised fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** Systematic review/meta-analysis found the human evidence for fasting-like regimens reducing chemotherapy side effects to be limited and heterogeneous.

**Why it matters:** Synthesis source to keep oncology claims cautious and mixed rather than overconfident.

**Potential experiment signals:** chemotherapy toxicity, quality of life, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mixed oncology-context review, not direct protocol support.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Cancer patients receiving chemotherapy in fasting or fasting-like regimen studies.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Therapeutic fasting, fasting-like regimens, or calorie restriction around chemotherapy.
- **Comparator/control:** Usual diet/control conditions where available.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Varies across oncology studies.
- **Endpoints:** chemotherapy side effects, toxicity, quality of life, adverse events
- **Effect estimate or direction:** Review concluded evidence for chemotherapy side-effect reduction remains limited and heterogeneous.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Safety is central but no single adverse-event rate extracted here.
- **Limitations:** Heterogeneous regimens, small studies, oncology-specific context.
- **Population mismatch:** Cancer/chemotherapy populations and fasting-like regimens.
- **Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** adjacent_variant
