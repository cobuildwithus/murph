This source is included for **metabolic timecourse and mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Accessible article text reported that human FGF21 did not notably increase until days 7-10, while ketones rose earlier; authors concluded endogenous FGF21 did not drive starvation-mediated ketogenesis in humans.

**Why it matters:** Sets an important boundary that FGF21 is a late fasting signal in humans, not necessarily a 24-72 hour marker.

**Potential experiment signals:** FGF21, beta-hydroxybutyrate, thermogenesis, adiponectin

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not claim that 24-72 hour fasting reliably raises FGF21 based on this 10-day study.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction boundaries:** Directness: `same_mechanism`. Population mismatch: 10-day supervised starvation is not the same exposure as a self-directed 24-72 hour protocol. Limitations: Longer than target protocol; medically supervised and supplemented; small sample. Safety notes: Medically supervised long fast; no protocol-specific adverse-event extraction for 24-72 hour use.
