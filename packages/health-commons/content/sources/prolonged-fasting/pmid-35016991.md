This source is included for **diabetes, medication, and hypoglycemia safety**.

**Findings:** The 2021 practical guidelines update Ramadan diabetes management, including high-risk populations such as type 1 diabetes, older adults, and pregnancy, and emphasizes risk stratification, monitoring, and medication adjustment.

**Why it matters:** Major IDF-DAR practical guideline for fasting with diabetes; highly relevant for exclusion and rescue/stop-fast boundaries even though Ramadan differs from 24-72 hour wellness fasting.

**Potential experiment signals:** hypoglycemia, ketoacidosis, blood glucose monitoring, medication adjustment.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as high-priority safety-only guidance for diabetes exclusions/supervision boundaries.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
