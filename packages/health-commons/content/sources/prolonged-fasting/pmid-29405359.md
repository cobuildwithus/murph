This source is included for **diabetes, medication, and hypoglycemia safety**.

**Findings:** The RCT reported that intermittent fasting increased hypoglycemia risk despite medication reduction while both intervention patterns showed improvements in weight, HbA1c, fasting glucose, and quality-of-life measures.

**Why it matters:** One of the more directly relevant RCTs for fasting-associated hypoglycemia in T2D, though not a 24-72 hour wellness fast in non-diabetic adults.

**Potential experiment signals:** hypoglycemia events, HbA1c, fasting glucose, body weight.

**Protocol takeaway:** Preserve as mixed adjacent evidence: possible metabolic improvements do not remove hypoglycemia safety concerns.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
