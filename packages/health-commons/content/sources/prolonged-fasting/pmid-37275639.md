This source is included for **FMD/VLCD and modified-fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** ADMF improved weight loss and some subjective sleep subscales versus daily calorie restriction, but total PSQI/ESS effects were not clearly different and some sleep-duration/latency signals worsened.

**Why it matters:** Preserves mixed sleep findings rather than treating fasting as uniformly sleep-improving.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:body-weight, sleep:subjective-sleep-quality, sleep:daytime-dysfunction, sleep:latency, sleep:duration.

**Protocol takeaway:** Adjacent modified-ADF context only; do not generalize sleep effects to zero-calorie 24–72 hour fasts.

**Directness and limitations:** Directness is `adjacent_variant`. Women-only overweight/obesity sample, self-reported sleep measures, short intervention, modified calories on fast days. Population mismatch: ADMF diet program in women with overweight/obesity, not a discrete prolonged-fast protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
