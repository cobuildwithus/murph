This source is included for **Clinical/residential supervised fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** In 20 healthy young women, a 10-day supervised residential fasting module reported changes in physiological parameters; small uncontrolled design and longer duration limit directness.

**Why it matters:** Residential healthy-volunteer context is closer to wellness than disease cohorts but still longer and supervised.

**Potential experiment signals:** weight, vital signs, CBC/biochemistry, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as residential/supervision context only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Healthy young female volunteers in a controlled residential environment.
- **Intervention/exposure:** 10-day medically supervised fasting module with preparatory and refeeding days.
- **Comparator/control:** Within-person before/after comparison.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 10-day program with one preparatory and one refeeding day; exceeds 72 hours.
- **Endpoints:** physiological parameters, body measures, biochemical markers, safety
- **Effect estimate or direction:** Reported positive changes in physiological parameters and called for further safety studies.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Safety was a motivation but detailed adverse-event rates were not extracted.
- **Limitations:** Small, young female-only, uncontrolled, 10-day residential design.
- **Population mismatch:** Long residential fast in young women, not 24–72 hour mixed-adult protocol.
- **Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** clinical_supervised
