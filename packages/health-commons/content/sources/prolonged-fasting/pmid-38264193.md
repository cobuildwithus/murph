This source is included for **eating-disorder and restriction-risk boundary**.

**Findings:** A narrative review found heterogeneous and limited evidence on fasting diets, eating behaviors, sleep, mood, and well-being.

**Why it matters:** Broad fasting-diet review covering eating behavior, sleep, mood, and well-being; useful for context but not sufficient for protocol-specific claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** eating behaviors, mood and anxiety, sleep, well-being.

**Protocol takeaway:** Track mood, sleep, appetite, and eating behavior rather than assuming a uniform behavioral response.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness and caveat:** Fasting diets broadly, including intermittent variants; not confined to 24–72-hour prolonged fasting. Not a systematic direct-protocol safety estimate.
