This source is included for **metabolic timecourse and mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Accessible abstract snippets report that chronotropic, lipolytic, and thermogenic effects of infused epinephrine were enhanced by prior 48-hour starvation despite lower plasma epinephrine.

**Why it matters:** Helps explain sympathetic/catecholamine-mediated thermogenic effects during the 48-hour window.

**Potential experiment signals:** epinephrine response, thermogenesis, lipolysis, heart rate

**Protocol takeaway:** 48-hour starvation can amplify responses to catecholamines; this is physiology context rather than a benefit claim.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction boundaries:** Directness: `direct_protocol`. Population mismatch: Not directly representative of unsupervised general fasting; epinephrine infusion context is artificial. Limitations: Participant count and numeric estimates not extracted; specialized infusion physiology study. Safety notes: No adverse-event signal extracted.
