This source is included for **eating-disorder and restriction-risk boundary**.

**Findings:** In a Chinese cohort (N=491) with 8-month follow-up, baseline current/past IF was associated with higher follow-up eating-disorder psychopathology and eating-related psychosocial impairment.

**Why it matters:** Prospective design strengthens temporal safety signal for intermittent fasting and eating-disorder symptoms; useful for risk disclosure and follow-up monitoring boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** restrictive behavior, eating disorder psychopathology, intuitive eating, screening and contraindications.

**Protocol takeaway:** Fasting protocols should require history and current-behavior screening plus follow-up checks for emerging ED symptoms.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness and caveat:** Chinese adults/young adults using intermittent fasting, not an intervention trial of prolonged fasting. The exposure was intermittent fasting and the study is observational.
