This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** After 72 hours of fasting, plasma free fatty acid levels, fluxes, and oxidation were approximately doubled; palmitate fluxes across forearm and leg increased; and insulin-stimulated glucose uptake across leg and forearm was reduced by about 60%.

**Why it matters:** This is direct 72-hour evidence for both desired fuel switching and a mixed/negative insulin-sensitivity signal.

**Potential experiment signals:** free fatty acids, fat oxidation, glucose uptake, fasting insulin, blood glucose, resting energy expenditure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as direct upper-dose metabolic evidence: fat flux/oxidation rises, but insulin-stimulated glucose uptake can fall during the fast.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
