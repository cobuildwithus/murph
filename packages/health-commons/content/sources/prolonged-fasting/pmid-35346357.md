This source is included for **diabetes, medication, and hypoglycemia safety**.

**Findings:** The case report describes euglycemic diabetic ketoacidosis in a person with T2D taking dapagliflozin/metformin in the setting of prolonged Ramadan fasting.

**Why it matters:** Directly named prolonged fasting plus SGLT2-associated euglycemic DKA; adverse-event source should be retained despite case-report design.

**Potential experiment signals:** euglycemic diabetic ketoacidosis, ketones, SGLT2 inhibitor exposure, fasting exposure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as an SGLT2 fasting adverse-event boundary, not as incidence evidence.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
