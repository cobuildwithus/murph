This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** The secondary analysis reported that lower baseline human growth hormone was associated with qualitatively greater HOMA-IR reductions from intermittent fasting, while the design used repeated 24-hour fasts rather than a one-off 60–72 hour fast.

**Why it matters:** The exposure includes water-only 24-hour fasts within the protocol duration family, but the repeated long-term schedule makes it adjacent to a single 24–72 hour protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** HOMA-IR, fasting insulin, fasting glucose, human growth hormone, body weight.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as adjacent repeated-24-hour context; do not treat 26-week intermittent fasting results as direct evidence for a single 60–72 hour fast.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
