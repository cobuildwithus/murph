This source is included for **FMD/VLCD and modified-fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** The guideline frames VLCKD as a structured obesity-management intervention requiring patient selection, contraindication screening, and professional monitoring.

**Why it matters:** Safety boundary for very-low-calorie ketogenic diets that may be confused with fasting.

**Potential experiment signals:** safety:clinical-supervision, biomarker:body-weight, biomarker:ketosis, safety:contraindications.

**Protocol takeaway:** Safety-only; do not use as direct evidence for unsupervised 24–72 hour fasting.

**Directness and limitations:** Directness is `adjacent_variant`. Guideline-level obesity-management evidence, not a source on discrete water-only fasts. Population mismatch: Adults with obesity in clinical VLCKD programs.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
