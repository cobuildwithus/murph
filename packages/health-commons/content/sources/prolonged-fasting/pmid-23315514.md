This source is included for **refeeding, electrolytes, and thiamine safety**.

**Findings:** In 243 adults started on artificial nutrition support, severe electrolyte shifts/fluid overload/organ dysfunction leading to a refeeding-syndrome diagnosis occurred in 3 patients (2%); starvation and low baseline magnesium were identified as predictors, and no deaths were attributed to refeeding syndrome in the extracted abstract data.

**Why it matters:** Prospective cohort source for occurrence during artificial nutrition support; adjacent but useful for monitoring outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:serum-phosphate, biomarker:serum-potassium, biomarker:serum-magnesium, biomarker:fluid-balance, biomarker:refeeding-syndrome-symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as monitored clinical occurrence context and risk-factor signal, especially starvation and low magnesium.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
