This source is included for **Clinical/residential supervised fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** In 174 hypertensive patients, a supervised water-only fasting/refeeding program reported large blood-pressure reductions and many participants below 140/90 mmHg, but without randomization and with fasts longer than 72 hours.

**Why it matters:** Foundational supervised water-only fasting cohort; useful for showing why disease-treatment and long-duration claims require clinical caveats.

**Potential experiment signals:** morning blood pressure, weight, medication changes, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for clinical-supervision boundary, not direct support for a healthy 24–72 hour fast.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Adults with hypertension in a medically supervised residential/inpatient water-only fasting program.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Prefast fruit/vegetable diet, water-only fasting, then low-fat low-sodium vegan refeeding.
- **Comparator/control:** No randomized control group.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Water-only fasting averaged about 10–11 days plus approximately 6–7 days of refeeding; exceeds 72 hours.
- **Endpoints:** blood pressure, body weight, medication status, adverse events
- **Effect estimate or direction:** Reported large blood-pressure reductions and high proportion reaching below 140/90 mmHg; observational and bundled with refeeding.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Adverse-event details were not extracted as rates; medical supervision was integral.
- **Limitations:** No randomized comparator; hypertension population; duration exceeds protocol; bundled diet/refeeding.
- **Population mismatch:** Clinical hypertension treatment setting differs from healthy 24–72 hour fasting.
- **Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** clinical_supervised
