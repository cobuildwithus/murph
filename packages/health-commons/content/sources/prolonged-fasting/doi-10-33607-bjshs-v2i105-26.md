This source is included for **FMD/VLCD and modified-fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** In nine overweight/obese men, a two-day VLCD did not detect clear heart-rate, blood-pressure, or cognitive changes; perceived vigor increased while subjective stress was measured as a safety/tolerability signal.

**Why it matters:** Defines a very-low-calorie boundary near a 48-hour fast while preserving that calories were consumed.

**Potential experiment signals:** mood:vigor, stress:perceived-stress, vitals:heart-rate, vitals:blood-pressure, cognitive:function.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as VLCD comparator context; do not generalize its mood or cognitive findings to zero-calorie prolonged fasting.

**Directness and limitations:** Directness is `adjacent_variant`. Very small male-only sample and two-day VLCD exposure; not powered for safety or clinical outcomes. Population mismatch: Young overweight/obese men on VLCD, not a mixed adult 24–72 hour water-only fast population.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
