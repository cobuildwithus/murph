This source is included for **metabolic timecourse and mechanisms**.

**Findings:** Accessible abstract snippets state that a 3-day fast increased circulating lipids and insulin resistance in healthy young subjects and related the response to skeletal muscle lipid-oxidation capacity/fiber composition.

**Why it matters:** Directly captures a negative/mixed physiologic signal: fasting-induced insulin resistance during the target 72-hour window.

**Potential experiment signals:** insulin resistance, circulating lipids, muscle lipid oxidation, fiber type

**Protocol takeaway:** A 3-day fast can acutely increase insulin resistance; do not frame all fasting glucose/insulin changes as beneficial.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Extraction boundaries:** Directness: `direct_protocol`. Population mismatch: Healthy young subjects only; not general safety or efficacy evidence. Limitations: Mechanistic young-subject study; sample size and complete numeric effects not extracted; title discrepancy noted between ledger and current search result, so PMID/DOI identity was retained. Safety notes: No adverse-event signal extracted.
