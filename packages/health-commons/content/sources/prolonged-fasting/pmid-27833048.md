This source is included for **FMD/VLCD and modified-fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** In 63 adults with type 2 diabetes, two-day severe energy restriction and continuous restriction produced similar HbA1c and weight changes in the pilot setting.

**Why it matters:** Diabetes medication and hypoglycemia context is important when fasting or severe restriction is attempted.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:hba1c, biomarker:body-weight, safety:hypoglycemia-risk, medication:diabetes-medication-adjustment.

**Protocol takeaway:** Safety-only boundary for people with type 2 diabetes; not direct self-experiment efficacy evidence.

**Directness and limitations:** Directness is `adjacent_variant`. Pilot trial, diabetes population, energy restriction rather than zero-calorie prolonged fasting. Population mismatch: Type 2 diabetes clinical population with medication considerations.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
