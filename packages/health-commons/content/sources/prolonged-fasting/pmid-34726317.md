This source is included for **FMD/VLCD and modified-fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** MIDDAS enrolled 39 intermittent and 40 continuous low-energy diet participants with type 2 diabetes; follow-up and app-use data supported feasibility, with diabetes medication context central to interpretation.

**Why it matters:** Shows practical monitoring/safety issues in diabetes when severe energy restriction is delivered remotely.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:hba1c, biomarker:body-weight, medication:diabetes-medication-change, behavior:app-use, safety:follow-up-retention.

**Protocol takeaway:** Safety-only diabetes boundary; not direct prolonged-fasting evidence.

**Directness and limitations:** Directness is `adjacent_variant`. Pilot feasibility study, diabetes population, low-energy diet rather than zero-calorie fasting. Population mismatch: Type 2 diabetes app-supported diet program, not broad 24–72 hour fasting.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
