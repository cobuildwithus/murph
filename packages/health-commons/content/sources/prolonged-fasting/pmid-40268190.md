This source is included for **safety and adverse events**.

**Findings:** The study found an acute inflammatory response during prolonged fasting, including elevated CRP, hepcidin, midkine, and IL-8, with CRP increase also observed in a 1422-person modified-fasting retrospective cohort. The study reported increased platelet degranulation and upregulation of complement/coagulation pathways during fasting/refeeding. The study reported 7.7% mean weight loss and significant beta-hydroxybutyrate increase confirming fasting adherence.

**Why it matters:** Recent cautionary molecular safety source for prolonged water-only fasting and refeeding.

**Potential experiment signals:** weight loss, beta-hydroxybutyrate, proteomics, CRP, hepcidin, midkine, IL-8, platelet degranulation, complement/coagulation

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as high-priority safety/caution source; do not ignore inflammation and platelet activation signals in longer fasts.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** adjacent_variant.

**Population and exposure:** Twenty middle-aged volunteers, mean age 52.2 years and mean BMI 28.8 kg/m² in extracted abstract. Exposure/intervention: Medically supervised water-only fast followed by guided refeeding. Comparator/control: Within-person baseline/end-of-fast/end-of-refeed observations; independent retrospective modified-fasting cohort for CRP validation. Duration/follow-up: Mean 9.8 ± 3.1 days fasting followed by 5.3 ± 2.4 days guided refeeding..

**Effect direction or estimates:** The study reported 7.7% mean weight loss and increased BHB, but also acute inflammatory response and platelet/complement/coagulation activation signals.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** Elevated CRP, hepcidin, midkine, IL-8, platelet degranulation, and complement/coagulation pathway activation raise safety concerns.

**Limitations:** Small supervised cohort; prolonged duration beyond protocol; molecular endpoints, not clinical event incidence; potential center conflicts disclosed.

**Population mismatch:** Adjacent prolonged clinical fast, not 24-72 hours.
