This source is included for **Clinical/residential supervised fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** In 117 young female adults, an observational supervised fasting study tracked daily symptoms, anthropometrics, vitamin/calcium/CBC measures, quality of life, mood, anxiety, and depression across a 10-day program.

**Why it matters:** Adds residential young-female safety/psychological measurement context.

**Potential experiment signals:** symptoms, mood/anxiety/depression, vitamin D/calcium/CBC, anthropometrics.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as supervised residential measurement context only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Young female adults in a medically supervised fasting program.
- **Intervention/exposure:** 10 days of medically supervised fasting.
- **Comparator/control:** Within-person baseline/end comparisons.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 10 days; exceeds 72 hours.
- **Endpoints:** daily symptoms, dietary intake, vitamin D, calcium, CBC, anthropometrics, quality of life, mood, anxiety, depression
- **Effect estimate or direction:** Observational study tracked physiological and psychological changes during supervised 10-day fasting; direct effect details not extracted beyond source endpoints.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Daily symptom scoring was part of the safety/physiology tracking.
- **Limitations:** Young female-only observational design; 10-day supervised fast; no randomized comparator.
- **Population mismatch:** Long medically supervised young-adult/female cohort.
- **Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** clinical_supervised
