This source is included for **diabetes, medication, and hypoglycemia safety**.

**Findings:** This colonoscopy-preparation review gives clinical-practice suggestions for diabetes medications during bowel prep and fasting, including SGLT2 inhibitor interruption several days before colonoscopy.

**Why it matters:** Clinical review with practical glucose-monitoring and medication-holding suggestions for a short-term fasting-like procedure setting.

**Potential experiment signals:** SGLT2 inhibitor hold, hypoglycemia, ketoacidosis, procedural fasting.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent clinical safety context for diabetes medication hold/supervision boundaries.
