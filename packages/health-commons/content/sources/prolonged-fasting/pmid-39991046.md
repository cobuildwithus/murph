This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** The secondary evaluation reported that water-only fasting increased human growth hormone and that fasting-induced HGH changes were independent of weight loss, with correlations to cardiometabolic variables.

**Why it matters:** Direct water-only fasting endocrine biomarker source, but HGH is mechanistic and not equivalent to a clinical outcome.

**Potential experiment signals:** human growth hormone, weight-loss-independent endocrine response, insulin resistance correlations, cardiometabolic markers.

**Protocol takeaway:** Water-only fasting can acutely increase HGH independent of weight loss; do not treat HGH rise as proof of rejuvenation or fat-loss efficacy.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Study design and directness:** Secondary evaluation of randomized controlled trial.. Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours): `direct_protocol`.

**Comparator/control:** Fed/control timing in underlying randomized design

**Duration/follow-up:** 24-hour-plus water-only fasting exposure in secondary evaluation

**Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event data extracted from this endocrine secondary evaluation.

**Limitations:** Secondary biomarker analysis; HGH increase does not equal clinical benefit.

**Population mismatch:** Underlying trial details and single-fast timing should be verified before dose-specific synthesis.
