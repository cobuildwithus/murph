This source is included for **adjacent fasting variants and terminology**.

**Findings:** The consensus statement found limited expert-recommendation support for IF in clinical cardiometabolic populations and recommended against IF in patients with diabetes while not issuing IF recommendations for adults with overweight/obesity or hypertension.

**Why it matters:** Professional consensus statement; useful for cautionary and clinical-boundary language, especially diabetes/hypertension.

**Potential experiment signals:** Body weight; Glycemic control; Blood pressure; Medication/safety boundaries.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only clinical boundary for diabetes, hypertension, and medication contexts.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
