This source is included for **safety and adverse events**.

**Findings:** In 1422 subjects, adverse effects were reported in less than 1% in extracted text, with no fatalities or permanent adverse effects, but two hospital admissions and transient AEs were described. The cohort reported increased well-being, weight and blood-pressure reductions, low-normal glucose, and increased ketones during supervised fasting. Extracted lab data showed increased uric acid and small sodium/potassium changes within reference ranges, underscoring monitoring needs.

**Why it matters:** Large supervised fasting safety/tolerability cohort with adverse-event and lab-marker data.

**Potential experiment signals:** adverse events, well-being, weight, blood pressure, glucose, ketones, uric acid, electrolytes

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as supervised adjacent safety context, especially for AEs and lab shifts; do not treat as direct water-only 24-72 hour proof.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** adjacent_variant.

**Population and exposure:** 1422 adults in a Buchinger-style supervised fasting program. Exposure/intervention: Buchinger periodic fasting for 4-21 days with low-calorie intake and daily supervision. Comparator/control: Within-person pre/post observations; no randomized comparator. Duration/follow-up: 4 to 21 days..

**Effect direction or estimates:** The study reported weight and blood-pressure reductions, low-normal glucose with increased ketones, increased well-being, and adverse effects in less than 1% of participants in extracted text.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** Transient symptoms, sleep disturbance, arrhythmia, hypoglycemia, gout, and two hospital admissions were described; no fatalities or permanent adverse effects in extracted text.

**Limitations:** Observational, supervised Buchinger-modified fast with calories; duration exceeds target and differs from water-only fasting.

**Population mismatch:** Adjacent clinical-supervised variant, not a community 24-72 hour water-only protocol.
