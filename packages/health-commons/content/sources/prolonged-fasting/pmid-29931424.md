This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** Steady-state glial acetate metabolism increased after 72 hours fasting; glucose fell, beta-hydroxybutyrate rose, and CGM showed time below 70 mg/dL and below 54 mg/dL, with hypoglycemia susceptibility correlating with glial acetate metabolism measures.

**Why it matters:** This is direct 72-hour safety-relevant evidence connecting fasting brain substrate metabolism with hypoglycemia susceptibility.

**Potential experiment signals:** continuous glucose, hypoglycemia time below range, beta-hydroxybutyrate, free fatty acids, glucagon, epinephrine, brain acetate metabolism.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only evidence that even metabolically healthy men can spend time below glucose thresholds during a 72-hour fast.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
