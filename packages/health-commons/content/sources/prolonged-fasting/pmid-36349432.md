This source is included for **FMD/VLCD and modified-fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** The meta-analysis compared ADF/modified ADF, 5:2 diets, and time-restricted eating; it reported ADF/mADF as promising for short-term weight loss while noting trial-duration and evidence-quality limits.

**Why it matters:** Useful landscape context for keeping different intermittent-fasting strategies distinct.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:body-weight, behavior:compliance, biomarker:cardiometabolic-risk.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only; not direct evidence for 24–72 hour prolonged fasting.

**Directness and limitations:** Directness is `adjacent_variant`. Heterogeneous diet strategies and trial durations; not water-only prolonged fasting. Population mismatch: Intermittent fasting diet-strategy synthesis rather than direct protocol evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
