This source is included for **FMD/VLCD and modified-fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** ADF did not produce superior adherence, weight loss, weight maintenance, or cardioprotection versus daily calorie restriction; dropout was highest in the ADF arm, and LDL cholesterol increased at 12 months versus daily calorie restriction.

**Why it matters:** Important negative/mixed boundary source that prevents overclaiming ADF as superior.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:body-weight, behavior:adherence, biomarker:lipids, vitals:blood-pressure, biomarker:insulin, biomarker:crp.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mixed adjacent-variant evidence and adherence caution, not as direct 24–72 hour fasting support.

**Directness and limitations:** Directness is `adjacent_variant`. Obesity-treatment trial with modified fast days, not zero-calorie prolonged fasts; dropout may bias outcomes. Population mismatch: Metabolically healthy obese adults in a year-long diet program.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
