This source is included for **direct protocol and dose evidence**.

**Findings:** The study reported increased intramyocellular lipid content during a water-only fast in the 72-hour range, with fasting metabolic sampling including glucose, insulin, ketones, free fatty acids, and body weight.

**Why it matters:** This is direct 72-hour water-only fasting evidence for muscle substrate storage and the insulin-resistance caution around the upper end of the protocol window.

**Potential experiment signals:** blood glucose, fasting insulin, beta-hydroxybutyrate, free fatty acids, muscle lipid by MRS, body weight.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as direct mechanistic evidence that the upper-dose fast shifts muscle fuel handling toward lipid storage/use; do not present as a clinical outcome benefit.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
