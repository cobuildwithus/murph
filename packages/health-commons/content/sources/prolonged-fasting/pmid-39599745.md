This source is included for **safety and adverse events**.

**Findings:** The single-arm trial enrolled 29 pre-approved participants and assessed safety using CTCAE v5.0 adverse-event recording. Extracted text reports sustained reductions in median systolic/diastolic blood pressure, body weight, abdominal circumference, and antihypertensive medication use at follow-up.

**Why it matters:** Recent supervised prolonged water-only fasting study with explicit safety/feasibility framing.

**Potential experiment signals:** adverse events, blood pressure, body weight, abdominal circumference, antihypertensive medication use, feasibility

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent clinical-supervision and hypertension safety context only; do not generalize to unsupervised users.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** adjacent_variant.

**Population and exposure:** Adults with stage 1 or 2 hypertension pre-approved for water-only fasting ≥7 days in a residential fasting center. Exposure/intervention: Prolonged water-only fasting followed by whole-plant-food diet/refeeding and follow-up. Comparator/control: Within-person baseline/follow-up; no randomized comparator. Duration/follow-up: At least 7 days of fasting eligibility; follow-up to six weeks and 12 months in extracted source text..

**Effect direction or estimates:** Extracted text reports 29 participants and sustained reductions in blood pressure, body weight, abdominal circumference, and antihypertensive medication use at follow-up; safety used CTCAE v5.0.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** Safety assessed via CTCAE v5.0 adverse events; detailed AE counts not extracted in this batch.

**Limitations:** Single-arm residential clinical program; prolonged fast ≥7 days; selected hypertensive population; conflicts/center-specific context should be considered.

**Population mismatch:** Adjacent clinical-supervised prolonged fasting, not community 24-72 hours.
