This source is included for **refeeding, electrolytes, and thiamine safety**.

**Findings:** The review frames refeeding syndrome as a clinically important complication after starvation or malnutrition, especially involving electrolyte disturbances such as hypophosphatemia with potential cardiac and systemic consequences.

**Why it matters:** Older foundational review; retained because it shaped awareness of the syndrome before modern consensus definitions.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:serum-phosphate, biomarker:serum-potassium, biomarker:serum-magnesium, biomarker:heart-rhythm, biomarker:refeeding-syndrome-symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as historical/context evidence for why refeed safety is not optional in high-risk populations.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
