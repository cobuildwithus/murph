This source is included for **diabetes, medication, and hypoglycemia safety**.

**Findings:** The 2024 systematic review concludes that SGLT2 inhibitors appear safe/effective for most T2D patients fasting during Ramadan, while noting that outcomes such as DKA, hospitalization, thrombosis, eGFR change, and dehydration were variably absent, not significant, or observed in limited studies.

**Why it matters:** Recent systematic review focused on SGLT2 safety/effectiveness in Ramadan fasting; useful for updated safety recall.

**Potential experiment signals:** hypoglycemia, ketoacidosis, dehydration, eGFR, HbA1c.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as cautious SGLT2 context with caveats, not as clearance for prolonged fasting.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
