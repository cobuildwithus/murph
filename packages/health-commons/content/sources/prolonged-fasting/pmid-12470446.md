This source is included for **Clinical/residential supervised fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** In 68 borderline-hypertension patients, a supervised water-only fasting/refeeding program reported blood-pressure reductions, but it was uncontrolled and longer than the protocol window.

**Why it matters:** Complements the hypertension cohort with borderline hypertension data while preserving the same supervision/duration boundary.

**Potential experiment signals:** morning blood pressure, body weight, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as supervised clinical boundary evidence only.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Adults with borderline hypertension in a medically supervised fasting program.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Medically supervised water-only fasting followed by refeeding.
- **Comparator/control:** No randomized control group.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Average fasting duration about 13.6 days with about 6 days refeeding; exceeds 72 hours.
- **Endpoints:** blood pressure, body weight, adverse events
- **Effect estimate or direction:** Reported average blood-pressure decrease and a high proportion achieving at-or-below 120/80 mmHg.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Adverse-event rates not extracted; supervision/refeeding were part of intervention.
- **Limitations:** Uncontrolled clinical cohort; longer than target; disease-specific and bundled with diet transition.
- **Population mismatch:** Borderline hypertension treatment setting is not healthy self-experiment evidence.
- **Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** clinical_supervised
