This source is included for **FMD/VLCD and modified-fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** The randomized FMD trial reported reductions in body weight, body fat, blood pressure, and IGF-1 after three cycles, with no serious adverse effects reported; risk-marker improvements were larger in participants with elevated baseline risk.

**Why it matters:** Key human FMD boundary trial; useful for FMD claims but not for water-only fasting claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:body-weight, anthropometric:waist-circumference, vitals:blood-pressure, biomarker:igf-1, biomarker:glucose, biomarker:lipids, biomarker:crp, safety:adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent FMD evidence only; do not promote to direct 24–72 hour prolonged-fasting efficacy.

**Directness and limitations:** Directness is `adjacent_variant`. FMD is 5-day formulated calorie intake, not zero-calorie fasting; biomarker/risk-marker outcomes rather than hard clinical outcomes. Population mismatch: Generally healthy adults using FMD cycles, not a single 24–72 hour water-only protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
