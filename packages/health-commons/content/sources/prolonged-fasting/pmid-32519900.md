This source is included for **Clinical/residential supervised fasting boundary**.

**Findings:** Narrative review describes fasting-regimen categories and glucose-to-ketone/metabolic-switch mechanisms; it does not provide direct protocol efficacy evidence.

**Why it matters:** Useful for defining dose/duration boundary and metabolic-switch language without overstating outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:** ketones, glucose, insulin, AMPK/mTOR.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for mechanism and classification context only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Human and translational fasting literature across clinical and nonclinical contexts.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Intermittent, long-term/prolonged, Buchinger, water-only, and fasting-mimicking approaches.
- **Comparator/control:** Not applicable to narrative taxonomy review.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Classifies intermittent fasting up to about 48 hours and prolonged/long-term fasting beyond about 2 days to weeks.
- **Endpoints:** glucose-to-ketone switch, mTOR/AMPK signaling, ketosis time course, protocol taxonomy
- **Effect estimate or direction:** Review describes mechanistic shifts and fasting-regimen taxonomy; not an efficacy trial.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Safety details not extracted as source-owned adverse-event rates.
- **Limitations:** Narrative review; mechanism/taxonomy context cannot establish protocol outcomes.
- **Population mismatch:** Broad review with longer fasts and clinical disease contexts.
- **Directness to Prolonged Fasting (24–72 Hours):** same_mechanism
