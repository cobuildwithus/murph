This source is included for **bone-density-turnover**.

**Findings:** Across 36 RCTs, joint outcomes appeared more consistently favorable than bone or muscle outcomes, while bone conclusions were limited and muscle effects often depended on exercise. Use this as a source-finding hub and to keep endpoint-specific certainty separated.

**Why it matters:** It provides recent trial inventory and endpoint-by-endpoint boundaries across bones, joints, and muscles.

**Potential experiment signals:** bone mineral density, joint pain, joint function, muscle strength, lean mass.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context only until each primary HCP-only trial is extracted by endpoint and co-intervention status.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Source type:** Systematic review (2025).
- **People studied or addressed:** Adults in randomized trials of oral type I collagen hydrolysate for bones, muscles, or joints; beauty, multi-ingredient, and uncontrolled studies were excluded.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Oral type I collagen hydrolysate supplementation.
- **Comparator or control:** Placebo or control arms in included RCTs.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Varied by included RCT.
- **Most relevant endpoints:** bone mineral density, joint pain, joint function, muscle strength, lean mass.
