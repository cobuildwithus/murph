This source is included for **tendon-loading-ligament**.

**Findings:** The tendinopathy supplement literature is heterogeneous and clinical-management oriented rather than clean HCP-alone evidence. Use to map formulations and identify primary studies, not to make HCP claims.

**Why it matters:** It supports variant/formulation separation for tendon supplement evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** tendinopathy pain, function, supplement formulation, clinical management.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context only; do not use as direct HCP efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
