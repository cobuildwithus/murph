This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:**

- **dose — Dose and duration:** Kviatkovsky 2023 adds long-duration 10-20 g/day active-adult evidence. Effect/direction: 10 g/day or 20 g/day collagen peptides over 3, 6, and 9 months.
- **mixed_result — KOOS ADL/pain and VR-12:** Active-adult results are promising but subgroup- and outcome-specific. Effect/direction: Selected outcomes favored collagen doses/subgroups: KOOS ADL and pain with 10 g/day over 6 months in relevant analyses; VR-12 MCS with 10 g/day and PCS with 20 g/day in females.
- **limitation — Attrition and subgroup signals:** Do not overgeneralize active-adult findings. Effect/direction: Completion declined by 9 months; some findings were limited to high-frequency exercisers or females.
- **population — Population mismatch:** Useful for active-adult function, not direct OA efficacy. Effect/direction: Middle-aged active adults, not OA-diagnosed patients.

**Why it matters:** Adds long-duration active-adult outcomes and 10-20 g/day dose comparison to the joint-function evidence map.

**Potential experiment signals:** KOOS activities of daily living, KOOS pain, VR-12 mental component score, VR-12 physical component score.

**Protocol takeaway:** 10-20 g/day over 6-9 months may affect selected function/quality-of-life outcomes in active adults, but evidence is mixed and non-OA.

**Murph takeaway:** Longer tracking windows may be needed for function/QoL outcomes; interpret subgroup signals cautiously.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness and boundary:** `direct_protocol`. Active adults, not diagnosed OA.

**Safety notes:** No detailed adverse-event extraction available from accessible summaries.

**Limitations:**

- Not an OA-diagnosed sample.
- Completion dropped over 9 months.
- Subgroup findings by exercise frequency and sex should be treated cautiously.
