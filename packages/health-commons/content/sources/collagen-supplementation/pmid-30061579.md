This source is included for **clinical-supervised-wound-nutrition**.

**Findings:**

- Participants were assigned to collagen hydrolysate A, collagen hydrolysate B, or placebo at 5 g twice daily for 16 weeks while receiving standard pressure-ulcer care. `[source_artifact:pmid-30061579]`
- The high Pro-Hyp/Hyp-Gly collagen hydrolysate arm reported lower PUSH, PSST, and wound-area outcomes versus placebo at week 16. `[source_artifact:pmid-30061579]`
- Reported adverse events included short-lived constipation or diarrhea in collagen groups and headache in placebo; laboratory analyses did not show clinically significant abnormalities. `[source_artifact:pmid-30061579]`

**Why it matters:** This is one of the clearest oral collagen hydrolysate clinical wound RCTs, useful for boundary and safety notes but not direct self-run protocol claims. `[source_artifact:pmid-30061579]`

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only: preserve details for wound-care boundary and adverse-event surveillance; do not promote as direct protocol efficacy evidence. `[source_artifact:pmid-30061579]`

**Population mismatch:** Adults aged 18-70 with Stage II or III pressure ulcers under standard care; 120 entered and 112 completed.

**Limitations:**

- Clinical Stage II/III pressure-ulcer population.
- Many exclusions, including malnutrition, diabetes-related ulcer contexts, burns, and major comorbidities.
- Sponsor/funder and author conflicts reported by the article.
- Wound-healing endpoints do not map to general wellness endpoints.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
