This source is included for **safety-quality-contaminants**.

**Findings:** Population/exposure: 20 volunteers, mostly male, with moderate-to-high aerobic fitness completing exercise/rest trials Intervention or exposure: Collagen peptides 10 g/day before exercise challenge. Comparator/control: Flavor-matched placebo/control exercise trial and non-exercise rest trial. Duration/follow-up: 7 days supplementation before trial plus dose 45 minutes before 70-minute run; acute post-exercise follow-up Endpoints: I-FABP, lactulose/rhamnose ratio, lipopolysaccharide, anti-LPS antibody, MCP-1, IL-6, IL-8, cortisol, ALP, subjective GI symptoms, physiological exercise variables. Direction/effect: Collagen peptides did not clearly change I-FABP, gut permeability, inflammatory markers, physiological variables, or subjective GI symptoms versus control; LPS increased in control at 2 hours post-exercise while remaining low after collagen peptides. Safety notes: No clear worsening of subjective GI symptoms was reported in the accessible abstract-level data. Limitations: Small crossover study.; Short exposure and acute exercise-stress model.; Mostly male fit volunteers; not routine collagen use in general population.; Safety/tolerability endpoints, not skin/joint efficacy endpoints.. Population mismatch: Exercise-stress model in volunteers rather than general wellness users.

**Why it matters:** Directly informs GI tolerability and exercise-induced gut-stress boundaries without becoming general efficacy evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** safety:gastrointestinal-tolerability, biomarker:I-FABP, biomarker:gut-permeability, biomarker:LPS.
