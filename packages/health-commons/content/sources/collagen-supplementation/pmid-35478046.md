This source is included for **bone-density-turnover**.

**Findings:**

- **Population:** Postmenopausal women and men aged 50 years and older in osteoporosis prevention/treatment contexts.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Prevention, risk assessment, diagnosis, pharmacologic treatment, calcium/vitamin D, exercise, fall prevention.
- **Comparator/control:** Not applicable.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Guideline.
- **Endpoints:** DXA, fracture risk, treatment thresholds, calcium/vitamin D, exercise/fall prevention.
- **Effect/direction:** Guide states osteoporosis is diagnosed/managed clinically, fracture risk should be assessed, and adequate calcium/vitamin D plus exercise/fall-prevention are adjunctive measures.
- **Adverse events/safety:** Medication safety details not extracted; supplement protocol should not delay clinical care.
- **Limitations:** Not a collagen source.; Use as clinical context only.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Clinical boundary/background.

**Why it matters:** It gives authoritative context for interpreting BMD and fracture-risk thresholds around the protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** DXA, fracture risk, calcium/vitamin D, exercise, fall prevention.

**Protocol takeaway:** Make clear that HCP is adjunctive/experimental and not osteoporosis treatment replacement.
