This source is included for **skin-aging**.

**Findings:** Sleep quality is a plausible confounder/modifier but has not been established as a tested collagen-sleep interaction in an RCT. Endpoints captured: skin hydration; skin elasticity; wrinkles; sleep quality; TEWL; oxidative stress hypothesis.

**Why it matters:** It links skin outcomes with sleep-quality context without creating direct causal claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep quality, skin hydration, TEWL, elasticity, wrinkles, confounding.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only to flag sleep as a possible confounder or context variable.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
