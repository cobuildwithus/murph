This source is included for **exercise-performance-recovery**.

**Findings:** Blend increased myofibrillar protein synthesis versus placebo in rested and exercised legs and increased muscle connective protein synthesis in the rested leg but not clearly in the exercised leg (P=0.11 for exercised leg muscle connective protein synthesis).

**Why it matters:** Adjacent co-intervention mechanistic source.

**Potential experiment signals:** MyoPS, muscle connective protein synthesis, plasma amino acids.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use whey-plus-collagen blend effects as isolated collagen peptide evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness and boundary:** `adjacent_variant`. High-whey blend and acute tracer endpoints.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event signal extracted from accessible abstract text.

**Limitations:** Adjacent variant: collagen was only 5 g in a whey-rich blend.; Young men only.; Acute synthesis endpoints, not long-term outcomes.

**Population mismatch:** Whey-plus-collagen blend in young men; not isolated hydrolyzed collagen peptides.
