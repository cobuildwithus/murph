This source is included for **skin-aging**.

**Findings:** Hydrolyzed collagen improved skin hydration, elasticity, and wrinkles in pooled skin-aging RCTs, with strong female-generalizability caveat. Use as direct skin-aging evidence, but disclose sex distribution and product heterogeneity.

**Why it matters:** It is a core meta-analysis for hydration, elasticity, and wrinkle endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** skin hydration, skin elasticity, wrinkles, skin firmness, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Directly supports tracking skin hydration/elasticity/wrinkles, but not a guarantee for all users.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction details

- **Source type:** Systematic review and meta-analysis (2021).
- **People studied or addressed:** Adults aged 20 to 70 years in randomized double-blind controlled trials; about 95% of participants were women.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Oral hydrolyzed collagen supplementation.
- **Comparator or control:** Placebo/control.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Varied; review reports 90 days as effective for wrinkles, elasticity, and hydration.
- **Most relevant endpoints:** skin hydration, skin elasticity, wrinkles, skin firmness, adverse events.
