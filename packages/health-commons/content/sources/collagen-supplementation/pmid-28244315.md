This source is included for **mechanism-bioavailability**.

## Findings

Human arm identified 17 transient collagen-derived peptides after ingestion; preclinical arm showed Gly-Pro-Hyp/Pro-Hyp-related distribution into skin compartments.

## Why it matters

It is a foundational mechanism source for claims that collagen-derived peptides can appear systemically and in skin-related compartments.

## Potential experiment signals

- Gly-Pro-Hyp exposure
- Pro-Hyp exposure
- skin peptide distribution
- source/formulation comparison

## Protocol takeaway

Use for mechanism/bioavailability language, not for skin-outcome efficacy claims.

## Safety and adverse events

No adverse-event signal extracted.

## Limitations and mismatch

- Small acute human PK sample.
- Includes mouse distribution work.
- No clinical efficacy endpoints.
- Product-formulation specificity matters.
- Population mismatch: Healthy acute PK participants plus mice; not clinical-outcome evidence.
