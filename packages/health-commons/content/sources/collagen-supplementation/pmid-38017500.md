This source is included for **tendon-loading-ligament**.

**Findings:** No efficacy results; this is a study protocol.

**Population and intervention:** Planned 76 athletes with patellar tendinopathy/jumper’s knee, aged 16-40 years, with symptoms for at least 12 weeks and sports participation at least weekly. Intervention/exposure: Hydrolyzed collagen plus vitamin C as an add-on to progressive tendon-loading exercise treatment. Comparator/control: Placebo plus the same progressive tendon-loading exercise treatment. Duration/follow-up: Planned 24-week primary endpoint.

**Endpoints:** VISA-P at 24 weeks and secondary tendon pain/function and exercise-treatment outcomes as defined in the protocol.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event results available because the source is a protocol.

**Limitations:** Protocol only; no outcome data.; Hydrolyzed collagen is combined with vitamin C and progressive tendon loading.; Use only for design and planned-endpoint context until results are available.

**Population mismatch:** Direct to patellar tendinopathy athletes, but not yet efficacy evidence.

**Why it matters:** It signals active clinical testing of hydrolyzed collagen as an add-on to tendon-loading therapy in jumper’s knee.

**Potential experiment signals:** VISA-P; pain during sport; loading adherence; patellar tendon imaging if reported in future results

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep this source in context-only status until trial results are published or registry results are available.
