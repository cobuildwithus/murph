This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:** Fifty-five healthy volunteers with activity-related knee discomfort were randomized to UC-II 40 mg/day or placebo for 120 days. Reported outcomes favored UC-II for knee extension and time to pain onset, and no product-related adverse events were reported. This is population-mismatched and variant-mismatched for HCP.

**Why it matters:** Helps classify low-dose UC-II joint-flexibility marketing separately from gram-dose HCP evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** knee extension, time to onset of knee discomfort, activity-related pain, safety

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent variant context only, never as direct HCP support.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
