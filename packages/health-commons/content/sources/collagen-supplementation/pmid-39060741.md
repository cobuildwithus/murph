This source is included for **exercise-performance-recovery**.

**Findings:** Collagen peptides plus training showed favorable pooled effects on fat-free mass, tendon morphology, muscle architecture, strength, and 48-hour reactive-strength recovery, but certainty varied. Do not generalize these findings to HCP without concurrent training.

**Why it matters:** It is the most relevant exercise/training synthesis but belongs in a co-intervention boundary bucket.

**Potential experiment signals:** lean mass, tendon morphology, muscle strength, exercise recovery.

**Protocol takeaway:** Training-plus-collagen effects should be labeled as such; not HCP-alone efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Source type:** Systematic review and meta-analysis (2024).
- **People studied or addressed:** Healthy normal-to-overweight adults older than 17 years undergoing long-term physical training.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Daily collagen peptide supplementation combined with physical training; authors note likely relevance of 15 g/day for at least 8 weeks.
- **Comparator or control:** Placebo/control supplementation with similar training context.
- **Duration or follow-up:** At least 8 weeks of supplementation and physical training in eligible studies.
- **Most relevant endpoints:** lean mass, tendon morphology, muscle strength, exercise recovery.

## Safety notes

## Limitations and mismatch

GRADE certainty ranged from moderate for body composition to very low for tendon morphology/mechanical properties and low for several other outcomes. Population mismatch: Healthy trained or training adults; evidence requires concurrent exercise rather than supplement-alone use.
