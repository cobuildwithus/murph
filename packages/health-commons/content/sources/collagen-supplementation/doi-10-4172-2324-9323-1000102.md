This source is included for **bone-density-turnover**.

**Findings:**

- **Population:** Postmenopausal women with osteopenia 1 to 5 years after menopause, not on HRT or bone-influencing medications in accessible abstract.
- **Intervention/exposure:** 5 g calcium-collagen chelate containing 500 mg calcium carbonate and 5 µg vitamin D daily.
- **Comparator/control:** 500 mg calcium carbonate plus 5 µg vitamin D daily.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 3 months.
- **Endpoints:** total body BMD, lumbar spine BMD, BAP/TRAP5b ratio.
- **Effect/direction:** Accessible abstract reports total body BMD increased versus control (p<0.05) and BAP/TRAP5b ratio percent change increased (p<0.05).
- **Adverse events/safety:** Adverse events not extracted.
- **Limitations:** Calcium-collagen chelate is not hydrolyzed collagen peptides alone.; Sample size not available in accessible abstract-level record.; Short duration and pilot context.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Postmenopausal osteopenia population matches, but intervention is a calcium-collagen chelate formulation.

**Why it matters:** It is historical adjacent evidence for collagen-containing mineral supplements, not the target protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** total body BMD, lumbar spine BMD, BAP/TRAP5b ratio.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only: calcium-collagen chelate data should not be cited as HCP-alone data.
