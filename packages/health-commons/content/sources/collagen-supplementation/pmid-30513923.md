This source is included for **skin-aging**.

**Findings:** Collagen peptide plus ornithine showed favorable TEWL/elasticity-related signals, but ornithine makes it an adjacent blend. Endpoints captured: skin elasticity; transepidermal water loss; skin moisture; pore number; collagen score; plasma IGF-1; adverse events.

**Why it matters:** This source gives granular skin-barrier and safety notes but remains adjacent because the intervention includes ornithine.

**Potential experiment signals:** TEWL, skin elasticity, plasma IGF-1, pore number, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not treat the TEWL/elasticity findings as collagen-only; cite only as a collagen-plus-ornithine boundary source.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
