This source is included for **exercise-performance-recovery**.

**Findings:** Review-level extraction reports significant differences favoring collagen for VAS pain (d=0.678) and fatigue (d=0.715), while CPK/LDH showed time effects without significant group differences.

**Why it matters:** Direct protocol evidence for short-term exercise-induced soreness/fatigue.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle soreness, fatigue, knee extension force, CPK, LDH.

**Protocol takeaway:** Collagen peptides may reduce subjective soreness/fatigue after exercise load in middle-aged men, but objective muscle-damage biomarkers were not clearly improved.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness and boundary:** `direct_protocol`. Small crossover study and mixed objective biomarker results.

**Safety notes:** Full-analysis safety set was reported as n=19; no specific adverse-event signal was extracted here.

**Limitations:** Small crossover trial.; Middle-aged males only.; Dose/timing should be verified from the full text before protocol dosing language.; Biomarker effects were mixed/null.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy middle-aged males; not women, athletes, or older adults with sarcopenia.
