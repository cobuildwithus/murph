This source is included for **skin-aging**.

**Findings:** Multi-ingredient collagen product reported skin/joint improvements, but the collagen contribution cannot be isolated. Endpoints captured: skin elasticity; skin hydration; histology; solar elastosis; joint pain; joint mobility; general wellbeing.

**Why it matters:** This is a relevant but non-isolatable skin/joint product trial, so it helps guard against over-generalizing blend outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:** skin elasticity, histology/solar elastosis, joint pain, joint mobility, multi-ingredient adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as context for multi-ingredient products; do not cite as direct hydrolyzed collagen peptide monotherapy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
