This source is included for **clinical-supervised-wound-nutrition**.

**Findings:**

- The guideline resource is included as current clinical pressure-injury prevention/treatment context, not as collagen peptide efficacy evidence. `[source_artifact:npiap-pressure-injury-guidelines-2026-04-25]`
- The online nutrition chapter emphasizes screening for nutrition risk, comprehensive assessment when indicated, individualized nutrition care plans, hydration, and clinician/dietitian involvement. `[source_artifact:npiap-pressure-injury-guidelines-2026-04-25]`
- Protein or nutrition supplementation is presented as part of clinical care for at-risk or malnourished individuals, not as a self-run wellness protocol. `[source_artifact:npiap-pressure-injury-guidelines-2026-04-25]`

**Why it matters:** Gives a current professional boundary for pressure-injury nutrition and prevents self-run protocol recommendations for wound care. `[source_artifact:npiap-pressure-injury-guidelines-2026-04-25]`
