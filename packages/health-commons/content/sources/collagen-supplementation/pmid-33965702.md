This source is included for **tendon-loading-ligament**.

**Findings:** Even in tendinopathy exercise trials, progression, individualization, and adherence reporting were often incomplete. Collagen-plus-loading studies should not be judged without evaluating the loading program.

**Why it matters:** It defines the loading-program comparator quality issue for tendon supplementation studies.

**Potential experiment signals:** loading program quality, training adherence, tendinopathy pain, function.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context only: assess loading quality/adherence before attributing tendon outcomes to HCP.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Source type:** Systematic review (2021).
- **People studied or addressed:** People with five lower-limb tendinopathies in resistance-training exercise intervention RCTs.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Resistance-training exercise interventions for lower limb tendinopathy.
- **Comparator or control:** Usual care, other exercise, placebo, or other comparators depending on trial.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Varied across RCTs.
- **Most relevant endpoints:** loading program quality, training adherence, tendinopathy pain, function.
