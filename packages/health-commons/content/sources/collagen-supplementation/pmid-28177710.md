This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:**

- **dose — Dose and duration:** Zdzieblik 2017 supports a 5 g/day activity-related knee-pain dose. Effect/direction: 5 g/day bioactive collagen peptides for 12 weeks.
- **outcome — Activity-related knee pain by VAS:** Specific collagen peptides improved activity-related knee pain versus placebo. Effect/direction: Participant ΔVAS 19.5 ± 2.4 vs 13.9 ± 2.1, p=0.046; physician ΔVAS 16.7 ± 1.8 vs 12.2 ± 1.8, p=0.021.
- **null_result — Rest pain/range of motion:** Effect appears tied to activity-related pain, not rest pain or ROM. Effect/direction: Rest pain was not significantly different versus placebo; range-of-motion change was not clearly detected due to high baseline mobility.
- **population — Population mismatch:** Use as activity-related discomfort evidence rather than OA evidence. Effect/direction: Athletic functional knee pain, not diagnosed OA.

**Why it matters:** Direct collagen-peptide RCT with extractable 5 g/day dose and activity-related endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** activity-related VAS pain, physician-assessed VAS pain, rest pain, range of motion, additional therapies.

**Protocol takeaway:** 5 g/day for 12 weeks may help activity-related knee discomfort in active people; OA claims need OA-specific sources.

**Murph takeaway:** Track pain during a standardized load/activity rather than only resting pain.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness and boundary:** `direct_protocol`. Young athletic functional knee pain, not knee OA.

**Safety notes:** No detailed adverse-event extraction available from accessible summaries.

**Limitations:**

- Functional knee pain in athletes is not diagnosed OA.
- Rest pain and range-of-motion outcomes were not clearly improved versus placebo.
