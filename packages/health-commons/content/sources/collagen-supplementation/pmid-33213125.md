This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:** The pilot included 30 knee-OA patients with effusion; 15 received a multi-ingredient oral preparation for four weeks after arthrocentesis and 15 served as arthrocentesis-only controls. Accessible summaries reported improvements in WOMAC/Lequesne/VAS indices and synovial cytokine changes. The product included HA, chondroitin sulfate, hydrolyzed collagen type II, and hydrolyzed keratin, so it is context-only.

**Why it matters:** Prevents multi-ingredient synovial-fluid evidence from being used as peptide-only support.

**Potential experiment signals:** WOMAC, Lequesne index, VAS pain, synovial IL-6, synovial IL-8, synovial IL-10

**Protocol takeaway:** Boundary evidence only; useful for possible inflammatory/synovial endpoints.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
