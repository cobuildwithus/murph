This source is included for **safety-quality-contaminants**.

**Findings:** Population/exposure: 277 dietary supplement products available in UAE markets; no human participants Intervention or exposure: Dietary supplement products from multiple categories. Comparator/control: Daily intake estimates compared with adopted daily intake limits. Duration/follow-up: Single market sampling/testing period Endpoints: cadmium, lead, arsenic, estimated daily intake, product-category risk factors. Direction/effect: Average estimated daily intakes for cadmium, lead, and arsenic were below adopted daily-intake limits; about 1.1% of products reportedly failed daily limits for these metals. Safety notes: Highlights that some dietary supplements can exceed contaminant limits and that multiple-supplement use may create cumulative exposure. Limitations: General dietary supplements, not collagen-specific.; UAE market sample may not generalize to other countries.; No human exposure biomarkers or clinical events.. Population mismatch: Product samples rather than HCP users.

**Why it matters:** Supports general supplement product-quality caution when collagen-specific data are limited.

**Potential experiment signals:** safety:heavy-metals, safety:dietary-supplement-quality, safety:cumulative-exposure.
