This source is included for **exercise-performance-recovery**.

**Findings:** Countermovement jump height was maintained at 24 h in the collagen group, whereas placebo declined at 24 h; soreness rose in both groups at 24 and 48 h; maximal voluntary isometric contraction showed time effects; collagen turnover biomarkers did not change significantly.

**Why it matters:** Direct eccentric-exercise recovery pilot in trained males.

**Potential experiment signals:** countermovement jump height, muscle soreness, MVIC, collagen turnover biomarkers.

**Protocol takeaway:** Collagen peptides may attenuate next-day jump-performance decline after damaging exercise, but the evidence is pilot-level and mixed.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness and boundary:** `direct_protocol`. Pilot study with very small groups.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event signal was extracted from accessible abstract text.

**Limitations:** Pilot sample (CP n=7, placebo n=8).; Short preloading and short follow-up.; Only resistance-trained males.; Full text is permission-required, so extraction relies on accessible abstract-level details.

**Population mismatch:** Resistance-trained male pilot cohort; not general adults.
