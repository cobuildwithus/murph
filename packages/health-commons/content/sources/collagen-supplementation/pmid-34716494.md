This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:** The white paper discusses collagen hydrolyzates and ultrahydrolyzates as potential OA-support supplements, describes bioactive peptide hypotheses, and emphasizes that evidence is based on small-scale trials and ongoing research rather than established OA treatment recommendations.

**Why it matters:** Provides product-composition and terminology context that can keep the protocol page from conflating collagen forms.

**Potential experiment signals:** product composition, bioactive peptides, cartilage mechanism, OA symptom endpoints

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for mechanism and terminology, not as clinical efficacy proof.
