This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:** A 12-week trial randomized 101 knee-OA adults to native type-II collagen, glucosamine plus chondroitin, or placebo; 89 completed. Native type-II collagen and glucosamine/chondroitin improved WOMAC total and pain versus placebo in accessible text, and safety monitoring did not raise major concerns in the extracted summary.

**Why it matters:** Important adjacent RCT but not directly usable for HCP.

**Potential experiment signals:** WOMAC total, WOMAC pain, WOMAC stiffness, WOMAC physical function, quality of life, safety labs

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only native-collagen evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
