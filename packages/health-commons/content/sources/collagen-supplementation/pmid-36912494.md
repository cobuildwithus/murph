This source is included for **safety-quality-contaminants**.

**Findings:** Population/exposure: 20 Middle Eastern adults, 18 women and 2 men, mean age about 44 years, skin type III-IV Intervention or exposure: Daily oral collagen supplement / oral collagen peptides. Comparator/control: Within-participant baseline; no placebo control. Duration/follow-up: 12 weeks daily use plus follow-up at week 16 Endpoints: adverse effects, GI tolerability, skin elasticity parameters, skin hydration, friction, dermis thickness, dermis echo density, participant satisfaction. Direction/effect: The accessible abstract reported a few gastrointestinal complications and described the product as safe and well tolerated; skin endpoints improved in the uncontrolled before-after design. Safety notes: A few gastrointestinal complications were reported; no severe adverse events were identified in the accessible abstract. Limitations: Small sample size.; No placebo or randomized comparator.; Skin efficacy endpoints are not extracted as protocol efficacy claims in this safety batch.; Exact product formulation and dose require full-text verification before implementation claims.. Population mismatch: Middle Eastern skin-study volunteers rather than broad users; uncontrolled design.

**Why it matters:** Directly reports tolerability but the design prevents strong comparative safety or efficacy claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** safety:gastrointestinal-tolerability, safety:adverse-events, outcome:skin-biophysics.
