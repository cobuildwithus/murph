This source is included for **bone-density-turnover**.

**Findings:**

- **Population:** Bone turnover marker testing context.
- **Intervention/exposure:** No collagen intervention.
- **Comparator/control:** Not applicable.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Not applicable.
- **Endpoints:** CTX-I, PINP, pre-analytical variability.
- **Effect/direction:** Recommendation emphasizes standardized collection; CTX-I should be collected consistently in the morning fasted state, while PINP is less affected by circadian/food effects.
- **Adverse events/safety:** Not applicable.
- **Limitations:** Marker-handling source only.; Does not establish efficacy of any intervention.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Background marker-quality source.

**Why it matters:** It helps interpret whether CTX/PINP changes in collagen studies are robust or potentially pre-analytical noise.

**Potential experiment signals:** fasted morning CTX, PINP stability, sample matrix, exercise/medication confounders.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for things-to-watch in biomarker interpretation.
