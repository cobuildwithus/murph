This source is included for **bone-density-turnover**.

**Findings:**

- **Population:** Community-dwelling asymptomatic adults; recommendation excludes known osteoporosis, prior osteoporotic fracture, increased fall risk, and vitamin D deficiency.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Vitamin D, calcium, or combined supplementation for primary fracture prevention.
- **Comparator/control:** No supplementation/usual care across evidence base.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Guideline/evidence review, not a single trial duration.
- **Endpoints:** fracture prevention, kidney stones, population boundary.
- **Effect/direction:** USPSTF recommends against ≤400 IU vitamin D and ≤1000 mg calcium for primary fracture prevention in community-dwelling postmenopausal women, and finds insufficient evidence for higher doses and for men/premenopausal women.
- **Adverse events/safety:** Guideline notes adequate evidence that vitamin D plus calcium increases kidney-stone incidence, with small magnitude of harm.
- **Limitations:** Not a collagen source.; Applies to primary prevention in community-dwelling asymptomatic adults and explicitly excludes osteoporosis/vitamin D deficiency contexts.; Update-in-progress status should be checked for future synthesis updates.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Calcium/vitamin D comparator and clinical-boundary source only.

**Why it matters:** Calcium/vitamin D appears in adjacent collagen trials; this source prevents conflating supplement stacks with standard guidance.

**Potential experiment signals:** fracture prevention, kidney stones, exclusion criteria.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as boundary context for calcium/D comparators and safety, not HCP efficacy.
