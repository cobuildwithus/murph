This source is included for **safety-quality-contaminants**.

**Findings:** Population/exposure: 25-year-old Japanese woman with recurrent anaphylaxis after fish-collagen-containing gummy candy Intervention or exposure: Gummy candy containing fish collagen peptide. Comparator/control: Prick testing with other gummy ingredients and healthy-volunteer controls in the diagnostic evaluation. Duration/follow-up: Acute reactions after ingestion; diagnostic evaluation during follow-up Endpoints: anaphylaxis, skin-prick testing, IgE/immunoblot evidence. Direction/effect: Prick-to-prick testing was positive to the gummy and fish collagen peptide and negative to other gummy ingredients in the accessible case report. Safety notes: Recurrent anaphylactic shock was attributed to fish collagen in a gummy candy. Limitations: Single case report.; Food product exposure, not a standalone HCP supplement.; Cannot estimate risk among all consumers.. Population mismatch: Single fish-collagen-allergic patient rather than general collagen users.

**Why it matters:** Adds a severe allergic-reaction signal for orally consumed fish collagen in processed food/supplement-like products.
