This source is included for **safety-quality-contaminants**.

**Findings:** Population/exposure: 30-year-old woman with atopic dermatitis and prior topical fish atelocollagen exposure Intervention or exposure: Ingestion of an oral product containing hydrolyzed fish collagen / fish collagen peptide. Comparator/control: No comparator. Duration/follow-up: Acute reaction within minutes; prior topical exposure over months reported Endpoints: anaphylaxis symptoms, IgE reactivity to fish collagen-related proteins. Direction/effect: Anaphylaxis occurred after ingestion; the authors considered epicutaneous sensitization from topical fish atelocollagen plausible. Safety notes: Severe immediate hypersensitivity reaction temporally linked to hydrolyzed fish collagen ingestion. Limitations: Single case report cannot estimate incidence.; Atopic dermatitis and topical exposure create a susceptibility context.; Product co-ingredients and commercial processing details may limit generalizability.. Population mismatch: Single susceptible patient rather than general adult collagen users.

**Why it matters:** Directly flags hydrolyzed fish collagen as a potential serious-allergy risk in susceptible individuals.
