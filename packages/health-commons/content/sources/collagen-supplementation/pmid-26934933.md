This source is included for **mechanism-bioavailability**.

## Findings

Accessible abstract reports postprandial increases in Gly-Pro-Hyp and Pro-Hyp after tripeptide preparations, with urinary peptide excretion depending on ingested Gly-Pro-Hyp.

## Why it matters

It clarifies that formulation can alter circulating and urinary peptide species.

## Potential experiment signals

- Gly-Pro-Hyp plasma exposure
- Pro-Hyp plasma exposure
- urinary Gly-Pro-Hyp
- urinary Pro-Hyp

## Protocol takeaway

Mechanism/bioavailability context only.

## Safety and adverse events

No adverse-event signal extracted.

## Limitations and mismatch

- Small acute PK sample.
- Specific collagen-tripeptide preparations may not generalize to all hydrolyzed collagen peptides.
- No clinical endpoint.
- Population mismatch: Healthy acute PK volunteers; formulation-specific exposure context.

`context-only`. This source should remain in its assigned evidence bucket and should not be promoted into direct Hydrolyzed Collagen Peptides protocol synthesis unless a later synthesis step explicitly resolves directness and endpoint scope.
