This source is included for **exercise-performance-recovery**.

**Findings:** PI3K-Akt and MAPK pathways were significantly upregulated at 1 and 4 h post-exercise, with significant between-group differences favoring collagen at 4 h; mTOR gene expression showed a visual trend but no statistically significant group difference.

**Why it matters:** Human mechanistic source for collagen-plus-resistance-exercise signaling.

**Potential experiment signals:** PI3K-Akt gene expression, MAPK gene expression, mTOR trend, biopsy timing.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mechanism context only: 15 g specific collagen peptides altered acute signaling, but long-term outcome translation remains uncertain.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness and boundary:** `same_mechanism`. Gene expression does not equal hypertrophy or functional benefit.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event signal extracted from accessible text.

**Limitations:** Acute gene-expression endpoints are not clinical outcomes.; Young/recreationally active men only.; Fasted standardized setting may not reflect real-world meals.; Product-specific peptides.

**Population mismatch:** Mechanistic male cohort; not a clinical outcome trial.
