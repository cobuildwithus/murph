This source is included for **metabolic-cardiovascular-adjacent**.

## Findings

Collagen hydrolysate did not significantly affect daytime interstitial glucose AUC (p=0.159) but increased several glycemic variability metrics and postprandial/fasting insulin-related measures; conclusion stated it did not improve glucose metabolism or other cardiometabolic risk markers.

## Why it matters

It prevents unsupported metabolic-benefit claims and flags glucose-variability outcomes to watch.

## Potential experiment signals

- daytime interstitial glucose AUC
- glycemic variability
- postprandial glucose AUC
- fasting insulin
- HOMA-IR

## Protocol takeaway

Use as context-only metabolic caution; not core outcome support.

## Safety and adverse events

No adverse-event signal extracted; metabolic marker worsening was small and characterized as of limited clinical relevance.

## Limitations and mismatch

- Short 4-week duration.
- Overweight/obesity metabolic population.
- Metabolic endpoints outside core protocol outcomes.
- Absolute changes may have limited clinical relevance.
- Population mismatch: Metabolic-risk/overweight population; not a skin/joint/tendon efficacy population.
