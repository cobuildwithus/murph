This source is included for **bone-density-turnover**.

**Findings:**

- **Population:** Postmenopausal women with osteoporosis, especially high fracture risk.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Pharmacologic osteoporosis therapies; bisphosphonates and other agents.
- **Comparator/control:** Clinical alternatives/usual care per guideline evidence.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Guideline, not a single intervention duration.
- **Endpoints:** fracture risk, pharmacologic treatment, bisphosphonate reassessment.
- **Effect/direction:** Guideline emphasizes treating high-risk individuals and considering bisphosphonates first line, with reassessment after 3-5 years of bisphosphonate therapy.
- **Adverse events/safety:** Drug-specific adverse-event discussion is outside this extraction; collagen is not addressed as treatment.
- **Limitations:** Not a collagen source.; Boundary for standard-of-care medication, not supplement efficacy.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Clinical standard-of-care boundary.

**Why it matters:** It anchors the protocol boundary for diagnosed osteoporosis and high fracture risk.

**Potential experiment signals:** fracture risk, bisphosphonate first line, treatment reassessment.

**Protocol takeaway:** State that collagen supplementation is not a replacement for osteoporosis pharmacologic management.
