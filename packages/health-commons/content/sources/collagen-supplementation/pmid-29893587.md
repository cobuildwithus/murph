This source is included for **adjacent-variant-context**.

## Findings

Standardized bone-broth preparations were reported as lower and less consistent in collagen-precursor amino acids than a 20 g reference collagen-supplement dose used in research contexts.

## Why it matters

It helps distinguish protocol-grade collagen peptide dosing from variable food preparations.

## Potential experiment signals

- hydroxyproline precursor exposure
- collagen-dose reliability
- bone-broth boundary

## Protocol takeaway

Use for dose and variant boundary notes only; do not use as evidence that hydrolyzed collagen peptides improve outcomes.

## Safety and adverse events

Not applicable; no participants.

## Limitations and mismatch

- No human intervention or outcome testing.
- Bone-broth recipes and commercial products may vary.
- Does not evaluate hydrolyzed collagen peptide efficacy.
- Population mismatch: No participant population; analytical food context only.

`context-only`. This source should remain in its assigned evidence bucket and should not be promoted into direct Hydrolyzed Collagen Peptides protocol synthesis unless a later synthesis step explicitly resolves directness and endpoint scope.
