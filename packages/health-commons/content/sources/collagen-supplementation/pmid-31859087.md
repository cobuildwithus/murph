This source is included for **clinical-supervised-wound-nutrition**.

**Findings:**

- The accessible abstract reports 31 male burn patients completed a 4-week randomized double-blind pilot trial of a hydrolyzed collagen-based supplement versus isocaloric placebo. `[source_artifact:pmid-31859087]`
- The intervention improved serum prealbumin at weeks 2 and 4 and reported faster wound healing; hospital stay was lower numerically but not statistically significant in the accessible abstract. `[source_artifact:pmid-31859087]`
- Adverse-event details were not extracted from the accessible abstract. `[source_artifact:pmid-31859087]`

**Why it matters:** Separates acute burn medical nutrition from wellness collagen supplementation and highlights that wound outcomes require clinical supervision. `[source_artifact:pmid-31859087]`

**Potential experiment signals:**

  - "serum prealbumin"
  - "burn wound healing rate"
  - "hospital length of stay"
  - "body weight and BMI"
  - "dietary intake"

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only: burn wound healing evidence should not be used for general Hydrolyzed Collagen Peptides claims. `[source_artifact:pmid-31859087]`

**Population mismatch:** Men aged 18-60 with 20-30% total body surface area burns in acute burn care.

**Limitations:**

- Small pilot study.
- Only men with acute 20-30% TBSA burns were studied.
- The supplement was a 1000 kcal clinical nutrition product rather than a simple HCP powder.
- Full text appears paywalled and exact collagen dose was not available from the accessible abstract.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
