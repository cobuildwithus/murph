This source is included for **tendon-loading-ligament**.

**Findings:** Progressive tendon-loading therapy showed better clinical outcomes at 24 weeks than eccentric exercise therapy in the extracted record, including larger VISA-P improvement and lower pain during tendon-specific exercise.

**Population and intervention:** 76 athletes with patellar tendinopathy. Intervention/exposure: Progressive tendon-loading exercise therapy. Comparator/control: Eccentric exercise therapy. Duration/follow-up: 24 weeks.

**Endpoints:** VISA-P, tendon-specific pain, return to sport, patient satisfaction.

**Safety notes:** No supplement safety data; not applicable.

**Limitations:** No collagen supplement intervention.; Exercise comparator context only.; Use to contextualize standard loading care, not HCP efficacy.

**Population mismatch:** Direct patellar tendinopathy condition, but loading-only intervention.

**Why it matters:** It anchors the exercise comparator used or referenced by collagen-plus-loading protocols.

**Potential experiment signals:** VISA-P; pain during tendon-specific exercise; return-to-sport rate; patient satisfaction

**Protocol takeaway:** For patellar tendinopathy, loading therapy is a core intervention; collagen should be framed as a possible add-on, not the main treatment.
