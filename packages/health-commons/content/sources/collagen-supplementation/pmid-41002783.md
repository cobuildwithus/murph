This source is included for **bone-density-turnover**.

**Findings:**

- **Population:** Menopausal women; osteopenia/osteoporosis status not extracted as an inclusion requirement.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Four arms: placebo, calcium 1000 mg plus vitamin D3 400 IU, 5 g fish-derived collagen, or calcium/vitamin D plus 5 g collagen for 6 months.
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo and/or calcium/vitamin D arms depending comparison.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 6 months.
- **Endpoints:** P1NP, bone alkaline phosphatase, osteocalcin, bone density, skin hydration/elasticity, urinary calcium, liver/kidney markers.
- **Effect/direction:** Accessible article reports no significant differences in bone biomarkers P1NP, bone alkaline phosphatase, or osteocalcin; skin hydration/elasticity improved in collagen-containing arms.
- **Adverse events/safety:** Liver and kidney markers remained within normal ranges; urinary calcium showed slight increases in calcium-containing groups in accessible article.
- **Limitations:** Mixed outcomes include skin endpoints, not only bone.; Six months may be short for BMD.; Calcium/D and collagen-alone arms exist, but bone-biomarker results were null in accessible data.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Menopausal women are relevant, but not specifically low-BMD and bone signal was null.

**Why it matters:** It is recent and separates collagen-only from calcium/D combinations but reports no clear bone-biomarker advantage.

**Potential experiment signals:** P1NP, bone alkaline phosphatase, osteocalcin, urinary calcium, skin elasticity.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only or mixed/null evidence for bone biomarkers; keep skin outcomes out of bone claims.
