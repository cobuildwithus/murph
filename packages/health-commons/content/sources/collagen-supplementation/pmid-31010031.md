This source is included for **exercise-performance-recovery**.

**Findings:** The treatment group had greater increases in fat-free mass, greater body-fat reduction, and higher gains in hand-grip strength; leg strength improved in both groups with a more pronounced improvement in the collagen group. Exact estimates were not extracted from accessible abstract text.

**Why it matters:** Adds a female-population RCT to the otherwise male-heavy training evidence base.

**Potential experiment signals:** fat-free mass, body fat percentage, hand-grip strength, leg strength.

**Protocol takeaway:** A 15 g/day specific collagen peptide regimen may improve training-adjunct body composition and strength markers in premenopausal women.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness and boundary:** `direct_protocol`. Not evidence for collagen without training or for all collagen products.

**Safety notes:** Screening and safety laboratory context was described; no specific adverse-event signal was extracted.

**Limitations:** Premenopausal women only.; Resistance training was a required co-intervention.; Body-composition measurement method and diet/training adherence may affect translation.

**Population mismatch:** Female premenopausal cohort; not older adults, men, or athletes.
