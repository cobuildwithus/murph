This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:** Collagen derivatives improved OA pain and function in pooled analysis, but derivative mixing means this is not direct HCP evidence. Use as review-context and safety boundary only until derivative-specific extraction is completed.

**Why it matters:** It is a large recent OA derivative review with explicit safety and evidence-quality framing.

**Potential experiment signals:** joint pain, joint function, adverse events, evidence quality.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context only; extract derivative-specific primary data before HCP protocol claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Source type:** Systematic review and meta-analysis (2024).
- **People studied or addressed:** People with osteoarthritis in randomized trials of collagen derivatives.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Collagen derivatives, including multiple derivative types.
- **Comparator or control:** Placebo/control.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Varied across RCTs.
- **Most relevant endpoints:** joint pain, joint function, adverse events, evidence quality.

## Safety notes

Safety details were extracted only where the accessible source text or abstract-level record included them. Otherwise, this page intentionally leaves adverse-event counts unresolved rather than inventing them.

## Limitations and mismatch

Derivative types are mixed; trial-sequential conclusions should not be promoted to HCP alone. Population mismatch: Mixed collagen derivative interventions and OA clinical populations.
