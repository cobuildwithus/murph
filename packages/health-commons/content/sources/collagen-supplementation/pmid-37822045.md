This source is included for **skin-aging**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study.
- **People studied or addressed:** Healthy adults.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Low-molecular-weight collagen peptide supplement; accessible secondary table reports 2.5 g/day fish-derived collagen peptides.
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 12 weeks with baseline and weeks 4, 8, and 12 assessments.
- **Role in Murph:** direct protocol evidence for Hydrolyzed Collagen Peptides.

**Findings:** Wrinkle roughness and height parameters improved versus placebo in accessible summaries. Elasticity, hydration, and skin-tone/whitening-related indexes improved versus placebo at 12 weeks in accessible summaries. No adverse events related to the test product were reported in accessible summaries.

**Adverse events or safety:** No adverse events related to the test product were reported in accessible summaries.

**Why it matters:** Recent direct LMWCP RCT with multiple biophysical skin endpoints and safety reporting.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- wrinkle roughness and height parameters
- overall/net/biological elasticity
- skin hydration
- melanin and erythema indexes
- adverse events

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports wrinkle, elasticity, and hydration endpoints over 12 weeks, with dose confirmation and tone/whitening boundary caveats.

**Limitations and population fit:**

- Primary article appears paywalled.
- Dose comes from an accessible secondary table rather than independently extracted full text.
- Healthy-adult sample; baseline skin concerns may vary.
- Skin-tone/whitening outcomes should be kept separate from hydration/wrinkle claims.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy adults may not match all Murph users; do not generalize to different ages, sexes, baseline skin status, or collagen product types without caveats.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
