This source is included for **exercise-performance-recovery**.

**Findings:** Whey protein was superior to leucine-matched collagen peptides for increasing muscle thickness; accessible indexed sources note the comparison was not clearly strength-superior.

**Why it matters:** Critical protein-quality comparator in resistance training.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle thickness, strength/performance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not claim collagen peptides are equivalent to whey for hypertrophy or muscle-thickness gains.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness and boundary:** `adjacent_variant`. Does not address joint/tendon or connective-tissue endpoints.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event signal extracted from accessible text.

**Limitations:** Small sample (n=11/group).; Comparator was whey protein, not placebo.; Untrained young adults only.; Collagen was leucine-matched, not a typical consumer collagen-only dose comparison.

**Population mismatch:** Young untrained training cohort; adjacent protein-quality comparator.

**Artifact notes:** No redistributable PDF stored; keep metadata/manifest candidate only unless rights are clearly open.
