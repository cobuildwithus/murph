This source is included for **bone-density-turnover**.

**Findings:**

- **Population:** Postmenopausal women with osteopenia.
- **Intervention/exposure:** 5 g collagen peptides plus 500 mg elemental calcium and 400 IU vitamin D3 daily.
- **Comparator/control:** 500 mg elemental calcium plus 400 IU vitamin D3 daily.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 12 months.
- **Endpoints:** pQCT tibia trabecular BMC/vBMD, pQCT cortical measures, DXA lumbar spine/hip aBMD, bone turnover markers.
- **Effect/direction:** Accessible records report significantly better tibial trabecular/cortical pQCT measures and lumbar-spine aBMD with collagen-combination versus calcium/D alone.
- **Adverse events/safety:** Safety details not extracted from accessible records.
- **Limitations:** Adjunct calcium/D design; not HCP-alone.; Open-label program context and small sample.; Specialized pQCT/DXA outcomes need full-text extraction for exact estimates.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Relevant osteopenia population but adjacent co-intervention.

**Why it matters:** It expands the same osteopenia program from short-term markers to 12-month BMD/geometry endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** pQCT trabecular vBMD, pQCT BMC, DXA lumbar spine aBMD, bone geometry.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent calcium/D-plus-collagen evidence, not collagen-alone proof.
