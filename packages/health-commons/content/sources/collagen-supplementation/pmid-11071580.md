This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:** The review reported that hydrolyzed gelatin/collagen hydrolysate products were generally regarded as safe and that some clinical studies suggested pain reduction with 10 g/day in knee or hip OA. It also preserved mixed evidence: one multicenter RCT did not show a statistically significant advantage in the total sample, although German sites and more severe symptom subgroups reportedly favored collagen hydrolysate. Adverse effects were described as minimal and mainly gastrointestinal fullness or unpleasant taste.

**Why it matters:** Anchors the historical collagen-hydrolysate joint/OA narrative while warning that early efficacy signals were inconsistent and subgroup-dependent.

**Potential experiment signals:** joint pain, joint function, gastrointestinal tolerability, cartilage mechanism, bone outcomes

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as direct-protocol historical context and safety/tolerability background; do not cite for a current efficacy estimate.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
