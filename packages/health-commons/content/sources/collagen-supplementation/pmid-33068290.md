This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:** The evidence map shows collagen-derivative evidence is broad and fragmented, with limited clean clinical evidence for OA therapeutic claims. Use to enforce variant separation and avoid over-attributing derivative evidence to HCP.

**Why it matters:** It is valuable for variant and evidence-map boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** joint pain, joint function, cartilage health, evidence gap.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context only; do not promote derivative-wide conclusions to HCP.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

- **Source type:** Systematic review (2020).
- **People studied or addressed:** Preclinical and clinical osteoarthritis/cartilage-repair evidence involving collagen derivatives.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Collagen derivatives, including hydrolyzed collagen, native collagen, and other derivative types depending on evidence map.
- **Comparator or control:** Mixed comparators across mapped studies.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Varied.
- **Most relevant endpoints:** joint pain, joint function, cartilage health, evidence gap.
