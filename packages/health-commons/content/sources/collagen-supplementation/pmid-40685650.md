This source is included for **metabolic-cardiovascular-adjacent**.

## Findings

After 7 days, energy intake at a post-exercise meal was about 10% lower with collagen peptides (p=0.037); GLP-1 and insulin responses increased, ghrelin/leptin were lower, and PYY/CCK/glucose were not different.

## Why it matters

It maps appetite and GI-symptom outcomes separately from core protocol claims.

## Potential experiment signals

- post-exercise energy intake
- subjective appetite
- GLP-1
- insulin
- ghrelin
- GI symptoms

## Protocol takeaway

Context-only for appetite, energy intake, and GI-symptom monitoring.

## Safety and adverse events

No difference in gastrointestinal symptoms was reported.

## Limitations and mismatch

- n=15.
- Healthy active females only.
- Short duration.
- Single meal test.
- Energy-intake difference may not be clinically meaningful.
- Population mismatch: Female acute exercise/appetite context rather than core HCP outcome population.
