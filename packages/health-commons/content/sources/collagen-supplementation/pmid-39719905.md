This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:**

- **dose — Multi-ingredient dose composition:** The intervention contains collagen but is not collagen-alone. Effect/direction: Daily 5.75 g sachet contained 2000 mg type I/III collagen peptides plus 40 mg hydrolyzed type II collagen and multiple non-collagen ingredients.
- **mixed_result — Pain, QoL, and physical function:** The supplement had favorable symptom signals, but effects are not attributable to collagen alone. Effect/direction: Pain/QoL scales improved in accessible excerpts; performance tests were mixed.
- **limitation — Attribution boundary:** Do not cite for direct hydrolyzed-collagen-peptide efficacy claims. Effect/direction: Glucosamine, chondroitin, MSM, hyaluronic acid, olibanum, and vitamin C confound collagen-specific attribution.
- **population — Direct OA but adjacent intervention:** Population is direct; treatment directness is adjacent. Effect/direction: KL grade 2-3 OA population is relevant, but intervention is a multi-ingredient stack.

**Why it matters:** Recent OA RCT includes hydrolyzed collagen components but crosses the multi-ingredient boundary.

**Potential experiment signals:** VAS pain, WOMAC, KOOS-PS, Oxford Knee Score, Tampa Scale of Kinesiophobia, SF-12, foot function, TUG, 6-minute walk, 5-times sit-to-stand, stair climb, Berg Balance Scale.

**Protocol takeaway:** Exclude from direct collagen-peptide-alone claims; cite only to explain adjacent product evidence and confounding.

**Murph takeaway:** Multi-ingredient stacks cannot reveal whether collagen itself helped.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness and boundary:** `adjacent_variant`. Direct OA population, but intervention is an adjacent multi-ingredient collagen-containing product rather than hydrolyzed collagen peptides alone.

**Safety notes:** No conflicts or funding were reported in accessible excerpts; adverse-event details were not extracted.

**Limitations:**

- Adjacent variant: multi-ingredient product includes glucosamine, chondroitin, MSM, hyaluronic acid, olibanum, and vitamin C, so collagen-specific effects are not isolatable.
- Small sample and short duration.
- Multiple outcomes increase interpretation burden.
