This source is included for **bone-density-turnover**.

**Findings:**

- **Population:** Postmenopausal women with osteopenia.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Daily sachet with 5 g collagen peptides, calcium lactate equivalent to 500 mg elemental calcium, and 400 IU vitamin D3.
- **Comparator/control:** Daily chewable tablet with calcium carbonate equivalent to 500 mg elemental calcium and 400 IU vitamin D3.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 3 months.
- **Endpoints:** P1NP, CTX, tolerability, compliance.
- **Effect/direction:** P1NP decreased by 13.1% (p<0.001) and CTX decreased by 11.4% (p=0.058) in the collagen-combination group; control did not change. Better compliance and no adverse events were reported in the collagen-combination group in accessible abstract.
- **Adverse events/safety:** Accessible abstract reports no adverse events in the collagen-combination group, whereas adverse events occurred in the comparator group; details require full text.
- **Limitations:** Open-label/no masking in registry record.; Co-intervention with calcium and vitamin D; not HCP-alone.; Short duration and biomarker endpoints only.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Postmenopausal osteopenia population matches, but intervention is calcium/vitamin D plus collagen peptides.

**Why it matters:** It directly tests adding collagen peptides to a calcium/D supplement in osteopenia and supplies boundary evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** P1NP, CTX, compliance, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only adjunct evidence; preserve the calcium/D comparator in any claim.
