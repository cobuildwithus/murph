This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:**

- **dose — Dose and duration:** Carrillo-Norte 2024 supports a 10 g/day six-month knee-OA protocol window. Effect/direction: 10 g/day hydrolyzed collagen peptides, molecular weight 1-3 kDa, for 6 months.
- **outcome — VAS pain and Lequesne algofunctional index:** Hydrolyzed collagen improved knee-OA pain and function versus placebo. Effect/direction: VAS pain and Lequesne scores decreased versus placebo at 6 months; accessible abstract reports p<0.001.
- **mechanism — CRP and ESR:** Inflammatory-marker signal is promising but should not be overinterpreted as mechanism proof. Effect/direction: CRP and ESR decreased versus placebo in accessible abstract (p<0.001).
- **safety — Adverse effects:** Safety signal is favorable for 10 g/day over 6 months. Effect/direction: No adverse effects reported and good tolerability in accessible abstract.

**Why it matters:** Recent open-access direct knee-OA RCT with clinically relevant pain, function, and safety outputs.

**Potential experiment signals:** VAS pain, Lequesne algofunctional index, C-reactive protein, erythrocyte sedimentation rate, safety/tolerability.

**Protocol takeaway:** 10 g/day for six months is a direct knee-OA dose-duration signal with pain/function and safety relevance.

**Murph takeaway:** VAS pain plus a function index such as Lequesne is a practical monitoring pair.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness and boundary:** `direct_protocol`. Direct knee-OA population.

**Safety notes:** No adverse effects were reported during the study in accessible abstract text; supplement was described as well tolerated.

**Limitations:**

- Exact inclusion VAS threshold differed across accessible snippets.
- One recent trial; replication needed before inflammatory-marker claims are generalized.
