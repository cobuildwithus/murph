This source is included for **bone-density-turnover**.

**Findings:**

- **Population:** Postmenopausal women over 60 years with osteopenia in Pakistan hospital settings.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Bioactive collagen peptide plus calcium lactate and vitamin D3.
- **Comparator/control:** Calcium plus vitamin D3 without collagen peptide.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 3 months in accessible abstract; recruitment March-May 2024.
- **Endpoints:** serum P1NP, bone mineral density in title/registry context.
- **Effect/direction:** Accessible abstract reports P1NP decreased from 61.3 ± 14.7 to 53.0 ± 14.1 in the collagen-combination group (p<0.001) and no significant change in control.
- **Adverse events/safety:** Adverse events not extracted from accessible abstract.
- **Limitations:** Small sample, short duration, single-blind design.; Co-intervention with calcium/vitamin D; not HCP-alone.; Accessible abstract emphasizes P1NP, despite title referring to BMD; DXA/BMD effect details require full extraction.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Postmenopausal osteopenia population matches; intervention is adjacent because calcium/vitamin D co-intervention prevents HCP-alone inference.

**Why it matters:** It is recent and protocol-adjacent, but calcium/vitamin D boundaries and small size are central.

**Potential experiment signals:** P1NP, DXA/BMD if verified, calcium/vitamin D co-intervention, trial registration.

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep as context-only adjunct evidence; do not cite as collagen-alone proof.
