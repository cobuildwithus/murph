This source is included for **exercise-performance-recovery**.

**Findings:** Indexed abstract-level extraction reports faster recovery of MVC (p=0.02, ηp²=0.11), RFD (p<0.01, ηp²=0.18), peak RFD (p<0.01, ηp²=0.15), and CMJ height (p=0.046, ηp²=0.06) in the collagen group; no effects on muscle soreness (p=0.66) or body composition (FM p=0.41; FFM p=0.56; BCM p=0.79; ECM p=0.58).

**Why it matters:** Direct RCT focused on recovery after exercise-induced muscle damage.

**Potential experiment signals:** MVC recovery, RFD recovery, CMJ height recovery, muscle soreness, body composition.

**Protocol takeaway:** Specific collagen peptides plus concurrent training may improve strength/power recovery after EIMD, but subjective soreness and body composition were not improved.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness and boundary:** `direct_protocol`. Mixed endpoints and companion-program context.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event signal extracted from accessible text.

**Limitations:** Male sedentary-to-moderately-active cohort only.; Concurrent training and EIMD challenge design.; Soreness and body composition were null despite biomechanical recovery signals.

**Population mismatch:** Men with concurrent training; not women or resistance-only protocols.
