This source is included for **skin-aging**.

**Findings:** Astaxanthin plus collagen hydrolysate improved some facial elasticity/barrier and MMP markers, but the blend prevents collagen-only attribution. Endpoints captured: facial skin elasticity; transepidermal water loss; MMP-1; MMP-12; procollagen type I mRNA; UV/photoaging markers.

**Why it matters:** This is a commonly cited photoaging-adjacent collagen blend trial and helps prevent over-attribution of multi-ingredient results to collagen peptides alone.

**Potential experiment signals:** skin elasticity, TEWL, MMP-1/MMP-12, photoaging markers, tolerability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use as direct collagen-only efficacy evidence; use only to mark the astaxanthin co-ingredient boundary.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
