This source is included for **exercise-performance-recovery**.

**Findings:** Training improved overall physical condition in all participants. The experimental group reported greater improvement in musculoskeletal discomfort (72.1% vs 10.3% in controls). Accessible abstract text suggests possible enhancement of muscle performance but exact endpoint estimates were not extracted.

**Why it matters:** Recent long-duration placebo-controlled enriched collagen trial with neuromuscular/body-composition endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** musculoskeletal discomfort, neuromuscular fitness, body composition, cardiorespiratory fitness.

**Protocol takeaway:** Treat as adjacent enriched-formulation context; training improved all participants and HCP-alone effects are not separable.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness and boundary:** `adjacent_variant`. Do not promote enriched product results to HCP-alone claims.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event signal extracted from accessible abstract text.

**Limitations:** Adjacent variant: enriched collagen product, not isolated HCP.; Participants had osteoarticular discomfort.; Exact body-composition/fitness effect estimates not extracted in this batch.

**Population mismatch:** Active middle-aged adults with discomfort using enriched collagen; not isolated HCP in healthy trainees.
