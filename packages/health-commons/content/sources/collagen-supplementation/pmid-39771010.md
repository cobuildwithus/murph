**Findings:** Time effects occurred after exercise, but accessible abstract snippets report no group or interaction effects for recovery outcomes (all p>0.05).

**Why it matters:** Recent direct comparator with active protein control and placebo.

**Potential experiment signals:** DOMS, muscle function, running economy, P1NP, CTX-1, hsCRP, IL-6, creatine kinase.

**Protocol takeaway:** In this acute EIMD model, collagen hydrolysate was not superior to dairy protein or placebo for recovery outcomes.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness and boundary:** `direct_protocol`. Short-term recovery trial; does not rule out longer training-adjunct effects.

**Safety notes:** No specific adverse-event signal extracted from accessible abstract snippets.

**Limitations:** Short acute recovery window.; Male-only recreationally active sample.; Direct comparison suggests no clear advantage over dairy protein or placebo in this protocol.

**Population mismatch:** Recreationally active males; not women, clinical populations, or chronic training adaptation.
