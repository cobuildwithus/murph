This source is included for **skin-disease-adjacent**.

**Findings:** Bioactive collagen peptides showed pilot AD signals, but disease context prevents cosmetic skin-aging extrapolation. Endpoints captured: rescue topical corticosteroid use; 5-D Pruritus Scale; SCORAD; TEWL; skin hydration; skin pH; body lotion use; adverse events.

**Why it matters:** It is directly oral BCP but clinically/disease-adjacent, preserving population mismatch and safety notes.

**Potential experiment signals:** 5-D Pruritus, SCORAD, TEWL, hydration, pH, corticosteroid rescue, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not claim HCP treats atopic dermatitis in a consumer protocol; include only as clinically supervised disease-adjacent context.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
