This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:** Fifty-four participants were enrolled and 52 completed a 12-week trial of a cartilage-supporting formula containing collagen type II, glucosamine HCl, and chondroitin sulfate versus placebo. KOOS symptom, sport/recreation, QoL, and stiffness signals favored the formula, but both groups improved in pain and chair-stand performance, other metrics were not significantly different, and the primary endpoint was not achieved.

**Why it matters:** A recent RCT with collagen-containing product, but mixed and non-isolating.

**Potential experiment signals:** KOOS symptoms, KOOS sport/recreation, KOOS QoL, KOOS pain, stiffness, chair-stand, safety

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only; cite only as combination-product boundary and mixed-result example.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
