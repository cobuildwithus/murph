This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:** Ninety-six healthy adults were randomized to UC-II 40 mg/day or placebo for 24 weeks. Accessible summaries reported greater improvement in knee flexion versus placebo and time-dependent extension improvement in the active arm. The result should stay in adjacent-variant context.

**Why it matters:** Useful to separate joint-flexibility wellness claims from HCP/OA claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** knee flexion, knee extension, range of motion, joint discomfort, safety

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not cite for HCP efficacy; use only for adjacent endpoint context.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
