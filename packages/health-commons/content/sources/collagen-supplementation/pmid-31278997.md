This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:** The OARSI guideline gives patient-focused recommendations for non-surgical management of knee, hip, and polyarticular OA based on evidence synthesis and expert consensus. It is included to frame comparator care and avoid overstating collagen relative to standard management.

**Why it matters:** OA protocol claims should not imply replacement of guideline-supported treatments.

**Potential experiment signals:** pain, function, mobility, analgesic use, standard-care comparison

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for background standard-care framing only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
