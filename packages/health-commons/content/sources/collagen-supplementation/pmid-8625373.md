This source is included for **bone-density-turnover**.

**Findings:**

- **Population:** Postmenopausal osteoporosis with BMD below 80% of premenopausal values.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Calcitonin 100 units intramuscularly twice weekly for 24 weeks plus 10 g/day oral collagen hydrolysate in 49 participants.
- **Comparator/control:** Calcitonin treatment without concurrent collagen hydrolysate.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 24 weeks plus 3-month post-discontinuation marker follow-up in accessible abstract.
- **Endpoints:** BMD, urinary pyridinoline, urinary deoxypyridinoline, urinary hydroxyproline, X-ray/osteometry.
- **Effect/direction:** BMD increased only insignificantly by 1.8%; collagen adjunct produced more marked and persistent decreases in urinary pyridinoline and deoxypyridinoline versus calcitonin alone.
- **Adverse events/safety:** Adverse events not extracted from accessible abstract.
- **Limitations:** Calcitonin medication confounding; clinical-supervised osteoporosis treatment.; Collagen-rich diet/hydrolysate adjunct is not HCP-alone.; Older study in Czech with English abstract; methods need full-text verification.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Adjacent clinical-supervised medication context, not consumer HCP protocol.

**Why it matters:** It is a frequently cited older clinical source but is medication-confounded and not protocol-direct.

**Potential experiment signals:** UPD, UDPD, BMD, calcitonin co-treatment.

**Protocol takeaway:** Historical adjacent evidence only; classify under clinical-supervised/calcitonin boundary.
