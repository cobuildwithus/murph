This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:** Thirty-nine knee-OA patients were randomized to acetaminophen alone or acetaminophen plus native type-II collagen 10 mg/day for three months. The accessible abstract reported a between-group walking-pain advantage for the collagen add-on arm, while urinary cartilage biomarkers did not improve. The collagen effect is not direct HCP evidence.

**Why it matters:** Shows native type-II symptomatic research while also preserving non-improvement in biomarkers.

**Potential experiment signals:** VAS pain at rest, VAS walking pain, WOMAC pain, WOMAC function, SF-36, urinary biomarkers

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only adjacent evidence; do not treat as hydrolyzed peptide evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
