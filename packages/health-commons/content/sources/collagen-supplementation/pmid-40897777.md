This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:** Sixty-eight adults with moderate knee OA were randomized to tablets containing hydrolyzed collagen plus undenatured type-II collagen or placebo for 12 weeks. Accessible summaries reported that both groups improved over time, but there were no significant between-group differences in pain, KOOS/function, rescue medication, or satisfaction; no adverse events were reported. The study is linked to NCT06229951.

**Why it matters:** Preserves a recent no-clear-advantage result for a collagen-containing OA product.

**Potential experiment signals:** numeric pain rating scale, KOOS, rescue medication, patient satisfaction, adverse events

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only and cautionary; do not use as HCP-alone efficacy support.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
