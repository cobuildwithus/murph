This source is included for **skin-aging**.

**Findings:** The review describes skin-health improvements and plausible mechanisms involving fibroblasts, macrophage phenotype, and oral tolerance-related pathways. Use for mechanism/context only, not as a primary efficacy source.

**Why it matters:** It separates mechanistic plausibility from clinical endpoint evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** skin hydration, skin elasticity, dermal collagen, fibroblast activity.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to explain possible mechanisms only after direct RCT claims are separately supported.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Source type:** Systematic review (2020).
- **People studied or addressed:** Human and mechanistic skin-health publications involving intact or hydrolyzed collagen supplementation.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Oral intact collagen or hydrolyzed collagen supplementation.
- **Comparator or control:** Mixed comparators depending on included publication.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Varied.
- **Most relevant endpoints:** skin hydration, skin elasticity, dermal collagen, fibroblast activity.
