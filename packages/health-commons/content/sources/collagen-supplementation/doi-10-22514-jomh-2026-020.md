This source is included for **metabolic-cardiovascular-adjacent**.

## Findings

Reported within-group improvement in RPE (p=0.037) and VAS pain (p=0.043) in the collagen group, but no specific significant ANOVA interaction for other included physical, functional, and metabolic outcomes.

## Why it matters

It prevents overgeneralizing athlete performance and metabolic endpoints while preserving an on-protocol exposure record.

## Protocol takeaway

Context-only athletic/metabolic boundary; not core support for general hydrolyzed-collagen-peptide outcomes.

## Safety and adverse events

GI symptoms were listed among endpoints; no adverse-event signal extracted from accessible abstract.

## Limitations and mismatch

- Very small sample.
- Young male soccer players only.
- Broad endpoint set.
- Population/outcome mismatch for core protocol claims.
- Published in 2026; independent replication not available.
- Population mismatch: Young male soccer athletes rather than general users or target clinical populations.
