This source is included for **skin-aging**.

**Findings:** Direct collagen-hydrolysate registry with skin and joint endpoints, but no posted results and an osteoarthritis population. Endpoints captured: KOOS pain; KOOS symptoms; activities of daily living; quality of life; PIIANP; CTX-II; COMP; skin viscoelasticity; skin hydration; dermal thickness; inflammatory markers.

**Why it matters:** It is a detailed unpublished/completed registry record with direct collagen hydrolysate dosing and skin measurements.

**Potential experiment signals:** KOOS, skin viscoelasticity, skin hydration, dermal thickness, collagen biomarkers, inflammation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as registry context only until results are posted or published.
