This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:** Hydrolyzed collagen may slightly mitigate knee-OA symptoms, but the evidence base was small and heterogeneous. Use for knee-OA source recall and as direct but low-certainty review context.

**Why it matters:** It is a direct HCP knee-OA review, useful for identifying older and lower-venue OA trials.

**Potential experiment signals:** joint pain, joint function, joint stiffness, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** May support cautious knee-OA symptom language, but primary RCT extraction is needed before strong claims.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction details

- **Source type:** Systematic review (2022).
- **People studied or addressed:** Adults with knee osteoarthritis in randomized placebo-controlled trials.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Hydrolyzed collagen supplementation.
- **Comparator or control:** Placebo.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Varied across the four included trials.
- **Most relevant endpoints:** joint pain, joint function, joint stiffness, adverse events.

## Safety notes

Safety details were extracted only where the accessible source text or abstract-level record included them. Otherwise, this page intentionally leaves adverse-event counts unresolved rather than inventing them.

## Limitations and mismatch

Only four studies were included; subject counts, collagen source/dose, demographics, blinding, and bias varied. Population mismatch: Clinical knee-OA population; not general joint-health users.
