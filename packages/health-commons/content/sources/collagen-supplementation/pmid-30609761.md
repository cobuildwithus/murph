This source is included for **tendon-loading-ligament**.

**Findings:** VISA-A improved more during the first 3 months in the collagen-first group than placebo-first group; after crossover, the group switching from placebo to collagen had a larger additional VISA-A increase. Microvascularity declined in both groups and was moderately associated with VISA-A.

**Population and intervention:** 20 participants with chronic midportion Achilles tendinopathy; 13 men and 7 women; mean age about 44 years; unilateral and bilateral cases included. Intervention/exposure: TENDOFORTE specific collagen peptides 2.5 g twice daily, taken 30 minutes before calf-strengthening exercises. Comparator/control: Maltodextrin placebo with the same calf-strengthening and return-to-running program. Duration/follow-up: Two 3-month crossover periods with calf-strengthening exercises.

**Endpoints:** VISA-A, pain/function, microvascularity by Power Doppler ultrasound, return-to-running context, adverse events.

**Safety notes:** No adverse events were reported; one participant reported minor gastrointestinal discomfort during the placebo period; delayed-onset muscle soreness was attributed to the exercise program.

**Limitations:** Small pilot trial.; Crossover design without a washout period.; Collagen peptides were combined with intensive calf-strengthening, so the supplement effect is add-on evidence rather than standalone evidence.; Not powered for return-to-running endpoints.

**Population mismatch:** Clinically relevant to midportion Achilles tendinopathy, but not necessarily to insertional Achilles pain, patellar tendinopathy, or healthy users.

**Why it matters:** It directly evaluates oral hydrolyzed/specific collagen peptides in a tendon pain rehabilitation setting and reports both efficacy and safety notes.

**Potential experiment signals:** VISA-A score; pain during tendon loading; return-to-running progression; Power Doppler microvascularity; GI tolerance

**Protocol takeaway:** For Achilles tendinopathy, collagen peptides are best framed as an add-on to a progressive calf-loading program rather than a replacement for loading.
