This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:** One hundred five knee-OA participants were allocated to UC-II treatment or a no-UC-II control for 90 days. Reported changes favored UC-II for VAS pain, WOMAC, and SF-12 physical quality of life. The study is adjacent and has a notable limitation: no placebo element.

**Why it matters:** Adds native type-II knee-OA outcome data while preserving design limitations.

**Potential experiment signals:** VAS pain, WOMAC, SF-12 physical, SF-12 mental, range of motion, side effects

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only; do not promote to HCP claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
