This source is included for **skin-aging**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study.
- **People studied or addressed:** Healthy adults with photoaged and dry facial skin; accessible summaries report 84 in efficacy analysis.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Low-molecular-weight collagen peptide test product; dose not confirmed from accessible primary abstract.
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 12 weeks.
- **Role in Murph:** direct protocol evidence for Hydrolyzed Collagen Peptides.

**Findings:** Crow’s-feet photo-scale and multiple wrinkle/roughness parameters improved versus placebo in accessible summaries. Elasticity, hydration, and TEWL improved versus placebo after 12 weeks. Accessible summaries report no adverse events or laboratory abnormalities associated with the test material.

**Adverse events or safety:** No adverse events or laboratory abnormalities associated with the test material were reported in accessible summaries.

**Why it matters:** Direct LMWCP RCT with wrinkles, hydration, elasticity, TEWL, and safety framing.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- crow’s-feet photo-scale score
- eye wrinkle volume and roughness parameters
- skin elasticity
- skin hydration
- transepidermal water loss
- safety labs/adverse events

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports wrinkle and biophysical skin-property endpoints for LMWCP over 12 weeks, while flagging dose uncertainty until full text is reviewed.

**Limitations and population fit:**

- Primary article is paywalled.
- Dose was not confirmed from accessible primary abstract-level sources.
- Photoaged/dry facial-skin sample may not generalize to low-symptom users.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy adults with photoaged and dry facial skin; accessible summaries report 84 in efficacy analysis may not match all Murph users; do not generalize to different ages, sexes, baseline skin status, or collagen product types without caveats.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
