This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:**

- **dose — Dose and duration:** Benito-Ruiz 2009 supports a 10 g/day six-month knee-OA dosing window. Effect/direction: 10 g/day collagen hydrolysate for 6 months.
- **outcome — VAS pain and WOMAC pain subscale:** A 250-person knee-OA RCT favored collagen hydrolysate for joint-comfort outcomes. Effect/direction: Positive direction; abstract reports significant improvement in knee joint comfort by VAS pain and WOMAC pain.
- **safety — Safety/tolerability:** Safety finding is usable as general tolerability context but not for event rates. Effect/direction: Abstract describes collagen hydrolysate as safe; detailed AE counts not extracted.
- **limitation — Subgroup interpretation:** Subgroup observations should be hypothesis-generating. Effect/direction: Greatest benefit was described in subgroups with more joint deterioration and lower meat-protein intake.

**Why it matters:** Large early direct knee-OA RCT with explicit symptom and safety framing.

**Potential experiment signals:** VAS knee pain, WOMAC pain subscale, joint comfort, safety.

**Protocol takeaway:** 10 g/day for six months has direct knee-OA comfort evidence from a large RCT.

**Murph takeaway:** Use VAS pain and WOMAC pain to detect whether personal symptoms improve over months, not days.
