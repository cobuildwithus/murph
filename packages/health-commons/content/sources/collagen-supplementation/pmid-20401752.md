This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:**

- **dose — Dose and duration:** Trč 2011 supports a 10 g/day EHC knee-OA regimen. Effect/direction: 10 g/day EHC for 90 consecutive days.
- **mixed_result — VAS and WOMAC versus glucosamine sulphate:** Active-comparator evidence supports context, not placebo-controlled efficacy. Effect/direction: EHC response was reported as favorable versus 1.5 g/day glucosamine sulphate in accessible summaries, but no placebo arm was used.
- **safety — Tolerability:** Safety context is useful but AE counts require full-text extraction. Effect/direction: Tolerability was assessed and EHC was generally described as well tolerated in summaries.
- **limitation — Comparator boundary:** Keep separate from placebo-controlled RCTs. Effect/direction: Active comparator without placebo means supplement-specific treatment effect is not isolated.

**Why it matters:** Useful direct collagen hydrolysate OA evidence and tolerability context, but comparator limits claim strength.

**Potential experiment signals:** VAS pain, WOMAC total, patient global assessment, investigator global assessment, joint assessment, rescue medication, SF-36 quality of life, tolerability.

**Protocol takeaway:** 10 g/day EHC has active-comparator knee-OA evidence; classify separately from placebo-controlled RCTs.

**Murph takeaway:** If comparing supplements, a crossover/control design matters because symptom fluctuation can be large.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness and boundary:** `direct_protocol`. Direct knee-OA population.

**Safety notes:** The study evaluated tolerance; accessible summaries describe EHC as generally well tolerated, but detailed adverse-event counts were not extracted here.

**Limitations:**

- Active-comparator design without placebo; cannot separate EHC effects from placebo/time effects.
- Open-access but older trial; effect estimates need full-text QA before protocol claims.
