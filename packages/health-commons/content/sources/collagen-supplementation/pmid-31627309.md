This source is included for **skin-aging**.

**Findings:** ELASTEN improved several skin metrics, but collagen was bundled with vitamins/minerals/acerola. Endpoints captured: skin hydration; skin elasticity; skin roughness/wrinkle depth; skin density; tolerability; adverse events; withdrawals.

**Why it matters:** This is a well-described open-access blend trial often cited in skin-collagen discussions, but it cannot isolate collagen.

**Potential experiment signals:** hydration, elasticity, wrinkle depth/roughness, skin density, adverse events, follow-up durability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep as multi-ingredient context; do not cite as HCP-alone evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
