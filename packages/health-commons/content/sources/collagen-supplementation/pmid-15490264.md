This source is included for **bone-density-turnover**.

**Findings:**

- **Population:** Growing male rats and calcium-deficient mature female rats.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Oral collagen peptide at human-equivalent, 10-fold, or 100-fold human-equivalent doses in growing rats; calcium-deficient mature rats received diets with or without collagen peptide during repletion.
- **Comparator/control:** Diet controls and normal-calcium comparator groups.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 4 weeks in growing rats; 9-week calcium deficiency followed by 8-week repletion in mature rats.
- **Endpoints:** femur BMD, lumbar spine BMD, kidney hypertrophy.
- **Effect/direction:** High-dose collagen peptide increased femur BMD in growing rats, and 10-fold dosing increased femur/lumbar spine BMD in calcium-deficient mature rats; 100-fold dosing caused kidney hypertrophy in growing rats.
- **Adverse events/safety:** Kidney hypertrophy was reported at the 100-fold human-equivalent dose in growing rats.
- **Limitations:** Animal model; no human efficacy inference.; Very high doses are not protocol doses.; Group sizes and all safety data not extracted from accessible abstract.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Mechanistic animal evidence only.

**Why it matters:** It provides preclinical rationale and a high-dose safety signal relevant to dose boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** femur BMD, lumbar spine BMD, kidney size, calcium-deficient state.

**Protocol takeaway:** Mechanistic context only; high-dose renal findings argue against extrapolating to very high intakes.
