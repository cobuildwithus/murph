This source is included for **exercise-performance-recovery**.

**Findings:** Fat-free mass increased more with collagen peptides than placebo; body fat mass was unchanged in the collagen group but increased in placebo; strength increased in both groups with no clear significant between-group strength advantage; type II fiber cross-sectional area increased in both groups without clear group difference.

**Why it matters:** Important healthy-men RCT showing a mixed training-adjunct profile.

**Potential experiment signals:** fat-free mass, body fat mass, strength, muscle fiber cross-sectional area.

**Protocol takeaway:** In healthy active men, collagen peptides alongside training showed body-composition signals but not a clean strength or fiber-size advantage.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness and boundary:** `direct_protocol`. Training drove many outcomes in both groups.

**Safety notes:** No specific adverse-event signal extracted from accessible text.

**Limitations:** Passive connective-tissue adaptation was hypothesized rather than directly proven.; Dietary intake was based on short food records.; Recreationally active men only.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy/recreationally active young male cohort; not sarcopenic, older female, or sedentary population.
