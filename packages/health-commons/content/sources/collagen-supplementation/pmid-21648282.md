This source is included for **background-general**.

## Findings

Accessible abstract reports biomarker differences including IGF-1, total/bone alkaline phosphatase, and TRAP behavior, concluding potential enhancement of bone remodeling during growth.

## Why it matters

It preserves a same-mechanism bone-biomarker signal while explicitly flagging major population mismatch.

## Potential experiment signals

- bone-remodeling biomarkers
- growth-stage bone modeling
- calcium co-exposure

## Protocol takeaway

Use only as background bone-remodeling context; not direct adult protocol evidence.

## Safety and adverse events

No safety signal extracted from accessible abstract text.

## Limitations and mismatch

- Pediatric growth-stage population.
- Biomarker outcomes rather than clinical endpoints.
- Calcium co-intervention in one arm.
- No adult musculoskeletal outcomes.
- Population mismatch: Major mismatch: pre-pubertal children rather than adults using hydrolyzed collagen peptides for skin, joint, tendon, or adult bone endpoints.
