This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:** Thirty-two meniscopathy patients were randomized to a product containing type I/III collagen peptides and type-II hydrolyzed collagen or placebo for eight weeks. Accessible abstract text reported improvements in pain, QoL, kinesiophobia, and foot function, while back strength, proprioception, balance, and multiple functional tests were not significantly different. Authors noted sample size, short duration, heterogeneous injuries, and product-attribution limitations.

**Why it matters:** Prevents knee-pain evidence from being overgeneralized to OA and preserves null functional outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:** VAS, WOMAC, KOOS-PS, Oxford Knee Score, SF-12, LEFS, TUG, 6MWT, 5STS, stair-climb, strength

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only knee-pathology mismatch; do not use as OA/HCP proof.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
