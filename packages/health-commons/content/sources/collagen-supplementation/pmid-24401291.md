This source is included for **skin-aging**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled wrinkle and dermal-matrix trial.
- **People studied or addressed:** Women aged 45–65 years.
- **Intervention/exposure:** 2.5 g/day specific bioactive collagen peptides.
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 8 weeks supplementation plus 4-week post-treatment regression/washout observation.
- **Role in Murph:** direct protocol evidence for Hydrolyzed Collagen Peptides.

**Findings:** Eye wrinkle volume was significantly reduced versus placebo after 4 and 8 weeks. After 8 weeks, the study reported roughly a 20% wrinkle-volume reduction and persistence after the 4-week washout. Dermal matrix markers increased for procollagen I and elastin; fibrillin change was reported as not significant.

**Adverse events or safety:** Adverse-event details were not extracted from accessible materials.

**Why it matters:** Foundational direct wrinkle RCT connecting visible wrinkle outcomes to dermal-matrix markers.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- eye wrinkle volume
- procollagen I
- elastin
- fibrillin

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports a direct wrinkle endpoint claim for specific bioactive collagen peptides while treating matrix markers as mechanism/context.

**Limitations and population fit:**

- Paywalled primary article limits table extraction.
- Women aged 45–65 only.
- Specific branded peptide preparation.
- Mechanistic suction-blister markers are supportive but not user-observable endpoints.

**Population mismatch:** Women aged 45–65 years may not match all Murph users; do not generalize to different ages, sexes, baseline skin status, or collagen product types without caveats.
