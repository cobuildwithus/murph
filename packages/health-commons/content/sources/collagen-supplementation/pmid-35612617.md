This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:** In women with knee OA, accessible summaries describe a three-arm trial comparing no intervention/control, UC-II, and exercise therapy. Both UC-II and exercise improved mobility/function tests versus control, but WOMAC quality-of-life/stiffness signals favored exercise more clearly. This is non-hydrolyzed collagen boundary evidence.

**Why it matters:** Useful because it explicitly labels the intervention non-hydrolyzed collagen and includes an exercise comparator.

**Potential experiment signals:** 6-minute walk test, Timed Up and Go, WOMAC pain, WOMAC stiffness, WOMAC quality of life

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to reinforce variant exclusion and standard-care/exercise context.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
