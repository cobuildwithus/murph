This source is included for **clinical-supervised-wound-nutrition**.

**Findings:**

- The review included 33 RCTs with 7920 participants evaluating nutritional interventions for pressure-ulcer prevention or treatment. `[source_artifact:pmid-38345088]`
- Overall certainty was limited or mixed across prevention and treatment interventions; collagen supplementation probably improved mean pressure-ulcer area change in one study with 74 participants. `[source_artifact:pmid-38345088]`
- Evidence on side effects for collagen supplementation was judged very uncertain in the review summary. `[source_artifact:pmid-38345088]`

**Why it matters:** This high-quality review puts collagen-related pressure-ulcer findings in context: potentially favorable but narrow, clinical, and not wellness evidence. `[source_artifact:pmid-38345088]`

**Potential experiment signals:**

  - "pressure ulcer incidence"
  - "complete healing"
  - "wound area"
  - "gastrointestinal side effects"
  - "certainty of evidence"

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only: mention only as wound-care boundary evidence with uncertainty; do not use for direct protocol outcomes. `[source_artifact:pmid-38345088]`

**Population mismatch:** People at risk for, or being treated for, pressure ulcers in clinical care contexts.

**Limitations:**

- Clinical pressure-ulcer context only.
- Collagen-specific signal came from one small study in the review summary.
- Heterogeneous interventions and outcomes.
- Side-effect evidence was very uncertain.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
