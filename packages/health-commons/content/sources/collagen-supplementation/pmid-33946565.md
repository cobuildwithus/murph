This source is included for **exercise-performance-recovery**.

**Findings:** All groups improved, but collagen vs placebo had higher fat-free-mass increase (p=0.010) and fat-mass decrease (p=0.023). Reported changes: FFM CP 3.42±2.54 kg, placebo 1.83±2.09 kg, whey 2.27±2.56 kg; FM CP −5.28±3.19 kg, placebo −3.39±3.13 kg, whey −4.08±2.80 kg. Exploratory whey comparisons did not show significant CP-vs-whey differences for FFM or FM.

**Why it matters:** Direct RCT extending the evidence base to middle-aged untrained men.

**Potential experiment signals:** fat-free mass, fat mass, isometric leg strength.

**Protocol takeaway:** In middle-aged untrained men, 15 g/day specific collagen peptides added to training improved DXA body-composition outcomes versus placebo.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness and boundary:** `direct_protocol`. Does not prove superiority over whey protein, and not a stand-alone supplement trial.

**Safety notes:** No specific adverse-event signal extracted from accessible abstract/indexed text.

**Limitations:** Men only, untrained middle-aged cohort.; The whey arm was exploratory.; All participants trained, so stand-alone effects cannot be inferred.

**Population mismatch:** Untrained middle-aged men; not women, older sarcopenic adults, or athletes.
