**Findings:** Hydrolyzed collagen favored skin moisture and elasticity outcomes, with 12 weeks highlighted as a practical regimen in the review. Use as a supportive skin review and source-recall hub, not as the sole basis for effect estimates.

**Why it matters:** It adds an open, recent skin-rejuvenation synthesis with hydration/elasticity endpoint focus.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports tracking moisture and elasticity over about 12 weeks, with evidence-quality caveats.
