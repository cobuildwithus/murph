This source is included for **skin-aging**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Six-week randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial.
- **People studied or addressed:** Women aged 30–65 years with Fitzpatrick phototypes I–IV.
- **Intervention/exposure:** 2.5 g/day low-molecular-weight collagen peptides.
- **Comparator/control:** Matched maltodextrin placebo with the same flavor/excipient base.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 6 weeks.
- **Role in Murph:** direct protocol evidence for Hydrolyzed Collagen Peptides.

**Findings:** Wrinkle volume, area, and depth were reported to decrease after 6 weeks versus placebo, with large percent reductions reported for volume and area. Skin moisturization was reported to increase significantly versus placebo after 6 weeks. Firmness and fatigue/resistance outcomes were not significantly modified, while elasticity showed slight improvement.

**Adverse events or safety:** Reported as well tolerated; specific adverse-event counts were not extracted from the accessible materials.

**Why it matters:** Recent open-access direct RCT focused on wrinkles and hydration at a practical 2.5 g/day dose.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- facial wrinkle volume, area, and depth
- skin moisturization/hydration
- elasticity, firmness, and fatigue/resistance measures

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports a cautious skin-aging outcome claim for low-molecular-weight collagen peptides, while preserving the mixed firmness/fatigue results and short-duration caveat.

**Limitations and population fit:**

- Short 6-week duration.
- Women-only, phototype-limited sample.
- Product-specific low-molecular-weight collagen peptide preparation.
- Some biophysical outcomes were null or only slightly changed.
- Adverse-event details were not fully extractable from accessible materials.

**Population mismatch:** Women aged 30–65 years with Fitzpatrick phototypes I–IV may not match all Murph users; do not generalize to different ages, sexes, baseline skin status, or collagen product types without caveats.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
