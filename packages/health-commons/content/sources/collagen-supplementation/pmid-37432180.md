This source is included for **skin-aging**.

**Findings:** Oral collagen improved skin hydration and elasticity in 26 RCTs, but future large trials and bias checks remain important. Use as direct skin-outcome evidence and a source-recall hub.

**Why it matters:** It is one of the largest open skin anti-aging collagen syntheses.

**Potential experiment signals:** skin hydration, skin elasticity, wrinkles, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports skin hydration/elasticity endpoints, with quality and heterogeneity caveats.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction details

- **Source type:** Systematic review and meta-analysis (2023).
- **People studied or addressed:** Participants in randomized controlled trials of oral collagen for skin anti-aging outcomes.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Oral hydrolyzed collagen or collagen-based supplements across included RCTs.
- **Comparator or control:** Placebo.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Varied, commonly several weeks to months across RCTs.
- **Most relevant endpoints:** skin hydration, skin elasticity, wrinkles, adverse events.
