This source is included for **clinical-supervised-wound-nutrition**.

**Findings:**

- This guideline update is included to mark professional pressure-ulcer treatment boundaries rather than collagen peptide efficacy. `[source_artifact:pmid-37970711]`
- Nutrition and wound-care decisions in pressure-ulcer treatment belong in clinical assessment and treatment planning. `[source_artifact:pmid-37970711]`
- No self-run hydrolyzed collagen peptide claim should be derived from this guideline. `[source_artifact:pmid-37970711]`

**Why it matters:** Prevents importing clinical wound-care nutrition recommendations into a general self-experiment protocol. `[source_artifact:pmid-37970711]`

**Potential experiment signals:**

  - "pressure-ulcer treatment boundary"
  - "nutrition assessment"
  - "dietitian referral"
  - "medical care escalation"

**Protocol takeaway:** Safety boundary: active pressure ulcers require clinician-supervised wound care; this guideline should not support self-run collagen claims. `[source_artifact:pmid-37970711]`

**Population mismatch:** People with pressure ulcers in professional wound-care treatment settings.

**Limitations:**

- Guideline, not primary trial evidence.
- Not specific to hydrolyzed collagen peptides as a protocol intervention.
- Treatment recommendations apply to pressure-ulcer clinical care, not wellness supplementation.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
