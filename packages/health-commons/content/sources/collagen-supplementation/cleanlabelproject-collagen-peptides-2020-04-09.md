This source is included for **safety-quality-contaminants**.

**Findings:** Population/exposure: 30 collagen products selected from Amazon best-seller and Clean Label Project sources; no human participants Intervention or exposure: Commercial collagen peptide products. Comparator/control: California Proposition 65 Safe Harbor levels and laboratory quantification thresholds. Duration/follow-up: Single product sampling/testing campaign Endpoints: total arsenic, cadmium, lead, mercury, per-serving comparison. Direction/effect: The white paper reported measurable arsenic in 64%, cadmium in 17%, lead in 37%, and trace mercury in 34% of the tested collagen products; it reported that none exceeded the arsenic threshold while some products exceeded lead/cadmium thresholds. Safety notes: No human adverse events; product-quality/contaminant testing only. Limitations: Not peer-reviewed.; Product selection and reporting may not represent all collagen products.; Prop 65 threshold interpretation is not equivalent to clinical harm or federal safety judgment.; Full product list and methods should be reviewed before product-specific claims.. Population mismatch: Commercial product samples rather than supplement users.

**Why it matters:** Directly collagen-specific consumer testing, but should remain safety/product-quality context because it is not peer-reviewed.
