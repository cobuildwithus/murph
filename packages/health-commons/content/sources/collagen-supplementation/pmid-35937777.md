This source is included for **tendon-loading-ligament**.

**Findings:** Nutrition-tendon evidence is broad and low certainty; collagen may be promising but dose, timing, duration, and type remain unclear. Use as same-mechanism context and to avoid overclaiming tendon benefits.

**Why it matters:** It helps place collagen within broader tendon nutrition evidence and uncertainty.

**Potential experiment signals:** tendon health, tendinopathy pain, tendon function, nutrition context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context only; tendon claims need collagen-specific primary trials.
