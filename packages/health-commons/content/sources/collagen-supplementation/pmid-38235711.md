This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:**

- **dose — Dose range:** Devasia 2024 provides product-specific dose-ranging knee-OA evidence. Effect/direction: Type J bovine collagen peptides at 2.5, 5, or 10 g/day; conventional collagen peptides at 10 g/day for 90 days.
- **outcome — WOMAC, pain, QoL, CTX-II, MOAKS:** Recent knee-OA RCT supports symptom and biomarker/MRI-score improvements, product-specifically. Effect/direction: All Type J doses and conventional collagen peptide were reported beneficial; Type J 2.5 g/day reportedly equivalent to conventional 10 g/day.
- **safety — Adverse events:** Safety signal is favorable but product-specific. Effect/direction: Accessible summaries state adverse events were ruled unrelated to investigational product.
- **limitation — Product specificity and small arms:** Do not generalize low-dose equivalence to all hydrolyzed collagen peptides. Effect/direction: Five-arm n=100 design implies small arm sizes; Type J is proprietary high-functional collagen peptide.

**Why it matters:** Recent direct knee-OA RCT includes symptoms, biomarker, and MRI-score endpoints plus active comparator.

**Potential experiment signals:** WOMAC, pain scale, quality of life, physician impression of change, CTX-II, MOAKS MRI, safety.

**Protocol takeaway:** Collagen peptide effects may be product- and dose-specific; Type J low-dose results should be labeled product-specific.

**Murph takeaway:** WOMAC/pain can be paired with functional/QoL tracking; structural claims remain cautious.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness and boundary:** `direct_protocol`. Direct knee-OA population.

**Safety notes:** Accessible summaries state adverse events were ruled unrelated to the investigational product.

**Limitations:**

- Product-specific novel collagen peptide with active comparator; findings may not transfer to all hydrolyzed collagen products.
- Multiple arms with n about 20 per arm limit precision.
