This source is included for **exercise-performance-recovery**.

**Findings:** Review included eight articles and 286 participants. It reported mixed biomarker evidence: Clifford and Kuwaba did not show significant marker differences, while Bischof showed smaller increases in MYO/CK/LDH with medium effect sizes. VAS pain and fatigue differences were reported only in Kuwaba, favoring collagen.

**Why it matters:** Recovery-specific review surfaces null, mixed, and positive sources.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle soreness, fatigue, CK, LDH, myoglobin.

**Protocol takeaway:** The recovery literature contains promising subjective and biomarker signals but remains mixed and heterogeneous.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness and boundary:** `direct_protocol`. Do not use as independent efficacy evidence when citing underlying trials.

**Safety notes:** Safety reporting varied across included studies; no unified adverse-event estimate extracted.

**Limitations:** Integrative/narrative review, not a new trial.; Study heterogeneity and mixed endpoint results.; Do not double-count review conclusions with underlying trials.

**Population mismatch:** Review-level aggregation across heterogeneous exercise populations.
