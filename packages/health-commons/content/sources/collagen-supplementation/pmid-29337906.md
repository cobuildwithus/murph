This source is included for **bone-density-turnover**.

**Findings:**

- **Population:** Postmenopausal women with age-related reduced BMD; accessible abstract reports age 64.3 ± 7.2 years and baseline lumbar-spine T-score about -2.4 ± 0.6.
- **Intervention/exposure:** 5 g/day specific collagen peptides for 12 months.
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo for 12 months.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 12 months.
- **Endpoints:** lumbar spine BMD, femoral neck BMD, P1NP, CTX1.
- **Effect/direction:** Lumbar spine and femoral-neck BMD T-scores increased versus placebo; P1NP increased in the collagen group, while CTX1 increased in controls in accessible abstract.
- **Adverse events/safety:** Accessible abstract does not foreground adverse events; use full text before making a safety claim.
- **Limitations:** Single branded specific collagen peptide preparation; generalizability to all hydrolyzed collagen products is uncertain.; BMD endpoint requires long follow-up and DXA precision context.; Published by authors including product-affiliated expertise; assess conflict-of-interest details in full text before synthesis.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Direct protocol evidence in target postmenopausal low-BMD population, but product-specific.

**Why it matters:** This is the clearest direct RCT for BMD and bone-marker outcomes in the protocol-relevant population.

**Potential experiment signals:** lumbar spine BMD, femoral neck BMD, P1NP, CTX1, 12-month adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** A cautious protocol page may cite this as direct positive BMD evidence for 5 g/day specific collagen peptides over 12 months in postmenopausal women with reduced BMD.
