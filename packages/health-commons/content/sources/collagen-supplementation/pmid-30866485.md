This source is included for **metabolic-cardiovascular-adjacent**.

## Findings

At 12 weeks, intervention versus control changes in percent body fat and body-fat mass were reported as -1.2% vs 2.7% (p=0.024) and -1.2 kg vs 0.3 kg (p=0.025).

## Why it matters

It separates body-fat claims from musculoskeletal and dermatologic claims.

## Potential experiment signals

- DEXA body-fat percentage
- DEXA fat mass
- tolerability
- body weight/BMI

## Protocol takeaway

Context-only body-composition boundary; do not use as core protocol support.

## Safety and adverse events

Application was reported as well tolerated with no notable adverse effect in either group.

## Limitations and mismatch

- Overweight adult body-fat endpoint.
- Product-specific marine skate-skin collagen.
- No core skin/joint/tendon endpoint.
- Industry/product context should be considered.
- Population mismatch: Overweight body-composition population rather than target musculoskeletal/skin protocol populations.
