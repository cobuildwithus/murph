## Quick read

- **Source type:** Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial.
- **People studied or addressed:** Adults in a skin-moisture/barrier trial; demographic details should be checked in full text before protocol synthesis.
- **Intervention/exposure:** 1 g/day enzymatically decomposed AP collagen peptides.
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 12 weeks.
- **Role in Murph:** direct protocol evidence for Hydrolyzed Collagen Peptides.

**Findings:** Skin moisture improved versus placebo. Roughness/texture improved versus placebo in accessible summaries. Natural moisturizing factor amino-acid content and ceramide measures increased versus placebo.

**Adverse events or safety:** Adverse-event details were not extracted from accessible abstract-level materials.

**Why it matters:** Direct RCT with hydration plus stratum-corneum ceramide and NMF endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- skin moisture
- skin roughness/texture
- stratum-corneum natural moisturizing factor
- stratum-corneum ceramide content

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports hydration/barrier claims for enzymatically decomposed collagen peptides, with barrier mechanisms kept separate from visible anti-aging claims.

**Limitations and population fit:**

- Population details require full-text verification before final synthesis.
- Barrier lipid/NMF findings are mechanistic support for hydration, not direct evidence of wrinkle reduction.
- Product-specific AP collagen peptide preparation.

**Population mismatch:** Adults in a skin-moisture/barrier trial; demographic details should be checked in full text before protocol synthesis may not match all Murph users; do not generalize to different ages, sexes, baseline skin status, or collagen product types without caveats.
