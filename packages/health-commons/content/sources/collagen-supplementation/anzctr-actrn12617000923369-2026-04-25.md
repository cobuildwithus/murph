This source is included for **tendon-loading-ligament**.

**Findings:** Registry record only; no outcome results extracted.

**Population and intervention:** Final sample size 16 healthy active males. Intervention/exposure: Proline/glycine-containing foods and supplements including hydrolysed collagen powder, liquid gelatin, solid gelatin, bone broth, whey, skim milk, and casein. Comparator/control: Active cross-comparisons among protein/test-food conditions. Duration/follow-up: Seven crossover trials separated by 72-hour washouts.

**Endpoints:** Plasma amino acid availability, urinary hydroxyproline, engineered ligament collagen content, engineered ligament tensile properties.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event results extracted from the registry record.

**Limitations:** Bioavailability/mechanistic registry, not clinical efficacy.; Multiple foods and proteins; hydrolyzed collagen is one arm.; No results extracted.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy male mechanistic population; not tendon pain or rehabilitation.

**Why it matters:** It helps bound the difference between collagen bioavailability studies and clinical tendon-loading trials.

**Potential experiment signals:** plasma glycine/proline/hydroxyproline; urinary hydroxyproline; engineered ligament collagen content; tensile properties

**Protocol takeaway:** Hydrolyzed collagen bioavailability data can inform mechanism, but it does not establish protocol efficacy.
