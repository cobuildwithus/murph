This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:** The current RACGP nutraceuticals page states it is unable to recommend either for or against collagen for people with knee and/or hip OA, labels the recommendation conditional neutral, and rates the evidence low for knee and very low for hip. It also states collagen is relatively safe but notes a non-statistically significant increase in adverse gastrointestinal events.

**Why it matters:** Provides up-to-date neutral recommendation and safety language for protocol boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** pain, function, GI adverse events, evidence quality, recommendation strength

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for guideline context, uncertainty, and GI monitoring language.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
