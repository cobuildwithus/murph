This source is included for **skin-aging**.

**Findings:** Japanese registry maps skin and scalp barrier endpoints but has no results. Endpoints captured: stratum corneum moisture; transepidermal water loss; skin elasticity; scalp elasticity/hardness; scalp water evaporation.

**Why it matters:** It broadens recall beyond US/EU registries and captures scalp endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** stratum corneum moisture, TEWL, skin elasticity, scalp hardness, scalp water evaporation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as registry context only; no efficacy or safety claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
