This source is included for **skin-aging**.

**Findings:** Dermatoporosis trial found no clear advantage for oral or topical hydrolyzed collagen in older postmenopausal women. Endpoints captured: clinical dermatoporosis score; quality of life; dermal elasticity; dermal thickness; dermal echogenicity; histology; immunohistochemistry; adverse events.

**Why it matters:** This source preserves a negative finding and a clinically distinct skin-fragility boundary.

**Potential experiment signals:** dermal elasticity, ultrasound dermal thickness, histology, dermatoporosis score, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not imply hydrolyzed collagen treats dermatoporosis; this RCT found no clear benefit in that population.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
