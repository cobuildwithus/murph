## Quick read

- **Source type:** Double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial using biophysical and imaging techniques.
- **People studied or addressed:** Adult females.
- **Intervention/exposure:** 10 g/day CollaSel Pro hydrolyzed collagen peptide without add-ons.
- **Comparator/control:** 10 g/day maltodextrin placebo.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 8 weeks.
- **Role in Murph:** direct protocol evidence for Hydrolyzed Collagen Peptides.

**Findings:** Elasticity, hydration, and roughness improved versus placebo in accessible abstract data. Facial soft-tissue sagging/RMS imaging improved from baseline in the collagen group in accessible summaries. The trial is notable for collagen peptide without add-on ingredients.

**Adverse events or safety:** Reported as safe and well tolerated in accessible summaries; detailed GI/adverse-event counts were not extracted here.

**Why it matters:** Recent direct RCT explicitly testing hydrolyzed collagen peptide without coingredients and including imaging plus safety framing.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- skin elasticity
- skin hydration
- skin roughness
- facial soft-tissue sagging/RMS imaging
- safety/tolerability

**Protocol takeaway:** Strong direct fit for the protocol: collagen-only HCP improved hydration/elasticity/roughness over 8 weeks, with safety and imaging caveats preserved.

**Limitations and population fit:**

- Adult-female-only population.
- Roughness unit/direction should be checked against the full table before final synthesis because accessible abstract units are odd.
- Eight-week duration.

**Population mismatch:** Adult females may not match all Murph users; do not generalize to different ages, sexes, baseline skin status, or collagen product types without caveats.
