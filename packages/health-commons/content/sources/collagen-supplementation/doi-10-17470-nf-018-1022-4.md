- **Duration/follow-up:** 30 to 90 days reported in secondary-accessible records.
- **Endpoints:** fracture healing, physician-rated healing.
- **Effect/direction:** Secondary-accessible records report better physician-rated fracture healing with collagen peptides, but primary article access is limited.
- **Adverse events/safety:** Adverse events not extracted.
- **Limitations:** Adjacent fracture-healing context, not BMD/osteopenia prevention.; Small sample; accessible details are sparse and partly from secondary summaries.; Do not use for direct protocol efficacy claims without full-text verification.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Bone-healing treatment context rather than prevention or BMD maintenance.

**Why it matters:** It shows a clinical bone-repair context but should not be mixed with BMD prevention evidence.
