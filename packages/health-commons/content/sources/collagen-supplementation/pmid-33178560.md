**Findings:** Population/exposure: 9-year-old girl with anaphylaxis after fish-collagen-containing gummy tablets Intervention or exposure: Fruit-flavored gummy tablets containing fish-derived collagen. Comparator/control: Testing against pork gelatin and other samples in diagnostic workup. Duration/follow-up: Acute ingestion reaction and diagnostic follow-up Endpoints: anaphylaxis, oral challenge, immunoblot band, mass spectrometry identification. Direction/effect: Oral fish-collagen challenge confirmed fish-collagen-induced anaphylaxis in the case report; diagnostic testing identified a fish-collagen band. Safety notes: Pediatric anaphylaxis from a fish-collagen-containing oral product. Limitations: Single pediatric case report.; Processed gummy tablet, not a standalone HCP powder.; Cannot estimate prevalence or predict risk outside susceptible patients.. Population mismatch: Single child with a specific allergy phenotype rather than adult supplement users.

**Why it matters:** Strengthens allergy warnings around fish collagen in oral products, including products marketed outside standard collagen powders.
