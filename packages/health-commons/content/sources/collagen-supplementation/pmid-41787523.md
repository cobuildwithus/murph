This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:** Seventy-four healthy volunteers with activity-related joint discomfort were randomized to native type-II collagen 40 mg/day or placebo for 180 days. Accessible summaries reported KOOS improvements in both groups by day 180, earlier baseline improvements and subgroup differences favoring active collagen, and CTX-II changes in opposite directions between groups. It remains adjacent variant evidence.

**Why it matters:** Important current boundary source for joint-health claims outside OA.

**Potential experiment signals:** KOOS pain, KOOS symptoms, KOOS QoL, exercise-recovery discomfort, CTX-II, tolerability

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only; separate native collagen wellness claims from HCP evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
