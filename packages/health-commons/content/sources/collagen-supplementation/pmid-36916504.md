## Quick read

- **Source type:** Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study.
- **People studied or addressed:** Women aged 30–60 years.
- **Intervention/exposure:** 1.65 g/day collagen peptide NS containing Gly-Pro and Pro-Hyp.
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 12 weeks.
- **Role in Murph:** direct protocol evidence for Hydrolyzed Collagen Peptides.

**Findings:** Desquamation and hydration improved significantly after 4 weeks in accessible summaries. Wrinkling and elasticity improved after 12 weeks. The source reports no side effects in accessible summaries.

**Adverse events or safety:** No side effects were reported in accessible summaries.

**Why it matters:** Direct RCT with hydration, desquamation, elasticity, and wrinkling endpoints at a low daily dose.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- skin hydration
- skin elasticity
- desquamation
- wrinkling

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports 4-week hydration/desquamation signals and 12-week wrinkle/elasticity signals for CPNS-like collagen peptides.

**Limitations and population fit:**

- Women-only 30–60 sample.
- Product-specific CPNS peptide profile.
- Full endpoint tables were not extracted into this batch artifact.

**Population mismatch:** Women aged 30–60 years may not match all Murph users; do not generalize to different ages, sexes, baseline skin status, or collagen product types without caveats.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
