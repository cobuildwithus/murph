This source is included for **tendon-loading-ligament**.

**Findings:** Registry record only; linked to the Shaw gelatin mechanistic publication.

**Population and intervention:** Final sample size 8 healthy active males. Intervention/exposure: 5 g or 15 g gelatin in vitamin C-containing beverage 1 hour before rope skipping. Comparator/control: Isocaloric placebo in vitamin C-containing beverage. Duration/follow-up: Acute crossover with 4-day washout and 3-day repeated phase.

**Endpoints:** Engineered ligament collagen content and mechanics, serum PINP, amino acid availability.

**Safety notes:** Registry eligibility excludes allergy to gelatin or fruit drink; no adverse-event results extracted.

**Limitations:** Gelatin rather than HCP.; Healthy men and mechanistic endpoints only.; Registry record is not an efficacy result.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy active males; not tendon pathology.

**Why it matters:** Documents the mechanistic protocol behind a commonly cited gelatin timing study.

**Potential experiment signals:** serum PINP; engineered ligament mechanics; amino acid availability

**Protocol takeaway:** Useful for timing and mechanistic lineage, but should remain separated from hydrolyzed collagen peptide clinical evidence.
