This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:**

- **dose — Dose and duration:** Pilot imaging RCT uses a standard 10 g/day dose. Effect/direction: 10 g/day collagen hydrolysate for 24 weeks.
- **outcome — dGEMRIC cartilage MRI:** Collagen hydrolysate showed region-specific cartilage-composition signal. Effect/direction: Mixed-positive structural surrogate: medial/lateral tibial dGEMRIC T1 improved versus placebo in available summaries.
- **null_result — Clinical outcomes and other MRI endpoints:** Do not infer symptomatic benefit or broad cartilage repair from this pilot. Effect/direction: No clear significant clinical outcome or T2 MRI advantage in accessible summaries.
- **limitation — Pilot sample size:** Small pilot limits precision and generalizability. Effect/direction: Only 30 randomized participants.

**Why it matters:** It is one of the few direct collagen hydrolysate trials with cartilage MRI endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** dGEMRIC T1 cartilage MRI, T2 MRI, clinical symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promise cartilage repair; this pilot supports only a cautious imaging-signal hypothesis.

**Murph takeaway:** For self-experiments, symptom/function outcomes are more practical than cartilage imaging.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness and boundary:** `direct_protocol`. Direct mild knee-OA population.

**Safety notes:** No detailed adverse-event extraction available from accessible summaries.

**Limitations:**

- Very small pilot sample.
- Imaging signal was region-specific and not accompanied by clear clinical improvement.
- Single-center design.
