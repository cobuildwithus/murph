This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:** DRKS00009553 describes a randomized, placebo-controlled, double-blind study of 5 g/day specific bioactive collagen peptides versus maltodextrin placebo for lower-extremity joint functionality. A linked publication context reports 182 adults with functional knee/hip pain over 12 weeks and improvements in physician-rated pain at rest/walking and participant stair/kneeling/squatting outcomes.

**Why it matters:** Direct oral collagen-peptide evidence for joint discomfort, but not an OA-specific source.

**Potential experiment signals:** pain at rest, pain walking, pain climbing stairs, pain kneeling, squatting restriction, mobility questionnaire

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only direct-protocol registry/publication boundary; preserve population mismatch.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
