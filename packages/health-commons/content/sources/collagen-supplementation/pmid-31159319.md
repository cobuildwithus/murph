This source is included for **mechanism-bioavailability**.

## Findings

Accessible abstracts report that hydrolyzed-collagen-enriched serum affected bone-cell activity and that preclinical supplementation attenuated bone loss versus control protein.

## Why it matters

It supports a mechanistic model while preserving the distinction from clinical bone efficacy.

## Potential experiment signals

- bone-cell activity
- osteoblast/osteoclast modulation
- collagen metabolite-enriched serum

## Protocol takeaway

Use for mechanism only; do not cite as clinical bone-outcome evidence.

## Safety and adverse events

No adverse-event signal extracted.

## Limitations and mismatch

- Serum/ex vivo model.
- Human sample count not confirmed in accessible text.
- Preclinical extension.
- No clinical BMD or fracture endpoint.
- Population mismatch: Mechanistic donor/cell/animal context rather than target clinical population.
