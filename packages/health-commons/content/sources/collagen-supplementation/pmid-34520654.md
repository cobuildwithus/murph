This source is included for **bone-density-turnover**.

**Findings:**

- **Population:** Postmenopausal women with osteopenia or osteoporosis/reduced BMD who completed the original program and continued follow-up.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Specific bioactive collagen peptides; accessible sources identify continuation to a total of 4 years.
- **Comparator/control:** No concurrent control group during long-term observation.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 4-year total long-term observation.
- **Endpoints:** DXA lumbar spine BMD, DXA femoral neck BMD, T-scores, fractures recorded.
- **Effect/direction:** Accessible abstract-level records report clinically relevant BMD increases at spine and consistent positive changes at femoral neck; without a concurrent control, causality is limited.
- **Adverse events/safety:** Safety details not extracted from accessible abstract-level records.
- **Limitations:** Open-label observation with no concurrent control.; Only 31 completers; selection/adherence bias likely.; Fracture outcomes were recorded but accessible records do not provide enough detail for a fracture-effect claim.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Direct population and intervention, but observational follow-up rather than randomized evidence.

**Why it matters:** It extends the time horizon beyond the 12-month RCT but must be separated from randomized evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** DXA lumbar spine BMD, DXA femoral neck BMD, fracture recording, long-term adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Cite as observational long-term support only, with clear caveat that there was no concurrent control.
