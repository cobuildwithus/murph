**Findings:** Population/exposure: Bloommy Biotin Collagen Keratin Capsules for Skin, Joint, and Hair, 60-count bottles distributed online Intervention or exposure: Collagen/biotin/keratin combination capsules containing a collagen-based ingredient with undeclared fish allergen. Comparator/control: No comparator. Duration/follow-up: Recall notice dated May 6-7, 2021 Endpoints: undeclared fish allergen, units issued, illness reports, label update. Direction/effect: FDA-posted notice reported 13,688 units issued and no illnesses reported to date; product was recalled due to fish allergen in a collagen-based ingredient. Safety notes: People with fish allergy or severe sensitivity were warned of serious or life-threatening allergic reaction risk if consumed. Limitations: Combination product, not HCP-alone.; Recall notice rather than clinical study.; Does not estimate allergy incidence.. Population mismatch: Product recall event rather than a supplement-user cohort.

**Why it matters:** Official recall evidence that fish-source collagen ingredients can create labeling/allergy risk in combination products.
