This source is included for **tendon-loading-ligament**.

**Findings:** Both groups improved pain; the nutraceutical group had a better pain outcome at 12 weeks in the extracted record. Ultrasound thickness decreased in both groups; absolute intergroup differences were not clearly significant, though percentage change favored the nutraceutical for AP thickness.

**Population and intervention:** 40 patients with Achilles tendinopathy. Intervention/exposure: Combination nutraceutical with collagen peptide type 1, low molecular weight chondroitin sulphate, sodium hyaluronate, and vitamin C. Comparator/control: Oral diclofenac sodium. Duration/follow-up: 12 weeks with assessments at baseline, 6 weeks, and 12 weeks.

**Endpoints:** VAS pain and ultrasound Achilles tendon thickness.

**Safety notes:** Diclofenac side effects were tracked, but specific adverse-event counts were not extracted from available snippets.

**Limitations:** Multi-ingredient product; collagen cannot be isolated.; Active comparator rather than placebo.; Limited adverse-event details in extracted record.; Not hydrolyzed collagen peptide-only.

**Population mismatch:** Clinical Achilles population but adjacent multi-ingredient intervention.

**Why it matters:** It is clinically relevant to Achilles tendinopathy but has a major ingredient-boundary problem.

**Potential experiment signals:** VAS pain; Achilles ultrasound AP/ML thickness; diclofenac adverse effects

**Protocol takeaway:** This source can inform adjacent tendinopathy context but should not support direct hydrolyzed collagen peptide efficacy statements.
