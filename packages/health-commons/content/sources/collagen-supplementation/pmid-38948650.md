This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:** Ninety-eight grade 1-3 knee-OA patients took a daily sachet containing hydrolyzed type-II collagen, MSM, glucosamine sulfate, and chondroitin sulfate for two months. VAS pain, WOMAC, and HAQ improved across visits, with high compliance, but the study was observational and noninterventional with no control group.

**Why it matters:** Avoids upgrading uncontrolled combination-supplement data into direct HCP evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** VAS pain, WOMAC, Health Assessment Questionnaire, compliance, adverse events

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only; do not use for causal HCP claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
