This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:** The trial compared UC-II with glucosamine plus chondroitin in knee OA. At 90 days, the abstract reported larger percentage reductions with UC-II in WOMAC, VAS, and Lequesne Functional Index than with glucosamine plus chondroitin. Because the product is undenatured type II collagen, this is boundary evidence only.

**Why it matters:** Frequently cited in joint-health collagen discussions, but the intervention and dose logic differ from hydrolyzed collagen peptides.

**Potential experiment signals:** WOMAC, VAS pain, Lequesne Functional Index, daily activities, safety

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep as adjacent UC-II evidence; do not use to support hydrolyzed collagen peptide claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
