This source is included for **skin-aging**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled clinical trial with washout observation.
- **People studied or addressed:** Healthy adults aged 20–59 years.
- **Intervention/exposure:** 1.65 g/day low-molecular-weight collagen peptide/NS Collagen Peptide containing functional Gly-Pro peptide.
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 8 weeks supplementation plus 2-week washout/maintenance observation.
- **Role in Murph:** direct protocol evidence for Hydrolyzed Collagen Peptides.

**Findings:** Wrinkle depth, height, and visual severity improved versus placebo in accessible summaries. Hydration and elasticity improved. Significant effects were reported to be maintained during the 2-week washout observation.

**Adverse events or safety:** No adverse events were reported in accessible summaries.

**Why it matters:** Newest direct open-access LMWCP RCT with moisturization, anti-aging, safety, and short washout framing.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- facial wrinkle depth, height, and visual severity
- skin hydration
- skin elasticity
- washout/maintenance
- adverse events

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports hydration, elasticity, wrinkle, and short-term persistence signals for LMWCP, with product-specific and short-washout caveats.

**Limitations and population fit:**

- Healthy-adult sample may include variable baseline skin concerns.
- Product-specific LMWCP/CPNS peptide profile.
- Two-week washout is short for durability claims.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy adults aged 20–59 years may not match all Murph users; do not generalize to different ages, sexes, baseline skin status, or collagen product types without caveats.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
