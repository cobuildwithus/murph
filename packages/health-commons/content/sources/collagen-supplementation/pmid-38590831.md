This source is included for **exercise-performance-recovery**.

**Findings:** Area-under-the-curve interactions favored collagen for myoglobin (p=0.004, ηp²=0.184), CK (p=0.010, ηp²=0.145), and LDH (p=0.016, ηp²=0.133); delta-AUC changes favored collagen for MYO p=0.017, CK p=0.039, and LDH p=0.016.

**Why it matters:** Direct companion evidence for recovery biomarkers after EIMD.

**Potential experiment signals:** myoglobin AUC, creatine kinase AUC, lactate dehydrogenase AUC, hsCRP.

**Protocol takeaway:** After 12 weeks of concurrent training, 15 g/day specific collagen peptides lowered selected systemic muscle-stress marker responses to EIMD.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness and boundary:** `direct_protocol`. Biomarker reductions should not be equated with symptom relief or injury prevention.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event signal extracted from accessible text.

**Limitations:** Men only.; Biomarker-focused companion outcome.; Product-specific collagen-peptide blend.; Concurrent training co-intervention.

**Population mismatch:** Sedentary-to-moderately-active male training cohort; not women or clinical populations.
