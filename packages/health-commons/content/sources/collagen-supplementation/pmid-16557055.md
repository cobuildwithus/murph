This source is included for **clinical-supervised-wound-nutrition**.

**Findings:**

- Available abstracts describe a randomized, prospective, multicenter trial in long-term-care residents with Stage II-IV pressure ulcers. `[source_artifact:pmid-16557055]`
- Participants received standard care plus a concentrated fortified collagen protein hydrolysate supplement or placebo three times daily for 8 weeks; PUSH Tool scores were tracked biweekly. `[source_artifact:pmid-16557055]`
- The accessible abstract reports significantly better pressure-ulcer healing scores in the supplement arm, but detailed adverse-event data were not available from the accessible abstract. `[source_artifact:pmid-16557055]`

**Why it matters:** Provides early direct oral collagen hydrolysate wound-care RCT context, but the medical-care population is outside the self-run protocol target. `[source_artifact:pmid-16557055]`

**Potential experiment signals:**

  - "PUSH score"
  - "pressure ulcer healing rate"
  - "standard-care co-interventions"
  - "adverse event monitoring"

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only: pressure-ulcer treatment evidence should not be merged into direct Hydrolyzed Collagen Peptides efficacy claims. `[source_artifact:pmid-16557055]`

**Population mismatch:** Long-term-care residents with Stage II, III, or IV pressure ulcers; 89 entered and 71 completed in available abstracts.

**Limitations:**

- Full text appears paywalled.
- Available extraction is abstract/snippet-based.
- Fortified collagen hydrolysate matrix prevents isolation of hydrolyzed collagen peptides alone.
- Clinical pressure-ulcer population is a major population mismatch.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
