This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:**

- **dose — Dose and duration:** Zdzieblik 2021 supports a 5 g/day dose in young active adults. Effect/direction: 5 g/day specific bioactive collagen peptides for 12 weeks.
- **outcome — Exercise-induced knee discomfort:** Specific collagen peptides improved exercise-induced knee discomfort versus placebo. Effect/direction: Pain reduction favored collagen peptides: −21.9 ± 18.3 vs −15.6 ± 18.5 mm, p=0.024; physician assessment p=0.003.
- **mixed_result — Rest pain, squat pain, mobility:** The benefit was specific to exercise-induced discomfort, not all joint endpoints. Effect/direction: Rest pain, squat pain, and mobility did not clearly differ versus placebo in accessible summaries.
- **population — Population mismatch:** Do not use as direct OA evidence. Effect/direction: Young active adults without diagnosed joint disease.

**Why it matters:** Open-access RCT directly tests collagen peptides in a young active population relevant to self-tracked joint discomfort.

**Potential experiment signals:** exercise-induced knee pain, physician-assessed knee pain, rest pain, squat pain, mobility.

**Protocol takeaway:** 5 g/day may help exercise-triggered knee discomfort after 12 weeks, but OA extrapolation is limited.

**Murph takeaway:** Standardize an exercise-provoked knee-pain measure if testing this use case.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness and boundary:** `direct_protocol`. Young active adults with exercise-related discomfort, not clinical OA.

**Safety notes:** No detailed adverse-event extraction available from accessible summaries.

**Limitations:**

- Young active population without joint disease; not OA.
- Some secondary outcomes were not significant.
