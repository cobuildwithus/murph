This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:** In 191 knee-OA participants, UC-II 40 mg/day, glucosamine plus chondroitin, or placebo were compared for 180 days. UC-II improved WOMAC total, pain, stiffness/function-related outcomes and used less rescue medication than comparators in accessible summaries; safety outcomes did not differ among groups. The evidence is adjacent because the intervention is undenatured type-II collagen.

**Why it matters:** Often conflated with collagen peptide trials; this source supports variant-boundary language.

**Potential experiment signals:** WOMAC total, WOMAC pain, WOMAC stiffness, WOMAC function, VAS, Lequesne Functional Index, rescue medication, safety

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use UC-II results as direct support for HCP; use for comparison and endpoint context.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
