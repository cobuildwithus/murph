This source is included for **metabolic-cardiovascular-adjacent**.

## Findings

Specific collagen peptide group showed greater running-distance improvement and fat-free-mass gain than control; fat mass declined over time in both groups without group differences, and strength improved over time without between-group differences.

## Why it matters

It helps classify exercise-cointervention evidence separately from simple oral HCP protocols.

- 1RM strength

Context-only because concurrent training was integral.

## Limitations and mismatch

- Concurrent training co-intervention.
- Recreationally active women only.
- Performance/body-composition endpoints outside core HCP-alone protocol.
- Diet and activity context influence interpretation.
- Population mismatch: Exercise-training context rather than standalone HCP use.
