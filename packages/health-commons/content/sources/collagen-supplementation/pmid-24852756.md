This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:**

- **dose — Dose and duration:** Kumar 2015 supports a 10 g/day collagen peptide dose over roughly three months. Effect/direction: 5 g twice daily (10 g/day) pork-skin or bovine-bone collagen peptide for about 13 weeks in available extracts.
- **outcome — WOMAC, VAS, and quality-of-life scores:** Small knee-OA comparisons favored collagen peptides over placebo on pain/function/QoL. Effect/direction: Positive direction; both PCP and BCP groups improved versus placebo by Visit 7 in available extracts.
- **safety — Safety/tolerability:** Usable only as safety-evaluated context until full tables are reviewed. Effect/direction: Safety and tolerability were evaluated, but adverse-event counts were not extracted.
- **limitation — Small and complex design:** Avoid precise pooled effect or AE-rate claims before full-text QA. Effect/direction: Two small source-material comparisons with denominators not fully reconciled in accessible snippets.

**Why it matters:** Direct OA RCT evidence with two collagen peptide source materials.

**Potential experiment signals:** WOMAC, VAS pain, quality of life, safety/tolerability.

**Protocol takeaway:** 10 g/day collagen peptide for 13 weeks is a direct knee-OA symptom signal, but sample size is small.

**Murph takeaway:** Pain/function scales over 12-14 weeks are appropriate for an n-of-1 tracking window.
