This source is included for **tendon-loading-ligament**.

**Findings:** Registry record only; no outcomes extracted.

**Population and intervention:** Final sample size 20; adults with chronic midportion Achilles tendinopathy. Intervention/exposure: TENDOFORTE P containing 5 g/day highly bioactive collagen peptides, taken as 2.5 g twice daily, plus eccentric exercise. Comparator/control: Maltodextrin placebo plus the same eccentric exercise; crossover without washout. Duration/follow-up: 24-week crossover with two 12-week periods.

**Endpoints:** Ultrasound Tissue Characterisation, CEUS, Power Doppler ultrasound, UTE MRI, VISA-A.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event results extracted from the registry record.

**Limitations:** Registry-only entry for this extraction.; Retrospectively registered.; Crossover without washout.; No results reported in extracted registry record.

**Population mismatch:** Direct Achilles tendinopathy protocol, but registry-only context.

**Why it matters:** It provides protocol details, including imaging endpoints and eccentric exercise constraints, for the core Achilles pilot.

**Potential experiment signals:** VISA-A; UTC imaging; Power Doppler ultrasound; UTE MRI; eccentric exercise adherence

**Protocol takeaway:** Registry data reinforces that the Achilles collagen protocol was paired with intensive eccentric exercise and imaging outcomes.
