This source is included for **skin-aging**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Four-week randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial with post-discontinuation follow-up.
- **People studied or addressed:** Postmenopausal women.
- **Intervention/exposure:** 5 g/day oral marine collagen hydrolysate from fish scale and skin.
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 4 weeks supplementation plus 4-week post-discontinuation follow-up.
- **Role in Murph:** direct protocol evidence for Hydrolyzed Collagen Peptides.

**Findings:** Skin elasticity improved significantly in sun-exposed areas after 4 weeks. Elasticity improvements in the cheek area were reported to remain at the 4-week follow-up after discontinuation. The study is primarily an elasticity/site-comparison signal, not a broad wrinkle or hydration trial.

**Adverse events or safety:** No severe adverse effects were reported in accessible summaries.

**Why it matters:** Useful short-duration direct evidence and site-specific context for sun-exposed skin.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- skin elasticity in sun-exposed facial areas
- site comparison with sun-protected areas
- post-discontinuation persistence

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports skin elasticity as a possible early signal after 4 weeks, especially in sun-exposed areas, but should not be overgeneralized to all skin endpoints.

**Limitations and population fit:**

- Small sample.
- Postmenopausal women only.
- Short active-treatment period.
- Endpoint focus is elasticity rather than a broad skin-aging panel.
- Primary article is paywalled.

**Population mismatch:** Postmenopausal women may not match all Murph users; do not generalize to different ages, sexes, baseline skin status, or collagen product types without caveats.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
