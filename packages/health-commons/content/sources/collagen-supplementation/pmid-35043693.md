This source is included for **clinical-supervised-wound-nutrition**.

**Findings:**

- The review screened oral nutritional supplement literature and included 28 studies across protein/amino acid, vitamin/mineral/antioxidant, probiotic, and mixed-nutrient categories. `[source_artifact:pmid-35043693]`
- The authors reported variable effects across wound types and supplement categories, with a need for more research and more rigorous evaluation of multi-nutrient formulations. `[source_artifact:pmid-35043693]`
- The review is not specific to hydrolyzed collagen peptides and should not be used as direct protocol efficacy evidence. `[source_artifact:pmid-35043693]`

**Why it matters:** Supports classification of collagen-containing wound studies within broader clinical nutrition and wound-healing evidence, not wellness supplementation. `[source_artifact:pmid-35043693]`

**Potential experiment signals:**

  - "wound healing"
  - "supplement category"
  - "study heterogeneity"
  - "clinical-care boundary"

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only: use for wound-nutrition boundary framing, not for protocol efficacy or dose recommendations. `[source_artifact:pmid-35043693]`

**Population mismatch:** Adult clinical wound populations across heterogeneous wound and nutrition-study contexts.

**Limitations:**

- Review is broad and heterogeneous rather than collagen-specific.
- Included wound types and supplement compositions varied.
- The accessible article page does not provide extractable adverse-event synthesis for collagen-specific use.
- Clinical wound-care context is outside the wellness protocol target.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
