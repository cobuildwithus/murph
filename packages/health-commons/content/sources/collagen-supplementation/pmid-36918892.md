This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:** A 160-participant, four-arm knee-OA pilot compared chicken essence plus HC-II, HC-II, glucosamine HCl, and placebo for 24 weeks, with resistance training in all groups. All groups had similar WOMAC improvement; HC-II had an early VAS-pain signal at 14 days versus placebo in repeated-measures analysis. The population was mostly early/mild OA, and authors called for larger studies.

**Why it matters:** Relevant knee-OA hydrolyzed type-II boundary source with a mixed symptom pattern.

**Potential experiment signals:** WOMAC, VAS pain, fat-free mass, grip strength, bone mass, resistance training adherence

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only; do not generalize to isolated hydrolyzed collagen peptides.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
