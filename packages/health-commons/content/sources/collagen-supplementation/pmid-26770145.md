This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:** A pilot RCT in eight healthy recreationally active adults tested 3 g/day BioCell Collagen for six weeks before a resistance-exercise challenge. Accessible summaries reported smaller post-exercise increases in CK, LDH, CRP, and less performance decrement versus placebo, with good tolerability. It is not OA evidence and the product is a hydrolyzed cartilage extract with glycosaminoglycans.

**Why it matters:** Prevents exercise-recovery cartilage-extract data from being misclassified as hydrolyzed collagen peptide joint evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** creatine kinase, LDH, CRP, bench press performance, exercise recovery, tolerability

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only adjacent source; useful for recovery endpoints and product-boundary notes.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
