This source is included for **exercise-performance-recovery**.

**Findings:** Body mass and fat-free mass increased more with collagen than placebo; strength improved in both groups with only slightly higher increases in collagen; proteomics found 221 higher-abundance proteins in collagen versus 44 in placebo, mainly linked to contractile fiber protein metabolism.

**Why it matters:** Mechanistic companion-style source for resistance training plus collagen peptides.

**Potential experiment signals:** skeletal muscle proteome, fat-free mass, strength.

**Protocol takeaway:** Collagen peptides may alter proteome signals during training, but this remains mechanistic context.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness and boundary:** `same_mechanism`. Proteomic signal does not prove patient-centered benefit.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event signal extracted from accessible text.

**Limitations:** Mechanistic/substudy sample.; Proteome changes are not direct clinical outcomes.; Young male training context only.

**Population mismatch:** Recreationally active young men; mechanistic context only.
