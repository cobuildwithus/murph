This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:** Eighty adults with hip or knee OA were randomized to 2 g/day BioCell Collagen or placebo for 70 days. The active product improved VAS pain at day 70 and WOMAC outcomes at day 35 and day 70, and was described as comparably tolerated. The product composition makes this adjacent/multi-ingredient evidence rather than isolated hydrolyzed collagen peptide evidence.

**Why it matters:** Many commercial claims cite cartilage extracts; this source prevents accidental promotion of multi-ingredient extract data into peptide-only claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** VAS pain, WOMAC total, WOMAC physical activities, tolerability

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only boundary evidence: mention only when distinguishing isolated HCP from hydrolyzed cartilage extracts.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
