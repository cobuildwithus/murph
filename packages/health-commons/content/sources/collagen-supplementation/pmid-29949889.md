This source is included for **skin-aging**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study.
- **People studied or addressed:** Women aged 40–60 years with photoaged skin; 53 completed the trial.
- **Intervention/exposure:** 1 g/day low-molecular-weight collagen peptide from sutchi catfish skin; product contained tripeptides including Gly-Pro-Hyp.
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo; accessible secondary summaries indicate the placebo background may have included vitamin C, so coingredient handling should be checked before final protocol wording.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 12 weeks with assessments at 6 and 12 weeks.
- **Role in Murph:** direct protocol evidence for Hydrolyzed Collagen Peptides.

**Findings:** Skin hydration was reported as significantly higher at 6 and 12 weeks. Several wrinkle and elasticity parameters improved over the 12-week trial. Accessible summaries report no adverse symptoms.

**Adverse events or safety:** No adverse symptoms were reported in accessible summaries.

**Why it matters:** Open-access core low-molecular-weight collagen-peptide RCT with a practical 1 g/day dose.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- skin hydration
- skin wrinkling
- skin elasticity
- skin barrier/biophysical measures

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports hydration, elasticity, and wrinkle endpoints for low-molecular-weight collagen peptide over 6–12 weeks, while preserving the coingredient-check caveat.

**Limitations and population fit:**

- Completed sample was smaller than randomized sample.
- Potential vitamin C background in both arms should be checked before using as a clean collagen-only claim.
- Female photoaged-skin population may not generalize to young or low-symptom users.

**Population mismatch:** Women aged 40–60 years with photoaged skin; 53 completed the trial may not match all Murph users; do not generalize to different ages, sexes, baseline skin status, or collagen product types without caveats.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
