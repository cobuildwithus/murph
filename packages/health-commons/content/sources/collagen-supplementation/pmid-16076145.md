This source is included for **mechanism-bioavailability**.

## Findings

After ingestion of gelatin hydrolysates from porcine skin, chicken feet, or cartilage, peptide-form hydroxyproline rose and major circulating peptides included Pro-Hyp with smaller amounts of other hydroxyproline-containing peptides.

## Why it matters

It underpins the mechanism that oral collagen hydrolysates can yield circulating peptide species rather than only free amino acids.

## Potential experiment signals

- Pro-Hyp plasma exposure
- peptide-form hydroxyproline
- time-to-peak peptide exposure

## Protocol takeaway

Use as mechanism/bioavailability context, not outcome evidence.

## Safety and adverse events

No adverse-event signal extracted.

## Limitations and mismatch

- Sample size not verified in accessible abstract.
- Gelatin hydrolysate rather than a defined hydrolyzed collagen peptide protocol.
- No clinical endpoint.
- Population mismatch: Healthy fasting volunteers in an acute PK study.
