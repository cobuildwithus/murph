This source is included for **exercise-performance-recovery**.

**Findings:** Most performance parameters were unaffected by collagen peptides; pooled strength performance was not significantly improved. Performance claims should be cautious and should not promise strength gains from HCP alone.

**Why it matters:** It is an important null/mixed review that prevents overclaiming exercise-performance benefits.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle strength, exercise performance, recovery.

**Protocol takeaway:** For performance, HCP should be framed as uncertain or mostly null unless primary trials and training contexts justify a narrow claim.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction details

- **Source type:** Systematic review and meta-analysis (2024).
- **People studied or addressed:** Healthy adults in collagen-peptide plus exercise or performance studies.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Collagen peptide supplementation, generally in conjunction with training/exercise exposure.
- **Comparator or control:** Placebo or control supplementation.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Varied across included performance trials.
- **Most relevant endpoints:** muscle strength, exercise performance, recovery.

## Limitations and mismatch

Performance outcomes, exercise protocols, and supplementation regimens varied; the synthesis concerns performance rather than skin or OA endpoints. Population mismatch: Healthy/training populations, not clinical OA or skin-aging populations.
