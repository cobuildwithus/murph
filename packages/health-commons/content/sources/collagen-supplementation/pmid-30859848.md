This source is included for **adjacent-variant-context**.

## Findings

Equivalent supplement doses increased circulating amino acids similarly; N-terminal peptide of procollagen tended to increase about 20% from baseline with gelatin and hydrolyzed collagen, but variability prevented significance.

## Why it matters

It helps separate acute collagen-synthesis marker hypotheses from durable outcome claims.

## Potential experiment signals

- serum amino acids
- procollagen marker response
- timing before loading

## Protocol takeaway

Context-only mechanism evidence; do not promote to direct outcome claims for hydrolyzed collagen peptides alone.

## Safety and adverse events

No adverse-event signal extracted from accessible abstract text.

## Limitations and mismatch

- Very small sample.
- Acute biomarker design.
- Vitamin C enrichment and exercise loading are co-interventions.
- No clinical tendon, joint, skin, or bone outcome.
- Population mismatch: Healthy/recreationally active male volunteers; not a target patient population for protocol outcomes.

## Claim use

`context-only`. This source should remain in its assigned evidence bucket and should not be promoted into direct Hydrolyzed Collagen Peptides protocol synthesis unless a later synthesis step explicitly resolves directness and endpoint scope.
