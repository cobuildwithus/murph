This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:**

- **dose — Dose and duration:** Park 2025 supports a standardized 3 g/day LMCP early-knee-OA dose. Effect/direction: 3000 mg/day LMCP for 180 days.
- **mixed_result — WOMAC pain/function/total and VAS pain:** LMCP improved WOMAC symptom/function scales but not all pain endpoints. Effect/direction: WOMAC pain, physical function, and total favored LMCP; VAS pain did not clearly differ between groups.
- **null_result — Joint-space width, ESR, hs-CRP:** Do not claim disease modification or anti-inflammatory mechanism from this trial. Effect/direction: No clear intergroup differences in structural or inflammatory markers in accessible summaries.
- **safety — Adverse events:** Safety signal is favorable in early knee OA over 180 days. Effect/direction: No serious adverse events; one urticaria event was considered unrelated.

**Why it matters:** Newest direct knee-OA RCT in the batch with explicit dose, molecular standardization, safety, and mixed endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** WOMAC pain, WOMAC stiffness, WOMAC physical function, WOMAC total, VAS pain, joint-space width, ESR, hs-CRP, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** 3 g/day LMCP may improve WOMAC symptom scores in early knee OA, but not all pain scales or markers respond.

**Murph takeaway:** Use WOMAC-like symptom/function tracking and avoid interpreting supplement response as cartilage repair.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness and boundary:** `direct_protocol`. Direct early knee-OA population.

**Safety notes:** No serious adverse events were reported in accessible summaries; one urticaria adverse event was considered unrelated.

**Limitations:**

- Per-protocol analysis can inflate apparent treatment effects.
- Absolute WOMAC pain change was modest and VAS pain/structural/inflammatory endpoints were null.
- Early-stage OA population only.
