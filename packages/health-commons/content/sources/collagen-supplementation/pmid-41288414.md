This source is included for **metabolic-cardiovascular-adjacent**.

## Findings

No significant effects on office SBP/DBP, 24 h SBP/DBP, mean arterial pressure, pulse pressure, nocturnal dipping, retinal microvascular calibers, serum lipids, or endothelial/inflammatory markers; movement reaction time increased by 29 ms and was interpreted cautiously.

## Why it matters

It prevents blood-pressure/cardiometabolic benefit claims where recent direct-exposure evidence found no clear advantage.

## Protocol takeaway

Context-only null cardiovascular/metabolic boundary.

## Safety and adverse events

No safety signal extracted beyond null cardiometabolic markers; cognitive reaction-time change likely chance/test-retest per article interpretation.

## Limitations and mismatch

- Short 4-week duration.
- Overweight/obesity population.
- 25% missing ambulatory BP data reported.
- No vascular function measurements.
- Higher-risk populations uncertain.
- Population mismatch: Overweight/obesity cardiometabolic population rather than core HCP outcome population.
