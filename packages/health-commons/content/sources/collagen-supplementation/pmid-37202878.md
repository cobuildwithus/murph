This source is included for **exercise-performance-recovery**.

**Findings:** Only whey increased myofibrillar protein synthesis versus placebo (WHEY 0.041±0.010, COLL 0.036±0.010, PLA 0.032±0.007%/h); muscle connective protein synthesis did not significantly differ between groups (WHEY 0.072±0.019, COLL 0.068±0.017, PLA 0.058±0.018%/h; P=0.09).

**Why it matters:** Negative mechanistic boundary source for connective-tissue synthesis claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** MyoPS, muscle connective protein synthesis, plasma amino acids.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not claim post-exercise collagen acutely increases muscle connective protein synthesis based on this trial.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness and boundary:** `safety_boundary`. Does not test long-term collagen plus training outcomes.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event signal extracted from accessible abstract text.

**Limitations:** Acute mechanistic endpoint only.; Collagen protein rather than necessarily the same HCP products as training trials.; No patient-centered outcomes.

**Population mismatch:** Young recreational athletes; safety/mechanistic boundary only.
