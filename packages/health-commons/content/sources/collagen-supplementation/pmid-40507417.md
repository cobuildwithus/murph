This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:**

- **dose — Dose and duration:** Demir-Dora 2025 supports 10 g/day CollaSel PRO in OA. Effect/direction: 10 g/day type I/III hydrolyzed collagen peptides for 8 weeks.
- **outcome — WOMAC and AOFAS-AHFS:** CollaSel PRO improved pain/stiffness/function scores versus placebo. Effect/direction: WOMAC fell from 53.7 ± 16.9 to 33.8 ± 16.5 by week 8; between-group WOMAC/AOFAS differences favored collagen at weeks 4 and 8 (p<0.001).
- **adverse_event — Adverse events:** Monitor GI symptoms and verify group attribution in QA. Effect/direction: 46 AEs in 24 patients were reported in accessible excerpts; nausea was most common (19.5% of AEs) and most AEs were mild.
- **limitation — Joint pooling and duration:** Joint-specific and longer-term effects remain uncertain. Effect/direction: Knee and hip WOMAC scores were analyzed together and follow-up was 8 weeks.

**Why it matters:** Recent open-access OA RCT with explicit efficacy and safety extraction value.

**Potential experiment signals:** WOMAC for knee/hip joints, AOFAS-AHFS for ankle joints, pain, stiffness, physical function, safety.

**Protocol takeaway:** 10 g/day type I/III hydrolyzed collagen peptides may improve OA pain/stiffness/function over 8 weeks in a product-specific trial.

**Murph takeaway:** Track pain, stiffness, and function separately; monitor GI symptoms such as nausea.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness and boundary:** `direct_protocol`. Direct OA population, but includes multiple joint sites rather than knee alone.

**Safety notes:** Accessible full-text excerpts report 46 adverse events in 24 patients, most commonly nausea (19.5% of AEs); most were mild, and the article concludes the product was safe/effective. Detailed group attribution should be verified during QA.

**Limitations:**

- Knee and hip WOMAC scores were analyzed together, limiting joint-specific interpretation.
- Short 8-week follow-up.
- Product-specific type I/III hydrolyzed collagen peptides.
