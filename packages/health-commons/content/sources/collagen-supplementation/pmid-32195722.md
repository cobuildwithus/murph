This source is included for **clinical-supervised-wound-nutrition**.

**Findings:**

- Hospitalized adults aged 65+ were randomized to an oral nutrition supplement containing 10 g collagen peptides once daily or control and followed at 2, 4, 6, and 8 weeks. `[source_artifact:pmid-32195722]`
- The intervention group showed increased stratum corneum hydration by week 8 and significant elasticity changes from baseline at weeks 6 and 8 in the accessible abstract. `[source_artifact:pmid-32195722]`
- Safety details were not extracted from the accessible abstract; the article reported industry support and author honoraria. `[source_artifact:pmid-32195722]`

**Why it matters:** Preserves a skin-hydration/elasticity signal in a clinical nutrition population while preventing overgeneralization to healthy users. `[source_artifact:pmid-32195722]`

**Potential experiment signals:**

  - "stratum corneum hydration"
  - "skin elasticity"
  - "adherence"
  - "inpatient nutritional risk"

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only: do not use as direct cosmetic/self-run protocol evidence because the supplement and population are clinical nutrition boundary conditions. `[source_artifact:pmid-32195722]`

**Population mismatch:** Hospitalized adults aged 65 years or older in a frail/inpatient clinical nutrition context.

**Limitations:**

- Small open-label inpatient trial.
- Multi-nutrient oral nutrition supplement matrix.
- Older hospitalized population is a population mismatch for general self-run supplementation.
- Industry support and honoraria were reported.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
