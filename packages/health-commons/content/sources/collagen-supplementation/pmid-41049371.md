This source is included for **bone-density-turnover**.

**Findings:** Collagen peptides were associated with favorable pooled bone-density, bone-turnover, and muscle-performance directions, but the review blends clean HCP evidence with adjunct and heterogeneous contexts. Use as a high-recall backbone source for bone/muscle trial identification, then verify each primary trial before writing protocol claims.

**Why it matters:** It is the newest bone/muscle-focused synthesis in the ledger and can identify primary trials for BMD, bone turnover, and muscle endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** bone mineral density, bone turnover, muscle performance, lean mass.

**Protocol takeaway:** Promising for bone-density and muscle endpoints, but protocol claims need primary-trial separation by co-intervention, population, and endpoint.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction details

- **Source type:** Systematic review and meta-analysis (2025).
- **People studied or addressed:** Adults in bone-health, muscle-health, postmenopausal, osteopenia/osteoporosis, and adjacent exercise contexts across included studies.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Collagen peptide supplementation; some included arms combined collagen peptides with calcium, vitamin D, or training context.
- **Comparator or control:** Placebo, control, or background co-interventions depending on included study.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Varied across included studies; source reports pooled bone, turnover, and muscle outcomes rather than one uniform follow-up.
- **Most relevant endpoints:** bone mineral density, bone turnover, muscle performance, lean mass.

## Safety notes

Safety details were extracted only where the accessible source text or abstract-level record included them. Otherwise, this page intentionally leaves adverse-event counts unresolved rather than inventing them.

## Limitations and mismatch

No fracture endpoint was extracted; heterogeneity and co-interventions mean pooled findings should not be treated as HCP-alone proof. Population mismatch: Some included evidence uses osteopenia/osteoporosis, postmenopausal, exercise-training, or calcium/vitamin D adjunct contexts rather than unsupervised HCP-alone use.
