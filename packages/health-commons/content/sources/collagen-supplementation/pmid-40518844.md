This source is included for **skin-aging**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled clinical trial plus in vitro fibroblast experiments.
- **People studied or addressed:** Women aged 45–60 years.
- **Intervention/exposure:** 2.5 g/day oral collagen oligopeptides.
- **Comparator/control:** Maltodextrin placebo.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 84 days with assessments at days 28, 56, and 84.
- **Role in Murph:** direct protocol evidence for Hydrolyzed Collagen Peptides.

**Findings:** Firmness improved versus placebo at days 28, 56, and 84 in accessible full-text summaries. Elasticity improved versus placebo at all assessed time points, with 100% of treated participants improved at day 84 in accessible reports. Hydration increased within both groups but did not show a significant between-group difference. In vitro molecular findings supported collagen/ECM mechanisms but are not direct clinical outcomes.

**Adverse events or safety:** Adverse-event details were not extracted from accessible materials.

**Why it matters:** Recent collagen-oligopeptide source with both clinical firmness/elasticity outcomes and molecular context.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- skin firmness
- skin elasticity
- skin hydration
- type I procollagen, decorin, biglycan, versican
- type I collagen synthesis
- TIMP-1
- hyaluronic acid
- MMP-1 after UV challenge

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports firmness and elasticity outcomes while explicitly preserving the null between-group hydration result and mechanism/clinical boundary.

**Limitations and population fit:**

- Hydration was a null between-group result despite firmness/elasticity benefits.
- In vitro molecular effects should be separated from direct clinical evidence.
- Women aged 45–60 only.
- Open-access licensing should be checked before storing PDFs.

**Population mismatch:** Women aged 45–60 years may not match all Murph users; do not generalize to different ages, sexes, baseline skin status, or collagen product types without caveats.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
