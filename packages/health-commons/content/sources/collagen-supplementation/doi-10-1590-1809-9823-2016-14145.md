This source is included for **bone-density-turnover**.

**Findings:** The review concludes collagen hydrolysate may have a complementary role in osteoporosis/OA, but its evidence base is old, heterogeneous, and partly preclinical. Use for historical source recall and mechanism boundaries, not as a primary efficacy citation for this protocol.

**Why it matters:** It helps map historical osteoporosis/OA rationale and separates mechanistic/preclinical claims from direct protocol claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** bone mineral density, joint pain, joint function, cartilage health.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context only: do not use this review to claim HCP efficacy without extracting the relevant human primary trials.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Source type:** Systematic review (2016).
- **People studied or addressed:** Human, animal, and in vitro osteoporosis/osteoarthritis studies published from 1994 to 2014.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Collagen hydrolysate or collagen supplementation; some reviewed evidence was animal or mechanistic.
- **Comparator or control:** Placebo, untreated, or disease-model comparators depending on included study.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Varied across the included human and preclinical studies.
- **Most relevant endpoints:** bone mineral density, joint pain, joint function, cartilage health.
