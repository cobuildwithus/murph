This source is included for **mechanism-bioavailability**.

## Findings

Each 10 g collagen hydrolysate elevated free hydroxyproline 6.2- to 9.9-fold; total hydroxyproline iAUC exceeded free hydroxyproline iAUC, indicating peptide-bound uptake; porcine and lower-molecular-weight bovine products showed higher total hydroxyproline iAUC than comparator products in the accessible report.

## Why it matters

It refines exposure language: collagen hydrolysate products differ in peptide-bound hydroxyproline exposure after equal-dose intake.

## Potential experiment signals

- free hydroxyproline
- total hydroxyproline iAUC
- peptide-bound hydroxyproline
- Pro-Hyp

## Protocol takeaway

Use for mechanism/bioavailability and product-difference context only.

## Safety and adverse events

All products were reported as well tolerated and no adverse events occurred during study days.

## Limitations and mismatch

- n=6.
- Acute PK design.
- Healthy volunteers only.
- No efficacy endpoints.
- Population mismatch: Healthy acute PK volunteers rather than a protocol outcome population.
