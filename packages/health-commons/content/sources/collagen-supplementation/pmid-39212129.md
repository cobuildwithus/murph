This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:** Updated RCT meta-analysis found collagen favored knee-OA pain and function, but heterogeneity was high. Use as direct OA evidence while naming heterogeneity and formulation boundaries.

**Why it matters:** It updates the older OA meta-analysis with more RCTs and current pain/function estimates.

**Potential experiment signals:** joint pain, joint function, joint stiffness, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports cautious knee-OA symptom language with high heterogeneity caveat.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction details

- **Source type:** Systematic review and meta-analysis (2025).
- **People studied or addressed:** Adults with knee osteoarthritis in randomized controlled trials.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Collagen supplementation, including collagen-based supplements across included trials.
- **Comparator or control:** Placebo/control.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Varied across included randomized trials.
- **Most relevant endpoints:** joint pain, joint function, joint stiffness, adverse events.
