This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:**

- **dose — Dose and duration:** Mohammed 2021 tests a lower-dose hydrolyzed type-II collagen product. Effect/direction: 2.5 g/day hydrolyzed chicken collagen type II for 8 weeks.
- **mixed_result — Overall WOMAC, stiffness, physical activity, pain/VAS:** HCII improved stiffness/function more consistently than pain. Effect/direction: Overall WOMAC, stiffness, and physical activity favored HCII; week-4 overall WOMAC reduction 36.9% vs 14.3%, p=0.027. Pain-domain/VAS signals were less consistently significant.
- **safety — Adverse events:** Safety was generally reassuring but withdrawals/AEs need detailed review. Effect/direction: No severe or life-threatening adverse events in accessible summaries; 11 mild-to-moderate AEs/withdrawals were described, attribution requiring QA.
- **population — Population mismatch:** Use as joint-discomfort evidence with OA caveat. Effect/direction: General joint discomfort in otherwise healthy adults, not diagnosed OA.

**Why it matters:** Open-access RCT directly tests a hydrolyzed collagen type II supplement for joint discomfort and safety.

**Potential experiment signals:** WOMAC overall plus wrist questions, WOMAC pain, WOMAC stiffness, WOMAC physical activity, VAS pain, mobility.

**Protocol takeaway:** 2.5 g/day hydrolyzed chicken type II collagen may improve stiffness/function over 8 weeks, but OA and pain claims need caveats.

**Murph takeaway:** Track stiffness/function separately from pain, because the pain signal is less uniform.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness and boundary:** `direct_protocol`. Joint-discomfort population without required OA diagnosis.

**Safety notes:** No severe or life-threatening adverse events were reported in accessible summaries; 11 mild-to-moderate adverse events/withdrawals were described, but detailed attribution requires full extraction.

**Limitations:**

- Population had general joint discomfort rather than diagnosed OA.
- Pain-domain evidence appears less consistent than stiffness/function evidence.
- Hydrolyzed chicken type II product is a specific collagen variant.
