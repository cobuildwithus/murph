This source is included for **skin-aging**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled clinical study with washout/sustained-effect period.
- **People studied or addressed:** Healthy female participants.
- **Intervention/exposure:** 5 g/day bioactive collagen peptides dissolved in water.
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 12 weeks supplementation plus 4-week washout/sustained-effect observation.
- **Role in Murph:** direct protocol evidence for Hydrolyzed Collagen Peptides.

**Findings:** Dermal density, hydration, and TEWL improved versus placebo at 12 weeks in accessible summaries. Dermal thickness increased and improvements were reported to persist through washout. No significant epidermis changes were reported in accessible summaries.

**Adverse events or safety:** Adverse-event details were not extracted from accessible summaries.

**Why it matters:** Direct RCT with sustained-effect/washout period and dermal imaging/structure endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- skin hydration
- transepidermal water loss
- skin elasticity
- dermal density
- dermal thickness
- epidermal changes
- washout durability

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports 12-week hydration/TEWL/dermal-density signals and short-term post-discontinuation persistence, while preserving null epidermal findings.

**Limitations and population fit:**

- Healthy female participants only.
- Washout duration was 4 weeks; longer durability remains unknown.
- Epidermal measures were null despite dermal/hydration signals.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy female participants may not match all Murph users; do not generalize to different ages, sexes, baseline skin status, or collagen product types without caveats.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
