This source is included for **safety-quality-contaminants**.

**Findings:** Population/exposure: 22 commercially available whey, vegan, and beef protein powders on the Hungarian market Intervention or exposure: Protein powder products. Comparator/control: Regulatory limits and detection thresholds. Duration/follow-up: Single market sampling/testing period Endpoints: heavy metals, arsenic, cadmium, mercury, lead, ICP-MS detection. Direction/effect: The tested powders did not show significant heavy-metal content above regulatory limits; several toxic elements were undetectable or below quantification in all powders. Safety notes: No clinical adverse events; product-quality snapshot only. Limitations: Protein powders broadly, not collagen-specific.; Small market sample from Hungary.; Snapshot cannot guarantee ongoing lot quality or grey-market products.. Population mismatch: Products rather than human users; not collagen-specific.

**Why it matters:** Provides a counterbalancing contaminant source showing that some protein-powder market samples may test reassuringly.
