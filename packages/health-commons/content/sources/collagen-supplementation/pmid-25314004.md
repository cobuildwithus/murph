This source is included for **bone-density-turnover**.

**Findings:**

- **Population:** Postmenopausal women with osteopenia.
- **Intervention/exposure:** 5 g calcium-collagen chelate containing 500 mg elemental calcium and 200 IU vitamin D daily.
- **Comparator/control:** 500 mg calcium plus 200 IU vitamin D daily.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 12 months; BMD at baseline, 6, and 12 months.
- **Endpoints:** total body BMD, lumbar BMD, hip BMD, sclerostin, TRAP5b, BAP/TRAP5b ratio.
- **Effect/direction:** Accessible abstract reports lower whole-body BMD loss in calcium-collagen chelate versus control at 12 months; sclerostin and TRAP5b were lower and BAP/TRAP5b ratio higher at 6 months.
- **Adverse events/safety:** Adverse events not extracted from accessible abstract.
- **Limitations:** Adjacent calcium-collagen chelate formulation, not HCP alone.; Small trial; supplement contains calcium/vitamin D.; Whole-body BMD loss was attenuated but site-specific and clinical fracture outcomes require caution.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Population matches osteopenia, but intervention does not match HCP-alone protocol.

**Why it matters:** It demonstrates that collagen-containing mineral complexes have separate evidence that should not be conflated with HCP.

**Potential experiment signals:** whole-body BMD, sclerostin, TRAP5b, BAP/TRAP5b ratio.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only adjacent formulation evidence; not proof for generic hydrolyzed collagen peptides.
