This source is included for **safety-quality-contaminants**.

**Findings:** Population/exposure: 120 samples from fish- and jellyfish-origin collagen supplement brands/batches; no human participants Intervention or exposure: Commercial collagen supplements of fish or jellyfish origin. Comparator/control: EU limits, tolerable daily intakes, species/brand comparisons. Duration/follow-up: Products purchased in 2022/2023; modeled daily intake at 5 or 10 g/day Endpoints: arsenic, lead, cadmium, chromium, mercury, acceptable daily intake, tolerable daily intake. Direction/effect: Arsenic predominated; mercury was detected only in a small subset; none of the tested samples exceeded applicable EU limits, and modeled average daily doses were below tolerable daily intakes under the authors' assumptions. Safety notes: No clinical adverse events; direct product-quality contaminant testing identified variability and the need for monitoring. Limitations: Marine collagen products only; not bovine or porcine HCP.; Risk assessment considered metals with available reference values and did not resolve cumulative multi-supplement exposure.; Labels often lacked species detail.. Population mismatch: Product samples and modeled intake rather than measured human exposure.

**Why it matters:** Direct collagen-specific contaminant evidence for fish/jellyfish products, useful for product-quality boundaries.
