This source is included for **skin-aging**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled clinical trial plus in vitro primary fibroblast experiments.
- **People studied or addressed:** Women in a skin-health clinical trial.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Tuna collagen peptides from tuna species, low molecular weight and enriched in di- and tripeptides; dose not confirmed from accessible abstract-level sources.
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 8 weeks supplementation plus 2-week follow-up in accessible summaries.
- **Role in Murph:** direct protocol evidence for Hydrolyzed Collagen Peptides.

**Findings:** Clinical summaries report increased skin hydration, elasticity, and density and decreased TEWL. In vitro fibroblast work showed dose-dependent collagen/elastin synthesis signals and reduced senescence markers. The in vitro results should be treated as mechanism/context, not as human efficacy evidence.

**Adverse events or safety:** Adverse-event details were not extracted from accessible materials.

**Why it matters:** Direct marine/tuna collagen peptide RCT plus mechanistic fibroblast data for hydration, TEWL, elasticity, and density.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- skin hydration
- transepidermal water loss
- skin elasticity
- skin density
- fibroblast collagen synthesis
- fibroblast elastin synthesis
- senescence markers

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports clinical hydration/TEWL/elasticity/density signals while keeping fibroblast mechanisms in a separate mechanism bucket.

**Limitations and population fit:**

- Dose was not confirmed from accessible abstract-level materials.
- Participant count comes from accessible secondary material rather than the PubMed abstract.
- Mechanistic in vitro results must be separated from the RCT evidence.
- Tuna-derived peptide preparation may not generalize to all products.

**Population mismatch:** Women in a skin-health clinical trial may not match all Murph users; do not generalize to different ages, sexes, baseline skin status, or collagen product types without caveats.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
