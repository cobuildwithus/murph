This source is included for **skin-aging**.

**Findings:** Overall skin-aging benefits were positive, but non-pharmaceutical-funded trials did not show effects, creating a major funding-quality caveat. Use this as a key mixed/critical source for skin claims and bias language.

**Why it matters:** It is a recent critical review that tempers the otherwise positive skin-aging literature.

**Potential experiment signals:** skin hydration, skin elasticity, wrinkles, funding bias.

**Protocol takeaway:** Skin claims should mention that benefits are less convincing in non-pharmaceutical-funded trials.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

- **People studied or addressed:** Participants in randomized controlled trials of collagen supplements for skin aging.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Collagen supplements, including hydrolyzed collagen/collagen-based products across trials.
- **Comparator or control:** Placebo/control.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Varied across RCTs.
