This source is included for **bone-density-turnover**.

**Findings:**

- **Population:** Middle-aged women not using estrogen; healthy normal-BMD controls and osteopenic women by WHO criteria.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Calcium 1000 mg plus vitamin D 250 IU daily plus vitamins C and B6 and proline (CB6Pro) to support collagen matrix.
- **Comparator/control:** Calcium/vitamin D in osteopenic controls and placebo healthy normal-BMD controls.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 1 year.
- **Endpoints:** cortical BMD, lumbar/trabecular BMD, bone formation markers, bone resorption markers, PTH/1,25(OH)2D3-induced osteoclastic activity.
- **Effect/direction:** Accessible abstract reports no further bone loss in the osteopenic Ca/D+CB6Pro group, while trabecular bone loss persisted in Ca/D-only osteopenic group.
- **Adverse events/safety:** Adverse events not extracted from accessible abstract.
- **Limitations:** Not a collagen peptide intervention; uses micronutrients/proline to support collagen matrix.; Small groups and multiple endpoints.; Comparator includes healthy normal-BMD placebo group plus osteopenic Ca/D group.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Adjacent calcium/vitamin D/collagen-matrix nutrient boundary; not HCP.

**Why it matters:** It explains why calcium/vitamin D comparators and collagen-matrix cofactors must be kept separate from HCP.

**Potential experiment signals:** lumbar BMD, cortical BMD, bone formation markers, resorption markers, PTH/vitamin D hormones.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context only for cofactor/calcium-D boundaries; do not use as collagen peptide evidence.
