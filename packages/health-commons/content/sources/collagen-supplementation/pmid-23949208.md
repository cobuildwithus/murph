This source is included for **skin-aging**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Double-blind, placebo-controlled dose-ranging trial.
- **People studied or addressed:** Women aged 35–55 years.
- **Intervention/exposure:** 2.5 g/day or 5.0 g/day specific collagen peptides/collagen hydrolysate.
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 8 weeks supplementation plus 4-week post-treatment follow-up for elasticity.
- **Role in Murph:** direct protocol evidence for Hydrolyzed Collagen Peptides.

**Findings:** Skin elasticity improved significantly versus placebo at weeks 4 and 8 in both collagen-dose groups. Elasticity improvement persisted through the 4-week post-treatment follow-up and was more pronounced in women older than 50 years. Moisture/hydration increased in older participants but was not statistically significant overall; TEWL and roughness did not show significant between-group differences.

**Adverse events or safety:** No side effects were reported in the accessible study summary.

**Why it matters:** Foundational dose-ranging skin-physiology RCT for oral specific collagen peptides.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- skin elasticity
- skin moisture/hydration
- transepidermal water loss
- skin roughness

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports elasticity as a plausible direct skin signal after about 4–8 weeks, but does not support a universal hydration, TEWL, or roughness improvement.

**Limitations and population fit:**

- Paywalled primary article limits independent extraction of full tables.
- Women aged 35–55 only.
- Product-specific collagen peptide preparation.
- Hydration, TEWL, and roughness were mixed or null despite elasticity benefit.

**Population mismatch:** Women aged 35–55 years may not match all Murph users; do not generalize to different ages, sexes, baseline skin status, or collagen product types without caveats.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
