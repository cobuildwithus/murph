This source is included for **skin-aging**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial.
- **People studied or addressed:** Healthy East Asian women aged 43–65 years using standardized skin care.
- **Intervention/exposure:** 5 g/day collagen peptides, described in accessible summaries as porcine-skin Peptan P.
- **Comparator/control:** 5 g/day maltodextrin placebo.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 84 days with day 28 and day 84 assessments.
- **Role in Murph:** direct protocol evidence for Hydrolyzed Collagen Peptides.

**Findings:** Dermis density and skin moisture improved significantly versus placebo after 84 days in accessible summaries. Elasticity, wrinkle visibility, nail color, and beauty perception showed earlier changes in the collagen group than placebo in accessible summaries. Nail outcomes are adjacent to the skin-aging protocol and should not be merged into skin claims.

**Adverse events or safety:** Adverse-event details were not extracted from accessible materials.

**Why it matters:** Recent direct RCT in an East Asian population with dermis density, moisture, elasticity, wrinkle-visibility, and nail-health outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- dermis density
- skin moisture
- skin elasticity
- wrinkle visibility
- nail color/brightness
- beauty perception

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports dermis density and skin moisture over 84 days, with nail outcomes and population fit handled as boundaries.

**Limitations and population fit:**

- East Asian women only; standardized skin-care context may reduce generalizability.
- Nail outcomes are adjacent protocol evidence.
- Full AE/tolerability tables were not extracted.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy East Asian women aged 43–65 years using standardized skin care may not match all Murph users; do not generalize to different ages, sexes, baseline skin status, or collagen product types without caveats.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
