This source is included for **tendon-loading-ligament**.

**Findings:** Additional supplementation with physiotherapy improved pain in pooled analysis but did not show a clear functional benefit. Any tendon claim must separate supplement, physiotherapy, and loading effects.

**Why it matters:** It shows how adjunct supplement claims can be entangled with physiotherapy outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:** tendinopathy pain, function, physiotherapy adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context only; do not cite as HCP efficacy without supplement-specific primary extraction.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Source type:** Systematic review and meta-analysis (2022).
- **People studied or addressed:** People with tendinopathy receiving physiotherapy with or without additional dietary supplementation.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Dietary supplementation adjuncts during physiotherapy; supplements varied and were not HCP-only.
- **Comparator or control:** Physiotherapy with placebo or no added supplement.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Varied across included studies.
- **Most relevant endpoints:** tendinopathy pain, function, physiotherapy adherence.

## Potential experiment signals

- tendinopathy pain
- function
- physiotherapy adherence

## Safety notes

Safety details were extracted only where the accessible source text or abstract-level record included them. Otherwise, this page intentionally leaves adverse-event counts unresolved rather than inventing them.

## Limitations and mismatch

Different supplements were pooled; not hydrolyzed collagen alone. Population mismatch: Clinical tendinopathy plus physiotherapy; mixed supplements.
