This source is included for **joint-osteoarthritis**.

**Findings:** Eighty moderately active adults with chronic/non-pathological knee pain completed a randomized controlled trial of a hydrolyzed-collagen-containing food supplement versus placebo for roughly 40 days. Accessible summaries reported morning/post-exercise pain and KOOS/WOMAC/SF-36 signals favoring active supplement. Diagnosed OA was excluded and other active ingredients were present.

**Why it matters:** Recent knee-pain source that overlaps user-facing joint claims but is not direct OA/HCP evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** morning pain, post-exercise pain, joint range, KOOS symptoms, WOMAC function, SF-36 QoL

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only; do not promote to hydrolyzed collagen peptide OA claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
