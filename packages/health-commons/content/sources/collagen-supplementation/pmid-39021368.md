This source is included for **skin-aging**.

**Findings:** Daily branded collagen-plus-vitamin-C supplement improved several skin measures; hair count/scalp endpoints were more mixed. Endpoints captured: confocal dermal collagen fragmentation; ultrasound LEP ratio; skin hydration; R2 elasticity; Rz wrinkles/profilometry; scalp scaling; hair count; hair appearance.

**Why it matters:** The source is recent and endpoint-rich but not a clean HCP-only trial.

**Potential experiment signals:** collagen fragmentation, skin hydration, R2 elasticity, Rz wrinkles, scalp scaling, hair count, hair appearance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context for branded collagen-plus-vitamin-C products; do not promote to direct collagen-only claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
