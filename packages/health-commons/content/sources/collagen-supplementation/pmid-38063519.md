**Findings:**

- **mixed_result — VAS activity pain, ROM, strength, KOOS:** Collagen-alone contribution is unclear in this quasi-randomized exercise-context trial. Effect/direction: VAS activity pain improved in all groups without between-group difference; exercise and combined arms improved strength/KOOS in accessible summaries.
- **limitation — Design and co-intervention:** Use as context-only and confounder evidence. Effect/direction: Quasi-randomization, no placebo supplement arm, and exercise co-intervention limit causal inference for collagen alone.
- **dose — Dose uncertainty:** Do not cite this study for protocol dose. Effect/direction: Collagen supplement dose was not extractable from accessible snippets.
- **population — Population context:** Population is relevant to early joint disease but supervised exercise context dominates. Effect/direction: Recreational athletes with early knee arthritis.
