- **Population:** Chinese college students.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Bedtime procrastination latent profiles and predictors including anxiety, depression, problematic smartphone use, and self-control.
- **Comparator/control:** Latent bedtime-procrastination profile classes and transition probabilities.

- **Endpoints:** bedtime procrastination classes, transitions, anxiety, depression, problematic smartphone use, self-control
- **Effect or direction:** Three bedtime-procrastination subgroups were identified; moderate and severe groups were relatively stable, and mild procrastination had a notable probability of moving to moderate. Predictors included anxiety, depression, problematic smartphone use, and self-control.
