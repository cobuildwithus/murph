## Extraction summary

- **Population:** New Zealand adolescents aged 13–17 years.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Screen use in the hour before bedtime and while in bed; attitudes and barriers to reduction.
- **Comparator/control:** Not applicable; descriptive survey of habits and acceptability.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Cross-sectional online survey.
- **Endpoints:** prebedtime screen activities, in-bed phone use, barriers to reducing screen time, intervention acceptability
- **Effect or direction:** Most adolescents reported social media (88%) and texting/instant messaging (77%) before bedtime; 86% reported using phones in bed; inability to communicate with friends was a common barrier (67%).
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse events; highlights adherence and social-connection burden of screen curfews.
