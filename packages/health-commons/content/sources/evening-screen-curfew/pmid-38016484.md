This source is included for **supplemental sleep-hygiene, insomnia, and broad guidance context** for the Digital Sunset protocol.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Adults over age 18 with chronic insomnia; literature on children and adolescents was not reviewed.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Guideline recommendations for diagnosis and treatment, including CBT-I in-person or digitally, pharmacologic options if CBT-I is ineffective, and adjunct therapies.
- **Comparator or control:** Guideline synthesis; no digital-sunset comparator.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Literature update from June 2016 through October 2022 plus further update through May 2023.
- **Endpoints:** insomnia diagnosis, CBT-I, digital CBT-I, medication duration, tolerance/dependence risk, adult-only population
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Recommendation-level direction: CBT-I first-line for adults with insomnia; medication generally limited to short-term use when needed; digital CBT-I should be evidence-based and quality-controlled.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Guideline cautions that drug treatments generally should be limited to at most 4 weeks with care because tolerance can develop; dose increases are not advised and may accelerate dependence.
- **Limitations and population mismatch:** Adult-only insomnia guideline, not a screen-curfew trial; children/adolescents not reviewed; digital CBT-I is distinct from simple device cutoffs.
- **Directness to Digital Sunset:** adjacent_variant
- **Claim-use boundary:** context-only
- **Artifact candidates and rights status:** Treat publisher/Wiley PDF as permission-required unless license is verified for redistribution; metadata/link-only for Git.

## Protocol boundary

Use for current adult-insomnia boundaries, medication caution, and the distinction between evidence-informed digital habits and evidence-based digital CBT-I.
