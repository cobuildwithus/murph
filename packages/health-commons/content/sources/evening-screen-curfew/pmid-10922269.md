This source is included for **light, circadian, and display-mechanism evidence** in the Digital Sunset extraction batch.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** acute physiology (2000).
- **People studied or addressed:** Healthy young adults in controlled laboratory circadian protocols.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Single 6.5-hour nocturnal light exposure at graded intensities during the early biological night.
- **Comparator/control:** Dimmer or brighter light-dose conditions within the dose-response protocol.
- **Role for Digital Sunset:** same_mechanism; context-only. This is not upgraded to direct protocol efficacy unless the source itself directly tested a no-personal-screen-before-bed behavior.

## Extracted source-local finding

Nocturnal light produced dose-response melatonin suppression and phase-delay responses; ordinary room-light-level exposure could produce meaningful physiological responses in this laboratory context.

## Endpoints extracted

plasma melatonin suppression, circadian phase resetting

## Duration or follow-up

9-day laboratory protocol; acute 6.5-hour exposure.
