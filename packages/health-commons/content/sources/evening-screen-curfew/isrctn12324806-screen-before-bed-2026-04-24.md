## Quick read

ISRCTN active RCT registry directly aligned with 30-minute pre-bed and overnight screen restriction in young adults with sleeping problems.

## Extracted intervention or exposure

- **Population / N:** Swedish university/college students or former students aged 20-29 with sleeping problems and iPhone/Android access (N=220 ; count kind: planned; 110 per group).
- **Intervention / exposure:** No mobile phone or screen use for 30 minutes before bedtime and during the night; phone outside bedroom, no other bedroom screens, and silent mode.
- **Comparator / control:** Live as usual for 10 days.
- **Duration / follow-up:** 10-day intervention with 3-, 6-, and 12-month follow-up

## Extracted endpoints and results

- **Endpoints:** Primary sleep time measured with actigraphy, screen-time screenshots, and sleep diaries at baseline and 3/6/12 months; secondary experiences of restriction via interviews/questionnaires.
- **Effect or direction:** Recruiting/ongoing registry; no efficacy results extracted.

## Directness and claim boundary

- **Directness to Digital Sunset:** direct_protocol.
- **Claim use:** context-only.
- **Boundary:** Registry-only, open-label, no results yet, young-adult Swedish sample, and randomization/masking text should be rechecked when results post.

## Safety / adverse events

Registry states no major risks, but time burden and possible withdrawal reactions among high screen users are expected safety/burden considerations.
