## Quick read

Recent three-arm online pilot in new career starters targeting evening screen time and bedtime procrastination; active control also improved.

## Extracted intervention or exposure

- **Population / N:** New career starters with bedtime procrastination (N=55 ; count kind: randomized new career starters; active control n=19, Prevent n=18, Substitute n=18).
- **Intervention / exposure:** REST-O online intervention targeting evening screen time with Prevent and Substitute arms, plus self-monitoring/coaching elements.
- **Comparator / control:** Active-control online condition.
- **Duration / follow-up:** Online intervention with daily assessments over about two weeks and follow-up through week 3

## Extracted endpoints and results

- **Endpoints:** Feasibility/acceptability, daily pre-sleep device use, bedtime procrastination, excessive device use, sleep quality, and sleep duration.
- **Effect or direction:** All groups reduced daily pre-sleep device use by a mean of 23.4 minutes/day and increased sleep duration by 12.7 minutes/day post-intervention. Intervention arms were reported to reduce pre-sleep device use by up to 53%, but the active control also improved.

## Directness and claim boundary

- **Directness to Digital Sunset:** direct_protocol.
- **Claim use:** supports-protocol.
- **Boundary:** Pilot sample, short follow-up, active control improvement makes between-arm efficacy cautious, and the intervention is a behavioral package rather than a simple screen curfew.

## Safety / adverse events

No adverse events were extracted. Daily survey completion was reported as 84.7%, indicating participant burden was material but feasible.
