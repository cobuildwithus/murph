## Quick read

- **Source type:** acute physiology (2020).
- **People studied or addressed:** Healthy adults exposed to evening OLED, LED, and dim-light conditions.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Evening organic light-emitting diode exposure.
- **Comparator/control:** Dim light and LED light-emitting conditions.
- **Role for Digital Sunset:** same_mechanism; context-only. This is not upgraded to direct protocol efficacy unless the source itself directly tested a no-personal-screen-before-bed behavior.

## Extracted source-local finding

Melatonin onset was significantly delayed under LED light compared with dim light, but not significantly delayed under OLED light; melatonin suppression, sleep parameters, and vigilance were similar among conditions in accessible summaries.

## Endpoints extracted

melatonin onset, melatonin suppression, sleep parameters, vigilance
