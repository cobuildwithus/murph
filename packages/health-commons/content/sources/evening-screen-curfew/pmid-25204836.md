- **Source key:** `source_artifact:pmid-25204836`

- **People studied or addressed:** Adolescents aged 12-17 years; accessible abstract reports mean age 14.8 years (SD 1.3) and 44.8% female.

- **Role for Digital Sunset:** adjacent_variant / context-only.

## Extracted facts

- **Intervention or exposure:** Electronic media use in bed before sleep, including TV/movies, video games, mobile talking/texting, and online time; smartphone ownership context.
- **Comparator or control:** Lower/no electronic media use in bed; conventional phone versus smartphone ownership context.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Cross-sectional questionnaire.
- **Endpoints:** sleep duration; sleep difficulties; depressive symptoms; bedtime.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Accessible abstracts report electronic media use in bed was negatively related to sleep duration and positively related to sleep difficulties; sleep disturbance partially mediated the relationship with depressive symptoms. Smartphone ownership was related to later bedtimes but not directly to sleep disturbance or depression in the accessible summary.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Mental-health/depressive-symptom context extracted; no adverse-event monitoring.

## Limitations and population mismatch

- Observational and self-reported.
- Mood mediation context is not a curfew intervention.
- Cannot determine directionality.
