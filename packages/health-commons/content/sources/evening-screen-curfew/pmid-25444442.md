This source is included for **supplemental sleep-hygiene, insomnia, and broad guidance context** for the Digital Sunset protocol.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Adolescents, primarily ages 12-18 years across 41 studies.
- **Intervention or exposure:** No intervention; meta-analyzed exposures/protective factors included technology use, evening light, sleep hygiene, physical activity, caffeine, and family/parent factors.
- **Comparator or control:** Different exposure levels within observational studies; no randomized comparator.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Study publications from 2003 through February 2014; follow-up varied and was not a protocol dose.
- **Endpoints:** bedtime, sleep onset latency, total sleep time, sleep hygiene context, evening technology and light exposure
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Directions reported in the accessible abstract: video gaming, phone, computer and internet use, and evening light related to delayed bedtime; computer use and evening light were among factors associated with decreased total sleep time; good sleep hygiene and parent-set bedtimes related to longer sleep length.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse events extracted; this was not a treatment trial.
- **Limitations and population mismatch:** Mostly association-level evidence; no personal-screen-before-bed curfew intervention; numeric weighted correlations were not extracted from the accessible abstract in this run.
- **Directness to Digital Sunset:** background
- **Claim-use boundary:** context-only
- **Artifact candidates and rights status:** Do not store publisher PDF in Git; metadata/link-only unless redistribution rights are verified.

## Protocol boundary

Context only: cautious technology use and parent-set bedtime rules appear relevant, but this meta-analysis does not test a digital-sunset protocol.
