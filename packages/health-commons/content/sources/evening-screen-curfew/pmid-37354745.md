## Extraction status
Draft extraction for `batch-002` / adjacent behavioral intervention variants. Directness is `same_mechanism` and claim use is `supports-protocol`. This page is not a protocol synthesis.

## Methods snapshot
- **Design:** Open-label randomized controlled trial with wait-list control.
- **Participants:** randomized; treatment n=32, wait-list n=28; Non-clinical young adults with frequent bedtime procrastination; mean age 21.33 years (SD 2.35), 86.7% female; excluded insomnia and psychopathology.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Behavioral bedtime-procrastination intervention using functional analysis and individualized behavioral strategies.
- **Comparator/control:** Wait-list control.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Intervention trial with weekly sleep-diary monitoring; exact treatment length not isolated in this extraction.
- **Endpoints extracted:** Bedtime Procrastination Scale, bedtime procrastination duration, sleep efficiency, time from bedtime to lights out, insomnia severity, daytime sleepiness.

## Results extracted
- Treatment reduced Bedtime Procrastination Scale scores by 35.56% versus wait-list (reported d=2.19).
- Bedtime procrastination duration decreased by 46.29 minutes (d=1.22).
- Sleep efficiency improved by 5.70% (d=1.25), and insomnia/daytime-sleepiness measures improved.

## Digital Sunset relevance
This source is `same_mechanism` for **Digital Sunset No Personal Screens Before Bed**. It should be used as `supports-protocol` evidence only. Adjacent or mechanism findings must not be promoted into direct protocol efficacy claims.
