This source is included for **light, circadian, and display-mechanism evidence** in the Digital Sunset extraction batch.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** crossover (2024).
- **People studied or addressed:** Male adolescents and male young adults in a within-subject evening reading protocol.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Ninety minutes of evening smartphone reading with or without a blue-light filter.
- **Comparator/control:** Ninety minutes of reading from a printed book.
- **Role for Digital Sunset:** same_mechanism; supports-protocol. This is not upgraded to direct protocol efficacy unless the source itself directly tested a no-personal-screen-before-bed behavior.

## Extracted source-local finding

Accessible abstracts report smartphone exposure affected melatonin/sleep physiology, with adults showing longer melatonin suppression than adolescents; conclusions support avoiding smartphone use in the last hour before bedtime, but this remains an acute exposure study.

## Endpoints extracted

polysomnography, melatonin secretion, subjective sleepiness, sleep-dependent declarative memory consolidation

## Duration or follow-up

Three experimental nights; 90-minute evening exposure.

## Safety/adverse events

No adverse-event extraction from accessible abstract/metadata; do not infer safety findings unless the full source reports them.

## Important limits and population mismatch

- Male-only sample.
- Acute within-subject exposure, not a behavior-change adherence trial.
- Smartphone reading may not represent all screen content.
