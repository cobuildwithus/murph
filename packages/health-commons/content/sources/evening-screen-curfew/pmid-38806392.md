This source is included for **light, circadian, and display-mechanism evidence** in the Digital Sunset extraction batch.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** guideline (2024).
- **People studied or addressed:** Children, adolescents, adults, and older adults across the lifespan.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Expert consensus evaluation of screen use, screen content, and behavioral strategies related to sleep health.
- **Comparator/control:** Not applicable; consensus statement using modified Delphi/RAND-UCLA methods.
- **Role for Digital Sunset:** background; context-only. This is not upgraded to direct protocol efficacy unless the source itself directly tested a no-personal-screen-before-bed behavior.

## Extracted source-local finding

The consensus panel reached agreement on some, but not all, screen-and-sleep statements; it supports cautious guideline context rather than primary efficacy claims.

## Endpoints extracted

sleep health, sleep duration, sleep quality, screen-related sleep impairment

## Duration or follow-up

Five virtual meetings in 2023; evidence search timeframe varied by topic.

## Safety/adverse events

No adverse-event extraction from accessible abstract/metadata; do not infer safety findings unless the full source reports them.

## Important limits and population mismatch

- Consensus statement, not a primary trial.
- Statements differ by age group and mechanism.
- Should not be used to infer direct causal benefit for Digital Sunset.
