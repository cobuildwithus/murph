## Quick read

- **Source type:** acute physiology (2005).
- **People studied or addressed:** Healthy adults exposed to short- and longer-wavelength light in the late evening.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Late-evening 460 nm short-wavelength light exposure.
- **Comparator/control:** Late-evening 550 nm longer-wavelength light exposure.
- **Role for Digital Sunset:** same_mechanism; context-only. This is not upgraded to direct protocol efficacy unless the source itself directly tested a no-personal-screen-before-bed behavior.

## Extracted source-local finding

Short-wavelength light produced greater melatonin suppression and alerting physiological responses than longer-wavelength light in the accessible abstract.
