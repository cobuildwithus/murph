This source is included for **light, circadian, and display-mechanism evidence** in the Digital Sunset extraction batch.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** acute physiology (2013).
- **People studied or addressed:** Healthy adults exposed to tablet displays and light-modifying conditions.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Self-luminous tablet exposure at high brightness and/or with blue-light goggle augmentation.
- **Comparator/control:** Dark/control or light-blocking/orange-lens conditions.
- **Role for Digital Sunset:** same_mechanism; supports-protocol. This is not upgraded to direct protocol efficacy unless the source itself directly tested a no-personal-screen-before-bed behavior.

## Extracted source-local finding

Tablet-only exposure was not clearly significant after 1 hour but was significant after 2 hours; tablet plus blue-light goggle exposure produced stronger suppression, illustrating dose and duration dependence.

## Endpoints extracted

melatonin suppression

## Duration or follow-up

Acute 1- and 2-hour exposure windows.

## Safety/adverse events

No adverse-event extraction from accessible abstract/metadata; do not infer safety findings unless the full source reports them.

## Important limits and population mismatch

- Acute biomarker study.
- Light conditions may not match ordinary phone/tablet use.
- Does not test stopping screens as a behavior-change protocol.
