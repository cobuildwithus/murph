## Extraction summary

- **Population:** Employees in a diary study.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Work-related smartphone use during non-work time in the evening.
- **Comparator/control:** Evenings with lower work-related smartphone use; sleep-quality moderation.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 10 working days.
- **Endpoints:** ego depletion, next-day self-control demands, sleep quality
- **Effect or direction:** After evenings with high work-related smartphone use, employees reported disproportionate ego depletion when facing next-day self-control demands; high sleep quality attenuated this interaction.
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse events; work-boundary strain and next-day depletion are burden notes.

## Limitations and mismatch
