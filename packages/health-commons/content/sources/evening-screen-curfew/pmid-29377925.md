This source page is a batch-009 draft for **Digital Sunset — No Personal Screens Before Bed**. It is included for measurement, adherence, or confounder interpretation, not for protocol synthesis.

## Quick read

- **People studied or addressed:** Healthy adults in a naturalistic longitudinal sleep-monitoring study.
- **Participant count:** 30
- **Intervention or exposure:** Daily sleep logs and wrist actigraphy during naturalistic monitoring.
- **Comparator or control:** Agreement between self-reported sleep logs and actigraphy.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Up to 16 consecutive weeks.
- **Endpoints:** Sleep onset, sleep offset, total sleep time, sleep-onset latency, wake after sleep onset, number of awakenings, compliance.
- **Directness to Digital Sunset:** background
- **Claim-use boundary:** context-only

## Extracted atomic finding

Sleep logs and actigraphy agreed better for sleep onset/offset than for wakefulness-related variables; compliance declined over time and was related to agreement.

## Safety and adverse events

No adverse-event signal was extracted for this source in this batch. Where this is a consumer-technology or clinical-guideline source, use it as a measurement or safety-boundary anchor rather than as a claim that consumer devices can diagnose or treat sleep problems.

## Limitations and population mismatch

- Small healthy-adult sample.
- Actigraphy was not compared directly with PSG in this study.
- Wake-related diary-actigraphy disagreement can affect self-experiment interpretation.
