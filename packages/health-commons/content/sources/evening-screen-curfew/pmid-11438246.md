This source page is a batch-009 draft for **Digital Sunset — No Personal Screens Before Bed**. It is included for measurement, adherence, or confounder interpretation, not for protocol synthesis.

## Quick read

- **People studied or addressed:** People with insomnia in sleep-clinic and insomnia-treatment research samples; exact eligibility criteria not extracted in this batch.
- **Participant count:** Two validation samples reported in accessible secondary text: 145 sleep-clinic insomnia patients and 78 older adults in insomnia-treatment research; do not treat as one pooled sample.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Insomnia Severity Index (ISI) validation and outcome-measure assessment.
- **Comparator or control:** Sleep diary measures, polysomnography change measures, and clinician/significant-other versions were described in accessible secondary text; exact analyses not fully extracted.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Baseline and treatment-change measurement windows; exact follow-up duration not extracted.
- **Endpoints:** Insomnia severity, sleep-onset latency, sleep quality, sleep diary/PSG comparisons, treatment-response sensitivity.
- **Directness to Digital Sunset:** background
- **Claim-use boundary:** context-only

## Extracted atomic finding

ISI is retained as an insomnia-severity outcome anchor; accessible text describes validity and sensitivity to change, but this is not screen-curfew efficacy evidence.
