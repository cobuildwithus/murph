## Extracted facts

- **Intervention or exposure:** Prolonged violent video-gaming before sleep (150 minutes).
- **Comparator or control:** Shorter violent video-gaming exposure before sleep (50 minutes).
- **Duration or follow-up:** Acute experimental presleep exposure comparison.
- **Endpoints:** total sleep time; sleep efficiency; sleep-onset latency; self-perceived insufficient sleep.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Accessible publisher snippets report that 150 minutes of violent video-gaming reduced total sleep time by about 27 minutes and sleep efficiency by about 7%, with subjective sleep-onset latency increasing by 17 ± 8 minutes.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event signal extracted.

## Limitations and population mismatch

- Acute violent-gaming exposure, not a general screen curfew.
- Sample size not extracted here.
- Content/arousal and duration are intertwined.
