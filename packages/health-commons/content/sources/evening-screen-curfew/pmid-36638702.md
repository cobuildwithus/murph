This source is included for **screen_media_sleep_reviews_guidelines** in batch `batch-003`.

**Findings:**
- Across 60 included studies, digital media use was generally associated with shorter sleep duration and poorer sleep quality, especially for bedtime/nighttime use. (source_artifact:pmid-36638702)
- Device-specific evidence was mixed; some device categories did not show consistent associations. (source_artifact:pmid-36638702)

**Population and exposure:**
-
  Population: Late adolescents and young adults aged approximately 16 to 25 years.
- Intervention or exposure: Digital media use, including bedtime and nighttime use and device-specific exposures.
- Comparator/control: Lower/no digital media use or different timing/device exposure depending on included study.
- Duration/follow-up: Cross-sectional and longitudinal windows varied across included studies.

**Endpoints:** sleep duration, sleep quality, sleep timing, sleep outcomes by device and timing
