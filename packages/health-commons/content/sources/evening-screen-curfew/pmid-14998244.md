## Quick read

- **Source key:** `source_artifact:pmid-14998244`
- **Design:** Cross-sectional observational study.
- **People studied or addressed:** Random sample of secondary-school children/students from 15 schools in Flanders, Belgium.
- **Participant count:** 2546 (reported_in_accessible_abstract).
- **Role for Digital Sunset:** adjacent_variant / context-only.

## Extracted facts

- **Intervention or exposure:** Bedroom media presence plus television viewing, computer-game playing, and Internet use.
- **Comparator or control:** Different device/activity exposure levels; observational comparisons only.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Cross-sectional questionnaire; average weekdays and weekend days were queried.
- **Endpoints:** self-reported time to bed; time out of bed; time spent in bed; tiredness.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Accessible abstract documents the device/activity comparison design; exact quantitative results were not extracted for this draft.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event monitoring extracted.

## Limitations and population mismatch

- Self-reported time-to-bed and rise-time outcomes.
- Older media environment; pre-smartphone era.
- Observational context only.

## Protocol boundary
