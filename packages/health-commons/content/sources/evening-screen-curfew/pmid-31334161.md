## Extraction summary

- **Population:** Saudi non-medical staff at King Saud University Medical City.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Smartphone usage at bedtime, including duration and social-media use.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower or shorter bedtime smartphone use.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Cross-sectional survey from January to July 2016.
- **Endpoints:** sleep quality, bedtime smartphone-use prevalence
- **Effect or direction:** More bedtime smartphone use, especially longer use, was associated with higher risk of poor sleep quality; the study reported very high bedtime smartphone use among staff.
- **Safety/adverse events:** No intervention adverse events; burden signal for employed adults.
