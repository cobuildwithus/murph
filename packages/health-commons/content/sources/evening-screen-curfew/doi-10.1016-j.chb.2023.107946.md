## Extraction summary

- **Population:** Adolescents from the United States in an electronic-diary study.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Evening smartphone use measured by electronic diary over two weeks.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower evening smartphone use / within-person and between-person contrasts.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Two-week diary study.
- **Endpoints:** sleep onset time, sleep-onset latency, sleep duration, sleep quality
- **Effect or direction:** The study reported a more complex association than broad harm claims; adolescent evening smartphone use was not uniformly detrimental, and some associations differed before school versus non-school days.
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse events; mixed/null boundary evidence.
