## Quick read

ClinicalTrials.gov protocol for a school-cluster teenage sleep trial including a Technology Restriction arm; no results yet.

## Extracted intervention or exposure

- **Population / N:** Teenage students aged 13-19 years in Swedish school settings (N=5100 ; count kind: planned school-cluster sample; effective enrollment not yet extracted from results).
- **Intervention / exposure:** Technology Restriction / TechRest arm delivers two lessons separated by about two weeks and targets limiting screen use in the evening one hour before bedtime, with individualized screen-stop timing from sleep forms.
- **Comparator / control:** School-as-usual control; Sleep on Schedule is a separate active arm.
- **Duration / follow-up:** School-based intervention with lessons over weeks; registry/protocol has no results extracted

## Extracted endpoints and results

- **Endpoints:** Sleep duration, sleep habits, daytime symptoms, perceived sleep quality, mobile use, grades, physical and mental health, motivation/process, and actigraphy validation in a subsample.
- **Effect or direction:** Protocol/registry only; no outcomes extracted.
