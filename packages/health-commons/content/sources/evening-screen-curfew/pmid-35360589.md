## Extraction summary

- **Population:** Public employees in the United States and China.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Off-time work-related smartphone use.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower off-time work-related smartphone use; cross-cultural comparison.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Two observational survey samples.
- **Endpoints:** bedtime procrastination, self-control depletion
- **Effect or direction:** Off-time work-related smartphone use was modeled as positively influencing bedtime procrastination through self-control depletion; cross-cultural differences were examined.
