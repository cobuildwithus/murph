# "Leave your smartphone out of bed": quantitative analysis of smartphone use effect on sleep quality
## Extraction boundary
- **Directness to Digital Sunset No Personal Screens Before Bed:** `same_mechanism`.
- **Claim-use boundary:** `supports-protocol`. Observational/context findings should not be written as causal intervention claims.
- **Artifact handling:** metadata/link only unless rights review explicitly clears redistribution.

## Population and design
- **Design:** Smartphone app-log and wearable-ring field study.
- **Participants:** 75 (complete_app_and_wearable_data); Adult smartphone users with wearable-ring sleep data; recruited sample included young adults (mean age reported as 27 in accessible text).
- **Duration/follow-up:** Field monitoring period; exact duration not captured in this extraction summary..

## Exposure and comparator
- **Exposure:** Objectively logged smartphone use while in bed versus outside bed.
- **Comparator/control:** Smartphone use outside bed and lower in-bed smartphone use.

## Endpoints
- Sleep latency
- Awake time
- Average heart rate
- Heart-rate variability
- Well-being

## Extracted result
In-bed smartphone use had significant adverse associations with sleep latency, awake time, average heart rate, and heart-rate variability; smartphone use outside bed did not show the same sleep-quality decrease.

## Safety/adverse events
No intervention adverse events reported; autonomic endpoints are physiological context.

## Limitations and population mismatch
- Observational field design; no causal inference.
- Modest complete-data sample.
- Does not directly randomize removing personal screens before bed.

## Protocol-page use note
Supports plausibility, not causal efficacy of the protocol. Any future protocol-page claim using this source should cite `source_artifact:pmid-36405389` and preserve the `supports-protocol` boundary.
