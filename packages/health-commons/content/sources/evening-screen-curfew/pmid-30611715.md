Draft extraction for `batch-002` / adjacent behavioral intervention variants. Directness is `adjacent_variant` and claim use is `context-only`. This page is not a protocol synthesis.

## Methods snapshot
- **Design:** Pilot cluster-randomized controlled trial.
- **Participants:** randomized; intervention n=192, control n=160; Adolescents, mean age 15.09 years (SD 1.65), 163 females.
- **Intervention/exposure:** 25-minute psychoeducative school intervention asking adolescents to avoid nighttime electronic media use and evening caffeine, plus parent leaflet.
- **Comparator/control:** Control condition without the active school intervention.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Baseline to post-intervention assessment approximately 4 weeks later.
- **Endpoints extracted:** bedtime electronic media use, caffeine consumption, sleep duration, sleep difficulties, daytime tiredness, mental well-being.

## Results extracted
- Bedtime electronic media use decreased significantly but modestly in the intervention group (reported d about -0.24).
- No intervention effect was found for caffeine consumption or sleep duration.
- No significant effects were reported for sleep difficulties, daytime tiredness, or mental well-being.

## Digital Sunset relevance
This source is `adjacent_variant` for **Digital Sunset No Personal Screens Before Bed**. It should be used as `context-only` evidence only. Adjacent or mechanism findings must not be promoted into direct protocol efficacy claims.

## Safety/adverse events
- **Adverse events:** Not reported in the extracted abstract/accepted text.
- **Safety notes:** No adverse-event signal extracted; intervention was educational and behavioral.

## Limitations and population mismatch
- Bundled nighttime media, caffeine, sleep-education, and parent-leaflet components prevent isolation of a personal-screen bedtime curfew.
- Pilot study with short follow-up.
- Adolescent school setting may not generalize to adults or household self-experiments.
