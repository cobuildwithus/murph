This source is included for **screen_media_sleep_reviews_guidelines** in batch `batch-003`.

**Findings:**
- Review indicated that habitual casual gaming up to about 7 hours/week was not consistently associated with negative sleep consequences. (source_artifact:pmid-39284267)
- Gaming addiction/problematic gaming and arousing video games before sleep were linked to worse sleep quality/timing, increased sleep onset latency, or reduced sleep efficiency. (source_artifact:pmid-39284267)
- Some non-arousing cognitively demanding games may enhance sleep continuity/stability/organization. (source_artifact:pmid-39284267)

**Population and exposure:**
-
  Population: Adults in video-gaming and sleep studies.
- Intervention or exposure: Video gaming, including habitual, casual, addictive/problematic, arousing pre-sleep, and non-arousing cognitively demanding gaming categories.
- Comparator/control: Lower/no gaming or other gaming categories.
- Duration/follow-up: Systematic review; exposure duration and timing varied.

**Endpoints:** sleep quality, sleep timing, sleep onset latency, sleep efficiency, sleep continuity
