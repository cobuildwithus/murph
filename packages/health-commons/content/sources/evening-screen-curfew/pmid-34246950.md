## Extraction summary

- **Population:** Chinese university students.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Smartphone addiction, bedtime procrastination, and self-control.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower smartphone-addiction scores and stronger self-control.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Cross-sectional survey / mediation-moderation analysis.
- **Endpoints:** depression symptoms, anxiety symptoms, bedtime procrastination, self-control
- **Effect or direction:** Bedtime procrastination partially mediated associations between smartphone addiction and depression/anxiety; self-control moderated some paths.
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse events; mood symptoms are safety/context endpoints.
