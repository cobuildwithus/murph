## Extraction summary

- **Population:** Community adult online sample.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Bedtime procrastination: failing to go to bed at the intended time while no external circumstances prevent doing so.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower bedtime procrastination / stronger self-regulation.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Cross-sectional online survey.
- **Endpoints:** bedtime procrastination construct, self-regulation, experienced insufficient sleep, hours of sleep, daytime fatigue
- **Effect or direction:** Bedtime procrastination was negatively associated with self-regulation and related to experienced insufficient sleep above demographics and self-regulation.
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse events; construct implies voluntary sleep loss and daytime fatigue burden.
