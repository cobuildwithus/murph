This source is included for **light, circadian, and display-mechanism evidence** in the Digital Sunset extraction batch.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** crossover (2011).
- **People studied or addressed:** Healthy young male adults in a controlled crossover laboratory setting.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Five-hour evening exposure to a white LED-backlit computer screen with higher short-wavelength emission.
- **Comparator/control:** White non-LED-backlit computer screen exposure.
- **Role for Digital Sunset:** same_mechanism; supports-protocol. This is not upgraded to direct protocol efficacy unless the source itself directly tested a no-personal-screen-before-bed behavior.

## Extracted source-local finding

LED-backlit screen exposure suppressed the evening melatonin rise and reduced sleepiness markers while improving some attention/memory performance during exposure.

## Endpoints extracted

melatonin rise, subjective sleepiness, objective sleepiness markers, attention and memory performance

## Duration or follow-up

Acute 5-hour evening exposure.

## Safety/adverse events

No adverse-event extraction from accessible abstract/metadata; do not infer safety findings unless the full source reports them.

## Important limits and population mismatch

- Small male-only laboratory sample.
- Comparator was another screen, not no screen.
- Alertness/performance during exposure is not a sleep-benefit outcome.
