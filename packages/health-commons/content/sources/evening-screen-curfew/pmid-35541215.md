## Extracted facts

- **Intervention or exposure:** Parent-set technology rules and adolescent compliance with those rules.
- **Comparator or control:** No rules, rules with noncompliance, and rules with reported compliance.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Cross-sectional survey.
- **Endpoints:** bedtime; lights-out time; total sleep time; sleep-onset latency; daytime sleepiness; FoMO; bedtime procrastination.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Presence of a parent-set technology rule was associated with earlier bedtimes regardless of compliance. Earlier lights-out times and increased sleep duration were observed among adolescents who always complied compared with noncompliers or those with no rules. Parent-set rules did not moderate FoMO/bedtime-procrastination links with sleep.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Daytime sleepiness and bedtime procrastination/FoMO are burden-context endpoints; no adverse-event monitoring.

## Limitations and population mismatch

- Cross-sectional; directionality cannot be established.
- Self-reported rules, compliance, and sleep.
- Parent-set rules in adolescents are adjacent to adult self-imposed digital sunset.
