This source is included for **direct_protocol_trials_and_registries** in batch `batch-001`.

## Quick read

Assessor-blinded toddler RCT/feasibility trial removed screen time during the hour before bed and found strong feasibility plus preliminary sleep signals.

## Extracted intervention or exposure

- **Population / N:** Toddlers aged 16-30 months with screen time in the hour before bed on at least three days per week (N=105 ; count kind: randomized families; PASTI n=35, bedtime-box n=36, no-intervention n=34).
- **Intervention / exposure:** Parent-administered PASTI intervention removed toddler screen time in the hour before bed and supplied bedtime-box alternative activities.
- **Comparator / control:** Bedtime-box-only active control and no-intervention/usual routine control.
- **Duration / follow-up:** Seven-week intervention

## Extracted endpoints and results

- **Endpoints:** Feasibility, retention, adherence, screen use, actigraphy sleep, eye-tracking/objective attention, and parent-reported attention.
- **Effect or direction:** Retention was 99% and PASTI adherence was 94%. Screen time was reduced versus no intervention and bedtime-box control. Sleep efficiency showed a preliminary improvement versus bedtime-box control, while several sleep and attention estimates were mixed or underpowered; no objective attention effect was observed.
