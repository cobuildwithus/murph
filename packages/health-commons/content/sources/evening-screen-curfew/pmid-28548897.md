This source is included for **light, circadian, and display-mechanism evidence** in the Digital Sunset extraction batch.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** acute physiology (2017).
- **People studied or addressed:** Participants exposed to computer-screen light conditions in the evening.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Evening short-wavelength computer-screen light exposure.
- **Comparator/control:** Other light wavelength/intensity screen conditions.
- **Role for Digital Sunset:** same_mechanism; supports-protocol. This is not upgraded to direct protocol efficacy unless the source itself directly tested a no-personal-screen-before-bed behavior.

## Extracted source-local finding

The accessible abstract reports that short-wavelength screen light disrupted sleep continuity and architecture, dampened nocturnal melatonin, altered body-temperature rhythms, and negatively affected morning attention.

## Endpoints extracted

sleep continuity, sleep architecture, melatonin, body temperature rhythm, morning attention

## Duration or follow-up

Acute evening exposure; exact duration not extracted.

## Important limits and population mismatch

- Laboratory screen-light manipulation, not a behavior-change curfew.
- Generalizability to ordinary device use is uncertain.
