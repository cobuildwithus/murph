## Extraction summary

- **Population:** Vietnamese university students.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Electronic device use before bedtime, especially within 2 hours and for more than 30 minutes.
- **Comparator/control:** No/less electronic device use before bedtime or shorter duration.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Cross-sectional survey.
- **Endpoints:** Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index, sleep duration, depressive symptoms, device-use prevalence
- **Effect or direction:** 98.1% used at least one electronic device within 2 hours before bedtime; electronic device use within 2 hours and for >30 minutes was associated with poorer sleep, and >30 minutes remained significant after adjusting for depression/lifestyle factors.
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse events; poor sleep prevalence and short sleep are burden endpoints.
