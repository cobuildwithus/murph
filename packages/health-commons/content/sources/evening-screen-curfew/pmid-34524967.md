## Extracted facts

- **Intervention or exposure:** Social media checking/posting/importance and parent-reported bedtime technology-use rules.
- **Comparator or control:** Lower social media engagement and presence versus absence of bedtime technology rules.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Cross-sectional survey.
- **Endpoints:** daytime sleepiness; bedtime technology rules.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Open-article extraction notes social media checking, posting, and importance were associated with higher daytime sleepiness, and bedtime rules moderated some associations; only about 34.12% of parents reported bedtime screen rules in extracted notes.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Daytime sleepiness is a burden/safety-relevant contextual endpoint; no adverse-event monitoring.

## Limitations and population mismatch

- Cross-sectional; cannot infer causality.
- Rules were parent-reported; enforcement/effectiveness were not established.
- Social media context is adjacent to the protocol.
