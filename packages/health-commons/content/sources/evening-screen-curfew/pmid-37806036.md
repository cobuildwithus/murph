## Extraction summary

- **Population:** Chinese college/university students.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Problematic smartphone use and bedtime procrastination across waves, with self-control as mediator.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower problematic smartphone use / lower bedtime procrastination.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Three-wave longitudinal design with 6-month intervals from November 2021 to November 2022.
- **Endpoints:** problematic smartphone use, bedtime procrastination, self-control
- **Effect or direction:** The study reported bidirectional longitudinal relationships between problematic smartphone use and bedtime procrastination, with self-control mediating the cycle.
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse events; problematic-use cycle is a burden/context signal.
