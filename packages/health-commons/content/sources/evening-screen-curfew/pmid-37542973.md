## Quick read

Direct randomized trial in Chinese undergraduates with problematic smartphone use; cognitive arousal appears to mediate sleep-quality improvement.

## Extracted intervention or exposure

- **Population / N:** Chinese undergraduates with problematic smartphone use and while-in-bed smartphone use (N=152 ; count kind: randomized; 76 intervention and 76 control in abstracted source).
- **Intervention / exposure:** Restriction of while-in-bed smartphone use.
- **Comparator / control:** Control condition; exact comparator dose was not extracted from abstract-level access.
- **Duration / follow-up:** Four-week follow-up with weekly measurement

## Extracted endpoints and results

- **Endpoints:** Sleep quality and pre-sleep cognitive arousal across baseline and weeks 1-4.
- **Effect or direction:** The intervention reportedly decreased pre-sleep cognitive arousal and improved sleep quality, with changes appearing within one week and maintained over four weeks. Mediation modeling suggested cognitive arousal as a pathway.
