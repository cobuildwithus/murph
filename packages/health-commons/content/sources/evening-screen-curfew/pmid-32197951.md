This source is included for **light, circadian, and display-mechanism evidence** in the Digital Sunset extraction batch.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** cohort (2020).
- **People studied or addressed:** Healthy adult volunteers completing online sleep and screen-exposure questionnaires.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Self-reported cumulative evening and nighttime media-device screen exposure.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower exposure, no exposure, timing strata, and blue-light-filter-use strata.
- **Role for Digital Sunset:** background; context-only. This is not upgraded to direct protocol efficacy unless the source itself directly tested a no-personal-screen-before-bed behavior.

## Extracted source-local finding

Longer cumulative evening screen-light exposure was associated with greater morning sleep inertia and longer workday sleep latency; screen exposure near bedtime/night awakenings was associated with several poorer subjective sleep-related outcomes.

## Endpoints extracted

sleep inertia, workday sleep latency, social jet lag, daytime dysfunction, subjective sleep quality, fatigue

## Duration or follow-up

Cross-sectional questionnaire assessment.

## Safety/adverse events

No adverse-event extraction from accessible abstract/metadata; do not infer safety findings unless the full source reports them.

## Important limits and population mismatch

- Cross-sectional self-report design.
- Cannot infer causality.
- Exposure and sleep outcomes may be affected by sleep problems driving device use.
