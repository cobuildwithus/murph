# TXT me I'm only sleeping: adolescents with mobile phones in their bedroom
## Extraction boundary
- **Directness to Digital Sunset No Personal Screens Before Bed:** `adjacent_variant`.
- **Claim-use boundary:** `context-only`. Observational/context findings should not be written as causal intervention claims.
- **Artifact handling:** metadata/link only unless rights review explicitly clears redistribution.

## Population and design
- **Design:** Cross-sectional pediatric primary-care survey.
- **Participants:** 454 (survey_respondents); Adolescent pediatric primary-care patients; 51.2% female.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Single assessment.

## Exposure and comparator
- **Exposure:** Mobile phones brought to bed or kept active nearby; texting after going to bed; text awakenings.
- **Comparator/control:** No active phone nearby/no bedtime texting.

## Endpoints
- Bedroom phone access
- Texting after bed
- Text-related awakenings

## Extracted result
More than one third texted after going to bed (36.7%), 7.9% were awakened by texts at least twice weekly, and 62.9% brought phones to bed or slept with active phones nearby.

## Safety/adverse events
No intervention adverse events reported.

## Limitations and population mismatch
- Cross-sectional descriptive study; no causal estimate extracted.
- Bedroom-phone access is adjacent to the protocol, not an intervention test.
- Effect estimates on sleep outcomes were not available in the accessible abstract-level extraction.
