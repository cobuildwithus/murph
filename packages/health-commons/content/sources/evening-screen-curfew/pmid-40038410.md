## Quick read

- **Source type:** Systematic Review and Meta-analysis (2025).
- **People studied or addressed:** Participants in social-media abstinence studies included in the review.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Social media abstinence.
- **Comparator/control:** Control, baseline, or comparison conditions across included studies.
- **Role for Digital Sunset:** adjacent_variant; context-only. This source is not upgraded to direct Digital Sunset efficacy unless the source itself directly tested no personal screens before bed.

## Extracted source-local finding

The meta-analysis included 10 studies with 4,674 participants and found no significant effects of social-media abstinence on positive affect, negative affect, or life satisfaction. Digital Sunset should not promise these outcomes from a bedtime screen curfew.

## Endpoints extracted

positive affect, negative affect, life satisfaction

## Duration or follow-up

Abstinence durations varied across included studies.

## Safety, adverse events, and clinical boundaries

No bedtime-protocol adverse-event evidence extracted. Use primarily to prevent overclaiming well-being benefits.

## Important limits and population mismatch

- Adjacent social-media abstinence evidence, not a bedtime personal-screen curfew.
- Outcomes focused on affective well-being and life satisfaction, not sleep safety.
- Included abstinence designs and durations varied.
