## Extraction summary

- **Population:** Adults in Saudi Arabia.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Bedtime technology/device use and devices in the bedroom.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower or no bedtime technology use.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Cross-sectional survey.
- **Endpoints:** sleep quality, excessive daytime sleepiness, device prevalence in bedroom
- **Effect or direction:** The study reported high bedroom smartphone availability and regular use, and evaluated associations of bedtime technology use with poor sleep quality and excessive daytime sleepiness.
- **Safety/adverse events:** No intervention adverse events; excessive daytime sleepiness is a burden endpoint.
