This source is included for **light, circadian, and display-mechanism evidence** in the Digital Sunset extraction batch.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** crossover trial (2016).
- **People studied or addressed:** Adults reading in bed under iPad and print-book conditions.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Reading from an iPad in bed.
- **Comparator/control:** Reading from a printed book in bed.
- **Role for Digital Sunset:** adjacent_variant; context-only. This is not upgraded to direct protocol efficacy unless the source itself directly tested a no-personal-screen-before-bed behavior.

## Extracted source-local finding

The abstract reports decreased subjective sleepiness, approximately 30-minute delay in slow-wave activity dynamics, and reduced slow-wave activity after sleep onset with iPad reading, while sleep state timing and sleep onset latency did not differ.

## Endpoints extracted

subjective sleepiness, sleep onset latency, sleep state timing, slow-wave activity dynamics

## Duration or follow-up

Acute bedtime reading condition; exact duration not extracted.
