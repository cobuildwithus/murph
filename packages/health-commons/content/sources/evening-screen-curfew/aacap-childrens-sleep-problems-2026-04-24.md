This source is included for **supplemental sleep-hygiene, insomnia, and broad guidance context** for the Digital Sunset protocol.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Children, adolescents, and families; includes common teen sleep-wake reversal context.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Guidance includes planned bed/wake times, relaxing bedtime routine, caffeine/exercise timing, electronics stopped within 1 hour before bedtime, and keeping electronics out of the bedroom preferred.
- **Comparator or control:** No comparator; expert guidance page.
- **Duration or follow-up:** No trial duration; recommends nightly bedtime routine and electronics cutoff within 1 hour before bedtime.
- **Endpoints:** sleep problems, bedtime routine, electronics cutoff, medical referral flags
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Recommendation-level direction only; no effect estimate reported.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Medical red flags include loud snoring/mouth breathing, restless legs or possible low iron, daytime sleepiness despite adequate sleep, and medical or psychiatric disorders affecting sleep; families are directed to pediatric/sleep/mental-health care for ongoing concerns.
- **Limitations and population mismatch:** Guidance page, not a systematic review or intervention trial; context-only for digital sunset.
- **Directness to Digital Sunset:** background
- **Claim-use boundary:** context-only
- **Artifact candidates and rights status:** Web page has reuse restrictions; store metadata/link only unless rights are clarified.
