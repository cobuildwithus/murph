## Methods snapshot
- **Design:** Two randomized trials.
- **Participants:** Study 1 n=383; Study 2 n=221; Undergraduate students.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Mental contrasting with implementation intentions (MCII) to reduce bedtime procrastination.
- **Comparator/control:** Study 1 positive-thinking control; Study 2 sleep-hygiene control.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Study 1 three weeks; Study 2 one week.
- **Endpoints extracted:** commitment to reduce bedtime procrastination, self-reported bedtime discrepancy, subjective bedtime procrastination, sleep duration/timing.

## Results extracted
- MCII increased commitment and reduced average minutes of bedtime procrastination per night.
- In Study 2, MCII reduced bedtime discrepancy versus sleep-hygiene control (b=-37.86 minutes; 95% CI -59.79 to -15.92; p<.001).
- Participants did not clearly sleep more, go to bed earlier, or wake later; discrepancy reduction partly reflected revised planned bedtimes.
