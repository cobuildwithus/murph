## Extraction summary

- **Population:** Employees in a workplace questionnaire field survey.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Bedtime smartphone usage.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower bedtime smartphone usage.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Cross-sectional field survey.
- **Endpoints:** sleep quality, work performance, interpersonal conflicts, work engagement
- **Effect or direction:** The study reported negative effects of bedtime smartphone usage on sleep quality, with sleep quality mediating associations with work performance, interpersonal conflicts, and work engagement.
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse events; workplace functioning and interpersonal conflict are burden notes.
