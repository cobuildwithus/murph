This source is included for **supplemental sleep-hygiene, insomnia, and broad guidance context** for the Digital Sunset protocol.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Adolescents aged 12-18 years in 103 peer-reviewed publications; specialist clinical cohorts were limited.
- **Intervention or exposure:** No unified intervention; modifiable parental factors such as bedtime rule-setting, parent sleep behaviours, family functioning, and parental warmth.
- **Comparator or control:** Different levels of parental factors across observational and reviewed studies; no direct digital-sunset comparator.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Search from database inception through November 2019; follow-up varied across included studies.
- **Endpoints:** sleep duration, sleep quality, daytime functioning, parental rule setting, family functioning
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Review reports convergent evidence for parental bedtime rule-setting and parent sleep behaviours with longer adolescent sleep duration; healthy parent sleep and family functioning with better sleep quality; parental warmth with better daytime functioning.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse events extracted; this was not a treatment trial.
- **Limitations and population mismatch:** Large share of included studies used cross-sectional observational designs and self-report; findings need intervention validation and may not apply to adolescents with specialist sleep/mental-health conditions.
- **Directness to Digital Sunset:** adjacent_variant
- **Claim-use boundary:** context-only
- **Artifact candidates and rights status:** Do not store publisher PDF in Git; metadata/link-only unless redistribution rights are verified.

## Protocol boundary

Use as family-rule context only; do not cite as direct proof that a personal-screen sunset improves sleep.
