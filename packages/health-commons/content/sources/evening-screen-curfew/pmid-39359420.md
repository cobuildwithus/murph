## Extraction summary

- **Population:** University students from Quaid-e-Azam University, Islamabad, Pakistan.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Problematic smartphone use, bedtime procrastination, sleep quality, and mental-health symptoms across two time points.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower baseline levels of the modeled constructs.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Two time points separated by 6 months.
- **Endpoints:** problematic smartphone use, bedtime procrastination, sleep quality, mental-health symptoms
- **Effect or direction:** Cross-lagged modeling found reciprocal associations among problematic smartphone use, bedtime procrastination, and mental-health symptoms; baseline PSU and bedtime procrastination predicted later sleep-quality variables.
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse events; mental-health symptoms and sleep quality are burden endpoints.
