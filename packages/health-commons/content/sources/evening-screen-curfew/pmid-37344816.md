## Extraction summary

- **Population:** College students in Shanghai, China, during COVID-19 outbreak.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Mobile phone dependency, bedtime procrastination, and fear of missing out.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower mobile phone dependency.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Cross-sectional online survey in May 2022.
- **Endpoints:** sleep quality, bedtime procrastination, fear of missing out, mobile phone dependency
- **Effect or direction:** Mobile phone dependency was positively associated with FoMO, bedtime procrastination, and poor sleep quality; indirect effects on sleep quality were reported through bedtime procrastination (0.030; 95% CI 0.022–0.041) and FoMO (0.013; 95% CI 0.003–0.023).
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse events; pandemic context and FoMO burden are contextual notes.
