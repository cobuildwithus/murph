## Quick read

Adolescent within-subject pilot manipulating phone-stop time one hour before bed for one school week; direct but not randomized.

## Extracted intervention or exposure

- **Population / N:** Adolescents aged 14-18 years (N=63 ; count kind: reported; adolescents completing/participating in pilot).
- **Intervention / exposure:** Individualized instruction to stop phone use one hour before bedtime.
- **Comparator / control:** Within-person baseline week.
- **Duration / follow-up:** Baseline school week followed by one intervention school week

## Extracted endpoints and results

- **Endpoints:** Online sleep diary phone-stop time, bedtime/lights-out timing, sleep latency, and total sleep time.
- **Effect or direction:** Adolescents stopped phone use about 80 minutes earlier, turned lights off about 17 minutes earlier, and slept about 21 minutes longer during the intervention week.

## Directness and claim boundary

- **Directness to Digital Sunset:** direct_protocol.
- **Claim use:** supports-protocol.
- **Boundary:** No randomized control, self-reported sleep diary outcomes, low recruitment fraction, adolescent population, and short follow-up.

## Safety / adverse events

No adverse events were extracted. Recruitment and adherence/motivation were practical feasibility issues.
