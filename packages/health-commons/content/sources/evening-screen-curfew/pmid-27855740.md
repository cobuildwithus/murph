## Extracted source-local finding

The orthosomnia case report/series described patients whose focus on consumer sleep-tracker data and ideal sleep metrics contributed to distress and potentially counterproductive sleep behaviors. Digital Sunset should avoid rigid, perfectionistic messaging and should not increase sleep anxiety.

## Endpoints extracted

sleep anxiety, insomnia symptoms, time in bed, tracker-data fixation

## Duration or follow-up

Clinical case encounters; follow-up varied by case.

## Safety, adverse events, and clinical boundaries

Boundary source for users who become anxious or rigid about sleep rules. Consider simplifying, pausing metrics, or seeking clinical support if the protocol worsens insomnia worry.

## Important limits and population mismatch

- Case report/series; no controlled comparison.
- Focuses on sleep-tracker metrics, not bedtime screen curfews.
- Does not quantify risk prevalence.
