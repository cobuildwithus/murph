This source is included for **screen_media_sleep_reviews_guidelines** in batch `batch-003`.

**Findings:**
- Bedtime portable device use was associated with inadequate sleep quantity, poor sleep quality, and excessive daytime sleepiness in children and adolescents. (source_artifact:pmid-27802500)
- Reported pooled estimates included OR 2.17 for inadequate sleep quantity, OR 1.46 for poor sleep quality, and OR 2.72 for excessive daytime sleepiness for bedtime device use. (source_artifact:pmid-27802500)

**Population and exposure:**
-
  Population: Children and adolescents aged 6 to 19 years in studies of portable screen-based media device access or use.
- Intervention or exposure: Portable screen-based media device access or use at bedtime or in the sleep environment.
- Comparator/control: No reported bedtime device access or use, or lower exposure depending on included study.
- Duration/follow-up: Review of mostly observational studies; exposure timing centered on bedtime or bedroom access, with study-specific windows.

**Endpoints:** sleep duration or inadequate sleep quantity, sleep quality, excessive daytime sleepiness

**Adverse events or safety notes:**
- No protocol adverse events were extracted from this source in this batch. (source_artifact:pmid-27802500)

**Limitations and mismatch:**

- Bedroom access/use is adjacent to, but not the same as, an instructed no-personal-screens-before-bed protocol. (source_artifact:pmid-27802500)
