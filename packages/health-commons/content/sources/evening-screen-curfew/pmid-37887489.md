## Extraction summary

- **Population:** Adults aged 18–60 years in Spain.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Problematic smartphone use and bedtime procrastination.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower problematic smartphone use and lower bedtime procrastination.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Cross-sectional online survey, February–May 2023.
- **Endpoints:** Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index, problematic smartphone use, bedtime procrastination, smartphone use in bed
- **Effect or direction:** Mediation analysis found PSU predicted bedtime procrastination (B=0.26, p<.001), bedtime procrastination predicted worse sleep quality (B=0.14, p<.001), and the PSU-to-sleep direct path was not significant (B=0.02, p=.312); indirect effect B=0.04 (95% CI 0.02–0.05).
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse events; 68.4% poor sleepers and sleep displacement by smartphone in bed indicate burden.
