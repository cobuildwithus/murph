## Quick read

- **Source type:** acute physiology (2019).
- **People studied or addressed:** Adults exposed to iPad display settings.
- **Exposure/intervention:** iPad Night Shift mode display conditions.
- **Comparator/control:** Other iPad spectral/brightness settings and dim-control conditions.
- **Role for Digital Sunset:** adjacent_variant; context-only. This is not upgraded to direct protocol efficacy unless the source itself directly tested a no-personal-screen-before-bed behavior.

## Extracted source-local finding

Melatonin suppression did not significantly differ between Night Shift interventions, suggesting spectral shifts without brightness reduction may be insufficient to prevent melatonin suppression.
