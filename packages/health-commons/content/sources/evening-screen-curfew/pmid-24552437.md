## Extraction summary

- **Population:** Emerging adults at university, first-year cohort at Time 1.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Television and online social networking use across three years.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower media use / reciprocal cross-lagged paths.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Three-year longitudinal survey.
- **Endpoints:** sleep duration, sleep problems, television use, online social networking use
- **Effect or direction:** Sleep problems predicted longer television and social-networking use, but media use did not predict sleep problems; sleep duration was not associated with media use.
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse events; reverse-causality/adherence context.
