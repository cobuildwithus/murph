## Methods snapshot
- **Design:** Randomized experimental manipulation.
- **Participants:** unselected participants randomized after baseline measurement; Unselected participants; extracted sex distribution female n=48, male n=43, did-not-specify n=2.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Suspend mobile-device social-media use for one week.
- **Comparator/control:** Use social media as normal.
- **Duration/follow-up:** One baseline week plus one intervention week.
- **Endpoints extracted:** anxiety, sleep quality, objective mobile-device social-media use.

## Results extracted
- The trial found no evidence of a causal effect of acute mobile social-media reduction on anxiety or sleep quality in unselected participants.
- This is a null boundary source for social-media restriction that is not bedtime-specific.

## Digital Sunset relevance
This source is `adjacent_variant` for **Digital Sunset No Personal Screens Before Bed**. It should be used as `context-only` evidence only. Adjacent or mechanism findings must not be promoted into direct protocol efficacy claims.

## Limitations and population mismatch
- Social-media restriction is not the same as no personal screens before bed.
- Mobile-device social-media use only; computer/other-device use not fully captured.
- Unselected participants may not have baseline sleep problems.
