## Methods snapshot
- **Design:** Randomized controlled comparative study.
- **Participants:** randomized; self-help book n=77, sleep-hygiene advice n=78; Adults with insomnia recruited through newspaper advertisements and a web-based survey.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Self-help book for insomnia.
- **Comparator/control:** Sleep hygiene advice.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Three-month follow-up.
- **Endpoints extracted:** sleep scales, sleep medication use.

## Results extracted
- The self-help book was superior to sleep hygiene advice for improving sleep and reducing sleep medication use.
- Sleep hygiene advice alone produced only small mixed changes in extracted sleep-scale results and is a boundary source against overvaluing generic sleep hygiene.
