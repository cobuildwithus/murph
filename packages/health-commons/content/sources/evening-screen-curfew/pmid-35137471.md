## Extraction summary

- **Population:** Free-living adults.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Media use before bed recorded in diaries, including timing, location, and multitasking.
- **Comparator/control:** No/less media use before sleep onset and different media-use contexts.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Media diary plus objective EEG sleep measurement; exact number of nights not extracted.
- **Endpoints:** bedtime, total sleep time, N3 sleep percent, REM sleep percent
- **Effect or direction:** Media use in the hour before sleep onset was associated with earlier bedtime in extracted abstracts; findings were described as moderated/complicated rather than uniformly harmful.
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse events; mixed findings are a cautionary safety/context note.
