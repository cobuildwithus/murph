## Extracted facts

- **Intervention or exposure:** Mobile-device-based Sleep Time-Related Information and Communication Technology (STRICT) use after bedtime, including texting/tweeting/messaging in bed and awakenings to texts.
- **Comparator or control:** No or lower after-bedtime STRICT exposure; observational comparisons only.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Cross-sectional study.
- **Endpoints:** insomnia; daytime sleepiness; eveningness; academic performance; bedtime; sleep duration.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Accessible abstract reports nearly 62% used STRICT after bedtime, 56.7% texted/tweeted/messaged in bed, and 20.8% awoke to texts; STRICT use was associated with insomnia, daytime sleepiness, eveningness, academic underperformance, later bedtimes, and shorter sleep duration.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No formal adverse-event monitoring; daytime sleepiness and academic underperformance are contextual outcomes.

## Limitations and population mismatch

- Cross-sectional self-report; causality cannot be inferred.
- Communication technology after bedtime is adjacent to but narrower than a general screen curfew.
