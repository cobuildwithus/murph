## Quick read

- **Source type:** crossover (2016).
- **People studied or addressed:** Participants exposed to tablet and book reading after daytime bright light.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Two hours of evening reading on a self-luminous tablet after daytime bright-light exposure.
- **Comparator/control:** Two hours of evening reading from a physical book.
- **Role for Digital Sunset:** adjacent_variant; context-only. This is not upgraded to direct protocol efficacy unless the source itself directly tested a no-personal-screen-before-bed behavior.

## Extracted source-local finding

The accessible abstract reports no differences in sleep parameters or pre-sleep salivary melatonin between tablet and physical book conditions after daytime bright-light exposure.
