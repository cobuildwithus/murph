This source is included for **safety, burden, withdrawal, and clinical-boundary evidence** in the Digital Sunset extraction batch.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Cross-sectional Survey Analysis (2024).
- **People studied or addressed:** Workers in the 6th Korean Working Conditions Survey.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Use of work-related communication devices outside regular working hours, with or without long working hours.
- **Comparator/control:** No long working hours and no outside-hours work-related communication-device use.
- **Role for Digital Sunset:** safety_boundary; context-only. This source is not upgraded to direct Digital Sunset efficacy unless the source itself directly tested no personal screens before bed.

## Extracted source-local finding

In 46,055 workers, use of work-related communication devices outside regular hours was associated with higher odds of anxiety symptoms, including OR 2.18 (95% CI 1.97-2.41) for outside-hours device use without long working hours and OR 2.57 (95% CI 2.24-2.97) when combined with long working hours.

## Endpoints extracted

anxiety symptoms, after-hours work communication, long working hours, occupational burden

## Duration or follow-up

Cross-sectional survey assessment.

## Safety, adverse events, and clinical boundaries

Occupational safety and burden context. Digital Sunset should include after-hours work boundaries where feasible and exceptions where required.

## Important limits and population mismatch

- Cross-sectional association; causality cannot be established.
- Work-related device use is not equivalent to all bedtime personal-screen use.
- Findings may depend on Korean occupational norms and survey measurement.
