This source is included for **screen_media_sleep_reviews_guidelines** in batch `batch-003`.

**Findings:**
- General electronic media use was associated with poorer sleep quality (pooled r = 0.28, 95% CI 0.21 to 0.35; k = 20). (source_artifact:pmid-38533835)
- Problematic electronic media use was associated with sleep problems (pooled r = 0.33, 95% CI 0.28 to 0.38; k = 36). (source_artifact:pmid-38533835)
- Subgroups varied; problematic internet use and general smartphone use had comparatively larger reported associations in the review. (source_artifact:pmid-38533835)

**Population and exposure:**
-
  Population: Participants from electronic-media-use and sleep-quality studies across more than 20 countries; age strata varied by included study.
- Intervention or exposure: Electronic media use, grouped as general use or problematic use.
- Comparator/control: Lower electronic media exposure or lower/problematic-use scores as defined by included studies.
- Duration/follow-up: Systematic review of studies from Jan 2018 to Oct 2023; individual study windows varied.
