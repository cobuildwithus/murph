## Extraction summary

- **Population:** Canadian adults from a national stratified random phone interview survey.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Occasional, moderate, or regular bedtime screen use in bed or within 1 hour of bedtime during the past month.
- **Comparator/control:** Different bedtime screen-use frequency groups.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Cross-sectional survey.
- **Endpoints:** RU-SATED sleep health, sleep regularity, satisfaction, alertness, timing, efficiency, duration
- **Effect or direction:** 45.3% reported bedtime screen use every day. After adjustment, both occasional and regular screen users reported the best overall sleep health; results varied by sleep-health dimension, and biological sex moderated the association with sleep regularity.
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse events; this is a cautionary mixed/complex source.
