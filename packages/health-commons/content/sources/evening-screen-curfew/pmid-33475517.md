## Extracted facts

- **Intervention or exposure:** Technology, games, online social networks, and YouTube use.
- **Comparator or control:** Lowest/never/rare use categories.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Cross-sectional survey.
- **Endpoints:** sleep-onset difficulties.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Open article abstract reports sleep-onset difficulties in 34.3% overall. Highest versus lowest electronic-device use OR 1.50 (95% CI 1.21-1.85); games OR 1.35 (1.11-1.64); online social networks always versus never/rarely OR 1.40 (1.09-1.81); YouTube OR 2.00 (1.50-2.66).
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Sleep-onset difficulty is an outcome/burden signal; no adverse-event monitoring extracted.
