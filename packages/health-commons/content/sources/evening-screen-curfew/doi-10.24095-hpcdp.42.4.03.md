This source is included for **screen_media_sleep_reviews_guidelines** in batch `batch-003`.

**Findings:**
- Evening screen-based sedentary behaviour and bedroom screen access were associated with reduced sleep duration and quality in children/youth. (source_artifact:doi-10.24095/hpcdp.42.4.03)
- The authors rated evidence as very low for sleep duration and low for sleep quality. (source_artifact:doi-10.24095/hpcdp.42.4.03)
- Intervention examples included reducing screen time after 9 pm and reducing mobile-phone use in the hour before bed, with reported sleep-duration increases of about 17 and 21 minutes/night respectively; certainty was very low. (source_artifact:doi-10.24095/hpcdp.42.4.03)

**Population and exposure:**
-
  Population: Healthy children and youth aged 5 to 17.99 years.
- Intervention or exposure: Timing of sedentary behaviour, screen-based sedentary behaviour, and access to sedentary activities/screens in the bedroom.
- Comparator/control: Different timing/access categories, including less evening screen-based sedentary behaviour or less bedroom access.
- Duration/follow-up: Review of observational studies and a small number of interventions; intervention windows varied.
