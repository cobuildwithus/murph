This source is included for **direct_protocol_trials_and_registries** in batch `batch-001`.

## Quick read

Two athlete training-camp randomized studies removed evening/overnight devices; effects were limited or null.

## Extracted intervention or exposure

- **Population / N:** Athletes in water polo and triathlon training camps (N=49 ; count kind: reported total across water polo athletes (n=26) and triathletes (n=23)).
- **Intervention / exposure:** No electronic devices after dinner and overnight.
- **Comparator / control:** Unrestricted electronic-device use.
- **Duration / follow-up:** Seven-night water polo camp and four-night triathlon camp

## Extracted endpoints and results

- **Endpoints:** Actigraphy sleep outcomes, nomophobia questionnaire, and psychomotor vigilance task in the triathlon camp.
- **Effect or direction:** Device removal produced an earlier bedtime and longer time-in-bed only on the first night in water polo athletes; otherwise sleep quantity, nomophobia, and psychomotor vigilance differences were not significant.

## Directness and claim boundary

- **Directness to Digital Sunset:** direct_protocol.

- **Boundary:** Short supervised training-camp setting, athlete sample, and not a self-directed adult bedtime curfew.
