This source is included for **light, circadian, and display-mechanism evidence** in the Digital Sunset extraction batch.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** acute physiology (2001).
- **People studied or addressed:** Healthy adults with normal color vision exposed to monochromatic light at night.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Full-field monochromatic light exposures across 420–600 nm.
- **Comparator/control:** Different wavelength and irradiance conditions.
- **Role for Digital Sunset:** same_mechanism; context-only. This is not upgraded to direct protocol efficacy unless the source itself directly tested a no-personal-screen-before-bed behavior.

## Extracted source-local finding

The action spectrum identified short-wavelength light, especially the 446–477 nm region, as highly potent for melatonin suppression.
