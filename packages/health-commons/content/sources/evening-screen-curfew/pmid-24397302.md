## Quick read

- **Source type:** crossover trial (2014).
- **People studied or addressed:** Adolescents exposed to tablet screenlight before bedtime.
- **Exposure/intervention:** One hour of bright tablet screenlight or short-wavelength-filtered tablet screenlight.
- **Comparator/control:** Alternative tablet-light/filter condition; full control details not extracted.
- **Role for Digital Sunset:** same_mechanism; context-only. This is not upgraded to direct protocol efficacy unless the source itself directly tested a no-personal-screen-before-bed behavior.

## Extracted source-local finding

The accessible title/metadata indicate this was a boundary test of whether one hour had a meaningful effect; exact effect estimates were not extracted, so it should be treated as a cautious adolescent short-exposure boundary source.
