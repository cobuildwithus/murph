## Extraction summary

- **Population:** Young adults with bedtime procrastination.
- **Exposure/intervention:** BED-PRO psychological intervention for decreasing bedtime procrastination.
- **Comparator/control:** Baseline within-person comparison; no full RCT comparator in this development study.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Intervention development/feasibility with follow-up measures; exact schedule not fully extracted.
- **Endpoints:** Bedtime Procrastination Scale, bedtime procrastination duration, wake after sleep onset, sleep efficiency, feeling refreshed
- **Effect or direction:** The development study reported changes in BPS scores and bedtime procrastination duration (Δ51 minutes; 63.8% reduction compared with baseline), plus sleep-diary improvements in WASO, sleep efficiency, and feeling refreshed.
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse events extracted; feasibility/acceptability emphasis.
