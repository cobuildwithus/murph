## Extraction summary

- **Population:** Young adults aged 20–24 years.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Mobile phone exposure variables including high frequency of use and accessibility stress.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower mobile-phone use/stress exposure.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Prospective cohort with 1-year follow-up.
- **Endpoints:** stress, sleep disturbances, symptoms of depression
- **Effect or direction:** High frequency of mobile phone use at baseline was reported as a risk factor for mental-health outcomes including stress, sleep disturbance, and depression symptoms at 1-year follow-up.
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse events; stress, sleep disturbance, and depression symptoms are burden endpoints.
