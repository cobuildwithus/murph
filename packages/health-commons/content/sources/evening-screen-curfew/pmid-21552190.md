This source is included for **light, circadian, and display-mechanism evidence** in the Digital Sunset extraction batch.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** acute physiology (2011).
- **People studied or addressed:** College students exposed to computer-monitor and light-modifying conditions.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Computer monitor exposure alone or with blue-light goggles.
- **Comparator/control:** Dark control and orange-tinted/light-blocking conditions.
- **Role for Digital Sunset:** same_mechanism; context-only. This is not upgraded to direct protocol efficacy unless the source itself directly tested a no-personal-screen-before-bed behavior.

## Extracted source-local finding

Blue-light goggles plus monitor exposure significantly reduced melatonin versus dark control and monitor-only conditions; monitor-only exposure produced a slight, non-significant reduction versus dark control.

## Endpoints extracted

melatonin concentration

Acute evening/night exposure; exact duration not extracted.
