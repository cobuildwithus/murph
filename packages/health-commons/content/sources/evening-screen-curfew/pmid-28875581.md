This source is included for **supplemental sleep-hygiene, insomnia, and broad guidance context** for the Digital Sunset protocol.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Adults with chronic insomnia disorder; not children/adolescents or general wellness users.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Clinical diagnosis and treatment recommendations including CBT-I, pharmacologic options, and diagnostic work-up.
- **Comparator or control:** Guideline recommendations based on meta-analyses; no digital-sunset comparator.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Evidence review of meta-analyses through June 2016; no protocol duration.
- **Endpoints:** insomnia diagnosis, sleep history, sleep diary, CBT-I, polysomnography referral, sleep hygiene boundary
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Recommendation-level direction: CBT-I was recommended as first-line treatment for chronic insomnia in adults; pharmacotherapy considered if CBT-I is ineffective or unavailable; some therapies had insufficient evidence or were not recommended.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Guideline recommends clinical interview including sleep habits/environment and sleep diary; polysomnography when other sleep disorders are suspected or insomnia is treatment-resistant.
- **Limitations and population mismatch:** Adjacent adult clinical guideline; not direct device-curfew evidence.
- **Directness to Digital Sunset:** adjacent_variant
- **Claim-use boundary:** context-only
- **Artifact candidates and rights status:** Do not store publisher PDF in Git; metadata/link-only unless redistribution rights are verified.

## Protocol boundary

Use for boundaries around chronic insomnia diagnosis/treatment and sleep diary/referral prompts.
