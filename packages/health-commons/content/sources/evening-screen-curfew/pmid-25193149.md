This source is included for **light, circadian, and display-mechanism evidence** in the Digital Sunset extraction batch.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** systematic review (2015).
- **People studied or addressed:** School-aged children and adolescents across observational screen-time studies.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Screen time across television, computers, video games, and mobile devices.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower screen exposure, no screen exposure, or different exposure strata across included studies.
- **Role for Digital Sunset:** background; context-only. This is not upgraded to direct protocol efficacy unless the source itself directly tested a no-personal-screen-before-bed behavior.

## Extracted source-local finding

The review reported adverse sleep associations in most included studies, especially shorter sleep duration and delayed sleep timing; it did not establish a causal screen-curfew effect.

## Endpoints extracted

sleep duration, sleep timing, sleep onset latency, sleep quality

## Duration or follow-up

Studies published 1999 through early 2014; follow-up varied by included study.

## Safety/adverse events

No adverse-event extraction from accessible abstract/metadata; do not infer safety findings unless the full source reports them.

## Important limits and population mismatch

- Mostly observational evidence.
- Screen exposure and sleep outcomes were heterogeneous.
- Reverse causality and content/arousal pathways were not resolved.
- Population is children/adolescents, not all Digital Sunset users.
