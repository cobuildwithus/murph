## Quick read

- **Source type:** Systematic Review and Meta-analysis (2024).
- **People studied or addressed:** Participants in social-media detox intervention studies included in the review.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Digital social-media detox interventions.
- **Comparator/control:** Control or comparison conditions within included studies.
- **Role for Digital Sunset:** adjacent_variant; context-only. This source is not upgraded to direct Digital Sunset efficacy unless the source itself directly tested no personal screens before bed.

## Extracted source-local finding

Across 10 included studies, the meta-analysis reported a statistically significant depression effect (SMD -0.29, 95% CI -0.51 to -0.07) but no significant pooled effects for life satisfaction, stress, or overall mental well-being. This supports cautious, context-only use for Digital Sunset.

## Endpoints extracted

depression, life satisfaction, stress, mental well-being

## Duration or follow-up

Included study durations varied; review included studies published from 2013 through 2023.

## Safety, adverse events, and clinical boundaries

No bedtime-protocol adverse-event evidence extracted. Use for cautious context and to avoid broad mental-health claims.

## Important limits and population mismatch

- Adjacent detox evidence, not bedtime-specific screen-curfew evidence.
- Only 10 included studies and heterogeneous outcomes.
- Adverse events and burden reporting were not the central extraction target in this source.
