## Quick read

Registered pediatric crossover-trial protocol testing screen use, activity, and meals before bed; no peer-reviewed outcomes yet.

## Extracted intervention or exposure

- **Population / N:** Children aged 10-14.9 years (N=72 ; count kind: planned).
- **Intervention / exposure:** Crossover conditions include avoiding all three prebedtime behaviors, using screens for at least 30 minutes, being active for at least 30 minutes, and eating a large meal during the hour before bed.
- **Comparator / control:** Avoid-all-three prebedtime behavior condition within the crossover design.
- **Duration / follow-up:** Baseline week plus crossover intervention weeks; results expected after protocol completion

## Extracted endpoints and results

- **Endpoints:** Adherence and shuteye latency via cameras, actigraphy total sleep time, sleep onset, and wake after sleep onset.
- **Effect or direction:** Protocol only; no peer-reviewed efficacy results extracted. The article trial registration is ANZCTR ACTRN12624000206527, so the ledger-suggested linkage to NCT05820555 should be treated as unverified or likely a separate trial unless confirmed elsewhere.
