## Methods snapshot
- **Design:** Cluster randomized controlled trial.
- **Participants:** high-school students randomized by cluster; High-school students in Qazvin, Iran; mean age 15.12 years (SD 1.50).
- **Intervention/exposure:** Theory-based adolescent sleep-hygiene program using TPB/HAPA constructs; four face-to-face student sessions plus one parent session.
- **Comparator/control:** Wait-list/control schools receiving educational material after the trial.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Baseline, 1-month follow-up, and 6-month follow-up.
- **Endpoints extracted:** Adolescent Sleep Hygiene Scale, daytime sleepiness, sleep quality, sleep disturbance, sleep duration, health-related quality of life, general health.

## Results extracted
- Sleep hygiene improved at 1 and 6 months (ASHS coefficients 0.16 and 0.19; 95% CIs 0.12-0.20 and 0.15-0.23).
- Daytime sleepiness, sleep quality, sleep disturbance, and sleep duration improved, but the intervention was a broad sleep-hygiene bundle.
- Sleep-hygiene items included pre-bed stimulating activities such as video games, television, and telephone, but no screen-curfew effect was isolated.
