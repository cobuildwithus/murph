## Extraction summary

- **Population:** Nationwide online survey of South Korean adults aged 20–69 years.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Frequency, duration, and type of bedtime digital media use, including smartphones/tablets, within bedtime routines.
- **Comparator/control:** Less frequent or shorter bedtime digital-media use.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Cross-sectional survey fielded January–February 2022.
- **Endpoints:** bedtime digital media prevalence, bedtime procrastination, Insomnia Severity Index thresholds
- **Effect or direction:** Daily bedtime digital media use was common (64.1%) and was associated with insomnia symptoms: ISI ≥10 adjusted OR 1.920 (95% CI 1.541–2.392); ISI ≥15 adjusted OR 1.872 (95% CI 1.320–2.656). Use >60 minutes was associated with ISI ≥15 adjusted OR 2.028 (95% CI 1.379–2.980).
- **Safety/adverse events:** No intervention adverse events; survey supports burden concerns around insomnia symptoms and late digital-media routines.

## Limitations and mismatch

- Self-reported sleep and media exposure.
- Survey during/near COVID-era context may affect bedtime media prevalence.
