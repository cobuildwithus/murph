This source is included for **Adolescent, family, school, and device-type observational context**.

## Quick read

- **Source key:** `source_artifact:pmid-40922948`
- **Design:** Observational or other study design.
- **People studied or addressed:** Healthy adolescents aged 13-15 years; open article reports mean age 14.2 years (SD 0.74) and 49% female.
- **Participant count:** 329 (reported_in_open_article).
- **Role for Digital Sunset:** adjacent_variant / context-only.

## Extracted facts

- **Intervention or exposure:** Parental rules about evening screen electronic device use, including fixed hour, fixed duration, airplane mode, no smartphone in room, and no phone use in evening.
- **Comparator or control:** No rules and less strict rule types.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Cross-sectional survey/sleep analysis.
- **Endpoints:** weeknight total sleep time; academic achievement; sleep duration by rule type.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Open extraction notes any rule was associated with about 40 minutes longer weeknight total sleep time; after Bonferroni, fixed hour, fixed duration, and airplane mode were not significant; no smartphone in room was associated with about 33 minutes more sleep and no phone use in evening with about 66 minutes more sleep, but the no-phone evening subgroup was small (n=17).
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event monitoring extracted.

## Limitations and population mismatch

- Self-reported sleep and rules.
- No covariates/no actual screen-time measure in extracted limitations.
- Rule compliance unknown; no-phone evening subgroup small.

## Protocol boundary

Use to compare fixed curfew rules against device-removal/no-evening-phone rules; preserve null results for some rule types.
