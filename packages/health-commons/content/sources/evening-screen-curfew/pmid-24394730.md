- **Source key:** `source_artifact:pmid-24394730`

## Extracted facts

- **Intervention or exposure:** Weekday bedtime use of six technologies.
- **Comparator or control:** Different technology-specific exposure categories; observational comparisons only.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Cross-sectional questionnaire study.
- **Endpoints:** sleep quantity; sleep quality; parasomnias.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Accessible abstract confirms technology-specific associations were tested across eight sleep parameters; exact technology-specific effect estimates were not extracted for this draft.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event monitoring extracted.

- Exact effect estimates not extracted here.
