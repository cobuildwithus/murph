This source is included for **light, circadian, and display-mechanism evidence** in the Digital Sunset extraction batch.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** crossover trial (2025).
- **People studied or addressed:** University students in China, with survey baseline and controlled crossover experimental phase.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Restricting pre-bed smartphone short-form-video use to 30 minutes or 0 minutes.
- **Comparator/control:** 60-minute baseline pre-bed smartphone condition.
- **Role for Digital Sunset:** same_mechanism; context-only. This is not upgraded to direct protocol efficacy unless the source itself directly tested a no-personal-screen-before-bed behavior.

## Extracted source-local finding

Compared with 60 minutes, 30- and 0-minute restrictions increased sleep efficiency by 5.43% and 11.51%, reduced WASO by 35.3 and 46.4 minutes, increased positive affect by 2.33 and 5.44 points, and improved N-back accuracy by 3.5% and 5.5%.

## Endpoints extracted

sleep efficiency, wake after sleep onset, positive affect, N-back accuracy

## Duration or follow-up

Sequential pre-bed exposure restrictions; exact washout/follow-up not extracted.
