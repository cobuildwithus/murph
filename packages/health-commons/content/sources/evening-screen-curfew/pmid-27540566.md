This source is included for **Adolescent, family, school, and device-type observational context**.

## Quick read

- **Source key:** `source_artifact:pmid-27540566`
- **Design:** Cross-sectional observational study.
- **People studied or addressed:** Age-9 children from an ethnically diverse national birth cohort, the Fragile Families and Child Wellbeing Study.
- **Participant count:** 3269 child reports; 2770 caregiver reports (reported_in_accessible_abstract).
- **Role for Digital Sunset:** adjacent_variant / context-only.

## Extracted facts

- **Intervention or exposure:** Interactive and passive screen time, including computer/video games and television.
- **Comparator or control:** Lower/no interactive or passive screen time; observational comparisons only.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Cross-sectional age-9 cohort assessment.
- **Endpoints:** nighttime sleep duration; bedtime context.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Accessible abstracts report both child and caregiver screen-time measures; prior extraction notes screen time was associated with shorter sleep, but no screen type was clearly significantly shorter than another.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event monitoring extracted.

## Limitations and population mismatch

- Age-9 children, not adolescents/adults.
- Cross-sectional and report-based.
- Interactive-versus-passive differences were not cleanly separable into a direct curfew recommendation.

## Protocol boundary

Use for interactive/passive boundary context and to preserve that device-type contrasts can be mixed.
