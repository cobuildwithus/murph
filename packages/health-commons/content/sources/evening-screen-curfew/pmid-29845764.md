- **Source type:** crossover (2018).
- **People studied or addressed:** Healthy adults in a controlled evening tablet-use comparison.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Unrestricted evening use of light-emitting tablet computers.
- **Comparator/control:** Evening reading from printed materials/control condition.
- **Role for Digital Sunset:** adjacent_variant; supports-protocol. This is not upgraded to direct protocol efficacy unless the source itself directly tested a no-personal-screen-before-bed behavior.

## Extracted source-local finding

Evening light-emitting tablet use delayed self-selected bedtime, suppressed melatonin secretion, disrupted circadian timing, and impaired next-morning alertness in accessible summaries.
