# Sleep Duration, Restfulness, and Screens in the Sleep Environment
## Extraction boundary
- **Directness to Digital Sunset No Personal Screens Before Bed:** `adjacent_variant`.
- **Claim-use boundary:** `context-only`. Observational/context findings should not be written as causal intervention claims.
- **Artifact handling:** metadata/link only unless rights review explicitly clears redistribution.

## Population and design
- **Design:** Cross-sectional school sample.
- **Participants:** 2048 (survey_respondents); Fourth- and seventh-grade children in Massachusetts participating in MA-CORD baseline surveys.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Single assessment.

## Exposure and comparator
- **Exposure:** Small screens and televisions in the sleep environment; screen time.
- **Comparator/control:** No small screen or television in the sleep environment.

## Endpoints
- Weekday sleep duration
- Perceived insufficient rest or sleep

## Extracted result
Having a small screen near the sleep environment was associated with 20.6 fewer minutes of weekday sleep (95% CI -29.7 to -11.4) and higher prevalence of perceived insufficient rest/sleep (PR 1.39, 95% CI 1.21-1.60).

## Safety/adverse events
No intervention adverse events reported.

## Limitations and population mismatch
- Cross-sectional self-report; no causal inference.
- Younger school-aged population may not generalize to adults.
- Sleep-environment access is adjacent to a bedtime personal-screen curfew.

## Protocol-page use note
Adjacent-variant child data; not a protocol trial. Any future protocol-page claim using this source should cite `source_artifact:pmid-25560435` and preserve the `context-only` boundary.
