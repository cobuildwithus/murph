# Mobile Phones in the Bedroom: Trajectories of Sleep Habits and Subsequent Adolescent Psychosocial Development
## Extraction boundary
- **Directness to Digital Sunset No Personal Screens Before Bed:** `adjacent_variant`.
- **Claim-use boundary:** `context-only`. Observational/context findings should not be written as causal intervention claims.
- **Artifact handling:** metadata/link only unless rights review explicitly clears redistribution.

## Population and design
- **Design:** Longitudinal adolescent cohort with latent growth models.
- **Participants:** 1101 (cohort_participants); Adolescents aged 13-16 years; 43% male.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Longitudinal trajectory analysis; exact extraction window not specified in accessible abstract..

## Exposure and comparator
- **Exposure:** Nighttime mobile phone use/mobile phones in the bedroom.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower nighttime mobile phone use trajectories.

## Endpoints
- Sleep behavior
- Depressed mood
- Externalizing behavior
- Self-esteem
- Coping

## Extracted result
Increased nighttime mobile phone use was associated with increased externalizing behavior and reduced self-esteem/coping; sleep behavior mediated early changes in nighttime phone use and later psychosocial outcomes.

## Safety/adverse events
No intervention adverse events reported; psychosocial outcomes are safety/context signals.

## Limitations and population mismatch
- Observational longitudinal design; mediation does not prove causality.
- Adolescent population differs from broad adult protocol users.
- Bedroom phone access is adjacent to a no-screens-before-bed protocol.

## Protocol-page use note
Adjacent observational context only. Any future protocol-page claim using this source should cite `source_artifact:pmid-28556891` and preserve the `context-only` boundary.
