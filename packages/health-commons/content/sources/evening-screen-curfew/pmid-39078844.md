This source is included for **screen_media_sleep_reviews_guidelines** in batch `batch-003`.

**Findings:**
- Nineteen of 23 studies reported a significant association between increased off-job work-related smartphone use and increased work-life conflict, usually small to moderate. (source_artifact:pmid-39078844)
- Three studies reported low/moderate positive associations with stress, distress, strain, or sleep problems. (source_artifact:pmid-39078844)

**Population and exposure:**
-
  Population: Working adults in studies of work-related smartphone use during off-job hours.
- Intervention or exposure: Work-related smartphone use during off-job hours.
- Comparator/control: Lower/no work-related smartphone use during off-job hours or different work-boundary conditions.
- Duration/follow-up: Scoping review; study windows varied.

**Endpoints:** work-life conflict, job stress, psychological distress, strain, sleep problems

**Adverse events or safety notes:**
- Work-life conflict, stress, strain, and sleep problems are implementation/safety-life-fit context, not adverse events from the digital-sunset protocol itself. (source_artifact:pmid-39078844)

**Limitations and mismatch:**
- Work-boundary context, not a sleep intervention. (source_artifact:pmid-39078844)
- Scoping review, not efficacy meta-analysis. (source_artifact:pmid-39078844)
- Some positive work outcomes or moderators may coexist. (source_artifact:pmid-39078844)
