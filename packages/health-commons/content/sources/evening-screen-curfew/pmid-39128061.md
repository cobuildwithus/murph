## Extraction summary

- **Population:** Adolescents aged 13–18 years.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Problematic smartphone use, smartphone habits in bed, and bedtime procrastination.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower problematic smartphone use / less in-bed smartphone use.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Cross-sectional mediation study.
- **Endpoints:** sleep quality, bedtime procrastination, problematic smartphone use signs, smartphone use in bed
- **Effect or direction:** 46.9% had PSU signs and 90.6% used smartphones in bed; poor sleep quality was positively associated with duration of smartphone use in bed, number of activities, PSU signs, and bedtime procrastination; bedtime procrastination mediated the PSU-sleep-quality relationship.
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse events; adolescent sleep-quality burden.
