## Extraction status
Draft extraction for `batch-002` / adjacent behavioral intervention variants. Directness is `same_mechanism` and claim use is `context-only`. This page is not a protocol synthesis.

## Methods snapshot
- **Design:** Pilot randomized controlled trial.
- **Participants:** randomized; tCBT n=16, wait-list n=16; University students with significant bedtime procrastination (BPS score >=28).
- **Intervention/exposure:** Transdiagnostic CBT / Unified Protocol targeting behavioral, cognitive, and emotional dimensions of bedtime procrastination; 20 weekly one-hour sessions.
- **Comparator/control:** Wait-list control.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 20 weekly sessions plus 3-month follow-up.
- **Endpoints extracted:** Bedtime Procrastination Scale, Dysfunctional Beliefs and Attitudes about Sleep, Difficulties in Emotion Regulation Scale.

## Results extracted
- Time-by-group interactions favored tCBT on bedtime procrastination, emotion regulation, and sleep-related beliefs (all p<.001).
- Large effects were maintained at three months (Hedges g approximately 1.22-1.29).
- Intervention included broader CBT elements and was not screen-specific.

## Digital Sunset relevance
This source is `same_mechanism` for **Digital Sunset No Personal Screens Before Bed**. It should be used as `context-only` evidence only. Adjacent or mechanism findings must not be promoted into direct protocol efficacy claims.

## Safety/adverse events
- **Adverse events:** Not extracted.
- **Safety notes:** No adverse-event signal extracted; intensive 20-session therapy is not a simple behavioral curfew.

## Limitations and population mismatch
- Small pilot sample.
- No active control.
- Three-month follow-up only.
- No dismantling analysis to isolate screen or behavioral components.
