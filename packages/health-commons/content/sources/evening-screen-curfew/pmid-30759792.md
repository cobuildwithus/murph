## Extracted facts

- **Intervention or exposure:** Screen time and nocturnal mobile phone-related awakenings.
- **Comparator or control:** No/lower nocturnal phone-related awakenings and lower screen exposure.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Prospective one-year follow-up in accessible summary.
- **Endpoints:** difficulty falling asleep; restless sleep; general health symptoms.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Accessible summary reports adolescents with nocturnal mobile phone-related awakenings were 3.5 times more likely to develop difficulties falling asleep and 5.4 times more likely to experience restless sleep over one year.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** General health symptoms and sleep disturbance are contextual safety/burden outcomes; no formal adverse-event reporting extracted.

## Limitations and population mismatch

- Effect details came from an accessible research-summary page, not directly from the full article text in this run.
