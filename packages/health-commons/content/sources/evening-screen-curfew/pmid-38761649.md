This source is included for **supplemental sleep-hygiene, insomnia, and broad guidance context** for the Digital Sunset protocol.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Adult studies using the phrase sleep hygiene in titles or abstracts; 250 observational and 298 intervention studies were identified.
- **Intervention or exposure:** No single intervention; bibliographic review of sleep-hygiene definitions and components.
- **Comparator or control:** Not applicable; bibliographic categorization.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Search from database inception through December 31, 2021.
- **Endpoints:** sleep hygiene definition, sleep hygiene components, caffeine, alcohol, exercise, sleep timing, light, wind-down routine
- **Effect estimates or direction:** No sleep-outcome effect estimate; review found only 44% of studies defined sleep hygiene, components were explicitly defined in up to 70% of observational studies and 35% of intervention studies, and common components varied.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse events extracted; not a treatment study.
- **Limitations and population mismatch:** Definition/construct review, not efficacy evidence; adult literature focus; broad sleep-hygiene construct may obscure which component matters.
- **Directness to Digital Sunset:** background
- **Claim-use boundary:** context-only
- **Artifact candidates and rights status:** Article is open access under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0; redistribution may be possible with proper attribution and license retention, but no manifest entry is requested in this batch.

## Protocol boundary

Use to define sleep hygiene narrowly and avoid treating bundled sleep-hygiene advice as evidence for a single screen-curfew component.
