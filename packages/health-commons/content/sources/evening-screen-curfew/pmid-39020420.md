## Extraction summary

- **Population:** Chinese first-year, sophomore, and junior university students.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Mobile phone addiction, physical activity, anxiety, and bedtime procrastination.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower mobile phone addiction and different mediator levels.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Cross-sectional survey over 10 weeks from October 2 to December 4, 2023.
- **Endpoints:** bedtime procrastination, physical activity, anxiety, mobile phone addiction
- **Effect or direction:** Mobile phone addiction correlated with bedtime procrastination (r=0.149, p<.01) and anxiety (r=0.497, p<.01), and negatively with physical activity (r=-0.447, p<.01). Physical activity and anxiety had substantial masking/mediating effects (50.3% and 25.1% respectively), with chain mediation 13.4%.
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse events; anxiety and low physical activity are burden/context factors.

## Limitations and mismatch

- Cross-sectional mediation; no causal intervention.
- Adjacent addiction exposure.
- Student sample in China.
