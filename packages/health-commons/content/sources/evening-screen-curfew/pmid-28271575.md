## Extraction summary

- **Population:** Convenience sample of young adults aged 18–25 years.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Electronic media use before bedtime and after getting into bed; separation of bedtime and shuteye time.
- **Comparator/control:** Immediate shuteye after going to bed or shorter shuteye latency.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Cross-sectional online survey.
- **Endpoints:** bedtime, shuteye latency, sleep quality, prebedtime media time
- **Effect or direction:** The study reported an average 39-minute gap between bedtime and shuteye time. Longer shuteye latency categories were more likely to be rated as poor sleepers, and shuteye latency was related to prebedtime media use.
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse events; identifies in-bed delay as a burden mechanism.
