## Methods snapshot
- **Design:** Randomized controlled comparative trial.
- **Participants:** planned/randomized per registry; completion sample extracted as 125 in accessible abstract snippets; Adult GP patients prescribed hypnotics.
- **Intervention/exposure:** CBT-I-focused self-help book.
- **Comparator/control:** Sleep hygiene advice.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Approximately 3-6 months / 4-5 months follow-up in accessible snippets.
- **Endpoints extracted:** hypnotic medication use, insomnia symptoms, anxiety, depression.

## Results extracted
- CBT-I self-help reduced daily hypnotic use and anxiety more favorably than sleep hygiene advice in extracted summary data.
- Insomnia symptoms improved in both groups; depression was not improved in extracted summary data.
- This is a boundary source for generic sleep-hygiene advice, not evidence for Digital Sunset.
