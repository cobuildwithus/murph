## Methods snapshot
- **Design:** Randomized controlled trial.
- **Participants:** randomized adolescents; Adolescents with chronic sleep reduction; mean age 15.44 years, 85.5% female.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Gradual sleep extension by advancing bedtimes, plus sleep hygiene advice.
- **Comparator/control:** Control group without sleep-extension instructions.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Three weeks including baseline week and two experimental weeks.
- **Endpoints extracted:** actigraphy bedtime, sleep onset, time in bed, sleep duration, sleep problems, depressive symptoms.

## Results extracted
- In the third week, the intervention group had earlier bedtimes and sleep onsets, more time in bed, and longer sleep duration.
- Chronic sleep reduction, insomnia symptoms, and depressive symptoms diminished significantly.
