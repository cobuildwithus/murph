This source is included for **light, circadian, and display-mechanism evidence** in the Digital Sunset extraction batch.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** crossover (2017).
- **People studied or addressed:** Healthy adults in randomized double-blind crossover placebo-controlled admissions.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Nighttime smartphone use with blue-light LED exposure.
- **Comparator/control:** Smartphone use with blue light blocked or placebo/control condition.
- **Role for Digital Sunset:** same_mechanism; context-only. This is not upgraded to direct protocol efficacy unless the source itself directly tested a no-personal-screen-before-bed behavior.

## Extracted source-local finding

Accessible abstracts describe a smartphone blue-light manipulation measuring endocrine and alertness outcomes; secondary summaries report increased alertness and reduced sleepiness with blue-light exposure, but exact estimates were not extracted.

## Endpoints extracted

serum melatonin, cortisol, body temperature, psychiatric measures, alertness/sleepiness

## Duration or follow-up

Two 3-day admissions; acute nighttime smartphone/light exposure.

## Safety/adverse events

No adverse-event extraction from accessible abstract/metadata; do not infer safety findings unless the full source reports them.

## Important limits and population mismatch

- Sample size and exact effects not extracted.
- Blue-light manipulation during smartphone use is not the same as no-screen curfew.
- Do not use for causal protocol claims without full-text review.
