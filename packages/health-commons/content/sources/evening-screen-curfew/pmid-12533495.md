This source is included for **light, circadian, and display-mechanism evidence** in the Digital Sunset extraction batch.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** acute physiology (2003).
- **People studied or addressed:** Male adults performing VDT tasks late at night.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Exciting or boring VDT tasks with a bright display at night.
- **Comparator/control:** VDT tasks with darker display conditions.
- **Role for Digital Sunset:** same_mechanism; context-only. This is not upgraded to direct protocol efficacy unless the source itself directly tested a no-personal-screen-before-bed behavior.

## Extracted source-local finding

Exciting VDT tasks and bright display conditions suppressed nocturnal decreases in temperature/heart rate and sleepiness; melatonin was significantly suppressed by the combination of exciting task and bright display.

## Endpoints extracted

melatonin, rectal temperature, heart rate, sleepiness

## Duration or follow-up

23:00–02:00 task period.

## Safety/adverse events

No adverse-event extraction from accessible abstract/metadata; do not infer safety findings unless the full source reports them.

## Important limits and population mismatch

- Very small male-only sample.
- VDT task and content arousal are confounded.
- Not a sleep-outcome or curfew trial.
