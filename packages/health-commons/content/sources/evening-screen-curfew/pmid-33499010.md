This source is included for **light, circadian, and display-mechanism evidence** in the Digital Sunset extraction batch.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** acute physiology (2021).
- **People studied or addressed:** Healthy male participants in preliminary evening smartphone/print conditions.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Evening smartphone use with short-wavelength light exposure.
- **Comparator/control:** Smartphone with blue-light filter and printed-material control.
- **Role for Digital Sunset:** adjacent_variant; context-only. This is not upgraded to direct protocol efficacy unless the source itself directly tested a no-personal-screen-before-bed behavior.

## Extracted source-local finding

Preliminary results indicate short-wavelength smartphone light affected circadian rhythm, evening sleepiness, sleep physiology, and morning alertness; a blue-light filter partially reduced negative effects.
