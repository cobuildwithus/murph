This source is included for **safety, burden, withdrawal, and clinical-boundary evidence** in the Digital Sunset extraction batch.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Cross-sectional Survey Analysis (2022).
- **People studied or addressed:** Korean waged workers with a marriage partner and at least one child in the 6th Korean Working Conditions Survey.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Use of work-related communication technology outside regular working hours.
- **Comparator/control:** No use of work-related communication technology outside regular working hours.
- **Role for Digital Sunset:** safety_boundary; context-only. This source is not upgraded to direct Digital Sunset efficacy unless the source itself directly tested no personal screens before bed.

## Extracted source-local finding

In 17,426 Korean waged workers with a spouse and at least one child, use of work-related communication technology outside regular working hours was associated with higher work-family conflict in fully adjusted analysis (OR 1.66, 95% CI 1.39-1.97).

## Endpoints extracted

work-family conflict, work interference with family, family interference with work, after-hours work communication

## Duration or follow-up

Cross-sectional survey assessment.

## Safety, adverse events, and clinical boundaries

Life-fit boundary. Digital Sunset should support realistic work boundaries and exceptions rather than ignoring after-hours job demands.

## Important limits and population mismatch

- Cross-sectional association; causality cannot be established.
- Occupational and family context may not generalize outside the Korean working-conditions sample.
- Not a bedtime screen-curfew intervention.
