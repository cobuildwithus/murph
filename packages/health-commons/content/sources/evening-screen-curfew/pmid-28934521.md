## Extraction summary

- **Population:** Nationally representative U.S. young adults aged 19–32 years.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Frequency of social media use in the 30 minutes before bed.
- **Comparator/control:** Rare/very rare social media checking before bed.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Cross-sectional survey.
- **Endpoints:** PROMIS sleep disturbance, prebed social media use
- **Effect or direction:** Often/very often checking social media before bed was associated with greater odds of sleep disturbance versus rarely/very rarely (adjusted OR 1.62; 95% CI 1.31–2.34; trend p=.007).
- **Safety/adverse events:** No intervention adverse events; population-level sleep-disturbance burden.

- Social media is one screen-content subtype, not all personal screens.
- Self-reported exposure and sleep disturbance.
