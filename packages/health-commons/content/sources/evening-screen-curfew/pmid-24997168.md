## Extraction summary

- **Population:** Representative Dutch adult general-population sample.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Self-regulation and bedtime procrastination as a pathway to sleep insufficiency.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower bedtime procrastination / stronger self-regulation.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Cross-sectional representative survey.
- **Endpoints:** actual vs intended bedtime, experienced insufficient sleep, self-regulation
- **Effect or direction:** A large proportion reported insufficient sleep and going to bed later than desired; bedtime procrastination mediated the association between self-regulation and experienced insufficient sleep.
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse events; highlights sleep-insufficiency burden from delayed bedtime.
