This source is included for **light, circadian, and display-mechanism evidence** in the Digital Sunset extraction batch.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** crossover (2005).
- **People studied or addressed:** Healthy male adults exposed to pre-sleep computer-game conditions.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Exciting computer game with a bright display before sleep.
- **Comparator/control:** Exciting game with darker display and lower-load control tasks with bright/dark displays.
- **Role for Digital Sunset:** same_mechanism; context-only. This is not upgraded to direct protocol efficacy unless the source itself directly tested a no-personal-screen-before-bed behavior.

## Extracted source-local finding

The exciting game condition prolonged sleep latency and shortened REM sleep; bright display effects on sleep variables were not clearly significant in this small study.

## Endpoints extracted

pre-sleep arousal, heart rate, sleep latency, slow-wave sleep, REM sleep

## Duration or follow-up

23:00–01:45 exposure, followed by scheduled sleep from 02:00–08:00.

## Safety/adverse events

No adverse-event extraction from accessible abstract/metadata; do not infer safety findings unless the full source reports them.

## Important limits and population mismatch

- Very small male-only sample.
- Combines content/arousal and display-light factors.
- Not a screen-curfew intervention.
