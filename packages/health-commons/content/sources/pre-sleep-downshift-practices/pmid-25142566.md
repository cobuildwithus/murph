Compared with self-monitoring, mindfulness arms showed greater baseline-to-post reductions in total wake time, presleep arousal, and insomnia severity; post hoc analyses found both interventions superior to self-monitor...

**Finding 1:** Compared with self-monitoring, mindfulness arms showed greater baseline-to-post reductions in total wake time, presleep arousal, and insomnia severity; post hoc analyses found both interventions superior to self-monitoring on patient-reported measures, with MBTI showing larger 6-month ISI reductions than MBSR and sustained response/remission signals.

**Finding 2:** The trial supports mindfulness-based treatment plausibility for chronic insomnia, but the intervention is a structured 8-week program with therapy elements and is not equivalent to a self-directed silent meditation before bed add-on.
