This source is included for **digital_app_guided_variants**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-33482627-online-group-mindfulness-sleep-mixed` — Online mindfulness training appeared equivalent for stress reduction, but PSQI sleep quality improved only in the prepandemic in-person group and not in pandemic in-person or videoconference groups, suggesting mixed or nonconfirmatory sleep findings.

**Why it matters:** A useful negative/mixed sleep-quality boundary in a digital group mindfulness context.

**Potential experiment signals:** `biomarker:sleep-onset-latency`.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use as support for silent bedtime meditation; sleep-quality findings were not clearly positive in the online pandemic group.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
