**Finding 1:** The meta-analysis found breathwork associated with lower stress in 12 RCTs with 785 adults (Hedges g=-0.35, 95% CI -0.55 to -0.14, p=.0009). Secondary outcomes were also lower for anxiety (k=20, g=-0.32, p<.0001) and depressive symptoms (k=18, g=-0.40, p<.0001). Authors urged caution because included studies had moderate risk of bias and objective outcomes were limited.

**Finding 2:** No lasting bad effects or adverse events were reported as directly attributable to breathwork in the included trials. One fast-paced breathwork study reported minor experiences such as hot flushes, shortness of breath, and sweating in a small subgroup, but participants continued and no dropouts were attributed to those events.
