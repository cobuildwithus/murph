This source is included for **measurement_endpoints_and_wearables**.

**Findings:** Actigraphy can underestimate sleep disturbance in older women with insomnia because wake epochs are often scored as sleep; accuracy declines when sleep is more disturbed.

**Why it matters:** Preserves a negative endpoint caveat: tracker sleep efficiency can look better precisely in people with more wake during sleep.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep-efficiency, wake-after-sleep-onset, total-sleep-time, sleep-onset-latency.

**Protocol takeaway:** When users report being awake despite a “good” wearable sleep score, do not dismiss the diary; the mismatch is expected in insomnia-like patterns.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
