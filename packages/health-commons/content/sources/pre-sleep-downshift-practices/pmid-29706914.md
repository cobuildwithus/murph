**Finding 1:** The brief pre-sleep mindfulness induction reduced pre-sleep arousal and improved perceived rest and overall sleep quality; sleep duration was not significantly changed. Reported estimates include pre-sleep arousal B=0.83 (95% CI 0.05 to 1.61), rest B=4.09 (0.92 to 7.26), overall sleep quality B=1.13 (0.26 to 2.00), and sleep duration B=0.40 (-0.38 to 1.18).
