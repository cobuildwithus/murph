**Finding 1:** Mixed-methods MBCT safety study nested in an RCT of 144 patients with bipolar disorder monitored AEs in 72 MBCT participants. Twenty-nine reported AEs, seven were not attributed to MBCT in interview, and reported AEs were not serious or lasting in this supervised setting. Baseline anxiety appeared to be a risk factor.
