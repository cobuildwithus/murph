This source is included for **measurement_endpoints_and_wearables**.

**Findings:** Adding heart-rate sensing did not solve the wake-detection problem for this multisensor device; low specificity means quiet wake can still be misclassified as sleep.

**Why it matters:** Shows that multisensor wearables can still have the classic actigraphy wake-detection problem.

**Potential experiment signals:** wearable-caveats, sleep-onset-latency, sleep-efficiency, total-sleep-time, wake-after-sleep-onset, sleep-staging.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not assume a multisensor wearable is reliable for wake during meditation or sleep onset; corroborate with diary.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
