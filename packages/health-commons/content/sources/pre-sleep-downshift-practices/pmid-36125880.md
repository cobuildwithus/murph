**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-36125880-headspace-calm-rct-evidence-quality` — The systematic review identified 14 Headspace RCTs and 1 Calm RCT through May 2021; the app evidence base was mostly nonclinical, often underpowered, and not sufficient to generalize brand claims to a silent bedtime protocol.
- `finding:pmid-36125880-conflict-and-preregistration-boundary` — The review found company-related conflicts of interest in a substantial fraction of Headspace trials and limited preregistration, supporting caution when interpreting app-brand efficacy claims.

**Why it matters:** Separates commercial app claims from protocol claims and highlights risk-of-bias and COI issues.

**Potential experiment signals:** None extracted as source-specific protocol endpoints; use as context/safety boundary.
