This source is included for **guidelines_and_comparator_context**.

**Findings:** The ACP pharmacologic evidence report found some short-term benefits for selected medications but judged trial harms evidence insufficient or low strength; observational evidence associated hypnotic use with dementia, fractures, and major injury, and FDA materials described risks including cognitive or behavioral changes and driving impairment. These findings are medication comparator and safety context, not meditation efficacy evidence.

**Why it matters:** It separates a low-risk behavioral self-experiment from medication evidence and medication harm trade-offs.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep-onset latency, total sleep time, global sleep outcome, adverse events, medication harms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for comparator and safety context only; medication findings should not be borrowed to support meditation efficacy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
