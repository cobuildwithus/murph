This source is included for **population_mismatch_special_groups**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-29101843-adolescent-sleep-behavior-mediation` — Postintervention improvements in social problems, attention problems, and aggressive behavior were mediated by moderate self-reported sleep-quality improvements, but not by actigraphy-assessed sleep-onset latency or diary sleep efficiency.

**Why it matters:** Useful as a pediatric/adolescent boundary source and as a reminder that perceived sleep quality can mediate downstream behavioral outcomes, but the intervention was a structured CBT-plus-mindfulness group program rather than silent bedtime meditation.

**Potential experiment signals:** `biomarker:sleep-onset-latency`, `biomarker:sleep-efficiency`

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use as direct support for general adult silent meditation before bed; treat as adolescent, structured group CBT/mindfulness context.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
