**Finding 1:** In 16 participants, both insomnia and control groups showed heart-rate and cardiac-output decreases plus stroke-volume and high-frequency HRV increases after sleep onset. Pre-ejection period increased after sleep onset in controls but remained unchanged in insomnia participants, and pre-ejection period was lower in insomnia participants before and after sleep onset, suggesting persistent sympathetic activation around sleep onset.
