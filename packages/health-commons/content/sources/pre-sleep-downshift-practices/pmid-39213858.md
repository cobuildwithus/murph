This source is included for **digital_app_guided_variants**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-39213858-smartphone-app-meta-sleep-effects` — The meta-analysis reported significant pooled effects favoring apps for self-reported insomnia (g=0.60, 95% CI 0.44 to 0.76; NNT=4.8) and sleep disturbances (g=0.70, 95% CI 0.58 to 0.83; NNT=4.1), plus smaller effects for awakenings, total sleep time, and sleep-onset latency.
- `finding:pmid-39213858-app-dropout-boundary` — The pooled app dropout rate was 13.1% and was higher than control conditions (OR=1.78, 95% CI 1.39 to 2.28); effects were nonsignificant for sleep efficiency, daytime sleepiness, dysfunctional beliefs, sleep hygiene, and wake after sleep onset.

**Why it matters:** Strong broad app-intervention context, while showing several endpoint and adherence boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** `biomarker:sleep-efficiency`, `biomarker:sleep-onset-latency`.

**Protocol takeaway:** Adjacent broad app evidence only; not meditation-specific and not silent bedtime practice evidence.
