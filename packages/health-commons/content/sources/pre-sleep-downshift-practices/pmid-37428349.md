**Finding 1:** In a 68-participant RCT, 3 minutes of nightly breathing relaxation with a huggable human-shaped device for 4 weeks reduced PSQI relative to control (F=3.81, p=0.025, η²=0.057), with stronger effects reported among participants without suicide risk and with fewer adverse childhood experiences.
