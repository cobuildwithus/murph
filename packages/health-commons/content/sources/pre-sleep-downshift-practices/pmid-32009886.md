**Finding 1:** In 176 participants, long sleep-onset latency was associated with higher LF normalized power after sleep onset and lower HF normalized power during the first 10 minutes in bed intending to sleep, before sleep onset, and after sleep onset. Long wake-after-sleep-onset groups also showed higher LF normalized power/LF-HF and lower HF normalized power for most cutoffs. Authors interpreted the pattern as greater autonomic arousal in people with sleep-onset and maintenance difficulty.
