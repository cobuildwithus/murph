This source is included for **digital_app_guided_variants**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-41862627-oura-app-sleep-hrv-result` — The trial reported significant group-by-time interactions for sleep efficiency, total sleep, deep and light sleep, and sleep-onset time; mindfulness participants improved after 10 days, with most gains persisting at follow-up except deep sleep, and session heart rate decreased while HRV increased.
- `finding:pmid-41862627-personal-burnout-and-population-boundary` — The mindfulness group showed increased personal burnout immediately after the intervention (p=0.021) that returned toward baseline at follow-up; the article was posted as an unedited early-access version and the population was healthy adults rather than people with insomnia.

**Why it matters:** Provides current healthy-adult wearable sleep and HRV context for digital mindfulness, with an explicit safety/mismatch caveat.

**Potential experiment signals:** `biomarker:sleep-efficiency`, `biomarker:sleep-onset-latency`, `biomarker:deep-sleep-minutes`, `biomarker:hrv-rmssd`, `biomarker:resting-heart-rate`.

**Protocol takeaway:** Adjacent healthy-adult app/wearable evidence only; not older adults, not clinical insomnia, and not silent pre-bed practice.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
