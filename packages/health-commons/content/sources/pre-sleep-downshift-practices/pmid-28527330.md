**Finding 1:** Across 43 studies (N=1427), participants completed about 64% of assigned home practice, roughly 30 minutes per day on 6 days per week, showing that standard programs carry a much larger practice burden than a short bedtime routine.

**Finding 2:** Across 28 studies (N=898), self-reported home practice showed a small significant association with outcomes (r=0.26, 95% CI 0.19 to 0.34); this is correlational and not sleep-specific.
