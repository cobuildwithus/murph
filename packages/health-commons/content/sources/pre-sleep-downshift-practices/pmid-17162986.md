This source is included for **mindfulness_sleep_reviews**.

**Findings:** Across 37 studies with 2,246 participants, the review reported reliable changes in several sleep parameters from psychological and behavioral therapies.

**Why it matters:** Frames the stronger behavioral-treatment comparator landscape behind later practice parameters.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep-onset latency, wake after sleep onset, sleep efficiency, total sleep time, global insomnia outcomes

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as comparator context for behavioral insomnia care; do not convert into a silent-meditation claim.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness to Silent Meditation Before Bed:** `same_mechanism`. Clinical insomnia treatment populations and therapist-delivered behavioral approaches differ from a brief self-administered pre-sleep meditation.

**Safety / adverse-event notes:** Adverse-event reporting was not prominent in the accessible summary.

**Limitations:** Evidence update covers older studies; heterogeneous behavioral packages; does not isolate mindfulness or silent bedtime meditation.
