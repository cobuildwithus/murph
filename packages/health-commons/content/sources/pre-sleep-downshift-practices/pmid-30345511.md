- `finding:pmid-30345511-youth-mbi-meta-analysis` — The meta-analysis found small positive effects across all RCTs for several cognition and mental-health outcomes; in active-control comparisons, significant effects were limited to mindfulness, depression, and anxiety/stress outcomes rather than sleep.

**Why it matters:** Helps preserve pediatric/adolescent population mismatch and broad MBI boundaries; benefits were small and active-control effects were narrower.

**Potential experiment signals:** No direct Murph sleep biomarker endpoint extracted; use as context only.
