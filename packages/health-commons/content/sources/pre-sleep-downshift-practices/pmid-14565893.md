**Finding 1:** In a 72-participant nap study, people with insomnia were more likely than good sleepers to report negative images; images were described as less controllable than verbal thoughts, and insomnia participants reported feeling less calm and relaxed after salient verbal thought. The source supports cognitive-content context, not protocol efficacy.
