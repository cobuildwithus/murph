This source is included for **measurement_endpoints_and_wearables**.

**Findings:** In depressed adults with insomnia, actigraphy was closer to PSG than sleep logs overall, but it still underestimated sleep latency and is vulnerable to quiet-wake misclassification; the depression population is an important mismatch for wellness claims.

**Why it matters:** Shows why sleep diary and actigraphy can disagree and why quiet wakefulness is a risk in populations with insomnia and mood symptoms.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep-onset-latency, sleep-efficiency, total-sleep-time, wake-after-sleep-onset, sleep-diary.

**Protocol takeaway:** Pair wearable metrics with a brief diary item on perceived sleep onset and mood/arousal; avoid causal claims from actigraphy alone.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
