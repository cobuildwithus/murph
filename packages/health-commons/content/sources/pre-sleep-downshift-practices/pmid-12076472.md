**Findings:** Older-adult sleep interventions require age-sensitive interpretation: established CBT-oriented approaches have separate comparator evidence, while silent bedtime meditation should not be promoted as equivalent based on this source.

**Why it matters:** Comparator context for older-adult sleep outcomes and for keeping meditation below better-established insomnia treatments.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep-onset-latency, sleep-efficiency, wake-after-sleep-onset, insomnia-symptoms, older-adult-comparator-context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only: silent meditation may be a low-burden wind-down experiment, but older adults with persistent insomnia should not be steered away from evidence-based insomnia care.
