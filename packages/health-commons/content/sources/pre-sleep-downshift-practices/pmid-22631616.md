This source is included for **mindfulness_sleep_reviews**.

**Findings:** The review concluded that CBT-I is effective for insomnia and may have more durable effects than medications, while evidence quality for some comparisons was low to moderate or very low.

**Why it matters:** Anchors the strongest behavioral comparator when interpreting mindfulness-based or simple pre-sleep practices.

**Potential experiment signals:** insomnia symptoms, sleep quality, sleep-onset latency, wake after sleep onset, psychological outcomes

**Protocol takeaway:** CBT-I remains a stronger comparator; silent meditation should be framed as a narrower downshift practice.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness to Silent Meditation Before Bed:** `same_mechanism`. CBT-I is multicomponent and usually structured, not a brief silent meditation before bed.

**Safety / adverse-event notes:** No protocol-specific adverse-event signal for meditation; medication comparisons are contextual only.

**Limitations:** Only a handful of direct CBT-I versus pharmacotherapy comparisons; evidence certainty varied.
