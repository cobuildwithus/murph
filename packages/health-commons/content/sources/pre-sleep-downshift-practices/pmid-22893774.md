This source is included for **presleep_arousal_rumination_mechanisms**.

**Findings:** In 182 primary insomnia patients and 54 controls, sleep-related cognitive arousal was more closely associated with sleep-onset and sleep-maintenance problems than general cognitive arousal.

**Why it matters:** It narrows the target from general mental activity to sleep-related cognitive arousal.

**Potential experiment signals:** Sleep-related cognitive arousal; Sleep-onset latency; Wake after sleep onset; Sleep efficiency

**Protocol takeaway:** Measure whether silent meditation reduces sleep-related arousal specifically; do not assume any quiet mental practice is beneficial.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

- **Population:** 182 primary insomnia patients and 54 healthy controls.
