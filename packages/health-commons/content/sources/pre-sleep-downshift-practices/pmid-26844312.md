This source is included for **mindfulness_sleep_reviews**.

**Findings:** The AHRQ review synthesized 181 unique studies and found CBT-I improved global and sleep-parameter outcomes, while harms evidence and several complementary-treatment questions remained limited.

**Why it matters:** Prevents overclaiming by showing how broad insomnia evidence is graded and where harms/effectiveness gaps remain.

**Potential experiment signals:** global insomnia outcomes, sleep-onset latency, wake after sleep onset, total sleep time, sleep efficiency, adverse effects

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as clinical landscape context and safety-evidence boundary, not as a direct meditation claim.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness to Silent Meditation Before Bed:** `same_mechanism`. Broad insomnia-management review, not a source for silent meditation before bed.

**Safety / adverse-event notes:** Harms evidence was limited, especially for many nonpharmacologic and complementary interventions.

**Limitations:** Heterogeneous treatments; varied outcome reporting and effect sizes; many short trials; large placebo response noted.
