This source is included for **guidelines_and_comparator_context**.

**Findings:** The AASM update stated that psychological and behavioral interventions are effective for chronic primary and secondary insomnia. It identified stimulus control, relaxation training, and cognitive behavioral therapy as individually effective standards, while sleep restriction, multicomponent therapy without cognitive therapy, biofeedback, and paradoxical intention were guideline-level options. It reported insufficient evidence for sleep hygiene education, imagery training, and cognitive therapy as single therapies.

**Why it matters:** It reinforces that effective behavioral insomnia care uses structured components such as stimulus control, relaxation training, sleep restriction, biofeedback, paradoxical intention, and CBT rather than generic sleep hygiene or meditation alone.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep-onset latency, sleep efficiency, insomnia symptoms, comparator hierarchy.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as comparator context only; it supports not treating silent meditation as equivalent to CBT-I or other structured behavioral therapies.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
