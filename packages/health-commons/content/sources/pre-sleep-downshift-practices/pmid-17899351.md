This source is included for **dose_duration_adherence_context**.

**Findings:** In an MBSR cohort, more formal practice was associated with improvements in mindfulness and several symptom/well-being outcomes, but the design does not establish causal dose-response and was not sleep-specific.

**Why it matters:** Provides adherence-dose context for longer MBSR assignments while guarding against causal claims from practice correlations.

**Potential experiment signals:** mindfulness facets, medical symptoms, psychological symptoms, well-being, home practice time.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as general practice-burden context only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
