**Finding 1:** From 5,000 randomly selected people, 2,327 completed the survey and 1,890 remained in the study sample after exclusions. The original PSAS was not judged adequate in this population; a PSAS-13 solution explained 48.5% of variance, with cognitive subscale α=.88 and somatic subscale α=.72. PSAS scores showed discriminant validity (R²=.24-.34), convergent relationships with sleep worry/beliefs, anxiety, and depression, and correlations with sleep parameters and daytime impairment.
