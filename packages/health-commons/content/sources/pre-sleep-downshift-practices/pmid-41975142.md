This source is included for **guidelines_and_comparator_context**.

**Findings:** The 2026 AASM guideline addresses combination treatment with CBT-I plus insomnia medication. It conditionally favors combination treatment over medication alone, but conditionally recommends against combination treatment over CBT-I alone. This supports comparator and escalation framing, not direct evidence for silent meditation.

**Why it matters:** It updates where combined CBT-I plus medication fits relative to CBT-I alone or medication alone, sharpening escalation boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** CBT-I comparator, pharmacotherapy comparator, total sleep time, daytime symptoms, treatment escalation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as comparator hierarchy and escalation context only; silent meditation should not be presented as comparable to CBT-I, medications, or combination care.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
