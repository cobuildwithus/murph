This source is included for **guidelines_and_comparator_context**.

**Findings:** The World Sleep Society endorsed the European guideline for insomnia diagnosis and treatment, providing international context for standard care. The endorsement is not direct evidence that silent meditation before bed improves sleep outcomes.

**Why it matters:** It broadens comparator context beyond U.S. sources and supports consistent international treatment-hierarchy framing.

**Potential experiment signals:** international guideline context, CBT-I comparator, diagnosis and treatment hierarchy.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only international endorsement; it does not create direct evidence for silent bedtime meditation.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
