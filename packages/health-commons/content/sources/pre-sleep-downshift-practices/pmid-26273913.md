This source is included for **guidelines_and_comparator_context**.

**Findings:** The SBSM guide provides practical guidance for actigraphy monitoring in clinical and research applications, including setup, scoring, and contextual interpretation. For Murph, it supports careful collection and diary pairing for objective sleep signals.

**Why it matters:** It gives practical guardrails for setting up, scoring, and pairing actigraphy with diaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** actigraphy, sleep diary pairing, sleep-onset latency, sleep efficiency, total sleep time.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as measurement-method support for combining diary and device data in a protocol self-test.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
