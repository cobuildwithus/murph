**Finding 1:** In a 21-day daily-measures study of 40 insomnia participants and 27 good sleepers, groups reported an equivalent number of minor stress events, but insomnia participants rated the impact of minor stressors and the intensity of major negative events higher, perceived life as more stressful, used more emotion-oriented coping, and reported greater pre-sleep arousal. The study concluded that arousal and coping skills have an important mediating role between stress and sleep.
