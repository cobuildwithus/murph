**Finding 1:** In this MS pilot trial, the primary Fitbit sleep-efficiency endpoint and PSQI did not show clear between-group superiority, while ISI improved at 10 weeks but not 16 weeks; quality-of-life and fatigue-related outcomes favored mindfulness cohorts.

**Finding 2:** The trial reported pain-related adverse events, including severe pain after a session and pain/shortness-of-breath after repositioning during body-scan practice; participants were advised to avoid or modify poses.

**Murph use:** Clinical comorbidity trial contributes boundary evidence; MS population and structured program limit generalization.
