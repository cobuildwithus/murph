This source is included for **mindfulness_sleep_reviews**.

**Findings:** The review found limited evidence for short-term subjective sleep improvements for some antidepressants and no evidence for amitriptyline or for long-term antidepressant use for insomnia.

**Why it matters:** Useful for contrasting a low-intensity behavioral downshift practice against medication safety uncertainties without making superiority claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** subjective sleep quality, sleep continuity, adverse events, tolerability
