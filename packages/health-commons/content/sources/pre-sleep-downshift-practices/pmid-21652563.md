This source is included for **measurement_endpoints_and_wearables**.

**Findings:** Wrist actigraphy can document rest/activity and nighttime sleep continuity over naturalistic periods, but sleep-onset latency is a weak endpoint and actigraphy should remain adjunctive to diaries and clinical assessment.

**Why it matters:** Clear practical source for what wrist actigraphy can and cannot measure in a self-experiment.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep-onset-latency, sleep-efficiency, total-sleep-time, wake-after-sleep-onset, circadian-patterns.

**Protocol takeaway:** Treat tracker sleep onset as noisy; prioritize repeated-night trends plus diary over single-night values.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
