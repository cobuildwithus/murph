This source is included for **mindfulness_sleep_reviews**.

**Findings:** The review reported reliable and durable changes in sleep parameters across nonpharmacologic insomnia treatments, with typical target symptoms such as sleep-onset latency and wake after sleep onset reduced toward about a 30-minute clinical threshold.

**Why it matters:** Provides historical comparator context for nonpharmacologic insomnia care and helps avoid treating a brief bedtime meditation as equivalent to full behavioral sleep therapy.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep-onset latency, wake after sleep onset, total sleep time, sleep efficiency, insomnia symptoms

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as background that behavioral downshift and nonpharmacologic insomnia treatments can improve sleep, while keeping claims about silent pre-sleep meditation indirect.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness to Silent Meditation Before Bed:** `same_mechanism`. Most included studies targeted clinical insomnia, not healthy self-experimenters using a brief silent meditation immediately before bed.

**Safety / adverse-event notes:** Adverse-event reporting was not a major focus in the accessible abstract-level record.

**Limitations:** Older evidence base; heterogeneous nonpharmacologic interventions; not specific to mindfulness or bedtime silent meditation.
