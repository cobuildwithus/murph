**Finding 1:** In an open-label randomized pilot among 69 caregivers, both HRV-BF groups had lower PSQI and higher HRV scores over 28 days, and the group adding home resonant-breathing practice before bedtime improved PSQI more than HRV-BF alone (p=0.001). The study lacked a no-HRVB control and did not use objective sleep measures.
