This source is included for **measurement_endpoints_and_wearables**.

**Findings:** In insomnia research, actigraphy may add limited or ambiguous information unless the study question is explicitly objective rest/activity; subjective insomnia experience and objective inactivity can diverge.

**Why it matters:** Preserves a modern caution against overvaluing actigraphy as the primary outcome in insomnia-like protocols.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep-onset-latency, sleep-efficiency, total-sleep-time, wake-after-sleep-onset, wearable-caveats.

**Protocol takeaway:** Make the diary and morning experience primary community outcomes; wearable data are secondary context.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
