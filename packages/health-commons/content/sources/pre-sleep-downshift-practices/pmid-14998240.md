**Finding 1:** Two experiments reported that participants exposed to anxious cognitive arousal or physiological arousal before sleep perceived longer sleep onset latency and shorter total sleep time, and showed larger discrepancies between self-report and actigraphy than no-manipulation participants. The source supports an arousal/sleep-perception mechanism rather than protocol efficacy.
