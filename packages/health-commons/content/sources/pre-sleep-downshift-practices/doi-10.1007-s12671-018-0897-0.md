**Finding 1:** Systematic review of 36 monitored RCTs of MBSR/MBCT found similar reported adverse-event counts in mindfulness and control groups (19 each). In MBSR trials, no serious adverse event was reported, and only 3 studies reported 6 intervention-related adverse events among 1231 participants. This supports a monitoring boundary but does not prove home bedtime practice is risk-free.
