This source is included for **measurement_endpoints_and_wearables**.

**Findings:** Actigraphy-derived SOL, TST, SE, and WASO are useful operational endpoints, but the movement-to-sleep assumption creates a quiet-wakefulness caveat for bedtime meditation studies.

**Why it matters:** Useful for naming and operationalizing Murph sleep endpoints while preserving the quiet-wake boundary.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep-onset-latency, sleep-efficiency, total-sleep-time, wake-after-sleep-onset, wearable-caveats.

**Protocol takeaway:** Define wearable sleep signals explicitly and record diary sleep onset so meditation stillness is not mistaken for sleep improvement.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
