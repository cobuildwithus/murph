This source is included for **mindfulness_sleep_reviews**.

**Findings:** The systematic review supports the accompanying AASM guideline on combination treatment; evidence for combination treatment was limited and nuanced, with related guidance suggesting combination therapy over medication alone but not over CBT-I alone.

**Why it matters:** Important current boundary source for chronic insomnia treatment choices, especially when users compare self-guided practices with CBT-I or medication.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep outcomes, total sleep time, daytime symptoms, adverse effects, treatment burden

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as current comparator and safety boundary; do not infer meditation efficacy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness to Silent Meditation Before Bed:** `general_guideline`. Combination clinical treatment with medication and CBT-I differs completely from silent meditation before bed.

**Safety / adverse-event notes:** Review evaluated potential treatment side effects and evidence gaps; medication-related safety considerations are not meditation-specific.

**Limitations:** Recent guideline-supporting review; evidence certainty for combination decisions was low or limited; not a meditation source.
