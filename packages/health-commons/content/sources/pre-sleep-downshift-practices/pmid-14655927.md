This source is included for **measurement_endpoints_and_wearables**.

**Findings:** In chronic insomnia, actigraphy and diary estimates diverged from PSG: actigraphy was more accurate than diary on several parameters and detected some treatment-related changes, but it underestimated sleep-onset latency and should be paired with diary/clinical context.

**Why it matters:** Direct insomnia-context source for interpreting actigraphy and sleep diary as endpoints; useful for avoiding overclaiming wearable sleep changes after a bedtime meditation experiment.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep-onset-latency, sleep-efficiency, total-sleep-time, wake-after-sleep-onset, sleep-diary.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use actigraphy/wearable estimates as trend signals paired with diary and subjective arousal; do not treat actigraphy changes as proof that silent meditation improved sleep physiology.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
