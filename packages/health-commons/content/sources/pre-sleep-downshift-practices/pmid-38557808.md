This source is included for **measurement_endpoints_and_wearables**.

**Findings:** Recent-generation wearables may estimate gross sleep duration reasonably, but wake specificity and multistate sleep staging remain imperfect and device-specific.

**Why it matters:** Recent review for common current devices; useful for device-specific caveats in community outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:** wearable-caveats, sleep-efficiency, total-sleep-time, wake-after-sleep-onset, sleep-onset-latency, sleep-staging.

**Protocol takeaway:** Treat deep sleep, REM, and stage-specific wearable changes as exploratory; prioritize diary and gross repeated-night trends.
