In 26 healthy adults, 4 weeks of mobile HRV-BF with 0.1 Hz breathing improved PSQI subjective sleep quality (t(24)=4.9127, p≤0.001, d=0.99) and increased some HRV indices, but did not improve actigraphy measures.
