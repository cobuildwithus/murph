**Finding 1:** The review included 58 studies and 72 interventions, with 54 interventions reported as effective for stress or anxiety outcomes. Effective practices tended to avoid fast-only or very brief protocols and often included guided training, multiple sessions, or longer-term practice.

**Finding 2:** The source highlights implementation caveats such as fast-only breathing, inadequate training for technical practices, and physically obstructive conditions; it does not directly test bedtime resonance breathing plus silent meditation for sleep.
