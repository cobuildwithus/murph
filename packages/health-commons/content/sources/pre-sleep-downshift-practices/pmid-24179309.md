**Finding 1:** Compared with PSG, wrist actigraphy showed high sleep sensitivity (0.965) and accuracy (0.863), but low wake specificity (0.329). Participant-specific accuracy was generally high, while wake after sleep onset was underestimated relative to PSG in the extracted results.
