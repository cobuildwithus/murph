**Finding 1:** In a cross-sectional survey of 364 healthy Chinese non-meditators, nonreactivity weakened the positive association between observing and poor sleep (β=-0.0154, p=.0123), with partial mediation through distress (β=-0.0065, 95% BCCI -0.0128 to -0.0004). The authors proposed that awareness plus acceptance/nonreactivity may improve sleep-related distress pathways, but the design is cross-sectional and not an intervention.
