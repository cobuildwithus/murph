**Finding 1:** The meta-analysis reported that mindfulness meditation may mildly improve some sleep parameters; overall analyses reported improvement for total wake time and sleep quality, while several other sleep outcomes were not significant.

**Finding 2:** The abstract and preview preserve a mixed pattern: no significant overall effects were reported for outcomes such as sleep-onset latency, total sleep time, wake after sleep onset, sleep efficiency, ISI, PSQI, and DBAS, while subgroup analyses showed some significant sleep-parameter signals.
