**Finding 1:** Systematic review analyzed 83 studies with 6703 participants; 55 studies reported at least one meditation-related adverse event. Reported total prevalence was 8.3% (95% CI 0.05 to 0.12), with 3.7% in experimental studies and 33.2% in observational studies. Use as safety landscape, not direct bedtime-protocol risk.
