This source is included for **guidelines_and_comparator_context**.

**Findings:** The 1999 AASM practice parameters identified stimulus control as a standard and several other behavioral approaches as guideline or option-level treatments, while single-component sleep hygiene education, imagery training, and cognitive therapy had insufficient evidence at that time. Meditation was not established as a stand-alone guideline comparator in this report.

**Why it matters:** It shows that formal insomnia evidence hierarchies long centered stimulus control, relaxation, sleep restriction, multicomponent CBT, and related behavioral treatments rather than meditation.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep-onset latency, wake after sleep onset, sleep efficiency, insomnia symptoms, comparator hierarchy.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as historical standard-of-care context; it does not establish efficacy for silent meditation before bed.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
