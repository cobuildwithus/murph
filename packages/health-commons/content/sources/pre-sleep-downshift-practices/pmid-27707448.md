This source is included for **measurement_endpoints_and_wearables**.

**Findings:** Reliable sleep assessment requires enough repeated nights and attention to sampling design; single-night wearable or diary changes are weak evidence for protocol effects.

**Why it matters:** Helps set minimum-night and diary design expectations for Murph experiments.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep-efficiency, total-sleep-time, sleep-diary, wearable-caveats.

**Protocol takeaway:** Require multi-night baselines and repeated protocol nights; avoid interpreting a one-night wearable improvement as a meaningful meditation effect.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
