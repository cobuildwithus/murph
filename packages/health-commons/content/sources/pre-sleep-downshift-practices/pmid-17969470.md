This source is included for **measurement_endpoints_and_wearables**.

**Findings:** Actigraphy has limited capacity to detect wake during sleep periods, especially when wakefulness is quiet or extended, so low movement can be scored as sleep despite PSG wake.

**Why it matters:** This is a core quiet-wakefulness caveat for any protocol that may involve lying still or meditating before sleep.

**Potential experiment signals:** wake-after-sleep-onset, sleep-efficiency, sleep-onset-latency, wearable-caveats.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not infer that a lower wearable sleep-onset latency after silent meditation necessarily means faster physiologic sleep onset; still meditation can reduce motion before true sleep.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
