**Finding 1:** The meta-analysis found clinically meaningful short-term CBT-I improvements, including about 19 minutes less sleep onset latency, about 26 minutes less wake after sleep onset, and about 10 percentage points higher sleep efficiency. This is CBT-I evidence, not evidence for a brief breathing/meditation bedtime routine.

**Finding 2:** Because CBT-I is a structured multi-component treatment, this review should be used as clinical-standard context and not as a direct protocol claim for pre-sleep resonance breathing or silent meditation.
