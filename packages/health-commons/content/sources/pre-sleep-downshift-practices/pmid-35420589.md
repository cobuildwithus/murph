This source is included for **digital_app_guided_variants**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-35420589-worry-related-sleep-self-report` — At two months, worry-related sleep disturbance decreased more with app-based mindfulness plus usual care than usual care alone (27% versus 6%; p=.001), with changes mediated by reduced worry and improved nonreactivity.
- `finding:pmid-35420589-objective-sleep-no-difference` — The trial reported no significant between-group differences in Fitbit total sleep time or sleep efficiency, making objective sleep effects mixed despite self-reported WRSD improvement.

**Why it matters:** Separates a plausible worry/nonreactivity mechanism from objective sleep endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** `biomarker:sleep-efficiency`.

**Protocol takeaway:** Adjacent mechanism evidence only: app-based anxiety-targeted training improved self-reported sleep disturbance but did not improve Fitbit TST or sleep efficiency between groups.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
