The pilot recruited 13 participants in 8 months, retained 77%, collected analyzable physiological and psychometric data at completion rates above 90%, and reported 91% app compliance among the 10 retained participants.

**Finding 1:** The pilot recruited 13 participants in 8 months, retained 77%, collected analyzable physiological and psychometric data at completion rates above 90%, and reported 91% app compliance among the 10 retained participants.

**Finding 2:** Uncontrolled preliminary subjective outcomes improved for PSQI (change −3.7, 95% CI −6.7 to −0.7), ISI (−4.5, 95% CI −7.7 to −1.4), and PSAS (−7.7, 95% CI −13.1 to −2.3), with a nondefinitive trend for FIRST (−2.5, 95% CI −5.9 to 0.9).

**Finding 3:** The intervention used 12-15 minute starter sleep-program tracks and later allowed 5-minute to more-than-1-hour tracks, but participants were instructed to practice 10-30 minutes at bedtime; 8 of 10 retained participants preferred shorter practice below 20 minutes, with 10-15 minutes most favored.
