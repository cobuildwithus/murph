MBSR versus waitlist showed a small improvement in subjective sleep quality (SMD -0.32, 95% CI -0.56 to -0.08) with substantial heterogeneity (I²=71%).

**Finding 1:** MBSR versus waitlist showed a small improvement in subjective sleep quality (SMD -0.32, 95% CI -0.56 to -0.08) with substantial heterogeneity (I²=71%).

**Finding 2:** The review reported no effect on sleep quality in chronic insomnia and cancer subgroups, and active-control comparisons did not improve sleep-related daytime impairments; this is a key overclaiming boundary.
