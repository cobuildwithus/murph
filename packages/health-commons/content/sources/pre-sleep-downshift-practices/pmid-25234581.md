In self-reported insomniacs, 20 minutes of slow paced breathing before sleep was reported to decrease sleep onset latency, number of awakenings, and awakening time during sleep, and to increase sleep efficiency.

**Finding 1:** In self-reported insomniacs, 20 minutes of slow paced breathing before sleep was reported to decrease sleep onset latency, number of awakenings, and awakening time during sleep, and to increase sleep efficiency.

**Finding 2:** The study reported lower high-frequency HRV power in self-reported insomniacs than in good sleepers and increased total HRV power during slow paced breathing among self-reported insomniacs.

**Finding 3:** The accessible abstract did not report adverse events or tolerability outcomes; full-text safety extraction was not available from the paywalled article.
