This source is included for **clinical_insomnia_mindfulness**.

**Findings:** The pre-post cohort reported ISI improvement after 8 weeks and reduced sedative/hypnotic use among many participants, but without a control group or isolated silent-bedtime exposure.

**Why it matters:** It adds a self-practice clinical insomnia source but highlights the limitations of pre-post and bundled sleep-hygiene designs.

**Potential experiment signals:** insomnia severity, sleep medication use, sleep quality, adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as clinical supervised/pre-post context only; do not treat as causal evidence for silent bedtime meditation.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
