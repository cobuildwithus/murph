This source is included for **population_mismatch_special_groups**.

**Findings:** Between-group PSQI changes favored mindfulness numerically at 6, 12, and 18 months but were not statistically significant; within the mindfulness arm, more weekly meditation practice was associated with improved sleep quality.

**Why it matters:** Preserve as population-mismatch sleep-quality evidence; weight-loss/eating/stress intervention is too bundled for protocol claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** PSQI, sleep-quality, adherence, mindfulness-practice.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as population-mismatch and null/mixed active-control evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
