This AASM guideline is included as a guardrail for overnight SpO₂ interpretation.

Consumer wearable oxygen trends can provide a useful personal signal, especially when repeated lows align with snoring, witnessed apneas, morning headaches, daytime sleepiness, or cardiometabolic risk. They should not be presented as a diagnosis of obstructive sleep apnea. Appropriate diagnosis depends on clinical evaluation and validated sleep testing.
