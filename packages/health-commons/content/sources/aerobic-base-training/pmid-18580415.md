This source is included for **direct_protocol_and_dose_evidence**.

## Findings

- **intervention_result: VO2max** — In 61 randomized healthy young adults, the 6-week moderate cycling arm increased VO2max by about 3.4 mL/kg/min (about 10%); higher-intensity arms produced larger VO2max improvements.
- **intervention_result: Resting heart rate and blood pressure** — The trial reported no significant changes in resting heart rate or blood pressure despite VO2max improvement.

## Why it matters

It is close in duration and aerobic modality, but the moderate arm used 4 days/week and 60-minute sessions, not 3 days/week.

## Potential experiment signals

- estimated VO2max
- resting heart rate
- morning blood pressure
- session adherence

## Protocol takeaway

Use as short-duration support that continuous moderate cycling can improve VO2max, while noting higher intensities improved VO2max more and resting HR/BP did not change.

## Claim use

`supports-protocol`.

## Directness and limitations

Young healthy sample and 4 days/week moderate arm differ from the target 3 days/week protocol.
