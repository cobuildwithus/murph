This source is included for **safety_clearance_boundaries**.

**Findings:** The ADA position statement treats physical activity and exercise in diabetes as beneficial but requiring attention to medications, hypoglycemia risk, glycemic monitoring, and diabetes complications.

**Why it matters:** Diabetes medications and glucose variability can change both safety and interpretation of cardio-session outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:** diabetes status, hypoglycemia symptoms, glucose-monitoring context, medication changes, glucose regulation, medication confounding.

**Protocol takeaway:** Users with diabetes should follow diabetes-specific clinical guidance and medication/glucose monitoring; the protocol should not override diabetes care plans.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
