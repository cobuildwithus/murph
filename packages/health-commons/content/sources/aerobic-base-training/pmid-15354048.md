This source is included for **direct_protocol_and_dose_evidence**.

## Findings

- **measurement_validation: Agreement between talk-test responses and ventilatory threshold** — In 16 healthy volunteers, the equivocal talk-test stage was not significantly different from ventilatory threshold on treadmill or cycle testing, and VO2 at ventilatory threshold and talk-test stages was well correlated.

## Why it matters

It supports the protocol’s conversational-intensity instruction across common cardio modalities, but it is not an outcome trial.

## Potential experiment signals

- talk-test pass/fail
- session heart-rate fidelity
- RPE
- estimated VO2max context

## Protocol takeaway

Use to explain that the talk test is a practical intensity anchor; do not use it to claim health outcomes by itself.

## Claim use

`supports-protocol`.

## Directness and limitations

Acute measurement validation only; it does not demonstrate training outcomes.
