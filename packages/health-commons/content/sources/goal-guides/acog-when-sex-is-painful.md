Use this guidance for pain at the vulva or vaginal opening and deeper pelvic pain during wanted sexual activity. Different causes need different treatment; persistent or severe symptoms, bleeding, sores, discharge, fever, or possible pregnancy with pelvic pain need clinical evaluation.
