This source is included for **Lens mechanism and melatonin suppression**.

**Findings:** Acute nighttime bright-light study showing that orange blue-blocker lenses prevented light-induced melatonin suppression while grey lenses did not.

**Why it matters:** Directly demonstrates that lens spectral filtering can change melatonin suppression even when illuminance is similar.

**Potential experiment signals:** salivary melatonin, light-induced melatonin suppression, lens spectral filtering

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mechanistic support for high-filtering amber/orange lenses, not as proof of improved sleep in ordinary users.
