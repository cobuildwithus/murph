This source is included for **Eye-strain claim calibration**.

**Why it matters:** Directly useful for blocking eye-strain, headache, and visual-performance overclaims on the bedtime sleep page.

**Potential experiment signals:** eye strain symptoms, headache, light sensitivity, visual performance

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep eye-care and visual-comfort claims out of the sleep experiment unless a separate page earns them.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`. Keep this classification when wiring claims so non-sleep spectacle claims do not drift onto the bedtime protocol.
