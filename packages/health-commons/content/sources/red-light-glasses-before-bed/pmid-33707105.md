This source is included for **Healthy-adult mixed/null trial**.

**Findings:** Healthy adults wearing blue-blocking glasses in the evening did not show clear objective sleep improvements. Some subjective signals were modest, but sleep duration and sleep quality were not reliably better. This is the main direct reminder that people with already-stable sleep may not see a measurable wearable effect.

**Why it matters:** Important check against overpromising benefits in already-healthy sleepers.

**Potential experiment signals:** actigraphy sleep duration, actigraphy sleep quality, subjective sleep, sleep diary

**Protocol takeaway:** Low-symptom users may see little or no objective signal.
