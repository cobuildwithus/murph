This source is included for **Direct adult device-use sleep/melatonin trial**.

**Findings:** This small adult crossover study tested blue-light shield eyewear for two hours before sleep during night-time device use. It reported higher melatonin and better sleep latency/efficiency versus control eyewear. It is one of the clearest small human sources for the two-hour pre-bed eyewear window, especially when screens are the exposure being managed.

**Why it matters:** One of the clearest small human sources for the two-hour pre-bed eyewear window.

**Potential experiment signals:** overnight melatonin secretion, sleep latency, sleep efficiency, subjective sleepiness

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports the protocol timing window, while the sample size keeps confidence modest.
