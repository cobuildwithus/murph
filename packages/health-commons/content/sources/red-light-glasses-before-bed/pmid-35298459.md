This source is included for **Light-dose guideline**.

**Findings:** This expert consensus recommends strong melanopic light exposure during the day, much lower melanopic exposure in the evening, and minimal light during sleep. The finding for this protocol is timing-specific: reduce bright evening light without treating daytime light as harmful.

**Why it matters:** Defines the broader practical target: adequate daytime light, low evening melanopic light, and very low light during sleep.

**Potential experiment signals:** melanopic equivalent daylight illuminance, daytime light, evening light, sleep-period light

**Protocol takeaway:** Use glasses as one possible evening-light tool, not an all-day blue-light avoidance rule.
