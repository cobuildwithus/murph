This source sits in the Murph shortlist bucket **Light-dose guideline**.

**Why it matters:** Defines the broader practical target: adequate daytime light, low evening melanopic light, and very low light during sleep.

**Potential Murph endpoints:** melanopic equivalent daylight illuminance, daytime light, evening light, sleep-period light

**Protocol takeaway:** Use glasses as one possible evening-light tool, not an all-day blue-light avoidance rule.
