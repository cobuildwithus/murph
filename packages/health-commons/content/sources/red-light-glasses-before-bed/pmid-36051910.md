This source is included for **Daytime/evening timing tradeoff**.

**Findings:** This systematic review found that blue light can improve alertness, reaction time, and cognitive performance in young adults. It also found that some studies reported worse sleep quality, shorter sleep duration, lower sleep efficiency, or longer sleep latency. The useful takeaway is not anti-blue-light; it is that daytime and evening exposure have different tradeoffs.

**Why it matters:** Helps avoid collapsing daytime light benefits into a blanket anti-blue-light message.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep timing, performance, alertness, wellbeing

**Protocol takeaway:** Preserve the distinction between healthy daytime light exposure and the evening reduction experiment.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. Keep this classification when wiring claims so timing-context reviews do not become direct support for the bedtime protocol.
