This source is included for **Clinical circadian split**.

**Findings:** This guideline treats circadian rhythm sleep-wake disorders as clinical timing problems, not generic sleep-hygiene issues. It supports targeted tools such as timed light, melatonin, and behavioral scheduling when matched to the disorder. For Murph, it separates clinician-guided circadian treatment from a general evening-glasses experiment.

**Why it matters:** Supports separating clinician-guided circadian-disorder protocols from a general bedtime eyewear self-experiment.

**Potential experiment signals:** circadian phase, sleep timing, guideline framing

**Protocol takeaway:** Disorder-specific timed light and darkness protocols belong on separate pages with clinical framing.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`. Keep this classification when wiring claims so clinical guidelines do not become direct evidence for the ordinary consumer protocol.
