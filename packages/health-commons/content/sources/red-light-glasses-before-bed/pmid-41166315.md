This source is included for **Pediatric adjacent variant**.

**Findings:** In Japanese schoolchildren with myopia, partial blue-blocking lenses worn before bed advanced bedtime and sleep onset. Irritability and disruptive daytime behavior improved, but salivary melatonin did not change. This supports a pediatric sleep-phase variant, not a direct adult wellness claim.

**Why it matters:** Useful adjacent evidence for separating pediatric sleep-phase questions from the ordinary adult bedtime protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** bedtime, sleep onset, behavior, melatonin

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep pediatric sleep-phase use on a separate page even when the underlying light mechanism overlaps.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. Keep this classification when wiring claims so pediatric trials do not become direct support for the ordinary adult bedtime protocol.
