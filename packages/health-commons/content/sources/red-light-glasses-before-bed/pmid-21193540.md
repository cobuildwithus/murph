This source is included for **Ambient room-light mechanism**.

**Findings:** Controlled room-light study showing ordinary evening room light can suppress melatonin and shorten melatonin duration; supports logging ambient light as a major confounder.

**Why it matters:** Shows why ambient room lighting can be as important as eyewear and should be tracked as a confounder.

**Potential experiment signals:** melatonin onset, melatonin duration, room-light exposure

**Protocol takeaway:** Log room light and avoid changing room lighting mid-experiment.
