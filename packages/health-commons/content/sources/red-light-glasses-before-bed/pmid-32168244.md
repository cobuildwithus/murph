This source is included for **Screen-filter and spectacle comparison**.

**Findings:** Smartphone study comparing software and spectacle strategies; blue-light-reducing spectacles reduced melatonin suppression, with coated lenses outperforming tinted lenses.

**Why it matters:** Supports separating lens spectral performance from generic “night mode” or tint labels.

**Potential experiment signals:** melatonin suppression, smartphone exposure, lens strategy comparison

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not treat software filters, tinted glasses, and coated high-filtering lenses as interchangeable.
