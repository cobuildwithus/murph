This source is included for **Objective sleep meta-analysis**.

**Findings:** This meta-analysis pooled three randomized crossover trials with actigraphy-measured sleep outcomes in adults using blue-light-blocking glasses. It found no statistically significant pooled improvement in sleep onset latency, total sleep time, sleep efficiency, or wake after sleep onset. This is the main objective-evidence check that keeps the protocol framed as a bounded self-test rather than a likely wearable improvement.

**Why it matters:** Best current null/objective counterweight: small adult crossover RCT synthesis with no statistically significant pooled actigraphy improvement.

**Potential experiment signals:** actigraphy sleep onset latency, total sleep time, sleep efficiency, wake after sleep onset

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to keep the protocol framed as a bounded self-experiment, not a reliable insomnia treatment.
