This source is included for **Safety-only psychiatric adjacent variant**.

**Findings:** In this newer inpatient mania RCT, blue-blocking glasses did not significantly improve Young Mania Rating Scale scores versus control lenses. The result contrasts with earlier enthusiasm for psychiatric virtual darkness. It reinforces that mood-disorder use needs clinician oversight and should not be folded into a general sleep experiment.

**Why it matters:** Strong reason to keep mania and mood-disorder protocols out of the general bedtime-glasses recipe.

**Potential experiment signals:** mania symptoms, psychiatric inpatient outcomes, safety

**Protocol takeaway:** Mood-disorder use needs clinician oversight and should not be folded into a general wellness protocol.
