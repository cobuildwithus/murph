This source is included for **Psychiatric virtual-darkness variant**.

**Findings:** Hospitalized patients with bipolar mania improved faster when blue-blocking glasses were added to usual treatment compared with placebo glasses. The study framed the intervention as virtual darkness under inpatient supervision. It supports the psychiatric mechanism, but it should not be generalized to wellness use.

**Why it matters:** Shows why mania treatment protocols and ordinary evening eyewear should remain separate.

**Potential experiment signals:** mania rating scales, actigraphy motor activity, adverse events

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep psychiatric virtual-darkness use clinician-guided and outside the ordinary bedtime self-experiment.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`. Keep this classification when wiring claims so psychiatric-adjacent trials do not become general support for the bedtime protocol.
