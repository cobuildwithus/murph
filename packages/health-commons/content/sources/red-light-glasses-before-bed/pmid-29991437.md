This source is included for **Wearable sleep-estimate context**.

**Findings:** The guideline supports actigraphy as a useful clinical tool for estimating sleep-wake patterns and circadian timing. It also emphasizes that actigraphy is a proxy and should be interpreted alongside sleep logs and clinical context. For Murph, wearable sleep data should be treated as trend evidence, not ground truth.

**Why it matters:** Keeps the evidence story honest about actigraphy and wearable sleep estimates.

**Potential experiment signals:** actigraphy sleep parameters, insomnia, circadian rhythm sleep-wake disorders

**Protocol takeaway:** Use wearable estimates as trend proxies and pair them with subjective sleep-onset logging.
