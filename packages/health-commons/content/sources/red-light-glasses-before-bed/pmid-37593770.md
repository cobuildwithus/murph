This source is included for **Claim calibration**.

**Findings:** The Cochrane review found little or no short-term benefit of blue-light filtering lenses for digital eye strain. Sleep-quality evidence was very uncertain and mixed across small trials, and several visual-performance or macular-health outcomes lacked trial evidence. This prevents using the bedtime protocol as an eye-health or retinal-protection claim.

**Why it matters:** Main overclaim guardrail for eye-strain, macular-health, and broad sleep claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** visual fatigue, sleep quality, macular health

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not present this protocol as eye care or retinal protection.
