This source is included for **Broad BBG clinical review**.

**Findings:** This systematic review found promising signals for blue-blocking glasses in insomnia, delayed sleep phase, jet lag, shift work, depression, and mania. It also showed the literature mixes very different populations and dosing strategies. The review is useful as a map, not as a single pooled claim for bedtime eyewear.

**Why it matters:** Maps the clinical landscape while warning against treating all blue-blocking glasses protocols as one thing.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep onset latency, insomnia symptoms, mood symptoms, circadian phase

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only evidence; split mood-disorder and circadian-disorder variants.
