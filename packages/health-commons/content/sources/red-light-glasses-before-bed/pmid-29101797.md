This source is included for **Ordinary bedtime glasses insomnia**.

**Findings:** Adults with insomnia symptoms wore amber lenses for two hours before bed in a randomized crossover trial. Compared with clear lenses, the amber condition improved several sleep and insomnia measures. This is one of the clearest dose anchors for the protocol, but the trial was small, short, and not enough to call the glasses an insomnia treatment.

**Why it matters:** Directly informs the 2-hour pre-bed design, while remaining a small pilot with imperfect blinding.

**Potential experiment signals:** insomnia symptoms, sleep diary, actigraphy total sleep time, sleep quality

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a practical dose anchor, not as definitive treatment evidence.
