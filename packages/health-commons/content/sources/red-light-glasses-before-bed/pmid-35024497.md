This source is included for **Adjacent pregnancy melatonin RCT**.

**Findings:** In third-trimester pregnancy, full blue-blocking glasses shifted the melatonin profile earlier compared with partial blockers. Melatonin onset and rise were the main signals. The study supports a circadian effect, but it does not establish that sleep will improve.

**Why it matters:** Shows melatonin timing can move even when sleep outcomes are not the main practical win.

**Potential experiment signals:** salivary melatonin onset, melatonin level, phase angle, subjective sleep

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not equate melatonin movement with reliable sleep improvement.
