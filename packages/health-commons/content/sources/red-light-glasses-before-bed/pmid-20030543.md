This source is included for **Early human RCT**.

**Findings:** In this small randomized trial, adults wearing amber lenses before bed reported better sleep quality than controls after two weeks. Positive affect also improved in the amber-lens group. The outcomes were subjective and the groups were imbalanced at baseline, so this is a useful early signal rather than a definitive result.

**Why it matters:** Useful historical positive anchor for subjective sleep and affect signals.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep quality, sleep diary, positive affect, negative affect

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports a bounded evening test but does not overcome the small-study and subjective-outcome limitations.
