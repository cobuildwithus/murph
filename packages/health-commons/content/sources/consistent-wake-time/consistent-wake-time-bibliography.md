This bibliography is the curation source for the Consistent Wake Time protocol page.

It preserves the research-run boundary: direct protocol evidence is short and mixed, observational sleep-regularity and social-jetlag evidence is context rather than causal proof, clinical insomnia and circadian-disorder sources are supervision or safety boundaries, and copyrighted PDFs are not stored in Git.

Use this page as the source map, not as an independent efficacy claim.
