This source is included for **Clinical lineage and population mismatch**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Supervised post-CABG cardiac-rehabilitation trial (2009).
- **People studied or addressed:** Coronary artery bypass surgery patients (59 participants).
- **Role in Murph:** clinical context and overclaim control; mixed evidence; screened or supervised clinical exercise, not unsupervised home training.
- **Most relevant Murph signals:** estimated VO2max / cardio-fitness proxy, quality-of-life context, clinician-supervised symptoms and adverse events.

## Why it matters for Norwegian 4x4

Shows another supervised cardiac-rehab setting in the 4x4 lineage and sharpens why cardiac-rehab evidence should stay separate from home self-experiments.

## What it found

**Findings:** This post-CABG randomized study compared aerobic interval training with continuous moderate exercise during supervised cardiac rehabilitation. It supports the clinical-rehabilitation lineage while reinforcing that post-surgical cardiac populations need separate safety framing.

## How Murph should use it

Adds cardiac-rehab lineage evidence while reinforcing that this is not unsupervised self-treatment evidence.

Use as cardiac-rehabilitation context and safety-boundary support only; do not use it to recommend unsupervised 4x4 after cardiac surgery.

## Important limits

Post-surgical cardiac rehabilitation has a different risk model and supervision structure.

The safe interpretation is narrower than “4x4 is always better.” Keep the population, supervision level, comparator, and exact interval dose visible before applying this source to a home wearable experiment.

## Plain-language takeaway

This helps explain where the 4x4 idea came from in supervised clinical exercise, but coronary-artery bypass patients are not the same as unscreened wellness users.
