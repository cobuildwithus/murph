This source is included for **Exact or close 4x4 trials**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Four-arm randomized training trial (2007).
- **People studied or addressed:** Moderately trained men (40 participants).
- **Role in Murph:** direct fit for the exact 4x4 claim; one of the first cards to read.
- **Most relevant Murph signals:** estimated VO2max / cardio-fitness proxy, session heart-rate fidelity, heart-rate recovery, symptoms and adherence.

## Why it matters for Norwegian 4x4

This is one of the cleanest direct tests of the classic long-interval 4x4 idea.

## What it found

**Findings:** Forty moderately trained men were randomized to 8 weeks of aerobic interval training, threshold training, or long slow distance-style training. The high-intensity interval group improved VO2max the most and also showed larger stroke-volume changes. This is one of the clearest direct demonstrations that a classic 4x4-style dose can improve cardiorespiratory fitness.

## How Murph should use it

Use this as one of the anchor studies for the exact 4x4 fitness claim.

## Important limits

The trial was small and controlled, so it should not be read as proof that every home user—or every wearable VO2max estimate—will respond the same way.

## Plain-language takeaway

If you want the simplest answer to “is the classic 4x4 dose biologically plausible for fitness?”, this is one of the best direct papers—but it is still a small, structured study.
