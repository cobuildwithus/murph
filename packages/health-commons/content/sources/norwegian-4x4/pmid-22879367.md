This source is included for **Safety and contraindications**.

**Why it matters:** Quantified supervised cardiac-rehabilitation safety registry; key low-but-not-zero risk framing.

**Findings:** The registry compared adverse events during supervised high-intensity and moderate-intensity exercise in coronary heart disease rehabilitation. Serious events were rare across many training hours, but they were not zero. The finding supports a low-risk-with-supervision frame, not a blanket claim that high intensity is safe for everyone.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use this source only within its `claimUse: safety-only` boundary. Do not use safety-only, mixed clinical, or adjacent-variant evidence as direct support for a general unsupervised self-experiment claim.
