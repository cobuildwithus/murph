This source is included for **Clinical lineage and population mismatch**.

**Findings:** This hypertension trial reported blood-pressure and myocardial-function improvements with aerobic interval training. That makes blood pressure a useful context signal, but the study should not turn the Murph protocol into a hypertension treatment plan.

**Why it matters:** Supports why morning blood pressure can be tracked as context, but also why hypertension treatment claims need clinician-supervised framing.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for blood-pressure context and hypertension guardrails only; the Murph protocol is not a hypertension treatment plan.
