This source is included for **Adjacent variants to split**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Three-arm randomized athlete interval-protocol trial (2023).
- **People studied or addressed:** Aerobically well-trained men (48 participants).
- **Role in Murph:** variant separation, not direct general-user prediction.
- **Most relevant Murph signals:** estimated VO2max / cardio-fitness proxy and protocol taxonomy.

## Why it matters for Norwegian 4x4

It helps keep sprint-interval training and aerobic 4x4 in different buckets.

## What it found

**Findings:** In well-trained men, aerobic high-intensity intervals improved VO2max more than sprint-interval comparators. The paper is useful because it separates 4x4-style aerobic intervals from supramaximal sprint work instead of treating all hard intervals as one thing.

## How Murph should use it

Use it for variant separation and taxonomy, not as a promise about average home-user results.

## Important limits

Well-trained athletes are not representative of most wearable users.

## Plain-language takeaway

Helpful for keeping sprint intervals and aerobic 4x4 separate; not a direct prediction for an average health-conscious user.
