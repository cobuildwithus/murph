This source is included for **Mixed and null clinical evidence**.

**Why it matters:** HFpEF clinical trial showing no significant primary peak-VO2 advantage for HIIT over comparators.

**Findings:** The HFpEF randomized trial found no statistically significant, clinically meaningful advantage for HIIT or moderate training over guideline-based physical-activity advice on peak VO2. Short-term changes did not become strong group differences at 12 months. The finding argues against applying healthy-adult 4x4 claims to HFpEF treatment.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use this source only within its `claimUse: context-only` boundary. Do not use safety-only, mixed clinical, or adjacent-variant evidence as direct support for a general unsupervised self-experiment claim.
