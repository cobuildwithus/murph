This source is included for **Protocol dose and variant separation**.

**Findings:** This 6-week comparison found the standard 4x4-style dose should be kept distinct from 1x4 HIIT and moderate continuous training when interpreting VO2max outcomes. It supports dose and variant separation while retaining the overweight/obese adult population caveat.

**Why it matters:** Directly supports keeping 4x4 separate from low-volume 1x4 and helps justify the 6-week wearable test window.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for 4x4 dose support and variant separation, while preserving the overweight/obese adult population caveat.
