This source is included for **Safety and contraindications**.

**Why it matters:** Cardiac-rehabilitation meta-analysis supporting efficacy and safety context under supervision.

**Findings:** The review found that HIIT and moderate continuous training can both improve fitness within cardiac rehabilitation. The included studies were supervised clinical programs, not ordinary home self-experiments. The finding supports safety and efficacy context only when screening and monitoring are part of the setup.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use this source only within its `claimUse: safety-only` boundary. Do not use safety-only, mixed clinical, or adjacent-variant evidence as direct support for a general unsupervised self-experiment claim.
