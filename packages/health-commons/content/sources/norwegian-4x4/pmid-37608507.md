This source is included for **Adjacent variants to split**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Three-arm randomized female athlete interval-protocol trial (2023).
- **People studied or addressed:** Aerobically well-trained females (81 participants).
- **Role in Murph:** variant separation, not direct general-user prediction.
- **Most relevant Murph signals:** estimated VO2max / cardio-fitness proxy and protocol taxonomy.

## Why it matters for Norwegian 4x4

It extends the same sprint-versus-aerobic interval split to well-trained women.

## What it found

**Findings:** In well-trained women, aerobic high-intensity intervals improved VO2max more than supramaximal sprint-interval training. That makes the paper useful for keeping 4x4-style aerobic intervals and sprint-interval protocols in different evidence buckets.

## How Murph should use it

Use it to support the sprint-versus-4x4 variant split, not as a direct prediction for unscreened home users.

## Important limits

The athlete setting is useful for taxonomy, but it is not the same as a general-population wearable experiment.

## Plain-language takeaway

Helpful for keeping sprint intervals and aerobic 4x4 separate, not for telling an average user exactly what to expect.
