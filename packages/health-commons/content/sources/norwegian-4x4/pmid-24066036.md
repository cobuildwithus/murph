This source is included for **Context-only rationale**.

**Why it matters:** Open-access meta-analysis supporting the general concept of VO2max trainability from HIIT while showing response variability.

**Findings:** This meta-analysis found that HIIT generally improves VO2max over short interventions. It also showed meaningful response variation across studies and participants. The finding supports estimated VO2max as a plausible signal while keeping expectations individualized.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use this source only within its `claimUse: context-only` boundary. Do not use safety-only, mixed clinical, or adjacent-variant evidence as direct support for a general unsupervised self-experiment claim.
