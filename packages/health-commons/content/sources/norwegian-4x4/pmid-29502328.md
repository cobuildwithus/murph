This source is included for **Clinical synthesis and overclaim guardrails**.

**Findings:** This updated CAD/HF systematic review and meta-analysis synthesizes interval-versus-continuous training evidence in clinical cardiovascular populations. It keeps disease-population outcomes visible while preventing those results from standing in for unscreened home 4x4 evidence.

**Why it matters:** Adds a clinical synthesis around CAD/HF trials so early positive disease-population studies do not dominate the practical claim set.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as clinical synthesis and overclaim guardrail; do not cite it as direct proof for unscreened home 4x4.
