This source is included for **Measurement and wearable endpoint context**.

**Findings:** The PROSPERO record registers a broad systematic review scope for blue-light filtering spectacle lenses; it is useful for protocol transparency and claim-audit boundaries, not efficacy extraction.

**Why it matters:** Separates broad spectacle-lens claims from bedtime sleep claims and preserves prospective-review scope for visual, macular, and sleep-wake endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** subjective sleep outcomes, visual performance, eye strain, macular health, protocol transparency

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as review-registration and claim-audit context; do not cite it as a completed review result.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Study design:** PROSPERO systematic-review registration.
- **Population or measurement target:** Adults in studies of blue-light filtering spectacle lenses.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Not applicable; review registration rather than intervention follow-up.
- **Directness to Red Light Glasses Before Bed:** `measurement_context`.
- **Population mismatch:** Broad spectacle-lens evidence scope, not specifically red/amber glasses before bed.
- **Safety/adverse-event notes:** No adverse-event finding is extracted from the PROSPERO registration.

## Limitations

- Registration source does not contain completed review effect estimates.
- Broad outcomes include vision and macular health, which are not the protocol target.
- Use the completed review, if extracted separately, as a separate source artifact.
