This source is included for **Safety, vision, or eye-health claim boundary**.

**Findings:** A Cochrane review of 17 randomized studies with 619 adults found no clear short-term visual-performance advantage for blue-light filtering spectacle lenses; sleep and macular-health evidence was uncertain or incomplete, and adverse events were infrequently reported.

**Why it matters:** This is a high-value boundary source for eye-strain, sleep, macular-health, and adverse-event claims around consumer blue-light filtering spectacles.

**Potential experiment signals:** subjective visual fatigue, critical flicker-fusion frequency, subjective sleep outcomes, macular health, adverse events

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety and claim-boundary evidence; do not present it as direct proof that red glasses before bed improve sleep.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Study design:** Cochrane systematic review of randomized trials.
- **Population or measurement target:** Adults in randomized trials of blue-light filtering spectacle lenses for visual, sleep, or macular-health outcomes.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Blue-light filtering spectacle lenses.
- **Comparator/control:** Non-blue-filtering or lower-filtering spectacle lenses.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Trial durations varied across included studies; many visual-performance outcomes were short-term.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** For short-term visual performance, the review did not show a clear advantage of blue-light filtering spectacle lenses over non-filtering lenses; sleep and macular-health conclusions were uncertain or not directly measured in several domains.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Adverse events were infrequently reported; reported events in included studies included headache, discomfort, lower mood, and depressive symptoms, with low certainty.
- **Limitations:** Heterogeneous lens products, outcomes, and follow-up durations.; Some outcomes relevant to marketing claims were not reported or were supported by low-certainty evidence.; The review is broader than the bedtime red/amber-glasses protocol.
- **Directness to Red Light Glasses Before Bed:** `measurement_context`.
- **Population mismatch:** Broad adult spectacle-lens trials; not limited to red lenses worn before bed in a home sleep experiment.
