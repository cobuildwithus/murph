This source is included for **Adjacent non-eyewear or population-mismatch context**.

**Findings:**

- The registry describes a planned or registered hospital-recovery trial testing whether filtering blue light at night after major cardiac surgery or SAH reduces inflammation or moderates recovery outcomes; no registry-owned efficacy result is extracted here.

**Why it matters:** Tracks a supervised hospital-recovery variant of blue-light filtering at night, separating surgical inflammation and recovery outcomes from home sleep-hygiene claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** inflammation, cognitive function, mood, sleep quality

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as registry and population-mismatch context only; do not use it to claim that consumer red-light glasses improve sleep or recovery.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Study design:** Clinical trial registry record for a hospital-recovery interventional study
- **Population or measurement target:** Adults in post-CABG, valve surgery, combined surgery, or SAH hospital recovery
- **Intervention/exposure or measurement:** nighttime blue-light-filtering goggles after surgery or SAH
- **Comparator/control:** Clear-lens or non-blue-filtering goggles/control goggles in a hospital-recovery registry design.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Nighttime hospital intervention with recovery follow-up; registry/listing context only
- **Directness to Red Light Glasses Before Bed:** `adjacent_variant`.
- **Population mismatch:** Hospitalized post-surgical or SAH recovery population with inflammation and recovery endpoints; not general adult bedtime use.
- **Safety/adverse-event notes:** No adverse-event result was extracted from the registry record; clinical surgical populations require clinician supervision.

## Limitations

- Registry-only extraction; no results were extracted.
- Clinical recovery and inflammation outcomes are not interchangeable with sleep-hygiene endpoints.
- Eligibility and comparator details should be rechecked before linking any later publication.
