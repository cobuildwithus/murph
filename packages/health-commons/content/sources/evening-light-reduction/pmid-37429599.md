This source is included for **Shift-work boundary guideline context**.

**Findings:**

- The Delphi guideline supports managing light exposure and sleep environment for shift workers, but it is consensus guidance and does not test bedtime red-light glasses.

**Why it matters:** Provides guideline-level support that light exposure and sleep environment matter for shift-worker sleep practices while keeping shift-work adaptation separate from bedtime glasses.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep timing, sleep environment, light exposure, shift-work safety

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as general guideline and shift-work boundary context only; do not present as glasses efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Study design:** Delphi consensus guideline
- **Population or measurement target:** Shift-worker sleep-practice guideline developed with experts invited from sleep, shift-work, and occupational-health fields
- **Intervention/exposure or measurement:** shift-worker sleep hygiene guidance including light exposure
- **Comparator/control:** No intervention comparator; Delphi consensus process with repeated expert review and consensus thresholds.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Delphi rounds; no intervention follow-up
- **Directness to Red Light Glasses Before Bed:** `general_guideline`.
- **Population mismatch:** Shift workers and occupational schedules differ from standard home bedtime use.
- **Safety/adverse-event notes:** Shift workers may need occupational-safety guidance; light avoidance at the wrong time can impair alertness during work or commute.

## Limitations

- Consensus guideline, not a randomized trial.
- Expert invite count is not an analyzed intervention sample.
- Guidance must be adapted for different shift schedules and safety-sensitive work.
