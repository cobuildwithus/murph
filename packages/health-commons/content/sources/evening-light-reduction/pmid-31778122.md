This source is included for **Measurement and wearable endpoint context**.

**Findings:** `finding:pmid-31778122-measurement-context`: Wristband Fitbit models generally provide convenient sleep estimates, but older nonsleep-staging models and stage-capable models have endpoint-specific biases; Fitbit outputs are not substitutes for PSG.

**Why it matters:** Device-specific systematic review/meta-analysis for a common consumer wearable category; useful for interpreting Fitbit-like sleep outputs.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep duration, sleep efficiency, WASO, sleep stages, consumer wearable validity

**Protocol takeaway:** Treat Fitbit metrics, especially sleep stages and WASO, as consumer-device estimates rather than definitive outcomes.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Fitbit models and firmware change; included studies are heterogeneous; not protocol efficacy evidence. Population mismatch: Measurement-method or device-validation source, not a direct bedtime red-light-glasses trial; population, device, or clinical context may not match ordinary self-experimenters.

**Safety notes:** No red-light-glasses adverse events were assessed.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
