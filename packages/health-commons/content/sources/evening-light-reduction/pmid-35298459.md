This source is included for **Lens dose and implementation guardrail**.

**Findings:** The consensus recommends high daytime melanopic exposure, evening melanopic exposure no higher than 10 lux starting at least three hours before bedtime, and very low light during sleep.

**Why it matters:** Sets the dose and timing boundary: evening light reduction should coexist with adequate daytime light and darkness during sleep.

**Potential experiment signals:** melanopic equivalent daylight illuminance, daytime indoor light exposure, evening indoor light exposure, sleep-period light exposure

**Protocol takeaway:** Use glasses as one possible evening-light tool, not an all-day blue-light avoidance rule.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction notes

- **Study design:** Expert consensus recommendations.
- **Population or measurement target:** Healthy adults aged 18 to 55 with regular day-active schedules.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Daily daytime, evening, sleep-period, and nighttime activity exposure windows.
- **Directness to Red Light Glasses Before Bed:** `same_mechanism`.
- **Population mismatch:** Recommendations target healthy adults 18 to 55 with regular schedules; special populations may need different thresholds.
- **Safety/adverse-event notes:** Avoid framing blue light as universally harmful; the same guidance supports adequate daytime melanopic light.

## Limitations

- Consensus guidance, not a randomized intervention trial.
- Does not evaluate red/amber glasses directly.
- Special populations, shift workers, children, and older adults are outside the main recommendation population.
