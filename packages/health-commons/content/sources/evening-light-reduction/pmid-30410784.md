This source is included for **Direct adult bedtime eyewear trial**.

**Findings:** `finding:pmid-30410784-feasibility-adherence`: The study reported high feasibility and acceptability: eligible screened participants consented, retention over the 2-week protocol was 92%, all completing participants finished the trial, median wear time exceeded the 180-minute target, and missing data were negligible.; `finding:pmid-30410784-preliminary-sleep-mood-safety`: Preliminary outcomes were in favorable directions for sleep/activity/mood, including lower evening activity and hypomanic-type symptom trajectories, but pulse oximetry sleep differences were not statistically significant; no adverse events or unintended effects were reported.

**Why it matters:** Direct protocol feasibility and adherence evidence in young adults with sleep complaints.

**Potential experiment signals:** feasibility, acceptability, adherence, sleep duration, sleep efficiency, activity level, hypomanic-type symptoms, adverse events

**Protocol takeaway:** The protocol was feasible and acceptable in young adults with sleep complaints, with no adverse events reported, but it was not powered as a definitive efficacy trial.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Small feasibility sample; 12 completed/analyzed; carry-over and washout limitations; not adequately powered for efficacy. Population mismatch: First-year undergraduates on campus with sleep complaints; younger and more schedule-disrupted than many adult users.

**Safety notes:** No adverse events or unintended effects were reported.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
