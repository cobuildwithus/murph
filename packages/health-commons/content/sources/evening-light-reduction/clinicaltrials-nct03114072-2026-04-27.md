This source is included for **Melanopic/evening-light mechanism**.

**Findings:** The registry describes a randomized pregnancy trial of nighttime blue-light-blocking glasses with melatonin and sleep-related endpoints; the source is a special-population registration rather than adult protocol efficacy evidence.

**Why it matters:** Shows that blue-light-blocking eyewear has been tested in a physiologically distinct pregnancy setting where sleep, mood, and melatonin endpoints matter.

**Potential experiment signals:** melatonin profile, sleep timing, actigraphy, mood

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as pregnancy/special-population context and mechanism boundary; do not generalize to ordinary adult red-light-glasses use.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Study design:** Clinical trial registry record for a randomized parallel-arm pregnancy trial.
- **Population or measurement target:** Nulliparous pregnant women in the third trimester.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Nighttime eyewear intervention during late pregnancy; registry-linked summaries describe a two-week intervention period.
- **Directness to Red Light Glasses Before Bed:** `clinical_supervised`.
- **Population mismatch:** Pregnancy, nulliparity, and third-trimester physiology are major population mismatches for the target adult bedtime protocol.
- **Safety/adverse-event notes:** No registry-owned adverse-event finding is extracted here; pregnancy-specific protocols should be treated as clinician-supervised rather than consumer self-experiment evidence.

## Limitations

- Registry record is not extracted as a source-owned efficacy result.
- Pregnancy population and comparator-lens properties may limit generalizability.
- Clinical status and registry status should be checked before claiming completion or outcomes.
