This source is included for **Direct adult bedtime eyewear trial**.

**Findings:** `finding:doi-10.1093-sleep-zsaa056.170-meta-analysis-mixed`: The abstract identified 10 studies (7 RCTs, 3 before-after) and reported inconsistent individual findings; it reported a modest objective SOL effect (Hedges g=-0.52, 95% CI -1.27 to 0.24, Z=-2.94, p=0.003), non-significant objective sleep efficiency, no significant objective TST/WASO, and a significant self-reported TST effect (Hedges g=0.61, 95% CI 0.14 to 1.09, p<0.01).; `finding:doi-10.1093-sleep-zsaa056.170-abstract-boundary`: This is an abstract-only synthesis; the reported objective SOL confidence interval appears to cross zero despite a significant p-value, and the authors noted that few studies had measured light levels or melatonin.

**Why it matters:** Useful historical synthesis, but abstract-only and superseded/qualified by later full syntheses.

**Potential experiment signals:** objective sleep onset latency, objective sleep efficiency, objective total sleep time, wake after sleep onset, self-reported total sleep time

**Protocol takeaway:** Abstract-level synthesis suggests mixed benefits; because it is abstract-only and internally hard to verify, use as context rather than a primary claim source.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Conference abstract; full PDF behind OUP rights; no participant count extracted; reported objective SOL p-value and confidence interval appear internally inconsistent, so preserve but flag uncertainty.
