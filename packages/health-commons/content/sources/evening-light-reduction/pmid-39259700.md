This source is included for **Lens dose and implementation guardrail**.

**Findings:** HEV-filtering lenses transmitted about 55% to 90% of 400-455 nm light and 75% to 95% of 455-500 nm light, indicating substantial residual blue/cyan transmission and product variability.

**Why it matters:** Prevents overgeneralizing HEV/blue-filtering lens claims to circadian red-light-glasses implementation.

**Potential experiment signals:** UV-C and UV-B blocking, UV-A blocking, 400-455 nm transmission, 455-500 nm transmission

**Protocol takeaway:** HEV-filtering or blue-control spectacle lenses should not be assumed to provide strong circadian-relevant filtering.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Study design:** Bench spectral-transmission study.
- **Population or measurement target:** Nine afocal plastic lenses: eight commercial HEV-filtering lenses and one clear control.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Bench measurement; no follow-up.
- **Directness to Red Light Glasses Before Bed:** `measurement_context`.
- **Population mismatch:** No human participants; lens testing only.
- **Safety/adverse-event notes:** All tested lenses blocked UV-C/UV-B; this does not establish evening circadian efficacy.

## Limitations

- No user outcomes.
- Only nine afocal plastic lenses tested.
- HEV hazard/filtering claims are not equivalent to circadian dose reduction.
