**Findings:**

- This review examines artificial blue-light safety for the eye, visual performance, and circadian functions, but it does not test bedtime glasses or justify eye-protection or sleep-efficacy claims for consumer eyewear.

**Why it matters:** Helps prevent the protocol page from overstating digital-eye-strain, retinal-protection, or device-safety claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** ocular safety, visual performance, circadian function, digital-device exposure

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for safety and claim boundaries only; cite eyewear sleep trials for protocol efficacy.

- **Comparator/control:** Not applicable; narrative safety review of artificial blue light from digital devices.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Not applicable

- Digital device exposure does not equal filtered eyewear before bed.
- Should not be used to claim eye disease prevention or sleep improvement.
