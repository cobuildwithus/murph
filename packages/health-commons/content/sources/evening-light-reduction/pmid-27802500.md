This source is included for **Adjacent non-eyewear or population-mismatch context**.

**Findings:** `finding:pmid-27802500-portable-screen-sleep-meta`: Bedtime device use was associated with inadequate sleep quantity, poor sleep quality, and excessive daytime sleepiness in meta-analysis, but this observational evidence does not test eyewear or prove causality.

**Why it matters:** Useful observational context for screen behavior, not evidence that glasses improve sleep.

**Potential experiment signals:** inadequate sleep quantity, poor sleep quality, daytime sleepiness, device access.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as screen-behavior context; do not infer eyewear efficacy or causality.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
