This source is included for **Direct adult bedtime eyewear trial**.

**Findings:** `finding:pmid-26730983-melatonin-sleep`: Blue-light shield eyewear was associated with higher overnight melatonin secretion than control eyewear (p<0.05), and sleep efficiency and sleep latency were reported as significantly superior for blue-light shield wearers (p<0.05 for both).; `finding:pmid-26730983-visual-quality-sleepiness`: Participants rated blue-light shield eyewear as providing acceptable visual quality, and reported greater sleepiness during portable-device use versus control eyewear; no specific adverse events were extracted.

**Why it matters:** Direct pre-bed eyewear-plus-device trial with both melatonin and sleep endpoints, close to the protocol mechanism.

**Potential experiment signals:** overnight melatonin secretion, sleep onset latency, sleep efficiency, sleepiness during device use, visual quality

**Protocol takeaway:** Blue-light shield eyewear during evening device use plausibly supports melatonin preservation and easier sleep initiation, but the study is small and device-specific.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Small sample; short-communication metadata; detailed adverse-event and long-term follow-up data were not extracted; device-use context may not match users who avoid screens. Population mismatch: Adults using a portable light-emitting device before bed; not a broad insomnia or healthy-sleeper trial.

**Safety notes:** Participants rated visual quality acceptable; no specific adverse events were extracted from accessible metadata.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
