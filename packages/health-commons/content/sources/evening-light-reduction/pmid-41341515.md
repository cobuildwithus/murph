This source is included for **Direct eyewear evidence synthesis**.

**Findings:** `finding:pmid-41341515-actigraphy-null`: Pooled effects were not statistically significant for SOL (MD -4.86 min; 95% CI -20.23 to 10.52; p=0.54), TST (MD 8.75 min; 95% CI -35.31 to 52.82; p=0.70), SE (MD -0.61; 95% CI -7.58 to 6.35; p=0.86), or WASO (MD -1.47 min; 95% CI -14.94 to 11.99; p=0.83).; `finding:pmid-41341515-study-base-limitations`: Only three small double-blind crossover RCTs met inclusion criteria, combining insomnia adults, recreational athletes, and healthy adults; the review concluded larger standardized trials are needed.

**Why it matters:** Best objective-sleep synthesis for direct adult eyewear trials; anchors null and mixed actigraphy expectations.

**Potential experiment signals:** actigraphy sleep onset latency, actigraphy total sleep time, actigraphy sleep efficiency, actigraphy wake after sleep onset

**Protocol takeaway:** Pooled actigraphy evidence did not show statistically significant improvements; protocol claims should avoid promising objective sleep gains for everyone.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Only three small crossover RCTs with n=49; objective actigraphy focus excludes subjective-only benefits; protocols and populations varied. Population mismatch: Combines insomnia adults, recreational athletes, and healthy adults.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event synthesis was extracted; review focused on actigraphy efficacy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
