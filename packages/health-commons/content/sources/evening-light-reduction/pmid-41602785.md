This source is included for **Safety, vision, or eye-health claim boundary**.

**Findings:** A 2026 updated narrative review found the effectiveness of blue-light-filtering spectacle lenses for visual fatigue, task performance, and AMD prevention remains inconclusive, noted subjective/objective sleep mismatch, and excluded some orange/amber/red broad-spectrum filters relevant to the protocol.

**Why it matters:** Most current review in this batch and useful for updated boundaries around symptoms, retinal protection, and sleep claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** vision-related symptoms, digital eye strain, retinal-protection claims, subjective sleep outcomes, objective sleep mismatch

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as current safety/context boundary; do not treat subjective-only sleep improvement as objective proof.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Study design:** Updated narrative review of blue-light filtering spectacle lenses.
- **Population or measurement target:** Users of blue-light-filtering spectacle lenses and people with vision-related symptoms.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Blue-light-filtering spectacle lenses.
- **Comparator/control:** Non-filtering lenses, other lens conditions, or usual care across reviewed sources.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Review source; no single intervention duration.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** The review concludes that evidence remains inconclusive for visual fatigue, task performance, and age-related macular degeneration prevention; it also notes that subjective sleep improvements are often not supported by objective measures and that broad-spectrum orange/amber/red filters were excluded.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No pooled adverse-event rate was extracted; main safety use is claim boundary and objective-subjective mismatch.
- **Limitations:** Narrative review.; Explicitly excludes some orange/amber/red broad-spectrum filters, limiting directness to the target protocol.
- **Directness to Red Light Glasses Before Bed:** `measurement_context`.
- **Population mismatch:** Blue-light filtering spectacle-lens review; red/amber bedtime lenses may be excluded or underrepresented.
