This source is included for **Melanopic/evening-light mechanism**.

**Findings:** `finding:doi-10.1177-1477153515584979-device-melatonin-suppression`: Accessible summaries report significant melatonin suppression after one and two hours of evening self-luminous-device exposure, with approximate suppression around 23% and 38%, respectively.

**Why it matters:** Supports the melanopic mechanism that evening short-wavelength device light can suppress melatonin, especially in adolescents.

**Potential experiment signals:** salivary melatonin, device exposure duration, ambient room light.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mechanism/context for evening light reduction; it is not an eyewear intervention and is adolescent-specific.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
