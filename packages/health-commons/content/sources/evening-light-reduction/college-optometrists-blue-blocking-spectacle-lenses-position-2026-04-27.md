**Findings:** The College of Optometrists position statement is a professional boundary source against broad blue-blocking spectacle-lens claims for visual performance, eye fatigue/discomfort, sleep quality, or macular health in the general population.

**Why it matters:** Strong claim-boundary source from an optometry professional body.

**Potential experiment signals:** visual performance, eye fatigue/discomfort, sleep quality, macular health

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only boundary language; do not promote visual-performance, eye-fatigue, sleep-quality, or macular-health claims from generic blue-blocking lenses.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
