**Findings:** `finding:pmid-24179309-measurement-context`: In 77 participants, actigraphy showed high overall sleep/wake accuracy and sensitivity for sleep, but low specificity for wake; WASO and sleep efficiency therefore need cautious interpretation.

**Why it matters:** Core validation source quantifying accuracy, sensitivity, and specificity of wrist actigraphy against PSG.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep duration, sleep efficiency, WASO, sleep-onset latency, wake detection

**Protocol takeaway:** Use actigraphy for trend-level sleep duration and efficiency signals, with special caution for wake and fragmentation metrics.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Validation context rather than intervention evidence; PSG-lab conditions and actigraphy algorithms may not match a user’s current wearable.
