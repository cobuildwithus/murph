This source is included for **Clinical or special-population supervised variant**.

**Findings:** The BLUES registry describes an ongoing supervised bipolar-disorder eyewear trial; it is a horizon-scanning and safety-boundary source rather than efficacy evidence for bedtime glasses.

**Why it matters:** Tracks a current supervised bipolar-disorder protocol that uses lens filtration dose, mood outcomes, sleep, actigraphy, and longer follow-up.

**Potential experiment signals:** mania symptoms, sleep, actigraphy, pupillometry, self-monitoring

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for horizon scanning and safety-boundary language; no efficacy claim should be made while the registry source lacks results.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Study design:** Clinical trial registry record for a randomized bipolar-disorder eyewear trial.
- **Population or measurement target:** Adults with bipolar disorder and manic symptoms in supervised clinical research.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Seven-day acute antimanic eyewear phase followed by state-adapted maintenance up to three months.
- **Directness to Red Light Glasses Before Bed:** `clinical_supervised`.
- **Population mismatch:** Bipolar disorder with manic symptoms and supervised psychiatric care.
- **Safety/adverse-event notes:** Supervised psychiatric use and exclusion criteria are important; no adverse-event result is extracted from the registry record here.

## Limitations

- Ongoing/enrolling registry source without extracted outcome results.
- Psychiatric symptoms and medication/clinical care contexts limit generalizability.
- Acute and maintenance eyewear schedules differ from a simple before-bed habit.
