This source is included for **Safety, vision, or eye-health claim boundary**.

**Findings:** AAO public guidance is a professional claim-boundary source for digital-device blue light, eye strain, and retinal-protection claims; it is not direct efficacy evidence for red glasses before bed.

**Why it matters:** Useful professional guidance for separating sleep/circadian hypotheses from eye-disease marketing claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** retinal-protection claim guardrails, digital eye strain, visual discomfort

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to avoid claiming that red glasses before bed protect the retina or cure digital eye strain.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

- **Population or measurement target:** General public and digital device users.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Digital-device blue-light exposure and blue-light blocking glasses guidance.
