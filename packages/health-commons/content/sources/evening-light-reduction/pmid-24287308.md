This source is included for **Lens dose and implementation guardrail**.

**Findings:** The review recommends photoreceptor-specific descriptions of light exposure, supporting melanopic/α-opic reporting for circadian protocol claims.

**Why it matters:** Reinforces that photopic lux and generic lens tint are inadequate for circadian claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** photoreceptor-specific light metrics, melanopsin-weighted exposure, reporting standards

**Protocol takeaway:** Describe light and lens effects with melanopic/photoreceptor-specific metrics when available; otherwise mark claims as uncertain.
