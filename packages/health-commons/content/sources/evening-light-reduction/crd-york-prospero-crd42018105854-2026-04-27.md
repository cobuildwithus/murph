This source is included for **Direct adult bedtime eyewear trial**.

**Findings:** The PROSPERO record registers a review of nighttime short-wavelength light-reduction interventions and sleep outcomes; it is context for review scope and protocol transparency, not an efficacy result.

**Why it matters:** Preserves a priori review scope for blue-light reduction at night and helps separate broad interventions from eyewear-specific claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** subjective sleep outcomes, objective sleep outcomes, short-wavelength light exposure, protocol transparency

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a review-registration/source-inventory anchor only; extract the completed review separately for effect findings.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

- **Study design:** PROSPERO systematic-review registration.
- **Population or measurement target:** Participants in trials of reducing short-wavelength light exposure at night.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Not applicable; review registration rather than intervention follow-up.
- **Directness to Red Light Glasses Before Bed:** `same_mechanism`.

- Intervention scope includes glasses and non-glasses approaches.
- Endpoint and eligibility details should be checked against the completed review source if used for efficacy.
