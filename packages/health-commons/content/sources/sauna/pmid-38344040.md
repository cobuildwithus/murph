This source is included for **safety and dose reality checks**.

**Findings:** Extreme-temperature sauna study used to reinforce that very high heat is a separate safety problem, especially for sporadic sauna users. Separates ordinary high-temperature dry sauna from extreme heat exposure and supports conservative entry dosing for sporadic users.

**Why it matters:** It gives concrete evidence that extreme sauna temperatures can create heat-stress symptoms and should not be normalized for sporadic users.

**Potential Murph endpoints/context:** heat distress symptoms, perceived strain, session heart rate, stop-condition design

**Protocol takeaway:** High-heat sauna variants need conservative onboarding, explicit stop conditions, and careful separation from ordinary dry-sauna experiments.
