This source is part of Murph's audited Finnish dry-sauna yes/likely corpus.

**Findings:** This meta-analysis pooled small RCT and quasi-experimental studies of sauna bathing and blood pressure in healthy adults. The main finding is a blood-pressure-lowering signal, but the evidence base is small and heterogeneous.

**Why it matters:** Expands the audited Finnish dry-sauna evidence corpus with a cardiovascular record marked lower priority for Murph v1 interpretation.

**Potential Murph endpoints/context:** blood pressure context, resting heart rate context

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as cardiovascular context around heat exposure, not as a guaranteed 21-day result-card endpoint.
