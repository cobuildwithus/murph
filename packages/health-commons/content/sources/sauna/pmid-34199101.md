This source is included for **Intervention design / reality checks**.

**Why it matters:** Shows that repeated sauna exposure can drive acclimation-style endurance adaptations, not just relaxation or acute recovery effects.

**Potential experiment signals:** Exercise HR, pace at fixed effort, HRV, resting HR, heat tolerance notes

**Protocol takeaway:** Expect some sauna effects to appear through repeated adaptation over weeks rather than through single-session recovery changes.
