**Findings:** This acute study measured recovery after sauna bathing and cardiac autonomic nervous system markers. The main finding is that post-sauna recovery can favorably modulate autonomic signals, making HRV and resting heart rate plausible near-term markers.

**Why it matters:** Directly relevant to HRV-style wearable outcomes
