This source is included for **Long-term Finnish cohort evidence**.

**Findings:** This prospective cohort study examined inflammation, sauna bathing, and all-cause mortality. The main finding is that inflammatory state may help explain or modify long-term sauna associations.

**Why it matters:** Suggests inflammatory state may partly shape the long-run relationship between sauna exposure and health outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:** Resting HR, HRV, illness/soreness logs, optional CRP or hs-CRP if lab-enabled experiments are added later

**Protocol takeaway:** If future experiments expand beyond wearables, inflammation markers are a sensible add-on for more mechanistic sauna experiments.
