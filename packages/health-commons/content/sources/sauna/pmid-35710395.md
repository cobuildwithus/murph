This source is included for **Intervention design / reality checks**.

**Findings:** This pilot randomized trial found no effect of four weeks of post-exercise sauna on targeted gut microbiota, intestinal barrier function, or hsCRP in healthy men. The main finding is useful null evidence.

**Why it matters:** Useful negative study

**Potential experiment signals:** avoid overclaiming gut effects

**Protocol takeaway:** Include null evidence in protocol notes.
