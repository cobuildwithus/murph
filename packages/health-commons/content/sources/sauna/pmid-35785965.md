**Findings:** This multi-arm randomized trial tested regular sauna bathing with exercise in sedentary adults with cardiovascular risk factors. The main finding is that exercise-plus-sauna can move cardiovascular outcomes in an intervention setting, but the combined design makes attribution harder.

**Why it matters:** Modern RCT combining exercise with sauna

**Potential experiment signals:** resting HR, BP, estimated VO2max
