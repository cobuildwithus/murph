**Findings:** This acute study measured cardiovascular function before and after a single sauna session in adults with cardiovascular risk factors. The main finding is that sauna can acutely change blood pressure, heart rate, arterial stiffness, and related markers.

**Why it matters:** One of the best acute Finnish-sauna physiology studies
