This source is included for **Evidence backbone**.

**Why it matters:** Best recent top-down read on post-exercise heating; it puts the mixed acute recovery literature next to the more promising longer adaptation signal.

**Potential experiment signals:** HRV, RHR, sleep, performance, soreness, training load

**Protocol takeaway:** Position post-exercise sauna as uncertain for immediate recovery but plausible for longer endurance adaptation, and design signals accordingly.

This review is particularly relevant for future exercise-plus-sauna variants, because it synthesizes post-exercise heat exposure rather than standalone sauna only.
