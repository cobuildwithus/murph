This source is included for **Acute and mechanistic**.

**Findings:** This Finnish-sauna physiology study compared single and repeated sauna sessions in trained and untrained men. The main finding is that repeated exposure may produce different immune and HSP-70 responses than a single session.

**Why it matters:** Shows repeated exposure may matter more than one-off sessions

**Potential experiment signals:** series vs single-session design

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports multi-week protocol instead of one-off.
