This source is included for **Acute and mechanistic**.

**Findings:** This acute physiology study compared sauna blood-pressure and heart-rate responses with submaximal dynamic exercise. The main finding is that a sauna bath can create cardiovascular responses comparable to light-to-moderate exercise stress.

**Why it matters:** Clarifies the immediate cardiovascular load of a sauna session and helps frame sauna as a measurable physiological stressor, not just a relaxation ritual.

**Potential experiment signals:** Session HR, pre/post BP, cooldown HR decay, perceived heat strain

**Protocol takeaway:** Treat a sauna session like a light-to-moderate cardiovascular stimulus and standardize cooldown if you want comparable pre/post data.
