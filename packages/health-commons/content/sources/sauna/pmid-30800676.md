This source is included for intervention-design and safety context.

**Findings:** Repeated dry-sauna physiology or body-composition study in young sedentary overweight men, useful for dose tolerance and body-size caveats. Adds body-size and sedentary-user tolerance context for safety and interpretation.

**Protocol takeaway:** transient body-mass loss and cardiovascular strain should be interpreted as heat and fluid physiology, not durable fat loss.
