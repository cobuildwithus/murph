This source is included for **Long-term Finnish cohort evidence**.

**Findings:** This prospective KIHD cohort paper examined sauna mortality associations across systolic blood-pressure context. The main finding is that baseline BP may modify how sauna exposure relates to long-term outcomes.

**Why it matters:** Highlights baseline blood pressure as a possible effect modifier, which is directly relevant to screening and personalization.

**Potential experiment signals:** Baseline BP, post-sauna BP, morning BP trend, resting HR

**Protocol takeaway:** Capture baseline BP before recommending higher-frequency sauna protocols and stratify interpretation by starting BP where possible.
