This source is included for **Intervention design / reality checks**.

**Findings:** This small crossover study tested three weeks of post-training sauna bathing in six competitive male distance runners. The main finding was a meaningful endurance-performance signal, probably related to blood-volume changes.

**Why it matters:** It adds a positive post-exercise sauna study to a mixed evidence picture. That matters for Bryan Johnson's source routine because the routine is usually performed after a workout.

**Potential experiment signals:** next-session performance, training load, soreness, hydration

**Protocol takeaway:** If sauna is stacked after training, do not judge the experiment from HRV alone. Track whether the next workout feels better, worse, or unchanged.

This is a small athlete study, so it should not be presented as proof that a high-burden daily Blueprint-style sauna routine will improve fitness or recovery for general users.
