This source is included for **evidence backbone** and claim calibration.

**Findings:** 2025 systematic review and meta-analysis of passive-heating RCTs used to calibrate cardiovascular and cardiometabolic claims with null and mixed evidence. Helps prevent overclaiming cardiometabolic or vascular outcomes by putting sauna and other passive-heating RCTs into one cautious synthesis.

**Why it matters:** The review found mostly null or mixed non-acute cardiometabolic and vascular results across passive-heating RCTs, with limited potential blood-pressure signal in subgroups.

**Potential Murph endpoints/context:** blood pressure context, resting heart rate context, HRV context, cardiometabolic-claim calibration

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep healthspan and cardiometabolic promises cautious, especially for short self-experiments.
