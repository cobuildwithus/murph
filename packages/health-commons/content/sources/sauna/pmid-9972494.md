This source is included for **fertility and male heat-safety guardrails**.

**Findings:** Short repeated-sauna human study used as additional male-fertility context because sperm movement parameters fell after exposure and recovered after cessation. Provides a second human sauna signal that scrotal heat exposure can transiently reduce sperm movement characteristics even when other semen measures are unchanged.

**Why it matters:** The study found reversible decreases in computer-assisted sperm movement parameters after two weeks of sauna exposure.

**Potential Murph endpoints/context:** fertility screening context, conception intent, safety guidance

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep fertility and trying-to-conceive caveats explicit on sauna protocols.
