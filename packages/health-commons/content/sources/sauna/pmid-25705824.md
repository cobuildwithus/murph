This source is included for **Long-term Finnish cohort evidence**.

**Findings:** This prospective KIHD cohort paper linked sauna frequency with fatal cardiovascular and all-cause mortality over long follow-up. The main finding is an inverse long-term association, not proof that a short experiment will move wearable metrics.

**Why it matters:** Landmark Kuopio cohort paper

**Potential experiment signals:** long-term dose logic, frequency framing

**Protocol takeaway:** Not a protocol paper, but strongest long-term signal.
