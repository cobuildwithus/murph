**Findings:** This prospective KIHD cohort paper associated frequent sauna bathing with lower pneumonia risk over long follow-up. The main finding is a respiratory long-horizon signal, not an immediate experiment outcome.

**Why it matters:** Respiratory-risk signal from Finnish cohort
