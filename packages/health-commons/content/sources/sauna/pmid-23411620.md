This source is included for **fertility and male heat-safety guardrails**.

**Findings:** Finnish-sauna longitudinal study used for male-fertility and heat-exposure guardrails, especially when a protocol mentions groin cooling or conception concerns. Adds direct human sauna evidence that repeated Finnish-sauna heat exposure can temporarily impair sperm parameters and sperm-cell molecular markers.

**Why it matters:** Repeated Finnish-sauna exposure in a small human time-course study was associated with reversible sperm-count, motility, mitochondrial-function, chromatin, and heat-stress-marker changes.

**Potential Murph endpoints/context:** fertility screening context, conception intent, safety guidance, heat-dose burden

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep fertility concerns in safety screening. Do not imply that source-specific groin-cooling tactics erase fertility risk without direct evidence.
