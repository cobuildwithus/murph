This source is included for modality separation and heat-strain context.

**Findings:** Dry-vs-steam sauna comparison showing that humidity and modality change heat strain, reinforcing why dry sauna and steam-room protocols should not be merged. Gives primary-study support for separating dry sauna from wet steam heat in protocol and interpretation.

**Protocol takeaway:** humidity can change physiological strain enough that dry-sauna and steam-room protocols should not be silently merged.
