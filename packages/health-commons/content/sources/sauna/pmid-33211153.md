This source is included for **Intervention design / reality checks**.

**Findings:** This controlled study tested intermittent post-exercise sauna bathing in trained middle-distance runners. The main finding is that repeated post-exercise sauna improved markers of exercise capacity in hot and temperate conditions.

**Why it matters:** Performance-oriented repeated sauna intervention

**Potential experiment signals:** time-to-exhaustion, subjective exertion, HRV

**Protocol takeaway:** Relevant for future athlete-focused protocols.
