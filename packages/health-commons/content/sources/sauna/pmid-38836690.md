This source is included for long-term Finnish cohort context.

**Findings:** KIHD study of 2,012 Finnish men evaluating cross-sectional and longitudinal associations between sauna exposure and cardiorespiratory fitness over an 11-year follow-up. Adds cardiorespiratory-fitness context to the KIHD sauna evidence base without turning sauna into an exercise substitute.

**Protocol takeaway:** it belongs in rationale and future fitness-context discussions, not in the near-term wearable result promise for the first dry-sauna protocol.
