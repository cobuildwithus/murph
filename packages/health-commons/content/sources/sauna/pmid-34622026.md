This source is included for **Acute and mechanistic**.

**Findings:** This crossover study compared standalone sauna with exercise followed by sauna in sauna-experienced adults. The main finding is that sauna-only and exercise-plus-sauna conditions produce different cardiovascular responses.

**Why it matters:** Separates sauna-only effects from exercise-plus-sauna effects, which is crucial for clean causal attribution.

**Potential experiment signals:** Resting HR, HRV, BP, arterial stiffness proxies if available, exercise covariate logging

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not mix post-workout sauna and standalone sauna in the same experiment unless exercise timing and load are explicitly tracked.
