This source is included for **sweat and detox-claim caveats**.

**Findings:** Adjacent sweat-toxicant review added only to calibrate toxin-excretion claims; it is not sauna-specific proof that a sauna protocol lowers body burden. Gives a review-level sweat toxicant context while making clear that sauna-specific toxin, microplastic, or clinical-clearance claims remain unproven for Murph protocols.

**Why it matters:** It helps calibrate sweat-toxicant language, but it does not establish that a sauna protocol lowers clinically meaningful toxin or microplastic burden.

**Potential Murph endpoints/context:** claim calibration, sweat context, safety copy

**Protocol takeaway:** Sauna pages should avoid translating sweat-detection evidence into detox outcome promises.
