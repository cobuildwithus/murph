This source is included for acute and mechanistic guardrails.

**Findings:** Finnish-sauna crossover study showing that one or two short sauna bouts did not acutely improve brachial artery flow-mediated dilation or reactive hyperemia in healthy middle-aged and older adults.

**Protocol takeaway:** a standard brief Finnish-sauna exposure may not move acute endothelial-function markers in healthy middle-aged and older adults, so near-term cardiovascular claims should stay cautious.
