This source is included for **Long-term Finnish cohort evidence**.

**Findings:** This prospective KIHD paper examined sauna frequency, socioeconomic status, and mortality. The main finding is that frequent sauna bathing may offset higher mortality risk linked with low socioeconomic status, but this remains observational and context-sensitive.

**Why it matters:** Shows that sauna associations may interact with broader baseline risk and context rather than acting as an isolated magic bullet.

**Potential experiment signals:** Context only, context only, not a short-term signal beyond exposure adherence and baseline risk capture

**Protocol takeaway:** Use this as framing evidence, not as a short-horizon outcome expectation; record baseline context and risk burden.
