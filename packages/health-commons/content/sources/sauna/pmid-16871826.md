This source is included for **Evidence backbone**.

**Findings:** This safety review summarizes older evidence on sauna physiology, benefits, and adverse contexts. The main finding is that sauna exposure affects circulation, thermoregulation, and symptoms enough to require risk screening.

**Why it matters:** Anchor review that framed many later sauna health discussions and remains useful for older safety and physiology context.

**Potential experiment signals:** Safety screening, session symptoms, BP context, general physiology framing

**Protocol takeaway:** Useful as an older anchor review and safety context source, but do not let it override newer modality-specific intervention studies.
