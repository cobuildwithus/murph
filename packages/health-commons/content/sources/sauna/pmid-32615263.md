This source is included for **acute and mechanistic** cardiovascular context.

**Findings:** Acute Finnish-sauna vascular study in 22 adults with stable coronary artery disease, used as cardiovascular-context evidence while preserving clinical-population and acute-outcome caveats. Adds a clinically relevant acute vascular response paper without implying that a brief self-experiment should promise durable vascular change.

**Why it matters:** It supports acute vascular-response plausibility while reinforcing that clinical-status and endpoint choice matter.

**Potential Murph endpoints/context:** blood pressure context, session heart rate, vascular-function context, safety screening

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context, not as a 21-day wearable-result promise.
