This source sits in the Murph shortlist bucket **Intervention design / reality checks**.

**Why it matters:** Provides a concrete progressive dosing model and shows that repeated sauna exposure can move functional outcomes over weeks, even in a clinical group.

**Potential Murph endpoints:** Resting HR, BP, activity tolerance, step count, subjective exertion, functional capacity proxies

**Protocol takeaway:** A ramped protocol matters; start short, monitor tolerance, and increase minutes across weeks rather than jumping to maximal heat exposure.
