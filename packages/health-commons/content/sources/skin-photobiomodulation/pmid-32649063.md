This source is included for **direct home/facial red+NIR or LED-mask benefit evidence**.

**Findings:** All 24 participants reportedly completed the pilot. The LED-treated side had higher skin elasticity than the control side at 6 and 8 weeks, and texture image analysis improved at 8 weeks. Accessible summaries report no pain or adverse reactions, but the full paper should be checked for adverse-event definitions.

**Why it matters:** It directly tests a home-use red plus near-infrared facial device and includes objective texture/elasticity measures relevant to self-tracking.

**Potential experiment signals:** Elasticity measures, standardized texture photos, side-to-side differences, discomfort, erythema, and adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as direct but small-study support for 637/854 nm home-use facial LED effects on elasticity and texture over roughly 6-8 weeks.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
