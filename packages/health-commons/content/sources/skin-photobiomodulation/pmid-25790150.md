This source is included for **eye and face-adjacent ocular safety boundary**.

**Findings:** The review covered 48 case reports and notes light-emitting therapy associated with pigmented-tissue damage leading to anterior uveitis and iris atrophy in the reviewed literature.

**Why it matters:** It broadens the face-adjacent ocular safety boundary across cosmetic modalities.

**Potential experiment signals:** eye inflammation, visual impairment, facial treatment proximity to eye.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only or context-only evidence; do not use for direct skin-outcome claims.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
