This source is included for **exclusion anchor: PDT-like, photoconverter, or adjunctive PRP protocol**.

**Findings:** The trial randomized 32 participants, with 30 completing, across KLOX LED plus gel/base-gel comparisons. Reported improvements included wrinkle and brow-position measures, and adverse events were generally transient and mild, but the gel/light activation protocol makes it out-of-scope for LED-only red/NIR PBM claims.

**Why it matters:** Facial rejuvenation outcomes are tempting to include, but the gel/light activation makes this an adjacent protocol family.

**Potential experiment signals:** Why-excluded status, wrinkle score, brow positioning, perioral wrinkling, patient satisfaction, transient erythema/edema, and no serious adverse events reported in the extracted source.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use as direct protocol evidence; include only to explain exclusion of gel/light activation protocols.

**Claim use:** `do-not-use`.
