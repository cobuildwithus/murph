This source is included for **adjunctive recent-procedure or filler context**.

**Findings:** In 15 subjects receiving IPL for facial photodamage, one facial side received LED photomodulation immediately after IPL and again at 24 hours. Indexed summaries report reduced erythema and discomfort after IPL, but the study does not test standalone LED rejuvenation.

**Why it matters:** Highlights that LED may be studied as a recovery adjunct after energy-based procedures, which is a separate protocol question.

**Potential experiment signals:** Post-IPL erythema, discomfort, immediate and 24-hour treatment timing, and one-week recovery assessment.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only for recent-procedure boundary language around IPL; do not cite for direct photoaging benefits of LED.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
