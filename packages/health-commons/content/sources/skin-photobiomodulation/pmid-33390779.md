This source is included for **eye and face-adjacent ocular safety boundary**.

**Findings:** The review describes PBM/NIR around 600–1000 nm, low irradiance intended to modulate rather than destroy tissue, hormetic/biphasic dose response, and NIR penetration through ocular media.

**Why it matters:** It separates intended ophthalmic PBM from unintended eye exposure during facial skin protocols.

**Potential experiment signals:** wavelength, irradiance, dose-response, retinal exposure, ocular disease status.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only or context-only evidence; do not use for direct skin-outcome claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
