This source is included for **wavelength sibling: red-only, amber/yellow, or broad-spectrum evidence**.

**Findings:** The source reports 90 participants treated with a 590 nm full-face LED array at 0.1 J/cm² for eight treatments over 4 weeks. Extracted summaries report photoaging reduction in 90% of patients, about 10% optical profilometry improvement, collagen I increases averaging 28%, MMP-1 reductions averaging 4%, and no side effects or pain. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-15654716`.

**Why it matters:** It links clinical appearance endpoints with mechanistic biopsy endpoints, but in an amber-light protocol rather than the target wavelength combination.

**Potential experiment signals:** digital imaging photoaging score, optical profilometry, collagen I, MMP-1, pain/side effects.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mechanism and endpoint context for collagen remodeling; do not use as direct red+NIR efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
