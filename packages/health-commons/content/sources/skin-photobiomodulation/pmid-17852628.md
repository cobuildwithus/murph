This source is included for **wavelength sibling: red-only, amber/yellow, or broad-spectrum evidence**.

**Findings:** The source reports a randomized double-blind placebo-controlled self-reporting study of daily 1072 nm light around the eyes for 6–8 weeks. Extracted summaries report that 52–57% of volunteers identified improvement in fine lines or wrinkles and 37–46% identified improvement in eye bags. Participant count and objective effect estimates were not extracted. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-17852628`.

**Why it matters:** It provides a NIR-adjacent periocular outcome signal while emphasizing that self-report improvements are not objective facial-photoaging evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** self-reported fine lines, self-reported wrinkles, self-reported eye bags, daily-use adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as wavelength-sibling context and a caution about self-reported outcomes.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
