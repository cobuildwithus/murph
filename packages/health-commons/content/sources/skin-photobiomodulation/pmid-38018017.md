This source is included for **skin-of-color, visible-light pigmentation, melasma, or PIH safety boundary**.

**Findings:** Review states PBM shows potential beyond melanogenesis inhibition, including reducing erythema/vascularization and improving dermal conditions, but evidence remains emerging.

**Why it matters:** Use to frame melasma/PIH as a special population requiring cautious monitoring, not as routine anti-aging evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** melasma exclusion/caution, pigment flare diary, photoprotection adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Melasma PBM literature suggests potential but remains heterogeneous and distinct from photoaging protocols. Do not generalize melasma treatment protocols to cosmetic skin texture dosing.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

### Extraction notes

- **Population:** PBM and melasma-treatment literature.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Photobiomodulation for melasma treatment across reviewed studies.
- **Comparator/control:** Varied comparator and non-comparator evidence across included literature.
