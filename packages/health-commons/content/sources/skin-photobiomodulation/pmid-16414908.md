This source is included for **core facial LED benefit evidence; clinic-supervised/non-wearable geometry**.

**Findings:** Thirty-one subjects with facial rhytids received nine combined 633 nm and 830 nm LED treatments. Extracted abstracts describe significant improvements in multiple periorbital profilometry measures by week 12, 52% of participants showing 25-50% photoaging-score improvement, and 81% reporting meaningful periorbital wrinkle improvement.

**Why it matters:** It is an early direct facial red+NIR study with wavelengths that recur throughout modern consumer LED-mask protocols.

**Potential experiment signals:** Periorbital profilometry, photoaging score, satisfaction, and whether a modern mask can deliver comparable coverage without professional supervision.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as supportive clinical evidence for 633/830 nm facial LED rejuvenation, not as a direct home-mask dosing template without caveats.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
