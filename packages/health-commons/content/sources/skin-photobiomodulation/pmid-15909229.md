This source is included for **mixed clinical/context evidence; clinic-supervised/non-wearable geometry**.

**Findings:** Twenty-three volunteers received randomized half-face treatment. Ninety-one percent reportedly noted visible change and blinded photo assessment found clinical response in 59%, but objective hydration and elasticity measurements did not show statistically significant improvement.

**Why it matters:** It preserves an early mixed result: visible/subjective responses were reported, but objective hydration and elasticity analyses were null.

**Potential experiment signals:** Separate user satisfaction, blinded photo ratings, hydration, and elasticity rather than collapsing them into one 'skin texture' claim.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mixed clinical/context evidence; it should temper claims about objective hydration or elasticity improvement and should not be cited under a clean supportive intervention-result claim.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
