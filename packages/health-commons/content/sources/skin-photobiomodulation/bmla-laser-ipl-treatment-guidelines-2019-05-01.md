This source is included for **professional light-device procedure safety guideline**.

**Findings:** The guideline emphasizes eye protection, training, documentation of treatment parameters, consent and risks, caution around active disorders or recent UV exposure, and recording or aborting treatment after adverse responses.

**Why it matters:** Helps define conservative exclusion and escalation anchors for people recently exposed to energy-based cosmetic procedures or with active skin reactions.

**Potential experiment signals:** Eye-protection requirements, recent UV/tanning boundary, active skin disorder exclusions, orbital-region caution, treatment-specification records, and adverse-event response.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only context for eye protection, recent UV/tanning and active-disorder caution, and adverse-response stop rules.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
