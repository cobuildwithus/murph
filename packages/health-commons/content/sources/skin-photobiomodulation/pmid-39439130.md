This source is included for **direct home/facial red+NIR or LED-mask benefit evidence**.

**Findings:** The study reports a face-fit surface-lighting micro-LED mask tested in 33 participants aged 35-55 in accessible summaries. Extracted publisher/press material reports improved wrinkles, sagging, and deep skin elasticity compared with conventional LED masks, and emphasizes reduced reflection from improved face fit. Detailed dose and adverse-event tables were not extracted.

**Why it matters:** It connects clinical outcomes to mask engineering issues such as face fit, reflection loss, and light-uniformity, which are highly relevant for consumer-device protocols.

**Potential experiment signals:** Mask fit, gaps from skin, light uniformity, elasticity, wrinkle/sagging metrics, heat sensations, and burn-risk monitoring.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as direct device-design and clinical-validation evidence, but do not generalize the reported elasticity effect without full comparator/dose extraction.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
