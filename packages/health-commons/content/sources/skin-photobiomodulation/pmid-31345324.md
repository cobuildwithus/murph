This source is included for **background review, method, or commentary context**.

**Findings:** This broad review covers lasers and light sources for skin rejuvenation. It is useful for boundary mapping, but because many included technologies are outside red/NIR LED PBM, it should remain low-priority context.

**Why it matters:** It helps define what should be excluded or downgraded as adjacent technology evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** technology type, skin rejuvenation endpoint, laser versus LED, ablative/nonablative distinction, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not import laser/IPL rejuvenation findings into nonablative red/NIR LED claims unless direct modality matching is established.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
