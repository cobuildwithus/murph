This source is included for **photosensitizing-medication and retinoid safety boundary**.

**Findings:** The review reported 393 different drugs or drug compounds with photosensitizing potential, while noting that the evidence level varies by drug; drug photosensitivity is mostly UVA-related but can involve UVB or visible light.

**Why it matters:** Use to support asking users to review medications rather than issuing a rigid universal exclusion list.

**Potential experiment signals:** medication evidence grade, photosensitive-drug flag, reaction diary.

**Protocol takeaway:** Many drugs have reported photosensitizing potential, but evidence strength differs. Medication-specific decisions require clinician/pharmacist review and wavelength relevance.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

### Extraction notes

- **Population:** Patients exposed to medications or compounds with photosensitizing potential.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Photosensitizing drugs with UVA, UVB, or visible-light exposure.
- **Comparator/control:** Not applicable.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Not applicable.
- **Endpoints:** Culprit drugs, mechanisms, phototoxic/photoallergic reactions, clinical consequences, and long-term risk context.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Clinical consequences include phototoxic/photoallergic eruptions and potential long-term concerns for certain agents.
- **Limitations:** Photosensitizing-potential lists vary in evidence strength.; Not specific to cosmetic red/NIR PBM.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Medication safety background only.
- **Artifact/rights status:** open_access.
