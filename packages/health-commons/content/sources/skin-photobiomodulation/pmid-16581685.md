This source is included for **adjunctive recent-procedure or filler context**.

**Findings:** In 10 patients after blepharoplasty and periocular ablative laser resurfacing, one side received red LED therapy and the contralateral side served as control. Indexed summaries report faster recovery and reduced side effects on the LED-treated side, but no significant wrinkle-improvement difference at six months.

**Why it matters:** Shows why post-procedure recovery findings must be separated from direct anti-photoaging PBM claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** Pain, erythema, edema, bruising, days to healing, periocular exposure, and recent-procedure timing.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a safety-boundary source for recent periocular procedures and recovery, not as standalone wrinkle-efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
