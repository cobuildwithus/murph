This source is included for **anatomy-specific sibling: neck, under-eye, or periocular evidence**.

**Findings:** The source reports 11 participants using a 633 nm/830 nm under-eye LED device for 6 weeks. Extracted summaries report a wrinkle score change from 20.05 to 19.72 that was not statistically significant, while participants self-reported improvements in wrinkles, texture, dark circles, bags, pigmentation, and erythema. No moderate or severe adverse events were reported in the extracted notes; transient mild erythema was described. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-39133416`.

**Why it matters:** It directly involves red plus NIR wavelengths but in a small under-eye device rather than a full-face protocol, and it preserves a null objective finding.

**Potential experiment signals:** under-eye wrinkle score, dark circles, under-eye bags, pigmentation, erythema, comfort/ease/satisfaction, mild erythema adverse event log.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent under-eye consumer-device context, with the non-significant wrinkle score treated as a key boundary.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
