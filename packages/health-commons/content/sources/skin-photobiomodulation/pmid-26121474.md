This source is included for **skin-of-color, visible-light pigmentation, melasma, or PIH safety boundary**.

**Findings:** Repeated visible-light exposure induced darker and more sustained pigmentation than single exposure; explants showed increased darkening/melanin and approximately threefold tyrosinase gene-expression increase by day 7 in the extracted record.

**Why it matters:** Use as mechanistic support for pigmentation tracking in susceptible users; do not generalize directly to red/NIR PBM efficacy.

**Potential experiment signals:** melanin index proxy, repeat-exposure darkening log, PIH watch.

**Protocol takeaway:** Repeated visible-light exposure activated melanogenesis and produced more persistent pigmentation. Visible-light melanogenesis mechanisms do not establish causality for red/NIR home-device outcomes.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

### Extraction notes

- **Population:** Human skin explants and in vivo volunteers exposed to visible light; multiple-exposure clinical group included 14 participants with mixed Fitzpatrick skin types.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Broad visible-light exposure; in vivo multiple exposure included 300 J/cm² followed by 150 J/cm² sessions, and explants were exposed to visible light.
- **Comparator/control:** Unexposed/control skin sites or baseline measurements.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Clinical and laboratory assessments through days after exposure; repeated exposure assessed short-term pigment persistence.
- **Endpoints:** Skin darkening, melanin content, tyrosinase gene expression/activity, melanin index, and visible pigmentation persistence.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Pigmentation/darkening is the relevant safety signal; the study was not a red/NIR anti-aging treatment trial.
- **Limitations:** Broad visible light, not isolated red/NIR PBM.; Small in vivo group and mechanistic/explant components.; Short-term pigmentation model, not long-term consumer-device safety.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Mechanistic context for pigmentation monitoring; indirect for red/NIR photoaging.
- **Artifact/rights status:** open_access.
