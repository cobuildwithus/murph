This source is included for **background review, method, or commentary context**.

**Findings:** The review discusses LED phototherapy across medical and aesthetic dermatology, including blue, red, and near-infrared device examples and cosmetic applications. It frames LED phototherapy as generally well tolerated but also discusses ALA-PDT-related stinging or burning, which should not be attributed to non-PDT red/NIR PBM.

**Why it matters:** It summarizes commonly cited aesthetic LED devices and indications but should not override primary trial extraction.

**Potential experiment signals:** wrinkle appearance, acne, wound healing, PDT-related discomfort, phototherapy adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep LED phototherapy and LED-PDT separate; cite primary trials for skin-texture claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
