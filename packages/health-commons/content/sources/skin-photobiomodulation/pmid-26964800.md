This source is included for **methods, dose-reporting, measurement, or device-geometry context**.

**Findings:** The review assessed 74 articles and found frequent omissions in irradiation-parameter reporting, including reliance on manufacturer values and missing information about power, irradiance, beam area, radiant energy, and measurement methods. It supports treating poorly reported PBM dosing as a major limitation.

**Why it matters:** Protocol comparisons can fail if nominal device settings are treated as measured skin dose.

**Potential experiment signals:** irradiance measurement, beam or exposure area, radiant energy, fluence, measurement device, distance from source.

**Protocol takeaway:** Dose reporting should include measured optical parameters whenever possible, not just manufacturer labels.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
