This source is included for **skin-of-color, visible-light pigmentation, melasma, or PIH safety boundary**.

**Findings:** In the extracted record, 21 women were randomized (active PBM n=10; sham PBM plus TXA n=11). No significant within- or between-group MASI/PGA differences were observed; study was underpowered (reported 51% power) due to early termination and treated as a pilot.

**Why it matters:** Preserve as a mixed/null-adjacent melasma finding; do not use to support anti-aging claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** MASI, MELASQoL, adverse-event log.

**Protocol takeaway:** Underpowered amber PBM versus tranexamic acid pilot found no clear MASI/PGA advantage. Pilot size and early termination limit efficacy conclusions.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

### Extraction notes

- **Population:** Women with melasma randomized to amber PBM or topical tranexamic acid comparator.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Active amber LED photobiomodulation with placebo cream for 12 weeks.
- **Comparator/control:** Sham PBM plus 5% liposomal tranexamic acid cream for 12 weeks.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 12 weeks.
- **Endpoints:** Primary MASI; secondary corneomelametry, Physician Global Assessment, MELASQoL, and adverse events.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Adverse events were a secondary outcome, but detailed adverse-event results were not captured in the extracted record.
- **Limitations:** Small underpowered pilot and early termination.; Melasma disease-treatment comparison, not photoaging protocol.; Amber LED differs from red/NIR target protocol.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Adjacent melasma PBM comparison; not direct protocol evidence.
- **Artifact/rights status:** unknown.
