This source is included for **red-light skin safety and dose-escalation boundary**.

**Findings:** Protocol source only; it documents the rationale for higher-fluence testing in Fitzpatrick I-III after prior skin-of-color MTD findings.

**Why it matters:** Use to justify skin-type-aware safety boundaries rather than a one-dose-fits-all recommendation.

**Potential experiment signals:** Fitzpatrick I-III dose escalation, photosensitivity screening, dose-limiting toxicity threshold.

**Protocol takeaway:** The STARS 2 protocol separated Fitzpatrick I-III safety testing from darker-skin dose limits. Completed trial data are needed for actual adverse-event rates.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

### Extraction notes

- **Population:** Healthy non-Hispanic Caucasian adults with Fitzpatrick skin types I-III; Fitzpatrick IV-VI were excluded because prior safety work established a lower MTD for darker skin phenotypes.
- **Intervention/exposure:** High-fluence LED red light at 480 and 640 J/cm² delivered to forearm skin.
- **Comparator/control:** Mock irradiation control.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Planned nine sessions, three times weekly for three weeks.
- **Endpoints:** Safety, dose-limiting toxicity, maximum tolerated dose, erythema, blistering, and cutaneous reactions.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No completed adverse-event outcomes in this protocol; planned DLT monitoring was central to the design.
- **Limitations:** Protocol paper without final outcome data.; Restricted to lighter-skin, non-Hispanic Caucasian participants.; Forearm safety design, not facial anti-aging evidence.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Direct for red LED skin-type safety design; indirect for consumer photoaging protocol outcomes.
- **Artifact/rights status:** open_access.
