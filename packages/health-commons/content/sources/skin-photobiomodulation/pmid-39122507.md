This source is included for **eye and face-adjacent ocular safety boundary**.

**Findings:** The authors analyzed 87 MAUDE reports; extracted secondary reporting included injury reports, malfunction reports, and medical visits. The passive database cannot estimate incidence or causality.

**Why it matters:** Consumer facial energy devices can generate eye-related adverse-event reports, making ocular symptom tracking and stop rules relevant to facial-light protocols.

**Potential experiment signals:** eye pain or discomfort, blurred vision, visual spots or flashes, medical visit for eye symptoms, device malfunction.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only or context-only evidence; do not use for direct skin-outcome claims.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
