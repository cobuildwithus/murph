This source is included for **exclusion anchor: PDT-like, photoconverter, or adjunctive PRP protocol**.

**Findings:** The trial randomized 48 participants in a split-face design comparing methyl aminolevulinate plus red light with placebo plus red light for facial photodamage. Indexed summaries report favorable PDT effects and one adverse-event discontinuation, but the source is excluded from direct PBM claims because it uses a photosensitizer.

**Why it matters:** This trial could otherwise be mistaken for direct red-light photoaging evidence; the photosensitizer makes it a different protocol family.

**Potential experiment signals:** PDT exclusion, photosensitizer use, facial photodamage scores, patient satisfaction, and adverse-event discontinuation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use as direct protocol evidence; use only as a why-excluded PDT example.

**Claim use:** `do-not-use`.
