This source is included for **mechanistic collagen/elastin or skin biology context**.

**Findings:** The study reported mean LED-versus-control differences in human reconstructed skin of approximately +31% type I procollagen and -18% MMP-1. In the clinical correlation, most treated participants reportedly had reductions in rhytid depth and surface roughness, and no adverse reactions or downtime were reported; however, the clinical sample size was not extracted here and the study had short follow-up and model limitations.

**Why it matters:** It links a red LED wavelength to collagen metabolism and visible skin-aging endpoints, while exposing limitations in older LED evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** type I procollagen, MMP-1, wrinkle depth, surface roughness, Fitzpatrick wrinkling severity, downtime/adverse reactions.

**Protocol takeaway:** Promising red-LED photoaging signals exist, but use this source as mechanistic/adjacent context and extract any primary clinical trial separately.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
