This source is included for **adjacent device or combination-intervention evidence**.

**Findings:** The source reports in vitro/ex vivo testing followed by a 28-day daily clinical evaluation of a device combining light with additional modalities. Extracted summaries report significant increases in skin density and radiance, reduced wrinkles, and no side effects. The LED contribution cannot be isolated from the bundled serum and mechanical modalities in the extracted information. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-32949447`.

**Why it matters:** It resembles a consumer facial anti-aging product but must remain separate from red+NIR light-only evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** skin density, skin radiance, wrinkle appearance, side-effect reporting, co-intervention inventory.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only to flag multimodal-device outcome domains and attribution limits.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
