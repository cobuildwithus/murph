This source is included for **direct facial LED trial registry and dose cross-check**.

**Findings:** NCT04911140 is the registry record for the LED mask facial rejuvenation frequency trial. It should be linked to the protocol/design article and later results paper, and not counted as a separate outcome study. Extracted materials indicate potential dose-reporting differences across registry and publications, so all dose fields should be reconciled before protocol dosing recommendations.

**Why it matters:** The registry anchors the Bragato/Motta facial rejuvenation trial, allowing dose, arm, and outcome verification against publications.

**Potential experiment signals:** Registry arm definitions, outcome definitions, schedule, enrollment/completion status, and publication linkage.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only registry evidence for the 660 nm facial LED mask frequency trial; do not treat it as separate clinical efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
