**Findings:** The source is a ClinicalTrials.gov record for a single-group virtual study of the iRestore Illumina LED Face Mask with a Neck & Chest Mask in 38 female participants aged 25–55. Extracted protocol snippets describe once-daily 10-minute evening use for 12 weeks, questionnaires every 2 weeks, photos at baseline and Week 12, dermatologist grading, and participant-perception endpoints. No outcome effects were extracted for this batch. Source key: `source_artifact:clinicaltrials-nct07025837-irestore-led-face-neck-chest-2024-09-06`.

**Why it matters:** It is close to consumer mask use and includes face plus neck/chest anatomy, but the single-group registry design prevents efficacy claims in this extraction.

**Potential experiment signals:** dermatologist grading, participant perception of plumpness, fine lines/wrinkles, pigmentation, tone/texture, redness, firmness, radiance, crepey skin, baseline/Week 12 photos.
