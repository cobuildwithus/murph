This source is included for **background review, method, or commentary context**.

**Findings:** This source focuses on establishing and validating efficacy assessment for home beauty devices used in facial rejuvenation. It is relevant to outcomes and measurement design, not direct red/NIR LED efficacy.

**Why it matters:** Outcome measurement is a weak point in cosmetic self-experiments and consumer-device studies.

**Potential experiment signals:** wrinkle assessment, skin texture, elasticity, hydration, image-based evaluation, user-reported satisfaction.

**Protocol takeaway:** Define measurement endpoints before starting rather than relying on subjective before/after impressions.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
