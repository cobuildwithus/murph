This source is included for **core facial LED benefit evidence; clinic-supervised/non-wearable geometry**.

**Findings:** Seventy-six participants were randomized to 830 nm, 633 nm, combination 830+633 nm, or sham settings in a split-face design. Extracted summaries report up to 36% wrinkle reduction and 19% elasticity increase from baseline in treatment groups, increased collagen and elastic fibers, activated fibroblasts, TIMP changes, and no adverse effects reported by participants.

**Why it matters:** It combines randomized placebo-controlled design with clinical, profilometric, histologic, ultrastructural, and biochemical endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** Wrinkle metrics, elasticity, safety/tolerability, and the distinction between clinical supervised evidence and home-use feasibility.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as high-value supportive evidence that red/NIR LED facial phototherapy can affect wrinkles, elasticity, and dermal remodeling, while flagging clinical-device geometry.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
