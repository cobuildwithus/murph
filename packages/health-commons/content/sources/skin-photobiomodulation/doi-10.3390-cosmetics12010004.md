This source is included for **direct home/facial red+NIR or LED-mask benefit evidence**.

**Findings:** Twenty-one female volunteers used a red-to-near-infrared LED facial mask for 4 weeks. The paper reports statistically significant improvements across mean brightening/anti-aging parameters, including facial and some scalp-related endpoints, and reports no adverse effects or irritation in the extracted material.

**Why it matters:** It is a recent open-access facial LED mask study with directly relevant red-to-NIR exposure, skin-brightening, anti-aging, and tolerability endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** Brightness, eye wrinkles, elasticity, global appearance, irritation, and adherence. Scalp sebum/dead-skin outcomes should not be used as direct facial-photoaging outcomes.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use cautiously as direct supportive mask evidence, but do not treat it as sham-controlled proof unless comparator details are verified in the full text.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
