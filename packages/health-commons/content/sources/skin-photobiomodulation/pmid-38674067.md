This source is included for **review or consensus context for dermatology PBM**.

**Findings:** This comprehensive review summarizes PBM mechanisms and applications in skin, including non-invasive use and minimal-side-effect framing. It is a narrative synthesis and should not be treated as direct red/NIR facial-photoaging outcome evidence.

**Why it matters:** It consolidates mechanism and application context for skin PBM and helps identify primary studies.

**Potential experiment signals:** collagen-related biomarkers, inflammation, skin healing, device parameters, side effects.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for background only; direct claims need primary trials in photoaging or texture outcomes.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
