This source is included for **wavelength sibling: red-only, amber/yellow, or broad-spectrum evidence**.

**Findings:** The source reports 30 participants randomized to 570 nm yellow+620 nm red+850 nm IR or 590 nm yellow+620 nm red+850 nm IR for three sessions per week over 8 weeks. Extracted summaries report improvements in pores, wrinkles, texture, and brown spots in both groups, with more significant effects for wrinkles and brown spots in the 570 nm composite group. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-41091280`.

**Why it matters:** It gives recent randomized evidence for composite LED photoaging endpoints while emphasizing that yellow-light additions are not interchangeable with the target protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** GSP score, VISIA pores, VISIA wrinkles, VISIA texture, VISIA brown spots.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent composite-wavelength evidence only; do not use to isolate red+NIR effects.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
