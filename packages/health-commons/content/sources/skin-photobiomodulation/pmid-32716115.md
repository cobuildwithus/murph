This source is included for **direct home/facial red+NIR or LED-mask benefit evidence with co-intervention boundary**.

**Findings:** Forty-eight participants were assigned to LED mask plus hyaluronic acid ampoule or hyaluronic acid ampoule alone for 4 weeks. Extracted summaries report that both groups improved, while the LED mask group improved faster and more on measures including facial-volume changes, lower-chin firmness area, pores, desquamation, and skin density.

**Why it matters:** It is a direct home-use 660/850 nm mask study with practical photoaging endpoints, but it also defines an important boundary for topical co-interventions.

**Potential experiment signals:** Facial volume, folds, firmness, pores, desquamation, density, daily adherence, and co-intervention logs for hyaluronic acid or other skincare.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as direct support for a home 660/850 nm mask plus topical HA context, not as clean LED-only evidence.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
