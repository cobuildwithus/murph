This source is included for **background review, method, or commentary context**.

**Findings:** The review describes available home optical devices for indications including acne, scars, hair removal, and wrinkles, using technologies such as IPL, LED, heat, and infrared. It is useful for safety and device-boundary context, not direct red/NIR LED efficacy.

**Why it matters:** It supports separating consumer home-device classes before applying evidence to a protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** device technology, approved indication, wrinkle claim, user instructions, safety concern.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not pool home IPL, infrared heat, laser, and LED wrinkle-device claims as if they were one intervention.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
