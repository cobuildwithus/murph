This source is included for **eye and face-adjacent ocular safety boundary**.

**Findings:** The review identified 21 case reports; injuries included iris atrophy, cataracts, anterior uveitis, glaucoma, visual-field defects, posterior synechiae, pupillary defects, severe eye pain, temporary vision loss, and conjunctival erythema.

**Why it matters:** It provides concrete injury categories and reinforces that eye protection can fail if absent, removed, inappropriate, or overheated.

**Potential experiment signals:** eye pain, temporary vision loss, eye protection removed, protection fit and wavelength match, shield discomfort.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only or context-only evidence; do not use for direct skin-outcome claims.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
