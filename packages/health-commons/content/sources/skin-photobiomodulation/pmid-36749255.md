This source is included for **direct facial LED trial protocol and dose cross-check**.

**Findings:** The paper describes a controlled, randomized, double-blind facial PBM trial and calculates a planned sample size of 108 after allowing for loss to follow-up. It links to NCT04911140 and specifies planned wrinkle, image-analysis, and satisfaction endpoints. It should not be counted as a separate efficacy result from the later results paper.

**Why it matters:** It documents the planned sham-controlled facial LED mask frequency trial, including outcome choices and registry linkage, which helps audit the later results source.

**Potential experiment signals:** Pre-registration linkage, planned session frequency, planned outcome hierarchy, and any mismatch between planned and reported endpoints.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as context for planned dose, endpoints, and registry linkage; do not treat it as efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
