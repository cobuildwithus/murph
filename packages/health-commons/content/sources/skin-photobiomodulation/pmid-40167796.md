This source is included for **direct facial LED trial and dose/frequency evidence**.

**Findings:** Ninety-five women were randomized to 660 nm red LED mask exposure either three times weekly, two times weekly, or sham over 4 weeks. Blinded Wrinkle Assessment Scale scores did not differ significantly across groups, while ImageJ analysis found significant reductions in glabellar and right periorbital wrinkle lengths in PBM groups versus control. Participant satisfaction favored both active groups, and the number of weekly sessions did not materially change the main reported results.

**Why it matters:** It is a blinded sham-controlled facial LED mask trial that preserves a mixed result: a clinical wrinkle scale was null while image-analysis and satisfaction endpoints improved.

**Potential experiment signals:** Wrinkle scales, image-derived wrinkle length, patient satisfaction, frequency adherence, and the possibility of mixed outcomes across assessment methods.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent red-only facial mask evidence for frequency and outcome design; do not cite it as red+NIR-specific efficacy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
