This source is included for **large-area/full-panel cosmetic PBM sibling evidence**.

**Findings:** The source reports clinical experience across about 3,500 LED photomodulation treatments in approximately 900 patients, with more than 300 patients receiving skin-rejuvenation monotherapy and about 600 receiving LED as an adjunct to other procedures. Extracted summaries do not provide controlled effect estimates suitable for the target red+NIR facial-mask protocol. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-16176771`.

**Why it matters:** It is a large historical experience base, but its uncontrolled, mixed-use nature makes it a weak source for protocol claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** skin rejuvenation clinical response, adjunctive recovery, treatment tolerability, co-intervention tracking.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as background on clinical practice patterns; do not use as direct outcome evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
