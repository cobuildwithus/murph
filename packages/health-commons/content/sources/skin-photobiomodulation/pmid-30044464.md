This source is included for **methods, dose-reporting, measurement, or device-geometry context**.

**Findings:** The review discusses similarities and differences between lasers and LEDs in PBM, including practical LED advantages such as lower laser-safety burden, large-area irradiation, home use, wearable formats, and cost. It supports device-boundary language rather than efficacy claims.

**Why it matters:** Many reviews mix lasers, LEDs, panels, masks, and beds; delivery geometry affects dose at skin.

**Potential experiment signals:** device type, illumination area, distance, coherence/noncoherence, home-use feasibility, wearable or panel geometry.

**Protocol takeaway:** Separate LED mask/panel evidence from laser evidence unless the claim is explicitly device-agnostic and dose-matched.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
