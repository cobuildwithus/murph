This source is included for **eye and face-adjacent ocular safety boundary**.

**Findings:** The statement describes PBM/NIR as far-red to NIR light around 630–1000 nm, says retinal-disease efficacy evidence is not established or robust enough, and notes dose/duration/frequency variation prevents consensus.

**Why it matters:** It is an ophthalmology position statement that cautions about evidence limitations and dosing heterogeneity.

**Potential experiment signals:** retinal disease status, dose, duration, frequency, clinical oversight.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only or context-only evidence; do not use for direct skin-outcome claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
