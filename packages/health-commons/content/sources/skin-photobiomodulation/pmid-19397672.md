This source is included for **adjunctive recent-procedure or filler context**.

**Findings:** In 20 patients, a randomly selected facial half received 590 nm LED photomodulation immediately after full-face fractional laser. Indexed summaries report less erythema at 24 hours on LED-treated halves, persistent 48-hour difference only in a high-energy subgroup, and no discernible difference by 96 hours.

**Why it matters:** A randomized split-face design can look strong but still has an adjacent procedure-specific endpoint.

**Potential experiment signals:** Post-laser erythema at 24, 48, and 96 hours, energy-setting subgroup, and recent-procedure timing.

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep as a safety-boundary/recent-laser source only.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
