This source is included for **wavelength sibling: red-only, amber/yellow, or broad-spectrum evidence**.

**Findings:** The source reports 20 healthy Caucasian women using a 630 ± 10 nm red LED mask at 15.6 J/cm² for 12 minutes twice weekly for 3 months. Extracted summaries report progressive improvements after 1, 2, and 3 months across overall skin quality and several clinical or biophysical endpoints, with persistence up to 1 month after stopping. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-37522497`.

**Why it matters:** It is a mask-format red-only sibling that provides endpoint ideas close to the target facial-device use case while remaining distinct from red+NIR evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** crow's-feet wrinkle depth, oval sagging score, Cutometer firmness/elasticity, ultrasound dermal density, cheek roughness, complexion colorimetry, pore diameter, sebum/porphyrin measures.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as red-only facial-mask context; do not attribute findings to NIR or to a sham-controlled red+NIR protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
