This source is included for **PDT/photosensitizer safety and exclusion boundary**.

**Findings:** The guideline concerns topical photodynamic therapy, where a photosensitizer and light are intentionally combined. It is relevant to photosensitivity and exclusion boundaries but not to PBM efficacy for skin texture or photoaging.

**Why it matters:** Prevents importing PDT outcomes or adverse-event profiles into a non-photosensitizer red/NIR PBM protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** Photosensitizer use, PDT mechanism, light-reactive adverse effects, erythema, blistering, pigment change, and screening boundaries.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a safety-only boundary for photosensitizer/PDT history and light-reactive medications or products.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
