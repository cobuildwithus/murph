This source is included for **wavelength sibling: red-only, amber/yellow, or broad-spectrum evidence**.

**Findings:** The source reports 52 adult female participants treated daily for 12 weeks with either 660 nm red LED or 411–777 nm white LED at 5.17 J. Extracted summaries report improved skin-replica wrinkle measurements in both groups, a slightly larger mean improvement in the red group without statistically significant between-group differences, and no significant differences in blinded dermatologist assessments. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-28195844`.

**Why it matters:** It tests a daily home-use facial light schedule and shows that subjective preference or baseline improvements do not necessarily prove a wavelength-specific advantage.

**Potential experiment signals:** skin-replica wrinkle measurement, periocular wrinkle photography, blinded dermatologist assessment, subject global assessment, tolerability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as red-only and broad-spectrum comparator context; do not treat as direct support for red+NIR superiority.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
