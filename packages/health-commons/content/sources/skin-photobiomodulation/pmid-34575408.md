This source is included for **PDT/photosensitizer safety and exclusion boundary**.

**Findings:** The review explains PDT as a photosensitizer-plus-light-plus-oxygen process that generates reactive oxygen species. This mechanism is distinct from LED-only PBM and supports excluding photosensitizer-driven findings from direct protocol claims.

**Why it matters:** Provides a mechanism-based rationale for excluding PDT-like studies from LED-only PBM evidence synthesis.

**Potential experiment signals:** Photosensitizer exposure, ROS generation, PDT/PBM distinction, and photosensitivity boundaries.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mechanism and safety-boundary background only; do not cite as efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
