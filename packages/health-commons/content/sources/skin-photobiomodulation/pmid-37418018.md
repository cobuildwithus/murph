This source is included for **direct home/facial red+NIR or LED-mask benefit evidence**.

**Findings:** The study evaluated a home-based commercial 633/830/1072 nm LED face mask in male participants over 6 weeks. Extracted abstract text reports favorable participant satisfaction, willingness to recommend, perceived improvements in fine lines/wrinkles, texture, and youthful appearance, and favorable digital photo-analysis changes in wrinkles, UV spots, brown spots, pores, and porphyrins. The sample size was not extracted from accessible primary abstract text.

**Why it matters:** It directly studies a home-based commercial LED face mask and addresses male facial rejuvenation, a population often underrepresented in cosmetic-device studies.

**Potential experiment signals:** Satisfaction, digital skin photography features, male-population boundary, and adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as direct home-mask context and male-population evidence, but avoid strong efficacy claims without comparator and sample-size confirmation.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
