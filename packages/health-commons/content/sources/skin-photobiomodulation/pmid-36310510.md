This source is included for **review or consensus context for dermatology PBM**.

**Findings:** The review screened a large LED skin-therapy literature and selected 31 studies for systematic review/meta-analysis. Because the article is paywalled and the accessible information is broad, its findings should be treated as context rather than direct protocol evidence.

**Why it matters:** It may summarize effect heterogeneity and outcome reporting across LED dermatology studies.

**Potential experiment signals:** LED indication subgroup, pooled effect direction, heterogeneity, adverse-event reporting.

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep as context unless direct red/NIR photoaging subgroup details are verified from the article.
