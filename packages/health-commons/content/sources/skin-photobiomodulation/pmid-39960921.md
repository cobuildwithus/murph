This source is included for **direct home/facial red+NIR or LED-mask benefit evidence**.

**Findings:** Sixty Asian-descent adults were randomized to an active 630 nm LED plus 850 nm IRED mask or sham for 16 weeks. Crow's Feet Grading Scale differences favored active treatment at weeks 8, 12, and 16. Reported global improvement rates also favored active treatment, and the abstract describes the treatment as safe, well tolerated, and painless.

**Why it matters:** This is one of the closest matches to the protocol: home use, sham comparator, red plus near-infrared wavelengths, periocular photoaging endpoint, and safety/tolerability monitoring.

**Potential experiment signals:** Periocular wrinkle grade, global aesthetic improvement, session adherence, discomfort, irritation, and any ocular or heat-related symptoms. Full dose extraction should verify the reported irradiance unit before protocol dosing claims.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as direct support that a home red plus near-infrared periocular LED/IRED mask can improve crow's-feet texture over 8-16 weeks, while keeping population and endpoint boundaries explicit.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
