This source is included for **oncologic and suspicious-lesion safety boundary**.

**Findings:** The review concluded that available clinical and preclinical evidence suggests PBM is oncologically safe for skin rejuvenation and found no clinical trial data linking PBM with significant adverse events including new or recurrent malignancy.

**Why it matters:** Supports cautious cancer-history language: do not treat suspicious lesions and involve clinicians for active or recent cancer history, while avoiding unsupported claims that PBM is carcinogenic.

**Potential experiment signals:** cancer history screening, suspicious lesion exclusion, new or changing lesion stop rule.

**Protocol takeaway:** Aesthetic PBM oncologic-safety review found no clinical signal of new or recurrent malignancy in available evidence. Absence of evidence is not a guarantee of safety for every cancer type, device, dose, or lesion.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

### Extraction notes

- **Population:** Clinical and preclinical evidence relevant to photobiomodulation/low-level light therapy for aesthetic skin rejuvenation and oncologic safety.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Photobiomodulation or low-level light therapy used for aesthetic skin rejuvenation.
- **Comparator/control:** Varied comparators across included clinical and preclinical studies.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Varied by included study.
- **Endpoints:** New or recurrent malignancy, oncologic adverse events, tumor biology signals, and safety outcomes.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No significant oncologic adverse-event signal was identified in the reviewed clinical evidence, but evidence remains indirect and limited for many cancer-history scenarios.
- **Limitations:** Systematic review depends on available heterogeneous clinical and preclinical literature.; Not a prospective cancer-history safety trial of a home facial device.; Suspicious lesions and active malignancy still require clinician evaluation.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Safety-boundary evidence for aesthetic PBM; not direct wrinkle/texture efficacy evidence.
- **Artifact/rights status:** open_access.
