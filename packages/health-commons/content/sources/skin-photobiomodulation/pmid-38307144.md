This source is included for **review or consensus context for dermatology PBM**.

**Findings:** The CME review describes PBM using red and near-infrared ranges and discusses applications including skin rejuvenation, wounds, scars, alopecia, acne, ulcers, and cancer-therapy-associated conditions. It emphasizes favorable safety and the need for counseling and better standardized evidence across skin types and populations.

**Why it matters:** It summarizes current dermatology practice context and the need to consider skin type, device instructions, and standardization.

**Potential experiment signals:** skin rejuvenation claim boundary, skin type/race/ethnicity reporting, home-use instructions, adverse events, standardized trial need.

**Protocol takeaway:** PBM may be discussed in relation to skin rejuvenation, but protocol claims should rely on direct trials and standardized parameters.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
