This source is included for **adjacent device or combination-intervention evidence**.

**Findings:** The source reports more than 60 patients across four centers using nonthermal blue and near-infrared light. Extracted summaries report reduced post-resurfacing erythema duration, pore-size and radiance improvements, fine-wrinkle smoothing in a subset, and stability at 3 months. These outcomes are not directly attributable to a red+NIR facial-mask intervention. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-16414904`.

**Why it matters:** It shows NIR-containing nonthermal light use in aesthetic settings while highlighting the importance of wavelength and co-procedure boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** post-resurfacing erythema duration, pore size, skin radiance, fine wrinkle smoothing, 3-month durability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as adjacent device context; do not generalize blue/NIR findings to red+NIR mask claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
