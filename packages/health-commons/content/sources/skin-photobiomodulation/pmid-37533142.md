This source is included for **eye and face-adjacent ocular safety boundary**.

**Findings:** The review emphasizes screening, consent, environment and operator controls, protective eyewear selection, corneal-shield decisions, and suspected eye-injury response.

**Why it matters:** It translates ocular hazard literature into practical controls adaptable to home facial light routines.

**Potential experiment signals:** protective eyewear type, corneal shield need, post-exposure eye symptoms, ocular risk history.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only or context-only evidence; do not use for direct skin-outcome claims.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
