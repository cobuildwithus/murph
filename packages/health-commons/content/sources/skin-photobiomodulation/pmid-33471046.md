This source is included for **background review, method, or commentary context**.

**Findings:** The review describes reasonable clinical-trial support for some low-energy red/NIR aesthetic applications while emphasizing methodological flaws, small cohorts, and industry funding. It also cautions that the effect of LED versus laser systems may be unclear in parts of the literature.

**Why it matters:** It highlights the gap between commercial success and independent empirical support in aesthetic PBM.

**Potential experiment signals:** skin rejuvenation, rhytids, dyschromia, industry funding, LED versus laser distinction, study independence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Promising aesthetic PBM claims should be tempered by study-quality and industry-funding caveats.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
