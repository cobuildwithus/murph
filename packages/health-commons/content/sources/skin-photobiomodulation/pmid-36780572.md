This source is included for **anatomy-specific sibling: neck, under-eye, or periocular evidence**.

**Findings:** The source reports 137 women randomized in a split-face comparison of 660 nm red light and 590 nm amber light at 3.8 J/cm² for 10 sessions over 4 weeks. Extracted summaries report wrinkle-volume reductions of about 31.6% for red and 29.9% for amber, quality-of-life improvements, and no improvement in hydration or viscoelasticity endpoints. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-36780572`.

**Why it matters:** It is a comparatively large facial-wrinkle RCT and helps separate red-only and amber-light claims from combined red+NIR facial-mask claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** VisioFace wrinkle volume, Corneometer hydration, Cutometer elasticity, MelasQoL-BP, Skindex-29.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as wavelength-sibling evidence for facial wrinkle-volume outcomes and for preserving null hydration/elasticity findings.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
