This source is included for **review or consensus context for dermatology PBM**.

**Findings:** The review included 31 randomized controlled trials. It reported stronger evidence for some dermatologic indications such as acne and wound-related uses than for many other indications, and noted recurring limitations including small samples, variable parameters, absent sham controls, and incomplete blinding.

**Why it matters:** It is a controlled-trial review that helps separate stronger LED indications from weaker or indirect cosmetic evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** condition-specific evidence grade, adverse-event reporting, trial blinding, sham/control use, wavelength and treatment schedule.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use condition-specific evidence cautiously; do not treat general LED dermatology support as direct evidence for photoaging unless the underlying trial is direct.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
