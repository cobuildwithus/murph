This source is included for **adjunctive recent-procedure or filler context**.

**Findings:** Indexed summaries describe 28 female patients after ablative Er:YAG/CO2 resurfacing receiving hemiface 830 nm followed by 633 nm LED treatments; healing time and sequelae were reportedly improved, while six-month wrinkle improvement was not significantly different.

**Why it matters:** The source is commonly adjacent to red/NIR facial LED claims but addresses recovery after ablative procedures, not primary anti-photoaging.

**Potential experiment signals:** Wound-healing time, post-resurfacing sequelae, visible/NIR combined timing, matched/no-LED comparison, and six-month wrinkle comparison.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use as direct protocol support; cite only when discussing recent laser-resurfacing boundaries or adjunctive recovery.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
