This source is included for **wavelength sibling: red-only, amber/yellow, or broad-spectrum evidence**.

**Findings:** The source reports 36 participants treated with 588 ± 10 nm LED photomodulation for 40 seconds once weekly over 8 weeks. Extracted summaries report that 30 blinded raters asked to identify before/after photographs for redness, wrinkles, dark spots, roughness, and general appearance performed no better than chance; consensus ratings were also not different from chance. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-19215260`.

**Why it matters:** It provides a negative/null adjacent-wavelength result, reducing overconfidence from uncontrolled LED before-after reports.

**Potential experiment signals:** blinded before/after photo identification, redness, wrinkles, dark spots, roughness, general appearance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a null-result boundary for adjacent LED photomodulation, not as direct evidence against red+NIR.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
