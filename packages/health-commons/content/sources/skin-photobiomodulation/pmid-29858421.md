**Findings:** The source is a study protocol for a randomized controlled trial of LED phototherapy for periorbital wrinkles. Extracted summaries identify planned endpoints including periorbital wrinkle volume, elasticity, satisfaction, and skin feel. It does not report participant outcomes, adverse events, or effect estimates. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-29858421`.

**Why it matters:** It shows how investigators planned to measure periorbital wrinkle volume and related skin outcomes, while preventing protocol-only claims from being mistaken for results.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only for protocol-design context, not efficacy or safety conclusions.
