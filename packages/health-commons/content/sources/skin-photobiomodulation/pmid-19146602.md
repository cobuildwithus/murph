This source is included for **core facial LED benefit evidence; adjacent handheld geometry**.

**Findings:** Twenty-two subjects with facial rhytides received eight treatments over 4 weeks with follow-up assessments at weeks 6, 9, and 12. Extracted summaries report that 74% of subjects noted visible improvement in fine lines and wrinkles at 8 weeks after treatment.

**Why it matters:** It informs practical consumer-device use and localized photoaging outcomes, while clearly separating handheld geometry from mask geometry.

**Potential experiment signals:** Fine-line/wrinkle appearance, photos, satisfaction, and coverage/adherence differences between handheld and mask delivery.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent supportive evidence only; do not directly translate handheld dosing or coverage to a full-face mask protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
