This source is included for **adjunctive recent-procedure or filler context**.

**Findings:** Indexed metadata identifies a 635 and 830 nm LED therapy report for local injection-site reactions after filler. The accessible extraction did not confirm sample size, detailed schedule, or effect estimates.

**Why it matters:** Flags recent filler/injectable reactions as a separate clinical scenario from home PBM for photoaging.

**Potential experiment signals:** Recent injectable-procedure boundary, filler reactions, 635/830 nm wavelength pair, and adverse-event context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as a cautionary adjacent source for people with recent fillers or injection-site reactions.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
