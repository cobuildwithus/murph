This source is included for **mechanistic collagen/elastin or skin biology context**.

**Findings:** Human dermal fibroblasts and skin explants were exposed to combined 640 nm red and 830 nm near-infrared LEDs at low irradiance. The study reported increases in ATP and collagen/elastin-related gene and protein markers, including procollagen I, collagen III, elastin, and LOXL1-related pathways.

**Why it matters:** It is directly mechanistic for the red+NIR wavelength combination often used in skin-aging protocols.

**Potential experiment signals:** procollagen I, collagen III, elastin, LOXL1, ATP, visible wrinkle or texture score in clinical translation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mechanism-only support for collagen/elastin plausibility; do not use as outcome evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
