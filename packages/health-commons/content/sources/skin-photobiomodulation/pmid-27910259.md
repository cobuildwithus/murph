This source is included for **adjacent device or combination-intervention evidence**.

**Findings:** The source reports a home-use device combining LED low-level light therapy with radiofrequency. Extracted summaries report an average 1.49-point reduction in Fitzpatrick wrinkle score by blinded photographic assessment, no unexpected adverse events, post-treatment erythema resolving within 1 hour, and some edema resolving within 24 hours. Because RF is a co-intervention, the source is not LED-only evidence. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-27910259`.

**Why it matters:** It is close to consumer home use but combines energy modalities, which makes it unsuitable for direct PBM efficacy claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** Fitzpatrick wrinkle score, blinded dermatologist photo assessment, erythema duration, edema duration, unexpected adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as combination-device context and short-lived local reaction safety context only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
