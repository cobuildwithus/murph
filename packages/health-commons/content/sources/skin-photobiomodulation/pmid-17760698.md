This source is included for **core facial LED benefit evidence; clinic-supervised/non-wearable geometry**.

**Findings:** Thirteen subjects with periorbital and nasolabial wrinkles/fine lines received nine 20-minute LED treatments using 633 and 830 nm wavelengths. Extracted summaries report moderate or slight investigator-rated responses, stronger periorbital than nasolabial effects, and at 12 weeks 91% reporting improved skin tone and 82% reporting smoother skin.

**Why it matters:** It reinforces that different facial regions may respond differently, which matters for tracking periocular versus nasolabial outcomes separately.

**Potential experiment signals:** Periorbital and nasolabial outcomes separately, tone, smoothness, and satisfaction.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as low-to-medium certainty supportive evidence and as a reminder to separate eye-area and nasolabial outcomes.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
