This source is included for **background review, method, or commentary context**.

**Findings:** The review describes red/NIR skin PBM mechanisms including mitochondrial chromophores, ATP, nitric oxide, blood flow, ROS, and signaling, and discusses potential uses in wrinkles, scars, healing, pigmentation, and inflammatory skin conditions. It is context-only for this protocol.

**Why it matters:** It is a commonly cited background source for skin PBM mechanisms and applications.

**Potential experiment signals:** wrinkles, skin repair, pigmentation, inflammation, adverse effects, dose parameters.

**Protocol takeaway:** Useful for background mechanism, but protocol efficacy claims must cite direct trials.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
