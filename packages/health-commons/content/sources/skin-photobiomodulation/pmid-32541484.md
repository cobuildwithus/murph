This source is included for **eye and face-adjacent ocular safety boundary**.

**Findings:** A 37-year-old woman reportedly used a blue LED face mask for 20 minutes every two days with eyes open and was diagnosed with blue LED-induced photochemical retinopathy.

**Why it matters:** It is face-mask-specific and highlights direct-gaze and eye-covering behavior as a core safety issue.

**Potential experiment signals:** direct gaze, eye-covering adherence, distorted vision, retinal imaging if clinically evaluated.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only or context-only evidence; do not use for direct skin-outcome claims.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
