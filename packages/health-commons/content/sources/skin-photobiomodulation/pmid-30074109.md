This source is included for **adjunctive recent-procedure or filler context**.

**Findings:** The controlled study included a split-face LED comparison group and a resurfacing-only comparison group. Indexed summaries report no significant LED-versus-non-LED difference within the split-face group for erythema index or TEWL, while comparisons with a CO2-only group suggested lower percent erythema index on several early post-procedure days.

**Why it matters:** Important guard against overclaiming post-procedure LED effects because this controlled study contains both null local comparisons and broader group-level signals.

**Potential experiment signals:** Post-procedure erythema index, TEWL, split-face local comparison, resurfacing-only comparison, and early recovery days.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as mixed recent-procedure context; explicitly preserve the null split-face local comparison.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
