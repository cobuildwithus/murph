This source is included for **anatomy-specific sibling: neck, under-eye, or periocular evidence**.

**Findings:** The source reports 70 participants randomized to an active LED neck device or sham device. The active schedule was 9 minutes per day, 5 days per week, for 60 sessions. Extracted summaries report significantly higher proportions of improved Lemperle Wrinkle Scale scores at Week 12 and across Weeks 8, 12, and 16, while investigator GAIS separated from sham more clearly than participant GAIS. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-39319750`.

**Why it matters:** It is a relatively rigorous home-use LED trial in adjacent neck anatomy and includes safety monitoring around the thyroid region.

**Potential experiment signals:** Lemperle Wrinkle Scale, investigator GAIS, participant GAIS, thyroid ultrasound safety monitoring, adherence to home-use schedule.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as anatomy-specific context for home-use LED adherence, neck outcomes, and thyroid-adjacent safety monitoring; do not pool as direct facial-mask efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
