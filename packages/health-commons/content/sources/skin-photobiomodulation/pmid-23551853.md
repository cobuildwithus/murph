This source is included for **adjunctive recent-procedure or filler context**.

**Findings:** Ten Korean subjects with Fitzpatrick III-IV skin received 635 nm LED photomodulation on one facial side after full-face fractional CO2 resurfacing, with daily treatment for seven days. Indexed summaries report faster erythema resolution, with significant differences first noted around day 4.

**Why it matters:** Adds a darker-skin-type post-resurfacing recovery context but remains adjacent to the direct protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** Post-CO2-laser erythema, chromometer measures, daily post-procedure LED timing, Fitzpatrick III-IV representation, and short-term recovery.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context for recent CO2 laser exclusion/caution; do not cite as direct red/NIR skin-texture evidence.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
