This source is included for **background review, method, or commentary context**.

**Findings:** The review evaluated hand-held home cosmetic laser and light devices across conditions including acne, hair removal, photoaging, scars, psoriasis, and hair regrowth. It reported that available evidence was often modest and limited by small samples, short follow-up, uncontrolled designs, and industry sponsorship.

**Why it matters:** Home LED masks and panels are common in real-world use, but evidence standards for cosmetic devices may be lower than clinical trials.

**Potential experiment signals:** home-use indication, FDA clearance context, study sponsorship, follow-up duration, adverse events, user misuse.

**Protocol takeaway:** Consumer-device claims require skepticism unless supported by direct, blinded, controlled studies.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
