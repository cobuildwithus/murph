**Findings:** The review discusses ophthalmic PBM mechanisms and clinical integration and should be treated as context, not evidence for skin texture or photoaging.

**Why it matters:** It prevents accidental promotion of ophthalmology PBM claims into skin-photoaging claims.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only or context-only evidence; do not use for direct skin-outcome claims.
