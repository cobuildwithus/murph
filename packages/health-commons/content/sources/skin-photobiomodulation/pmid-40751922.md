This source is included for **review or consensus context for dermatology PBM**.

**Findings:** The review presents LED as a promising non-invasive cosmetic dermatology modality and discusses near-infrared and combination-wavelength applications. Because it is a narrative review and includes PDT and non-red/NIR indications, it should remain context-only.

**Why it matters:** It helps keep the protocol aware of newer cosmetic LED discussions while preserving boundaries around direct evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** cosmetic indication, wavelength combination, skin-aging endpoint, PDT co-intervention flag, safety reporting.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote narrative cosmetic-review optimism into trial-level red/NIR photoaging claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
