This source is included for **eye and face-adjacent ocular safety boundary**.

**Findings:** The review surveys ophthalmology PBM mechanisms and translation across preclinical and clinical contexts, not skin outcomes or consumer face-mask safety.

**Why it matters:** It contextualizes red/NIR PBM as a distinct ophthalmic research area while preserving directness boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** retinal function, ocular disease status, PBM dose, translation stage.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only or context-only evidence; do not use for direct skin-outcome claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
