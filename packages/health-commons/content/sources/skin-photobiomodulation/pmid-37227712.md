This source is included for **eye and face-adjacent ocular safety boundary**.

**Findings:** The case report describes retinal damage in a 12-year-old female patient with myopia after repeated low-level red-light laser exposure.

**Why it matters:** It is a counterweight to adjacent myopia trials by preserving a reported retinal injury boundary.

**Potential experiment signals:** visual acuity change, retinal imaging abnormality, afterimage or visual disturbance, direct ocular exposure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only or context-only evidence; do not use for direct skin-outcome claims.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
