This source is included for **review or consensus context for dermatology PBM**.

**Findings:** The review describes absorption by photoreceptors including mitochondrial cytochrome c oxidase and downstream modulation of ATP, reactive oxygen species, calcium, and signaling pathways. It also flags parameter dependence and generally favorable tolerability, with erythema described as a common self-limited adverse effect.

**Why it matters:** Mechanistic language can otherwise become overconfident; this source supports cautious mechanism wording and adverse-effect taxonomy.

**Potential experiment signals:** erythema, warmth or discomfort, dose parameters, treatment duration, mechanism markers.

**Protocol takeaway:** Mechanism claims should be framed as plausibility, not proof of visible rejuvenation.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
