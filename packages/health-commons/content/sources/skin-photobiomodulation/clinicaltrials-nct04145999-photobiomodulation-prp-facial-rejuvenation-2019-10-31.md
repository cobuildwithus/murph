This source is included for **exclusion anchor: PDT-like, photoconverter, or adjunctive PRP protocol**.

**Findings:** The registry describes photobiomodulation combined with intradermal platelet-rich plasma for facial rejuvenation. Because PRP injection is a co-intervention and no peer-reviewed results were extracted, it should not support LED-only red/NIR protocol claims.

**Why it matters:** Prevents adjunctive injectable protocols from being folded into a home red/NIR skin-texture evidence base.

**Potential experiment signals:** Why-excluded status, PRP co-intervention, facial rejuvenation registry design, trial status, and combination-procedure boundary.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use for direct protocol claims; keep as a why-excluded adjunctive-procedure registry.

**Claim use:** `do-not-use`.
