This source is included for **eye and face-adjacent ocular safety boundary**.

**Findings:** In 264 myopic children over 12 months, extracted summaries report less myopia progression versus spectacles alone and no severe adverse events, functional visual loss, or OCT structural damage.

**Why it matters:** It shows intentional retinal red-light exposure under ophthalmic study conditions, which should not be conflated with consumer face-mask leakage.

**Potential experiment signals:** visual acuity, OCT structural change, afterimage, axial length, retinal exposure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only or context-only evidence; do not use for direct skin-outcome claims.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
