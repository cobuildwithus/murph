This source is included for **contemporary cosmetic light-mask registry context**.

**Findings:** The registry describes a 12-week at-home cosmetic-light-mask study using daily LED light plus vibration on most days, with skin-characteristic and perception outcomes. It is directly protocol-relevant as a design anchor but is not peer-reviewed outcome evidence in this batch.

**Why it matters:** Provides a current, direct-protocol registry anchor for a consumer LED mask and highlights outcome domains likely to matter for Murph tracking.

**Potential experiment signals:** Wrinkle and skin-characteristic measures, consumer-perception endpoints, image collection, adherence schedule, tolerability, and exclusion criteria around ocular sensitivity, medications, skin disorders, and planned cosmetic procedures.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as design and exclusion-context evidence only until peer-reviewed results or registry results are available and verified.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
