This source is included for **eye and face-adjacent ocular safety boundary**.

**Findings:** Extracted detail indicates review of ocular complications such as glaucoma, cataract, corneal injury, and retinal abnormalities after laser or light-based therapy; causality can be difficult to establish.

**Why it matters:** It keeps rare but serious ocular outcomes visible in a dermatology-adjacent source set.

**Potential experiment signals:** new eye pain, vision changes, clinician referral, ocular diagnosis after exposure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only or context-only evidence; do not use for direct skin-outcome claims.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
