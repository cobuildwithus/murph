This source is included for **professional light-device procedure safety guideline**.

**Findings:** The guidance covers light-device hazards and operational controls, including trained use, controlled areas, risk assessment, eye and skin hazard awareness, and incident reporting or escalation after suspected exposure events.

**Why it matters:** Provides conservative safety-boundary language for optical-radiation hazards and responsible device use.

**Potential experiment signals:** Eye protection, direct-gaze avoidance, operator training, controlled-area concepts, incident reporting, stop conditions, and prompt eye assessment after exposure incidents.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only guidance for eye and skin hazard awareness, not as efficacy support for home PBM.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
