This source is included for **skin safety and screening boundary**.

**Findings:** Review frames infrared exposure as context-dependent: potentially beneficial in controlled PBM settings but harmful at excessive irradiance/dose or thermal exposure.

**Why it matters:** Use to emphasize nonthermal use, device heat checks, and avoidance of treating heat sensation as a desired endpoint.

**Potential experiment signals:** temperature rise, warmth/burning symptom log, device irradiance audit.

**Protocol takeaway:** Infrared skin effects depend on wavelength, irradiance, dose, and heating context. Does not define a target protocol dose by itself.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

### Extraction notes

- **Population:** Infrared and near-infrared skin photobiology literature.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Infrared radiation and near-infrared-related exposures.
- **Comparator/control:** Narrative comparison of beneficial PBM-like effects versus harmful high-intensity/thermal exposures.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Not applicable.
- **Endpoints:** Photoaging, skin repair, heat/thermal effects, oxidative stress, MMP pathways, photoprotection, and PBM effects.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Thermal or high-intensity infrared can contribute to unwanted skin effects; nonthermal PBM should be separated from heat exposure.
- **Limitations:** Narrative review without pooled effect estimates.; Infrared exposure categories are heterogeneous.; Not a specific red/NIR facial photoaging trial.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Safety/background for infrared/NIR boundary language.
- **Artifact/rights status:** open_access.
