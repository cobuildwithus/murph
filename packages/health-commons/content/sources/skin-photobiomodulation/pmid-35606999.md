This source is included for **eye and face-adjacent ocular safety boundary**.

**Findings:** The guideline revises exposure-limit concepts for broadband incoherent visible and infrared radiation, including retinal hazard functions and dependence on wavelength, exposure duration, and source size.

**Why it matters:** It supplies optical-radiation boundary logic when consumer device dose parameters are evaluated.

**Potential experiment signals:** wavelength, irradiance, session duration, source size, retinal exposure boundary.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only or context-only evidence; do not use for direct skin-outcome claims.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
