This source is included for **eye and face-adjacent ocular safety boundary**.

**Findings:** The review identified 119 reported cases of eye injury associated with dermatologic laser treatments; secondary extraction indicates many were considered preventable with proper ocular protection.

**Why it matters:** It quantifies a case-review corpus and supports prevention-oriented protocol wording.

**Potential experiment signals:** eye-protection use, eyelid treatment proximity, direct ocular exposure, injury preventability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only or context-only evidence; do not use for direct skin-outcome claims.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
