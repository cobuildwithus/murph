This source is included for **large-area/full-panel cosmetic PBM sibling evidence**.

**Findings:** The source reports 136 volunteers, including 113 treated participants and 23 controls, with twice-weekly sessions for 30 sessions. Reported endpoints included blinded photo assessment, digital profilometry, ultrasound collagen-density measures, and participant satisfaction. Improvements were reported for complexion/skin feel, roughness, and collagen density, while the broadband red/NIR devices did not show a clear advantage over red-only exposure. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-24286286`.

**Why it matters:** It is one of the larger controlled cosmetic PBM studies and reports objective texture and collagen-density endpoints, while also warning against assuming extra benefit from broader red/NIR spectra.

**Potential experiment signals:** skin roughness/profilometry, ultrasound collagen density, complexion/skin-feel questionnaire, standardized clinical photographs.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent large-area evidence for measurement strategy and plausible outcome domains, not as direct proof for a facial red+NIR mask.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
