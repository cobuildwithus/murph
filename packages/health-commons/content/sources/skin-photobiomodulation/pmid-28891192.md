This source is included for **eye and face-adjacent ocular safety boundary**.

**Findings:** The review included 43 articles; ocular complaints ranged from 0% to 45%, with no clear dose relationship and no evidence of ocular damage except one maculopathy case in a person using clomipramine.

**Why it matters:** It preserves a mixed safety picture: symptoms can occur, serious damage was rare in studied populations, and susceptible groups remain uncertain.

**Potential experiment signals:** ocular discomfort, vision problems, photosensitizing medication, preexisting eye condition.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only or context-only evidence; do not use for direct skin-outcome claims.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
