This source is included for **methods, dose-reporting, measurement, or device-geometry context**.

**Findings:** Human fibroblasts were treated with 630 nm red LED conditions at 8 J/cm², comparing continuous and pulsed illumination parameters. The study supports pulse-parameter reporting as a mechanistic and dose-method issue, not direct clinical efficacy.

**Why it matters:** Device pulse structure is often omitted from consumer-facing protocols but may matter biologically.

**Potential experiment signals:** continuous versus pulsed mode, pulse duration, pulse frequency, collagen marker, device mode reporting.

**Protocol takeaway:** Report pulse mode and do not generalize effects across pulsed and continuous devices without direct evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
