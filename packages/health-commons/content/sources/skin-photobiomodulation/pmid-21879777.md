This source is included for **photosensitizing-medication and retinoid safety boundary**.

**Findings:** Review states diagnosis is based primarily on medication history and sun-exposed eruption pattern; prevention includes counseling and sun-protective measures.

**Why it matters:** Use for conservative intake questions and referral triggers if rash occurs.

**Potential experiment signals:** medication history, sun-exposed rash screen, phototoxic/photoallergic symptom differentiation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Foundational review supports medication-history screening for light-associated eruptions. Do not use as a device-specific contraindication list.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

### Extraction notes

- **Population:** Patients taking medications associated with photosensitivity.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Photosensitizing medications plus UV or visible radiation.
- **Comparator/control:** Not applicable.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Not applicable.
- **Endpoints:** Diagnosis, phototesting/photopatch testing, prevention, sun protection, discontinuation of culprit medication, and corticosteroid treatment.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Photosensitive eruptions can require stopping the culprit drug and treating symptoms.
- **Limitations:** Review predates newer medications and devices.; Not specific to red/NIR PBM.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Medication screening safety boundary only.
- **Artifact/rights status:** paywalled.
