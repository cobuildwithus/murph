This source is included for **eye and face-adjacent ocular safety boundary**.

**Findings:** The review notes ocular damage can range from mild eyelid swelling or erythema to potentially blinding macular damage and emphasizes appropriate safety eyewear and eye shields.

**Why it matters:** It provides ophthalmology-oriented prevention guidance for light and laser ocular hazards.

**Potential experiment signals:** eyelid swelling, eye redness, macular injury concern, eye shield use.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only or context-only evidence; do not use for direct skin-outcome claims.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
