This source is included for **methods, dose-reporting, measurement, or device-geometry context**.

**Findings:** The review describes biphasic responses in LLLT/PBM, including ATP and reactive-oxygen-species effects, and notes that too little or too much light may produce different or less favorable responses. It is dose-context evidence, not a protocol-specific dose prescription.

**Why it matters:** Consumer protocols often assume longer or stronger sessions are better; dose-response biology argues against that assumption.

**Potential experiment signals:** session duration, fluence, irradiance, response plateau, irritation or adverse symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Avoid saying more red/NIR light is better; specify conservative dose windows and monitor tolerability.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
