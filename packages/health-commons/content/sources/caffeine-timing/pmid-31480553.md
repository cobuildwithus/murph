This source is included for **daytime_function_performance**.

**Findings:** The study reported no significant differences in cardiorespiratory assessment or 800-m performance after caffeine versus placebo. Caffeine impaired actigraphy sleep efficiency, actual wake time, awakenings, and subjective sleep-quality items including calm sleep, ease of falling asleep, and feeling refreshed.

**Why it matters:** Important mixed tradeoff source: no acute performance benefit but clear sleep-quality harms in trained runners.

**Potential experiment signals:** 800-m performance, sleep efficiency, wake after sleep onset, awakenings, sleep quality, feeling refreshed.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only/safety-boundary: supports avoiding evening caffeine when sleep quality is the target, but not direct 14-day curfew evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
