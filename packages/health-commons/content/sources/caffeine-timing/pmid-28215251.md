This source is included for **pharmacology_individual_differences**.

**Findings:** Caffeine consumption correlated with sleep latency and EEG/sleep-stage variables in ADORA2A T-allele carriers but not in CC genotype carriers, indicating genotype-dependent associations rather than a uniform caffeine-sleep relationship.

**Why it matters:** Objective sleep variables may respond differently to caffeine exposure depending on ADORA2A genotype, which supports keeping individual sensitivity in the protocol logic.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep-onset latency, deep sleep minutes, sleep efficiency, sleep-stage distribution.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as observational, same-mechanism support for personalized curfew strictness; do not present as proof of the curfew intervention.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
