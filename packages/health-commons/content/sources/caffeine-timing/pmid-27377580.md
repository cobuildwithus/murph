**Findings:** Adjusted NHANES models showed mixed/limited caffeine-insomnia associations and suggested the relationship may depend on habitual sleep duration rather than simple caffeine amount alone.

**Why it matters:** It distinguishes habitual population associations from intervention/timing claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** Insomnia symptoms; Sleep duration; Non-restorative sleep.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context only: habitual caffeine intake data cannot establish efficacy of a timing curfew.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
