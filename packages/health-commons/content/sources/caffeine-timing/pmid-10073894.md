This source is included for **withdrawal_tolerance_dose_reset**.

**Findings:** The review frames caffeine dependence as nuanced: withdrawal can occur after sudden cessation, but symptoms are described as moderate/transient in a portion of users and caffeine meets dependence criteria only partly or contextually.

**Why it matters:** Balances the protocol’s withdrawal caution with a conservative dependence framing.

**Potential experiment signals:** withdrawal symptoms, dependence risk, tolerance, adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only language: withdrawal is real but should be described proportionately.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

- **Intervention/exposure:** Coffee/caffeine consumption, cessation, tolerance, and dependence models.
- **Comparator/control:** Narrative comparison across human and animal evidence.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Not a single intervention duration.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Useful counterpoint against over-pathologizing caffeine users while still acknowledging withdrawal.
