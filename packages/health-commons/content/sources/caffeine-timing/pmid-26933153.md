This source is included for **withdrawal_tolerance_dose_reset**.

**Findings:** Being told one had consumed caffeine reduced craving and several withdrawal symptoms despite receiving decaf coffee in the extracted summary.

**Why it matters:** Helps interpret app-reported symptoms and placebo/nocebo effects during a reset.

**Potential experiment signals:** withdrawal symptoms, craving, fatigue, alertness, expectancy.

**Protocol takeaway:** Blinding is impossible for home protocols, so expectancy should be acknowledged in subjective outcomes.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Regular coffee drinkers who were abstinent for 24 hours; exact sample size was not extracted.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Decaffeinated coffee with an expectancy manipulation: told caffeine versus told decaf.
- **Comparator/control:** Different expectancy instructions without actual caffeine.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 24-hour abstinence before expectancy manipulation.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Subjective withdrawal symptoms can be expectancy-sensitive, which matters for self-reported outcomes.
- **Limitations:** Expectancy/placebo experiment; no direct curfew or sleep endpoint; sample count not extracted.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Subjective-withdrawal interpretation context.
