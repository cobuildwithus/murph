This source is included for **adjacent_curfew_morning_evening_context**.

**Findings:** Evening caffeine within 4 hours of bedtime was not associated with actigraphy-estimated sleep duration, sleep efficiency, or WASO in adjusted night-to-night models.

**Why it matters:** It preserves a real-world null/mixed caffeine finding and prevents overclaiming that any evening caffeine exposure always worsens sleep.

**Potential experiment signals:** Sleep duration; Sleep efficiency; Wake after sleep onset; Evening stimulant exposure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Adjacent boundary evidence: no association for self-reported caffeine within 4 hours of bedtime in this older African American cohort, so curfew claims should be framed as individualized and not universal.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
