This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:** Most participants (89%) reported caffeine use. Higher caffeine use was associated with lower insomnia symptom severity because those reporting no caffeine use had elevated insomnia severity; caffeine use was not associated with other sleep disturbance measures or PTSD symptoms.

**Why it matters:** Preserves null/mixed psychiatric sleep evidence and population mismatch.

**Potential experiment signals:** Caffeine-use prevalence; Insomnia symptom severity; Other sleep disturbance measures; PTSD symptom clusters.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as clinical context; do not infer caffeine improves insomnia.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
