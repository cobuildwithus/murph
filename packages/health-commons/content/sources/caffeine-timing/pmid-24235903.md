This source is included for **direct_protocol_and_dose_timing**.

**Findings:** Caffeine taken at bedtime, three hours, or six hours before bedtime significantly disrupted sleep compared with placebo; the six-hour condition reduced total sleep time by more than one hour in accessible summaries.

**Why it matters:** Direct anchor for a caffeine curfew: a six-hour cutoff may still be too short for a high dose.

**Potential experiment signals:** total sleep time, sleep efficiency, sleep onset latency, wake after sleep onset, sleep diary, portable sleep monitor

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports avoiding high-dose caffeine several hours before bed; an 8-hour boundary is cautious relative to this six-hour disruption finding.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
