This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:** At 150 mg, caffeine-induced anxiety was associated with ADORA2A rs5751876, rs2298383, rs4822492, and DRD2 rs1110976; 50 mg did not increase anxiety and 450 mg increased anxiety in the majority of subjects.

**Why it matters:** Reinforces that caffeine safety/tolerability can be individual-specific and dose-specific.

**Potential experiment signals:** Subjective anxiety by visual analog scales; ADORA2A and DRD2 polymorphism associations; Dose-response anxiety signals.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for anxiety-sensitivity monitoring and personalized dose ceilings, not for direct efficacy claims.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
