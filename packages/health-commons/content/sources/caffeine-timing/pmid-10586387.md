This source is included for **withdrawal_tolerance_dose_reset**.

**Findings:** The source was designed to estimate withdrawal frequency/severity in population and blinded settings; numeric prevalence estimates were not extracted in this batch.

**Why it matters:** Population and blinded-pilot context helps separate common withdrawal experiences from individual protocol response.

**Potential experiment signals:** withdrawal symptom frequency, headache, fatigue, adherence, daytime sleepiness.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as support for asking participants to log withdrawal symptoms during the reset rather than relying only on sleep metrics.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** General-population survey respondents plus participants in a controlled blinded pilot experiment; exact counts were not extracted in this batch.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Caffeine cessation/deprivation after habitual consumption.
- **Comparator/control:** Continued usual intake or blinded control condition as applicable to the survey/pilot design.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Withdrawal windows were assessed after cessation/deprivation; precise pilot duration was not extracted.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Withdrawal symptoms can be frequent enough to matter for adherence and self-rated outcomes.
- **Limitations:** Combined survey and pilot design; extraction lacks numeric rates and pilot sample size.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Withdrawal-frequency context rather than direct caffeine-curfew efficacy evidence.
