This source is included for **pharmacology_individual_differences**.

**Findings:** A urinary caffeine metabolite ratio based on paraxanthine metabolism reflected systemic caffeine clearance in a small human sample, supporting metabolite ratios as clearance phenotyping tools.

**Why it matters:** It supports the idea that caffeine clearance is measurable and variable, which underlies why a simple fixed curfew may not fit every user equally.

**Potential experiment signals:** caffeine metabolic ratio, sleep-onset latency, sleep efficiency.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as measurement context for caffeine clearance, not as evidence that a particular cutoff works.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
