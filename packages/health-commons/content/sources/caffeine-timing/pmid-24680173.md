This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:** The primary meta-analysis found caffeine exposure weakly associated with reduced AF risk (RR 0.90, 95% CI 0.81-1.01), and dose-response analysis suggested lower AF risk per 300 mg/day caffeine increment (RR 0.94, 95% CI 0.90-0.99).

**Why it matters:** Balances cardiovascular cautions by preserving a null-to-weakly protective cohort signal.

**Potential experiment signals:** Incident atrial fibrillation; Dose-response by caffeine intake.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as arrhythmia context; keep clinical guidance for diagnosed AF or palpitations.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
