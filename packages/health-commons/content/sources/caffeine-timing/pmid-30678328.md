This source is included for **caffeine_source_dose_audit**.

**Findings:** In a profile of 100 multi-ingredient pre-workout supplements, caffeine appeared in 86% of products with an average labeled amount of 254.0 ± 79.5 mg, while 44.3% of ingredients were included in proprietary blends with undisclosed amounts.

**Why it matters:** Pre-workout labels often contain caffeine and proprietary blends, making supplement source audits necessary.

**Potential experiment signals:** pre-workout caffeine dose, source fidelity, hidden caffeine source count.

**Protocol takeaway:** Screen for multi-ingredient pre-workouts and proprietary blends; do not assume all active ingredients or caffeine amounts are fully disclosed.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
