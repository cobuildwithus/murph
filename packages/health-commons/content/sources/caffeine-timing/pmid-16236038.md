This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-16236038-fluvoxamine-clearance-half-life`: Fluvoxamine greatly reduced apparent oral caffeine clearance and prolonged caffeine elimination half-life from about 4.9 hours to 56 hours after a single caffeine dose.
- `finding:pmid-16236038-pharmacodynamics-not-augmented`: Although caffeine produced CNS-stimulating effects, fluvoxamine coadministration did not detectably augment acute psychomotor, alertness, or EEG effects; the authors still cautioned that daily caffeine accumulation was not ruled out.

**Why it matters:** CYP1A2 inhibition can convert an ordinary caffeine dose into a much longer exposure window, making a fixed curfew insufficient for some medication contexts.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- biomarker:caffeine-clearance
- biomarker:caffeine-half-life
- safety:drug-interaction

**Protocol takeaway:** Flag fluvoxamine and similar CYP1A2-inhibiting contexts as reasons to seek clinician guidance and avoid assuming standard caffeine clearance.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
