This source is included for **daytime_function_performance**.

**Findings:** The case study examined caffeine exposure in relation to postcompetition sleep, but the observational design and competition context prevent causal attribution. The study should be used as a real-world sport boundary source rather than efficacy evidence for a caffeine curfew.

**Why it matters:** Small real-world sport boundary source for how competition-day caffeine and sleep are measured outside laboratory settings.

**Potential experiment signals:** salivary caffeine, postcompetition sleep duration, sleep timing, sleep efficiency.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only: useful for real-world measurement context, not causal evidence for or against caffeine curfews.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
