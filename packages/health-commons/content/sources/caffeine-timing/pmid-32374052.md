This source is included for **direct_protocol_and_dose_timing**.

**Findings:** Abstinence did not produce significant mean-level or growth-trajectory improvements in total sleep time or sleep efficiency, and mean sleep-onset latency did not differ; a small quadratic latency effect suggested early decrease followed by rebound above baseline.

**Why it matters:** Closest reset analogue in poor sleepers, and it cautions against assuming complete abstinence produces long-term sleep improvement.

**Potential experiment signals:** actigraphic total sleep time, actigraphic sleep efficiency, sleep onset latency, daily caffeine diary

**Protocol takeaway:** A 14-day caffeine curfew/reset should be treated as an experiment with measurable endpoints, not a guaranteed sleep cure.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
