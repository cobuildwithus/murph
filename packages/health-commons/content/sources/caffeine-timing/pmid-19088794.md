This source is included for **systematic_reviews_meta_analyses**.

**Findings:** This sports-performance review reported that moderate caffeine doses, often around 3 mg/kg body mass, can improve endurance, stop-and-go, and sustained high-intensity exercise performance, but it did not evaluate caffeine curfews or subsequent sleep.

**Why it matters:** Explains a legitimate competing reason people may use caffeine later in the day: acute performance and alertness benefits.

**Potential experiment signals:** Workout performance, perceived exertion, estimated VO2max trend.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as boundary context: caffeine has performance uses, so curfews may require tradeoffs around afternoon training.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
