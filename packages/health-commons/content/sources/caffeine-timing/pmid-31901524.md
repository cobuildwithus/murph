This source is included for **measurement_wearables_psg_actigraphy**.

**Findings:** The review found that average sleep-onset estimates could look similar for some devices, but individual variability was substantial; EEG-based devices were generally less variable than behavioral devices.

**Why it matters:** Sleep onset latency is a likely Murph endpoint for caffeine curfew experiments and is especially sensitive to wearable measurement bias.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep onset latency.

**Protocol takeaway:** Treat wearable sleep-onset latency as a noisy within-person trend and consider sleep diaries as complementary context.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
