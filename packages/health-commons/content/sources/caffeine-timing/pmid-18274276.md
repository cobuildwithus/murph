This source is included for **measurement_wearables_psg_actigraphy**.

**Findings:** Actigraphy corresponded reasonably well with PSG but showed endpoint-specific and subgroup bias; for example, PIM-mode TST and SE differed from PSG and bias varied among short sleepers or low sleep-efficiency users.

**Why it matters:** Age-sensitive evidence that actigraphy performance can vary in older adults.

**Potential experiment signals:** total sleep time, sleep efficiency, wake after sleep onset.

**Protocol takeaway:** Interpret wearable outcomes in older users with extra caution and consider sleep-disorder screening.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
