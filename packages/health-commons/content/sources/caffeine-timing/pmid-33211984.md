This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-33211984-01`: The systematic review and meta-analysis found that energy drink consumption significantly increased odds of insomnia and jitteriness/activeness compared with control.
- `finding:pmid-33211984-02`: The review reports frequent pediatric adverse events including insomnia, stress, and depressive mood, and adult adverse events including jitteriness/restlessness/shaking hands, insomnia, and gastrointestinal upset.
- `finding:pmid-33211984-03`: The authors recommend avoiding frequent energy-drink consumption and avoiding co-consumption with alcohol, with particular regulatory concern for pediatric populations.

**Why it matters:** Preserves adverse and mixed energy-drink findings, especially insomnia/jitteriness, without treating energy drinks as a clean caffeine-curfew intervention.

- biomarker:adverse-events
