This source is included for **direct_protocol_and_dose_timing**.

**Findings:** A 200 mg morning caffeine dose affected subsequent nighttime sleep and EEG power spectra in nine healthy men, showing that earlier-in-day dosing can still have nighttime physiological effects.

**Why it matters:** Important guardrail against assuming morning caffeine is always physiologically neutral.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep stages, sleep EEG power spectra, sleep efficiency, light sleep, slow-wave activity

**Protocol takeaway:** The protocol’s late-morning cutoff may help many users, but morning dose size and individual clearance still matter.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
