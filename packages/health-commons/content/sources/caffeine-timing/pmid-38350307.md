This source is included for **systematic_reviews_meta_analyses**.

**Findings:** This adolescent systematic review/meta-analysis reported that higher caffeine intake was associated with greater odds of sleep problems (OR 1.67, 95% CI 1.28-2.17), but most included studies were cross-sectional/self-reported and intervention validation was still needed.

**Why it matters:** Provides youth safety/population-boundary context and supports caution around caffeine in adolescents.

**Potential experiment signals:** Sleep problems, sleep-onset latency, sleep duration.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adolescent boundary evidence: youth caffeine/sleep associations support caution, but this is not adult curfew efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
