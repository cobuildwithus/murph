This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:** The review included 22 records: 15 randomized trials, 6 cross-sectional studies, and 1 genome-wide association study; CYP1A2 was associated with cognitive-function outcomes and ADORA2A with anxiety and sleep disturbance.

**Why it matters:** Supports individual-response framing for caffeine timing, especially anxiety and sleep endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** Cognitive performance; Anxiety; Sleep disturbance/insomnia.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as genetics context; do not infer protocol efficacy or personalized medical recommendations.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
