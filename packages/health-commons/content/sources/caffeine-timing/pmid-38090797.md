This source is included for **measurement_wearables_psg_actigraphy**.

**Findings:** Zmachine showed moderate agreement with PSG for TST, WASO, and SE, while Fitbit showed notable disagreement with PSG and sleep logs; Fitbit accuracy was reduced in the presence of comorbid clinical symptoms.

**Why it matters:** Clinical-boundary validation for consumer-device and diary limitations in psychiatric populations.

**Potential experiment signals:** total sleep time, sleep efficiency, wake after sleep onset, sleep diary agreement.

**Protocol takeaway:** If mood disorder, insomnia, or OSA symptoms are present, interpret wearable and diary sleep outcomes as context and avoid efficacy claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
