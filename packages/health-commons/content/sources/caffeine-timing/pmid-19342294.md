**Findings:** Caffeine decreased sleep efficiency, sleep duration, slow-wave sleep, and REM sleep during daytime recovery sleep and reduced NREM EEG synchronization in the extracted summary.

**Why it matters:** Highlights sensitivity to circadian phase, age, and recovery-sleep context.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep efficiency, total sleep time, deep sleep, REM sleep, recovery sleep.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as boundary evidence that caffeine near recovery sleep can disturb architecture, especially in non-ordinary sleep contexts.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Young and middle-aged participants in a daytime recovery sleep study; exact sample size not extracted.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Caffeine challenge before daytime recovery sleep after sleep-wake-cycle disruption.
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo/control recovery sleep condition.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Daytime recovery sleep after challenge; exact pre-sleep timing not extracted here.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Caffeine can impair recovery sleep architecture in challenged/aging contexts.
- **Limitations:** Recovery sleep/daytime and age-related challenge context; not ordinary nighttime curfew use.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Boundary sleep evidence rather than direct morning-only curfew evidence.
