This source is included for **measurement_wearables_psg_actigraphy**.

**Findings:** Combined actigraphy and subjective data did not significantly differ from PSG for TST and sleep efficiency, but measurement validity depended on endpoint and sleep-disordered context.

**Why it matters:** Warns against assuming actigraphy or subjective sleep estimates equal PSG in sleep-disordered users.

**Potential experiment signals:** total sleep time, sleep efficiency, wake after sleep onset, sleep diary agreement.

**Protocol takeaway:** Ask about known sleep disorders and treat diary/device signals as complementary rather than definitive.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
