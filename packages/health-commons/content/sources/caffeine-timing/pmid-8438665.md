**Findings:** The older-adult observational report suggests caffeine-insomnia associations may be complicated by poor sleepers reducing caffeine intake, creating possible reverse causation.

**Why it matters:** It preserves an older-adult reverse-causation boundary for caffeine/sleep associations.

**Potential experiment signals:** Subjective insomnia; Coffee intake; Tea intake.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context only: do not infer from older-adult caffeine intake patterns that caffeine is or is not causing insomnia.
