This source is included for **direct_protocol_and_dose_timing**.

**Findings:** More than two million common SNPs were tested in 2,402 coffee-drinking Australian-twin-registry participants; no SNP reached genome-wide significance, while results supported follow-up of adenosine-receptor sensitivity signals.

**Why it matters:** Preserves mixed genetic evidence: adenosine-receptor biology is plausible, but findings were not a direct protocol test.

**Potential experiment signals:** self-reported caffeine-induced insomnia, genetic association, ADORA2A-related signal

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as context for individual sensitivity; it does not show that a caffeine curfew improves sleep.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
