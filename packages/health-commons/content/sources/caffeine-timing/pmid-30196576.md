This source is included for **caffeine_source_dose_audit**.

**Findings:** Analysis of 15 Australian pre-workout supplements found caffeine contents of 91-387 mg/serve; only 6 labels listed caffeine, analyzed amounts were 59%-176% of claims, and all but one product had practically significant within- or between-batch variation of at least 40 mg/serve.

**Why it matters:** Evening exercise pre-workouts can quietly deliver several beverage-equivalent caffeine doses.

**Potential experiment signals:** pre-workout caffeine dose, caffeinated product after cutoff, source fidelity, adverse caffeine symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Ask about pre-workout powders and servings, especially before evening training; label absence or batch variation should be treated as dose uncertainty.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
