This source is included for **systematic_reviews_meta_analyses**.

**Findings:** A quantitative systematic review of 44 studies including 21,438 healthy adults found lower short-term HRV normal values than historical norms and very large interindividual variability, emphasizing that HRV interpretation is method- and person-dependent.

**Why it matters:** Supports caution when using RMSSD or other HRV values as a consumer wearable endpoint.

**Potential experiment signals:** HRV RMSSD, resting heart rate.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to contextualize HRV variability and avoid treating a single HRV change as protocol proof.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
