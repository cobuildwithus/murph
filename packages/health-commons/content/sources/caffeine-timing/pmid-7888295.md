This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-7888295-ciprofloxacin-caffeine-breath-test`: Ciprofloxacin significantly reduced two-hour cumulative labeled CO2 exhalation in the caffeine breath test, indicating inhibited caffeine metabolism in children with cystic fibrosis.
- `finding:pmid-7888295-pediatric-cf-mismatch`: The source is useful for interaction caution but has a major population mismatch because it studied six children with cystic fibrosis rather than adult curfew participants.

**Why it matters:** Reinforces ciprofloxacin as a caffeine-clearance modifier and warns against applying adult caffeine-curfew defaults to children or disease-specific contexts.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- biomarker:caffeine-clearance
- safety:drug-interaction
- population:pediatric

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep ciprofloxacin on the interaction audit list and keep pediatric caffeine advice outside the adult curfew protocol.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
