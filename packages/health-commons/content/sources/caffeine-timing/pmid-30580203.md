This source is included for **caffeine_source_dose_audit**.

**Findings:** In a randomized delayed-treatment trial, a manual-only gradual caffeine reduction/cessation program reduced self-reported caffeine consumption and caffeine-related distress, with salivary caffeine and community observers corroborating reductions; this is adjacent reduction evidence, not curfew sleep evidence.

**Why it matters:** It supports feasibility of gradual caffeine reduction with self-monitoring, but not the specific 10-11am/8-hour curfew or sleep outcome.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily caffeine dose, caffeine cutback adherence, caffeine-related distress, salivary caffeine.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent evidence for structured caffeine reduction and self-monitoring, not as direct sleep-curfew efficacy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
