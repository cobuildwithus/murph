This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:** The review identified 57 experimental and 9 survey studies, validated 10 withdrawal symptom categories, estimated headache incidence around 50%, and clinically significant distress or functional impairment around 13%.

**Why it matters:** A 14-day curfew or dose reset can unintentionally lower total caffeine, so withdrawal symptoms must be separated from sleep-improvement or fatigue outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:** Withdrawal headache; Fatigue and low energy; Alertness, mood, concentration, irritability; Functional impairment.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to warn that abrupt reductions can cause withdrawal for several days and can confound early protocol outcomes.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
