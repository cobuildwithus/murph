This source is included for **measurement_wearables_psg_actigraphy**.

**Findings:** The systematic review included 8 articles; WHOOP showed the least average disagreement for TST, light sleep, and deep sleep but the largest REM disagreement, while Fitbit and Garmin had moderate stage and TST accuracy; all devices still needed improvement for specific stages.

**Why it matters:** Device-specific review useful for guarding against overinterpreting REM/deep sleep and stage outputs from common trackers.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep stages, REM sleep, deep sleep, total sleep time, sleep efficiency.

**Protocol takeaway:** When users submit Fitbit/Garmin/WHOOP data, prioritize TST/SE trends over stage claims and record model/version.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
