This source is included for **direct_protocol_and_dose_timing**.

**Findings:** Repeated high-dose caffeine increased metabolic rate, significantly reduced sleep efficiency, and increased MSLT latency; some adaptation occurred and rebound sleep or sleepiness was not observed after withdrawal.

**Why it matters:** Shows caffeine can objectively reduce sleep efficiency and alter daytime sleepiness physiology, but the 1,200 mg/day dosing is far outside a simple curfew experiment.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep efficiency, multiple sleep latency test, metabolic rate, withdrawal or rebound sleepiness

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports caffeine as a sleep-disrupting exposure; should be framed as high-dose mechanistic/adjacent evidence rather than a practical 10–11am curfew test.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
