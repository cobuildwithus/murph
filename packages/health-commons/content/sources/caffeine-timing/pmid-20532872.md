This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:** The review reports heritability estimates for caffeine-related traits of about 0.36-0.58 and links adenosine/dopamine receptor variation to caffeine-induced anxiety and sleep disturbance in the literature.

**Why it matters:** Helps explain variable response to caffeine timing and why some users may need more conservative cutoffs.

**Potential experiment signals:** Caffeine consumption heritability; Withdrawal, insomnia, anxiety; Adenosine/dopamine receptor polymorphisms; CYP1A2-related metabolic response.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as individual-difference context, not as protocol efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
