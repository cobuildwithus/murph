This source is included for **withdrawal_tolerance_dose_reset**.

**Findings:** The Caffeine Withdrawal Symptom Questionnaire grouped 23 items into seven symptom factors: fatigue/drowsiness, low alertness/difficulty concentrating, mood disturbances, low sociability/motivation to work, nausea/upset stomach, flu-like feelings, and headache.

**Why it matters:** Provides a structured symptom family list for a 14-day reset/offramp.

**Potential experiment signals:** headache, fatigue/drowsiness, low alertness, mood disturbance, nausea, flu-like feelings, motivation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use CWSQ-like symptom categories to track reset tolerability.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Habitual caffeine consumers who completed a withdrawal symptom questionnaire after abstinence.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Caffeine abstinence before symptom questionnaire completion.
- **Comparator/control:** Questionnaire development/factor structure rather than treatment comparator.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Abstinence before questionnaire completion; available snippet described a 16-hour abstinence window.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Questionnaire factors identify symptom domains that may become adverse experiences during dose reset.
- **Limitations:** Measurement-development source, not an efficacy trial.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Withdrawal measurement context rather than direct curfew evidence.
