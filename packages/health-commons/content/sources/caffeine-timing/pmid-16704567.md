This source is included for **direct_protocol_and_dose_timing**.

**Findings:** The double-blind crossover study tested 200 mg evening caffeine in 12 young and 12 middle-aged moderate consumers; accessible summaries report increases in sleep latency and reductions in sleep duration/efficiency in both age groups, with age comparison as a key boundary.

**Why it matters:** Relevant to curfew personalization because it tests an evening dose in habitual moderate consumers and includes an age comparison.

**Potential experiment signals:** polysomnographic sleep variables, sleep EEG, sleep latency, sleep duration, sleep efficiency

**Protocol takeaway:** Even 200 mg in the evening is a plausible sleep challenge; age and habitual-use context should be preserved rather than assuming a universal response.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
