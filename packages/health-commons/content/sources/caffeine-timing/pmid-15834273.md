This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:** Across 16 randomized controlled studies, caffeine/coffee increased systolic blood pressure by about 2.04 mmHg and diastolic blood pressure by about 0.73 mmHg; caffeine-only trials showed larger effects than coffee trials.

**Why it matters:** Supports blood-pressure caution for users who retain high total caffeine dose during a curfew or use caffeine supplements.

**Potential experiment signals:** Systolic blood pressure; Diastolic blood pressure; Heart rate.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only evidence: caffeine can raise blood pressure, especially as supplemental caffeine, so hypertensive users should use clinical guidance.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
