This source is included for **caffeine_source_dose_audit**.

**Findings:** A nationally representative 7-day diary analysis of 37,602 U.S. consumers aged 2 years and older reported that 85% consumed at least one caffeinated beverage per day, mean beverage caffeine intake was 165 ± 1 mg/day, the 90th percentile was 380 mg/day, and coffee was the primary contributor overall.

**Why it matters:** It contextualizes typical beverage caffeine intake and source mix, preventing protocol pages from treating coffee alone as the exposure.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily caffeine dose, beverage source mix, population caffeine intake percentile.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as U.S. population context for baseline caffeine dose and source distribution; do not use as causal sleep evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
