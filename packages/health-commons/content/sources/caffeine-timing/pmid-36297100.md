This source is included for **caffeine_source_dose_audit**.

**Findings:** The Brazilian caffeine content table (BraCaffT) compiled 57 items across 11 source categories, including coffees, teas/infusions, cocoa/chocolate products, soft drinks, energy drinks, guaraná powder, dietary supplements, and medications.

**Why it matters:** The table explicitly includes medications and supplements, categories often missed in beverage-focused caffeine logs.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily caffeine dose, medication caffeine dose, supplement caffeine dose, source-fidelity checklist.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as support for broad caffeine source inventories spanning drinks, foods, medications, and supplements.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
