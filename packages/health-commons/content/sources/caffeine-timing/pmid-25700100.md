This source is included for **daytime_function_performance**.

**Findings:** Plasma caffeine peaked 100 minutes after ingestion; clearance was 0.95 ± 0.14 mL/min/kg and elimination half-life was 17.63 ± 8.06 hours. Sleep latency and subsequent sleep quality were impaired after caffeine (p<0.05), while feelings upon awakening did not differ between trials.

**Why it matters:** Highlights a population boundary: caffeine metabolism and sleep effects may be prolonged in some female athletes using oral contraceptives.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep onset latency, sleep quality, caffeine half-life, awakening alertness, caffeine metabolites.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only/safety-boundary: supports individualized earlier caffeine cutoffs for slower-metabolizing or oral-contraceptive users, but it is not direct protocol evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
