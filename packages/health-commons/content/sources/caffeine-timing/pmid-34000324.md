This source is included for **systematic_reviews_meta_analyses**.

**Findings:** This focused review of 28 military-population articles reported average daily caffeine intake around 212-285 mg/day, associations between greater caffeine intake and shorter sleep/psychological symptoms, and performance benefits during prolonged sleep deprivation.

**Why it matters:** Provides safety and population-boundary context for high caffeine use and energy drinks, especially when sleep restriction is present.

**Potential experiment signals:** Sleep duration, sleep efficiency, daytime alertness, anxiety or irritability notes.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-boundary context: caffeine can be operationally useful during sleep deprivation but may worsen sleep patterns and symptoms when intake is high.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
