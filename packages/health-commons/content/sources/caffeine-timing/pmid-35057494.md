This source is included for **caffeine_source_dose_audit**.

**Findings:** This product-profile study of top-selling U.S. energy drinks and shots reported high prevalence of caffeine and B-vitamins, with many formulations exceeding recommended daily values for B-vitamins; sample count and exact caffeine distribution were not fully extracted in this batch.

**Why it matters:** Energy products may combine caffeine with other ingredients, and concentrated shots require explicit source logging.

**Potential experiment signals:** energy drink caffeine dose, source fidelity, adverse caffeine symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Log energy products by drink/shot category and serving size, and flag multi-ingredient energy products as dose/safety-relevant.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
