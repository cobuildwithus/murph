This source is included for **same_mechanism_context**.

**Findings:** Oura summary sleep duration/WASO estimates were often close to PSG, but wake specificity and stage classification were limited, including underestimation of N3 and overestimation of REM.

**Why it matters:** It helps bound claims based on Oura-derived sleep stages in caffeine self-tracking.

**Potential experiment signals:** Total sleep time; Wake after sleep onset; Deep sleep minutes; REM sleep estimates.

**Protocol takeaway:** Measurement context only: Oura stage outputs should not be treated as PSG-equivalent evidence of caffeine-curfew effects.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
