This source is included for **direct_protocol_and_dose_timing**.

**Findings:** After repeated daily caffeine, PSG total sleep time, sleep latency, sleep architecture, and subjective sleep quality did not significantly differ, while sleep EEG sigma power was reduced in caffeine and withdrawal conditions versus placebo.

**Why it matters:** Shows daily caffeine can alter sleep EEG without necessarily changing macro PSG sleep measures, preventing oversimplified claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** polysomnographic total sleep time, sleep latency, sleep architecture, subjective sleep quality, EEG sigma power

**Protocol takeaway:** Repeated daytime caffeine may not always shift macro sleep metrics, so self-experiments should include multiple endpoints and not rely only on total sleep time.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
