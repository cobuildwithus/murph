This source is included for **withdrawal_tolerance_dose_reset**.

**Findings:** The extracted abstract summary supports the hypothesis that many psychostimulant and related effects of caffeine in consumers may reflect withdrawal reversal; effects were absent or limited in habitual non-consumers.

**Why it matters:** Tests whether caffeine benefits persist when withdrawal is unlikely.

**Potential experiment signals:** alertness, mood, psychomotor performance, withdrawal reversal.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not assume caffeine benefits or reset benefits generalize to non-consumers.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Habitual non-consumers of caffeine compared with acutely withdrawn consumers in an experimental design; exact count not extracted.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Caffeine in habitual non-consumers and withdrawn consumers.
- **Comparator/control:** No-caffeine/control drink and consumer-status groups.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Acute dosing across test days; exact schedule not extracted.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Not an adverse-event source; it is a boundary source for interpreting benefit claims.
- **Limitations:** Non-consumer boundary design; no sleep endpoints and no count extracted.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Adjacent withdrawal-reversal evidence, not direct curfew evidence.
