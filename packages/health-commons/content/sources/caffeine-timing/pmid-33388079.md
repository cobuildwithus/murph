This source is included for **caffeine_source_dose_audit**.

**Findings:** The ISSN position stand summarizes caffeine as an ergogenic aid commonly at 3-6 mg/kg body mass and recognizes forms such as gums, gels/chews, energy drinks, and pre-workout products, making it adjacent source and dosing context for active users.

**Why it matters:** Exercise-focused caffeine sources can be hidden in gels, gums, energy products, and pre-workouts.

**Potential experiment signals:** supplement caffeine dose, exercise caffeine source, adverse caffeine symptoms, sleep disruption context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent sports-source context; avoid using performance benefits as a reason to relax the sleep-focused curfew.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
