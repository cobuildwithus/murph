This source is included for **withdrawal_tolerance_dose_reset**.

**Findings:** The review describes behavioral effects of caffeine withdrawal, including mood and sleepiness-related symptoms; no single pooled effect estimate is provided.

**Why it matters:** Preserves older and mixed interpretations around caffeine withdrawal so the protocol does not overstate simple dose-reset benefits.

**Potential experiment signals:** mood, sleepiness, irritability, headache, fatigue.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as background context for withdrawal symptom families, not as direct efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Human caffeine users and related behavioral literature summarized in a review.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Caffeine withdrawal and related caffeine-behavior conditions.
- **Comparator/control:** Narrative comparison across withdrawal, acute caffeine, and dependence-related studies.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Not a single intervention duration; summarizes multiple withdrawal time courses.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Withdrawal-related dysphoria, irritability, and sleepiness may be relevant adverse experiences during reduction.
- **Limitations:** Narrative review with older literature and potentially mixed interpretations; not a protocol trial.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** General withdrawal review, not direct curfew evidence.
