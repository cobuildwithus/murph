This source is included for **withdrawal_tolerance_dose_reset**.

**Findings:** The authors reported cognitive and mood improvements in both consumers and non-consumers in patterns that did not support a pure withdrawal-alleviation model.

**Why it matters:** Important counterpoint: not all caffeine effects can be reduced to withdrawal reversal.

**Potential experiment signals:** mood, cognitive performance, alertness, consumer status.

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep wording balanced: withdrawal matters, but caffeine can have acute effects in some non-withdrawn people too.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
