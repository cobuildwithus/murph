This source is included for **pharmacology_individual_differences**.

**Findings:** A saliva-based caffeine test was used to estimate CYP1A2 activity in 863 healthy Caucasians, supporting large-sample phenotyping of caffeine metabolism variability.

**Why it matters:** Large-scale phenotyping context supports that caffeine metabolism varies across healthy adults and may influence curfew response.

**Potential experiment signals:** caffeine metabolic ratio, sleep-onset latency, sleep efficiency.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as measurement/population variability context, not direct evidence for a protocol claim.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
