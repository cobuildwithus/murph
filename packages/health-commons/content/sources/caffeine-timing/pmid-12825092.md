This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:** A significant association was reported between self-reported anxiety after caffeine and two linked A2a receptor polymorphisms, with T/T genotypes showing greater caffeine-related anxiety.

**Why it matters:** Shows that anxiety response to caffeine can vary by adenosine receptor genotype, supporting individualized caution during caffeine timing and dose-reset experiments.

**Potential experiment signals:** Self-reported anxiety after caffeine; ADORA2A/A1 polymorphism associations; Tolerability signal for caffeine-sensitive users.

**Protocol takeaway:** Useful for anxiety-sensitivity caution language and monitoring, not for proving curfew efficacy.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
