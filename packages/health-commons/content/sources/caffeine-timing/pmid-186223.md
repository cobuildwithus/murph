This source is included for **direct_protocol_and_dose_timing**.

**Findings:** Regular coffee produced dose-related changes in standard EEG-EOG sleep parameters; four-cup-equivalent regular coffee and caffeine produced comparable effects, while decaffeinated coffee had no effect in accessible summaries.

**Why it matters:** Older but directly relevant evidence that dose matters, not only clock timing.

**Potential experiment signals:** EEG-EOG sleep parameters, REM timing, stage 3 and 4 sleep timing, subjective sleep characteristics

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports the dose-reset half of the protocol, while exact dose thresholds should be taken from newer/direct timing trials.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
