This source is included for **caffeine_source_dose_audit**.

**Findings:** Analysis of 10 energy drinks, 19 carbonated sodas, and seven other beverages found caffeine ranges of none detected to 141.1 mg/serving for energy drinks, none detected to 48.2 mg/serving for sodas, and below 2.7 to 105.7 mg/serving for other beverages.

**Why it matters:** Energy drinks, sodas, and other beverages can meaningfully affect daily caffeine accounting and protocol adherence.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily caffeine dose, beverage source fidelity, caffeinated product after cutoff.

**Protocol takeaway:** Include energy drinks, sodas, bottled teas, and other caffeinated beverages in the no-caffeine-after-cutoff audit.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
