This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-25179792-pregnancy-dose-response-adverse-outcomes`: The meta-analysis reported dose-response associations between higher caffeine intake and increased risks for spontaneous abortion, stillbirth, low birth weight, and small-for-gestational-age outcomes.
- `finding:pmid-25179792-preterm-delivery-null-mixed`: The preterm-delivery estimate per 100 mg/day caffeine was near null and not statistically significant, preserving an endpoint-specific mixed/null pregnancy finding.

**Why it matters:** Pregnancy endpoints vary: several fetal-growth or pregnancy-loss outcomes trend higher with caffeine dose, while preterm delivery evidence is more null/mixed.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- safety:pregnancy
- safety:fetal-growth
- safety:pregnancy-loss
- safety:preterm-birth

**Protocol takeaway:** Preserve endpoint-specific caution: pregnancy caffeine guidance should be clinician-led and should not rely on an ordinary curfew protocol.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
