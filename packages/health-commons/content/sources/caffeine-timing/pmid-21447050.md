This source is included for **measurement_wearables_psg_actigraphy**.

**Findings:** Actigraphy was closer to PSG than sleep diaries for several variables, but latency to persistent sleep was underestimated and broad limits of agreement remained; sleep diaries differed significantly from PSG for all sleep variables.

**Why it matters:** Important boundary source for subjective-objective discrepancy in insomnia-like populations.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep onset latency, total sleep time, sleep efficiency, wake after sleep onset, sleep diary agreement.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use diaries to capture experience and adherence, but avoid treating diary sleep outcomes as objective PSG substitutes.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
