This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-6954898-pregnancy-half-life-prolonged`: Accessible study summary reports caffeine half-life increasing from about 3 hours in nonpregnant women to about 10.5 hours during the last four weeks of pregnancy.
- `finding:pmid-6954898-accumulation-warning`: The source warns that caffeine accumulation may occur unless intake is reduced and that nonspecific symptoms of caffeine toxicity can make the cause harder to recognize.

**Why it matters:** Pregnancy can lengthen caffeine half-life dramatically, making a fixed 8-hour or late-morning cutoff potentially inadequate.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- biomarker:caffeine-half-life
- biomarker:caffeine-clearance
- safety:pregnancy

**Protocol takeaway:** Pregnancy should be handled as a safety exclusion or clinician-guided modifier rather than ordinary caffeine-curfew experimentation.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
