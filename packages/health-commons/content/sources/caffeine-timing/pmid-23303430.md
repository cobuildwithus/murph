This source is included for **caffeine_source_dose_audit**.

**Findings:** The article is a JAMA Internal Medicine caffeine-content audit of dietary supplements consumed on military bases, useful for hidden supplement-source tracking; detailed product counts and numeric results were not extracted from accessible metadata in this batch.

**Why it matters:** Military-base supplement use is a high-relevance context for caffeine pills, powders, and stimulant blends that can undermine dose logs.

**Potential experiment signals:** supplement caffeine dose, hidden caffeine source count, adverse caffeine symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Flag as supplement source-fidelity evidence; verify full article before using product counts or numerical label-accuracy claims.
