This source is included for **withdrawal_tolerance_dose_reset**.

**Findings:** The intervention reduced self-reported caffeine and salivary caffeine, with no significant post-treatment increases through follow-up in the extracted summary.

**Why it matters:** Shows that structured manualized support can be relevant for caffeine reduction when self-guided reset is difficult.

**Potential experiment signals:** caffeine intake, salivary caffeine, adherence, problematic caffeine use.

**Protocol takeaway:** Offer structured taper/support guidance for users who struggle with the 14-day reset.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** People with problematic caffeine use in a randomized controlled trial; exact sample size was not extracted in this batch.
- **Intervention/exposure:** A brief therapist-guided/manualized treatment, including a one-hour session and take-home booklet.
- **Comparator/control:** Waitlist/control comparison in the randomized trial.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Post-treatment follow-up through up to one year in the extracted summary.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Structured support may help people who cannot reduce caffeine on their own.
- **Limitations:** Clinical treatment context and problematic-use population; sample count not extracted here.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Treatment/reduction adherence evidence, not direct sleep-curfew evidence.
