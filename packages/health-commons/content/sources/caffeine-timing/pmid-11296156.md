This source is included for **caffeine_source_dose_audit**.

**Findings:** In 481 adults from southern Ontario, the study evaluated misclassification created by using coffee as a surrogate for total caffeine intake and collected caffeine exposure from foods, beverages, and medications, supporting all-source rather than coffee-only tracking.

**Why it matters:** A coffee-only protocol log can miss caffeine from tea, soda, chocolate, medications, and supplements.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily caffeine dose, source-fidelity checklist, hidden caffeine source count.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not treat “coffee cups” as the protocol exposure; require an all-source caffeine inventory.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
