**Findings:** This older review summarized caffeine consumption from multiple sources and estimated mean daily caffeine intake among U.S. consumers at about 4 mg/kg, providing historical exposure context rather than curfew evidence.

**Why it matters:** Historical exposure reviews help frame caffeine as a multi-source dietary exposure rather than a single beverage.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily caffeine dose, population caffeine intake context, source mix.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as historical context for caffeine exposure ranges and source diversity.
