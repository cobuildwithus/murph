**Findings:** The systematic review surveys non-laboratory objective sleep measurement options and supports the broad caveat that home methods are alternatives for some endpoints, not blanket PSG replacements.

**Why it matters:** Useful for explaining why home-based measurements differ from laboratory PSG.

**Potential experiment signals:** total sleep time, sleep efficiency, wake after sleep onset, sleep stages.

**Protocol takeaway:** Describe non-lab device outcomes as pragmatic field signals rather than gold-standard sleep architecture.
