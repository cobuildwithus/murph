This source is included for **pharmacology_individual_differences**.

**Findings:** Dietary caffeine with random urine sampling was evaluated as a practical CYP1A2 phenotyping method, with accessible abstract information indicating random urine assessment was possible for many habitual caffeine consumers.

**Why it matters:** It supports practical measurement context: CYP1A2 activity can be estimated from habitual caffeine exposure, not only from tightly controlled lab probes.

**Potential experiment signals:** caffeine metabolic ratio, dose log completeness, smoking status.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as measurement context for caffeine metabolism and smoking covariates, not as direct sleep evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
