This source is included for **adjacent_curfew_morning_evening_context**.

**Findings:** On days adolescents consumed caffeine, sleep onset/midpoint were delayed by about 17 minutes and wake time by about 19 minutes, with no significant within-person association for duration, maintenance efficiency, or subjective quality.

**Why it matters:** It adds objective actigraphy timing context and preserves null findings for duration/efficiency/subjective sleep quality.

**Potential experiment signals:** Sleep onset timing; Wake timing; Sleep duration; Sleep maintenance efficiency; Sleep variability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Adjacent adolescent evidence: caffeine was linked to later sleep timing, but not shorter duration or worse quality, and timing/dose of caffeine were not measured.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
