This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:** Coffee consumption was associated with 58 daily premature atrial contractions versus 53 on avoidance days, a higher premature ventricular contraction signal, more daily steps, about 30 minutes less sleep, and no clear glucose difference.

**Why it matters:** Shows tradeoffs: acute coffee can affect sleep and activity while arrhythmia findings are nuanced.

**Potential experiment signals:** Premature atrial contractions; Premature ventricular contractions; Sleep duration; Step count; Glucose.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only for monitoring sleep and palpitations, not as direct curfew evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
