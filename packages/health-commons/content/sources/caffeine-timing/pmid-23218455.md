This source is included for **direct_protocol_and_dose_timing**.

**Findings:** The pilot reported no significant between-group differences and no significant sleep-quality changes, suggesting evening caffeine abstinence may not help all habitual low/moderate consumers.

**Why it matters:** Important direct null/mixed source: abstaining in the evening did not clearly improve sleep in this small pilot.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep-wake parameters, self-reported sleep quality, level of refreshment

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promise that caffeine abstinence or curfew automatically improves sleep, especially in low/moderate habitual consumers who already sleep well.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
