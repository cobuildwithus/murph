This source is included for **pharmacology_individual_differences**.

**Findings:** The study reported that ADORA2A genetic variation contributes to individual sensitivity to caffeine effects on sleep, supporting pharmacodynamic receptor sensitivity as a contributor to different bedtime caffeine responses.

**Why it matters:** Adenosine A2A receptor sensitivity may explain why some users need a stricter cutoff than the default 8-hour rule even when caffeine clearance is not unusually slow.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep-onset latency, sleep efficiency, deep sleep minutes, subjective caffeine sensitivity.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as same-mechanism support for personalized caffeine-curfew boundaries, not as a direct efficacy trial of the 10-11am/8-hour curfew.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
