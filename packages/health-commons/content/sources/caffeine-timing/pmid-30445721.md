This source is included for **caffeine_source_dose_audit**.

**Findings:** This review discusses caffeine sources such as coffee, tea, caffeinated sodas, and energy drinks and reviews food-based dietary guidance across 90 countries, making it broad consumption/guideline context rather than curfew evidence.

**Why it matters:** It frames caffeine guidance as jurisdiction- and culture-dependent, useful for cautious protocol wording.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily caffeine dose, country caffeine guidance, source mix, adverse caffeine symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as broad guidance context; cite official local authorities when making jurisdiction-specific safety claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
