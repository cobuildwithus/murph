## Extracted evidence fields

**Population:** Caffeine users across performance and mood studies; not a sleep-curfew trial cohort.

**Intervention or exposure:** Caffeine consumption, abstinence, and withdrawal/reversal conditions in the reviewed literature.

**Comparator or control:** Abstinence, placebo, or non-withdrawn comparator contexts depending on study.

**Duration or follow-up:** Review spans acute and longer-term caffeine/withdrawal contexts; no single protocol duration.

**Endpoints:**
  - mood
  - performance
  - alertness
  - withdrawal symptoms
  - tolerance

**Effect estimates or direction:** Narrative conclusion: there was little evidence for net performance or mood benefit under long-term caffeine use compared with abstinence; acute apparent benefits may often reflect reversal of withdrawal.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** Withdrawal symptoms and tolerance are the relevant safety/adherence issues; no sleep-specific adverse-event extraction.

**Limitations:** Narrative interpretive review and not direct sleep-curfew efficacy evidence.

**Population mismatch:** Same-mechanism context only; not a sleep-timing intervention or curfew trial.

**Artifact candidates and rights status:** Publisher content requires permission; do not store full text without rights.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-16001109-withdrawal-reversal-confounding` — The review argues that many acute caffeine benefits in habitual users may be explained by reversal of withdrawal rather than a durable net advantage over abstinence.
- `finding:pmid-16001109-dose-reset-interpretation` — For a caffeine dose reset, early negative mood or alertness changes and later perceived caffeine benefits should be interpreted alongside withdrawal status and habituation.

**Why it matters:** During a caffeine dose reset, early fatigue or mood changes may reflect withdrawal, and later perceived benefits from caffeine reintroduction may partly reflect withdrawal reversal.

**Potential experiment signals:** morning energy, daytime sleepiness, mood, headache, perceived productivity, protocol adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Interpret performance and mood changes cautiously during caffeine curfews; withdrawal reversal can confound perceived caffeine benefits and early reset discomfort.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
