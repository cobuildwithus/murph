This source is included for **pharmacology_individual_differences**.

**Findings:** Oral contraceptive steroids increased caffeine residence time by about a factor of two in young women, with rapid onset during the first cycle.

**Why it matters:** It adds timing-of-onset evidence that caffeine persistence can change quickly when hormonal exposure changes.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep-onset latency, sleep efficiency, caffeine side-effect log.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a boundary/modifier note: recent oral contraceptive changes may change caffeine persistence and require a stricter curfew.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
