This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:** Moderate and high evening doses improved rowing performance and power output compared with placebo, but impaired sleep quality and increased daytime sleepiness; high dose had more reported adverse effects.

**Why it matters:** Shows a dose-response tradeoff: high evening caffeine can improve performance while worsening sleep/recovery markers.

**Potential experiment signals:** 2,000 m rowing performance; Power output; Subjective sleep quality; Daytime sleepiness; Adverse effects: headache, insomnia, gastrointestinal discomfort.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent context that evening caffeine can carry sleep and adverse-effect costs, especially at 6-9 mg/kg.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
