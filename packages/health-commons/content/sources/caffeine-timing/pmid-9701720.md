**Potential experiment signals:** sleep quality, headache, mood, performance, fatigue.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context that caffeine changes can affect both sleep and daytime symptoms; do not quote unextracted effect sizes.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

- **Intervention/exposure:** Acute and chronic caffeine manipulations.
- **Comparator/control:** Double-blind placebo-controlled crossover conditions.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Acute and chronic study periods; exact schedule not extracted here.
