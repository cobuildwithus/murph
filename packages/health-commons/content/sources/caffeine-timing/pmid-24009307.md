This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:** The meta-analysis concluded that caffeine exposure was not associated with increased atrial fibrillation risk; low-dose caffeine exposure may have shown a protective association.

**Why it matters:** Prevents overbroad claims that all caffeine necessarily increases AF risk, while still allowing caution for diagnosed or symptomatic patients.

**Potential experiment signals:** Atrial fibrillation risk; Dose/exposure subgroup patterns.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety context against blanket AF exclusions; individual cardiac symptoms still warrant medical guidance.
