This source is included for **systematic_reviews_meta_analyses**.

**Findings:** This narrative chapter links caffeine to improved alertness and mitigation of sleep-deprivation-related cognitive deficits, but also notes that caffeine can impair subsequent sleep and create next-day sleepiness tradeoffs.

**Why it matters:** Frames the tradeoff between daytime alertness benefits and sleep disruption risk.

**Potential experiment signals:** Daytime energy, reaction time or cognitive performance proxy, sleep-onset latency.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to explain why cutting off caffeine can have both sleep upside and daytime-performance tradeoffs.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
