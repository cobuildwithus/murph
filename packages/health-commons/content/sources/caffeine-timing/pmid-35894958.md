This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:** Evening caffeine did not improve 100-m swimming time trial performance either 60 minutes post-ingestion or the next morning; reported group differences in sleep diary outcomes and side-effect ratings were not statistically significant.

**Why it matters:** Preserves a null/mixed adjacent evening-caffeine trial so the protocol does not overstate universal performance or sleep effects.

**Potential experiment signals:** Evening 100-m swimming performance; Next-morning 100-m swimming performance; Self-reported sleep diary; Side effects: anxiety, tachycardia, alertness.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only adjacent evidence, not as proof against curfew benefits.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
