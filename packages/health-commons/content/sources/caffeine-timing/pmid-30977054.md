This source is included for **systematic_reviews_meta_analyses**.

**Findings:** This review describes caffeine’s ergogenic effects as well established but identifies unresolved questions around habitual use, time of day, age, sex, genotype, sleep, anxiety, and post-exercise recovery.

**Why it matters:** Helps keep performance evidence nuanced and flags sleep/recovery as undermeasured adjacent outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:** Workout performance, sleep efficiency, HRV RMSSD.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to frame performance tradeoffs and research gaps in sleep/recovery after caffeine.
