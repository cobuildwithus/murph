This source is included for **withdrawal_tolerance_dose_reset**.

**Findings:** Brief deprivation decreased vigor and increased fatigue, sleepiness/yawning, and lowered blood pressure by about 5–6 mm Hg; psychomotor performance changes were not detected in the extracted summary.

**Why it matters:** Shows that morning or next-day caffeine gaps can create measurable withdrawal-like symptoms.

**Potential experiment signals:** fatigue, sleepiness, vigor, blood pressure, yawning.

**Protocol takeaway:** Expect some users to feel withdrawal before the 14-day reset is complete.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Habitual coffee/caffeine consumers after brief caffeine deprivation; exact sample size was not extracted.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Brief caffeine deprivation comparable to missing usual morning caffeine.
- **Comparator/control:** Caffeinated/undeprived conditions as structured in the crossover design.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Brief deprivation; exact hours not extracted here.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Even brief deprivation can produce unpleasant symptoms relevant to adherence.
- **Limitations:** Brief deprivation context, not multi-day curfew; sample size not extracted.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Adjacent deprivation evidence; overlap with morning-curfew gaps but not bedtime sleep protection.
