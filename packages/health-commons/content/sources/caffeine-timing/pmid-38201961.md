**Findings:** Caffeine abstinence was associated with more sleep disturbances and higher odds of short sleep among older females, with no associations in males; the authors suggested possible reverse causation.

**Why it matters:** It preserves population-level complexity and reverse-causation risk for caffeine/sleep associations.

**Potential experiment signals:** Sleep disturbances; Short sleep duration; Perceived sleep quality.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context only: habitual caffeine abstinence/consumption in older Dutch adults should not be used to infer curfew efficacy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
