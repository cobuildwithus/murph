This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:** Among the 9 remaining participants after one early withdrawal, caffeine withdrawal triggered severe migraine attacks in 7 and typical caffeine withdrawal syndrome in 2; caffeine continuation did not trigger migraine.

**Why it matters:** A curfew/dose reset can precipitate headaches or migraine if it abruptly lowers habitual caffeine.

**Potential experiment signals:** Migraine attacks; Withdrawal syndrome; Headache and sleep measures; Actigraphy.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for migraine/headache caution and taper guidance rather than protocol efficacy.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
