This source is included for **caffeine_source_dose_audit**.

**Findings:** Analysis of 299 popular coffee samples found takeaway coffees had about three times higher mean caffeine than homemade coffees; Americano averaged 143 mg, teaspoon-pour coffee averaged 23 mg, more than 200 mg per serving occurred in 4% of samples, and four to five servings of many on-the-go coffees could exceed 400 mg.

**Why it matters:** Recent coffee-content evidence reinforces that serving size and product type matter for dose reset.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily caffeine dose, coffee source fidelity, single-serving caffeine dose.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use recent coffee-specific dose data to support product/serving-size logging and caution that multiple large coffees can exceed adult daily context values.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
