This source is included for **caffeine_source_dose_audit**.

**Findings:** This review identified 18 nationally representative caffeine-intake reports covering more than 275,000 people and summarized caffeine sources and intake patterns across children, adolescents, and adults, providing broad source-mix context rather than direct curfew evidence.

**Why it matters:** Population source mix differs by country and age, so a source audit should not assume one universal caffeine pattern.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily caffeine dose, source mix, population caffeine intake context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as broad source-mix context, not as a product-specific caffeine table or sleep claim.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
