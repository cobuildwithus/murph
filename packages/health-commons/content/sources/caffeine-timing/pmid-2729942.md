This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-2729942-ciprofloxacin-prolonged-half-life`: Ciprofloxacin prolonged caffeine half-life, increased caffeine AUC, and reduced caffeine clearance compared with caffeine alone.
- `finding:pmid-2729942-clinical-significance-undetermined`: The study identified a pharmacokinetic interaction but stated that clinical significance was undetermined, so the source supports caution rather than a quantified adverse-event prediction.

**Why it matters:** Ciprofloxacin can prolong caffeine exposure enough that a normal intake timing window may not imply normal clearance.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- biomarker:caffeine-clearance
- biomarker:caffeine-half-life
- safety:drug-interaction

**Protocol takeaway:** Flag ciprofloxacin use as a reason to avoid ordinary caffeine-curfew assumptions and seek clinician guidance.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
