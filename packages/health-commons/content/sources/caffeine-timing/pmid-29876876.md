This source is included for **systematic_reviews_meta_analyses**.

**Findings:** This systematic review/meta-analysis found that acute caffeine ingestion can improve endurance-performance outcomes at moderate doses, but it did not assess caffeine curfews, subsequent sleep, or a 14-day reset; correction records should be checked before quoting exact effect sizes.

**Why it matters:** Balances sleep-oriented recommendations with evidence that caffeine can have acute endurance-performance benefits.

**Potential experiment signals:** Workout performance, perceived exertion, estimated VO2max trend.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only to discuss performance tradeoffs and why late-day caffeine may be attractive to athletes.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
