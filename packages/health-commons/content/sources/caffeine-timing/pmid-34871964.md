This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:** Ten studies met inclusion criteria; in panic-attack studies, a large proportion of panic-disorder patients experienced panic after caffeine and none after placebo, with patients much more vulnerable than healthy controls in comparative studies.

**Why it matters:** Supports strong caution for panic disorder and severe anxiety sensitivity during caffeine experiments.

**Potential experiment signals:** Panic attacks; Subjective anxiety; Dose-response feasibility.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for panic/anxiety caution; do not generalize high-dose panic challenge results to all adults.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
