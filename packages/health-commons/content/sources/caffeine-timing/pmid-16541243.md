This source is included for **withdrawal_tolerance_dose_reset**.

**Findings:** Caffeine increased blood pressure and subjective arousal/positive mood/high; it improved vigilance hits and reaction time but impaired a memory task in the extracted summary.

**Why it matters:** Shows acute caffeine can have mixed effects even when withdrawal reversal is less likely.

**Potential experiment signals:** blood pressure, vigilance, reaction time, memory, mood/arousal.

**Protocol takeaway:** Avoid claiming all caffeine effects are withdrawal reversal; also monitor pressor/jittery effects.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** One hundred two light, nondependent caffeine users.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Acute caffeine doses of 0, 50, 150, and 450 mg.
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo/0 mg and lower versus higher caffeine doses.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Acute dosing session(s); exact follow-up not extracted.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Blood pressure increase and memory impairment are relevant mixed/adverse signals.
- **Limitations:** Light nondependent users and acute dosing; not representative of dependent heavy users or a curfew reset.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Boundary evidence for acute effects without dependence, not direct protocol evidence.
