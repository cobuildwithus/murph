This source is included for **caffeine_source_dose_audit**.

**Findings:** In young adults from the Raine Study, energy drink intake was reported to be positively associated with insomnia and decreased daytime functioning in females, while associations were not significant in males; the observational design does not establish causality.

**Why it matters:** Energy drinks are not just a dose-accounting issue; they are source-specific observational sleep-risk context, with sex-specific uncertainty.

**Potential experiment signals:** energy drink caffeine dose, insomnia symptoms, daytime functioning.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context that energy drink exposure may relate to sleep and daytime functioning, but do not claim causality or direct curfew benefit.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
