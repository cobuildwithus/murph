This source is included for **withdrawal_tolerance_dose_reset**.

**Findings:** Acute abstinence and caffeine administration changed cerebral blood flow velocity, EEG, and subjective effects; the article title frames tolerance and absence of net benefit from chronic administration.

**Why it matters:** It supports the idea that chronic caffeine effects can be partly tolerance and withdrawal-reversal rather than net enhancement.

**Potential experiment signals:** withdrawal symptoms, alertness, headache proxy, EEG context, cerebral blood flow.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mechanistic context for why a reset may produce transient symptoms and why caffeine benefits may fade with maintenance.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
