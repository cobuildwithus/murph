This source is included for **measurement_wearables_psg_actigraphy**.

**Findings:** Caffeine increased autonomic/QT variability signals during sleep, including significant caffeine-by-stage interactions for LF/HF and QT variability index in REM sleep.

**Why it matters:** Directly links presleep caffeine exposure with autonomic physiology during sleep, which helps interpret nocturnal RHR/HRV changes during caffeine curfew tracking.

**Potential experiment signals:** resting heart rate, HRV RMSSD, sleep stages.

**Protocol takeaway:** If users track nocturnal RHR or HRV, treat changes as physiological context and not as standalone proof of caffeine-curfew efficacy.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
