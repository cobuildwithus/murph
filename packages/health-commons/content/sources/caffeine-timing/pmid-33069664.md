This source is included for **direct_protocol_and_dose_timing**.

**Findings:** Caffeine reduced subjective sleepiness, but accessible summaries describe no strong group-level reduction in sleep quality or slow-wave sleep and substantial individual variability; circadian delay signals were not robust at group level.

**Why it matters:** Adolescents are not the target adult protocol population, but findings preserve age/population mismatch and individual variability.

**Potential experiment signals:** subjective sleepiness, sleep quality, slow-wave sleep, dim-light melatonin onset, caffeine dose by body weight

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not generalize adult curfew claims directly to adolescents; caffeine can affect sleepiness, but group-level sleep/circadian effects were variable.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
