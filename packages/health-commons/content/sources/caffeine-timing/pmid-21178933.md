This source is included for **daytime_function_performance**.

**Findings:** Caffeine improved selected repeated-sprint outcomes, including a next-day best sprint-time measure, compared with placebo. The study reported no significant differences for reactive agility time or actigraphy-derived sleep measures.

**Why it matters:** Captures a boundary case where a high acute performance dose appeared ergogenic without measured sleep change in a small athlete sample.

**Potential experiment signals:** repeated sprint ability, reactive agility, total sleep time, sleep efficiency, next-day performance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only: performance benefit and null actigraphy findings should not be promoted as direct support for late caffeine use.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
