This source is included for **same_mechanism_context**.

**Findings:** Caffeine 150 mg was used as a pharmacologic model of insomnia and produced insomnia-like effects, supporting mechanism but not curfew efficacy.

**Why it matters:** It shows caffeine has been used experimentally to induce insomnia-like sleep disturbance.

**Potential experiment signals:** Sleep latency; Model insomnia; Hypnotic rescue response.

**Protocol takeaway:** Mechanistic/context only: caffeine can serve as an insomnia model, but this does not test a caffeine curfew.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
