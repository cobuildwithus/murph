This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-25560302-01`: The systematic review reports that the most common adverse events associated with energy drinks affected the cardiovascular and neurological systems, with caffeine considered the primary ingredient implicated.
- `finding:pmid-25560302-02`: The review describes energy-drink consumption as primarily a health issue in adolescent and young adult males and notes associations with substance use and risk-taking behaviors.

**Why it matters:** Energy-drink adverse-event evidence supports safety language around caffeine source and formulation, not direct curfew efficacy.
