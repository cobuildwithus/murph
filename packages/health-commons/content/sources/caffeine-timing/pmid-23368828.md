This source is included for **same_mechanism_context**.

**Findings:** Moderate-dose caffeine decreased total sleep time after four days (p=0.022) without changing energy expenditure or physical activity patterns.

**Why it matters:** It shows moderate caffeine exposure can reduce sleep time in a free-living randomized setting, but it does not isolate curfew timing.

**Potential experiment signals:** Total sleep time; Physical activity energy expenditure; Resting energy expenditure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Same-mechanism context: caffeine dose can reduce sleep time, but the intervention was repeated daily dosing, not a no-caffeine-after-10-11am curfew.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
