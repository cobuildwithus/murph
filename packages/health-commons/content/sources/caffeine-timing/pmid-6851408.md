This source is included for **direct_protocol_and_dose_timing**.

**Findings:** The early pharmacokinetic study linked coffee-attributed sleep disturbance with caffeine metabolism differences, suggesting that slower clearance can make a fixed timing rule less protective for some people.

**Why it matters:** Supports individual-difference logic for a timing rule because slower caffeine clearance may extend sleep risk.

**Potential experiment signals:** caffeine metabolism, plasma caffeine half-life, coffee-attributed sleep disturbance

**Protocol takeaway:** Treat the curfew as dose- and clearance-sensitive; people who metabolize caffeine slowly may need a stricter cutoff.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
