This source is included for **caffeine_source_dose_audit**.

**Findings:** This Austrian-market study analyzed 124 caffeinated food and beverage products and validated a Caffeine Assessment Tool, reporting r²=0.817 against a 3-day dietary record and r²=0.427 against saliva caffeine concentrations.

**Why it matters:** It combines food-item caffeine values with a validation tool, directly relevant to caffeine source fidelity.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily caffeine dose, source-fidelity checklist, saliva caffeine concentration.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as support for structured caffeine assessment tools and product-specific tables rather than informal recall alone.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
