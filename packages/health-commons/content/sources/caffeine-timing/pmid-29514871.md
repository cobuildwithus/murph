This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-29514871-clearance-modifiers`: The review identifies multiple genetic and non-genetic factors that can alter caffeine metabolism and clearance, including CYP1A2-related factors and medication or physiologic contexts.
- `finding:pmid-29514871-sensitivity-consumption-variation`: The review links caffeine consumption and response variability to individual physiologic and genetic differences, supporting personalization rather than a universal cutoff claim.

**Why it matters:** A single caffeine cutoff may not fit all users because clearance and sensitivity differ across physiology, genetics, and medication contexts.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- biomarker:caffeine-clearance
- context:individual-variation
- biomarker:caffeine-sensitivity

**Protocol takeaway:** Present the curfew as a default self-experiment that should be personalized by sensitivity, medications, pregnancy status, and clearance modifiers.
