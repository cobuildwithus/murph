This source is included for **pharmacology_individual_differences**.

**Findings:** The urinary metabolite method quantified caffeine metabolites for enzyme activity assessment and confirmed expected CYP1A2 ratio differences by smoking and oral contraceptive use, while NAT and xanthine oxidase ratios did not show those subgroup differences.

**Why it matters:** This validates urinary metabolite measurement and independently supports smoking/oral contraceptive covariates for caffeine clearance.

**Potential experiment signals:** caffeine metabolic ratio, smoking status, oral contraceptive status.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as measurement context and covariate support; do not use as direct protocol efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
