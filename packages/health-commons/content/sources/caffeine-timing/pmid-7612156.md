This source is included for **direct_protocol_and_dose_timing**.

**Findings:** Bedtime caffeine prolonged sleep latency, reduced sleep efficiency and stage 4 NREM sleep, and reduced low-frequency delta activity in the human sleep EEG.

**Why it matters:** Useful for explaining why a user may feel less restored even if wearable total sleep time looks normal.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep latency, sleep efficiency, stage 4 NREM sleep, low-frequency delta activity, slow-wave activity

**Protocol takeaway:** Caffeine may change sleep depth/EEG architecture; curfew results should not be reduced to total sleep time alone.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
