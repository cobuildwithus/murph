This source is included for **same_mechanism_context**.

**Findings:** Caffeine attenuated waking and sleep EEG markers of sleep homeostasis in humans, supporting an adenosine/sleep-pressure mechanism relevant to late-caffeine effects.

**Why it matters:** It supports the mechanistic claim that caffeine can blunt EEG markers of accumulated sleep pressure.

**Potential experiment signals:** Waking EEG theta activity; NREM EEG slow-wave/delta activity; Sleep homeostasis markers.

**Protocol takeaway:** Same-mechanism support only; it does not test a morning curfew or 14-day dose reset.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
