This source is included for **withdrawal_tolerance_dose_reset**.

**Findings:** Withdrawal adversely affected cognitive performance; caffeine did not clearly improve cognitive performance in the absence of withdrawal effects under the extracted summary.

**Why it matters:** It warns that poor daytime outcomes during a reset may be withdrawal plus sleep debt rather than failure of the sleep-curfew target.

**Potential experiment signals:** daytime alertness, reaction time, mood, sleep restriction symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Track sleep debt and withdrawal together; do not interpret daytime fatigue alone as evidence against the curfew.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Habitual caffeine consumers studied under caffeine withdrawal and sleep restriction conditions; exact count was not extracted in this batch.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Caffeine withdrawal and caffeine administration during sleep restriction.
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo/non-withdrawal conditions as structured in the crossover design.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Sleep restriction and withdrawal laboratory periods; exact schedule not fully extracted here.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Withdrawal can worsen daytime cognitive performance, especially when sleep is already restricted.
- **Limitations:** Sleep-restriction context may not generalize to ordinary nightly sleep; sample size not extracted.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Adjacent withdrawal/sleep-loss context, not a curfew intervention.
