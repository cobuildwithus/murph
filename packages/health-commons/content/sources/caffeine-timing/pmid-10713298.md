This source is included for **daytime_function_performance**.

**Findings:** High caffeine exposure disrupted nocturnal sleep, although the disturbance was reported as less pronounced than in some earlier work. Exercise attenuated selected caffeine-related sleep disturbances only modestly; exercise effects were not greater after caffeine than placebo, and exercise-related slow-wave sleep increases were smaller under caffeine.

**Why it matters:** Boundary evidence that exercise does not reliably neutralize the sleep impact of very high caffeine exposure near bedtime.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep onset latency, total sleep time, sleep efficiency, sleep architecture, slow-wave sleep.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only/safety-boundary: do not use exercise as a justification for late high-dose caffeine when sleep is the target.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
