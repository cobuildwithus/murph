This source is included for **caffeine_source_dose_audit**.

**Findings:** The ISSN energy-drinks/energy-shots position stand frames these products as caffeine-containing functional products and identifies caffeine dose, often above 200 mg or above 3 mg/kg, as a major contributor to acute performance effects.

**Why it matters:** Energy shots are concentrated caffeine sources and need special treatment in dose/reset logs.

**Potential experiment signals:** energy drink caffeine dose, energy shot caffeine dose, adverse caffeine symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for context that energy drinks/shots are distinct, caffeine-driven product categories; do not use performance claims to support sleep outcomes.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
