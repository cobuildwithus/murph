This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:** The review concluded that evidence is not conclusive; acute increases in caffeine may precede manic symptoms in some reports, caffeine may suppress lithium concentrations, and reduced caffeine may raise lithium concentrations in some cases.

**Why it matters:** Supports strong caution against unsupervised caffeine changes in people with bipolar disorder, especially if taking lithium.

**Potential experiment signals:** Manic/hypomanic/mixed symptoms; Lithium concentrations; Suicidality; Clinical course.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for psychiatric exclusion/caution language, not efficacy.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
