This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-4029248-oral-contraceptives-prolong-half-life`: Oral-contraceptive users had longer caffeine elimination half-life and lower plasma clearance than matched controls after a caffeine test dose.
- `finding:pmid-4029248-steady-state-accumulation-context`: The study predicted higher steady-state caffeine concentrations with chronic caffeine consumption in oral-contraceptive users, supporting caution around accumulation.

**Why it matters:** Hormonal contraceptive use may prolong caffeine exposure, which can change how late a morning dose remains biologically relevant.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- biomarker:caffeine-clearance
- biomarker:caffeine-half-life
- context:hormonal-clearance-modifier

**Protocol takeaway:** Consider estrogen-containing oral contraceptive use as a potential reason for a stricter caffeine dose/timing reset.
