This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:** AF or atrial flutter recurrence was 47% in the caffeinated coffee group versus 64% in the abstinence group, with time-to-recurrence HR 0.61 (95% CI 0.42-0.89); no deaths occurred in either group.

**Why it matters:** Important recent boundary evidence against assuming caffeine abstinence is always beneficial in AF patients, but not a self-experiment recommendation.

**Potential experiment signals:** AF or atrial flutter recurrence; Time to AF recurrence; Emergency visits and hospitalizations; Deaths.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only: AF caffeine decisions should be clinician-guided and disease-specific.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
