This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-12519715-01`: The narrative review concluded that for healthy adults, moderate caffeine intake up to 400 mg/day was not associated with adverse effects across several reviewed domains, including general toxicity and cardiovascular effects.
- `finding:pmid-12519715-02`: The review identified reproductive-aged women and children as groups requiring more cautious interpretation of caffeine safety compared with healthy adults.

**Why it matters:** Provides legacy safety-dose context, but should not be treated as direct evidence for a timing reset.
