This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-31317857-01`: The surveillance analysis included 357 single-product CAERS cases, 12,822 single-product NPDS cases, 153 multiple-product CAERS cases, and 931 multiple-product NPDS cases.
- `finding:pmid-31317857-02`: Among single-product CAERS cases, 35 deaths were reported and other serious outcomes included hospitalizations, life-threatening events, and other serious reports; the authors state that most death reports lacked enough medical information to link consumption to deaths.
- `finding:pmid-31317857-03`: The authors identify passive reporting, under-reporting, bias toward serious cases, possible misclassification, product-formulation changes, and mixed ingredients as limitations, and explicitly frame the work as descriptive surveillance rather than agency determination.

**Why it matters:** Important safety-only source for energy-drink adverse-event reporting, with explicit limitations against causal interpretation.
