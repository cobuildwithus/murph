This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:** The systematic review included 26 studies and reported consistent associations of CYP1A2/AHR-region variants with caffeine consumption, several studies showing ADORA2A differences in anxiogenic consequences, and equivocal evidence for CYP1A2 and ergogenic benefit.

**Why it matters:** Supports source-owned context that genotype and habitual use can shape caffeine response.

**Potential experiment signals:** Habitual caffeine use; Ergogenic effects; Anxiogenic effects; Cardiovascular response in selected studies.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for individual-difference context and cautious personalization language.
