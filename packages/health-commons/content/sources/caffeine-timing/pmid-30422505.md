This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-30422505-01`: The StatPearls clinical summary states that caffeine can quickly reach toxic levels in excess and that seizures and tachydysrhythmias are common concerns in toxic overdose.
- `finding:pmid-30422505-02`: The summary reports lethal doses at blood concentrations of 80-100 micrograms/mL, which can result from ingesting approximately 10 g or more of caffeine.

**Why it matters:** Provides stop-condition and escalation language for suspected caffeine overdose or severe symptoms.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- biomarker:caffeine-dose
- biomarker:heart-rate
- biomarker:heart-rhythm

**Protocol takeaway:** Any severe symptoms or suspected overdose is outside the self-experiment and requires urgent medical/Poison Control pathways.
