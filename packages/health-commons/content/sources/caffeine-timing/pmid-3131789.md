This source is included for **withdrawal_tolerance_dose_reset**.

**Findings:** The review documents physical-dependence and withdrawal evidence and notes that withdrawal has been documented after short-term high-dose exposure; it also frames dependence and reinforcement as nuanced rather than all-or-none.

**Why it matters:** Keeps older dependence evidence in the evidence map without treating it as protocol efficacy.

**Potential experiment signals:** withdrawal symptoms, tolerance, reinforcement, adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as background dependence framing; do not use it to claim curfew efficacy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Human and laboratory animal caffeine-dependence studies summarized in a review.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Caffeine exposure and withdrawal across human and animal studies.
- **Comparator/control:** Narrative comparison across exposure durations, doses, and dependence models.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Reviewed studies included short-term and longer exposure paradigms.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Physical dependence can shape adherence and withdrawal symptoms.
- **Limitations:** Older narrative review and includes animal evidence; not direct curfew evidence.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Foundational dependence context only.
