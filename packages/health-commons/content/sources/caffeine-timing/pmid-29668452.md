This source is included for **measurement_wearables_psg_actigraphy**.

**Findings:** The systematic review retrieved 308 articles and included 18 studies; wearable HRV measurement was promising but evidence was still inconclusive, especially outside stationary resting conditions.

**Why it matters:** Boundary evidence for interpreting HRV/RMSSD from consumer wearables during caffeine experiments.

**Potential experiment signals:** HRV RMSSD, resting heart rate.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use HRV as a same-device trend under similar conditions, not as a cross-device clinical endpoint.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
