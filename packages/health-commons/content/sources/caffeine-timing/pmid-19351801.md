This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:** Bedtime caffeine delayed sleep onset and decreased total sleep time, sleep efficiency, and stage 2 sleep; zolpidem or trazodone attenuated some caffeine effects in this pharmacology study.

**Why it matters:** Supports the plausibility of avoiding late caffeine for sleep but should not be framed as a 14-day curfew test.

**Potential experiment signals:** Sleep onset latency; Total sleep time; Sleep efficiency; Sleep stages and microarchitecture.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety/mechanistic evidence for late-caffeine sleep disruption; avoid medication self-management claims.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
