This source is included for **caffeine_source_dose_audit**.

**Findings:** Brewed tea caffeine varied by category and steeping: caffeinated white, green, and black teas ranged 14-61 mg per 6/8 oz serving, decaffeinated teas were below 12 mg/serving, herbal teas had no detected caffeine, and steep time affected content.

**Why it matters:** Tea caffeine depends on type and brewing, so generic “tea” entries may misstate dose.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily caffeine dose, tea source fidelity, caffeinated product after cutoff.

**Protocol takeaway:** Ask tea drinkers to record tea type, serving size, and whether it is herbal or caffeinated; steep time can change caffeine exposure.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
