This source is included for **caffeine_source_dose_audit**.

**Findings:** Decaffeinated coffee still contained measurable caffeine: ten outlet samples ranged 0-13.9 mg per 16 oz serving, Starbucks decaf espresso ranged 3.0-15.8 mg per shot, and repeated brewed Starbucks decaf samples ranged 12.0-13.4 mg per 16 oz.

**Why it matters:** Decaf is not always caffeine-free, which matters for strict adherence windows and sensitive users.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily caffeine dose, hidden caffeine source count, curfew adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Treat decaf coffee as low-caffeine, not zero-caffeine, unless exact product data show otherwise.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
