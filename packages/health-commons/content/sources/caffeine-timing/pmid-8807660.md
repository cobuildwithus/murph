This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-8807660-fluvoxamine-clearance-half-life`: Fluvoxamine decreased caffeine total clearance from 107 to 21 mL/min and increased caffeine half-life from 5 to 31 hours.
- `finding:pmid-8807660-caffeine-intoxication-caution`: The study authors cautioned that caffeine intake during fluvoxamine treatment may lead to caffeine intoxication, supporting a medication-interaction safety boundary.

**Why it matters:** This is a second fluvoxamine source supporting CYP1A2 inhibitor cautions and potential caffeine accumulation.
