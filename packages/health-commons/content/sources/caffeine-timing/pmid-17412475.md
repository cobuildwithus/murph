This source is included for **caffeine_source_dose_audit**.

**Findings:** Retail espresso coffee drinks varied substantially, with reported mean caffeine of 106 ± 38 mg per serve, range 25-214 mg per serve, and a subset of samples at or above 120 mg or above 167 mg per serve.

**Why it matters:** Retail coffee dose uncertainty can undermine both dose reset and cutoff adherence if participants only log drink names.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily caffeine dose, coffee source fidelity, caffeinated product after cutoff.

**Protocol takeaway:** For retail coffee, capture shop, drink type, size, and best available caffeine estimate rather than assuming one standard cup.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
