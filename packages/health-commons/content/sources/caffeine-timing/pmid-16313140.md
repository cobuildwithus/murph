This source is included for **daytime_function_performance**.

**Findings:** Multiple caffeine doses maintained vigilance during early morning sleep-deprivation operations, supporting caffeine's acute alertness role.

**Why it matters:** Shows why users and shift workers may value caffeine despite sleep-timing tradeoffs.

**Potential experiment signals:** psychomotor vigilance, subjective sleepiness, alertness.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only: caffeine can maintain vigilance in constrained operations, but this does not rebut curfew logic for ordinary sleep goals.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
