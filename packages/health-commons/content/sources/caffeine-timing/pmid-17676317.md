This source is included for **caffeine_source_dose_audit**.

**Findings:** Analysis of 53 caffeine-containing dietary supplements found product means from 1 to 829 mg caffeine/day by labeled directions; among 28 products with label comparisons, 25 were within -16% to +16% of stated caffeine, and most products had less than 10% lot-to-lot variability.

**Why it matters:** Supplements can contain caffeine through botanicals and blends that are easy to miss in beverage-only logs.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily caffeine dose, supplement caffeine dose, source fidelity, adverse caffeine symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Ask participants to list supplements and botanicals, then translate labeled serving directions into caffeine mg/day when possible.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
