This source is included for **systematic_reviews_meta_analyses**.

**Findings:** This clinical review found limited empirical support for sleep hygiene as a standalone insomnia treatment and described mixed evidence for individual sleep hygiene manipulations, including presleep caffeine, especially outside multimodal insomnia care.

**Why it matters:** Protects against presenting a caffeine rule as a stand-alone insomnia treatment.

**Potential experiment signals:** Sleep-onset latency, sleep efficiency, subjective insomnia symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Caffeine timing can be a sleep-hygiene component, but this source argues against treating sleep hygiene alone as proven insomnia treatment.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
