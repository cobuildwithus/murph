This source is included for **direct_protocol_and_dose_timing**.

**Findings:** After repeated daily caffeine intake, caffeine-related metabolites and recovery markers indicated that recovery from daily use can extend beyond an immediately expected clearance window, supporting a distinct reset period.

**Why it matters:** Supports the reset concept by showing physiological recovery from daily caffeine may not be instantaneous.

**Potential experiment signals:** salivary caffeine, paraxanthine, caffeine withdrawal symptoms, sleepiness, recovery timing

**Protocol takeaway:** A 14-day reset window is reasonable as a behavior-change observation period because caffeine-related physiology may persist beyond a single missed dose.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
