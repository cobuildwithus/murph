This source is included for **systematic_reviews_meta_analyses**.

**Findings:** This systematic review/meta-analysis of young-adult sleep habits and substance use found caffeine-related associations with sleep disturbance but inconsistent sleep-duration findings and limited evidence on self-medication directionality.

**Why it matters:** Useful for self-medication framing: people may consume caffeine because they sleep poorly, making causal direction hard to infer.

**Potential experiment signals:** Subjective sleep disturbance, daytime energy, caffeine craving or self-medication notes.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as observational context and self-medication caveat, not as causal proof for curfew efficacy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
