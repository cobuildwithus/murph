This source is included for **caffeine_source_dose_audit**.

**Findings:** A cross-sectional survey of 10,358 Icelandic adolescents aged 13-15 estimated beverage caffeine exposure and found energy drink consumers were more likely to exceed EFSA no-safety-concern thresholds, with beverage caffeine associated with self-reported sleep duration and quality.

**Why it matters:** It flags youth/energy-drink source risks and reinforces not generalizing adult dose guidance to adolescents.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily caffeine dose, energy drink caffeine dose, sleep duration, sleep quality.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adolescent/source-specific context and safety boundary; do not extrapolate adult protocol claims from it.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
