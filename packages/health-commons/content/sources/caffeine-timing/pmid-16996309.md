This source is included for **direct_protocol_and_dose_timing**.

**Findings:** High sleep-reactivity participants had significantly prolonged latency to persistent sleep in response to caffeine compared with low-reactivity participants, supporting individual sensitivity as a moderator.

**Why it matters:** Shows individual vulnerability may moderate caffeine-related sleep disruption, which matters when designing a curfew/reset self-experiment.

**Potential experiment signals:** latency to persistent sleep, sleep efficiency, total sleep time, sleep stage percentages

**Protocol takeaway:** A curfew may have larger effects in people with high sleep reactivity; this is personalization evidence, not proof that everyone benefits.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
