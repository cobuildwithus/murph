This source is included for **same_mechanism_context**.

**Findings:** Measured plasma caffeine concentrations during sleep were dose-dependently associated with reduced NREM delta activity and heart rate and increased HF-HRV versus placebo.

**Why it matters:** It directly links measured caffeine exposure during sleep to EEG and cardiac-autonomic sleep physiology, supporting mechanism while not testing the protocol itself.

**Potential experiment signals:** EEG delta activity; Heart rate during sleep; High-frequency HRV; Plasma caffeine concentration.

**Protocol takeaway:** Same-mechanism support: caffeine exposure during sleep can alter sleep physiology, but the study used bedtime delayed-release caffeine and a short sleep opportunity, not a 14-day curfew.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
