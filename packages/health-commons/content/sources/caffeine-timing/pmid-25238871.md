This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-25238871-low-birth-weight-dose-response`: Compared with no or very low caffeine intake, low, moderate, and high intake categories were associated with higher low-birth-weight risk; each 100 mg/day increment was associated with a higher relative risk of low birth weight.
- `finding:pmid-25238871-lower-mean-birth-weight`: Moderate and high caffeine intake categories were associated with lower mean birth weight in continuous birth-weight analyses.

**Why it matters:** Supports conservative pregnancy language around caffeine dose because fetal-growth associations appear dose-related in prospective studies.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- safety:pregnancy
- safety:fetal-growth
- safety:low-birth-weight

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not present a 10-11am caffeine curfew as sufficient pregnancy safety guidance; pregnancy dose decisions need clinician-aligned limits.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
