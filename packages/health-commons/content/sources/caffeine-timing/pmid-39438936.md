This source is included for **pharmacology_individual_differences**.

**Findings:** The systematic review found multiple metabolism and reward genes associated with habitual caffeine intake/metabolism, with reported per-allele intake effect differences and some ethnicity-specific associations.

**Why it matters:** It helps contextualize genetic drivers of caffeine intake and metabolism, including population-specific signals that could affect adherence and dosing.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily caffeine dose, caffeine craving, sleep-onset latency, subjective sleep quality.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context for genotype-informed personalization and adherence risk; do not use as direct sleep-curfew evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
