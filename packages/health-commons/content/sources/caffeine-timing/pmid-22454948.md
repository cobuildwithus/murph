This source is included for **direct_protocol_and_dose_timing**.

**Findings:** Caffeine increased intervening wakefulness and increased stage 2 sleep time in a small late-middle-aged sample, indicating altered sleep architecture and continuity.

**Why it matters:** Age-relevant direct evidence for sleep architecture and wakefulness outcomes in an older group.

**Potential experiment signals:** EEG sleep staging, stage 2 sleep, intervening wakefulness, sleep architecture

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports caution for older or more sensitive users; the tiny sample means it should not drive exact rules.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
