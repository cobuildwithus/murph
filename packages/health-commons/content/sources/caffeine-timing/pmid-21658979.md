This source is included for **withdrawal_tolerance_dose_reset**.

**Findings:** The review indicates that HRV during sleep is sleep-stage dependent and reflects autonomic regulation rather than a simple single sleep-quality score.

**Why it matters:** Useful for interpreting nocturnal HRV changes during caffeine reset without over-attributing HRV to caffeine alone.

**Potential experiment signals:** HRV, resting heart rate, sleep-stage context, REM/NREM balance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as measurement context for wearable HRV and sleep-stage-dependent interpretation.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
