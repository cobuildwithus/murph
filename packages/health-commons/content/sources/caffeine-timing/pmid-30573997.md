This source is included for **systematic_reviews_meta_analyses**.

**Findings:** This narrative review frames caffeine as potentially helpful for daytime physical and cognitive functioning but potentially harmful to sleep quality and quantity, with tolerance and withdrawal-reversal effects complicating interpretation.

**Why it matters:** Good accessible background for balancing sleep and daytime-function claims while avoiding overstatement.

**Potential experiment signals:** Sleep-onset latency, sleep efficiency, daytime energy.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context for the sleep/daytime tradeoff and withdrawal/tolerance caveats, not as a direct protocol claim.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
