This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:** Twenty-four hours after caffeine discontinuation, children showed decreased reaction-time performance on a sustained-attention task, and deterioration appeared to persist for one week.

**Why it matters:** Shows caffeine withdrawal effects can occur in children, supporting pediatric caution and non-generalization.

**Potential experiment signals:** Self-reported symptoms; Sustained-attention reaction time; Motor performance; Processing speed; Memory.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as pediatric context only; do not extrapolate adult curfew efficacy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
