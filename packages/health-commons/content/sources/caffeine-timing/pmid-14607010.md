This source is included for **caffeine_source_dose_audit**.

**Findings:** Specialty coffee caffeine content varied widely: caffeinated coffees ranged 58-259 mg/dose with a mean of 188 mg per 16 oz serving, and the same beverage at the same outlet over six days ranged 259-564 mg/dose; decaf specialty coffees were below 17.7 mg/dose.

**Why it matters:** Cup counts can be misleading when specialty coffee caffeine varies widely by product, outlet, and day.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily caffeine dose, coffee source fidelity, caffeinated product after cutoff.

**Protocol takeaway:** Log coffee by product, size, and retailer when possible; do not assign false precision to generic “one coffee.”

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
