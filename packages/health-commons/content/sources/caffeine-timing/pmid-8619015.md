This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-8619015-poor-sleepers-pk-variability`: Poor sleepers showed greater variability in caffeine pharmacokinetic measures than normal sleepers after an acute caffeine dose.
- `finding:pmid-8619015-poor-sleepers-pd-profile`: Poor sleepers differed from normal sleepers on several psychophysiologic and performance measures before and after caffeine, supporting phenotype-specific sensitivity context rather than a curfew efficacy claim.

**Why it matters:** Poor sleepers may differ in caffeine PK/PD response patterns, supporting sensitivity screening and conservative cutoffs for vulnerable sleepers.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- endpoint:sleep-quality
- endpoint:daytime-performance
- biomarker:caffeine-clearance
- biomarker:caffeine-sensitivity

**Protocol takeaway:** Use self-rated poor sleep or high caffeine sensitivity as a reason to personalize or tighten caffeine timing rather than assume a universal cutoff.
