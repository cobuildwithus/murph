This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:** The meta-analysis included 8 articles and 14 studies with 546 participants and found caffeine associated with elevated anxiety (SMD 0.94, 95% CI 0.28-1.60) with high heterogeneity; high-dose subgroup effects were larger.

**Why it matters:** Supports anxiety as a tracked adverse endpoint, especially if dose is high or compressed into the morning.

**Potential experiment signals:** Anxiety symptoms; Dose subgroup anxiety effect.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for general anxiety caution; combine with panic-disorder-specific evidence for clinical exclusions.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
