This source is included for **systematic_reviews_meta_analyses**.

**Findings:** This commentary summarizes evidence that individual caffeine-related sleep sensitivity may depend partly on ADORA2A genotype, supporting personalization rather than a one-size-fits-all caffeine-curfew claim.

**Why it matters:** Explains why a fixed curfew may work differently across users.

**Potential experiment signals:** Sleep-onset latency, subjective caffeine sensitivity, night awakenings.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to justify personalization and caution that a single cutoff may not fit everyone.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
