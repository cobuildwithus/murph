This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:** ADORA2A rs5751876 TT was associated with greater susceptibility to caffeine-induced anxiety; medium/high habitual consumers showed lower anxiety response, while placebo after abstinence decreased alertness and increased headache.

**Why it matters:** Shows that withdrawal reversal, tolerance, and genotype can alter perceived benefits or harms during caffeine changes.

**Potential experiment signals:** Anxiety; Alertness; Headache; Habitual-use tolerance; ADORA2A/ADORA1 genotype associations.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to separate true protocol benefit from withdrawal reversal and tolerance effects.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
