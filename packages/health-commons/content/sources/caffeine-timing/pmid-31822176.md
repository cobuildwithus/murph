This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:** The case presented severe sudden frontal headache soon after abruptly stopping caffeine tablets, with diffuse subarachnoid hemorrhage and angiographic artery irregularity; subsequent scanning showed normalization and the patient recovered well.

**Why it matters:** Reinforces severe headache/neurologic symptoms as stop-and-seek-care events during caffeine withdrawal.

**Potential experiment signals:** Thunderclap headache; Subarachnoid hemorrhage; Cerebral artery irregularity; Radiologic normalization.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as red-flag safety-only evidence.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
