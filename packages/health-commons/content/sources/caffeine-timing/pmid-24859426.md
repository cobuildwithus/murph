This source is included for **withdrawal_tolerance_dose_reset**.

**Findings:** The source developed a model for caffeine effects on human vigilance during total sleep deprivation; no ordinary sleep-curfew effect estimate was extracted.

**Why it matters:** Provides dose-timing context for daytime function but should not justify late caffeine in ordinary users.

**Potential experiment signals:** PVT lapses, reaction time, sleep deprivation, dose timing.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only to contextualize daytime-performance tradeoffs and not as evidence against curfew sleep protection.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Sleep-deprived human subjects represented in a dose-dependent vigilance model; original dataset counts not extracted here.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Caffeine dose and timing during total sleep deprivation modeled against vigilance performance.
- **Comparator/control:** Predicted/observed vigilance under different caffeine dose schedules and sleep-deprivation time courses.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Total sleep deprivation modeling context; exact modeled duration depends on source datasets.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** High sleep-deprivation contexts are not wellness-use analogues and may require operational/medical judgment.
- **Limitations:** Modeling source under total sleep deprivation; not a sleep-protection trial.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Indirect performance model, not ordinary nightly caffeine curfew.
