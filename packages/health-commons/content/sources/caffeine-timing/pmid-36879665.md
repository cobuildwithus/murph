This source is included for **measurement_wearables_psg_actigraphy**.

**Findings:** Fitbit Inspire 2 significantly overstated TST, deep sleep, and REM compared with PSG in this small study; TIB, SE, and WASO were overestimated and light sleep underestimated without statistical significance.

**Why it matters:** Recent Fitbit model validation for users with current-generation Fitbit sleep data.

**Potential experiment signals:** total sleep time, sleep efficiency, wake after sleep onset, sleep stages, REM sleep, deep sleep.

**Protocol takeaway:** Fitbit Inspire 2 stage changes should be exploratory; require repeated patterns and diary corroboration.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
