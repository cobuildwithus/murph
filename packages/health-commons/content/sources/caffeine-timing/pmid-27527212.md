**Findings:** Higher habitual caffeine intake was associated with poorer PSQI-defined sleep and shorter estimated sleep time in a small cross-sectional adult sample.

**Why it matters:** It provides observational context for habitual caffeine amount and sleep quality, while remaining non-causal and not timing-specific.

**Potential experiment signals:** PSQI sleep quality; Estimated sleep time; Caffeine intake.

**Protocol takeaway:** Adjacent observational context only; do not use to claim a curfew effect.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
