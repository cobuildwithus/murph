This source is included for **measurement_wearables_psg_actigraphy**.

**Findings:** TST did not differ from PSG for all devices and correlated strongly, but sleep efficiency and light/deep sleep measures varied substantially; data loss was greatest for Fitbit and Misfit, and no device provided reliable staging data.

**Why it matters:** Classic consumer-device validation showing why duration can look acceptable while stages and data capture fail.

**Potential experiment signals:** total sleep time, sleep efficiency, sleep stages, deep sleep.

**Protocol takeaway:** Track device failures and missing nights, not just sleep metrics.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
