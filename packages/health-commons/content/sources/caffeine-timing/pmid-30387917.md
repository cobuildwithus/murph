This source is included for **pharmacology_individual_differences**.

**Findings:** Across five caffeine challenge occasions, intraindividual caffeine metabolic-ratio variation was generally 30% or less in this exploratory sample, supporting the metabolic ratio as a repeatable phenotyping signal while requiring prospective confirmation.

**Why it matters:** This source helps distinguish stable metabolic phenotype measurement from day-to-day noise when interpreting why caffeine effects differ by person.

**Potential experiment signals:** caffeine metabolic ratio, dose log completeness, sleep-onset latency.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as measurement/metabolism context for individual clearance variability, not as direct evidence of sleep improvement from caffeine curfew.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
