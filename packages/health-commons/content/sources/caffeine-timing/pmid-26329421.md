This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-26329421-high-intake-pregnancy-loss`: High and very high caffeine intake categories were associated with increased pregnancy-loss risk compared with no or very low intake.
- `finding:pmid-26329421-low-moderate-uncertain`: Lower and moderate intake categories were less clearly associated with pregnancy-loss risk, and the review retained uncertainty from observational confounding and possible publication bias.

**Why it matters:** Pregnancy-loss risk is a high-stakes boundary where a self-experiment curfew should not replace clinician guidance.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- safety:pregnancy
- safety:pregnancy-loss

**Protocol takeaway:** Pregnant participants should not use this protocol to self-set caffeine safety thresholds; preserve pregnancy-loss caution separately from sleep-efficacy claims.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
