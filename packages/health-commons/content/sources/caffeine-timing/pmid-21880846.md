This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:** In hypertensive individuals, 200-300 mg caffeine produced acute blood-pressure increases for at least 3 hours; available longer-term coffee trials and cohort studies did not support higher long-term blood pressure or CVD risk from habitual coffee.

**Why it matters:** A caffeine curfew may not reduce total caffeine if dose is shifted earlier, so hypertensive users still need dose and BP caution.

**Potential experiment signals:** Acute blood pressure; Longer-term blood pressure; Cardiovascular disease risk.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for clinician-guidance language in hypertension and acute BP sensitivity.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
