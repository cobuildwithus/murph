This source is included for **direct_protocol_and_dose_timing**.

**Findings:** Evening caffeine delayed the human circadian melatonin rhythm by about 40 minutes in accessible summaries and lengthened circadian period in vitro via adenosine/cAMP-related mechanisms.

**Why it matters:** Explains why caffeine close to bedtime may affect more than simple sleep pressure; it may also shift circadian phase.

**Potential experiment signals:** dim-light melatonin onset, circadian phase, cellular circadian period, sleep timing context

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mechanistic support for avoiding evening caffeine; do not treat as direct evidence that a 10/11am cutoff improves sleep.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
