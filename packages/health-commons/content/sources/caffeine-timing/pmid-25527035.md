This source is included for **daytime_function_performance**.

**Findings:** Caffeine improved reaction time, vigilance, and logical reasoning during extended periods with restricted sleep opportunities. The study context involved high-dose supervised caffeine under restricted sleep, so it should not be generalized as routine free-living caffeine-timing guidance.

**Why it matters:** Supports the performance/alertness side of the caffeine tradeoff without directly testing timing harms or curfew benefits.

**Potential experiment signals:** reaction time, psychomotor vigilance, logical reasoning, daytime alertness.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only: caffeine can preserve performance under restricted sleep, but that is not evidence against sleep-focused caffeine curfews.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
