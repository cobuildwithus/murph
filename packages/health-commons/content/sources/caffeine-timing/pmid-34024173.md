This source is included for **direct_protocol_and_dose_timing**.

**Findings:** Regular caffeine intake delayed REM sleep promotion and was associated with more difficulty awakening and tiredness after waking, while total sleep time and broad sleep architecture were not consistently changed.

**Why it matters:** Relevant to daily-use patterns and sleep architecture rather than only sleep duration.

**Potential experiment signals:** REM sleep latency, REM sleep proportion accumulation, sleep quality, awakening difficulty, tiredness on wake, sleep architecture

**Protocol takeaway:** Regular caffeine can affect REM timing and waking quality even when total sleep time is not the main signal.
