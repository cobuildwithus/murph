This source is included for **daytime_function_performance**.

**Findings:** Caffeine improved selected futsal performance metrics, including jump height, sprint time, high-speed distance, body impacts, and accelerations/decelerations. The study reported no statistically significant differences in side-effect prevalence, including insomnia and gastrointestinal symptoms, between caffeine and placebo.

**Why it matters:** Shows a lower performance dose with monitored side effects, preserving the fact that not every athlete study finds statistically significant sleep/tolerability harms.

**Potential experiment signals:** futsal performance, sprint time, high-speed distance, insomnia symptoms, gastrointestinal symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only: performance benefit with low side-effect prevalence does not directly inform a sleep-focused curfew.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
