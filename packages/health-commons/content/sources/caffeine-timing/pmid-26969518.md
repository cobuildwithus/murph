**Findings:** The wristband showed good agreement with PSG and ECG for selected sleep and heart-rate metrics in healthy adolescents, while the authors called for more validation.

**Why it matters:** Population-boundary evidence for interpreting wearable sleep/cardiac metrics outside adult samples.

**Potential experiment signals:** total sleep time, sleep efficiency, resting heart rate, HRV RMSSD.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not generalize adult wearable validation to adolescents without caveats.
