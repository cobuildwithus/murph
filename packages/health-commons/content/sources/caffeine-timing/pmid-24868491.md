This source is included for **direct_protocol_and_dose_timing**.

**Findings:** Bedtime caffeine mainly disrupted sleep early in exposure, including lower actigraphic sleep efficiency and poorer self-rated sleep quality; over repeated days, only lower sleep efficiency clearly persisted and next-day performance/mood findings were mixed or confounded.

**Why it matters:** Direct repeated-use evidence with next-day outcomes, while showing tolerance/confounding rather than simple universal harm.

**Potential experiment signals:** actigraphic sleep efficiency, activity score, sleep fragmentation, sleep diary, self-rated sleep quality, next-day cognitive performance, mood

**Protocol takeaway:** Bedtime caffeine can disrupt sleep, especially early in exposure, but repeated-use and next-day performance findings are mixed and may involve tolerance or withdrawal reversal.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
