This source is included for **clinical_safety_boundaries**.

**Findings:**
- `finding:pmid-24761279-01`: The comprehensive review summarizes evidence for caffeine dependence and clinically meaningful distress/functional impairment among some habitual caffeine users, including difficulty reducing use.
- `finding:pmid-24761279-02`: The review frames caffeine use disorder as an area requiring clinical and research attention, supporting referral language when caffeine reduction causes significant distress, impairment, or inability to function.

**Why it matters:** A 14-day dose reset may trigger withdrawal or distress in heavy/problematic caffeine users; this source supports safety and referral boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- biomarker:caffeine-dose

**Protocol takeaway:** If caffeine reduction causes marked distress, impairment, or inability to reduce despite harm, the protocol should not be framed as simple self-tracking; referral or clinician support may be needed.
