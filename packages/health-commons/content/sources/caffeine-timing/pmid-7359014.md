This source is included for **pharmacology_individual_differences**.

**Findings:** Oral contraceptive steroid users had prolonged caffeine half-life and lower clearance after caffeine dosing, indicating hormone-related differences can extend caffeine persistence.

**Why it matters:** Oral contraceptive use can extend caffeine persistence, making an 8-hour bedtime window potentially insufficient for some users.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep-onset latency, sleep efficiency, caffeine side-effect log.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a pharmacokinetic boundary note: people using oral contraceptives may need an earlier cutoff or lower total dose.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
