This source is included for **daytime_function_performance**.

**Findings:** Caffeine ingestion improved performance during fitness tests. Caffeine did not alter activity during simulated games, limiting performance generalizability.

**Why it matters:** Performance-heavy boundary source: caffeine may help controlled fitness tests without changing game activity, while sleep data are too limited for curfew claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** fitness-test performance, simulated-game activity, insomnia symptoms, adverse effects.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only: use for performance tradeoff framing and adverse-effect measurement design, not sleep-efficacy claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
