This source is included for **systematic_reviews_meta_analyses**.

**Findings:** This umbrella review of 21 published meta-analyses concluded that caffeine supplementation is ergogenic across multiple exercise-performance domains, but it did not evaluate subsequent sleep or caffeine-curfew protocols.

**Why it matters:** Strong boundary context for why some users may prioritize caffeine for performance rather than sleep.

**Potential experiment signals:** Workout performance, estimated VO2max trend.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as performance tradeoff context, not as evidence against or for the curfew sleep endpoint.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
