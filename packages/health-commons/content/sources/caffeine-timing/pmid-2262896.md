This source is included for **withdrawal_tolerance_dose_reset**.

**Findings:** The study investigated and reported low-dose caffeine physical dependence in humans after terminating 100 mg/day caffeine exposure.

**Why it matters:** Supports not dismissing withdrawal in users who report only modest caffeine intake.

**Potential experiment signals:** withdrawal symptoms, headache, fatigue, adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Include low-dose users in withdrawal cautions, especially if they abruptly eliminate caffeine.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Seven normal human participants in a low-dose caffeine dependence experiment.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Termination/substitution after low-dose caffeine exposure around 100 mg/day.
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo substitution or low-dose caffeine continuation in crossover conditions.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Low-dose exposure followed by withdrawal assessment; exact schedule not fully extracted here.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Even low daily intake may matter for withdrawal in some people.
- **Limitations:** Very small sample and older laboratory paradigm.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Low-dose cessation experiment, not a caffeine-curfew trial.
