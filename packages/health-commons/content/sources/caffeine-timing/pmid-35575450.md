**Findings:** This state-of-the-science review summarizes adenosine and caffeine mechanisms in sleep-wake regulation and distinguishes mechanistic plausibility from direct evidence about any specific caffeine-curfew protocol.

**Why it matters:** Mechanistic anchor for why timing caffeine away from sleep pressure and bedtime is biologically plausible.

**Potential experiment signals:** Sleep-onset latency, sleep efficiency, deep sleep minutes.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to explain plausibility through adenosine/sleep-wake regulation, not to claim direct protocol efficacy.
