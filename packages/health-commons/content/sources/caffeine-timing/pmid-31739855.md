This source is included for **measurement_wearables_psg_actigraphy**.

**Findings:** In suspected/confirmed OSA clinical context, Fitbit outputs had notable discrepancies from PSG, including underestimation of WASO and overestimation of TST in reported summaries; REM sleep was less discrepant than several other outputs.

**Why it matters:** Important clinical-boundary source against generalizing healthy wearable validation to OSA users.

**Potential experiment signals:** total sleep time, sleep efficiency, wake after sleep onset, sleep stages, REM sleep, deep sleep.

**Protocol takeaway:** If OSA is suspected or diagnosed, avoid using Fitbit sleep outputs as efficacy claims and encourage clinical evaluation.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
