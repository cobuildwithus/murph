This source is included for **Emerging disease-specific whole-body PBM variants**.

**Findings:** This pilot enrolled 16 adults with long-COVID brain fog and reported 14 completers, with seven assigned to whole-body PBM and seven to transcranial PBM over 4 weeks and 12 treatments. The whole-body arm used a NovoTHOR bed with 660 and 850 nm LEDs for 14 minutes at 24 mW/cm² and 20.2 J/cm². The whole-body group showed improvement on DSST and Trail Making B, with significant qEEG gains in P300 voltage, Flanker time, and Flanker voltage, but physical reaction time was not significant and the study had no placebo arm. Because the paper mixes modalities and remains a small open-label pilot, it should stay in same-mechanism context only despite no adverse events being reported.

**Why it matters:** It helps Murph separate symptomatic-population and mixed-modality context from direct protocol evidence, while still preserving useful tolerability and endpoint detail.

**Potential experiment signals:** MoCA, digit symbol substitution test, Trail Making A, Trail Making B, physical reaction time, qEEG P300, qEEG Flanker, symptom reports

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for context, tolerability, and endpoint recall only. Do not treat it as direct efficacy evidence for the target protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
