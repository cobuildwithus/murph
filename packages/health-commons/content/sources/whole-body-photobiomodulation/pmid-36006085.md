This source is included for **Exercise-timed whole-body PBM sibling variant**.

**Findings:** This retrospective team implementation study followed 12 Division I women's soccer players across one competitive season while they used a NovoTHOR whole-body PBM bed up to twice weekly for 20 minutes on non-consecutive days. Across 93 analyzed sessions, sleeping heart rate on the subsequent night was lower after PBM, but total sleep time, REM duration, and light sleep duration were also lower. Average HRV increased numerically but did not reach conventional significance. Because the study is observational and uses Oura-based sleep staging, it is useful for operational and wearable-endpoint context but not for causal efficacy claims. It also preserves a potentially unwanted sleep-duration direction that should not be hidden.

**Why it matters:** It gives unusually practical repeated-use whole-body PBM data and highlights that an autonomic recovery signal can coexist with shorter sleep.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleeping heart rate, HRV, total sleep time, REM sleep minutes, light sleep duration, training-day versus rest-day context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as observational adjacent-variant context and as a things-to-watch source, especially for sleep-duration changes.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
