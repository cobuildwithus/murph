This source is included for **Chronic pain/fibromyalgia whole-body PBM sibling variant**.

**Findings:** This tripled-blinded RCT focused on physiologic endpoints after whole-body PBM in fibromyalgia. The paper reports significant differences in circadian blood pressure after treatment, significant increases in pain pressure threshold at several tender points, and significant elastography changes in the trapezius and forearm distal dorsal third. The intervention used the same supervised whole-body device program as the broader fibromyalgia trial family.

**Why it matters:** This is the batch's clearest direct mechanistic paper for whole-body PBM in fibromyalgia, covering autonomic, tenderness, and tissue-quality signals.

**Potential experiment signals:** circadian blood pressure, pain pressure threshold, tissue elasticity, autonomic symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for mechanistic signal selection and physiologic monitoring ideas, not as broad efficacy proof outside supervised fibromyalgia settings.

**Safety / adverse events:** No dedicated adverse-event table was highlighted in the accessible record used here; the paper is more informative for mechanistic endpoints than for safety rates.

**Limitations:** Small sample; blood pressure can be affected by external factors; treatment sessions were not held at a fixed daily time; fibromyalgia-only population.

**Population mismatch / directness:** Direct whole-body PBM delivery is relevant, but the evidence is confined to a mechanistic supervised fibromyalgia cohort rather than a generalized protocol population.

**Artifact candidates / rights:** A public PMCID-backed article page is available and is the safest manifest candidate. The article is open access via MDPI.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
