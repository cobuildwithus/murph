This source is included for **Chronic pain/fibromyalgia whole-body PBM sibling variant**.

**Findings:** The review included 17 studies comprising 857 participants with fibromyalgia and compared localized with whole-body PBMT delivery. The authors report significant benefits across pain, function, fatigue, and quality-of-life domains and argue that whole-body PBMT may produce greater and more sustained effects than localized approaches. Because the synthesis mixes delivery modes and protocols, it is more useful for sibling-variant mapping than for direct protocol claims.

**Why it matters:** This review is the broadest fibromyalgia-specific PBMT synthesis in the batch and helps separate whole-body sibling-variant signals from localized PBMT literature.

**Potential experiment signals:** pain, physical function, fatigue, sleep quality, quality of life, kinesiophobia.

**Protocol takeaway:** Background-only and sibling-variant comparison source: do not promote its pooled conclusions into direct whole-body red/NIR claims.

**Safety / adverse events:** The review notes that adverse-event reporting was limited and inconsistent across the literature, which weakens confidence in broad safety generalization.

**Limitations:** High protocol heterogeneity; small samples; English-language restriction; short follow-up in many studies; high or unclear risk of bias; incomplete adverse-event reporting.

**Population mismatch / directness:** Only some included studies used whole-body PBMT; many used localized treatments, so the review is adjacent rather than direct protocol evidence.
