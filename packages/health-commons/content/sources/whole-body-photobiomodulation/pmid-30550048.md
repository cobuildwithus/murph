This source is included for **Dose, device, and implementation reporting**.

**Findings:** This review states that PBM still lacks agreement on clinical parameters and protocols, and it organizes the literature around wavelength, energy or fluence, power or irradiance, pulse mode, treatment duration, and repetition. It also distinguishes tissues with higher versus lower mitochondrial density, reinforcing that parameter-response relationships may not translate cleanly across targets. In practice, it is useful for whole-body PBM because it treats contradictory results as a parameter-reporting and dose-translation problem rather than as proof of a single uniform modality effect.

**Why it matters:** Whole-body light beds combine wavelength, irradiance, exposure time, geometry, and repetition into one delivered dose, so a source that explicitly warns about parameter sensitivity is more useful than generic PBM enthusiasm.

**Potential experiment signals:** wavelength, irradiance, fluence, session length, repeat-cadence tolerance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as background for dose reporting, biphasic-dose caution, and tissue-context interpretation; do not promote it into a direct efficacy claim.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
