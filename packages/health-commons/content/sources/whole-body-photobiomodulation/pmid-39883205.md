This source is included for **Exercise-timed whole-body PBM sibling variant**.

**Findings:** This systematic review searched PubMed, Cochrane Library, EBSCO, and Google Scholar for human whole-body or full-body PBM studies used before or after exercise. Five studies totaling 105 physically active participants met inclusion criteria. The review reports that two studies found better sleep-related recovery signals, including higher melatonin or lower nocturnal heart rate, but none of the five studies showed benefit for biomarkers of fatigue or exercise performance. The review also flags heterogeneity in devices, timing, and source-to-skin geometry, and it points to ongoing registries rather than a mature efficacy literature.

**Why it matters:** It is the strongest single map of the athlete-oriented whole-body PBM literature and it preserves the negative and mixed results that should prevent overclaiming.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep quality, melatonin, resting heart rate, HRV, creatine kinase, delayed-onset muscle soreness, performance tests.

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep this in the adjacent-variant bucket: it supports source recall and boundary setting, not direct protocol benefit claims.
