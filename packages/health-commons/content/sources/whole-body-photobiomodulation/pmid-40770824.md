This source is included for **Chronic pain/fibromyalgia whole-body PBM sibling variant**.

**Findings:** The umbrella review synthesized 15 meta-analyses covering 204 RCTs and over 9000 participants across 35 endpoints and 15 conditions. For fibromyalgia specifically, it found significant associations for fibromyalgia impact, fatigue, and stiffness, but not for pain severity, tender points, depression, or anxiety. The paper repeatedly emphasizes that most certainty ratings were low or very low and that no endpoint reached high certainty.

**Why it matters:** This is the batch's best source for preserving certainty boundaries, mixed results, and null fibromyalgia endpoints within the larger PBM evidence base.

**Potential experiment signals:** fibromyalgia impact, fatigue, stiffness, pain severity, tender points, certainty of evidence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as evidence-quality context only and preserve its null and low-certainty fibromyalgia findings.

**Safety / adverse events:** This source is primarily about effect sizes and certainty rather than adverse-event detail.

**Limitations:** Highly heterogeneous conditions and PBM modalities; most outcomes low or very low certainty; many re-analyses were nonsignificant; umbrella-review findings are indirect for any one protocol variant.

**Population mismatch / directness:** The review spans many non-fibromyalgia and non-whole-body PBM modalities, so it should remain a background source only.
