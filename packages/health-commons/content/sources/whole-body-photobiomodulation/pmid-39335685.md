This source is included for **Safety, contraindication, and population-boundary evidence**.

**Findings:** The review summarizes ocular complications reported with facial aesthetic laser and light treatments, including conjunctival burns, corneal damage, retinal phototoxicity, and transient vision disturbances. It highlights inadequate protection, patient movement, and operator experience as key risk factors, and emphasizes appropriate eye protection and careful positioning as core preventive measures. This is adjacent evidence rather than a direct whole-body PBM record, but it is useful for Murph eye-safety instructions.

**Why it matters:** This source reinforces that eye safety depends not only on wavelength but also on protection, positioning, and operator control.

**Potential experiment signals:** transient vision disturbance, ocular pain or irritation, protective eyewear adherence, eye positioning.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent eye-injury prevention context for devices used near the face or eyes.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Skin Photobiomodulation Note

This source is included for **eye and face-adjacent ocular safety boundary**.

**Findings:** The review identifies conjunctival burns, corneal damage, retinal phototoxicity, and transient vision disturbances, with risk influenced by proximity, direct or scattered exposure, protection, patient movement, and operator experience.

**Why it matters:** It groups plausible face-adjacent ocular hazards and prevention controls relevant to facial light-device protocols.

**Potential experiment signals:** eye irritation, corneal symptoms, transient vision disturbance, eye-protection adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only or context-only evidence; do not use for direct skin-outcome claims.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
