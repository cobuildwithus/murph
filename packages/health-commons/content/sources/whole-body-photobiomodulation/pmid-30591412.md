This source is included for **Exercise-timed whole-body PBM sibling variant**.

**Findings:** This recommendations paper is not a whole-body efficacy trial. Its main value is methodological: it argues that PBMT for exercise performance and post-exercise recovery has a therapeutic dose window and that future trials should follow stronger reporting standards such as CONSORT and TIDieR. It also notes that the existing evidence base has low-to-moderate certainty because of risk of bias and imprecision. For this protocol, it belongs in background context because the cited evidence is predominantly localized muscle PBM, not whole-body red and near-infrared exposure.

**Why it matters:** It helps explain why sports whole-body studies often borrow localized PBM assumptions and why dosing or reporting heterogeneity is a major limitation.

**Potential experiment signals:** dose planning, treatment timing, trial reporting quality, exercise outcomes.

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep as background context only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
