This source is included for **Exercise-timed whole-body PBM sibling variant**.

**Findings:** This suspended Brazilian registry planned a randomized double-blind crossover test of whole-body PBM in 20 male rugby athletes. The active intervention was listed as a 10-minute whole-body Joovv exposure at about 30.5 J/cm^2 and 51 mW/cm^2, applied 6 hours before and 5 minutes before isokinetic dynamometry. Planned outcomes included fatigue index, repetitions, total work, acceleration time, time to peak torque, torque, peak torque, power, and delayed-onset muscle pain. No results were posted in the extracted registry record.

**Why it matters:** It helps map the preconditioning variant and the sports endpoint menu even though the trial did not complete to usable results.

**Potential experiment signals:** fatigue index, peak torque, total work, DOMS, timing of preconditioning sessions.

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep for recall only; suspended registry without results.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
