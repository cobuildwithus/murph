This source is included for **Adjacent-variant and exclusion boundary anchors**.

**Findings:** WHO's booklet explicitly concerns artificial tanning devices that emit ultraviolet radiation and discusses public-health interventions to manage sunbeds. It cites IARC's 2009 classification of UV-emitting tanning devices as carcinogenic to humans and summarizes how multiple national and subnational authorities have enacted restrictions or bans. This is a device-class and safety boundary rather than efficacy evidence. The whole point of including it is to prevent UV-tanning literature or sunbed analogies from contaminating whole-body red/NIR PBM claims.

**Why it matters:** It creates a hard separation between therapeutic red/NIR PBM and UV-emitting tanning devices.

**Potential experiment signals:** UV exposure behavior, skin-cancer risk counseling, device-classification boundary.

**Protocol takeaway:** Treat as safety-boundary context only. Do not use tanning-device literature to support PBM claims.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
