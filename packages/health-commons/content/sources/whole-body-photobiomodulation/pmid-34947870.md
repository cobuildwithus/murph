This source is included for **Exercise-timed whole-body PBM sibling variant**.

**Findings:** This narrative review surveys PBM in sports medicine and physiotherapy, discussing possible benefits for performance, fatigue reduction, and muscle repair. Its abstract also states that there are no unanimously accepted dosage and treatment parameters. For the target protocol, the review is background context only because it is broad, non-pooled, and dominated by localized PBM literature rather than direct whole-body red and near-infrared trials. Its main value is mapping the sports-PBM rationale and preserving uncertainty about parameter standardization.

**Why it matters:** It helps explain why sports PBM narratives often sound more confident than the small and heterogeneous whole-body study set actually justifies.

**Potential experiment signals:** exercise performance, fatigue, muscle recovery, treatment-parameter standardization.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use in the background bucket for mechanism and source recall, not for claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
