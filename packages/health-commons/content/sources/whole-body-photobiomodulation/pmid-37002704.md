This source is included for **Adjacent-variant and exclusion boundary anchors**.

**Findings:** The accessible primary excerpts report pooled improvement in wake after sleep onset, including standardized mean difference -0.61 (95% CI -1.11 to -0.11) for objective wake after sleep onset and diary wake-after-sleep-onset improvement of roughly 36 minutes in one pooled estimate. The same excerpts report no significant pooled effects on sleep latency, total sleep time, or sleep efficiency. The review also notes an important timing distinction: morning light tends to advance circadian timing, whereas evening light tends to delay it. Modality mismatch is explicit because these are bright-light insomnia protocols, not red/NIR whole-body photobiomodulation. The accessible sources did not expose a stable pooled participant total, so that detail should be rechecked from the full article before downstream protocol copy.

**Why it matters:** It keeps insomnia-related bright-light evidence in the right bucket: useful for endpoint and timing context, but not direct PBM evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** wake after sleep onset, sleep latency, total sleep time, sleep efficiency, timing of light exposure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use this only as adjacent-variant insomnia-light context. Do not merge its pooled bright-light effects into whole-body red/NIR claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
