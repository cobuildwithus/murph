This source is included for **Skin/cosmetic large-area or full-body PBM sibling variant**.

**Findings:** This early cohort report described a separate cohort of 93 patients with a wide range of Fitzpatrick skin types treated with the Gentlewaves full-panel 590 nm high-energy LED array using a specific pulsing sequence. The abstract reported improvement in signs of photoaging in 90% of patients. Most participants showed improvement in peri-ocular wrinkles, Fitzpatrick photoaging classification, global skin texture, background erythema, and pigmentation. No side effects were noted in the accessible abstract. Because the study was uncontrolled, face-focused, and used 590 nm rather than a direct whole-body red/NIR intervention, it is a population and modality mismatch for Murph.

**Why it matters:** It is historically relevant to panel-style PBM, but it is best treated as face-only cosmetic context.

**Potential experiment signals:** wrinkles, skin texture, erythema, pigmentation, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep this source strictly in the adjacent-variant bucket because it is an uncontrolled 590 nm facial study with cosmetic endpoints only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Skin Photobiomodulation Note

This source is included for **large-area/full-panel cosmetic PBM sibling evidence**.

**Findings:** The source reports clinical experience with 590 nm LED photomodulation and extracted summaries describe improvement in photoaging signs in about 90% of treated patients, including periocular wrinkles, texture, erythema, and pigmentation. The extracted summaries also state that no side effects were observed. The design details and participant accounting are limited in the available extracted records. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-15624743`.

**Why it matters:** It represents early clinic-supervised LED photomodulation experience and includes both improvement and safety descriptions, but with limited extractable design detail.

**Potential experiment signals:** periocular wrinkles, global texture, background erythema, pigmentation, Fitzpatrick photoaging classification, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as historical amber-light clinical context; do not treat as direct red+NIR home-mask evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
