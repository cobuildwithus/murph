This source is included for **Mechanism and review context**.

**Findings:** This compact review/commentary frames PBM as a potentially useful non-pharmacologic sleep intervention and emphasizes a nocturnal transcranial hypothesis rather than direct whole-body exposure evidence. Its main value here is source recall and mechanism framing: it points toward sleep-state dependence, brain function at night, and related pathway hypotheses for future work. Population mismatch is substantial because the focus is largely transcranial and brain-centered, not whole-body red/NIR exposure. No direct whole-body human efficacy data were extracted from this source.

**Why it matters:** It helps map the older sleep-PBM conversation and preserves a clear boundary between whole-body claims and broader PBM sleep speculation.

**Potential experiment signals:** subjective sleep quality, sleep efficiency, next-day alertness.

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep this source in the background bucket only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
