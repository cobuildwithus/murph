This source is included for **Exercise-timed whole-body PBM sibling variant**.

**Findings:** Twelve resistance-trained men completed a randomized crossover study with a whole-body NovoTHOR light bed used for 15 minutes before and 15 minutes after a high-intensity resistance training session. The device used red 660 nm and near-infrared 850 nm LEDs at approximately 17 mW/cm^2 and 25 J/cm^2. Creatine kinase was followed through 72 hours and salivary IL-6 through 120 minutes. No significant differences were reported for CK area under the curve (p=0.669) or IL-6 area under the curve (p=0.155), although CK percent change trended modestly lower at some time points. The authors also note limitations in blinding, absence of a robust sham, and non-contact exposure geometry compared with localized PBM practice.

**Why it matters:** It is one of the main direct human athlete studies for a commercial whole-body light bed, and its null biomarker result should constrain downstream claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** creatine kinase, interleukin-6, delayed soreness, recovery time course, tolerability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for dosing and boundary evidence only; do not present it as proof of enhanced muscle recovery.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
