This source is included for **Exercise-timed whole-body PBM sibling variant**.

**Findings:** This recruiting ClinicalTrials.gov registry plans to randomize 24 professional soccer players to whole-body PBM or placebo during an 8-week state championship. The intervention uses a six-panel Joovv Elite system at about 20 cm from the body for 15 minutes total, split between anterior and posterior exposure, twice weekly on non-consecutive days after training. Planned endpoints include weekly soreness ratings, jump tests, manual dynamometry, baseline and end-of-season complete blood count, and PSQI in Portuguese. The protocol also lists practical risk notes such as transient skin warmth, eye-protection requirements, and minor blood-draw discomfort. No efficacy results are available yet.

**Why it matters:** It adds a modern repeated-use sports protocol with stronger longitudinal follow-up than most published whole-body athlete studies in the batch.

**Potential experiment signals:** DOMS, jump height, quadriceps strength, PSQI, CBC, adherence, tolerability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep as registry-only implementation context until results are posted or published.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
