This source is included for **Adjacent-variant and exclusion boundary anchors**.

**Findings:** The review identified 17 original data studies focused on major depressive disorder and described preliminary clinical evidence of antidepressant effects with good tolerability. It also summarized a core proposed mechanism: enhancement of mitochondrial metabolism via cytochrome c oxidase, alongside other biological pathways relevant to depression. However, the review emphasizes that clinical studies were heterogeneous in population and stimulation parameters and that most lacked an appropriate control group. Population mismatch remains substantial because the reviewed interventions were transcranial or otherwise non-whole-body clinical PBM approaches in psychiatric contexts.

**Why it matters:** It provides mood-claim context while preserving the limits of the underlying clinical evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** depression symptoms, tolerability, mitochondrial function, control adequacy.

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep this as same-mechanism clinical context only. It does not directly establish benefit from whole-body red/NIR exposure.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
