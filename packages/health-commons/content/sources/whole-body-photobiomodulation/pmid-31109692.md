This source is included for **Safety, contraindication, and population-boundary evidence**.

**Findings:** A systematic review searched Scopus, MEDLINE/PubMed, and Embase and included 27 articles covering PBMT for oral mucositis, lymphedema, radiodermatitis, and peripheral neuropathy in oncology settings. Most included studies reported no side effects with PBMT, and the review concluded that the current literature did not identify tumor-safety issues linked to supportive-care PBMT use. This is reassuring but still indirect for an unsupervised whole-body wellness protocol because the evidence base came from supervised oncology applications with heterogeneous designs and variable follow-up.

**Why it matters:** This is a backbone safety-only review for addressing cancer-related contraindication questions without turning supportive-care oncology PBMT into a whole-body efficacy claim.

**Potential experiment signals:** adverse events, tumor recurrence, cancer progression, treatment tolerability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as clinical-supervised safety-boundary evidence: reassuring on observed oncology PBMT harms, but not direct proof for healthy-user whole-body protocols.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
