This source is included for **Exercise-timed whole-body PBM sibling variant**.

**Findings:** Twenty elite female basketball players were randomized to 14 nights of whole-body red-light irradiation or placebo. The intervention lasted 30 minutes each night. After 14 days, the active group showed improved Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index scores, higher serum melatonin, and better 12-minute run performance, all reported as statistically significant. Changes in sleep quality also correlated with melatonin change (r = -0.695, p = 0.006). This study is an important positive adjacent source, but it remains a red-only athlete trial rather than a direct whole-body red-plus-near-infrared protocol match.

**Why it matters:** It is the clearest positive repeated-exposure athlete study in the batch and it ties sleep physiology to performance.

**Potential experiment signals:** PSQI, serum melatonin, 12-minute run performance, adherence to nightly sessions.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent red-light evidence only, with a clear note that the spectrum and population differ from the target protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
