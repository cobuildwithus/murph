This source is included for **Chronic pain/fibromyalgia whole-body PBM sibling variant**.

**Findings:** The review included 14 randomized trials across fibromyalgia, peripheral neuropathies, orofacial pain, and musculoskeletal pain. The authors report that most trials showed significant pain reduction, particularly in fibromyalgia and neuropathy, and that some trials also found functional or quality-of-life gains. The source is valuable as chronic-pain context, but it mixes localized and non-localized PBM approaches and is not restricted to whole-body protocols.

**Why it matters:** This review is helpful for preserving wider chronic-pain PBM context and for identifying how often adverse events were explicitly reported in the RCT literature.

**Potential experiment signals:** pain, function, quality of life, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Background-only source: useful for literature recall and safety context, not for direct whole-body red/NIR claims.

**Safety / adverse events:** Across the included RCTs, 13 of 14 studies reportedly found no adverse effects, while one study described low-grade side effects such as transient warmth, mild discomfort, or temporary tingling in both intervention and control groups; no serious intervention-related dropouts were reported.

**Limitations:** Heterogeneous conditions, wavelengths, doses, devices, comparators, and follow-up windows; many small trials; incomplete adverse-event reporting; no quantitative meta-analysis.

**Population mismatch / directness:** This is broader chronic-pain PBM context with mixed modalities and only partial overlap with whole-body fibromyalgia protocols.
