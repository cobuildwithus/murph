This source is included for **Chronic pain/fibromyalgia whole-body PBM sibling variant**.

**Findings:** The NIHR listing identifies a UK whole-body PBM chronic-pain trial and gives public recruitment context, including a recruitment window in 2022. It also links the chronic-pain program to the broader feasibility pathway described in the published protocol literature. No outcome or adverse-event tables were available from the listing used here.

**Why it matters:** The NIHR listing is a public-facing trace of the UK chronic-pain pathway and helps anchor the real-world trial ecosystem around the BMJ protocol paper.

**Potential experiment signals:** pain, fatigue, sleep disturbance, mood.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as recruitment and implementation context only.

**Safety / adverse events:** This listing does not provide outcome-level safety data.

**Limitations:** Public recruitment listing rather than a results record; sparse detail in accessible extract; no extracted sample size; no direct effect estimates.

**Population mismatch / directness:** Relevant at the device/program level, but the listing reflects a supervised chronic-pain clinical study and not a generalized protocol variant.

**Artifact candidates / rights:** Best manifest candidate is an HTML snapshot of the NIHR page. Terms for redistribution were not clearly verified from the reviewed sources.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
