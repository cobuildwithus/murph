This source is included for **Chronic pain/fibromyalgia whole-body PBM sibling variant**.

**Findings:** The protocol planned to randomize 44 participants with fibromyalgia to active or placebo whole-body PBM. The active program specified 660- and 850-nm LEDs in a 50:50 ratio, 25.2 J/cm^2 fluence, 1200-second treatments, total emitted power of 967 W, and a schedule of three sessions per week for 4 weeks. Primary outcomes were pain measures, with secondary outcomes covering functionality, soft-tissue quality, central sensitisation, and psychological factors through 6-month follow-up.

**Why it matters:** This is the anchor protocol for the most developed whole-body fibromyalgia RCT program in the batch and the clearest source for pre-specified parameters and follow-up timing.

**Potential experiment signals:** pain, fibromyalgia impact, leisure physical activity, tissue elasticity, pain pressure threshold, autonomic symptoms, kinesiophobia, self-efficacy.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use it for exact protocol structure and sham-comparator details, not for claims of benefit.

**Safety / adverse events:** The source is valuable for sham-controlled trial architecture and prospective monitoring plans, but it does not provide observed adverse-event results because it is a protocol paper.

**Limitations:** Protocol-only publication; no outcome data; private-clinic recruitment; fibromyalgia-only population; target enrollment rather than completed sample.

**Population mismatch / directness:** Direct whole-body red/NIR exposure is relevant, but the evidence remains limited to a supervised fibromyalgia trial design rather than the generalized protocol variant.
