This source is included for **Dose, device, and implementation reporting**.

**Findings:** This short editorial is included because it argues for the preferred term photobiomodulation therapy instead of older low-level light or low-level laser terminology. It is also notable as a terminology-indexing source because the field’s adoption of PBMT language improved definitional precision and literature retrieval. No abstract or study-results section was exposed in the records reviewed here, so extraction is intentionally limited to naming context.

**Why it matters:** Murph family and protocol names should use the field’s preferred term when possible, while still preserving older aliases for source recall.

**Potential experiment signals:** literature-search terminology only.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to standardize naming and aliases. Do not treat it as efficacy, safety, or dosing evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
