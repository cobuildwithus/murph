This source is included for **Adjacent-variant and exclusion boundary anchors**.

**Findings:** The review describes intranasal PBM as either a nostril-based portable intervention or an implanted deep-nasal concept intended to deliver local or partially systemic effects. Accessible abstract text states that nostril-based intranasal PBM may improve blood rheology and cerebral blood flow and may resemble peripheral intravenous laser irradiation without puncture. The review also notes that no clinical studies of implanted deep-nasal PBMT had yet been conducted and that sham-controlled and transcranial-comparative studies were warranted. Population and modality mismatch are obvious because the exposure is local intranasal delivery rather than whole-body red/NIR exposure.

**Why it matters:** It helps keep intranasal device narratives from being mistaken for direct whole-body evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** cerebral blood flow, blood rheology, neurologic symptoms, comparative sham response.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as same-mechanism context and boundary-setting only. Do not promote intranasal PBM findings into whole-body claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
