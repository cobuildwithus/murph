This source is included for **Safety, contraindication, and population-boundary evidence**.

**Findings:** This PRISMA-guided review included 67 studies: 43 in vitro, 15 in vivo, and 9 clinical. The review found conflicting in vitro results, but in vivo studies and clinical trials with follow-up generally supported PBMT safety with respect to tumor growth when recommended parameters were used. That makes the paper important for oncology safety framing, while also preserving a strong directness caveat because the evidence base is supervised oncology care rather than whole-body wellness exposure.

**Why it matters:** This is a major mixed-evidence oncology review that helps keep theory-heavy tumor concerns separate from what has actually been observed in animal and human follow-up studies.

**Potential experiment signals:** tumor growth or recurrence surveillance, overall survival, adverse events, parameter reporting.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-boundary context when malignancy is relevant: preclinical findings were mixed, but supervised in vivo and clinical evidence was more reassuring.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
