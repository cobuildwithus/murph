This source is included for **Exercise-timed whole-body PBM sibling variant**.

**Findings:** This recruiting Brazilian registry plans a randomized double-blind crossover trial in 34 trained young men. The active full-body PBM condition uses 10 minutes of exposure to the anterior and posterior body 5 minutes before assessment, at about 30.57 J/cm^2 and 50.95 mW/cm^2, with a 7-day washout between treatments. Planned outcomes include peak torque, fatigue index, total work, agility testing, delayed-onset muscle soreness by numeric scale and pain map, and perceived recovery. No results are available yet.

**Why it matters:** It expands the registry map for a larger trained-men performance study and shows how later investigators combined agility and recovery endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** peak torque, fatigue index, total work, agility, DOMS, perceived recovery.

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep as future-tracking context only until the trial reports results.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
