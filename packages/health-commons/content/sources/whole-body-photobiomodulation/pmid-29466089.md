This source is included for **Safety, contraindication, and population-boundary evidence**.

**Findings:** The review describes why PBM and cancer remains a contested topic: some cell-culture work has suggested growth stimulation or greater aggressiveness in certain lines, while other experimental and clinical data do not show the same pattern. The source does not provide direct whole-body safety data. Its main function is to preserve uncertainty and stop Murph from collapsing mixed oncology signals into a universal yes-or-no claim.

**Why it matters:** This is a foundational uncertainty source that keeps Murph from making overly absolute claims about PBM and malignancy.

**Potential experiment signals:** tumor growth concerns, cell proliferation concerns, recurrence surveillance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as background oncology caution context, especially where users ask whether PBM is universally contraindicated or universally safe in malignancy.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
