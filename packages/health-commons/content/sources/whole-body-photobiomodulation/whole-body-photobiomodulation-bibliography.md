This bibliography is the curation layer behind the whole-body photobiomodulation package.

It intentionally mixes five evidence roles without collapsing them into one efficacy story:

1. direct whole-body records
2. dose and device interpretation sources
3. sibling-variant literatures such as fibromyalgia, pain, performance, and cosmetic/photoaging
4. safety and screening boundaries
5. adjacent modalities that should not be treated as the same protocol

The package-level conclusion stays narrow: the direct evidence for a general whole-body red/NIR wellness-sleep protocol is still limited and parameter-sensitive, so the protocol is packaged as cautious and non-onboarding.
