This source is included for **Adjacent-variant and exclusion boundary anchors**.

**Findings:** This guideline concluded that specific PBM protocols can be recommended for prevention of oral mucositis and related pain in hematopoietic stem cell transplant, head-and-neck radiotherapy without chemotherapy, and head-and-neck radiotherapy with chemotherapy. It also concluded that no guideline was possible for treatment of established oral mucositis or for chemotherapy-only oral mucositis because evidence was inadequate and settings were too variable. Safety reporting in the analyzed randomized trials did not identify short- or long-term adverse events, although one cohort study reported a non-painful burning sensation in 15% of participants. Population mismatch is explicit: these are localized supervised oncology protocols, not whole-body wellness exposure.

**Why it matters:** It prevents localized oncology PBM protocols from being overgeneralized into whole-body claims and highlights the need to preserve parameter specificity.

**Potential experiment signals:** oral mucositis severity, oral pain, parameter fidelity, setting-specific safety.

**Protocol takeaway:** Treat this as a safety and clinical-supervision boundary source only. Parameter-specific oral-mucositis recommendations should not be ported into whole-body protocol copy.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
