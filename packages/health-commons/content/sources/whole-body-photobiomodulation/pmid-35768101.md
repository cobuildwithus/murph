This source is included for **Chronic pain/fibromyalgia whole-body PBM sibling variant**.

**Findings:** The protocol planned a non-probability sample of 20 adults with chronic pain conditions seen in an NHS pain service. Participants were to receive 18 whole-body photobiomodulation sessions over 6 weeks using a NovoTHOR bed, with a 6-month follow-up and an embedded qualitative component. The stated emphasis was feasibility and acceptability, with exploratory participant-reported and performance outcomes rather than a confirmatory efficacy analysis.

**Why it matters:** This is the clearest source in the batch for how a UK chronic-pain service intended to deliver whole-body PBM, including session count, follow-up horizon, and feasibility framing.

**Potential experiment signals:** pain severity, fatigue, sleep disturbance, mood, acceptability, adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Treat as implementation context only: it defines a supervised chronic-pain course but does not establish benefit.

**Safety / adverse events:** This protocol describes supervised clinical delivery and trial eligibility boundaries, but it does not contribute outcome-level adverse-event data because it is a pre-results publication.

**Limitations:** Protocol-only source; single-arm design; small target enrollment; referral-clinic chronic-pain population; no effect estimates available.

**Population mismatch / directness:** Direct whole-body exposure is relevant to the protocol family, but the population is a chronic-pain referral cohort managed in a supervised NHS pathway rather than a general wellness population.
