This source is included for **Chronic pain/fibromyalgia whole-body PBM sibling variant**.

**Findings:** The scoping review surveyed academic evidence on photobiomodulation therapy in fibromyalgia and mapped both clinical and experiential outcomes against NICE recommendation concerns. The authors report that the literature appears plentiful and promising but heterogeneous, and they emphasize that UK standard-of-care and cost-effectiveness evidence remain important unresolved steps for policy integration.

**Why it matters:** This source is valuable for policy and implementation framing because it explicitly organizes the fibromyalgia PBMT literature around NICE concerns and remaining gaps.

**Potential experiment signals:** clinical outcomes, participant experience, cost-effectiveness, standard-of-care gaps.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as policy and evidence-map context only.

**Safety / adverse events:** This review is not a dedicated safety synthesis, although it can help locate where safety and tolerability details are missing or inconsistently reported.

**Limitations:** Scoping review design; no pooled effect sizes; heterogeneous outcomes; policy-facing conclusions depend on the underlying studies rather than new comparative analysis.

**Population mismatch / directness:** The review covers fibromyalgia PBMT literature broadly and is therefore background context rather than direct protocol evidence for generalized whole-body red/NIR exposure.

**Artifact candidates / rights:** The article is open access through MDPI. A conservative manifest entry should use the article HTML page unless a direct file URL is separately verified.
