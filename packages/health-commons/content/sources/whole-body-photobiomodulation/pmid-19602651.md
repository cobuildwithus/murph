This source is included for **Adjacent-variant and exclusion boundary anchors**.

**Findings:** The review searched major databases and identified nine relevant human studies. It concluded that there was limited moderate evidence supporting normalization of blood pressure and use in congestive heart failure, fair evidence from a single study for chronic pain, and weak evidence from single studies for chronic fatigue syndrome and obesity. It also reported consistent fair evidence refuting cholesterol-lowering claims. Modality mismatch is substantial because far-infrared sauna involves thermal heating and sauna physiology rather than the nonthermal red/NIR photobiomodulation protocols under review here.

**Why it matters:** It keeps infrared-sauna marketing claims from being merged into PBM evidence and preserves null findings such as the cholesterol result.

**Potential experiment signals:** blood pressure, heart-failure symptoms, pain, cholesterol.

**Protocol takeaway:** Treat as adjacent thermal-sauna context only. Do not use it as evidence for whole-body PBM efficacy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
