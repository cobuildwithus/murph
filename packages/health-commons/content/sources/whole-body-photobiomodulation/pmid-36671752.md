This source is included for **Starter whole-body wellness/sleep evidence**.

**Findings:** A double-blind placebo-controlled human trial tested 850 nm PBM at 1, 4, and 6.5 J/cm^2 against placebo over 4 weeks. The most consistent positive signals appeared only at 6.5 J/cm^2 and only in winter, with improved mood, lower drowsiness, lower IFN-gamma, and lower resting heart rate. No significant effects on sleep or circadian rhythms were reported. The population was otherwise healthy and the illumination was limited to face, neck, and hands rather than a literal whole-body bed. No adverse-event table was exposed in the accessible records used for this extraction.

**Why it matters:** It gives the clearest controlled human signal in this batch for daytime systemic-wellness PBM outcomes while also preserving the null sleep finding and the partial-body directness limitation.

**Potential experiment signals:** mood, daytime drowsiness, resting heart rate, inflammatory markers, sleep quality.

**Protocol takeaway:** Treat as adjacent partial-body evidence: the highest tested dose showed winter-only mood, drowsiness, IFN-gamma, and resting-heart-rate signals, without significant sleep or circadian gains; do not use it as literal whole-body light-bed efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
