This source is included for **Skin/cosmetic large-area or full-body PBM sibling variant**.

**Findings:** Fifty subjects were screened, 38 were selected, and 31 completed the trial. Participants received nine Omnilux treatments over five weeks combining 633 nm light at 126 J/cm² and 830 nm light at 66 J/cm², both delivered for 20 minutes. Key profilometry measures (Sq, Sa, Sp, and St) improved significantly by week 12. Photoaging scores improved at all follow-up points, with 51.6% of completers showing 25%–50% improvement and 12.9% showing 50%–75% improvement by week 12. Most subjects reported softer periorbital wrinkles, smoother skin, and better firmness. Local tolerance was generally good, but one participant withdrew because of a mild facial herpes simplex reaction, and mild erythema was recorded in one subject at day 8 and seven subjects at day 15.

**Why it matters:** It is a good adjacent record for preserving early 633/830 nm facial settings and mild adverse-event patterns.

**Potential experiment signals:** wrinkle scores, profilometry, tolerability, erythema, herpes reactivation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Treat it as adjacent facial red-plus-NIR context only; the local wrinkle and tolerability outcomes should not be promoted into whole-body efficacy claims.
