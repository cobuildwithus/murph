This source is included for **Reviews and evidence maps**.

**Findings:** The review reports modest weight loss, lower waist circumference, energy deficits, and improvements in BMI, fat mass, lean body mass, systolic blood pressure, fasting glucose, fasting insulin, and HbA1c. Subgroup interpretation suggested that health improvements were primarily due to energy deficit, followed by alignment with eating time of day.

**Why it matters:** Essential for separating “when you eat” from “how much you eat” in protocol language.

**Potential experiment signals:** body weight, waist circumference, energy intake, fasting glucose, insulin, HbA1c, blood pressure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to avoid claiming calorie-independent 18:6 benefits unless direct isocaloric evidence supports that claim.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
