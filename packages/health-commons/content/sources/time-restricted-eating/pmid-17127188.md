This source is included for **Reviews and evidence maps**.

**Findings:** The review identifies rapid weight loss and prolonged fasting as contexts associated with increased gallstone formation risk.; Gallbladder history or right-upper-quadrant abdominal pain during fasting should be treated as a clinical safety boundary rather than a normal adaptation symptom.

**Why it matters:** This is an adjacent safety-boundary source for fasting plus rapid weight loss, especially for users with gallbladder history or right-upper-quadrant symptoms.

**Potential experiment signals:** gallstone formation, rapid weight loss, prolonged fasting, gallbladder emptying, abdominal pain.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not frame TRE as risk-free for people with gallbladder disease or rapid weight-loss goals; include symptom off-ramps and clinical referral.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
