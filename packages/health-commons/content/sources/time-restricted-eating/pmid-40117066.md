This source is included for **Reviews and evidence maps**.

**Findings:** The review frames fasting and caloric-intake fluctuation as potential headache or migraine triggers in susceptible individuals.; The source is symptom and mechanism context rather than evidence that TRE improves or worsens headache outcomes in an 18:6 protocol.

**Why it matters:** This is a symptom safety-boundary source for headache-prone users and supports explicit off-ramps for fasting-related headaches.

**Potential experiment signals:** headache, migraine, fasting trigger, dehydration, caloric-intake fluctuation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Treat fasting-related headache as a tolerability signal that may require hydration review, window relaxation, food-timing changes, or clinical advice.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
