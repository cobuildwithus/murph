This source is included for **Direct 18:6 and six-hour TRE evidence**.

## Findings
- **Sleep quality, duration, insomnia severity, and obstructive sleep apnea symptoms.**: Sleep quality, wake time, bedtime, sleep duration, sleep latency, insomnia severity, and obstructive sleep apnea symptoms did not clearly change after 8 weeks of 4-hour or 6-hour TRF.
- **Interpretation of sleep endpoints.**: Because sleep outcomes were self-reported and secondary, the study is useful for a no-clear-sleep-benefit boundary rather than definitive sleep efficacy or harm conclusions.

## Why it matters
Provides one of the closest sleep-specific checks for a 6-hour eating window and helps avoid promising sleep improvements.

## Potential experiment signals
Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index, sleep duration, sleep latency, insomnia severity, obstructive sleep apnea symptoms

## Protocol takeaway
A 6-hour TRE arm did not clearly improve or worsen self-reported sleep outcomes over 8 weeks in adults with obesity.
