This source is included for **Direct 18:6 and six-hour TRE evidence**.

## Findings
- **Twenty-four-hour energy expenditure.**: Early time-restricted feeding did not clearly increase 24-hour energy expenditure, arguing against a metabolic-rate explanation for weight loss.
- **Ghrelin, hunger pattern, metabolic flexibility, respiratory quotient, and fat oxidation.**: The source reported lower ghrelin or appetite-related signals and greater fat-oxidation/metabolic-flexibility signals, making it relevant to mechanism but not sufficient for long-term outcome claims.

## Why it matters
Clarifies mechanism: early 6-hour TRF did not increase energy expenditure, so weight claims should not be framed as metabolic-rate boosting.

## Potential experiment signals
energy expenditure, ghrelin, hunger, fullness, fat oxidation, metabolic flexibility

## Protocol takeaway
Use as a mechanism/context source: appetite and fat-oxidation signals were observed, but 24-hour energy expenditure did not increase.

## Claim use
`context-only`.
