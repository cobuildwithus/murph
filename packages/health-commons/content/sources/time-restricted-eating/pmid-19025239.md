# Fasting increases risk for onset of binge eating and bulimic pathology: a 5-year prospective study
This source is included for **Special populations and contraindications**.
## Findings
- `finding:pmid-19025239-01` — In a 5-year prospective study of 496 adolescent girls, fasting predicted greater risk for later binge eating and bulimic pathology.
- `finding:pmid-19025239-02` — This source should not be overgeneralized to adults following structured TRE, but it is highly relevant to youth exclusions and binge/bulimia screening.

## Why it matters
This is one of the strongest reasons not to generalize adult TRE protocols to adolescents or users with binge/bulimic vulnerability.

## Potential experiment signals
- fasting for weight control
- binge eating
- bulimic symptoms
- adolescent age

## Protocol takeaway
Adolescents and users with binge/bulimic risk should not be offered self-directed 18:6 fasting; referral/supervision is required.

## Claim use
`safety-only`.
