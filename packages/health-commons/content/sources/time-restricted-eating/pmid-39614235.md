This source is included for **Graded windows and adjacent TRE variants**.

**Findings:** Forty-one volunteers were randomized to TRE 16:8, TRE 14:10, TRE 12:12, or no dietary restriction; the 16:8 window was 10:00–18:00, 14:10 was 09:00–19:00, and 12:12 was 08:00–20:00. In intention-to-treat analysis, TRE 16:8 reduced body mass by -2.46% and absolute fat mass by -8.65%, with no reported change in lean soft tissue or calorie intake; shorter TRE arms did not show significant changes. The trial reported that none of the other measured biochemical outcomes significantly changed after 8 weeks.

**Why it matters:** Gives dose/window-duration context: 16-hour fasting showed body-composition changes, while shorter 12–14-hour windows did not clearly shift measured outcomes in this healthy sample.

**Potential experiment signals:** body mass, fat mass, lean soft tissue, lipid profile, fasting glucose, leptin, insulin, testosterone, calorie intake.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent duration-dose evidence supporting 16-hour fasting as a more potent step than 12:12 or 14:10 in healthy adults, while keeping the population mismatch explicit.
