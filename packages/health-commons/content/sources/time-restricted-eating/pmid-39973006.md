This source is included for **Calorie restriction and diet-confounding**.

**Findings:**
- Compared with unrestricted eating, weight change was -1.4 kg for TRE (95% CI, -4.5 to 1.7; P=.53) and -2.5 kg for caloric restriction (95% CI, -5.8 to 0.8; P=.18). Weight, body composition, caloric intake, and glycemic measures were similar across groups.
- Final eating windows were longer than the target in the TRE group (about 9.8 hours), and eating-window reduction correlated with decreased calorie intake and visceral fat, pointing to adherence and intake as key explanatory variables.

**Why it matters:** This trial directly tests timing, calorie reduction, and unrestricted eating in parallel, making it a high-value calorie- and adherence-confounding source.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- Body weight

- Metabolic flexibility
- Glycemic measures
- Visceral fat

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only null/mixed evidence: self-selected 8-hour TRE did not significantly improve weight or metabolic outcomes versus unrestricted eating or caloric reduction over 12 weeks.
