This source is included for **Reviews and evidence maps**.

**Findings:** The review emphasizes medication-specific planning and clinical oversight for fasting, particularly for insulin and sulfonylurea regimens with higher hypoglycemia risk.; The review notes limited safety data and frames guidance as clinical management rather than proof that unsupervised fasting is safe for all diabetes patients.; Blood-glucose monitoring and individualized risk assessment are central to safe fasting management in diabetes.

**Why it matters:** This is a core safety-boundary source for any TRE protocol that might be attempted by users with diabetes or glucose-lowering medications.

**Potential experiment signals:** hypoglycemia risk, medication adjustment, blood glucose monitoring, clinical supervision.

**Protocol takeaway:** People with diabetes, especially those using insulin or sulfonylureas, need clinician involvement before changing fasting duration or meal timing.
