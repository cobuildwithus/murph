This source is included for **Reviews and evidence maps**.

**Findings:** The review describes human TRF as daily intake restricted to feeding windows of about 3 to 12 hours, with fasting during the rest of the day. The accessible abstract-level record notes that no specific guidance on the appropriate feeding time has been firmly promoted by the evidence base.

**Why it matters:** This source is important for not over-specifying optimal start/end times for an 18:6 protocol without direct head-to-head evidence.

**Population mismatch / directness:** Window-focused human TRF evidence is broader than, and not specific to, 18:6 graded windows.

**Potential experiment signals:** feeding-window duration and clock timing, body weight, glucose and insulin markers, lipids and blood pressure where reported.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to acknowledge that window timing and duration matter but remain unsettled in human evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
