This source is included for **Direct 18:6 and six-hour TRE evidence**.

## Findings
- **Diet quality, energy-dense food intake, weight, and adherence.**: The pilot suggested early 6-hour TRE may support weight reduction and reduce energy-dense foods commonly eaten in the evening or late night, but this was preliminary.
- **Feasibility and attrition.**: The study was interrupted by COVID-19 campus closure and had high early-TRE attrition, so feasibility caveats should accompany any diet-quality or weight signal.

## Why it matters
Directly targets a nonclinical college population and therefore adds wellness-relevant feasibility and diet-quality context.

## Potential experiment signals
diet quality, energy-dense evening foods, body weight, adherence, attrition

## Protocol takeaway
A 6-hour early window may reduce evening energy-dense intake, but feasibility and attrition were major caveats in this small interrupted pilot.

`supports-protocol`.
