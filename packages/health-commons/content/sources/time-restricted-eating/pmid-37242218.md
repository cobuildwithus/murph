This source is included for **Graded windows and adjacent TRE variants**.

**Findings:** The study reported that adherence estimates differed by method, with objective and subjective adherence each giving imperfect pictures of whether participants followed TRE across weeks. Reported barriers included work schedules, social events, and family life; the source supports personalization rather than a single rigid window for every user.

**Why it matters:** This source is directly relevant to starter-window burden because it compares adherence measurement methods and names daily-life barriers that can defeat TRE even when the protocol seems simple.

**Potential experiment signals:** adherence, objective adherence, self-reported adherence, work schedules, social events, family life, personalization.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adherence-context evidence: work, social, and family constraints should be expected and personalized starter windows are likely necessary.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
