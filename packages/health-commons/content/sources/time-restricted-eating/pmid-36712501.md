This source is included for **Reviews and evidence maps**.

**Findings:** The review reported wide adherence rates across included TRE clinical trials, with values ranging from 47% to 95%.; Only a subset of reviewed trials reported family, social, work, or miscellaneous barriers, making adherence burden easy to underestimate.; The review recommends better adherence and barrier monitoring and behavioral strategies to address barriers rather than relying only on eating-window prescriptions.

**Why it matters:** This directly supports preserving behavioral burden and implementation constraints instead of reporting only metabolic outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:** adherence rates, barriers to adherence, self-monitoring, family burden, social burden, work burden.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use this to justify graded starter windows, barrier prompts, and adherence measurement rather than assuming a six-hour window is behaviorally neutral.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
