This source is included for **Graded windows and adjacent TRE variants**.

**Findings:** The RCT reported improvements in glucose control and modest reductions in body weight or fat mass with TRE plus counseling compared with counseling alone in adults with metabolic syndrome. The metabolic-syndrome population, personalized 8- to 10-hour window, and standard-counseling co-intervention mean this should not be treated as direct evidence for unsupervised 18:6 in generally healthy users.

**Why it matters:** This recent RCT is a major adjacent source for an 8- to 10-hour daily TRE window in a cardiometabolic-risk population.

**Potential experiment signals:** glycemic control, body weight, fat mass, cardiometabolic risk, adherence, app logging.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent clinical RCT evidence: personalized 8- to 10-hour TRE can improve some glycemic and body-composition endpoints in metabolic syndrome, but population and window differ from 18:6 starter protocols.
