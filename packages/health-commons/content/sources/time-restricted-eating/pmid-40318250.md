This source is included for **Reviews and evidence maps**.

**Findings:** Four studies contributed to the primary hunger meta-analysis in 323 participants; TRE increased hunger compared with isocaloric control (MD 2.05; 95% CI 1.46 to 2.64; I²=0%). The review identified elevated hunger as a feasibility consideration that may affect long-term adherence or rebound eating during compressed windows.

**Why it matters:** Hunger is a direct implementation risk for 18:6, especially during transition from 12:12 or 14:10 starter windows.

**Potential experiment signals:** hunger, appetite, acceptability, adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to support hunger tracking, graded progression, and rollback thresholds.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
