This source is included for **Direct 18:6 and six-hour TRE evidence**.

## Findings
- **Body weight, anaerobic power, and aerobic endurance.**: Both six-hour TRE windows reduced body weight and improved anaerobic power; extracted results indicated greater weight reduction with early TRE and greater anaerobic-power improvement with delayed TRE, while aerobic endurance was unchanged.
- **Generalizability to cardiometabolic 18:6 claims.**: The source is direct for 6-hour windows but mainly informs performance/timing context rather than obesity, diabetes, or broader cardiometabolic claims.

## Why it matters
Adds direct 6-hour timing and performance context, including a comparison of early versus delayed windows in young untrained adults.

## Potential experiment signals
body weight, anaerobic power, aerobic endurance, window timing

## Protocol takeaway
Both 6-hour windows reduced body weight and improved anaerobic power, with early timing stronger for weight and delayed timing stronger for anaerobic power in this young untrained sample.

## Claim use
`context-only`.
