This source is included for **Graded windows and adjacent TRE variants**.

**Findings:**
- TRE produced a small within-group weight decrease but no significant between-group weight advantage and no clear secondary cardiometabolic improvements versus control.
- The extracted report noted lean-mass and appendicular-lean-mass decreases in the TRE in-person subgroup, making body-composition monitoring relevant.

**Why it matters:** This is a central adjacent null trial that guards against overclaiming TRE benefits when no calorie guidance is provided.

**Potential experiment signals:** body weight, lean mass, appendicular lean mass, fasting glucose, fasting insulin, lipids, blood pressure.

**Protocol takeaway:** A 16:8 noon-to-evening schedule did not clearly outperform control; do not extrapolate it into a guaranteed 18:6 benefit.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction caveat:** The intervention was an 8 h noon-to-evening window, not 18:6, and did not prescribe calorie quality or amount.
