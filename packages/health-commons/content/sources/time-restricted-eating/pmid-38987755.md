This source is included for **Reviews and evidence maps**.

**Findings:** The meta-analysis found no significant group differences for fatigue, headache, or dropout, with risk differences near zero in the pooled analysis.; A subgroup analysis reported a numerically higher dizziness risk in an intermittent-fasting subgroup, but the estimate did not reach conventional statistical significance.; The review does not establish safety for pregnancy, youth, insulin or sulfonylurea use, active eating disorders, or other high-risk clinical contexts.

**Why it matters:** This is a safety-only aggregate source for common adverse-event expectations in the most common TRE trial population, without covering medication, pregnancy, youth, or eating-disorder boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** fatigue, headache, dizziness, dropout, overall adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as reassurance only for common short-term adverse events in overweight/obesity trial populations; do not extend it to special populations or medication-risk groups.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
