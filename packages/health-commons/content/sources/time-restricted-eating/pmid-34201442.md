This source is included for **Graded windows and adjacent TRE variants**.

**Findings:** Among 50 enrolled adults, the TRE phase compressed the eating window from 16 h 04 min to 11 h 54 min; the paper reports 64% logging adherence and 47% adherence to the 10-hour window. The pilot reported body-weight change of -2.1 ± 3.0 kg, waist-circumference change of -2.2 ± 4.6 cm, and systolic blood-pressure change of -12 ± 11 mmHg during the 90-day TRE period. The study did not include a randomized control group and did not isolate energy intake, diet quality, physical activity, or regression-to-the-mean effects; adverse-event details were not extracted from the accessible summary.

**Why it matters:** Supports the feasibility of a self-selected 10-hour starter or fallback eating window before stricter 18:6, while preserving the no-control pilot limitation.

**Potential experiment signals:** eating-window duration, app logging adherence, body weight, waist circumference, systolic blood pressure, resting heart rate.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent starter-window evidence for app-supported 10-hour TRE; do not promote its weight or blood-pressure changes as strict 18:6 causal effects.
