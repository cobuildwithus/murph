This source is included for **Direct 18:6 and six-hour TRE evidence**.

## Findings
- **Insulin sensitivity, beta-cell responsiveness, blood pressure, oxidative stress, and appetite.**: The early 6-hour window improved insulin-related endpoints, blood pressure, oxidative-stress markers, and appetite measures despite a design intended to avoid weight loss.
- **Feasibility and lipid-safety boundary.**: The study is too small for safety reassurance and included practical difficulty with compressing all meals early in the day; a triglyceride-related signal was noted as needing further study.

## Why it matters
This foundational 6-hour early-window study shows cardiometabolic improvements even without weight loss, but only in a tiny, highly supervised male prediabetes cohort.

## Potential experiment signals
insulin sensitivity, beta-cell response, morning blood pressure, oxidative stress, evening appetite

## Protocol takeaway
Directly supports a possible insulin-sensitivity and blood-pressure rationale for early 18:6, while requiring strong caveats about sex, prediabetes, sample size, and supervision.
