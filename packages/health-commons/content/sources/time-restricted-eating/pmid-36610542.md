This source is included for **Reviews and evidence maps**.

**Findings:** The review found higher appetite at bedtime, lower or unchanged morning fasting appetite, and mixed evening appetite findings. Disordered-eating questionnaires generally did not differ across studies, but qualitative themes included fear of hunger, eating in the absence of hunger, and stressors that warrant monitoring.

**Why it matters:** The graded 18:6 protocol must manage hunger and avoid triggering rigid or distressed eating behaviors.

**Potential experiment signals:** hunger, appetite timing, disordered-eating questionnaires, qualitative eating behavior.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for behavioral safety and tolerability claims; do not infer metabolic efficacy.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
