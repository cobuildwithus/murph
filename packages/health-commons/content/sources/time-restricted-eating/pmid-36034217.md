This source is included for **Direct 18:6 and six-hour TRE evidence**.

## Findings
- **Body weight compared with ad libitum control.**: Both early and later 6-hour TRE produced comparable body-weight loss versus ad libitum control in overweight or obese young adults.
- **Blood pressure, mean glucose, fasting insulin, insulin resistance, leptin, thyroid-axis activity, sleep, and activity.**: The early 6-hour window showed broader metabolic changes, including reductions in systolic blood pressure, mean glucose, fasting insulin, insulin resistance, leptin, and thyroid-axis activity; the later window mainly reduced leptin, and sleep or physical activity did not clearly change.

## Why it matters
Directly tests 6-hour windows and separates early from later timing in a younger free-living population.

## Potential experiment signals
body weight, systolic blood pressure, mean glucose, fasting insulin, HOMA-IR, leptin, sleep quality

## Protocol takeaway
Both 6-hour windows supported weight loss versus control, while the early window had broader metabolic signals than the later window.
