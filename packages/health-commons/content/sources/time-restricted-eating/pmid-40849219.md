This source is included for **Reviews and evidence maps**.

**Findings:** In 19,831 adults over a median 8.1 years, eating duration <8 hours was associated with higher cardiovascular mortality (HR 2.35; 95% CI 1.39 to 3.98), but not with all-cause or cancer mortality. The authors state that further research is needed to determine whether the association is due to eating duration itself or residual confounding from contributing factors.

**Why it matters:** This is a safety-context signal that should not be converted into causality for or against 18:6 protocols.

**Potential experiment signals:** all-cause mortality, cardiovascular mortality, cancer mortality.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only observational safety signal; do not cite as causal evidence that 18:6 causes cardiovascular mortality.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
