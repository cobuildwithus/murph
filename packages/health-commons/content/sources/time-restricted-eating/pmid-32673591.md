This source is included for **Direct 18:6 and six-hour TRE evidence**.

## Findings
- **Body weight and energy intake.**: The 6-hour and 4-hour TRF arms produced similar weight loss of about 3% and reduced energy intake compared with control despite no calorie-counting prescription.
- **Insulin resistance and oxidative stress.**: Both short-window TRF arms improved insulin-resistance and oxidative-stress measures versus control in the extracted trial summary.
- **Protocol generalizability.**: This source is direct for a 6-hour window but not for a graded starter protocol, early-day timing, or non-obesity wellness users.

## Why it matters
One of the most central free-living trials with a direct 6-hour arm and no calorie counting.

## Potential experiment signals
body weight, energy intake, insulin resistance, oxidative stress, adherence

## Protocol takeaway
A 6-hour arm produced weight loss and cardiometabolic signals similar to a 4-hour arm, but the trial was in adults with obesity and used an afternoon/evening window.

## Claim use
`context-only`.
