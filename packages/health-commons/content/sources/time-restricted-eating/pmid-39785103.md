This source is included for **Reviews and evidence maps**.

**Findings:** The umbrella review reported no consensus or evidence-based guideline for Ramadan fasting during pregnancy.; The review noted lack of review-level evidence on rare but important outcomes including stillbirth, miscarriage, congenital anomalies, and neonatal death.; Limited or inconclusive pregnancy evidence should not be converted into reassurance for unsupervised TRE during pregnancy.

**Why it matters:** This is a pregnancy safety-boundary source; absence of clear harm in limited reviews is not evidence that unsupervised TRE is safe in pregnancy.

**Potential experiment signals:** pregnancy outcomes, birth outcomes, stillbirth, miscarriage, congenital anomalies, neonatal death, gestational diabetes, pre-eclampsia.

**Protocol takeaway:** Pregnancy should remain outside unsupervised wellness TRE protocol claims; fasting decisions require obstetric/clinical guidance.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
