This source is included for **Calorie restriction and diet-confounding**.

**Findings:**
- IHTG content decreased similarly in the TRE and daily-calorie-restriction groups over 12 months, and extracted summaries reported no additional benefits for reducing IHTG, body fat, or metabolic risk factors compared with calorie restriction alone.
- Because participants had NAFLD and both groups received calorie restriction, the source should not be generalized to healthy users or cited as a calorie-independent TRE effect.

**Why it matters:** The trial is a strong example of disease-specific calorie-restriction confounding: both arms lost liver fat under calorie restriction, but TRE did not add clear benefit.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- Intrahepatic triglyceride content
- Body weight
- Body fat
- Liver enzymes
- Metabolic risk factors
- Adverse events

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only NAFLD evidence showing no additional benefit of TRE over daily calorie restriction for liver fat or metabolic risk.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
