This source is included for **Reviews and evidence maps**.

**Findings:** Cochrane included 22 trials/1995 adults and found intermittent fasting did not show a clear clinically meaningful weight-loss advantage over regular dietary advice or no/waitlist control; interpretation should remain comparator- and certainty-bound. The accessible abstracted result reports very uncertain adverse-event evidence, including an adverse-event analysis of 7 studies/619 participants with RR 1.45 (95% CI 0.64 to 3.28).

**Why it matters:** This source helps prevent overclaiming: it is an intermittent-fasting umbrella review, not a direct 18:6 graded-window trial, and adverse-event certainty is limited.

**Potential experiment signals:** body weight, quality of life, adverse events, certainty of evidence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context for balanced claims about intermittent fasting/TRE rather than as direct efficacy proof for the 18:6 protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
