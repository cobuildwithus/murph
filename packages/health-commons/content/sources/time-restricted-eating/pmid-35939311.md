This source is included for **Direct 18:6 and six-hour TRE evidence**.

## Findings
- **Weight loss, fat loss, blood pressure, mood, sleep, and cardiometabolic measures.**: Early 8-hour TRE plus energy restriction produced greater weight loss than an extended-window energy-restriction comparator, while body-fat and many cardiometabolic or sleep endpoints did not show a clear additional advantage.
- **Adherence, continuation intent, and adverse events.**: The trial provides useful adherence and tolerability context, including generally mild adverse-event reporting and schedule-related withdrawals, but it is not direct 18:6 evidence.

## Why it matters
It is a comparatively larger early-TRE obesity trial, but the eating window was 8 hours and paired with energy restriction, so it is adjacent rather than a clean 18:6 test.

## Potential experiment signals
body weight, body fat, diastolic blood pressure, mood, sleep, adherence

## Protocol takeaway
Useful adjacent evidence that early TRE can add weight-loss benefit under calorie restriction; it should not be described as direct 18:6 evidence.
