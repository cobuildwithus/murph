# Eating Disorders: Recognition and Treatment
This source is included for **Guidelines and external safety context**.
## Findings
- `finding:pmid-28654225-01` — The NICE guideline provides a recognition-and-treatment framework for eating disorders; for TRE it functions as a contraindication/referral boundary rather than intervention evidence.
- `finding:pmid-28654225-02` — The guideline highlights pregnancy/antenatal and medical-risk management needs in eating-disorder care, supporting special caution before any fasting-window protocol.

## Why it matters
Any fasting protocol can be harmful in people with active or past eating-disorder symptoms. This source should shape contraindications and escalation, not efficacy claims.

## Potential experiment signals
- screening for restriction/binge/purge behaviors
- BMI or underweight risk
- electrolytes/ECG when clinically indicated
- pregnancy/antenatal risk
- referral status

## Protocol takeaway
Screen for current or past eating disorders, underweight status, rapid weight loss, purging, bingeing, compulsive restriction, pregnancy, and medical instability before offering 18:6.
