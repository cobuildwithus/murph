This source is included for **Graded windows and adjacent TRE variants**.

**Findings:**
- In the 6-month trial, TRE produced greater weight loss than control and was reported as more effective for weight loss than calorie restriction in extracted summaries; HbA1c fell in both TRE and calorie-restriction arms, with extracted summaries reporting similar HbA1c changes between TRE and calorie restriction.
- No serious adverse events were reported in extracted trial summaries, but diabetes medication management and hypoglycemia risk make this a safety-boundary source rather than a direct self-directed protocol claim.

**Why it matters:** This source speaks to spontaneous energy restriction and glycemic outcomes in a medication-relevant population, while also creating a safety boundary for diabetes users.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- Body weight
- HbA1c
- Glucose time in range
- Medication effect score
- Adverse events
- Hypoglycemia monitoring

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only evidence that 8-hour TRE without calorie counting can reduce weight and HbA1c in supervised adults with type 2 diabetes, but do not apply it directly to unsupervised 18:6 users on glucose-lowering medications.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
