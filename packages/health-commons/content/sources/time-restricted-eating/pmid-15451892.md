This source is included for **Diabetes, medication, and hypoglycemia safety**.

**Findings:** In EPIDIAR, severe hypoglycemia during Ramadan was reported to increase 4.7-fold in type 1 diabetes and 7.5-fold in type 2 diabetes, supporting diabetes as a fasting safety boundary.

**Why it matters:** It provides scale and severity context for why diabetes fasting cannot be treated as benign self-experimentation.

**Potential experiment signals:** severe hypoglycemia; severe hyperglycemia; hospitalization; medication changes.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only evidence that diabetes fasting is associated with severe event risk, especially without individualized planning.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
