This source is included for **Behavioral tolerability, eating behavior, and adherence**.

**Findings:** Participants described simplicity, versatility, a non-dieting or non-obsessive mindset, and a supportive environment as important facilitators of continued TRE. Barriers included hunger or food cravings, initially obsessive thinking, conflicting schedules, social eating, and holidays; coping strategies and flexibility were central to maintaining TRE.

**Why it matters:** This recent qualitative source gives practical adherence strategies and pitfalls beyond clinical-trial protocols.

**Potential experiment signals:** adherence, barriers, facilitators, hunger, food cravings, social eating, holidays, mindset, coping strategies.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent behavioral evidence: simplicity and flexibility can support adherence, but hunger/cravings, obsessive thinking, conflicting schedules, and social eating can require coping strategies and off-ramps.
