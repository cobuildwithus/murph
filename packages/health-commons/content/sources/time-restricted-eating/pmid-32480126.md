This source is included for **Reviews and evidence maps**.

**Findings:** The review describes TRE as consolidating all calorie intake into about a 6- to 10-hour period during the active phase. The review discusses circadian and metabolic mechanisms alongside practical challenges in translating TRE into sustainable human interventions.

**Why it matters:** This review is useful for explaining why meal timing may matter and why implementation challenges can affect adherence.

**Population mismatch / directness:** Broad mechanisms and translation; no direct 18:6 graded starter-window outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:** circadian alignment, glucose tolerance, body weight, lipids and blood pressure, implementation barriers.

**Protocol takeaway:** Mechanistic plausibility supports testing TRE, but translational challenges mean the protocol should prioritize gradual adoption and monitoring.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
