This source is included for **Graded windows and adjacent TRE variants**.

**Findings:**
- All 41 randomized participants completed the study. At 12 weeks, weight decreased by 2.3 kg in TRE and 2.6 kg in the usual-eating-pattern group; the average difference was 0.3 kg (95% CI, -1.2 to 1.9 kg). Glycemic measures did not differ between groups.
- The small, single-site controlled-feeding design improves calorie control but limits generalizability and does not test spontaneous energy reduction in free-living 18:6 users.

**Why it matters:** The trial directly separates timing from calorie intake and therefore constrains claims that TRE itself causes weight loss independent of reduced energy intake.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- Body weight
- Fasting glucose
- HbA1c
- Continuous glucose metrics
- Calorie stability
- Eating-window timing

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only evidence that, under stable calorie intake, TRE did not decrease weight or improve glucose homeostasis relative to a usual eating pattern.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
