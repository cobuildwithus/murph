This source is included for **Mental health, stress, mood, and wellbeing context**.

**Findings:** Exploratory study of 80 adolescents reported short-term improvements across POMS mood domains after a single half-day outdoor CWI group intervention; most participants enjoyed the experience. There was no control group and the adolescent supervised course context differs from adult cold plunge.

**Why it matters:** Direct mood measurement after outdoor CWI but with major age, supervision, and design boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** POMS mood state, total mood disturbance, enjoyment, acceptability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adolescent adjacent mood context only; not applicable to adult cold-plunge efficacy claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
