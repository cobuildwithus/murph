This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** This systematic review included 17 studies with 366 total swimmers and mapped hypothesized mechanisms of swimming-related death, including cardiac and temperature-related pathways. It provides safety context for cold-water exposure but is not efficacy or direct cold-plunge evidence.

**Why it matters:** Provides mechanism context for why cardiac, temperature, and drowning risks need separation from benefit claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** arrhythmia symptoms, drowning/near-miss events, hypothermia symptoms, exercise-water exposure interaction.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for safety landscape and mechanism context; do not turn swimming-fatality mechanisms into causal claims about controlled plunges.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction notes

- Directness to Cold Plunge: `same_mechanism`.
- Population mismatch: Swimming populations and fatality mechanisms are not equivalent to stationary tub plunging.
- Limitations: Swimming-related death mechanisms are adjacent to controlled plunge safety.; Mechanistic and observational evidence cannot estimate routine cold-plunge risk.; Exercise/swimming, water exposure, and cold may be confounded.
