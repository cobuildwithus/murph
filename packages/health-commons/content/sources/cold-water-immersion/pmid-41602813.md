This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** The study reported increased heart rate and respiratory rate during cold-water immersion, peaking around 30 seconds and normalizing after about 60 seconds. All participants completed the protocol and no symptoms or adverse events were reported, but the small supervised pediatric sample limits generalization.

**Why it matters:** Provides pediatric cold-shock safety context and supports separating minors from adult protocols.

**Potential experiment signals:** heart rate; respiratory rate; adverse events; age group.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only pediatric context; do not extrapolate to adult benefit claims.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy children under medical-style supervision differ from adult cold-plunge users and from unsupervised minors.

**Limitations:** Very small pediatric sample.; One-minute supervised exposure only.; Healthy children; not general pediatric or adult populations.
