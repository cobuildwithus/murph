This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** This source highlights that cold-water immersion can be associated with sudden death as well as unusual prolonged-survival rescue scenarios. It is safety context for acute cold-water hazards, not deliberate cold-plunge efficacy evidence.

**Why it matters:** Useful historical safety context for why cold-water exposure requires early-risk and rescue framing.

**Potential experiment signals:** collapse/syncope, cold shock, submersion risk, hypothermia symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for safety-only context around sudden deterioration and rescue planning.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
