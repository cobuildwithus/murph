This source is included for **Mental health, stress, mood, and wellbeing context**.

**Findings:** This scoping review identified 14 studies on mental health and wellbeing benefits of open-water swimming. It maps a heterogeneous evidence base where cold exposure, swimming, social participation, and nature/blue-space context are usually bundled.

**Why it matters:** Provides a landscape map for mental health/wellbeing claims and helps prevent over-promoting open-water swimming data as direct plunge evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** wellbeing, mood, anxiety, depression, social connectedness.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to frame the broader swimming-in-nature landscape and evidence gaps, not as a direct cold-plunge claim source.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
