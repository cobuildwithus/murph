## Extraction summary

This source is included for **Immune, inflammation, and illness context**.

**Findings:** The review frames voluntary cold-water health effects as debated, with possible beneficial signals but substantial uncertainty from heterogeneous studies and self-selected populations.; Healthy-user selection, variable exposure dose, and mixed modalities limit direct transfer from the reviewed literature to a standardized cold-plunge protocol.

**Why it matters:** It is a source-level reminder that many popular cold-water claims are still debated and confounded by healthy-user selection and heterogeneous protocols.

**Potential experiment signals:** immune function; insulin sensitivity; adipose tissue; cardiometabolic markers; safety outcomes.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as broad context and caveat support; do not use as direct proof of cold-plunge benefits.
