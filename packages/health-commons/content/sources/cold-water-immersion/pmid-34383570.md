This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** The case report describes three runners who developed symptomatic mild hypothermia requiring rewarming after cold-water immersion treatment for exertional heat illness.

**Why it matters:** Concrete adverse-event example for overcooling and rewarming monitoring.

**Potential experiment signals:** core/body temperature; shivering; symptoms after immersion; rewarming requirement.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only evidence for overcooling risk and post-immersion monitoring.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Medical heat-illness cooling context differs from healthy voluntary cold plunge.

**Limitations:** Case report/series; no denominator or incidence estimate.; Exertional heat illness treatment population differs from wellness cold plunge.; Does not estimate routine cold-plunge adverse-event rates.
