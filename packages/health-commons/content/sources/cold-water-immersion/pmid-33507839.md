This source is included for **Sleep, HRV, and recovery context**.

**Findings:** The study did not retain a beneficial effect of CWI on circulating inflammatory recovery markers after Ironman racing.; A neutrophil signal was retained in extraction, so immune-marker responses should not be oversimplified as anti-inflammatory benefit.

**Why it matters:** Adds a high-stress endurance-recovery null/safety-context source and prevents broad anti-inflammatory claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:inflammation, biomarker:immune_cell_count, self_report:recovery.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not claim CWI reliably improves systemic inflammation based on this source.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
