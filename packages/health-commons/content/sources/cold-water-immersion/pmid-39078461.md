- **Participant count:** Not clearly reported in the retrieved metadata.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Broad cold-water-therapy literature spanning mixed modalities and study designs.
- **Comparator or control:** Mixed or study-specific across the underlying literature.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Literature search up to July 2024
- **Endpoints:** Healthy-aging and healthspan-relevant physiology, mental well-being, cardiovascular, metabolic, and immune outcomes.

- **Limitations:** Narrative review over heterogeneous modalities and populations; cannot stand in for direct protocol efficacy evidence.
