This source is included for **Direct cold-plunge intervention evidence**.

**Findings:** Repeated 15 °C immersions reduced respiratory and heart-rate responses to subsequent cold-water exposure, indicating partial habituation. The source supports habituation of components of cold shock, not elimination of cold-water risk.

**Why it matters:** Key direct source for habituation of cold-shock responses.

**Potential experiment signals:** cold-shock response; ventilation; respiratory frequency; heart rate; habituation; temperature dependence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to discuss possible habituation of initial responses, while avoiding claims of complete safety or broad adaptation.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy male lab participants; not general consumer plungers.

**Limitations:** Healthy male volunteers only.; Brief lab immersions.; Safety/habituation physiology, not health efficacy.
