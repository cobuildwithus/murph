This source is included for **Direct cold-plunge intervention evidence**.

**Findings:** The observational study reported improvements in several vascular, inflammation, lipid, and fat-accumulation markers after repeated CWI, while controls had no significant changes. Because this was an observational pilot with seasonal/environmental variation and male low-risk volunteers, the results cannot establish causal cold-plunge benefit.

**Why it matters:** Potential long-term cardiometabolic signal, but observational design prevents causal protocol claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** carotid intima-media thickness; augmentation index; pulse wave velocity; PCSK9; hsCRP; lipids; liver fat; subcutaneous fat.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as observational cardiometabolic context only; do not claim repeated plunges reduce atherosclerosis or liver fat causally.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Male low cardiovascular-risk volunteers in a supervised seasonal outdoor context; not general home cold-plunge users.

**Limitations:** Observational pilot rather than randomized trial.; Male low-risk volunteer sample.; Seasonal/environmental variation.; Completer attrition and small sample.
