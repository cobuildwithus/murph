This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** The WMS drowning guideline update reviews drowning prevention, rescue, resuscitation, and disposition in out-of-hospital/emergency settings. It supports cold-plunge water-safety planning but does not provide cold-plunge efficacy evidence.

**Why it matters:** Cold plunge protocols need a credible water-safety and rescue boundary, especially around submersion and unsupervised use.

**Potential experiment signals:** drowning/near-miss, rescue plan, loss of consciousness, oxygenation, post-immersion symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for drowning-prevention and emergency-response planning; do not use as efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
