This source is included for **Adjacent physical-performance time-course review**.

**Findings:** Sixty-eight studies were included. CWI improved short-term recovery of endurance performance at 1 h, but impaired sprint performance at 1 h and jump performance at 6 h. Accessible summaries also reported longer-term improvements in jump performance (24 h and 96 h), strength (24 h), CK (24-72 h), soreness (1-72 h), and perceived recovery (72 h), with effects depending on preceding exercise type and environmental conditions.

**Why it matters:** This review is useful because it prevents oversimplification: post-exercise CWI can help some later recovery windows while impairing some immediate performance outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:** sprint, jump, endurance, strength, CK, soreness

**Protocol takeaway:** Treat as adjacent sports-recovery context with mixed time-course effects, not as straightforward support for a general plunge protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
