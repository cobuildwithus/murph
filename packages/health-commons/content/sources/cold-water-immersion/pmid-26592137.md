This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** In 17 healthy participants, short-term cold-water immersion around 12°C was used to study cardiorespiratory, cerebral blood-flow, thermal perception, and dyspnea responses relevant to float-first safety behavior. This is survival-context evidence, not a cold-plunge benefit claim.

**Why it matters:** Cold-plunge pages need to avoid importing open-water survival behavior as benefit evidence while preserving drowning-risk education.

**Potential experiment signals:** dyspnea, panic/gasping, respiratory rate, swim failure risk, cerebral blood-flow proxy.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for cold-shock and open-water safety education, especially not swimming immediately after accidental immersion.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
