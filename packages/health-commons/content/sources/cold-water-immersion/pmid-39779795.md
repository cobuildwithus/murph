This source is included for **Direct cold-plunge intervention evidence**.

**Findings:** Noradrenaline increased after both morning and evening ice baths, while adrenaline and cortisol were not significantly changed by the ice-bath exposure in accessible results. Morning ice bath exposure increased fatty acids relative to extracted comparator patterns, while most hormonal/lipidome responses were similar across time-of-day.

**Why it matters:** Direct 5-minute ice-bath source with time-of-day and catecholamine/lipidome endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** noradrenaline; adrenaline; cortisol; blood lipidome; fatty acids; sex differences.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports acute noradrenaline/lipidome mechanism claims, not broad efficacy or time-of-day prescriptions.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy young adults in supervised lab conditions; not high-risk users.

**Limitations:** Small n=12 sample.; Menstrual-cycle control limitations were noted.; Mechanistic biomarkers rather than health outcomes.
