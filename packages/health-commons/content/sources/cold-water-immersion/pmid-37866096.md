This source is included for **Direct cold-plunge dose and habituation evidence**.

**Findings:** The source reported lower negative mood and cortisol at 180 minutes after immersion, while positive mood and beta-endorphins did not clearly change. The source reported minimal peripheral shear-stress change during immersion and reduced shear after immersion, making cardiovascular interpretation mixed rather than clearly beneficial.

**Why it matters:** This is a direct 10 °C, 15-minute CWI source with both cardiovascular and affective endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** mood; negative affect; cortisol; beta-endorphin; peripheral shear stress; cardiovascular response.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports cautious acute mood/stress-signal discussion, not broad or durable benefit claims.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy young adults under supervised lab conditions; not clinical, older, or unsupervised users.

**Limitations:** Small acute healthy-adult sample.; No long-term follow-up.; Mood signal was mixed: negative mood/cortisol changed, positive mood and beta-endorphins did not.
