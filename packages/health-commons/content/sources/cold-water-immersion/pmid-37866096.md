This source is included for **Direct acute healthy-adult mood and cardiovascular response**.

**Findings:** The clearest favorable signal was a delayed reduction in negative affect and cortisol about 3 hours after immersion. The study does not support a strong immediate positive-affect or broad peripheral vascular benefit claim.

**Why it matters:** One of the most protocol-like acute cold-plunge lab studies in healthy adults.

**Potential experiment signals:** negative affect, cortisol, heart rate, vascular function

**Protocol takeaway:** A single plunge may feel subjectively better later rather than immediately, and broad cardiovascular benefit should not be assumed from this study alone.
