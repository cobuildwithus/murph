This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** Thirty-two volunteers completed two 2.5-minute 11°C head-out immersions in a breath-hold performance experiment. The source is relevant to cold-shock and breath-hold safety boundaries, not to claims that cold plunges improve health outcomes.

**Why it matters:** Helps separate cold-water performance/breath-hold research from safe protocol behavior.

**Potential experiment signals:** breath-hold duration, respiratory rate, dyspnea, panic/gasping.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only to support breath-hold caution and cold-shock education; do not encourage breath holding as part of cold plunging.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
