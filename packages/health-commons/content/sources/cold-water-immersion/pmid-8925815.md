This source is included for **Direct cold-plunge intervention evidence**.

**Findings:** The accessible abstract summary reported minimal immune-system effect after a single 14 °C, 1-hour immersion. Repeated exposures produced small immune-marker increases, but the biological significance was unclear.

**Why it matters:** Foundational immune-marker source that preserves the “biological significance unclear” boundary.

**Potential experiment signals:** monocytes; lymphocytes; CD25; TNF-alpha; IL-6; acute phase proteins; immune activation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only for immune-marker context; do not claim immune-system enhancement or illness prevention.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Cold-adapted/athletic participants and long exposures do not match typical novice cold-plunge users.

**Limitations:** Older small study; participant count not retained in accessible extracted text.; Biomarker outcomes only.; Long exposures and athletic/cold-adapted population mismatch.
