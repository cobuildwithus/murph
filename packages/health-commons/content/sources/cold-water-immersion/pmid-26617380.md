This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** This guideline emphasizes that cold-exposure injury risk is shaped by preparedness, preexisting medical conditions, and individual physiologic response. It supports safety screening for cold-plunge protocols rather than efficacy claims.

**Why it matters:** Supports pre-participation screening and preparedness language for cold-plunge experiments.

**Potential experiment signals:** medical history flags, cold injury symptoms, medication/comorbidity screen, preparedness.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for screening, risk-factor review, and referral language before cold exposure.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
