This source is included for **Adjacent muscle soreness dose-response review**.

**Findings:** Nine studies suggested CWI was slightly better than passive recovery for muscle soreness immediately (MD 0.290, 95% CI 0.037 to 0.543) and at delayed follow-up (MD 0.315, 95% CI 0.048 to 0.581). Dose-response analyses suggested the best effects around 11-15°C and 11-15 minutes.

**Why it matters:** This review is often cited for athlete-recovery dose windows; the key boundary is that it speaks to post-exercise soreness, not general cold-plunge aims.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle soreness, temperature, immersion duration

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep as soreness-specific adjacent dose context only; do not import its suggested athlete-recovery window as a direct cold-plunge protocol prescription.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
