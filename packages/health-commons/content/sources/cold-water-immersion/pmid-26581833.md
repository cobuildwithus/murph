This source is included for **Sports recovery and training-adaptation boundary**.

**Findings:** The available evidence suggested CWI was slightly better than passive recovery for soreness, with 11–15 °C and 11–15 minutes appearing most favorable in the review; effect sizes were small and evidence quality was limited.

**Why it matters:** This is a dose-boundary source: it can inform why sport-recovery dosing is often 10–15 minutes around 10–15 °C, but not prove general health benefits.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle soreness, perceived recovery, immersion temperature, immersion duration.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent dose context for soreness only; avoid converting sport-recovery dose findings into direct protocol efficacy claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
