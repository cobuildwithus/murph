This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** ECG monitoring during helicopter underwater escape training reported arrhythmias across training runs, mostly after submersion, and attenuation of heart-rate response by later runs in the available summary. This is submersion-training safety context, not cold-plunge efficacy evidence.

**Why it matters:** Supports boundaries around submersion, panic/escape stress, and arrhythmia monitoring in cold-water contexts.

**Potential experiment signals:** palpitations, ECG rhythm, panic/escape stress, fitness context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only to discourage submersion/escape-style challenges and to flag arrhythmia risk.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
