This source is included for **Sports recovery and training-adaptation boundary**.

**Findings:** Accessible text reports that CWI enhanced recovery of submaximal muscle function while reducing soreness, swelling, myoglobin, and muscle temperature compared with active recovery.; The same accessible text reports no alteration in recovery of maximal strength or countermovement jump performance, limiting broad performance claims.

**Why it matters:** It captures the tension between acute recovery benefits and longer-term adaptation concerns.

**Potential experiment signals:** submaximal muscle function; maximal strength; countermovement jump; muscle soreness; swelling; myoglobin; muscle temperature.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent recovery evidence, with explicit separation from hypertrophy/adaptation outcomes.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Post-resistance-exercise recovery participants, not resting wellness users.

**Limitations:** Acute resistance-exercise recovery only.; Sample size not extracted here.; Acute functional recovery does not establish long-term adaptation benefit.
