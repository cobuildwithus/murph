This source is included for **Resistance-exercise recovery trial**.



## Findings

In a randomized crossover design, 10 physically active men completed high-intensity resistance exercise followed by either 10 minutes of 10°C cold-water immersion or 10 minutes of low-intensity cycling recovery.

Cold-water immersion did not improve maximal muscle function at 2 or 4 hours, but during a 6-hour submaximal squat test participants completed greater total load in the final three sets after cold-water immersion than after active recovery. The study also reported lower plasma myoglobin, higher IL-6, and sustained reductions in intramuscular temperature after immersion.

These mixed findings matter for protocol boundaries: the study supports some task-specific recovery effects, but it does not support a broad claim that cold plunges universally restore strength or performance.



## Why it matters

Useful for preserving mixed recovery findings. It shows why some cold-water immersion benefits appear task-specific and do not automatically extend to all performance or recovery outcomes.
