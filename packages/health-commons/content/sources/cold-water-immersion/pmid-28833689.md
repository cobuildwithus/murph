This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** This review treats cold-water immersion as both a possible treatment context and a hazard. It identifies cold shock, drowning, cardiac arrest/arrhythmia risk, and hypothermia as core safety boundaries and does not provide direct evidence that consumer cold plunges improve health outcomes.

**Why it matters:** This is a backbone safety and physiology review for keeping cold shock, drowning, cardiac, and hypothermia risks explicit in the cold-plunge page.

**Potential experiment signals:** cold-shock symptoms, heart rate, blood pressure, arrhythmia symptoms, core temperature symptoms, drowning/near-miss events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for safety framing, screening, supervision, and stop-rule language; do not cite it as proof that cold plunges improve health outcomes.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction notes

- Directness to Cold Plunge: `same_mechanism`.
- Population mismatch: Mixed accidental, recreational, and therapeutic populations; not a single controlled home-plunge population.
- Limitations: Narrative review rather than a controlled trial.; Covers many cold-water contexts, not a standardized consumer cold-plunge protocol.; Should not be used as isolated efficacy evidence.
