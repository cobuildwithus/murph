This source is included for **Direct affect and brain-network study**.

**Findings:** A brief mild-cold immersion shifted affect in a favorable direction immediately after exposure. The mood shift was accompanied by altered large-scale brain-network interaction, giving a plausible mechanistic bridge for affect changes.

**Why it matters:** Pairs subjective mood change with a plausible brain-network mechanism after immersion.

**Potential experiment signals:** positive affect, negative affect, alertness, mechanism map

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports affect-related hypotheses, but temperature and duration were milder than many deliberate cold plunges.
