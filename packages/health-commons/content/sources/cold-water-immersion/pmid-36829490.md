This source is included for **Direct cold-plunge dose and habituation evidence**.

**Findings:** The source reported increased positive affect and increased interaction among large-scale brain networks after short head-out whole-body CWI; because the exposure was warmer than many cold-plunge protocols, this remains mechanistic context. The study is directly water-immersion based but temperature and acute neuroimaging endpoints limit transfer to ≤15 °C cold-plunge health claims.

**Why it matters:** A directly relevant head-out immersion neuroaffective study, but its warmer exposure and mechanistic endpoints prevent it from carrying protocol efficacy claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** positive affect; negative affect; brain connectivity; fMRI network interaction.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as affect/neuroimaging context; do not use as direct evidence that colder plunges improve mood or health.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy adult laboratory sample; not older adults, clinical populations, cardiovascular-risk populations, or unsupervised home users.

**Limitations:** Warmer-water exposure than many cold-plunge protocols.; Small acute pre-post design; no long-term outcomes extracted.; Participant count not confirmed from accessible extracted text.
