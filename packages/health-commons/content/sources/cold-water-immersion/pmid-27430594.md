This source is included for **Sports recovery and training-adaptation boundary**.

**Findings:** The trial compared 9 °C and 14 °C post-exercise CWI against control; extracted records indicate earlier soreness/recovery effects for some outcomes and a delayed MVIC signal with 14 °C, but effects were dose- and endpoint-specific rather than uniformly superior.

**Why it matters:** This source helps bound dose claims and shows that different recovery endpoints may respond differently to water temperature.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle soreness, creatine kinase, maximal voluntary contraction, perceived recovery.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent dose-response context for post-exercise recovery; avoid broad protocol claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
