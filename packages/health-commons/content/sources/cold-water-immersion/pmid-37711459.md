This source is included for **Direct cold-plunge dose and habituation evidence**.

**Findings:** The exploratory study found leukocyte count decreased in both CWI and control groups, with CWI reducing neutrophil number/proportion but no other subtype changes; authors concluded no relevant leukocyte-count effect. MAP and HR decreased in the CWI group, but the authors cautioned that interpretation was unclear because of design and measurement limitations.

**Why it matters:** Direct repeated-CWI study preserving null/mixed immune and cautious cardiovascular findings.

**Potential experiment signals:** leukocyte count; neutrophils; monocytes; lymphocytes; mean arterial pressure; heart rate.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mixed repeated-dose evidence: no relevant leukocyte-count benefit and unclear cardiovascular signal.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Population mismatch:** Non-cold-adapted men only; arms-out sternum-level immersion differs from some plunges.

**Limitations:** Small exploratory male-only study.; Control group was free-living and not thermoneutral bath matched.; Baseline MAP issue and BP measured once.; Leukocyte counts are not immune-function measures.
