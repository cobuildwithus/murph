This source is included for **Direct repeated-exposure healthy-adult study**.

**Findings:** The clearest positive physiological signal in this study was lower resting cardiovascular measures after repeated exposure. The study does not support a broad repeated-immersion immune-enhancement claim.

**Why it matters:** One of the few repeated cold-plunge-like studies in healthy non-athlete adults.

**Potential experiment signals:** resting heart rate, blood pressure, immune markers

**Protocol takeaway:** Repeated use may change resting cardiovascular measures, but immune adaptation claims should stay cautious.
