This source is included for **Direct cold-plunge dose and habituation evidence**.

**Findings:** Repeated skin cooling habituated early ventilatory cold-shock responses, supporting a safety-habituation mechanism. Metabolic habituation required repeated skin and deep-body temperature falls, indicating tissue-temperature specificity and limiting simple adaptation claims.

**Why it matters:** Backbone habituation paper separating ventilatory from metabolic adaptation.

**Potential experiment signals:** metabolic response; ventilatory response; cold-shock habituation; deep-body temperature; skin temperature.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for habituation mechanisms and safety boundaries, not health benefit claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Male lab participants and prolonged exposures do not represent routine short home plunges.

**Limitations:** Male sample.; Long/severe immersion conditions.; Physiological habituation, not wellness outcomes.
