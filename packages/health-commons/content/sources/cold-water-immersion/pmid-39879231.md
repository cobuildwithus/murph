This source is included for **Sleep, HRV, and recovery context**.

**Findings:** The meta-analysis reported acute increases in inflammation immediately and at 1 hour, while stress reduction was reported at 12 hours only, showing time-dependent and mixed effects. The review did not establish clear immediate/1-hour immune advantage and emphasized limited, heterogeneous, small, and non-diverse evidence; one illness-related trial was a cold-shower study rather than a plunge.

**Why it matters:** Best broad recall scaffold for health and wellbeing claims, but it preserves mixed and time-dependent results.

**Potential experiment signals:** inflammation; stress; immunity; sleep quality; quality of life; wellbeing.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to bound the evidence base and identify mixed/null outcomes; do not convert pooled mechanisms into direct protocol promises.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Review mixes modalities and populations; not all included studies map to healthy-adult cold-plunge use.

**Limitations:** Only 11 included studies.; Small and non-diverse samples; many male participants.; Heterogeneous modalities and dose; one cold-shower trial is not a tub/plunge protocol.; Post-exercise and wellness contexts require separation.
