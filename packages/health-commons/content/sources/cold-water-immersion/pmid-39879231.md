This source is included for **Direct protocol review in healthy adults**.

**Findings:** The clearest pooled signal was delayed stress reduction around 12 hours after immersion rather than an immediate effect. The review did not support an immediate immune-function benefit after cold-water immersion.

**Why it matters:** Best current high-level review for deliberate healthy-adult cold-water immersion.

**Potential experiment signals:** perceived stress, sleep quality, quality of life, immune markers

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as the backbone source for mixed/null claim calibration, not for a blanket benefit claim.
