This source is included for **Depression-adjacent supervised open-water study**.

## Findings
- **Source kind / design:** Open-label feasibility study (journal_article).
- **Population:** Adults aged 20-69 with diagnosed depression from an outpatient clinic.
- **Participant count:** 13 (reported).
- **Intervention or exposure:** Twice-weekly, group-based, supervised cold-water swimming as an add-on treatment.
- **Comparator or control:** No control arm in the retrieved feasibility description.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Twice-weekly supervised group cold-water swimming program
- **Endpoints:** Feasibility, regular participation, and safety/tolerability in a depression clinic population.
- **Effect estimate or direction:** The retrieved summary says regular supervised participation was possible and safe; 13 participants were recruited and only 5 participated regularly.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** The main usable signal is feasibility/safety in a supervised clinical context rather than efficacy.
- **Limitations:** Tiny open-label uncontrolled sample in a clinical population; not generalizable to the default Murph self-experiment population.
- **Population mismatch:** This source does not cleanly match a default self-directed deliberate cold-plunge protocol.
- **Directness to Cold Plunge:** clinical_supervised.

## Why it matters
Preserves clinical feasibility context without overstating efficacy or generalizing to the default protocol population.
