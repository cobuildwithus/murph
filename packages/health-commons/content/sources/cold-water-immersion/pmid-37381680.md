This source is included for **Sleep, HRV, and recovery context**.

**Findings:** The study reported that supervised cold-water swimming was feasible and safe for evaluated participants, with favorable well-being/sleep-quality signals but limited causal certainty.; The source should not be used to claim that unsupervised cold plunge treats depression.

**Why it matters:** Provides clinical-supervised mental-health context and safety feasibility, while separating swimming/group care from cold-plunge self-experimentation.

**Potential experiment signals:** self_report:depression, self_report:wellbeing, self_report:sleep_quality, safety:adverse_events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as supervised clinical cold-water swimming context; do not claim cold plunge treats depression.
