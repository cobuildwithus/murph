This source is included for **Sports recovery and training-adaptation boundary**.

**Findings:** The review found massage and cold exposure among stronger options for inflammation markers and recommended CWI and massage for perceptual recovery up to 72 hours, while emphasizing the need for higher-quality evidence for physical and physiological recovery.

**Why it matters:** This source helps compare CWI with other recovery modalities and prevents treating CWI as uniquely proven for all recovery domains.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle soreness, fatigue, inflammation markers, perceived recovery.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent comparative recovery context; subjective recovery evidence is stronger than broad objective recovery evidence.
