This source is included for **Cardiovascular/autonomic physiology context**.

**Findings:** The study reported sex differences in cognitive performance and autonomic functions during cold-air exposure and cold-water partial immersion. Because the protocol mixed cold-air and partial-immersion conditions with cognitive tasks, it should be used as context rather than direct cold-plunge evidence.

**Why it matters:** Supports tracking sex and cognitive/autonomic context rather than assuming one-size-fits-all responses.

**Potential experiment signals:** heart-rate variability; electrodermal activity; cognitive task performance; sex; cold-air exposure context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only evidence for sex/autonomic/cognitive modifiers.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Partial immersion plus cold-air operational tasks differs from short head-out cold plunge.

**Limitations:** Mixed cold-air and cold-water partial-immersion protocol.; Operational/cognitive setting differs from wellness cold plunge.; Context-only; no long-term health outcome.
