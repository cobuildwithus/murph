## Extraction summary

This source is included for **Immune, inflammation, and illness context**.

**Findings:** The study reported increased antibodies and cytokine markers including IL-2 and IL-4 after regular cold shower exposure compared with hot shower exposure.; Because outcomes were immune biomarkers in a cold-shower protocol, this source should not be used as direct evidence that cold plunges reduce infections or improve clinical immunity.

**Why it matters:** It adds newer randomized immune-marker evidence while preserving the boundary between cold showers, biomarkers, and clinical illness outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:** immunoglobulins; IL-2; IL-4; interferon-gamma; cytokines; infection outcomes.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent biomarker context only; do not claim cold plunges reduce infection or improve immune function clinically from this source alone.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
