This source is included for **Breathwork-plus-cold multi-component review**.

## Findings
- **Source kind / design:** Systematic review (review).
- **Population:** Healthy and non-healthy participants across Wim Hof Method studies.
- **Participant count:** Not clearly reported in the retrieved metadata.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Wim Hof Method interventions across included studies, combining breathing with cold exposure and sometimes meditation/mindset components.
- **Comparator or control:** Varied by included trial.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Literature search covering studies published from 2014 to July 2022
- **Endpoints:** Inflammatory markers, catecholamine response, exercise performance, and psychological outcomes.
- **Effect estimate or direction:** The review found promising anti-inflammatory signals, including epinephrine/IL-10 related changes, while exercise-performance findings were mixed.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** The review was not a protocol-specific safety study for cold plunge.
- **Limitations:** Cold exposure was bundled with other components, so effects cannot be attributed to cold plunge alone.
- **Population mismatch:** This source does not cleanly match a default self-directed deliberate cold-plunge protocol.
- **Directness to Cold Plunge:** adjacent_variant.

## Why it matters
Helps separate cold-plunge claims from combined-method claims that users may cite from outside the protocol page.
