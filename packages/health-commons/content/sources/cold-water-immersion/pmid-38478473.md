This source is included for **Immune, inflammation, and illness context**.

**Findings:** The systematic review found promising inflammatory-response signals, including higher epinephrine and IL-10 with lower pro-inflammatory cytokines, but emphasized caution and mixed findings for other endpoints.; Because WHM combines multiple components and includes breathing-only variants, the review should not be used as cold-plunge-only efficacy evidence.
