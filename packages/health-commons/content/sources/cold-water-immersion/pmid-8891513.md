This source is included for **Direct cold-plunge intervention evidence**.

**Findings:** Single 14 °C immersion increased sympathetic activity and noradrenaline, with peripheral vasoconstriction and small blood-pressure increase but no significant heart-rate change in the accessible abstract summary. Repeated exposure did not clearly change sympathetic reactivity, hormone response, or cardiovascular parameters, preserving a null/mixed adaptation boundary.

**Why it matters:** Foundational sympathetic/cardiovascular physiology source, but not a wellness outcome source.

**Potential experiment signals:** sympathetic activity; noradrenaline; adrenaline; dopamine; renin-angiotensin activity; heart rate; blood pressure; peripheral vasoconstriction.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for mechanism and safety boundary; avoid efficacy claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy athletic men; not general or high-risk users.

**Limitations:** Older small study; participant count not retained in accessible extracted text.; Healthy athletic men only.; Long 1-hour exposure exceeds typical wellness plunge dosing.
