This source is included for **Mental health, stress, mood, and wellbeing context**.

**Findings:** Single-arm unblinded feasibility study recruited 61 participants; 53 were included in analysis. Attendance was high, and large within-person effects were reported for depression and anxiety from baseline to end of course and at 3-month follow-up, but the uncontrolled design means regression, expectancy, social, exercise, and outdoor effects cannot be separated.

**Why it matters:** Important boundary source for depression/anxiety claims because it is promising but uncontrolled and sea-swimming specific.

**Potential experiment signals:** depression symptoms, anxiety symptoms, functioning, attendance, acceptability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent clinical feasibility context only; do not claim cold plunge treats depression or anxiety from this source.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
