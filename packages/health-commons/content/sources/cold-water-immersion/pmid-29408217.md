This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** The study supports autonomic conflict as a pro-arrhythmic mechanism in long-QT-associated ventricular arrhythmia contexts.

**Why it matters:** Adds mechanistic support to arrhythmia-related screening and contraindication language.

**Potential experiment signals:** known arrhythmia history; palpitations; heart-rate response; medication/long-QT risk context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only mechanism evidence for arrhythmia-vulnerable users.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Long-QT mechanistic arrhythmia model differs from healthy adult cold-plunge users.

**Limitations:** Mechanistic model, not a human cold-plunge trial.; Long-QT susceptibility context may not apply to healthy screened adults.; Does not quantify cold-plunge event rates.
