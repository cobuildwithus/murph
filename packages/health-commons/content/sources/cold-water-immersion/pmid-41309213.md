This source is included for **Mental health, stress, mood, and wellbeing context**.

**Findings:** Pilot RCT randomized 141 female adults to four 3-week resilience interventions. All groups reduced depressive symptoms and perceived stress at post-intervention without clear between-group differences; as-treated analyses suggested WHM-style fast breathing plus cold showers maintained reduced depression at 3 months and had greater positive affect versus control, requiring replication.

**Why it matters:** Recent controlled resilience source showing mixed/no-clear-advantage main results and preserving breathwork/cold-shower bundling.

**Potential experiment signals:** depressive symptoms, perceived stress, positive affect, cortisol reactivity.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mixed adjacent bundled-intervention evidence; do not claim cold plunge improves depression/stress from this source.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
