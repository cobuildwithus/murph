This source is included for **Adjacent and measurement context**.

**Findings:** The systematic review abstract reports 386 records identified and eight studies included; effects varied across studies, with some positive effects and some no effect, heterogeneity preventing meta-analysis, and the authors concluding that shorter/moderate immersions may be more effective while cautioning that protocols should be tailored. The abstract notes that temporary neural responses may cause short-term instability, making this source relevant to post-immersion fall-risk and timing boundaries even though evidence is mixed.

**Why it matters:** Provides adjacent postural-control and fall-risk context, preserving mixed findings and the authors’ caution around heterogeneity and dose tailoring.

**Potential experiment signals:** balance; postural control; fall risk; time after immersion; immersion temperature; immersion duration.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent mixed evidence for balance/postural-control monitoring and safety timing after cold plunge, not as a direct wellness benefit claim.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Young healthy participants in experimental/recovery contexts may not generalize to older, clinical, cardiovascular-risk, or consumer wellness populations.

**Limitations:** Systematic review with only eight included studies and heterogeneity preventing meta-analysis.; Population was young and healthy, often performance/recovery oriented, not a general wellness cold-plunge population.; Balance/postural control is adjacent to cold-plunge safety/performance, not a primary wellness endpoint.; Total participant count was not available in accessible extraction..
