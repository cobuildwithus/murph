This source is included for **Mental health, stress, mood, and wellbeing context**.

**Findings:** Semi-randomized controlled trial with 404 healthy adults found WHM groups showed greater momentary energy, clarity, and ability to handle stress versus meditation in extracted results, with nuanced/mixed stress and anxiety findings. Pre-post improvements occurred across conditions, but extracted results did not show significant between-condition depression or stress differences; meditation showed greater DASS anxiety decrease than WHM-remote at completion, not maintained. Trait-level effects were minimal/nuanced.

**Why it matters:** Large recent bundled-intervention trial that preserves mixed momentary-vs-trait findings and component boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** momentary energy, clarity, stress handling, DASS depression/anxiety/stress, executive function, sleep.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent WHM/breathwork-cold context only; momentary benefits cannot be treated as isolated cold-plunge effects.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
