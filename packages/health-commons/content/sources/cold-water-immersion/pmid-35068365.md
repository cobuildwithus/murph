This source is included for **Adjacent resistance-training strength meta-analysis**.

**Findings:** Ten studies (n=170; 92% male) showed attenuated strength gains with post-exercise CWI overall (ES -0.23, 95% CI -0.45 to -0.01). Limb-only CWI attenuated gains (ES -0.31, 95% CI -0.61 to -0.01), whereas whole-body CWI was not significantly different from control (ES -0.08, 95% CI -0.53 to 0.38).

**Why it matters:** This source sharpens the training-timing caveat from broader adaptation reviews by focusing specifically on strength gains after resistance training.

**Potential experiment signals:** strength progress, program adaptation, post-lift plunge timing

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a training-adaptation caution, not as a blanket claim against all cold plunges.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
