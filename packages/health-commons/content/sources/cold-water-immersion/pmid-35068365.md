This source is included for **Sports recovery and training-adaptation boundary**.

**Findings:** The meta-analysis found that post-resistance-exercise CWI attenuated muscular strength gains in males overall, but the whole-body CWI subgroup did not show a significant difference versus control.

**Why it matters:** This is a focused synthesis for strength-gain attenuation and supports cautious post-lift timing guidance.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscular strength, training adaptation, cold-exposure timing.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adaptation-boundary evidence; preserve the whole-body subgroup caveat.
