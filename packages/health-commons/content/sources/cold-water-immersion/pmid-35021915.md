## Extraction summary

This source is included for **Immune, inflammation, and illness context**.

**Findings:** The RCT reported improvements in pain and quality-of-life related outcomes in a gout arthritis population, with pain alleviation mediating quality-of-life effects.; Because the exposure was warmer local joint immersion in a clinical disease population, it should not be used as direct cold-plunge wellness or immunity evidence.

**Why it matters:** It provides a clinical-population boundary: positive pain/QoL findings at 20-30°C should not be promoted as general cold-plunge immunity evidence.
