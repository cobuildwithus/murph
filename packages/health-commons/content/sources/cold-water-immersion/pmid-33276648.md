This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** The narrative review discusses potential benefits and risks of cold-water swimming, including cold shock, hypothermia, drowning, and cardiovascular stress. Because open-water swimming bundles exercise, cold, social, and environmental exposures, it should remain adjacent context for cold plunging.

**Why it matters:** Useful adjacent-variant review for risk framing and for preventing overgeneralization from winter swimming to cold plunge.

**Potential experiment signals:** wellbeing self-report, cardiovascular symptoms, hypothermia symptoms, cold-shock response.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent risk/benefit context only; source-owned claims should keep swimming and plunge variants separate.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction notes

- Directness to Cold Plunge: `adjacent_variant`.
- Population mismatch: Open-water swimmers may be self-selected, trained, and exposed to exercise/open-water risks beyond stationary plunging.
- Limitations: Narrative review of a bundled open-water swimming exposure.; Exercise, water immersion, cold, social, and outdoor context are not isolated.; Not a direct cold-plunge trial.
