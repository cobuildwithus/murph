This source is included for **Sports recovery and training-adaptation boundary**.

**Findings:** CWI accelerated post-exercise parasympathetic reactivation, while HWI blunted it, but effects were not evident 4 hours after exercise.; The autonomic signal was short-lasting, limiting its use for durable health claims.

**Why it matters:** It is useful for HRV measurement timing and temperature-response context.

**Potential experiment signals:** mean R-R; ln rMSSD; ln SD1; parasympathetic reactivation; heart rate variability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as acute autonomic-measurement context; do not claim durable HRV or cardiovascular benefits.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Young physically active men after exercise, not general cold-plunge users.

**Limitations:** Eight young men.; Post-exercise HRV physiology endpoint.; No long-term clinical or wellness outcomes.
