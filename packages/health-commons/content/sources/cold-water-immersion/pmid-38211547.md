This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** The meta-analysis reported habituation of cold-shock variables after repeated cold-water immersion, including lower heart-rate, respiratory-frequency, minute-ventilation, and tidal-volume responses. The source supports safety/dose context for first exposure versus habituated exposure but does not establish durable wellness or disease-outcome benefits.

**Why it matters:** Provides synthesis-level support for why first exposure and repeated exposure should be treated differently in safety guidance.

**Potential experiment signals:** heart rate; respiratory rate; minute ventilation; tidal volume; first-session vs habituated response.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only habituation evidence: repeated exposure can reduce cold-shock intensity, but does not remove screening and abort-rule needs.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Cold-shock habituation studies may involve controlled survival/immersion protocols rather than consumer cold-plunge routines.

**Limitations:** Aggregate source with heterogeneous study protocols.; Pooled participant count not extracted in this batch.; Habituation endpoints are safety/physiology endpoints, not wellness efficacy outcomes.
