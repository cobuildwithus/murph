This source is included for **Sleep, HRV, and recovery context**.

**Findings:** The study provides thermal-recovery sleep context, with mattress-related sleep signals and cryotherapy as an adjacent non-CWI modality.; Because neither the main mattress intervention nor cryotherapy condition is cold-water immersion, the source should not be used as direct cold-plunge evidence.

**Why it matters:** Helps separate nocturnal heat-transfer interventions and cryotherapy from cold plunge, even when all are discussed under thermal recovery.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:sleep-efficiency, biomarker:deep-sleep-minutes, self_report:recovery.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as thermal sleep context only, not cold-water immersion or cold-plunge evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
