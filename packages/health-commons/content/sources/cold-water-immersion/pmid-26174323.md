This source is included for **Sports recovery and training-adaptation boundary**.

**Findings:** Post-exercise CWI was reported to attenuate acute anabolic signalling and long-term muscle adaptations to strength training, making this a training-adaptation boundary source rather than direct wellness evidence.

**Why it matters:** This source anchors the caution that cold exposure immediately after resistance training may interfere with desired muscle adaptation.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscular strength, muscle hypertrophy proxy, training adaptation, post-exercise soreness.

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep as a boundary source for timing cold plunges away from strength-training sessions when hypertrophy or strength adaptation is the goal.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
