This source is included for **Sports recovery and training-adaptation boundary**.

**Findings:** The review compared CWI with multiple recovery modalities and found CWI favorable for some recovery outcomes, particularly soreness, but the effect was not uniform across outcomes or comparators. Because comparator modality, timing, and performance endpoint modified interpretation, this source should not be used as proof that any single cold-plunge dose is universally superior.

**Why it matters:** Useful as a comparator-aware recovery synthesis: it supports cautious sports-recovery framing while warning against treating CWI as universally superior.

**Potential experiment signals:** self_report:muscle_soreness; biomarker:creatine_kinase; performance:muscular_power; performance:muscular_strength; performance:flexibility.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent sports-recovery evidence only; do not convert comparator-dependent recovery effects into direct cold-plunge wellness or adaptation claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness to Cold Plunge:** `adjacent_variant`.

**Population mismatch:** Athletic/physically active acute recovery studies; not a general-population wellness cold plunge trial.

**Intervention or exposure:** Post-exercise cold-water immersion.

**Comparator or control:** Other recovery modalities including active recovery, thermoneutral/contrast water, cryotherapy, compression, massage, electrical stimulation, and passive-like comparators depending on included study.

**Duration or follow-up:** Acute post-exercise recovery windows; outcome timing varied by included study.

**Endpoints:** athletic performance recovery; muscle soreness; creatine kinase; muscular power; muscular strength; flexibility.

**Effect estimates or direction:** CWI appeared more favorable for some recovery outcomes, especially soreness, but effects varied by comparator and outcome; some comparator modalities performed similarly or better for strength/immediate power outcomes.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** No source-owned adverse-event extraction was available from the accessible abstract/metadata used for this batch.

**Limitations:** Comparator heterogeneity across recovery modalities. Mostly physically active/athletic recovery contexts rather than general wellness cold plunging. Protocol temperatures, immersion depths, and timing varied across included trials. Review does not establish long-term training adaptation benefits for a Murph Cold Plunge protocol.
