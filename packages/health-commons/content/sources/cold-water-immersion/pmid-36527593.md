This source is included for **Adjacent athlete recovery-versus-other-modalities review**.

**Findings:** In 28 studies, CWI was superior to other methods for muscle soreness, similar for muscular power and flexibility, more effective than active recovery, contrast water therapy, and warm-water immersion for most outcomes, but air cryotherapy was more effective for muscular strength recovery and immediate muscular power recovery at 1 h.

**Why it matters:** This review is useful when evaluating whether CWI is special versus other recovery modalities, but the whole question remains embedded in sports-recovery settings.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle soreness, strength recovery, power recovery, perceived recovery

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep as adjacent athlete-recovery context; it should not inflate direct protocol claims for general cold plunging outside exercise recovery.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
