This source is included for **Sports recovery and training-adaptation boundary**.

**Findings:** The meta-analysis reported lower delayed-onset muscle soreness at 24, 48, and 96 hours and lower perceived exertion at 24 hours, but no significant pooled effect on objective recovery variables over 96 hours; risk of performance and detection bias was high.

**Why it matters:** This source is a broad post-exercise recovery synthesis: useful for soreness expectations, but it also shows objective recovery evidence is not consistently positive.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle soreness, perceived exertion, performance recovery, creatine kinase.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for adjacent recovery context only: CWI/cryotherapy may reduce soreness perception, while objective recovery effects remain uncertain.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
