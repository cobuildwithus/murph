This source is included for **Direct cold-plunge dose and habituation evidence**.

**Findings:** Six brief 12 °C immersions reduced peak minute ventilation and produced perceptual habituation across shivering, thermal comfort, and thermal sensation. The accessible abstract reported no evidence of habituation for oxygen consumption, metabolic heat production, heart rate, mean arterial pressure, core temperature, or skin temperature.

**Why it matters:** Direct brief-dose habituation source highly relevant to practical repeated exposure, while preserving null domains.

**Potential experiment signals:** minute ventilation; perceptual habituation; shivering; thermal comfort; thermal sensation; oxygen consumption; metabolic heat production; heart rate; mean arterial pressure; core temperature; skin temperature.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports rapid ventilatory/perceptual habituation after repeated 12 °C immersions, but not cardiovascular/metabolic/thermal adaptation or health benefits.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy male lab participants; not mixed-sex, older, clinical, or unsupervised plungers.

**Limitations:** Male-only small sample.; Habituation physiology, not wellness outcome.; Preprint/abstract source was used to supplement published metadata; full published extraction should confirm details.
