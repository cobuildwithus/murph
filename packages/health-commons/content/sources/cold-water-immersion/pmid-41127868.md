This source is included for **Direct cold-plunge intervention evidence**.

**Findings:** The cross-sectional study reported better mental health/shorter URTI and sick-leave duration among plungers and an inverted U-shaped frequency association, with up to twice/week appearing most favorable. Because exposure was self-selected and cross-sectional, the source cannot establish that a given plunge frequency causes mental-health or infection benefits.

**Why it matters:** High-recall frequency context source that strongly requires observational boundary language.

**Potential experiment signals:** GHQ-28 mental health; upper respiratory tract infection duration; sick leave duration; depressive symptoms; frequency response.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as observational frequency context; do not claim twice-weekly plunging causes better mental health or fewer infections.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Self-selected polar plungers; not randomized cold-plunge beginners or clinical users.

**Limitations:** Cross-sectional self-report cannot establish causality.; Selection and healthy-user effects likely.; Exposure was polar plunging, not a standardized protocol.
