This source is included for **Sports recovery and training-adaptation boundary**.

**Findings:** Exercise increased inflammatory and cell-stress responses, but changes did not differ significantly between CWI and active recovery; CWI was no more effective than active recovery for minimizing these responses.

**Why it matters:** This source challenges a simple claim that CWI reduces skeletal-muscle inflammation after resistance exercise.

**Potential experiment signals:** inflammatory cytokines, muscle stress markers, creatine kinase.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mechanistic boundary: do not claim CWI broadly suppresses post-exercise muscle inflammation based on this evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
