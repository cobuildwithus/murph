This source is included for **Direct cold-plunge dose and habituation evidence**.

**Findings:** The review reported a linear dose-response relationship for reducing muscle tissue temperature and suggested an example minimum dose around 11 minutes at 10 °C for full-body immersion at 30 mm depth.

**Why it matters:** Useful quantitative dose-response source for cooling effect, not wellness outcome.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle tissue temperature; water temperature; duration; immersion position; measurement depth; dose-response.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports that colder/longer/deeper CWI cools muscle tissue; do not treat tissue cooling as a standalone benefit.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Population mismatch:** Sports/recovery physiology may not map to general wellness cold-plunge goals.

**Limitations:** Sports/recovery tissue-temperature context.; Paywalled full text.; Muscle temperature is a surrogate endpoint.
