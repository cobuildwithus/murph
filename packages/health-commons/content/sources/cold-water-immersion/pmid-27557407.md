This source is included for **Sports recovery and training-adaptation boundary**.

**Findings:** The study suggested CWI may help recovery of stretch-shortening cycle performance, particularly with a 15 °C soreness signal, but did not clearly restore maximal contractile function.

**Why it matters:** This source separates performance dimensions: explosive/stretch-shortening recovery is not the same as maximal strength recovery.

**Potential experiment signals:** jump performance, muscle soreness, maximal voluntary contraction.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent recovery evidence with mixed performance boundaries.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
