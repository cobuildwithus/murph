This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** This study evaluated short-term immersion below 4°C under very cold ambient conditions in young healthy men. Because it focuses on extreme exposure and acute physiological/blood parameters, it is a safety-boundary source rather than evidence that routine cold plunges are beneficial or safe for general users.

**Why it matters:** Reinforces that sub-4°C exposure is an extreme dose and not a default consumer protocol target.

**Potential experiment signals:** blood markers, symptoms after extreme cold, cardiovascular/vascular safety flags.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as an extreme-dose caution; do not use to recommend sub-4°C plunges.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
