This source is included for **Extreme-temperature boundary test**.

**Findings:** The exposure changed multiple blood markers acutely, but not in a way the authors interpreted as overt pathology in this population. In screened young healthy men under supervision, one extreme exposure was tolerated without the authors labeling it hazardous.

**Why it matters:** Defines an upper-bound cold-exposure safety context without endorsing extreme temperatures as routine.

**Potential experiment signals:** safety, tolerance, biochemical stress markers

**Protocol takeaway:** Extreme sub-4 °C immersion is a safety-boundary source, not a target-dose source.

**Claim use:** `safety-only` — Use only for safety-boundary language, not for efficacy or target-dose promotion. Cite `source_artifact:pmid-40408371`.
