This source is included for **Sports recovery and training-adaptation boundary**.

**Findings:** The study reported a limited effect of 3-minute CWI on inflammatory markers during post-exercise recovery; IL-6 was higher after CWI-rest than rest-only recovery and lysosomal enzyme activities did not change.

**Why it matters:** This source is useful for a nuanced inflammation boundary: short CWI did not produce a simple anti-inflammatory pattern.

**Potential experiment signals:** IL-6, IL-10, TNF-alpha, inflammatory markers.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent mechanistic context; do not claim general anti-inflammatory benefit.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
