This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** In 37 men with chronic heart failure, coronary artery disease, or healthy-control status, Finnish sauna followed by 12°C head-out cold-water immersion was supervised and reported as well tolerated, with increased cardiac output/heart rate, higher systolic blood pressure after CWI, and no complex arrhythmias in the available summary. This is supervised adjacent-variant cardiac context.

**Why it matters:** Provides cardiac clinical context with an adjacent sauna-plus-cold sequence, preserving supervision and stability caveats.

**Potential experiment signals:** blood pressure, heart rate, arrhythmia symptoms, cardiac diagnosis, post-sauna context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for cardiac-risk screening and for avoiding sauna-plus-plunge extrapolation into direct claims.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction notes

- Directness to Cold Plunge: `adjacent_variant`.
- Population mismatch: Stable cardiac patients in supervised testing differ from general or high-risk home users.
- Limitations: Small supervised male sample.; Sauna plus cold-water sequence is not isolated cold plunge.; Findings should not be generalized to unscreened or unstable cardiac patients.
