This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** The review describes cold shock as a powerful respiratory response to cold-water immersion, including initial gasp and hyperventilation that matter for airway safety. The source supports treating cold-water immersion as higher risk when airway control, breath control, or alertness is impaired.

**Why it matters:** Supports explicit breath-control and airway-safety instructions for cold-plunge onboarding.

**Potential experiment signals:** respiratory rate; gasp response; breath control; subjective panic/anxiety.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for respiratory safety boundaries, especially first exposure and any context where airway control is uncertain.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Mechanistic/clinical respiratory contexts differ from screened, head-out, voluntary cold plunge.

**Limitations:** Narrative review, not direct efficacy evidence.; Includes clinical and accidental immersion contexts.; Does not provide a cold-plunge wellness endpoint estimate.
