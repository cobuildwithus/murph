**Findings:** The source addresses cytokine-related adaptation in regular ice-cold-water swimmers, an observational adjacent variant of cold-water exposure.; Because the exposure is habitual and observational, it should not be used to claim cold plunging causes immune adaptation.

**Why it matters:** It preserves observational adaptation literature while avoiding causal immune claims.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as context on habitual cold-water exposure; do not infer causality.
