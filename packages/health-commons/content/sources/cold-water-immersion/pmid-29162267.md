This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** The review states that prehospital warming is safe and advantageous, especially for non-shivering hypothermic patients, while no single insulation/heating combination clearly dominates.

**Why it matters:** Supports conservative rewarming and emergency escalation boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** core temperature if available; shivering status; mental status; rewarming need.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for hypothermia safety and rewarming context only.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Emergency hypothermia management differs from intentional short cold plunge.

**Limitations:** Clinical prehospital population mismatch.; Does not test wellness cold-plunge protocols.; Not an efficacy source for cold-plunge benefits.
