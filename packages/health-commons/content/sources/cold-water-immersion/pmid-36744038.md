This source is included for **Adjacent athlete fatigue-recovery meta-analysis**.

**Findings:** Across 20 studies, CWI reduced muscle soreness and accelerated fatigue recovery; accessible summary data indicated CK fell at 24 h and lactate at 24-48 h, with no clear effect on CRP or IL-6. Subgroup analysis reported no important differences by immersion site or water temperature, and immediate post-immersion jump performance could be temporarily reduced.

**Why it matters:** This source helps keep fatigue-recovery claims tied to post-exercise sport contexts and highlights that not every measured marker moves in the same direction.

**Potential experiment signals:** fatigue, DOMS, CK, lactate, CMJ

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as adjacent post-exercise recovery context; do not generalize its mixed high-intensity-exercise findings into a broad cold-plunge recommendation.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
