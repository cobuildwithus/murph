This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** In 12 subjects completing 36 breath-hold submersions at 5°C or 10°C, bradycardia occurred in 10 subjects and ectopic arrhythmias in 11 subjects. This supports a strong safety boundary against breath-hold submersion during cold plunges.

**Why it matters:** Strong source for avoiding breath holding and head submersion in cold-plunge protocols.

**Potential experiment signals:** bradycardia, palpitations, ECG rhythm, syncope symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to prohibit breath-hold submersion during cold plunges.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
