This source is included for **Cryotherapy adjacent variant**.

## Findings
- **Source kind / design:** Controlled acute preliminary study (journal_article).
- **Population:** Apparently healthy adults in an acute laboratory comparison of partial-body cryotherapy, cold-water immersion, and control.
- **Participant count:** Not clearly reported in the retrieved metadata.
- **Intervention or exposure:** A single partial-body cryotherapy exposure in a study that also compared cold-water immersion and control conditions.
- **Comparator or control:** Partial-body cryotherapy versus CWI versus control.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Single exposure with short-term follow-up
- **Endpoints:** Circulating peripheral blood mononuclear cells / lymphocyte redistribution.
- **Effect estimate or direction:** The title and retrieved abstract snippets support an acute redistribution of circulating lymphocytes after PBC; the retrieved direct snippets did not provide a robust comparative effect estimate against CWI/control.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No clear safety signal was available in the retrieved abstract snippets.
- **Limitations:** Preliminary acute study with limited directly retrieved effect-detail and a modality mismatch: partial-body cryotherapy is not interchangeable with deliberate cold plunge.
- **Population mismatch:** This source does not cleanly match a default self-directed deliberate cold-plunge protocol.
- **Directness to Cold Plunge:** adjacent_variant.

## Why it matters
Prevents cryotherapy evidence from being overgeneralized to cold-plunge claims while still preserving immune-cell mechanism context.
