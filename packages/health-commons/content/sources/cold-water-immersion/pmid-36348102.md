## Extraction summary

This source is included for **Immune, inflammation, and illness context**.

**Findings:** The source reported acute immune-cell redistribution, with partial body cryotherapy producing greater CD8+ T-cell increase than CWI or control and NK-cell increases after partial body cryotherapy.; The strongest signal was for partial body cryotherapy, so this should not be promoted as a direct cold-plunge immunity effect.

**Why it matters:** It prevents overgeneralizing cryotherapy immune-cell mobilization to cold plunge and distinguishes biomarker redistribution from clinical benefit.

**Potential experiment signals:** CD8+ T cells; natural killer cells; lymphocyte mobilization; heart rate; blood pressure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent cryotherapy/CWI biomarker context; do not use to claim cold plunge improves immunity.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
