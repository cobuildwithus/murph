This source is included for **Direct cold-plunge intervention evidence**.

**Findings:** The study found that initial responses at 10 °C could be as threatening as 5 °C during the first seconds of immersion. Prior voluntary hyperventilation did not attenuate the initial respiratory response, so it should not be treated as a safety mitigation.

**Why it matters:** Foundational direct immersion safety source for initial cold-shock response.

**Potential experiment signals:** initial respiratory response; cold shock; heart rate; temperature response; hyperventilation condition.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only for safety boundaries around first seconds of immersion, not benefits.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy lab participants; not general unsupervised users, children, older adults, or clinical risk groups.

**Limitations:** Small physiology sample.; Naked/stirred-water laboratory condition differs from typical consumer plunge.; Safety/physiology source, not efficacy evidence.
