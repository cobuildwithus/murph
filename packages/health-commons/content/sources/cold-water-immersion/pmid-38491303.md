This source is included for **Sports recovery and training-adaptation boundary**.

**Findings:** The protocol planned 60 adults with chronic SCI randomized 1:1:1 to WHM with cold exposure, WHM without cold exposure, or usual care; primary outcome was MHI-5, with no efficacy or adverse-event results reported in the protocol article.

**Why it matters:** Useful for current-trial context and endpoint planning, especially mental health and inflammatory outcomes, but it is not result evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** questionnaire:mhi_5; biomarker:inflammatory_markers; biomarker:metabolic_markers; measure:pulmonary_function; self_report:sleep_quality; self_report:pain; self_report:psychological_stress.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as clinical-trial context and endpoint inspiration; do not cite as evidence that cold exposure improves mental or physical health.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness to Cold Plunge:** `clinical_supervised`.

**Population mismatch:** Chronic spinal cord injury population and bundled Wim Hof Method protocol, not general Cold Plunge users.

**Intervention or exposure:** Wim Hof Method breathing and mindset with cold exposure (WHM-C) or without cold exposure (WHM-NC).

**Comparator or control:** Usual care control arm in a three-arm randomized trial.

**Duration or follow-up:** Seven-week intervention with weekly rehabilitation-center practice and daily home sessions; outcomes planned pre/post and follow-up as specified by trial protocol.

**Endpoints:** Mental Health Inventory-5; inflammatory markers; metabolic markers; pulmonary function; body composition; sleep quality; spasticity; chronic pain; psychological stress.

**Effect estimates or direction:** No efficacy effect estimate is available because the article is a trial protocol.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** No outcome adverse events are reported in the protocol extraction; the SCI population and supervised setting are a safety/directness boundary.

**Limitations:** Trial protocol without results. Bundled intervention includes breathing exercises and mindset, so cold exposure is not isolated except through the WHM-C versus WHM-NC contrast if results become available. Chronic spinal cord injury population is clinically distinct from general cold-plunge users. Cold exposure is supervised/clinical and not necessarily equivalent to a home cold plunge.
