This source is included for **Sleep, HRV, and recovery context**.

**Findings:** The crossover study reported higher HRV indices and better subjective sleep-quality ratings during the CWI block than the control/recovery condition.; The source should be treated as sport-recovery context because trained swimmers may tolerate and respond to cold water differently from novice cold-plunge users.

**Why it matters:** Connects repeated CWI with HRV and subjective recovery signals, while keeping athlete recovery distinct from general cold-plunge efficacy.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:hrv-rmssd, self_report:sleep_quality, self_report:recovery.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent athlete evidence for HRV and subjective sleep/recovery tracking, not as a general wellness claim.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
