This source is included for **Adjacent athlete passive-recovery comparison review**.

**Findings:** Across 52 studies, CWI improved muscular power 24 h after eccentric exercise (SMD 0.34, 95% CI 0.06 to 0.62) and high-intensity exercise (SMD 0.22, 95% CI 0.004 to 0.43), reduced CK 24 h after high-intensity exercise (SMD -0.85, 95% CI -1.61 to -0.08), improved soreness (SMD -0.89, 95% CI -1.48 to -0.29) and perceived recovery (SMD 0.66, 95% CI 0.29 to 1.03) after high-intensity exercise, but showed no significant strength-recovery benefit.

**Why it matters:** This is one of the stronger adjacent athlete-recovery syntheses, but its benefits cluster around post-exercise recovery windows rather than everyday cold-plunge use.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle soreness, perceived recovery, creatine kinase, jump/power recovery

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent post-exercise recovery context only; do not convert its 24-hour athlete-recovery findings into a general cold-plunge efficacy claim.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
