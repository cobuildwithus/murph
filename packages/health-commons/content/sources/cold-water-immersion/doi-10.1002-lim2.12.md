This source is included for **Mental health, stress, mood, and wellbeing context**.

**Findings:** In a feasibility cohort, 61 novice swimmers and 22 controls were measured with POMS and SWEMWBS. Swimmers showed acute positive mood changes around swims and larger reductions in negative mood subscales than controls, while wellbeing increased in both groups. The study cannot isolate cold water from outdoor swimming, exercise, social contact, or nature exposure.

**Why it matters:** Useful adjacent evidence for mood/wellbeing signals in people newly exposed to outdoor cold-water swimming, while keeping environmental and social confounding explicit.

**Potential experiment signals:** mood state, positive affect, negative affect, subjective wellbeing, adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Can inform mood/wellbeing endpoints and expectation-setting, but should not be used as isolated cold-plunge efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
