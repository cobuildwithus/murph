This source is included for **Sports recovery and training-adaptation boundary**.

**Findings:** The review assessed regular CWI during training and is a central boundary source for possible mode-specific training-adaptation effects: concerns are strongest for resistance/strength adaptation, while endurance adaptation may differ.

**Why it matters:** This is a key synthesis for regular CWI use and training-induced changes, especially for users who combine cold plunges with strength training.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscular strength, estimated VO2max, training adaptation, hypertrophy proxy.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as boundary evidence for post-training timing and mode-specific adaptation caution.
