This source is included for **Adjacent training-adaptation caution review**.

**Findings:** Across 8 articles, regular CWI during resistance training had a harmful effect on one-repetition maximum, maximum isometric strength, and strength endurance (overall SMD -0.60, 95% CI -0.87 to -0.33) and on ballistic efforts (overall SMD -0.61, 95% CI -1.11 to -0.11), with no clear effect on maximal aerobic power, time-trial mean power, or time-trial duration.

**Why it matters:** This review preserves a negative adjacent finding that matters for protocol timing: using cold immersion right after repeated resistance sessions may trade away desired adaptation.

**Potential experiment signals:** strength progress, explosive performance, training block outcomes

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not treat this as a general anti-plunge result; use it as a timing/adaptation boundary, especially around repeated resistance-training exposure.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
