This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** The study observed supraventricular extrasystoles and ventricular bigeminy during face immersion, supraventricular extrasystoles during body submersion, and a more pronounced heart-rate response during face immersion. The authors concluded severe arrhythmias were low risk for brief body immersion in healthy adolescents, while risk may increase with face submersion and apnea.

**Why it matters:** Adds age- and face-immersion-specific safety context for arrhythmia and heart-rate monitoring.

**Potential experiment signals:** arrhythmia symptoms; heart rate; face immersion/apnea context; age group.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only evidence; do not generalize adolescent face immersion into routine adult benefit claims.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Adolescent face/body submersion protocol differs from adult head-out cold plunge.

**Limitations:** Adolescent healthy population, not adult consumer users.; Face immersion/apnea differs from head-out cold plunge.; Brief supervised protocol; not unsupervised home use.
