This source is included for **Direct dose-response and responder heterogeneity**.

**Findings:** Very different thermal-tolerance profiles did not translate into uniformly different performance or stress-marker outcomes.

**Why it matters:** Helps explain why subjective and physiological tolerance can vary markedly at the same water temperature.

**Potential experiment signals:** tolerance, dropout risk, perceived strain, stress markers

**Protocol takeaway:** Temperature alone does not define strain; individual response heterogeneity matters.
