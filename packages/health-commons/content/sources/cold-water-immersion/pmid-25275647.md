This source is included for **Direct cold-plunge dose and habituation evidence**.

**Findings:** Fast- and slow-cooling groups showed similar motor, cognitive, and stress-marker responses despite different cold strain, with mixed immune-marker patterns. Exposure durations of about 96 to 170 minutes make this a cold-strain safety boundary, not a routine cold-plunge protocol.

**Why it matters:** Preserves cold-strain and severe-dose boundaries while retaining null/mixed motor/cognitive findings.

**Potential experiment signals:** motor performance; cognition; immune markers; HPA-axis markers; sympathetic markers; cold strain.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for safety/dose boundary and cold-strain physiology only.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Cold-tolerance severe-exposure study; major mismatch to short recreational cold plunge.

**Limitations:** Healthy young men only.; Severe prolonged exposure, not a practical plunge dose.; Biomarker and performance endpoints, not wellness efficacy.
