This source is included for **Autonomic recovery dose trial**.



## Findings

One hundred participants completed a jump-plus-Wingate exercise protocol and were then assigned to control recovery, 5 minutes at 9°C, 5 minutes at 14°C, 15 minutes at 9°C, or 15 minutes at 14°C.

The abstract reports earlier return to baseline for mean RR, VLF, and LF in all immersion groups, while SDNN and SD2 returned to baseline only in the 15-minute 14°C group. That same 15-minute 14°C condition also exceeded control for some HRV measures.

This makes the study useful for adjacent autonomic-recovery implementation nuance, but it remains a post-exercise recovery trial rather than direct evidence for a general cold-plunge protocol.



## Why it matters

This is a primary athletic-recovery HRV study, so it can help calibrate autonomic language and measurement choices without being mistaken for direct cold-plunge wellness evidence.
