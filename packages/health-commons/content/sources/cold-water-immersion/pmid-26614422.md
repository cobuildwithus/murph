This source is included for **Sports recovery and training-adaptation boundary**.

**Findings:** All CWI groups showed earlier return to baseline for MeanRR, VLF, and LF; SDNN and SD2 returned earlier only in the 15 min at 14 °C group, which also had higher values than control. The paper recommended 15 min at 14 °C if the goal is restoration of cardiac autonomic modulation after exercise.

**Why it matters:** This source is useful for HRV measurement context and dose-response boundaries, but it should not be treated as direct evidence that cold plunges improve baseline HRV.

**Potential experiment signals:** hrv-rmssd, heart-rate recovery, resting heart rate, immersion temperature, immersion duration.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent HRV/dose context only; do not use as direct protocol support for everyday HRV improvement.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
