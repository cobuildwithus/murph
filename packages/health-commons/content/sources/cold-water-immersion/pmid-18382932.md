## Extraction summary

This source is included for **Immune, inflammation, and illness context**.

**Findings:** The source reported sustained cold-induced norepinephrine stimulation, habituation-related lower ACTH/cortisol responses, and no clear changes in IL-1beta, IL-6, or TNF-alpha.; The study is mechanistic and modality-mixed, so it should not be used as direct evidence for a standard cold-plunge clinical or inflammation claim.

**Why it matters:** This source separates catecholamine adaptation from cytokine outcomes, helping avoid overclaiming inflammation effects from repeated cold exposure.

**Potential experiment signals:** norepinephrine; cortisol; ACTH; IL-6; TNF-alpha; beta-endorphin.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as repeated-exposure biomarker context; do not claim cytokine reduction or clinical benefit from this source.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
