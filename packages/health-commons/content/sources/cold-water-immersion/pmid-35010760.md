This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** The update summarizes accidental hypothermia recognition and management as a clinical safety context for excessive cooling, rewarming, and escalation decisions.

**Why it matters:** Provides modern clinical context for excessive-cooling risk and escalation boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** core temperature if available; mental status; shivering; rewarming requirement; cardiac symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for safety-only hypothermia boundary language, not benefits.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Accidental hypothermia populations are not representative of screened wellness users.

**Limitations:** Clinical review, not an intervention trial.; Emergency/accidental exposure context differs from voluntary cold plunge.; Does not provide efficacy evidence for cold exposure.
