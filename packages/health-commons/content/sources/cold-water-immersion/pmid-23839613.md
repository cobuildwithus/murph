This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** Three cold-induced urticaria cases show variable triggers and presentations. Because cold-water immersion can provoke systemic reactions in susceptible people, this source supports screening for cold-triggered hives, angioedema, respiratory symptoms, or prior anaphylaxis.

**Why it matters:** Cold-induced urticaria is a specific screenable safety issue that can make cold-water exposure dangerous.

**Potential experiment signals:** hives/wheals after cold, angioedema, respiratory symptoms, syncope/anaphylaxis history.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for screening: cold-triggered hives, swelling, or anaphylaxis history should prompt avoidance or clinician guidance before cold plunging.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
