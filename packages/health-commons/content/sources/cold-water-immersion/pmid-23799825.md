This source is included for **Sleep, HRV, and recovery context**.

**Findings:** The combined recovery intervention favored soreness and some sleep/recovery signals, but the contribution of CWI alone cannot be isolated.; Any protocol claim must label the exposure as a recovery bundle rather than a cold-plunge-only intervention.

**Why it matters:** Useful as a boundary source: multicomponent recovery packages should not be cited as if cold plunge alone caused the effect.

**Potential experiment signals:** self_report:muscle_soreness, self_report:recovery, biomarker:sleep-efficiency, self_report:sleep_quality.

**Protocol takeaway:** Cite only as multicomponent athlete recovery context, not direct cold-plunge evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
