This source is included for **Mental health, stress, mood, and wellbeing context**.

**Findings:** Study protocol planned a feasibility randomized trial of an 8-session outdoor swimming course plus usual care versus usual care in 88 adults with mild-to-moderate depression symptoms, followed through post-intervention and 8-week follow-up. It is protocol-only and contains no efficacy results. Protocol excluded people with recent self-harm/suicide risk, severe mental health conditions, cardiac history or family cardiac events, cold-triggered respiratory issues, cold urticaria, Raynaud’s, and non-freezing cold injury; serious adverse reactions were included as a feasibility/safety endpoint.

**Why it matters:** Useful for endpoint selection, safety screening, and clinical-supervised trial design boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** PHQ-9, GAD-7, wellbeing, quality of life, serious adverse reactions, screening exclusions.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for measurement and safety-screening context only; no efficacy claim because it is protocol-only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
