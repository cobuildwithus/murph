## Extraction summary

This source is included for **Immune, inflammation, and illness context**.

**Findings:** The study found no significant differences across groups for URTI presence, symptom score, duration, or sick days, and no negative relationship between cold-swimming exposure and URTI outcomes.; Exercise, self-selection, BMI differences, subjective symptom reporting, and uncertain cold dose limit transfer from habitual swimming to a stationary cold-plunge protocol.

**Why it matters:** It directly checks illness symptoms in habitual cold-water swimmers and preserves a null/mixed immune-outcome signal.

**Potential experiment signals:** URTI presence; URTI duration; sick days; symptom score; quality of life.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent evidence that habitual cold-water swimming did not clearly reduce URTI outcomes; do not claim cold plunge prevents colds.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
