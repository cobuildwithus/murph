This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** The WMS accidental hypothermia guideline supports emergency recognition, out-of-hospital treatment, rewarming, and escalation logic for cold-exposed people. It is safety guidance, not evidence that deliberate cold plunges are beneficial.

**Why it matters:** A cold-plunge protocol needs clear emergency boundaries for when cold exposure stops being recreational and becomes medical.

**Potential experiment signals:** core-temperature concern, responsiveness, mental status, rewarming needs, EMS activation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a safety-only source for hypothermia recognition, rescue/rewarming, and escalation language.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
