This source is included for **Cardiovascular/autonomic physiology context**.

**Findings:** The study reports that beta-adrenoceptor blockers reduced the heart-rate increase during cold exposure while plasma noradrenaline rose, making medication status relevant to interpreting cold-exposure responses. Because this is a cold-exposure pharmacology study rather than a cold-water immersion protocol, use it for cardiovascular/medication context only.

**Why it matters:** Supports medication/cardiovascular context for safety screening and interpretation of heart-rate response.

**Potential experiment signals:** blood pressure; heart rate; forearm blood flow; medication/beta-blocker status; noradrenaline.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as cardiovascular medication-context evidence only.
