This source is included for **Adjacent broad soreness review**.

**Findings:** The review included 17 small trials (366 participants). There was some evidence that CWI reduced muscle soreness at 24, 48, 72, and 96 h versus passive recovery. Evidence was insufficient for many other outcomes or comparators; pooled pain data showed no clear difference versus contrast immersion or warm-water immersion at several timepoints.

**Why it matters:** This source is valuable mostly because it preserves uncertainty and limitations that later athletic-recovery reviews sometimes gloss over.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle soreness, fatigue, recovery perception

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as historical context only; keep its low-quality DOMS signal and safety-reporting gap visible rather than treating it as strong direct evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
