This source is included for **Sleep, HRV, and recovery context**.

**Findings:** The field study observed marked autonomic/HRV changes during cold-water dives, making HRV a plausible monitoring endpoint but not an efficacy claim.; The source discusses serious cardiovascular risks in cold-water diving contexts, which should inform safety boundaries rather than benefit claims.

**Why it matters:** Cold-water exposure can acutely shift autonomic tone; diving and very cold exposure create safety boundaries that should not be generalized to casual plunge benefits.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:hrv-rmssd, biomarker:resting-heart-rate, safety:arrhythmia, safety:syncope.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only for autonomic safety context and caution around very cold/deep water exposure.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
