This source is included for **Mental health, stress, mood, and wellbeing context**.

**Findings:** Study of 49 winter swimmers and 33 controls found that after four months swimmers reported lower tension, fatigue, memory and negative mood state scores and higher vigor/activity; swimmers with rheumatism, fibromyalgia, or asthma reported pain relief. The study is observational and winter-swimming-specific.

**Why it matters:** Foundational winter-swimming wellbeing source, but must remain adjacent and historically bounded.

**Potential experiment signals:** vigor, fatigue, tension, general wellbeing, pain reports.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent historical winter-swimming context, not direct cold-plunge efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
