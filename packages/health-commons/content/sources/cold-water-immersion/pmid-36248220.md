This source is included for **Mental health, stress, mood, and wellbeing context**.

**Findings:** Randomized trial of 86 individuals found the combined breathing plus cold exposure group had a medium-to-large positive effect on perceived stress compared with control, while breathing-only and cold-only conditions were not reported as substantial. This supports a bundled-intervention boundary rather than cold-alone efficacy.

**Why it matters:** Important component-boundary RCT showing the combined stack, not cold alone, was the apparent stress signal.

**Potential experiment signals:** perceived stress, stress questionnaire scores, adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use the combined-intervention effect as cold-plunge-only evidence; preserve the cold-only boundary.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
