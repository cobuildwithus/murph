This source is included for **Sports recovery and training-adaptation boundary**.

**Findings:** CWI improved some 24-hour outcomes such as 10-m sprint and wellness ratings, including sleep quality, stress, perceived fatigue, and DOMS signals, while immediate sprint/power ability was impaired after CWI.

**Why it matters:** This source is valuable because it shows both next-day subjective/performance gains and immediate performance impairment.

**Potential experiment signals:** sleep quality, stress, perceived fatigue, sprint performance, muscle soreness.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent, mixed athlete-recovery evidence and preserve immediate-performance caveat.
