This source is included for **Cardiovascular/autonomic physiology context**.

**Findings:** The study concluded that mechanisms producing habituation of initial cold-water immersion responses are located more centrally than in peripheral receptors. The source is useful for cold-shock habituation mechanism and safety ramping, but it does not establish durable wellness efficacy.

**Why it matters:** Foundational context for distinguishing habituation mechanisms from direct benefit claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** respiratory response; heart rate/cardiac response; habituation status; first-exposure status.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mechanistic/context evidence for habituation, not direct protocol efficacy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Mechanistic repeated immersion study differs from a turnkey wellness cold-plunge protocol.

**Limitations:** Exact sample size and protocol details not re-extracted in this batch.; Mechanistic endpoint, not clinical or wellness outcome.; Older study context.
