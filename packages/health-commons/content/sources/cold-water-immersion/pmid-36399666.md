This source is included for **Sports recovery and training-adaptation boundary**.

**Findings:** The updated review concluded CWI was better than control for muscle soreness, that water temperature and protocol did not drive the result, and that short and medium immersion times showed positive effects; immediate effects appeared strongest after endurance exercise while delayed effects appeared after endurance and resistance exercise.

**Why it matters:** This source updates the soreness dose-parameter evidence while keeping it bounded to post-exercise muscle soreness.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle soreness, water temperature, immersion duration, exercise type.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent dose-parameter evidence for soreness only.
