This source is included for **Adjacent soreness-parameter review**.

**Findings:** Across 44 studies, CWI was better than control for muscle soreness. Accessible abstract data indicated that temperature and protocol were not major moderators overall, but short and medium immersion durations showed positive effects. Immediate effects were strongest after endurance exercise, while delayed effects were superior after both endurance and resistance exercise.

**Why it matters:** This source narrows one adjacent claim cluster to DOMS specifically and helps avoid overgeneralizing soreness findings to every cold-plunge outcome.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle soreness, DOMS, immersion duration

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as post-exercise soreness context; do not promote its DOMS findings into a broad cold-plunge effectiveness claim.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
