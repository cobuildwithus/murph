This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** This safety guidance frames cold water as an acute stressor and emphasizes risks such as gasping, hyperventilation, stress responses, hypertension, and arrhythmias. It supports risk-minimization guidance for cold-water therapies, not efficacy claims.

**Why it matters:** Directly relevant to public cold-water therapy safety language.

**Potential experiment signals:** gasping/hyperventilation, blood pressure, arrhythmia symptoms, panic, supervision/setting.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for risk-minimization language and to avoid presenting cold-water therapy as universally safe.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
