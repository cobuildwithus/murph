This source is included for **Direct inflammatory and stress-marker study**.

**Findings:** A short 14 °C immersion was clearly a physiological stressor rather than an immediately calming exposure at the biomarker level. Downstream immune changes were delayed and selective, not uniformly anti-inflammatory.

**Why it matters:** Useful for preserving acute stress physiology and delayed biomarker timing after a protocol-like plunge.

**Potential experiment signals:** cortisol, catecholamines, heart rate, inflammatory markers

**Protocol takeaway:** A short plunge can look acutely stressful in biomarkers even if later immune-marker changes are mixed.
