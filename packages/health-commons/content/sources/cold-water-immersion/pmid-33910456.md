This source is included for **Direct cold-plunge intervention evidence**.

**Findings:** The source reported marked epinephrine, norepinephrine, and cortisol release along with cold-stress physiology after short whole-body CWI. The source reported delayed IL-6 and leukocyte-percentage changes, TNF-alpha decrease, IL-1beta unaffected up to 48 hours, and no common-cold symptoms within 48 hours.

**Why it matters:** Direct short whole-body CWI source for stress-hormone and immune-marker timing.

**Potential experiment signals:** cortisol; epinephrine; norepinephrine; IL-6; TNF-alpha; IL-1beta; white blood cell count; common cold symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mechanistic direct evidence; avoid claiming immune benefit or illness prevention.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy young men only; not women, older adults, or clinical risk groups.

**Limitations:** Small male-only sample.; Mechanistic biomarkers, not clinical outcomes.; Short controlled exposure and laboratory setting.
