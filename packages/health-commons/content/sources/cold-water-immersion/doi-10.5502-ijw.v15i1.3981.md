This source is included for **Mental health, stress, mood, and wellbeing context**.

**Findings:** Scoping review identified 419 records and included 13 sources, coding 34 findings into eight categories and four themes: physical and psychological health benefits, connection to nature, sense of connectedness, and personal growth. The authors call for more parameter research and the evidence remains heterogeneous.

**Why it matters:** A recent wellbeing-specific landscape source for organizing possible endpoints and qualitative claims conservatively.

**Potential experiment signals:** wellbeing, connectedness, personal growth, mood, nature connection.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for landscape and endpoint taxonomy, not as direct efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
