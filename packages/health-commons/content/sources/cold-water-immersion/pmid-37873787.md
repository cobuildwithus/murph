This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** This pediatric case series and literature review identifies cold anaphylaxis as a serious cold-exposure risk in children and notes swimming as a common trigger in reported cases. It supports pediatric caution and cold-urticaria/anaphylaxis screening.

**Why it matters:** Adds pediatric and family-safety boundary to the cold urticaria/anaphylaxis screen.

**Potential experiment signals:** child/adolescent user status, cold hives, systemic reaction history, swimming-triggered symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for pediatric caution and medical-clearance/avoidance language for cold-triggered reactions.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
