This source is included for **Direct single-immersion mood trial**.

**Findings:** The immersion group showed a clear short-term improvement in total mood disturbance and several mood subscales compared with control. This paper supports using screening and conservative onboarding, because the favorable mood result came from a pre-screened healthy sample.

**Why it matters:** One of the clearest single-session mood-positive studies in healthy young adults.

**Potential experiment signals:** mood disturbance, tension, fatigue, vigour

**Protocol takeaway:** This supports testing mood as a near-term subjective outcome after a single plunge, especially in healthy beginners.
