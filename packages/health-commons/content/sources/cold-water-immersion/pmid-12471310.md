## Extraction summary

This source is included for **Immune, inflammation, and illness context**.

**Findings:** The source reviews human evidence showing that immune and cytokine responses to cold depend on protocol context and are not reducible to a simple immune-boosting or immune-suppressing claim.; Exercise and fatigue contexts can modify immune responses during cold exposure, creating a boundary for applying these data to resting cold-plunge protocols.

**Why it matters:** It keeps immune-marker interpretation cautious by emphasizing human cold-exposure heterogeneity and exercise/fatigue interactions.

**Potential experiment signals:** intracellular cytokine expression; immune response; fatigue; exercise context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mechanistic context only; avoid converting cytokine-expression changes into protocol health claims.
