This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** This review addresses cold-water immersion syndrome and whitewater recreation fatalities. It provides adjacent safety context for open-water cold exposure and should not be used to estimate risk or efficacy for controlled cold plunges.

**Why it matters:** Context for not overgeneralizing cold-plunge advice to open-water/whitewater exposure.

**Potential experiment signals:** open-water exposure, drowning/near-miss, cold-shock symptoms, rescue delay.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for adjacent open-water safety boundary only.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction notes

- Directness to Cold Plunge: `adjacent_variant`.
- Population mismatch: Whitewater recreation involves currents, exertion, entrapment, and rescue delays absent from many plunge settings.
- Limitations: Whitewater/open-water fatalities are adjacent and high-risk, not controlled cold plunging.; Cannot infer incidence for home tub users.
