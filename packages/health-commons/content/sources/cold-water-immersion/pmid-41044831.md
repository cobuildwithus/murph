This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** This clinical review describes cold urticaria as a spectrum from wheals to cold-induced anaphylaxis. It supports explicit screening for cold-triggered hives, swelling, or systemic reactions before cold-water immersion.

**Why it matters:** Current review supports keeping cold urticaria/anaphylaxis screening prominent.

**Potential experiment signals:** cold-triggered wheals, angioedema, oropharyngeal symptoms, anaphylaxis history.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for screening and medical-clearance/avoidance language around cold urticaria and cold anaphylaxis.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
