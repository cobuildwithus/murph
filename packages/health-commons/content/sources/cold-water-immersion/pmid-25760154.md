This source is included for **Sports recovery and training-adaptation boundary**.

**Findings:** Accessible metadata reports that regular post-exercise cold application might attenuate muscular and vascular adaptations to resistance training.; The local muscle-cooling design differs from full-body cold plunging and should be treated as adjacent boundary evidence.

**Why it matters:** It reinforces the training-adaptation caution from later reviews and earlier Yamane work.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscular adaptation; vascular adaptation; strength; muscle thickness; brachial artery diameter; flow-mediated dilation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent boundary evidence for routine cold exposure immediately after resistance training.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Local resistance-training adaptation context, not general wellness use.

**Limitations:** Small male sample.; Local cold application and wrist-flexion training differ from full-body plunging.; Exact effect sizes not extracted.
