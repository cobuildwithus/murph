This source is included for **Sleep, HRV, and recovery context**.

**Findings:** Whole-body CWI showed favorable early-night sleep-architecture and arousal/movement signals compared with control, with modality depth influencing thermal/autonomic response.; The study did not retain clear improvements in fatigue, muscle-damage markers, or functional recovery over 48 hours despite sleep-architecture signals.

**Why it matters:** One of the most relevant controlled CWI sleep-architecture studies, with a mixed result that separates sleep architecture from downstream recovery.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:deep-sleep-minutes, biomarker:sleep-efficiency, biomarker:hrv-rmssd, biomarker:creatine_kinase, self_report:fatigue.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent athlete CWI evidence with mixed interpretation: sleep-architecture signals without clear recovery-marker improvements.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
