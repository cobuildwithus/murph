This source is included for **Sports recovery and training-adaptation boundary**.

**Findings:** CWI appeared to help restore stretch-shortening-cycle performance but not concentric power after sprint exercise, and changes did not appear to be explained by inflammatory modulation.; The study cautioned that excessive CWI duration may exacerbate circulating cytokine concentrations after exercise.

**Why it matters:** It is one of the more detailed dose/context sources and preserves that longer is not necessarily better.

**Potential experiment signals:** IL-6; IL-8; myeloperoxidase; squat jump; drop jump; soreness; impairment.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent dose-boundary evidence; avoid assuming longer or colder post-exercise CWI is safer or better.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Recreationally active men after sprint exercise, not general wellness users.

**Limitations:** Eight male participants.; High-intensity sprint exercise context.; Inflammatory changes were not clearly linked to performance restoration.
