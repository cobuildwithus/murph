This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** The Revised Swiss System recommendation stages accidental hypothermia by cardiac-arrest risk and AVPU responsiveness rather than only core temperature or shivering. It supports symptom- and responsiveness-based escalation rules for cold exposure.

**Why it matters:** Cold plunge safety language should not depend solely on exact temperature measurement; responsiveness and symptoms matter.

**Potential experiment signals:** AVPU responsiveness, confusion, shivering status, collapse, EMS escalation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for emergency escalation logic when a cold-exposed person becomes confused, unresponsive, or otherwise clinically concerning.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

- Directness to Cold Plunge: `general_guideline`.
- Population mismatch: Accidental hypothermia patients are not typical brief cold-plunge users.
