This source is included for **Direct cold-plunge intervention evidence**.

**Findings:** The study assessed cardiovascular stress and cold-induced vasodilation during partial-limb cold-water immersion, not full-body cold plunge. Because exposure was limited to hand and foot immersion, findings should not be promoted into whole-body cold-plunge efficacy claims.

**Why it matters:** Useful cardiovascular and peripheral vasodilation safety/mechanism context, but exposure is not a full cold plunge.

**Potential experiment signals:** cardiovascular stress; cold-induced vasodilation; blood pressure; heart rate; sex differences; ambient heat stress.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety/mechanistic context only; do not use for whole-body plunge efficacy.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Partial-limb exposure and controlled ambient stress do not directly match cold-plunge practice.

**Limitations:** Partial hand/foot immersion rather than whole-body/head-out cold plunge.; Small n=14 physiology sample.; No wellness outcome.
