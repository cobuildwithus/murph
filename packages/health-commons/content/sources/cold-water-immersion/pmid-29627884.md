This source is included for **Sports recovery and training-adaptation boundary**.

**Findings:** The review reported preliminary evidence that post-exercise CWI may blunt resistance-exercise signalling and longer-term resistance adaptations, while endurance-related signals may differ; it is a mechanistic/adaptation boundary, not direct wellness evidence.

**Why it matters:** This review keeps adaptive-response claims separated from acute recovery claims and flags timing tradeoffs.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscular strength, hypertrophy proxy, estimated VO2max, training adaptation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context for separating resistance-training and endurance-training timing decisions.
