## Findings
- **Source kind / design:** Prospective observational comparison (journal_article).
- **Population:** 25 Finnish winter swimmers and 11 controls.
- **Participant count:** 36 (reported).
- **Intervention or exposure:** Winter swimming during a prospective study period.
- **Comparator or control:** 25 winter swimmers versus 11 controls.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Prospective winter-season follow-up
- **Endpoints:** Neurotic psychopathology and alexithymia measures.
- **Effect estimate or direction:** The retrieved abstract summary indicates no major between-group differences in neurotic psychopathology or alexithymia.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No major safety outcome was highlighted in retrieved metadata.
- **Limitations:** Small self-selected sample; psychological measures do not directly establish benefits or harms of a deliberate plunge protocol.
- **Population mismatch:** This source does not cleanly match a default self-directed deliberate cold-plunge protocol.
- **Directness to Cold Plunge:** adjacent_variant.
