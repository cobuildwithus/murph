This source is included for **Mental health, stress, mood, and wellbeing context**.

**Findings:** Prospective study followed 25 winter swimmers and 11 controls from October to May. Winter swimmers reported subjective positive effects and more positive mood, but no major differences between groups were found on CCEI or TAS-20 variables.

**Why it matters:** Preserves mixed evidence: subjective positive experiences without major structured psychopathology/alexithymia differences.

**Potential experiment signals:** mood, alexithymia, anxiety, depression, subjective wellbeing.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mixed adjacent context; do not overstate mental-health effects from subjective reports alone.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
