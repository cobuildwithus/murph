This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** The AHA drowning resuscitation update supports trained-rescuer emergency response, including in-water rescue breathing when safely possible and chest compressions once patient and rescuer are in a safe environment. It is emergency guidance, not cold-plunge efficacy evidence.

**Why it matters:** Useful for emergency-plan wording without making clinical efficacy claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** rescue plan, loss of consciousness, drowning/near-miss, CPR readiness.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only for emergency response and supervision planning.
