**Findings:** Dose-response findings were outcome-specific: 15 min at 14 °C was favorable for creatine kinase at 72 hours, 5 min at 14 °C for peak lactate and perceived recovery, while pain was not better than control immediately post-intervention.

**Why it matters:** This source is useful for dose variability and for preserving mixed, endpoint-specific recovery findings.
