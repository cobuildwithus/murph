This source is included for **Sports recovery and training-adaptation boundary**.

**Findings:** The review describes post-exercise CWI as mode-dependent: resistance-training adaptations may be blunted, whereas aerobic-training adaptations are less consistently impaired.; The review describes common post-exercise CWI practice as approximately 5–20 minutes in about 8–15 °C water to waist or mid-torso depth, which is adjacent implementation context rather than proof of benefit.

**Why it matters:** It provides a useful conceptual boundary: recovery convenience and long-term adaptation goals can conflict.

**Potential experiment signals:** training adaptation; hypertrophy; strength; aerobic adaptation; performance; molecular signaling.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context for advising users to separate cold plunges from hypertrophy-prioritized lifting sessions unless recovery needs dominate.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Training populations and post-exercise timing, not general cold-plunge health users.

**Limitations:** Narrative/mini-review design.; Mechanistic and training evidence mixed by exercise mode and protocol timing.; Not direct evidence for resting wellness cold plunges.
