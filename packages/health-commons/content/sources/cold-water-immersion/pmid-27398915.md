This source is included for **Adjacent team-sport hydrotherapy review**.

**Findings:** Across 23 articles (n=606), CWI was beneficial at 24 h for countermovement jump and all-out sprint recovery, and at 72 h for fatigue; it improved neuromuscular recovery 24 h after team sport. CWT showed a fatigue benefit at 48 h. Neither CWI nor CWT clearly improved perceived muscle soreness, and CWI was not beneficial for accumulated sprinting.

**Why it matters:** This review keeps one common athlete-recovery use case—team sport—separate from broader cold-plunge claims and preserves mixed outcome patterns.

**Potential experiment signals:** CMJ, sprint, fatigue, muscle soreness

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as team-sport recovery context; do not promote its outcome pattern into a general-purpose plunge claim.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
