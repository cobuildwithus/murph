This source is included for **Adjacent protocol-dose network meta-analysis**.

**Findings:** Fifty-five RCTs were included. Medium-duration medium-temperature CWI (10-15 min, 11-15°C) and medium-duration low-temperature CWI (10-15 min, 5-10°C) significantly reduced DOMS; medium-duration low-temperature CWI and medium-duration medium-temperature CWI improved jump performance; both also reduced CK. Ranking analysis suggested 10-15 min at 5-10°C was best for jump and CK, whereas 10-15 min at 11-15°C was best for DOMS.

**Why it matters:** This is a recent adjacent dosing synthesis, but its scope is still acute sports-recovery outcomes rather than general health or daily cold-plunge use.

**Potential experiment signals:** DOMS, jump performance, CK, temperature, duration

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent athlete-recovery dose context only; do not promote its preferred ranges into a direct general cold-plunge recommendation.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
