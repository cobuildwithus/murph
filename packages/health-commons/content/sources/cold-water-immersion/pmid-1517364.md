This source is included for **Neuroendocrine acute cold-stress response**.

**Findings:** Acute cold stress increased sympathetic signaling and blood pressure while suppressing vasopressin. The endocrine response was selective rather than a generalized across-the-board stress-hormone surge.

**Why it matters:** Useful mechanism context for cold stress, with clear modality mismatch to cold plunge.

**Potential experiment signals:** norepinephrine, blood pressure, vasopressin

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep this separate from direct plunge evidence; it is a cold-stress mechanism paper, not a plunge-outcome study.

**Claim use:** `context-only` — Use only for context, mechanism, or boundary framing. Do not use it as a standalone efficacy citation. Cite `source_artifact:pmid-1517364` only for that limited purpose.
