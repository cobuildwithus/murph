This source is included for **Mental health, stress, mood, and wellbeing context**.

**Findings:** Acute cold stress caused a 7.8 ± 1.4% fall in plasma volume and significant increases in systolic blood pressure and plasma norepinephrine, with no change in epinephrine or ANF; AVP fell and no significant CRF or ACTH changes occurred. This is cold-stress mechanism evidence, not a plunge outcome trial.

**Why it matters:** Mechanistic context for acute sympathetic and endocrine responses relevant to cold exposure timing and safety framing.

**Potential experiment signals:** norepinephrine, systolic blood pressure, plasma volume, AVP, ACTH, cortisol.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as acute stress physiology context; it does not support mental-health efficacy claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
