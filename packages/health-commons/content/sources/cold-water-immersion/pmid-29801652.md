This source is included for **Sleep, HRV, and recovery context**.

**Findings:** Adding CWI did not retain a clear sleep advantage beyond the comparator heat-training conditions.; Heat-training exposure itself was associated with worse sleep quantity/quality, so training stress is a key contextual confounder.

**Why it matters:** Reinforces the null heat-training sleep finding from the earlier abstract with the full journal article.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:sleep-efficiency, biomarker:sleep-onset-latency, self_report:sleep_quality.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent negative evidence for sleep benefits after strenuous heat-training recovery.
