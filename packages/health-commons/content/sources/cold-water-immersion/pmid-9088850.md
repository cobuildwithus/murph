This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** In a 15°C, three-minute head-out immersion experiment, alcohol reduced initial respiratory frequency by about 10% in the first 20 seconds while several other physiological measures did not differ in the available summary. Use as alcohol-related safety context, not a benefit claim.

**Why it matters:** Provides human cold-water alcohol response context for a no-alcohol rule.

**Potential experiment signals:** alcohol use before plunge, respiratory response, judgment/coordination risk, drowning risk.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only support for avoiding alcohol around cold plunges.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
