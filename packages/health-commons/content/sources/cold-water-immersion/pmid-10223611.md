This source is included for **Mental health, stress, mood, and wellbeing context**.

**Findings:** Cold stress increased uterine artery pulsatility index in late pregnant women with and without preeclampsia; in two preeclampsia cases with fetal growth restriction, cold stimulus was associated with decreased fetal heart-rate variability in extracted secondary material. This is a pregnancy safety-boundary source, not cold-plunge efficacy evidence.

**Why it matters:** Safety-boundary source indicating pregnancy/preeclampsia should not be normalized into a wellness cold-plunge recommendation.

**Potential experiment signals:** pregnancy safety, uterine blood flow, fetal heart-rate variability, blood pressure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as a safety-boundary source for pregnancy/preeclampsia caution; not for efficacy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
