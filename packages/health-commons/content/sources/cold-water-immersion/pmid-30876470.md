This source is included for **Sleep, HRV, and recovery context**.

**Findings:** A 180-second cryostimulation dose reduced night movements versus control, while subjective sleep quality was similar and sleep-efficiency findings were less definitive.; Small sample size and excluded actigraphy records limit certainty for protocol translation.

**Why it matters:** Adds an adjacent cold-air dose signal for sleep movement/efficiency, but not a water-immersion or subjective sleep-quality result.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:sleep-efficiency, self_report:sleep_quality, biomarker:restless_movement.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent cryostimulation context only; do not cite as direct cold-plunge sleep evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
