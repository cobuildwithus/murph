This source is included for **Mental health, stress, mood, and wellbeing context**.

**Findings:** Study protocol outlines a planned systematic review/meta-analysis of cold-water exposure effects on adult mental health outcomes. Eligible study designs include RCTs, controlled clinical trials, cohort studies, and case-control studies. It provides no efficacy results but helps define outcome scope and evidence standards.

**Why it matters:** Defines mental-health endpoints and study-design standards for future review, while marking the current evidence base as fragmented.

**Potential experiment signals:** depression, anxiety, perceived stress, wellbeing, physiological stress markers, risk of bias.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for measurement-context and evidence-standards framing only; no efficacy claim.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
