This source is included for **Temperature-response physiology**.

**Findings:** The coldest condition produced the clearest thermoregulatory and sympathetic load. The paper shows a graded response rather than a simple cold-versus-not-cold binary.

**Why it matters:** Anchors temperature-dose interpretation better than one-temperature studies.

**Potential experiment signals:** heart rate, blood pressure, core temperature, norepinephrine

**Protocol takeaway:** Colder is not automatically better; temperature meaningfully changes strain.
