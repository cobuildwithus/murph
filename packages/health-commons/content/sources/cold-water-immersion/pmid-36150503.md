This source is included for **Duration comparison and metabolic markers**.

**Findings:** A more typical short 10-minute immersion did not show a strong lipid signal over 2 days. The long-duration protocol amplified stress and triglyceride changes, making it a boundary condition rather than a normal plunge analogue.

**Why it matters:** One of the clearest duration-comparison papers for separating short-plunge from extreme-long-exposure physiology.

**Potential experiment signals:** triglycerides, catecholamines, cortisol, dose boundary

**Protocol takeaway:** Short and long exposures should not be treated as physiologically interchangeable.

**Claim use:** `context-only` — Use for duration-boundary framing and not as default protocol proof. Cite `source_artifact:pmid-36150503` only within that narrower boundary.
