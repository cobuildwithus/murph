This source is included for **Direct cold-plunge dose and habituation evidence**.

**Findings:** The study examined two-day kinetics of stress hormones and lipid indicators after short and long whole-body CWI; full lipid effect estimates were not retained in accessible extraction. The 170-minute exposure arm should be treated as a severe-dose boundary and not generalized to short cold-plunge protocols.

**Why it matters:** Direct CWI lipid-kinetics source, but extracted effects are incomplete and the long-duration arm is adjacent/safety-boundary.

**Potential experiment signals:** cortisol; epinephrine; norepinephrine; total cholesterol; HDL; LDL; triglycerides; lipid kinetics.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context for lipid and stress-hormone kinetics only until full article extraction confirms direction and estimates.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy young men; long-duration arm does not represent routine plunging.

**Limitations:** Male-only sample.; Short and severe long-duration arms mixed.; Detailed lipid effect estimates not retained in accessible extraction.
