This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** Repeated cold-water immersion can lead to long-lasting habituation of the initial cold-shock response, with persistence reported over 7 to 14 months. This is a safety/mechanistic finding and should not be promoted as a health-benefit claim.

**Why it matters:** Helps distinguish mechanistic acclimatization/habituation from health benefits.

**Potential experiment signals:** cold-shock symptoms, respiratory response, heart rate/blood pressure response, session history.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for mechanism and acclimatization caution; do not claim health improvement from habituation.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
