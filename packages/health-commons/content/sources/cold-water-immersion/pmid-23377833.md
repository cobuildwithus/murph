This source is included for **Sleep, HRV, and recovery context**.

**Findings:** The study did not retain a clear whole-night sleep benefit for CWI compared with exercise-only or no-exercise control.; Because the CWI followed evening exercise, the source is not direct evidence for a standalone bedtime cold-plunge protocol.

**Why it matters:** Preserves a direct sleep-focused null finding for evening CWI, especially relevant for users considering cold plunges close to bedtime.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:sleep-efficiency, biomarker:sleep-onset-latency, biomarker:deep-sleep-minutes, self_report:sleep_quality.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not claim that evening post-exercise cold plunge reliably improves whole-night sleep based on this source.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
