This source is included for **Sleep, HRV, and recovery context**.

**Findings:** The RCT reported reduced rumination after daily stressful events in the WHM group versus active control, while both groups improved on some other mental-health/stress outcomes.; The source cannot isolate cold showers, and cold showers are not immersion; it should not support cold-plunge-alone mental-health claims.

**Why it matters:** Important clinical mental-health context, but it is a combined behavioral stack with cold showers rather than cold-water immersion or plunge alone.

**Potential experiment signals:** self_report:depression, self_report:rumination, self_report:negative_affect, biomarker:cortisol.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as combined WHM/cold-shower clinical context; do not attribute effects to cold plunge alone.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
