This source is included for **Sports recovery and training-adaptation boundary**.

**Findings:** The crossover study is cited for faster post-exercise parasympathetic reactivation after CWI compared with control in male cyclists.; The endpoint is acute post-exercise HRV, so it should not be used to claim durable cardiovascular benefit from cold plunging.

**Why it matters:** It is frequently cited for HRV/autonomic recovery but should not be overgeneralized to health claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** heart rate variability; parasympathetic reactivation; heart rate recovery; rectal temperature.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent autonomic-recovery evidence, not proof of improved cardiovascular health.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Male cyclists and post-exercise recovery setting.

**Limitations:** Ten male cyclists.; Post-exercise autonomic endpoint, not resting cold-plunge wellness outcome.; Short 5-minute exposure and warm-environment control.
