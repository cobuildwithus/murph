This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** A single emergency department case report describes anaphylaxis caused by swimming in a patient with cold-induced urticaria. It supports screening and avoidance/medical-clearance language for cold-triggered hives, swelling, or systemic symptoms.

**Why it matters:** High-salience adverse-event report for cold-triggered anaphylaxis screening.

**Potential experiment signals:** cold-triggered hives, angioedema, wheezing/respiratory symptoms, syncope, epinephrine use.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for strict screening and avoidance/medical-clearance language in anyone with cold urticaria or cold-triggered systemic symptoms.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
