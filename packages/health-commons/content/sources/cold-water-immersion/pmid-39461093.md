This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** In 20 participants completing a prolonged outdoor cold-water swimming event in approximately 15°C water, the available summary indicates QT interval prolongation without reported cardiac functional impairment. This is adjacent endurance/open-water cardiac context, not direct cold-plunge evidence.

**Why it matters:** Adds contemporary cardiac electrophysiology context from an adjacent cold-water event.

**Potential experiment signals:** QT interval, ECG rhythm, palpitations, exercise/cold interaction.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent cardiac safety context only, not as direct protocol evidence.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
