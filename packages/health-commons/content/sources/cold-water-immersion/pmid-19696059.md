This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** In 22 male patients with stable coronary artery disease or chronic heart failure, moderately cold 22°C water immersion/swimming was supervised; in chronic heart failure patients, PVCs increased from 15±41 to 76±163 beats per 30 minutes, while no ventricular tachycardia was reported in the available summary. This supports cardiac screening/clearance boundaries.

**Why it matters:** Directly supports cardiac-disease screening and clinician guidance before cold-water exposure.

**Potential experiment signals:** known CAD/HF, palpitations, ECG rhythm, blood pressure, exercise-water interaction.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for medical-clearance boundary in people with coronary disease, heart failure, palpitations, or arrhythmia history.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

## Extraction notes

- Directness to Cold Plunge: `clinical_supervised`.
