This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and cold-shock boundaries**.

**Findings:** A healthy 32-year-old man developed fast atrial fibrillation after helicopter underwater escape training with cold-water submersion and required direct-current cardioversion. This is a single adverse-event report supporting arrhythmia caution and submersion boundaries.

**Why it matters:** Supports strong caution around submersion and palpitations after cold-water exposure.

**Potential experiment signals:** palpitations, atrial fibrillation symptoms, dizziness, chest symptoms, submersion behavior.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for adverse-event boundary and exit/medical-evaluation language for palpitations or chest symptoms.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
