This source is included for **Direct cold-plunge dose and habituation evidence**.

**Findings:** The study reported reduced sleep disturbances and improved Trail Making Test performance by week 3, with no detrimental cognitive effect in accessible abstract. The accessible abstract reported no Stroop or well-being differences, preserving a mixed/neutral boundary for broad well-being claims.

**Why it matters:** Direct repeated-CWI source with sleep and cognition outcomes, but small and mixed.

**Potential experiment signals:** cognitive performance; Stroop; Trail Making Test; well-being; worry; anxiety; happiness; sleep quality; PSQI sleep disturbances.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports cautious discussion of sleep-disturbance and cognition signals without claiming broad well-being improvement.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy young adults; not diagnosed insomnia, anxiety, depression, older adults, or high-risk users.

**Limitations:** Small n=13 sample.; Healthy young adults.; No comparator arm retained in accessible extract.; Mixed outcomes: some cognitive/sleep signals, neutral mood/well-being scales.
