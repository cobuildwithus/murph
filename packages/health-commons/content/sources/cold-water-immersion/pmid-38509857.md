This source is included for **Mental health, stress, mood, and wellbeing context**.

**Findings:** Case-control study used matched groups of 77 controls, 77 CWI-only participants, and 77 CWI-plus-breathwork participants. CWI-only users had better mental-health indices than controls, while the combined programme was associated with better general mental health and fewer somatic complaints than CWI-only in extracted material. Observational design prevents causal inference. The same case-control study reported shorter upper-respiratory-tract infection duration in CWI users than controls and further reduction in the combined training programme group versus CWI-only in extracted material.

**Why it matters:** Useful for separating CWI-only and breathwork-stacked claims while preserving observational boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** general mental health, depression, anxiety, mindfulness, somatic complaints, URTI duration.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as observational adjacent context only; do not claim causality or cold-alone effects from combined-programme associations.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
