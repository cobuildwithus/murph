This source is included for **Direct cold-plunge dose and habituation evidence**.

**Findings:** The source reported delayed proinflammatory cytokine production during recovery from severe cold stress, with WBC and stress-hormone changes over time. The duration and severity make this a safety/mechanistic boundary, not evidence for routine short cold-plunge benefits.

**Why it matters:** Important boundary source for cytokine timing after severe cold stress.

**Potential experiment signals:** IL-6; IL-1beta; TNF-alpha; white blood cells; cortisol; epinephrine; norepinephrine; body temperature recovery.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for severe-exposure immune/inflammation timing and safety boundary only.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Severe cold-stress protocol in young men is not a typical cold-plunge intervention.

**Limitations:** Very prolonged/severe exposure.; Young healthy men only.; Mechanistic biomarkers; final sample count not retained.
