This source is included for **Direct delayed inflammatory response**.

**Findings:** The title and available abstract material support a delayed inflammatory-timing interpretation during rewarming after prolonged cold stress.

**Why it matters:** Preserves delayed inflammatory dynamics that could otherwise be oversimplified into generic anti-inflammatory claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** inflammatory markers, recovery timing, things to watch

**Protocol takeaway:** Delayed biomarker responses after very long immersion should not be confused with the effect of a short plunge.
