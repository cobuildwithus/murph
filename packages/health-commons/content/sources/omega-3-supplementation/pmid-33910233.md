This source is included for **safety_adverse_events**.

**Findings:** Regulatory summaries of this meta-analysis report increased incident AF versus placebo (IRR 1.37, 95% CI 1.22-1.54, P<0.001).

**Why it matters:** Independent AF-focused safety signal.

**Potential experiment signals:** adverse_event:atrial-fibrillation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only evidence only: Regulatory summaries of this meta-analysis report increased incident AF versus placebo (IRR 1.37, 95% CI 1.22-1.54, P<0.001).

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Population mismatch:** RCT populations may include high-risk cardiovascular patients, not healthy consumer users.

**Limitations:** Brief meta-analysis/correspondence; exact included-trial characteristics and n were not extracted here.
