This source is included for **safety_adverse_events**.

**Findings:** Incident AF occurred in 469 (3.7%) assigned EPA+DHA vs 431 (3.4%) assigned placebo; HR 1.09 (95% CI 0.96-1.24), P=0.19.

**Why it matters:** At a common supplement dose, VITAL Rhythm did not show a statistically significant increase or reduction in incident AF.

**Potential experiment signals:** adverse_event:atrial-fibrillation, dose:epa-dha-grams-per-day.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only evidence only: Incident AF occurred in 469 (3.7%) assigned EPA+DHA vs 431 (3.4%) assigned placebo; HR 1.09 (95% CI 0.96-1.24), P=0.19.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Population mismatch:** More direct than prescription trials but still a large primary-prevention RCT, not personalized n-of-1 supplementation.

**Limitations:** AF was ascertained without continuous rhythm monitoring; single dose; population excluded prior AF and prior CVD/cancer, limiting high-risk extrapolation.
