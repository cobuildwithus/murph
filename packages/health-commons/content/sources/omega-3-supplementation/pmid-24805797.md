This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** Comprehensive meta-analysis concluded omega-3 PUFAs were effective in patients with MDD and in depressive patients without MDD diagnosis; mainly EPA preparations and adjuvant therapy appeared to influence efficacy.

**Why it matters:** Supportive depression context source.

**Potential experiment signals:** depression severity.

**Protocol takeaway:** Broad RCT meta-analysis supported depression effects, especially EPA/adjuvant patterns. Must be balanced against skeptical and prevention sources.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Randomized clinical trials of depressive disorders or depressive symptoms
- **Intervention/exposure:** Omega-3 PUFA supplementation, EPA/DHA examined in subgroup/meta-regression analyses
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo/control
- **Duration/follow-up:** Varied across trials
- **Endpoints:** depression severity
- **Effect estimate or direction:** Comprehensive meta-analysis concluded omega-3 PUFAs were effective in patients with MDD and in depressive patients without MDD diagnosis; mainly EPA preparations and adjuvant therapy appeared to influence efficacy.
- **Adverse events / safety notes:** Safety details were not extracted in this batch.
- **Population mismatch:** Adjacent depression-treatment evidence, not direct healthy-adult protocol evidence.
- **Artifact rights status:** open_access

## Limitations

- Older meta-analysis.
- Clinical/depressive populations and adjuvant therapy context.
- Needs balance against later Cochrane/prevention reviews.
