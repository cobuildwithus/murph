This source is included for **dry_eye_treatment_prevention**.

**Findings:** Population: Patients/eyes with dry eye syndrome at two referral eye centers. Intervention/exposure: One 500 mg capsule twice daily containing 325 mg EPA and 175 mg DHA for 3 months. Comparator/control: Placebo capsules. Duration/follow-up: 3 months. Endpoints: dry-eye symptoms, dry-eye clinical signs, blepharitis/MGD subgroup context. Effect/direction: Accessible summary reports that omega-3 fatty acids had a definite role for dry eye syndrome, with more marked benefit in blepharitis and meibomian gland disease; exact endpoint estimates were not extracted in this batch. Safety/adverse events: No adverse-event signal extracted from accessible summary.

**Why it matters:** This trial is often represented in dry-eye meta-analyses and helps explain early positive signals.

**Potential experiment signals:** dry-eye symptoms, TBUT, Schirmer test, blepharitis/MGD signs.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only dry-eye treatment evidence; do not generalize beyond clinically diagnosed dry eye.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Accessible summary reports 264 eyes in omega-3 and 254 eyes in placebo, not confirmed participant counts.; Older trial; exact outcome estimates were not extracted here.; Dry-eye treatment setting may include subtype-specific effects. Population mismatch: Treatment trial in dry-eye patients/eyes; not prevention or general wellness population.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
