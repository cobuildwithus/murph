**Findings:** No statistically significant effects were reported for CRP, IL-6, or TNF-alpha in CKD patients. [source_artifact:pmid-29271576]

**Why it matters:** Important mixed/null evidence in a high-inflammatory-risk population.

**Potential experiment signals:** CRP, IL-6, TNF-alpha, CKD.

**Protocol takeaway:** High-risk disease populations do not guarantee inflammatory-marker response.

**Population mismatch:** Adjacent variant and disease-boundary evidence.

**Safety notes:** Safety outcomes were not extracted.

**Limitations:** CKD clinical population differs markedly from general supplement users. Meta-analysis described the issue as controversial, consistent with mixed trial quality and heterogeneity.
