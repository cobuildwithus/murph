This source is included for **omega3_index_kinetics_formulation**.

**Findings:** Erythrocyte DHA increased in proportion to DHA dose; accessible summary reported a positive dose-response correlation. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-17977469`.

**Why it matters:** Older direct dose-response evidence helps bound DHA-specific biomarker response.

**Potential experiment signals:** erythrocyte DHA, blood lipids, dose-response

**Safety notes:** Safety details were not extracted.

**Limitations / boundary:** Participant count and exact dose arms were not available in the accessible extraction record.; DHA-rich product is not identical to balanced EPA+DHA supplementation.; Biomarker and lipid endpoints only. Population mismatch: Direct oral marine omega-3 supplementation, but DHA-rich formulation may not generalize to EPA-dominant products.

**Protocol takeaway:** DHA dose can be expected to influence erythrocyte DHA, but product composition matters.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`. Directness: `direct_protocol`.
