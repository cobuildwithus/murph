This source is included for **lipids_triglycerides_dose_response**.

**Findings:** Dose-response analysis reported near-linear reductions in triglycerides and non-HDL-C with higher omega-3 intake; LDL-C and HDL-C patterns were described as nonlinear/J-shaped in extracted notes.

**Why it matters:** Broad dose-response anchor for triglycerides and non-HDL-C across intake levels and population strata.

**Potential experiment signals:** fasting triglycerides, non-HDL-C, LDL-C, HDL-C, dose logged as EPA+DHA grams/day

**Protocol takeaway:** Higher omega-3 intake is most defensible as a triglyceride/non-HDL-C intervention signal; LDL-C/HDL-C changes need full lipid-panel interpretation.

**Population mismatch:** Includes broad RCT populations, not only supplement users matching a home self-experiment protocol.

**Safety notes:** Not an adverse-event-focused extraction.

**Limitations:**
- Formulations, doses, trial populations, and baseline lipid status were heterogeneous.
- Dose-response curves are aggregate evidence and do not identify a single best OTC product.
- LDL-C and HDL-C responses were not simple linear improvements.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
