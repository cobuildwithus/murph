This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** Both groups improved, with no significant overall between-group difference. Among women without major depressive episode at baseline, omega-3 showed better adjusted changes on PGWB, HSCL-D-20, and HAM-D-21.

**Why it matters:** Potential boundary source for subclinical distress tracking.

**Potential experiment signals:** psychological well-being, depressive symptoms, HAM-D.

**Protocol takeaway:** EPA-rich trial in distressed women was overall null but showed a non-MDE subgroup signal. Do not generalize subgroup result to all users.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Middle-aged women with moderate-to-severe psychological distress and depressive symptoms; some met major depressive episode criteria
- **Intervention/exposure:** Ethyl-EPA 1.05 g/day plus ethyl-DHA 0.15 g/day
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo
- **Duration/follow-up:** 8 weeks
- **Endpoints:** psychological well-being, depressive symptoms, HAM-D
- **Effect estimate or direction:** Both groups improved, with no significant overall between-group difference. Among women without major depressive episode at baseline, omega-3 showed better adjusted changes on PGWB, HSCL-D-20, and HAM-D-21.
- **Adverse events / safety notes:** Adverse-event details were not extracted in this batch.
- **Population mismatch:** Closer to subclinical distress than MDD treatment, but sex- and symptom-specific.
- **Artifact rights status:** unknown

## Limitations

- Subgroup benefit was post/secondary and limited to women without MDE.
- Middle-aged women only; not generalizable to all adults.
