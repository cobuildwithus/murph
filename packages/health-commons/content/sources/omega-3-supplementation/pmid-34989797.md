This source is included for **clinical_cardiovascular_lipid_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- The pooled TRILOGY analysis included 520 randomized patients with severe hypertriglyceridemia and compared omega-3 PL/FFA 4 g/day with cornstarch placebo for 26 weeks.
- At week 12, triglycerides fell 26.0% with omega-3 PL/FFA versus 15.1% with placebo, for a treatment difference of -10.9% (p=.02); the treatment difference persisted at week 26.
- LDL-C and ApoB were not significantly increased; most adverse events were mild or moderate, serious adverse events were similar, one serious atrial-fibrillation adverse event occurred in the active group, and bleeding/GI event rates were similar.

**Why it matters:** Shows formulation-specific triglyceride and LDL-C/ApoB signals in severe hypertriglyceridemia, plus safety watchpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** triglycerides; non-HDL-C; LDL-C; ApoB; ApoC-III; bleeding events; GI events; atrial fibrillation; statin subgroup.

**Protocol takeaway:** Krill-derived pharmacologic formulations are adjacent variants, not direct support for ordinary oral EPA/DHA supplementation.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
