This source is included for **omega3_index_kinetics_formulation**.

**Findings:** The >95% rTG product produced greater erythrocyte phospholipid fatty-acid incorporation at week 16 and higher serum EPA:AA and EPA+DHA:AA ratios at week 4 than the comparator product. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-36706088`.

**Why it matters:** Direct formulation evidence can help explain why the same labeled class of fish oil may produce different biomarker responses.

**Potential experiment signals:** erythrocyte EPA+DHA, serum EPA:AA, serum EPA+DHA:AA, adverse events

**Safety notes:** No serious adverse events were reported in the accessible trial record.

**Limitations / boundary:** Young healthy population.; Industry affiliations/funding should be considered.; The comparison included a modest active-dose difference as well as formulation-processing differences. Population mismatch: Direct fish-oil formulation evidence, but not a clinical-outcome trial.

**Protocol takeaway:** Track fish-oil formulation and actual active dose when evaluating omega-3 status changes.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`. Directness: `direct_protocol`.
