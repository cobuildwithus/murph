This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** The ISSN position stand summarizes sports-nutrition evidence for long-chain omega-3 PUFAs. It supports cautious language: supplementation may decrease subjective soreness or influence immune and recovery responses, while objective muscle-damage, inflammation, strength, and adaptation evidence remains mixed across the literature.

**Why it matters:** It offers expert boundary language for sports claims and helps keep recovery, immune, sleep, and strength claims appropriately qualified.

**Potential experiment signals:** subjective soreness, illness days, sleep quality, training adaptation notes.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for background and safety/claims framing only; do not use as direct RCT evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
