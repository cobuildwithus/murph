This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** Meta-analysis of 13 trials found a small, non-significant benefit (SMD 0.11; 95% CI -0.04 to 0.26); trim-and-fill analysis reduced the estimate to SMD 0.01 (95% CI -0.13 to 0.15).

**Why it matters:** Key counterweight to supportive depression meta-analyses.

**Potential experiment signals:** depression severity.

**Protocol takeaway:** Skeptical meta-analysis found no significant depression benefit after bias adjustment. Older meta-analysis but important for bias concerns.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Randomized placebo-controlled trials of omega-3 for major depressive disorder
- **Intervention/exposure:** Omega-3 fatty acid supplementation
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo
- **Duration/follow-up:** Varied across trials
- **Endpoints:** depression severity
- **Effect estimate or direction:** Meta-analysis of 13 trials found a small, non-significant benefit (SMD 0.11; 95% CI -0.04 to 0.26); trim-and-fill analysis reduced the estimate to SMD 0.01 (95% CI -0.13 to 0.15).
- **Adverse events / safety notes:** Adverse-event details were not extracted.
- **Population mismatch:** Clinical treatment evidence; not direct general wellness evidence.
- **Artifact rights status:** open_access

## Limitations

- Publication bias and heterogeneity concerns.
- Clinical MDD treatment context.
