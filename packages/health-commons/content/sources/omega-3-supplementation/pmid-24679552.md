This source is included for **omega3_index_kinetics_formulation**.

**Findings:** In the supplemented low-status subset, omega-3 index increased from about 3.1% to 4.8%, but remained below the commonly cited 8% target for many participants. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-24679552`.

**Why it matters:** This boundary source is useful for vegan/non-fish EPA+DHA variants and low-baseline response.

**Potential experiment signals:** omega-3 index, blood DHA, blood EPA, vegan baseline status

**Safety notes:** Safety details were not extracted.

**Limitations / boundary:** Adjacent variant: vegan low-intake population and algal-derived low-dose product.; Not a conventional RCT for the supplement subset as extracted.; Biomarker endpoint only. Population mismatch: Adjacent low-intake population and non-fish source variant.

**Protocol takeaway:** Algal EPA/DHA can raise low baseline status, but dose may be insufficient to reach higher omega-3 index targets.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. Directness: `adjacent_variant`.
