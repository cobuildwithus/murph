This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** Overview included 9 systematic reviews and 14 RCTs; accessible summaries report a statistically significant but modest MMSE improvement (effect size 0.16, 95% CI 0.01-0.32). Safety note: Safety not extracted.

**Why it matters:** Recent overview helps compare and bound cognition meta-analyses.

**Potential experiment signals:** MMSE, global cognitive function, cognitive decline.

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep as background and cite primary meta-analyses/RCTs for protocol claims.

**Population mismatch:** MCI/non-dementia review context, not direct general healthy-adult evidence.

**Limitations:** Overview of reviews can double-count underlying trials.; Mixes non-dementia and MCI populations.; A modest MMSE effect may not be clinically meaningful.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
