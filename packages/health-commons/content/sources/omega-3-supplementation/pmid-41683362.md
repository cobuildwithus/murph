This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** Omega-3 modified fatty-acid profiles and several inflammatory/secretory-factor responses to exercise, while anaerobic performance parameters did not significantly change.

**Why it matters:** Useful for modern mechanistic context and for separating biomarker shifts from performance outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:** PUFA content, SII, FGF-23, IL-1β, IL-1Ra, IL-6, IL-10, anaerobic performance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as short-term biomarker context; do not present as evidence that 21 days improves performance.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `direct_protocol`.

**Limitations and caveats:** Short 21-day intervention. Physically active young men only. Performance endpoints did not show significant improvement.
