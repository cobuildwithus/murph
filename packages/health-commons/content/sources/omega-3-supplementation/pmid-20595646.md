This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** Supplementation reduced depressive symptoms and improved health-related quality of life; compliance was supported by increased erythrocyte EPA and DHA.

**Why it matters:** Age-specific clinical context, not a general protocol claim.

**Potential experiment signals:** Geriatric Depression Scale, health-related quality of life, erythrocyte fatty acids.

**Protocol takeaway:** Small elderly-women RCT found depression and quality-of-life improvement. Small, restricted population.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Depressed elderly women aged 66–95 years in a nursing-home setting
- **Intervention/exposure:** 2.5 g/day n-3 LCPUFA providing 1.67 g EPA and 0.83 g DHA
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo
- **Duration/follow-up:** 8 weeks / 2 months
- **Endpoints:** Geriatric Depression Scale, health-related quality of life, erythrocyte fatty acids
- **Effect estimate or direction:** Supplementation reduced depressive symptoms and improved health-related quality of life; compliance was supported by increased erythrocyte EPA and DHA.
- **Adverse events / safety notes:** Compliance was good; detailed adverse-event extraction was not performed.
- **Population mismatch:** Age- and setting-specific clinical evidence, not general wellness evidence.
- **Artifact rights status:** unknown

## Limitations

- Small nursing-home sample.
- Elderly women only.
- Clinical depression context.
