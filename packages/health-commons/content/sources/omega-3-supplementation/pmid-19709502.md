This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** In the ITT sample, HAM-D-17 improved more in EPA than placebo but between-group difference was not statistically significant (P=.123); ITT response 38% EPA vs 21% placebo (P=.45).

**Why it matters:** Useful for preserving null/mixed EPA monotherapy evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** HAM-D-17, response, remission, tolerability.

**Protocol takeaway:** EPA monotherapy pilot suggested a trend but no statistically significant MDD benefit. Small sample and low completion limit interpretation.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Adults with DSM-IV major depressive disorder
- **Intervention/exposure:** Ethyl-eicosapentaenoate 1 g/day monotherapy
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo
- **Duration/follow-up:** 8 weeks
- **Endpoints:** HAM-D-17, response, remission, tolerability
- **Effect estimate or direction:** In the ITT sample, HAM-D-17 improved more in EPA than placebo but between-group difference was not statistically significant (P=.123); ITT response 38% EPA vs 21% placebo (P=.45).
- **Adverse events / safety notes:** Tolerability was examined, but detailed adverse-event extraction was not performed.
- **Population mismatch:** Clinical monotherapy MDD evidence; adjacent to community oral EPA/DHA.
- **Artifact rights status:** open_access

## Limitations

- Small pilot trial.
- Low completer count.
- Clinical MDD monotherapy context.
