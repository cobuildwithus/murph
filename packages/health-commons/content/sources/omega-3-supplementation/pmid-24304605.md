This source is included for **omega3_index_kinetics_formulation**.

**Findings:** Krill oil increased omega-3 index and n-3 biomarkers more than the fish-oil comparator in this short crossover study; total cholesterol and LDL cholesterol increases versus control were reported in accessible summaries, while triglycerides and HDL were unchanged. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-24304605`.

**Why it matters:** This source protects against overgeneralizing from fish-oil capsules to all marine omega-3 products.

**Potential experiment signals:** omega-3 index, plasma EPA+DHA, RBC EPA+DHA, LDL cholesterol, adverse events

**Safety notes:** No adverse events were reported in the accessible abstract summary.

**Limitations / boundary:** Adjacent variant because krill oil is a phospholipid-rich product, not the core fish-oil EPA/DHA protocol.; Short duration and small sample.; Dose/formulation/source are confounded. Population mismatch: Adjacent-variant evidence; should not be promoted as direct fish-oil EPA/DHA protocol evidence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep krill-oil findings as adjacent formulation context, not direct oral EPA/DHA supplement claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. Directness: `adjacent_variant`.
