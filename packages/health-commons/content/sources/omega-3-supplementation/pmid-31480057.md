This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** Guideline recommends clinical interview/measurement-based assessment and states that pure EPA or EPA/DHA ratio >2 may be effective, with 1–2 g/day net EPA recommended for MDD treatment.

**Why it matters:** Provides external guideline context for EPA-heavy clinical depression use.

**Potential experiment signals:** major depressive disorder treatment, monitoring, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** ISNPR guideline recommends EPA-forward omega-3 for MDD treatment under clinical assessment. Should not become a community protocol recommendation to self-treat MDD.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Adults with major depressive disorder in a nutritional psychiatry guideline context
- **Intervention/exposure:** Omega-3 fatty acids for MDD treatment, emphasizing EPA-containing formulations
- **Comparator/control:** Guideline evidence assessment
- **Duration/follow-up:** Not applicable
- **Endpoints:** major depressive disorder treatment, monitoring, adverse events
- **Effect estimate or direction:** Guideline recommends clinical interview/measurement-based assessment and states that pure EPA or EPA/DHA ratio >2 may be effective, with 1–2 g/day net EPA recommended for MDD treatment.
- **Adverse events / safety notes:** Guideline advises quality monitoring and attention to gastrointestinal and dermatologic adverse effects and metabolic panels.
- **Population mismatch:** Clinician-managed MDD treatment differs from Oral EPA/DHA supplementation for general users.
- **Artifact rights status:** unknown

## Limitations

- Specialty clinical guideline, not a direct self-experiment protocol.
- Applies to diagnosed MDD treatment under professional oversight.
