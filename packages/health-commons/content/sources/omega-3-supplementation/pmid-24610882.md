This source is included for **cardiovascular_outcomes_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- **outcome / blood pressure:** EPA/DHA may modestly lower blood pressure, strongest in untreated hypertension. Effect/direction: Untreated hypertensive subgroup: SBP -4.51 mmHg and DBP -3.05 mmHg; normotensive subgroup: SBP -1.25 mmHg and DBP -0.62 mmHg.
- **dose / diastolic blood pressure by dose:** Blood-pressure tracking can be a candidate experiment signal, especially where dose and baseline BP are documented. Effect/direction: Authors reported DBP reductions at doses of at least 2 g/day.

**Why it matters:** Identifies a measurable signal that is closer to self-experimentation than hard CVD outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- systolic blood pressure
- diastolic blood pressure

**Protocol takeaway:** Blood pressure may be worth tracking, especially in users with elevated baseline BP, but disease treatment claims should be avoided.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
