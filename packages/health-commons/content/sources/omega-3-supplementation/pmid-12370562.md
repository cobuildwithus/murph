This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** The trial concluded that fish oil or isoflavones at the supplemented doses were not effective for ameliorating DOMS under the study protocol.

**Why it matters:** Important negative early evidence to prevent one-sided DOMS claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** DOMS symptoms, physical parameters associated with soreness.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a negative adjacent-variant boundary; do not hide null DOMS evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `adjacent_variant`.

**Limitations and caveats:** Small n=22 older trial. Full text not available in retrieved material. Fish oil and isoflavone arms complicate clean EPA/DHA attribution.
