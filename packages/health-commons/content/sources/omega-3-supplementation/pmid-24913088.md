**Findings:** In mice, omega-3 index rose with dietary EPA+DHA and correlated strongly with plasma and gastrointestinal tissue EPA+DHA measures. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-24913088`.

**Why it matters:** This clarifies what omega-3 index may represent biologically while preserving animal-evidence boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** omega-3 index, intestinal membrane EPA+DHA, plasma EPA+DHA

**Safety notes:** Not applicable to protocol safety.

**Limitations / boundary:** Animal mechanistic study; not human protocol evidence.; Tissue-proxy inference only.; Duration and exact sample count were not extracted. Population mismatch: Same-mechanism animal/tissue-proxy evidence with major population mismatch.

**Protocol takeaway:** RBC omega-3 index may track tissue EPA+DHA in mechanistic models, but human protocol claims need human evidence.
