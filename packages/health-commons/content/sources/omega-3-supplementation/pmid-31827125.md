This source is included for **inflammation_immune_markers**.

**Findings:** The review reported omega-3 supplementation may be associated with lower inflammatory biomarkers and improved triglycerides in diabetic/CVD populations; extracted summaries also flagged possible increases in fasting glucose and LDL-C in some analyses. [source_artifact:pmid-31827125]

**Why it matters:** Cardiometabolic boundary evidence with both favorable and cautionary signals.

**Potential experiment signals:** inflammatory biomarkers, triglycerides, LDL-C, fasting glucose.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports measuring lipids and glucose alongside inflammation in higher-risk users.

**Population mismatch:** Adjacent variant due to disease populations.

**Safety notes:** Adverse-event reporting was not extracted; metabolic/lipid worsening signals should be treated as monitoring notes.

**Limitations:** Clinical cardiometabolic disease populations differ from general supplement users. Exact inflammatory effect estimates were not extracted. Some lipid/glucose signals may move unfavorably and require endpoint-specific verification.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
