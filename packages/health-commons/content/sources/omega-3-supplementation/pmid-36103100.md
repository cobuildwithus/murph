This source is included for **safety_adverse_events**.

**Findings:** Accessible abstract text reported lower major cardiovascular events (RR 0.95, 95% CI 0.91-0.99), myocardial infarction (RR 0.90, 95% CI 0.83-0.98), and cardiovascular death (RR 0.94, 95% CI 0.88-0.99), while AF was increased (RR 1.25, 95% CI 1.10-1.41). GI problems, bleeding-related disorders, and cancer did not differ significantly overall; the authors cautioned about bleeding risk with prescription EPA ethyl ester formulations.

**Why it matters:** Preserves a mixed benefit/harm profile: possible cardiovascular benefit, AF increase, and formulation-specific bleeding caution.

**Potential experiment signals:** adverse_event:atrial-fibrillation, adverse_event:bleeding, symptom:gastrointestinal-adverse-effects.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only evidence only: Accessible abstract text reported lower major cardiovascular events (RR 0.95, 95% CI 0.91-0.99), myocardial infarction (RR 0.90, 95% CI 0.83-0.98), and cardiovascular death (RR 0.94, 95% CI 0.88-0.99), while AF was increased (RR 1.25, 95% CI 1.10-1.41). GI problems, bleeding-related disorders, and cancer did not differ significantly overall; the authors cautioned about bleeding risk with prescription EPA ethyl ester formulations.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Cardiovascular-prevention trials are not the same as general wellness oral EPA/DHA self-experimentation.

**Limitations:** Pooled results span heterogeneous formulations, doses, populations, and cardiovascular-risk settings; exact participant count not extracted for this batch source page.
