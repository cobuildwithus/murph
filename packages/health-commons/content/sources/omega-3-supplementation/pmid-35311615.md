This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** Guideline summaries report positive directionality and varying support for adjunctive omega-3 in mood disorders, while omega-3 monotherapy was not supported for some uses and no nutraceutical had sufficient evidence in anxiety-related disorders.

**Why it matters:** Boundary source for clinical psychiatric claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** psychiatric-disorder treatment recommendations.

**Protocol takeaway:** WFSBP/CANMAT guideline provides condition-specific nutraceutical context. Do not generalize mood-disorder adjunct recommendations to anxiety or healthy users.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

- **Intervention/exposure:** Nutraceuticals and phytoceuticals including omega-3 fatty acids
- **Comparator/control:** Guideline evidence grading

- **Endpoints:** psychiatric-disorder treatment recommendations
