This source is included for **omega3_index_kinetics_formulation**.

**Findings:** Fish-oil supplementation increased omega-3 index and total omega-3 fatty acids and decreased omega-6 PUFA measures in this peripheral artery disease population. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-28457318`.

**Why it matters:** Clinical populations may respond differently, so this source helps flag response predictors and mismatch.

**Potential experiment signals:** omega-3 index, total omega-3, omega-6 PUFA, response predictors

**Safety notes:** Safety details were not extracted.

**Limitations / boundary:** Clinical peripheral artery disease population may not generalize to healthy adults.; Participant count and exact dose were not extracted from accessible metadata.; Biomarker-response predictor source, not a general efficacy trial. Population mismatch: Direct oral fish-oil evidence but with clinical-population mismatch.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as direct but population-bounded evidence for short-term omega-3 index response.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`. Directness: `direct_protocol`.
