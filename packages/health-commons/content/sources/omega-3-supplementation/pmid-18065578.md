This source is included for **omega3_index_kinetics_formulation**.

**Findings:** Both fish and capsule intake increased blood-cell and plasma phospholipid n-3 fatty acids; accessible abstract text reports RBC EPA+DHA rose in both groups over 16 weeks. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-18065578`.

**Why it matters:** This source helps distinguish supplement protocols from dietary fish exposure while showing shared biomarker endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** RBC EPA+DHA, plasma phospholipid EPA+DHA, fish intake, capsule intake

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event details were extracted.

**Limitations / boundary:** Adjacent-variant source because one arm is fish food rather than supplementation.; At least one group size was visible in the accessible abstract, but the full participant count was not retained in this extraction.; Use only for food-versus-capsule boundary context. Population mismatch: Adjacent variant: dietary fish is not the same as oral supplement dosing.

**Protocol takeaway:** Fish intake and capsules can both affect omega-3 biomarkers, but protocol claims should keep food and supplement routes separate.
