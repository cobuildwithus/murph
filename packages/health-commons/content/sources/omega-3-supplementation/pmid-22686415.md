This source is included for **safety_adverse_events**.

**Findings:** Daily 1 g n-3 fatty-acid ethyl esters did not reduce cardiovascular death, major vascular events, all-cause death, or death from arrhythmia. Triglycerides were modestly reduced, and adverse effects were similar between groups.

**Why it matters:** Large long-term trial provides safety and null cardiovascular context in a comorbidity/medication-heavy population.

**Potential experiment signals:** cardiovascular death, major vascular events, all-cause death, death from arrhythmia, triglycerides, adverse effects.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context/safety only: 1 g/day n-3 ethyl esters did not reduce cardiovascular outcomes in dysglycemia, and adverse effects were similar to placebo.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness:** `safety_boundary` for `protocol_variant:omega-3-supplementation/oral-epa-dha-supplementation`.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Clinical high-risk dysglycemia population; not designed to evaluate ordinary supplement-user symptoms or short-term community endpoints.
