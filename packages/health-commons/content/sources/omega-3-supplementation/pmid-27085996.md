This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** This randomized double-blind placebo-controlled parallel-group trial tested EPA/DHA-rich fish oil before eccentric contractions in healthy young men. The abstract-level finding was attenuation of strength loss, restricted range of motion, and soreness after the exercise challenge.

**Why it matters:** It directly tests mixed EPA/DHA fish oil against placebo for functional recovery after eccentric contractions.

**Potential experiment signals:** strength recovery, range of motion, DOMS score.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports a plausible recovery effect after eccentric exercise, with population and exercise-challenge boundaries.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
