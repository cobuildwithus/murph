This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** The omega-3 plus protein supplement was associated with lower lower-body soreness and better countermovement-jump maintenance during pre-season training versus protein-based placebo.

**Why it matters:** Shows potentially useful athlete-recovery signals while clearly marking multi-nutrient and training-load confounding.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily soreness, countermovement jump, fatigue, sleep, stress, mood.

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep as adjacent-variant context; do not use it to claim isolated fish oil improves recovery in general users.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `adjacent_variant`.

**Limitations and caveats:** Omega-3 was added to a protein-based supplement rather than tested as isolated EPA/DHA. Professional team-sport population. Pre-season training load and field context create confounding.
