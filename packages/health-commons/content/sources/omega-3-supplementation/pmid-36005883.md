This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** In completers, only median PBMC TNF decreased at 2 g/day EPA in accessible snippets; EPA 4 g/day was reported to show a medium effect size for response rates versus placebo and may alleviate MDD in overweight adults with elevated inflammation. Safety note: Tolerability was part of trial context, but detailed adverse events were not extracted.

**Why it matters:** Biomarker-enriched MDD trial helps separate clinical treatment from wellness claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** depressive symptoms, IDS-C30 response, IL-6, PBMC cytokines, TNF production, hs-CRP.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only for high-inflammation MDD; not protocol-level mood guidance.

**Population mismatch:** High-inflammation MDD treatment trial; adjacent/clinical supervised.

**Limitations:** Small dose-finding sample with clinical MDD and inflammatory enrichment.; Clinician-managed psychiatric condition; not general mood wellness.; EPA-only formulation rather than EPA+DHA.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
