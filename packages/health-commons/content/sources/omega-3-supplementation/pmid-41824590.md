This source is included for **clinical_cardiovascular_lipid_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- The guideline is a current US multisociety document for evaluation, management, and monitoring of dyslipidemias, including hypertriglyceridemia and elevated lipoprotein(a).
- It replaces the 2018 blood cholesterol guideline and therefore is a temporal boundary source for modern lipid-management language.
- The extraction uses it only to keep abnormal lipid results and pharmacologic treatment decisions in a clinician-managed domain.

**Why it matters:** Provides the current US guideline boundary for lipid disorders and helps prevent protocol overreach into medical treatment claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** fasting lipid panel; triglycerides; LDL-C; non-HDL-C; lipoprotein(a); medication review and ASCVD risk classification.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context that abnormal lipid findings may need guideline-based evaluation rather than supplement self-management.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
