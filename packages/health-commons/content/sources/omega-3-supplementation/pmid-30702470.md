This source is included for **dry_eye_treatment_prevention**.

**Findings:** Population: Patients with dry eye disease enrolled in randomized clinical trials. Intervention/exposure: Omega-3 fatty acid supplementation across included RCTs. Comparator/control: Placebo in included RCTs. Duration/follow-up: Varied across included RCTs; optimal duration not established. Endpoints: dry-eye symptoms, tear breakup time, Schirmer test, corneal fluorescein staining. Effect/direction: Reported favorable pooled effects versus placebo for symptoms (SDM 0.968; 95% CI 0.554-1.383; p<0.001), corneal fluorescein staining (SDM 0.517; 95% CI 0.043-0.991; p=0.032), tear breakup time (SDM 0.905; 95% CI 0.564-1.246; p<0.001), and Schirmer test values (SDM 0.905; 95% CI 0.564-1.246; p<0.001). Safety/adverse events: No specific adverse-event estimate extracted from accessible abstract material.

**Why it matters:** This source explains why older and smaller dry-eye trials can look positive even when larger anchors are more mixed.

**Potential experiment signals:** OSDI or dry-eye symptoms, TBUT, Schirmer test, corneal fluorescein staining.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for context-only dry-eye treatment discussion with explicit heterogeneity and trial-population caveats.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Optimal dosage and duration were not defined.; Included trials likely varied by geography, dry-eye subtype, formulation, and co-interventions.; Meta-regression suggested geographic differences, supporting caution in generalization. Population mismatch: Dry-eye treatment population; not prevention and not a general wellness cohort.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
