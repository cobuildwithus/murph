This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** Meta-analysis of 9 RCTs found mixed efficacy findings for treating depressive symptoms in adults aged 60 and above.

**Why it matters:** Boundary source for older-adult mood claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** depressive symptoms in older adults.

**Protocol takeaway:** Older-adult depression meta-analysis found mixed results. Evidence base was small and underpowered for moderator conclusions.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Older adults aged 60 years and above in 9 RCTs
- **Intervention/exposure:** n-3 PUFA supplementation
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo/control
- **Duration/follow-up:** Varied across included trials
- **Endpoints:** depressive symptoms in older adults
- **Effect estimate or direction:** Meta-analysis of 9 RCTs found mixed efficacy findings for treating depressive symptoms in adults aged 60 and above.
- **Adverse events / safety notes:** Adverse-event details were not extracted.
- **Population mismatch:** Older-adult and depression-focused evidence; not general adult wellness evidence.
- **Artifact rights status:** unknown

## Limitations

- Only 9 studies; limited power for moderator analysis.
- Findings described as suggestive only.
