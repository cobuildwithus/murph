This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** Pre- and post-exercise omega-3 plus whey conditions reduced DOMS at early time points and improved selected strength/jump outcomes compared with placebo, while swelling, pressure pain threshold, flexibility, and several other outcomes did not clearly differ.

**Why it matters:** Provides female-athlete context while reinforcing that multi-nutrient stacks must not be promoted as direct EPA/DHA evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** DOMS VAS, vertical jump, MVIC, thigh swelling, pressure pain threshold, range of motion.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent-variant context only; it is not evidence that fish oil alone improves futsal recovery.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `adjacent_variant`.

**Limitations and caveats:** Whey protein co-intervention. Acute timing design. Small female-athlete sample and short washout/recovery windows.
