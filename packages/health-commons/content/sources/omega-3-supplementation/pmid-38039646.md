This source is included for **variant_boundaries_external_context**.

**Findings:** Krill oil lowered several lipid endpoints in pooled RCTs but did not show broad cardiovascular-risk-factor effects; this is adjacent-variant evidence only. [source_artifact:pmid-38039646].

**Why it matters:** Do not use krill-specific lipid findings to claim oral EPA/DHA fish-oil or algal-oil effects. [source_artifact:pmid-38039646].

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, triglycerides, total cholesterol, blood pressure, adverse events

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use krill-specific lipid findings to claim oral EPA/DHA fish-oil or algal-oil effects. [source_artifact:pmid-38039646].

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction limitations:**
- Adjacent variant: krill oil differs from fish oil by phospholipid form, astaxanthin content, and product matrix.
- Heterogeneity and study design, dose, BMI, and duration differences affected certainty.
- Not direct evidence for a purified or generic oral EPA/DHA supplementation protocol.
