This source is included for **inflammation_immune_markers**.

**Findings:** EPA and DHA did not significantly change serum hsCRP, TNF-alpha, IL-6, IL-10, or MCP-1, but differentially modulated LPS-stimulated monocyte gene expression and cytokine secretion; DHA showed broader monocyte cytokine effects than EPA. [source_artifact:pmid-33303222]

**Why it matters:** Separates serum biomarker outcomes from immune-cell response mechanisms.

**Potential experiment signals:** monocyte cytokine response, SPMs, serum hsCRP null, EPA vs DHA.

**Protocol takeaway:** Immune-cell pathway effects may occur even when serum CRP/IL-6/TNF markers do not move.

**Population mismatch:** Same-mechanism mechanistic evidence; not primary direct protocol efficacy evidence.

**Safety notes:** Three related registry dropouts occurred in the linked trial before active phases or for non-study/family reasons; detailed adverse events were not extracted here.

**Limitations:** Mechanistic endpoints and small sample size. Serum biomarker null results contrast with ex vivo monocyte response effects. High-dose single-component EPA/DHA periods differ from common mixed products.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
