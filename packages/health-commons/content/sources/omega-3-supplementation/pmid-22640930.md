This source is included for **inflammation_immune_markers**.

**Findings:** Omega-3 supplementation lowered inflammation relative to control; accessible summaries report TNF-alpha increased in controls while remaining stable or decreasing in omega-3 arms. [source_artifact:pmid-22640930]

**Why it matters:** Direct generally healthy adult RCT with positive inflammation findings.

**Potential experiment signals:** TNF-alpha, IL-6, overweight sedentary adults.

**Protocol takeaway:** Inflammatory-marker benefits may be more observable in higher-baseline-risk healthy adults.

**Population mismatch:** Direct protocol evidence, but population has age/body-composition features that may increase baseline inflammatory signal.

**Safety notes:** Adverse-event details were not extracted.

**Limitations:** Overweight sedentary middle-aged/older adults may not represent young healthy users. Exact dose values and all effect estimates should be verified from full text before quantitative protocol claims.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
