This source is included for **omega3_index_kinetics_formulation**.

**Findings:** Fingertip whole blood, plasma, and erythrocyte fatty acids all reflected n-3 intake, with dose-dependent increases after supplementation. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-33922507`.

**Why it matters:** This source informs both dose response and practical testing logistics.

**Potential experiment signals:** fingertip whole-blood EPA+DHA, plasma EPA+DHA, erythrocyte EPA+DHA, dose-response

**Safety notes:** Adverse events were not the extraction focus.

**Limitations / boundary:** Women-only sample.; Short 8-week biomarker trial.; Fingertip whole blood is a practical matrix but may not be interchangeable with lab-measured omega-3 index without validation. Population mismatch: Direct for EPA/DHA biomarker response and sample-matrix interpretation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Whole-blood fingertip testing may be useful for tracking change, but matrix-specific interpretation matters.
