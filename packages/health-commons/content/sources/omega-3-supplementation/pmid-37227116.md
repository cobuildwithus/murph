This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** The ACR guideline addresses exercise, rehabilitation, diet, and integrative interventions in rheumatoid arthritis and emphasizes conditional recommendations and shared decision-making. It is not direct evidence for healthy adults using oral EPA/DHA for exercise recovery.

**Why it matters:** It prevents inflammatory-disease guidance from being misused as general EPA/DHA evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** do-not-generalize disease-specific inflammation claims, clinical supervision flag.

**Protocol takeaway:** Background only; RA guideline evidence should not support general exercise-recovery or healthy-adult claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
