This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** DHA alone or with other nutrients did not modulate cerebral hemodynamics or cognitive function.

**Why it matters:** Helps preserve null findings and avoid multi-nutrient overattribution.

**Potential experiment signals:** cerebral hemodynamics, cognitive function.

**Protocol takeaway:** DHA supplementation did not show cognitive/hemodynamic benefit in this older-adult context.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy older adults; adjacent due DHA-only and multi-nutrient arms.

**Limitations:** Combination arms must be separated from DHA-only when synthesizing.; Participant count and exact duration not extracted here.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
