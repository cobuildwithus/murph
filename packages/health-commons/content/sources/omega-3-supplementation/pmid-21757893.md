This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** Marine omega-3 fatty acids reduced serum sICAM-1, but had no effect on serum systemic inflammation markers and oxidative stress in hemodialysis patients. Safety note: No adverse-event details extracted.

**Why it matters:** Included to preserve CKD/hemodialysis population mismatch and mixed biomarker effects.

**Potential experiment signals:** sICAM-1, systemic inflammation markers, oxidative stress.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as adjacent biomarker context with strong population caveat.

**Population mismatch:** Hemodialysis patients; safety/adjacent inflammation boundary only.

**Limitations:** Hemodialysis/CKD population is a strong population mismatch.; Inflammation/oxidative stress endpoints are not mood or cognition efficacy outcomes.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
