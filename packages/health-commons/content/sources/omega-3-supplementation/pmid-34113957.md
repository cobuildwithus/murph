This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** EPA-rich oil improved global accuracy and speed compared with placebo and DHA-rich oil; DHA-rich oil did not show the same global cognitive benefit. Safety note: Safety not extracted from accessible summaries.

**Why it matters:** Ingredient-comparison RCT is useful for EPA-vs-DHA cognitive subclaims.

**Potential experiment signals:** global cognitive accuracy, global cognitive speed, memory accuracy, prefrontal cortex oxygenated hemoglobin.

**Protocol takeaway:** Any cognition claim should specify EPA-rich formulation, young-adult population, and measured endpoints.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy young adults; not older-adult prevention or clinical treatment.

**Limitations:** Healthy-young-adult sample; may not extend to older or impaired populations.; Ingredient-comparison trial; not all EPA/DHA ratios are equivalent.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
