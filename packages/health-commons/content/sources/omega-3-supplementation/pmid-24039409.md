This source is included for **dry_eye_treatment_prevention**.

**Findings:** Reported conclusion: oral omega-3 fatty acids, 1.5 g/day, may be beneficial for MGD mainly by improving tear stability.

**Why it matters:** Central MGD subgroup trial often cited as positive despite formulation caveats.

**Potential experiment signals:** TBUT, OSDI, MGD score, lid-margin inflammation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Suggests a possible MGD subgroup signal, but co-intervention and antioxidant-containing product limit attribution to EPA/DHA alone.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch / boundary:** Direct to oral omega-3 in MGD but formulation/co-intervention mismatch for a broad wellness protocol.

**Safety notes:** No specific safety signal extracted.

**Limitations:** Small single-condition trial.; Supplement contained antioxidants and may not represent generic EPA/DHA capsules.; Both arms included lid hygiene..

**Artifact handling:** Source page draft only. PDF rights status is `open_access`; no copyrighted PDF is proposed for Git storage.
