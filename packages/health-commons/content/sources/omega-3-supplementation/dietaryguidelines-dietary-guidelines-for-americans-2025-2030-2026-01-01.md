**Findings:** The Dietary Guidelines are a food-pattern context source and should be used to bound, not prove, supplement protocol claims. [source_artifact:dietaryguidelines-dietary-guidelines-for-americans-2025-2030-2026-01-01].

**Why it matters:** Protocol pages should not treat seafood guidance as equivalent to oral EPA/DHA supplementation. [source_artifact:dietaryguidelines-dietary-guidelines-for-americans-2025-2030-2026-01-01].

**Potential experiment signals:** dietary seafood intake, EPA/DHA intake context, food-vs-supplement boundary

**Protocol takeaway:** Protocol pages should not treat seafood guidance as equivalent to oral EPA/DHA supplementation. [source_artifact:dietaryguidelines-dietary-guidelines-for-americans-2025-2030-2026-01-01].

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction limitations:**
- Guideline context only; not a clinical trial.
- Food-based seafood guidance cannot be converted into direct claims about supplement efficacy.
- Does not evaluate product oxidation, dose adherence, or supplement form.
