This source is included for **dry_eye_treatment_prevention**.

**Findings:** OSDI change was not significantly different: −13.9 omega-3 vs −12.5 placebo; mean difference −1.9 points (95% CI −5.0 to 1.1; p=0.21). Conjunctival staining, corneal staining, TBUT, and Schirmer changes also showed no significant differences.

**Why it matters:** Largest and highest-priority direct dry-eye treatment RCT; anchors null/mixed interpretation.

**Potential experiment signals:** OSDI, TBUT, Schirmer, staining, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Backbone negative evidence: oral EPA/DHA should not be presented as a reliably effective dry-eye treatment for general users.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch / boundary:** Direct clinical-treatment evidence, not a self-experiment prevention endpoint.

**Safety notes:** Adverse-event profiles were reported as similar between groups in available summaries.

**Limitations:** Concurrent usual dry-eye treatments allowed.; Olive-oil placebo may have biological effects.; Findings apply to symptomatic dry-eye treatment, not general wellness..
