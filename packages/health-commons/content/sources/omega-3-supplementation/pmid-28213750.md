This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** The high-EPA acute dose showed a significant group effect for squat-jump decrement and a similar trend for countermovement jump, while many functional and perceptual measures showed time effects without clear group interactions.

**Why it matters:** Important timing boundary: acute rescue dosing is not the same as a chronic supplementation protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** squat jump, countermovement jump, isokinetic strength, perceived soreness.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as acute-dosing context only; do not fold directly into chronic oral EPA/DHA expectations.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `direct_protocol`.

**Limitations and caveats:** Acute single-dose design. Male physically active sample. Functional signals were clearer than perceptual soreness outcomes.
