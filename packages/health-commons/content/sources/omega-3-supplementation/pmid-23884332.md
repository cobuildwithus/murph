This source is included for **dry_eye_treatment_prevention**.

**Findings:** At 24 weeks, OSDI was 21±4 vs 34±5 (p=0.05) and surface asymmetry index was lower with supplement (0.37±0.03 vs 0.51±0.03; p=0.005). No effect on tear production, TBUT, or corneal/conjunctival staining.

**Why it matters:** Preserves positive adjacent formula evidence and null objective tear/staining findings.

**Potential experiment signals:** OSDI, corneal smoothness, HLA-DR, CD11c, TBUT, Schirmer.

**Protocol takeaway:** Positive adjacent combined-fatty-acid evidence, but not assignable to EPA/DHA alone.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch / boundary:** Adjacent variant: mixed omega-6/omega-3 formula and postmenopausal KCS.

**Safety notes:** No specific adverse-event signal extracted.

**Limitations:** Small n=38.; Postmenopausal KCS phenotype.; Combination GLA plus n-3 product..
