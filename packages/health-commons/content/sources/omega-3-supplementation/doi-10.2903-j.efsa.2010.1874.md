This source is included for **safety_product_quality_oxidation_contaminants**.

**Findings:** EFSA recognized fish-oil oxidation/rancidity as a safety-quality issue but could not set universal refined-oil limits and excluded final consumer packages. [source_artifact:doi-10.2903/j.efsa.2010.1874].

**Why it matters:** Use to frame rancidity as a quality concern while respecting scope limits. [source_artifact:doi-10.2903/j.efsa.2010.1874].

**Potential experiment signals:** rancidity, peroxide value, anisidine value, storage conditions

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to frame rancidity as a quality concern while respecting scope limits. [source_artifact:doi-10.2903/j.efsa.2010.1874].

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Extraction limitations:**
- Scope excludes encapsulated final consumer supplements.
- No human clinical outcomes.
- Insufficient evidence to set universal rancidity limits for all refined fish oils.
