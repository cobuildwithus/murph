This source is included for **omega3_index_kinetics_formulation**.

**Findings:** The scoping review identified 58 studies and reported that study design, baseline omega-3 index, dose, duration, and formulation affect achieved omega-3 index; the authors’ practical recommendation was roughly 1,000-1,500 mg/day EPA+DHA as triglycerides for at least 12 weeks when increasing omega-3 index is the goal. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-36742439`.

**Why it matters:** This review provides a high-yield map of omega-3 index intervention heterogeneity and practical dosing context.

**Potential experiment signals:** omega-3 index, dose, duration, formulation, baseline omega-3 index

**Safety notes:** Not extracted as a safety review.

**Limitations / boundary:** Scoping review rather than quantitative meta-analysis.; Heterogeneous products, doses, diets, durations, and baseline statuses.; Useful for context but not a single direct effect estimate. Population mismatch: Directly relevant to omega-3 index interpretation, but interventions included diet and fortified foods as well as supplements.

**Protocol takeaway:** Protocol pages can cite this as context for why omega-3 index response depends on multiple design variables.
