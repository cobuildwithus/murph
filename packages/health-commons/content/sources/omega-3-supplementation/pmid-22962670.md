This source is included for **clinical_cardiovascular_lipid_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- The guideline recommends diagnosis based on fasting triglycerides and treats mild/moderate elevations differently from severe/very severe elevations associated with pancreatitis risk.
- It recommends lifestyle therapy as initial treatment for mild-to-moderate hypertriglyceridemia and non-HDL-C as a treatment goal in moderate hypertriglyceridemia.
- For severe or very severe hypertriglyceridemia, it recommends diet changes plus drug treatment and fibrate first-line for pancreatitis-risk reduction; n-3 fatty acids are one possible drug-class option in moderate-to-severe triglyceride elevations.

**Why it matters:** Gives concrete severity and treatment-goal boundaries for interpreting lipid-panel experiments.

**Potential experiment signals:** fasting triglycerides; non-HDL-C; pancreatitis-risk thresholds; secondary-cause review; family history; drug/supplement adverse tolerability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use lipid results to decide when to stop self-experiment framing and seek clinician-guided care.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
