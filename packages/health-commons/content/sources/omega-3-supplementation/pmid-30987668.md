This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** EPA-rich fish oil reduced several markers of exercise-induced muscle damage after eccentric elbow-flexor contractions compared with placebo.

**Why it matters:** One of the clearest direct protocol-supporting trials in this batch for muscle-damage recovery endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle stiffness, MVC torque, range of motion, soreness, arm circumference, muscle echo intensity.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports soreness/stiffness and strength/ROM tracking, while keeping expectations cautious because the trial is small and localized.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness:** `direct_protocol`.

**Limitations and caveats:** Small n=16 sample. Male-only, localized elbow-flexor model. Short post-exercise follow-up.
