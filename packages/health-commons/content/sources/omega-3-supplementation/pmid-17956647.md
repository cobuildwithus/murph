This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** Substantially increasing EPA+DHA intake for 3 months had no beneficial or harmful effects on mood; cognitive and other mood measures were similarly little affected.

**Why it matters:** Direct null counterweight for mood/cognition outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:** depressed mood, cognitive function, BDI, attentional bias.

**Protocol takeaway:** Adult depressed-mood RCT found no mood or cognitive benefit. Does not address all doses or clinical MDD adjunctive use.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Adults with mild to moderate depressed mood
- **Intervention/exposure:** EPA+DHA supplementation, about 1.5 g/day
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo
- **Duration/follow-up:** 3 months / 12 weeks
- **Endpoints:** depressed mood, cognitive function, BDI, attentional bias
- **Effect estimate or direction:** Substantially increasing EPA+DHA intake for 3 months had no beneficial or harmful effects on mood; cognitive and other mood measures were similarly little affected.
- **Adverse events / safety notes:** No beneficial or harmful mood effects were reported in accessible summary.
- **Population mismatch:** Direct to oral EPA/DHA and depressed mood, but not broad healthy-adult cognition/mood claims.
- **Artifact rights status:** permission_required

## Limitations

- Mild/moderate depressed mood population, not diagnosed MDD treatment.
- Detailed adverse-event table not extracted.
