This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** Long-chain omega-3 probably had little or no effect on new neurocognitive illness (RR 0.98, 95% CI 0.87-1.10), new cognitive impairment (RR 0.99, 95% CI 0.92-1.06), or MMSE global cognition (MD 0.10, 95% CI 0.03-0.16). Effects did not differ by dose, duration, intervention type, or replacement. Safety note: Safety/adverse events were not the focus of the extracted cognition results.

**Why it matters:** This broad randomized-trial synthesis is central for null/mixed cognition boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** new neurocognitive illness, new cognitive impairment, global cognition, executive function, processing speed, memory.

**Protocol takeaway:** Avoid promising protection from cognitive decline from oral EPA/DHA supplementation.

**Population mismatch:** Many included studies were older-adult prevention contexts; not directly a short-term nootropic trial.

**Limitations:** Aggregate cognition outcomes may obscure specific subgroup signals.; Focuses on prevention/cognition rather than mood or biomarkers.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
