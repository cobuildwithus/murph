This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** The review included 13 RCTs in physically healthy adults. Intervention durations ranged from 1 day to 26 weeks and doses from 250 mg/day to 6 g/day EPA/DHA. CK and LDH tended to be higher in control arms in several studies; CRP was higher in control in only some studies; IL-6 and GSH/GSSG showed favorable signals; TNF-alpha, DOMS, and sports performance were mixed or not clearly improved. The review also noted common omega-3 side effects such as belching, heartburn, stomach pain, vomiting, constipation, diarrhea, nausea, and taste changes, while three included studies reported no harm or side effects.

**Why it matters:** It covers the main exercise-recovery endpoint families and directly separates biomarker, soreness, oxidative, and performance claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** soreness score, perceived recovery, training-performance notes, CK/CRP if available, GI side-effect diary.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a balanced direct-protocol review: plausible biomarker benefits, mixed DOMS and performance, and more evidence needed.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
