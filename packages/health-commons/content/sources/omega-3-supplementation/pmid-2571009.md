This source is included for **cardiovascular_outcomes_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- **outcome / all-cause mortality after MI:** DART is historical dietary fish evidence, adjacent rather than direct supplement evidence. Effect/direction: Fish-advice group had about 29% lower 2-year all-cause mortality than no fish-advice control.

**Why it matters:** Often cited historically; needs clear separation from modern supplement trials.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- all-cause mortality
- myocardial reinfarction
- cardiac death

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not convert fish-advice mortality findings into supplement efficacy claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Source kind:** journal_article
- **Study design:** rct
- **Participants:** 2033 (reported)
- **Population:** men recovering from myocardial infarction
- **Intervention/exposure:** dietary advice to increase fatty fish intake, with fish oil used when needed; other fat/fibre advice arms
- **Comparator/control:** no specific fish advice/control dietary advice
- **Duration/follow-up:** 2 years
- **Endpoints:** all-cause mortality, myocardial reinfarction, cardiac death
- **Effect or direction:** DART reported about a 29% reduction in 2-year all-cause mortality among participants advised to eat fatty fish compared with those not advised.
- **Adverse events/safety:** No batch-specific adverse-event extraction.
- **Population mismatch:** Male MI survivors; dietary intervention rather than standardized EPA/DHA supplement.
- **Directness:** `adjacent_variant`
- **Artifact rights status:** `permission_required`

## Limitations

- Dietary advice/fish intake, not a pure supplement protocol.
- Post-MI male secondary-prevention population.
- Older trial era.
