This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** Meta-analysis showed an overall beneficial effect on depression symptoms (SMD -0.28; P=0.004). EPA-pure or EPA-major formulations showed benefit; DHA-pure or DHA-major formulations did not.

**Why it matters:** Useful for EPA-vs-DHA boundary framing in depression evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** depression symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Depression meta-analysis found a small overall benefit and EPA-favored subgroup pattern. Must be balanced against null/prevention and Cochrane findings.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** 26 studies of participants with depressive symptoms or depressive disorders
- **Intervention/exposure:** Omega-3 PUFA supplementation, with EPA- and DHA-related subgroup analyses
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo or control
- **Duration/follow-up:** Varied across included trials
- **Endpoints:** depression symptoms
- **Effect estimate or direction:** Meta-analysis showed an overall beneficial effect on depression symptoms (SMD -0.28; P=0.004). EPA-pure or EPA-major formulations showed benefit; DHA-pure or DHA-major formulations did not.
- **Adverse events / safety notes:** Adverse-event details were not extracted in this batch.
- **Population mismatch:** Mostly clinical or depressive-symptom populations; not a healthy adult mood claim.
- **Artifact rights status:** open_access

## Limitations

- Depression-treatment evidence, not general-wellness evidence.
- Ingredient subgroup findings should be balanced against Cochrane and prevention reviews.
