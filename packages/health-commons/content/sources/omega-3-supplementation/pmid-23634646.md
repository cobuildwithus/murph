This source is included for **safety_adverse_events**.

**Findings:** Ten RCTs involving 994 older adults used daily fish-oil doses ranging from 0.03 g to 1.86 g EPA and/or DHA for 6 to 52 weeks. No serious adverse events were reported; total adverse-event rates were numerically higher with fish oil but not statistically significant.

**Why it matters:** Older adults are more likely to have comorbidities and anticoagulant/antiplatelet exposure, so tolerability data from this age group helps define safety-watch boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** adverse events, serious adverse events, GI symptoms, bleeding symptoms, older-adult tolerability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as older-adult safety context: available RCT evidence reported no serious adverse events and only mild-to-moderate adverse-event concerns, but adverse-event reporting was limited.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness:** `safety_boundary` for `protocol_variant:omega-3-supplementation/oral-epa-dha-supplementation`.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Safety data were incompletely and inconsistently reported, higher-dose evidence was limited, and the included studies were not powered for rare serious events.
