**Why it matters:** Do not assume krill-oil, krill-meal, and fish-oil studies are interchangeable. [source_artifact:pmid-25884846].

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction limitations:**
- Adjacent krill-oil variant.
- Small healthy-volunteer sample.
- Single-dose bioavailability does not prove long-term clinical outcomes.
