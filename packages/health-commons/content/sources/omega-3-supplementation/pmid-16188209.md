This source is included for **variant_boundaries_external_context**.

**Findings:** ALA conversion to EPA is limited and conversion to DHA is very low in many adult settings, making ALA sources adjacent rather than direct EPA/DHA evidence. [source_artifact:pmid-16188209].

**Why it matters:** Protocol should not treat flax, chia, walnut, or ALA oil interventions as direct EPA/DHA supplementation. [source_artifact:pmid-16188209].

**Potential experiment signals:** blood EPA, blood DHA, omega-3 index, ALA intake

**Protocol takeaway:** Protocol should not treat flax, chia, walnut, or ALA oil interventions as direct EPA/DHA supplementation. [source_artifact:pmid-16188209].

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction limitations:**
- Narrative review with older studies and varying methods.
- ALA conversion is not the same exposure as preformed EPA/DHA.
- Sex, background diet, and fatty-acid pools influence interpretation.
