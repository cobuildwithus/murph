This source is included for **cardiovascular_outcomes_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- **null_result / mortality and cardiovascular events:** High-recall review anchors conservative hard-outcome language. Effect/direction: Cochrane: increasing EPA/DHA has little or no effect on deaths or cardiovascular events.
- **outcome / triglycerides:** Biomarker changes should be separated from clinical event reduction. Effect/direction: Review context notes triglyceride lowering while hard cardiovascular endpoints remained little/no effect.

**Why it matters:** High-recall systematic review that anchors cautious language around primary and secondary prevention.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- all-cause mortality
- cardiovascular events
- coronary death
- stroke
- arrhythmia
- triglycerides

**Protocol takeaway:** Hard CVD-event prevention should remain a boundary, not a protocol promise.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Source kind:** review
- **Study design:** meta_analysis
- **Participants:** 162796 (included_participants)
- **Population:** adults in primary- and secondary-prevention cardiovascular evidence streams
- **Intervention/exposure:** long-chain omega-3 supplements and other omega-3 exposures
- **Comparator/control:** placebo/usual diet/control
- **Duration/follow-up:** at least 12 months in included RCT evidence
- **Endpoints:** all-cause mortality, cardiovascular events, coronary death, stroke, arrhythmia, triglycerides
- **Effect or direction:** Cochrane review concluded that increasing EPA/DHA has little or no effect on deaths or cardiovascular events, and probably little/no effect on coronary death/events, stroke, or arrhythmia; triglyceride lowering was observed in some analyses.
- **Adverse events/safety:** Adverse-event streams were reviewed, but no source-specific safety signal was extracted here.
- **Population mismatch:** Primary and secondary prevention evidence mixed together; not only healthy adults taking oral EPA/DHA.
- **Directness:** `direct_protocol`
- **Artifact rights status:** `open_access`

## Limitations

- Broad review spans supplement, diet, and ALA evidence; endpoint-specific certainty differs.
- Hard cardiovascular endpoints are context-only for a wellness protocol.
- Clinical populations vary.
