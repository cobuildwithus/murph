This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** Re-esterified DHA/EPA supplementation lowered several inflammatory and muscle-damage biomarkers and soreness after exercise, but did not significantly improve isokinetic strength deficit.

**Why it matters:** Useful boundary source for trained endurance athletes and specialized EPA/DHA formulations.

**Potential experiment signals:** IL-1β, IL-6, CPK, LDH-5, soreness, isokinetic strength deficit.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not convert this specialized athlete-formulation trial into a general direct claim; use it as context for biomarkers and soreness.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `adjacent_variant`.

**Limitations and caveats:** Only 15 endurance athletes. Crossover design with specialized re-esterified, high-DHA formulation. Strength recovery did not clearly improve.
