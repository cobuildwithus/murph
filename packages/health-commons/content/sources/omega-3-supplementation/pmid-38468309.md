This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** A dose-response meta-analysis of 24 studies reported a potential executive-function benefit trend within the first 12 months, especially with daily n-3 PUFA intake exceeding 500 mg and up to 420 mg EPA; broad cognitive-enhancement effects remained doubtful. Safety note: Adverse events were not highlighted in accessible abstract snippets.

**Why it matters:** This source refines cognition boundaries in non-demented adults and supplies dose-response context.

**Potential experiment signals:** executive function, global cognition, cognitive domains.

**Protocol takeaway:** Executive function may be the most plausible cognition signal in non-demented middle-aged/older adults, but claims should remain cautious.

**Population mismatch:** Not a young healthy-adult cognition study; applies mainly to age ≥40 without dementia.

**Limitations:** Restricted to adults without dementia but still primarily middle-aged/older populations.; Findings emphasize executive-function trends rather than global cognitive benefit.; Dose-response interpretation depends on underlying trial heterogeneity.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
