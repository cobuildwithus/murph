This source is included for **safety_adverse_events**.

**Findings:** Primary outcome occurred in 12.0% with omega-3 CA vs 12.2% with corn oil (HR 0.99, 95% CI 0.90-1.09; P=0.84). AF was 2.2% vs 1.3% (P<0.001), GI adverse events 24.7% vs 14.7%, and TIMI major bleeding 0.8% vs 0.7%.

**Why it matters:** High-dose EPA+DHA formulation had no cardiovascular advantage and more AF/GI adverse events in this high-risk population.

**Potential experiment signals:** outcome:major-adverse-cardiovascular-events, adverse_event:atrial-fibrillation, symptom:gastrointestinal-adverse-effects, adverse_event:bleeding.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only evidence only: Primary outcome occurred in 12.0% with omega-3 CA vs 12.2% with corn oil (HR 0.99, 95% CI 0.90-1.09; P=0.84). AF was 2.2% vs 1.3% (P<0.001), GI adverse events 24.7% vs 14.7%, and TIMI major bleeding 0.8% vs 0.7%.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Clinical supervised high-dose EPA+DHA in high-risk patients, not routine oral EPA/DHA supplementation for wellness.

**Limitations:** Prescription carboxylic-acid formulation, high dose, high-risk statin-treated population, corn oil comparator; not a consumer supplement trial.
