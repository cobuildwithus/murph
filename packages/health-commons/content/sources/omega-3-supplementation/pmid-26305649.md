This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** In AREDS2, oral LCPUFA or lutein/zeaxanthin supplementation had no statistically significant effect on cognitive function. Safety note: Safety not extracted for this cognition finding.

**Why it matters:** Large RCT helps prevent overclaiming cognition benefits.

**Potential experiment signals:** cognitive function.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only null evidence because of AMD and multi-nutrient design.

**Population mismatch:** AMD trial participants are not representative of general supplement users.

**Limitations:** Disease-specific AMD/nutrient factorial design.; Multi-nutrient context makes omega-3 monotherapy interpretation less direct.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
