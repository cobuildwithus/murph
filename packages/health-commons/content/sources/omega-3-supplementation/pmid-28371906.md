**Findings:** A krill-oil RCT meta-analysis found lipid-marker reductions, but these are adjacent-variant findings and not direct oral EPA/DHA protocol claims. [source_artifact:pmid-28371906].

**Why it matters:** Keep krill oil separate unless protocol explicitly uses krill oil. [source_artifact:pmid-28371906].

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, triglycerides, total cholesterol

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep krill oil separate unless protocol explicitly uses krill oil. [source_artifact:pmid-28371906].

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction limitations:**
- Adjacent krill-oil variant.
- Small evidence base of seven trials.
- Lipid-marker changes should not be generalized to non-krill EPA/DHA products.
