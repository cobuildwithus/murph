This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** In 24 overweight or obese males, four weeks of omega-3 supplementation before cycling HIIT increased omega-3 index in the supplemented group and was associated with lower CK, reduced calf pain, and faster recovery of leg strength. CRP was not a strong positive signal in the extraction notes.

**Why it matters:** It is recent, direct, and open access, but strongly population- and challenge-specific.

**Potential experiment signals:** calf soreness, leg-strength recovery, CK if tested, omega-3 index.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports recovery after a specific HIIT challenge in overweight or obese males; do not generalize to all adults or all exercise types.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
