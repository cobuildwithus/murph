This source is included for **inflammation_immune_markers**.

**Findings:** Meta-regression found EPA:DHA ratio was not associated with most lipid outcomes, but higher EPA:DHA ratio was associated with greater CRP reduction; in 2-6 g/day studies, ratio was also associated with SBP. [source_artifact:pmid-33418065]

**Why it matters:** Helps distinguish dose from EPA:DHA composition as an implementation variable.

**Potential experiment signals:** EPA:DHA ratio, CRP, blood pressure, lipids.

**Protocol takeaway:** EPA:DHA product ratio may be worth recording in protocol metadata.

**Population mismatch:** Direct to EPA/DHA products but analyzes study-level ratios, not a randomized ratio assignment within a single protocol.

**Safety notes:** Safety outcomes were not extracted.

**Limitations:** Meta-regression is between-study and vulnerable to ecological confounding. Ratio findings do not prove that changing an individual product ratio causes the same effect.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
