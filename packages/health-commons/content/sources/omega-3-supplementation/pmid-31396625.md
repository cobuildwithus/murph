This source is included for **omega3_index_kinetics_formulation**.

**Findings:** Omega-3 index increased from roughly 4.9% to 8.1% in supplemented participants; baseline omega-3 index, EPA+DHA dose, and chemical formulation explained much of the response, with triglyceride formulations predicted to produce about one percentage point more omega-3 index gain than ethyl esters at comparable dose. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-31396625`.

**Why it matters:** This is a backbone dose-to-status source for estimating how oral EPA/DHA supplementation changes the omega-3 index.

**Potential experiment signals:** omega-3 index, erythrocyte EPA+DHA, dose-response slope, formulation-adjusted response

**Safety notes:** Not a safety-focused analysis; adverse events were not the extraction focus.

**Limitations / boundary:** Pooled model across heterogeneous trials rather than a single dosing protocol.; Predicts biomarker response, not clinical outcomes.; Individual response can differ from model predictions. Population mismatch: Low mismatch for biomarker kinetics, but included populations and protocols were heterogeneous.

**Protocol takeaway:** EPA+DHA dose, baseline status, and chemical form should be tracked when interpreting omega-3 index changes.
