This source is included for **cardiovascular_outcomes_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- **outcome / death, nonfatal MI, stroke after recent MI:** Historical positive post-MI evidence is adjacent and should not become a general wellness claim. Effect/direction: GISSI-Prevenzione reported significant benefit from n-3 PUFA over 3.5 years; vitamin E no benefit.
- **population / population mismatch:** Population and care context differ materially from general adult oral EPA/DHA supplementation. Effect/direction: Recent myocardial infarction survivors in an older secondary-prevention trial era.

**Why it matters:** Explains why older secondary-prevention narratives differ from newer primary-prevention trials.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- death
- nonfatal myocardial infarction
- stroke
- fatal cardiovascular events

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not generalize post-MI secondary-prevention benefits to ordinary oral EPA/DHA use.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
