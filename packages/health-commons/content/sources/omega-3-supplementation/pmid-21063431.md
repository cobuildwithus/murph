This source is included for **omega3_index_kinetics_formulation**.

**Findings:** Omega-3 index increased with both active forms, with a greater increase reported for the triglyceride form than for ethyl esters over long-term supplementation. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-21063431`.

**Why it matters:** The chemical form of EPA/DHA may alter achieved omega-3 index at similar active doses.

**Potential experiment signals:** omega-3 index, triglyceride vs ethyl ester form

**Safety notes:** Safety details were not extracted.

**Limitations / boundary:** Exact sample size and eligibility were not verified from the accessible record.; Formulation finding is biomarker-based.; Product-specific forms may not represent every triglyceride or ethyl-ester supplement. Population mismatch: Direct formulation evidence for oral EPA/DHA biomarker response.

**Protocol takeaway:** When comparing products, record whether EPA/DHA is in triglyceride/re-esterified triglyceride or ethyl-ester form.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`. Directness: `direct_protocol`.
