This source is included for **safety_adverse_events**.

**Findings:** A warfarin-treated patient had a significant INR rise after doubling fish-oil intake. The case supports education and monitoring for possible supplement-drug interactions, especially around dose changes.

**Why it matters:** Frequently cited low-level evidence for possible INR interaction that informs anticoagulant caution language.

**Potential experiment signals:** warfarin use, INR, bruising, bleeding, fish-oil dose changes.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as low-certainty anticoagulant warning: warfarin users should not change fish-oil dose without INR monitoring and clinician guidance.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness:** `safety_boundary` for `protocol_variant:omega-3-supplementation/oral-epa-dha-supplementation`.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Single uncontrolled case with potential confounding; stronger trial evidence generally does not show clinically significant anticoagulation changes at studied doses.
