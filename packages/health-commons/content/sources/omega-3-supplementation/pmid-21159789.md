This source is included for **inflammation_immune_markers**.

**Findings:** The 3.4 g/day dose lowered triglycerides by about 27% versus placebo, while inflammatory markers and endothelial function did not change; 0.85 g/day had no clear lipid effect. [source_artifact:pmid-21159789]

**Why it matters:** Directly separates lipid response from inflammatory-marker non-response.

**Potential experiment signals:** triglycerides, hsCRP, IL-6, TNF-alpha, dose response.

**Protocol takeaway:** Inflammation should not be assumed to improve even when triglycerides respond.

**Population mismatch:** Direct oral EPA+DHA evidence, but high-dose dose-response in a small hypertriglyceridemic sample.

**Safety notes:** Adverse-event details were not extracted.

**Limitations:** Small sample size. Moderate hypertriglyceridemia population may differ from general users. Inflammation markers may be insensitive over 8 weeks or at baseline levels.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
