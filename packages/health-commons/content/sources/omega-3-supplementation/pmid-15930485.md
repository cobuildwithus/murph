This source is included for **lipids_triglycerides_dose_response**.

**Findings:** DHA versus control: TG changes −43 vs −14 mg/dL (p=0.015) and −21% vs −7% (p=0.009); total cholesterol +12 vs +3 mg/dL and 6% vs 2%; LDL-C +17 vs +3 mg/dL and 12% vs 3%; TG/HDL-C ratio −1.33 vs −0.50; percentage of LDL cholesterol carried by small dense particles −10% vs −3%.

**Why it matters:** DHA-only lipid-response RCT in a low-HDL population.

**Potential experiment signals:** triglycerides, LDL-C, TG/HDL ratio, small dense LDL fraction, HDL-C

**Protocol takeaway:** DHA may improve TG-related markers while raising LDL-C; users should not rely on a single lipid endpoint.

**Population mismatch:** Selected below-average HDL-C population may differ from general supplement users.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event extraction available.

**Limitations:**
- Low-HDL selected population.
- DHA-only algal product.
- Short 6-week duration.
- LDL-C increased despite favorable TG/small dense LDL changes.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
