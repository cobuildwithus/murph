This source is included for **omega3_index_kinetics_formulation**.

**Findings:** Re-esterified triglyceride formulations showed higher short-term bioavailability than ethyl esters in accessible summaries; free-fatty-acid and natural triglyceride comparisons varied by comparator. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-20638827`.

**Why it matters:** Chemical form may affect absorption and early blood exposure.

**Potential experiment signals:** plasma EPA+DHA, formulation, bioavailability

**Safety notes:** Safety details were not extracted.

**Limitations / boundary:** Short bioavailability trial.; Same-mechanism context, not omega-3 index endpoint evidence.; Formulation comparisons may not generalize to all products. Population mismatch: Same-mechanism formulation evidence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Record chemical form and avoid assuming equal bioavailability across EPA/DHA products.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. Directness: `same_mechanism`.
