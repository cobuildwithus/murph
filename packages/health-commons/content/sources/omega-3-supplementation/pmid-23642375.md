This source is included for **dry_eye_treatment_prevention**.

**Findings:** Treatment vs placebo changes: TBUT 71% vs 3.3% (p<0.001), dry-eye symptoms 26% vs 4% (p=0.004), Schirmer 22.3% vs 5.1% (p=0.033).

**Why it matters:** Frequently cited early positive RCT with concrete dose and short duration.

**Potential experiment signals:** TBUT, OSDI, Schirmer.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports a possible short-term clinical dry-eye signal, but small size and short duration make it fragile compared with DREAM.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch / boundary:** Direct dry-eye treatment evidence but not prevention/wellness context.

**Safety notes:** No specific adverse-event signal extracted from abstract.

**Limitations:** Only 30 days of supplementation.; Small study.; Dry-eye symptomatic clinical population..
