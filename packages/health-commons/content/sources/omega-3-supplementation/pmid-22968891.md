This source is included for **cardiovascular_outcomes_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- **null_result / major cardiovascular outcomes:** Foundational 2012 meta-analysis provides null cardiovascular outcome context for omega-3 supplementation. Effect/direction: No significant association with reduced all-cause mortality, cardiac death, sudden death, MI, or stroke in 20 studies / 68,680 participants.

**Why it matters:** Preserves a historically important null/mixed synthesis so the protocol does not overstate CVD benefits.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- all-cause mortality
- cardiac death
- sudden death
- myocardial infarction
- stroke

**Protocol takeaway:** Cardiovascular-event reduction should not be claimed as a general oral EPA/DHA benefit from this source.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Limitations

- Older meta-analysis with heterogeneous doses, populations, and trial eras.
- Cardiovascular-event outcomes should remain context-only for wellness supplementation.
- Aggregate evidence does not identify who might respond.
