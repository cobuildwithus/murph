This source is included for **inflammation_immune_markers**.

**Findings:** Compared with control, DHA lowered CRP, IL-6, IL-18, and TNF-alpha and increased adiponectin; EPA lowered IL-6. DHA exceeded EPA for IL-18 reduction and adiponectin increase, while DHA-versus-EPA differences for CRP, IL-6, and TNF-alpha were not significant. [source_artifact:pmid-27281302]

**Why it matters:** Central direct trial for separating EPA-specific and DHA-specific inflammatory effects.

**Potential experiment signals:** CRP, IL-6, IL-18, TNF-alpha, adiponectin, EPA vs DHA.

**Protocol takeaway:** Component composition may matter for inflammatory markers.

**Population mismatch:** Direct EPA/DHA oral evidence, but higher-dose component-specific trial in a selected metabolic-risk cohort.

**Safety notes:** Adverse-event details were not extracted in this batch.

**Limitations:** Crossover trial in a selected at-risk population, not a general-population wellness trial. Component-specific effects may not translate to mixed EPA+DHA products. Some DHA-versus-EPA biomarker comparisons were not statistically significant.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
