This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** DHA improved learning and memory in age-related cognitive decline; accessible sources report improvement on Paired Associate Learning and plasma DHA approximately doubled with correlation to improved PAL. Safety note: DHA was reported well tolerated with no treatment-related serious adverse events in accessible summaries.

**Why it matters:** Classic MIDAS-style DHA cognition trial anchors positive age-related memory evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** CANTAB Paired Associate Learning, learning, memory, plasma DHA, safety.

**Protocol takeaway:** Consider as context for older adults with memory complaints, with clear population and ingredient caveats.

**Population mismatch:** Older adults with cognitive complaints/decline; adjacent to but not identical with general supplementation.

**Limitations:** DHA-only adjacent variant rather than combined EPA/DHA protocol.; Age-related cognitive decline population is not general healthy adult population.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
