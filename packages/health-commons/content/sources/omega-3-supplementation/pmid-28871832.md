This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** Fish oil added to a whey/leucine/carbohydrate supplement was associated with lower soreness area-under-curve than protein or carbohydrate comparators after eccentric exercise, while maximal force remained impaired during recovery.

**Why it matters:** Useful for athlete stack boundaries and for distinguishing direct fish-oil evidence from multi-nutrient products.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle soreness AUC, maximal force, creatine kinase.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent-variant context; do not attribute the effect solely to EPA/DHA.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `adjacent_variant`.

**Limitations and caveats:** Multi-nutrient stack includes whey protein, leucine, and carbohydrate. Competitive soccer population. PubMed title differs from ledger title and should be corrected downstream.
