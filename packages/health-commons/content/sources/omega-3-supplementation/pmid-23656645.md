This source is included for **cardiovascular_outcomes_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- **null_result / cardiovascular death or cardiovascular hospitalization:** Risk and Prevention found no clear outcome benefit in high-risk adults without prior MI. Effect/direction: 733/6239 (11.8%) vs 745/6266 (11.9%); adjusted HR 0.97, 95% CI 0.88-1.08; P=0.58.

**Why it matters:** Major large RCT that keeps event-reduction claims conservative.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- cardiovascular death
- hospital admission for cardiovascular causes
- myocardial infarction
- stroke

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not claim cardiovascular morbidity/mortality reduction based on this high-risk trial.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Source kind:** journal_article
- **Study design:** rct
- **Participants:** 12513 (reported)
- **Population:** Italian general-practice patients with multiple cardiovascular risk factors or atherosclerotic vascular disease but no prior myocardial infarction
- **Intervention/exposure:** 1 g/day n-3 fatty acids
- **Comparator/control:** placebo
- **Duration/follow-up:** median about 5 years
- **Endpoints:** cardiovascular death, hospital admission for cardiovascular causes, myocardial infarction, stroke
- **Effect or direction:** Primary endpoint occurred in 733 of 6239 n-3 participants (11.8%) and 745 of 6266 placebo participants (11.9%); adjusted HR 0.97, 95% CI 0.88-1.08; P=0.58.
- **Adverse events/safety:** No detailed adverse-event extraction in this batch.
- **Population mismatch:** Multiple cardiovascular risk factors/vascular disease without MI; not a general supplementation population.
- **Directness:** `direct_protocol`

## Limitations

- High-risk clinical population; not healthy adults.
- Event definitions were clinical and disease-focused.
