This source is included for **lipids_triglycerides_dose_response**.

**Findings:** In the analyzed sample, 4 g/day EPA+DHA for 12 weeks reduced TG by 26±4% and increased LDL-C by 13±3%; LDL1 increased 20%, LDL2 64%, LDL3 26%, and LDL4 17% (LDL4 not significant), with negligible phenotype A/B changes.

**Why it matters:** Direct RCT for TG and LDL-C/subfraction tradeoffs in hypertriglyceridemic adults.

**Potential experiment signals:** fasting triglycerides, LDL-C, LDL subfractions, LDL phenotype

**Protocol takeaway:** A 4 g/day EPA+DHA intervention can lower TG substantially while raising LDL-C, so full lipid panels are essential.

**Population mismatch:** Hypertriglyceridemic responder-like analysis sample differs from general supplement users.

**Safety notes:** Accessible abstract stated the supplementation was generally well tolerated.

**Limitations:**
- Secondary analysis sample of 42 adults; original trial enrolled 60 and 57 completed.
- Active fish-oil formulations were collapsed.
- Hypertriglyceridemic adults with significant TG lowering may overrepresent responders.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
