This source is included for **inflammation_immune_markers**.

**Findings:** High-dose omega-3 fatty acids modulated metabolism and inflammation in obesity without weight/body-composition changes; some markers returned toward baseline after one month off supplementation. [source_artifact:pmid-37215211]

**Why it matters:** Mechanistic boundary source for dose, obesity, and inflammatory/transcriptomic endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** obesity, high dose, inflammation, metabolism, washout.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports mechanistic plausibility and washout considerations.

**Population mismatch:** Same-mechanism population/dose boundary evidence.

**Safety notes:** Safety outcomes were not extracted.

**Limitations:** Obesity and high-dose design are major boundary conditions. Mechanistic/pre-post endpoints should not be treated as general clinical efficacy. Participant count was not extracted.
