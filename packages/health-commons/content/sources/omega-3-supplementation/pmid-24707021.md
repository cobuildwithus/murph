This source is included for **safety_adverse_events**.

**Findings:** Twenty-five adolescents were randomized to Lovaza providing approximately 3.36 g/day DHA+EPA or placebo for 6 months. TG levels declined in the active group but the between-group difference was not significant; side effects did not differ between groups.

**Why it matters:** Direct pediatric high-dose EPA+DHA trial with side-effect reporting.

**Potential experiment signals:** triglycerides, side effects, blood pressure, BMI, C-reactive protein, pediatric hypertriglyceridemia.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as pediatric high-dose boundary: 3.36 g/day EPA+DHA was well tolerated in a very small adolescent trial, but TG lowering was not significantly better than placebo.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness:** `safety_boundary` for `protocol_variant:omega-3-supplementation/oral-epa-dha-supplementation`.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Small sample, adolescent clinical population, high-dose prescription product, and limited power for rare adverse events.
