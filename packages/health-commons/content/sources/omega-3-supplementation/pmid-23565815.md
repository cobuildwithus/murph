This source is included for **inflammation_immune_markers**.

**Findings:** Batch rationale identifies this as a direct young-adult RCT with null inflammatory outcomes; exact biomarker estimates were not extracted. [source_artifact:pmid-23565815]

**Why it matters:** Prevents overgeneralizing positive inflammation findings from higher-risk groups.

**Potential experiment signals:** inflammatory markers, healthy young adults, null response.

**Protocol takeaway:** Protocol claims should allow for no measurable inflammatory change in healthy low-risk users.

**Population mismatch:** Direct oral fish-oil evidence, but young healthy low-risk participants may differ from cardiometabolic or inflammatory populations.

**Safety notes:** Safety notes were not extracted.

**Limitations:** Participant count, dose, and exact inflammation results were not extracted from accessible metadata. Young healthy population may have low baseline inflammation and limited room for improvement.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
