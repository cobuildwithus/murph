This source is included for **inflammation_immune_markers**.

**Findings:** The review concluded that EPA/DHA-containing supplements modulate cardiovascular risk factors, including anti-inflammatory effects; exact inflammatory estimates were not extracted. [source_artifact:pmid-28675488]

**Why it matters:** Broad direct supplement meta-analysis bridging lipid and inflammation endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** triglycerides, inflammatory markers, blood pressure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports protocol monitoring of lipids and selected inflammatory markers as biologically relevant endpoints.

**Population mismatch:** Direct supplement format, but pooled CVD risk-factor trials may differ from a low-risk self-experiment population.

**Safety notes:** Safety endpoints were not extracted from the accessible metadata.

**Limitations:** Inflammation is one of several cardiovascular risk-factor domains, not the only focus. Exact inflammatory-marker effect sizes were not available in the batch extraction.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
