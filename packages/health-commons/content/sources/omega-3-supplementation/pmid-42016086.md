This source is included for **variant_boundaries_external_context**.

**Findings:** EFSA safety context supports cautious dose ceilings: no formal UL, but no identified adult safety concern up to 5 g/day supplemental EPA+DHA. [source_artifact:pmid-42016086].

**Why it matters:** Dose-boundary language can reference adult safety context without treating 5 g/day as a recommended dose. [source_artifact:pmid-42016086].

**Potential experiment signals:** EPA+DHA dose, bleeding events, LDL-C, glucose homeostasis, immune markers

**Protocol takeaway:** Dose-boundary language can reference adult safety context without treating 5 g/day as a recommended dose. [source_artifact:pmid-42016086].

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction limitations:**
- Safety assessment, not efficacy evidence.
- Insufficient data to set a formal UL.
- Adult safety context does not override clinician management for anticoagulants, atrial fibrillation risk, pregnancy, or disease states.
