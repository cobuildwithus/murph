This source is included for **omega3_index_kinetics_formulation**.

**Findings:** Omega-3 index increased dose-dependently across EPA+DHA doses, supporting oral EPA+DHA dose as a determinant of erythrocyte omega-3 status. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-24252845`.

**Why it matters:** This trial directly informs expected omega-3 index changes across practical daily EPA+DHA doses.

**Potential experiment signals:** omega-3 index, erythrocyte EPA+DHA, dose-response, body weight/determinants

**Safety notes:** Adverse events were not the extraction focus.

**Limitations / boundary:** Healthy young adult population may not represent older or clinical populations.; Biomarker endpoint rather than clinical endpoint.; Individual determinants beyond dose can affect response. Population mismatch: Low mismatch for direct protocol biomarker response.

**Protocol takeaway:** EPA+DHA dose should be specified in mg/day and paired with status testing when possible.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`. Directness: `direct_protocol`.
