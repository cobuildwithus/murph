This source is included for **omega3_index_kinetics_formulation**.

**Findings:** Plasma phospholipid DHA and EPA bioavailability from microalgal oil was reported as statistically non-inferior to fish oil, supporting microalgal oil as a bioavailable EPA/DHA source. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-41096614`.

**Why it matters:** This directly addresses whether non-fish EPA/DHA products can raise blood EPA/DHA comparably to fish oil.

**Potential experiment signals:** plasma phospholipid EPA, plasma phospholipid DHA, source comparison

**Safety notes:** Safety details were not extracted beyond the randomized controlled design.

**Limitations / boundary:** Recent source; extraction relied on accessible abstract/full-text metadata.; Endpoint was plasma phospholipid bioavailability, not long-term clinical outcomes. Population mismatch: Direct oral EPA/DHA evidence; applies especially to source/formulation choice.

**Protocol takeaway:** Microalgal EPA/DHA can be treated as a direct source variant, with product-specific dose verification.
