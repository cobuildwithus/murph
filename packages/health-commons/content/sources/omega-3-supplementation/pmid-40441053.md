This source is included for **safety_adverse_events**.

**Findings:** Twenty RCTs with 1,615 participants were included. Omega-3 PUFA improved GGT and ultrasound-assessed hepatic steatosis but did not significantly affect AST, ALT, MRI/MRS fat measures, hepatic stiffness, or histology. Overall adverse events were more likely in omega-3 groups, although reporting was limited.

**Why it matters:** Recent liver-disease meta-analysis gives hepatic biomarker and adverse-event context for users with NAFLD/MASLD.

**Potential experiment signals:** ALT, AST, GGT, hepatic steatosis, overall adverse events, NAFLD/MASLD status.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as liver-disease safety boundary: some liver markers improved but overall adverse events were more likely in omega-3 groups and AE reporting was limited.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness:** `safety_boundary` for `protocol_variant:omega-3-supplementation/oral-epa-dha-supplementation`.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Substantial heterogeneity, limited ultrasound-based studies, publication bias concerns, and insufficient AE reporting for confident long-term safety estimates.
