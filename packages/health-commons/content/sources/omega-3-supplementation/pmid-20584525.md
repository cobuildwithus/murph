This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** A trend toward superiority of omega-3 over placebo in reducing depressive symptoms was observed; the study is not a decisive positive monotherapy finding. Safety note: Treatment was reported well tolerated, with 83.6% completing the planned 8-week study at recommended dosage in accessible summaries.

**Why it matters:** Major-depression RCT anchors clinical mood-claim boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** depressive symptoms, major depressive episode.

**Protocol takeaway:** Mood claims should remain cautious and clinical-depression contexts require clinician care.

**Population mismatch:** Clinician-managed MDD/MDE treatment boundary.

**Limitations:** Clinical major depression, not general mood wellness.; Trend-level result; avoid overclaiming.; Placebo contained a small amount of fish oil.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
