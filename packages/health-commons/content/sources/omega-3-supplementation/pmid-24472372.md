This source is included for **safety_adverse_events**.

**Findings:** The review frames omega-3 effects on platelet and hemostasis pathways while reporting that clinical observations did not show increased clinically significant bleeding in monotherapy or combination-therapy settings.

**Why it matters:** Mechanistic bridge source for explaining why platelet-function concerns do not automatically equal clinical bleeding risk.

**Potential experiment signals:** platelet aggregation, bleeding symptoms, antiplatelet therapy, anticoagulant therapy.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mechanism/context only: n-3 fatty acids can affect hemostasis markers, but reviewed clinical observations did not show clinically significant bleeding increases.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness:** `same_mechanism` for `protocol_variant:omega-3-supplementation/oral-epa-dha-supplementation`.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Narrative synthesis may be selective and does not provide a single pooled effect estimate.
