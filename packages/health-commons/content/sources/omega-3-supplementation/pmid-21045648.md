This source is included for **dry_eye_treatment_prevention**.

**Findings:** Reported: 70% of omega-3 participants became asymptomatic versus 37% (or 7% in one news report) of placebo participants; Schirmer/fluorophotometry suggested increased tear secretion, while meibum lipid composition and aqueous tear evaporation did not differ.

**Why it matters:** Foundational early mixed RCT that cautions against over-reading symptom improvements.

**Potential experiment signals:** symptoms, Schirmer, tear volume, evaporation, meibum lipid composition.

**Protocol takeaway:** Early mixed signal: symptom and tear-volume changes do not establish a consistent dry-eye mechanism.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch / boundary:** Direct dry-eye trial but small and includes ALA/flaxseed oil.

**Safety notes:** No specific adverse-event signal extracted.

**Limitations:** Pilot sample.; Combination fish oil plus flaxseed oil.; Some secondary reports disagree on placebo asymptomatic percentage; use full text before precise synthesis..
