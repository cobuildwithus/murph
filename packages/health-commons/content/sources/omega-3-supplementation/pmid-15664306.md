This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** No evidence that fish oil improved mood compared with placebo despite increased circulating omega-3.

**Why it matters:** Direct null source for community-relevant depressed mood.

**Potential experiment signals:** depression ratings, mood.

**Protocol takeaway:** Nonclinical depression fish-oil trial found no mood improvement. Small early trial.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** People with non-clinical depression
- **Intervention/exposure:** Fish oil supplementation; one extracted table reports 0.6 g EPA plus 2.4 g DHA daily
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo
- **Duration/follow-up:** 12 weeks
- **Endpoints:** depression ratings, mood
- **Effect estimate or direction:** No evidence that fish oil improved mood compared with placebo despite increased circulating omega-3.
- **Adverse events / safety notes:** Adverse-event details were not extracted.
- **Population mismatch:** Direct to nonclinical mood but limited sample and formulation.
- **Artifact rights status:** unknown

## Limitations

- Early small trial.
- Nonclinical depression population.
- Dose/formulation may differ from common EPA-forward products.
