This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** Neither EPA-enriched nor DHA-enriched n-3 was superior to placebo for MDD; response and remission rates were in similar ranges across groups.

**Why it matters:** Strong ingredient-comparison null source.

**Potential experiment signals:** MDD symptoms, response, remission, adverse discontinuation.

**Protocol takeaway:** EPA- vs DHA-enriched MDD monotherapy trial found neither superior to placebo. Clinical MDD monotherapy only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Adults with DSM-IV major depressive disorder and baseline HDRS-17 score >=15
- **Intervention/exposure:** EPA-enriched n-3 1000 mg/day or DHA-enriched n-3 1000 mg/day as monotherapy
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo
- **Duration/follow-up:** 8 weeks
- **Endpoints:** MDD symptoms, response, remission, adverse discontinuation
- **Effect estimate or direction:** Neither EPA-enriched nor DHA-enriched n-3 was superior to placebo for MDD; response and remission rates were in similar ranges across groups.
- **Adverse events / safety notes:** One EPA-enriched participant discontinued due to worsening depression; one placebo participant discontinued due an unspecified negative reaction to pills.
- **Population mismatch:** Clinical MDD monotherapy evidence; adjacent variant.
- **Artifact rights status:** unknown

## Limitations

- Clinical monotherapy MDD trial.
- Does not test adjunctive antidepressant use.
- Population mismatch for wellness users.
