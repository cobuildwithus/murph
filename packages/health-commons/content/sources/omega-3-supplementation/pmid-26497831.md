This source is included for **inflammation_immune_markers**.

**Findings:** Erythrocyte EPA+DHA rose substantially, but CRP, IL-6, and ex vivo proinflammatory cytokine production were not significantly affected. [source_artifact:pmid-26497831]

**Why it matters:** Strong direct null evidence for healthy-adult inflammatory biomarkers.

**Potential experiment signals:** CRP, IL-6, cytokine production, omega-3 status.

**Protocol takeaway:** A protocol should not promise CRP or IL-6 improvements in healthy users.

**Population mismatch:** Direct oral EPA+DHA evidence in healthy adults; good protocol match but low baseline inflammation may limit outcome sensitivity.

**Safety notes:** Adverse-event details were not extracted from the batch source material.

**Limitations:** Healthy participants may not have elevated inflammatory markers at baseline. Null results apply to the tested dose and 18-week window.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
