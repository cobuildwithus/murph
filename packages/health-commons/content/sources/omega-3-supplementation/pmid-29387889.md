This source is included for **cardiovascular_outcomes_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- **null_result / fatal/nonfatal CHD and major vascular events:** Large collaborative meta-analysis argues against routine CVD-event prevention claims. Effect/direction: No significant association in 10 large RCTs involving 77,917 individuals.

**Why it matters:** Balances later dose-response-positive meta-analyses with a large null synthesis.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- fatal coronary heart disease
- nonfatal myocardial infarction
- major vascular events
- stroke

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not frame routine oral EPA/DHA as proven to prevent major vascular events.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
