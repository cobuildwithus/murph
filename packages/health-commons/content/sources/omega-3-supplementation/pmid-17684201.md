This source is included for **lipids_triglycerides_dose_response**.

**Findings:** At 45 days, DHA decreased fasting TG by 24%, large VLDL by 92%, IDL by 53%, and VLDL diameter by 11.1 nm; it increased LDL-C by 12.6%, small VLDL by 133%, large LDL by 120%, and LDL diameter by 0.6 nm. Postprandial AUC was similar, while small dense LDL particles declined 21%; no further change occurred after 45 days in extracted abstract.

**Why it matters:** DHA-only RCT with fasting and postprandial lipid-profile outcomes in hypertriglyceridemic adults.

**Potential experiment signals:** fasting triglycerides, postprandial lipids, LDL-C, LDL particle size, VLDL/IDL fractions

**Protocol takeaway:** DHA can materially improve TG-rich lipoproteins, but LDL-C increases are a key thing to watch.

**Population mismatch:** Hypertriglyceridemic men at high DHA dose may not match general supplement users.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event extraction available in accessible abstract text.

**Limitations:**
- Men only.
- Hypertriglyceridemic population.
- DHA-only high-dose oil rather than mixed EPA+DHA.
- LDL-C increased while other lipoprotein features improved.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
