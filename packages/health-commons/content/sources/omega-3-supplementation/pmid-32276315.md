This source is included for **omega3_index_kinetics_formulation**.

**Findings:** EPA+DHA increased significantly in the 0.7 and 1.0 g/day groups across erythrocytes, plasma, and whole blood; the 1.0 g/day group increased erythrocyte EPA+DHA from about 5.69% to 7.59%, plasma from 2.94% to 5.48%, and whole blood from 3.81% to 6.03%, with high inter-individual variability. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-32276315`.

**Why it matters:** Variability is a key “things to watch” issue for self-experiment interpretation.

**Potential experiment signals:** erythrocyte EPA+DHA, plasma EPA+DHA, whole-blood EPA+DHA, responder variability

**Safety notes:** Adverse events were not the extraction focus.

**Limitations / boundary:** Women-only sample.; High variability means a single expected-change estimate may mislead.; Biomarker outcomes only. Population mismatch: Direct biomarker response evidence, with sex/population limits.

**Protocol takeaway:** Dose raises group-average EPA+DHA status, but individual testing is useful because response varies.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`. Directness: `direct_protocol`.
