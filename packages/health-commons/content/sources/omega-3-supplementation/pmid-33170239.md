This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** Across six primary endpoints—blood pressure, nonvertebral fractures, physical performance, cognitive performance, and infections—interventions did not show statistically significant clinical benefits.

**Why it matters:** Important broad null boundary to avoid projecting omega-3 exercise-recovery findings into unrelated clinical outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:** blood pressure, fractures, SPPB, MoCA, infections.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as older-adult clinical context and population-mismatch boundary for exercise-recovery pages.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `direct_protocol`.

**Limitations and caveats:** Not a DOMS or muscle-damage trial. Older-adult population differs from exercise-recovery self-tracking users. Factorial design with vitamin D and exercise co-interventions.
