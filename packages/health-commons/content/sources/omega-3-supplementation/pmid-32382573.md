This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** The review concluded that, based on low-quality evidence, omega-3 PUFA supplementation did not produce a clinically important reduction in muscle soreness after eccentric exercise. This source is a key null/mixed anchor for soreness claims.

**Why it matters:** It directly challenges simplistic claims that omega-3 reliably reduces post-exercise soreness.

**Potential experiment signals:** DOMS score, range of motion, return-to-baseline function.

**Protocol takeaway:** Direct soreness evidence remains uncertain; do not promise clinically important soreness reduction.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
