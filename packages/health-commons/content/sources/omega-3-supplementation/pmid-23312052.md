This source is included for **clinical_cardiovascular_lipid_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- MARINE randomized 229 patients with triglycerides at least 500 mg/dL to icosapent ethyl 4 g/day, 2 g/day, or placebo for 12 weeks.
- The 4 g/day arm reduced large VLDL, total LDL, small LDL, and total HDL particle concentrations and VLDL particle size compared with placebo; related reports found triglyceride reductions without a significant LDL-C increase.
- The intervention was high-purity prescription EPA, not mixed EPA+DHA.

**Why it matters:** Helps distinguish EPA-only severe-TG treatment from ordinary mixed EPA/DHA supplement use.

**Potential experiment signals:** triglycerides; LDL-C; ApoB; VLDL and LDL particle concentrations; dose; severe-TG clinical status.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not generalize MARINE to non-prescription EPA/DHA; use it to explain high-dose prescription TG treatment boundaries.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
