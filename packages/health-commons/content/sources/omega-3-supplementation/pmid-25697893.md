This source is included for **dry_eye_treatment_prevention**.

**Findings:** After 3 months, 70% of omega-3 participants were symptom-free versus 14.9% placebo; TBUT increase was 3.2±1.8 s vs 0.4±0.6 s (p<0.001).

**Why it matters:** Large screen-user subgroup trial with dose and symptom-free proportion.

**Potential experiment signals:** symptoms, TBUT, Schirmer, Nelson grade.

**Protocol takeaway:** Screen-use subgroup may be relevant to some self-experimenters but remains clinical phenotype evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch / boundary:** Adjacent screen-use dry-eye subtype; not general dry-eye efficacy.

**Safety notes:** No specific adverse-event signal extracted.

**Limitations:** Specific screen-use phenotype.; Participant counts in reports include randomized and analyzed numbers; here n=456 from 220+236 groups.; Study-specific DESS symptoms..
