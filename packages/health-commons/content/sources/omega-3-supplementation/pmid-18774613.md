This source is included for **lipids_triglycerides_dose_response**.

**Findings:** Fish oil reduced fasting triglycerides by −0.34 mmol/L (95% CI −0.41 to −0.27), showed a small LDL-C increase of 0.06 mmol/L (95% CI 0.03 to 0.09), and did not show significant total cholesterol or HDL-C differences in the extracted review.

**Why it matters:** Foundational quantitative review for hyperlipidemia and dose/baseline triglyceride response.

**Potential experiment signals:** fasting triglycerides, LDL-C, HDL-C, total cholesterol, GI tolerance

**Protocol takeaway:** Fish oil’s lipid effect is most consistent for triglycerides; LDL-C and GI tolerability should be watched.

**Population mismatch:** Hyperlipidemia/risk-factor trial populations may have higher baseline triglycerides than typical wellness users.

**Safety notes:** Gastrointestinal adverse events were reported; no significant serious adverse events were identified in the extracted review summary.

**Limitations:**
- Considerable heterogeneity and reporting limitations.
- Publication bias was suspected for several lipid endpoints.
- Hyperlipidemia/risk-factor populations differ from low-risk self-experiment users.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
