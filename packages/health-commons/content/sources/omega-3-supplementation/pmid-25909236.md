This source is included for **dry_eye_treatment_prevention**.

**Findings:** Contrast sensitivity improved in 7/8 testing conditions in the omega-3 group versus 3/8 in placebo; OSDI and dry-eye signs improved in both groups, with more improvement reported in treatment.

**Why it matters:** Adds visual-function endpoint to dry-eye/MGD evidence base.

**Potential experiment signals:** contrast sensitivity, OSDI, TBUT, Schirmer, meibum quality.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports possible visual-function/MGD context but not a standalone wellness endpoint.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch / boundary:** Direct MGD clinical treatment context, not broad prevention/wellness.

**Safety notes:** No specific adverse-event signal extracted.

**Limitations:** Moderate MGD-specific population.; Background therapy and symptom improvements in both arms.; Effect estimates beyond direction not extracted..
