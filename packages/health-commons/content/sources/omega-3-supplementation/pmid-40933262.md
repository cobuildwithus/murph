This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** Both EPA-rich and DHA-rich arms increased omega-3 index above 8% and reduced submaximal heart rate and RPE; all groups improved time-trial performance without significant between-group performance gains.

**Why it matters:** Modern support for omega-3 index and exercise-physiology signals, but not a direct soreness endpoint source.

**Potential experiment signals:** omega-3 index, submaximal heart rate, RPE, RER, 24-km time trial.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for physiology and component-comparison context, not as proof of endurance performance or soreness benefit.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness:** `adjacent_variant`.

**Limitations and caveats:** Male endurance-trained amateurs only. Not a DOMS/muscle-damage trial. Performance improved across all groups without clear omega-3 superiority.
