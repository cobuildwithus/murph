This source is included for **clinical_cardiovascular_lipid_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- The review included 32 randomized studies with 15,903 participants and trial durations from 4 to 48 weeks.
- Omega-3 monotherapy decreased triglycerides, total cholesterol, VLDL-C, and non-HDL-C but increased LDL-C and HDL-C; ApoB and ApoAI were not significantly changed.
- Omega-3 plus statin therapy decreased triglycerides, total cholesterol, VLDL-C, non-HDL-C, ApoB, and ApoAI, while LDL-C and HDL-C were not significantly changed; the authors emphasized high heterogeneity and the need for cautious interpretation.

**Why it matters:** Supports a mixed lipid-monitoring stance and identifies LDL-C/non-HDL-C/ApoB as important watchpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** triglycerides; non-HDL-C; LDL-C; HDL-C; VLDL-C; ApoB; ApoAI; statin use; adverse event tracking.

**Protocol takeaway:** Measure more than triglycerides; LDL-C, non-HDL-C, and ApoB can move in different directions depending on formulation and statin context.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
