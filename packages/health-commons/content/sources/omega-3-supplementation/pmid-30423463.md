This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** BDI-II scores improved from baseline in both placebo and omega-3 groups, but there was no significant between-group difference in BDI-II change after 12 weeks (reported difference 0.7; 95% CI -0.7 to 2.1; p=0.30). Safety note: No safety finding extracted.

**Why it matters:** Combined-intervention depression trial must not be treated as omega-3 monotherapy evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** BDI-II depressive symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use this as support for a standalone mood benefit.

**Population mismatch:** Clinical/subclinical depression intervention context; adjacent to general wellness.

**Limitations:** Combined psychological-intervention trial, not clean monotherapy.; Workplace mild-to-moderate depression context.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
