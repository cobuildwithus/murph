This source is included for **clinical_cardiovascular_lipid_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- The pathway defines persistent hypertriglyceridemia after lifestyle intervention, stable maximally tolerated statin therapy when indicated, and management of secondary causes.
- For adults without ASCVD or diabetes, the ACC summary states there is no indication for nonstatin triglyceride-lowering treatment for mild to moderate elevations; prescription omega-3 or fibrate consideration appears in higher-risk or severe triglyceride strata.
- The extraction treats this as a clinical boundary source, not evidence that routine oral EPA/DHA supplements improve cardiovascular outcomes.

**Why it matters:** Defines the safety and escalation boundary between self-tracked supplementation and clinician-managed hypertriglyceridemia care.

**Potential experiment signals:** fasting triglycerides; LDL-C and non-HDL-C; diabetes/ASCVD risk category; alcohol intake; secondary-cause review; clinician referral triggers.

**Protocol takeaway:** Elevated or persistent triglycerides should be interpreted against guideline thresholds and risk context; severe or persistent elevations belong in medical care.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
