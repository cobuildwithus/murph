This source is included for **dry_eye_treatment_prevention**.

**Findings:** Population: Human dry eye disease participants in RCTs comparing omega-3 supplementation with placebo. Intervention/exposure: Omega-3 fatty acid supplementation. Comparator/control: Placebo in RCTs. Duration/follow-up: Varied across included trials. Endpoints: OSDI, TBUT, corneal staining, Schirmer test. Effect/direction: Data synthesis showed improvement in mean change in OSDI from baseline to final assessment, but no evident improvement in corneal staining, TBUT, or Schirmer score. Safety/adverse events: No safety signal extracted from accessible abstract text.

**Why it matters:** It triangulates positive meta-analytic symptom findings against objective null/mixed outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:** OSDI, TBUT, corneal staining, Schirmer test.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as context-only dry-eye evidence with symptom/sign distinction.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Eight RCTs and 1107 subjects only.; No included study achieved low risk of bias.; Considerable heterogeneity was reported for all four outcome measures. Population mismatch: Dry-eye treatment trials; not prevention and not a broad wellness population.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
