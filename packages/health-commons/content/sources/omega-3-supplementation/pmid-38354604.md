This source is included for **safety_adverse_events**.

**Findings:** The position statement summarizes evidence that omega-3-rich foods or supplements are associated with reduced preterm-birth risk and recommends pregnancy-specific supply for those with inadequate DHA intake/status.

**Why it matters:** Professional obstetric position statement reinforces that omega-3 in pregnancy is a specialized risk-reduction intervention, not a generic wellness supplement claim.

**Potential experiment signals:** pregnancy status, DHA intake, preterm birth risk, gestational timing, obstetric care.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as obstetric position-statement context: pregnancy use should be clinician-guided and separated from the adult self-experiment protocol.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness:** `safety_boundary` for `protocol_variant:omega-3-supplementation/oral-epa-dha-supplementation`.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Guideline/position source; evidence and recommendations are pregnancy-specific and should not be transferred to non-pregnant adult protocols.
