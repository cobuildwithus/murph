This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** In 16 moderately trained young males, high-dose fish oil for four weeks before eccentric exercise failed to modulate muscle-damage and repair indices during acute recovery. This is a direct negative boundary source.

**Why it matters:** It directly opposes blanket recovery claims and suggests training status may change observed benefit.

**Potential experiment signals:** CK if tested, myoglobin if tested, strength recovery, training status notes.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not claim fish oil reliably improves recovery in trained males; training status is a key boundary.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
