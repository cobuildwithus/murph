This source is included for **safety_adverse_events**.

**Findings:** Six finned fish-allergic subjects had negative testing/challenges to the fish-oil products studied. This suggests purified fish-oil products may be tolerated by some fish-allergic patients, but the sample is very small.

**Why it matters:** Sparse direct evidence for fish-oil tolerability in people with finned fish allergy.

**Potential experiment signals:** fish allergy, shellfish allergy, skin-test response, oral challenge reaction, anaphylaxis plan.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as allergy boundary evidence: six fish-allergic subjects tolerated tested fish-oil products, but the sample was too small to declare all products safe.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness:** `safety_boundary` for `protocol_variant:omega-3-supplementation/oral-epa-dha-supplementation`.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Very small n, product-specific testing, short challenge window, and no guarantee that other fish-oil supplements are equivalently free of allergenic protein.
