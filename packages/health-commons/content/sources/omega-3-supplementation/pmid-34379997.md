This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** Eight weeks of DHA-rich fish oil increased omega-3 index and slowed resting heart rate, but did not alter heart-rate responsiveness or HRV during autonomic reflex challenges.

**Why it matters:** Useful for measurement context: omega-3 index can move over 8 weeks and resting heart rate may be a possible secondary signal.

**Potential experiment signals:** omega-3 index, resting heart rate, HRV, autonomic reflex modulation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for biomarker and physiology context only; it is not a muscle-damage recovery trial.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness:** `same_mechanism`.

**Limitations and caveats:** No exercise-recovery or soreness endpoint. Small young healthy-adult sample. DHA-rich formulation may not generalize to EPA-rich fish oil.
