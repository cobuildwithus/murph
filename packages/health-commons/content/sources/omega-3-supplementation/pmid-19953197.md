This source is included for **omega3_index_kinetics_formulation**.

**Findings:** EPA+DHA supplement use and fish consumption were leading determinants of blood-cell omega-3 content, alongside triglycerides, age, and cholesterol history; accessible summaries indicate intake variables explained roughly half of variability. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-19953197`.

**Why it matters:** Response interpretation requires more than dose alone.

**Potential experiment signals:** blood-cell omega-3, supplement use, fish intake, triglycerides, age

**Safety notes:** Not applicable.

**Limitations / boundary:** Observational design; no causal supplementation inference.; Outpatient population may not represent healthy adults. Population mismatch: Background determinant context only.

**Protocol takeaway:** Baseline determinants and co-exposures should be documented in omega-3 experiments.
