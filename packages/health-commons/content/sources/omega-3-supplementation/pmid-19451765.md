This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** No treatment differences were reported before exercise, immediately after, or at 24 hours for pain and range of motion; differences emerged at 48 hours, and thigh circumference differed at 24 and 48 hours.

**Why it matters:** Boundary source for early DOMS evidence and timing: symptom differences may appear late rather than immediately.

**Potential experiment signals:** perceived pain, range of motion, thigh circumference.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use cautiously as older direct DOMS context in untrained men, not as a modern high-confidence dosing claim.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `direct_protocol`.

**Limitations and caveats:** Full text not available in retrieved material. Older small male-only trial. Supplement duration and complete dose details were not fully verified from accessible text.
