This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** Fish oil increased blood EPA+DHA and improved selected bench-press and relative squat strength outcomes beyond placebo, while lean mass and body-fat changes also occurred with training over time.

**Why it matters:** Relevant for strength-training boundary claims, but not for acute soreness recovery.

**Potential experiment signals:** blood EPA+DHA, 1RM bench press, 1RM squat, lean body mass, body-fat percentage.

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep as adjacent training-adaptation context; do not use as DOMS recovery evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `adjacent_variant`.

**Limitations and caveats:** Completed sample was 21 after withdrawals. Resistance-training program is a strong co-intervention. Exact EPA/DHA dose details should be checked from full text before dose synthesis.
