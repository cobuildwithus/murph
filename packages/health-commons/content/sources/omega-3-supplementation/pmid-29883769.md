This source is included for **dry_eye_treatment_prevention**.

**Findings:** Population: Moderate-to-severe DED participants seeking intervention despite current treatments; mean age 58.0 ± 13.2 years and mean baseline OSDI 44.4 ± 14.2 in accessible summary. Intervention/exposure: Oral omega-3 supplementation in the DREAM trial. Comparator/control: Placebo/control as designed in the DREAM randomized trial. Duration/follow-up: 12-month primary trial design with extension components referenced in registry context. Endpoints: OSDI, conjunctival staining, corneal staining, TBUT, Schirmer test, DED signs and symptoms. Effect/direction: Not efficacy evidence; source describes design and baseline characteristics. Baseline extracted signs included conjunctival staining 3.0 ± 1.4, corneal staining 3.9 ± 2.7, TBUT 3.1 ± 1.5 seconds, and Schirmer 9.6 ± 6.5 mm/5 min. Safety/adverse events: Design source assessed efficacy and safety but no adverse-event result is extracted from this design article.

**Why it matters:** Trial-design details clarify who was studied and which clinical endpoints matter before interpreting DREAM outcome papers.

**Potential experiment signals:** OSDI, conjunctival staining, corneal staining, TBUT, Schirmer test.

**Protocol takeaway:** Design context only; separate from efficacy findings.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Design/baseline article should not be used as outcome evidence.; DREAM treatment population should not be extrapolated to dry-eye prevention. Population mismatch: Treatment design for established moderate-to-severe DED; not prevention or wellness.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
