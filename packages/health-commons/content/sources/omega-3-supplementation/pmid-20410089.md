This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** No cognitive-function benefit was reported after 2 years of EPA+DHA supplementation.

**Why it matters:** Large long-duration direct RCT provides a major no-clear-advantage cognition signal.

**Potential experiment signals:** cognitive function, cognitive decline.

**Protocol takeaway:** EPA+DHA supplementation should not be framed as reliably improving cognition in healthy older adults.

**Population mismatch:** Older cognitively healthy adults, not younger adults or MCI/dementia treatment.

**Limitations:** Older-adult prevention setting.; Dietary dose may not match higher-dose protocols.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
