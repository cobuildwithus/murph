This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** In 22 untrained men, four weeks of EPA-rich fish oil before elbow-flexor eccentric contractions was reported to partially protect against muscle damage, with benefits for joint flexibility and muscle-fiber protection. The title and abstract framing emphasize partial protection rather than uniform improvement across all recovery outcomes.

**Why it matters:** It tests a shorter preloading period than longer fish-oil RCTs and therefore informs duration questions.

**Potential experiment signals:** range of motion, soreness, strength recovery, localized swelling.

**Protocol takeaway:** A 4-week EPA-rich protocol may partially protect EIMD outcomes, but the evidence remains context-only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
