This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** Incident interferon-induced depression was lower with EPA (10%) than placebo (30%; p=.037); DHA was 28% and not significant versus placebo. EPA and DHA delayed onset versus placebo (about 12.0 and 11.7 weeks versus 5.3 weeks; p=.002). Safety note: EPA and DHA were reported well tolerated.

**Why it matters:** Clear EPA-vs-DHA difference in a controlled inflammatory/medical mood model.

**Potential experiment signals:** interferon-alpha-induced depression, time to depression onset, tolerability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only for clinical-supervised mood-context boundaries.

**Population mismatch:** Clinical prevention of interferon-alpha-induced depression; adjacent clinical-supervised evidence.

**Limitations:** Medically induced depression context, not general depression prevention.; Hepatitis C/interferon-alpha clinical context limits generalizability.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
