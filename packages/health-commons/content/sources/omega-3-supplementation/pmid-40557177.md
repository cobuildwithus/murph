This source is included for **dry_eye_treatment_prevention**.

**Findings:** Dry-eye symptom score was lower with omega-3 than placebo (4.85±4.10 vs 8.27±5.72; p=0.001); Schirmer improved within both groups but between-group difference was insignificant; sialometry approached normalization (p=0.053).

**Why it matters:** Latest disease-specific RCT and important boundary from wellness claims.
