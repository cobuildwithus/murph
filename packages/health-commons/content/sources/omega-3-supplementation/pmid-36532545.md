This source is included for **inflammation_immune_markers**.

**Findings:** Omega-3 reduced CRP, prostaglandin E2, and granulocyte/lymphocyte ratio, but not LTB4; RBC DHA rather than EPA was inversely correlated with PGE2 in extracted summaries. [source_artifact:pmid-36532545]

**Why it matters:** Population-specific inflammation-focused crossover trial with tolerability notes.

**Potential experiment signals:** CRP, PGE2, granulocyte/lymphocyte ratio, GI symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Inflammatory benefits may be more visible in high-inflammatory-risk groups, but design limits confidence.

**Population mismatch:** Adjacent variant due to smoking-related high-inflammatory-risk population and open-label design.

**Safety notes:** Supplementation was reported as well tolerated, with minor gastrointestinal symptoms in a subset and negligible adverse health effects.

**Limitations:** Open-label design. Heavy-smoker inflammatory-risk population limits generalization. Crossover and long observational window may include behavior/confounding changes.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
