This source is included for **dry_eye_treatment_prevention**.

**Findings:** OSDI change from months 12 to 24 was similar: mean difference in change −0.6 points (95% CI −10.7 to 9.5; p=0.91); secondary signs and adverse events were also similar.

**Why it matters:** Important negative durability evidence from the pivotal DREAM population.

**Potential experiment signals:** OSDI symptom score, tear break-up time, Schirmer test, ocular-surface staining, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Durability/withdrawal evidence does not support a clear need to continue oral EPA/DHA for dry-eye symptom control after a year in DREAM-like patients.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch / boundary:** Clinical dry-eye patients already in DREAM; context-only for wellness oral EPA/DHA use.

**Safety notes:** Adverse event rates were similar between continued omega-3 and placebo in the extension reports.

**Limitations:** Small withdrawal extension sample (22 continued omega-3, 21 placebo).; Only patients from the active arm of the parent DREAM trial were eligible.; Not a prevention trial and not a healthy-user wellness population..
