This source is included for **variant_boundaries_external_context**.

**Findings:** ALA can increase EPA/DPA but generally does not increase DHA in adult Western-diet studies, so plant ALA sources remain adjacent-variant context. [source_artifact:pmid-19269799].

**Why it matters:** Do not group flax/chia/walnut/ALA-oil trials with EPA/DHA supplement trials unless preformed EPA/DHA is present. [source_artifact:pmid-19269799].

**Potential experiment signals:** blood EPA, blood DPA, blood DHA, omega-3 index

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not group flax/chia/walnut/ALA-oil trials with EPA/DHA supplement trials unless preformed EPA/DHA is present. [source_artifact:pmid-19269799].

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction limitations:**
- Review of heterogeneous conversion studies.
- ALA metabolism depends on background diet and life stage.
- Not direct evidence for preformed oral EPA/DHA.
