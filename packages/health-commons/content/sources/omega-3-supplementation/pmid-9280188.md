This source is included for **lipids_triglycerides_dose_response**.

**Findings:** Extracted review summaries: EPA and DHA decreased triglycerides by about 21% and 26%, respectively; DHA raised HDL-C while EPA lowered total cholesterol and apolipoproteins A-1/B in the summarized report.

**Why it matters:** Older direct EPA-versus-DHA comparison retained for foundational product-specific evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** triglycerides, HDL-C, total cholesterol, serum fatty acids, EPA:DHA composition

**Protocol takeaway:** EPA and DHA can both lower TG, but component-specific secondary lipid effects should be logged and interpreted separately.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy male volunteers differ from hypertriglyceridemic or mixed-sex supplement users.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event extraction available.

**Limitations:**
- Healthy men only.
- High purified doses and short duration.
- Older trial; exact full lipid table was not re-extracted here.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
