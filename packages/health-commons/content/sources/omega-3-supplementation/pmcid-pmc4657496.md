**Findings:** Cod liver oil contributes multiple nutrients, reinforcing that it is not equivalent to EPA/DHA-only supplementation. [source_artifact:pmcid-pmc4657496].

**Why it matters:** Safety and dose notes should account for vitamins A and D when users choose cod liver oil. [source_artifact:pmcid-pmc4657496].

**Potential experiment signals:** vitamin A intake, vitamin D intake, EPA intake, DHA intake

**Protocol takeaway:** Safety and dose notes should account for vitamins A and D when users choose cod liver oil. [source_artifact:pmcid-pmc4657496].

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction limitations:**
- Observational nutrient-exposure context, not causal protocol evidence.
- Different nutrient endpoints used different EPIC-Norfolk subsets.
- Cod liver oil is a mixed exposure.
