This source is included for **omega3_index_kinetics_formulation**.

**Findings:** Plasma EPA+DHA changed substantially after an acute dose, whereas red-blood-cell EPA+DHA changed little or not at all over the same window. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-24079284`.

**Why it matters:** Timing and sample matrix can strongly affect what a blood omega-3 result means.

**Potential experiment signals:** plasma EPA+DHA, RBC EPA+DHA, post-dose timing

**Safety notes:** Safety details were not extracted.

**Limitations / boundary:** Acute physiology source, not a supplementation efficacy trial.; Exact sample size and dose were not extracted.; RBC findings are about timing, not nonresponse to chronic supplementation. Population mismatch: Same-mechanism evidence for sample timing; not direct protocol outcome evidence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use RBC/omega-3 index for longer-term status and plasma for shorter-term exposure questions.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. Directness: `same_mechanism`.
