This source is included for **inflammation_immune_markers**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries indicate n-3 PUFA-containing supplements increased n-3 fatty-acid status but did not lower serum CRP in healthy subjects. [source_artifact:pmid-12654170]

**Why it matters:** Older foundational null CRP dose-response evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** CRP, dose response, healthy subjects.

**Protocol takeaway:** Raises caution that increasing n-3 status does not guarantee lower CRP.

**Population mismatch:** Direct/near-direct evidence for dietary oral n-3 exposure, but details require full-text confirmation.

**Safety notes:** Safety outcomes were not extracted.

**Limitations:** Older dose-response study with incomplete extraction of dose and sample size. Healthy subjects may have low baseline CRP.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
