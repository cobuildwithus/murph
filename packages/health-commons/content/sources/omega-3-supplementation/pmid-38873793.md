This source is included for **clinical_cardiovascular_lipid_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- RESPECT-EPA randomized 2,460 Japanese patients with chronic coronary artery disease on statin therapy to icosapent ethyl 1800 mg/day or control and followed them for 5 years.
- The primary composite outcome occurred in 9.1% of the icosapent ethyl group versus 12.6% of control (HR 0.79, 95% CI 0.62-1.00; p=0.055), while a coronary-event secondary outcome was lower with icosapent ethyl.
- Gastrointestinal disorders and new-onset atrial fibrillation were more frequent with icosapent ethyl; the trial was underpowered and not placebo controlled.

**Why it matters:** Adds a recent mixed EPA-only cardiovascular outcomes boundary source with safety signals.

**Potential experiment signals:** MACE; coronary events; atrial fibrillation; GI disorders; statin use; EPA/AA ratio.

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep EPA-only secondary-prevention evidence separate from consumer mixed EPA/DHA supplementation.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
