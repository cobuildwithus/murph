This source is included for **clinical_cardiovascular_lipid_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- In the ANCHOR particle analysis, placebo participants (n=211) were compared with icosapent ethyl 4 g/day participants (n=216) over 12 weeks.
- Icosapent ethyl reduced total, large, and medium VLDL particles; total and small LDL particles; and total and large HDL particles compared with placebo.
- The trial population was statin-treated and high-risk, and the intervention was EPA-only prescription icosapent ethyl.

**Why it matters:** Separates EPA-only pharmacologic lipid-particle data from mixed EPA+DHA supplement expectations.

**Potential experiment signals:** VLDL particles; LDL particles; small LDL particles; HDL particles; ApoB; triglycerides; statin background.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as boundary context for ApoB/particle endpoints, not as direct evidence for the oral EPA/DHA supplement protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
