This source is included for **cardiovascular_outcomes_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- **null_result / major cardiovascular event:** VITAL did not significantly reduce its primary major cardiovascular endpoint at 1 g/day marine n-3. Effect/direction: 386 vs 419 events; HR 0.92, 95% CI 0.80-1.06; P=0.24.
- **mixed_result / myocardial infarction secondary endpoint:** MI signal should be labeled secondary/hypothesis-generating, not a primary endpoint success. Effect/direction: Total MI HR 0.72, 95% CI 0.59-0.90; stroke HR 1.04 and cardiovascular death HR 0.96 were not lower.
- **safety / bleeding and serious adverse events:** At VITAL dosing/duration, serious safety signals were not increased in the trial report. Effect/direction: No excess bleeding or other serious adverse events reported in the abstracted source.

**Why it matters:** Large primary-prevention RCT most directly resembles consumer oral EPA/DHA use.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- major cardiovascular event
- myocardial infarction
- stroke
- cardiovascular death
- invasive cancer
- bleeding
- serious adverse events

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not claim major CVD-event prevention at 1 g/day; note MI signal only as secondary context.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Source kind:** journal_article
- **Study design:** rct
- **Participants:** 25871 (reported)
- **Population:** US men aged 50 years or older and women aged 55 years or older without prior heart disease, stroke, or cancer at baseline
- **Intervention/exposure:** 1 g/day marine n-3 fatty acids (about 460 mg EPA + 380 mg DHA) in VITAL factorial trial
- **Comparator/control:** placebo
- **Duration/follow-up:** median 5.3 years
- **Endpoints:** major cardiovascular event, myocardial infarction, stroke, cardiovascular death, invasive cancer, bleeding, serious adverse events
- **Effect or direction:** Primary major cardiovascular event was not significantly reduced (386 vs 419; HR 0.92, 95% CI 0.80-1.06; P=0.24). Invasive cancer was not reduced (HR 1.03, 95% CI 0.93-1.13; P=0.56). MI was lower as a secondary endpoint (HR 0.72, 95% CI 0.59-0.90), while stroke and cardiovascular death were not.
- **Adverse events/safety:** No excess bleeding or other serious adverse events reported in the abstracted source.
- **Population mismatch:** Older primary-prevention adults rather than all adult wellness users; factorial vitamin D context.
- **Directness:** `direct_protocol`
- **Artifact rights status:** `permission_required`

## Limitations

- Primary cardiovascular and cancer endpoints were null.
- MI signal was a secondary endpoint and should not be overpromoted.
- Older US primary-prevention cohort; not all users match age/risk profile.
