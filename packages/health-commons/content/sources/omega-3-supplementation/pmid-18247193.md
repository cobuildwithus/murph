This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** EPA and fluoxetine appeared equally effective; EPA plus fluoxetine was superior to either alone from week 4. Response rates were 50% fluoxetine, 56% EPA, and 81% combination.

**Why it matters:** Clinical adjunctive-treatment context only.

**Potential experiment signals:** HDRS, response.

**Protocol takeaway:** EPA performed similarly to fluoxetine and combination therapy looked stronger in a small MDD trial. Do not use as a self-treatment claim.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Patients with major depressive disorder completing at least 4 weeks of treatment
- **Intervention/exposure:** EPA, fluoxetine, or EPA plus fluoxetine
- **Comparator/control:** Active comparator between EPA and fluoxetine, plus combination arm
- **Duration/follow-up:** 8 weeks
- **Endpoints:** HDRS, response
- **Effect estimate or direction:** EPA and fluoxetine appeared equally effective; EPA plus fluoxetine was superior to either alone from week 4. Response rates were 50% fluoxetine, 56% EPA, and 81% combination.
- **Adverse events / safety notes:** Adverse-event details were not extracted in this batch.
- **Population mismatch:** Psychiatric treatment comparison, not starter protocol evidence.
- **Artifact rights status:** unknown

## Limitations

- Small clinical active-comparator trial.
- Combination with antidepressant is clinical-supervised evidence.
- No placebo-only control in the extracted comparison.
