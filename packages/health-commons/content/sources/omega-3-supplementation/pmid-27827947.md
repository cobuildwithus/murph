**Findings:** This small ANZ product survey found label and oxidation compliance, preserving mixed evidence for product-quality claims. [source_artifact:pmid-27827947].

**Why it matters:** Product-quality evidence is mixed and should be framed as market-specific rather than universal. [source_artifact:pmid-27827947].

**Potential experiment signals:** label accuracy, EPA+DHA content, peroxide value, p-anisidine value, TOTOX

**Protocol takeaway:** Product-quality evidence is mixed and should be framed as market-specific rather than universal. [source_artifact:pmid-27827947].

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Extraction limitations:**
- Only ten products; market and date specific.
- Some p-anisidine interpretation may be confounded by flavorings.
- No human exposure or efficacy outcomes.
