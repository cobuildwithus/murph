This source is included for **lipids_triglycerides_dose_response**.

**Findings:** Extracted summary-level context: older broad systematic review retained as foundational context; triglyceride effects were a central marker, while HDL-C and LDL-C effects in hyperlipidemic subjects were not clearly favorable in accessible snippets.

**Why it matters:** Older broad serum-marker systematic review retained as foundational context.

**Potential experiment signals:** triglycerides, LDL-C, HDL-C, total cholesterol

**Protocol takeaway:** Retain as historical context; rely on newer/quantitative lipid reviews for specific claims.

**Population mismatch:** Broad serum-marker literature includes multiple omega-3 sources and populations.

**Safety notes:** Not a safety-focused extraction.

**Limitations:**
- Older broad systematic review.
- Exact participant counts, included study counts, and effect estimates were not extracted from accessible record.
- Serum-marker scope spans more than the direct oral EPA/DHA protocol.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
