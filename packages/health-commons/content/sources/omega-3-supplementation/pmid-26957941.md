This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** In 27 women, 3000 mg/day algae-derived DHA for 7 days before eccentric strength exercise was reported to attenuate soreness and stiffness and protect against loss of range of motion. Skin temperature and salivary CRP did not show the same clear signal.

**Why it matters:** It adds sex-specific evidence and distinguishes DHA-only findings from mixed EPA/DHA protocols.

**Potential experiment signals:** soreness score, stiffness rating, range of motion, inflammation symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** DHA-only soreness findings are promising but should be labeled as context-only for mixed EPA/DHA.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
