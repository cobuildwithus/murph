This source is included for **dry_eye_treatment_prevention**.

**Findings:** Reported: Oxford corneal staining, OSDI, and DEQ improved in omega-3 group (p<0.05), and MMP-9 increase was lower (p=0.027); effect seemed more anti-inflammatory than tear-secretory.

**Why it matters:** Boundaries procedure-related dry eye from general supplementation.

**Potential experiment signals:** OSDI, DEQ, Oxford staining, MMP-9, Schirmer.

**Protocol takeaway:** Procedure-specific adjunctive evidence; cannot generalize to nonsurgical users.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch / boundary:** Adjacent variant: cataract-surgery recovery context.

**Safety notes:** No specific adverse-event signal extracted.

**Limitations:** Postoperative population.; Short 2-month treatment.; Not placebo-blinded in all summaries; full-text methods should be checked..
