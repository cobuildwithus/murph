This source is included for **inflammation_immune_markers**.

**Findings:** The meta-analysis reported a significant CRP reduction in type 2 diabetes; sparse data were available for other cytokines. [source_artifact:pmid-27544079]

**Why it matters:** Preserves disease-specific positive CRP evidence and its limitations.

**Potential experiment signals:** CRP, type 2 diabetes, cytokine sparsity.

**Protocol takeaway:** Diabetes evidence is useful for boundary context, not direct wellness claims.

**Population mismatch:** Adjacent variant because diabetes-specific inflammation does not represent general protocol users.

**Safety notes:** Safety outcomes were not extracted in this batch.

**Limitations:** Disease-specific type 2 diabetes population. Few trials reported IL-2, IL-6, or TNF-alpha. Authors called for larger rigorous RCTs.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
