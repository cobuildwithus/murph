This source is included for **safety_adverse_events**.

**Findings:** Combined AF/micro-AF occurred in 46 (11.9%) n-3 PUFA vs 24 (6.5%) placebo; HR 1.90 (95% CI 1.16-3.11), P=0.011. Change in EPA was reported as an independent predictor/mediator of AF/micro-AF risk.

**Why it matters:** Direct AF-focused follow-up from an older post-MI EPA+DHA trial; high-risk population.

**Potential experiment signals:** adverse_event:atrial-fibrillation, adverse_event:micro-atrial-fibrillation, biomarker:epa.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only evidence only: Combined AF/micro-AF occurred in 46 (11.9%) n-3 PUFA vs 24 (6.5%) placebo; HR 1.90 (95% CI 1.16-3.11), P=0.011. Change in EPA was reported as an independent predictor/mediator of AF/micro-AF risk.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Older post-MI population and trial biomarker analysis, not healthy supplement users.

**Limitations:** Secondary analysis; analyzed denominator not extracted here; older post-MI population; intermittent detection may miss AF; micro-AF endpoint is not the same as clinical AF.
