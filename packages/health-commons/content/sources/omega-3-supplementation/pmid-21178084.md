This source is included for **lipids_triglycerides_dose_response**.

**Findings:** Search-result extraction: 2 g/day algal DHA for 4.5 months reduced VLDL triglycerides and total triglycerides, increased large LDL and HDL particle fractions, and decreased small LDL and medium HDL particles.

**Why it matters:** Direct adult algal-DHA lipid trial relevant to non-fish oil protocol variants.

**Potential experiment signals:** fasting triglycerides, VLDL triglycerides, LDL particle size/subfractions, HDL particle size/subfractions

**Protocol takeaway:** Algal DHA may lower TG-related lipoprotein signals and shift particle size, but evidence is small and DHA-specific.

**Population mismatch:** Overweight/obese adult trial may not represent lean or normotriglyceridemic users.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event extraction available in the accessible record used here.

**Limitations:**
- Small RCT.
- Overweight/obese population and DHA-only algal oil.
- Exact lipid effect sizes beyond directional particle changes were not fully extracted here.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
