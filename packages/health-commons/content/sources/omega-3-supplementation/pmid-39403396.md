This source is included for **lipids_triglycerides_dose_response**.

**Findings:** Updated review included 24 publications from 9 unique RCTs; both EPA and DHA lowered triglycerides, DHA likely slightly greater, both increased HDL2 with greater DHA effect, and DHA appeared to increase LDL-C and LDL particle size.

**Why it matters:** Updates the EPA-versus-DHA evidence base after the 2018 systematic review.

**Potential experiment signals:** EPA grams/day, DHA grams/day, triglycerides, LDL-C, HDL2-C, LDL particle size

**Protocol takeaway:** The updated RCT review strengthens the need to log EPA:DHA ratio and monitor LDL-C when using DHA-heavy products.

**Population mismatch:** Near-pure EPA/DHA high-dose RCTs may not match lower-dose mixed EPA+DHA supplements.

**Safety notes:** Not a safety-focused extraction.

**Limitations:**
- Small number of high-quality head-to-head RCTs.
- Evidence focused on risk factors, not hard clinical outcomes.
- Participant count not extracted in accessible text used here.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
