This source is included for **dry_eye_treatment_prevention**.

**Findings:** Population: One 32-year-old male with paracentral scotoma and repeated macular hemorrhage without choroidal neovascularization. Intervention/exposure: Regular high-dose fish-oil supplementation; exact dose not extracted from accessible abstract. Comparator/control: No comparator. Duration/follow-up: Fish oil began approximately one month before first hemorrhage; second hemorrhage occurred after continued use; no recurrence over 12 months after discontinuation. Endpoints: macular hemorrhage, visual symptoms, resolution after discontinuation, recurrence. Effect/direction: Temporal association: first lesion resolved over 2 months; another lesion developed 2 weeks later while fish oil continued; after discontinuation, the second hemorrhage resolved over 8 weeks with no recurrence over 12 months. Safety/adverse events: Ocular safety signal only; authors cautioned against high-dose fish-oil use in patients with repetitive retinal hemorrhage.

**Why it matters:** Safety-only sources preserve rare or unusual adverse-event signals without overstating population risk.

**Potential experiment signals:** macular hemorrhage, visual symptoms, recurrence after discontinuation, bleeding-risk history.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only for safety review and contraindication screening, especially in people with retinal hemorrhage history or bleeding-risk contexts.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Single case report with no risk estimate.; Temporal association does not prove causality.; Exact high-dose exposure was not extracted in this batch. Population mismatch: Single ocular adverse-event case; cannot estimate risk for the protocol population.
