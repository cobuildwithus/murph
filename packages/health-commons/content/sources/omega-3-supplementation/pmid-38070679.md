This source is included for **safety_adverse_events**.

**Findings:** The guideline concludes that women of childbearing age should obtain at least 250 mg/day DHA+EPA from diet or supplements, pregnancy should add at least 100 to 200 mg/day DHA, and pregnant individuals with low DHA intake/status may need about 600 to 1000 mg/day DHA+EPA or DHA alone under guidance.

**Why it matters:** Recent pregnancy-specific guidance gives dose and timing boundaries for a population that should be excluded from unsupervised adult self-experimentation.

**Potential experiment signals:** pregnancy status, DHA intake, DHA blood status, preterm birth, early preterm birth, gestational timing.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as pregnancy boundary guidance: pregnancy dosing and timing require obstetric pathway and should not be folded into a general adult supplement protocol.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness:** `safety_boundary` for `protocol_variant:omega-3-supplementation/oral-epa-dha-supplementation`.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Pregnancy-specific recommendations, baseline DHA status dependent, and not direct evidence for non-pregnant adult supplementation.
