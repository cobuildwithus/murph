This source is included for **omega3_index_kinetics_formulation**.

**Findings:** Supplementation significantly increased omega-3 index/RBC EPA and DHA and reduced the RBC omega-6:omega-3 ratio; both groups reduced liver fat and the overall between-group liver-fat difference was not clear in the accessible report. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-30127297`.

**Why it matters:** This trial is direct for omega-3 status correction and important for preserving mixed clinical-outcome boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** omega-3 index, RBC EPA+DHA, omega-6:omega-3 ratio, liver fat, adverse events

**Safety notes:** Adverse events and clinical safety labs were monitored; detailed event counts were not extracted.

**Limitations / boundary:** NAFLD clinical population and standard-of-care diet cointervention limit generalizability.; Ethyl-ester high-dose product.; Biomarker effect was clear, but liver-fat outcome was mixed/null overall. Population mismatch: Direct oral EPA/DHA biomarker evidence with metabolic-disease population mismatch.

**Protocol takeaway:** High-dose EPA/DHA can raise RBC omega-3 status in NAFLD, but clinical endpoint claims must remain bounded.
