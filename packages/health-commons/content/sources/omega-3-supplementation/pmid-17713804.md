This source is included for **omega3_index_kinetics_formulation**.

**Findings:** DHA status increased dose-dependently and algal DHA oils/capsules were reported as bioequivalent for raising plasma phospholipid and erythrocyte DHA. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-17713804`.

**Why it matters:** Useful boundary evidence for algae-derived supplement forms.

**Potential experiment signals:** plasma phospholipid DHA, erythrocyte DHA, dose-response, tolerability

**Safety notes:** Accessible summaries describe algal DHA oils as safe and well tolerated in the study, but detailed adverse-event counts were not extracted.

**Limitations / boundary:** DHA-specific rather than EPA+DHA.; Exact total randomized count was not verified from accessible metadata.; DOI was not present in the canonical ledger even though secondary records report one. Population mismatch: Direct for algal DHA supplementation; adjacent to mixed oral EPA/DHA protocols.

**Protocol takeaway:** Algal DHA can raise DHA biomarkers, but EPA+DHA protocol claims need source-specific dosing.
