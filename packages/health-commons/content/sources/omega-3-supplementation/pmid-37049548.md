This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** Fish oil supplementation during strength training was associated with improved repeated-bout effect and redox-balance markers compared with control, including lower injury/inflammation biomarker responses.

**Why it matters:** Adds training-adaptation context but requires clear adjacent-variant labeling.

**Potential experiment signals:** CK, LDH, IL-6, CRP, GSH/GSSG, oxidative-stress markers.

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep as adjacent-variant training-context evidence; do not claim isolated fish oil alone improved recovery independent of training.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `adjacent_variant`.

**Limitations and caveats:** Only 16 young men. Training adaptation and repeated-bout effect are central to the result. Dose details were not reliably captured in retrieved material.
