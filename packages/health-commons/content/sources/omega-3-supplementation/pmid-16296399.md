This source is included for **omega3_index_kinetics_formulation**.

**Findings:** DHA supplementation increased omega-3 index in vegetarians; accessible summaries report that 69% of the DHA group and none of the placebo group reached an omega-3 index above 8%. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-16296399`.

**Why it matters:** This boundary source helps interpret vegan/vegetarian and low-baseline EPA/DHA variants.

**Potential experiment signals:** omega-3 index, erythrocyte EPA+DHA, plasma phospholipid EPA+DHA

**Safety notes:** Safety details were not extracted.

**Limitations / boundary:** Adjacent variant because this is DHA-only and vegetarian/low-baseline population.; Participant count was not extracted.; Short duration and biomarker endpoint only. Population mismatch: Adjacent variant for low-baseline vegetarian adults and DHA-only supplementation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Vegetarian/low-baseline status can produce large DHA-status changes, but results are not generic mixed EPA+DHA claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. Directness: `adjacent_variant`.
