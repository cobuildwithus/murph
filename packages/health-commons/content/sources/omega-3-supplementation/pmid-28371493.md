This source is included for **dry_eye_treatment_prevention**.

**Findings:** Population: Patients with dry eye syndrome in RCTs published between 2005 and 2015. Intervention/exposure: Nutritional supplements mostly containing omega-3 and omega-6 in different proportions. Comparator/control: Control or placebo in included randomized trials. Duration/follow-up: Varied across included trials. Endpoints: OSDI, DESS, TBUT, Schirmer test, tear-function parameters. Effect/direction: Significant subjective improvement favored experimental groups in seven studies; TBUT significantly increased in nine studies and Schirmer test in four studies. The review concluded evidence was not strong enough for systematic standalone recommendation. Safety/adverse events: No adverse-event estimate extracted in this batch.

**Why it matters:** Many ocular supplements combine omega-3 with omega-6, so this source protects against route/formulation drift.

**Potential experiment signals:** OSDI, DESS, TBUT, Schirmer test.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only adjacent-variant evidence.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Adjacent mixed omega-3/omega-6 formulations.; Results were study-count based in accessible extraction, not pooled effect estimates.; Etiology-specific recommendations were not supported. Population mismatch: Mostly mixed omega-3/omega-6 products; adjacent variant rather than EPA/DHA-only evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
