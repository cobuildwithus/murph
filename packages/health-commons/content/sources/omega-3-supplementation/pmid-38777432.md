This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** This meta-analysis addresses omega-3 supplementation combined with resistance training and reported favorable skeletal-muscle outcomes such as muscle strength. Because resistance training is a co-intervention and the endpoints are adaptation-focused, it is adjacent evidence for the oral EPA/DHA protocol.

**Why it matters:** It helps separate omega-3 plus training adaptation from omega-3 alone recovery claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** strength trend, training volume, muscle mass if measured.

**Protocol takeaway:** Adjacent variant only: do not promote resistance-training co-intervention outcomes into direct EPA/DHA-alone recovery claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
