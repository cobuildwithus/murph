This source is included for **dry_eye_treatment_prevention**.

**Findings:** At 8 weeks, TBUT was 6.00±1.62 s vs 5.08±1.28 s (p=0.034) and MGD score 7.2±1.8 vs 8.1±2.6 (p=0.033) favoring omega-3; no participant reported side effects during 8 weeks.

**Why it matters:** Provides exact high-DHA formulation evidence in MGD after DREAM.

**Potential experiment signals:** TBUT, MGD score, OSDI, corneal staining, side effects.

**Protocol takeaway:** Suggests an MGD-specific signal for a high-DHA formulation but not a broad dry-eye cure claim.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch / boundary:** Direct to MGD-related dry eye, but high-DHA formulation and clinical phenotype may not generalize.

**Safety notes:** No side effects were reported among the 50 included patients during the 8-week trial.

**Limitations:** Small sample size.; Single site.; Dietary habits and lipid profiles were not fully controlled/evaluated.; Short-term follow-up only..
