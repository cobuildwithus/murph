This source is included for **omega3_index_kinetics_formulation**.

**Findings:** Whole-blood omega-3 status increased in a product-dependent order: re-esterified triglyceride fish oil > ethyl-ester fish oil > triglyceride salmon oil > phospholipid krill oil at the manufacturers’ recommended doses. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-24952576`.

**Why it matters:** Real-world product selection can meaningfully alter achieved blood omega-3 status.

**Potential experiment signals:** whole-blood EPA+DHA, product type, reported adverse events, adherence/discontinuation

**Safety notes:** Three participants discontinued; two discontinuations were due to minor adverse events and one due to time limitations.

**Limitations / boundary:** Small crossover trial.; Product doses were not EPA+DHA matched, so formulation and dose are confounded.; Whole-blood fatty acids rather than clinical outcomes. Population mismatch: Direct oral supplement evidence, but product-specific dosing limits generalization.

**Protocol takeaway:** Record dose per serving and formulation/source when using oral EPA/DHA products.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`. Directness: `direct_protocol`.
