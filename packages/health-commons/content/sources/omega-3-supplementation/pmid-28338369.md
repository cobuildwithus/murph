This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** DHA-rich fish oil increased omega-3 index and lowered oxygen cost during cycling, but did not clearly improve sprint, 5-minute time-trial power, or maximal voluntary contraction force.

**Why it matters:** Useful adjacent exercise physiology within direct oral EPA/DHA exposure, but not a soreness trial.

**Potential experiment signals:** omega-3 index, relative oxygen consumption, cycling time-trial power, sprint performance, MVC force.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for exercise-physiology and omega-3-index context; do not cite as DOMS recovery evidence.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness:** `direct_protocol`.

**Limitations and caveats:** Trained male sample. DHA-rich, low-EPA formulation. No soreness or muscle-damage primary endpoint.
