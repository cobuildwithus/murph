**Findings:** Population: People with ocular-surface conditions, including dry eye, across nutrition-related evidence streams. Intervention/exposure: Nutrition and dietary factors, including omega-3/omega-6 fatty acids and dietary assessment. Comparator/control: Not applicable across the review as a whole. Duration/follow-up: Not applicable across the review as a whole. Endpoints: ocular-surface health, dry-eye disease management, omega-3 status, supplement form and composition, dose and duration, omega-3:omega-6 ratio. Effect/direction: Not a pooled efficacy estimate. Extracted text emphasizes that future studies should identify responsive dry-eye subgroups, baseline omega-3 status, preferred form/composition, dose, duration, and omega-3:omega-6 ratio. Safety/adverse events: No batch-extracted adverse-event estimate; relevance is research design and clinical context.

**Why it matters:** It explains why dry-eye omega-3 findings are hard to generalize: baseline status, formulation, subtype, and ratio may matter.

**Potential experiment signals:** omega-3 status, dry-eye subtype, OSDI, TBUT, omega-3:omega-6 ratio, dietary intake.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only design guidance for any future dry-eye-specific variant.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Narrative/professional review, not a single direct trial.; Omega-3 evidence is framed as unresolved and dependent on patient subgroup and study design. Population mismatch: Broad ocular-surface nutrition review; not a direct oral EPA/DHA RCT.
