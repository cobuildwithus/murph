This source is included for **safety_adverse_events**.

**Findings:** Pregnant women were randomized to DHA+EPA doses of 225+90, 450+180, 675+270, or 900+360 mg/day. Regression estimated about 750 mg/day DHA+EPA to reach 8 g% RBC DHA+EPA and about 1000 mg/day to reach 1 g% mature milk DHA+EPA; milk AA was not adversely affected and EPA/AA remained physiologic.

**Why it matters:** Pregnancy/lactation dose-to-biomarker source helps separate biomarker targets from general adult dosing and flags lactation as a special population.

**Potential experiment signals:** RBC DHA+EPA, mature milk DHA+EPA, milk arachidonic acid, milk EPA/AA ratio, pregnancy/lactation status.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as pregnancy/lactation biomarker boundary: doses needed to change milk/RBC DHA+EPA are special-population targets, not adult starter-protocol targets.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness:** `safety_boundary` for `protocol_variant:omega-3-supplementation/oral-epa-dha-supplementation`.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Small sample, biomarker endpoints, pregnancy/lactation population, and no general adult safety-outcome inference.
