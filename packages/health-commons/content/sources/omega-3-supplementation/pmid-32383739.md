This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** The review included 32 studies in athletes or trained populations. It reported consistent signals for selected outcomes such as reaction time, mood, cardiovascular dynamics in cyclists, skeletal-muscle recovery, TNF-alpha, and post-exercise nitric oxide, but no clear effects on endurance performance, lung function, muscle force, or training adaptation. Negative outcomes were sparse, but the authors highlighted heterogeneity and limited effect-size reporting.

**Why it matters:** It maps the athlete literature and shows why recovery and performance outcomes need careful boundary language.

**Potential experiment signals:** post-exercise soreness, perceived recovery, muscle performance after training, adverse effects diary.

**Protocol takeaway:** Athlete fish-oil evidence is mixed and should be used as context, not as a promise that oral EPA/DHA improves performance.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
