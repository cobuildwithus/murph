This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** DHA improved memory and reaction time in healthy young adults; accessible snippets report episodic memory improvement and working-memory reaction-time improvement with sex-modulated responses.

**Why it matters:** One of the few direct healthy-young-adult cognitive endpoint trials in the batch.

**Potential experiment signals:** episodic memory, working-memory reaction time, sex-stratified cognitive outcomes.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not generalize without baseline intake and DHA-heavy formulation caveats.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy young adults with low DHA intake; not representative of all adults.

**Limitations:** Dose, duration, and participant count require full-text verification.; Low habitual DHA intake may be a key eligibility modifier.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
