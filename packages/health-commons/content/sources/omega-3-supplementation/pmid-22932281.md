This source is included for **omega3_index_kinetics_formulation**.

**Findings:** EPA and DHA were incorporated into measured lipid pools after supplementation; the source is useful for comparing lipid-pool interpretation and intake schedules. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-22932281`.

**Why it matters:** Different lipid pools can show different incorporation patterns after oral EPA/DHA.

**Potential experiment signals:** lipid-pool EPA+DHA, plasma EPA+DHA, erythrocyte EPA+DHA, intake schedule

**Safety notes:** Safety details were not extracted.

**Limitations / boundary:** Participant count, exact arms, and duration were not extracted from accessible metadata.; Lipid-pool biomarker source, not clinical-outcome evidence. Population mismatch: Direct oral EPA/DHA supplementation, but with dietary-intake-equivalent dosing rather than a single supplement dose.

**Protocol takeaway:** Lipid compartment and dosing schedule should be documented when comparing omega-3 status changes.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`. Directness: `direct_protocol`.
