This source is included for **safety_adverse_events**.

**Findings:** Perioperative fish oil did not reduce postoperative AF; primary event rates were about 30% in each group (reported result OR 0.96, 95% CI 0.77-1.20; P=0.74).

**Why it matters:** Adjacent perioperative source; no clear postoperative AF benefit and no clear bleeding excess in associated OPERA safety reporting.

**Potential experiment signals:** adverse_event:postoperative-atrial-fibrillation, adverse_event:bleeding, dose:loading-dose.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only evidence only: Perioperative fish oil did not reduce postoperative AF; primary event rates were about 30% in each group (reported result OR 0.96, 95% CI 0.77-1.20; P=0.74).

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Perioperative prevention of postoperative AF is an adjacent clinical scenario.

**Limitations:** Cardiac surgery/perioperative loading context; very short treatment window and high loading dose; not routine chronic consumer supplementation.
