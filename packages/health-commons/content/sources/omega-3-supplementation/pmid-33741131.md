This source is included for **safety_adverse_events**.

**Findings:** The consensus statement uses a benefit-harm framework for preoperative supplement management and includes fish oil/omega-3 among supplement-specific recommendations. It is a practical perioperative boundary source rather than efficacy evidence.

**Why it matters:** Professional perioperative guidance directly addresses whether omega-3/fish oil should be continued or stopped before surgery.

**Potential experiment signals:** planned procedures, surgeon disclosure, bleeding history, anticoagulant use, fish oil dose above 3 g/day.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as perioperative guidance context: disclose supplement use and defer to the surgical team; SPAQI guidance supports continuing fish oil/omega-3 in many cases.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness:** `safety_boundary` for `protocol_variant:omega-3-supplementation/oral-epa-dha-supplementation`.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Guideline/consensus source; actual perioperative management should account for procedure bleeding risk, dose, indication, and patient medications.
