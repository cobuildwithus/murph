This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** Soreness was lower at 24 hours in the omega-3 arm; IL-6 rose within placebo but not omega-3, yet between-group differences for IL-6, CK, TNF-alpha, MVIC, and peak power were limited or absent.

**Why it matters:** Useful for avoiding overinterpretation of a soreness signal as broad recovery improvement.

**Potential experiment signals:** soreness, IL-6, TNF-alpha, creatine kinase, MVIC, peak power.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mixed direct evidence: symptom benefit at one time point, but not a robust multi-endpoint recovery win.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `direct_protocol`.

**Limitations and caveats:** Only 14 participants. Healthy male sample. Many endpoints did not show between-group separation.
