This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** This systematic review identified 61 included studies across cardiopulmonary function, exercise recovery, cognitive function, injury recovery, and strength or power. It is most useful for high-intensity occupational context, not direct supplementation claims for ordinary adults.

**Why it matters:** It is a useful adjacent high-load occupational source but not a direct oral EPA/DHA recovery RCT.

**Potential experiment signals:** recovery after demanding training, cognitive fatigue, injury-recovery context, occupational training load.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only for military/high-intensity populations; do not generalize to broad healthy-adult claims.
