This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** The trial tested whether DHA-rich fish oil slowed cognitive decline; accessible secondary summaries report no effect on cognitive performance over 18 months. Safety note: No safety signal extracted from accessible summaries.

**Why it matters:** A direct long-duration normal-older-adult RCT helps avoid overgeneralizing positive memory trials.

**Potential experiment signals:** cognitive performance, age-related cognitive decline.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promise prevention of age-related cognitive decline in cognitively normal older adults.

**Population mismatch:** Older-adult prevention context, not general mood or acute cognition.

**Limitations:** Participant count was not extracted from accessible abstracts during this run.; Cognitively normal older-adult prevention context may not match younger or symptomatic users.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
