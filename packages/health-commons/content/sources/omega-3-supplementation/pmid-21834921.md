This source is included for **dry_eye_treatment_prevention**.

**Findings:** In the per-protocol set, percentage of HLA-DR-positive cells fell significantly in fatty-acids group (p=0.021); fluorescence intensity was also reduced in FAS (p=0.041) and PPS (p=0.017); signs/symptoms were not significantly different.

**Why it matters:** Preserves biomarker-positive but clinical-sign/symptom-null boundary evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** HLA-DR, symptom score, clinical dry-eye signs.

**Protocol takeaway:** Mechanistic/biomarker context only; effects cannot be assigned to oral EPA/DHA alone.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch / boundary:** Adjacent mechanism/formula evidence, not a direct isolated EPA/DHA claim.

**Safety notes:** No specific adverse-event signal extracted.

**Limitations:** Combination supplement prevents EPA/DHA attribution.; Biomarker response did not translate into significant signs/symptoms.; Not a broad wellness population..
