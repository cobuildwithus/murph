**Potential experiment signals:** erythrocyte DHA, adherence, dietary self-report

**Limitations / boundary:** Advice/adherence study rather than a controlled supplement dosing trial.; Exact participant count and intervention details were not extracted. Population mismatch: Same-mechanism adherence-monitoring evidence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Blood biomarkers can reveal adherence patterns that diet logs may miss.
