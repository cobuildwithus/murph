This source is included for **lipids_triglycerides_dose_response**.

**Findings:** The accessible abstract reported pure EPA ranked best for secondary prevention (HR 0.72, 95% CI 0.65 to 0.81, in a 39-RCT/88,359-participant secondary-prevention NMA) and no evidence of omega-3 PUFA efficacy in primary prevention.

**Why it matters:** Helps separate EPA-only and clinical secondary-prevention claims from the oral EPA/DHA supplement protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** major cardiovascular events boundary, formulation type, primary vs secondary prevention status

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only for formulation and clinical-boundary language, not as direct support for a consumer EPA+DHA lipid protocol.

**Population mismatch:** Many trials involve clinical cardiovascular prevention, concomitant medications, or prescription formulations rather than home supplement self-experiments.

**Safety notes:** Not a safety-focused extraction for the oral EPA/DHA supplement protocol.

**Limitations:**
- Clinical event outcomes are not the same as lipid dose-response biomarkers.
- Pure EPA and prescription/formulation comparisons should not be promoted as direct OTC EPA+DHA supplement claims.
- Primary-prevention results were null in the accessible abstract.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
