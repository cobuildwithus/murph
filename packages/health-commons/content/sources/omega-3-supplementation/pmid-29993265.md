This source is included for **inflammation_immune_markers**.

**Findings:** The abstract-level extraction indicates EPA/DHA had shared effects on CRP concentration and EPA lowered systolic blood pressure; exact inflammatory effect sizes were not extracted. [source_artifact:pmid-29993265]

**Why it matters:** Directly names EPA, DHA, and inflammatory factors, making it a protocol-relevant review.

**Potential experiment signals:** CRP, blood pressure, EPA versus DHA distinction.

**Protocol takeaway:** Can support a cautious protocol claim that inflammation effects are plausible but should be tracked rather than assumed.

**Population mismatch:** Direct EPA/DHA evidence, but pooled trial populations and doses may not match a single self-experiment protocol.

**Safety notes:** Safety outcomes were not extracted from the accessible metadata.

**Limitations:** Total participant count and exact inflammatory effect estimates were not available in the extracted metadata. Meta-analysis combines distinct EPA and DHA exposures.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
