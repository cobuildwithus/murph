This source is included for **lipids_triglycerides_dose_response**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries reported non-HDL-C median percent change −9.0% with omega-3/simvastatin versus −2.2% with placebo/simvastatin, TG reduction about 29.5% with combination therapy, and LDL-C median percent change +0.7% versus −2.9% with placebo/simvastatin.

**Why it matters:** Statin-adjunct prescription EPA+DHA trial relevant to lipid-treatment boundary.

**Potential experiment signals:** non-HDL-C, triglycerides, LDL-C, GI adverse events, prescription vs supplement status

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep prescription/statin-adjunct evidence separate from oral supplement self-experiment claims.

**Population mismatch:** Statin-treated persistent hypertriglyceridemia population under clinical management.

**Safety notes:** Adverse events more frequent in omega-3 group at ≥1% included nasopharyngitis (3.3%), upper respiratory tract infection (3.3%), diarrhea (2.5%), and dyspepsia (2.5%); no serious adverse events were considered treatment-related in accessible summary.

**Limitations:**
- Prescription omega-3 ethyl esters plus statin is an adjacent clinical variant, not an OTC supplement-only protocol.
- Mostly white clinical hypertriglyceridemia population.
- Short 8-week follow-up.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
