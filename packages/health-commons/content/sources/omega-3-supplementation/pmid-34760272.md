This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** The meta-analysis concluded that omega-3 supplementation reduced indirect serum markers of exercise-induced muscle damage, particularly CK, LDH, and myoglobin. It is most useful for biomarker direction, not for proving user-visible recovery improvements.

**Why it matters:** It helps avoid conflating blood-marker movement with a user-felt recovery benefit.

**Potential experiment signals:** creatine kinase if tested, muscle soreness score, recovery time after exercise challenge.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as direct-protocol biomarker context; do not claim that biomarker reductions necessarily translate into improved performance or less soreness.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
