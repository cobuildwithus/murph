This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** Omega-3 augmentation of citalopram was reported more effective than monotherapy in decreasing MDD signs and symptoms during 8 weeks. Safety note: No detailed adverse events extracted.

**Why it matters:** Separates supplement-only protocol evidence from antidepressant augmentation.

**Potential experiment signals:** MDD symptoms, depressive signs/symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as clinician-supervised context, not a self-directed protocol claim.

**Population mismatch:** Major depressive disorder with pharmacotherapy; clinical-supervised boundary.

**Limitations:** Adjunctive antidepressant trial, not supplement monotherapy.; Clinical psychiatric treatment setting.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
