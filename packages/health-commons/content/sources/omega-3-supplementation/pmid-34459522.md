This source is included for **inflammation_immune_markers**.

**Findings:** Fish oil could reduce CRP in hemodialysis patients, especially among patients with CRP at least 5 mg/L, but extracted summaries report no significant effects on IL-6 or TNF-alpha. [source_artifact:pmid-34459522]

**Why it matters:** Preserves disease-specific mixed endpoint evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** CRP, IL-6, TNF-alpha, hemodialysis.

**Protocol takeaway:** Inflammatory effects can be endpoint- and baseline-risk-specific.

**Population mismatch:** Adjacent disease-boundary evidence.

**Safety notes:** Safety outcomes were not extracted.

**Limitations:** Hemodialysis population is a major mismatch. Subgroup CRP signal may depend on elevated baseline CRP.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
