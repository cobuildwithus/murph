This source is included for **dry_eye_treatment_prevention**.

**Findings:** Population: Patients with dry eye disease being evaluated or managed in clinical eye-care settings. Intervention/exposure: Dry-eye management options including tear, lid, anti-inflammatory, surgical, environmental, dietary, and complementary strategies. Comparator/control: Not applicable. Duration/follow-up: Not applicable. Endpoints: dry-eye symptom management, tear insufficiency, lid abnormalities, inflammation, environmental and dietary management, safety screening. Effect/direction: Not efficacy evidence; provides clinical management boundaries and staged care context. Safety/adverse events: Extracted professional guidance flags that systemic contraindications such as liver disease, atrial fibrillation, bleeding disorders, and high-dose omega-3 exposure warrant medical advice before supplementation.

**Why it matters:** Dry-eye management often requires subtype-directed care rather than supplementation alone.

**Potential experiment signals:** dry-eye symptom burden, MGD/lid findings, tear insufficiency, need for prescription or procedural therapy, contraindication screen.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only for clinical supervision and contraindication language.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Guideline/report does not isolate an effect estimate for oral EPA/DHA.; Omega-3 is one option among many dry-eye management strategies. Population mismatch: Guideline/report context; not a direct EPA/DHA efficacy trial.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
