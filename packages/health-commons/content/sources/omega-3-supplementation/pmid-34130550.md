This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** In healthy young women, omega-3 supplementation after resistance exercise did not produce a significant group-time advantage over placebo. Both groups showed expected muscle-damage or soreness patterns after exercise.

**Why it matters:** It preserves a negative/mixed finding and prevents overgeneralization from positive male or preloading trials.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle soreness, strength loss, muscle damage markers.

**Protocol takeaway:** Short post-exercise supplementation in young women did not show clear benefit; duration and timing likely matter.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
