This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** No evidence was found for efficacy of adding ethyl-EPA to antidepressants for reducing depressive symptoms in diabetic patients with comorbid major depression. Erythrocyte EPA tripled in the active group.

**Why it matters:** Important comorbidity-specific null source.

**Potential experiment signals:** depressive symptoms, erythrocyte EPA levels.

**Protocol takeaway:** Diabetes/MDD add-on EPA trial found no depressive-symptom efficacy. Very small and clinically specific.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Patients with diabetes mellitus and comorbid major depression using antidepressant medication
- **Intervention/exposure:** Ethyl-EPA 1 g/day added to antidepressant medication
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo add-on
- **Duration/follow-up:** 12 weeks
- **Endpoints:** depressive symptoms, erythrocyte EPA levels
- **Effect estimate or direction:** No evidence was found for efficacy of adding ethyl-EPA to antidepressants for reducing depressive symptoms in diabetic patients with comorbid major depression. Erythrocyte EPA tripled in the active group.
- **Adverse events / safety notes:** Adverse-event details were not extracted.
- **Population mismatch:** Disease-specific clinical add-on evidence; adjacent variant.
- **Artifact rights status:** unknown

## Limitations

- Small disease-specific trial.
- Diabetes and antidepressant comorbidity context.
- Not generalizable to healthy adults.
