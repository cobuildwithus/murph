This source is included for **inflammation_immune_markers**.

**Findings:** The meta-analysis reported significant reductions in CRP, IL-6, and TNF-alpha with marine-derived n-3 PUFA supplementation overall, with subgroup heterogeneity by population, duration, and BMI. [source_artifact:pmid-24505395]

**Why it matters:** This is the backbone inflammation synthesis for direct EPA/DHA-type oral supplementation evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** CRP, IL-6, TNF-alpha, population subgroup modifiers.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports including inflammatory biomarkers as plausible but heterogeneous protocol endpoints.

**Population mismatch:** Broad supplementation meta-analysis; not restricted to generally healthy adults using a single EPA/DHA dose.

**Safety notes:** Adverse events were not a primary extraction focus for this biomarker meta-analysis.

**Limitations:** Aggregates heterogeneous populations, doses, and trial durations. Biomarker effects varied by disease group, BMI, and duration. Not all included interventions map cleanly to a starter oral EPA/DHA protocol.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
