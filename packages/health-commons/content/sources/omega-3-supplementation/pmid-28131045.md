This source is included for **inflammation_immune_markers**.

**Findings:** No significant differences were reported between EPA and DHA for the genes investigated; high-dose EPA and DHA had broadly similar effects on many inflammatory genes in immune cells. [source_artifact:pmid-28131045]

**Why it matters:** Mechanistic companion to a direct EPA-vs-DHA inflammation trial.

**Potential experiment signals:** gene expression, EPA vs DHA, ComparED.

**Protocol takeaway:** Mechanistic evidence does not always mirror serum biomarker differences.

**Population mismatch:** Same-mechanism context only.

**Safety notes:** Safety outcomes were not extracted.

**Limitations:** Mechanistic gene-expression companion paper, not clinical outcome evidence. Exact gene-by-gene estimates were not fully extracted.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
