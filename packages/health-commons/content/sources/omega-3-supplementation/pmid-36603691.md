This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** Scoping review identified 78 RCTs; accessible summaries report that 43.6% had a positive cognitive outcome, highlighting heterogeneity rather than a pooled efficacy estimate. Safety note: Safety not extracted.

**Why it matters:** Trial-design heterogeneity is central to interpreting cognition claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** trial design, cognitive outcomes, population heterogeneity.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to frame uncertainty, not to claim efficacy.

**Population mismatch:** Background source mixing many populations; not direct consumer evidence.

**Limitations:** Scoping review is not a quantitative effect source.; Positive-outcome proportion is vulnerable to endpoint and reporting heterogeneity.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
