This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** The meta-analysis incorporated 41 randomized controlled trials on EPA/DHA supplementation and reported moderate reductions in IL-6, TNF-alpha, CK, and DOMS, with more dispersed CRP responses. The abstract-level subgroup signal favored mixed EPA+DHA at at least 2 g/day for at least 6 weeks, especially in recreational rather than elite athletes.

**Why it matters:** It provides the most recent sports-recovery synthesis in the batch and adds dose-duration hypotheses for later synthesis.

**Potential experiment signals:** DOMS score 24-72 hours after exercise, CK if tested, CRP if tested, training status subgroup notes.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only direct-protocol synthesis for exercise-recovery outcomes; keep dose-duration claims as hypotheses until checked against primary trials.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
