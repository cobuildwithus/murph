This source is included for **omega3_index_kinetics_formulation**.

**Findings:** DHA increased omega-3 index more than EPA at the same high dose; accessible data report increases of about +5.6 percentage points for DHA and +3.3 percentage points for EPA versus control. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-28515020`.

**Why it matters:** EPA and DHA can have different effects on the omega-3 index even at matched dose.

**Potential experiment signals:** omega-3 index, EPA-only dose, DHA-only dose, washout

**Safety notes:** Safety details were not extracted.

**Limitations / boundary:** High-dose single-fatty-acid design differs from mixed EPA+DHA products.; Population had abdominal obesity and inflammatory phenotype.; Biomarker endpoint only. Population mismatch: Direct oral omega-3 biomarker evidence, but EPA-only/DHA-only high-dose arms differ from common mixed products.

**Protocol takeaway:** Track EPA:DHA ratio separately from total EPA+DHA dose.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`. Directness: `direct_protocol`.
