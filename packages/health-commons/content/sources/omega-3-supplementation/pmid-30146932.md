This source is included for **safety_adverse_events**.

**Findings:** Adults with diabetes and no known cardiovascular disease were randomized to omega-3 fatty acid 840 mg/day or placebo. Major adverse cardiovascular events were not reduced; atrial fibrillation was similar between groups, while a ventricular arrhythmia signal was reported in trial summaries.

**Why it matters:** Large long-term low-dose mixed EPA+DHA RCT provides context for cardiovascular and arrhythmia safety in a medication/comorbidity population.

**Potential experiment signals:** major adverse cardiovascular events, atrial fibrillation, ventricular arrhythmia, diabetes status, medication use.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety/context only: low-dose omega-3 did not prevent major cardiovascular events in diabetes and had no atrial-fibrillation advantage; not a wellness-efficacy claim.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness:** `safety_boundary` for `protocol_variant:omega-3-supplementation/oral-epa-dha-supplementation`.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Clinical diabetes population and long-term cardiovascular outcomes are not direct short-term wellness endpoints; safety details are not fully available from the abstract alone.
