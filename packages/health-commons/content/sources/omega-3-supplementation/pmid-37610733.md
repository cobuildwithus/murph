This source is included for **variant_boundaries_external_context**.

**Findings:** A U.S. label study found widespread health claims and substantial dose heterogeneity, with median EPA+DHA far below prescription-like dosing. [source_artifact:pmid-37610733].

**Why it matters:** Protocol claims should specify actual EPA+DHA dose rather than relying on “fish oil” label presence. [source_artifact:pmid-37610733].

**Potential experiment signals:** EPA dose, DHA dose, total EPA+DHA dose, health claims

**Protocol takeaway:** Protocol claims should specify actual EPA+DHA dose rather than relying on “fish oil” label presence. [source_artifact:pmid-37610733].

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction limitations:**
- Label database study; not a product assay or clinical outcome trial.
- Dose subset smaller than full label sample.
- Claims do not verify efficacy or quality.
