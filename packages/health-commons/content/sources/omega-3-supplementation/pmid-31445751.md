This source is included for **dry_eye_treatment_prevention**.

**Findings:** The paper concluded omega-3 was not beneficial relative to placebo for most exploratory measures; a tear-osmolarity difference was observed but clinical significance was unclear.

**Why it matters:** Retains exploratory null/mixed DREAM context separately from the primary RCT.

**Potential experiment signals:** tear osmolarity, exploratory signs, objective measures.

**Protocol takeaway:** Mechanistic/exploratory context only; does not overturn the primary DREAM null result.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch / boundary:** Direct to DREAM clinical population, but not primary efficacy evidence.

**Safety notes:** No separate safety signal extracted; parent DREAM safety should be referenced for adverse events.

**Limitations:** Secondary analysis.; Multiple exploratory outcomes.; Endpoint-specific sample sizes vary..
