This source is included for **inflammation_immune_markers**.

**Findings:** There were no between-treatment differences for circulating inflammatory markers. Within treatment, n-3 and n-6 interventions each lowered selected circulating markers, while adipose transcriptomic patterns differed by oil type. [source_artifact:pmid-40058591]

**Why it matters:** Recent evidence highlighting mixed biomarker interpretation and active-comparator effects.

**Potential experiment signals:** circulating inflammatory markers, adipose transcriptomics, active comparator.

**Protocol takeaway:** Comparator choice matters; within-arm biomarker changes are not enough for a direct protocol claim.

**Population mismatch:** Direct oral marine n-3 supplementation, but ledger classifies it as context-only because of active n-6 comparator and specific population.

**Safety notes:** Adverse events were not extracted.

**Limitations:** Between-treatment null finding is primary for circulating markers. Comparator is another active PUFA oil rather than inert placebo. Abdominal obesity population and high doses limit generalization.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
