This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** In 11 healthy adults, short-term omega-3 supplementation around eccentric biceps exercise was associated with a smaller increase in arm soreness. Arm circumference, arm volume, and skin-temperature signals did not provide equally strong between-condition support.

**Why it matters:** It is an early direct omega-3 eccentric-exercise study that helps map subjective soreness versus objective marker mismatch.

**Potential experiment signals:** arm soreness, localized swelling, skin temperature, perceived recovery.

**Protocol takeaway:** Promising but small soreness signal; treat as context-only and not a stand-alone efficacy claim.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
