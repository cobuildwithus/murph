This source is included for **dry_eye_treatment_prevention**.

**Findings:** Population: Patients with dry eye disease across clinical subtypes and contributing mechanisms. Intervention/exposure: Dry-eye management strategies organized around current evidence and patient/driver-specific algorithms. Comparator/control: Not applicable. Duration/follow-up: Not applicable. Endpoints: dry-eye management strategy, subtype-specific treatment selection, ocular-surface symptoms and signs. Effect/direction: Not efficacy evidence; extracted summaries describe an evidence-based management review and ongoing uncertainty about optimal omega-3 source, dosage, and omega-3:omega-6 ratio. Safety/adverse events: No batch-extracted adverse-event estimate; safety relevance is clinical triage and treatment selection.

**Why it matters:** It is the current professional boundary source for whether dry-eye content belongs in a separate clinically supervised variant.

**Potential experiment signals:** dry-eye subtype, tear-film stability, ocular-surface inflammation, symptom burden, clinical treatment needs.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only and avoid converting dry-eye guidance into general Oral EPA/DHA claims.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Full report details were not fully extracted in this batch.; Guideline statements should not replace source-specific RCT findings.; Omega-3 questions remain formulation- and subgroup-dependent. Population mismatch: Guideline/report context; not a direct supplementation trial or prevention cohort.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
