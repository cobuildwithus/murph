This source is included for **safety_adverse_events**.

**Findings:** The review included 32 publications in healthy subjects and 20 publications in surgical patients. Fish oil reduced platelet aggregation in healthy subjects, but fish-oil exposure in surgical patients did not increase bleeding or transfusion during or after surgery.

**Why it matters:** Directly addresses the common instruction to stop fish oil before surgery and separates biochemical platelet effects from clinical bleeding outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:** platelet aggregation, perioperative bleeding, transfusions, procedure planning, bruising or nosebleeds.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as perioperative bleeding context: fish oil can reduce platelet aggregation in healthy subjects, but the review did not find increased surgical bleeding or transfusion risk.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness:** `safety_boundary` for `protocol_variant:omega-3-supplementation/oral-epa-dha-supplementation`.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Clinical bleeding was not consistently a primary endpoint, publication/reporting bias was possible, and the review did not produce a pooled bleeding-risk estimate.
