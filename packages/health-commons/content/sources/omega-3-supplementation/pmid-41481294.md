This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** The trial reported no significant benefit over placebo in depression severity for pediatric MDD. Safety note: A total of 76 serious adverse events were reported in 97 participants, including 28 suicide attempts; no deaths or permanent disabilities were reported, and none were judged causally related to study medication in accessible summaries.

**Why it matters:** High-quality pediatric trial prevents overclaiming and highlights safety monitoring.

**Potential experiment signals:** pediatric depression severity, quality of life, antidepressant medication need, serious adverse events, suicide attempts.

**Protocol takeaway:** Pediatric MDD is a do-not-generalize clinical context; omega-3 did not outperform placebo.

**Population mismatch:** Pediatric MDD boundary; not adult general mood support.

**Limitations:** Pediatric psychiatric population; not adult evidence.; Adjunctive/clinical treatment context.; High acuity safety context requires clinician oversight.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
