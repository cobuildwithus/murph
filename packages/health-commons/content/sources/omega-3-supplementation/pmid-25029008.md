This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** DHA supplementation reduced acute CK and IL-6 responses, but did not clearly improve strength, range of motion, or DOMS; longer follow-up signals were borderline or mixed.

**Why it matters:** Useful component-boundary source for DHA-specific effects.

**Potential experiment signals:** CK, IL-6, DOMS, strength, ROM, CRP.

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep as mixed DHA-only evidence; do not merge it into broad combined EPA/DHA recovery claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `direct_protocol`.

**Limitations and caveats:** DHA-only intervention. Untrained male sample. Some participants did not complete the protocol; exact analysis population should be checked before quantitative synthesis.
