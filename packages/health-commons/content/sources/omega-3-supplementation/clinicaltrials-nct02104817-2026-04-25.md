This source is included for **clinical_cardiovascular_lipid_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- The registry record describes a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group outcomes study of Epanova 4 g/day versus corn-oil placebo in high-risk statin-treated patients with persistent hypertriglyceridemia and low HDL-C.
- The primary outcome was time to first occurrence of a MACE composite including cardiovascular death, nonfatal MI, nonfatal stroke, coronary revascularization, or hospitalization for unstable angina.
- This registry page is included for design, product, dose, population, and endpoint boundaries; published outcome results belong on the corresponding journal source page.

**Why it matters:** Separates prescription omega-3 carboxylic-acid cardiovascular-outcomes testing from ordinary EPA/DHA supplementation.

**Potential experiment signals:** MACE composite; triglycerides; HDL-C; statin therapy; corn-oil comparator; product formulation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Epanova/STRENGTH is a prescription free-fatty-acid product in high-risk patients and should remain boundary evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
