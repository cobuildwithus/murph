This source is included for **cardiovascular_outcomes_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- **mixed_result / coronary/CVD events:** Updated meta-analysis gives mixed favorable CVD context while preserving no-stroke and heterogeneity caveats. Effect/direction: Excluding REDUCE-IT: lower MI, CHD death, total CHD, CVD death, and total CVD; no stroke benefit.
- **limitation / trial heterogeneity:** Do not generalize high-dose or prescription-like trial effects to ordinary EPA/DHA supplementation. Effect/direction: Including REDUCE-IT strengthened associations but increased heterogeneity and mixed prescription-like/high-dose context.

**Why it matters:** Helps explain why outcome evidence differs across newer meta-analyses.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- myocardial infarction
- CHD death
- total CHD
- CVD death
- total CVD
- stroke
- major vascular events

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as context for heterogeneous cardiovascular outcomes and dose-response debate.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
