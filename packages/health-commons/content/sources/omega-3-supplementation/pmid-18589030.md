This source is included for **omega3_index_kinetics_formulation**.

**Findings:** Algal-oil capsules and cooked salmon were reported as bioequivalent for delivering DHA to plasma and red blood cells. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-18589030`.

**Why it matters:** This source is important for non-fish supplement variants and DHA-specific status response.

**Potential experiment signals:** plasma DHA, red-blood-cell DHA, source equivalence

**Safety notes:** No detailed adverse-event extraction was available from the accessible record.

**Limitations / boundary:** DHA-focused rather than combined EPA+DHA.; Short duration.; Comparator is a food source, so food-versus-capsule conclusions are bounded. Population mismatch: Direct for algal DHA capsules, but adjacent for full EPA+DHA fish-oil protocols.

**Protocol takeaway:** Algal DHA capsules can raise DHA status similarly to a food comparator over short follow-up.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`. Directness: `direct_protocol`.
