This source is included for **inflammation_immune_markers**.

**Findings:** The umbrella review concluded that n-3 PUFA supplementation in adults can improve CRP, TNF-alpha, and IL-6 under various health conditions. [source_artifact:pmid-35914448]

**Why it matters:** This updates the broad evidence map for inflammatory biomarkers through 2022.

**Potential experiment signals:** CRP, IL-6, TNF-alpha, umbrella-level certainty.

**Protocol takeaway:** Useful as high-level context for inflammatory-marker plausibility.

**Population mismatch:** Broad adult and clinical populations create population mismatch for a general wellness protocol.

**Safety notes:** No batch-level safety extraction was available for this umbrella review.

**Limitations:** Umbrella-level evidence depends on included meta-analysis quality and overlap. Not restricted to healthy adults or to a single oral EPA/DHA formulation.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
