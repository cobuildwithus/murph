This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** Vertical jump, soreness, and creatine kinase changed after the eccentric bout, but the omega-3 groups did not show between-group recovery advantages over placebo at post-exercise time points.

**Why it matters:** Important null boundary for trained users who may already be adapted to eccentric loading.

**Potential experiment signals:** vertical jump height, perceived muscle soreness, creatine kinase, range of motion, repetitions to fatigue.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promise recovery benefits in resistance-trained males; this direct trial found no clear advantage at 6 or 8 g/day.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `direct_protocol`.

**Limitations and caveats:** Small sample and unequal arm sizes. Male-only resistance-trained sample. Creatine kinase remained elevated even when soreness and jump height returned toward baseline, complicating endpoint interpretation.
