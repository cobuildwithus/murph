This source is included for **safety_adverse_events**.

**Findings:** Primary endpoint occurred in 108 (21.4%) n-3 PUFA vs 102 (20.0%) placebo (HR 1.08, 95% CI 0.82-1.41; P=0.60). New AF occurred in 28 (7.2%) vs 15 (4.0%) (HR 1.84, 95% CI 0.98-3.45; P=0.06). Major bleeding occurred in 54 (10.7%) vs 56 (11.0%) (P=0.87).

**Why it matters:** Older post-MI/high-risk boundary source; AF imbalance did not meet conventional significance, bleeding was similar.

**Potential experiment signals:** outcome:major-adverse-cardiovascular-events, adverse_event:atrial-fibrillation, adverse_event:bleeding.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only evidence only: Primary endpoint occurred in 108 (21.4%) n-3 PUFA vs 102 (20.0%) placebo (HR 1.08, 95% CI 0.82-1.41; P=0.60). New AF occurred in 28 (7.2%) vs 15 (4.0%) (HR 1.84, 95% CI 0.98-3.45; P=0.06). Major bleeding occurred in 54 (10.7%) vs 56 (11.0%) (P=0.87).

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Secondary-prevention elderly post-MI trial, not general wellness supplementation.

**Limitations:** Older Norwegian post-MI population, high baseline fish intake possible, high cardiovascular risk; AF endpoint not the primary outcome.
