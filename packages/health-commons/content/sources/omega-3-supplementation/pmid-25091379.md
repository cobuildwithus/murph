This source is included for **inflammation_immune_markers**.

**Findings:** Supplemental EPA+DHA did not significantly affect IL-6 or CRP and only showed a marginal treatment effect for TNF-alpha; baseline erythrocyte DHA status was inversely associated with TNF-alpha. [source_artifact:pmid-25091379]

**Why it matters:** Direct healthy-adult null/mixed evidence prevents overclaiming inflammation benefits.

**Potential experiment signals:** CRP, IL-6, TNF-alpha, erythrocyte DHA, healthy adults.

**Protocol takeaway:** Healthy users may need lipid/omega-3-index endpoints rather than expecting CRP movement.

**Population mismatch:** Direct oral EPA+DHA evidence in healthy adults, but null clinical biomarker response is expectedly low-signal.

**Safety notes:** Safety notes were not extracted.

**Limitations:** Healthy low-risk participants may have low baseline inflammation. Baseline fatty-acid associations are observational within the trial cohort and not causal.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
