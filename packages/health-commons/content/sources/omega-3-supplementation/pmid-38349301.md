**Findings:** Population: Patients evaluated or treated for dry eye syndrome in ophthalmology/eye-care settings. **Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only boundary language when dry eye is mentioned.

Population mismatch: Clinical management guidance for diagnosed dry eye, not a direct test of oral EPA/DHA in generally healthy adults.
