This source is included for **omega3_index_kinetics_formulation**.

**Findings:** Monoglyceride form produced a higher 24-hour plasma EPA exposure than ethyl esters and triglycerides; reported side effects were similar across forms and included eructation, dysgeusia, abdominal discomfort, nausea, and bloating. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-33564872`.

**Why it matters:** Short-term absorption differences may explain some product-level response differences.

**Potential experiment signals:** plasma EPA AUC, plasma DHA AUC, eructation, nausea, bloating

**Safety notes:** Similar mild gastrointestinal/sensory side effects were reported across supplement forms.

**Limitations / boundary:** Acute pharmacokinetic study, not long-term omega-3 index response.; Small sample.; Same-mechanism context rather than direct protocol-outcome evidence. Population mismatch: Same-mechanism formulation evidence, not a direct long-term protocol trial.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use formulation PK evidence as mechanism context and capture GI tolerability signals.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. Directness: `same_mechanism`.
