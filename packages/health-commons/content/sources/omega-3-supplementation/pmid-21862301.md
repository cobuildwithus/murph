This source is included for **clinical_cardiovascular_lipid_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- The trial randomized 150 statin-treated dyslipidemic participants to corn-oil placebo or fish oil in re-esterified triacylglycerol or ethyl-ester form, using identical EPA+DHA doses of 1.01 g EPA plus 0.67 g DHA daily.
- Re-esterified triacylglycerol EPA+DHA significantly reduced fasting serum triglycerides from baseline after 3 and 6 months; the ethyl-ester form did not, and there was no clear significant difference between the two omega-3 groups.
- No changes were reported for total cholesterol, HDL-C, or LDL-C.

**Why it matters:** Directly relevant to EPA+DHA formulation but still a clinician-managed dyslipidemia boundary trial.

**Potential experiment signals:** fasting triglycerides; LDL-C; HDL-C; total cholesterol; formulation; adherence; background statin therapy.

**Protocol takeaway:** Formulation may matter for triglyceride response; do not assume all EPA+DHA products behave identically.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
