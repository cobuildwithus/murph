This source is included for **cardiovascular_outcomes_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- **mixed_result / MI and CHD outcomes:** Dose-response meta-analysis suggests coronary signals but remains heterogeneous and context-only. Effect/direction: RRs favored supplementation for MI (0.87), CHD events (0.90), fatal MI (0.65), and CHD mortality (0.91); CVD events borderline RR 0.95.
- **dose / dose-response for CVD events and MI:** Dose may influence some coronary endpoints, but ecological meta-regression should not be treated as individual-level dosing guidance. Effect/direction: Meta-regression reported dose-response patterns for CVD events and MI.

**Why it matters:** Shows why cardiovascular evidence is mixed rather than uniformly null, while preserving dose/formulation caveats.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- myocardial infarction
- coronary heart disease events
- fatal myocardial infarction
- coronary heart disease mortality
- cardiovascular events

**Protocol takeaway:** Dose may matter for some coronary endpoints, but evidence is context-only and heterogeneous.

## Limitations

- Meta-regression is ecological and cannot prove an individual dose-response.
- Formulations, background risk, and doses vary widely.
- Includes clinical/high-risk contexts and should remain context-only.
