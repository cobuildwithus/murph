This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** This 2025 RCT tested acute fish oil around eccentric contraction-induced muscle damage in healthy young adults. Public abstract-level information describes modest soreness or recovery support, with inflammatory changes such as TNF-alpha not clearly isolated to treatment.

**Why it matters:** It tests whether a same-day fish-oil strategy has measurable recovery effects, a distinct variant from sustained supplementation.

**Potential experiment signals:** DOMS at 48 hours, strength recovery, functional recovery notes.

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep acute fish-oil findings separate from sustained oral EPA/DHA supplementation claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
