This source is included for **safety_adverse_events**.

**Findings:** A fish-allergic patient developed flushing and pruritus after prescription omega-3-acid ethyl ester exposure; symptoms resolved after discontinuation according to accessible summaries.

**Why it matters:** Case-report boundary for allergic symptoms after a prescription fish-oil product in a patient with fish allergy.

**Potential experiment signals:** fish allergy, flushing, pruritus, respiratory symptoms, product discontinuation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as low-level allergy safety evidence: prescription fish-oil products can still trigger symptoms in fish-allergic patients.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness:** `safety_boundary` for `protocol_variant:omega-3-supplementation/oral-epa-dha-supplementation`.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Single case report, possible confounding, and no population-level risk estimate.
