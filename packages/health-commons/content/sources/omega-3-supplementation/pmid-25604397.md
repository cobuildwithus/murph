**Findings:** A 2015 NZ market survey found major label shortfalls and oxidation-threshold exceedances in sampled fish-oil supplements. [source_artifact:pmid-25604397].

**Why it matters:** Use as a safety-only caution about product selection and verification. [source_artifact:pmid-25604397].

**Potential experiment signals:** EPA+DHA label content, peroxide value, anisidine value, TOTOX

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a safety-only caution about product selection and verification. [source_artifact:pmid-25604397].

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Extraction limitations:**
- New Zealand market sample and study date specific.
- Later surveys in similar markets reported different results, so this cannot be universalized.
- No human clinical outcomes.
