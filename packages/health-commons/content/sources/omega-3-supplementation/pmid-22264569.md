This source is included for **lipids_triglycerides_dose_response**.

**Findings:** Focused review context: EPA/DHA lipid effects include triglyceride lowering, while LDL-C responses can differ by component; DHA is more often associated with LDL-C increases than EPA in related extracted review evidence.

**Why it matters:** Focused LDL-C review useful for preserving mixed and LDL-raising findings.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, ApoB if available, triglycerides, HDL-C, EPA:DHA ratio

**Protocol takeaway:** The protocol should treat LDL-C change as a watch item, especially for DHA-containing or DHA-heavy products.

**Population mismatch:** Review spans product types and clinical contexts, not one home supplement protocol.

**Safety notes:** Not a safety-focused extraction.

**Limitations:**
- Narrative review rather than a primary RCT or de novo pooled estimate for this extraction.
- Exact participant counts and pooled effect sizes were not extracted.
- Should be used to preserve mixed LDL-C interpretation, not as a standalone efficacy estimate.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
