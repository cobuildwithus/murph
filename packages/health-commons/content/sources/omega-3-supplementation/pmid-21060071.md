This source is included for **cardiovascular_outcomes_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- **null_result / sudden cardiac death after MI:** Modern post-MI therapy context showed no added omega-3 advantage. Effect/direction: Omega-3 did not reduce sudden cardiac death or individual cardiac outcomes in the 1-year OMEGA trial.

**Why it matters:** Counters older post-MI positive evidence in a more contemporary treatment setting.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- sudden cardiac death
- total mortality
- major adverse cardiovascular and cerebrovascular events
- triglycerides

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not infer post-MI benefit in modern care, and do not generalize to wellness users.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Source kind:** journal_article
- **Study design:** rct
- **Participants:** 3851 (reported)
- **Population:** patients after acute MI receiving modern guideline-adjusted therapy
- **Intervention/exposure:** highly purified omega-3 fatty acids added to standard post-MI therapy
- **Comparator/control:** placebo/standard therapy
- **Duration/follow-up:** 1 year
- **Endpoints:** sudden cardiac death, total mortality, major adverse cardiovascular and cerebrovascular events, triglycerides
- **Effect or direction:** OMEGA found no benefit: omega-3 treatment did not reduce sudden cardiac death or individual cardiac outcomes; summaries also report no follow-up triglyceride difference.
- **Adverse events/safety:** No batch-specific adverse-event extraction.
- **Population mismatch:** Recent MI patients on guideline therapy.
- **Directness:** `adjacent_variant`
- **Artifact rights status:** `permission_required`

## Limitations

- Post-MI secondary prevention and low event rate under modern therapy.
- Only 1-year follow-up.
- Not a general wellness trial.
