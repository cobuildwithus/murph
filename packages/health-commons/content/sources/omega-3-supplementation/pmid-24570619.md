This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** Participants with higher omega-3 index had less late DOMS at 72–96 hours and some inflammatory/wellbeing signals, but extension, strength, and CK did not clearly differ.

**Why it matters:** Useful for designing self-tracking around omega-3 index rather than assuming dose alone predicts recovery.

**Potential experiment signals:** omega-3 index, DOMS, CRP, CK, blood lactate, POMS/QOL, strength/extension.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mixed measurement-context evidence: omega-3 status may matter, but endpoint results are not uniformly positive.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `direct_protocol`.

**Limitations and caveats:** Design combines supplementation and biomarker subgroup interpretation. Causal inference from omega-3-index strata is limited. No adverse events were reported, but safety power was low.
