This source is included for **safety_adverse_events**.

**Findings:** The report describes recurrent anaphylaxis temporally associated with fish-oil capsule ingestion. It is low-level evidence but important for warning against unsupervised initiation in people with severe allergy histories.

**Why it matters:** Rare severe hypersensitivity case that prevents over-reassurance in allergy screening language.

**Potential experiment signals:** anaphylaxis history, fish allergy, shellfish allergy, acute hypersensitivity symptoms, emergency plan.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as rare severe allergy warning: fish-oil products can be implicated in anaphylaxis case reports even if many purified products are tolerated.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness:** `safety_boundary` for `protocol_variant:omega-3-supplementation/oral-epa-dha-supplementation`.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Single case report, uncertain product composition, and limited generalizability.
