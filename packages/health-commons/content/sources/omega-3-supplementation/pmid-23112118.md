This source is included for **variant_boundaries_external_context**.

**Findings:** This BMJ review found fish-intake associations but no significant supplement RCT effect on cerebrovascular risk, supporting separation of fish, biomarker, and supplement evidence streams. [source_artifact:pmid-23112118].

**Why it matters:** Use as background context, not as direct proof of EPA/DHA supplementation benefit. [source_artifact:pmid-23112118].

**Potential experiment signals:** fish intake, circulating omega-3 status, cerebrovascular outcomes

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as background context, not as direct proof of EPA/DHA supplementation benefit. [source_artifact:pmid-23112118].

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction limitations:**
- Ledger title appeared to map to a different fatty-acid coronary-risk article; this draft uses the PMID/DOI-matched BMJ cerebrovascular-disease title.
- Combines dietary, biomarker, and supplement evidence streams that differ in directness.
- Observational associations should not be framed as supplement causality.
