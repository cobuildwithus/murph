**Findings:** This review supports a krill-oil versus fish-oil boundary and does not justify treating the products as interchangeable. [source_artifact:pmid-26357480].

**Why it matters:** Keep krill-oil findings separate from protocol claims unless the protocol explicitly uses krill oil. [source_artifact:pmid-26357480].

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep krill-oil findings separate from protocol claims unless the protocol explicitly uses krill oil. [source_artifact:pmid-26357480].

- Narrative review.
- Limited and heterogeneous krill-oil comparative evidence.
- Bioavailability is not the same as clinical benefit.
