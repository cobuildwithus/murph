This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** In young untrained women, fish oil was reported to produce lower static and functional soreness effect sizes after resistance exercise, but the available extraction notes indicate these soreness differences were not clearly statistically significant and circumference changes did not separate groups.

**Why it matters:** It provides a young-women RCT counterweight to male-heavy eccentric-exercise studies.

**Potential experiment signals:** static soreness, functional soreness, arm or thigh circumference.

**Protocol takeaway:** Soreness may trend lower, but this is mixed context-only evidence with statistical uncertainty.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
