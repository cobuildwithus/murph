This source is included for **inflammation_immune_markers**.

**Findings:** DHA supplementation decreased serum CRP and other inflammation markers in hypertriglyceridemic men; accessible summaries also describe lower IL-6 and neutrophil-related changes. [source_artifact:pmid-19158225]

**Why it matters:** Direct RCT linking DHA to CRP change in a relevant cardiometabolic subgroup.

**Potential experiment signals:** CRP, IL-6, hypertriglyceridemia, DHA-only.

**Protocol takeaway:** Inflammatory-marker improvement may be more detectable in high-triglyceride or higher-risk subgroups.

**Population mismatch:** Direct oral DHA evidence but not mixed EPA/DHA and not a general healthy population.

**Safety notes:** Adverse-event details were not extracted.

**Limitations:** Small, male-only hypertriglyceridemic sample. DHA-only intervention may not represent combined EPA/DHA products.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
