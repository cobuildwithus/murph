This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** Older meta-analysis summaries suggest improvements in some cognitive domains among adults with mild age-related cognitive impairment, but later larger syntheses have reported more null/mixed findings. Safety note: No safety extraction.

**Why it matters:** Maps earlier positive cognition signals and trial heterogeneity.

**Potential experiment signals:** immediate recall, attention, processing speed, cognitive performance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep as context-only and avoid using it as stand-alone evidence for broad cognitive benefit.

**Population mismatch:** Cognitive-impairment enrichment limits general healthy-adult interpretation.

**Limitations:** Older synthesis superseded in part by larger later meta-analyses.; Participant count and detailed effect sizes not extracted in this run.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
