This source is included for **omega3_index_kinetics_formulation**.

**Findings:** The study characterized incorporation and clearance of omega-3 fatty acids in erythrocyte membranes and plasma phospholipids, supporting the interpretation that blood compartments respond on different time scales. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-17053155`.

**Why it matters:** Timing matters: plasma and erythrocyte biomarkers do not represent the same exposure window.

**Potential experiment signals:** erythrocyte membrane EPA+DHA, plasma phospholipid EPA+DHA, incorporation, clearance

**Safety notes:** Safety data were not extracted.

**Limitations / boundary:** Exact sample size and dosing details were not extracted.; Kinetics source rather than clinical-outcome evidence. Population mismatch: Direct mechanism/kinetics evidence for oral omega-3 status tracking.

**Protocol takeaway:** Omega-3 index/RBC outcomes should be measured after sufficient duration and interpreted as slower-moving status markers.
