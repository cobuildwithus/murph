This source is included for **lipids_triglycerides_dose_response**.

**Findings:** Fish oil versus corn oil lowered triglycerides (WMD −25.50 mg/dL, 95% CI −42.44 to −8.57) and raised HDL-C (WMD 2.54 mg/dL, 95% CI 0.55 to 4.52), with no significant total cholesterol or LDL-C changes in the extracted full-text lines.

**Why it matters:** Recent comparator-specific synthesis useful for placebo/control-oil interpretation.

**Potential experiment signals:** fasting triglycerides, HDL-C, LDL-C, total cholesterol, control-oil type

**Protocol takeaway:** Fish oil versus corn oil supports TG lowering, but trial heterogeneity and comparator choice should be made explicit.

**Population mismatch:** Heterogeneous populations, including metabolic and disease states, not only healthy supplement users.

**Safety notes:** Not a safety-focused extraction.

**Limitations:**
- Health status and baseline lipids varied widely.
- High heterogeneity remained unresolved.
- Comparator is corn oil, which may not represent all placebo/control oils.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
