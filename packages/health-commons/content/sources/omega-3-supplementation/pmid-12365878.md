This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** The 1 g/day ethyl-EPA group was significantly better than placebo on all three rating scales; ITT response was 53% for 1 g/day versus 29% placebo. The 2 g group showed little efficacy and 4 g showed nonsignificant trends.

**Why it matters:** Historical EPA-dose context only.

**Potential experiment signals:** depression rating scales, response, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Early dose-ranging depression trial found strongest response at 1 g/day ethyl-EPA. Small clinical trial; not a wellness dose rule.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Patients with ongoing depression despite apparently adequate treatment with standard antidepressant drugs
- **Intervention/exposure:** Ethyl-eicosapentaenoate at 1, 2, or 4 g/day added to existing treatment
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo add-on
- **Duration/follow-up:** 12 weeks
- **Endpoints:** depression rating scales, response, adverse events
- **Effect estimate or direction:** The 1 g/day ethyl-EPA group was significantly better than placebo on all three rating scales; ITT response was 53% for 1 g/day versus 29% placebo. The 2 g group showed little efficacy and 4 g showed nonsignificant trends.
- **Adverse events / safety notes:** 46/52 EPA recipients and 14/18 placebo recipients completed; no serious adverse events were reported in accessible abstract.
- **Population mismatch:** Clinical supervised adjunctive depression treatment, not general protocol evidence.
- **Artifact rights status:** unknown

## Limitations

- Small early dose-ranging psychiatric-treatment trial.
- Adjunctive antidepressant context.
- Nonlinear dose findings require replication.
