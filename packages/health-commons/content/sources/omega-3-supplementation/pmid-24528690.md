This source is included for **clinical_cardiovascular_lipid_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- EVOLVE randomized 399 adults with triglycerides at least 500 and under 2000 mg/dL to olive-oil control or omega-3 free fatty acids at 2, 3, or 4 g/day for 12 weeks.
- Median triglyceride changes were -25.9%, -25.5%, and -30.9% in the 2, 3, and 4 g/day groups versus -4.3% with control; non-HDL-C and several other lipid parameters also decreased.
- LDL-C increased significantly with 2 and 4 g/day but not 3 g/day; adverse events were more frequent with omega-3 free fatty acids, and discontinuation due to adverse or gastrointestinal events occurred in 5-7% versus 0% with control.

**Why it matters:** Illustrates dose-response and safety boundaries for pharmacologic omega-3 mixtures containing EPA and DHA.

**Potential experiment signals:** triglycerides; non-HDL-C; LDL-C; ApoB if available; GI adverse events; discontinuation; dose.

**Protocol takeaway:** High-dose omega-3 lipid changes should be monitored with LDL-C and adverse-event tracking, especially in severe hypertriglyceridemia.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
