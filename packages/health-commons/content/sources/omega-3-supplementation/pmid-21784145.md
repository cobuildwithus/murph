This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** Omega-3 group showed about 20% lower anxiety symptoms and 14% lower LPS-stimulated IL-6 versus controls; depressive symptoms did not significantly change.

**Why it matters:** Direct source for anxiety and inflammation tracking, not depression claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** anxiety symptoms, depressive symptoms, stimulated IL-6, TNF-alpha.

**Protocol takeaway:** EPA-rich omega-3 lowered anxiety and inflammatory response in medical students, but not depression. Small population-specific trial.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Healthy young adult medical students under academic stress
- **Intervention/exposure:** Omega-3 capsules providing about 2.5 g/day total n-3, including 2085 mg EPA and 348 mg DHA
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo capsules mimicking typical American diet fats
- **Duration/follow-up:** 12 weeks
- **Endpoints:** anxiety symptoms, depressive symptoms, stimulated IL-6, TNF-alpha
- **Effect estimate or direction:** Omega-3 group showed about 20% lower anxiety symptoms and 14% lower LPS-stimulated IL-6 versus controls; depressive symptoms did not significantly change.
- **Adverse events / safety notes:** Safety/adverse-event details were not extracted in this batch.
- **Population mismatch:** Direct healthy-adult oral EPA/DHA evidence, but narrow student/stress context.
- **Artifact rights status:** open_access

## Limitations

- Small sample of medical students.
- Stress context; anxiety benefit should not be generalized to all mood outcomes.
- No significant depression effect.
