This source is included for **lipids_triglycerides_dose_response**.

**Findings:** Accessible snippets state dietary DHA, in the absence of EPA, affected lipoprotein cholesterol and triglyceride levels in patients with combined hyperlipidemia; exact source effect sizes were not extracted in this batch. A later accessible citation context reports a 13.6% LDL-C increase in male subjects from this study.

**Why it matters:** Older DHA-only randomized trial in combined hyperlipidemia.

**Potential experiment signals:** triglycerides, LDL-C, HDL-C, non-HDL-C, phospholipid DHA

**Protocol takeaway:** Use cautiously as DHA-only direct evidence; numeric claims need full-text extraction.

**Population mismatch:** Combined hyperlipidemia patients may differ from general wellness users.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event extraction available.

**Limitations:**
- Participant count, dose, duration, and exact source effect sizes were not extracted from accessible abstract/snippet.
- Older DHA-only trial.
- Combined hyperlipidemia population.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
