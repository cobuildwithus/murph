This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** All omega-3 arms increased the omega-3 index versus placebo; EPA+DHA showed only a nonsignificant trend for soreness and strength, while EPA-only and DHA-only arms had faster recovery for selected jump-power metrics.

**Why it matters:** Key source for component-specific uncertainty in oral EPA/DHA exercise-recovery claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** omega-3 index, muscle soreness, leg strength, jump power, inflammation, range of motion, swelling.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mixed direct evidence: omega-3 status changed, but combined EPA/DHA was not clearly superior across recovery outcomes.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `direct_protocol`.

**Limitations and caveats:** Small arm sizes. Male-only sample. Endpoint-specific effects make any single recovery headline too broad.
