This source is included for **variant_boundaries_external_context**.

**Findings:** The scoping review supports separate treatment of ALA-based plant sources and preformed algal EPA/DHA products. [source_artifact:pmid-33576691].

**Why it matters:** For vegan users, algal DHA/EPA may be protocol-relevant while ALA-only sources remain adjacent. [source_artifact:pmid-33576691].

**Potential experiment signals:** vegan supplement form, blood EPA, blood DHA, omega-3 index

**Protocol takeaway:** For vegan users, algal DHA/EPA may be protocol-relevant while ALA-only sources remain adjacent. [source_artifact:pmid-33576691].

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction limitations:**
- Scoping review design.
- Few trials focused on actual vegan/vegetarian populations.
- Plant ALA and algal preformed DHA/EPA are different interventions.
