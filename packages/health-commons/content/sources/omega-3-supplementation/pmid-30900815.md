This source is included for **inflammation_immune_markers**.

**Findings:** EPA+DHA, but not ALA, improved lipid and inflammatory status; low and high EPA+DHA doses decreased triglycerides, and high-dose EPA+DHA produced the greatest decrease in PBMC IL-6 production. [source_artifact:pmid-30900815]

**Why it matters:** Useful direct oral EPA+DHA evidence plus boundary comparison against ALA.

**Potential experiment signals:** triglycerides, PBMC IL-6, EPA+DHA vs ALA.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports differentiating marine EPA/DHA from plant ALA in protocol claims.

**Population mismatch:** Direct EPA+DHA evidence, but disease-risk population and cell-stimulation endpoint are not a general wellness endpoint.

**Safety notes:** Adverse-event details were not extracted.

**Limitations:** Hypercholesterolemic population and PBMC stimulation endpoint may not generalize to healthy adults. An erratum exists for the article; final numeric values should be checked before protocol synthesis.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
