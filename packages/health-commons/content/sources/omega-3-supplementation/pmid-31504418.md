This source is included for **clinical_cardiovascular_lipid_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- The guideline recommends statin treatment as first drug choice for reducing CVD risk in high-risk people with hypertriglyceridemia.
- For high-risk or higher patients with triglycerides 135-499 mg/dL despite statin treatment, the guideline considered high-dose icosapent ethyl 2 g twice daily in combination with statins.
- Broader omega-3 cardiovascular outcome evidence was described as inconclusive, with many older trials using about 1 g/day that did not substantially affect plasma lipids.

**Why it matters:** International guideline context for lipid targets, clinical risk category, and prescription omega-3 exceptions.

**Potential experiment signals:** triglycerides; LDL-C; statin use; risk category; prescription IPE eligibility.

**Protocol takeaway:** Abnormal triglycerides should be contextualized within risk category and clinician-guided lipid therapy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
