**Findings:** Long-chain omega-3 fatty acids were reported to improve brain function and structure in older adults.

**Why it matters:** Provides mechanistic-adjacent support for brain endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** brain function, brain structure, cognition.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use cautiously for brain-structure/function signals, not broad cognitive claims.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy older adults only.

**Limitations:** Small sample size.; Brain-structure/function endpoints are mechanistic-adjacent and may not translate to everyday cognition.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
