This source is included for **dry_eye_treatment_prevention**.

**Findings:** OSDI change at 6 and 12 weeks: −20.5 and −22.7 in omega-3 vs −15.1 and −18.8 in grape-seed oil; between-group differences were not significant (6 weeks p=.12; 12 weeks p=.28). No supplement-related adverse events or safety parameter changes were reported.

**Why it matters:** Recent high-quality counterweight to earlier positive formulation-specific trials.

**Potential experiment signals:** OSDI, TBUT, MMP-9, MGD signs, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Important direct negative/mixed evidence for not over-promoting re-esterified EPA/DHA in dry-eye disease.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Population mismatch / boundary:** Direct dry-eye/MGD clinical population; context-only for non-clinical self-experiment claims.

**Safety notes:** No changes in visual acuity or intraocular pressure safety parameters, and no adverse events related to supplements in either group.

**Limitations:** Under 60 completers per group at 12 weeks.; Grape-seed oil may have biological activity.; Secondary outcomes should be hypothesis-generating..
