This source is included for **exercise_recovery_soreness**.

**Findings:** In a 20-male double-blind RCT, six weeks of fish oil before 200 eccentric knee contractions reduced selected oxidative-stress markers, including TBARS at 48 and 72 hours, but did not reduce CK, protein carbonyls, DNA damage, soreness, or maximal voluntary contraction changes.

**Why it matters:** It clearly shows biomarker movement without a matching soreness improvement.

**Potential experiment signals:** soreness score, strength recovery, oxidative stress markers if available.

**Protocol takeaway:** Biomarker improvements do not guarantee less soreness or better muscle function.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
