This source is included for **safety_adverse_events**.

**Findings:** The advisory focuses on prescription omega-3 fatty acids for triglyceride lowering, typically at 4 g/day. Safety framing includes tolerability, gastrointestinal/fishy-taste effects, lipid monitoring, and the need to distinguish EPA-only from EPA+DHA products.

**Why it matters:** Frames the clinical-dose boundary: prescription EPA+DHA or EPA-only products at about 4 g/day are a supervised triglyceride-lowering intervention, not a general starter supplement protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** triglycerides, LDL-C, GI side effects, fishy taste or eructation, clinical supervision boundary.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as a clinical-supervised dose boundary for hypertriglyceridemia and safety monitoring, not as support for unsupervised high-dose supplementation.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness:** `clinical_supervised` for `protocol_variant:omega-3-supplementation/oral-epa-dha-supplementation`.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Professional advisory rather than new trial data; clinical hypertriglyceridemia populations and prescription doses differ from a non-prescription community protocol.
