This source is included for **variant_boundaries_external_context**.

**Findings:** ALA-rich interventions generally increase EPA but not DHA or omega-3 index in the reviewed obesity-related context. [source_artifact:pmid-35889342].

**Why it matters:** Protocol should avoid using ALA studies as direct EPA/DHA evidence, especially across different metabolic populations. [source_artifact:pmid-35889342].

**Potential experiment signals:** EPA, DHA, omega-3 index, desaturase activity

**Protocol takeaway:** Protocol should avoid using ALA studies as direct EPA/DHA evidence, especially across different metabolic populations. [source_artifact:pmid-35889342].

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction limitations:**
- ALA exposure is not preformed EPA/DHA.
- Obesity-related metabolism may not generalize to healthy adults.
- Review-level evidence; no single protocol outcome.
