This source is included for **clinical_cardiovascular_lipid_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- JELIS randomized 18,645 Japanese patients with hypercholesterolemia to statin therapy with or without 1.8 g/day EPA and followed them for a mean of 4.6 years.
- Major coronary events occurred in 2.8% of EPA-treated patients and 3.5% of controls, a reported 19% relative reduction; primary-prevention subgroup results were not statistically significant while secondary-prevention results were significant.
- Adverse events were higher with EPA plus statin, especially gastrointestinal disorders; the trial was EPA-only, open-label, and not a placebo-controlled consumer-supplement trial.

**Why it matters:** Important boundary trial showing why EPA-only prescription/statin-adjunct outcomes must be kept separate from consumer EPA/DHA protocol claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** major coronary events; unstable angina; nonfatal MI; coronary death; statin use; GI adverse events; background fish intake context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not cite JELIS as direct evidence for ordinary EPA+DHA supplementation; cite it only for the EPA-only clinical-treatment boundary.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
