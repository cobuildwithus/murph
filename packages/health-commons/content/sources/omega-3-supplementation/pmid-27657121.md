This source is included for **safety_adverse_events**.

**Findings:** Fish/krill oil supplementation did not significantly affect warfarin time in therapeutic range, bleeding incidence, or thromboembolic events.

**Why it matters:** Medication-interaction evidence suggests routine fish/krill oil co-use did not meaningfully worsen warfarin control in this retrospective cohort, but monitoring remains prudent.

**Potential experiment signals:** interaction:warfarin, biomarker:inr, adverse_event:bleeding, adverse_event:thromboembolism.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only evidence only: Fish/krill oil supplementation did not significantly affect warfarin time in therapeutic range, bleeding incidence, or thromboembolic events.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Warfarin-treated clinical population, not general supplement users; interaction/safety-only source.

**Limitations:** Retrospective observational design; supplement dose and adherence may vary; cannot rule out rare bleeding interactions or high-dose effects.
