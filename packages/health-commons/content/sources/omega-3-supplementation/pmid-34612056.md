This source is included for **safety_adverse_events**.

**Findings:** Marine omega-3 supplements were associated with increased AF (2,905 events; HR 1.25, 95% CI 1.07-1.46). Dose strata showed HR 1.49 (95% CI 1.04-2.15) for >1 g/day and HR 1.12 (95% CI 1.03-1.22) for ≤1 g/day; each 1 g/day increase was associated with HR 1.11 (95% CI 1.06-1.15).

**Why it matters:** The article is safety-boundary evidence for long-term AF risk and dose escalation.

**Potential experiment signals:** adverse_event:atrial-fibrillation, dose:epa-dha-grams-per-day.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only evidence only: Marine omega-3 supplements were associated with increased AF (2,905 events; HR 1.25, 95% CI 1.07-1.46). Dose strata showed HR 1.49 (95% CI 1.04-2.15) for >1 g/day and HR 1.12 (95% CI 1.03-1.22) for ≤1 g/day; each 1 g/day increase was associated with HR 1.11 (95% CI 1.06-1.15).

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Population mismatch:** High cardiovascular-risk trial populations and long follow-up; not a general healthy-adult supplement trial.

**Limitations:** Limited to large cardiovascular outcome RCTs, not short-term recurrent/postoperative AF trials; trial-level dose-response; populations are older/high-risk relative to typical consumer users.
