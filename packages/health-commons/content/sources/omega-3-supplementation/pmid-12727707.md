This source is included for **mood_cognition**.

**Findings:** DHA response rates were similar to placebo (27.8% DHA vs 23.5% placebo in accessible summary), indicating no clear superiority.

**Why it matters:** Important EPA-vs-DHA boundary source.

**Potential experiment signals:** MADRS response, major depression symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** DHA-only MDD trial found no clear advantage over placebo. Small clinical trial; not a general DHA endpoint conclusion.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction details

- **Population:** Adults with major depression; 35 evaluable participants
- **Intervention/exposure:** DHA 2 g/day
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo
- **Duration/follow-up:** 6 weeks
- **Endpoints:** MADRS response, major depression symptoms
- **Effect estimate or direction:** DHA response rates were similar to placebo (27.8% DHA vs 23.5% placebo in accessible summary), indicating no clear superiority.
- **Adverse events / safety notes:** Adverse-event details were not extracted.
- **Population mismatch:** DHA-only clinical depression trial; adjacent to oral EPA/DHA but not general supplementation evidence.
- **Artifact rights status:** unknown

## Limitations

- Small sample.
- DHA-only formulation.
- Clinical MDD treatment context.
