This source is included for **safety_adverse_events**.

**Findings:** Omega-3 fatty acids increased incident AF (RR 1.26, 95% CI 1.08-1.48). EPA monotherapy was associated with higher total bleeding (RR 1.49, 95% CI 1.20-1.84) and AF (RR 1.35, 95% CI 1.10-1.66); overall GI adverse events, bleeding-related disorders, and cancer were not significantly different in the broader analysis.

**Why it matters:** Useful for separating EPA-only prescription effects from EPA+DHA; certainty for some safety outcomes was low.

**Potential experiment signals:** adverse_event:atrial-fibrillation, adverse_event:bleeding, symptom:gastrointestinal-adverse-effects.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only evidence only: Omega-3 fatty acids increased incident AF (RR 1.26, 95% CI 1.08-1.48). EPA monotherapy was associated with higher total bleeding (RR 1.49, 95% CI 1.20-1.84) and AF (RR 1.35, 95% CI 1.10-1.66); overall GI adverse events, bleeding-related disorders, and cancer were not significantly different in the broader analysis.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Cardiovascular outcome trials and prescription formulations are adjacent to, not identical with, consumer oral EPA/DHA supplementation.

**Limitations:** Trial-level data; no DHA-monotherapy trials; EPA-only benefits/safety should not be generalized to consumer EPA+DHA products.
