This source is included for **omega3_index_kinetics_formulation**.

**Findings:** Higher combined dietary and supplemental EPA+DHA exposure was associated with higher omega-3 index; accessible summaries report substantial product-dose variability and that many users did not reach an omega-3 index of 8%. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-31757628`.

**Why it matters:** Real-world supplement products vary widely, and diet plus supplements jointly influence status.

**Potential experiment signals:** omega-3 index, fish intake, supplemental EPA+DHA dose, product dose variability

**Safety notes:** Not applicable.

**Limitations / boundary:** Observational cross-sectional design cannot prove causality.; Self-reported intake and product variability.; Exact participant count was not extracted from accessible metadata. Population mismatch: Background observational context only.

**Protocol takeaway:** Record both diet and supplemental dose when interpreting omega-3 index.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. Directness: `background`.
