This source is included for **safety_adverse_events**.

**Findings:** Primary endpoint: 17.2% with icosapent ethyl vs 22.0% placebo (HR 0.75, 95% CI 0.68-0.83). AF/flutter was 5.3% vs 3.9%; serious adverse bleeding events 2.7% vs 2.1% (P=0.06).

**Why it matters:** Important EPA-only high-dose benefit/harm boundary; not a nutritional EPA+DHA supplement claim source.

**Potential experiment signals:** outcome:major-adverse-cardiovascular-events, adverse_event:atrial-fibrillation, adverse_event:atrial-flutter, adverse_event:bleeding.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only evidence only: Primary endpoint: 17.2% with icosapent ethyl vs 22.0% placebo (HR 0.75, 95% CI 0.68-0.83). AF/flutter was 5.3% vs 3.9%; serious adverse bleeding events 2.7% vs 2.1% (P=0.06).

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Population mismatch:** Adjacent prescription EPA-only therapy, not direct oral EPA/DHA supplementation.

**Limitations:** EPA-only prescription formulation, mineral oil placebo, high-risk/statin-treated population; benefits cannot be promoted for mixed EPA+DHA consumer supplements.
