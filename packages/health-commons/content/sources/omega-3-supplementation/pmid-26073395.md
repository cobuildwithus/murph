This source is included for **clinical_cardiovascular_lipid_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- Twenty-five individuals completed a double-blind crossover study comparing 0, 0.85, and 3.4 g/day EPA+DHA across 8-week periods with 6-week washouts.
- The 3.4 g/day dose reduced triglycerides by 27% in the companion report and significantly decreased ApoB, ApoC-III, and VLDL-C; the 0.85 g/day dose had no measurable effect on these outcomes.
- No clear inflammatory-marker or endothelial-function benefit was detected, and the study did not evaluate clinical cardiovascular events.

**Why it matters:** Useful dose-response boundary for lipid biomarker expectations and ApoB/ApoC-III watchpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** triglycerides; ApoB; ApoC-III; VLDL-C; Lp-PLA2; inflammatory markers; dose and baseline triglycerides.

**Protocol takeaway:** Expect lipid biomarker effects, if any, to depend strongly on dose and baseline triglycerides.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
