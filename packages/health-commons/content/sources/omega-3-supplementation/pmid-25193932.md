This source is included for **dry_eye_treatment_prevention**.

**Findings:** Population: Participants with dry eye syndrome in comparative studies published through June 1, 2014. Intervention/exposure: Omega-3 fatty acid supplementation. Comparator/control: Placebo fatty acid/control in included comparative studies. Duration/follow-up: Varied across included studies. Endpoints: TBUT, Schirmer test, OSDI. Effect/direction: TBUT was significantly greater by WMD 1.58 seconds (95% CI 0.60-2.55; p=0.007), and Schirmer test improved by WMD 0.74 mm (95% CI 0.29-1.19; p=0.001). OSDI did not significantly improve (WMD -4.54; 95% CI -9.85 to 0.78; p=0.09). Safety/adverse events: No adverse-event extraction available from the accessible review abstract.

**Why it matters:** It captures the early small-trial signal before larger trials and modern reviews complicated the picture.

**Potential experiment signals:** TBUT, Schirmer test, OSDI.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only dry-eye evidence; show alongside later mixed and null anchors.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Older small-trial evidence before later large dry-eye trials.; Limited outcome set and heterogeneous included studies.; OSDI symptom effect was not statistically significant. Population mismatch: Older dry-eye treatment literature; not prevention or general wellness.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
