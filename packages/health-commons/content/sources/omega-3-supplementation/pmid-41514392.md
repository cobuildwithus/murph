This source is included for **clinical_cardiovascular_lipid_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- The pilot trial randomized 47 participants and analyzed 44 completers, 22 per group, after 12 weeks.
- Participants received phospholipid-bound EPA+DHA 825 mg/day or standard EPA+DHA 903 mg/day; triglycerides decreased slightly in the phospholipid group (-9.1 mg/dL) and increased in the standard group (+15.2 mg/dL), but the between-group difference was not statistically significant (p=0.416).
- Omega-3 index rose in both groups without a significant between-group difference; a larger proportion in the phospholipid group reached TG ≤150 mg/dL (36.4% vs 13.6%; p=0.041), no serious adverse events were reported, and the trial was retrospectively registered after first enrollment.

**Why it matters:** Recent direct-adjacent formulation trial preserving null and mixed findings.

**Potential experiment signals:** triglycerides; omega-3 index; responder rate; adherence; safety parameters; inflammatory markers.

**Protocol takeaway:** Formulation advantages remain uncertain at low/moderate doses; track triglycerides and omega-3 index rather than assuming effect.
