## Quick read

- **Source type:** Seven-week double-blind randomized sugar-sweetened beverage physiology trial.
- **People studied or addressed:** Healthy men.
- **Duration or horizon:** 7 weeks.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `same_mechanism`.

## What it contributes

Use as mechanism context for reducing fructose/sucrose-containing beverages; do not claim clinical benefit from no-added-sugar diets.

## Potential Murph endpoints

sugar beverage type and dose, hepatic DNL surrogate, fasting triglycerides/VLDL where measured, overall energy intake

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Healthy men only; not a mixed general population or whole-diet intervention.
- Surrogate hepatic DNL endpoint.
- Healthy male sample.
- The study did not examine hepatic fat in the extracted article summary.
- Safety note: No adverse-event findings were extracted.

## Plain-language takeaway

Beverage sugar type and liquid exposure may matter mechanistically; practical tracking should still focus on food sources, adherence, and symptoms.
