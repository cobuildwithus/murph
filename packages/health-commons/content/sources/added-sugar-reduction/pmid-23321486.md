This source is included for **guidelines-and-synthesis**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Systematic review and meta-analysis of RCTs and cohort studies.
- **People studied or addressed:** Adults and children in randomized trials and prospective cohorts.
- **Duration or horizon:** Varied trial and cohort follow-up.
- **Protocol role:** supports-protocol; directness: `same_mechanism`.

## What it contributes

Use as synthesis evidence that reducing free sugars can affect body weight in ad libitum settings, while noting energy-intake mediation.

## Potential Murph endpoints

Body weight, BMI or adiposity, Sugar-sweetened beverage intake, Energy intake

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Includes varied populations, exposures, and study designs.
- Combines randomized trials and cohort evidence.
- Exposure includes free sugars and sugar-sweetened beverages, not only added sugars.
- Body-weight direction may be mediated by total energy intake.
- Safety note: No adverse-event data extracted.

## Plain-language takeaway

Weight change is plausible, but the protocol should monitor replacement calories and not imply a sugar-specific effect independent of energy balance.
