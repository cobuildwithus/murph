## Quick read

- **Source type:** 12-week randomized controlled trial with free-sugar restriction, ketogenic diet, and control arms.
- **People studied or addressed:** Healthy adults.
- **Duration or horizon:** 12 weeks.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `direct_protocol`.

## What it contributes

Free-sugar restriction should not be conflated with ketogenic restriction; benefits and tradeoffs differed across arms.

## Potential Murph endpoints

fat mass, LDL-C, energy expenditure, glucose tolerance, apoB/CRP context, gut microbiome beta diversity

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Healthy adults; not pediatric, NAFLD, diabetes, or disease-treatment population.
- Participant count extracted from accessible secondary/source snippet; full CONSORT details should be rechecked.
- Free-sugar restriction is not identical to a strict no-added-sugar self-experiment.
- Ketogenic arm is adjacent-variant evidence only.
- Safety note: Ketogenic comparator showed risk-relevant transient lipoprotein and inflammation changes; these should remain separate from free-sugar restriction.

## Plain-language takeaway

Use as a direct but mixed healthy-adult anchor, especially to prevent keto findings from leaking into no-added-sugar claims.
