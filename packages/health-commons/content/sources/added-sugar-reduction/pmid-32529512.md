## Potential Murph endpoints

obesity, type 2 diabetes, hypertension, all-cause mortality

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Prospective beverage cohorts, not direct no-added-sugar intervention evidence.
- Prospective observational studies only.
- Residual confounding and reverse causation are especially relevant for ASB exposure.
- Beverage dose-response does not isolate all added sugars.
- Study populations and exposure measures varied.
- Safety note: ['ASB estimates are cautionary safety/context signals rather than causal adverse-event evidence.']

## Plain-language takeaway

Consider body weight, blood pressure, diabetes status, and beverage type as separate measurement axes.
