This source is included for **sweetener-substitution**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Open-label randomized controlled equivalence trial.
- **People studied or addressed:** Adults with BMI 27-35 who regularly consumed cold beverages; some were NNS-naive.
- **Duration or horizon:** 12 weeks.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `adjacent_variant`.

## What it contributes

Treat as adjacent substitution evidence only; it does not define the no-added-sugar protocol.

## Potential Murph endpoints

Weight change, Waist circumference, HbA1c, Sweetener consumption, Biomarkers

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Adults with overweight/obesity in weekly behavioral weight-management groups.
- Open-label.
- Behavioral program co-intervention.
- Industry funding noted in accessible extraction.
- Safety note: No adverse-event signal extracted; secondary biomarkers were mixed.

## Plain-language takeaway

NNS beverages may be an optional transition tool comparable to water for some adults during active weight loss.
