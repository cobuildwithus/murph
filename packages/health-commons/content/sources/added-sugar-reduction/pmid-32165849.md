## Quick read

- **Source type:** Six-month nonrandomized single-arm social-network feasibility trial.

- **Duration or horizon:** 6 months.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `same_mechanism`.

## What it contributes

Build optional peer/social support and community-context prompts, especially for users whose sugar environment is social.

## Potential Murph endpoints

Group-session attendance, Satisfaction, Added sugar intake, SSB reduction, Peer educator/network outreach

## Plain-language takeaway

Social support may help some communities reduce SSB and added sugar, but this feasibility study needs randomized confirmation.
