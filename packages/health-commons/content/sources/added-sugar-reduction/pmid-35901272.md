## Quick read

- **Source type:** Systematic review and meta-analysis of prospective cohort substitution studies.

- **Duration or horizon:** Prospective cohort follow-up durations varied; exact range not extracted for this run.

## What it contributes

Use only with explicit substitution comparator and observational caveat.

## Potential Murph endpoints

body weight, obesity, coronary heart disease, cardiovascular mortality, total mortality, type 2 diabetes

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Prospective adult cohort substitution models, not randomized no-added-sugar protocol evidence.
- Prospective observational substitution models cannot establish causal replacement effects.
- Residual confounding and health-status changes may affect LNCSB interpretation.
- Comparator-specific findings should not be generalized to all sweetener use.
- Not a no-added-sugar diet intervention.
- Safety note: ['No adverse associations were extracted for LNCSB-for-water models, but this does not prove long-term safety.']

## Plain-language takeaway

Track replacement choices and recognize that modeled substitutions are not randomized changes.
