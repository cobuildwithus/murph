## Potential Murph endpoints

soft drink servings, waist circumference, fasting glucose, triglycerides, HDL cholesterol, blood pressure

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Middle-aged community cohort; soft drinks, not full no-added-sugar diet.
- Observational design.
- Soft-drink category is broader than added sugar alone.
- Potential residual confounding and dietary-pattern collinearity.
- Some endpoint associations were not statistically significant.
- Safety note: ['No intervention adverse events apply.']

## Plain-language takeaway

If monitoring cardiometabolic response, distinguish waist, lipids, glucose, and blood pressure rather than assuming uniform effects.
