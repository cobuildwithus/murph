## Quick read

- **Source type:** Systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized trials.
- **People studied or addressed:** Participants in randomized trials substituting SSBs with non-caloric alternatives for at least 6 months.
- **Duration or horizon:** At least 6 months; one trial included post-intervention follow-up.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `adjacent_variant`.

## What it contributes

Use as SSB-replacement context only; water and artificial-sweetener alternatives must remain separated when possible.

## Potential Murph endpoints

BMI, Weight change, Persistence after intervention

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Randomized SSB-substitution trials, not whole-diet added-sugar avoidance.
- Only six trials in accessible extraction.
- Alternatives include both water and artificially sweetened beverages.
- Benefit may not persist after intervention stops.
- Safety note: No adverse-event extraction in this batch.

## Plain-language takeaway

Sustained replacement matters; stopping intervention may reduce benefit.
