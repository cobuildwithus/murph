## Quick read

- **Source type:** Randomized clinical trial.
- **People studied or addressed:** Overweight/obese adult women who were regular diet-beverage consumers.
- **Duration or horizon:** 24 weeks.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `adjacent_variant`.

## What it contributes

Water should remain a clean default option; diet beverages are not required and may not be superior in every population.

## Potential Murph endpoints

Body weight, Insulin sensitivity, 2-hour postprandial glucose, Waist circumference, Fasting glucose, Lipids

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Overweight/obese women, regular diet-beverage consumers, hypoenergetic diet setting.
- Not general population.
- Only women in accessible extraction.
- Energy restriction co-intervention.
- Safety note: No adverse-event signal extracted; metabolic endpoint results were mixed.

## Plain-language takeaway

A diet-beverage taper to water can be a reasonable tracked variant for current diet-beverage users.
