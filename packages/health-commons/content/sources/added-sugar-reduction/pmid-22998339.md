## Quick read

- **Source type:** Randomized controlled trial.
- **People studied or addressed:** Overweight and obese adolescents who regularly consumed sugar-sweetened beverages..
- **Duration or horizon:** One-year intervention plus one-year post-intervention follow-up..
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `adjacent_variant`.

## What it contributes

A 1-year adolescent SSB-reduction program lowered intake and short-term BMI/weight, but not the overall 2-year primary BMI endpoint. Boundary: Adjacent SSB-only evidence: use to inform beverage substitution, implementation, measurement, and population-specific outcome expectations, not as direct proof that a full no-added-sugar diet works.

## Potential Murph endpoints

SSB servings/day, BMI, body weight, body fat percentage, adverse events

## Plain-language takeaway

Useful for setting expectations: beverage-focused changes may show early weight signals, but maintenance and subgroup effects matter. Use only with its mixed/null boundaries preserved.
