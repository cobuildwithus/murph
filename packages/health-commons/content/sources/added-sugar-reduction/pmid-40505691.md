## Quick read

- **Source type:** Cluster randomized controlled trial maintenance analysis.
- **People studied or addressed:** Rural Appalachian 7th-grade students and caregivers with complete maintenance analysis data..
- **Duration or horizon:** 19-month maintenance follow-up after baseline..
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `adjacent_variant`.

## What it contributes

Kids SIPsmartER maintained student SSB reductions at 19 months, but caregiver reductions were not sustained. Boundary: Adjacent SSB-only evidence: use to inform beverage substitution, implementation, measurement, and population-specific outcome expectations, not as direct proof that a full no-added-sugar diet works.

## Potential Murph endpoints

Student SSB intake maintenance, caregiver SSB intake maintenance

## Plain-language takeaway

Important maintenance boundary: adolescent school-based effects may persist, but low-touch caregiver SMS support may need more reinforcement. Use only with its mixed/null boundaries preserved.
