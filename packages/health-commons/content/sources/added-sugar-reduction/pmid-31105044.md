- Population boundary: Inpatient metabolic-ward setting; UPF contrast matched for sugar; not no-added-sugar intervention.
- Short inpatient trial.
- UPF pattern was the intervention, not added sugar.
- Diets were matched for sugar, so sugar-specific conclusions are inappropriate.
- Small sample size.
- Safety note: ['No no-added-sugar safety inference should be drawn from this UPF trial.']

## Plain-language takeaway

Food processing and eating rate can affect intake independent of sugar targets.
