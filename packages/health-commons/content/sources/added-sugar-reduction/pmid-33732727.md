## Quick read

- **Source type:** Prospective cohort.
- **People studied or addressed:** Older Australian adults in the Blue Mountains Eye Study.
- **Duration or horizon:** 15 years with dietary data at baseline and three 5-year intervals.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `background`.

## What it contributes

Use to preserve null older-adult weight evidence and avoid assuming uniform weight effects.

## Potential Murph endpoints

Body weight change, Added sugar percent energy, Beverage vs non-beverage added sugar

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Older Australian adults; results may differ for younger or higher-sugar users.
- Secondary analysis of observational data.
- Added-sugar intake change may have measurement error.
- Body-weight changes may be affected by illness, aging, and lifestyle factors.
- No direct no-added-sugar assignment.
- Safety note: No adverse-event data extracted; use safety interpretation only where the source itself addresses population or risk boundaries.

## Plain-language takeaway

Use to preserve null older-adult weight evidence and avoid assuming uniform weight effects. Preserve confounding, measurement-error, and comparator uncertainty.
