## Quick read

- **Source type:** USDA Nutrition Evidence Systematic Review for dietary-guidelines evidence.
- **People studied or addressed:** Generally healthy adults and children in Dietary Guidelines Advisory Committee scope.
- **Duration or horizon:** Varied study follow-up.
- **Protocol role:** supports-protocol; directness: `same_mechanism`.

## What it contributes

Use as evidence-grade context: CVD mortality associations exist in limited evidence, but many CVD claims remain insufficient.

## Potential Murph endpoints

CVD mortality, CVD risk profile, Stroke, Ischemic CVD events, Peripheral artery disease, Heart failure

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Dietary-guidelines evidence scope, not protocol intervention.
- Limited adult mortality evidence based primarily on sugar-sweetened beverages.
- Insufficient evidence for several adult and child CVD outcomes.
- Observational confounding and exposure-measure inconsistency remain important.
- Safety note: No adverse-event data extracted.

## Plain-language takeaway

Cardiovascular claims should be cautious and separated by endpoint, especially SSB-based mortality versus intermediate risk-profile evidence.
