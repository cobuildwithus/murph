## Quick read

- **Source type:** Systematic review of RCTs to enhance adherence to dietary advice.
- **People studied or addressed:** Adults in chronic disease prevention or management dietary-advice trials; 38 studies.
- **Duration or horizon:** Varied; generally short duration in included studies.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `same_mechanism`.

## What it contributes

Use multi-component adherence supports cautiously and label them as implementation aids rather than proven sugar-specific mechanisms.

## Potential Murph endpoints

Dietary advice adherence, Follow-up contacts, Feedback, Nutrition tools, Complex interventions

## Plain-language takeaway

Diet adherence support often needs follow-up and feedback, but evidence is mixed and not specific to no-added-sugar diets.
