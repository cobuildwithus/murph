## What it contributes

Child SSB-reduction interventions are relevant but body-fatness findings require careful study-by-study boundaries. Boundary: Adjacent SSB-only evidence: use to inform beverage substitution, implementation, measurement, and population-specific outcome expectations, not as direct proof that a full no-added-sugar diet works.
