This source is included for **sweetener-substitution**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Women’s Health Initiative cohort analysis plus network meta-analysis of prospective studies.
- **People studied or addressed:** Postmenopausal women in the Women’s Health Initiative and prospective-study evidence base.
- **Duration or horizon:** WHI 1993–2021 follow-up window; prospective-study durations varied.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `adjacent_variant`.

## What it contributes

Use cautiously as CVD context; do not collapse added sugar, SSB, and ASB into one protocol claim.

## Potential Murph endpoints

total cardiovascular disease, coronary heart disease, stroke

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Postmenopausal women and network meta-analysis context, not a general no-added-sugar diet trial.
- WHI population was postmenopausal women.
- Observational analyses are vulnerable to residual confounding.
- Network meta-analysis had substantial heterogeneity and no consistent pattern.
- Added sugar, SSB, and ASB are not interchangeable exposures.
- Safety note: ['ASB cardiovascular associations are safety/context signals only, not causal adverse-event evidence.']

## Plain-language takeaway

Separate total added sugar, SSBs, and ASBs when measuring cardiovascular-risk context.
