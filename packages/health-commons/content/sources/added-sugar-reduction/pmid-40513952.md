A digital behavioral SSB-reduction intervention was reported as efficacious with high engagement and retention. Boundary: Adjacent SSB-only evidence: use to inform beverage substitution, implementation, measurement, and population-specific outcome expectations, not as direct proof that a full no-added-sugar diet works.
