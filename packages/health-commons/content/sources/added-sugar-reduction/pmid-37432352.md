This source is included for **sweetener-substitution**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Unblinded three-group randomized parallel trial.
- **People studied or addressed:** Young healthy adults who regularly consumed sugar-sweetened beverages.
- **Duration or horizon:** 12 weeks.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `adjacent_variant`.

## What it contributes

Do not claim that ASB switching produces broad metabolic or cognitive improvements over 12 weeks in healthy young adults.

## Potential Murph endpoints

Verbal memory, Waist-to-height ratio, Adiposity, Glucose tolerance, Sweet taste liking

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Young healthy adults, not overweight/clinical or broad adult users.
- Small sample.
- Unblinded.
- Young healthy adults and beverage-only intervention.
- Safety note: No adverse-event signal extracted; metabolic null results preserved.

## Plain-language takeaway

Water replacement may help reduce strong-sweet liking; ASB and water should remain separate arms.
