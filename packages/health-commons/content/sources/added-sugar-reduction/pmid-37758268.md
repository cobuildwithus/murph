## Quick read

- **Source type:** Prospective cohort.
- **People studied or addressed:** US adults in three prospective cohorts free of major chronic disease at baseline.
- **Duration or horizon:** Four-year weight-change intervals over cohort follow-up periods.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `background`.

## What it contributes

Use as weight-change and carbohydrate-quality context; it is not a no-added-sugar diet intervention.

## Potential Murph endpoints

Body weight change, Added sugar change, Carbohydrate quality, Starch intake

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Health-professional cohorts and women/men cohorts may not represent general users.
- Broad carbohydrate-quality exposure, not only added sugar.
- Observational repeated-diet design cannot establish intervention effects.
- Replacement carbohydrates and total diet quality are central comparators.
- Self-reported diet and weight measurement issues may apply.
- Safety note: No adverse-event data extracted; use safety interpretation only where the source itself addresses population or risk boundaries.

## Plain-language takeaway

Use as weight-change and carbohydrate-quality context; it is not a no-added-sugar diet intervention. Preserve confounding, measurement-error, and comparator uncertainty.
