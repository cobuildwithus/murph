This source is included for **sweetener-substitution**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Double-blind randomized controlled trial.
- **People studied or addressed:** Primarily normal-weight children aged roughly 5-12 years.
- **Duration or horizon:** 18 months.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `adjacent_variant`.

## What it contributes

Use as adjacent SSB-substitution evidence, not a blanket recommendation for artificial sweeteners in children.

## Potential Murph endpoints

BMI z score, Body weight, Skinfold thickness, Waist circumference, Fat mass, Adverse events

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Children in a school-linked trial, not adult self-experimenters.
- Pediatric population.
- Daily beverage substitution only.
- Some children stopped consuming assigned beverages.
- Safety note: Accessible extraction reports adverse events as minor; no serious adverse-event claim is made here.

## Plain-language takeaway

For families, water/unsweetened replacements should remain distinguishable from artificially sweetened replacements.
