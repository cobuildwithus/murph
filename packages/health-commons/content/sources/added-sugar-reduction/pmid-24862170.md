This source is included for **sweetener-substitution**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Randomized clinical trial.
- **People studied or addressed:** Adults with overweight/obesity who were regular NNS beverage consumers.
- **Duration or horizon:** 12 weeks.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `adjacent_variant`.

## What it contributes

Use as context for current diet-beverage users, not as a required no-added-sugar protocol component.

## Potential Murph endpoints

Body weight, 5% weight-loss achievement, Hunger, Waist circumference, Glucose, Lipids, Blood pressure

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Regular NNS beverage consumers in a structured weight-loss program.
- Not NNS-naive participants.
- Short 12-week duration.
- Behavioral weight-loss treatment co-intervention.
- Safety note: No adverse-event signal extracted; cardiometabolic endpoints were mixed/null in accessible extraction.

## Plain-language takeaway

A current NNS-drinker subgroup may find diet beverages easier during weight loss, but endpoints should be tracked separately.
