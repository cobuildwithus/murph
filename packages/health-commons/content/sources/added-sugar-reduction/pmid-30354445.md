## What it contributes

Use water-first framing and retain caution for children and long-term use.

## Potential Murph endpoints

Cardiometabolic health, Children guidance, Substitution boundaries, Evidence uncertainty

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Guidance document across populations, not a Murph protocol trial.
- No long-term RCTs with clinical outcomes were emphasized in accessible advisory materials.
- Observational evidence cannot establish causality.
- Pediatric caution should be preserved.
- Safety note: Advisory cautions against prolonged LCS beverage consumption by children except in specific clinical contexts such as pediatric diabetes.

## Plain-language takeaway

For adults who rely on sweet drinks, low-calorie sweetened beverages can be an optional bridge, not the default definition of no-added-sugar.
