This source is included for **mechanism-and-dose**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Six-week high-fructose-corn-syrup food restriction study (ledger-labeled RCT).
- **People studied or addressed:** Obese school children aged 6–11 years with high fructose consumption.
- **Duration or horizon:** 6 weeks.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `same_mechanism`.

## What it contributes

Use as an example of high-fructose-food restriction plausibility with explicit confounding caveats.

## Potential Murph endpoints

reported fructose intake, total energy intake, carbohydrate intake, triglycerides, liver ultrasound/steatosis grade

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Obese children; not general adults or a clean no-added-sugar intervention.
- Energy intake reportedly fell along with fructose intake.
- Carbohydrate intake also decreased.
- Comparator/randomization details are not sufficient here to isolate fructose effects.
- Safety note: No adverse-event findings were extracted.

## Plain-language takeaway

Track total energy and carbohydrate changes when reducing added sugar, because food substitutions may drive outcomes.
