Ischemic stroke context, Heart failure context, Atrial fibrillation context, Abdominal aortic aneurysm context, Sweetened beverage intake

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Swedish adults aged 45-83 with local dietary patterns.
- Endpoint associations are mixed and source-dependent.
- Lowest-intake risk signals may reflect confounding or selection.
- Sweet treats and sweetened beverages should not be combined.
- Dietary assessment and residual confounding remain likely.
- Safety note: No adverse-event data extracted; use safety interpretation only where the source itself addresses population or risk boundaries.

## Plain-language takeaway

Use for recent source-specific CVD context and to avoid overgeneralizing from total added-sugar grams alone. Preserve confounding, measurement-error, and comparator uncertainty.
