## Quick read

- **Source type:** WHO evidence-informed global guideline.
- **People studied or addressed:** Adults and children globally.
- **Duration or horizon:** Life-course public-health guidance.
- **Protocol role:** supports-protocol; directness: `same_mechanism`.

## What it contributes

Use WHO for free-sugars thresholds and evidence-grade language, not as a direct no-added-sugar trial.

## Potential Murph endpoints

Free sugars percent total energy, Unhealthy weight gain, Dental caries

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Policy guidance for adults and children, not individual trial evidence.
- Guideline-level evidence.
- Free sugars are not the same as added sugars.
- The <5% target is conditional and evidence-grade boundaries must be retained.
- Safety note: No adverse-event data extracted.

## Plain-language takeaway

A no-added-sugar diet may be stricter in some ways and looser in others than WHO free-sugars guidance; define the sugar category clearly.
