## Quick read

- **Source type:** Systematic review and meta-analysis of prospective cohorts and randomized controlled trials.
- **People studied or addressed:** Children and adults in included SSB cohort studies and RCTs.
- **Duration or horizon:** Search through September 8, 2022; follow-up windows varied by study.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `adjacent_variant`.

## What it contributes

Use as SSB-specific context only unless a section explicitly labels it as beverage evidence.

## Potential Murph endpoints

SSB servings, BMI, body weight, beverage substitution adherence

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Mixed child and adult evidence, beverage-specific, heterogeneous comparators.
- SSB-specific exposure.
- RCTs often used noncaloric controls or targeted beverage reduction, not full no-added-sugar diets.
- Cohort evidence is observational.
- Study populations and follow-up windows varied.
- Safety note: ['No no-added-sugar safety inference should be drawn from this SSB meta-analysis.']

## Plain-language takeaway

Beverage substitution may be a measurable sub-behavior, but whole-protocol claims require direct evidence.
