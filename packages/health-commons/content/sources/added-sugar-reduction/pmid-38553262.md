## Quick read

- **Source type:** Randomized crossover trial.
- **People studied or addressed:** Healthy adults with overweight/obesity in England and France.
- **Duration or horizon:** Acute test and three 2-week intervention periods with washouts.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `same_mechanism`.

## What it contributes

Use as acute/two-week mechanism context only; avoid treating sweetener-reformulated foods as necessary for no-added-sugar adherence.

## Potential Murph endpoints

Composite appetite score, Insulin, Glucose, Ghrelin, GLP-1, Pancreatic polypeptide

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Adults with overweight/obesity in controlled test settings.
- Short two-week exposure.
- Single food format.
- No long-term weight or microbiome outcomes.
- Safety note: No clinically meaningful acute versus two-week differences extracted; no adverse-event table extracted.

## Plain-language takeaway

Replacing sucrose in a specific food can change postprandial endocrine response without proving long-term benefit.
