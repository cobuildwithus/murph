## Quick read

- **Source type:** Guideline.
- **People studied or addressed:** Pregnant people with preexisting diabetes or gestational diabetes.
- **Duration or horizon:** 2026 Standards of Care guideline.
- **Protocol role:** safety-only; directness: `safety_boundary`.

## What it contributes

Pregnant users with diabetes should not pursue strict sugar/carbohydrate rules without their diabetes pregnancy care team.

## Potential Murph endpoints

safety, burden

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Pregnant people with preexisting diabetes or gestational diabetes.
- Guideline, not intervention trial evidence.
- Does not evaluate no-added-sugar specifically.
- Nutrition recommendations are individualized by diabetes type, pregnancy stage, and medication.
- Safety note: Hypoglycemia, fetal growth, ketosis, and medication safety require clinical monitoring.

## Plain-language takeaway

Add pregnancy-diabetes pathway with carbohydrate adequacy and medication-safety language.
