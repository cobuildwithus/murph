## Quick read

- **Source type:** Protocol for double-blind randomized parallel-group feeding study.
- **People studied or addressed:** Clinically stable adults with cystic fibrosis without CF-related diabetes and high baseline added-sugar intake.
- **Duration or horizon:** 8 weeks.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `clinical_supervised`.

## What it contributes

This is a disease-specific protocol and should not be used as efficacy evidence.

## Potential Murph endpoints

beta-cell responsiveness, insulin sensitivity, visceral adipose tissue, ectopic fat, cysteine redox potential, diet tolerance, body weight, compliance

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Clinically stable adults with cystic fibrosis without CF-related diabetes; not general wellness users.
- Protocol only.
- Adults with cystic fibrosis.
- All-food-provided and isocaloric design differs from self-directed no-added-sugar diet.
- No results available in this source.
- Safety note: Diet tolerance, body weight, and compliance are monitored weekly by an unblinded dietitian in the protocol.

## Plain-language takeaway

Use for clinical-supervised safety and endpoint context only.
