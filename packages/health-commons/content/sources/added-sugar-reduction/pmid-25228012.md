- **Protocol role:** supports-protocol; directness: `direct_protocol`.

Use as context for dental conservatism, not as the sole basis for a protocol dose target.

## Potential Murph endpoints

Dental caries, Free sugars as percent energy, Adult tooth-surface caries burden

- Population boundary: Population dental evidence, not self-experiment evidence.
- Narrative reappraisal, not a new randomized trial.
- Uses dental outcomes only.
- Very low target proposals require clear evidence-grade caveats.
- Safety note: No adverse-event data extracted.

## Plain-language takeaway

Dental risk may not disappear at 10% energy; a stricter target can be discussed with caveats.
