This source is included for **mechanism-and-dose**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Three-period acute fructose dose-response crossover physiology study.
- **People studied or addressed:** Healthy adult men.
- **Duration or horizon:** Acute feeding assessments; 9.5-hour fructose administration.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `same_mechanism`.

## What it contributes

Use to explain fructose dose-response plausibility; avoid translating acute dose-response into outcome claims.

## Potential Murph endpoints

fructose dose/exposure amount, hepatic de novo lipogenesis, time since fructose exposure, individual susceptibility markers

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Healthy male physiology study; not mixed-sex, free-living, or no-added-sugar intervention.
- Seventeen healthy male subjects were enrolled and ten completed all three periods.
- Acute feeding loads do not represent usual diet changes.
- DNL is a surrogate endpoint.
- Safety note: No adverse-event findings were extracted.

## Plain-language takeaway

Dose and exposure pattern matter; acute physiology cannot replace real-world symptom, intake, or clinical tracking.
