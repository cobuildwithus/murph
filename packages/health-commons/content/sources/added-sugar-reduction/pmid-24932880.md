## What it contributes

Use as diabetes outcome context only; flag heterogeneity and BMI/confounding limitations.

## Potential Murph endpoints

incident type 2 diabetes

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Prospective cohort beverage exposure rather than randomized no-added-sugar protocol evidence.
- Prospective observational studies only.
- High heterogeneity was reported.
- BMI adjustment and reverse causation can alter ASB interpretation.
- Soft-drink exposure does not equal whole-diet added-sugar avoidance.
- Safety note: ['ASB association is a cautionary observational signal, not proof of harm or safety.']

## Plain-language takeaway

If beverage substitutions are tracked, record whether intake is SSB, ASB, fruit juice, or water rather than collapsing them into sugar avoidance.
