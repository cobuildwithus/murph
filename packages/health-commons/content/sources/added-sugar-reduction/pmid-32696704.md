## Quick read

- **Source type:** Randomized controlled beverage-substitution trial.
- **People studied or addressed:** Adults who habitually consumed sugar-sweetened beverages.
- **Duration or horizon:** 12 months.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `same_mechanism`.

## What it contributes

Use as same-mechanism beverage RCT context. Do not generalize the primary null result to all no-added-sugar outcomes.

## Potential Murph endpoints

triglyceride-to-HDL cholesterol ratio, body weight, body composition, sweet taste preference

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Habitual SSB-consuming adults in a beverage intervention, not general no-added-sugar diet users.
- Beverage-only intervention, not whole-diet added-sugar avoidance.
- Population was habitual SSB consumers.
- Primary endpoint null result does not exclude secondary endpoint differences.
- Full detailed effect estimates were not extracted in this batch draft.
- Safety note: ['No specific adverse events were extracted.', 'ASB and unsweetened replacements should remain distinct comparators.']

## Plain-language takeaway

A self-experiment could track both cardiometabolic biomarkers and sweet-taste preference if beverage replacement is central.
