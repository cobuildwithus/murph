This source is included for **mechanism-and-dose**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Five-month randomized reduced-simple-sugar taste-perception study.
- **People studied or addressed:** Healthy adults aged 21–54 years.
- **Duration or horizon:** 5 months total; 3-month low-sugar assignment after baseline.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `same_mechanism`.

## What it contributes

Frame reduced sugar exposure as potentially changing perception, not as guaranteed to reduce sweet-food preference.

## Potential Murph endpoints

sweetness intensity rating, pleasantness rating, desire for sweet foods, adherence to low-sugar instruction

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Healthy adults in a controlled sensory study, not a whole-diet no-added-sugar clinical trial.
- Low-sugar group n=16, with 13 completing key manipulations; control group n=17, with 16 completing.
- Pleasantness did not change.
- Does not establish clinical or metabolic benefit.
- Safety note: No adverse-event findings were extracted.

## Plain-language takeaway

Track subjective sweetness intensity and pleasantness separately; they may move differently.
