This source is included for **mechanism-and-dose**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Eight-week randomized low-free-sugar diet DNL substudy in adolescent boys with NAFLD.
- **People studied or addressed:** Adolescent boys with NAFLD; DNL substudy from an 8-week randomized trial.
- **Duration or horizon:** 8 weeks.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `same_mechanism`.

## What it contributes

Use for pediatric NAFLD mechanistic plausibility only; not for general adult no-added-sugar outcome claims.

## Potential Murph endpoints

free-sugar intake, hepatic DNL, liver fat, fasting insulin, ALT, body weight as confounder

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Adolescent boys with NAFLD; not general adults or self-directed no-added-sugar experiments.
- DNL substudy included 29 participants, while 40 completed the parent treatment study.
- Pediatric male NAFLD population.
- DNL and hepatic fat are specialized surrogate/clinical endpoints.
- Safety note: No adverse-event findings were extracted.

## Plain-language takeaway

Sugar reduction may affect liver-related pathways in selected clinical populations, but clinical interpretation should be supervised.
