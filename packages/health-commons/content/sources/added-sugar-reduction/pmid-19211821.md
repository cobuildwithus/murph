This source is included for **observational-context**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Prospective cohort analysis in the Nurses’ Health Study.
- **People studied or addressed:** Women in the Nurses’ Health Study without CHD, stroke, or diabetes at baseline.
- **Duration or horizon:** 1980 to 2004 follow-up; repeated food-frequency questionnaires.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `adjacent_variant`.

## What it contributes

Use as adjacent cardiovascular context only; do not use the HR as an intervention effect.

## Potential Murph endpoints

sugar-sweetened beverage servings, blood pressure, lipids, long-term cardiovascular outcomes

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Women-only health-professional cohort and beverage exposure; not a direct diet trial.
- Observational design.
- Self-reported diet.
- Residual confounding remains possible.
- Mediator adjustment attenuated the association.
- Safety note: ['No intervention-related adverse events apply.']

## Plain-language takeaway

For protocol tracking, CHD endpoints are long-term population outcomes rather than short self-experiment outcomes.
