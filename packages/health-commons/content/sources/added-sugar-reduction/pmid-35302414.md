## Quick read

- **Source type:** Ten-year update systematic review of sugars intake and dental caries.
- **People studied or addressed:** Children and adults across dental caries studies.
- **Duration or horizon:** Varied observational follow-up.
- **Protocol role:** supports-protocol; directness: `direct_protocol`.

## What it contributes

Use as updated dental threshold evidence, with mixed results and evidence grades retained.

## Potential Murph endpoints

Dental caries, Free sugars percent energy, Positive/null/negative study associations

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Not an intervention trial of a no-added-sugar diet.
- Heterogeneous caries studies.
- Some studies showed null or negative associations.
- Endpoint is dental caries, not metabolic health.
- Safety note: No adverse-event data extracted.

## Plain-language takeaway

Dental-caries risk can be a major protocol rationale, but findings include null and negative study associations that should stay visible.
