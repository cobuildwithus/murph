This source is included for **direct-free-sugar-intervention**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** 12-week randomized parallel-group dietary recommendation trial.
- **People studied or addressed:** UK adults consuming >5% total energy intake from free sugars.
- **Duration or horizon:** 12 weeks.
- **Protocol role:** supports-protocol; directness: `direct_protocol`.

## What it contributes

Simple recommendations can reduce free-sugar intake over 12 weeks; do not overstate body-weight or diet-quality changes.

## Potential Murph endpoints

free sugar percent energy, adherence, energy intake, diet composition, sugar-sweetened foods, low-calorie-sweetened foods, body weight

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Adults with free-sugar intakes above guideline threshold; not necessarily high-sugar clinical population.
- Recommendation intervention rather than strict no-added-sugar elimination.
- UK adult volunteers consuming >5% energy from free sugars.
- No clear differences across recommendation styles.
- Body-weight association with sugar reduction was weak.
- Safety note: No adverse-event signal was extracted from the abstract; dietary profile changes were limited.

## Plain-language takeaway

Use as a high-priority adult direct source for dose/adherence expectations and variant comparisons.
