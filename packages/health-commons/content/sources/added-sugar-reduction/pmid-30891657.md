This source is included for **direct-free-sugar-intervention**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** 2 x 2 factorial randomized trial of inhibitory-control training and gamification.
- **People studied or addressed:** Adults in a weight-loss/dietary behavior intervention.
- **Duration or horizon:** 42 daily trainings plus two weekly boosters; 8-week weight outcome.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `adjacent_variant`.

## What it contributes

Daily cognitive-training tools may help some high-sweet-preference participants, but this is not diet-only efficacy evidence.

## Potential Murph endpoints

training completion, sweet-food preference subgroup, weight change, diet adherence burden

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Adults seeking weight/diet behavior change; not a simple no-added-sugar self-experiment.
- No diet-only comparator.
- Training and gamification were the randomized components.
- Weight-loss context may not match maintenance-oriented wellness users.
- Safety note: No diet-specific adverse-event signal was extracted; burden includes daily at-home training.

## Plain-language takeaway

Use as adherence context and cue-control inspiration; do not use it to claim no-added-sugar diet efficacy.
