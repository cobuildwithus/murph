This source is included for **mechanism-and-dose**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Nine-day isocaloric fructose-restriction d-lactate mechanism analysis.
- **People studied or addressed:** Children with obesity and metabolic syndrome from a larger fructose-restriction cohort.
- **Duration or horizon:** 9 days.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `same_mechanism`.

## What it contributes

Use to frame d-lactate/DNL as experimental mechanisms only; not as outcomes for most users.

## Potential Murph endpoints

d-lactate where clinically/research measured, hepatic DNL-AUC, liver fat, insulin sensitivity, body weight change

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Children with obesity/metabolic syndrome; specialized lab endpoints.
- Twenty of 52 recruited participants had paired samples available for this analysis.
- d-Lactate is a whole-body methylglyoxal marker and may reflect gut/microbiota sources.
- Average weight declined despite attempted weight maintenance.
- Safety note: No adverse-event findings were extracted.

## Plain-language takeaway

Mechanistic biomarkers can be hypothesis-generating but are usually not practical self-experiment endpoints.
