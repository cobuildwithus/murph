## Quick read

- **Source type:** Prospective cohort analysis in the European Prospective Investigation into Cancer and Nutrition.
- **People studied or addressed:** Adults in 10 European countries participating in EPIC.
- **Duration or horizon:** Mean follow-up 16.4 years.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `adjacent_variant`.

## What it contributes

Use only as soft-drink mortality context and flag residual confounding/reverse causality.

## Potential Murph endpoints

all-cause mortality, cause-specific mortality

## Important limits

- Population boundary: European cohort soft-drink consumption, not a diet protocol trial.
- Prospective observational design.
- Residual confounding and reverse causality are possible.
- Soft-drink exposure is not a no-added-sugar intervention.
- Exact category-specific effect estimates were not extracted for this draft.
- Safety note: ['ASB mortality associations are safety/context signals only, not causal adverse-event evidence.']

## Plain-language takeaway

When tracking outcomes, separate total soft drinks, SSBs, and ASBs.
