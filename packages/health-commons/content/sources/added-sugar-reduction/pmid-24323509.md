This source is included for **guidelines-and-synthesis**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Systematic review of sugars restriction and dental caries.
- **People studied or addressed:** Children and adults across dental caries studies.
- **Duration or horizon:** Varied; life-course relevance.
- **Protocol role:** supports-protocol; directness: `direct_protocol`.

## What it contributes

Use for dental threshold context, not for metabolic efficacy or no-added-sugar causality.

## Potential Murph endpoints

Dental caries, Free sugars as percent energy, Life-course caries risk

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Dental caries studies across heterogeneous populations, not a Murph protocol trial.
- Evidence below 5% energy was judged very low quality in the review.
- Not a cardiometabolic outcome source.
- Exposure is free sugars/dietary sugars, not strictly added sugars.
- Safety note: No adverse-event data extracted.

## Plain-language takeaway

Dental benefits are an important reason to track free sugars, with evidence-grade boundaries for 10% and 5% thresholds.
