## Quick read

- **Source type:** Prospective cohort analysis in Nurses’ Health Study and Health Professionals Follow-up Study.
- **People studied or addressed:** US adults in two health-professional cohorts, free of major chronic disease at baseline in extracted description.
- **Duration or horizon:** NHS 1980–2014 and HPFS 1986–2014 follow-up windows.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `adjacent_variant`.

## What it contributes

Use as long-term beverage mortality context only; avoid causal or substitution claims.

## Potential Murph endpoints

all-cause mortality, cardiovascular mortality, cancer mortality

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Health-professional cohort exposure rather than no-added-sugar diet intervention.
- Prospective observational design.
- Health-professional cohorts may not generalize to all users.
- Beverage intake was self-reported.
- ASB estimates are vulnerable to reverse causation and residual confounding.
- Safety note: ['ASB mortality findings are observational safety/context signals only.', 'No intervention adverse events apply.']

## Plain-language takeaway

Track beverage categories and avoid treating diet beverages as a guaranteed risk-free replacement.
