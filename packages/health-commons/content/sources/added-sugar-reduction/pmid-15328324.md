This source is included for **observational-context**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Prospective cohort analysis in the Nurses’ Health Study II.
- **People studied or addressed:** Young and middle-aged women in the Nurses’ Health Study II, free of diabetes and major chronic disease at baseline.
- **Duration or horizon:** 1991 to 1999 follow-up; 716,300 person-years for diabetes incidence.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `adjacent_variant`.

## What it contributes

Use as adjacent SSB risk context only; do not convert into a direct diet-protocol effect estimate.

## Potential Murph endpoints

sugar-sweetened beverage servings, weight change, fasting glucose or HbA1c, incident diabetes history

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Female nurses; not a mixed adult trial population and not a no-added-sugar diet intervention.
- Observational design.
- Population limited to women in a health-professional cohort.
- Exposure focused on beverages rather than all added sugars.
- BMI adjustment attenuated the diabetes association.
- Safety note: ['No intervention-related adverse events apply.']

## Plain-language takeaway

Beverage intake can be tracked as a high-signal sugar source, while causal claims need trials or direct protocol evidence.
