This source is included for **mechanism-and-dose**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** One-week controlled feeding reinforcement-value mechanism study.
- **People studied or addressed:** Adults with obesity or normal weight habitually consuming more than 10% of calories from added sugars.
- **Duration or horizon:** 1 week.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `same_mechanism`.

## What it contributes

Warn that cravings/reward value may rise early in restriction; frame it as a barrier to plan for, not evidence against the protocol.

## Potential Murph endpoints

craving rating, reinforcing value/urge for high-sugar foods, lapse frequency, adherence difficulty, withdrawal-like symptoms

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Adults selected for high added-sugar intake; short controlled feeding rather than self-directed protocol.
- One-week duration.
- Reinforcing-value task is a behavioral mechanism endpoint.
- The study calls for future work separating added sugar from other sugars and longer-term adaptation.
- Safety note: No adverse-event findings were extracted.

## Plain-language takeaway

Measure cravings, reward value, lapses, and perceived effort during the first week.
