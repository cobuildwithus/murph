## What it contributes

Use only for low-calorie-sweetener substitution context; do not generalize to added-sugar avoidance or long-term safety.

## Potential Murph endpoints

body weight, BMI, fat mass, waist circumference

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Mixed trial/cohort populations and sweetener substitution rather than whole-diet no-added-sugar intervention.
- Combines RCT and observational designs.
- Comparators differed substantially across studies.
- Low-calorie sweetener replacement is not the same as avoiding added sugar.
- Long-term safety cannot be concluded from modest weight effects.
- Safety note: ['No adverse-event ledger was extracted; cohort BMI signal should remain a context finding.']

## Plain-language takeaway

Record whether a person replaces sugar with low-calorie sweeteners, water, or no replacement.
