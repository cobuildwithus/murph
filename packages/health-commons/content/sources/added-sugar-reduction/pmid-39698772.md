This source is included for **mechanism-and-dose**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Six-day randomized whole-diet sweet-taste exposure trial.
- **People studied or addressed:** Adults randomized to increase, decrease, or not change sweet-tasting food and beverage consumption.
- **Duration or horizon:** 6 days.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `same_mechanism`.

## What it contributes

Do not promise rapid reductions in desire or intake from sweet-taste reduction; measure it.

## Potential Murph endpoints

sweetness intensity rating, pleasantness rating, desire-to-eat rating, sweet-food intake at ad libitum meal, adherence difficulty

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Sweet-taste exposure trial, not added-sugar-specific elimination.
- Short six-day duration.
- Sweet-taste exposure includes sugars and non-sugar sweet tastes depending on diet choices.
- No pleasantness/desire/intake effect detected.
- Safety note: No adverse-event findings were extracted.

## Plain-language takeaway

Track sweetness perception, desire, intake, and perceived difficulty separately during the first week.
