- **Source type:** Randomized brief motivational intervention within a workplace SSB context.

## What it contributes

Craving moderated response to workplace SSB reduction; a brief motivational component may help high-craving consumers. Boundary: Adjacent SSB-only evidence: use to inform beverage substitution, implementation, measurement, and population-specific outcome expectations, not as direct proof that a full no-added-sugar diet works.
