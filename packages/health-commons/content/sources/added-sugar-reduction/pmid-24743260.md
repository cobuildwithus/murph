## What it contributes

Use as fruit-juice boundary evidence only. Do not cite as proof that fruit juice is equivalent to no-added-sugar avoidance.

## Potential Murph endpoints

fasting glucose, fasting insulin, HbA1c, HOMA-IR

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Adults in small fruit-juice trials, not a no-added-sugar diet intervention population.
- Small trial evidence base.
- Comparators and fruit-juice types varied.
- Fruit juice is not added-sugar elimination.
- Neutral glycemic results do not establish long-term cardiometabolic safety or substitution benefit.
- Safety note: ['No adverse events relevant to a no-added-sugar diet were extracted.', 'Fruit juice should remain separate from unsweetened whole-fruit and added-sugar categories.']

## Plain-language takeaway

Track fruit juice separately from added sugar, whole fruit, and sweetened beverages.
