This source is included for **observational-context**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Belgian prospective cohort with two assessments.
- **People studied or addressed:** Middle-aged Belgian adults who returned for follow-up from the original cohort.
- **Duration or horizon:** 10 years between 2002 and 2012 assessments.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `adjacent_variant`.

## What it contributes

Use as null/methodological context; do not suppress or reinterpret as supportive evidence.

## Potential Murph endpoints

SSB energy percent, body weight, total energy intake, physical activity

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Middle-aged Belgian cohort with 36% return from original participants; not a direct no-added-sugar intervention.
- Only 36% of the original participants returned for follow-up.
- Observational design.
- Changing energy intake and physical activity affect interpretation.
- SSB exposure was not a comprehensive added-sugar measure.
- Safety note: ['No intervention adverse events apply.']

## Plain-language takeaway

When tracking intake, analyze energy intake and activity changes alongside SSB changes.
