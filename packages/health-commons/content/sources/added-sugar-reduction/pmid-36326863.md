This source is included for **mechanism-and-dose**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Randomized open-label 24-hour crossover sugar/carbohydrate restriction study.
- **People studied or addressed:** Healthy men (n=10) and women (n=15).
- **Duration or horizon:** 24 hours per diet condition.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `same_mechanism`.

## What it contributes

Do not claim that acute sugar restriction automatically reduces energy intake or changes activity; treat appetite/activity as measured outcomes.

## Potential Murph endpoints

24-hour energy intake, physical activity energy expenditure, postprandial glucose/insulin, LDL cholesterol, leptin, substrate oxidation

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Healthy adults in a single-day crossover study; not sustained no-added-sugar intervention.
- Twenty-four-hour exposure only.
- Low-sugar condition differed from no-added-sugar protocols.
- Open-label crossover design.
- Safety note: Low-carbohydrate condition produced acute LDL-cholesterol increase; this is not specific to sugar restriction.

## Plain-language takeaway

Track energy intake, activity, hunger, and carbohydrate intake separately in self-experiments.
