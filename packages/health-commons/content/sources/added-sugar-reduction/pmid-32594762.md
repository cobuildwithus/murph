This source is included for **observational-context**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** CARDIA prospective cohort with repeated diet assessment and CT adipose-tissue outcomes.
- **People studied or addressed:** CARDIA participants with diet assessments at baseline, year 7, and year 20 and CT adipose-tissue measurements after 25 years.
- **Duration or horizon:** 25 years of follow-up for CT adipose-tissue outcomes.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `adjacent_variant`.

## What it contributes

Use as added-sugar adiposity context only; not as proof that the protocol reduces ectopic fat.

## Potential Murph endpoints

added sugar grams/day, SSB servings, waist circumference, body fat percentage

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Young adult cohort followed for imaging; observational exposure, not diet intervention.
- Observational design.
- Diet was self-reported at repeated visits.
- Ectopic fat imaging outcomes are not standard Murph self-experiment endpoints.
- Residual confounding possible.
- Safety note: ['No intervention adverse events apply.']

## Plain-language takeaway

Waist circumference or body-composition proxies may be reasonable exploratory endpoints.
