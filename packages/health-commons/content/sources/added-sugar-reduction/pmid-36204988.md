## Quick read

- **Source type:** Prospective cohort.
- **People studied or addressed:** UK Biobank participants with at least one Oxford WebQ dietary assessment.
- **Duration or horizon:** Mean 12.3 years follow-up.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `background`.

## What it contributes

Use to distinguish free sugars, intrinsic sugars, beverages, and solid sources.

## Potential Murph endpoints

Free sugar intake, Intrinsic sugar intake, All-cause mortality context, Beverage source categories

## Important limits

- Population boundary: UK Biobank volunteer population; free-sugar classification differs from added-sugar-only protocol framing.
- Observational design cannot establish causality.
- Free sugar and added sugar are related but not identical.
- Source-specific patterns should not be collapsed.
- Dietary assessment and residual confounding remain important.
- Safety note: No adverse-event data extracted; use safety interpretation only where the source itself addresses population or risk boundaries.

## Plain-language takeaway

Use to distinguish free sugars, intrinsic sugars, beverages, and solid sources. Preserve confounding, measurement-error, and comparator uncertainty.
