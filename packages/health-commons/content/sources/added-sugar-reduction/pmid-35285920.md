This source is included for **sweetener-substitution**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Systematic review and network meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.
- **People studied or addressed:** Adults with overweight or obesity, with or at risk for diabetes, and related intervention populations.
- **Duration or horizon:** Randomized trials of at least 2 weeks; exact individual durations varied.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `adjacent_variant`.

## What it contributes

Use as beverage-replacement context only. It can inform a user who already chooses to replace SSBs, but it does not prove no-added-sugar diet efficacy.

## Potential Murph endpoints

body weight, BMI, body fat, intrahepatocellular lipid, HbA1c, blood pressure

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Adults with overweight/obesity or diabetes risk in beverage RCTs, not general no-added-sugar self-experimenters.
- Replacement-specific RCTs, not whole-diet no-added-sugar interventions.
- Participants were often adults with overweight/obesity or diabetes risk.
- Trial durations were moderate/short relative to chronic disease outcomes.
- LNCSB results should not be generalized to all non-sugar sweeteners or high intake.
- Safety note: ['The extracted review reported small improvements without evidence of harm in the studied trials, but this does not establish long-term safety.']

## Plain-language takeaway

If LNCSBs are used, track them as a replacement category and monitor weight, glycemic, and blood-pressure markers separately.
