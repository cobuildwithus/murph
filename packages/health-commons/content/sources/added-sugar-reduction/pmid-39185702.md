## Quick read

- **Source type:** Exploratory randomized periodontal-therapy adjunct trial.
- **People studied or addressed:** Patients with untreated periodontitis and daily free-sugar intake.
- **Duration or horizon:** 4-week sugar-avoidance intervention with 8-week follow-up.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `clinical_supervised`.

## What it contributes

Free-sugar avoidance may be an adjunct in periodontal therapy, but it is not broad no-added-sugar efficacy evidence.

## Potential Murph endpoints

bleeding on probing, plaque control record, body weight, visceral fat, free sugar intake, vitamin C from fruit

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Untreated periodontitis patients undergoing periodontal therapy; not general wellness users.
- Very small sample.
- Periodontal therapy cointervention.
- Clinical dental population.
- Both groups improved on primary periodontal endpoint.
- Exploratory design.
- Safety note: No major adverse-event signal was extracted from the article/registry summary.

## Plain-language takeaway

Use as dental clinical context and a things-to-watch endpoint source, not as a primary protocol claim.
