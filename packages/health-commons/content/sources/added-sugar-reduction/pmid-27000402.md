## Quick read

- **Source type:** Type 1 effectiveness-implementation hybrid randomized controlled trial.
- **People studied or addressed:** Rural southwest Virginia adults with high SSB intake; enrolled sample was predominantly female and White with substantial low-income and low-health-literacy representation..
- **Duration or horizon:** 6 months..
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `adjacent_variant`.

## What it contributes

A health-literacy SSB program reduced beverage calories and BMI over 6 months. Boundary: Adjacent SSB-only evidence: use to inform beverage substitution, implementation, measurement, and population-specific outcome expectations, not as direct proof that a full no-added-sugar diet works.

## Potential Murph endpoints

SSB kilocalories/day, BMI, retention, engagement, health-literacy moderation

## Plain-language takeaway

Strong adjacent implementation evidence for behavior-change scaffolds when SSBs are the main added-sugar source. Use only as adjacent SSB-specific evidence.
