## Quick read

- **Source type:** Guideline.

## What it contributes

Targets should not compromise nutrition adequacy, and absence of a UL is not proof that zero is required.

## Potential Murph endpoints

dental, cardiometabolic, weight/adiposity, hepatic

## Important limits

- Population boundary: European general population; adults and children.
- Scientific opinion, not intervention evidence.
- Could not establish a tolerable upper intake level or safe intake level.
- Endpoint evidence varies by dental, adiposity, metabolic, and other outcomes.
- Safety note: Dietary adequacy boundary is explicit.

## Plain-language takeaway

Use nuanced dose language rather than absolutist claims.
