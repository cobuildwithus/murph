## Quick read

- **Source type:** Systematic review and meta-analysis of prospective cohort studies.
- **People studied or addressed:** Adults in prospective cohort studies included in the meta-analysis.
- **Duration or horizon:** At least 3,197,528 person-years across included cohorts.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `adjacent_variant`.

## What it contributes

Use as SSB-hypertension context only and flag heterogeneity.

## Potential Murph endpoints

sugar-sweetened beverage servings, home blood pressure, hypertension diagnosis

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Population and exposure varied across cohorts; not direct added-sugar elimination.
- Prospective observational studies only.
- Substantial heterogeneity was reported (I²=62%).
- Residual confounding and broader dietary-pattern collinearity remain possible.
- Exposure was SSB intake rather than all added sugar.
- Safety note: ['No intervention adverse events apply.']

## Plain-language takeaway

Blood pressure may be tracked, but this source does not prove that removing added sugar lowers blood pressure in an individual.
