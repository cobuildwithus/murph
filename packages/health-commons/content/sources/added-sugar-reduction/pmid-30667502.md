This source is included for **clinical-free-sugar-intervention**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** 8-week randomized outpatient feeding trial.
- **People studied or addressed:** Adolescent boys aged 11-16 years with NAFLD.
- **Duration or horizon:** 8 weeks.
- **Protocol role:** supports-protocol; directness: `clinical_supervised`.

## What it contributes

A strict, provided low-free-sugar diet can improve pediatric NAFLD markers, but this does not establish a low-burden adult wellness effect.

## Potential Murph endpoints

MRI-PDFF hepatic steatosis, ALT, AST, GGT, total cholesterol, weight, insulin or HOMA-IR

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Adolescent boys with NAFLD, not adult wellness users.
- Small sample.
- Adolescent boys only.
- Largely Hispanic clinical NAFLD population.
- Lower energy intake in the low-free-sugar group may confound sugar-specific effects.
- No long-term morbidity or mortality outcomes.
- Safety note: No adverse events related to participation were reported in accessible extraction notes.

## Plain-language takeaway

Use as high-priority clinical-supervised evidence and explicitly caveat sex, age, ethnicity, disease state, and food-delivery intensity.
