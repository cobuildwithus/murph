## Quick read

- **Source type:** Systematic review and meta-analysis of prospective cohorts with population-attributable-fraction estimation.
- **People studied or addressed:** Prospective adult cohorts without diabetes at baseline.
- **Duration or horizon:** Prospective cohort follow-up durations varied; exact range not extracted for this run.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `adjacent_variant`.

## What it contributes

Use as beverage-type diabetes context only; do not equate ASB or fruit juice with no-added-sugar avoidance.

## Potential Murph endpoints

incident type 2 diabetes, population-attributable fraction

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Beverage exposures in cohorts rather than randomized no-added-sugar diet intervention.
- Prospective observational design.
- High heterogeneity was reported for beverage categories.
- Adiposity adjustment changed estimates.
- ASB and fruit-juice findings are vulnerable to reverse causation, residual confounding, and publication bias.
- Safety note: ['ASB and fruit juice are cautionary substitution boundaries, not adverse-event evidence.']

## Plain-language takeaway

Track SSB, ASB, and fruit juice as separate beverage exposures.
