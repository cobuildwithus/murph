This source is included for **direct-free-sugar-intervention**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** 4-week randomized reduced-sugar diet trial after 2-week baseline.
- **People studied or addressed:** Normal-weight to obese adults without diabetes; periodontitis trial context.
- **Duration or horizon:** 2-week baseline plus 4-week intervention.
- **Protocol role:** supports-protocol; directness: `direct_protocol`.

## What it contributes

A short reduced-sugar intervention may lower intake and body weight but should not promise CGM improvements in people without diabetes.

## Potential Murph endpoints

sugar grams, energy intake, body weight, body fat, visceral fat, mean 24-hour glucose, glycemic variability, HbA1c

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Adults without diabetes, many in a dental/periodontal treatment context; not a broad metabolic cohort.
- Very small sample.
- Participants came from a periodontitis-treatment context.
- Intervention combined added-sugar avoidance with white-flour avoidance.
- Moderate sugar reduction and increased fruit intake may limit interpretation.
- Safety note: No adverse-event signal was extracted; HbA1c and CGM endpoints showed no glycemic worsening in the reported window.

## Plain-language takeaway

Use as a direct mixed result and a guardrail for CGM expectations.
