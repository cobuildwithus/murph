This source is included for **safety-special-populations**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Mechanistic human study.
- **People studied or addressed:** Clinical cohorts plus small human intervention and mechanistic studies.
- **Duration or horizon:** Pilot intervention included sustained plasma erythritol elevation for more than two days in accessible summaries.
- **Protocol role:** safety-only; directness: `safety_boundary`.

## What it contributes

Prefer whole-food and unsweetened replacements before sugar-alcohol substitutes; flag uncertainty.

## Potential Murph endpoints

cardiovascular events, thrombosis, safety

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Clinical cohorts plus small human intervention and mechanistic studies.
- Observational associations can be confounded.
- Small pilot intervention.
- Mechanistic findings are adjacent to clinical outcomes.
- Safety note: CVD-risk users should avoid assuming sugar-free erythritol products are automatically safer.

## Plain-language takeaway

Treat erythritol as optional and safety-bounded, not a required sugar replacement.
