This source is included for **mechanism-and-dose**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Nine-day isocaloric fructose-restriction stable-isotope study.
- **People studied or addressed:** Latino and African-American children aged 9–18 years with obesity and high habitual sugar consumption.
- **Duration or horizon:** 9 days.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `same_mechanism`.

## What it contributes

Use to explain why fructose-containing added sugars may be a mechanistic target; avoid general adult or self-directed efficacy claims.

## Potential Murph endpoints

liver fat where clinically measured, visceral adipose tissue, de novo lipogenesis surrogate, fasting insulin/insulin kinetics, body weight change

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Children with obesity and habitual high sugar intake; not general adult protocol users.
- No long-term follow-up extracted for sustained clinical outcomes.
- Controlled feeding and pediatric high-risk population limit generalizability.
- Within-person acute design does not establish free-living adherence efficacy.
- Safety note: No adverse-event findings were extracted for protocol use.

## Plain-language takeaway

If medically relevant, liver fat and insulin-related biomarkers are downstream clinical outcomes, but they require clinician-supervised measurement and careful confounder tracking.
