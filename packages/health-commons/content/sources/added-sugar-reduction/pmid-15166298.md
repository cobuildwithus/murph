This source is included for **observational-context**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Prospective adolescent cohort analysis from the Growing Up Today Study.
- **People studied or addressed:** US adolescents aged 9–14 years in the Growing Up Today Study.
- **Duration or horizon:** Two 1-year follow-up periods from 1996 to 1998.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `adjacent_variant`.

## What it contributes

Use only as adjacent pediatric SSB context. Do not cite as direct no-added-sugar diet efficacy.

## Potential Murph endpoints

daily sugar-added beverage servings, body weight, BMI, total energy intake

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Adolescents, not adult self-experimenters; beverage exposure rather than full added-sugar elimination.
- Observational cohort design cannot establish causality.
- Beverage intake was self-reported.
- Effects were attenuated after total-energy adjustment.
- Pediatric population limits adult protocol generalizability.
- Safety note: ['No adverse-event extraction relevant to a no-added-sugar diet was available from the accessible abstract-level evidence.']

## Plain-language takeaway

Track beverage changes and total energy together if this source is used to motivate measurement.
