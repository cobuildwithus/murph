This source is included for **observational-context**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Prospective NutriNet-Santé cohort study with repeated 24-hour dietary records.
- **People studied or addressed:** French NutriNet-Santé adult cohort participants.
- **Duration or horizon:** Median 5.2 years follow-up; 518,208 person-years.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `adjacent_variant`.

## What it contributes

Use as UPF background only; do not convert into no-added-sugar efficacy.

## Potential Murph endpoints

UPF percentage, diet quality, blood pressure/lipids as intermediate context, long-term CVD context

## Important limits

- Population boundary: French observational cohort; UPF pattern rather than added-sugar elimination.
- Observational design.
- UPF exposure is not an added-sugar-only variable.
- Causality remains to be established.
- Residual confounding possible despite dietary-quality adjustment.
- Safety note: ['No intervention adverse events apply.']

## Plain-language takeaway

UPF intake can be tracked separately if the protocol wants a boundary variable.
