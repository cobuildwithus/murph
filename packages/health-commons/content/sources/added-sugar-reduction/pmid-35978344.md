## Quick read

- **Source type:** Clinical practice guideline.
- **People studied or addressed:** people with eating disorders, including people at higher weight.
- **Duration or horizon:** Clinical practice guideline; no trial duration.
- **Protocol role:** safety-only; directness: `safety_boundary`.

## What it contributes

Users with active or past eating disorders should not start rigid sugar rules without professional support.

## Potential Murph endpoints

restrictive-diet risk, screening, weight stigma, clinical safety

## Important limits

- Population boundary: People with eating disorders, including people at higher weight.
- Guideline, not primary intervention evidence.
- No effect size or sample size applies.
- Does not evaluate added-sugar restriction specifically.
- Safety note: Professional eating-disorder care should supersede self-directed diet challenges.

## Plain-language takeaway

Screen for restrictive/binge patterns without assuming body size indicates risk level.
