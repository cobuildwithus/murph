## Quick read

- **Source type:** Nine-day isocaloric fructose-restriction ceramide mechanism analysis.
- **People studied or addressed:** Children with obesity and cardiometabolic risk from a fructose-reduction cohort.
- **Duration or horizon:** 9 days.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `same_mechanism`.

## What it contributes

Use only as biomarker mechanism context; do not turn ceramide changes into clinical benefit claims for no-added-sugar diets.

## Potential Murph endpoints

ceramide species in research settings, insulin sensitivity index, hepatic DNL, weight change

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Children with obesity/cardiometabolic risk; not general protocol users.
- Analyses were completed on 43 participants from a prior cohort.
- Mean weight decreased by -0.9 ± 1.1 kg.
- Ceramides are surrogate lipidomic endpoints.
- Safety note: No adverse-event findings were extracted.

## Plain-language takeaway

Specialized lipidomics are research endpoints, not routine self-experiment measurements.
