This source is included for **sweetener-substitution**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Multi-centre randomized controlled trial.
- **People studied or addressed:** 341 adults and 38 children with overweight or obesity; adult microbiota subgroup reported separately.
- **Duration or horizon:** Adults: 2-month low-energy diet followed by 10-month healthy ad libitum diet; total 1 year.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `adjacent_variant`.

## What it contributes

Use as context-only substitution evidence; do not make artificial sweeteners a requirement of the No Added Sugar Diet.

## Potential Murph endpoints

Body weight, Gut microbiota composition, Cardiometabolic markers, Children subgroup outcomes

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Adults and children with overweight/obesity; not a general no-added-sugar self-experiment.
- S&SE replacement is not identical to removing added sugar without sweeteners.
- Adult results should not be generalized to children when child between-group differences were not clear.
- Dropout and dietary-reporting uncertainty should be preserved.
- Safety note: Accessible metadata reported no significant extracted cardiometabolic marker advantage; long-term safety beyond one year is not established by this source.

## Plain-language takeaway

Sweeteners and sweetness enhancers may be one optional replacement strategy, with microbiome and long-term safety boundaries tracked separately.
