This source is included for **sweetener-substitution**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Systematic review and meta-analysis.
- **People studied or addressed:** Human and animal studies; sustained intervention, short-term intake, and cohort evidence.
- **Duration or horizon:** Short-term meals to sustained interventions from weeks to months/years.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `adjacent_variant`.

## What it contributes

Use for context on replacement design only; never treat LES use as equivalent to avoiding added sugar.

## Potential Murph endpoints

Energy intake, Body weight, BMI, Evidence consistency

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Human and animal evidence mixed; not a single Murph-like intervention population.
- Animal studies may not reflect human use patterns.
- Prospective cohorts can be affected by reverse causation.
- Comparator choice drives interpretation.
- Safety note: No adverse-event extraction from this batch; not a long-term safety review for all NNS exposures.

## Plain-language takeaway

Ask what the sweetener replaced: sugar, water, nothing, or habitual diet.
