## Quick read

- **Source type:** Randomized crossover trial of no/low added sugar diet combined with fasting regimens for Long COVID.
- **People studied or addressed:** Adults with Long COVID symptoms enrolled in an online/pragmatic trial.
- **Duration or horizon:** 2-week run-in plus two 4-week randomized crossover periods.
- **Protocol role:** context-only; directness: `adjacent_variant`.

## What it contributes

Do not use as direct no-added-sugar efficacy. Use only to flag combination-protocol and fasting safety boundaries.

## Potential Murph endpoints

Long COVID symptom score, symptom count, fasting adherence, adverse events, electrolyte symptoms

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Long COVID population; combination fasting protocol; subjective symptom outcomes; online/pragmatic design.
- No isolated no-sugar-only arm.
- Disease-specific Long COVID population.
- Subjective symptom endpoints.
- Fasting exclusions limit generalizability.
- Adverse-event reporting should not be generalized beyond the screened sample.
- Safety note: ['Accessible summaries state no major adverse safety events recorded, but the protocol excluded higher-risk groups including low BMI, eating disorder history, pregnancy/breastfeeding, diabetes, heart condition, and liver disease; electrolyte guidance was used during fasts.']

## Plain-language takeaway

Avoid bundling fasting and no-added-sugar claims; fasting protocols require separate screening and safety handling.
