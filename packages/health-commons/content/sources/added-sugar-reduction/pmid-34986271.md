This source is included for **guidelines-and-synthesis**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Cochrane systematic review of intervention trials.
- **People studied or addressed:** General population without established cardiovascular disease.
- **Duration or horizon:** Trials current to July 2021; varied duration.
- **Protocol role:** supports-protocol; directness: `same_mechanism`.

## What it contributes

Use as high-priority uncertainty-preserving synthesis: low added sugar may shift BP/lipids slightly but does not prove CVD-event benefit.

## Potential Murph endpoints

Blood pressure, Blood lipids, Blood glucose, Cardiovascular events or mortality not measured

## Important limits

- Population boundary: Low-added-sugar interventions are not always no-added-sugar elimination.
- Evidence was uncertain and low quality.
- Clinical relevance of small changes was uncertain.
- No cardiovascular events or death outcomes were assessed.
- Blood sugar showed no effect in the Cochrane summary.
- Safety note: No adverse-event signal extracted.

## Plain-language takeaway

Track BP and lipids if relevant, but avoid promising cardiovascular disease prevention from a short self-experiment.
