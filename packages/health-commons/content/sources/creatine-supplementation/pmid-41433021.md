This source is included for **population_boundary_adjacent_claims**.

**Findings:** Across 61 trials and 1,457 participants, creatine plus RT increased fat-free mass (WMD 1.39 kg; 95% CI 1.07 to 1.70) and body mass (WMD 0.89 kg; 95% CI 0.76 to 1.01), with no significant effects on fat mass, BMI, or body-fat percentage. Experienced lifters had a larger FFM point estimate than novices, but the between-group difference was not significant.

**Why it matters:** Large recent RT synthesis with response-modifier analyses.

**Population:** Novice and experienced lifters in resistance-training studies.

**Intervention or exposure:** Creatine supplementation during resistance training with dose-response and training-status subgroup analyses.

**Comparator or control:** Placebo/control during resistance training.

**Duration or follow-up:** Varied across 61 trials.

**Endpoints:**
- fat-free mass
- body mass
- fat mass
- BMI
- body-fat percentage
- training-status response

**Effect estimate or direction:** Across 61 trials and 1,457 participants, creatine plus RT increased fat-free mass (WMD 1.39 kg; 95% CI 1.07 to 1.70) and body mass (WMD 0.89 kg; 95% CI 0.76 to 1.01), with no significant effects on fat mass, BMI, or body-fat percentage. Experienced lifters had a larger FFM point estimate than novices, but the between-group difference was not significant.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** Safety outcomes were not the focus of this batch extraction.

**Limitations:**
- Blends training statuses, doses, and programs.
- Training-status subgroup difference was not statistically significant.

**Population mismatch:** Direct RT evidence, but not creatine-only and subgroup signals should not be overinterpreted.
