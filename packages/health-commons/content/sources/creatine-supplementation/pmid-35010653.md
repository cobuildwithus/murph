This source is included for **timing_coingestion**.

**Findings:** [source_artifact:pmid-35010653] randomized 14 elite female handball players to morning versus evening creatine intake for 12 weeks. Both schedules were compatible with training changes, and the study did not show a clear absolute timing advantage across the extracted endpoints.

**Why it matters:** It broadens timing evidence beyond male bodybuilding and supports flexible time-of-day language.

**Potential experiment signals:** Clock time, adherence, body mass/water, and sport performance tests.

**Protocol takeaway:** Morning or evening creatine can both fit a protocol; this source does not justify a universal preferred time of day.

**Limitations / boundary notes:** N=14; elite female handball population; team-training context; body-composition and performance tests may not generalize to all users.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
