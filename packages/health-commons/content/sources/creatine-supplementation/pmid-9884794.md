This source is included for **safety_tolerability_adverse_events**.

**Findings:**
- The review concluded that creatine supplementation results in weight gain due to water retention, which may impede performance in mass-dependent activities such as running and swimming.
- It stated that short-term use at recommended doses had not been shown to cause significant adverse effects.
- The review also noted that early studies involved small subject numbers and lacked sample-size calculations.

**Why it matters:**
Early safety reviews shaped persistent concerns about weight gain, GI effects, cramps, and activity-specific tradeoffs.
