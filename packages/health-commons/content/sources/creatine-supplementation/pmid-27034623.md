**Findings:**
- **short-term tolerability of creatine nitrate** — The study reported creatine nitrate, including 3 g dosing, was well tolerated over the studied short-term window and showed some performance signals, but it is not monohydrate evidence. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-27034623`.

**Why it matters:** Nitrate products are marketed as alternative creatine forms and may carry nitrate-specific physiology.

**Potential experiment signals:** safety:adverse-events, performance:exercise-performance, formulation:creatine-nitrate.

**Protocol takeaway:** Creatine nitrate should be treated as a separate variant and safety boundary.
