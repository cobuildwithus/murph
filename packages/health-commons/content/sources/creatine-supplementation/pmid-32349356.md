This source is included for **population_boundary_adjacent_claims**.

**Findings:** Review/meta-analysis reports creatine increased lean tissue mass, type II fiber area, IGF-1, muscular strength/endurance, Wingate mean power, and some brain-function measures in vegetarian participants. Evidence was mixed on whether vegetarians improved more than omnivores, and included studies had moderate-to-high risk of bias.

**Why it matters:** Diet pattern is a plausible response modifier.
