This source is included for **population_boundary_adjacent_claims**.

**Findings:** RT improved strength/function/fiber measures in both groups; creatine produced greater total mass and FFM versus placebo, greater isometric knee-extension strength in men and women, greater dorsiflexion strength in men only, and increased intramuscular total creatine.

**Why it matters:** Direct older-adult RCT with body-composition, strength, and tissue creatine endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- total mass
- fat-free mass
- isometric knee extension strength
- isometric dorsiflexion strength
- muscle total creatine
- side effects

**Protocol takeaway:** Creatine during RT can improve FFM and some isometric strength endpoints in older adults.

**Murph takeaway:** Track functional strength and body composition, not just scale weight.

**Population:** Healthy men and women older than 65 years undertaking whole-body resistance exercise.

**Intervention or exposure:** Creatine supplementation during whole-body resistance training.

**Comparator or control:** Placebo during the same training program.

**Duration or follow-up:** 14 weeks; resistance exercise 3 days/week.

**Endpoints:**
- total mass
- fat-free mass
- isometric knee extension strength
- isometric dorsiflexion strength
- muscle total creatine
- side effects

**Effect estimate or direction:** RT improved strength/function/fiber measures in both groups; creatine produced greater total mass and FFM versus placebo, greater isometric knee-extension strength in men and women, greater dorsiflexion strength in men only, and increased intramuscular total creatine.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** Accessible abstract-level extraction reported no significant side effects.

**Limitations:**
- Small older-adult sample.
- Training co-intervention.

**Population mismatch:** Older adults >65 in supervised RT.
