This source is included for **safety_tolerability_adverse_events**.

**Findings:**
- The review searched MEDLINE, Embase, Cochrane, and PsycINFO through September 30, 2025 and included six articles from five RCTs.
- Included arms totaled creatine n=126 and placebo n=112; dosing ranged from 2 to 10 g/day for 4-8 weeks as adjunct treatment.
- Creatine was generally well tolerated, but two creatine-treated participants out of 17 experienced hypomania/mania in available abstract text.

**Why it matters:**
Recent psychiatric claims around creatine are emerging; this source keeps them quarantined from the core protocol.

**Protocol takeaway:**
Use as psychiatric clinical boundary and safety context; do not add mental-health claims to the default creatine monohydrate protocol.

**Population mismatch / boundary:**
Mental-disorder clinical populations; outside default healthy-adult protocol.

**Limitations:**
- Small psychiatric RCT evidence base; only MDD and bipolar depression were represented.
- Adjunct treatment context differs from default supplementation.
- Some trials had risk-of-bias concerns.
