This source is included for **strength_hypertrophy_synthesis**.

**Findings:**
- **outcome / Bench/chest press and squat strength:** Creatine improved bench/chest press and squat strength in this large meta-analysis. Directness: `direct_protocol`. Claim use: `supports-protocol`.
- **outcome / Vertical jump and Wingate peak power:** Creatine improved selected power outcomes. Directness: `direct_protocol`. Claim use: `supports-protocol`.
- **null_result / Handgrip and leg press:** Not all strength endpoints were significant in the 2025 synthesis. Directness: `direct_protocol`. Claim use: `supports-protocol`.
- **mixed_result / Age and sex subgroup effects:** Subgroup findings suggest a need for age/sex boundary language. Directness: `direct_protocol`. Claim use: `supports-protocol`.

**Why it matters:** This 2025 meta-analysis found creatine improved several strength and power endpoints, while handgrip and leg press were not significant and subgroup effects differed by age/sex.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- bench/chest press
- squat
- vertical jump
- Wingate peak power
- handgrip

**Protocol takeaway:** Modern evidence supports strength/power effects but should be endpoint-specific rather than blanket.

**Limitations and boundary notes:**
- Protocol heterogeneity
- Endpoint units and exercise tests varied
- High heterogeneity was reported for some power endpoints
- Task-specific effects
- Endpoint-specific evidence
- Leg-press null conflicts with some older lower-limb syntheses and needs synthesis-level reconciliation
- Subgroup power may vary
- Exact subgroup estimates not extracted for every endpoint

- None beyond the source-specific caveats above.
