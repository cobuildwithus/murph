This source is included for **timing_coingestion**.

**Findings:** [source_artifact:pmid-36465581] randomized collegiate athletes to placebo, pre-exercise creatine, or post-exercise creatine for 8 weeks while co-ingesting whey protein and maltodextrin. The source did not find a clear timing advantage and reported mild gastrointestinal-type adverse events and some withdrawals for undesirable weight gain possibly related to supplementation.

**Why it matters:** It is a direct, modern timing trial and the key safety/tolerability source in this batch.

**Potential experiment signals:** Pre/post timing adherence, body weight, fat-free mass, strength, GI symptoms, nausea, headache, and withdrawal/weight-gain logs.

**Protocol takeaway:** Pre- versus post-workout timing did not clearly matter in this 8-week athlete trial; co-ingestion and adverse-event context should be logged.

**Limitations / boundary notes:** Co-ingested whey and maltodextrin; 34 completed after 47 randomized; no loading phase; collegiate athlete population; some adverse-event and withdrawal data need safety-boundary handling.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
