This source is included for **safety_tolerability_adverse_events**.

**Findings:**
- The Cochrane review included 14 trials with 364 randomized participants in muscle disorders.
- For muscular dystrophies, meta-analysis of six trials with 192 participants found muscle strength increased by mean difference 8.47% (95% CI 3.55 to 13.38) versus placebo.
- For metabolic myopathies, three cross-over trials with 33 participants showed no significant muscle-strength difference; high-dose creatine in McArdle disease increased muscle pain and worsened activities of daily living in one trial.

**Why it matters:**
Cochrane reviews are high-quality but this one is a disease-treatment boundary source.

**Protocol takeaway:**
Preserve disease-treatment and adverse-event context without applying it to healthy-adult supplementation.

**Population mismatch / boundary:**
Hereditary muscle disorders; not default healthy adults.

**Limitations:**
- Disease populations differ substantially from healthy users.
- Clinical endpoints and dosing may not match supplement protocols.
- Some subgroups have small trial counts.
