This source is included for **timing_coingestion**.

**Findings:** [source_artifact:pmid-23919405] compared 5 g creatine monohydrate immediately pre- versus post-workout for 4 weeks in 19 recreational male bodybuilders. Post-workout dosing was directionally favored for fat-free mass, fat mass, and bench press in the authors' interpretation, but the source should be treated as mixed because of small size and limited between-group certainty.

**Why it matters:** It is direct timing evidence but also a reason to keep protocol language modest.

**Potential experiment signals:** Dose timing adherence, lean mass proxies, body weight, strength progression, and training volume.

**Protocol takeaway:** Reasonable direct evidence to log pre/post timing, but not strong enough alone to mandate post-workout dosing.

**Limitations / boundary notes:** Small sample; male bodybuilders only; 4-week duration; no placebo; magnitude-based inference should not be overpromoted.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
