- **null_result / Repeated interval swimming:** Creatine supplementation did not improve repeated interval swimming performance in the pooled analysis. Directness: `adjacent_variant`. Claim use: `context-only`.
- **null_result / Physiological response and body composition:** Creatine did not significantly alter physiological-response or body-composition outcomes among swimmers. Directness: `adjacent_variant`. Claim use: `context-only`.

**Why it matters:** This swimmer-specific RCT meta-analysis found no significant overall effects of creatine supplementation on swimming performance, physiological responses, or body composition.
