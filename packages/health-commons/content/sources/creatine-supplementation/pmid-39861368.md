This source is included for **repeated_sprint_power_synthesis**.

**Findings:**
- **mixed_result / Strength and power performance:** Strength/power evidence in active females was mixed rather than consistently positive. Directness: `direct_protocol`. Claim use: `supports-protocol`.
- **mixed_result / Anaerobic performance:** Anaerobic outcomes in active females showed only minority improvement. Directness: `direct_protocol`. Claim use: `supports-protocol`.
- **null_result / Aerobic performance:** Aerobic performance evidence in active females was mostly null. Directness: `direct_protocol`. Claim use: `supports-protocol`.
- **limitation / Study design and female-specific reporting:** Female-specific design/reporting limitations should be visible in protocol synthesis. Directness: `direct_protocol`. Claim use: `supports-protocol`.

**Why it matters:** This active-female systematic review found the evidence inconclusive, with only a minority of strength/power, anaerobic, and aerobic outcomes improving versus placebo.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- strength/power tests
- anaerobic performance
- aerobic performance
- female-specific cycle/hormonal context

**Protocol takeaway:** For active females, present creatine as plausible but not uniformly performance-enhancing across endpoints.

**Limitations and boundary notes:**
- Outcome heterogeneity
- Female-specific methodological reporting was limited
- Heterogeneous tests and protocols
- Study calibre varied
- Small endpoint count
- Aerobic outcomes are adjacent to the main creatine mechanism
- Systematic review did not pool all outcomes into one effect estimate

- None beyond the source-specific caveats above.
