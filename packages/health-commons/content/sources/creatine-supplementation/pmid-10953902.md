This source is included for **renal_safety_labs**.

**Findings:** Measured indices remained within normal ranges and the authors reported no impairment of renal or hepatic function across the protocols.

**Adverse events / safety notes:** No renal/liver/muscle-damage signal in young healthy participants over the studied durations.

**Limitations:** Young healthy sample; Short-to-moderate duration; Not designed for rare adverse events

**Population mismatch:** Healthy adults; not clinical kidney population.

**Why it matters:** Direct trial reporting renal-function markers alongside broader safety labs.

**Potential experiment signals:** hematological indices, muscle damage markers, hepatic function, renal function, serum creatinine

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports routine safety-boundary language for healthy users, not disease extrapolation.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
