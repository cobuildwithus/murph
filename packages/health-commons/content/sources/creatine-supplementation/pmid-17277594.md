**Findings:** [source_artifact:pmid-17277594] examined whey isolate, creatine, and resistance training in recreational male bodybuilders and reported favorable muscle morphology/strength-related outcomes in supplement contexts. Because the intervention is not a clean creatine timing contrast, it is adjacent-variant context only.

**Why it matters:** It provides context for common creatine-with-whey use while maintaining evidence boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** Protein shake use, lean mass, strength, and training volume.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not promote as creatine timing evidence; use only as adjacent protein/creatine training context.

**Limitations / boundary notes:** Male bodybuilders; protein co-supplement; no isolated timing comparison; hypertrophy mechanisms may not transfer to general users.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
