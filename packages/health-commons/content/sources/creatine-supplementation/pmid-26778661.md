This source is included for **repeated_sprint_power_trials**.

**Findings:** Both plyometric groups improved multiple outcomes; accessible summary reported creatine plus plyometrics improved jumps and repeated sprinting more than comparison groups.

**Population and intervention:** Young female soccer players; Creatine supplementation combined with plyometric training. Comparator: Plyometric training plus placebo and/or control placebo group. Duration/follow-up: 6 weeks of plyometric training plus supplementation.

**Endpoints:** jump performance, sprint performance, repeated sprinting, endurance, change of direction.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event extraction was available from the accessible record for this batch.

**Limitations:** Adjacent combined-training design. Plyometric training confounds supplement-specific attribution. Young female soccer sample.

**Population mismatch:** Female soccer players in training intervention.

**Why it matters:** Female soccer evidence with sprint/jump outcomes, but combined training must be quarantined.

**Potential experiment signals:** jump performance, sprint performance, repeated sprinting, endurance, change of direction.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only combined-training evidence.

**Murph takeaway:** Record concurrent training changes when interpreting creatine response.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. Directness: `adjacent_variant`. Result boundary: `mixed`.
