This source is included for **renal_safety_labs**.

**Findings:** Available excerpts report no between-group differences in creatinine, eGFR, KIM-1, or MCP-1; supplemented groups showed within-normal creatinine/eGFR shifts on paired analysis.

**Adverse events / safety notes:** Novel biomarker data support no short-term renal-injury signal at 3–5 g/day in healthy men.

**Limitations:** Short duration; Healthy men only; Small sample; Within-normal creatinine/eGFR shifts need context

**Population mismatch:** Healthy young men; not CKD or women.

**Why it matters:** Modern healthy-adult RCT using newer renal biomarkers beyond routine serum creatinine.

**Potential experiment signals:** serum creatinine, eGFR, cystatin C, KIM-1, MCP-1, novel renal biomarkers

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports explaining that creatinine/eGFR movement within reference ranges can occur without novel biomarker injury signal.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
