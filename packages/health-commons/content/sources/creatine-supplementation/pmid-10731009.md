This source is included for **strength_hypertrophy_trials**.

**Findings:** `source_artifact:pmid-10731009` — Creatine participants received 5 g creatine monohydrate four times daily for five days and then 2 g/day while performing six weeks of progressive arm-flexor training. Reported changes favored creatine for arm-flexor 1RM, upper-arm muscle area, body mass, and fat-free mass; fat mass and percent body fat did not change.

**Why it matters:** Direct resistance-training evidence with reported numeric pre/post strength and body-composition changes.

**Potential experiment signals:** arm flexor 1RM; upper-arm muscle area; body mass; fat-free mass; fat mass; percent body fat.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports creatine monohydrate as a resistance-training adjunct for strength and fat-free-mass/upper-arm muscle-area gains in a small male sample.

**Limitations and extraction boundary:** Small male-only sample. Arm-flexor-specific training may not generalize to whole-body programs. Accessible abstract did not report adverse events. Sucrose-containing drinks mean the supplement context was not creatine-only in the literal beverage formulation.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
