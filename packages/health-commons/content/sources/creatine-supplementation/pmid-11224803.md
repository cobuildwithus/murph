This source is included for **renal_safety_labs**.

**Findings:** Available excerpts report no broad adverse-health pattern; creatinine and total protein differences were noted but not framed as renal dysfunction.

**Adverse events / safety notes:** Retained as real-world safety context with observational limitations.

**Limitations:** Retrospective design; Small sample; Exposure self-selection and possible recall bias

**Population mismatch:** Athletes/trained adults only; not CKD evidence.

**Why it matters:** Retrospective athlete safety study that broadens duration coverage beyond shorter trials.

**Potential experiment signals:** clinical health markers, serum creatinine, total protein, self-reported adverse effects

**Protocol takeaway:** Useful for real-world safety framing, but weaker than controlled renal-function trials.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
