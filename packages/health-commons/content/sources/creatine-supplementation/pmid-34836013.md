This source is included for **population_boundary_adjacent_claims**.

**Findings:** Across 10 RCTs (n=211 older females), creatine plus RT significantly improved upper-body strength overall (7 studies, n=142, P=0.04), but did not improve lower-body strength or muscle mass overall. ≥24-week subgroup analyses found upper- and lower-body strength improvements.

**Why it matters:** Focused older-female synthesis.

**Protocol takeaway:** Older-female evidence supports upper-body strength more consistently than lower-body strength or muscle mass overall.

**Murph takeaway:** Track upper and lower strength separately and include duration in interpretation.

**Intervention or exposure:** Creatine supplementation combined with RT.

**Comparator or control:** Placebo/control combined with RT.
