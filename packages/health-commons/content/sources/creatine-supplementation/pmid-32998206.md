**Findings:**
- **Repeated Sprint Ability Test (RAST)** — Magnesium creatine chelate supplementation was associated with repeated-sprint performance improvements versus placebo in a small elite-soccer study, but no monohydrate comparator was included. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-32998206`.
- **sample completion** — Twenty players were enrolled and 16 completed the experiment. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-32998206`.

**Why it matters:** Longer-duration chelate evidence is relevant to form separation, especially in elite athletes.

**Potential experiment signals:** performance:repeated-sprint-ability, formulation:magnesium-creatine-chelate, population:elite-soccer.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not cite chelate effects as evidence for monohydrate unless directly compared.
