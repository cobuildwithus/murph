This source is included for **renal_safety_labs**.

**Findings:** Measured GFR and renal markers did not differ significantly between groups; no participants had albuminuria or proteinuria in available report.

**Adverse events / safety notes:** Important high-protein boundary trial with measured GFR.

**Limitations:** High withdrawal reduced analyzed sample; Healthy resistance-trained men only; 12-week follow-up; not CKD

**Population mismatch:** Healthy trained high-protein consumers; not disease safety evidence.

**Why it matters:** Directly tests a common higher-intake boundary condition: creatine plus high-protein diet.

**Potential experiment signals:** 51Cr-EDTA measured GFR, creatinine clearance, serum and urinary urea, electrolytes, proteinuria, albuminuria

**Protocol takeaway:** Useful when users combine creatine with high-protein diets, while preserving CKD caveats.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
