**Why it matters:** This adults-under-50 resistance-training meta-analysis found greater upper- and lower-body strength gains with creatine, while female subgroup evidence was sparse and not significant.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- upper-body strength
- lower-body strength

- sex subgroup

**Protocol takeaway:** Strength claims are strongest when creatine is paired with resistance training; communicate the weaker female-specific evidence base.

**Limitations and boundary notes:**
- Mostly male samples
- Adults under 50 only
- Exercise context required
- Female subgroup was small
- Non-significance may reflect low power
- Not a dose-response conclusion
- High-dose lower-body trend was not definitive
