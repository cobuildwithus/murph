This source is included for **repeated_sprint_power_trials**.

**Findings:** No between-group differences in fastest time, mean time, fatigue index, or lactate; body mass increased by about 0.7 kg in creatine group.

**Population and intervention:** Physically active men; Creatine monohydrate plus small maltodextrin dose, reported as four daily doses of 5 g creatine monohydrate plus 1 g maltodextrin. Comparator: Maltodextrin placebo. Duration/follow-up: 5 days of supplementation.

**Endpoints:** 15 × 30 m sprint running, fastest sprint time, mean sprint time, fatigue index, post-test lactate, body mass.

**Safety notes:** Body mass increased by about 0.7 kg after loading; no serious adverse-event signal was extracted.

**Limitations:** DOI conflict exists in candidate records; canonical DOI left null. Physically active male sample. 5-day loading may not represent maintenance protocols.

**Population mismatch:** Physically active men; not elite athletes or female users.

**Why it matters:** Direct high-recall source that balances positive repeated-sprint evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** 15 × 30 m sprint running, fastest sprint time, mean sprint time, fatigue index, post-test lactate, body mass.

**Protocol takeaway:** Creatine did not improve multiple 30 m sprint performance in this study.

**Murph takeaway:** Track body weight and repeated-sprint times because weight gain can coexist with no sprint gain.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`. Directness: `direct_protocol`. Result boundary: `no_clear_advantage`.
