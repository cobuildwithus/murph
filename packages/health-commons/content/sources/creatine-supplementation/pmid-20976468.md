This source is included for **renal_safety_labs**.

**Findings:** Available excerpts report no group-by-time change in measured GFR and no meaningful changes in creatinine clearance, urea, electrolytes, proteinuria, or albuminuria.

**Adverse events / safety notes:** Controlled clinical-population evidence that creatine did not impair measured kidney function over 12 weeks in T2D patients under study conditions.

**Limitations:** Short duration; Exercise co-intervention; Clinical monitoring; not uncontrolled self-use; Analyzed sample smaller than randomized sample

**Population mismatch:** Type 2 diabetes population; supervised trial, not routine consumer protocol.

**Why it matters:** Key higher-risk boundary trial in a metabolic-disease population often excluded from generic supplement claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** 51Cr-EDTA measured GFR, creatinine clearance, serum/urinary urea, electrolytes, proteinuria, albuminuria

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports cautious boundary evidence that measured renal endpoints can remain stable beyond healthy athletes.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
