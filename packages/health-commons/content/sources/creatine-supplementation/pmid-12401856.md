**Adverse events / safety notes:** Disease-population safety boundary only; not a consumer kidney-function clearance.

**Limitations:** Hemodialysis population; Small/older clinical study; Renal function cannot be assessed conventionally in dialysis patients

**Population mismatch:** End-stage kidney disease on dialysis; not direct healthy-user protocol evidence.

**Why it matters:** Early renal-disease treatment paper showing creatine use inside a clearly non-healthy population boundary.
