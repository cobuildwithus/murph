This source is included for **timing_coingestion**.

**Findings:** [source_artifact:pmid-17095924] found better resistance-training adaptations when a mixed supplement was taken immediately before/after workouts compared with away from training. Because the product included protein and carbohydrate as well as creatine, the finding is adjacent-variant context only.

**Why it matters:** It explains why creatine timing is discussed, while setting a strict boundary around direct protocol claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** Workout proximity, co-ingested protein/carbohydrate, strength, and body-composition outcomes.

**Protocol takeaway:** This supports a broader workout-nutrition timing hypothesis, not a clean creatine monohydrate timing claim.

**Limitations / boundary notes:** Small male bodybuilding sample; multi-ingredient supplement; creatine-only effect and timing cannot be isolated.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
