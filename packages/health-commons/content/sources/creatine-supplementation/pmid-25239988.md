**Findings:**
- **serum creatinine and renal-lab interpretation** — Serum creatinine elevation occurred during creatine ethyl ester use and resolved after stopping, without evidence of underlying kidney pathology in the case report. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-25239988`.
- **generalizability** — Single case report; formulation was creatine ethyl ester rather than monohydrate. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-25239988`.

**Why it matters:** It flags a known lab-confounding issue with an adjacent formulation that can be mistaken for renal dysfunction.

**Potential experiment signals:** safety:serum-creatinine, safety:kidney-labs, formulation:creatine-ethyl-ester.

**Protocol takeaway:** When creatinine rises, document the exact creatine form; ethyl ester is not the same exposure as monohydrate.
