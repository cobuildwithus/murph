This source is included for **safety_tolerability_adverse_events**.

**Findings:**
- The review focused on safety of creatine supplementation in active adolescents and youth.
- Available summaries emphasize limited adverse-event reporting; several included adolescent studies did not report adverse-event occurrence.
- The source supports a pediatric/adolescent boundary rather than routine default recommendations.

**Why it matters:**
Many users ask whether teens should use creatine; this source anchors the protocol boundary.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- population / youth
- safety / pediatric adolescent
- adverse_event / reported adverse events
- evidence gap / underreporting

**Protocol takeaway:**
Use for minors caution language and evidence-gap transparency, not for default adult protocol support.

**Population mismatch / boundary:**
Children/adolescents are outside the default healthy-adult protocol.

**Limitations:**
- Brief narrative review.
- Youth trials are limited and often underpowered for safety.
- Adverse events may be absent or simply not systematically reported.
