**Findings:** Accessible snippets indicate 20 g/day for one week did not improve maximal static strength or dynamic peak torque, but improved mean strength and endurance of repeated contractions; full numeric effects were not recovered. Safety/adverse-event extraction: Adverse events were not extracted from accessible abstract/indexing material. Limitations: Older sport-specific female trial.; Outcome details were only partially recovered from snippets.; Japanese/softball context may limit broad application.

**Why it matters:** Women-specific loading plus disappearance/washout context fills a population gap.

**Potential experiment signals:** isometric strength, isokinetic torque, endurance reps, loss of effect after stopping.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use cautiously as direct, women-specific, mixed evidence for loading and washout boundaries.
