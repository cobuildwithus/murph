**Findings:**
- **muscle creatine content** — Creatine monohydrate increased muscle creatine content after 14 days; creatyl-L-leucine and placebo did not show that increase. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-36007881`.
- **supplement dose** — Arms used 5 g/day creatine monohydrate, 5 g/day creatyl-L-leucine, or placebo. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-36007881`.

**Why it matters:** It directly tests a marketed creatine-related compound against monohydrate using muscle creatine content.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:muscle-creatine-content, formulation:creatyl-l-leucine, exercise:resistance-exercise.

**Protocol takeaway:** Creatyl-L-leucine should not be treated as creatine monohydrate or a proven substitute.
