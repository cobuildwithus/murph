This source is included for **formulation_variant_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- **alternative creatine-form evidence base** — Seventeen randomized placebo-controlled trials were identified, but only a minority directly compared an alternative form with creatine monohydrate; the evidence did not consistently show superior performance or body-composition outcomes for alternatives. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-36000773`.
- **cost and evidence hierarchy** — Creatine monohydrate remained the lowest-cost and most-studied reference form in the review. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-36000773`.

**Why it matters:** It directly addresses the protocol boundary: creatine monohydrate is the comparator with the deepest evidence base, whereas alternative forms are unevenly studied.

**Potential experiment signals:** formulation:alternative-creatine-forms, performance:exercise-performance, body-composition:body-composition.

**Protocol takeaway:** For the monohydrate protocol, do not promote hydrochloride, citrate, pyruvate, nitrate, ethyl ester, or chelated variants as superior on the basis of formulation claims alone.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
