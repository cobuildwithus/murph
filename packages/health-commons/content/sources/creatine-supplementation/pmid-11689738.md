This source is included for **repeated_sprint_power_trials**.

**Findings:** Accessible records identify an acute force-time study; extracted details do not support a numeric sprint/power claim.

**Population and intervention:** Sedentary college-aged males; Creatine supplementation, reported as 20 g/day split into 5 g doses. Comparator: Placebo. Duration/follow-up: 5 days of creatine loading in accessible summaries.

**Endpoints:** isometric force-time curve characteristics, maximal strength/force production, rate-of-force development.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event extraction was available from the accessible record for this batch. 

**Limitations:** Sedentary male sample. Isometric force-time curve is not the same as sport sprint performance. Exact effect estimates not extracted.

**Population mismatch:** Sedentary college-aged males, not trained athletes.

**Why it matters:** Force-time curve outcomes may map to power signals but are not direct sprint tests.

**Potential experiment signals:** isometric force-time curve characteristics, maximal strength/force production, rate-of-force development.

**Protocol takeaway:** Relevant direct physiology source with narrow endpoint scope.

**Murph takeaway:** Track force/power metrics separately from sprint times.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`. Directness: `direct_protocol`. Result boundary: `mixed`.
