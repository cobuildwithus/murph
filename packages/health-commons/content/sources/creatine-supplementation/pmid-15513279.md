This source is included for **formulation_variant_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- **product creatine content and plasma creatine response** — Creatine Serum contained far less creatine than claimed and a single dose did not meaningfully raise plasma creatine; creatine monohydrate powder did raise plasma creatine acutely. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-15513279`.

**Why it matters:** It directly challenges a marketed liquid creatine product's claimed creatine delivery.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:plasma-creatine, formulation:creatine-serum, quality:product-content.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not count Creatine Serum as creatine monohydrate exposure.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
