This source is included for **population_boundary_adjacent_claims**.

**Findings:** Meta-analysis reported a small body-fat percentage reduction with creatine during RT (-0.55%, P=0.04), while absolute fat-mass reduction was not significant (-0.50 kg, P=0.13).

**Why it matters:** Directly addresses fat outcomes in adults ≥50.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- body-fat percentage
- absolute fat mass
- resistance-training body composition

**Protocol takeaway:** Creatine plus RT may slightly reduce body-fat percentage in adults ≥50, but absolute fat mass is not clearly reduced.

**Murph takeaway:** Interpret body-fat percentage estimates cautiously.

**Population:** Adults aged 50 years or older in resistance-training studies.

**Intervention or exposure:** Creatine supplementation during RT.

**Comparator or control:** Placebo/control during RT.

**Duration or follow-up:** Varied across included studies.

**Endpoints:**
- body-fat percentage
- absolute fat mass
- resistance-training body composition

**Effect estimate or direction:** Meta-analysis reported a small body-fat percentage reduction with creatine during RT (-0.55%, P=0.04), while absolute fat-mass reduction was not significant (-0.50 kg, P=0.13).

**Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event details were recovered in the accessible batch extraction.

**Limitations:**
- Older-adult body-composition synthesis; participant count not recovered.
- Absolute fat-mass result was not significant.

**Population mismatch:** Adults ≥50 years.
