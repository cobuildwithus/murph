This source is included for **strength_hypertrophy_synthesis**.

**Findings:**
- **outcome / Body-fat percentage:** Creatine plus resistance exercise reduced body-fat percentage in adults under 50. Directness: `direct_protocol`. Claim use: `supports-protocol`.
- **null_result / Absolute fat mass:** Absolute fat mass did not clearly decrease despite lower body-fat percentage. Directness: `direct_protocol`. Claim use: `supports-protocol`.
- **limitation / Moderators:** Moderator evidence did not identify a clear subgroup explanation for the fat-percentage effect. Directness: `direct_protocol`. Claim use: `supports-protocol`.

**Why it matters:** This adults-under-50 synthesis found creatine plus resistance exercise reduced body-fat percentage but did not clearly reduce absolute fat mass.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- body-fat percentage
- fat mass
- body weight
- resistance-training adherence

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not frame creatine as a fat-loss supplement; body-fat percentage may change partly because lean mass/body mass changes.

**Limitations and boundary notes:**
- Adults under 50 only
- Body-fat percentage can change because lean mass changes
- Exact fat-mass point estimate should be verified from final article before protocol synthesis
- Small number of studies
- Moderator analyses may be underpowered

- None beyond the source-specific caveats above.
