This source is included for **timing_coingestion**.

**Findings:** [source_artifact:pmid-20035494] reported that creatine combined with smaller quantities of essential amino acids, simple sugars, and protein stimulated insulin release and augmented whole-body retention to a similar extent as a larger sugar bolus in seven young healthy males.

**Why it matters:** It supports the practical shift from high-sugar co-ingestion toward mixed nutrient dosing.

**Potential experiment signals:** Meal composition, subjective tolerance, adherence, and body mass.

**Protocol takeaway:** Mixed protein/amino acid/carbohydrate co-ingestion can support retention acutely, but this should not become a required protocol step.

**Limitations / boundary notes:** N=7; acute retention and urine endpoints; no muscle biopsy or training outcomes; co-ingestion composition may affect tolerance.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
