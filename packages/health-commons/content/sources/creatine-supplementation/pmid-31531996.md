This source is included for **renal_safety_labs**.

**Findings:** Available abstract reports creatine may alleviate MIS and attenuate lean-body-mass loss compared with placebo; food intake and quality of life did not change.

**Adverse events / safety notes:** Hemodialysis tolerability context only, not direct renal-function safety evidence.

**Limitations:** Pilot study; Dialysis population; Short duration; Not a healthy-user renal-function trial

**Population mismatch:** Hemodialysis/CKD population under clinical care.

**Why it matters:** Pilot randomized dialysis trial that broadens the CKD/ESKD boundary evidence beyond case reports.

**Potential experiment signals:** malnutrition-inflammation score, lean body mass, gait speed, BMI, quality of life, adverse events/tolerability

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as renal-boundary clinical context only.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
