This source is included for **renal_safety_labs**.

**Findings:** Study compared dose-splitting effects on urinary creatine and methylamine excretion; useful for retention/metabolite interpretation, not renal injury.

**Adverse events / safety notes:** Do not infer kidney harm from urinary excretion alone.

**Limitations:** Small sample; Healthy young men; Metabolite endpoint rather than clinical kidney function

**Population mismatch:** Metabolism/lab context; not renal safety outcome trial.

**Why it matters:** Useful for mechanistic boundary questions about methylamine/formaldehyde-related kidney concerns.

**Potential experiment signals:** urinary creatine, urinary methylamine, whole-body retention, formaldehyde/metabolite context

**Protocol takeaway:** Useful for interpreting urine/metabolite signals during loading protocols.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
