This source is included for **repeated_sprint_power_trials**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries indicate maximum power output increased in the creatine group while anaerobic working capacity was unchanged.

**Population and intervention:** College-aged men; Creatine supplementation during HIIT. Comparator: Placebo/control during HIIT. Duration/follow-up: 4 weeks of high-intensity interval training.

**Endpoints:** critical power, anaerobic working capacity, maximum power output.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event extraction was available from the accessible record for this batch. 

**Limitations:** Adjacent combined-training design. Sample size and exact effects not extracted. Not an isolated creatine-only sprint test.

**Population mismatch:** College-aged men in HIIT program.

**Why it matters:** Boundary source for training adaptations during interval programs.

**Potential experiment signals:** critical power, anaerobic working capacity, maximum power output.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only, mixed endpoint evidence.

**Murph takeaway:** Separate supplement effects from interval-training adaptations.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. Directness: `adjacent_variant`. Result boundary: `mixed`.
