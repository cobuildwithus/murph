This source is included for **timing_coingestion**.

**Findings:** [source_artifact:pmid-1327657] reported that repeated 5 g oral creatine monohydrate doses raised quadriceps total creatine in normal subjects. The study sampled muscle rather than training outcomes, so it supports uptake plausibility rather than a specific pre/post or meal-timing rule.

**Why it matters:** It anchors the mechanistic chain for the timing/co-ingestion batch by showing that oral monohydrate can increase the target tissue pool.

**Potential experiment signals:** Muscle total creatine and phosphocreatine are research-only signals; Murph-facing proxies would be dosing adherence, body mass change, and performance trends over weeks.

**Protocol takeaway:** Creatine monohydrate loading can raise muscle creatine stores; do not use this study alone to claim a preferred workout timing window.

**Limitations / boundary notes:** Small early physiology study; no placebo-controlled training endpoint; exact transfer to everyday gym outcomes is indirect.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
