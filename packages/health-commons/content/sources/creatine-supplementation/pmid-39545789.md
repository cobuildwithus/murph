This source is included for **formulation_variant_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- **strength, hypertrophy, body composition, and hormone markers** — Creatine hydrochloride and creatine monohydrate groups showed improvements versus placebo/training context, but HCl did not differ significantly from monohydrate loading or non-loading groups on key outcomes. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-39545789`.
- **loading versus no-loading monohydrate comparison** — Monohydrate was tested with a loading phase and without a loading phase, while HCl used 0.03 g/kg/day; no clear HCl advantage was observed. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-39545789`.

**Why it matters:** It is a recent open direct HCl-versus-monohydrate RCT with practical training endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** performance:bench-press, performance:leg-press, body-composition:skeletal-muscle-mass, body-composition:percent-body-fat, biomarker:gh-igf1-cortisol-acth, formulation:creatine-hydrochloride.

**Protocol takeaway:** Monohydrate loading or non-loading protocols remain defensible; HCl is not supported as a superior swap.
