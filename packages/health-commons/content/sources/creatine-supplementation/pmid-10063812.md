This source is included for **dose_loading_maintenance**.

**Findings:** The title and abstract/indexing material state that phosphocreatine resynthesis was not affected by creatine loading. Safety/adverse-event extraction: Adverse events were not extracted from the accessible abstract/indexing material. Limitations: Participant count and full dose schedule were not recovered in accessible extraction material.; Mechanistic endpoint rather than user-facing performance outcome.; Null finding applies to PCr resynthesis under the tested conditions, not all creatine effects.

**Why it matters:** Null mechanistic evidence is important for balancing saturation and recovery claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** perceived recovery between sets, repeat-bout performance, fatigue pattern.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a mechanism boundary: loading can raise creatine stores without necessarily accelerating PCr resynthesis under every test condition.
