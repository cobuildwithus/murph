**Population mismatch:** Elite swimmers and single-event swim setting.

**Why it matters:** Important sport-specific boundary source for swimming performance.

**Potential experiment signals:** single-effort sprint swimming, 50 m swim sprint, body mass.

**Protocol takeaway:** Swimming evidence is sport- and protocol-specific, not uniformly supportive.

**Murph takeaway:** Track body mass and distinguish single-effort from repeated-effort swim outcomes.
