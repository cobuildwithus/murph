This source is included for **strength_hypertrophy_trials**.

**Findings:** `source_artifact:pmid-14685870` — Resistance-trained men received creatine monohydrate 0.3 g/kg/day or placebo during a 4-week high-volume overreaching block and 2-week taper. Creatine maintained muscular performance during the early overreaching phase and was associated with greater bench-press explosive power, body mass, and leg lean body mass at six weeks; a squat 1RM trend was reported but did not reach the same threshold.

**Why it matters:** Tests a high-stress training scenario where creatine’s phosphocreatine and recovery-support rationale is especially relevant.

**Potential experiment signals:** bench press explosive power; bench press 1RM; squat 1RM; body mass; leg lean body mass; training overreaching tolerance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports creatine for maintaining and rebounding muscular performance during short-term high-volume resistance-training overreaching, with body-mass and leg-lean-mass signals.

**Limitations and extraction boundary:** Small male-only resistance-trained cohort. Overreaching/taper design may not generalize to lower-volume programs. Accessible record did not provide adverse-event reporting. Hormone endpoints were exploratory/contextual rather than direct protocol outcomes.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
