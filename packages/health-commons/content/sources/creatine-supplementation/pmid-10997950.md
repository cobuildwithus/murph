This source is included for **dose_loading_maintenance**.

**Findings:** Accessible abstracts report more total work until fatigue, greater improvements in peak force and peak power, and longer maintenance of elevated mean peak power in creatine subjects. Safety/adverse-event extraction: Adverse events were not extracted from accessible abstract/indexing material. Limitations: Participant count was not recovered in accessible extraction material.; Short duration and resistance-training context.; Dose was higher than common 3-5 g/day maintenance but lower than classic loading.

**Why it matters:** This directly addresses no-load or lower-dose continuous supplementation.

**Potential experiment signals:** peak force, peak power, work until fatigue, training volume.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as direct evidence that a continuous lower-dose strategy can affect performance over weeks.
