This source is included for **timing_coingestion**.

**Findings:** [source_artifact:pmid-17679046] compared differing protein sources and creatine-containing formulas during 12 weeks of resistance training. Some creatine-formula groups had favorable body-composition changes, but the intervention is multi-ingredient and adjacent to the creatine monohydrate timing protocol.

**Why it matters:** It supports boundary handling for protein/creatine formulas.

**Potential experiment signals:** Protein source, body mass/FFM/body water, strength, and endurance/anaerobic outcomes.

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep as adjacent multi-ingredient/protein-source evidence; do not use for direct timing claims.

**Limitations / boundary notes:** Formula/protein-source confounding; no clean monohydrate-only timing arm; resistance-trained population only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
