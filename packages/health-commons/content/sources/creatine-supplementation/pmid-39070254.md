This source is included for **population_boundary_adjacent_claims**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Systematic review and meta-analysis of adult cognitive-function RCTs (2024).
- **People studied or addressed:** Adults aged approximately 20.8 to 76.4 years in cognition-focused RCTs; most studies healthy, with some disease populations.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Creatine monohydrate supplementation; included regimens varied by dose and duration.
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo in included RCTs.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Varied; review compared less than 4 weeks versus 4 weeks or longer.
- **Role in Murph:** context-only / adjacent_variant.

## Findings

**Findings:** This meta-analysis included 16 RCTs and 492 participants; all included studies used creatine monohydrate, with reported effects for memory (SMD 0.31, 95% CI 0.18 to 0.44), attention time (SMD -0.31, 95% CI -0.58 to -0.03), and processing-speed time (SMD -0.51, 95% CI -1.01 to -0.01), but no significant overall cognition or executive-function improvement [source_artifact:pmid-39070254].

## Why it matters

It is a recent monohydrate-specific cognition synthesis, but the endpoints and populations remain outside the default protocol’s core outcomes. [source_artifact:pmid-39070254]

## Potential experiment signals

Optional memory or timed-attention tests could be exploratory only; subgroup and disease-population findings should stay quarantined [source_artifact:pmid-39070254].

## Protocol takeaway

Keep the reported cognitive-domain signals in an adjacent-claims bucket and do not synthesize them as default protocol benefits. [source_artifact:pmid-39070254]

## Key limitations and population mismatch

- Cognition endpoints, not gym-performance endpoints [source_artifact:pmid-39070254]
- GRADE certainty varied and was low for several domains [source_artifact:pmid-39070254]
- Includes some disease populations and subgroup claims [source_artifact:pmid-39070254]
- Population mismatch: Adult cognition studies and disease subgroups are adjacent to a healthy-adult gym protocol despite using monohydrate. [source_artifact:pmid-39070254]
