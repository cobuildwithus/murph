This source is included for **renal_safety_labs**.

**Findings:** Review argues that creatine may increase serum creatinine without necessarily indicating kidney dysfunction and that controlled evidence using reliable methods does not support kidney failure in healthy users; data remain limited for pre-existing kidney disease.

**Adverse events / safety notes:** Strong recent source for separating creatinine artifacts from true renal injury.

**Limitations:** Narrative review; Relies on heterogeneous underlying studies; Calls for more work in pre-existing kidney disease

**Population mismatch:** Safety-boundary review, not direct protocol efficacy evidence.

**Why it matters:** Recent dedicated review of kidney-failure claims; useful for adjudicating case reports and lab-interpretation issues.

**Potential experiment signals:** serum creatinine, eGFR, measured GFR, kidney-failure case reports, lab interpretation

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports protocol language that abnormal labs should be interpreted clinically rather than assumed benign or harmful.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
