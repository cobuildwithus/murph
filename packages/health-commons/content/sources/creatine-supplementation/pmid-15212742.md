This source is included for **background_guidelines_external**.

**Findings:**
- From `source_artifact:pmid-15212742`: the review states that many studies have found creatine supplementation combined with resistance training to augment strength and muscle-size adaptations.
- The review describes increased acute weightlifting performance and training volume as possible routes by which creatine could support training adaptations.
- Mechanistic discussion includes fiber hypertrophy, myosin heavy-chain expression, cell swelling, and glycogen-related hypotheses, while noting that mechanisms remain unclear.
- Effects on protein synthesis and degradation were not consistently detected in the literature summarized by the review.

**Why it matters:** It connects creatine to resistance-training adaptations and plausible mechanisms, while also highlighting uncertainty in mechanism.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- training volume
- bench press or squat strength
- lean mass
- body weight
- subjective recovery

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only: consistent with creatine plus training as a plausible performance strategy, but not a direct source for new protocol claims.

**Murph takeaway:** Creatine may pair best with training, but the exact mechanisms and athlete-specific context should be treated cautiously.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Older narrative review; athlete-specific; not a single protocol trial; mechanism claims are hypothesis-generating.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
