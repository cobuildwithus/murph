This source is included for **renal_safety_labs**.

**Findings:** Creatine increased fat-free mass/total body water but available excerpts report no significant effect on plasma creatinine, estimated creatinine clearance, blood pressure, or CK activity.

**Adverse events / safety notes:** No acute renal-lab or blood-pressure signal was reported in this short loading study.

**Limitations:** Very short duration; Healthy adults only; Estimated rather than measured GFR

**Population mismatch:** Healthy participants; not CKD or older-adult safety evidence.

**Why it matters:** Acute-loading trial with explicit plasma creatinine reporting in both sexes.

**Potential experiment signals:** plasma creatinine, estimated creatinine clearance, blood pressure, CK activity, fat-free mass, total body water

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports separating expected body-water change from renal-injury claims during loading.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
