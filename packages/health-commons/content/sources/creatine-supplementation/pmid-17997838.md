This source is included for **formulation_variant_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- **plasma creatine AUC and peak concentration** — Creatine pyruvate produced higher peak plasma creatine and AUC than creatine monohydrate or citrate in a six-person acute cross-over study; monohydrate and citrate did not show a clear difference. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-17997838`.
- **clinical translation** — No muscle creatine content, training adaptation, or performance outcome was measured. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-17997838`.

**Why it matters:** It is often cited in alternative-form marketing, but it should not be promoted as monohydrate efficacy evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:plasma-creatine, mechanism:bioavailability, formulation:creatine-pyruvate, formulation:creatine-citrate.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not infer superior protocol outcomes from acute plasma kinetics alone.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
