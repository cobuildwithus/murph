This source is included for **repeated_sprint_power_trials**.

**Findings:** Accessible record/title reports enhanced muscle force recovery after eccentric exercise-induced muscle damage.

**Population and intervention:** Healthy untrained male participants exposed to eccentric muscle-damage protocol; Creatine plus carbohydrate supplementation around eccentric exercise. Comparator: Carbohydrate placebo. Duration/follow-up: 5 days before eccentric exercise and 14 days after exercise.

**Endpoints:** muscle force recovery, eccentric exercise-induced muscle damage, muscle proteins/recovery markers.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event extraction was available from the accessible record for this batch. 

**Limitations:** Untrained male sample. Creatine plus carbohydrate design complicates monohydrate-only interpretation. Recovery/damage protocol rather than acute sprint performance.

**Population mismatch:** Untrained men and eccentric damage setting differ from trained repeated-sprint users.

**Why it matters:** Useful direct creatine recovery source for post-exercise performance endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle force recovery, eccentric exercise-induced muscle damage, muscle proteins/recovery markers.

**Protocol takeaway:** Creatine may aid force recovery after damaging exercise.

**Murph takeaway:** Track soreness, force recovery, and next-session performance.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`. Directness: `direct_protocol`. Result boundary: `positive`.
