This source is included for **repeated_sprint_power_trials**.

**Findings:** Accessible records indicate creatine altered submaximal graded-cycling responses but did not provide a clear repeated-sprint or strength-performance claim.

**Population and intervention:** Trained adults, including men and women, performing graded cycle ergometer testing; Creatine supplementation. Comparator: Placebo. Duration/follow-up: Creatine supplementation before graded cycling test; exact duration not extracted.

**Endpoints:** graded cycling response, oxygen consumption, heart rate, anaerobic threshold/test duration.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event extraction was available from the accessible record for this batch.

**Limitations:** Adjacent endurance/graded-test design. Exact numeric effects not extracted here.

**Population mismatch:** Graded cycling physiology differs from resistance training and repeated sprinting.

**Why it matters:** Helps separate high-intensity sprint/power evidence from aerobic-adjacent physiology.

**Potential experiment signals:** graded cycling response, oxygen consumption, heart rate, anaerobic threshold/test duration.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context source only; not direct evidence for repeated-sprint improvement.

**Murph takeaway:** Do not expect graded-aerobic metrics to mirror sprint/power outcomes.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. Directness: `adjacent_variant`. Result boundary: `not_efficacy_evidence`.
