**Findings:**
- **muscle creatine, body composition, strength, and power** — Creatine ethyl ester did not outperform creatine monohydrate or placebo on body-composition or training outcomes over 7 weeks. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-19228401`.
- **serum creatinine** — Serum creatinine increased more in the creatine ethyl ester arm than in monohydrate or placebo arms. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-19228401`.

**Why it matters:** Creatine ethyl ester is a major marketed alternative; this direct human comparator is central for boundary-setting.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:muscle-creatine-content, biomarker:serum-creatinine, performance:strength-power, body-composition:lean-mass, formulation:creatine-ethyl-ester.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not recommend ethyl ester as a superior or lower-dose replacement for monohydrate.
