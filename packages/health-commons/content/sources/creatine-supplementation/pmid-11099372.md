This source is included for **dose_loading_maintenance**.

**Findings:** Accessible extracts report increased dorsiflexion MVC torque by 6.6% and lactate by 20.8%, with no gender-specific response detected. Safety/adverse-event extraction: Adverse events were not extracted from accessible abstract/indexing material. Limitations: Short loading-only periods.; Small sex-stratified sample.; Crossover washout helps design but does not establish long-term maintenance effects.

**Why it matters:** This adds direct loading, crossover washout, and sex-comparison evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** high-intensity output, strength/power, sex-specific response, loading tolerance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as direct short-loading evidence and note that no sex-specific response was detected in this small crossover trial.
