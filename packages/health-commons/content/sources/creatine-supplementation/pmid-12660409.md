This source is included for **dose_loading_maintenance**.

**Findings:** All loading protocols increased skeletal-muscle total creatine; creatine plus glucose produced a larger post-loading increase than creatine alone or creatine plus exercise. Maintenance at 2 or 5 g/day preserved post-loading muscle total creatine for 6 weeks, while 0 g/day declined but was not fully back to baseline by 6 weeks. Safety/adverse-event extraction: Adverse events were not extracted from the accessible abstract/indexing material. Limitations: Small male-only physiology sample.; Muscle biopsy and urinary excretion endpoints, not a clinical outcome trial.; Glucose co-ingestion and exercise co-intervention arms do not isolate standard creatine-only maintenance.

**Why it matters:** This is one of the most directly relevant human papers for loading, maintenance, retention, and washout timing.

**Potential experiment signals:** body-weight change, muscle performance after loading, GI tolerance during split doses, whether benefits fade after stopping.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as backbone evidence that loading can saturate muscle stores quickly and that 2-5 g/day maintenance can preserve them.
