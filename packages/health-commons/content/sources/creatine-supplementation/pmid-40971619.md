This source is included for **population_boundary_adjacent_claims**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Systematic review of creatine and cognition evidence in older adults (2026).
- **People studied or addressed:** Older adults aged 55 years or older, mostly healthy community-dwelling populations with one overweight older-women study.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Creatine supplementation or dietary creatine intake; two intervention studies used creatine monohydrate and four were cross-sectional dietary-intake studies.
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo/control in intervention studies or lower intake/reference groups in observational studies.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Varied across intervention and observational studies.
- **Role in Murph:** context-only / adjacent_variant.

## Findings

**Findings:** This systematic review included 6 studies and 1542 older adults; two studies were creatine-monohydrate interventions and four were cross-sectional dietary-intake studies, with five of six reporting a positive relationship or effect, especially for memory and attention [source_artifact:pmid-40971619].

## Why it matters

It is a new aging-cognition synthesis that may be cited for brain-health claims; the source page must preserve observational and quality limits. [source_artifact:pmid-40971619]

## Potential experiment signals

Optional memory/attention signals and dietary-creatine context should remain in an older-adult cognition variant; cross-sectional dietary findings cannot support causal protocol claims [source_artifact:pmid-40971619].

## Protocol takeaway

Use only as older-adult cognition context; do not convert dietary or cognition associations into default protocol claims. [source_artifact:pmid-40971619]

## Key limitations and population mismatch

- Only 6 included studies [source_artifact:pmid-40971619]
- Four studies were cross-sectional dietary-intake analyses [source_artifact:pmid-40971619]
- Methodological quality was mixed [source_artifact:pmid-40971619]
- Older-adult cognition context is not a default gym protocol outcome [source_artifact:pmid-40971619]
- Population mismatch: Older-adult cognition and dietary-intake evidence is adjacent to healthy-adult creatine-monohydrate gym use. [source_artifact:pmid-40971619]
