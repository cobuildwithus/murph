This source is included for **body_weight_water_gi**.

**Findings:**
- The study assessed body composition by multi-frequency bioelectrical impedance following creatine supplementation.
- Available summaries state that one week of creatine increased total body water as detected by InBody 770 and that water changes appeared as fat-free-mass changes on BIA/DXA-style outputs.
- This is direct measurement-context evidence rather than an efficacy endpoint.

**Why it matters:**
Many users track BIA; this source helps explain why BIA-derived lean mass can change after creatine.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- biomarker / total body water
- biomarker / fat free mass estimate
- method / bioelectrical impedance
- method / dxa comparison

**Protocol takeaway:**
Use as method-context evidence: creatine can shift measurement outputs, not necessarily new muscle tissue.

**Population mismatch / boundary:**
Healthy adults, but method-specific rather than outcome efficacy.

**Limitations:**
- Participant count and exact protocol details not available in extracted record.
- BIA methods are equation- and hydration-sensitive.
- Short duration.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
