This source is included for **population_boundary_adjacent_claims**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries report vegetarians had lower baseline muscle total creatine. In creatine users, phosphocreatine, total creatine, bench-press strength, isokinetic work, type II fiber area, and whole-body lean tissue increased versus placebo. Vegetarian creatine users had greater increases in total creatine, phosphocreatine, lean tissue, and total work than nonvegetarian creatine users.
