This source is included for **timing_coingestion**.

**Findings:** [source_artifact:pmid-11591884] reported greater lean tissue mass and selected strength outcomes for whey plus creatine compared with some comparators during resistance training. Because whey and training are integral to the design, this remains adjacent-variant context.

**Why it matters:** It informs protein-shake co-use without supporting a direct timing claim.

**Potential experiment signals:** Protein co-ingestion, lean mass, strength, and training volume.

**Protocol takeaway:** Creatine with whey during training has adjacent support, but this source cannot isolate timing or meal effects of creatine monohydrate alone.

**Limitations / boundary notes:** Male training sample; protein co-supplement; no isolated creatine timing comparison; short duration.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
