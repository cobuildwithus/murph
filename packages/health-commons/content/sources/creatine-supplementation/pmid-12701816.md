This source is included for **renal_safety_labs**.

**Findings:** Available excerpts report no significant adverse effect on clinical markers and no apparent urine-panel differences versus nonusers.

**Adverse events / safety notes:** No broad clinical-marker signal in long-term athlete monitoring.

**Limitations:** Participant count not captured in extract; Athlete cohort; Observational/monitoring context

**Population mismatch:** College athletes; not clinical kidney population.

**Why it matters:** One of the most cited long-duration athlete safety cohorts for clinical chemistry panels.

**Potential experiment signals:** clinical health markers, serum creatinine, urinary markers, 15-item urine panel

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports safety-boundary context when healthy athletes use creatine over months.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
