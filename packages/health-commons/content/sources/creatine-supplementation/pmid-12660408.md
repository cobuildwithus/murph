This source is included for **strength_hypertrophy_trials**.

**Findings:** `source_artifact:pmid-12660408` — College-aged women received creatine monohydrate or placebo at 0.5 g/kg fat-free mass/day for five days. Creatine improved selected 50-repetition quadriceps measures, including time to peak torque extension and average power, while placebo did not. Body weight, body composition, fat-free mass, thigh circumference, and skinfold measures did not change; no side-effect complaints were reported.

**Why it matters:** Adds a women-only direct trial and includes explicit tolerability reporting.

**Potential experiment signals:** time to peak torque extension; average power extension; average power flexion; body weight; fat-free mass; thigh circumference; side-effect complaints.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports selected acute quadriceps performance effects in women, but not body-composition change or long-term training adaptation.

**Limitations and extraction boundary:** Short loading-only design with resistance exercise prohibited. Small college-aged women sample. Positive effects were selected repeated-contraction measures, not all maximal contraction/body-composition outcomes. Does not establish long-term training adaptation.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
