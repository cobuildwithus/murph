This source is included for **population_boundary_adjacent_claims**.

**Findings:** Review summarizes favorable effects on aging muscle and some body-composition/performance outcomes in healthy older adults, while evidence is very limited or absent for several clinical conditions such as osteosarcopenia, sarcopenic obesity, physical frailty, and cachexia.

**Why it matters:** Frames where older-adult evidence is stronger and where clinical applications are under-studied.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context for older-adult boundaries and future clinical applications, not standalone efficacy evidence.

**Murph takeaway:** Older adults with frailty or disease need clinician-supervised decisions.

**Population:** Older adults, including healthy aging and potential clinical applications.

**Intervention or exposure:** Creatine supplementation, primarily with resistance training in reviewed evidence.

**Comparator or control:** Narrative review; no single comparator.
