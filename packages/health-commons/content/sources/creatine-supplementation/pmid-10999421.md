This source is included for **renal_safety_labs**.

**Findings:** Review conclusion: monitored healthy-athlete studies did not show deleterious renal effects; case reports and theoretical concerns remain safety-boundary context.

**Adverse events / safety notes:** No renal adverse pattern was identified in monitored healthy users, but the review is not a renal-disease clearance to self-supplement.

**Limitations:** Narrative review rather than quantitative synthesis; Older literature with small cohorts; Limited applicability to people with kidney disease or nephrotoxic co-exposures

**Population mismatch:** Mostly healthy/athletic populations; not CKD-specific.

**Why it matters:** Foundational adverse-effects review from early widespread creatine use; worth retaining despite age.

**Potential experiment signals:** adverse events, serum creatinine, renal function, protein excretion, GI/body-water safety context

**Protocol takeaway:** Useful as historical safety-boundary framing, not as efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
