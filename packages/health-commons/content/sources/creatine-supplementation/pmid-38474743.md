This source is included for **renal_safety_labs**.

**Findings:** Available abstracts report increases in FFM/SMMI and intracellular water in the creatine group, while MIS did not change.

**Adverse events / safety notes:** Longer hemodialysis clinical-boundary source; not a healthy-user renal safety trial.

**Limitations:** Exploratory design; Hemodialysis population; Participant count not captured in available extract; Body-water measures may reflect dialysis-specific physiology

**Population mismatch:** Hemodialysis/CKD population mismatch for protocol claims.

**Why it matters:** Longer-duration dialysis study relevant to higher-risk exclusion language and duration boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** fat-free mass, skeletal muscle mass index, intracellular water, total body water, malnutrition-inflammation score, adverse events/tolerability

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as clinical-supervised CKD/hemodialysis context and fluid-compartment signal.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
