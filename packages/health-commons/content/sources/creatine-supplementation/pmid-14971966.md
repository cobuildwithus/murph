This source is included for **renal_safety_labs**.

**Findings:** Available excerpts indicate 30-day washout restored plasma and urine creatine markers but not muscle phosphocreatine fully to baseline.

**Adverse events / safety notes:** Useful for interpreting urinary/plasma creatine after repeated loading; not kidney injury evidence.

**Limitations:** Single-person case; No control group; Washout/metabolism rather than safety outcome

**Population mismatch:** Single healthy trained male; not population safety evidence.

**Why it matters:** Direct washout evidence showing how muscle, plasma, and urine markers can recover on different timelines.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle creatine, plasma creatine, urinary creatine, washout timeline

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports washout and lab-interpretation caveats for self-experiments.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
