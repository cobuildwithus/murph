This source is included for **dose_loading_maintenance**.

**Findings:** Accessible PubMed/PMC snippets conclude 5-day creatine supplementation had no impact on upper-body muscle anaerobic power output in consecutive upper-body intermittent sprint tests; heart-rate responses tended to be higher in the creatine group in one accessible snippet. Safety/adverse-event extraction: No clear adverse-event extraction; higher heart-rate response was reported as a physiological observation, not necessarily an adverse event. Limitations: Sport-specific trained wrestlers.; Participant count not recovered in accessible extraction material.; Null upper-body protocol may not generalize to lower-body or resistance-training outcomes.

**Why it matters:** This is an open-access modern null result important for balance.

**Potential experiment signals:** upper-body sprint power, competition-day repeat tests, heart rate, training status.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as direct null evidence for wrestlers/upper-body intermittent sprint testing.
