This source is included for **population_boundary_adjacent_claims**.

**Findings:** Systematic review/meta-analysis reported a very small pooled regional hypertrophy effect favoring creatine (standardized mean estimate 0.11; 95% credible interval -0.02 to 0.25), with small muscle-thickness effects around 0.10 to 0.16 cm.

**Why it matters:** Direct hypertrophy synthesis that tempers broad lean-mass claims with imaging data.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- regional muscle hypertrophy
- muscle thickness
- upper/lower body regional measures

**Protocol takeaway:** Creatine may add only a small regional hypertrophy benefit during resistance training.

**Murph takeaway:** Expect modest circumference/thickness changes; strength and body weight may be more visible.

**Population:** Healthy adults in ≥6-week resistance-training trials with regional hypertrophy measured by direct imaging.

**Intervention or exposure:** Creatine supplementation combined with resistance training.

**Comparator or control:** Placebo/control plus resistance training.

**Duration or follow-up:** At least 6 weeks in included studies.

**Endpoints:**
- regional muscle hypertrophy
- muscle thickness
- upper/lower body regional measures

**Effect estimate or direction:** Systematic review/meta-analysis reported a very small pooled regional hypertrophy effect favoring creatine (standardized mean estimate 0.11; 95% credible interval -0.02 to 0.25), with small muscle-thickness effects around 0.10 to 0.16 cm.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** Safety was not the main extraction focus for this hypertrophy synthesis.

**Limitations:**
- The pooled imaging effect was small and compatible with no clear advantage.
- Participant count was not recovered from accessible extraction.

**Population mismatch:** Direct adult RT evidence, but imaging endpoints are narrower than common Murph body-composition signals.
