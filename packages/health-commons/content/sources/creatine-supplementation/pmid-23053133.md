This source is included for **population_boundary_adjacent_claims**.

**Findings:** Small RCT found creatine increased training volume, 1RM bench press, knee extension, biceps curl, fat-free mass, and muscle mass versus placebo. Body mass and body-fat percentage did not significantly change in extracted results.

**Why it matters:** Direct women-specific 5 g/day RT trial.

**Protocol takeaway:** 5 g/day creatine may improve strength/training volume and FFM in older women doing RT.

**Murph takeaway:** Track training volume and multiple lifts; scale weight may miss change.

**Population:** Healthy older women, mean age about 65 years.

**Intervention or exposure:** 5 g/day creatine monohydrate during resistance training.

**Comparator or control:** Maltodextrin placebo during the same RT program.

**Duration or follow-up:** 12 weeks of RT, 3 days/week.

**Endpoints:**
- training volume
- 1RM bench press
- knee extension
- biceps curl
- fat-free mass
- muscle mass
- submaximal functional tests
- body mass
- body-fat percentage

**Effect estimate or direction:** Small RCT found creatine increased training volume, 1RM bench press, knee extension, biceps curl, fat-free mass, and muscle mass versus placebo. Body mass and body-fat percentage did not significantly change in extracted results.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event details were recovered in the accessible batch extraction.

**Limitations:**
- Very small: 9 participants/group.
- Older women only.

**Population mismatch:** Older-women supervised RT evidence.
