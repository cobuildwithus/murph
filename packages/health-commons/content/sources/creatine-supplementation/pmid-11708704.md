This source is included for **repeated_sprint_power_trials**.

**Findings:** Accessible abstract snippet reported no differences in mean or peak power as a consequence of creatine ingestion.

**Population and intervention:** Healthy resistance-trained adults; Creatine supplementation. Comparator: Placebo/control condition. Duration/follow-up: Creatine supplementation before repeated upper- and lower-body Wingate testing; exact duration not extracted.

**Endpoints:** upper-body Wingate peak power, upper-body Wingate mean power, lower-body Wingate peak power, lower-body Wingate mean power.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event extraction was available from the accessible record for this batch. 

**Limitations:** Sample size and dosing details not extracted. Wingate lab outputs may not reflect all sport tasks.

**Population mismatch:** Resistance-trained adults in lab cycling/arm-crank test context.

**Why it matters:** Direct Wingate evidence prevents overclaiming power benefits.

**Potential experiment signals:** upper-body Wingate peak power, upper-body Wingate mean power, lower-body Wingate peak power, lower-body Wingate mean power.

**Protocol takeaway:** Creatine did not clearly improve repeated Wingate mean or peak power in the accessible summary.

**Murph takeaway:** Track mean power and peak power separately; effects may differ.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`. Directness: `direct_protocol`. Result boundary: `no_clear_advantage`.
