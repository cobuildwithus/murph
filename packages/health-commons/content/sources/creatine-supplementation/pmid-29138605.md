This source is included for **population_boundary_adjacent_claims**.

**Findings:** Meta-analysis of 22 studies and 721 participants found creatine plus resistance training increased lean tissue mass by MD 1.37 kg (95% CI 0.97 to 1.76), chest-press strength by SMD 0.35 (95% CI 0.16 to 0.53), and leg-press strength by SMD 0.24 (95% CI 0.05 to 0.43).

**Why it matters:** Backbone older-adult synthesis for lean mass and strength outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- lean tissue mass
- chest press strength
- leg press strength

**Protocol takeaway:** For older adults doing resistance training, creatine has synthesis-level support for lean-tissue and strength gains.

**Murph takeaway:** Track lean mass/body weight plus strength during a training block.

**Population:** Older adults in resistance-training trials, with mean ages across studies roughly 57 to 70 years.

**Intervention or exposure:** Creatine supplementation during resistance training.

**Comparator or control:** Placebo or resistance training without creatine.

**Duration or follow-up:** 7 to 52 weeks across included trials.

**Endpoints:**
- lean tissue mass
- chest press strength
- leg press strength

**Effect estimate or direction:** Meta-analysis of 22 studies and 721 participants found creatine plus resistance training increased lean tissue mass by MD 1.37 kg (95% CI 0.97 to 1.76), chest-press strength by SMD 0.35 (95% CI 0.16 to 0.53), and leg-press strength by SMD 0.24 (95% CI 0.05 to 0.43).

**Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event details were recovered in the accessible batch extraction.

**Limitations:**
- Older-adult RT synthesis; do not generalize effect size to all adults.
- Dose, duration, and training programs varied.

**Population mismatch:** Older-adult resistance-training evidence, not adult-general creatine-only evidence.
