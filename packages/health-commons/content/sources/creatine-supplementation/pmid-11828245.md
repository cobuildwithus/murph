**Findings:** Accessible records identify a broad direct trial of creatine on muscle power, endurance, and sprint performance; numeric effects were not extracted in this batch.

**Population and intervention:** Healthy trained men; Creatine supplementation. Comparator: Placebo/control condition. Duration/follow-up: Supplementation before power, endurance, and sprint testing; exact duration not extracted.

**Endpoints:** muscle power, sprint performance, muscular endurance.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event extraction was available from the accessible record for this batch. 

**Limitations:** Sample size and numeric effects not extracted. Multiple endpoint domains require endpoint-specific interpretation.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy trained men, not all users.

**Why it matters:** Covers multiple Murph-relevant outcome domains in one direct creatine study.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle power, sprint performance, muscular endurance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Direct performance source, but endpoint-specific details remain to be extracted.

**Murph takeaway:** Track power, sprint, and endurance separately to avoid conflating effects.
