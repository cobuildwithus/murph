This source is included for **repeated_sprint_power_trials**.

**Findings:** Accessible records describe performance and muscle-fiber adaptations during heavy resistance training; numeric endpoint effects were not extracted in this batch.

**Population and intervention:** Healthy resistance-trained men; Creatine supplementation during heavy resistance training. Comparator: Placebo during the same resistance-training program. Duration/follow-up: 12 weeks of periodized heavy resistance training.

**Endpoints:** maximal strength, training performance, muscle fiber adaptations, lean mass/body mass.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event extraction was available from the accessible record for this batch. 

**Limitations:** Exact endpoint estimates were not extracted from accessible records here. Male resistance-trained population limits generalization.

**Population mismatch:** Resistance-trained men; not a general sedentary or female sample.

**Why it matters:** One of the key direct heavy-resistance-training trials in the creatine literature.

**Potential experiment signals:** maximal strength, training performance, muscle fiber adaptations, lean mass/body mass.

**Protocol takeaway:** Direct training study relevant to performance adaptations, with detailed effects requiring full-text extraction.

**Murph takeaway:** Track strength progress, training volume, body mass, and lean-mass proxies.
