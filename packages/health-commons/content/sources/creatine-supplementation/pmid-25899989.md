This source is included for **renal_safety_labs**.

**Findings:** Available abstract reports creatine was well tolerated and free of adverse effects, but did not improve muscle function or other measured parameters.

**Adverse events / safety notes:** Clinical pediatric disease-population tolerability boundary only.

**Limitations:** Pediatric inflammatory disease population; Small sample not captured; Crossover details need full-text confirmation

**Population mismatch:** Juvenile dermatomyositis is not the target adult self-experiment population.

**Why it matters:** Pediatric inflammatory-disease boundary study; useful for population mismatch and exclusion notes.

**Potential experiment signals:** adverse events, renal safety/tolerability, muscle function, intramuscular phosphocreatine

**Protocol takeaway:** Retain as clinical safety/tolerability boundary, not protocol efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
