This source is included for **dose_loading_maintenance**.

**Findings:** Accessible abstract text reports significant increases in muscle creatine, body mass, and total body water in the creatine group without altered fluid distribution. Safety/adverse-event extraction: No specific adverse events were extracted; fluid-distribution findings did not show altered distribution in the abstract. Limitations: Participant count was not recovered in accessible extraction material.; Physiology/body-water study, not an efficacy trial.; Short maintenance period.

**Why it matters:** This is a direct body-water source for things-to-watch during loading/maintenance.

**Potential experiment signals:** scale weight, bloating/water retention, hydration perception, training performance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as direct evidence that body mass and total body water can rise without a change in fluid distribution.
