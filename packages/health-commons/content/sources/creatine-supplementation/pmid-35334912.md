This source is included for **strength_hypertrophy_synthesis**.

**Findings:**
- From `source_artifact:pmid-35334912`: the scoping review maps randomized clinical trials on creatine supplementation and muscle growth from 2012 to 2021.
- The review's conclusion supports creatine as efficient for muscle growth in healthy young people with adequate training across varied doses and activities.
- It also identifies the need for larger, longer, better-designed RCTs in older adults and muscle disease-related populations.
- Because it is a scoping review, it should not be treated as a pooled effect-size source.

**Why it matters:** It helps downstream synthesis distinguish evidence-rich young/training contexts from less certain older or clinical populations.

**Murph takeaway:** Expectations for muscle gain should depend on training and population; more evidence is needed outside healthy young trained groups.
