This source is included for **repeated_sprint_power_trials**.

**Findings:** Accessible abstract summaries reported that four days of creatine supplementation did not influence multiple sprint performance, plasma lactate, blood pH, or excess base.

**Population and intervention:** Recreationally active males; Oral creatine monohydrate supplementation. Comparator: Placebo/control condition. Duration/follow-up: 16-day experiment with short-term oral creatine monohydrate supplementation.

**Endpoints:** repeated 10-second cycle ergometer sprints, multiple sprint cycle performance, plasma lactate, blood pH.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event extraction was available from the accessible record for this batch. 

**Limitations:** Older article with limited accessible details. Short-term dose/loading details were not fully extracted. Recreational male sample only.

**Population mismatch:** Recreationally active males in lab cycling protocol.

**Why it matters:** Balances positive repeated-sprint evidence with a direct early null trial.

**Potential experiment signals:** repeated 10-second cycle ergometer sprints, multiple sprint cycle performance, plasma lactate, blood pH.

**Protocol takeaway:** Short-term creatine did not improve multiple cycle sprints in this accessible summary.

**Murph takeaway:** Track dose duration and repeated-sprint protocol because short loading may not help all users.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`. Directness: `direct_protocol`. Result boundary: `no_clear_advantage`.
