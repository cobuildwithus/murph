This source is included for **dose_loading_maintenance**.

**Findings:** Accessible extracts report increased muscle phosphocreatine after loading, improved muscle performance during resistance training, and return of muscle phosphocreatine toward normal within about 4 weeks after cessation. Safety/adverse-event extraction: Adverse events were not extracted from the accessible abstract/indexing material. Limitations: Small sample of young women.; Exercise training co-intervention makes it a training-plus-creatine trial rather than creatine alone.; Detailed adverse-event extraction was not available.

**Why it matters:** This is a key women-specific longer-term trial with loading/maintenance and washout relevance.

**Potential experiment signals:** strength progression, training volume, body weight, effects after stopping.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as direct outcome evidence for resistance-training contexts and as a washout reminder.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`. Directness: `direct_protocol`. Rights status: `paywalled`.
