This source is included for **dose_loading_maintenance**.

**Findings:** Accessible abstracts conclude creatine during resistance training had a small beneficial effect on regional muscle thickness; an indexed extract reports larger leg-press strength increases in men on creatine than women on creatine, but full subgroup context was not recovered. Safety/adverse-event extraction: Adverse events were not extracted from accessible abstract/indexing material. Limitations: Participant count and full dosing schedule not recovered in accessible extraction material.; Resistance-training co-intervention.; Frequency/timing findings should not be generalized to all users without full-text extraction.

**Why it matters:** This is a direct frequency/timing source for maintenance-design questions.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle thickness, strength progression, adherence by dosing frequency.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as contextual direct evidence that frequency around training may matter, but not as a replacement for daily protocol guidance.
