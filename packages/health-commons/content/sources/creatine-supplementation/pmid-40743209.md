This source is included for **renal_safety_labs**.

**Findings:** Open-label pre/post study found improved SPPB and muscle/body-composition measures; extracellular water and dry weight remained stable, serum creatinine rose, and authors reported no adverse effects requiring discontinuation.

**Adverse events / safety notes:** Clinical dialysis tolerability and fluid-compartment context only.

**Limitations:** Open-label single-arm design; Small convenience sample; No control group; Diet and activity confounding

**Population mismatch:** Hemodialysis patients; not direct protocol evidence for healthy users.

**Why it matters:** Newest dialysis trial found in this shard; useful for keeping special-population evidence current.

**Potential experiment signals:** SPPB, handgrip strength, fat-free mass, skeletal muscle mass, total/intracellular/extracellular water, serum creatinine, adverse effects

**Protocol takeaway:** Retain as disease-boundary/tolerability context, especially for fluid-compartment interpretation.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
