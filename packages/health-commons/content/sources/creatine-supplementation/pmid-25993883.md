This source is included for **population_boundary_adjacent_claims**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries indicate creatine, independent of timing, increased leg-press and chest-press strength more than placebo. Post-exercise creatine was described as augmenting lean tissue mass versus RT alone; before- and after-session creatine produced similar overall results.

**Why it matters:** Practical timing/dosing source in healthy older adults.

**Comparator or control:** Placebo/control supplementation during RT.

**Duration or follow-up:** Training period; exact duration not fully recovered.
