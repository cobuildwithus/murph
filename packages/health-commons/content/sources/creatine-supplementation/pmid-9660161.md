This source is included for **body_weight_water_gi**.

**Findings:**
- Six well-trained subjects were studied before and after losing about 3.0-4.3% of body mass in available summaries.
- PubMed summaries state creatine versus placebo during rapid body-mass reduction may help maintain muscle peak torque and work capacity measures.
- Because this is a rapid weight-loss context, the source is adjacent and context-only.

**Why it matters:**
Weight-class athletes may encounter creatine/water-weight tradeoffs; this study is relevant but not generalizable.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- biomarker / body mass reduction
- outcome / isokinetic peak torque
- outcome / muscle work capacity
- metabolism / exercise metabolism

**Protocol takeaway:**
Use only as adjacent context for athletes manipulating body mass, not default protocol support.

**Population mismatch / boundary:**
Weight-cutting/well-trained subjects; adjacent variant.

**Limitations:**
- Extremely small sample.
- Rapid body-mass reduction is not normal supplementation.
- Mixed performance/metabolism endpoints not directly relevant to default protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
