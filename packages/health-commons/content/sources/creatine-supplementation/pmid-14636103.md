This source is included for **body_weight_water_gi**.

**Findings:**
- Seventeen active males were paired and assigned to creatine or placebo in a double-blind design.
- Creatine supplementation increased body weight from 90.42 ± 14.74 to 92.12 ± 15.19 kg and body water from 53.77 ± 1.75 to 57.15 ± 2.01 L in available summaries.
- Percent body fat and daily caloric intake were not significantly changed in the creatine group; no significant changes were noted for placebo.

**Why it matters:**
This is a direct monohydrate source focused on body weight and body-fat estimates.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- biomarker / body weight
- biomarker / body water
- biomarker / percent body fat
- behavior / dietary caloric intake

**Protocol takeaway:**
Supports the protocol caveat that early scale weight can reflect water-related mass rather than fat gain.

**Population mismatch / boundary:**
Active male strength-training context; not general adult sample.

**Limitations:**
- Small sample of active males.
- High-dose protocol differs from common 3-5 g/day maintenance dosing.
- Body water estimated by BIA, which is sensitive to equations and hydration state.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
