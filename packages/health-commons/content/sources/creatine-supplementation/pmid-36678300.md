This source is included for **body_weight_water_gi**.

**Findings:**
- Thirty moderately active females, either naturally menstruating or using hormonal contraceptives, were randomized to creatine or placebo/control.
- The trial measured body mass, total body water, extracellular fluid, and intracellular fluid across menstrual-cycle phases.
- Available summaries indicate fluid compartments changed in a phase-dependent way, with no simple uniform body-mass interpretation.

**Why it matters:**
Direct women-specific RCT evidence for fluid distribution is important because creatine water/weight claims often come from male athlete studies.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- biomarker / body mass
- biomarker / total body water
- biomarker / extracellular fluid
- biomarker / intracellular fluid
- population / menstrual cycle phase

**Protocol takeaway:**
Supports adding sex/menstrual-cycle nuance to water-retention interpretation.

**Population mismatch / boundary:**
Moderately active women; direct but specific to menstrual/hormonal context.

**Limitations:**
- Small sample and cycle-phase stratification.
- Short loading protocol.
- Detailed effect estimates were not fully available in the extracted record.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
