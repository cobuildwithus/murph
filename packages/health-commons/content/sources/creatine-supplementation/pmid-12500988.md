This source is included for **renal_safety_labs**.

**Findings:** Available excerpts report no long-term detrimental effects on kidney or liver function in highly trained college athletes.

**Adverse events / safety notes:** No clinical renal or hepatic lab injury signal in this athletic cohort.

**Limitations:** Non-randomized voluntary supplement use; Small cohort; Athletes under sport-specific training conditions

**Population mismatch:** Highly trained college football players; not general adults or kidney disease.

**Why it matters:** Longer-duration athlete study with explicit kidney and liver lab monitoring.

**Potential experiment signals:** kidney-function labs, liver-function labs, serum creatinine, adverse events

**Protocol takeaway:** Adds athlete-safety context for long-term use, not efficacy.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
