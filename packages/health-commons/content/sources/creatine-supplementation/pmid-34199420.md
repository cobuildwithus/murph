This source is included for **population_boundary_adjacent_claims**.

**Findings:** Meta-analysis found creatine plus RT augmented lean tissue mass and strength in older adults. Creatine only on RT days increased lean tissue mass and strength versus placebo. Loading plus lower dose increased chest press, and >5 g/day with or without loading was significant for leg press. When loading studies were excluded, no greater effect on chest/leg press versus placebo was extracted.
