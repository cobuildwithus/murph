This source is included for **timing_coingestion**.

**Findings:** [source_artifact:pmid-34610729] found no clear difference between pre- and post-training creatine timing for unilateral hypertrophy or strength outcomes in young adults. This is direct protocol evidence supporting no-clear-advantage timing language.

**Why it matters:** It directly balances the earlier post-workout-favorable Antonio/Ciccone signal.

**Potential experiment signals:** Timing adherence, muscle thickness, exercise-specific strength, and training volume.

**Protocol takeaway:** Creatine timing around unilateral resistance training did not show a clear pre/post advantage in this small study.

**Limitations / boundary notes:** N=10; within-subject unilateral design; training-days-only supplementation; short duration and limited population diversity.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
