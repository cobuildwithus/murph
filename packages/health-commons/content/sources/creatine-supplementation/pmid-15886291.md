This source is included for **renal_safety_labs**.

**Findings:** Review concluded creatine minimally impacts creatinine and renal function in young healthy adults; creatinine can rise slightly, especially with longer use, but evidence in elderly or renal insufficiency was limited.

**Adverse events / safety notes:** Supports lab-interpretation caution: serum creatinine may not equal kidney injury in creatine users.

**Limitations:** Narrative review; Evidence mostly young healthy adults; Limited data in elderly or renal insufficiency

**Population mismatch:** Not a CKD or older-adult evidence base.

**Why it matters:** Focused renal-safety review that predates later meta-analyses and still surfaces early case and trial literature.

**Potential experiment signals:** serum creatinine, creatinine clearance estimates, kidney function tests, adverse events

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports explaining why creatinine-based eGFR can be misleading during supplementation.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
