This source is included for **background_guidelines_external**.

**Findings:**
- From `source_artifact:pmid-12793840`: the review describes creatine pharmacokinetics as nonlinear with respect to dose size and dosing frequency.
- From `source_artifact:pmid-12793840`: skeletal muscle is described as the largest depot, but storage is finite; distribution and clearance depend on transport, renal elimination, and degradation to creatinine.
- The authors emphasize that available pharmacokinetic data were limited, especially for multiple-dose behavior and the plasma-to-muscle relationship.

**Why it matters:** It explains dose-response limits, clearance, and why plasma creatine is not the same as stored muscle creatine.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- body weight
- muscle creatine where measured
- plasma creatine
- creatinine
- GI tolerance

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only: use for pharmacokinetic rationale and caution against assuming linear dose escalation.

**Murph takeaway:** Creatine retention depends on absorption, transport, tissue storage, and clearance; bigger doses are not automatically better.
