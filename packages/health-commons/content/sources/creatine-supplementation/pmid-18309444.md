This source is included for **population_boundary_adjacent_claims**.

**Findings:** Body-composition article compares creatine and/or whey arms versus placebo in older men. This extraction did not recover a clear creatine-only body-composition effect estimate; related summaries from the same intervention context describe no additional strength benefit from creatine and/or whey beyond RT, so it is kept as adjacent co-supplement mixed context.

**Why it matters:** Flags whey/protein co-supplement confounding.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- total body composition
- regional body composition
- lean body mass
- co-supplementation

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not cite creatine+whey data as creatine-only evidence without arm-specific extraction.

**Murph takeaway:** Record other supplements, especially protein/whey.

**Population:** Men aged 48 to 72 years during resistance training.

**Intervention or exposure:** Creatine, whey protein, or creatine plus whey after training sessions during RT.

**Comparator or control:** Placebo beverage during same RT program.

**Duration or follow-up:** 14 weeks of RT, 3 days/week.

**Endpoints:**
- total body composition
- regional body composition
- lean body mass
- co-supplementation

**Effect estimate or direction:** Body-composition article compares creatine and/or whey arms versus placebo in older men. This extraction did not recover a clear creatine-only body-composition effect estimate; related summaries from the same intervention context describe no additional strength benefit from creatine and/or whey beyond RT, so it is kept as adjacent co-supplement mixed context.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event details were recovered in the accessible batch extraction.

**Limitations:**
- Co-supplement arms complicate creatine-only inference.
- Exact creatine-only body-composition effects not recovered.
