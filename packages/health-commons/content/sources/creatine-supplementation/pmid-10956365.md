This source is included for **timing_coingestion**.

**Findings:** [source_artifact:pmid-10956365] reported that 50 g protein plus 47 g carbohydrate with creatine increased insulin and whole-body creatine retention comparably to a much larger carbohydrate condition in healthy men. This is a direct co-ingestion mechanism finding.

**Why it matters:** It informs practical claims about taking creatine with a meal or protein/carbohydrate feeding.

**Potential experiment signals:** Meal timing, protein/carbohydrate co-ingestion, GI tolerance, and adherence are practical signals.

**Protocol takeaway:** Creatine with protein plus carbohydrate can increase retention acutely; this can justify with-meal dosing but not mandatory high-calorie co-ingestion.

**Limitations / boundary notes:** Small acute male sample; whole-body retention rather than muscle biopsy or long-term training outcomes; high macronutrient doses may not fit all users.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
