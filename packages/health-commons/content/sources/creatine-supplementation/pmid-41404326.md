**Findings:**
- The review addresses common safety concerns regarding creatine ingestion.
- Available summaries state that gastrointestinal upset can occur, particularly with high single doses greater than 10 g or with other supplements such as caffeine.
- The review concludes that creatine monohydrate is generally safe when used appropriately, while further research is needed in specific populations.

**Why it matters:**
