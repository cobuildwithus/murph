This source is included for **population_boundary_adjacent_claims**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries indicate both pre- and post-training creatine timing groups increased lean tissue mass, limb muscle thickness, and upper/lower body strength. No clear timing superiority was recovered.

**Why it matters:** Direct timing question source.

**Potential experiment signals:**

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not claim a strong before-versus-after timing rule from this study.

**Murph takeaway:** Consistency likely matters more than exact pre/post timing for most users.

**Population:** Healthy older adults undertaking supervised resistance training.

**Intervention or exposure:** Creatine immediately before resistance-training sessions.

**Comparator or control:** Creatine immediately after resistance-training sessions.

**Duration or follow-up:** 12 weeks.

**Endpoints:**
- whole-body lean tissue mass
- limb muscle thickness
- upper-body strength
- lower-body strength
- timing comparison

**Effect estimate or direction:** Accessible summaries indicate both pre- and post-training creatine timing groups increased lean tissue mass, limb muscle thickness, and upper/lower body strength. No clear timing superiority was recovered.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event details were recovered in the accessible batch extraction.

**Limitations:**
- Participant count and full effect estimates not recovered.
- Timing comparison rather than placebo efficacy contrast.

**Population mismatch:** Older-adult supervised RT timing study.
