This source is included for **repeated_sprint_power_trials**.

**Findings:** Accessible records identify an underpowered elite volleyball jump-performance RCT; endpoint direction/significance was not extracted.

**Population and intervention:** Elite male university volleyball players; Creatine supplementation. Comparator: Placebo. Duration/follow-up: 4 weeks of supplementation.

**Endpoints:** jumping performance, repeated block jumps, fatigue during jumps.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event extraction was available from the accessible record for this batch.

**Limitations:** Only 8 athletes analyzed after injuries/outliers. Exact effects not extracted. Jump sport endpoint is adjacent to sprint/power protocol.

**Population mismatch:** Elite male volleyball athletes; not general users.

**Why it matters:** Jump performance is a practical power signal but this source is small.

**Potential experiment signals:** jumping performance, repeated block jumps, fatigue during jumps.

**Protocol takeaway:** Contextual jump-performance source with high uncertainty.

**Murph takeaway:** Use jump metrics, but avoid overinterpreting small samples.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. Directness: `adjacent_variant`. Result boundary: `mixed`.
