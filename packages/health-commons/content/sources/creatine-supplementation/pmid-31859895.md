This source is included for **renal_safety_labs**.

**Findings:** Available abstracts emphasize that creatine can transiently raise serum creatinine and mimic kidney disease, especially when interpreted without context.

**Adverse events / safety notes:** Supports lab-interpretation caveat and kidney-disease caution.

**Limitations:** Narrative review; Spanish-language clinical context; No pooled effect sizes

**Population mismatch:** Clinical review; not direct trial evidence.

**Why it matters:** Nephrology-facing review that is especially relevant to lab interpretation and false-positive renal concern.

**Potential experiment signals:** serum creatinine, creatinine-based eGFR, lab interpretation, renal disease caveats

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports recommending clinicians interpret creatinine/eGFR in light of supplementation and consider alternative markers.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
