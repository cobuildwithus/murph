This source is included for **dose_loading_maintenance**.

**Findings:** Open article text reports resting muscle total creatine increased 24.5 ± 10.0% and creatine phosphate 38.4 ± 23.9% in the creatine group; oxygen consumption near the end of submaximal exercise was reduced and plasma-volume response differed, but final sprint performance was not improved versus placebo. Safety/adverse-event extraction: Adverse events were not emphasized in the extracted article text; performance null and plasma-volume changes should be treated as physiology outcomes, not broad safety claims. Limitations: Small male cyclist sample.; Endurance-trained cycling simulation may not generalize to strength or team-sport users.; No classic loading phase; findings apply to 3 g/day for 28 days.

**Why it matters:** This is one of the clearest open-access no-load uptake/performance boundary trials.

**Potential experiment signals:** body weight, sprint after endurance, perceived exertion, steady-state oxygen cost.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as direct evidence that 3 g/day can raise muscle stores over 28 days, while performance effects can remain null in endurance simulations.
