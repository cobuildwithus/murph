**Findings:**
- The source is a formal risk assessment for creatine monohydrate.
- Available secondary summaries indicate common intakes such as 3 g/day were considered unlikely to pose risk when purity is adequate, with some longer adult athlete studies using higher intakes without harmful effects.
- Use cautiously because the extracted source record did not expose the full risk-assessment derivation.
