This source is included for **renal_safety_labs**.

**Findings:** Available excerpts report no negative effects on blood or urinary clinical markers; CK increased and some non-renal analytes shifted.

**Adverse events / safety notes:** No urinary/kidney-marker harm signal over 8 weeks in football players.

**Limitations:** Small sample; Athlete-only population; Training context may influence CK and other markers

**Population mismatch:** Athletes under sport training; not general or CKD population.

**Why it matters:** Football-player trial focused on blood and urinary clinical health markers, including renal labs.

**Potential experiment signals:** blood clinical markers, urinary markers, kidney-function labs, CK, glucose, uric acid

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports lab-monitoring context for athletes using loading plus maintenance.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
