This source is included for **renal_safety_labs**.

**Findings:** Review included 15 studies qualitatively and 6 quantitatively; authors concluded available evidence did not support renal damage in studied amounts/durations. Some summary-statistic details require full-text verification because public snippets are inconsistent.

**Adverse events / safety notes:** Anchor synthesis before newer 2025 meta-analysis; supports cautious no-damage conclusion within studied populations.

**Limitations:** Heterogeneous studies; Many healthy/athlete samples; Public snippet inconsistencies on effect estimates; exact statistics should be verified before protocol prose

**Population mismatch:** Included populations mostly do not match CKD or high-risk users.

**Why it matters:** Earlier dedicated renal-function synthesis that anchors the trial map before the newer 2025 update.

**Potential experiment signals:** serum creatinine, plasma urea, creatinine clearance/eGFR context, renal adverse events

**Protocol takeaway:** Useful synthesis anchor, but protocol prose should avoid overclaiming due to heterogeneity.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
