This source is included for **dose_loading_maintenance**.

**Findings:** Accessible abstracts report that loading-related body-mass gain was maintained after 6 weeks and accounted for by fat-free mass; prolonged supplementation did not increase muscle or whole-body oxidative capacity or influence substrate utilization/endurance cycling performance. Safety/adverse-event extraction: Adverse events were not extracted from accessible abstract/indexing material. Limitations: Participant count and detailed dose schedule were not recovered in accessible extraction material.; Mixed endpoints: body composition changed while endurance/fuel-selection outcomes did not.; Endurance-cycling context may not generalize to strength/power users.

**Why it matters:** This directly informs maintenance, body composition, and endurance boundary claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** scale weight, fat-free mass, time trial, substrate-use proxy, endurance output.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mixed direct evidence: expect possible mass/fat-free-mass changes, but not necessarily endurance fuel-selection or cycling benefits.
