This source is included for **population_boundary_adjacent_claims**.

**Findings:** Creatine increased body mass by 1.56 ± 0.57 kg, FFM by 1.15 ± 0.94 kg, muscle creatine by 18.8 ± 13.1 mmol/kg, and total creatine by 30.8 ± 21.2 mmol/kg; placebo did not change muscle creatine markers. Phosphocreatine did not change, and repeated-sprint peak/mean power did not improve.

**Why it matters:** Shows tissue/body changes can occur without immediate sprint performance gain.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- body mass
- fat-free mass
- muscle creatine
- total creatine
- phosphocreatine
- repeated sprint peak power
- repeated sprint mean power

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not equate increased muscle creatine or FFM with immediate sprint-performance improvement.

**Murph takeaway:** Dietary subgroup users may see weight/FFM changes quickly; performance still needs direct testing.

**Population:** Healthy young adult vegans and vegetarians.

**Intervention or exposure:** Creatine monohydrate 0.3 g/kg/day divided into four daily doses.

**Comparator or control:** Maltodextrin placebo 0.3 g/kg/day.

**Duration or follow-up:** 7 days.

**Endpoints:**
- body mass
- fat-free mass
- muscle creatine
- total creatine
- phosphocreatine
- repeated sprint peak power
- repeated sprint mean power

**Effect estimate or direction:** Creatine increased body mass by 1.56 ± 0.57 kg, FFM by 1.15 ± 0.94 kg, muscle creatine by 18.8 ± 13.1 mmol/kg, and total creatine by 30.8 ± 21.2 mmol/kg; placebo did not change muscle creatine markers. Phosphocreatine did not change, and repeated-sprint peak/mean power did not improve.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event details were recovered in the accessible batch extraction.

**Limitations:**
- Very small diet-defined subgroup.
- Short 7-day loading-style protocol.
- Sprint endpoints were null despite tissue/body changes.

**Population mismatch:** Young adult vegans/vegetarians; adjacent subgroup evidence.
