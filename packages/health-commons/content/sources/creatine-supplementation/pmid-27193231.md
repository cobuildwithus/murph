This source is included for **timing_coingestion**.

**Findings:** [source_artifact:pmid-27193231] reported that creatine ingestion augmented dietary carbohydrate-mediated muscle glycogen supercompensation during the first 24 h of recovery after prolonged exhaustive exercise. The same source reported no apparent treatment differences in insulin sensitivity, GLUT4 mRNA, osmotic stress markers, or muscle water content responses, so the mechanism remained uncertain.

**Why it matters:** It is useful direct evidence for carbohydrate/recovery context and for mechanistic uncertainty.

**Potential experiment signals:** Hard-exercise recovery context, carbohydrate intake, body mass/water, and next-session performance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Creatine with carbohydrate may be most relevant when the user's goal is glycogen recovery after exhaustive exercise, not necessarily routine daily timing.

**Limitations / boundary notes:** Recovery physiology context; recreationally active sample; biopsy/lab endpoints; not a long-term resistance-training timing trial.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
