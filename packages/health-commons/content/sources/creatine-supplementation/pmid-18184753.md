This source is included for **safety_tolerability_adverse_events**.

**Findings:**
- The review addresses the claim that creatine supplementation leads to muscle cramps and dehydration.
- Available abstracts state that little evidence exists that creatine supplementation in heat presents additional risk.
- The review notes that some reports suggest creatine may support plasma volume or thermoregulation in hot/humid exercise settings.

**Why it matters:**
Cramping/dehydration myths often affect adherence and warning language.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- symptom / muscle cramps
- safety / dehydration
