This source is included for **population_boundary_adjacent_claims**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** 12-month randomized placebo-controlled trial during resistance training (2015).
- **People studied or addressed:** Postmenopausal women participating in supervised resistance training.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Creatine approximately 0.1 g/kg/day during resistance training.
- **Comparator/control:** Placebo during resistance training.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 12 months.
- **Role in Murph:** context-only / adjacent_variant.

## Findings

**Findings:** In postmenopausal women randomized to creatine or placebo during resistance training, the creatine group was reported to attenuate femoral-neck BMD loss and increase femoral-shaft subperiosteal width; 47 women were randomized and 33 were analyzed at 12 months [source_artifact:pmid-25386713].
