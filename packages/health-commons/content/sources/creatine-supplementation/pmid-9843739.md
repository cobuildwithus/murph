This source is included for **timing_coingestion**.

**Findings:** [source_artifact:pmid-9843739] used insulin-clamp methods in seven men and found enhanced creatine accumulation only at sufficiently high insulin exposure. This supports an insulin-mediated uptake mechanism but is not a practical dosing protocol.

**Why it matters:** It explains the mechanistic rationale for carbohydrate/protein co-ingestion studies.

**Potential experiment signals:** Meal timing, glucose/insulin context if available, and tolerance rather than direct insulin targeting.

**Protocol takeaway:** Insulin can influence uptake in controlled physiology, but regular creatine use should not require extreme insulin-stimulating strategies.

**Limitations / boundary notes:** N=7; artificial infusion model; no training outcomes; high insulin exposure may not map to ordinary meals.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
