This source is included for **repeated_sprint_power_trials**.

**Findings:** Creatine monohydrate did not augment fitness, performance, or body-composition adaptations to 4 weeks of HIIT in the accessible record/title.

**Population and intervention:** Young females; Creatine monohydrate supplementation during HIIT. Comparator: Placebo during HIIT. Duration/follow-up: 4 weeks of high-intensity interval training.

**Endpoints:** fitness, performance, body composition.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event extraction was available from the accessible record for this batch. 

**Limitations:** Adjacent training-adaptation design rather than isolated repeated sprint test. Small sample.

**Population mismatch:** Young females in HIIT training program.

**Why it matters:** Important female null/context evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** fitness, performance, body composition.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not assume creatine improves every interval-training adaptation.

**Murph takeaway:** Track training program and adaptation metrics separately from supplementation.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. Directness: `adjacent_variant`. Result boundary: `no_clear_advantage`.
