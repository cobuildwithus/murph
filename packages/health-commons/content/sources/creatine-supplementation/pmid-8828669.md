This source is included for **dose_loading_maintenance**.

**Findings:** The abstract/indexing summary states that 20 g/day for 6 days rapidly loads human skeletal muscle, that elevated stores can be maintained by about 2 g/day thereafter, and that 3 g/day over the long term is likely to be as effective at raising tissue levels as the higher loading dose. Safety/adverse-event extraction: Adverse events were not extracted from the accessible abstract/indexing material. Limitations: Participant count was not recovered in the accessible extraction material.; Healthy men only.; Physiology-focused tissue-loading study rather than performance or safety trial.

**Why it matters:** This is a foundational dose-design source for loading, maintenance, and no-load strategies.

**Potential experiment signals:** time to perceived effect, body-weight change, tolerance of split dosing, washout after stopping.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as backbone evidence for 20 g/day for about a week followed by low-dose maintenance, while noting that 3 g/day can likely saturate stores more slowly.
