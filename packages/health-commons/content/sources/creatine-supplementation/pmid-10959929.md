This source is included for **safety_tolerability_adverse_events**.

**Findings:**
- The survey included 219 select Division I collegiate athletes.
- Available summaries report that 41% used creatine and that 34 athletes reported perceived negative effects, including gastrointestinal distress and muscle cramping.
- The perceived effects may have been influenced by training, supplement interactions, diet, hydration, and heat/humidity.

**Why it matters:**
Self-reported side effects drive real adherence decisions even when causal attribution is uncertain.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- behavior / creatine use patterns
- adverse_event / perceived negative effects
- symptom / gastrointestinal distress
- symptom / muscle cramping
- biomarker / body weight perceived

**Protocol takeaway:**
Use only as observational perceived-effect context; it cannot establish causality.

**Population mismatch / boundary:**
Division I athletes; not general adult users.

**Limitations:**
- Self-reported observational survey.
- No randomized causality.
- Athlete sample only.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
