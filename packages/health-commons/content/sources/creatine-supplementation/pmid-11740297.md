This source is included for **timing_coingestion**.

**Findings:** [source_artifact:pmid-11740297] compared creatine-dextrose with protein-dextrose during training. Body mass increased more with creatine-dextrose, but fat-free mass and strength outcomes did not show a clean unique advantage across all endpoints, so this is adjacent mixed evidence.

**Why it matters:** It is a useful boundary source for dextrose co-ingestion claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** Body mass, FFM, strength, and co-ingested carbohydrate/protein.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not cite this as proof that creatine plus carbohydrate uniquely improves strength beyond protein/dextrose during training.

**Limitations / boundary notes:** Small male sample; dextrose-containing formulations; no creatine-only or timing comparison; strength effects not uniquely attributable to creatine timing.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
