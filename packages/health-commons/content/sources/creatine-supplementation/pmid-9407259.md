This source is included for **repeated_sprint_power_trials**.

**Findings:** Accessible summaries reported improved repetitive swim and arm-ergometer sprint performance after creatine.

**Population and intervention:** Junior competitive swimmers; Creatine supplementation, reported as 21 g/day for 9 days in accessible summaries. Comparator: Placebo/control condition. Duration/follow-up: 9 days of supplementation in accessible summaries.

**Endpoints:** three 100 m freestyle swims, arm-ergometer repeated sprints, body composition.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event extraction was available from the accessible record for this batch. 

**Limitations:** Detailed numeric effects not extracted. Aquatic performance may be affected by body-mass/water changes.

**Population mismatch:** Junior competitive swimmers, not broader population.

**Why it matters:** Sport-specific repeated-sprint trial in swimmers.

**Potential experiment signals:** three 100 m freestyle swims, arm-ergometer repeated sprints, body composition.

**Protocol takeaway:** Creatine may help repeated swim sprint performance, unlike some single-effort swim findings.

**Murph takeaway:** Track repeated-effort swim times and body mass.
