This source is included for **renal_safety_labs**.

**Findings:** The review included 21 studies; serum-creatinine meta-analysis included 12 studies with 177 creatine and 263 control participants. Available extract reports a small serum-creatinine increase but no significant GFR change.

**Adverse events / safety notes:** Newest renal-function synthesis; supports distinguishing creatinine rise from GFR impairment.

**Limitations:** Heterogeneous populations and protocols; Serum creatinine is vulnerable to supplementation artifact; Clinical kidney-disease subgroup evidence remains limited

**Population mismatch:** Mostly healthy or mixed populations; not a standalone CKD recommendation.

**Why it matters:** Newest dedicated renal-function synthesis; strong entry point for direct and boundary kidney outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:** serum creatinine, GFR/eGFR, cystatin C, renal adverse events

**Protocol takeaway:** Best synthesis anchor for renal lab interpretation and safety-boundary wording.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
