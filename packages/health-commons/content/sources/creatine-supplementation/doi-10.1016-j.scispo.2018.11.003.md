This source is included for **dose_loading_maintenance**.

**Findings:** Accessible publisher/indexing extracts describe improved sprint, agility, and leg-strength performance after short-term loading without weight gain. Safety/adverse-event extraction: No adverse events were extracted from the accessible abstract/indexing material; reported weight gain was absent in accessible extracts. Limitations: Participant count was not recovered in accessible extraction material.; Sport-specific female futsal population.; Muscle creatine was not directly captured in the accessible extracts.

**Why it matters:** This adds women/team-sport loading evidence and a weight-gain boundary.

**Potential experiment signals:** sprint repeatability, agility drills, leg strength, scale weight.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as direct but sport-specific evidence that loading can improve short-term high-intensity outputs without inevitable weight gain.
