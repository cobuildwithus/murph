This source is included for **safety_tolerability_adverse_events**.

**Findings:**
- The Cochrane review included two RCTs with 194 Parkinson disease participants.
- No clear evidence of benefit was found for motor function, activities of daily living, or quality of life in available summaries.
- One trial reported serious adverse events not attributed to creatine, and one trial observed higher gastrointestinal effects at two years.

**Why it matters:**
Preserves null/mixed disease-treatment evidence and prevents neurodegenerative extrapolation.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- population / parkinsons disease
- outcome / motor function
- outcome / activities of daily living
- outcome / quality of life
- adverse_event / gastrointestinal effects

**Protocol takeaway:**
Use as clinical boundary and GI-adverse-event context only.

**Population mismatch / boundary:**
Parkinson disease patients; outside default healthy-adult protocol.

**Limitations:**
- Only two trials and disease-specific endpoints.
- Clinical treatment context with different risk-benefit considerations.
- Not default protocol evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
