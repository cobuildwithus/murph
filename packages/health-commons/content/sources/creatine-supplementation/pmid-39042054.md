**Findings:**
- **outcome / Body mass:** Creatine protocols increased body mass by less than 1 kg on average in this synthesis. Directness: `adjacent_variant`. Claim use: `supports-protocol`.
- **outcome / Fat-free mass and body-fat percentage:** Fat-free mass increased modestly and body-fat percentage decreased slightly. Directness: `adjacent_variant`. Claim use: `supports-protocol`.
- **mixed_result / Protocol modifiers:** Training context and protocol structure may modify body-composition response. Directness: `adjacent_variant`. Claim use: `supports-protocol`.

**Why it matters:** This GRADE-assessed dose-response meta-analysis found small increases in body mass and fat-free mass and a small reduction in body-fat percentage across creatine protocols.

**Protocol takeaway:** Creatine body-composition effects are measurable but modest; weight gain is expected and not necessarily fat gain.

**Limitations and boundary notes:**
- Protocol heterogeneity
- Not all studies necessarily monohydrate-only
- Small effect sizes
- Body-composition methods may vary
- Study-level dose-response and heterogeneous protocols
- Needs careful synthesis before protocol-level dosing claims
