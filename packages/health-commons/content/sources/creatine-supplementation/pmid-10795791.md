This source is included for **strength_hypertrophy_trials**.

**Findings:** `source_artifact:pmid-10795791` — Active but untrained men took creatine plus glucose or glucose placebo four times daily for five days. The trial found no significant group-by-time-by-set interaction and no creatine-placebo main effect for repeated isokinetic quadriceps peak torque. Body weight increased in the creatine group.

**Why it matters:** Preserves a direct negative/mixed trial that prevents overclaiming acute loading as universally ergogenic.

**Potential experiment signals:** quadriceps peak torque; fatigue across five 30-contraction sets; body weight.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only: five days of creatine loading increased body weight but did not improve repeated isokinetic torque in this trial.

**Limitations and extraction boundary:** Short loading-only trial with no resistance training. Active but untrained male sample. Not designed to test hypertrophy or long-term strength gains. Accessible record did not report adverse events.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
