This source is included for **renal_safety_labs**.

**Findings:** Case described acute renal failure/proteinuria with biopsy-reported interstitial nephritis and recovery after stopping supplements; attribution to creatine alone is confounded by multiple products.

**Adverse events / safety notes:** Important adverse-event boundary; do not treat as direct monohydrate causality.

**Limitations:** Single case; Multiple co-supplements; No denominator; Confounding by products and behaviors

**Population mismatch:** Adjacent/confounded exposure rather than isolated creatine monohydrate.

**Why it matters:** Confounded adverse-event case that should not be over-weighted but is still part of the historical safety signal set.

**Potential experiment signals:** acute renal failure, proteinuria, interstitial nephritis, adverse event, co-interventions

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports safety screening for stacked supplements and abnormal renal symptoms/labs.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
