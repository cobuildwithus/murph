This source is included for **formulation_variant_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- **serum creatinine after creatine ethyl ester** — A renal transplant recipient had marked creatinine elevation during creatine ethyl ester use, with no biopsy evidence of rejection/pathology and normalization after stopping the supplement. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-31384454`.
- **population mismatch** — Renal transplant status is a major mismatch from healthy adult monohydrate self-experimentation. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-31384454`.

**Why it matters:** It preserves a high-risk population mismatch and highlights why abnormal labs should be clinically reviewed.

**Potential experiment signals:** safety:serum-creatinine, safety:renal-transplant, formulation:creatine-ethyl-ester.

**Protocol takeaway:** Renal transplant or kidney-disease contexts are outside a self-experiment monohydrate protocol unless supervised clinically.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
