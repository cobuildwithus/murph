**Findings:**
- **dose / Muscle phosphocreatine saturation:** Gradual lower-dose dosing can raise stores, even if loading is faster. Directness: `background`. Claim use: `context-only`.
- **outcome / Repeated powerful activity versus aerobic/isometric endpoints:** The source helps separate repeated-power benefits from aerobic and isometric null boundaries. Directness: `background`. Claim use: `context-only`.
- **safety / GI, renal, and muscle cramping complications:** Historical safety discussion did not establish major causal harms, but also was not a definitive safety guarantee. Directness: `background`. Claim use: `context-only`.

**Why it matters:** This ACSM roundtable provides historical background on muscle phosphocreatine saturation, repeated high-power exercise, null aerobic/isometric endpoints, and safety uncertainty.
