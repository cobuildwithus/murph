This source is included for **safety_tolerability_adverse_events**.

**Findings:**
- The trial randomized 1741 early treated Parkinson disease patients at 45 US and Canadian sites.
- Creatine monohydrate 10 g/day was compared with placebo and the study was terminated early for futility; median follow-up was about 4 years.
- Available summaries report no clinical outcomes benefit and no detectable differences in adverse events or serious adverse events by body system; creatine 10 g/day was generally well tolerated.

**Why it matters:**
Large long-duration trial helps preserve null evidence and safety context without extrapolating disease treatment claims.

**Protocol takeaway:**
Strong clinical null result and safety context, but not general adult protocol efficacy evidence.

**Population mismatch / boundary:**
Parkinson disease patients; outside default protocol.

**Limitations:**
- Disease-treatment trial, not supplement protocol for healthy adults.
- Concurrent Parkinson treatments and disease progression context.
- Stopped for futility before the full planned follow-up.
