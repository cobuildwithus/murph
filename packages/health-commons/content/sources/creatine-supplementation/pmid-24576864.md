This source is included for **population_boundary_adjacent_claims**.

**Findings:** Meta-analysis of older adults found creatine plus RT increased body mass (WMD 1.00 kg; 95% CI 0.32 to 1.67) and FFM (WMD 1.33 kg; 95% CI 0.79 to 1.86), improved chest press and leg press 1RM, and improved chair-stand in extracted summary. It found no fat-mass effect and no clear differences for several other strength/torque endpoints.
