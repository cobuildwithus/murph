This source is included for **repeated_sprint_power_trials**.

**Findings:** Creatine group showed greater repeated-sprint power measures and blunted TNF-alpha/CRP and LDH increases, but did not inhibit oxidative stress marker increases.

**Population and intervention:** Under-20 soccer/field-sport players; Creatine supplementation 0.3 g/kg for 7 days. Comparator: Placebo. Duration/follow-up: 7 days of supplementation.

**Endpoints:** two repeated anaerobic sprint tests, average power, maximum power, minimum power, TNF-alpha, CRP, oxidative stress markers.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event extraction was available from the accessible record for this batch. 

**Limitations:** Young athlete sample. Biomarker outcomes are not direct user performance outcomes. Oxidative-stress markers were not broadly prevented.

**Population mismatch:** Under-20 athletes; not general adult population.

**Why it matters:** Combines direct repeated-sprint performance and recovery biomarker outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:** two repeated anaerobic sprint tests, average power, maximum power, minimum power, TNF-alpha, CRP, oxidative stress markers.

**Protocol takeaway:** Creatine may improve repeated sprint power and modulate some inflammatory markers.

**Murph takeaway:** Track repeated sprint output and recovery markers separately.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`. Directness: `direct_protocol`. Result boundary: `mixed`.
