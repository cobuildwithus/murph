This source is included for **renal_safety_labs**.

**Findings:** Available abstract excerpts report no meaningful differences in renal clearances or protein excretion and normal GFR/tubular/glomerular indicators.

**Adverse events / safety notes:** No renal impairment signal in healthy athletes monitored in this cohort.

**Limitations:** Sample size not captured in available extract; Athletes only; Older methods and limited power

**Population mismatch:** Healthy athletes; not people with CKD or nephrotoxic co-exposures.

**Why it matters:** Foundational long-term athlete study repeatedly cited in later safety syntheses.

**Potential experiment signals:** plasma creatinine, urea, albumin excretion, creatinine clearance, renal function

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports lab-interpretation reassurance for healthy users, with monitoring caveats.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
