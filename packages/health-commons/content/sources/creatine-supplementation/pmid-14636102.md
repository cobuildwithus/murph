This source is included for **strength_hypertrophy_synthesis**.

**Findings:**
- From `source_artifact:pmid-14636102`: the review reportedly included 22 creatine plus resistance-training studies.
- The review reported that average muscle-strength increases were about 20% with creatine plus resistance training versus about 12% with placebo plus resistance training, an average difference of about 8%.
- The review reported average weightlifting-performance increases of about 26% with creatine plus resistance training versus about 12% with placebo plus resistance training, an average difference of about 14%.
- The range of responses was wide, so the source should preserve heterogeneity rather than imply a guaranteed effect.

**Why it matters:** It captures early synthesis evidence for creatine with resistance training while preserving variability and age of evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- bench press strength
- squat or leg press strength
- training volume
- lean mass
- body weight

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only despite direct topic relevance: creatine plus resistance training showed positive average effects in this older review, but do not use alone for modern protocol estimates.

**Murph takeaway:** Creatine may amplify resistance-training gains, but exact magnitude varies across studies.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Older narrative review; study heterogeneity; paywalled; aggregate participant count not extracted; not a primary trial.
