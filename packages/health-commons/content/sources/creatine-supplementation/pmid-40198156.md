This source is included for **safety_tolerability_adverse_events**.

**Findings:**
- The study evaluated side effects reported from 685 human clinical trials on creatine supplementation and adverse-event report databases.
- Available abstracts describe creatine supplementation as generally well tolerated and not associated with clinically significant side effects in individual studies.
- Because reporting practices differ across trials, absence of a signal should not be converted into a guarantee for every user.

**Why it matters:**
This is the batch’s broadest modern safety source and helps prevent over-weighting anecdotal side-effect reports.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- safety / adverse events
- symptom / gastrointestinal distress
- symptom / water retention
- symptom / muscle cramps
- safety / general tolerability

**Protocol takeaway:**
Use as high-level safety-only support for absence of a strong broad side-effect signal, not as a substitute for individual contraindication screening.

**Population mismatch / boundary:**
Broad mixed populations; use for safety boundaries rather than default-protocol outcomes.

**Limitations:**
- Aggregated safety review; individual trial populations and doses vary.
- Adverse events may be underreported or inconsistently categorized.
- Not efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
