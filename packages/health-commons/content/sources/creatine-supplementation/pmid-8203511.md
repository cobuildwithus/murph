This source is included for **dose_loading_maintenance**.

**Findings:** Accessible PubMed/Semantic Scholar snippets state that diet-induced increases in muscle total creatine can increase phosphocreatine resynthesis during the second minute of recovery from intense contraction. Safety/adverse-event extraction: Adverse events were not extracted from accessible abstract/indexing material. Limitations: Participant count and full dosing details not recovered.; Mechanistic recovery endpoint, not clinical or broad performance evidence.; Later mechanistic evidence includes null findings under other conditions, so endpoint context matters.

**Why it matters:** This is foundational mechanism evidence for PCr recovery after creatine loading.

**Potential experiment signals:** between-set recovery, repeat-bout fatigue, short rest interval performance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as direct mechanism evidence with a condition-specific caveat.
