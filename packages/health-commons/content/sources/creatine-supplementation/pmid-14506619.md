This source is included for **formulation_variant_boundary**.

**Findings:**
- **body water and isokinetic strength/power** — Magnesium-creatine chelate showed changes in intracellular/extracellular water and some quadriceps performance measures versus placebo in a two-week trial. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-14506619`.
- **tolerability** — The accessible abstract reported participants completed the study without complaints. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-14506619`.

**Why it matters:** Alternative creatine forms can have co-ingredient effects and body-water endpoints that should not be merged with monohydrate.

**Potential experiment signals:** body-composition:body-water, performance:isokinetic-quadriceps, formulation:magnesium-creatine-chelate.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use chelated magnesium-creatine as monohydrate-only body-water evidence.
