**Findings:**
- **intermittent maximal high-intensity exercise** — Creatine pyruvate and creatine citrate were reported to improve some high-intensity exercise performance outcomes versus placebo over 28 days, but there was no monohydrate comparator arm. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-18269769`.

**Why it matters:** It is often cited around creatine salts but is not a direct monohydrate comparison.

**Potential experiment signals:** performance:high-intensity-exercise, formulation:creatine-pyruvate, formulation:creatine-citrate.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use pyruvate/citrate results as direct support for monohydrate outcomes.
