This source is included for **repeated_sprint_power_trials**.

**Findings:** Creatine-electrolyte supplementation increased overall peak power by about 4% and overall mean power by about 5%; repeated-sprint gains were significant only in the first sprint effort in the accessible abstract.

**Population and intervention:** Male recreational cyclists; Creatine-electrolyte supplement formulation. Comparator: Placebo. Duration/follow-up: 6 weeks of creatine-electrolyte supplementation.

**Endpoints:** five 15-second cycling sprints, overall peak power, overall mean power, first sprint peak and mean power, repeated sprint cycling performance.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event extraction was available from the accessible record for this batch. 

**Limitations:** Adjacent formulation includes electrolytes, so do not attribute effects solely to creatine monohydrate. Male recreational cyclists only. Repeated-sprint effect was concentrated in the first sprint effort.

**Population mismatch:** Male recreational cyclists and mixed supplement formulation.

**Why it matters:** Important open-access mixed-formulation source to separate from direct monohydrate evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** five 15-second cycling sprints, overall peak power, overall mean power, first sprint peak and mean power, repeated sprint cycling performance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Adjacent formulation improved cycling sprint power; not clean monohydrate-only evidence.

**Murph takeaway:** Track formulation ingredients and sprint number when interpreting outcomes.
