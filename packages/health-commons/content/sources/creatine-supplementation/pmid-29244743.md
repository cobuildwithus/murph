**Findings:**
- **hematological and hemodynamic responses** — Creatine nitrate at 3 g and 6 g for 6 days appeared safe on measured hematological and hemodynamic endpoints in the short-term crossover study. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-29244743`.
- **resistance and cycling performance** — Bench-press measures improved in some conditions, while 4 km cycling time-trial performance was not affected. Source key: `source_artifact:pmid-29244743`.

**Why it matters:** Nitrate adds a hemodynamic dimension not present in plain monohydrate.

**Potential experiment signals:** safety:hemodynamics, safety:hematology, performance:bench-press, performance:cycling-time-trial, formulation:creatine-nitrate.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use nitrate findings for monohydrate; use them only to flag alternate-form safety considerations.
