This source is included for **dose_loading_maintenance**.

**Findings:** Accessible extracts report combined creatine plus glycerol hyperhydration increased hyperhydration and reduced thermal/cardiovascular responses before exercise in heat; creatine-specific effects cannot be isolated from the glycerol co-intervention. Safety/adverse-event extraction: Heat/hyperhydration safety context is relevant, but specific adverse events were not extracted. Limitations: Adjacent variant because glycerol co-intervention prevents creatine-only attribution.; Although N=24 and key regimen details were recovered, the glycerol co-intervention prevents creatine-only attribution.; Heat-exercise setting is not default protocol use.

**Why it matters:** The batch includes body-water and heat-context candidates, but this one must be quarantined as adjacent.

**Potential experiment signals:** body water, heat tolerance, GI tolerance, weight gain.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as adjacent hydration context.

**Claim use:** `context-only`. Directness: `adjacent_variant`. Rights status: `paywalled`.
