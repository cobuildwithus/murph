This source is included for **dose_loading_maintenance**.

**Findings:** Accessible abstracts report muscle total creatine increased by 23.1 ± 4.7 mmol/kg dry matter and total work during two bouts increased by approximately 4%, with changes in peak/total work correlated with muscle total creatine increase. Safety/adverse-event extraction: Adverse events were not extracted from accessible abstract/indexing material. Limitations: Very small male sample.; Short laboratory cycling protocol.; Pre/post physiology design in accessible extracts; full randomization/control details not extracted.

**Why it matters:** This source directly links muscle total creatine increase to high-intensity work output.

**Potential experiment signals:** repeat-bout work, peak output, time-to-fatigue, short-rest intervals.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as direct evidence that classic loading can raise muscle creatine and improve short maximal work under controlled conditions.
