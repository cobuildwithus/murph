This source is included for **repeated_sprint_power_trials**.

**Findings:** Accessible abstract reported significantly extended capacity to maintain high-intensity intermittent work after creatine.

**Population and intervention:** Healthy active adults; Creatine monohydrate 18.75 g/day for 5 days, then 2.25 g/day during testing. Comparator: Placebo. Duration/follow-up: 5 days of loading followed by lower-dose supplementation during testing.

**Endpoints:** high-intensity intermittent work capacity, fatigue, power/work maintenance.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event extraction was available from the accessible record for this batch. 

**Limitations:** Small sample. Protocol-specific intermittent work test; not a broad endurance claim.

**Population mismatch:** Healthy active adults in lab protocol.

**Why it matters:** Directly aligned with repeated high-intensity effort claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** high-intensity intermittent work capacity, fatigue, power/work maintenance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Creatine may improve capacity to sustain repeated high-intensity work.

**Murph takeaway:** Track total work completed and fatigue slope across intervals.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`. Directness: `direct_protocol`. Result boundary: `positive`.
