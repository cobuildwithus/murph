This source is included for **timing_coingestion**.

**Findings:** [source_artifact:pmid-8944667] found higher skeletal-muscle creatine accumulation when creatine was paired with carbohydrate compared with creatine without carbohydrate in healthy men. This is direct uptake evidence, not a direct strength or body-composition outcome.

**Why it matters:** It provides direct protocol-adjacent evidence for the co-ingestion option and explains why insulin-mediated uptake appears in timing discussions.

**Potential experiment signals:** Dosing adherence, meal context, GI comfort, and body-mass changes are more feasible Murph signals than muscle biopsies.

**Protocol takeaway:** Carbohydrate can augment creatine accumulation during loading, but the high carbohydrate dose is not required for basic creatine use and may not be practical for all users.

**Limitations / boundary notes:** Short loading-phase design; large carbohydrate burden; male-only sample; no performance endpoint.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
