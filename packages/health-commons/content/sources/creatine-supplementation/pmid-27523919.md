This source is included for **population_boundary_adjacent_claims**.

**Findings:** Drop-set RT improved muscle mass, strength, endurance, and functionality. Adding creatine significantly increased body mass (P=0.002) and muscle mass (P=0.007) versus placebo/control in extracted summaries.

**Why it matters:** Untrained aging population and resistance-training variant source.

**Intervention or exposure:** Creatine supplementation plus drop-set resistance training.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event details were recovered in the accessible batch extraction.
