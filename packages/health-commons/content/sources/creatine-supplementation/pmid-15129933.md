This source is included for **population_boundary_adjacent_claims**.

**Findings:** Creatine improved soccer-specific skill performance versus placebo in the accessible abstract; secondary summaries describe improved sprint power and countermovement jump.

**Why it matters:** Youth athlete boundary trial.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- soccer-specific skill performance
- sprint power
- countermovement jump

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep adolescent soccer-player data outside adult protocol claims.

**Murph takeaway:** Youth use requires separate policy/clinical handling.

**Population:** Young male soccer players, mean age about 16.6 years.

**Intervention or exposure:** Creatine monohydrate 30 g/day split as three 10-g doses/day.

**Comparator or control:** Placebo.

**Duration or follow-up:** 7 days.

**Endpoints:**
- soccer-specific skill performance
- sprint power
- countermovement jump

**Effect estimate or direction:** Creatine improved soccer-specific skill performance versus placebo in the accessible abstract; secondary summaries describe improved sprint power and countermovement jump.

**Adverse events or safety notes:** No reliable adverse-event extraction was recovered; do not infer youth safety from this source alone.

**Limitations:**
- Youth population outside default adult protocol.
- High short-term dose and small sample.

**Population mismatch:** Youth athletes; context-only.
