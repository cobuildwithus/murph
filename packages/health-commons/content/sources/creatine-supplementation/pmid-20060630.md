This source is included for **renal_safety_labs**.

**Findings:** Measured GFR remained essentially stable while serum creatinine rose from 1.03 to 1.27 mg/dL, illustrating a possible false creatinine-based impairment signal.

**Adverse events / safety notes:** Prospective single case suggests measured GFR may remain stable despite serum creatinine rise, but this is not a broad CKD clearance.

**Limitations:** Single participant; Single kidney but no active kidney damage; High-protein diet and close clinical monitoring

**Population mismatch:** Clinical boundary case; supervised and not generalizable to unsupervised CKD use.

**Why it matters:** Frequently cited single-kidney boundary case with measured GFR rather than creatinine alone.

**Potential experiment signals:** 51Cr-EDTA measured GFR, serum creatinine, single-kidney safety boundary

**Protocol takeaway:** Strong lab-interpretation example for why creatinine-based eGFR can mislead during supplementation.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
