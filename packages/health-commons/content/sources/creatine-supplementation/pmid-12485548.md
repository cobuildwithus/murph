This source is included for **strength_hypertrophy_synthesis**.

**Findings:**
- **outcome / Bench press strength:** Creatine was associated with greater bench-press strength gains in this early meta-analysis. Directness: `same_mechanism`. Claim use: `context-only`.
- **outcome / Squat strength:** Creatine was associated with greater squat strength gains. Directness: `same_mechanism`. Claim use: `context-only`.
- **null_result / Arm flexor strength and cycle ergometer peak power:** Not all strength or power endpoints improved in the meta-analysis. Directness: `same_mechanism`. Claim use: `context-only`.
- **limitation / Risk of bias and generalizability:** The review itself warns that study flaws could overestimate benefits. Directness: `same_mechanism`. Claim use: `context-only`.

**Why it matters:** This older strength meta-analysis found positive bench-press and squat effects in mostly young men, while some endpoints were null and trial quality was limited.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- bench press 1RM
- squat 1RM
- cycle peak power
- training duration

**Protocol takeaway:** Treat this as early same-mechanism support for strength outcomes with clear population and quality caveats.

**Limitations and boundary notes:**
- Older review
- Mostly young men
- Methodological quality concerns
- Small endpoint sample
- Older trials
- Endpoint-specific small samples
- Heterogeneity reported for cycle peak power
- Historical review standards
- Insufficient women and older-adult evidence

- None beyond the source-specific caveats above.
