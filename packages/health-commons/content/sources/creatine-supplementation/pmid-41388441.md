This source is included for **population_boundary_adjacent_claims**.

**Findings:** Meta-analysis of 8 RCTs (n=482) found lower-limb strength improved (SMD 0.29; 95% CI 0.00 to 0.57; P=0.05) and lean tissue mass improved (SMD 0.27; 95% CI 0.02 to 0.53; P=0.03). Upper-extremity strength was not significant overall; ≤32-week subgroup analyses favored upper-limb strength and lean tissue mass.

**Why it matters:** Recent aged-population RT synthesis with endpoint-specific nuance.

**Intervention or exposure:** Creatine supplementation associated with resistance training.

**Duration or follow-up:** 8 to 104 weeks across included trials.

**Endpoints:**

**Effect estimate or direction:** Meta-analysis of 8 RCTs (n=482) found lower-limb strength improved (SMD 0.29; 95% CI 0.00 to 0.57; P=0.05) and lean tissue mass improved (SMD 0.27; 95% CI 0.02 to 0.53; P=0.03). Upper-extremity strength was not significant overall; ≤32-week subgroup analyses favored upper-limb strength and lean tissue mass.
