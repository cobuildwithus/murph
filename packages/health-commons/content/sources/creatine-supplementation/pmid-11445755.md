This source is included for **timing_coingestion**.

**Findings:** [source_artifact:pmid-11445755] found enhanced glycogen supercompensation after a 5-day creatine loading phase in young men. The study supports a glycogen/recovery mechanism but is not direct proof of superior routine workout timing.

**Why it matters:** It provides same-mechanism context for carbohydrate co-ingestion and recovery claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** Recovery context, carbohydrate intake, body weight/water, and training load are practical log fields.

**Protocol takeaway:** Creatine may interact with glycogen-loading physiology, but this should be framed as recovery/mechanism context rather than a universal timing rule.

**Limitations / boundary notes:** Glycogen-depletion/loading laboratory model; small male sample; no long-term performance or body-composition endpoint.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
