This source is included for **timing_coingestion**.

**Findings:** [source_artifact:pmid-15903360] reported faster swim times after both creatine-loading regimens, but no between-group performance advantage from adding carbohydrate. This source should be retained as a direct but null/mixed co-ingestion performance finding.

**Why it matters:** It prevents overclaiming carbohydrate co-ingestion as a performance enhancer.

**Potential experiment signals:** Interval performance, sport context, body mass, and high-carbohydrate tolerability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Carbohydrate may increase creatine retention in physiology studies, but this swimmer trial did not show an added performance advantage over creatine alone.

**Limitations / boundary notes:** Only 10 elite swimmers; sport-specific repeated maximal swimming endpoint; no creatine-free placebo arm in the extracted comparison; not general gym-strength evidence.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
