This source is included for **body_weight_water_gi**.

**Findings:**
- Female collegiate dancers received creatine monohydrate or placebo/control in an RCT.
- Available summaries report significant increases in total body water (32.2 ± 3.5 to 32.7 ± 3.6 kg; p=0.024) and DXA lean mass (39.8 ± 3.6 to 41.5 ± 4.5 kg; p=0.020) in the creatine group.
- The authors caution that larger samples with resistance training are needed to determine whether changes translate to muscle mass or performance.

**Why it matters:**
This directly links creatine monohydrate, total body water, and DXA lean mass in a female athletic population.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- biomarker / total body water
- biomarker / dxa lean mass
- biomarker / body composition
- outcome / performance cognition secondary

**Protocol takeaway:**
Supports explaining that DXA lean mass can be partly confounded by water shifts after creatine.

**Population mismatch / boundary:**
Female collegiate dancers; direct protocol variant but specialized athletic/aesthetic context.

**Limitations:**
- Participant count was not captured in the extracted record.
- Female collegiate dancers are a specialized population.
- DXA lean mass can be influenced by water content.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
