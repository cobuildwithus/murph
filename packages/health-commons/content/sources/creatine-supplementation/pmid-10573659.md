This source is included for **repeated_sprint_power_trials**.

**Findings:** Creatine improved cycle-sprint mean/peak power at 10 days and improved on-ice sprint split times; placebo did not show comparable change in the accessible abstract.

**Population and intervention:** Elite ice-hockey players; Creatine monohydrate 5 g four times daily for 5 days, then 5 g/day maintenance. Comparator: Placebo under double-blind allocation. Duration/follow-up: 10 days and 10 weeks after a 5-day loading phase plus maintenance.

**Endpoints:** five 15-second cycle sprints, 80 m on-ice sprint time, 47 m split time, peak and mean power.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event extraction was available from the accessible record for this batch. 

**Limitations:** Small sport-specific sample. Effects may depend on athlete training status and sprint protocol.

**Population mismatch:** Elite team-sport athletes may not represent community users.

**Why it matters:** A sport-specific direct repeated-sprint trial with both lab and on-ice outcomes.

**Potential experiment signals:** five 15-second cycle sprints, 80 m on-ice sprint time, 47 m split time, peak and mean power.

**Protocol takeaway:** Creatine can improve repeated sprint power in trained field/ice-sport settings.

**Murph takeaway:** Useful signals include split times, repeated sprint power, and fatigue across bouts.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`. Directness: `direct_protocol`. Result boundary: `positive`.
