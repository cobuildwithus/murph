This source is included for **repeated_sprint_power_trials**.

**Findings:** Sets 2-10 in the squash ghosting test were shorter after creatine than placebo in the accessible abstract.

**Population and intervention:** Competitive squash players; Oral creatine supplementation before on-court intermittent exercise. Comparator: Placebo in a double-blind crossover design. Duration/follow-up: Acute/short-term crossover supplementation; exact washout not extracted.

**Endpoints:** on-court ghosting performance, set times across 10 repeated high-intensity bouts, intermittent exercise fatigue.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event extraction was available from the accessible record for this batch. 

**Limitations:** Very small sample. Sport-specific on-court protocol may not map to gym measures.

**Population mismatch:** Competitive squash players; not general adult users.

**Why it matters:** Direct sport-specific intermittent performance trial with crossover design.

**Potential experiment signals:** on-court ghosting performance, set times across 10 repeated high-intensity bouts, intermittent exercise fatigue.

**Protocol takeaway:** Creatine may help repeated high-intensity sport efforts.

**Murph takeaway:** Repeated set time or drill completion time are appropriate user-facing signals.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`. Directness: `direct_protocol`. Result boundary: `positive`.
