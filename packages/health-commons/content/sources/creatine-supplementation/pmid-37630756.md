This source is included for **dose_loading_maintenance**.

**Findings:** Accessible article/indexing extracts report potential benefits in the high-hormone/luteal phase, including an interaction for fatigue index; HRV was not clearly influenced and most sprint measures were mixed or not significant. Safety/adverse-event extraction: Adverse events were not extracted from accessible article text; non-caloric placebo and short loading should not be interpreted as long-term safety evidence. Limitations: Short loading duration.; Active women with menstrual-cycle phase design; not all women or hormonal profiles.; Mixed endpoints with HRV null findings and limited significant sprint outcomes.

**Why it matters:** This is modern open-access women-specific loading evidence and fills a major population gap.

**Potential experiment signals:** fatigue index, repeat sprint power, HRV, menstrual-cycle phase, perceived recovery.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as direct women-specific mixed evidence for loading; cite fatigue-index/high-hormone context separately from HRV null findings.
