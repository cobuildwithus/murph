This source is included for **repeated_sprint_power_trials**.

**Findings:** Accessible abstract reported no ergogenic effect on single or repeated 40 m sprint times in highly trained athletes.

**Population and intervention:** Highly trained sprint athletes; Creatine monohydrate 0.35 g/kg/day for 7 days. Comparator: Placebo crossover condition. Duration/follow-up: 7-day high-dose supplementation in a crossover design with washout.

**Endpoints:** single 40 m sprint, intermittent 40 m sprints, sprint times with varied rest.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event extraction was available from the accessible record for this batch.

**Limitations:** Small sample of highly trained athletes. Ceiling effects may limit detectable benefit.

**Population mismatch:** Highly trained sprinters may differ from recreational users.

**Why it matters:** A high-training-status null result helps prevent overgeneralized sprint claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** single 40 m sprint, intermittent 40 m sprints, sprint times with varied rest.

**Protocol takeaway:** Sprint benefits are not guaranteed, especially in highly trained athletes.

**Murph takeaway:** Individual response and training status should be tracked.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`. Directness: `direct_protocol`. Result boundary: `no_clear_advantage`.
