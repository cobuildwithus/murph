This source is included for **background_guidelines_external**.

**Findings:**
- **safety / Renal function/kidney safety:** The review supports conservative safety language for healthy users at recommended doses. Directness: `background`. Claim use: `context-only`.
- **safety / Water retention and body weight:** Water/weight changes should be expected and monitored rather than treated as fat gain. Directness: `background`. Claim use: `context-only`.
- **safety / Dehydration, muscle cramping, hair loss, anabolic-steroid misconception:** The source helps avoid repeating common unsupported creatine misconceptions. Directness: `background`. Claim use: `context-only`.

**Why it matters:** This narrative review addresses common safety misconceptions, including kidney function, dehydration/cramping, water retention, and hair-loss claims.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- body weight
- GI symptoms
- hydration/cramping notes
- creatinine/renal-lab context

**Protocol takeaway:** Creatine safety language should separate normal weight/water changes from unsupported claims about dehydration, cramps, kidney damage in healthy users, or hair loss.

**Limitations and boundary notes:**
- Narrative review
- Healthy-user boundary; renal disease not directly covered as low-risk home use
- Narrative synthesis
- Individual response varies
- Absence of evidence for common myths is not proof of zero risk for all people
