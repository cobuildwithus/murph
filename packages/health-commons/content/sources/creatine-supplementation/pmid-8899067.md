This source is included for **timing_coingestion**.

**Findings:** [source_artifact:pmid-8899067] reported that carbohydrate co-ingestion augmented creatine retention during feeding and that retention was not further increased when exercise was performed before ingestion in the tested groups.

**Why it matters:** It provides direct co-ingestion evidence with a preserved null add-on finding.

**Potential experiment signals:** Meal context, exercise timing, body mass, GI tolerance, and adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Carbohydrate co-ingestion can augment retention acutely; exercise-before-dose should not be claimed as additive based on this source.

**Limitations / boundary notes:** Short-term retention endpoint; high carbohydrate dose; male healthy sample; no training outcomes.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
