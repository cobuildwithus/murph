This source is included for **renal_safety_labs**.

**Findings:** Available abstract reports eight weeks of creatine was safe and did not compromise renal function in PAD patients.

**Adverse events / safety notes:** Clinical comorbidity boundary evidence; not healthy-user efficacy.

**Limitations:** Small clinical trial; Peripheral artery disease population; Short duration

**Population mismatch:** PAD population differs from general self-experimenters.

**Why it matters:** Boundary trial in vascular disease, useful for exclusion language and generalizability limits.

**Potential experiment signals:** creatinine clearance, renal function, serum creatinine, adverse events

**Protocol takeaway:** Adds clinical-population boundary context with creatinine-clearance endpoint.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
