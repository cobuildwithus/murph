**Findings:** Case report described acute renal failure with increased creatinine/proteinuria and recovery after stopping creatine; causality cannot be quantified from a single case.

**Adverse events / safety notes:** Important adverse-event boundary even though evidence strength is low.

**Limitations:** Single case; No denominator; Potential unmeasured confounders; Cannot estimate incidence or causality

**Population mismatch:** Adverse-event case, not routine protocol evidence.

**Why it matters:** Frequently cited recommended-dose case report; important even if single-patient evidence is weak.

**Potential experiment signals:** acute renal failure, serum creatinine, proteinuria, kidney biopsy/recovery context

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports conservative warning to pause and seek care if renal labs become abnormal.
