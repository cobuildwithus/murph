This source is included for **dose_loading_maintenance**.

**Findings:** Accessible repository text reports interval swim times improved after acute loading, with 3 of 22 swimmers not responding positively; after 2 months, neither creatine nor placebo group showed significant changes. Safety/adverse-event extraction: Adverse events were not extracted from the accessible abstract/indexing material. Limitations: Elite swimming population limits generalization.; Sport-specific interval outcome.; Mixed acute versus longer-term findings.

**Why it matters:** This is a direct mixed/null source with maintenance evidence and nonresponse information.

**Potential experiment signals:** repeat interval performance, response/nonresponse, body weight, maintenance benefit over weeks.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mixed sport-specific evidence: loading may help acute repeated swim intervals, but longer maintenance did not clearly add benefit in this trial.
