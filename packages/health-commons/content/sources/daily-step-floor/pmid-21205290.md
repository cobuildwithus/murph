This source is included for **guidelines_external_protocol_context**.

**Findings:** The RE-AIM analysis reported 90% organizational awareness, 36% adoption, a 52% implementation score, and higher self-reported leisure-time PA among citizens aware of 10,000 Steps, but the design was observational and implementation-focused.

**Why it matters:** Shows reach and implementation issues for external 10,000-step campaigns but cannot establish step-floor causality.

**Potential experiment signals:** program_reach, program_adoption, physical_activity_minutes, program_maintenance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only implementation evidence; do not treat awareness-associated activity differences as causal Daily Step Floor effects.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Heads of department from 69 organizations and 755 citizens living in the work area of adopting organizations in Belgium/Flanders dissemination context.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Wide-scale dissemination of a whole-community 10,000 Steps project using media strategies and professional peer networks.
- **Comparator/control:** Aware versus unaware citizens and adopting versus non-adopting organizations; no randomized control group.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Post-dissemination RE-AIM assessment; individual long-term maintenance was not yet available in the accessible extract.
- **Endpoints:** organizational awareness; adoption; implementation score; intended continuation; citizen awareness; self-reported physical activity minutes
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Of organizations, 90% were aware and 36% adopted the project, with a global implementation score of 52%; 35% of citizens were aware and aware citizens reported higher leisure-time PA than unaware citizens (256 ± 237 vs 207 ± 216 min/week; p < .005).
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse events reported.
- **Limitations:** Implementation/reach study; self-reported physical activity; awareness comparisons are observational and not causal; maintenance undecided for many organizations.
- **Population mismatch:** External community dissemination context, not a direct personal Daily Step Floor protocol.
- **Artifact rights:** open_access
