This source is included for **dose_response_cut_points**.

**Findings:** In 111,309 individuals from 12 studies, significant risk reductions were estimated at about 2517 steps/day for all-cause mortality and 2735 steps/day for incident CVD versus a 2000-step reference, with nonlinear risk reductions up to estimated optimal doses near 8763 and 7126 steps/day, respectively.

**Why it matters:** Backbone for minimum/optimal step-count dose-response context.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily step count, days meeting step floor, weekly mean steps, all-cause mortality, incident cardiovascular disease, cadence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context for attainable minimum thresholds and upper dose-response flattening, not as protocol proof.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** General-population observational cohorts with objectively measured steps.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Daily step counts and cadence categories.
- **Comparator/control:** 2000 steps/day reference and lower cadence categories.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Searches through October 2022; follow-up varied by included cohort.
- **Endpoints:** all-cause mortality, incident cardiovascular disease, daily step count, cadence
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse-event signal specific to a step-floor intervention was extracted from this source in this batch.
- **Limitations:** Observational meta-analysis; device placement influenced risk estimates; cadence and steps are correlated.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Does not test Daily Step Floor adherence or randomized assignment.
- **Boundary:** This source is observational, review-based, registry-only, or otherwise adjacent unless explicitly noted; it must not be promoted into direct Daily Step Floor protocol evidence.
