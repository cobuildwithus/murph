This source is included for **sedentary_activity_pattern**.

**Findings:** Systematic review of 18 RCTs found positive effects on physical activity, especially steps and intensity-related outcomes in the short and medium term; some self-reported sedentary behavior reductions were reported, but objective sedentary behavior improvements were not reported in accessible summaries.

**Why it matters:** Strongly preserves the distinction between step/PA success and objective sedentary-time uncertainty.

**Potential experiment signals:** steps per day, intensity-related physical activity, self-reported sedentary behavior, objectively measured sedentary behavior, behavior change techniques.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only adjacent evidence for older-adult tracker components and sedentary-outcome uncertainty.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
