This source is included for **safety_special_populations**.

**Findings:** In a 239-participant COPD RCT, an internet-mediated pedometer program increased daily steps by about 779 steps/day at 4 months and improved some quality-of-life domains, while mean total SGRQ difference was not significant.

**Why it matters:** Shows a pedometer walking program can increase steps in COPD over 4 months while requiring symptom and adverse-event monitoring.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily-step-count, SGRQ quality-of-life, dyspnea, dizziness, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not generalize COPD results to unscreened users; Daily Step Floor should have dyspnea/dizziness off-ramps and clinician involvement for COPD.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness boundary:** clinical_supervised adjacent variant. Do not promote this source into direct Daily Step Floor claims beyond the stated claim-use boundary.

**Safety/adverse events:** COPD symptoms and adverse events require monitoring; source is clinical-supervised special-population evidence.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Mostly male veterans with COPD; short-term effects; not a general Daily Step Floor trial. COPD clinical population; safety-only for general protocol.
