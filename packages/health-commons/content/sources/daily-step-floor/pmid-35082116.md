This source is included for **direct_protocol_reviews**.

**Findings:** Overall physical activity, expressed as daily steps equivalent: The review reported an effect on physical activity of SMD 0.42 (95% CI 0.28 to 0.55), estimated as equivalent to 1235 additional daily steps.; Moderate-to-vigorous physical activity: The review reported an MVPA effect of SMD 0.23 (95% CI 0.16 to 0.30), estimated as equivalent to 48.5 additional minutes/week.; Sedentary time: The review reported a small non-significant sedentary-time effect (SMD -0.12; 95% CI -0.25 to 0.01), estimated as about 9.9 fewer sedentary minutes/day.

**Why it matters:** This broad modern RCT synthesis supports the mechanism that feedback from activity monitors increases activity, but it is less protocol-specific than pedometer and step-monitor reviews.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily-step-count, weekly-mvpa-minutes, sedentary-minutes.

**Protocol takeaway:** Activity-monitor feedback supports a Daily Step Floor’s self-monitoring mechanism, while sedentary-time claims should remain cautious.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
