This source is included for **cadence_intensity_bouts**.

**Findings:** Laboratory treadmill study in 43 adults found ~100 steps/min aligned with absolute moderate intensity, while relative and vigorous thresholds were higher.

**Why it matters:** Supports the common 3000 steps in 30 minutes / 100 spm heuristic but reinforces that intensity thresholds vary by definition.

**Potential experiment signals:** walking_cadence, mvpa_minutes, activity_intensity_classification.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use 100 spm as optional absolute-MVPA context only; avoid promising that every user at 100 spm meets their personalized intensity threshold.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Forty-three adults (25 women; mean age about 39 years) in a staged treadmill walking protocol.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Step rate during treadmill walking at progressively faster speeds with indirect calorimetry.
- **Comparator/control:** Absolute and relative intensity thresholds derived from metabolic criteria.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Single laboratory protocol with six 6-minute treadmill stages.
- **Endpoints:** Step-rate thresholds for moderate and vigorous physical activity; validity of 3000 steps in 30 minutes message.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Mathematical modeling yielded approximate thresholds of ~100 spm for absolute moderate intensity, ~125 spm for relative moderate intensity, ~133 spm for absolute vigorous intensity, and ~139 spm for relative vigorous intensity.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse events or safety outcomes were reported in the accessible extract.
- **Limitations:** Small lab sample; treadmill protocol; not free-living; thresholds may not apply to older adults or clinical populations.
- **Population mismatch:** Cadence-intensity measurement evidence, not a Daily Step Floor intervention.
