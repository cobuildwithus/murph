This source is included for **guidelines_external_protocol_context**.

**Findings:** The sustainability study reported 50% organizational continuation, stable implementation scoring, and higher self-reported activity among citizens aware of 10,000 Steps, but the evidence is implementation-focused and observational.

**Why it matters:** Highlights maintenance barriers and facilitators for external step programs, useful for implementation caveats.

**Potential experiment signals:** program_maintenance, program_engagement, physical_activity_minutes.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only implementation/sustainability evidence; avoid causal efficacy claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Department heads of 24 public services that adopted 10,000 Steps and 483 nearby citizens in the Flanders dissemination context.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Continuation and institutionalization of a whole-community 10,000 Steps project after initial implementation.
- **Comparator/control:** Organizations/citizens with differing continuation or awareness status; no randomized comparator.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Approximately 1.5 years after initially reported project implementation.
- **Endpoints:** project continuation; institutionalization; implementation score; adaptations; citizen awareness; self-reported leisure, household, and moderate-intensity physical activity
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Half of organizations continued 10,000 Steps, often in cycles; global implementation score remained about 58%; citizens aware of 10,000 Steps reported more leisure-time, household-related, and moderate-intensity PA than unaware citizens.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse events reported.
- **Limitations:** Implementation sustainability study; self-reported PA; observational awareness comparison; organization-level continuation may not translate to individual step-floor adherence.
- **Population mismatch:** External community program sustainability evidence, not a direct Daily Step Floor intervention.
- **Artifact rights:** open_access
