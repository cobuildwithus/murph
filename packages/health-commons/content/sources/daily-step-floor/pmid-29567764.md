This source is included for **cadence_intensity_bouts**.

**Findings:** NHANES cohort analysis found that all-cause mortality reductions associated with MVPA did not depend on accumulating MVPA in longer bouts.

**Why it matters:** Helps avoid over-prescribing long bouts: total activity appears important even when accumulated sporadically.

**Potential experiment signals:** mvpa_minutes, activity_bout_duration, all_cause_mortality.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not require Daily Step Floor steps to occur in 10-minute MVPA bouts based on this adjacent MVPA evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** US adults aged 40 years or older in NHANES 2003-2006 with accelerometer data.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Total MVPA and MVPA accumulated in bouts with 5-minute or 10-minute criteria.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower versus higher MVPA, and bouted versus sporadic accumulation patterns.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Accelerometer exposure assessment with mortality follow-up through 2011; accessible abstract reports about 6.6 years of follow-up.
- **Endpoints:** All-cause mortality.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Mortality risk reductions associated with MVPA were reported to be independent of whether activity was accumulated in bouts; greater bouted proportion did not add mortality reduction beyond total MVPA.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse events or intervention safety outcomes were reported.
- **Limitations:** Observational analysis; MVPA rather than step-count protocol; bouted MVPA may not map directly to daily steps; residual confounding possible.
- **Population mismatch:** Adjacent MVPA-bout evidence; not direct Daily Step Floor evidence.
