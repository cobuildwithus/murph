This source is included for **measurement_validity**.

**Findings:** Six consumer monitors were less valid in chronic heart failure than in healthy free-living adults, and treadmill accuracy deteriorated sharply at slower speeds.

**Why it matters:** Slow clinical gait can reduce step-count validity even for devices that perform well in healthy people.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily_step_count, step_count_accuracy, device_placement_or_carriage, gait_speed_or_gait_aid_context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only evidence that chronic heart failure and slow speed can degrade monitor validity.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Patients with chronic heart failure and healthy individuals; participant count was not available in the accessible extraction.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Six consumer-level activity monitors: Withings Go, Omron HJ-322U-E, SmartLAB walk+, Garmin vívofit, Garmin vívofit 3, and Fitbit Charge 2.
- **Comparator/control:** ActiGraph criterion/reference in 3-day field study; treadmill walking protocol at 2.4, 3.0, 3.6, and 4.2 km/h.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 3-day free-living field study plus laboratory treadmill testing
- **Endpoints:** step-count validity, concordance correlation coefficient, mean absolute percentage error, walking-speed effects
- **Effect estimates or direction:** In healthy individuals, all but one monitor had CCC ≥0.95 and MAPE <10%. In heart failure patients, only Garmin vívofit 3 and Withings Go reached at least moderate correlation (CCC ≥0.90), and no device had MAPE <10%. At treadmill speeds 4.2 and 3.6 km/h devices performed well (MAPE 1%-3%), but accuracy deteriorated at slower speeds, with MAPE 10%-45% at 2.4 km/h.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse events or safety outcomes were reported in the accessible extract.
- **Limitations:** Participant count was not extracted for this draft.; Criterion was an accelerometer, not direct observation in the field study.; Clinical heart failure gait may not generalize to all users.
- **Population mismatch:** Heart-failure measurement context; not Daily Step Floor efficacy evidence.
- **Artifact rights:** open_access
