This source is included for **safety_special_populations**.

**Findings:** A systematic review and meta-analysis on preventing diabetic foot ulcers in at-risk people with diabetes provides clinical-prevention context but no extracted daily step-floor efficacy estimate.

**Why it matters:** Anchors Daily Step Floor diabetic-foot safety language in the broader prevention literature.

**Potential experiment signals:** foot-ulcer incidence, foot-protection adherence, daily-step-count, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Users at risk of diabetic foot ulceration need foot-protection and clinician-guided prevention strategies before generic step-floor escalation.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness boundary:** general_guideline safety boundary for diabetic-foot risk. Do not promote this source into direct Daily Step Floor claims beyond the stated claim-use boundary.

**Safety/adverse events:** Supports the premise that diabetic-foot prevention is a specialized clinical domain; step increases should not override foot-protection/offloading guidance.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Review of ulcer-prevention interventions rather than a daily step-goal intervention; detailed subgroup estimates were not extracted in this batch. Diabetic-foot-risk population; safety-only context.
