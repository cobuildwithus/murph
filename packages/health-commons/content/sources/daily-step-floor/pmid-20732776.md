This source is included for **cardiometabolic_fitness_endpoints**.

**Findings:** An RCT in type 2 diabetes found a pedometer-based behavioral program with face-to-face counseling and telephone follow-up increased steps and total physical activity and reduced sedentary behavior, with effects persisting at 1 year. It is adjacent because phone support and behavioral counseling are integral.

**Why it matters:** Shows a durable activity/sedentary behavior effect when step tracking is paired with support in T2D.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:daily-steps, biomarker:physical-activity-minutes, biomarker:sedentary-time.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent context that support-enhanced pedometer programs can improve behavior; do not attribute effects to step floor alone.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness boundary:** `adjacent_variant`. This source should not be promoted beyond that scope in the Daily Step Floor protocol.

**Safety/adverse events:** No adverse-event signal extracted from accessible abstract text.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Behavioral counseling and telephone support make it more intensive than a standalone step floor. Population mismatch: Type 2 diabetes with BMI/inclusion criteria; not general Daily Step Floor users.
