This source is included for **measurement_validity**.

**Findings:** An open-source smartphone step-counting algorithm showed small mean bias in cross-body validation and was reported as reliable across sensor locations and clinical/nonclinical scenarios.

**Why it matters:** Open algorithms can reduce ambiguity in step-count measurement compared with opaque proprietary trackers.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily_step_count, step_count_accuracy, device_placement_or_carriage, gait_speed_or_gait_aid_context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only evidence that transparent smartphone algorithms can be validated, while not equating them to all phone app counts.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Healthy adults and patients with cancer; participant counts for each validation scenario were not available in the accessible extraction.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Open-source step-counting method using raw subsecond smartphone accelerometer data.
- **Comparator/control:** Cross-body validation, visually assessed ground truth, and Fitbit Charge 2 comparison in patients with advanced cancer.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Multiple validation scenarios; exact durations not extracted.
- **Endpoints:** step-count bias, limits of agreement, sensor-location robustness, clinical/nonclinical generalizability
- **Effect estimates or direction:** In cross-body validation datasets, accessible abstract text reported mean 751.7 steps and mean bias −7.2 steps (−0.5%) with limits of agreement −47.6 to 33.3 steps. The authors concluded the method provided reliable step counts across sensor locations, scenarios, and populations.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse events or safety outcomes were reported in the accessible extract.
- **Limitations:** Participant counts and full subgroup details were not extracted.; Algorithm validation may not match proprietary consumer-device counts.; Clinical cancer scenario may not generalize to all Daily Step Floor users.
- **Population mismatch:** Algorithm measurement context; not a Daily Step Floor intervention.
- **Artifact rights:** open_access
