This source is included for **cardiometabolic_fitness_endpoints**.

**Findings:** A systematic review/meta-analysis in adults with cardiometabolic conditions found wearable tracker interventions were associated with increased physical activity, especially pedometer interventions and those with health-professional consultation. This is adjacent because it includes broader wearables and clinical/coaching components rather than a simple daily step floor.

**Why it matters:** Supports wearable-assisted activity change in cardiometabolic populations but underscores intervention support and heterogeneity.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:daily-steps, biomarker:physical-activity-minutes.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent context that trackers can improve PA in cardiometabolic populations, not as direct evidence that a step floor alone improves biomarkers.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness boundary:** `adjacent_variant`. This source should not be promoted beyond that scope in the Daily Step Floor protocol.

**Safety/adverse events:** No adverse-event signal extracted from accessible article sections reviewed.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** High heterogeneity, broad cardiometabolic populations, and combined tracker/coaching interventions; activity improvements may remain below public-health targets. Population mismatch: Adults with cardiometabolic conditions; broad tracker interventions are not identical to Daily Step Floor.
