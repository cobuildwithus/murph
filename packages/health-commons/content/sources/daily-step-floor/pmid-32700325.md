This source is included for **cardiometabolic_fitness_endpoints**.

**Findings:** A Cochrane review of workplace pedometer interventions found low/very-low certainty and little clear evidence for sustained physical activity or BP effects; BMI may be slightly reduced and injuries may be fewer, but evidence was limited. It directly concerns pedometer interventions but is mixed and cautious.

**Why it matters:** Sets a sober boundary for workplace pedometer claims and sustainability expectations.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:daily-steps, biomarker:bmi, biomarker:morning-blood-pressure, adverse-event:injury.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as mixed direct evidence: workplace pedometers may not reliably sustain activity or reduce BP; do not oversell workplace generalization.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness boundary:** `direct_protocol`. This source should not be promoted beyond that scope in the Daily Step Floor protocol.

**Safety/adverse events:** Adverse effects/injuries may have been reduced, but certainty was low.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Overall unclear/high risk of bias, high heterogeneity, and low/very-low certainty for several outcomes. Population mismatch: Workplace settings in high-income countries; not necessarily clinical or remote Daily Step Floor users.
