This source is included for **cadence_intensity_bouts**.

**Findings:** Overground validation in 174 adults aged 41-85 found >=100 steps/min classified moderate-intensity walking with about 75% accuracy.

**Why it matters:** Adds overground support for the cadence heuristic across the adult lifespan.

**Potential experiment signals:** walking_cadence, activity_intensity_classification, mvpa_minutes.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use >=100 spm as optional overground moderate-intensity context, not as a mandatory step-floor rule.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Adults aged 41-85 years (48.3% female) completing an indoor 5-minute overground preferred-pace walking trial.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Cadence during overground walking at self-selected preferred pace.
- **Comparator/control:** Indirect calorimetry-defined moderate intensity (>=3 METs).
- **Duration/follow-up:** Single 5-minute overground walking trial.
- **Endpoints:** Classification accuracy of >=100 steps/min for moderate-intensity overground walking.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Accessible summaries report that >=100 steps/min had overall accuracy of about 74.7% for classifying moderate intensity, with similar accuracy in middle-aged and older-aged adults.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse events or safety outcomes were reported in accessible summaries.
- **Limitations:** Single short lab-based overground trial; specificity was low in accessible summaries; not free-living; not intervention evidence.
- **Population mismatch:** Same-mechanism cadence validation only.
