This source is included for **mental_health_sleep_qol**.

**Findings:** In a 12-week RCT of 184 first-year university students, a pedometer intervention with logs/emails did not produce between-group differences in physical activity, health-related quality of life, or psychological well-being.

**Why it matters:** Direct pedometer RCT with null/mixed mental-health and QoL outcomes that should constrain claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:daily-steps, biomarker:quality-of-life, biomarker:psychological-well-being.

**Protocol takeaway:** A low-intensity pedometer intervention did not improve HRQoL or psychological well-being in first-year students versus control.
