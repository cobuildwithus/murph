This source is included for **baseline_plus_ramp_trials**.

**Findings:** A 14-week smartphone intervention analysis found adaptive goals and rewards associated with about +2650 steps/day from baseline to completion in 20 overweight sedentary adults.

**Why it matters:** Mechanistic/context source for adaptive goal trajectories rather than a standalone RCT claim.

**Potential experiment signals:** steps/day trajectory, adaptive goal size, reward points, satisfaction.

**Protocol takeaway:** Adaptive goals may produce nonlinear improvement; monitor plateaus and trajectory inflection.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extracted study details

- **Population:** Overweight sedentary adults; mostly female, mean BMI about 33.8.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Just Walk smartphone app with adaptive step goals and rewards based on baseline median steps and a pseudo-random algorithm.
- **Comparator or control:** No separate randomized comparator in this longitudinal analysis.
- **Duration or follow-up:** 14 weeks.
- **Endpoints:** daily steps, growth trajectory, acceptability/satisfaction
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Participants increased by about +2650 steps/day from baseline to completion (t=8.25, p<.01) with a nonlinear trajectory and inflection near the midpoint; the intervention was described as feasible with high satisfaction.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Adverse-event reporting was not extracted.
- **Limitations:** Small sample, no standalone randomized comparator for this analysis, rewards included.
- **Population mismatch:** Overweight sedentary adults, mostly female.
- **Directness to Daily Step Floor:** `direct_protocol`
