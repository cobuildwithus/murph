This source is included for **baseline_plus_ramp_trials**.

**Findings:** A practical target of 3000 steps in 30 minutes is used to translate moderate-intensity walking guidance into a pedometer-based cadence/bout goal.

**Why it matters:** Helps distinguish total daily step floors from cadence/intensity bouts.

**Potential experiment signals:** steps per minute, 30-minute bout completion, MVPA minutes.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use 3000-in-30 as an optional intensity signal, not as proof of a total daily step floor.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extracted study details

- **Population:** Community-dwelling adults, including 60 women.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Translation of moderate-intensity physical activity guidance into pedometer cadence/bout goals, especially 3000 steps in 30 minutes.
- **Comparator or control:** No intervention comparator; mechanistic/translation context.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Acute/measurement translation context.
- **Endpoints:** step cadence, moderate-intensity walking, steps in 30 minutes
- **Effect estimates or direction:** The source translates walking-intensity recommendations into a practical step target of about 3000 steps in 30 minutes; this is cadence/bout context rather than daily-floor efficacy evidence.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No safety outcome extracted.
- **Limitations:** Not a Daily Step Floor intervention trial; paywalled details.
- **Population mismatch:** General adult cadence context, not necessarily low-active or clinical.
