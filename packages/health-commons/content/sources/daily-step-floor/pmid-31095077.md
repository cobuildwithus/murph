This source is included for **dose_response_cut_points**.

**Findings:** The review framed daily step counts as an interpretable physical-activity exposure metric and summarized evidence linking step volume to health outcomes while noting evidence gaps around dose-response precision and intensity.

**Why it matters:** Supports the use of steps/day as a practical Murph tracking signal.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily step count, days meeting step floor, weekly mean steps, physical activity exposure measurement, health outcomes.

**Protocol takeaway:** Useful measurement context; does not establish causal floor targets.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Adults represented in studies reviewed for step-count exposure and health outcomes.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Daily step counts as an exposure metric for physical activity.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower versus higher daily step counts and related activity metrics.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Evidence review for the 2018 Physical Activity Guidelines Advisory Committee.
- **Endpoints:** daily step count, physical activity exposure measurement, health outcomes
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse-event signal specific to a step-floor intervention was extracted from this source in this batch.
- **Limitations:** Guideline evidence review context; heterogeneous studies and limited direct intervention evidence.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Measurement-focused evidence does not specify a Daily Step Floor behavioral protocol.
- **Boundary:** This source is observational, review-based, registry-only, or otherwise adjacent unless explicitly noted; it must not be promoted into direct Daily Step Floor protocol evidence.
