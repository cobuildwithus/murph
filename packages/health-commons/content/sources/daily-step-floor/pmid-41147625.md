This source is included for **safety_special_populations**.

**Findings:** In a 41-participant Mindful Steps pilot RCT, step differences favored intervention through 9 months but not at 12 months; one serious reportable possibly related noncardiac chest-pain event and several mild-to-moderate musculoskeletal events were reported.

**Why it matters:** Provides modern cardiopulmonary pilot data with explicit adverse-event reporting and a 12-month waning signal.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily-step-count, retention, chest pain, musculoskeletal pain, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Daily Step Floor should include chest-pain escalation and cardiopulmonary symptom off-ramps; do not use Mindful Steps as definitive efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness boundary:** clinical_supervised adjacent variant. Do not promote this source into direct Daily Step Floor claims beyond the stated claim-use boundary.

**Safety/adverse events:** One serious reportable event was judged possibly related: noncardiac chest pain in a heart-failure participant during 6-month testing; nonreportable expected possibly related events included mild-to-moderate musculoskeletal pain.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Small pilot feasibility trial; not powered as definitive efficacy or safety trial; pandemic-era adaptations and mixed cardiopulmonary sample. Chronic cardiopulmonary disease; safety-only special-population evidence.
