This source is included for **mental_health_sleep_qol**.

**Findings:** A 15-study dose-response meta-analysis of 191,130 adults found lower incident depression risk at half and full recommended physical-activity volumes versus no reported activity, with diminishing potential benefits at higher volumes.

**Why it matters:** Adjacent high-quality background for depression-prevention context, separated from step-specific claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:physical-activity, biomarker:depressive-symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Any increase in physical activity may be associated with lower depression risk, but this cannot be used as causal Daily Step Floor evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness boundary:** This source is classified as `adjacent_variant` for Daily Step Floor. Do not promote adjacent, observational, registry/protocol, or clinical-population findings into direct protocol claims.

**Safety/adverse events:** Not a safety or adverse-event source for daily step floors.
