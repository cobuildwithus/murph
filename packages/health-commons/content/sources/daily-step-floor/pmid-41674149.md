This source is included for **mental_health_sleep_qol**.

**Findings:** A smartphone longitudinal study of 181 participants with lifetime MDD and 195 controls found MDD participants had lower daily steps and that higher daily/weekly steps were associated with lower depressive symptom scores.

**Why it matters:** Recent same-mechanism evidence linking smartphone-recorded daily steps and depressive symptoms in MDD.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:daily-steps, biomarker:depressive-symptoms, biomarker:step-count-measurement-validity.

**Protocol takeaway:** Daily steps can function as a mental-health context signal, but this is not evidence that prescribing a step floor improves MDD.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness boundary:** This source is classified as `same_mechanism` for Daily Step Floor. Do not promote adjacent, observational, registry/protocol, or clinical-population findings into direct protocol claims.

**Safety/adverse events:** Observational app-monitoring study; adverse events were not applicable/extracted.
