This source is included for **sedentary_activity_pattern**.

**Findings:** Umbrella review of 51 systematic reviews and 302 unique primary studies found wearable devices may increase physical activity, including a median increase around 1312 steps/day and 57.8 MVPA minutes/week; sedentary-behavior evidence was less definitive and many reviews were critically low quality.

**Why it matters:** Helps calibrate confidence in wearable tracker claims and avoid overgeneralizing.

**Potential experiment signals:** steps, moderate-to-vigorous physical activity, sedentary behavior, review quality.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only evidence for wearable-device plausibility and evidence-quality caveats.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
