This source is included for **baseline_plus_ramp_trials**.

**Findings:** A 12-week RCT compared a 3000-steps-in-30-min cadence goal with self-selected and 10,000-step goals among Latina women, but detailed effects were not extracted here.

**Why it matters:** Important boundary source separating cadence goals from daily step floors.

**Potential experiment signals:** cadence minutes, MVPA, steps/day.

**Protocol takeaway:** Cadence goals are adjacent and should not be conflated with a total daily floor.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extracted study details

- **Population:** Latina women aged 18 to 55 years with mean BMI around 31, recruited through community centers.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Step cadence goal of 3000 steps in 30 minutes as a moderate-to-vigorous physical activity strategy.
- **Comparator or control:** Self-selected step goal and 10,000 steps/day goal arms.
- **Duration or follow-up:** 12 weeks.
- **Endpoints:** MVPA, daily steps, cadence goal adherence
- **Effect estimates or direction:** This trial compared cadence/intensity goals with other step goals; detailed between-group results were not extracted from accessible records.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Adverse-event reporting was not extracted.
- **Limitations:** Cadence/intensity focus and Latina women sample; not a total daily step-floor trial.
- **Population mismatch:** Community Latina women; cadence-focused intervention.
- **Directness to Daily Step Floor:** `adjacent_variant`
