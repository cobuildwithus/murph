This source is included for **guidelines_external_protocol_context**.

**Findings:** This guideline summary recommends physical activity using weekly minutes and intensity categories and emphasizes that moving more and sitting less benefits nearly everyone, without specifying a daily step floor.

**Why it matters:** Provides peer-reviewed summary of the official U.S. guideline that can contextualize a walking/steps protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** physical_activity_minutes, sedentary_time, muscle_strengthening, daily_step_count.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only official-guideline evidence; do not use it to claim a specific daily step floor is guideline-mandated.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Multiple population groups addressed by the Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans, including children, adolescents, adults, older adults, pregnant/postpartum women, and adults with chronic conditions or disabilities.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Guideline recommendations for aerobic activity, muscle strengthening, multicomponent activity for older adults, and moving more/sitting less; not a step-count intervention.
- **Comparator/control:** No comparator or control group; guideline summary.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Not applicable; guideline summary.
- **Endpoints:** physical activity minutes; activity intensity; muscle-strengthening activity; sedentary behaviour; health benefits from guideline evidence synthesis
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Summarizes guideline recommendations including adult weekly aerobic activity ranges and strengthening activities; no direct daily step-count threshold is tested.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse-event dataset; safety is addressed through guideline-level population-specific advice.
- **Limitations:** Guideline summary rather than a trial; not a Daily Step Floor intervention; does not establish a step-count dose-response.
- **Population mismatch:** General U.S. public-health guidance, not a wearable-tracked daily step-floor protocol.
