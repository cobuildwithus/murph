This source is included for **safety_special_populations**.

**Findings:** In an 18-participant maintenance cardiac rehab pilot, pedometer feedback with individualized step goals increased daily steps by about 42% over 8 weeks, while usual care did not change comparably.

**Why it matters:** Provides small-sample precedent for individualized step goals in maintenance cardiac rehab.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily-step-count, cardiac symptoms, exercise adherence, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only supervised cardiac rehab evidence; do not generalize to unscreened cardiac users.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness boundary:** clinical_supervised adjacent variant. Do not promote this source into direct Daily Step Floor claims beyond the stated claim-use boundary.

**Safety/adverse events:** Cardiac rehab population should remain medically supervised; source was not extracted as rare-event safety evidence.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Very small pilot sample; maintenance cardiac rehab context; not a community Daily Step Floor trial. Cardiac rehabilitation special population.
