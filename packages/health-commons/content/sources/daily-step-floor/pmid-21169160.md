This source is included for **direct_step_floor_trials**.

**Findings:**

- `finding:pmid-21169160-steps-no-between-arm` — Across both arms, intention-to-treat participants increased steps by an average of 1,888 ± 2,400 steps/day; the accessible source reported no significant between-arm difference in step change attributable to the online community.
- `finding:pmid-21169160-adherence-community` — Completion was 79% in the online community group versus 66% in the no-community group (P=.02), and community engagement was associated with lower dropout hazard (HR 0.47, 95% CI 0.25 to 0.90).

**Why it matters:** Same-mechanism evidence for self-monitoring, goal feedback, and social support around walking adherence.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- daily steps
- program completion
- engagement
- social support

**Protocol takeaway:** Online community may improve adherence/retention, but this source does not show a clear added step-count advantage over self-monitoring and goal feedback alone.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction boundaries

- **Directness:** `same_mechanism`.
- **Population:** Sedentary ambulatory adults with BMI ≥25, type 2 diabetes, or coronary artery disease risk context
- **Intervention/exposure:** Enhanced pedometer, weekly goals, graphs and tailored messages plus online community.
- **Comparator/control:** Enhanced pedometer, weekly goals, graphs and tailored messages without online community.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 16 weeks
- **Endpoints extracted:** daily steps, program completion, website engagement, social support.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event signal was extracted from the accessible source record; absence of extracted adverse events should not be interpreted as proof of no adverse events unless the finding explicitly says so.
- **Limitations:** Overweight or clinical-risk population; bundled walking program in both arms; community effect is adherence-focused, not a clean step-floor effect.
- **Population mismatch:** Participants had BMI/clinical-risk inclusion criteria rather than being general wellness users.
- **Artifact/rights boundary:** `open_access`; no copyrighted PDF is stored in Git in this draft.
