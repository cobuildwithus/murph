This source is included for **direct_step_floor_trials**.

**Findings:**

- `finding:pmid-31441936-constant-incentive` — During the intervention, pooled treatment arms added 135.0 daily steps versus control; constant incentives generated 306.7 more steps/day versus control, decreasing incentives 96.9 more, and increasing incentives no significant change in the accessible abstract-level source.
- `finding:pmid-31441936-dissipated` — The accessible source reported no significant differences in steps in the weeks after incentives ended, indicating quick dissipation.

**Why it matters:** Adjacent incentive-structure evidence that highlights short-lived step increases and the need to separate incentives from step floors.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- daily steps
- post-incentive maintenance
- cost per additional step

**Protocol takeaway:** Constant incentives can briefly increase daily steps, but incentive effects dissipated quickly; not direct evidence for a no-incentive step floor.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction boundaries

- **Directness:** `adjacent_variant`.
- **Population:** Users of an online platform that records daily steps and awards redeemable points
- **Intervention/exposure:** 20-fold increased per-step incentive distributed at constant, increasing, or decreasing rates over 2 weeks.
- **Comparator/control:** Control participants receiving a very small constant per-step rate.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 2-week intervention plus postintervention observation
- **Endpoints extracted:** daily steps, postintervention maintenance, cost-effectiveness per dollar.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event signal was extracted from the accessible source record; absence of extracted adverse events should not be interpreted as proof of no adverse events unless the finding explicitly says so.
- **Limitations:** Very brief two-week intervention; platform-user population; incentives paid per step rather than a floor threshold; limited demographics.
- **Population mismatch:** Online platform users, not a general health-coaching sample.
- **Artifact/rights boundary:** `open_access`; no copyrighted PDF is stored in Git in this draft.
