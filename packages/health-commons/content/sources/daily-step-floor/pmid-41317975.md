This source is included for **sedentary_activity_pattern**.

**Findings:** The intervention improved total physical activity and daily step count and reduced the frequency and total duration of ≥30-minute sedentary bouts; no significant changes were found for total sedentary time or sedentary breaks in the accessible abstract.

**Why it matters:** Relevant to activity-pattern endpoints because it distinguishes prolonged-bout change from total sedentary time.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily step count, total physical activity, total sedentary time, frequency of ≥30-minute sedentary bouts, duration of ≥30-minute sedentary bouts, sedentary breaks.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only adjacent evidence for wearable feedback and bout reduction, not direct step-floor efficacy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Healthy older women, mean age about 72.6 years, in a randomized wearable-feedback trial.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Real-time activity feedback via wearable accelerometer plus 12-week behavior-change intervention including PA/SB education, exercise consultation, movement notifications, and goal setting.
- **Comparator/control:** Control group maintaining usual lifestyle while wearing an accelerometer without feedback.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 12 weeks
- **Endpoints:** daily step count, total physical activity, total sedentary time, frequency of ≥30-minute sedentary bouts, duration of ≥30-minute sedentary bouts, sedentary breaks
- **Effect estimates or direction:** The intervention improved total physical activity and daily step count and reduced the frequency and total duration of ≥30-minute sedentary bouts; no significant changes were found for total sedentary time or sedentary breaks in the accessible abstract.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Adverse events were not extracted from the accessible abstract.
- **Limitations:** Small sample of healthy older women.; Intervention combined feedback, notifications, education, consultation, and goal setting.; No significant total sedentary-time change was reported despite reduced prolonged-bout outcomes.
- **Population mismatch:** Adjacent wearable feedback/BCT RCT, not Daily Step Floor.
