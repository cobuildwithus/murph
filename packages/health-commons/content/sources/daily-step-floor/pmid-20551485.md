This source is included for **mental_health_sleep_qol**.

**Findings:** In a small 15-week RCT of sedentary adults, a prescribed 10,000-step/day walking program produced significant group-by-time effects for postexercise heart rate, total cholesterol, and personal growth; other functional and well-being measures were favorable but not clearly significant.

**Why it matters:** A direct fixed 10,000-step/day prescription with psychological well-being outcomes, useful as foundational but small trial evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:daily-steps, biomarker:psychological-well-being, biomarker:functional-fitness.

**Protocol takeaway:** A prescribed daily step goal can be associated with improved personal-growth well-being and selected physical outcomes in sedentary adults, but the small sample and limited adverse-event extraction require cautious wording.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
