This source is included for **measurement_validity**.

**Findings:** In daily-life validation, four smart devices showed strong correlations and good agreement for step counts at day and 15-minute levels, but MVPA validity was weaker and wearables systematically overestimated steps in Bland-Altman analyses.

**Why it matters:** Daily Step Floor outcomes depend on step accuracy at day and subday scales.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily_step_count, step_count_accuracy, device_placement_or_carriage, gait_speed_or_gait_aid_context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only evidence that step estimates can show good agreement while MVPA estimates may be less reliable and device bias can remain.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Free-living adults; participant count was not available in the accessible extraction.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Four consumer-level smart devices measuring steps and MVPA in daily life.
- **Comparator/control:** Reference physical-activity measurement used in the validation study.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Daily-life monitoring; exact duration not available in accessible extraction.
- **Endpoints:** daily steps, 15-minute step estimates, MVPA estimates, agreement/correlation
- **Effect estimates or direction:** On a day level, devices showed strong step correlations (Spearman r=.757-.892) and good step agreement (ICC=.695-.885). On a 15-minute level, step correlations remained strong (r=.752-.917) with good agreement (ICC=.792-.887). MVPA estimates showed weaker validity, and Bland-Altman analyses indicated systematic step overestimation by wearables.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse events or safety outcomes were reported in the accessible extract.
- **Limitations:** Participant count and full device protocol were not extracted.; Smart-device firmware and algorithms change over time.; Step validity was better than MVPA validity.
- **Population mismatch:** Measurement-validation context only; no Daily Step Floor intervention.
- **Artifact rights:** open_access
