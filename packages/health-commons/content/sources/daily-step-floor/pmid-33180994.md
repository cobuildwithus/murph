This source is included for **mental_health_sleep_qol**.

**Findings:** The SAD protocol planned a 400-patient multicenter RCT comparing treatment as usual with treatment as usual plus pedometer intervention, with primary outcomes of clinician-rated depression and accelerometer-measured steps.

**Why it matters:** Defines the SAD pedometer intervention, outcomes, and motivation/volition measures for linking to results.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:daily-steps, biomarker:depressive-symptoms, biomarker:motivation, biomarker:quality-of-life.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to understand trial design and planned endpoints, not as efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Safety/adverse events:** Protocol extraction did not identify adverse-event results; not a safety outcome source.
