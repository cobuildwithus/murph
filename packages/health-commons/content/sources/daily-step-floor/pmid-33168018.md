This source is included for **cadence_intensity_bouts**.

**Findings:** CADENCE-Adults middle-aged adult study supported 100 and 130 steps/min as heuristic thresholds for moderate and vigorous absolute intensity.

**Why it matters:** Shows the young-adult cadence heuristic remains broadly supported through middle age.

**Potential experiment signals:** walking_cadence, mvpa_minutes, vigorous_activity, activity_intensity_classification.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use 100 spm as optional moderate-intensity context for middle-aged adults, not as a required step-floor element.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Healthy adults aged 41-60 years in the CADENCE-Adults treadmill calibration study.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Observed cadence during treadmill walking with indirect calorimetry.
- **Comparator/control:** Regression and ROC thresholds for 3 METs and 6 METs.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Single laboratory visit with sequential 5-minute treadmill bouts.
- **Endpoints:** Cadence thresholds for absolute moderate and vigorous ambulatory intensity.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Accessible summaries report cadence thresholds of about 100 and 130 spm as heuristic values associated with 3 and 6 METs in adults aged 41-60 years.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse events or safety outcomes were reported in accessible summaries.
- **Limitations:** Healthy middle-aged adults; treadmill setting; not free-living; not a Daily Step Floor intervention.
- **Population mismatch:** Same-mechanism cadence calibration only.
