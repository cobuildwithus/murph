This source is included for **mental_health_sleep_qol**.

**Findings:** In a 45-randomized/26-analyzed COPD pilot RCT, a 6-week pedometer step-target program increased daily steps and improved COPD Assessment Test scores versus control, but dropout was high and several clinical endpoints were not significantly different between groups.

**Why it matters:** Direct step-target walking program with QoL-related COPD symptom endpoint and adherence burden.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:daily-steps, biomarker:quality-of-life, biomarker:dyspnea, biomarker:adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** A gradual pedometer step-target program improved steps and CAT in analyzed COPD patients, but high dropout and clinical context limit generalization.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness boundary:** This source is classified as `direct_protocol` for Daily Step Floor. Do not promote adjacent, observational, registry/protocol, or clinical-population findings into direct protocol claims.

**Safety/adverse events:** High dropout occurred; dropouts were attributed to comorbidities or lack of motivation in the open article, not clearly to intervention-related adverse events.
