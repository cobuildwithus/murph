This source is included for **cardiometabolic_fitness_endpoints**.

**Findings:** A 2024 systematic review/meta-analysis of pedometer/accelerometer interventions in type 2 diabetes reported improvements in HbA1c, triglycerides, and step counts, while calling for larger higher-quality studies. It is direct but clinical-population and heterogeneity caveats remain.

**Why it matters:** Most recent meta-analysis in the batch with positive glycemic, triglyceride, and step-count signals for T2D.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:daily-steps, biomarker:hba1c, biomarker:triglycerides.

**Protocol takeaway:** Can support a clinical T2D context claim that step-monitor interventions may improve HbA1c and triglycerides, while noting heterogeneity and need for better trials.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness boundary:** `direct_protocol`. This source should not be promoted beyond that scope in the Daily Step Floor protocol.

**Safety/adverse events:** No adverse-event signal extracted from accessible abstract text.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Heterogeneity, type 2 diabetes population, and authors’ call for larger and higher-quality studies. Population mismatch: Type 2 diabetes clinical trial population; not general-population Daily Step Floor evidence.
