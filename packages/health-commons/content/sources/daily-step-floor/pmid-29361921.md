This source is included for **mental_health_sleep_qol**.

**Findings:** In a 100-day 10,000-step challenge analytic sample of 1,963 adults, depression, anxiety, stress, and well-being improved from baseline, but step averages were not significantly correlated with mental-health change scores.

**Why it matters:** Direct 10,000-step challenge with mental-health outcomes, while preserving a major causal limitation.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:daily-steps, biomarker:depressive-symptoms, biomarker:anxiety, biomarker:stress, biomarker:well-being.

**Protocol takeaway:** A 10,000-step challenge coincided with better DASS-21 and well-being scores, but without randomization and without step-change correlation it should be framed as supportive but not causal.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
