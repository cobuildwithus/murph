This source is included for **mental_health_sleep_qol**.

**Findings:** A longitudinal actigraphy substudy in 66 Veterans with MDD reported associations between higher daily steps and lower depressive-symptom severity, but adjusted-model significance was not clear from accessible summaries.

**Why it matters:** Same-mechanism context in a depressed clinical population, with uncertainty about adjusted estimates.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:daily-steps, biomarker:depressive-symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Daily steps may track with depressive-symptom severity in MDD, but this is not evidence that a step floor treats depression.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness boundary:** This source is classified as `same_mechanism` for Daily Step Floor. Do not promote adjacent, observational, registry/protocol, or clinical-population findings into direct protocol claims.

**Safety/adverse events:** Observational exposure study; adverse events were not applicable/extracted.
