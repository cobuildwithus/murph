This source is included for **dose_response_cut_points**.

**Findings:** The review included 11 systematic reviews with meta-analyses and 14 cohort studies and reported a nonlinear association between daily steps and all-cause mortality, with an updated pooled HR of 0.91 per 1000 steps/day and a minimum protective threshold around 3143 steps/day.

**Why it matters:** Recent umbrella context on variability of mortality estimates and threshold claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily step count, days meeting step floor, weekly mean steps, all-cause mortality.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to communicate uncertainty and variability in step thresholds.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Systematic reviews and cohort studies of adult daily steps and all-cause mortality.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Daily step count categories and increments.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower step-count reference groups.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Searches through May 2024; follow-up varied by included cohort.
- **Endpoints:** all-cause mortality, daily step count
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse-event signal specific to a step-floor intervention was extracted from this source in this batch.
- **Limitations:** Umbrella review with overlapping reviews and cohort heterogeneity; observational evidence cannot establish causality.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Mortality synthesis is context-only for a behavioral floor protocol.
- **Boundary:** This source is observational, review-based, registry-only, or otherwise adjacent unless explicitly noted; it must not be promoted into direct Daily Step Floor protocol evidence.
