This source is included for **direct_step_floor_trials**.

**Findings:**

- `finding:pmid-26881417-loss-framing-goal` — Mean participant-day goal achievement was reported as 0.30 for control, 0.35 for gain, 0.36 for lottery, and 0.45 for loss-framed incentives, indicating the strongest effect for loss framing.

**Why it matters:** Adjacent variant with an explicit daily step-goal threshold; helps separate step-floor effects from financial framing.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- step-goal attainment
- daily steps
- post-incentive maintenance

**Protocol takeaway:** Loss framing improved goal attainment, but the result belongs to an incentive-framed 7,000-step variant rather than a simple Daily Step Floor.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction boundaries

- **Directness:** `adjacent_variant`.
- **Population:** Overweight and obese adults
- **Intervention/exposure:** Daily feedback plus gain-framed, lottery-framed, or loss-framed financial incentives for achieving a 7,000-step goal.
- **Comparator/control:** Daily feedback control with the same 7,000-step goal.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 13-week intervention plus follow-up in the original trial
- **Endpoints extracted:** days achieving 7,000-step goal, daily steps.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event signal was extracted from the accessible source record; absence of extracted adverse events should not be interpreted as proof of no adverse events unless the finding explicitly says so.
- **Limitations:** Incentive framing is bundled with the daily goal; overweight/obese sample; goal was 7,000 steps, not necessarily 10,000.
- **Population mismatch:** Overweight and obese adult sample; external validity to non-incentivized wellness users is limited.
