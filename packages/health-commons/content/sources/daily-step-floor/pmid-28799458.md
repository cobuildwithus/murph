This source is included for **mental_health_sleep_qol**.

**Findings:** In a 37-recruited/30-completed asthma RCT, pedometer-supported individualized step targets increased daily steps and 6MWT at 12 weeks, but differences were no longer significant at 24-28 weeks.

**Why it matters:** Direct pedometer step-target RCT with psychological endpoints listed and a clear sustainability limitation.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:daily-steps, biomarker:walking-capacity, biomarker:quality-of-life, biomarker:anxiety, biomarker:depressive-symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Pedometer targets increased steps short term in moderate-to-severe asthma, but sustained benefit was not shown at later follow-up.
