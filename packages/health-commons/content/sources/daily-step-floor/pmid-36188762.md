This source is included for **measurement_validity**.

**Findings:** Among people with multiple sclerosis, several commercial wearables showed poor criterion validity for step count/activity minutes, and perceived inaccuracies contributed to distrust and frustration.

**Why it matters:** Inaccurate feedback can undermine both measurement and user trust in a step-floor protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily_step_count, step_count_accuracy, device_placement_or_carriage, gait_speed_or_gait_aid_context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only evidence that MS populations may need validated devices and careful acceptability monitoring.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Nineteen ambulatory adults with multiple sclerosis, mean age 52.1 years; 15 also completed 3-day acceptability testing/interviews.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Fitbit Alta, Fitbit Zip, Garmin Vivofit 4, Yamax Digi-Walker SW200, Letscom monitor, and activPAL3μ during nine everyday activities.
- **Comparator/control:** Manually counted steps; ActiGraph GT3X for activity minutes.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Nine everyday activities plus three consecutive days of acceptability wear for a subset.
- **Endpoints:** step-count mean percentage error, activity-minute error, device acceptability, user trust/frustration
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Mean percentage error for step count ranged from 12.1% for Yamax SW200 to −112.3% for Letscom. Mean step count differed from manual counts for Fitbit Alta, Garmin Vivofit 4, Letscom, and activPAL3μ. Commercial devices demonstrated poor criterion validity for step count and activity time, and perceived inaccuracies affected acceptability.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse events were reported; negative acceptability experiences included distrust/frustration from perceived inaccuracies.
- **Limitations:** Small MS sample.; Everyday-activity protocol and short acceptability wear may not capture all free-living contexts.; Device findings are model-specific.
- **Population mismatch:** Multiple sclerosis measurement/acceptability context; no step-floor intervention.
- **Artifact rights:** open_access
