This source is included for **cardiometabolic_fitness_endpoints**.

**Findings:** The Sophia Step Study tested self-monitoring of daily steps with and without counselling for 24 months in prediabetes/type 2 diabetes. It prevented decline or increased MVPA but did not improve metabolic control or cardiometabolic risk factors, and no unintended effects were reported.

**Why it matters:** One of the longest direct step self-monitoring trials; it preserves a mixed signal across behavior and metabolic endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:daily-steps, biomarker:physical-activity-minutes, biomarker:hba1c, biomarker:cardiometabolic-risk.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to support PA/MVPA behavior outcomes while clearly stating no demonstrated metabolic-control benefit in this trial.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness boundary:** `direct_protocol`. This source should not be promoted beyond that scope in the Daily Step Floor protocol.

**Safety/adverse events:** No unintended effects reported in extracted summary.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Some participants already active; accelerometer limits; repeated assessment influence; possible diffusion; dropouts less healthy; underpowered and mixed prediabetes/T2D population. Population mismatch: Prediabetes/T2D adults, not a general daily-step-floor sample.
