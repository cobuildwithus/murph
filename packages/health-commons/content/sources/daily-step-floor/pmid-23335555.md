This source is included for **cadence_intensity_bouts**.

**Findings:** Cross-sectional calibration in 226 Chinese young adults suggested >=105 steps/min for a 30-minute moderate-intensity walking recommendation.

**Why it matters:** Shows cadence thresholds can vary by population and protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** walking_cadence, mvpa_minutes, activity_intensity_classification.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context that the common 100 spm threshold is not universal; avoid rigid cadence prescriptions.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Chinese young adults (117 men and 109 women; mean age about 22 years) completing overground walking trials.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Step rate during overground walking at graded speeds.
- **Comparator/control:** Energy-expenditure-defined physical-activity guidelines/intensity thresholds.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Single field/lab protocol with four 6-minute overground walking speeds.
- **Endpoints:** Step-rate cut point for moderate-intensity walking and practical walking recommendation.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Accessible summaries report a recommendation for Chinese young adults to walk at least 30 minutes at >=105 steps/min, equivalent to about 3150 steps or 2 km at the same step rate per day.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse events or safety outcomes were reported in accessible summaries.
- **Limitations:** Young Chinese adult population; cross-sectional calibration; overground protocol but not free-living; threshold differs from 100 spm heuristic.
- **Population mismatch:** Adjacent population-specific calibration, not direct Daily Step Floor evidence.
