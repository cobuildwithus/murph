This source is included for **baseline_plus_ramp_trials**.

**Findings:** A randomized study assigned 96 middle-aged adults to different step-goal difficulty groups to test daily steps and goal achievement, but detailed effects were not extracted in this run.

**Why it matters:** Implementation context for goal variation and achievement frequency.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily steps, goal achievement, goal difficulty group.

**Protocol takeaway:** Goal variation is an implementation variable; direct claims require full effect extraction.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extracted study details

- **Population:** Middle-aged adults.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Assignment to Easy, Medium, Difficult, Do-your-best, or No-goal step goal groups.
- **Comparator or control:** Different goal-difficulty and no-goal groups.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Duration not extracted from accessible records.
- **Endpoints:** daily steps, goal achievement frequency, goal difficulty
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Ninety-six adults were randomized across five goal groups (Easy n=19, Medium n=19, Difficult n=19, Do-your-best n=19, No goal n=20). Detailed between-group step results were not extracted here; accessible snippets indicate the study evaluated how varying goal levels affect steps and achievement.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Adverse-event reporting was not extracted.
- **Limitations:** Permission-required article; effect estimates not extracted; goal-difficulty context rather than clinical endpoint trial.
- **Population mismatch:** Middle-aged adults; not necessarily low-active or clinical.
