This source is included for **cadence_intensity_bouts**.

**Findings:** Overground validation in 75 young adults found that >=100 steps/min classified minimally moderate intensity with about 73% accuracy and 80% positive predictive value.

**Why it matters:** Extends treadmill cadence heuristics into overground walking, a closer context for daily walking.

**Potential experiment signals:** walking_cadence, activity_intensity_classification, mvpa_minutes.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use >=100 spm as optional overground intensity context in young adults, with classification caveats.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Adults aged 21-40 years completing a 5-minute overground self-selected walking trial.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Overground walking cadence at self-selected preferred pace.
- **Comparator/control:** Indirect calorimetry-defined minimally moderate intensity (>=3 METs).
- **Duration/follow-up:** Single 5-minute overground walking trial.
- **Endpoints:** Classification accuracy of >=100 steps/min for minimally moderate-intensity overground walking.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** A >=100 steps/min threshold had overall accuracy of 73.3% and positive predictive value of 80.3% for predicting >=3 METs in accessible summaries.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse events or safety outcomes were reported in accessible summaries.
- **Limitations:** Young healthy adult sample; single overground preferred-pace trial; specificity limitations; not free-living or intervention evidence.
- **Population mismatch:** Same-mechanism measurement evidence, not direct Daily Step Floor efficacy evidence.
