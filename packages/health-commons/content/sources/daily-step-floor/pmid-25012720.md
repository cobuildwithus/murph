This source is included for **safety_special_populations**.

**Findings:** A systematic review of seven pedometer intervention studies in musculoskeletal disease reported step-count increases but limited adverse-event reporting, requiring caution for pain populations.

**Why it matters:** Summarizes pedometer intervention evidence in pain/musculoskeletal populations where step increases may provoke symptoms.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily-step-count, pain intensity, function, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Daily Step Floor should not assume musculoskeletal-disease users can follow generic floors without pain/function monitoring.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness boundary:** clinical_supervised adjacent variant. Do not promote this source into direct Daily Step Floor claims beyond the stated claim-use boundary.

**Safety/adverse events:** Musculoskeletal populations require pain-flare monitoring; the review did not provide strong adverse-event quantification.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Few heterogeneous studies; varied conditions and intervention designs; limited adverse-event reporting. Musculoskeletal disease population; adjacent evidence, not general protocol proof.
