This source is included for **mental_health_sleep_qol**.

**Findings:** In a 12-week workplace RCT, a pedometer-driven walking program substantially increased daily steps and self-reported activity, but SF-36 physical and mental component outcomes were not significantly different.

**Why it matters:** Direct workplace pedometer RCT preserving the mixed signal that steps improved strongly while HRQoL did not.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:daily-steps, biomarker:quality-of-life, biomarker:adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** A pedometer walking program can substantially raise steps in a workplace, but quality-of-life gains should not be assumed.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
