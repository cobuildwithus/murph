This source is included for **direct_step_floor_trials**.

**Findings:**

- `finding:pmid-28973115-gamification-intervention` — During the 12-week intervention, the gamification arm achieved step goals on 0.53 versus 0.32 participant-days and had a greater mean daily-step increase from baseline than control (1,661 versus 636; adjusted difference 953 steps/day, 95% CI 505 to 1,401).
- `finding:pmid-28973115-gamification-followup` — During follow-up, effects attenuated but remained higher than control for step-goal achievement (0.44 versus 0.33; adjusted difference 0.12) and daily steps (adjusted difference 494 steps/day, 95% CI 170 to 818).

**Why it matters:** Adjacent social/gamification variant using step goals; important for adherence mechanics but not a simple floor.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- step-goal attainment
- daily steps
- follow-up maintenance

**Protocol takeaway:** Gamification increased step-goal achievement and daily steps versus feedback control, but it is an adjacent social-incentive design.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
