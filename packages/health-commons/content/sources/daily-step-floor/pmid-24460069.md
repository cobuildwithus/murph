This source is included for **safety_special_populations**.

**Findings:** A pilot randomized study during adjuvant chemotherapy reported that a 10-week daily walking target of 10,000 steps plus weekly supervised walking was feasible with high adherence, but active treatment symptoms make this a supervised special-population context.

**Why it matters:** Highlights feasibility and symptom constraints for high step targets during active cancer treatment.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily-step-count, adherence, fatigue, pain, chemotherapy symptoms, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Daily Step Floor should not apply generic floors during chemotherapy without oncology-team clearance and symptom off-ramps.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness boundary:** clinical_supervised special-population context. Do not promote this source into direct Daily Step Floor claims beyond the stated claim-use boundary.

**Safety/adverse events:** Chemotherapy patients may have fatigue, infection risk, anemia, neuropathy, and symptom fluctuations; walking goals need oncology clearance and symptom monitoring.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Pilot study; participant count not extracted here; chemotherapy special population; not direct Daily Step Floor evidence. Cancer chemotherapy population; safety-only/low-priority context for general protocol.
