This source is included for **cadence_intensity_bouts**.

**Findings:** Cadence-threshold study in 48 Taiwanese obese young adults found sex-specific moderate-to-vigorous thresholds above the generic 100 spm heuristic.

**Why it matters:** Reinforces that cadence targets may need population-specific calibration when used for intensity.

**Potential experiment signals:** walking_cadence, mvpa_minutes, sex_specific_thresholds, activity_intensity_classification.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not make a universal cadence prescription; use cadence only as optional individualized context.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Taiwanese obese young adults in an incremental walking protocol.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Walking cadence during incremental walking speeds, with sex-specific threshold modeling.
- **Comparator/control:** Moderate and vigorous intensity thresholds by sex and individualized criteria.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Single incremental walking protocol across 3.2 to 6.4 km/h speeds; exact bout duration not extracted.
- **Endpoints:** Sex-specific cadence thresholds for moderate and vigorous intensity.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Accessible abstract reports thresholds for males of 114 spm for MPA, 119 spm for MPA_young, and 124 spm for VPA; for females, 115, 125, and 131 spm respectively; practical ranges were 119-124 spm for males and 125-131 spm for females.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse events or safety outcomes were reported in accessible summaries.
- **Limitations:** Small sample; Taiwanese obese young adults only; lab/incremental walking design; population-specific thresholds may not generalize.
- **Population mismatch:** Adjacent population-specific calibration, not direct Daily Step Floor evidence.
