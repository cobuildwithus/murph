This source is included for **safety_special_populations**.

**Findings:** In the 12-month COPD follow-up, between-group differences in daily steps and SGRQ total were not significant at 12 months; the initial step advantage faded during maintenance.

**Why it matters:** Preserves the negative/mixed durability finding for COPD pedometer walking.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily-step-count, quality-of-life, engagement, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Daily Step Floor should not infer durable COPD step gains without maintenance support; monitor drop-off and symptoms.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness boundary:** clinical_supervised adjacent variant. Do not promote this source into direct Daily Step Floor claims beyond the stated claim-use boundary.

**Safety/adverse events:** All self-reported adverse events were recorded; COPD symptom safety remains relevant even when efficacy wanes.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Engagement declined over time; mostly male veteran COPD sample; not a simple step floor. COPD clinical population; adjacent safety/context only.
