This source is included for **cardiometabolic_fitness_endpoints**.

**Findings:** A controlled study in preclinical medical students found pedometers/Fitbits were perceived as motivating, but adherence declined and 1-year run-time differences were not statistically significant. This is adjacent negative/neutral fitness evidence.

**Why it matters:** Important null/neutral evidence for cardiorespiratory-fitness claims and adherence decay.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:daily-steps, biomarker:estimated-vo2max, biomarker:cardiorespiratory-fitness.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not claim pedometry alone prevents fitness decline; monitor adherence and fitness separately.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness boundary:** `adjacent_variant`. This source should not be promoted beyond that scope in the Daily Step Floor protocol.

**Safety/adverse events:** No adverse-event signal extracted from accessible abstract text.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Student sample, adherence decay, and no significant fitness effect. Population mismatch: Young medical students differ from general, older, or clinical cardiometabolic populations.
