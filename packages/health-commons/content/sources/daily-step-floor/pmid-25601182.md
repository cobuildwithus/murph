This source is included for **mental_health_sleep_qol**.

**Findings:** A 42-study review of outdoor walking groups found improvements in several health outcomes and reduced depression symptoms, with high adherence, low attrition, and no notable adverse effects reported in accessible summaries.

**Why it matters:** Adjacent evidence for walking’s mental-health and adherence effects, with safety context.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:depressive-symptoms, biomarker:quality-of-life, biomarker:adherence, biomarker:adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Group walking has broad health and depression-symptom benefits, but these should not be imported as exact Daily Step Floor effects.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness boundary:** This source is classified as `adjacent_variant` for Daily Step Floor. Do not promote adjacent, observational, registry/protocol, or clinical-population findings into direct protocol claims.

**Safety/adverse events:** The review reported high adherence, low attrition, and no notable adverse effects in accessible summaries.
