This source is included for **direct_step_floor_trials**.

**Findings:**

- `finding:pmid-16979410-activity-increase` — The active group increased physical activity by 69% (P=.002), whereas the control group did not change from baseline.
- `finding:pmid-16979410-mixed-metabolic` — HDL cholesterol and resting energy expenditure increased (P<.05) and PAI-1 was reduced versus control (P=.03), but the accessible abstract reported no differences between groups in other study parameters over 6 weeks.

**Why it matters:** Direct 10,000-step prescription in a clinical metabolic population; useful for behavior and cardiometabolic-boundary evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- daily steps
- HbA1c or glucose context
- HDL cholesterol
- resting energy expenditure
- PAI-1

**Protocol takeaway:** The 10,000-step prescription increased activity in type 2 diabetes, but clinical endpoint effects were mixed and population-specific.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction boundaries

- **Directness:** `direct_protocol`.
- **Population:** Adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus, free from advanced complications, on oral therapy
- **Intervention/exposure:** Walk at least 10,000 steps/day on 5 or more days per week using a pedometer.
- **Comparator/control:** Continue baseline activity for 6 weeks.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 10-day baseline plus 6-week intervention
- **Endpoints extracted:** daily steps, glycemic control, HDL cholesterol, resting energy expenditure, PAI-1, cardiovascular risk markers.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event signal was extracted from the accessible source record; absence of extracted adverse events should not be interpreted as proof of no adverse events unless the finding explicitly says so.
- **Limitations:** Small n=30 trial; 6-week duration; type 2 diabetes population; accessible record reported no differences in several unspecified parameters.
- **Population mismatch:** Clinical type 2 diabetes sample, not a general wellness population.
