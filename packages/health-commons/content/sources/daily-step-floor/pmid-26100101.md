This source is included for **mental_health_sleep_qol**.

**Findings:** In a 12-week RCT of 106 postmenopausal women, pedometer-based walking with gradual step increases was associated with improved monthly step counts and lower depression, anxiety, and insomnia scores versus control in accessible abstracts.

**Why it matters:** Direct pedometer walking RCT with depression, anxiety, and insomnia outcomes in a population often affected by sleep symptoms.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:daily-steps, biomarker:depressive-symptoms, biomarker:anxiety, biomarker:insomnia.

**Protocol takeaway:** Pedometer-supported walking may improve depression, anxiety, and insomnia symptoms in postmenopausal women, but the population is narrow and safety extraction is incomplete.
