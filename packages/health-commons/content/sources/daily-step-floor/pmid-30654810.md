This source is included for **cadence_intensity_bouts**.

**Findings:** CADENCE-Adults young-adult treadmill study selected 100 and 130 steps/min as heuristic thresholds for moderate and vigorous absolutely-defined intensity.

**Why it matters:** Provides strong calibration support for optional cadence labels in younger adults.

**Potential experiment signals:** walking_cadence, mvpa_minutes, vigorous_activity, activity_intensity_classification.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use 100 spm and 130 spm as optional younger-adult intensity context, not as required Daily Step Floor rules.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Healthy adults aged 21-40 years in the CADENCE-Adults treadmill calibration study.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Observed cadence during treadmill walking with indirect calorimetry.
- **Comparator/control:** Cadence thresholds derived from regression and ROC models for 3 METs and 6 METs.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Single laboratory visit with sequential 5-minute treadmill bouts.
- **Endpoints:** Cadence thresholds for absolutely-defined moderate and vigorous ambulatory intensity.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Regression thresholds were 102 spm for 3 METs and 129 spm for 6 METs; ROC thresholds were 96 and 120 spm; heuristic values of 100 and 130 spm were selected for moderate and vigorous intensity.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse events or safety outcomes were reported in the accessible extract.
- **Limitations:** Healthy young adults; treadmill setting; controlled bouts; not a clinical or free-living step-floor intervention.
- **Population mismatch:** Same-mechanism cadence calibration only; not direct Daily Step Floor evidence.
