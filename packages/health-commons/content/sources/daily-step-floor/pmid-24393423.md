This source is included for **baseline_plus_ramp_trials**.

**Findings:** The SMARTER design paper specifies a physician-delivered step-count prescription model for adults with diabetes and hypertension, with cardiometabolic endpoints planned.

**Why it matters:** Clarifies clinical implementation details and supervised boundaries for step prescriptions.

**Potential experiment signals:** steps/day, blood pressure, HbA1c, arterial stiffness, waist circumference.

**Protocol takeaway:** Clinical step prescriptions should be framed separately from unsupervised consumer step floors.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extracted study details

- **Population:** Adults with type 2 diabetes and hypertension or related cardiometabolic risk targeted by the SMARTER trial.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Physician step-count prescription and monitoring through pedometer records, with planned step-count increases.
- **Comparator or control:** Control condition in planned randomized trial.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Planned 1-year clinical trial context.
- **Endpoints:** steps/day, blood pressure, arterial stiffness, HbA1c, body weight, waist circumference
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Protocol/design source; no efficacy result.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Clinical eligibility and supervision context; detailed adverse-event results not available because this is a design paper.
- **Limitations:** Protocol-only source and clinical-supervised context.
- **Population mismatch:** Diabetes/hypertension clinical population; not low-risk consumer setting.
