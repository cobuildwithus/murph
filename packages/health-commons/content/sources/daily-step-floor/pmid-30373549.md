This source is included for **measurement_validity**.

**Findings:** In patients with cancer, smartphone step counts were feasible, valid against accelerometer, and reliable, but detecting real change required about a 2300-step difference in the accessible extract.

**Why it matters:** Smartphone steps may be usable in clinical monitoring, but change thresholds and completeness matter.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily_step_count, step_count_accuracy, device_placement_or_carriage, gait_speed_or_gait_aid_context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only evidence that smartphone step counts can be valid in some clinical settings, while noting large change thresholds.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Seventy-two patients with cancer.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Smartphone step-count and smartphone six-minute-walk-test measurements.
- **Comparator/control:** Accelerometer for step count during the first 7 days; hospital 6MWT for fitness.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 14 days of smartphone step-count monitoring with first 7 days concurrent accelerometer; repeated 6MWT.
- **Endpoints:** step-count feasibility, step-count validity, test-retest reliability, minimal detectable difference
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Step-count measurement completeness was approximately 90%; validity for step count was excellent (ICC=0.97, p<.001) and test-retest reliability was excellent (ICC=0.91, p<.001). A minimal difference of about 2300 steps was needed to detect real change with 95% certainty.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse events were reported in the accessible extract; tolerability was described as feasible/well tolerated.
- **Limitations:** Cancer clinical population; smartphone results depend on carrying/wearing the phone.; Physical fitness application had fair validity and lower completeness.; Responsiveness to change remained uncertain.
- **Population mismatch:** Cancer measurement context; not a Daily Step Floor intervention or healthy-adult efficacy source.
- **Artifact rights:** open_access
