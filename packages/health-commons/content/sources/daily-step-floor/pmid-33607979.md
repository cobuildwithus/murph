This source is included for **safety_special_populations**.

**Findings:** A 174-participant RCT tested a physiotherapist-guided individualized pedometer-driven walking program for chronic low back pain with 12-week intervention and 12-month follow-up, including pain/function and adverse-event or difficulty endpoints.

**Why it matters:** Provides a modern individualized pedometer-walking design for chronic low back pain with physiotherapist involvement.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily-step-count, pain intensity, Oswestry Disability Index, physical activity, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** For chronic low back pain, Daily Step Floor should use individualized progression and pain/difficulty monitoring rather than rigid floors.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness boundary:** clinical_supervised adjacent variant. Do not promote this source into direct Daily Step Floor claims beyond the stated claim-use boundary.

**Safety/adverse events:** The protocol/outcomes included adverse events or difficulties; chronic low-back-pain users need pain-flare monitoring.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Outcome effect sizes were not extracted in this batch; chronic-pain clinical population; not a generic Daily Step Floor trial. Chronic low back pain population; safety/adjacent evidence only.
