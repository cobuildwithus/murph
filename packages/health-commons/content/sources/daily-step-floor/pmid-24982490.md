This source is included for **direct_step_floor_trials**.

**Findings:**

- `finding:pmid-24982490-app-step-effect` — After adjustment, the difference in mean improvement in daily step count from week 1 to week 8 was 1,029 steps/day (95% CI 214 to 1,843; P=0.009) favouring the app intervention.
- `finding:pmid-24982490-secondary-endpoints` — Accessible results reported no significant changes in secondary outcomes of systolic or diastolic blood pressure, weight, or BMI, although point estimates favoured the intervention.

**Why it matters:** Directly relevant to phone-mediated daily floor variants because it used a simple smartphone app to increase step counts in primary care.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- daily steps
- app adherence
- blood pressure
- body weight
- BMI

**Protocol takeaway:** A simple smartphone app can increase daily steps over 8 weeks in primary care; secondary clinical outcomes did not show clear significant changes in the extracted record.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction boundaries

- **Directness:** `direct_protocol`.
- **Population:** Adults in rural primary care in the west of Ireland using Android smartphones
- **Intervention/exposure:** Smartphone app with physical activity goals and detailed instructions for achieving them.
- **Comparator/control:** Similar physical activity goals and exercise information without the app.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 8 weeks
- **Endpoints extracted:** daily steps, blood pressure, weight, BMI.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event signal was extracted from the accessible source record; absence of extracted adverse events should not be interpreted as proof of no adverse events unless the finding explicitly says so.
- **Limitations:** Open-label trial; 90 randomized but fewer had baseline/outcome step data; Android-user primary-care setting may limit generalizability.
- **Population mismatch:** Primary-care smartphone users in rural Ireland; not all wellness users or wearable types.
- **Artifact/rights boundary:** `open_access`; no copyrighted PDF is stored in Git in this draft.
