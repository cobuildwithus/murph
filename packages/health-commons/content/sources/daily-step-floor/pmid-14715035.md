This source is included for **dose_response_cut_points**.

**Findings:** The review proposed a preliminary adult step-index: <5000 steps/day as sedentary, 5000–7499 as low active, 7500–9999 as somewhat active, ≥10000 as active, and ≥12500 as highly active.

**Why it matters:** Provides historical context for common 10,000-step and sedentary/low-active cut-points.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily step count, days meeting step floor, weekly mean steps, daily step count categories, physical-activity classification.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as classification context; do not present these categories as causal Daily Step Floor thresholds.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Healthy adults; cut-points were proposed as preliminary public-health indices rather than trial-tested treatment targets.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Pedometer-measured total steps per day.
- **Comparator/control:** Step-index categories: <5000, 5000–7499, 7500–9999, ≥10000, and ≥12500 steps/day.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Not applicable; narrative cut-point review.
- **Endpoints:** daily step count categories, physical-activity classification
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse-event signal specific to a step-floor intervention was extracted from this source in this batch.
- **Limitations:** The indices were preliminary and descriptive, not derived from randomized evidence or protocol adherence testing.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Applies to broad healthy-adult classification; may not fit older adults, chronic disease, disability, or individual baseline capacity.
- **Boundary:** This source is observational, review-based, registry-only, or otherwise adjacent unless explicitly noted; it must not be promoted into direct Daily Step Floor protocol evidence.
