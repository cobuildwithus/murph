This source is included for **baseline_plus_ramp_trials**.

**Findings:** ACTIVE REWARD used personalized step goals with loss-framed incentives in ischemic heart disease and reported greater step increases than control across ramp-up, maintenance, and follow-up phases; exact sample size was not extracted here.

**Why it matters:** Adjacent clinical evidence for baseline-derived ramps and incentives, not pure Daily Step Floor.

**Potential experiment signals:** weekly 15% ramp, steps/day, maintenance, post-incentive follow-up.

**Protocol takeaway:** Baseline-derived ramps can work in cardiac populations, but incentives and disease context limit direct claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extracted study details

- **Population:** Adults with ischemic heart disease.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Wearable-device intervention with personalized step goals, daily feedback, and loss-framed financial incentives; ramp goals increased weekly by about 15% from baseline up to 10,000 steps/day before maintenance.
- **Comparator or control:** Control with wearable monitoring but no incentive/goal intervention details fully extracted.
- **Duration or follow-up:** 24 weeks: 8-week ramp-up incentive phase, 8-week maintenance phase, and 8-week follow-up.
- **Endpoints:** steps/day, ramp-up phase steps, maintenance phase steps, follow-up steps
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Accessible summaries report significantly greater step increases versus control during ramp-up, maintenance, and follow-up phases; exact sample size was not extracted from accessible records in this run.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Adverse-event reporting was not extracted.
- **Limitations:** Clinical ischemic heart disease population, incentives, and incomplete extraction of sample/safety details.
- **Population mismatch:** Cardiac clinical population; adjacent to consumer step floors.
- **Directness to Daily Step Floor:** `adjacent_variant`
