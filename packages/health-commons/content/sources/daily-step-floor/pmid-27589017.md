This source is included for **cardiometabolic_fitness_endpoints**.

**Findings:** A cluster RCT of a pragmatic group education programme incorporating pedometer use in people at high risk of type 2 diabetes found a modest 12-month increase in ambulatory activity, not sustained at 36 months. It is adjacent because the intervention is a structured diabetes-prevention education programme rather than a Daily Step Floor protocol.

**Why it matters:** Preserves a modest, time-limited activity effect from a real-world low-resource programme.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:daily-steps, biomarker:physical-activity-minutes, biomarker:type-2-diabetes-risk.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent context that pedometer-containing education can modestly increase steps short term; do not assume durable maintenance.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness boundary:** `adjacent_variant`. This source should not be promoted beyond that scope in the Daily Step Floor protocol.

**Safety/adverse events:** No adverse-event signal extracted from accessible abstract text.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Cluster design, group education and refresher components, and 12-month effect not sustained at 36 months. Population mismatch: Adults at high diabetes risk, not a general Daily Step Floor population.
