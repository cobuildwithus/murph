This source is included for **guidelines_external_protocol_context**.

**Findings:** The Rockhampton establishment paper describes a multi-strategy community physical-activity project built around 10,000 steps/day, but reports program design rather than effectiveness outcomes.

**Why it matters:** Documents how the 10,000-step target was used in a multi-strategy community campaign, while providing no direct personal step-floor result.

**Potential experiment signals:** program_design, daily_step_count, community_reach, physical_activity_minutes.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only history of external 10,000-step program design, not as protocol efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Rockhampton, Queensland community context; key informants and community stakeholders were involved, but no outcome participant count was reported in the accessible extract.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Development of a multi-strategy community physical-activity promotion project centered on 10,000 steps/day, with media, health-service, social-support, policy/environment, and community-fund strategies.
- **Comparator/control:** No comparator or control group in this establishment report.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Project establishment period; outcome follow-up not reported in this paper.
- **Endpoints:** program design; community strategies; 10,000 steps/day messaging; implementation rationale
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Identified five strategy areas for a community pedometer/10,000-step program; the paper explicitly leaves outcome effectiveness to later evaluation.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse events reported.
- **Limitations:** Development/history paper; no direct outcome evaluation; authors noted it remained to be seen whether physical activity would increase and whether disadvantaged groups would be reached.
- **Population mismatch:** External whole-community intervention design, not a personal Daily Step Floor protocol.
