This source is included for **measurement_validity**.

**Findings:** In adults with transtibial amputation, Fitbit Inspire 3 counted about 1,094 more daily steps than activPAL 3 on average despite high correlation; equivalence was not demonstrated.

**Why it matters:** A Daily Step Floor threshold may classify adherence differently depending on whether Fitbit or activPAL is used.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily_step_count, step_count_accuracy, device_placement_or_carriage, gait_speed_or_gait_aid_context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only evidence not to compare absolute step thresholds across these devices in amputation populations.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Seventy-nine adults with transtibial amputation; mean age 58.1 ± 14.8 years, 22 females.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Fitbit Inspire 3 daily step count during waking wear periods.
- **Comparator/control:** activPAL 3 daily step count matched to the same waking wear periods.
- **Duration/follow-up:** At least four valid days required for inclusion.
- **Endpoints:** daily step count, device correlation, mean difference, equivalence testing, Bland-Altman agreement
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Fitbit Inspire 3 and activPAL 3 were highly correlated (r=0.93), but the Fitbit recorded 5,768 ± 3,750 versus activPAL 4,674 ± 3,081 daily steps, an average of 1,094 ± 1,423 more steps. Equivalence could not be claimed.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse events or safety outcomes were reported in the accessible extract.
- **Limitations:** Method comparison between two devices, not direct observation.; Findings apply to transtibial amputation and may reflect device-specific sensitivity.; Only waking wear periods were analyzed.
- **Population mismatch:** Transtibial-amputation measurement context; not Daily Step Floor efficacy evidence.
- **Artifact rights:** open_access
