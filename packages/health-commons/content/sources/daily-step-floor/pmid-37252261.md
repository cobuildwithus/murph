This source is included for **sedentary_activity_pattern**.

**Findings:** Meta-analysis of 45 studies found wearable activity trackers increased daily steps, weekly MVPA, and total daily activity, and produced a small reduction in sedentary time (SMD about −0.10, 95% CI −0.19 to −0.01).

**Why it matters:** Relevant to Daily Step Floor because it connects tracker-based goals to steps and sedentary time in older adults, but the effect is small and indirect.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily steps, weekly MVPA, total daily physical activity, sedentary time.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent/context evidence, not direct protocol confirmation.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
