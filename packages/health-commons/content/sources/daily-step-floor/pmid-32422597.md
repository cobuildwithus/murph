This source is included for **mental_health_sleep_qol**.

**Findings:** A six-week daily diary randomized trial found no time, group, or interaction effects for psychological well-being or body trusting when comparing fitness-app self-tracking, daily step targets, and activity documentation.

**Why it matters:** Preserves null/mixed psychological-well-being evidence for app-based step target self-tracking.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:daily-steps, biomarker:psychological-well-being, biomarker:body-awareness.

**Protocol takeaway:** Fitness-app self-tracking and step targets did not substantially influence psychological well-being or body trusting in accessible summaries.
