This source is included for **direct_step_floor_trials**.

**Findings:**

- `finding:pmid-36396151-additional-components-post` — Across 85 studies with 12,057 participants identified, the postintervention mean difference was 926 steps/day (95% CI 651 to 1,201) for add-ons versus self-monitoring alone.
- `finding:pmid-36396151-additional-components-followup` — At follow-up, the mean difference was 413 steps/day (95% CI 210 to 615), suggesting a modest sustained additional benefit.
- `finding:pmid-36396151-component-moderators` — Interventions with a prescribed goal and human counselling, especially by phone or video, were associated with greater step-count differences than interventions adding print materials, websites, smartphone apps, or incentives.

**Why it matters:** Same-mechanism synthesis helps isolate what add-ons contribute beyond self-monitoring, without treating add-ons as direct protocol evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- daily steps
- self-monitoring adherence
- human counseling effects
- goal-setting effects

**Protocol takeaway:** Additional components provide a modest net step-count benefit beyond self-monitoring; prescribed goals and human counselling appear stronger than print/web/app/incentive add-ons.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
