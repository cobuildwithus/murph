This source is included for **dose_response_cut_points**.

**Findings:** This meta-analysis reported nonlinear inverse associations of step counts with all-cause mortality and cardiovascular events; accelerometer-restricted analyses reported lower risk in higher step-count categories, including around 8959 versus 4183 steps/day for mortality and 9500 versus 3500 steps/day for cardiovascular events.

**Why it matters:** Adds dose-response context for mortality and cardiovascular-event cut-points.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily step count, days meeting step floor, weekly mean steps, all-cause mortality, cardiovascular events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as observational context and keep causal language out.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Middle-aged and older adults in observational studies of daily step count.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Step-count categories and dose-response step counts.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower versus higher step-count groups.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Searches through 9 July 2021; follow-up varied by included study.
- **Endpoints:** all-cause mortality, cardiovascular events, daily step count
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse-event signal specific to a step-floor intervention was extracted from this source in this batch.
- **Limitations:** Observational evidence with heterogeneous populations and devices; not a behavioral target trial.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Associations by step count do not establish that a floor intervention causes event reduction.
- **Boundary:** This source is observational, review-based, registry-only, or otherwise adjacent unless explicitly noted; it must not be promoted into direct Daily Step Floor protocol evidence.
