This source is included for **direct_step_floor_trials**.

**Findings:**

- `finding:pmid-25274710-add-ons-no-advantage` — During the 16-week intervention, goal-achievement days did not differ versus comparison: financial 39.7% (P=.78), peer 24.9% (P=.08), combined 36.0% (P=.77), and comparison 36.0%.
- `finding:pmid-25274710-follow-up-boundary` — During follow-up, the peer-network arm had lower goal achievement than comparison (18.7% versus 34.5%; P=.025); financial and combined arms were not significantly different from comparison.

**Why it matters:** Adjacent variant that tests whether incentives and peer networks add benefit to a walking-goal floor in older adults.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- step-goal attainment
- adherence
- follow-up maintenance

**Protocol takeaway:** Incentive and peer add-ons did not outperform the comparison walking-goal program and peer networks were lower at follow-up in the extracted results.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction boundaries

- **Directness:** `adjacent_variant`.
- **Population:** Older adults with computer and internet access
- **Intervention/exposure:** Daily walking goals with pedometer, weekly feedback, and financial incentive and/or peer network components.
- **Comparator/control:** Pedometer, daily walking goals, and weekly feedback alone.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 16-week intervention plus 8-week follow-up
- **Endpoints extracted:** days walking goals met, adherence, older-adult acceptability.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event signal was extracted from the accessible source record; absence of extracted adverse events should not be interpreted as proof of no adverse events unless the finding explicitly says so.
- **Limitations:** Small older-adult sample; all groups received goal-setting and feedback; interventions tested add-ons rather than simple Daily Step Floor alone.
- **Population mismatch:** Older adults with internet access; not general users.
