This guideline covers physical activity, sedentary behaviour, and sleep throughout the first postpartum year. It recommends an individualized, gradual return; daily pelvic-floor muscle training; less sedentary time; and, for people without contraindications, building toward 120 minutes of moderate-to-vigorous physical activity over at least four days each week, including aerobic and strength activities.

Use the recommendations as a destination, not as an immediate prescription after birth. Healing, bleeding, pelvic-floor symptoms, surgical or perineal wounds, complications, sleep, prior activity, and personal priorities should shape the starting dose and rate of progression. Any progress can be useful when the full target is not currently feasible.
