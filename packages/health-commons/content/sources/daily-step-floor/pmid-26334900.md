This source is included for **sedentary_activity_pattern**.

**Findings:** Meta-analysis of 15 RCTs reported a small reduction in sedentary time with step-counter use (SMD −0.20, 95% CI −0.33 to −0.07), approximated by the authors as about 23 minutes/day; the subgroup with step goals had a larger reduction.

**Why it matters:** This is one of the closest batch sources to the protocol because it links step-counter use and goals to sedentary time, while still not testing a floor/ramp rule.

**Potential experiment signals:** sedentary time, daily steps, physical activity.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only/adjacent evidence that step goals may modestly reduce sedentary time; do not claim direct protocol efficacy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Adults in randomized step-counter intervention trials.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Step-counter or pedometer use, often including activity goals and self-monitoring.
- **Comparator/control:** No step counter, usual care, or control comparison in included trials.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Varied across 15 randomized trials; not extracted study-by-study in this pass.
- **Endpoints:** sedentary time, daily steps, physical activity
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Meta-analysis of 15 RCTs reported a small reduction in sedentary time with step-counter use (SMD −0.20, 95% CI −0.33 to −0.07), approximated by the authors as about 23 minutes/day; the subgroup with step goals had a larger reduction.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Adverse events were not extracted from the accessible summary.
- **Limitations:** Review aggregates heterogeneous step-counter interventions rather than a single daily floor rule.; Sedentary-time estimates are small and may depend on goal setting and self-monitoring components.; Publication year and device types may predate current consumer trackers.
- **Population mismatch:** Adjacent step-counter evidence with sedentary-time outcomes; not a direct Daily Step Floor protocol.
- **Artifact candidates and rights status:** open_access; no copyrighted PDF is included in this draft artifact package.
