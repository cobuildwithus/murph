This source is included for **direct_step_floor_trials**.

**Findings:**

- `finding:pmid-27717766-mvpa-mixed` — At 6 months, cash and charity arms increased MVPA versus control, while Fitbit-only was not significant; at 12 months Fitbit-only and charity remained higher versus control but cash was not.
- `finding:pmid-27717766-no-health-outcomes` — The accessible source reported no improvements in health outcomes at either 6 or 12 months.

**Why it matters:** Major adjacent tracker/incentive trial that helps avoid overclaiming wearable or incentive-based step programs.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- weekly MVPA
- daily steps
- weekly step-goal attainment
- weight
- blood pressure
- quality of life

**Protocol takeaway:** Tracker and incentive effects were mixed and did not improve health outcomes, so this is a boundary source rather than direct support for a simple step floor.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
