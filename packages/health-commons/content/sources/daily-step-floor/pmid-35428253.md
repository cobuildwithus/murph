This source is included for **dose_response_cut_points**.

**Findings:** HCHS/SOL analyses suggested lower diabetes risk with more steps/day, including an estimated 2% lower risk per additional 1000 steps/day with confidence intervals near the null; higher step intensity showed related associations.

**Why it matters:** Adds population-diverse diabetes context and near-null uncertainty.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily step count, days meeting step floor, weekly mean steps, incident diabetes, step intensity.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context with mixed/attenuated association language.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Hispanic/Latino adults in HCHS/SOL without baseline diabetes in the analytic sample.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Accelerometer-derived steps/day and step-intensity metrics.
- **Comparator/control:** Per-1000-step increments and modelled step-count scenarios.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Prospective follow-up for diabetes ascertainment.
- **Endpoints:** incident diabetes, daily step count, step intensity
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse-event signal specific to a step-floor intervention was extracted from this source in this batch.
- **Limitations:** Observational cohort; some estimates were near the null and residual confounding is possible.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Specific Hispanic/Latino cohort and diabetes endpoint are context-only for Daily Step Floor.
- **Boundary:** This source is observational, review-based, registry-only, or otherwise adjacent unless explicitly noted; it must not be promoted into direct Daily Step Floor protocol evidence.
