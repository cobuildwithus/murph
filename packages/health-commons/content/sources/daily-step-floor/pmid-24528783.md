This source is included for **cardiometabolic_fitness_endpoints**.

**Findings:** This is a published WalkMore study protocol, not a results paper. It is useful for planned endpoint selection around 10,000 steps/day with and without intensity messaging, but it should not be used as efficacy evidence.

**Why it matters:** Useful for endpoint and comparator design: it separates step volume from intensity/cadence messaging.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:daily-steps, biomarker:morning-blood-pressure, biomarker:fasting-glucose, biomarker:fasting-insulin, biomarker:flow-mediated-dilation, biomarker:gait-speed.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for design context only; do not cite as evidence that a step floor improves BP or metabolic outcomes.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness boundary:** `direct_protocol`. This source should not be promoted beyond that scope in the Daily Step Floor protocol.

**Safety/adverse events:** No adverse-event results reported in the protocol source.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Protocol-only source; planned sample and endpoints do not establish effects. Population mismatch: Planned postmenopausal women aged 45–74; not general population or male-inclusive evidence.
