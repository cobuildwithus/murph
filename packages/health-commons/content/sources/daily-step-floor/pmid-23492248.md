This source is included for **cardiometabolic_fitness_endpoints**.

**Findings:** A follow-up analysis of a type 2 diabetes pedometer/telephone-support intervention found no significant group differences in health outcomes, although people who increased at least 4,000 steps/day showed HbA1c improvement. This is adjacent and mixed because the subgroup threshold did not generalize to other outcomes.

**Why it matters:** Preserves a null clinical-outcome finding and a potential large-delta step threshold signal for HbA1c.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:daily-steps, biomarker:hba1c, biomarker:fasting-glucose, biomarker:morning-blood-pressure, biomarker:bmi, biomarker:waist-circumference, biomarker:triglycerides.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not claim broad cardiometabolic improvements from support-enhanced pedometer interventions; a large step increase may matter for HbA1c in some T2D participants.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness boundary:** `adjacent_variant`. This source should not be promoted beyond that scope in the Daily Step Floor protocol.

**Safety/adverse events:** No adverse-event signal extracted from accessible abstract text.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Secondary/relationship analysis, subgroup threshold, and telephone-support program; threshold not applicable to other outcomes. Population mismatch: Type 2 diabetes adults with specific inclusion profile; not general users.
