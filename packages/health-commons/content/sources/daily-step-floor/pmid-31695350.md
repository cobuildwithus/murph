This source is included for **sedentary_activity_pattern**.

**Findings:** Pilot estimates suggested more steps and lower blood pressure in the wearable/behavioral arm, but sedentary-time differences were imprecise and confidence intervals crossed no effect in accessible summaries.

**Why it matters:** Preserves an important mixed signal: step increases may not guarantee sedentary-time reduction.

**Potential experiment signals:** sedentary time, steps, non-exercise physical activity, blood pressure, CVD risk.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only evidence for older-adult wearable interventions and uncertainty.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Sedentary older adults aged at least 60 years with moderate-to-high coronary heart disease risk.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Wearable technology plus behavioral monitoring/feedback layered onto center-based exercise.
- **Comparator/control:** Exercise intervention without the added wearable/NEPA behavior-change component.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 20 weeks, including 8 weeks of center-based exercise and follow-up.
- **Endpoints:** sedentary time, steps, non-exercise physical activity, blood pressure, CVD risk
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Pilot estimates suggested more steps and lower blood pressure in the wearable/behavioral arm, but sedentary-time differences were imprecise and confidence intervals crossed no effect in accessible summaries.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse events were extracted from the accessible summary.
- **Limitations:** Pilot RCT with small sample.; Clinical older-adult cardiovascular-risk population.; Intervention combined exercise, wearable feedback, and behavioral counseling, not a simple daily step floor.
- **Population mismatch:** Adjacent clinical/pilot wearable intervention; not direct protocol evidence.
- **Artifact candidates and rights status:** open_access; no copyrighted PDF is included in this draft artifact package.
