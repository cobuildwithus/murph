This source is included for **cadence_intensity_bouts**.

**Findings:** CADENCE-Adults relative-intensity study found age-stratified cadence thresholds for moderate/vigorous relative effort that are generally higher than the 100 spm absolute-intensity heuristic.

**Why it matters:** Prevents overclaiming that 100 spm is enough for every relative-intensity definition.

**Potential experiment signals:** walking_cadence, relative_intensity, heart_rate, rating_of_perceived_exertion.

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep relative-intensity cadence thresholds as context; the base Daily Step Floor should not require them.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Adults aged 21-60 years completing treadmill walking bouts with heart-rate and perceived-exertion relative-intensity criteria.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Cadence during treadmill walking, evaluated against relative intensity indicators by age group.
- **Comparator/control:** Relative moderate and vigorous intensity indicators based on HRmax, HR reserve, and rating of perceived exertion.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Single laboratory protocol with up to twelve 5-minute treadmill bouts.
- **Endpoints:** Age-stratified cadence thresholds for relatively-defined moderate and vigorous walking intensity.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Accessible summaries report moderate-intensity thresholds by age group around 120, 120, 115, and 110/105 spm and vigorous thresholds around 135, 130, 125, and 120 spm for 21-30, 31-40, 41-50, and 51-60 years; a later correction affected some exact thresholds.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse events or safety outcomes were reported in accessible summaries.
- **Limitations:** Relative intensity thresholds are higher and age-specific; correction history creates exact-threshold caution; treadmill study, not a step-floor intervention.
- **Population mismatch:** Same-mechanism relative-intensity calibration, context-only for a simple step floor.
