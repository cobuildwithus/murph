This source is included for **cardiometabolic_fitness_endpoints**.

**Findings:** A systematic review/meta-analysis of activity monitors in type 2 diabetes found increased physical activity but no significant improvement in HbA1c, BMI, blood pressure, or lipid profile. It is directly relevant for step-monitoring but mixed for clinical endpoints.

**Why it matters:** Provides a high-value boundary: step/monitoring interventions can change activity without reliably changing clinical biomarkers.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:daily-steps, biomarker:hba1c, biomarker:bmi, biomarker:morning-blood-pressure, biomarker:lipids.

**Protocol takeaway:** Support PA/steps as primary endpoints; present glycemic, BMI, BP, and lipid changes as uncertain or not confirmed by this review.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness boundary:** `direct_protocol`. This source should not be promoted beyond that scope in the Daily Step Floor protocol.

**Safety/adverse events:** No adverse-event signal extracted from accessible abstract text.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Heterogeneity, co-interventions, enhanced usual care, mobile participants without major complications, and lower applicability to people with higher HbA1c or complications. Population mismatch: Type 2 diabetes trials with relatively mobile participants; not a general step-floor population.
