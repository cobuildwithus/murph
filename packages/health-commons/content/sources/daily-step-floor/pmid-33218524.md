This source is included for **mental_health_sleep_qol**.

**Findings:** In a 4-week RCT of 54 young adult volunteers, a pedometer-based walking intervention did not show statistically significant between-group improvements in global sleep quality, perceived stress, or life satisfaction, although some within-group measures improved.

**Why it matters:** Direct pedometer walking RCT with sleep/stress/life-satisfaction endpoints and a null between-group signal.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:daily-steps, biomarker:sleep-quality, biomarker:stress, biomarker:life-satisfaction.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not claim that a brief pedometer walking intervention reliably improves sleep, stress, or life satisfaction in young adults; between-group evidence was not significant.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
