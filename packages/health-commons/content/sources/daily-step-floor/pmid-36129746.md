This source is included for **guidelines_external_protocol_context**.

**Findings:** The Duesseldorf protocol planned a 1-year nonrandomized multilevel 10,000-step intervention in adults aged 25-75 years with control city districts, but it reported no efficacy results.

**Why it matters:** Useful for external protocol design and registry context, not for efficacy claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily_step_count, physical_activity_minutes, program_reach, step_goal_attainment.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only external protocol/registry evidence; do not cite as outcome evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Community-dwelling adults aged 25-75 years in intervention city districts in Duesseldorf and control city districts in Wuppertal; 626 enrolled as of March 2022.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Population-based 10,000 Steps Duesseldorf multilevel intervention with individual website self-monitoring, organizational PA promotion, community media campaign, environmental changes, and 10,000 steps/day messaging.
- **Comparator/control:** Control city districts in Wuppertal.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Planned 1-year intervention with baseline and follow-up assessment.
- **Endpoints:** physical activity; step counts; secondary outcomes; implementation of multilevel strategy
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Protocol only; no efficacy results were available in this source.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse-event results; protocol does not report completed safety outcomes.
- **Limitations:** Protocol paper; nonrandomized design; district-level intervention rather than whole-city; COVID-19 recruitment constraints; no completed result in this source.
- **Population mismatch:** External population-based multilevel intervention, not a personal Daily Step Floor protocol.
- **Artifact rights:** open_access
