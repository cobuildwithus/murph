This source is included for **safety_special_populations**.

**Findings:** The Taking Healthy Steps design paper described a 239-participant COPD pedometer RCT with COPD-specific monitoring including severe shortness of breath, dizziness, adverse events, quality of life, and daily steps.

**Why it matters:** Identifies COPD-specific symptom and adverse-event endpoints for pedometer walking interventions.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily-step-count, shortness-of-breath, dizziness, SGRQ quality-of-life, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** For COPD users, Daily Step Floor needs dyspnea/dizziness symptom boundaries and clinical oversight; this design paper is not efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness boundary:** clinical_supervised COPD protocol context. Do not promote this source into direct Daily Step Floor claims beyond the stated claim-use boundary.

**Safety/adverse events:** The trial design included monitoring for severe shortness of breath, dizziness, and adverse events in COPD.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Design/baseline source; efficacy and safety outcomes require the completed trial reports. COPD veteran clinical population; safety context only.
