This source is included for **sedentary_activity_pattern**.

**Findings:** Network meta-analysis of 12 RCTs reported wearable plus online intervention had significantly better effects in reducing sedentary time than control, comparison, or wearable-only strategies; no wearable-based intervention effectively reduced prolonged sitting in the extracted results.

**Why it matters:** Highly relevant to activity-pattern outcomes because it separates total sedentary time from prolonged sitting.

**Potential experiment signals:** sedentary time, prolonged sitting.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent/context evidence that wearable-only step goals may be weaker than wearable plus online support, and prolonged-sitting outcomes need direct measurement.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Healthy adults in randomized trials of wearable device intervention strategies for sedentary time and prolonged sitting.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Wearable-only, wearable plus online intervention, wearable plus offline intervention, and comparison interventions.
- **Comparator/control:** Control group or traditional non-wearable comparison interventions.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Varied by included RCT; literature searched through 1 June 2024.
- **Endpoints:** sedentary time, prolonged sitting
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Network meta-analysis of 12 RCTs reported wearable plus online intervention had significantly better effects in reducing sedentary time than control, comparison, or wearable-only strategies; no wearable-based intervention effectively reduced prolonged sitting in the extracted results.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Adverse events were not extracted from the accessible summary.
- **Limitations:** Network meta-analysis with indirect comparisons.; Healthy-adult sedentary interventions, not Daily Step Floor.; Prolonged sitting was not clearly reduced.
- **Population mismatch:** Adjacent wearable-device sedentary-time NMA; not direct protocol evidence.
