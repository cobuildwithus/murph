This source is included for **direct_step_floor_trials**.

**Findings:**

- `finding:pmid-29335328-steps-and-adherence` — During the last 4 weeks, the intervention group averaged 6,507 ± 1,716 steps/day versus 5,064 ± 1,837 in controls; the reported between-group difference was 2,913 steps/day (95% CI 1,274 to 4,551). Only 6.1% achieved 10,000 steps/day and 18.7% achieved 7,500 steps/day.
- `finding:pmid-29335328-hba1c-boundary` — Endline HbA1c was lower in the intervention group than control with a reported mean difference of -0.74% (95% CI -1.32 to -0.02; P=0.015), while anthropometric and cardiovascular indices did not significantly change in the accessible source.

**Why it matters:** Very direct daily-step-floor trial, with important clinical-population and adherence boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- daily steps

**Protocol takeaway:** The intervention increased steps and improved HbA1c in type 2 diabetes, but few participants achieved 10,000 steps/day, so feasibility should be framed as an adherence boundary.
