This source is included for **mental_health_sleep_qol**.

**Findings:** In a 192-participant multicenter RCT during/after inpatient depression treatment, adding a pedometer intervention and activity diary to treatment as usual did not significantly improve clinician-rated depression or accelerometer-measured daily steps at discharge versus treatment as usual.

**Why it matters:** Direct pedometer intervention in depression but with a null primary-outcome result that must be preserved.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:daily-steps, biomarker:depressive-symptoms, biomarker:adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** A pedometer-plus-diary add-on did not outperform treatment as usual for depression or daily steps in severe inpatient MDD, so Daily Step Floor should not be framed as a depression treatment.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness boundary:** This source is classified as `direct_protocol` for Daily Step Floor. Do not promote adjacent, observational, registry/protocol, or clinical-population findings into direct protocol claims.

**Safety/adverse events:** Acceptability reports noted moderate helpfulness and practical disadvantages with accelerometer handling; no specific adverse-event signal was extracted in this batch.
