This source is included for **dose_response_cut_points**.

**Findings:** The whole-community “10,000 steps Ghent” intervention reportedly reached the community and increased pedometer-determined physical activity after one year.

**Why it matters:** Adjacent intervention context for 10,000-step public messaging.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily step count, days meeting step floor, weekly mean steps, community physical activity awareness.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as community-intervention context only, not as causal proof for an individual protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Adults in the Ghent community exposed to a public 10,000-steps campaign.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Multi-strategy community campaign promoting 10,000 steps/day.
- **Comparator/control:** Pre/post and comparison-community context as reported by the intervention evaluation.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Approximately one-year community intervention follow-up.
- **Endpoints:** daily step count, community physical activity awareness
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse-event signal specific to a step-floor intervention was extracted from this source in this batch.
- **Limitations:** Community intervention design with multiple components; not a randomized individual Daily Step Floor protocol.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Community-level campaign differs from individual self-experiment step-floor adherence.
- **Boundary:** This source is observational, review-based, registry-only, or otherwise adjacent unless explicitly noted; it must not be promoted into direct Daily Step Floor protocol evidence.
