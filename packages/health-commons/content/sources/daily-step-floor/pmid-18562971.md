This source is included for **dose_response_cut_points**.

**Findings:** The review revisited adult step targets and described the common guideline translation that roughly 30 minutes of moderate-intensity walking can be approximated by 3000–4000 steps, often using about 100 steps/minute as a moderate-intensity heuristic.

**Why it matters:** Helps distinguish total daily step floors from add-on moderate-intensity step goals.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily step count, days meeting step floor, weekly mean steps, moderate-intensity walking cadence, guideline translation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as measurement and cut-point context, not as direct evidence that one floor target is optimal.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Adults in public-health guideline translation contexts.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Daily steps and cadence approximations for moderate-intensity walking.
- **Comparator/control:** Guideline-equivalent step additions above baseline versus total daily step categories.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Not applicable; narrative review.
- **Endpoints:** daily step count, moderate-intensity walking cadence, guideline translation
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse-event signal specific to a step-floor intervention was extracted from this source in this batch.
- **Limitations:** Heuristic guideline translation; not a test of health outcomes from a step-floor intervention.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Cadence and step totals vary by age, height, fitness, device, and baseline activity.
- **Boundary:** This source is observational, review-based, registry-only, or otherwise adjacent unless explicitly noted; it must not be promoted into direct Daily Step Floor protocol evidence.
