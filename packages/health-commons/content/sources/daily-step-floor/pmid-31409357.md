This source is included for **sedentary_activity_pattern**.

**Findings:** Meta-analysis of 19 studies reported reduced total sedentary time (Hedges g about 0.32) and occupational sedentary time (g about 0.56), with no significant effect on sedentary breaks in accessible summaries.

**Why it matters:** Daily Step Floor uses self-monitoring, but sedentary-break effects should still be measured rather than assumed.

**Potential experiment signals:** total sedentary time, occupational sedentary time, sedentary breaks, self-monitoring modality.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only evidence that objective monitoring and SB-specific goals may matter.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
