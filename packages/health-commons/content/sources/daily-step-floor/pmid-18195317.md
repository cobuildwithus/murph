This source is included for **direct_protocol_reviews**.

**Findings:** Body weight: Across 9 cohorts with 307 participants, the pooled mean weight change was -1.27 kg (95% CI -1.85 to -0.70; p<0.001).; Weight loss: Longer program duration was associated with greater weight loss in the review-level analysis (p=0.003).; Evidence certainty: The DARE appraisal emphasized small samples, clinical/methodological diversity, dropout, and questionable pooling; the estimate should be treated as low-confidence for broad protocol claims.

**Why it matters:** This review provides direct pedometer-based evidence for a weight-related endpoint, while making clear that effect size is modest and population-specific.

**Potential experiment signals:** body-weight, body-mass-index, daily-step-count.

**Protocol takeaway:** A Daily Step Floor may support modest weight change in sedentary overweight adults when it increases walking, but should not be positioned as a standalone weight-loss guarantee.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
