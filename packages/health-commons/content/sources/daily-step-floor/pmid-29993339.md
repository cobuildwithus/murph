This source is included for **safety_special_populations**.

**Findings:** A 15-trial COPD meta-analysis found step-counter interventions increased physical activity and exercise capacity overall, but effects were not confirmed in severe COPD or interventions lasting 6 months or more.

**Why it matters:** Summarizes COPD step-counter effects while preserving the subgroup caveat for severe COPD and longer programs.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily-step-count, six-minute-walk-distance, dyspnea, quality-of-life, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use COPD step-counter benefits only as clinical-supervised adjacent evidence; do not promise durable effects in severe COPD or longer follow-up.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness boundary:** clinical_supervised adjacent variant. Do not promote this source into direct Daily Step Floor claims beyond the stated claim-use boundary.

**Safety/adverse events:** COPD severity and dyspnea/symptom monitoring remain safety boundaries; the meta-analysis is not a clearance protocol.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Heterogeneous trials and subgroups; not Daily Step Floor-specific; longer-duration effects were not confirmed. COPD clinical population; safety-only/adjacent evidence.
