This source is included for **cadence_intensity_bouts**.

**Findings:** CADENCE-Adults older-adult study associated 100, 110, and 120 steps/min with 3, 4, and 5 METs; vigorous threshold evidence was limited.

**Why it matters:** Shows 100 spm can still indicate minimum moderate intensity in older adults, but higher intensities need careful interpretation.

**Potential experiment signals:** walking_cadence, mvpa_minutes, activity_intensity_classification.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use 100 spm as optional minimum moderate-intensity context in healthy older adults; avoid vigorous-cadence claims.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Adults aged 61-85 years in the CADENCE-Adults treadmill calibration study.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Observed cadence during treadmill walking with indirect calorimetry.
- **Comparator/control:** Cadence thresholds for 3, 4, 5, and 6 METs.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Single laboratory visit with sequential 5-minute treadmill bouts.
- **Endpoints:** Older-adult cadence thresholds for absolute intensity categories.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Heuristic thresholds of 100, 110, and 120 spm were associated with 3, 4, and 5 METs, respectively; data were insufficient to establish a >=6 MET vigorous threshold because only 6 of 98 participants achieved that intensity.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse events or safety outcomes were reported in accessible summaries.
- **Limitations:** Older but ostensibly healthy lab sample; treadmill protocol; limited data for vigorous intensity; not an intervention.
- **Population mismatch:** Same-mechanism calibration evidence, not direct Daily Step Floor evidence.
