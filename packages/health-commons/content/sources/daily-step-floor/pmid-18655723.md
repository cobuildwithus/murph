This source is included for **baseline_plus_ramp_trials**.

**Findings:** The Walking for Wellbeing protocol specified a practical baseline-plus ramp: add 3000 steps/day above baseline on at least 5 days/week by week 6, then maintain through week 12.

**Why it matters:** Provides direct implementation details for a baseline-plus step-floor ramp.

**Potential experiment signals:** baseline steps, weekly ramp completion, five-days-per-week adherence, 12-month follow-up.

**Protocol takeaway:** A +3000 steps/day target can be implemented as a gradual 6-week ramp rather than an immediate fixed floor.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extracted study details

- **Population:** Low-active community adults aged 18 to 65 years able to walk.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Graduated pedometer walking programme plus physical activity consultation targeting +3000 steps over baseline on at least 5 days/week by week 6 and maintenance to week 12.
- **Comparator or control:** 12-week wait-list control followed by a minimal pedometer intervention.
- **Duration or follow-up:** 12-week intervention with planned 12-month follow-up.
- **Endpoints:** steps/day, psychological outcomes, physiological outcomes, environmental measures, qualitative process data, economic evaluation
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Protocol source; no efficacy result.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Eligibility required ability to walk and excluded people for whom the programme was inappropriate; detailed adverse-event results were not reported because this is a protocol.
- **Limitations:** Protocol paper; not outcome evidence. Wait-list design means no full-duration untreated comparator after crossover.
- **Population mismatch:** Community adults in Scotland; not necessarily clinical or global population.
