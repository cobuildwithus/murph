This source is included for **safety_special_populations**.

**Findings:** In a 99-participant phase III cardiac rehabilitation trial, pedometer feedback arms increased daily steps more than usual care over 12 weeks.

**Why it matters:** Adds cardiac-rehab evidence that step-count feedback can raise activity in a supervised cardiac population.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily-step-count, cardiac symptoms, adverse-events, exercise adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as cardiac-rehab context; Daily Step Floor should not independently manage cardiac rehab progression.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness boundary:** clinical_supervised adjacent variant. Do not promote this source into direct Daily Step Floor claims beyond the stated claim-use boundary.

**Safety/adverse events:** Cardiac rehabilitation population requires clinical clearance/supervision; adverse-event details were not extracted here.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Cardiac rehab special population; intervention arms include feedback/messages, not a minimum daily floor. Cardiac rehab participants; context-only for general Daily Step Floor.
