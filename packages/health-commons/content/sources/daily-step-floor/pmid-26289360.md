This source is included for **safety_special_populations**.

**Findings:** A descriptive adverse-event analysis from a chronic back-pain walking trial reported about 600 adverse events, commonly worsening back pain, musculoskeletal events, cardiovascular events, infections, and procedures.

**Why it matters:** Gives pain-specific adverse-event categories that a Daily Step Floor safety ledger should anticipate.

**Potential experiment signals:** adverse-events, worsening back pain, musculoskeletal pain, cardiovascular symptoms, temporary suspension.

**Protocol takeaway:** Daily Step Floor should distinguish ordinary pain fluctuation from reportable worsening pain and cardiovascular symptoms.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness boundary:** clinical_supervised adverse-event context. Do not promote this source into direct Daily Step Floor claims beyond the stated claim-use boundary.

**Safety/adverse events:** Reported categories included worsening back pain (about 27%), musculoskeletal events (about 19%), cardiovascular events (about 14%), infections (about 11%), and procedures (about 9%).

**Limitations and mismatch:** Descriptive adverse-event reporting; not designed to estimate a causal effect of walking on each event category. Chronic back-pain trial population; safety context only.
