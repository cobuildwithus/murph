This source is included for **cardiometabolic_fitness_endpoints**.

**Findings:** A 36-week 10,000-steps/day goal study in overweight adults reported increased walking volume and reductions in weight, BMI, body fat, and waist/hip measures among completers. It directly uses a 10,000-steps/day goal, but completer/adherence and study-design limitations remain important.

**Why it matters:** This is one of the clearest direct 10,000-steps/day goal sources in the batch for weight/body-composition endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:daily-steps, biomarker:body-weight, biomarker:bmi, biomarker:waist-circumference, biomarker:hdl-cholesterol.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports describing 10,000 steps/day as a potentially useful floor for overweight adults, with adherence and non-randomized/completer caveats.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness boundary:** `direct_protocol`. This source should not be promoted beyond that scope in the Daily Step Floor protocol.

**Safety/adverse events:** No adverse-event signal extracted from accessible abstract text.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Completer/adherence analysis and lack of an extracted separate control arm limit causal inference; adults were overweight/obese rather than general users. Population mismatch: Overweight/obese adult sample; not necessarily representative of already-active, older, diabetic, hypertensive, or mobility-limited users.
