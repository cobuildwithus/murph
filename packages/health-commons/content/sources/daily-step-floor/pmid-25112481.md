This source is included for **sedentary_activity_pattern**.

**Findings:** The review found sedentary-behavior-focused interventions reduced sedentary time more clearly than physical-activity-only interventions in the accessible abstract and discovery notes.

**Why it matters:** Daily Step Floor may increase steps without necessarily reducing sedentary bouts unless the protocol also addresses sit-less or break-up-sitting behaviors.

**Potential experiment signals:** sedentary time, physical activity.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only evidence that sedentary outcomes should be measured separately from step-count outcomes.
