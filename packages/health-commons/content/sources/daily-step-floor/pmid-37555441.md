This source is included for **dose_response_cut_points**.

**Findings:** This meta-analysis of 17 studies and 226,889 participants reported inverse associations between daily steps and both all-cause and cardiovascular mortality, including lower-risk cut-points around 3967 steps/day for all-cause mortality and 2337 steps/day for cardiovascular mortality, plus lower risk with each additional 1000 or 500 steps depending on endpoint.

**Why it matters:** Context for lower observed thresholds and “more steps, lower risk” messaging.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily step count, days meeting step floor, weekly mean steps, all-cause mortality, cardiovascular mortality.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as broad observational context with strong caveats against causal interpretation.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Generally healthy adults or adults at cardiovascular risk across cohort studies.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Daily step count increments and dose-response thresholds.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower versus higher daily step counts.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Median follow-up about 7.1 years across included studies.
- **Endpoints:** all-cause mortality, cardiovascular mortality, daily step count
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse-event signal specific to a step-floor intervention was extracted from this source in this batch.
- **Limitations:** Cohort evidence with heterogeneous populations and step measures; cut-points are statistical thresholds, not randomized prescriptions.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Mortality meta-analysis does not directly test a Daily Step Floor protocol.
- **Boundary:** This source is observational, review-based, registry-only, or otherwise adjacent unless explicitly noted; it must not be promoted into direct Daily Step Floor protocol evidence.
