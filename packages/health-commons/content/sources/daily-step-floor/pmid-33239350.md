This source is included for **guidelines_external_protocol_context**.

**Findings:** The WHO 2020 guideline summary recommends physical activity and sedentary-behaviour targets across age and special-population groups, but does not establish a daily step-floor intervention.

**Why it matters:** Provides global guideline backdrop and special-population boundaries for a walking/steps protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** physical_activity_minutes, sedentary_time, muscle_strengthening, daily_step_count.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only global guideline evidence; do not present it as direct support for a daily step floor.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Children, adolescents, adults, older adults, pregnant/postpartum women, and people living with chronic conditions or disability.
- **Exposure/intervention:** WHO 2020 physical activity and sedentary behaviour recommendations; not a daily step-count intervention.
- **Comparator/control:** No comparator or control group; guideline summary.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Not applicable; guideline summary.
- **Endpoints:** physical activity frequency, intensity and duration; sedentary behaviour; muscle-strengthening; health outcomes considered by guideline evidence reviews
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Summarizes WHO guidance including adult weekly aerobic activity ranges, muscle-strengthening, and sedentary-behaviour reduction; no step floor is tested.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** Guideline-level benefits/harms and population-specific recommendations are addressed; no intervention adverse-event dataset is reported.
- **Limitations:** Guideline summary, not a step-floor intervention or dose-response trial.
- **Population mismatch:** Broad global guideline context rather than a self-tracked daily step-floor protocol.
- **Artifact rights:** open_access
