This source is included for **direct_step_floor_trials**.

**Findings:**

- `finding:pmid-31237875-cardio-fracture-signal` — Routine data were available for 1,297 of 1,321 participants. Reported hazard ratios favoured intervention for cardiovascular events (HR 0.24, 95% CI 0.07 to 0.77) and fractures (HR 0.56, 95% CI 0.35 to 0.90).
- `finding:pmid-31237875-null-safety-outcomes` — The accessible record reported no significant differences for diabetes (HR 0.77, 95% CI 0.43 to 1.38), depression (HR 0.98, 95% CI 0.46 to 2.07), or falls (IRR 1.09, 95% CI 0.83 to 1.43).

**Why it matters:** Long-term health-outcome and safety context for primary-care pedometer walking interventions close to a step-floor protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- cardiovascular events
- fractures
- falls
- diabetes incidence
- depression

**Protocol takeaway:** Long-term follow-up suggests possible reductions in cardiovascular events and fractures after pedometer walking interventions, but event counts were low and several outcomes were null.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction boundaries

- **Directness:** `direct_protocol`.
- **Population:** Adults aged 45-75 years from PACE-UP and PACE-Lift primary-care pedometer walking trials
- **Intervention/exposure:** Prior 12-week pedometer-based walking interventions that increased walking and MVPA.
- **Comparator/control:** Original usual-activity/control groups in the parent RCTs.
- **Duration/follow-up:** About 4 years after intervention via routine primary-care data
- **Endpoints extracted:** cardiovascular events, diabetes, depression, fractures, falls.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event signal was extracted from the accessible source record; absence of extracted adverse events should not be interpreted as proof of no adverse events unless the finding explicitly says so.
- **Limitations:** Secondary long-term routine-data follow-up; low event counts for several outcomes; interventions were primary-care programs, not a simple direct-to-user daily floor.
- **Population mismatch:** Middle-aged and older primary-care trial participants aged 45-75 years.
- **Artifact/rights boundary:** `open_access`; no copyrighted PDF is stored in Git in this draft.
