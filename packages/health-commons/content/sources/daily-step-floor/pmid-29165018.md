This source is included for **baseline_plus_ramp_trials**.

**Findings:** A 12-week RCT in inactive older adults reported greater step-count increases with pedometer interventions than control, with tailored internet-mediated feedback outperforming pedometer-only.

**Why it matters:** Supports weekly percentage ramps and tailored digital feedback in older adults, with extraction caveats.

**Potential experiment signals:** steps/day, weekly +10% ramp adherence, retention, tailored feedback engagement.

**Protocol takeaway:** A weekly percentage ramp toward a fixed target can increase steps, especially with tailored feedback.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extracted study details

- **Population:** Inactive older adults.
- **Intervention or exposure:** Pedometer-only or tailored internet-mediated pedometer intervention; pedometer groups were instructed to increase steps by 10% weekly until 10,000 steps/day.
- **Comparator or control:** Control group.
- **Duration or follow-up:** 12 weeks.
- **Endpoints:** steps/day, retention, tailored internet feedback
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Pedometer-only and tailored internet-mediated pedometer groups increased step count versus control at 12 weeks; the tailored internet-mediated group exceeded pedometer-only. Retention was reported as 75.3%.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** Adverse-event reporting was not extracted from accessible records.
- **Limitations:** Permission-required article; exact step effects and safety details were not extracted; older-adult sample.
- **Population mismatch:** Inactive older adults.
