This source is included for **dose_response_cut_points**.

**Findings:** This 2025 dose-response meta-analysis included 57 studies from 35 cohorts and found that, compared with 2000 steps/day, 7000 steps/day was associated with lower risk for several outcomes including all-cause mortality, CVD incidence, dementia, depressive symptoms, type 2 diabetes, and falls; evidence certainty varied by outcome.

**Why it matters:** Most current broad dose-response synthesis in this batch.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily step count, days meeting step floor, weekly mean steps, all-cause mortality, cardiovascular disease, cardiovascular mortality, cancer incidence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Useful for current landscape context around 7000 steps/day; do not use as causal efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Adults in prospective studies of device-measured daily steps and multiple health outcomes.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Device-measured daily step count; comparisons around 2000 versus 7000 steps/day and dose-response curves.
- **Comparator/control:** Low step-count reference categories, including 2000 steps/day.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Searches from 2014 through 14 February 2025; follow-up varied.
- **Endpoints:** all-cause mortality, cardiovascular disease, cardiovascular mortality, cancer incidence, cancer mortality, type 2 diabetes, dementia, depressive symptoms, falls, daily step count
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse-event signal specific to a step-floor intervention was extracted from this source in this batch.
- **Limitations:** Prospective observational evidence with residual confounding, limited data for some outcomes, and low or very low certainty for several endpoints.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Broad health-outcome synthesis does not test a Daily Step Floor intervention.
- **Boundary:** This source is observational, review-based, registry-only, or otherwise adjacent unless explicitly noted; it must not be promoted into direct Daily Step Floor protocol evidence.
