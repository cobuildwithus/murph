This source is included for **direct_step_floor_trials**.

**Findings:**

- `finding:pmid-32348263-prescription-steps` — The accessible source reported that prescribing walking increased steps by an average of 4,806 steps/day regardless of sex or age (P<0.001), but both arms used a pedometer app and 10,000-step daily goal.

**Why it matters:** Includes a 10,000-step app target but is bundled with full in-person counseling and weight-loss treatment, making it adjacent rather than direct simple-floor evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- daily steps
- body composition
- weight
- BMI
- app self-monitoring

**Protocol takeaway:** Personalized walking prescription increased steps within a bundled weight-loss/counseling program; attribution to a simple daily floor alone is not appropriate.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction boundaries

- **Directness:** `adjacent_variant`.
- **Population:** Overweight adults with mean BMI about 32.5 kg/m²
- **Intervention/exposure:** Pedometer app, 10,000-steps/day goal, 24-week in-person behavioral weight-control program, plus structured information delivery, personalized physical activity prescription and follow-up.
- **Comparator/control:** Pedometer app, 10,000-steps/day goal, and the same 24-week behavioral weight-control program without the additional structured prescription/follow-up.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 24 weeks
- **Endpoints extracted:** daily steps, body composition, weight-loss outcomes.
- **Adverse events or safety notes:** No adverse-event signal was extracted from the accessible source record; absence of extracted adverse events should not be interpreted as proof of no adverse events unless the finding explicitly says so.
- **Limitations:** Both arms had a pedometer app and 10,000-step goal; intervention added counseling and personalized prescription; body-composition attribution to the step floor alone is confounded.
- **Population mismatch:** Overweight adults in a weight-loss program, not a general daily-step-floor cohort.
- **Artifact/rights boundary:** `open_access`; no copyrighted PDF is stored in Git in this draft.
