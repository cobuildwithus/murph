This source is included for **sedentary_activity_pattern**.

**Findings:** Meta-analysis of 17 RCTs reported reduced sitting time by about 41 minutes/day (95% CI roughly −61 to −22) in accessible summaries; heterogeneity was substantial.

**Why it matters:** Supports measuring sedentary time as an outcome for tech-supported activity experiments while keeping intervention specificity clear.

**Potential experiment signals:** sitting time, sedentary time, technology intervention components.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only evidence that digital prompts/self-monitoring can affect sitting time when the intervention targets sedentary behavior.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
