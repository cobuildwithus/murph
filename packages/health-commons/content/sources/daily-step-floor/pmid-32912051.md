This source is included for **safety_special_populations**.

**Findings:** In a 53-participant pilot RCT of older kidney transplant recipients, a SystemCHANGE activity-tracker intervention increased daily steps at 3 months by about 608 steps/day versus control.

**Why it matters:** Adds a transplant-specific physical-activity intervention signal and underscores the need for clinical supervision.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily-step-count, heart-rate, adherence, adverse-events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not apply Daily Step Floor generically to kidney transplant recipients without transplant-team clearance and monitoring.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness boundary:** clinical_supervised adjacent variant. Do not promote this source into direct Daily Step Floor claims beyond the stated claim-use boundary.

**Safety/adverse events:** Kidney transplant recipients require individualized clinical oversight and transplant-specific safety considerations.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Pilot sample; older transplant population; not a general Daily Step Floor trial. Kidney transplant special population; safety-only/adjacent evidence.
