This source is included for **guidelines_external_protocol_context**.

**Findings:** In the four-year follow-up of 10,000 Steps Ghent, daily steps increased slightly in the intervention community and decreased in the comparison community, but the authors concluded that the positive 1-year effects were not maintained after 4 years.

**Why it matters:** Important mixed/negative sustainability context for external 10,000-step campaigns.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily_step_count, physical_activity_minutes, program_maintenance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent mixed context; preserve the finding that 1-year positive effects were not maintained at 4 years.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Adults from the original 2005-2006 10,000 Steps Ghent intervention and comparison communities; 866 original participants were recontacted and 420 were analyzed for long-term effects.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Prior 10,000 Steps Ghent community intervention with pedometer-step messaging and community-level supports.
- **Comparator/control:** Comparison community Aalst versus intervention community Ghent in repeated follow-up analysis.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Four-year follow-up after the 2005-2006 intervention.
- **Endpoints:** 7-day pedometer log daily step counts; International Physical Activity Questionnaire self-reported physical activity; subgroup analyses by demographics and health/risk profile
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Daily step counts increased slightly in Ghent and decreased in Aalst (time × community P=0.008), but authors concluded the positive 1-year effects were not maintained after 4 years; the comparison-community decline was prevented in Ghent except among participants with poor-to-moderate health.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse events reported in the accessible extract.
- **Limitations:** Long-term follow-up attrition; subgroup findings; external whole-community intervention; not a personal step-floor protocol.
- **Population mismatch:** External multicomponent community campaign with follow-up, not a self-tracked Daily Step Floor intervention.
