This source is included for **cardiometabolic_fitness_endpoints**.

**Findings:** A Cochrane review of walking for hypertension found moderate-certainty evidence that walking reduces systolic BP and low-certainty evidence for DBP and heart-rate reductions. It is same-mechanism context because it concerns walking interventions broadly rather than a daily step floor.

**Why it matters:** Strong same-mechanism BP context for walking volume/intensity; also captures sparse safety reporting.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:morning-blood-pressure, biomarker:resting-heart-rate, adverse-event:knee-injury.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as walking-mechanism context for BP/RHR, not as direct step-floor efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness boundary:** `same_mechanism`. This source should not be promoted beyond that scope in the Daily Step Floor protocol.

**Safety/adverse events:** Only 21 studies reported adverse events; 16 reported none and five reported eight events, with knee injury reported five times.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Walking interventions are broad, not step-floor-specific; many studies at risk of selection/performance bias. Population mismatch: Walking intervention populations include broad ages/settings and do not specifically test daily step floors or device adherence.
