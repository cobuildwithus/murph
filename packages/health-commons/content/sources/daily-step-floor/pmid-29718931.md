This source is included for **safety_special_populations**.

**Findings:** In a 138-participant diabetes/prediabetes RCT, automated text reminders and goal setting improved some engagement signals but daily steps decreased across all arms and the randomized components did not increase steps overall.

**Why it matters:** Preserves a null/mixed result: reminders and goal elicitation improved engagement signals but did not increase daily steps.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily-step-count, wear adherence, goal-setting frequency, text-message response.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not assume automated reminders or goal setting alone will raise steps in diabetes; distinguish adherence data from behavior change.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness boundary:** clinical_supervised adjacent digital-step context. Do not promote this source into direct Daily Step Floor claims beyond the stated claim-use boundary.

**Safety/adverse events:** No safety signal was extracted for Daily Step Floor; diabetes populations still need foot and cardiometabolic context.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Diabetes/prediabetes sample; behavior-change components did not raise steps; not a step-floor target trial. Diabetes/prediabetes clinical population; context-only/mixed evidence.
