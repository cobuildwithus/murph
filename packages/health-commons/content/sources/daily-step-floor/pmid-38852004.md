This source is included for **cadence_intensity_bouts**.

**Findings:** UK Biobank validation comparison found cadence-based intensity estimates can work at group level but are problematic for individual-level intensity classification versus machine-learning methods.

**Why it matters:** Warns against over-interpreting cadence-derived MVPA for a single user.

**Potential experiment signals:** walking_cadence, activity_intensity_classification, mvpa_minutes, measurement_error.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use cadence as optional context and avoid making individual-level MVPA claims solely from cadence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** UK Biobank participants aged 40 years or older with wrist accelerometer data.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Cadence-based physical-activity intensity classification methods.
- **Comparator/control:** Machine-learning-based physical-activity intensity classification schema.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Wrist accelerometer monitoring period in UK Biobank; exact wear duration not extracted from accessible abstract.
- **Endpoints:** Group-level and individual-level agreement for MVPA, moderate, vigorous, and light activity duration.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Accessible abstract reports acceptable group-level performance for some cadence estimates but high individual-level differences, including large mean absolute errors for MVPA/moderate activity; individual-level cadence classification was not considered appropriate.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse events or safety outcomes were reported.
- **Limitations:** Measurement comparison, not intervention evidence; wrist accelerometer methods; exact individual-level errors and subgroup performance require full-text review.
- **Population mismatch:** Measurement context in UK Biobank, not Daily Step Floor efficacy evidence.
