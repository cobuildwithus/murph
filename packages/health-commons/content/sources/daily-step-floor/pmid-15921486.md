This source is included for **safety_special_populations**.

**Findings:** The Behavior Change Consortium report described safety screening and adverse-event procedures across physical-activity interventions and reported that few adverse events were attributed to the interventions across more than 5,500 participants.

**Why it matters:** Provides a precedent for explicit screening and adverse-event monitoring in behavior-change protocols that increase activity.

**Potential experiment signals:** adverse-events, falls, musculoskeletal pain, cardiovascular symptoms, protocol suspension.

**Protocol takeaway:** Daily Step Floor should include screening and clear adverse-event/off-ramp definitions rather than treating walking goals as universally risk-free.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.

**Directness boundary:** clinical_supervised safety-monitoring context. Do not promote this source into direct Daily Step Floor claims beyond the stated claim-use boundary.

**Safety/adverse events:** Emphasizes screening, safety procedures, reporting definitions, and adverse-event adjudication as part of behavior-change trials.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Programmatic safety report rather than a daily step-count intervention; trial populations and procedures varied. General PA interventions; not specific to step floors or pedometer goals.
