This source is included for **dose_response_cut_points**.

**Findings:** This meta-analysis of seven cohorts reported an inverse association between daily steps and all-cause mortality, including a pooled hazard ratio of 0.88 per additional 1000 steps/day and a nonlinear dose-response pattern across approximately 2700 to 17,000 steps/day.

**Why it matters:** Provides quantitative dose-response context for mortality.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily step count, days meeting step floor, weekly mean steps, all-cause mortality.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context for step-volume gradients; do not convert into a causal prescription.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Adults in prospective cohort studies of daily steps and all-cause mortality.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Daily step count; per-1000-step increments and dose-response curve.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower versus higher daily step counts.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Prospective follow-up; 175,370 person-years reported across included cohorts.
- **Endpoints:** all-cause mortality, daily step count
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse-event signal specific to a step-floor intervention was extracted from this source in this batch.
- **Limitations:** Small number of cohorts, high heterogeneity, observational data, and potential residual confounding.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** All-cause mortality cohorts are not Daily Step Floor intervention trials.
- **Boundary:** This source is observational, review-based, registry-only, or otherwise adjacent unless explicitly noted; it must not be promoted into direct Daily Step Floor protocol evidence.
