This source is included for **measurement_validity**.

**Findings:** Across 40 volunteers and four walking/jogging conditions, Garmin Vivosmart HR+ and Leaf Health Tracker were reliable and valid in all tested situations, while other devices had condition-specific limitations.

**Why it matters:** Activity mode and testing environment can change which devices are acceptable for step floors.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily_step_count, step_count_accuracy, device_placement_or_carriage, gait_speed_or_gait_aid_context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only evidence that device validity is condition-specific, even among common wearables.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Forty volunteers.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Samsung Gear 2, Fitbit Surge, Polar A360, Garmin Vivosmart HR+, and Leaf Health Tracker worn during walking and jogging.
- **Comparator/control:** Mean of two manual step counters.
- **Duration/follow-up:** 5-minute walking and jogging protocols in free-motion and treadmill settings.
- **Endpoints:** step-count reliability, step-count validity, environment and activity effects
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Garmin Vivosmart HR+ and Leaf Health Tracker were reliable and valid for all tested situations. Fitbit Surge was reliable and valid except treadmill walking, where it was reliable but not valid. Samsung Gear 2 was reliable and valid for jogging but not walking; Polar A360 was reliable for treadmill jogging only and not valid in any condition.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse events or safety outcomes were reported in the accessible extract.
- **Limitations:** Healthy-volunteer sample.; Short protocols rather than free-living daily use.; Older device models and proprietary algorithms.
- **Population mismatch:** Healthy device-validation context; not Daily Step Floor efficacy evidence.
- **Artifact rights:** open_access
