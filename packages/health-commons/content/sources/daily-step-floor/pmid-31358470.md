This source is included for **mental_health_sleep_qol**.

**Findings:** In a 59-person 4-week walking-intervention dataset, active minutes and within-person higher-activity days were associated with better self-reported sleep quality, and more active days were associated with longer sleep duration.

**Why it matters:** Useful daily-level step/activity and sleep relationship source with within-person signal.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:daily-steps, biomarker:sleep-quality, biomarker:sleep-duration.

**Protocol takeaway:** Low-impact daily activity may track with sleep quality/duration on active days, but causal protocol claims should be avoided.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
