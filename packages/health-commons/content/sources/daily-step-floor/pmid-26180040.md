This source is included for **guidelines_external_protocol_context**.

**Findings:** In 10,000 Steps Australia users, app-only and web-plus-app use, challenges, and logging days were associated with lower nonusage attrition compared with web-only use, but the study was observational and engagement-focused.

**Why it matters:** Useful for design of tracking and engagement supports, especially attrition risk.

**Potential experiment signals:** program_engagement, nonusage_attrition, daily_step_count, step_goal_adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only engagement evidence; do not infer health or direct step-floor efficacy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Adults using the freely available 10,000 Steps Australia website and/or iOS app who logged physical activity through the program.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Use of website-only, app-only, or web-and-app platforms, individual/workplace challenges, and step-log engagement features.
- **Comparator/control:** Platform-use groups and engagement-feature differences; no randomized comparator.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Users registered during a 297-day extraction period; attrition examined over the first 3 months in a subsample.
- **Endpoints:** duration of use; number of challenges; physical-activity logging days; nonusage attrition; step log entries
- **Effect estimates or direction:** In the 3-month subsample, 50% stopped logging after 30 days. App-only users (HR 0.63, 95% CI 0.58-0.68) and web-and-app users (HR 0.86, 95% CI 0.81-0.93) had lower nonusage attrition risk than web-only users; individual challenges and logging days were also associated with reduced attrition.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse events reported.
- **Limitations:** Real-world observational engagement analysis; includes only users who logged at least one day; logged steps are not objective total physical activity and platform selection is self-selected.
- **Population mismatch:** External digital program engagement study, not direct Daily Step Floor efficacy evidence.
- **Artifact rights:** open_access
