This source is included for **cadence_intensity_bouts**.

**Findings:** Systematic review found health benefits associated with physical activity accumulated in bouts shorter than 10 minutes as well as longer bouts, supporting removal of rigid bout requirements in guidelines.

**Why it matters:** Reinforces that step-floor steps need not be discarded when accumulated in brief walking fragments.

**Potential experiment signals:** activity_bout_duration, mvpa_minutes, cardiometabolic_markers, all_cause_mortality.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not require a 10-minute bout minimum for Daily Step Floor completion.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Physical-activity bout-duration literature; accessible summaries report 29 included articles.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Physical activity accumulated in bouts shorter than 10 minutes versus bouts of 10 minutes or longer.
- **Comparator/control:** Longer-bout versus shorter-bout physical activity accumulation; evidence varied by design.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Systematic review; source follow-up durations varied.
- **Endpoints:** Health outcomes including mortality and cardiometabolic outcomes; randomized and observational evidence considered.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** The review concluded that physical activity of any bout duration is associated with improved health outcomes, but randomized studies mostly tested bouts of 10 minutes or more.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse-event synthesis was extracted from accessible summaries.
- **Limitations:** Review covers physical activity broadly rather than step floors; randomized evidence for <10-minute bouts was limited; heterogeneous outcomes.
- **Population mismatch:** Adjacent guideline/bout-duration evidence; not a direct Daily Step Floor test.
