This source is included for **mental_health_sleep_qol**.

**Findings:** A 377-participant RCT in breast cancer survivors found that print materials and/or step pedometers improved physical activity and were associated with QoL/fatigue benefits versus recommendation-only in accessible abstracts, but exact effect estimates were not extracted.

**Why it matters:** Direct pedometer RCT with QoL/fatigue outcomes, but in a clinical survivorship population.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:daily-steps, biomarker:quality-of-life, biomarker:fatigue.

**Protocol takeaway:** Pedometers may support PA and QoL/fatigue outcomes in breast cancer survivors, but this should be treated as clinical-population evidence.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Directness boundary:** This source is classified as `direct_protocol` for Daily Step Floor. Do not promote adjacent, observational, registry/protocol, or clinical-population findings into direct protocol claims.

**Safety/adverse events:** No adverse-event details were extracted from accessible abstract/metadata.
