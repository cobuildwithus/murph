This source is included for **dose_response_cut_points**.

**Findings:** This umbrella review summarized 20 studies with 94 outcomes and reported generally negative correlations between higher step counts and several adverse outcomes, including metabolic disease, cardiovascular disease, all-cause mortality, and some cognitive/mental-health outcomes; some outcomes, such as blood-pressure improvement in outdoor walking groups, were not clearly associated.

**Why it matters:** Captures broader health-outcome context and mixed findings.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily step count, days meeting step floor, weekly mean steps, metabolic outcomes, cardiovascular outcomes, all-cause mortality, postural balance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as broad context while preserving endpoint-specific uncertainty.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Healthy populations represented in systematic reviews and meta-analyses of step counts and health outcomes.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Daily step count and dose-response step-count metrics.
- **Comparator/control:** Lower versus higher step counts in included evidence syntheses.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Not applicable; umbrella review of published syntheses.
- **Endpoints:** metabolic outcomes, cardiovascular outcomes, all-cause mortality, postural balance, cognitive function, mental health
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse-event signal specific to a step-floor intervention was extracted from this source in this batch.
- **Limitations:** Umbrella-review overlap, heterogeneous endpoints, and varying certainty across outcomes; not causal intervention evidence.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Broad multiple-outcome umbrella review is adjacent landscape evidence only.
- **Boundary:** This source is observational, review-based, registry-only, or otherwise adjacent unless explicitly noted; it must not be promoted into direct Daily Step Floor protocol evidence.
