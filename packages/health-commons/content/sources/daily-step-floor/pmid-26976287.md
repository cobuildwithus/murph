This source is included for **direct_step_floor_trials**.

**Findings:**

- `finding:pmid-26976287-combined-incentive` — Combined incentives improved goal achievement versus control (0.35 versus 0.18 participant-days; difference 0.17, 95% CI 0.07 to 0.28; P<0.001) and increased daily steps by 1,446 versus control (95% CI 448 to 2,444).
- `finding:pmid-26976287-follow-up-not-sustained` — The accessible source reported no differences among arms during follow-up after incentives ended.

**Why it matters:** Adjacent incentive architecture source that uses a concrete daily step threshold.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- step-goal attainment
- daily steps
- post-incentive maintenance

**Protocol takeaway:** Combined incentives improved goal attainment and steps during the intervention, but standalone team/individual incentives were weaker and effects did not persist after incentives ended.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
