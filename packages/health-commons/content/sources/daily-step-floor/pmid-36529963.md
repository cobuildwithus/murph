This source is included for **mental_health_sleep_qol**.

**Findings:** In a 1,451-participant observational cohort analysis, higher physical activity was associated with lower depression scores, while anxiety and sleep-quality associations were not significant in accessible summaries.

**Why it matters:** Preserves mixed same-mechanism mental-health/sleep evidence: depression association present, anxiety/sleep not confirmed.

**Potential experiment signals:** biomarker:daily-steps, biomarker:depressive-symptoms, biomarker:anxiety, biomarker:sleep-quality.

**Protocol takeaway:** Device-measured activity may relate to depressive symptoms, but this source does not support broad anxiety or sleep-quality claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness boundary:** This source is classified as `same_mechanism` for Daily Step Floor. Do not promote adjacent, observational, registry/protocol, or clinical-population findings into direct protocol claims.

**Safety/adverse events:** Observational exposure study; adverse events were not applicable/extracted.
