This source is included for **cadence_intensity_bouts**.

**Findings:** Small treadmill calibration study found sex-specific moderate-intensity thresholds near 100 spm and vigorous thresholds above 125 spm.

**Why it matters:** Early empirical support for the 100 spm heuristic.

**Potential experiment signals:** walking_cadence, activity_intensity_classification, mvpa_minutes.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as historical support for optional cadence context; do not rely on it alone for protocol claims.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Nine men and ten women, mean age about 29 years, in a treadmill walking/running protocol.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Step rate during treadmill walking/running with metabolic cost measurement.
- **Comparator/control:** Nonlinear regression thresholds corresponding to moderate and vigorous intensity.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Single laboratory protocol across multiple treadmill speeds.
- **Endpoints:** Step-rate thresholds for moderate and vigorous physical activity intensity.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Accessible summaries report thresholds of 94 and 125 spm for men and 99 and 135 spm for women for moderate and vigorous intensity; authors concluded 100 spm may serve as a practical moderate-intensity recommendation.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse events or safety outcomes were reported in accessible summaries.
- **Limitations:** Very small sample; treadmill walking/running; early calibration study; not free-living or intervention evidence.
- **Population mismatch:** Same-mechanism cadence validation only.
