This source is included for **cadence_intensity_bouts**.

**Findings:** Narrative review found cadence is a feasible but underdeveloped behavioral target in physical-activity interventions, with only a small intervention literature.

**Why it matters:** Supports optional cadence add-ons while underscoring that the protocol should stay simple.

**Potential experiment signals:** walking_cadence, behavior_change, mvpa_minutes, daily_step_count.

**Protocol takeaway:** Cadence can be an optional behavior-change target after the step floor is established; evidence is not mature enough to require it.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Behavioral interventions that used cadence; accessible review summary reports 6 included studies from 232 unique records.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Cadence targets delivered through cues such as subjective instructions, time goals, step counting, auditory/visual prompts, pedometers, or media devices.
- **Comparator/control:** Different cadence implementation strategies across included interventions.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Narrative review; included intervention durations varied.
- **Endpoints:** Physical activity intervention feasibility; cadence target implementation; MVPA-related walking behavior.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** The review found that cadence has been implemented in a small number of behavioral interventions and appears feasible, but application is nascent and reporting details are limited.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse-event synthesis was extracted from accessible summaries.
- **Limitations:** Narrative review with only six included studies; cadence instructions often under-described; no consensus on best implementation.
- **Population mismatch:** Adjacent behavioral target literature, not direct Daily Step Floor evidence.
