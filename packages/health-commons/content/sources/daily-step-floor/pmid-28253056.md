This source is included for **cadence_intensity_bouts**.

**Findings:** Small randomized/controlled cadence-prescription study in inactive older adults reported increased moderate-intensity 10-minute walking bouts when individualized cadence and pedometer feedback were used.

**Why it matters:** Shows cadence can be used as an intervention cue to increase walking intensity, but it should remain an optional tactic for a step-floor protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** walking_cadence, mvpa_minutes, moderate_intensity_bouts, daily_step_count.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports optional cadence coaching as an adjacent strategy; do not make cadence mandatory for the Daily Step Floor.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Inactive older adults; accessible abstract reports intervention n=23 and control n=22.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Individualized walking cadence prescription plus pedometer feedback to help older adults accumulate moderate-intensity walking.
- **Comparator/control:** Walking recommendation without individualized cadence prescription/pedometer intensity tracking.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Two-phase walking recommendation/prescription study; exact phase duration was not extracted from the accessible abstract.
- **Endpoints:** Time walked at moderate intensity; 10-minute moderate-intensity walking bouts; global physical activity recommendation attainment.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Accessible abstracts report that older adults increased weekly time walked at moderate intensity in 10-minute bouts when using individualized cadence plus a pedometer.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse events or safety signals were reported in the accessible extract.
- **Limitations:** Small sample; older inactive adult population; adjacent cadence-prescription intervention rather than a simple daily step-floor protocol; exact effect sizes were not extracted.
- **Population mismatch:** Older inactive adults in a cadence-prescription intervention; not general unsupervised Daily Step Floor evidence.
