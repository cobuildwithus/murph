**Findings:** Wearable-monitor validity requires calibration and validation against appropriate criterion measures, with attention to placement, protocol, and intended use.

**Why it matters:** Useful for setting methodological standards around device-derived Daily Step Floor endpoints.

**Potential experiment signals:** Daily steps, step-count measurement validity, device/placement bias, wear-time, and multi-day reliability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context for measurement standards, not as a device-specific accuracy claim.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness boundary:** This is measurement-context evidence for Daily Step Floor. It should not be promoted into a direct claim that the protocol increases steps, improves biomarkers, or causes health outcomes.
