This source is included for **dose_response_cut_points**.

**Findings:** The review summarized normative data indicating healthy older adults often average about 2000–9000 steps/day and special populations about 1200–8800 steps/day; it also summarized pedometer interventions as smaller average increases in older adults than some other special-population groups.

**Why it matters:** Adds population-boundary context for step floors and conservative starting levels.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily step count, days meeting step floor, weekly mean steps, step-count norms, pedometer intervention step increases.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to justify caution and individualization for older or clinically limited users; not direct Daily Step Floor proof.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Older adults and special populations including chronic disease and disability groups.
- **Exposure/intervention:** Daily steps and pedometer-based interventions.
- **Comparator/control:** Normative ranges and pre/post pedometer intervention changes.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Not applicable; narrative review.
- **Endpoints:** daily step count, step-count norms, pedometer intervention step increases
- **Safety/adverse events:** No adverse-event signal specific to a step-floor intervention was extracted from this source in this batch.
- **Limitations:** Narrative synthesis with heterogeneous populations and intervention designs.
- **Population mismatch/directness:** Explicitly highlights that one universal target may not fit older adults or special populations.
- **Boundary:** This source is observational, review-based, registry-only, or otherwise adjacent unless explicitly noted; it must not be promoted into direct Daily Step Floor protocol evidence.
