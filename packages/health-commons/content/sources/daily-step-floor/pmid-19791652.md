This source is included for **direct_protocol_reviews**.

**Findings:** Physical activity and daily steps: The meta-analysis reported a moderate positive effect size of 0.68 (95% CI 0.55 to 0.81; I2=77%) and interpreted the benefit as approximately 2000 additional steps/day compared with comparator or baseline.; Physical activity effect size: Reported effects were larger in female samples (ES 0.80; 95% CI 0.64 to 0.97) and in studies using a 10,000-steps/day goal (ES 0.84; 95% CI 0.43 to 1.24).; Evidence certainty: The DARE appraisal noted absence of a stated validity assessment and limited detail about included study designs, so effect estimates should be used cautiously.

**Why it matters:** This direct pedometer meta-analysis supplies an interpretable activity effect size and explicitly links pedometer interventions to approximately 2000 extra steps/day.

**Potential experiment signals:** daily-step-count, weekly-active-minutes.

**Protocol takeaway:** A Daily Step Floor is consistent with evidence that pedometer-based motivational tools can raise daily step counts, especially when paired with clear goals.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
