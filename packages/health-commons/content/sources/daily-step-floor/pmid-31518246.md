This source is included for **cadence_intensity_bouts**.

**Findings:** Small older-adult modeling study suggested moderate-intensity step-rate thresholds closer to ~110 spm and vigorous thresholds above ~130 spm.

**Why it matters:** Adds age/body-size caveats to the 100 spm heuristic.

**Potential experiment signals:** walking_cadence, anthropometrics, mvpa_minutes, activity_intensity_classification.

**Protocol takeaway:** For older adults, do not assume 100 spm always captures relative or individualized moderate intensity.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Extraction notes

- **Population:** Older adults (7 women; mean age about 69 years) in a staged treadmill protocol.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Step rate during treadmill walking and anthropometric factors including BMI, height, and leg length.
- **Comparator/control:** Absolute and relative moderate/vigorous intensity thresholds from metabolic criteria.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Single laboratory protocol with multiple 6-minute treadmill stages.
- **Endpoints:** Step-rate thresholds for MPA and VPA; anthropometric model contribution.
- **Effect estimates or direction:** Accessible summaries report older adults generally needed faster than 100 spm—approximately 110 spm—to reach moderate intensity and more than approximately 130 spm for vigorous intensity; BMI was more informative than height or leg length in modeling.
- **Adverse events/safety notes:** No adverse events or safety outcomes were reported in accessible summaries.
- **Limitations:** Very small older-adult treadmill sample; modeling study; not free-living; not a step-floor intervention.
- **Population mismatch:** Same-mechanism measurement context, not direct Daily Step Floor evidence.
