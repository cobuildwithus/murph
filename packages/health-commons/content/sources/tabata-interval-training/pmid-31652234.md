This source is included for **Rhabdomyolysis, heat, and serious exertional adverse-event safety**.

**Findings:** The study reviewed 431 rhabdomyolysis cases in 430 patients and identified 20 exertional rhabdomyolysis cases. Reported context included one recurrence, no deaths attributed to ER, common causes such as endurance activity/manual labor/weight lifting, and complications including kidney injury in five cases.

**Why it matters:** It broadens clinical context beyond highly selected case reports.

**Potential experiment signals:** exertional case status, kidney injury, recurrence, activity cause, and mortality attribution.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as population-based context for exertional rhabdomyolysis safety language.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
