This source is included for **Reviews, definitions, and variant taxonomy**.

**Findings:** Review reported generally moderate-to-large aerobic-fitness and subjective-response effects favoring HIMT, mixed muscular-fitness outcomes, and high risk of bias.

**Study design and population:** Systematic review; Apparently healthy populations in high-intensity multimodal training studies.

**Intervention or exposure:** High-intensity multimodal training combining aerobic and resistance-style elements in a single session.

**Comparator or control:** Controls or alternative exercise conditions depending on included study.

**Duration or follow-up:** Chronic or long-term interventions of at least four weeks were in scope.

**Endpoints:** Aerobic fitness, muscular fitness, subjective responses, exercise enjoyment, study terminology and heterogeneity.

**Safety notes:** No batch-level adverse-event estimate extracted; subjective response and tolerability are relevant.

**Limitations and mismatch:** High risk of bias, heterogeneity in protocols and outcomes, limited comparison across HIMT styles and other concurrent training protocols. Population mismatch: Apparently healthy HIMT studies are adjacent to, not direct evidence for, original Tabata 20/10 cycling.

**Why it matters:** It helps separate functional workout evidence from original Tabata protocol evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** estimated VO2max, muscular fitness proxy, exercise enjoyment, adherence, symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** A bodyweight or multimodal Tabata variant should be labeled as adjacent HIMT evidence unless it directly tests the target protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
