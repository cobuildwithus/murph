This source is included for **practical_or_population_boundary_tabata**.

**Findings:** In `source_artifact:pmid-22994393`, 22 recreationally active young women were allocated to whole-body 20/10 interval training, endurance treadmill training, or non-training control. The interval group improved VO2peak by roughly 8% and improved several muscular-endurance tests; the endurance group improved VO2peak by roughly 7%; the control group did not show clear changes.

**Why it matters:** It is a commonly cited practical Tabata-style adaptation and helps separate bodyweight 20/10 claims from original supramaximal cycle-ergometer Tabata claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** Estimated VO2max, muscular-endurance tests, enjoyment, and repeat-intention ratings.

**Protocol takeaway:** Adjacent support only. Use for feasibility and plausible outcomes of bodyweight 20/10 work, not as direct proof of the Murph starter protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
