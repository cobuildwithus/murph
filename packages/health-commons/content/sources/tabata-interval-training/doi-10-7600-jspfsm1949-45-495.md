This source is included for **direct_original_dose_evidence**.

**Findings:** The English abstract reports six subjects training 5 days per week for 6 weeks with seven to eight 20-second bouts at about 170% VO2max and 10-second rests. Maximal oxygen deficit increased from 64.3 ± 5.0 to 75.1 ± 5.7 mL/kg and VO2max increased from 52.0 ± 2.7 to 57.6 ± 2.9 mL/kg/min; a later combined resistance phase further increased maximal oxygen deficit without a further significant VO2max increase.

**Why it matters:** It supports the same direct supramaximal 20s:10s cycling dose and adds a resistance-training phase for anaerobic capacity context.

**Potential experiment signals:** VO2max, maximal oxygen deficit, resistance-training add-on response, body-weight stability, thigh circumference.

**Protocol takeaway:** This source supports direct adaptation claims for the cycling protocol, but the combined resistance phase should not be merged into pure Tabata claims.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
