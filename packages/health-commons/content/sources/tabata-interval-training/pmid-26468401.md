This source is included for **safety_cardiac_screening**.

**Findings:** Exercise-related SCA in London was rare, predominantly male, and had better survival than broader OHCA comparators. This is a source-local safety finding for `source_artifact:pmid-26468401` and is not direct Tabata 20/10 efficacy evidence.

**Why it matters:** It provides open-access, setting-level data on the emergency-response side of exercise-related cardiac arrest.

**Potential experiment signals:** exercise-related SCA incidence; bystander CPR; bystander defibrillation; survival to discharge.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for emergency preparedness and serious adverse-event context only.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`. Use this source for safety screening, stop-condition, risk-context, or emergency-readiness language only. Do not use it to claim Tabata 20/10 improves outcomes or is safe for unscreened high-risk users.
