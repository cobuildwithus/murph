## Findings

- [source_artifact:pmid-41030318] Twenty-four elite swimmers were assigned to upper-body HIIT or MICT for 4 weeks, 3 sessions per week.
- [source_artifact:pmid-41030318] The HIIT protocol used eight resistance-band exercises, each 20 seconds maximal intensity with 10 seconds rest, repeated across three sets.
- [source_artifact:pmid-41030318] HIIT showed significant improvements in VO2max and energy-metabolism indicators versus MICT, but the authors warned against generalizing to total-body HIIT.

## Why it matters

It widens the boundary map for 20/10 timing in sport-specific upper-body training while warning against generalizing modality-specific adaptations.

## Protocol takeaway

Use as athlete/modality context only: the positive physiology signal does not translate automatically to full-body, self-directed Tabata.
