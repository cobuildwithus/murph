This source is included for **Safety: musculoskeletal injury / HIFT-CrossFit boundary**.

**Findings:** The review reported shoulders, knees, and back as the areas mainly presenting higher injury risk; 11 of the 12 included studies were rated poor methodological quality by the NIH tool. The source is classified as `adjacent_variant` and `safety-only` for the Tabata 20/10 protocol. Citation key: `source_artifact:pmid-32774557`.

**Why it matters:** Use the body-region pattern as a safety checklist, not as a precise injury-rate estimate for Tabata 20/10.

**Potential experiment signals:** Track shoulder/back/knee symptoms, pain severity, missed workouts, medical-care seeking, and load-scaling decisions when the Tabata-style workout is implemented as a functional circuit.

**Protocol takeaway:** Safety-only adjacent evidence: flag shoulder, knee, and back symptoms as “things to watch,” while noting low certainty.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`. The review is adjacent and heterogeneous; it cannot support direct protocol efficacy or causal injury claims.
