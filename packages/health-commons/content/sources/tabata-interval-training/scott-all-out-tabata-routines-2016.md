This source is included for **direct_original_dose_evidence**.

**Findings:** Fifteen physically active adults completed all-out 4-minute 20s:10s routines. Total energy cost was lowest for resistance exercises compared with calisthenic and plyometric routines; RPE and component energy costs also varied by routine.

**Why it matters:** It directly shows that a generic 4-minute Tabata label does not imply a single energy cost.

**Potential experiment signals:** total energy cost, aerobic cost, anaerobic lactate estimate, EPOC, RPE, repetitions.

**Protocol takeaway:** Energy-cost claims for Tabata-style workouts need exercise-mode specificity.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
