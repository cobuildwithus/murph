## Findings

- [source_artifact:doi-10.3390/life16040646] Thirty-two male amateur soccer players completed small-sided games and running-based Tabata-HIIT on separate days.
- [source_artifact:doi-10.3390/life16040646] Both conditions reached about 90% HRmax, but Tabata reduced countermovement-jump performance and increased recovery mental effort/RPE.
- [source_artifact:doi-10.3390/life16040646] No inter-protocol differences were reported for squat jump, sprint, or agility; cognitive gains after both conditions may include practice effects.

## Why it matters

It identifies an athlete/performance boundary: the same 20/10 structure can add acute neuromuscular and mental-effort burden compared with game-like conditioning.

## Protocol takeaway

Use as athlete-context evidence for acute fatigue and perceptual load, not as adult health-outcome support.
