This source is included for **direct_practical_20_10_trials**.

## Findings

- [source_artifact:pmid-37781251] Forty-two young adults were randomized to HIIT, Tabata, or sedentary control, with Tabata lasting 12 minutes and targeting 80–95% HRmax.
- [source_artifact:pmid-37781251] Mean Tabata HR and RPE were vigorous, but Stroop/Simon performance did not show a significant condition-by-time-by-group benefit.
- [source_artifact:pmid-37781251] fNIRS signals changed in prefrontal regions, so the paper is mechanism/context evidence rather than outcome support.

## Why it matters

It offers a direct acute Tabata exposure with HR/RPE monitoring while showing that performance outcomes can be null even when brain-activation signals change.
