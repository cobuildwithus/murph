This source is included for **Rhabdomyolysis, heat, and serious exertional adverse-event safety**.

**Findings:** The case describes a 21-year-old healthy man with bilateral lower-limb pain and dark urine after lower-extremity training, CK reported at 140,500 IU/L, myoglobin at 8,632 mg/L, elevated creatinine/proteinuria, and acute kidney injury; renal function returned after treatment during a six-day hospitalization.

**Why it matters:** It maps to the same muscle-breakdown mechanism relevant to squat-heavy interval variants.

**Potential experiment signals:** lower-limb pain, dark urine, CK, myoglobin, serum creatinine, proteinuria, hospitalization, and renal recovery.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as same-mechanism safety evidence for lower-body overload.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
