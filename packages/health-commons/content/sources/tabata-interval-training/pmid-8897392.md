This source is included for **direct_original_dose_evidence**.

**Findings:** The high-intensity intermittent subgroup performed seven to eight 20-second cycle bouts at about 170% VO2max with 10-second rests, 5 days per week for 6 weeks. VO2max increased by about 7 mL/kg/min and anaerobic capacity increased by 28%; moderate endurance training increased VO2max but not anaerobic capacity.

**Why it matters:** It anchors the original Tabata training dose, including the 20-second work, 10-second rest structure and the combined aerobic/anaerobic adaptation claim.

**Potential experiment signals:** VO2max, maximal accumulated oxygen deficit, anaerobic capacity, protocol completion, training frequency.

**Protocol takeaway:** When citing Tabata efficacy, the claim should point to exhaustive supramaximal cycling performed frequently over 6 weeks, not generic 4-minute exercise circuits.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
