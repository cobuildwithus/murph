**Findings:** In an acute comparison, short 4×8×20-second maximal running intervals and long 4×4-minute intervals had similar recovery oxygen volume; short intervals were faster, had lower breathing RPE, and produced longer EPOC (`source_artifact:pmid-30539113`).

**Why it matters:** It is useful for long-versus-short interval boundaries, not for direct Tabata training claims (`source_artifact:pmid-30539113`).

**Potential experiment signals:** RPE, breathing RPE, heart rate, EPOC proxy, lactate, and velocity.

**Protocol takeaway:** Adjacent acute variant only.
