This source is included for **safety_cardiac_screening**.

**Findings:** Medical-clearance decisions should depend on current activity, signs or symptoms, known disease, and desired exercise intensity. This is a source-local safety finding for `source_artifact:pmid-26473759` and is not direct Tabata 20/10 efficacy evidence.

**Why it matters:** The original 20/10 format is not a gentle entry-level dose, so the intended-intensity element of the ACSM algorithm is directly relevant to safety wording.

**Potential experiment signals:** known cardiovascular, metabolic, or renal disease; current physical activity status; planned vigorous-intensity exercise; new symptoms before or during training.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as the primary safety-screening source for deciding when medical clearance is appropriate before vigorous intervals.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`. Use this source for safety screening, stop-condition, risk-context, or emergency-readiness language only. Do not use it to claim Tabata 20/10 improves outcomes or is safe for unscreened high-risk users.
