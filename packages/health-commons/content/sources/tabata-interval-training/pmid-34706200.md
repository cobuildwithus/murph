**Findings:** The review included randomized asthma HIIT/SIT studies and reported possible benefits on FEV1 or VO2max, but the protocols and quality were heterogeneous. Use as respiratory safety context only.

**Why it matters:** It covers the respiratory clinical population most likely to need extra safety language around hard intervals.

**Potential experiment signals:** FEV1, VO2max, asthma symptoms, exacerbations, adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Asthma users need clinician-guided intensity and medication planning; Tabata claims should remain safety-limited.
