This source is included for **Rhabdomyolysis, heat, and serious exertional adverse-event safety**.

**Findings:** The retrospective cohort reviewed 29 rhabdomyolysis cases, including 14 admissions attributed to spinning. Median admission CK was reported higher for spinning-associated cases than comparison cases.

**Why it matters:** It gives class-based, cycle-adjacent evidence beyond CrossFit reports.

**Potential experiment signals:** spinning exposure, CK, hospital admission, and unaccustomed/group-exercise context.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent cycle/group-exercise safety context.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
