## Findings

- [source_artifact:pmid-41034815] A small-sized elementary school with 1,162 grade 3–6 students tested a recess intervention with baseline and 1-week post-test measures.
- [source_artifact:pmid-41034815] Structured recess changed to Tabata activity and significantly increased MVPA and VPA duration for boys and girls.
- [source_artifact:pmid-41034815] Unstructured recess game markings increased observed participation, but duration changes were mostly non-significant except boys’ VPA.

## Why it matters

It preserves the school-child boundary and prevents pediatric recess activity findings from being promoted as adult Tabata claims.
