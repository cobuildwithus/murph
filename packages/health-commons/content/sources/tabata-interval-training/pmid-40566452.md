**Findings:** Seventy-two overweight/obese participants were randomized to control, HIIT, extract, or HIIT+extract. The combined HIIT+extract group improved selected HRV and pulmonary/body-composition measures; HIIT alone improved peak blood flow and maximum expiratory pressure in accessible results.
