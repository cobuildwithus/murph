This source is included for **Rhabdomyolysis, heat, and serious exertional adverse-event safety**.

**Findings:** The retrospective cohort included 41 hospitalized patients with exertional rhabdomyolysis. Reported outcomes included no deaths, no compartment syndrome or severe electrolyte abnormalities, and two uncomplicated readmissions attributed to high-intensity interval training.

**Why it matters:** It contextualizes what happens after exertional rhabdomyolysis reaches hospital care.

**Potential experiment signals:** hospitalization, CK, length of stay, readmission, compartment syndrome, severe electrolyte abnormalities, and death.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only clinical-outcome context.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
