This source is included for **safety_msk_rhabdo_heat_respiratory**.

**Findings:** In trained athletes, orthostatic HR and HRV responded to overload and recovery phases, but correlations with short-term performance were weak or inconsistent. Use for recovery monitoring boundaries only.

**Why it matters:** It helps frame HRV/resting-HR changes as recovery-context signals during concentrated high-intensity stress.

**Potential experiment signals:** orthostatic HR, LnRMSSD, resting HR, sleep and readiness, performance drop.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use HRV and resting HR as things-to-watch, not primary proof of benefit.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
