This source is included for **Safety: musculoskeletal injury / HIFT-CrossFit boundary**.

**Findings:** The review included 25 studies involving 12,079 CrossFit practitioners. Mean injury prevalence was 35.3%, incidence rates varied from 0.2 to 18.9 injuries per 1000 training hours, and the most injured areas were shoulder (26%), spine (24%), and knee (18%). Among studies reporting surgery, the mean percentage of injuries requiring surgery was 8.7%. The source is classified as `safety_boundary` and `safety-only` for the Tabata 20/10 protocol. Citation key: `source_artifact:pmid-33322981`.

**Why it matters:** Use for safety boundary language and endpoint selection, not direct protocol claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** Track shoulder/back/knee symptoms, pain severity, missed workouts, medical-care seeking, and load-scaling decisions when the Tabata-style workout is implemented as a functional circuit.

**Protocol takeaway:** Safety-only boundary evidence: track shoulder, spine/back, knee, and surgery/medical-care outcomes if functional circuits are used.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`. Permission-required source; no copyrighted full text should be stored, and conclusions remain limited by underlying study quality.
