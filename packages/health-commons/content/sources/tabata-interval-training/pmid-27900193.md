This source is included for **Rhabdomyolysis, heat, and serious exertional adverse-event safety**.

**Findings:** The review describes exertional rhabdomyolysis as muscle breakdown after strenuous or contextually extreme exercise, with severe pain, CK elevation, and possible myoglobinuria. It highlights that recurrent or unusually severe presentations may indicate an underlying myopathy.

**Why it matters:** It helps prevent over-normalizing severe CK/pain signals after intense or unaccustomed exercise.

**Potential experiment signals:** severe pain, dark urine, very high or recurrent CK elevations when tested, repeat episodes, and disproportionate response to ordinary exercise.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to separate expected soreness from red flags that warrant medical evaluation.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
