This source is included for **safety_cardiac_screening**.

**Findings:** Exercise-related SCA cases more often had shockable rhythms and bystander CPR, with better survival than at-rest SCA in this cohort. This is a source-local safety finding for `source_artifact:pmid-30094241` and is not direct Tabata 20/10 efficacy evidence.

**Why it matters:** It emphasizes that bystander response and shockable rhythm are central safety-context variables in exercise-related events.

**Potential experiment signals:** bystander CPR; initial rhythm; survival to discharge; exercise-related versus rest SCA.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for serious-adverse-event context and emergency-response language only.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`. Use this source for safety screening, stop-condition, risk-context, or emergency-readiness language only. Do not use it to claim Tabata 20/10 improves outcomes or is safe for unscreened high-risk users.
