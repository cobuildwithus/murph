This source is included for **Rhabdomyolysis, heat, and serious exertional adverse-event safety**.

**Findings:** The case involved a 27-year-old previously healthy and active man after 720 squat repetitions, with dark urine, lower-extremity myalgia, CK reported at 54,250 U/L, normal creatinine/BUN, hospitalization for two days, and IV hydration.

**Why it matters:** It preserves a population mismatch: healthy and active status did not prevent an adverse event under extreme volume.

**Potential experiment signals:** repetition volume, dark urine, severe myalgia, CK, renal function, and hospitalization duration.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent-variant warning against abrupt high-volume squat protocols.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
