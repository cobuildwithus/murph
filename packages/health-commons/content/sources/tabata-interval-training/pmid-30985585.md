This source is included for **safety_cardiac_screening**.

**Findings:** Updated ACSM screening substantially reduced medical-referral classifications in a young university cohort. This is a source-local safety finding for `source_artifact:pmid-30985585` and is not direct Tabata 20/10 efficacy evidence.

**Why it matters:** It prevents overbroad safety language that might medicalize every young healthy user before any vigorous activity.

**Potential experiment signals:** screening category; medical clearance recommendation; vigorous-only clearance; young-adult population mismatch.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for screening burden/population mismatch context.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`. Use this source for safety screening, stop-condition, risk-context, or emergency-readiness language only. Do not use it to claim Tabata 20/10 improves outcomes or is safe for unscreened high-risk users.
