This source is included for **adjacent_hiit_sit_boundary**.

**Findings:** In Study 1, both placebo-plus-HIIT and chlorella-plus-HIIT increased VO2max, with greater VO2max change in the chlorella condition; Study 2 linked chlorella to stroke-volume/cardiac-output changes, and Study 3 assessed rat cardiac maladaptation markers (`source_artifact:pmid-40871685`).

**Why it matters:** It is an important boundary source because the supplement co-intervention and animal component mean the results must not be attributed to Tabata 20/10 alone (`source_artifact:pmid-40871685`).

**Potential experiment signals:** VO2max, MAOD proxy, heart-rate/cardiac-function measures where available, supplement exposure, and adverse-effect notes.

**Protocol takeaway:** Adjacent supplement co-intervention only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
