**Findings:** Thirteen active young adults completed 10/50, 20/100, and 40/200 s cycling HIIT protocols; HRpeak and RPE rose with longer work bouts, time-domain HRV changed acutely, and all HRV time-domain measures recovered before 60 minutes.
