This source is included for **direct_original_dose_evidence**.

**Findings:** Sixteen participants performed randomized 20s:10s cycling protocols at 115%, 130%, and 170% PPO. The authors reported significant group and intensity effects, dominant aerobic contribution across protocols, lower-intensity dominance of aerobic contribution, and inability to complete the initially proposed number of sprints at 170% PPO.

**Why it matters:** It provides open-access evidence on how 20s:10s interval intensity changes session duration, oxygen uptake, lactate, heart rate, and energy-system contribution across training backgrounds.

**Potential experiment signals:** protocol duration, completed sprints, oxygen uptake, blood lactate, peak heart rate, energy-system contribution.

**Protocol takeaway:** A 20s:10s format can be mostly aerobic in relative energetic contribution even when work bouts are supramaximal, and 170% PPO may be too intense to complete the initially proposed number of sprints.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
