**Findings:** After a single 4-minute 20/10 cycling session at 140% VO2max, pulse rate and systolic blood pressure rose acutely, circulating EV number changed, and 20 EV proteins were reported to change across sampled time points (`source_artifact:pmid-34276412`).

**Why it matters:** It gives mechanistic context for acute biological response but is not a chronic training or public-protocol efficacy source (`source_artifact:pmid-34276412`).

**Potential experiment signals:** Heart rate, blood pressure, perceived exertion, and recovery state.
