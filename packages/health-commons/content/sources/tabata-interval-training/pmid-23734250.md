This source is included for **adjacent_hiit_sit_boundary**.

**Findings:** In inactive overweight men, both 1×4-minute and 4×4-minute aerobic interval training increased VO2max and improved work economy; FMD was unchanged, and lipid/body-fat changes were more limited to the 4×4 arm (`source_artifact:pmid-23734250`).

**Why it matters:** It helps separate low-volume aerobic intervals from Tabata 20/10 and flags that low-volume benefits do not automatically replace broader activity guidance (`source_artifact:pmid-23734250`).

**Potential experiment signals:** VO2max proxy, blood pressure, fasting glucose, body weight, RPE, and adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Adjacent variant only; the interval structure, intensity prescription, and population differ from Tabata 20/10.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
