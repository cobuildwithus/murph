This source is included for **cardiometabolic_body_composition**.

**Findings:** Generic HIIT improved several cardiometabolic markers in some adult groups, with clearer body-composition signals in longer-term or overweight/obese contexts. Directionally positive for some cardiometabolic markers, but body-composition and metabolic effects vary by subgroup and intervention duration.

**Why it matters:** This review helps frame which cardiometabolic outcomes are plausible in generic HIIT but does not define the effect of a 4-minute Tabata protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- VO2max
- waist-circumference
- body-fat-percentage
- resting-heart-rate
- blood-pressure
- fasting-glucose

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as broad adjacent HIIT context only.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse-event counts or safety event rates were extracted from accessible metadata/abstract details.  Key limitations: Total participant count was not extracted from accessible metadata.; Protocol, duration, and population heterogeneity were substantial.; Normal-weight and short-term contexts may show weaker or null body-composition effects.

**Population mismatch:** Generic adult HIIT studies may not match bodyweight or home Tabata 20/10 sessions.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
