This source is included for **safety_cardiac_screening**.

**Findings:** Heavy exertion was associated with a short-term MI trigger risk, especially for people who rarely exercised. This is a source-local safety finding for `source_artifact:pmid-8232456` and is not direct Tabata 20/10 efficacy evidence.

**Why it matters:** The protocol is heavy/vigorous exertion by design; this source quantifies why “start all-out today” is different from “train regularly over time.”

**Potential experiment signals:** heavy exertion exposure; habitual exercise frequency; MI symptoms; one-hour risk window.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for acute-event warning and ramp-up language.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`. Use this source for safety screening, stop-condition, risk-context, or emergency-readiness language only. Do not use it to claim Tabata 20/10 improves outcomes or is safe for unscreened high-risk users.
