This source is included for **cardiometabolic_body_composition**.

**Findings:** HIIT and MICT showed broadly similar body-composition efficacy in overweight/obese adults, with HIIT framed as less time demanding. HIIT appeared similar to MICT for body composition and may require less time, but no direct Tabata-specific fat-loss effect was extracted.

**Why it matters:** This source helps avoid overstating fat-loss claims for Tabata-style exercise from generic HIIT literature.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- body-fat-mass
- body-fat-percentage
- waist-circumference
- body-weight

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use this to claim Tabata 20/10 is superior for fat loss; use as adjacent context for body-composition endpoints.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse-event counts or safety event rates were extracted from accessible metadata/abstract details.  Key limitations: Participant total was not extracted from accessible metadata.; Overweight/obese adult population may not generalize to all users.; No direct 20/10 Tabata subgroup was extracted.

**Population mismatch:** Clinical or weight-management study context does not match general community Tabata use.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
