**Findings:** Supervised HIIT improved HRR and VO2peak in coronary heart-disease patients, but this is clinical adjacent evidence rather than unsupervised Tabata proof. This is a source-local safety finding for `source_artifact:pmid-28648356` and is not direct Tabata 20/10 efficacy evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** 1-minute HRR; 2-minute HRR; VO2peak; clinical supervision status; CHD diagnosis.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as adjacent clinical-supervised HRR endpoint context.
