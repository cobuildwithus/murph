This bibliography page records the research corpus used to draft the Murph Health Commons package for Tabata 20/10 Interval Training.

The package keeps the original supervised cycle-ergometer Tabata evidence separate from practical 20/10 variants, public Tabata-style workouts, 40/20 intervals, AMRAP/EMOM/metcon formats, Wingate/SIT protocols, clinical HIIT, safety-only sources, and external marketing claims.

Claims on the protocol page should cite individual source keys. This bibliography should mainly support corpus recall, auditability, and source-page navigation.
