This source is included for **Reviews, definitions, and variant taxonomy**.

**Findings:** No results yet; do not use for effect claims.

**Study design and population:** Systematic review and meta-analysis protocol; Children and adolescents aged 6-18 planned for a future evidence synthesis.

**Intervention or exposure:** Tabata training defined for the planned review as repeated 20-second high-intensity bouts with 10-second rest, typically 7-8 bouts per set, across school, sport, community, or clinical settings.

**Comparator or control:** Planned comparators in future systematic review; not an outcome study.

**Duration or follow-up:** Protocol only; no completed follow-up results.

**Endpoints:** Planned physical fitness and mental health outcomes in children and adolescents.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event results yet; pediatric high-intensity protocols require special safety and supervision boundaries.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Protocol paper only; it registers a planned evidence synthesis and provides no completed meta-analysis results. Population mismatch: Pediatric/adolescent evidence will not automatically generalize to adult Murph users, and adult data will not automatically generalize to youth.

**Why it matters:** It prevents accidental adult-to-youth generalization and marks a source to revisit.

**Potential experiment signals:** future pediatric physical fitness outcomes, future mental health outcomes, youth adherence and safety.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not make pediatric claims from this source; use it as a tracking record until results are published.
