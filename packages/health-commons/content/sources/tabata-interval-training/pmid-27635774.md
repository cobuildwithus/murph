This source is included for **Reviews, definitions, and variant taxonomy**.

**Findings:** Raises a definitional and practical-fidelity challenge rather than reporting outcomes.

**Study design and population:** Commentary/letter; Not a participant study; commentary addresses researchers, practitioners, and Tabata-protocol interpretation.

**Intervention or exposure:** Discussion of original Tabata intensity and modern Tabata-label use.

**Comparator or control:** Not applicable.

**Duration or follow-up:** Not applicable.

**Endpoints:** Protocol definition, intensity fidelity, feasibility of original intensity.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event data; very high intensity is part of the feasibility concern.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Commentary, not primary efficacy evidence; no participant outcome dataset extracted. Population mismatch: Not a trial and not an unsupervised consumer protocol.

**Why it matters:** It keeps the protocol page honest about definition ambiguity and over-branding.

**Potential experiment signals:** protocol fidelity, RPE, completion of bouts, symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** State exactly which protocol is being discussed; do not treat all 20/10 labels as the original Tabata protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
