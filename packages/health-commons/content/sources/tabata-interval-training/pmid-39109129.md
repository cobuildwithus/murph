This source is included for **Rhabdomyolysis, heat, and serious exertional adverse-event safety**.

**Findings:** The case describes a 45-year-old experienced CrossFit coach with upper-limb pain and dark urine after a routine workout. CPK was reported as 126,891 U/L, with hydration treatment, improvement by the fifth hospital day, and discharge without reported complications.

**Why it matters:** It preserves a recent adverse-event signal in an experienced exerciser.

**Potential experiment signals:** upper-limb pain, dark urine, CK, liver enzymes, hydration treatment, and discharge status.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent-variant safety evidence for experienced-user risk boundaries.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
