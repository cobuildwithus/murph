This source is included for **Rhabdomyolysis, heat, and serious exertional adverse-event safety**.

**Findings:** The consensus emphasizes recognition, management, and return to activity after exertional heat illness. It supports safety-first language for high-intensity exercise under heat stress, especially when severe symptoms or exertional heat stroke are possible.

**Why it matters:** It gives current safety-boundary language for hot-condition training and return after heat illness.

**Potential experiment signals:** heat symptoms, altered mental status, cooling response, environmental heat exposure, and return-to-activity clearance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Heat illness symptoms override the interval prescription; stop first and manage the emergency.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
