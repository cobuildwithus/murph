This source is included for **Safety: musculoskeletal injury / HIFT-CrossFit boundary**.

**Findings:** No single pooled effect estimate; the review discusses benefits and risks of extreme conditioning programs and highlights injury/rhabdomyolysis as safety topics. The source is classified as `safety_boundary` and `safety-only` for the Tabata 20/10 protocol. Citation key: `source_artifact:pmid-30498574`.

**Why it matters:** Use as background for safety disclaimers around aggressive functional Tabata-style circuits, not as efficacy evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** Track shoulder/back/knee symptoms, pain severity, missed workouts, medical-care seeking, and load-scaling decisions when the Tabata-style workout is implemented as a functional circuit.

**Protocol takeaway:** Safety-only background: functional interval formats with high intensity and short rest should include conservative scaling and clear stop rules.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`. Narrative review; no pooled estimates and no direct Tabata 20/10 intervention.
