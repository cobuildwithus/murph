This source is included for **safety_msk_rhabdo_heat_respiratory**.

**Findings:** This professional guideline addresses exercise prescription and progression for apparently healthy adults. It gives safety and dose context for vigorous exercise but is not direct evidence for Tabata 20/10.

**Why it matters:** It anchors general safety language around vigorous exercise dose, progression, and population fit before translating Tabata-style intervals to unsupervised settings.

**Potential experiment signals:** exercise readiness, weekly activity background, symptoms during vigorous work, progression tolerance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a safety and prescription boundary; do not treat it as proof that a specific Tabata protocol works.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
