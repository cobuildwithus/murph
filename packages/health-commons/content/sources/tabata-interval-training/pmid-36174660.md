This source is included for **Safety: musculoskeletal injury / HIFT-CrossFit boundary**.

**Findings:** From 9,452 identified publications, the review included 3 prospective cohort studies with 691 participants, of whom 172 sustained an injury. The review found limited evidence that switching between prescribed and scaled loads was associated with increased injury risk and that increased duration of participation was protective. The source is classified as `safety_boundary` and `safety-only` for the Tabata 20/10 protocol. Citation key: `source_artifact:pmid-36174660`.

**Why it matters:** Use for progression, scaling, and supervision cautions when functional circuits are used in a Tabata-style format.

**Potential experiment signals:** Track shoulder/back/knee symptoms, pain severity, missed workouts, medical-care seeking, and load-scaling decisions when the Tabata-style workout is implemented as a functional circuit.

**Protocol takeaway:** Safety-only boundary evidence: beginners and users changing loads/scaling may need extra supervision and conservative progression.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`. Evidence is limited and CrossFit-specific; it should not be applied to cycling or aquatic Tabata claims.
