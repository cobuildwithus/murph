This source is included for **direct_practical_20_10_trials**.

## Findings

- [source_artifact:pmid-40859494] Fifteen male wheelchair basketball athletes were assigned to experimental Tabata training or control for 8 weeks.
- [source_artifact:pmid-40859494] Between-group differences favored Tabata for maximal heart rate, several respiratory-function measures, maximal lactate, body weight, and 20 m sprint performance.
- [source_artifact:pmid-40859494] Resting heart rate, oximetry, several respiratory values, RAST power, and anaerobic power did not show significant between-group differences.

## Why it matters

It is one of the batch’s direct 20/10 repeated-training sources, but it must be interpreted through adaptive-sport and small-sample boundaries.

## Protocol takeaway

Use as cautious direct-protocol support for repeated 20/10 training, while preserving the disability-sport population and null endpoints.

## Population and directness boundary

Directness is `direct_protocol` for 20/10 timing, but population mismatch is high because the sample was small, male, adaptive-sport-specific, and training was sport-supervised.
