This source is included for **cardiometabolic_body_composition**.

**Findings:** HIIT and MICT did not differ clearly for weight, BMI, waist circumference, or body-fat outcomes, while insulin sensitivity showed a moderate beneficial signal. No significant differences for weight, BMI, waist circumference, body-fat percentage, or fat mass; insulin sensitivity showed a moderate beneficial effect.

**Why it matters:** This recent open-access review preserves a mixed outcome pattern that prevents overstating body-composition effects.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- insulin-sensitivity
- body-weight
- BMI
- waist-circumference
- body-fat-percentage
- fat-mass

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context for insulin and body-composition endpoints, not as direct support for Tabata 20/10.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse-event counts or safety event rates were extracted from accessible metadata/abstract details.  Key limitations: Study heterogeneity and risk of bias were reported.; Waist-circumference evidence was rated lower.; Total participant count was not extracted from accessible abstract lines.; No direct Tabata 20/10 subgroup was extracted.

**Population mismatch:** Overweight/obese adult clinical training context may not match healthy community Tabata sessions.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
