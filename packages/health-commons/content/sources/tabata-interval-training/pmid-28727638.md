This source is included for **Rhabdomyolysis, heat, and serious exertional adverse-event safety**.

**Findings:** The case describes a 31-year-old woman with bilateral upper-extremity pain after a CrossFit workout, CK reported at 18,441 U/L, and hospitalization with IV fluids. It is adjacent-variant safety evidence only.

**Why it matters:** It preserves a peer-reviewed adverse-event signal for functional high-intensity exercise.

**Potential experiment signals:** severe localized pain, CK, liver enzymes, hospitalization, IV fluids, and symptom timing after high-intensity functional exercise.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent-variant safety-only evidence.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
