This source is included for **direct_original_dose_evidence**.

**Findings:** The poster reports 15 physically active participants performing eight cycles of maximal-effort bodyweight squat jumps with 20 seconds work and 10 seconds rest. It measured VO2 before, during, and after the bout; because the source is a conference poster/abstract, use its numerical results only as lower-certainty context.

**Why it matters:** It is frequently cited in public Tabata energy-expenditure claims, so it needs a guarded claim boundary.

**Potential experiment signals:** exercise VO2, peak VO2, recovery VO2, estimated caloric cost, poster-level uncertainty.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use the poster as a backbone efficacy source; cite only as context for acute squat-jump energy cost.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
