This source is included for **adjacent_hiit_sit_boundary**.

**Findings:** In active but untrained adults, six weeks of low-volume Wingate-style SIT produced metabolic adaptations during exercise that were reported as comparable to traditional endurance cycling despite much lower time and training volume (`source_artifact:pmid-17991697`).

**Why it matters:** It anchors the broader SIT time-efficiency discussion while preserving the boundary that 30-second all-out Wingate intervals are not the Tabata 20/10 protocol (`source_artifact:pmid-17991697`).

**Potential experiment signals:** VO2 or fitness proxy, cycling performance, perceived exertion, post-session fatigue, and recovery-heart-rate trends.

**Protocol takeaway:** Adjacent variant only: useful for mechanism and dose-boundary language, not for direct Tabata efficacy claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
