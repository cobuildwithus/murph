This source is included for **adjacent_hiit_sit_boundary**.

**Findings:** After six weeks of 20/10 supramaximal cycling in young men, VO2max, MAOD, thigh muscle CSA, sprint power, and muscle gene-expression profiles changed, but the study had no control group (`source_artifact:pmid-30429512`).

**Why it matters:** It is mechanistically close to the 20/10 supramaximal cycling rationale while remaining unsuitable as a standalone efficacy claim (`source_artifact:pmid-30429512`).

**Potential experiment signals:** VO2max, anaerobic capacity proxy, power output, and recovery/fatigue signals.

**Protocol takeaway:** Same-mechanism context; not controlled efficacy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
