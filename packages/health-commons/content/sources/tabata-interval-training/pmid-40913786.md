## Findings

- [source_artifact:pmid-40913786] Sixteen active young adults completed a modified HIFT Tabata session using 32 efforts of 5 seconds with 10 seconds passive rest.
- [source_artifact:pmid-40913786] Participants spent about 6 minutes, or 75% of exercise volume, in the 90–100% HRmax range and showed no significant sex differences in HR-related values.
- [source_artifact:pmid-40913786] Females reported higher affective-valence and future-intention scores, but the study was small and acute.

## Why it matters

It shows how changing work-bout length can change tolerability and fatigue, which is valuable boundary context for modified HIFT variants.

## Protocol takeaway

Use as adjacent modified-HIFT context only; do not use it to claim outcomes for classical 20-second Tabata intervals.

## Population and directness boundary

Directness is `adjacent_variant` because the intervention is modified HIFT with short 5-second efforts, not the classical 20-second work / 10-second rest Tabata block.
