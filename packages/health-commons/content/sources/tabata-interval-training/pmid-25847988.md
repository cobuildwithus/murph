This source is included for **safety_cardiac_screening**.

**Findings:** Sports-associated SCA in middle age was rare but often occurred in people with cardiovascular risk factors or recent warning symptoms. This is a source-local safety finding for `source_artifact:pmid-25847988` and is not direct Tabata 20/10 efficacy evidence.

**Why it matters:** Middle-aged public users are a plausible Tabata audience; this source helps frame warning-symptom and AED/CPR relevance.

**Potential experiment signals:** prior cardiovascular disease; cardiovascular risk factors; symptoms in prior week; witnessed arrest; bystander CPR.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for warning-symptom and emergency-response safety language.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`. Use this source for safety screening, stop-condition, risk-context, or emergency-readiness language only. Do not use it to claim Tabata 20/10 improves outcomes or is safe for unscreened high-risk users.
