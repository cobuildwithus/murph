This source is included for **Reviews, definitions, and variant taxonomy**.

**Findings:** Highlights that acute and chronic responses depend on multiple programming variables; this supports protocol-fidelity cautions.

**Study design and population:** Narrative review; Athletes and exercisers discussed across HIIT programming examples.

**Intervention or exposure:** High-intensity interval training with manipulation of work interval, relief interval, recovery, series, intensity, and exercise mode.

**Comparator or control:** Different HIIT programming variables and training cycles.

**Duration or follow-up:** Review source; no single duration.

**Endpoints:** Cardiopulmonary responses, time at VO2max, training-cycle design, exercise mode.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event rate extracted; programming variables can influence tolerability and load.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Narrative review; not a Tabata 20/10 trial. Population mismatch: Sport-programming examples may not match consumer home protocols.

**Why it matters:** It provides a clear framework for HIIT dose variables relevant to Tabata-style design.

**Potential experiment signals:** work/rest compliance, session intensity, RPE, heart-rate response, training load.

**Protocol takeaway:** Tabata protocol pages should record work duration, rest duration, mode, intensity, recovery, and progression, not just the 20/10 timer.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
