This source is included for **safety_cardiac_screening**.

**Findings:** Applying the ACSM algorithm to U.S. adults highlighted intensity-specific clearance decisions, including a subset referred only before vigorous exercise. This is a source-local safety finding for `source_artifact:pmid-28557860` and is not direct Tabata 20/10 efficacy evidence.

**Why it matters:** It operationalizes the ACSM screening model in a representative adult sample, making the intensity gate more concrete.

**Potential experiment signals:** medical clearance required before vigorous exercise; known cardiovascular/metabolic/renal disease; symptom status; screening burden.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for the safety checklist and medical-clearance decision boundary.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`. Use this source for safety screening, stop-condition, risk-context, or emergency-readiness language only. Do not use it to claim Tabata 20/10 improves outcomes or is safe for unscreened high-risk users.
