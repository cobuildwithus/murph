This source is included for **safety_cardiac_screening**.

**Findings:** Vigorous exertion transiently increased sudden cardiac death risk, but the absolute risk per episode was extremely low and habitual exercise attenuated the trigger risk. This is a source-local safety finding for `source_artifact:pmid-11070099` and is not direct Tabata 20/10 efficacy evidence.

**Why it matters:** It quantifies the acute-trigger paradox that intense bouts can be risky in the moment while regular activity can lower or attenuate risk.

**Potential experiment signals:** sudden cardiac death risk window; habitual vigorous activity frequency; acute exertion exposure; absolute event rarity.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to justify gradual progression and risk screening before maximal 20/10 intervals.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`. Use this source for safety screening, stop-condition, risk-context, or emergency-readiness language only. Do not use it to claim Tabata 20/10 improves outcomes or is safe for unscreened high-risk users.
