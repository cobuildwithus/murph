## Findings

- [source_artifact:pmid-26664271] The Tabata arm performed 8 rounds of 20 seconds at 170% VO2max with 10 seconds rest, three times weekly for 8 weeks.
- [source_artifact:pmid-26664271] VO2max, peak power, and Wingate outcomes improved across groups, with no significant between-group advantage for Tabata.
- [source_artifact:pmid-26664271] Tabata was significantly less enjoyable and enjoyment declined, making adherence and post-session distress important boundaries.

## Why it matters

It is one of the clearest practical comparison studies for preserving mixed/null findings and tolerability concerns around Tabata-branded interval work.

## Protocol takeaway

Use as context-only evidence that Tabata-like cycling may improve fitness but did not outperform longer steady-state training in this sample.

## Population and directness boundary

The study is adjacent rather than direct for Murph because the cycling intensity prescription is supramaximal/laboratory-style and not a general home bodyweight Tabata protocol.
