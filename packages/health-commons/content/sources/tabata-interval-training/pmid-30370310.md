This source is included for **Safety: musculoskeletal injury / HIFT-CrossFit boundary**.

**Findings:** 30.5% reported a CrossFit-related injury in the previous 12 months; common locations were shoulders (39%), back (36%), knees (15%), elbows (12%), and wrists (11%); reported rates ranged from 0.27 to 0.74 injuries per 1000 hours depending on exposure assumptions. The source is classified as `adjacent_variant` and `safety-only` for the Tabata 20/10 protocol. Citation key: `source_artifact:pmid-30370310`.

**Why it matters:** Use this source to justify symptom tracking, conservative scaling, and stop rules when Tabata-style sessions use CrossFit-like functional movements.

**Potential experiment signals:** Track shoulder/back/knee symptoms, pain severity, missed workouts, medical-care seeking, and load-scaling decisions when the Tabata-style workout is implemented as a functional circuit.

**Protocol takeaway:** Safety-only adjacent evidence: monitor shoulder, back, knee, elbow, and wrist symptoms when a Tabata-style workout uses functional movements or loaded circuits.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`. Do not treat these CrossFit injury rates as direct risk estimates for cycling, rowing, or aquatic Tabata 20/10 sessions.
