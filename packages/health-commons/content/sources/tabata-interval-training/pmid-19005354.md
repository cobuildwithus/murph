This source is included for **Rhabdomyolysis, heat, and serious exertional adverse-event safety**.

**Findings:** The review describes exertional rhabdomyolysis recognition and emphasizes risk stratification before returning to physical activity. It is a safety-only source for escalation and graded return, not a Tabata outcome source.

**Why it matters:** It supports conservative recovery and medical-clearance language after severe exertional muscle injury.

**Potential experiment signals:** resolution of severe muscle pain, dark urine, clinically ordered CK/renal markers, and graded return status.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for post-rhabdomyolysis return-to-activity boundaries only.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
