This source is included for **external_protocol_claims**.

**Findings:**
- The guide defines Tabata as eight rounds of 20 seconds of exercise followed by 10 seconds of rest, totaling about four minutes.
- It advises beginners to start with lower-impact options, add recovery if needed, and seek medical or professional guidance when relevant.

**Why it matters:** It contrasts exact timing claims with cautious public safety advice for beginners and users with health considerations.

**Potential experiment signals:** 20/10 timing fidelity, extra rest or recovery needs, clinician or trainer clearance, overexertion risk with weighted movements.

**Protocol takeaway:** A beginner-facing protocol page should allow scaled intensity, added rest, and clinician/trainer guidance rather than treating 20/10 as mandatory for all users.

**Limitations and boundaries:**
- No controlled trial data or endpoint results.
- Frequency and adaptation advice are consumer guidance, not protocol-tested dose-response evidence.
- No adverse-event counts are reported.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
