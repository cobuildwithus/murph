This source is included for **safety_cardiac_screening**.

**Findings:** Vigorous exertion can transiently raise acute cardiac event risk in susceptible people, while habitual activity remains protective at the population level. This is a source-local safety finding for `source_artifact:pmid-17468391` and is not direct Tabata 20/10 efficacy evidence.

**Why it matters:** The protocol is intentionally vigorous or supramaximal; this source supports safety language that separates population benefits of exercise from transient risk around acute exertion.

**Potential experiment signals:** pre-exercise symptom checklist; known cardiovascular disease or risk status; unusual chest pain, syncope, dyspnea, palpitations during intervals.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for safety screening and stop-condition framing only.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`. Use this source for safety screening, stop-condition, risk-context, or emergency-readiness language only. Do not use it to claim Tabata 20/10 improves outcomes or is safe for unscreened high-risk users.
