**Findings:** Both endurance training and 20/10 sprint-interval cycling produced group-level changes in VO2peak, lactate threshold, and submaximal HR, but individual responses varied and non-response occurred for specific variables (`source_artifact:pmid-27936084`).

**Why it matters:** It is important boundary evidence because it prevents universal-response claims and highlights the need to change or individualize the stimulus for some people (`source_artifact:pmid-27936084`).

**Potential experiment signals:** VO2peak proxy, submaximal HR, lactate-threshold proxy, RPE, and response category.

**Protocol takeaway:** Adjacent variant; close 20/10 cycling structure but not direct public-protocol evidence.
