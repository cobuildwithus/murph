This source is included for **Rhabdomyolysis, heat, and serious exertional adverse-event safety**.

**Findings:** The review describes exertional rhabdomyolysis in athletes, including symptoms, elevated CK, hydration-based management, serious complications, and return-to-play risk stratification. It is safety-only evidence for warning signs and escalation.

**Why it matters:** It provides practical safety framing for severe muscle-breakdown symptoms after intense exercise.

**Potential experiment signals:** severe pain, weakness, swelling, dark urine, CK/renal testing when clinically ordered, and return-to-play status.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for rhabdomyolysis recognition and return-to-play caution.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
