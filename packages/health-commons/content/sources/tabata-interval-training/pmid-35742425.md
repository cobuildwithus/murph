This source is included for **direct_practical_20_10_trials**.

**Findings:** In `source_artifact:pmid-35742425`, 21 inactive female university students contributed two weeks of ActiGraph data. Low-volume HIIE was associated with increases in moderate, vigorous, and total physical activity, but also increased sedentary time and reduced light physical activity.

**Why it matters:** This is direct practical context for compensatory movement behavior after low-volume high-intensity exercise.

**Potential experiment signals:** Daily sedentary time, LPA/MPA/VPA minutes, sleep duration, and adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only. Use to justify measuring the full day, not to claim fitness adaptation.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
