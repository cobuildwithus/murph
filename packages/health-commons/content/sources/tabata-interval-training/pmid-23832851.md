This source is included for **Reviews, definitions, and variant taxonomy**.

**Findings:** Supports caution that intense intervals can impose neuromuscular and musculoskeletal load beyond cardiopulmonary targets.

**Study design and population:** Narrative review; Athletes and exercisers discussed across HIIT programming examples.

**Intervention or exposure:** High-intensity interval training with emphasis on anaerobic glycolytic contribution, neuromuscular load, musculoskeletal strain, and periodization.

**Comparator or control:** Different HIIT programming variables and practical applications.

**Duration or follow-up:** Review source; no single duration.

**Endpoints:** Anaerobic contribution, neuromuscular load, musculoskeletal strain, periodization, practical application.

**Safety notes:** No event-rate extraction, but the review is relevant to overload, pain, and injury-risk guardrails.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Narrative review; not a direct Tabata trial. Population mismatch: Sport-specific programming examples may not generalize to unscreened home users.

**Why it matters:** It protects against framing Tabata as only a cardiopulmonary stimulus.

**Potential experiment signals:** muscle soreness, joint pain, RPE, completion rate, fatigue.

**Protocol takeaway:** A 20/10 protocol should include progression and stopping rules because neuromuscular and musculoskeletal load can be high.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
