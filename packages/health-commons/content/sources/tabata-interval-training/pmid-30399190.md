This source is included for **Rhabdomyolysis, heat, and serious exertional adverse-event safety**.

**Findings:** In 58 healthy volunteers, one high-intensity resistance session increased pain ratings, CK, myoglobin, serum creatinine in men, and urinary kidney-injury biomarkers. Three men met KDIGO AKI criteria; urinary biomarkers were reported to return to baseline by 24 hours.

**Why it matters:** It provides controlled adjacent-mechanism evidence that intense resistance exercise can transiently stress muscle and kidney biomarkers.

**Potential experiment signals:** pain rating, CK, myoglobin, creatinine, urinary biomarkers, and 24-hour recovery.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent-variant safety/mechanism evidence for resistance-style Tabata scaling.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
