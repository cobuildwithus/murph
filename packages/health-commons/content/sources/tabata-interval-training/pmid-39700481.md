This source is included for **Reviews, definitions, and variant taxonomy**.

**Findings:** The perspective emphasizes that the original protocol was a specific exhaustive supramaximal cycling protocol and that later Tabata-style variants require separate interpretation.

**Study design and population:** Narrative perspective review; Athletes and broader populations discussed across approximately three decades of Tabata and Tabata-style research.

**Intervention or exposure:** Original 6-8 bouts of 20-second supramaximal cycling with 10-second recovery, plus later Tabata-style, sport-specific, cross-training, and weight-bearing applications.

**Comparator or control:** Narrative comparisons across original and adapted Tabata-style uses.

**Duration or follow-up:** Perspective review; no single intervention duration.

**Endpoints:** Protocol definition, VO2max, maximal accumulated oxygen deficit, adaptation domains, public and applied translation.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event rate extracted; very high intensity and exhaustion remain practical safety boundaries.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Perspective article; should not replace primary outcome papers or systematic review effect estimates. Population mismatch: Broad applications described in the review may not match a consumer self-experiment with limited equipment or screening.

**Why it matters:** It updates the definition boundary from the protocol originator and is valuable for variant taxonomy.

**Potential experiment signals:** session intensity fidelity, estimated VO2max, anaerobic-capacity proxy where available, symptoms and recovery burden.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use this source to label modern Tabata-style protocols explicitly rather than assuming they replicate original supramaximal cycling.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
