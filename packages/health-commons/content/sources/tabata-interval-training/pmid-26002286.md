This source is included for **safety_msk_rhabdo_heat_respiratory**.

**Findings:** The consensus recommends heat-risk mitigation strategies such as heat acclimatization, euhydration, cooling, shade, recovery, and scheduling considerations. Ledger metadata supplied an alternate BJSM DOI/PMCID; this draft uses the verified PMID-linked Sports Medicine article metadata.

**Why it matters:** The heat setting can turn an otherwise tolerable intense session into a higher-risk exposure, especially for new users, dehydrated users, or users training outdoors.

**Potential experiment signals:** ambient temperature and humidity, hydration status, dizziness, excessive heat symptoms, recovery time after heat exposure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for heat and hydration safety boundaries; avoid maximal Tabata sessions in unsafe heat or when dehydrated.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
