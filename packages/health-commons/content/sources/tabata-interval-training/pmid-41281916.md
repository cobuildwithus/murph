This source is included for **safety_msk_rhabdo_heat_respiratory**.

**Findings:** Thirty male badminton players completed 6 weeks of Tabata with or without BFR. The BFR arm improved selected anaerobic outcomes and reported no adverse events or subjective discomfort, but this is an adjacent co-intervention variant.

**Why it matters:** The timing matches Tabata but the BFR co-intervention makes it a boundary source rather than direct protocol evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** adverse events, subjective discomfort, Wingate peak power, badminton-specific endurance, jump tests.

**Protocol takeaway:** Tabata plus BFR must be separated from standard Tabata; no adverse events in 30 athletes does not establish broad safety.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
