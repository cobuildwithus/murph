This source is included for **direct_practical_20_10_trials**.

**Findings:** In `source_artifact:pmid-24901767`, an acute crossover experiment compared 8 x 20 seconds at about 170% VO2peak with endurance exercise, and a separate 6-week training experiment compared the same low-volume HIT pattern against endurance training. Both protocols produced comparable aerobic-capacity and skeletal-muscle adaptation signals; LV-HIT showed greater anaerobic/glycolytic improvements.

**Why it matters:** It provides direct mechanistic evidence for the 20/10 cycling dose while making clear that lab-controlled cycling evidence should not be generalized automatically to all practical Tabata variants.

**Potential experiment signals:** Estimated VO2max, power output, work-capacity time trials, and subjective recovery after high-intensity sessions.

**Protocol takeaway:** Direct mechanistic support for 20/10 cycling; adjacent-only for home bodyweight protocols.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
