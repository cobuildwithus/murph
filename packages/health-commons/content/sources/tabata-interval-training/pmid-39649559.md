This source is included for **cardiometabolic_body_composition**.

**Findings:** Youth HIIT/SIT reduced several cardiometabolic markers but not overall glucose or diastolic blood pressure. Insulin, insulin resistance, triglycerides, total cholesterol, LDL-C, and systolic blood pressure decreased and HDL-C increased; overall glucose and diastolic blood pressure did not clearly change.

**Why it matters:** This source gives a recent youth-specific cardiometabolic map while preserving null glucose/DBP findings.

**Potential experiment signals:**

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as youth context only; do not cite as direct evidence for Tabata 20/10.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse-event counts or safety event rates were extracted from accessible metadata/abstract details.  Key limitations: Included 23 RCTs and 8 nonrandomized trials.; Substantial heterogeneity was reported.; Some subgroup findings were stronger for SIT and obesity subgroups.; Not specific to Tabata 20/10.

**Population mismatch:** Children/adolescents and SIT-heavy evidence may not generalize to adult Tabata 20/10.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
