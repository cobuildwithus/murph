**Findings:** A meta-analysis of 10 clinical studies and 273 patients found larger VO2peak improvements with HIIT than moderate continuous training. Use only as supervised clinical context, not direct Tabata evidence.

**Why it matters:** It helps prevent healthy-adult or athlete Tabata assumptions from being applied to people with cardiometabolic disease.

**Potential experiment signals:** VO2peak, clinical diagnosis, supervision level, exercise tolerance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context for clinical-population boundary language only.
