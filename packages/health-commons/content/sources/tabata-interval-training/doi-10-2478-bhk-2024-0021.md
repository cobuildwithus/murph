This source is included for **safety_msk_rhabdo_heat_respiratory**.

**Findings:** In 81 participants, lower-limb Tabata was followed by DOMS monitoring for 72 hours. Foam rolling shifted the soreness trajectory, with reported group differences at 48 and 72 hours, but the study should be used for recovery-burden and soreness language only.

**Why it matters:** It is one of the most directly relevant sources for soreness after a Tabata-style lower-limb session.

**Potential experiment signals:** quadriceps soreness VAS, 24-72 hour soreness trajectory, thermal imaging, self-massage adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Warn users that lower-limb Tabata can produce soreness; use recovery logging and conservative progression.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
