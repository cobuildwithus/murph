This source is included for **Rhabdomyolysis, heat, and serious exertional adverse-event safety**.

**Findings:** The case involved a 35-year-old experienced participant after a two-day extreme conditioning competition with five workouts. CK was reported at 77,590 U/L three days after the event; renal function was reported as normal, with prolonged myalgia during follow-up.

**Why it matters:** It prevents narrowing safety warnings only to novices.

**Potential experiment signals:** competition volume, CK, myalgia duration, liver enzymes, renal function, and follow-up recovery.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent-variant safety evidence for excessive volume and competitive intensity.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
