This source is included for **safety_msk_rhabdo_heat_respiratory**.

**Findings:** Six small RCTs with 153 participants found greater exercise-capacity gains with aerobic interval training but limited or similar metabolic risk-factor changes. Use only as older clinical context.

**Why it matters:** It is a low-priority boundary source showing the difference between clinical interval training and consumer Tabata claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** VO2peak, fasting glucose, metabolic risk factors, diagnosis, supervision.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for population-mismatch context and mixed clinical endpoint language.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
