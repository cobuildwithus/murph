This source is included for **Rhabdomyolysis, heat, and serious exertional adverse-event safety**.

**Findings:** The case series analyzed 11 patients diagnosed with rhabdomyolysis after their first indoor cycling session. Renal failure was reported in two patients, and CK decreased with rest and intensive intravenous hydration.

**Why it matters:** It is a mode-relevant safety boundary because the original Tabata protocol used cycling, while this source addresses unaccustomed cycling-class overload.

**Potential experiment signals:** first-session status, CK, renal failure, rest, IV hydration, and gradual-start advice.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only evidence for unaccustomed cycling overload.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
