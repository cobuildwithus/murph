This source is included for **Rhabdomyolysis, heat, and serious exertional adverse-event safety**.

**Findings:** The study evaluated 34 collegiate swimmers after a novel upper-body workout; six were hospitalized and seven were treated and released. Reported risk-factor context included exceptional motivation, extreme soreness, arm locking, brown urine, and other clinical/training markers.

**Why it matters:** It adds risk-factor context for novelty, motivation, and group-training pressure.

**Potential experiment signals:** novel workout, hospitalization/treat-and-release status, soreness, brown urine, motivation, and arm locking.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only context for unaccustomed movement and high motivation/pressure.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
