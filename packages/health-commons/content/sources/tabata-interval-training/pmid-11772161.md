This source is included for **Reviews, definitions, and variant taxonomy**.

**Findings:** Supports the concept that trained athletes may require high-intensity interval work for further endurance gains, but does not isolate Tabata 20/10.

**Study design and population:** Narrative review; Highly trained endurance athletes discussed in a narrative review.

**Intervention or exposure:** High-intensity interval training programs for performance enhancement.

**Comparator or control:** Endurance training volume and other programming approaches discussed narratively.

**Duration or follow-up:** Review source; no single intervention duration.

**Endpoints:** Endurance performance, VO2peak/VO2max context, muscle enzyme activity, training optimization.

**Safety notes:** No adverse-event data extracted; programming optimization implies load-management considerations.

**Limitations and mismatch:** Older narrative review and athlete-focused; not direct to general consumer self-experimentation. Population mismatch: Highly trained endurance athletes differ from many Murph users.

**Why it matters:** It provides background on why intensity and programming variables matter in HIIT.

**Potential experiment signals:** estimated VO2max, performance test, training load, symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not cite athlete HIIT programming reviews as direct proof for the Tabata 20/10 self-experiment.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
