## Findings

- [source_artifact:pmid-40440544] 334 male gendarmerie cadets were assigned to running or calisthenics Tabata, 3 times weekly for 5 weeks.
- [source_artifact:pmid-40440544] Both groups improved all reported endurance, strength, and power tests, with no time-by-group interactions except crunch performance.
- [source_artifact:pmid-40440544] The source is tactical-population context; adverse events were not reported in the accessible abstract.

## Why it matters

It supports a practical mode boundary: calisthenics-based Tabata may be comparable to running Tabata in a tactical group, but this is not general adult proof.
