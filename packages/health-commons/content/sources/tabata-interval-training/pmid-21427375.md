This source is included for **safety_cardiac_screening**.

**Findings:** Episodic physical activity can trigger acute cardiac events, but absolute excess risk is small and habitual activity reduces the trigger effect. This is a source-local safety finding for `source_artifact:pmid-21427375` and is not direct Tabata 20/10 efficacy evidence.

**Why it matters:** This is an aggregate safety source that can support balanced acute-risk language without relying on a single older trigger study.

**Potential experiment signals:** MI trigger risk; SCD trigger risk; absolute risk per person-year; habitual activity as risk modifier.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for safety-only risk framing and gradual-exposure rationale.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`. Use this source for safety screening, stop-condition, risk-context, or emergency-readiness language only. Do not use it to claim Tabata 20/10 improves outcomes or is safe for unscreened high-risk users.
