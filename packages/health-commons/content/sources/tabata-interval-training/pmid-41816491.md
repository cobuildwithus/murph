This source is included for **safety_msk_rhabdo_heat_respiratory**.

**Findings:** Across 7 RCTs and 518 participants, pooled outcomes including ACQ-5, VO2peak, FEV1%, FeNO, and MiniAQLQ were not statistically significant versus control, with high heterogeneity and inadequate safety reporting.

**Why it matters:** It preserves a recent null/mixed synthesis and prevents overclaiming from older or smaller asthma studies.

**Potential experiment signals:** ACQ-5, VO2peak, FEV1%, FeNO, MiniAQLQ, adverse-event reporting.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only respiratory context with explicit uncertainty and safety-reporting gaps.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
