This source is included for **Rhabdomyolysis, heat, and serious exertional adverse-event safety**.

**Findings:** The series reports 11 patients with CrossFit-associated rhabdomyolysis at one academic institution. It is useful for adverse-event recall but cannot estimate Tabata-specific incidence.

**Why it matters:** It broadens adverse-event recall beyond isolated single cases.

**Potential experiment signals:** dark urine, symptom duration, CK, emergency-care presentation, and hospitalization.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent-variant safety-only case-series evidence.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
