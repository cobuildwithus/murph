This source is included for **adjacent_hiit_sit_boundary**.

**Findings:** In the primary running/bicycle cross-exercise protocol, oxygen uptake during the last bicycle bout exceeded bicycle VO2max and was similar to running; arm-cranking/occlusion experiments supported an EPOC-summation explanation (`source_artifact:pmid-38463671`).

**Why it matters:** It is a mechanistic cross-exercise extension, not a chronic Tabata training study (`source_artifact:pmid-38463671`).

**Potential experiment signals:** Heart rate, exertion, recovery duration, and post-exercise fatigue.

**Protocol takeaway:** Adjacent acute physiology only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
