This source is included for **practical_or_population_boundary_tabata**.

**Findings:** In `source_artifact:pmid-36673761`, 28 healthy young women completed a single 30-minute Tabata session. Heart rate, blood lactate, and RPE increased over the session; affective measures worsened over time; fitness grouping did not create clear physiological-response differences.

**Why it matters:** It helps define user-experience and safety boundaries for longer Tabata sessions, especially high perceived exertion and affective discomfort.

**Potential experiment signals:** HRpeak percentage, RPE, affect/feeling scale, and recovery rating.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only and safety-relevant. This acute long-session source should not be used to claim chronic fitness gains.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
