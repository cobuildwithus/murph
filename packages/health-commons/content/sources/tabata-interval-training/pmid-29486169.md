This source is included for **direct_original_dose_evidence**.

**Findings:** Thirteen active young men completed 20s:10s cycling trials at 115%, 130%, and 170% iVO2max. The study reported about 7 ± 1 bouts at 115%, 5 ± 1 at 130%, and 4 ± 1 at 170%, with no clear advantage in highest oxygen uptake at the highest intensity and more time above 90% VO2max at 115% than at 170%.

**Why it matters:** It directly tests whether the popular 170% interpretation is feasible and shows why Tabata claims need intensity and bout-count precision.

**Potential experiment signals:** bout count, oxygen uptake, time above 90% VO2max, maximal heart-rate response, clinical-screening need.

**Protocol takeaway:** For cycle-ergometer Tabata, 20/10 timing alone is not enough; the intensity prescription determines whether the session resembles the original bout count.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
