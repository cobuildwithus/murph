This source is included for **Rhabdomyolysis, heat, and serious exertional adverse-event safety**.

**Findings:** The review summarizes mechanisms and clinical management of rhabdomyolysis-associated acute kidney injury. It is background context for medical severity, not Tabata-specific evidence.

**Why it matters:** It explains the kidney-risk mechanism behind rhabdomyolysis safety warnings.

**Potential experiment signals:** myoglobin, renal function, serum creatinine, AKI diagnosis, and clinical treatment needs.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as background context for why severe rhabdomyolysis symptoms need medical evaluation.
