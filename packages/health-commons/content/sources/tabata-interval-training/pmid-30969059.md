This source is included for **Rhabdomyolysis, heat, and serious exertional adverse-event safety**.

**Findings:** In 24 healthy students, CK increased from a median 104 IU/L before exercise to 6,071 IU/L on day 4 after one intensive workout; 58% exceeded 5,000 IU/L and three participants met a rhabdomyolysis definition that combined CK >5,000 with muscle pain or positive urine dipstick. Greater prior strength-training frequency was negatively correlated with CK increase.

**Why it matters:** It is the batch source closest to real-world Tabata misuse: repeated 20-second work and 10-second rest intervals at high volume.

**Potential experiment signals:** CK, muscle pain, urine dipstick blood, prior strength-training frequency, and day-4 response.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only evidence that high-volume 20/10 workouts can produce large CK responses.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
