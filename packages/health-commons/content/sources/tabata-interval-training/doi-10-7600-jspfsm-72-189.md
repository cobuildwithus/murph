This source is included for **direct_original_dose_evidence**.

**Findings:** Six healthy men trained four days weekly for four weeks using at least eight 20-second cycling bouts at 170% VO2max with 10-second recovery. VO2max and MAOD increased significantly; exercise time, stroke volume, and cardiac output improved, while some 10-second oxygen-uptake and oxygen-deficit time-course measures did not show significant differences.

**Why it matters:** It extends the original Tabata-style dose to modern measurements of circulatory and anaerobic capacity changes.

**Potential experiment signals:** VO2max, MAOD, stroke volume, cardiac output, 30-second exhaustive-exercise time, oxygen uptake time course.

**Protocol takeaway:** Direct 20s:10s cycling can improve capacity markers over 4 weeks in healthy young men, but protocol claims should preserve small-sample and mixed-endpoint limits.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
