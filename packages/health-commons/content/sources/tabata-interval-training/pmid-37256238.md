This source is included for **safety_msk_rhabdo_heat_respiratory**.

**Findings:** The RCT randomized 150 untrained adults with symptomatic asthma. The primary responder endpoint was not statistically significant, while daily ICS dose improved as a secondary outcome; the intervention was reported safe and well tolerated.

**Why it matters:** It is a comparatively large asthma HIIT RCT with safety/tolerability relevance.

**Potential experiment signals:** ICS dose, asthma control, adherence, exacerbations, exercise tolerance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as supervised clinical asthma context; keep Tabata respiratory warnings in place.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
