This source is included for **safety_cardiac_screening**.

**Findings:** Vigorous exercise transiently increased primary cardiac arrest risk, with much higher relative risk in men with low habitual vigorous activity. This is a source-local safety finding for `source_artifact:pmid-6472399` and is not direct Tabata 20/10 efficacy evidence.

**Why it matters:** It is a classic source for the “acute vigorous exposure versus habitual conditioning” safety distinction.

**Potential experiment signals:** habitual vigorous activity level; vigorous exercise exposure; primary cardiac arrest; risk status unknown.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as historical safety context, especially for sedentary-to-maximal transitions.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`. Use this source for safety screening, stop-condition, risk-context, or emergency-readiness language only. Do not use it to claim Tabata 20/10 improves outcomes or is safe for unscreened high-risk users.
