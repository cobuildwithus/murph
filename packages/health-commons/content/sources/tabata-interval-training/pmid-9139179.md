This source is included for **direct_original_dose_evidence**.

**Findings:** In nine subjects, IE1 used 6-7 bouts of 20 seconds at about 170% VO2max with 10-second rests. Its accumulated oxygen deficit was higher than the longer-rest IE2 protocol and was not different from maximal accumulated oxygen deficit, while peak oxygen uptake in the final 10 seconds was not significantly below VO2max.

**Why it matters:** It defines the metabolic stress profile behind the original Tabata rationale and helps distinguish the original protocol from longer-rest sprint intervals.

**Potential experiment signals:** oxygen uptake, accumulated oxygen deficit, VO2max proximity, MAOD proximity, exhaustion.

**Protocol takeaway:** The original 20s:10s cycling protocol is best framed as a near-maximal metabolic stressor, not merely a short workout format.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.
