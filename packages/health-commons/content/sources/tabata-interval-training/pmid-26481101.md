This source is included for **cardiometabolic_body_composition**.

**Findings:** Generic HIIT showed glucose-regulation and insulin-resistance signals, especially in metabolic-risk populations. Fasting glucose reduction was reported in people at risk of or with type 2 diabetes; overall results should be interpreted as generic HIIT evidence.

**Why it matters:** This is a core adjacent cardiometabolic review but should be used to define plausible endpoint tracking, not to promise glycemic benefits from a specific 20/10 protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- fasting-glucose
- fasting-insulin
- HOMA-IR
- HbA1c

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context-only evidence that glycemic markers are plausible HIIT endpoints.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse-event counts or safety event rates were extracted from accessible metadata/abstract details.  Key limitations: Intervention protocols, comparators, and populations were heterogeneous.; Effects appear more relevant in type 2 diabetes or metabolic-risk populations.; No direct Tabata 20/10 subgroup was extracted.

**Population mismatch:** Metabolic-risk and type 2 diabetes samples may not match generally healthy Tabata users.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
