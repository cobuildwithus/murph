This source is included for **safety_msk_rhabdo_heat_respiratory**.

**Findings:** The meta-analysis found improvements in some cardiometabolic markers versus control but no significant triglyceride change. Use as clinical context and population-boundary evidence only.

**Why it matters:** It provides a useful clinical-population boundary and shows mixed cardiometabolic endpoint patterns.

**Potential experiment signals:** blood glucose, blood pressure, waist circumference, HDL-C, triglycerides.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for population-mismatch cautions and mixed-endpoint language in clinical groups.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
