## Findings

- [source_artifact:pmid-31782930] Young adults were randomized to control, whole-body Tabata, or vigorous continuous training for 4 weeks with 2-month follow-up.
- [source_artifact:pmid-31782930] Both exercise arms improved time-to-fatigue and 5 km time trial at post-test, but maintenance at follow-up was reported for VICT only.
- [source_artifact:pmid-31782930] Tabata had the stronger push-up responder signal, so the evidence is mixed by endpoint.

## Why it matters

It is a high-value mixed-result source for preventing the protocol page from implying that whole-body Tabata reliably beats vigorous continuous training.

## Protocol takeaway

Use as context-only mixed evidence: whole-body Tabata may help muscular endurance but was less favorable than VICT for cardiorespiratory response profiles.

## Population and directness boundary

Directness is `adjacent_variant` because the intervention is whole-body Tabata-style, not the original cycle protocol or a default adult self-directed 4-minute Tabata block.
