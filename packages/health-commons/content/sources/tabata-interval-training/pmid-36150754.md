This source is included for **Rhabdomyolysis, heat, and serious exertional adverse-event safety**.

**Findings:** The consensus addresses protection of athletes competing in heat, including environmental monitoring and cooling/shade resources for event organizers. It is safety-only context for hot-condition interval sessions.

**Why it matters:** It provides high-level heat-event safety controls that are relevant whenever intense intervals occur in hot conditions.

**Potential experiment signals:** environmental heat, cooling availability, shade, event modification, and heat illness symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for hot-environment boundary conditions and event-style safety planning.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
