This source is included for **safety_msk_rhabdo_heat_respiratory**.

**Findings:** The guideline defines and manages exercise-induced bronchoconstriction and supports respiratory safety boundaries for intense exercise. It is safety guidance rather than Tabata efficacy evidence.

**Why it matters:** Twenty-ten intervals can be highly ventilatory; this source sets a conservative respiratory boundary for people with asthma-like symptoms or known EIB.

**Potential experiment signals:** wheeze or cough after intervals, chest tightness, rescue inhaler use, post-exercise breathing recovery.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for respiratory risk warnings and clinician referral language, not for performance or health-effect claims.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
