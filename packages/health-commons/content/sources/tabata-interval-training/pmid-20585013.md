**Findings:** CPET interpretation belongs in a clinical monitoring context, especially for adults with disease or symptoms. This is a source-local safety finding for `source_artifact:pmid-20585013` and is not direct Tabata 20/10 efficacy evidence.

**Why it matters:** Tabata discourse often uses VO2max and heart-rate terms; CPET guidance keeps these endpoints within a clinical interpretation boundary.

**Potential experiment signals:** VO2peak; heart rate response; oxygen uptake response; test termination symptoms.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for clinical-monitoring context and things-to-watch, not user-facing diagnosis.
