This source is included for **safety_msk_rhabdo_heat_respiratory**.

**Findings:** The guideline highlights the need for assessment, intensity targeting, and monitoring in clinical populations. It should be used as a safety boundary rather than as evidence for Tabata efficacy.

**Why it matters:** It sets the supervised-clinical boundary for high-intensity interval programs.

**Potential experiment signals:** resting symptoms, medications, individual target heart-rate zone, session RPE, clinician clearance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to route clinical populations toward supervised or clinician-guided HIIT rather than maximal home Tabata.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
