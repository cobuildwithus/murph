This source is included for **safety_msk_rhabdo_heat_respiratory**.

**Findings:** A small crossover study in adults with and without EIB found similar airway responsiveness after sprint-interval and continuous exercise. It is respiratory safety-boundary evidence, not direct Tabata proof.

**Why it matters:** It directly addresses the respiratory response to high-intensity intervals in people with EIB.

**Potential experiment signals:** FEV1 change, wheeze or cough, chest tightness, post-exercise respiratory recovery.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to support EIB/asthma stop-condition language for intense intervals.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
