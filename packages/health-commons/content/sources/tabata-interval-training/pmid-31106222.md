This source is included for **Safety: musculoskeletal injury / HIFT-CrossFit boundary**.

**Findings:** Overall, 50.85% reported injury; CrossFit participants reported 60.67% injury versus 46.71% in traditional weightlifting. The article reported CrossFit participants were 1.30 times more likely to be injured, 1.86 times more likely to seek medical attention after injury, and 2.26 times more likely to be injured after adjustment. Common locations included shoulder, lower back, and hip. The source is classified as `adjacent_variant` and `safety-only` for the Tabata 20/10 protocol. Citation key: `source_artifact:pmid-31106222`.

**Why it matters:** Use as a safety reminder for functional Tabata-style circuits that include weightlifting or complex loaded movements.

**Potential experiment signals:** Track shoulder/back/knee symptoms, pain severity, missed workouts, medical-care seeking, and load-scaling decisions when the Tabata-style workout is implemented as a functional circuit.

**Protocol takeaway:** Safety-only adjacent evidence: loaded functional movements may warrant extra technique checks and medical-care watchpoints.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`. The comparison is observational and self-reported; do not generalize to all Tabata modalities or infer causality.
