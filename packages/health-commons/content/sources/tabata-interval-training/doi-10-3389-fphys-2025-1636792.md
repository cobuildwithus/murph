This source is included for **cardiometabolic_body_composition**.

**Findings:** HIIT was broadly comparable to MICT for body composition and blood pressure in overweight adolescents, with a small body-fat-percentage signal and no clear overall BP advantage. Reported modest body-fat-percentage reduction but no statistically relevant differences for body weight, BMI, fat-free mass, or blood pressure; MICT was favored for waist circumference in a running subgroup.

**Why it matters:** This helps prevent overgeneralizing adult or generic HIIT claims into youth/school Tabata-style contexts.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- body-fat-percentage
- waist-circumference
- BMI
- systolic-blood-pressure
- diastolic-blood-pressure

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as youth adjacent-context evidence; do not claim Tabata 20/10 improves adolescent BP or adiposity from this source.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse-event counts or safety event rates were extracted from accessible metadata/abstract details.  Key limitations: Small included study sizes.; No large RCTs were available.; Allocation and blinding details were often unclear.; Exercise protocols were heterogeneous.; Subgroup analyses were post hoc.

**Population mismatch:** Overweight adolescent population and supervised training context do not directly match adult or community Tabata 20/10 use.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
