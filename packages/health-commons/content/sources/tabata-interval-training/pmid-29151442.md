This source is included for **direct_original_dose_evidence**.

**Findings:** The diet experiment measured ten men in a metabolic chamber on days with versus without 6-7 bouts of 20-second cycling at 170% VO2max separated by 10-second rests, preceded by a 10-minute warm-up. Oxygen uptake during HIIE, EPOC, and diet-induced oxygen uptake were quantified, and the diet-induced increase was correlated with VO2max.

**Why it matters:** It uses the original-style 170% VO2max 20s:10s bicycle exposure and quantifies post-exercise and meal-related oxygen uptake.

**Potential experiment signals:** HIIE oxygen uptake, EPOC, postprandial oxygen uptake, VO2max relationship.

**Protocol takeaway:** This source can describe acute metabolic consequences of the original 20/10 exposure, but it should not be used to claim chronic fitness benefits.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
