This source is included for **cardiometabolic_body_composition**.

**Findings:** Interval training was not clearly superior to MICT for body adiposity, and short-term adiposity effects may be small. No clear superiority of interval training versus MICT for adiposity outcomes was extracted.

**Why it matters:** This review is an important counterweight to claims that brief HIIT protocols automatically produce meaningful fat loss.

**Potential experiment signals:**
- body-fat-percentage
- fat-mass
- waist-circumference
- body-weight

**Protocol takeaway:** Use to temper claims: body-composition changes likely require adequate duration, energy balance context, and measurement.

**Safety and limitations:** No adverse-event counts or safety event rates were extracted from accessible metadata/abstract details.  Key limitations: Only four included studies reportedly had more than 20 participants per group.; Small samples and short interventions limit certainty.; No direct Tabata 20/10 subgroup was extracted.

**Population mismatch:** Broad adiposity trials may not match Tabata 20/10 sessions or the protocol's likely intensity/volume.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
