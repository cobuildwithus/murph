This source is included for **safety_msk_rhabdo_heat_respiratory**.

**Findings:** After a direct Tabata squat exposure, foam rolling improved DOMS and hip ROM but not jump-performance recovery. Use for soreness and recovery burden, not efficacy.

**Why it matters:** It directly uses 20 seconds work / 10 seconds rest repeated 8 times and measures recovery burden.

**Potential experiment signals:** DOMS, hip range of motion, countermovement jump, squat jump, 48-hour performance drop.

**Protocol takeaway:** Frame foam rolling as possible soreness support, not a guarantee of faster performance recovery.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
