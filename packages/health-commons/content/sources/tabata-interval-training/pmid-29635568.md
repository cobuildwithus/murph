This source is included for **Rhabdomyolysis, heat, and serious exertional adverse-event safety**.

**Findings:** The report describes 44 cadets exposed to an extreme exercise program, with 11 hospitalized for exertional rhabdomyolysis. It is a safety-only cluster source for sudden high-volume exertion and group-pressure contexts.

**Why it matters:** It highlights group and incentive conditions that can turn intense exercise into an adverse-event cluster.

**Potential experiment signals:** exposed group size, hospitalizations, exercise-program volume, motivation/pressure context, and clinical diagnosis.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as safety-only evidence against sudden extreme challenges and pressure-driven intensity.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
