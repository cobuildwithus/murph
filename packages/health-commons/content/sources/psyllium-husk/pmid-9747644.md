**Findings:** In type II diabetes, Plantago psyllium 5 g three times daily before meals was associated in accessible snippets with significant decreases in glucose, total cholesterol, LDL-C, and triglycerides and increased HDL-C.

**Why it matters:** Diabetes-specific evidence supports metabolic context but should not be promoted as direct general adult cholesterol evidence.
