This source is included for **Direct protocol synthesis and dose-response evidence**.

## Source extraction notes

**Findings:** Psyllium was associated with lower total cholesterol (-0.375 mmol/L) and LDL-C (-0.278 mmol/L); triglycerides were not significantly changed and HDL-C was slightly lower. The DARE appraisal cautioned that the review did not report a formal validity assessment and included small, short, and incompletely described trials.

**Why it matters:** This source is a direct dose-and-duration backbone for deciding protocol length and dose range.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C; total cholesterol; HDL-C; triglycerides.

**Protocol takeaway:** Psyllium shows LDL-C and total cholesterol reductions across 2-to-26-week trials, with dose and treatment duration influencing estimates.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Evidence boundary

- **Directness:** `direct_protocol`.
- **Population mismatch:** Low mismatch for mild-to-moderate hypercholesterolemia; less direct for lower-risk or normolipidemic users.
- **Limitations:** DARE noted no formal validity assessment and limited reporting detail. Small and short trials contribute to uncertainty. HDL-C reduction was small and directionally not a desired outcome.
