This source is included for **Mechanism: viscosity, bile-acid, sterol, and fecal-excretion context**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Randomized placebo-controlled trial in T2DM outpatients (2005).
- **People studied or addressed:** Adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus treated as outpatients.
- **Intervention or exposure:** 5.1 g psyllium husk twice daily, 30 minutes before breakfast and dinner.
- **Comparator or control:** Placebo.
- **Duration or follow-up:** 8 weeks.
- **Endpoints:** Fasting blood sugar, HbA1c, insulin, total cholesterol, LDL-C, HDL-C, triglycerides, LDL/HDL ratio.
- **Directness:** same_mechanism.
- **Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Findings

- Psyllium significantly decreased fasting blood sugar and HbA1c, increased HDL-C, and decreased the LDL/HDL ratio in accessible trial text.
- Accessible text describes psyllium as well tolerated, with no serious treatment-related adverse events extracted and improved tolerance of metformin-associated gastric events.

## Why it matters

Helps separate diabetes/glycemic psyllium evidence from LDL-C protocol claims.

## Potential experiment signals

- HbA1c

## Protocol takeaway

Use as adjacent diabetes-population context; do not make a main cholesterol claim from this study alone.

## Safety and adverse events

The intervention was described as well tolerated, with no serious treatment-related adverse events extracted; psyllium improved tolerance of metformin-associated gastric events in the accessible text.

## Limitations and population fit

Diabetes/glycemic trial; lipid endpoints are secondary or context in this batch.

**Population mismatch:** T2DM population and glycemic focus limit directness to a cholesterol protocol.
