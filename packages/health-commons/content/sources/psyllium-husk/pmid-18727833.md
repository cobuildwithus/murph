This source is included for **Direct protocol trials, dose, timing, formulation, and subgroup evidence**.

**Findings:** In 19 hypercholesterolemic women, 15 g/day psyllium for 6 weeks produced mixed menopausal-status responses: postmenopausal women had lower total cholesterol but also lower HDL-C, with no significant LDL-C or triglyceride changes.

**Why it matters:** Provides a cautionary subgroup boundary: LDL-C lowering was not confirmed in this small women-only study.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C, HDL-C, triglycerides, apoA1, apoB, menopausal status.

**Protocol takeaway:** Provides a cautionary subgroup boundary: LDL-C lowering was not confirmed in this small women-only study.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction caveat:** Small sample and mixed endpoints.
