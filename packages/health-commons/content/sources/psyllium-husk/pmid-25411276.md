This source is included for **Adjacent variants, soluble-fiber comparators, and population mismatch**.

**Findings:** Oat beta-glucan reduced LDL-C by 0.25 mmol/L (95% CI 0.20 to 0.30 reported as reduction) and total cholesterol by 0.30 mmol/L (95% CI 0.24 to 0.35 reported as reduction); HDL-C and triglycerides were not significantly changed. Dose range was 3.0-12.4 g/day and treatment duration 2-12 weeks.

**Safety / adverse events:** No adverse-event data were extracted from the accessible review summary.

**Why it matters:** Widely cited oat beta-glucan comparator that helps define the boundary between oat beta-glucan and psyllium husk.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, total cholesterol, HDL-C, triglycerides.

**Limitations / population mismatch:** Oat beta-glucan source/matrix and trial population differences limit transfer to psyllium; diabetes subgroup effects were based on few studies.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context only; do not treat oat beta-glucan effect sizes as psyllium effect sizes.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
