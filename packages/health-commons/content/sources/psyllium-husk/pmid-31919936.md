This source is included for **Direct protocol trials, dose, timing, formulation, and subgroup evidence**.

**Findings:** A diabetes-specific RCT meta-analysis reported LDL-C and triglyceride reductions with psyllium, including LDL-C WMD −8.96 mg/dL and triglyceride WMD −19.18 mg/dL, while total cholesterol, HDL-C, BMI, and weight were not significantly changed.

**Why it matters:** Helps bound diabetes-subgroup expectations but should not be used as primary adult non-diabetes protocol evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, triglycerides, total cholesterol, HDL-C, fasting blood sugar, HbA1c, body weight, BMI.

**Protocol takeaway:** Helps bound diabetes-subgroup expectations but should not be used as primary adult non-diabetes protocol evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction caveat:** Diabetes-only population and high heterogeneity in accessible snippets.
