## Quick read

- **Source type:** Animal comparative dietary modification study in rats (1995).
- **Population/model:** Rats fed diets modified with soluble fibers and cholesterol-related comparators.
- **Intervention/exposure:** Dietary psyllium hydrocolloid, pectin, cholesterol, and cholestyramine dietary modifications.
- **Comparator/control:** Cellulose or other dietary comparator groups; exact group-level extraction was limited.
- **Duration/follow-up:** Not extracted from accessible abstract/metadata.
- **Role in Murph:** Context-only mechanism evidence for the Psyllium Husk For Cholesterol protocol.

## Findings

- **Cholesterol 7 alpha-hydroxylase activity and bile acid pool size.:** Accessible extraction indicates this rat study evaluated increased cholesterol 7 alpha-hydroxylase activity under dietary modification; later source text reports Matheson et al. found greater 7 alpha-hydroxylase activity and bile acid pool size in rats fed 5% psyllium versus cellulose, but complete group-level estimates were not extracted here.
