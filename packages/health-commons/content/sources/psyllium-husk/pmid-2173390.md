This source is included for **Direct protocol trials, dose, timing, formulation, and subgroup evidence**.

**Findings:** In 58 hypercholesterolemic men, psyllium-enriched cereal added to a Step 1/prudent diet lowered total cholesterol and LDL-C by an additional 5.9% and 5.7%, respectively, over 6 weeks versus control cereal context.

**Why it matters:** Supports cereal/food-format psyllium as a direct LDL-lowering formulation in adults.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C, HDL-C, triglycerides, body weight.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports cereal/food-format psyllium as a direct LDL-lowering formulation in adults.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Extraction caveat:** Male-only sample and food matrix may differ from loose psyllium husk.
