This source is included for **Direct protocol trials, dose, timing, formulation, and subgroup evidence**.

**Findings:** A psyllium-enriched cereal used with a prudent diet lowered total cholesterol and LDL-C versus a control cereal in adults with mild-to-moderate hypercholesterolemia; accessible artifacts did not expose a reusable exact effect size.

**Why it matters:** Supports food-format delivery as a direct adult psyllium formulation, while leaving magnitude uncertain from accessible artifacts.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C, HDL-C, triglycerides.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports food-format delivery as a direct adult psyllium formulation, while leaving magnitude uncertain from accessible artifacts.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Extraction caveat:** Cereal matrix may not translate exactly to loose husk powder dosing.
