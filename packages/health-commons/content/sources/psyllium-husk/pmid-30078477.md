This source is included for **Adjacent variants, soluble-fiber comparators, and population mismatch**.

**Findings:** Across three statin-adjunct trials totaling 204 participants, psyllium plus statin significantly improved cholesterol lowering versus statin alone; the abstract-level record states that LDL-C reductions were equivalent to doubling the statin dose.

**Why it matters:** This is the clearest combination-therapy boundary source and should not be merged with direct psyllium-only evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, total cholesterol.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as statin-adjunct context only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
