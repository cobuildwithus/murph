This source is included for **Direct protocol synthesis and dose-response evidence**.

## Source extraction notes

**Findings:** Psyllium lowered LDL-C by -0.33 mmol/L, non-HDL-C by -0.39 mmol/L, and apoB by -0.05 g/L versus control in random-effects meta-analysis.

**Why it matters:** This is one of the most directly relevant modern pooled sources for LDL-C and alternative atherogenic lipid targets.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C; non-HDL-C; apolipoprotein B; total cholesterol.

**Protocol takeaway:** LDL-C remains the main endpoint, but non-HDL-C and apoB are useful secondary endpoints when available.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Evidence boundary

- **Directness:** `direct_protocol`.
- **Population mismatch:** Moderate-to-low mismatch: includes adults both with and without hypercholesterolemia.
- **Limitations:** Moderate quality for LDL-C and non-HDL-C because of inconsistency. Median dose does not define every included trial dose. Paywalled/full text access may limit extraction of all individual trial details.
- **Safety notes:** This batch source is focused on lipid efficacy, not adverse-event enumeration.
