This source is included for **Adjacent variants and implementation context**.

**Findings:** In 59 subjects with moderate hypercholesterolemia treated long-term with 20 g/day mixed dietary fibers for an additional 36 weeks after a 15-week controlled phase, total cholesterol fell about 5%, LDL-C about 9%, and LDL/HDL ratio about 11% at 51 weeks, with no significant effects on triglycerides or HDL-C; the blend did not include psyllium.

**Why it matters:** Useful for long-duration mixed-fiber context and to avoid attributing mixed-fiber effects to psyllium.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C, LDL/HDL ratio, HDL-C, triglycerides.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only mixed-fiber source.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
