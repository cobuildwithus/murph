This source is included for **Adjacent variants, soluble-fiber comparators, and population mismatch**.

**Findings:** Across 14 glucomannan studies with 531 participants, glucomannan significantly lowered total cholesterol (-19.28 mg/dL), LDL-C (-15.99 mg/dL), triglycerides (-11.08 mg/dL), body weight (-0.79 kg), and fasting blood glucose (-7.44 mg/dL), but did not significantly alter other endpoints such as HDL-C or blood pressure.

**Why it matters:** Useful to compare viscous soluble fiber class effects without generalizing them directly to psyllium.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C, triglycerides, body weight, fasting blood glucose, HDL-C, blood pressure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as soluble-fiber comparator context.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
