This source is included for **Adjacent variants, soluble-fiber comparators, and population mismatch**.

**Findings:** Glucomannan decreased total cholesterol (SMD -3.299, 95% CI -4.955 to -1.664, P<0.001; I²=95.41%), LDL-C (SMD -2.993, 95% CI -4.958 to -1.028, P=0.006; I²=95.49%), and Apo B1 (SMD -2.2, 95% CI -3.58 to -0.82, P=0.01). It did not alter triglycerides, Apo A1, APO-B/A1 ratio, or LDL-C/HDL-C ratio in the PubMed abstract.

**Safety / adverse events:** No adverse-event data were extracted from the PubMed abstract in this batch.

**Why it matters:** Recent adult-only GRADE synthesis for another viscous soluble fiber, useful for heterogeneity and certainty caveats.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C, triglycerides, HDL-C, ApoB, ApoA1.

**Limitations / population mismatch:** High heterogeneity for major lipid endpoints; source is glucomannan, not psyllium. GRADE certainty was reported as low for TC/LDL-C and very low for TG/HDL-C in accessible source summaries.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent glucomannan context only; do not import effect sizes into psyllium.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
