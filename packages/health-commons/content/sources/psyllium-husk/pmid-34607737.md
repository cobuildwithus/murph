This source is included for **Adjacent variants, soluble-fiber comparators, and population mismatch**.

**Findings:** This systematic review/meta-analysis reported that guar gum supplementation significantly reduced LDL-C and total cholesterol in patients with cardiometabolic problems, while effects on triglycerides and HDL-C were neutral. It is a non-psyllium comparator source.

**Why it matters:** Useful for soluble-fiber comparator context and endpoint boundaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, total cholesterol, triglycerides, HDL-C.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as non-psyllium soluble-fiber comparator only.
