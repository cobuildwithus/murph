This source is included for **Adjacent variants, soluble-fiber comparators, and population mismatch**.

**Findings:** Pooled analysis of 19 RCTs found total cholesterol reduction (WMD -19.34 mg/dL, 95% CI -26.18 to -12.49, p<0.001) and LDL reduction (WMD -16.19 mg/dL, 95% CI -25.54 to -6.83, p=0.001); no effect was found on triglycerides or HDL-C.

**Safety / adverse events:** No adverse-event data were extracted from the accessible abstract/record in this batch.

**Why it matters:** Strong adjacent viscous-fiber comparator with GRADE framing and dose-response intent.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C, HDL-C, triglycerides.

**Limitations / population mismatch:** Authors called for future large RCTs in varied populations; guar gum is an adjacent viscous fiber with different physicochemical and tolerability properties from psyllium.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as guar-gum comparator evidence only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
