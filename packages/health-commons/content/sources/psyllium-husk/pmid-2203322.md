This source is included for **Direct protocol adult lipid trial evidence**.

## Source extraction notes

**Findings:** Psyllium reduced total cholesterol by about 5.6% and LDL-C by about 8.6%; placebo was unchanged and triglycerides were not substantially changed. The accessible abstract-level record reported compliance greater than 95% and described psyllium as a useful and safe adjunct, but detailed adverse-event counts were not extracted.

**Why it matters:** This is a direct cellulose-controlled trial, useful for distinguishing psyllium from insoluble-fiber placebo.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C; total cholesterol; HDL-C; triglycerides; adherence; tolerability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Psyllium 5.1 g twice daily for 16 weeks lowered LDL-C and total cholesterol while triglycerides were not substantially changed.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Evidence boundary

- **Directness:** `direct_protocol`.
- **Population mismatch:** Low mismatch for mild-to-moderate hypercholesterolemia; older diet-stabilization design may differ from unsupervised use.
- **Limitations:** Small sample size. Older trial with limited accessible detail on adverse-event collection. Prudent diet co-intervention may affect generalizability.
- **Safety notes:** Compliance was reported above 95% in accessible abstract-level extraction; detailed adverse-event data were not extracted.
