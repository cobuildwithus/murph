This source is included for **Adjacent variants and implementation context**.

**Findings:** Nine studies indicated total cholesterol and triacylglycerol could be significantly decreased in subjects with hyperlipidemia, whereas effects were absent in normal subjects. The accessible DARE abstract did not provide pooled numeric effect sizes.

**Safety / adverse events:** No adverse-event data were extracted from the accessible DARE abstract.

**Why it matters:** Preserves mixed, population-specific inulin/fructan findings and prevents extending viscous-fiber claims to all soluble fibers.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, triglycerides.

**Limitations / population mismatch:** Older review; numeric effect estimates and participant count were not extracted from the accessible abstract; inulin-type fructans are prebiotic/non-viscous soluble fibers and not psyllium.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as non-psyllium, non-viscous/prebiotic context only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
