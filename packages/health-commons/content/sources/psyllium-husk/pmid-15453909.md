This source is included for **Direct protocol trials, dose, timing, formulation, and subgroup evidence**.

**Findings:** In a randomized crossover timing trial of 63 normocholesterolemic or slightly hypercholesterolemic adults, average 12.7 g/day psyllium showed no cholesterol-lowering effect and no timing advantage between morning and evening use.

**Why it matters:** Adds a null boundary: benefit may depend on elevated baseline cholesterol, and timing alone is not a proven lever.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C, HDL-C, triglycerides, administration timing.

**Protocol takeaway:** Adds a null boundary: benefit may depend on elevated baseline cholesterol, and timing alone is not a proven lever.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction caveat:** Not a high-LDL hypercholesterolemia sample.
