This source is included for **Regulatory, guideline, and external health-claim context**.

**Findings:** The regulation authorizes claim language that diets low in saturated fat and cholesterol and including soluble fiber from certain foods may reduce CHD risk; it associates psyllium seed husk with 7 g/day soluble fiber and qualifying foods with at least 1.7 g soluble fiber per RACC.

**Why it matters:** Provides the clearest U.S. label-claim dose and wording boundary for psyllium soluble fiber.

**Potential experiment signals:** soluble fiber grams/day, serving soluble fiber, LDL-C, total cholesterol, hydration tolerance

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for label-claim and safety-boundary context only; cite primary trials or meta-analyses for efficacy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
