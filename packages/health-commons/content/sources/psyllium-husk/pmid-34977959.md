This source is included for **Adjacent variants, soluble-fiber comparators, and population mismatch**.

**Findings:** Compared with controls without oats, oat supplementation interventions lowered total cholesterol (WMD -0.42 mmol/L, 95% CI -0.61 to -0.22) and LDL-C (WMD -0.29 mmol/L, 95% CI -0.37 to -0.20), with additional reported reductions in glucose, BMI, body weight, and waist circumference. Evidence for inflammation and oxidative-stress outcomes was scarce or inconsistent.

**Safety / adverse events:** No adverse-event data were extracted in this batch; the review focused on cardiovascular risk markers.

**Why it matters:** Modern oat intervention review that explicitly highlights background diet and comparator-arm issues.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C, blood glucose, BMI, body weight, waist circumference.

**Limitations / population mismatch:** Dietary background and control arms were heterogeneous; 81.1% of studies had some risk-of-bias concerns; oat interventions are not psyllium monotherapy.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as oat-intervention context only; do not generalize to psyllium dosing or expected LDL-C response.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
