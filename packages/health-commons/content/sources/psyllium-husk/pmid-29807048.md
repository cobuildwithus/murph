This source is included for **Adjacent variants, soluble-fiber comparators, and population mismatch**.

**Findings:** In seven controlled Portfolio Diet comparisons with 439 participants, the dietary pattern reduced LDL-C by about 17% (MD -0.73 mmol/L) and improved several cardiometabolic endpoints, but the multi-component diet prevents attributing effects to psyllium alone.

**Why it matters:** Important for claims discipline around Portfolio Diet studies that include viscous-fiber components.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, non-HDL-C, apoB, total cholesterol, triglycerides, blood pressure, C-reactive protein, 10-year CHD risk.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as whole-diet context only, not psyllium monotherapy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
