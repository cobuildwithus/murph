This source is included for **Adjacent variants, soluble-fiber comparators, and population mismatch**.

**Findings:** This randomized study compared isapgol plus atorvastatin versus atorvastatin alone for LDL-C and total cholesterol reduction after 12 weeks, making it a medication-adjunct boundary source rather than psyllium monotherapy evidence.

**Why it matters:** It helps prevent statin-adjunct effects from being folded into psyllium monotherapy claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, total cholesterol, safety.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use only as clinical combination context; no monotherapy LDL-C effect estimate was extracted.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
