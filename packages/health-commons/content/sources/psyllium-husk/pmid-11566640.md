This source is included for **Direct protocol trials, dose, timing, formulation, and subgroup evidence**.

**Findings:** In a 68-participant crossover subgroup trial, 15 g/day psyllium lowered LDL-C by about 7-9% without affecting HDL-C, while triglyceride response varied by sex/hormonal status.

**Why it matters:** Supports LDL-lowering potential but emphasizes heterogeneity, especially for triglyceride response.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, HDL-C, triglycerides, sex interaction, hormonal status.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports LDL-lowering potential but emphasizes heterogeneity, especially for triglyceride response.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction caveat:** Subgroup-focused trial; not a simple average-effect protocol anchor.
