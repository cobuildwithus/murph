This source is included for **Direct protocol trials, dose, timing, formulation, and subgroup evidence**.

**Findings:** In 42 men and women with hyperlipidemia, psyllium cereal providing 6.7 g/day psyllium fiber lowered total cholesterol and LDL-C versus matched wheat-bran cereal in short crossover periods; triglycerides were not affected.

**Why it matters:** Supports psyllium food-format LDL lowering and suggests response was not limited to one sex in this trial.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C, HDL-C, LDL/HDL ratio, triglycerides, sex interaction.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports psyllium food-format LDL lowering and suggests response was not limited to one sex in this trial.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Extraction caveat:** Short treatment periods and cereal matrix.
