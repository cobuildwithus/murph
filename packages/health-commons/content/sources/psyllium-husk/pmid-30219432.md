This source is included for **Direct protocol trials, dose, timing, formulation, and subgroup evidence**.

**Findings:** In 51 patients with type 2 diabetes and chronic constipation, 10 g psyllium twice daily reportedly improved constipation and reduced blood sugar, cholesterol, and body weight versus control, but exact lipid effect sizes were not extracted.

**Why it matters:** Relevant clinical subgroup context, especially where constipation coexists, but not a primary cholesterol protocol source.

**Potential experiment signals:** constipation symptoms, weight, glycemia, cholesterol, lipids.

**Protocol takeaway:** Relevant clinical subgroup context, especially where constipation coexists, but not a primary cholesterol protocol source.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction caveat:** Population and symptom target differ from the adult cholesterol protocol.
