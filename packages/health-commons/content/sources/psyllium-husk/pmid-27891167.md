This source is included for **Adjacent variants, soluble-fiber comparators, and population mismatch**.

**Findings:** In a 52-week double-blind randomized trial with 159 overweight/obese Australian adults assigned to rice flour control, PGX, or proprietary psyllium, PGX was reported as better than psyllium for maintaining dietary changes and weight loss over 12 months; this is implementation and body-composition context, not cholesterol efficacy evidence.

**Why it matters:** Useful for adherence and long-duration supplementation context linked to psyllium trial infrastructure.

**Potential experiment signals:** body weight, body composition, dietary adherence, exercise.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as implementation and metabolic-context evidence; not LDL-C protocol efficacy.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
