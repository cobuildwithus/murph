This source is included for **Adjacent variants and implementation context**.

**Findings:** Overall, ITF reduced only LDL-C (MD -0.15 mmol/L, 95% CI -0.29 to -0.02, P=0.03) and did not affect other endpoints. In the type 2 diabetes subgroup, ITF was associated with decreased fasting insulin (MD -4.01, 95% CI -5.92 to -2.09), increased HDL-C (MD 0.07, 95% CI 0 to 0.14), and a non-significant fasting-glucose trend (MD -0.42, 95% CI -0.90 to 0.06).

**Safety / adverse events:** No adverse-event data were extracted from the accessible abstract/source summary.

**Why it matters:** Modernizes inulin/fructan context and demonstrates limited/mixed lipid endpoints versus viscous-fiber evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, HDL-C, blood glucose, fasting insulin.

**Limitations / population mismatch:** Potential publication bias and few type 2 diabetes subgroup trials were reported; inulin-type fructans are not psyllium and may be less viscous/prebiotic rather than gel-forming.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as non-psyllium prebiotic soluble-fiber boundary evidence only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
