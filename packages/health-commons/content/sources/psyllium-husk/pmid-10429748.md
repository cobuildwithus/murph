This source is included for **Adjacent variants and implementation context**.

**Findings:** After NCEP Step 1 diet stabilization, 169 subjects were randomized to 20 g/day mixed-fiber supplement or placebo for 15 weeks; compliant fiber-group participants had LDL-C change of -0.51 mmol/L (-12.1%) versus -0.05 mmol/L (-1.3%) with placebo, without significant effects on HDL-C, triglycerides, iron, ferritin, or vitamins A/E; effects were similar during a 36-week extension.

**Why it matters:** Useful for implementation and long-duration mixed-fiber context, not psyllium-specific claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, total cholesterol, LDL-C/HDL-C ratio, HDL-C, triglycerides, iron, ferritin, vitamins A and E.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only: mixed fiber supplement, no psyllium-specific separability.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
