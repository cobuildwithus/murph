This source is included for **Direct protocol trials, dose, timing, formulation, and subgroup evidence**.

**Findings:** In men with type 2 diabetes and hypercholesterolemia, psyllium 5.1 g twice daily was studied against cellulose placebo for 8 weeks with serum lipid and glucose endpoints; accessible snippets indicate LDL-C lowering but did not expose a complete effect statement for reuse.

**Why it matters:** Useful for diabetes/metabolic subgroup context but should not be generalized as primary adult cholesterol evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, total cholesterol, glucose response, serum lipids.

**Protocol takeaway:** Useful for diabetes/metabolic subgroup context but should not be generalized as primary adult cholesterol evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction caveat:** Male-only type 2 diabetes population and incomplete accessible effect details.
