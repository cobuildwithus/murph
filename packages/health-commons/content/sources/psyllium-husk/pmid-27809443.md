This source is included for **Regulatory, guideline, and external health-claim context**.

**Findings:** The chapter states that psyllium can be added to the CHILD-2 LDL-lowering diet to aid total and LDL-C lowering, but that evidence is insufficient for a specific recommendation; it gives pediatric doses of 6 g/day for ages 2-12 and 12 g/day for age 12 and older.

**Why it matters:** Clearly separates pediatric clinical nutrition context from adult self-experiment interpretation.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, total cholesterol, age group, dose adherence, GI tolerance

**Protocol takeaway:** Use for pediatric boundary and evidence-limitation language, not for adult dose-response claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
