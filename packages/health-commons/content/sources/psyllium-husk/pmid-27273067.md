This source is included for **Adjacent variants, soluble-fiber comparators, and population mismatch**.

**Findings:** Barley beta-glucan reduced LDL-C (MD -0.25 mmol/L, 95% CI -0.30 to -0.20) and non-HDL-C (MD -0.31 mmol/L, 95% CI -0.39 to -0.23), with no significant apoB change. Considerable unexplained heterogeneity was reported for non-HDL-C (I²=98%).

**Safety / adverse events:** No adverse-event data were extracted from the accessible abstract/summary.

**Why it matters:** Adds modern barley beta-glucan endpoints such as non-HDL-C and apoB to the comparator landscape.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, non-HDL-C, ApoB.

**Limitations / population mismatch:** Barley beta-glucan is an adjacent cereal-fiber variant; non-HDL-C heterogeneity was considerable and transfer to psyllium is indirect.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent barley beta-glucan context only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
