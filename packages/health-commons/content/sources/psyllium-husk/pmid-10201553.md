This source is included for **Direct protocol trials, dose, timing, formulation, and subgroup evidence**.

**Findings:** In 340 adults with mild-to-moderate hypercholesterolaemia, diet plus ispaghula husk 7.0 or 10.5 g/day lowered LDL-C more than placebo over 12 weeks, with abstract-reported reductions of 8.7% and 9.7% from baseline.

**Why it matters:** Supports a practical adult dose range around 7-10.5 g/day as a diet adjunct.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, total cholesterol, tolerability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports a practical adult dose range around 7-10.5 g/day as a diet adjunct.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Extraction caveat:** The source uses ispaghula terminology and detailed adverse-event counts were not extracted.
