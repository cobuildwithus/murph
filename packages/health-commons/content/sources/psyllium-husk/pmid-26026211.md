This source is included for **Adjacent variants, soluble-fiber comparators, and population mismatch**.

**Findings:** In 17 RCTs with 916 hypercholesterolemic subjects, beta-glucan significantly decreased total cholesterol by -0.26 mmol/L and LDL-C by -0.21 mmol/L, with no significant effects on HDL-C, triglycerides, or glucose; no adverse effects were reported in the eligible trials.

**Why it matters:** Helps frame soluble-fiber class comparisons while preventing cross-fiber overgeneralization.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C, HDL-C, triglycerides, glucose, adverse effects.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as comparator context only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
