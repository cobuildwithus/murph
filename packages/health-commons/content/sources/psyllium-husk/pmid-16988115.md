This source is included for **Mechanism: viscosity, bile-acid, sterol, and fecal-excretion context**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled crossover trial (2006).
- **People studied or addressed:** Hypercholesterolemic adults aged 35–65 years with BMI 25–35 kg/m².
- **Intervention or exposure:** Cookies containing 7.68 g/day psyllium plus 2.6 g/day plant sterols.
- **Comparator or control:** Placebo cookies.
- **Duration or follow-up:** Two 4-week treatment periods separated by 3-week washout.
- **Endpoints:** Total cholesterol, LDL-C, IDL, LDL/HDL particles, apolipoprotein B.
- **Directness:** adjacent_variant.
- **Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Findings

- The combination reduced total cholesterol from 5.65 to 5.28 mmol/L and LDL-C from 3.48 to 3.14 mmol/L versus placebo in accessible text, with reductions in IDL, LDL/HDL particles, and apoB.

## Why it matters

Clarifies that some strong LDL-C effects come from combination products, not psyllium alone.

## Potential experiment signals

- LDL cholesterol
- total cholesterol
- apolipoprotein B
- lipoprotein particles

## Protocol takeaway

Do not attribute the combination effect to psyllium alone.

## Safety and adverse events

No adverse-event extraction was available from accessible text.

## Limitations and population fit

Combination therapy; cannot isolate psyllium effect.

**Population mismatch:** Intervention includes plant sterols and cookies rather than psyllium monotherapy.
