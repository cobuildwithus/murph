This source is included for **Direct protocol trials, dose, timing, formulation, and subgroup evidence**.

**Findings:** In a randomized crossover trial in 47 adolescent males, 6 g/day psyllium improved lipid-profile context and fat-distribution outcomes, but exact reusable lipid effect estimates were not extracted here.

**Why it matters:** Provides adolescent boundary evidence and a lower-dose pediatric/adolescent formulation context.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, lipid profile, fat distribution, body composition.

**Protocol takeaway:** Provides adolescent boundary evidence and a lower-dose pediatric/adolescent formulation context.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction caveat:** Adolescent males are a population mismatch for the adult cholesterol protocol.
