This source is included for **Adjacent variants, soluble-fiber comparators, and population mismatch**.

**Findings:** In an observational comparison of 176 elderly psyllium hydrophilic mucilloid users versus 741 non-users over 1 year, serum cholesterol decreased slightly in both groups; the extracted abstract-level record does not support causal psyllium efficacy claims.

**Why it matters:** Useful for population and long-term use context, but observational exposure cannot support causal protocol claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** serum cholesterol.

**Protocol takeaway:** Keep as elderly-population context only.
