This source is included for **Direct protocol trials, dose, timing, formulation, and subgroup evidence**.

**Findings:** An administration-method trial concluded that the cholesterol-lowering effect of psyllium depends on method of administration, with maximum effect when psyllium is mixed with foods; exact effect estimates were not extracted.

**Why it matters:** Supports logging preparation method and avoiding claims that all psyllium delivery formats are equivalent.

**Potential experiment signals:** serum cholesterol, administration method, LDL-C.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports logging preparation method and avoiding claims that all psyllium delivery formats are equivalent.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Extraction caveat:** Exact sample size/effect details were not extracted.
