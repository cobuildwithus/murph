This source is included for **Direct protocol adult lipid trial evidence**.

## Source extraction notes

**Findings:** Psyllium reduced total cholesterol by 5.8% and LDL-C by 7.2% on the high-fat diet, and total cholesterol by 4.2% and LDL-C by 6.4% on the low-fat diet; the difference between diet backgrounds was not significant. The trial monitored clinical chemistry, hematology, and urinalysis, but accessible extraction did not provide detailed adverse-event counts for source-owned reuse.

**Why it matters:** This source tests whether background dietary fat modifies psyllium response, a key confounder for protocol interpretation.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C; total cholesterol; dietary fat intake; clinical chemistry safety markers.

**Protocol takeaway:** Psyllium 5.1 g twice daily reduced LDL-C and total cholesterol on both high- and low-fat diets, so diet background should be logged but may not fully determine response.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Evidence boundary

- **Directness:** `direct_protocol`.
- **Population mismatch:** Low mismatch for hypercholesterolemic adults; controlled diet background differs from free-living self-experimentation.
- **Limitations:** Diet background was assigned/controlled more than in typical home use. Older trial with limited extracted adverse-event details. Only 8 weeks of active treatment after baseline phase.
- **Safety notes:** Clinical chemistry, hematology, and urinalysis were measured, but detailed adverse-event counts were not extracted from the accessible abstract-level record.
