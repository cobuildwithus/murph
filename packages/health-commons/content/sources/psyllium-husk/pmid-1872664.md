This source is included for **Direct protocol adult lipid trial evidence**.

## Source extraction notes

**Findings:** Psyllium produced an incremental LDL-C reduction of about 8.8% versus placebo; methylcellulose was not significant and calcium polycarbophil did not show benefit. Compliance was reported as high and side effects were described as only mild gastrointestinal symptoms in the accessible abstract-level record.

**Why it matters:** This helps separate psyllium-specific lipid effects from generic bulk-fiber claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C; total cholesterol; GI tolerability; adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Psyllium showed an incremental LDL-C reduction over diet lead-in, unlike methylcellulose or calcium polycarbophil in this trial.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Evidence boundary

- **Directness:** `direct_protocol`.
- **Population mismatch:** Low mismatch for mild-to-moderate hypercholesterolemia; older diet lead-in design may differ from unsupervised home use.
- **Limitations:** Only 105 participants completed the diet lead-in and supplement phase after 163 were recruited. Diet lead-in and high compliance may not reflect real-world use. Exact psyllium dose is not captured in the accessible abstract extract used here.
- **Safety notes:** Only mild gastrointestinal side effects were reported in accessible abstract-level extraction.
