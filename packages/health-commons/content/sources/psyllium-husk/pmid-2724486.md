This source is included for **Direct protocol trials, dose, timing, formulation, and subgroup evidence**.

**Findings:** In an older adult diet-adjunct trial, psyllium hydrophilic mucilloid 10.2 g/day reduced total cholesterol and LDL-C versus control; accessible snippets report about 4.2% and 7.7% reductions, respectively.

**Why it matters:** Foundational adult trial supporting around 10 g/day psyllium as an adjunct to a prudent diet.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C, HDL-C, triglycerides.

**Protocol takeaway:** Foundational adult trial supporting around 10 g/day psyllium as an adjunct to a prudent diet.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Extraction caveat:** Full article tables and adverse-event details were not extracted.
