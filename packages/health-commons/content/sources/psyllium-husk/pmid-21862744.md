This source is included for **Adjacent variants, soluble-fiber comparators, and population mismatch**.

**Findings:** Modified intention-to-treat analysis reported LDL-C reductions from a mean 171 mg/dL baseline: intensive portfolio -13.8% (-26 mg/dL), routine portfolio -13.1% (-24 mg/dL), and control -3.0% (-8 mg/dL). Each portfolio arm differed from control (P<0.001), while intensive and routine portfolio advice did not differ (P=0.66). LDL-C reduction correlated with dietary adherence (r=-0.34, n=157, P<0.001).

**Safety / adverse events:** Attrition was reported by arm (18% intensive, 23% routine, 26% control); no source-level adverse-event signal was extracted for psyllium or isolated viscous fiber.

**Why it matters:** Shows how viscous fibers can appear inside a broader cholesterol-lowering dietary pattern and why whole-diet evidence should be separated from monotherapy evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, dietary adherence.

**Limitations / population mismatch:** Whole-diet, multi-component intervention; effect cannot be attributed to psyllium or even to viscous fiber alone. Attrition and adherence affect interpretation.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a portfolio-diet boundary source only; do not cite it as psyllium-husk monotherapy evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
