This source is included for **Direct protocol trials, dose, timing, formulation, and subgroup evidence**.

**Findings:** In 286 hypercholesterolemic adults followed for 24 weeks, psyllium-seed-husk foods at 10.2 g/day kept LDL-C about 5.3% below control at week 24 in accessible reporting; HDL-C and triglycerides were not significantly different.

**Why it matters:** Supports long-duration adult food-format dosing around 10 g/day, with a modest LDL effect and no clear HDL/TG benefit.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, total cholesterol, HDL-C, triglycerides, dose response, compliance.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports long-duration adult food-format dosing around 10 g/day, with a modest LDL effect and no clear HDL/TG benefit.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Extraction caveat:** Compliance/substitution concerns and food matrix limit dose-response interpretation.
