This source is included for **Adjacent variants, soluble-fiber comparators, and population mismatch**.

**Findings:** In a 77-person single-blind randomized trial of constipated patients with type 2 diabetes, flaxseed or psyllium cookies were compared with placebo over 12 weeks for constipation, weight, glycemic, and lipid outcomes; the abstract-level conclusion reported flaxseed appeared superior to psyllium, so the source is a T2D/constipation boundary rather than direct cholesterol-protocol evidence.

**Why it matters:** Useful for T2D/constipation caveats and to avoid conflating flaxseed superiority with psyllium efficacy.

**Potential experiment signals:** constipation symptoms, BMI, fasting plasma glucose, HbA1c, lipid profile.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as population-mismatch context; extract arm-level lipid results from full text before any stronger claim.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
