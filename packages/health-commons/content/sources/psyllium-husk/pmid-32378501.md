This source is included for **Adjacent variants, soluble-fiber comparators, and population mismatch**.

**Findings:** The review reported fasting total cholesterol decreased by about -0.27 mmol/L and LDL-C by -0.26 mmol/L (95% CI -0.32 to -0.20, P<0.001 for LDL-C), with no significant HDL-C effect; combined solid-plus-liquid matrices ranked best, followed by liquid and then solid matrices.

**Safety / adverse events:** No adverse-event data were extracted from the accessible article summary.

**Why it matters:** Directly supports a matrix/processing boundary: even within oat beta-glucan, delivery form matters.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C, HDL-C, triglycerides.

**Limitations / population mismatch:** High heterogeneity was reported for LDL-C and HDL-C analyses; delivery matrix, processing, and beta-glucan source complicate transfer to psyllium husk.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent matrix-context only; avoid assuming psyllium powder/capsule effects from oat beta-glucan food matrices.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
