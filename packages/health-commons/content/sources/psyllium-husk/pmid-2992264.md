This source is included for **Adjacent variants, soluble-fiber comparators, and population mismatch**.

**Findings:** Neither pectin, cellulose, nor lignin significantly altered serum total cholesterol, triglycerides, HDL-C, or HDL/total cholesterol ratio in healthy normolipidemic subjects.

**Safety / adverse events:** No adverse-event data were extracted from the accessible summary.

**Why it matters:** Important negative comparator showing not all fiber components lower cholesterol in all populations.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, HDL-C, triglycerides.

**Limitations / population mismatch:** Small sample; healthy normolipidemic population; non-psyllium fiber components; not powered to guide psyllium use in hypercholesterolemia.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a guardrail against blanket “all fiber lowers LDL-C” claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
