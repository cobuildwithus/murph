This source is included for **Adjacent variants, soluble-fiber comparators, and population mismatch**.

**Findings:** In 34 hyperlipidemic participants, LDL-C decreased 8.5% after control diet, 33.3% after lovastatin, and 29.6% after a dietary portfolio that included viscous fibers from several foods including psyllium. The psyllium contribution was not separable.

**Why it matters:** This is a central boundary source: psyllium appears inside the intervention, but the outcome belongs to the full portfolio.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, fasting blood lipids.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use this as monotherapy evidence for psyllium; use as whole-diet context.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
