This source is included for **Adjacent variants, soluble-fiber comparators, and population mismatch**.

**Findings:** Across 12 konjac glucomannan RCTs with 370 participants, glucomannan significantly lowered LDL-C by -0.35 mmol/L and non-HDL-C by -0.32 mmol/L; six trials suggested no meaningful apoB effect. The fiber is not psyllium.

**Why it matters:** Provides comparator context for LDL-C, non-HDL-C, and apoB endpoints without being psyllium evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, non-HDL-C, apoB.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as soluble-fiber comparator only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
