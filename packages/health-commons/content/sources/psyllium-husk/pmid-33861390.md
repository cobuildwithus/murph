This source is included for **Direct protocol trials, dose, timing, formulation, and subgroup evidence**.

**Findings:** In adolescents with obesity, 10 g/day Plantago psyllium for 7 weeks reduced small dense LDL and IL-6 in accessible snippets, with no HDL-subclass differences reported.

**Why it matters:** Useful for pediatric obesity and small-dense-LDL context, not direct adult LDL-C protocol evidence.

**Potential experiment signals:** small dense LDL, IL-6, HDL subclasses, adverse effects.

**Protocol takeaway:** Useful for pediatric obesity and small-dense-LDL context, not direct adult LDL-C protocol evidence.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction caveat:** Population and endpoints differ from adult hypercholesterolemia self-experimentation.
