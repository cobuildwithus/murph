This source is included for **Direct protocol adult lipid trial evidence**.

## Source extraction notes

**Findings:** Psyllium reduced LDL-C by about 12.3% at lower MUFA intake and 15.3% at higher MUFA intake versus wheat bran; HDL-C also decreased, and higher MUFA intake lowered triglycerides over control. The study reported increased chenodeoxycholate synthesis in a subset and lower LDL-to-HDL ratio with psyllium, supporting bile-acid mechanism context rather than a standalone protocol endpoint.

**Why it matters:** This source explores background fat composition and helps bound LDL-C effects under controlled diets.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C; total cholesterol; HDL-C; triglycerides; LDL-to-HDL ratio; bile acid synthesis.

**Protocol takeaway:** Psyllium lowered LDL-C at both MUFA intakes, but HDL-C reductions and controlled metabolic diets require nuanced interpretation.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Evidence boundary

- **Directness:** `direct_protocol`.
- **Population mismatch:** Moderate mismatch: tightly controlled metabolic diets differ from home use, though the participants were hyperlipidemic adults.
- **Limitations:** Controlled feeding and crossover design may not generalize to free-living use. HDL-C decreased along with LDL-C. Short 1-month periods limit long-term inference.
