This source is included for **Adjacent variants, soluble-fiber comparators, and population mismatch**.

**Findings:** In 46 hyperlipidemic adults over 1 month, LDL-C decreased by 8.0% on control diet, 30.9% with lovastatin, and 28.6% with a portfolio diet containing multiple cholesterol-lowering foods. The result is not separable to psyllium or viscous fiber alone.

**Why it matters:** Portfolio-diet results are often adjacent to psyllium claims and need explicit boundary handling.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, serum lipids, C-reactive protein, blood pressure, body weight.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not attribute the whole portfolio LDL-C reduction to psyllium husk alone.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
