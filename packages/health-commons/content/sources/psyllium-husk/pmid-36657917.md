This source is included for **Adjacent variants, soluble-fiber comparators, and population mismatch**.

**Findings:** Oat interventions decreased total cholesterol and LDL-C in both parallel-arm and crossover analyses. Isolated beta-glucan parallel-arm studies decreased total cholesterol (SMD -0.73, 95% CI -1.01 to -0.45), LDL-C (SMD -0.58, 95% CI -0.85 to -0.32), and triglycerides (SMD -0.30, 95% CI -0.49 to -0.12); HDL-C was not altered by oat or isolated beta-glucan.

**Safety / adverse events:** No adverse-event data were extracted from the accessible abstract/summary.

**Why it matters:** Directly addresses whole-food versus isolated-fiber separation, a key overclaim guardrail.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C, HDL-C, triglycerides.

**Limitations / population mismatch:** Some trials did not match total fiber intake, none were rigorously controlled feeding trials for background fat intake, and processing/molecular weight may change viscosity and lipid effects. Evidence is oat/beta-glucan, not psyllium.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as matrix/isolate boundary evidence only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
