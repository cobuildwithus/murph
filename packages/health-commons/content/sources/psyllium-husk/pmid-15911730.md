This source is included for **Adjacent variants, soluble-fiber comparators, and population mismatch**.

**Findings:** In a 68-person blinded placebo-controlled trial, simvastatin 10 mg plus psyllium 15 g/day lowered LDL-C by 63 mg/dL after 8 weeks versus 55 mg/dL with simvastatin 10 mg plus placebo (P=.03), with similar total cholesterol and apoB patterns; triglycerides and HDL-C did not show significant changes.

**Why it matters:** Important statin-combination boundary source with extractable apoB and tolerability context.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, total cholesterol, apoB, triglycerides, HDL-C, tolerability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as clinical-supervised combination context; do not translate directly into a psyllium-only protocol effect.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
