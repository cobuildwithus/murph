This source is included for **Direct protocol adult lipid trial evidence**.

## Source extraction notes

**Findings:** During the fiber phase, Plantago ovata husk reduced LDL-C and total cholesterol by about 6%, triglycerides by about 21.6%, apoB-100 by about 6.7%, and oxidized LDL versus placebo. The trial also reported lower insulin and systolic blood pressure versus placebo; these are useful context signals but secondary to the cholesterol protocol.

**Why it matters:** This is a modern adult hypercholesterolemia trial with direct husk exposure and multiple lipid/CVD biomarkers.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C; total cholesterol; triglycerides; apolipoprotein B; oxidized LDL; fasting insulin; systolic blood pressure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Plantago ovata husk 14 g/day for 8 weeks directly supports LDL-C reduction in mild-to-moderate hypercholesterolemic patients, with apoB and oxidized LDL as higher-resolution biomarkers when available.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Evidence boundary

- **Directness:** `direct_protocol`.
- **Population mismatch:** Low-to-moderate mismatch: direct mild-to-moderate hypercholesterolemia trial, though age range and diet co-intervention may differ from unsupervised home use.
- **Limitations:** All participants received low-saturated-fat diet advice. Age range was 43 to 67 years and may not generalize to younger adults. Later statin add-on phase should not be attributed to psyllium alone.
