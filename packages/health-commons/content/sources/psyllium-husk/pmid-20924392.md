This source is included for **Adjacent variants, soluble-fiber comparators, and population mismatch**.

**Findings:** Barley/barley beta-glucan lowered total cholesterol by 0.30 mmol/L (95% CI -0.39 to -0.21) and LDL-C by 0.27 mmol/L (95% CI -0.34 to -0.20); the review summary reported no dose-response pattern and no significant subgroup differences by intervention type or food matrix.

**Safety / adverse events:** No adverse-event data were extracted from the accessible review summary.

**Why it matters:** Adds a barley beta-glucan counterpart to oat evidence and shows why cereal beta-glucan should remain an adjacent comparator.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C, HDL-C, triglycerides.

**Limitations / population mismatch:** Participant count was not extractable from the accessible summary; barley beta-glucan source and food-matrix differences limit transfer to psyllium husk.

**Protocol takeaway:** Do not use barley beta-glucan effect estimates as psyllium effect estimates.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
