This source is included for **Direct protocol adult lipid trial evidence**.

## Source extraction notes

**Findings:** Psyllium produced an additional 5.5% total-cholesterol reduction versus diet alone; combined lead-in plus psyllium showed larger total cholesterol and LDL-C reductions, especially in men. Diet lead-in changes and sex-stratified reporting mean the trial supports diet-adjunct use but should not be read as a simple psyllium-alone estimate.

**Why it matters:** This source is a direct older high-dose psyllium trial often included in pooled evidence, but it needs careful boundary language.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C; total cholesterol.

**Protocol takeaway:** High-dose psyllium added to diet produced additional total-cholesterol reduction, but the lead-in and sex-specific reporting require cautious interpretation.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Evidence boundary

- **Directness:** `direct_protocol`.
- **Population mismatch:** Low-to-moderate mismatch: hypercholesterolemic adults, but high dose and supervised diet lead-in may differ from common home protocols.
- **Limitations:** High psyllium dose may not match usual protocol doses. Lead-in diet effects and sex-stratified results complicate attribution. Accessible extraction did not provide detailed adverse-event counts.
