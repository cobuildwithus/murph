This source is included for **Direct protocol trials, dose, timing, formulation, and subgroup evidence**.

**Findings:** In overweight/obese adults followed for 12 months, psyllium showed time-limited lipid improvements in accessible snippets: total cholesterol lower at 3 and 6 months and LDL-C lower at 3 months only.

**Why it matters:** Useful long-duration adult metabolic context, but not a clean hypercholesterolemia efficacy anchor.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C, HDL-C, insulin, glucose, body weight, BMI.

**Protocol takeaway:** Useful long-duration adult metabolic context, but not a clean hypercholesterolemia efficacy anchor.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction caveat:** Population and comparator structure create protocol mismatch; participant count was not extracted here.
