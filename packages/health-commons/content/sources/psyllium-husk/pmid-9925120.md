This source is included for **Adjacent variants, soluble-fiber comparators, and population mismatch**.

**Findings:** This meta-analysis of 67 controlled trials with 2,990 participants found soluble fiber intake of 2-10 g/day produced small significant reductions in total cholesterol (-0.045 mmol/L per gram) and LDL-C (-0.057 mmol/L per gram), with no significant influence on HDL-C or triglycerides; oat, psyllium, and pectin effects did not significantly differ.

**Why it matters:** Useful for broad soluble-fiber magnitude and for preventing broad category claims from becoming direct protocol claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C, HDL-C, triglycerides.
