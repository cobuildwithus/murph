This source is included for **Mechanism: viscosity, bile-acid, sterol, and fecal-excretion context**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Randomized crossover diet-supplementation study (1996).
- **People studied or addressed:** Healthy human participants consuming controlled high-fat diet periods.
- **Intervention or exposure:** 20 g/day psyllium husk fiber during soybean-oil or coconut-oil diet periods.
- **Comparator or control:** Soybean-oil or coconut-oil diet periods without psyllium.
- **Duration or follow-up:** 28-day study with four 7-day experimental periods.
- **Endpoints:** Serum cholesterol, LDL-C, apolipoprotein B, VLDL, HDL, apolipoprotein A1.
- **Directness:** direct_protocol.
- **Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Findings

- Psyllium reduced serum cholesterol during both soybean-oil and coconut-oil diets; LDL-C and apoB reductions paralleled total cholesterol, while VLDL, HDL, and apoA1 were unaffected in accessible text.

## Why it matters

Useful direct-ingredient context for diet-background boundaries, not the main hypercholesterolemia evidence base.
