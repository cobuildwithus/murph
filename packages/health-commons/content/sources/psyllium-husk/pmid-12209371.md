This source is included for **Direct protocol trials, dose, timing, formulation, and subgroup evidence**.

**Findings:** In type 2 diabetic patients, psyllium/Plantago ovata fiber was associated with accessible snippet-reported reductions in total cholesterol and LDL-C of 7.7% and 9.2%, respectively.

**Why it matters:** Supports possible lipid benefit in diabetes but remains a population-boundary source for the adult cholesterol protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C, glucose-related endpoints, uric acid, vitamin/mineral measures.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports possible lipid benefit in diabetes but remains a population-boundary source for the adult cholesterol protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
