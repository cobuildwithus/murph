This source is included for **Registries and unpublished protocols**.

**Findings:** Registry protocol used 6 g/day psyllium capsules versus potato-starch placebo in obese adolescent males in a cross-over design, with insulin sensitivity rather than lipid outcomes as the registered primary target.

**Why it matters:** Provides pediatric dose and capsule-administration context, and flags a linked lipid-profile publication as separate from the registry record.

**Potential experiment signals:** Matsuda insulin sensitivity index, BMI, 24-hour ambulatory blood pressure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent metabolic/pediatric context only; the registry endpoints are not lipid-primary and do not directly support adult cholesterol claims.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `adjacent_variant`.

**Population mismatch:** Adolescent male obesity population; not adults using psyllium for cholesterol lowering.

**Limitations:** Pediatric male-only obesity population.; Registry endpoints are insulin sensitivity, BMI, and BP rather than LDL-C.; Linked lipid-profile publication should be extracted separately if used.

**Safety notes:** No registry-extracted adverse-event results.
