## Quick read

- **Source type:** Randomized controlled trial in adults with type 2 diabetes (2016).
- **People studied or addressed:** Non-smoking adults with type 2 diabetes older than 35 years.
- **Intervention or exposure:** 10.5 g/day soluble fiber from psyllium plus regular diet.
- **Comparator or control:** Regular diet control.
- **Duration or follow-up:** 8 weeks.
- **Endpoints:** Fasting blood sugar, HbA1c, insulin, C-peptide, HOMA-IR, HOMA-β, BMI, body weight; lipid profile mentioned but not fully extracted.
