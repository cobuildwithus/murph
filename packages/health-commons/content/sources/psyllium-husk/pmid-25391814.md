This source is included for **Direct protocol trials, dose, timing, formulation, and subgroup evidence**.

**Findings:** In 51 children/adolescents with mild-to-moderate hypercholesterolemia, 7 g/day psyllium for 8 weeks lowered total cholesterol and LDL-C versus cellulose placebo by 7.7% and 10.7% in accessible abstract reporting.

**Why it matters:** Strong pediatric direct evidence, but should be labeled as a population-boundary finding for an adult protocol.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, total cholesterol, acceptability, adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** Strong pediatric direct evidence, but should be labeled as a population-boundary finding for an adult protocol.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction caveat:** Age 6-19 population and Step 2 diet lead-in limit direct adult transfer.
