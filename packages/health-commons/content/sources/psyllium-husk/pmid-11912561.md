This source is included for **Direct protocol trials, dose, timing, formulation, and subgroup evidence**.

**Findings:** A sex/hormonal-status crossover study found about 8% average LDL-C lowering with 15 g/day psyllium, but triglyceride and apolipoprotein remodeling responses varied by sex/hormonal status.

**Why it matters:** Supports logging sex/hormonal status as a possible response modifier, not a separate average-effect claim.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, triglycerides, apo CIII, apoE, insulin, VLDL composition, LDL composition.

**Protocol takeaway:** Supports logging sex/hormonal status as a possible response modifier, not a separate average-effect claim.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Extraction caveat:** Likely overlapping cohort with the companion plasma-lipid paper and mechanistic endpoints.
