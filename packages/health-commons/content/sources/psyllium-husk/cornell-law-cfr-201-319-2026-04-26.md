- `finding:cornell-law-cfr-201-319-2026-04-26-cfr-fluid-directions` — Required directions specify taking or mixing these products with at least 8 ounces/full glass of water or other fluid.

**Why it matters:** Regulatory basis for required choking/obstruction warnings and directions for psyllium-containing bulk-forming products.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- choking
- obstruction
- hydration
- dysphagia

**Protocol takeaway:** Protocol instructions should require immediate mixing or ingestion with a full glass of liquid and exclude people with swallowing difficulty unless clinician-directed.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Regulatory source; not a clinical trial.; Applies broadly to water-soluble gums and hydrophilic mucilloids, not only cholesterol-directed psyllium use. Population mismatch: General OTC labeling context rather than a lipid-lowering trial population.
