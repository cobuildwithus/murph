This source is included for **Adjacent variants, soluble-fiber comparators, and population mismatch**.

**Findings:** Glucomannan reduced total cholesterol (SMD -2.26, 95% CI -2.98 to -1.55, p<0.001) and LDL-C (SMD -2.57, 95% CI -3.41 to -1.74, p<0.001), but did not significantly affect HDL-C (SMD -0.33, 95% CI -0.70 to 0.03, p=0.075) or triglycerides (SMD -0.16, 95% CI -0.59 to 0.27, p=0.473). The article abstract/source summary noted the statistically significant reductions were not clinically important.

**Safety / adverse events:** No adverse-event data were extracted from the accessible abstract/source summary.

**Why it matters:** Useful overclaim guardrail because it separates statistical lipid changes from clinical importance.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C, HDL-C, triglycerides.

**Limitations / population mismatch:** Glucomannan, not psyllium; reported statistical significance may not be clinically important, and participant count was not extractable from accessible source summaries.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as adjacent glucomannan context and clinical-importance caveat only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
