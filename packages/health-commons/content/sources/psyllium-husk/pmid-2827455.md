This source is included for **Mechanism: viscosity, bile-acid, sterol, and fecal-excretion context**.

## Quick read

- **Source type:** Small controlled feeding intervention (1988).
- **People studied or addressed:** Men consuming a nutritionally controlled diet.
- **Intervention or exposure:** 21 g/day per person psyllium husk added to a controlled diet.
- **Comparator or control:** Pre-supplement controlled diet period.
- **Duration or follow-up:** 3-week control diet followed by 3 weeks with psyllium husk.
- **Endpoints:** Plasma total, LDL, and HDL cholesterol; fecal steroid excretion; carbohydrate absorption.
- **Directness:** same_mechanism.
- **Claim use:** `context-only`.

## Findings

- Accessible extraction reports decreases in total, LDL, and HDL cholesterol after psyllium, but fecal steroid excretion from 5-day collections was not affected.

## Why it matters

Preserves mixed mechanistic evidence around fecal steroid excretion.

## Potential experiment signals

- LDL cholesterol
- HDL cholesterol
- fecal steroid excretion

## Protocol takeaway

Use as mixed mechanism evidence, not a primary efficacy claim.

No adverse-event extraction available.

## Limitations and population fit

Very small, single-sequence design; healthy or non-targeted population; no placebo control.

**Population mismatch:** Mechanistic boundary study rather than robust hypercholesterolemia RCT.
