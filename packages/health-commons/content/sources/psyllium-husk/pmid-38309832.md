This source is included for **Adjacent variants and implementation context**.

**Findings:** Pooled estimates reduced LDL-C (MD -0.14 mmol/L, 95% CI -0.24 to -0.05; 38 RCTs, 1879 participants; very low certainty), triglycerides (MD -0.06 mmol/L, 95% CI -0.12 to -0.01; 40 RCTs, 1732 participants; low certainty), and body weight (MD -0.97 kg, 95% CI -1.28 to -0.66; 36 RCTs, 1672 participants; low certainty), with little to no significant effect on other CVD risk factors.

**Safety / adverse events:** No adverse-event data were extracted from the accessible abstract/source summary.

**Why it matters:** Recent certainty-graded inulin/fructan review that supports low-certainty, non-viscous soluble-fiber boundary language.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, triglycerides, body weight, total cholesterol, HDL-C.

**Limitations / population mismatch:** Low to very low certainty of evidence; inulin-type fructans are adjacent prebiotic fibers and not psyllium; the review itself calls for further well-designed trials.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as low-certainty inulin/fructan context only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
