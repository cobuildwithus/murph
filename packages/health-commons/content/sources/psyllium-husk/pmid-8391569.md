This source is included for **Adjacent variants, soluble-fiber comparators, and population mismatch**.

**Findings:** In 20 children completing a randomized double-blind crossover trial, 6 g/day psyllium cereal for 4-5 weeks produced no statistically or clinically significant differences in total cholesterol, LDL-C, or HDL-C versus control cereal; triglycerides increased after control cereal compared with psyllium. Growth and vitamin/mineral measures were not affected.

**Why it matters:** Preserves null/mixed pediatric evidence and prevents applying child data directly to adult protocol claims.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C, HDL-C, triglycerides, growth, vitamins, minerals.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as pediatric boundary and null/mixed finding, not as adult protocol contradiction.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
