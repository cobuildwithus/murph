This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and drug-interaction boundaries**.

**Findings:**

- `finding:pmid-9737361-lt4-psyllium-absorption` — In this small study, psyllium coadministration did not significantly reduce detectable levothyroxine absorption compared with levothyroxine alone, though median absorption time was reported as longer with psyllium.

**Why it matters:** Clinically relevant medication-absorption source for timing guidance in thyroid-hormone users.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- medication absorption
- drug interactions

**Protocol takeaway:** This source prevents overclaiming that psyllium necessarily blocks levothyroxine absorption, while still supporting caution for medication timing because labels and SmPCs advise separation.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Small sample of volunteers.; Single-dose pharmacokinetic method rather than chronic thyroid-control outcomes.; No cholesterol endpoints. Population mismatch: Levothyroxine absorption in volunteers, not LDL-C response.

**Claim use:** `safety-only`.
