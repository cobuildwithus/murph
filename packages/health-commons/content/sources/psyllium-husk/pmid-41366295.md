This source is included for **Direct protocol synthesis and dose-response evidence**.

## Source extraction notes

**Findings:** The meta-analysis reported lower total cholesterol (-9.05 mg/dL) and LDL-C (-8.55 mg/dL), while HDL-C and triglyceride pooled effects were not statistically significant; heterogeneity was high. Reported adverse events were mostly gastrointestinal or tolerability-related and were not uniformly captured across trials, so safety conclusions remain incomplete.

**Why it matters:** This is the newest direct lipid-profile synthesis in the batch and provides an open-access source page for updated pooled estimates and adverse-event summaries.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C; total cholesterol; HDL-C; triglycerides; GI adverse events.

**Protocol takeaway:** The updated pooled evidence remains directionally positive for LDL-C and total cholesterol but mixed for HDL-C and triglycerides, with heterogeneity requiring cautious wording.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

## Evidence boundary

- **Directness:** `direct_protocol`.
- **Population mismatch:** Moderate-to-low mismatch: direct adult RCT synthesis, but included trials span heterogeneous populations and doses.
- **Limitations:** High heterogeneity was reported for lipid outcomes. HDL-C and triglyceride effects were not statistically significant. Dose range was broad, so protocol-specific dosing needs narrower interpretation.
- **Safety notes:** The open-access article lists mostly GI-type adverse events and notes that events were not uniformly reported across included studies.
