This source is included for **Adjacent variants, soluble-fiber comparators, and population mismatch**.

**Findings:** Pooled total-cholesterol change was -0.13 mmol/L (-5.9 mg/dL; 95% CI -0.19 to -0.017 mmol/L), and wheat-control trials showed -0.11 mmol/L (-4.4 mg/dL; 95% CI -0.21 to -0.01 mmol/L). Larger reductions were reported when baseline total cholesterol was ≥5.9 mmol/L and soluble-fiber dose was ≥3 g/day.

**Safety / adverse events:** No adverse-event data were extracted from the PubMed abstract.

**Why it matters:** Preserves foundational oat evidence and the early dose/baseline-cholesterol context without merging oat food effects with psyllium.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C.

**Limitations / population mismatch:** Older oat-product meta-analysis with no extractable participant count from the abstract; oat food/product evidence should not be treated as psyllium monotherapy evidence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as historical adjacent oat comparator evidence only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
