This source is included for **Direct protocol trials, dose, timing, formulation, and subgroup evidence**.

**Findings:** In adults with primary hypercholesterolemia using an AHA Step I diet, psyllium 5.1 g twice daily lowered total cholesterol and LDL-C versus cellulose placebo over 24-26 weeks; accessible snippets report 4.7% and 6.7% relative differences, respectively.

**Why it matters:** Provides a long-duration adult dose anchor for 5.1 g twice daily psyllium as a diet adjunct.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL-C, total cholesterol, HDL-C, triglycerides, tolerability/adherence.

**Protocol takeaway:** Provides a long-duration adult dose anchor for 5.1 g twice daily psyllium as a diet adjunct.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Extraction caveat:** Full adverse-event details and participant count were not extracted from accessible artifacts.
