This source is included for **Safety, adverse events, and drug-interaction boundaries**.

**Findings:**

- `finding:pmid-11403757-guar-meta-negative-gi-aes` — The meta-analysis found no statistically significant body-weight advantage for guar gum and reported frequent GI adverse events including abdominal pain, flatulence, diarrhea, and cramps, with 11 dropouts due to adverse events.

**Why it matters:** Not a cholesterol efficacy source, but preserves negative and adverse-event evidence for a viscous soluble fiber.

**Potential experiment signals:**

- safety/tolerability
- adverse events
- do-not-overclaim
- adjacent guar variant

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as a caution against assuming all viscous fibers have favorable benefit/tolerability tradeoffs across indications.

**Limitations and population mismatch:** Adjacent fiber and adjacent endpoint.; Do not transpose effect estimates to psyllium LDL-C. Population mismatch: Guar gum weight-loss trials; not psyllium husk for cholesterol.
