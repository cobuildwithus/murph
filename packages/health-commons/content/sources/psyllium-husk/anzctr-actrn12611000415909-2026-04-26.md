This source is included for **Registries and unpublished protocols**.

**Findings:** Registered 52-week trial used 5 g psyllium in 250 mL water three times daily before meals and measured fasting lipid and other cardiometabolic endpoints, but the registry page does not provide cholesterol effect estimates.

**Why it matters:** Provides long-duration, practical dosing context for psyllium taken before meals with water, while the population and primary aim are weight/metabolic risk rather than cholesterol treatment.

**Potential experiment signals:** fasting lipid profile, body weight and body composition, waist and hip circumference, fasting glucose and insulin, blood pressure.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as context for feasible meal-timed dosing and lipid-measurement scheduling, not as a source of direct cholesterol-lowering effect because registry extraction does not provide results and the trial targets overweight/obesity outcomes.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `direct_protocol`.

**Population mismatch:** Population is overweight/obese adults rather than specifically hypercholesterolemic adults.

**Limitations:** Registry/protocol source; no registry-extracted effect estimates.; Overweight/obesity population rather than adults seeking LDL-C lowering.; Includes a PGX comparator and weight/metabolic-risk framing.

**Safety notes:** No registry-extracted adverse-event results; general water instructions were specified with dosing.
