## Findings

- The review summarizes psyllium lipid-lowering studies and mechanisms, including viscous gel behavior, bile-salt binding/excretion, and LDL-receptor-related hypotheses; primary sources should be cited for effect claims.
- The review describes generally good tolerability in cited psyllium studies but notes possible nutrient-absorption/allergy concerns and the importance of taking higher doses with sufficient water.

## Safety and adverse events

The review notes psyllium is generally well tolerated in a 52-week healthy-subject example, with most adverse events minor and short-duration, but also notes potential effects on nutrient absorption/allergy and the need for sufficient water at higher doses.

## Limitations and population fit

Review/bibliography source; do not use as primary evidence where primary studies are available.
