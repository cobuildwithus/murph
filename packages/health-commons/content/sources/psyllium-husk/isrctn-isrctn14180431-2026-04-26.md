This source is included for **Registries and unpublished protocols**.

**Findings:** Registry record tested 10 g/day psyllium versus rice flour placebo for 7 weeks in adolescents with obesity, with LDL/HDL subclass, lipid profile, IL-6, and metabolic endpoints.

**Why it matters:** Gives concrete psyllium dose, timing, water instructions, lipid subfraction endpoints, and adverse-symptom monitoring, but the adolescent obesity population limits adult protocol transfer.

**Potential experiment signals:** LDL and HDL subclasses, IL-6, lipid profile, atherogenic index, blood pressure, glucose and HOMA-IR, bloating/flatulence/fullness.

**Protocol takeaway:** Useful for pediatric/population-mismatch context and safety monitoring; do not generalize adolescent obesity results to adults without separate supporting adult data.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.

**Directness:** `direct_protocol`.

**Population mismatch:** Adolescents with obesity and cardiovascular risk factors, not general adults using psyllium for LDL-C lowering.

**Limitations:** Pediatric adolescent obesity population.; Registry source rather than a fully extracted result article.; Adverse events are listed as possible risks/monitoring questions, not quantified registry results.

**Safety notes:** Registry lists possible allergy/intolerance, increased evacuation/defecation, and abdominal distension; monitoring included defecation, bloating, flatulence, and fullness.
