This source is included for **Direct protocol trials, dose, timing, formulation, and subgroup evidence**.

**Findings:** In 26 hypercholesterolemic men, psyllium hydrophilic mucilloid 10.2 g/day for 8 weeks reduced total cholesterol by 14.8% and LDL-C by 20.2% in accessible abstract reporting.

**Why it matters:** Early direct adult dose evidence for three-times-daily 3.4 g dosing, with a notably large effect that should be interpreted alongside later trials.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C, LDL/HDL ratio, HDL-C, triglycerides, tolerability.

**Protocol takeaway:** Early direct adult dose evidence for three-times-daily 3.4 g dosing, with a notably large effect that should be interpreted alongside later trials.

**Claim use:** `supports-protocol`.

**Extraction caveat:** Small male-only sample and early-trial context.
