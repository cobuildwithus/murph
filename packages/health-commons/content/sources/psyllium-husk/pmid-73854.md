This source is included for **Adjacent variants, soluble-fiber comparators, and population mismatch**.

**Findings:** Pectin 12 g/day for 3 weeks decreased total cholesterol by 0.48 ± 0.18 mmol/L (7.9 ± 2.6%) and LDL-C by 0.45 ± 0.19 mmol/L; apolipoprotein B fell by 0.11 ± 0.04 g/L. Stool weight increased from 150 ± 10 to 186 ± 15 g/24 h. No clear change was extracted for triglycerides, VLDL, or whole-gut transit time.

**Safety / adverse events:** No adverse-event signal was extracted from the accessible summary; stool-weight change was reported.

**Why it matters:** Preserves older direct pectin evidence while preventing it from being generalized to all soluble fibers.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C, triglycerides, stool weight.

**Limitations / population mismatch:** Very small, older pectin trial in healthy men; pectin is not psyllium, and the population does not match hypercholesterolemia treatment populations.

**Protocol takeaway:** Use as pectin comparator context only.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
