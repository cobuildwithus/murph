This source is included for **Adjacent variants and implementation context**.

**Findings:** Across 29 RCTs with 2,769 adult participants, the 2024 Plantago meta-analysis reported reductions in total cholesterol by 0.28 mmol/L and LDL-C by 0.35 mmol/L, with no substantial effects on HDL-C or triglycerides; the exposure is Plantago-wide rather than psyllium-husk-specific.

**Why it matters:** Useful for taxonomy and subgroup boundary decisions around Plantago versus psyllium husk.

**Potential experiment signals:** total cholesterol, LDL-C, HDL-C, triglycerides.

**Protocol takeaway:** Context-only unless psyllium-husk subgroup data are extracted and identity-resolved.

**Claim use:** `context-only`.
