## Claim-Safe Summary

DRKS00035887 is a prospectively registered German randomized controlled trial of social media reduction in heavy-using adolescents and young adults aged 16 to 29. It includes sleep quality as a primary outcome measured with the Insomnia Severity Index, alongside stress and depressive symptoms, but no results are posted. Because the intervention is a 4-week reduction protocol rather than 24-hour, 72-hour, or 7-day abstinence, use this source only as measurement context or adjacent intervention context, not as evidence for social media fast effects.
