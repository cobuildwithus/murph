## Claim-Safe Summary

This RCT article suggests that reducing social media use to 1 hour per day for 3 weeks may reduce loneliness in youth with anxiety and depressive symptoms, with no moderation by gender or baseline social comparison. It should be treated as adjacent reduction evidence only, not direct evidence for social media abstinence or fast variants.
