## Claim-Safe Summary

This randomized Instagram image-exposure experiment can support only mechanism-context claims: in young women, brief exposure to attractive celebrity or peer images sourced from Instagram worsened immediate negative mood and body dissatisfaction compared with travel images, with appearance comparison implicated as a mediator. It should not be used as evidence that a 24-hour, 72-hour, or 7-day social media fast improves or worsens outcomes.
