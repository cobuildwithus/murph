## Claim-Safe Summary

Use as boundary/context for broad digital detox evidence and to preserve mixed findings. It can support caution that digital detox evidence is heterogeneous and not clearly policy-ready, but it should not be used as direct proof that 24-hour, 72-hour, or 7-day social media-only fasting improves wellbeing.
