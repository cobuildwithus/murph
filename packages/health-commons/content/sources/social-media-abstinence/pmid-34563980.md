## Claim-Safe Summary

This source can support only external context that adolescent social media-use and mental-health evidence is mixed, heterogeneous, often weak or inconsistent, and limited by cross-sectional designs, construct heterogeneity, and self-report measurement. It cannot support any claim that a 24-hour, 72-hour, or 7-day social media fast improves or harms mood, depression, anxiety, well-being, adherence, or safety.
