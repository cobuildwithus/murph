## Claim-Safe Summary

Adjacent context only. This randomized COVID-era German adult study tested 14 days of reducing social media use by 30 minutes per day, increasing physical activity by 30 minutes per day, or both, compared with no behavioral instruction. It found decreases in social media use, depressive symptoms, addictive social media use, and COVID-19 burden, plus increases in physical activity and positive mental-health measures, with many effects strongest in the combined social media reduction plus physical activity arm. It is not claim-safe for 24-hour, 72-hour, or 7-day social media abstinence or fast effects because it did not test abstinence, did not use protocol-scope durations, and included physical activity cointervention arms.
