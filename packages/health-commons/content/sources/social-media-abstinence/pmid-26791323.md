## Claim-Safe Summary

Use only as observational background or confounder context: in a nationally representative U.S. young-adult sample, heavier naturalistic social media use was associated with greater odds of sleep disturbance. Do not use this source to claim that a 24-hour, 72-hour, or 7-day social media fast improves or worsens sleep, because it did not test abstinence or any intervention.
