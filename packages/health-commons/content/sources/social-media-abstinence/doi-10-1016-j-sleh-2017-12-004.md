## Claim-Safe Summary

This source can support contextual statements that in-bed electronic social media use is associated with insomnia, anxiety, and shorter weeknight sleep duration in an adult cross-sectional sample, and can help justify sleep and mood endpoints or bedtime-use confounding. It must not be used to claim that a 24-hour, 72-hour, or 7-day social media fast improves sleep, energy, mood, or mental health.
