## Claim-Safe Summary

Mechanism context only: in young UK university-affiliated women, a 10-minute Facebook exposure experiment found worse immediate negative mood versus an appearance-neutral control and greater face/hair/skin appearance discrepancy only among participants high in appearance comparison tendency. It provides no direct evidence about benefits, harms, adherence, safety, or symptom effects of 24-hour, 72-hour, or 7-day social media abstinence or fasting.
