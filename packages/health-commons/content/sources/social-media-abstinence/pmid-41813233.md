## Claim-Safe Summary

Use this source only as adjacent external context on evidence quality and broad nonpharmacological interventions for internet, smartphone, and gaming addictions. It supports a cautious statement that adjacent digital-addiction treatment reviews report symptom reductions but with low or very low certainty, high heterogeneity, and possible publication bias. It must not be used as direct evidence that a 24-hour, 72-hour, or 7-day social media fast improves outcomes or is safe.
