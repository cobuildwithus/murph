## Claim-Safe Summary

Use as secondary measurement and duration-boundary context only. It supports preserving the possibility that effects differ by intervention length, including negative findings for shorter-than-1-week interventions, but it should not be used as a primary claim that 24-hour, 72-hour, or 7-day social media fasts improve health.
