## Claim-Safe Summary

Use this source only as adjacent clinician-boundary context: CBT-IA is a clinical Internet addiction treatment model involving behavior modification, cognitive restructuring, and harm reduction therapy, and it cautions that full Internet abstinence is often impractical because legitimate Internet use remains necessary. Do not use it as evidence for benefits or harms of a 24-hour, 72-hour, or 7-day social media fast.
