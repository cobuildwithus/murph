## Claim-Safe Summary

Use this source only as an adjacent clinician-guidance and safety-boundary citation. It supports the statement that people with suspected internet/video game/social-media-related behavioral addiction, functional impairment, withdrawal/craving patterns, relapse risk, or psychiatric comorbidity may need clinical assessment and individualized treatment rather than a self-directed fast. It does not support any claim that a 24-hour, 72-hour, or 7-day social media fast improves mental health, wellbeing, sleep, productivity, or social functioning.
