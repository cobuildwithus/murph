- Population: Youth aged 12 to 17 years with anxiety or depression symptoms who use social media more than 3 hours per day; caregiver dyads; Canada site context reported in mirrors.
- Intervention: Two-phase REWIRE program. Phase 1 pilot feasibility; Phase 2 randomized family-based behavioral program aiming to reduce social media use to about 50% of each youth's baseline and reinforce individualized offline alternatives such as in-person social activity, hobbies, physical activity, outdoor time, sleep hygiene, and volunteering. Includes weekly group sessions, individual goal review, daily smartphone-use screenshots, EMA, accelerometry, neurocognitive assessment, and neuroimaging.
- Comparator: Phase 2 psychoeducation control about social media, screen time, lifestyle behavior, and mental health, attention-matched but without active behavioral change targets or social media reduction targets.

## Claim-Safe Summary

Use only to say that a registered adolescent REWIRE protocol plans to test a 12-week social-media-use reduction program and includes sleep-related measurement context. Do not use for efficacy, safety, adult, or 24-hour/72-hour/7-day Social Media Fast claims.
