## Claim-Safe Summary

This RCT is a boundary source showing that a family media-use planning intervention in parent-adolescent dyads did not improve the primary media-rule engagement endpoint and produced only a small favorable secondary signal for perceived technology importance, with no detected harms. It should not be used to claim that 24-hour, 72-hour, or 7-day social media fasting improves outcomes.
