## Claim-Safe Summary

Use this source only to define and contextualize how university students understand and self-report social media detoxification, including variable duration and burden signals. Do not use it as causal evidence that a 24-hour, 72-hour, or 7-day social media fast improves mood, anxiety, productivity, or sleep.
