## Claim-Safe Summary

This cluster RCT provides adjacent evidence that an app-based multibehavior challenge modestly increased the proportion of vocational students reducing social media by at least 20 minutes per day, but it was not a standardized social media abstinence fast and should not be used as direct evidence for 24-hour, 72-hour, or 7-day social media fasting.
