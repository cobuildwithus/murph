## Claim-Safe Summary

Use only as external mechanism context: social networking site use, especially passive use, may increase social comparison, and contrastive upward comparison is typically linked with poorer subjective well-being or negative affect in reviewed literature. This source does not test social media abstinence, does not cover 24-hour, 72-hour, or 7-day fast variants, and must not be used to support protocol effect claims about benefits or harms of a social media fast.
