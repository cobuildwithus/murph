- Population: Ten peer-reviewed studies with adult samples, total N = 4,674, approximately 65% female; many samples were from developed countries and several recruited students.
- Intervention: Complete social media abstinence interventions, with variation in platforms and devices. The review excluded interventions that were smartphone abstinence, reductions rather than abstinence, outcomes outside affective well-being/life satisfaction, or minor-only samples.
- Comparator: Varied by included study, including between-subject control groups and within-subject pre/post designs; synthesis included 38 effect sizes.
- Duration: Included abstinence durations ranged from 1 to 28 days; 7 days was the most common duration. Duration meta-regression was non-significant for all three main outcomes. No specific 72-hour synthesis was extracted.
- Effect direction: overall_null_mixed_review_level: pooled effects on positive affect, negative affect, and life satisfaction were not significant; individual studies varied across positive, null, and negative findings; duration evidence was non-significant.

## Claim-Safe Summary

Use as external review-level context that direct social media abstinence evidence is null/mixed for affective well-being and life satisfaction, with limited duration evidence. Do not use alone as primary evidence for protocol recommendations.
