## Claim-Safe Summary

Use this source for adjacent safety and burden considerations around 72-hour device restriction, especially withdrawal and FoMO. Do not use it to claim that social media abstinence improves or worsens wellbeing, because the intervention removed smartphone access broadly rather than social media specifically.
