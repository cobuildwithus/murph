## Claim-Safe Summary

Qualitative evidence from six Australian young adults suggests self-initiated Facebook sabbaticals of 3 days or more may be experienced as helpful for perceived technostress and problematic overuse, while also creating burdens such as reduced social connection, loneliness, FoMO, peer pressure, and difficulty maintaining abstinence. This source should be used only for burden, safety, and implementation-context claims, not for efficacy estimates or protocol-specific claims about 24-hour, 72-hour, or 7-day Social Media Fast outcomes.
