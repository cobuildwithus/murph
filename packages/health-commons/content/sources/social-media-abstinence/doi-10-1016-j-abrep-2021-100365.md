## Claim-Safe Summary

Use this source only as adjacent 7-day mobile social-media reduction evidence and as adherence, burden, measurement, and duration-boundary context. It is not a social media abstinence or fast trial. In 76 student smartphone users, a 7-day intended 50% reduction in mobile Facebook, Instagram, Snapchat, and YouTube use was compared with an intended 10% active reduction. The control group reduced use far more than intended, compromising the contrast. The study found no clear differential benefit of the more intensive reduction on behavioral sustained attention or emotional well-being, and actual reduction did not predict outcome changes.
