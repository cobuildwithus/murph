## Claim-Safe Summary

Use only for external context, exclusion boundaries, and burden signals. It supports that the digital detox literature is heterogeneous, includes 24-hour and one-week variants, and contains mixed/null/negative findings, but it should not be used as direct evidence that a Social Media Fast protocol works.
