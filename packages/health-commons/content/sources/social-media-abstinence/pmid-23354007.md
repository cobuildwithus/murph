## Claim-Safe Summary

Use only to define clinical internet-addiction treatment as adjacent and outside direct Social Media Fast evidence. Do not use to infer benefits, harms, adherence, relapse, or optimal duration for 24-hour, 72-hour, or 7-day social media abstinence.
