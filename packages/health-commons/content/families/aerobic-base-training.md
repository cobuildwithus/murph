Aerobic Base Training covers self-experiments that add sustainable easy aerobic volume and track whether it is repeatable, tolerable, and worth continuing.

Keep this family separate from cardiac or pulmonary rehabilitation, diabetes-specific exercise guidance, pregnancy/postpartum exercise guidance, high-intensity interval training, sport performance plans, and injury rehabilitation.
