# Social Media Abstinence

This family covers bounded breaks from selected social media apps. It excludes generic screen-time reduction, full smartphone abstinence, Digital Sunset or bedtime-only changes, app-blocker tooling as the primary intervention, and clinician-led problematic-use treatment unless a protocol explicitly says otherwise.
