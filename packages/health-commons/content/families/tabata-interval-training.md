Tabata Interval Training is the Health Commons family for protocols built around the 20 seconds work / 10 seconds rest structure associated with the Tabata research lineage.

The family is deliberately narrow. It can hold Murph protocols that preserve the timing structure and require dose-fidelity logging, but it should not become a catch-all bucket for every short, hard workout marketed as Tabata.

## Current canonical protocol

- `protocol_variant:tabata-interval-training/tabata-20-10-interval-training` — a conservative, field-testable Murph starter protocol using one eight-round 20/10 block twice weekly.

## Boundary rules

Keep the following separate unless a page explicitly labels them as adjacent or external variants:

- original supervised cycle-ergometer evidence versus Murph field implementation;
- practical 20/10 circuits and school or sport programs;
- CrossFit Tabata WODs and public workout recipes;
- 40/20 intervals, AMRAP, EMOM, and metcon formats;
- Wingate/sprint-interval protocols;
- clinical rehabilitation HIIT and disease-treatment protocols;
- safety-only sources, mechanism-only sources, and marketing claims.

## Evidence posture

The family can support cautious cardio-fitness self-experiments, but the safety and variant-boundary language should stay stronger than the efficacy language. Direct protocol evidence is small and supervised; adjacent practical variants are heterogeneous; external protocol claims should not be merged into Murph outcomes.
