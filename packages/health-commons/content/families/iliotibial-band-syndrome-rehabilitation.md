Iliotibial Band Syndrome Rehabilitation is the family for active, conservative runner ITBS care and return-to-run tracking. The first Murph variant is **IT Band Rehab**.

## Family boundary

The family includes symptom-guided running load reduction, hip and glute strengthening, movement-control work, optional gait-retraining experiments, and graded run/walk return. It does not collapse cyclist bike-fit problems, injection care, medication, or surgery into the self-guided runner protocol.

## Evidence posture

The corpus is useful but uneven. Direct runner rehabilitation evidence exists, including recent reviews, a pilot randomized study, and case-level gait-retraining reports. Mechanism and risk-factor sources are larger but mostly observational. Safety and differential-diagnosis sources are strong enough to require visible stop conditions.
