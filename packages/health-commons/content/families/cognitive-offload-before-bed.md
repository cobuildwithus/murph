Cognitive Offload Before Bed is for nights when unfinished tasks keep recycling after the day is over. The default intervention is a short, concrete list of tomorrow's tasks and first actions, then stopping.

This family does not treat every racing thought as a productivity problem. Open-ended worry journaling, emotional processing, trauma writing, gratitude journaling, meditation, relaxation therapy, and CBT-I are different interventions with different risk and evidence boundaries.

If writing increases activation, perfectionism, panic, trauma recall, or time spent planning, the right response is to shorten it, move it earlier, or stop—not to write a better list.
