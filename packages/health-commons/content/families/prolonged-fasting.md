Prolonged Fasting is the family for experiments that deliberately extend fasting beyond an ordinary overnight fast.

## What belongs in this family

Use this family when the protocol is fundamentally about a continuous fasting exposure and the dose is defined by elapsed hours without energy intake, hydration status, activity conditions, and refeeding plan.

## What stays separate

This family should not blur together water-only fasting, dry fasting, fasting-mimicking diets, very-low-calorie diets, time-restricted eating, alternate-day fasting, Ramadan fasting, fasted exercise, oncology-support fasting, or supervised disease-treatment programs. Those variants differ in calorie dose, fluid exposure, duration, supervision, population, or safety requirements.

## How to read the evidence

The current prolonged-fasting corpus is broad but uneven. Direct 24–72 hour records mainly support acute time-course, physiology, measurement, and safety-boundary language. Adjacent and supervised sources are valuable for defining what not to claim, what to screen, and what to keep separate.
