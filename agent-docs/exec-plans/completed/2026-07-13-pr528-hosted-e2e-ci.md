# PR 528 hosted E2E CI repair

## Goal

Restore PR 528's Cloudflare Hosted E2E gate without weakening the shutdown
checkpoint durability proof.

Success criteria:

- The failing exact PR head is traced to a concrete runtime or harness cause.
- The conversation-ahead scenario still proves one checkpointed restore, two
  exact replies, two provider requests, zero mailbox lag, and no duplicate work.
- Focused tests and the Cloudflare typecheck pass before the branch is pushed.
- ReviewGPT and CI run against the pushed PR-specific head.

## Evidence

- The exact PR head reproduced the failure after a clean workflow rebuild.
- Both attempts completed the two expected replies and provider requests with
  zero mailbox lag and no runtime error.
- The final status remained in flight after a cleanup-only assistant pass.
- The scenario configures a 180-second idle checkpoint window, but its final
  quiescence helper allowed only 60 seconds.
- The final one-shot SIGTERM can land on the just-completed restore invocation;
  Temporal may then start the already-committed follow-up cleanup invocation in
  a fresh container, outside that signal's scope.

## Approach

1. Replace the single final stop plus undersized passive wait with a bounded
   helper that gracefully stops each observed active invocation and requires a
   stable quiescent status.
2. Keep the durability, exact reply/provider-count, and no-duplicate assertions
   unchanged.
3. Run focused Cloudflare tests/typecheck and the routed completion reviews.
4. Commit, push, and run ReviewGPT concurrently with the new CI head.

## Constraints

- Do not change production lifecycle behavior for this harness timing race.
- Do not increase timeouts as a substitute for quiescence proof.
- Preserve unrelated active-plan and working-tree changes.
- Do not expose secrets or direct personal identifiers in artifacts.
