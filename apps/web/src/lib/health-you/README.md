# Private You views

The seven GET handlers under `health/you` serve the authenticated member. The model is a research comparison of mortality-equivalent age. Reference-only profiles show a building view. Unsupported reference sex, ages outside 20 through 80, pregnancy, early postpartum and an age equivalent outside the training range withhold the precise headline. The uncertainty interval estimates missing-panel spread.

## Dated inputs

`inputs.ts` loads one published core, metrics index, requested metric buckets and lab shard. A readable source with no qualifying observations contributes the age- and sex-specific model expectation. A failed required read yields unavailable, and a matching previously sealed view can be labelled stale. Narrow optional fields absent on old publications remain unknown. Per-input availability distinguishes observed, stale, absent and unavailable evidence. R2 separately decides whether an observation qualifies for a coefficient.

| R2 input | Source, units and limits |
| --- | --- |
| Reference age, sex, join date | Canonical demographics supply birth year and reference sex. Calendar-year precision persists throughout reconstruction. Member creation time supplies the local join date. Vault meals metadata supplies the local time zone. |
| Sleep duration, pulse | Dated device total sleep in minutes and resting heart rate in bpm. Reject naps and combine only consistent source-day observations. The scorer needs seven observations within fourteen days and a latest reading within two days. |
| Steps, hip minutes, reported activity | Steps require complete wear or 1,296 measured wear minutes. Ordinary daily steps have no completeness guarantee. Comparable intensity requires moderate and vigorous minutes or a verified vendor-equivalent protocol. Total activity minutes can supply a weekly progress row. They cannot establish the R2 intensity dose. The replica needs wear and intensity metadata to support these bridges. |
| Bedtime, sleep regularity, L5 midpoint | Nightly start/end instants and a local time zone can describe timing. A bedtime target also requires an identifiable comparison window. SRI requires paired sleep epochs and L5 requires the least-active five-hour window. Bedtime alone supplies neither coefficient. |
| BMI, waist | Dated body-height (cm), body-weight (kg) and waist-circumference (cm). Pair weight with height as of the weighing date. Static profile body values never populate earlier dates. The weight and waist eligibility window is 180 days. |
| Blood and urine | Numeric normalized lab results and canonical units, resolved by catalog key. R2 fields include albumin, creatinine, RDW, WBC, hs-CRP, HbA1c, GGT, ALP, BUN, phosphate, globulin, uric acid, lymphocyte percentage, MCV, hemoglobin, platelets, carbon dioxide, sodium, potassium, chloride, calcium, total bilirubin, AST, ALT, total protein, urine ACR, non-HDL, homocysteine, ferritin and vitamin D. ACR needs the ratio. Non-HDL may use total cholesterol minus HDL on the same identified draw. LDL needs a stable-LDL qualifier. Lp(a) needs mass units. Clinical panels expire after 365 days except the inherited Lp(a) term. This reader bounds both to the retained horizon. Other catalog markers can appear in the blood detail without an age effect. |
| Systolic pressure | Canonical measurement pairs need a shared action ID, local measurement time, both mmHg values and setting. Office readings require two qualifying dates, validated home readings three dates with morning and evening pairs. Daily aggregate SBP and DBP alone cannot establish this evidence. |
| Cardio fitness | Dated Apple or Garmin estimated VO2 max in mL/kg/min with two consistent source dates in 42 days. The pure scorer also accepts identified lab CPET within 180 days and a verified NHANES submaximal test. A generic watch estimate cannot establish either clinical protocol. |
| Grip, floor rise, balance, gait, knee strength, FEV1 | Protocol, equipment, verification, date and units determine eligibility. Grip needs two hand maxima from one verified session. Home Combine entries can record performance. Self-counted entries cannot establish the verified clinical or supervised test protocols. |
| Smoking, cessation, serious disease, diabetes, general health, PHQ-9, alcohol | Versioned categorical or numeric canonical assessments. Smoking cessation needs a dated quit response, PHQ-9 needs a completed instrument, and alcohol needs habitual grams/day. Qualifying dated pregnancy, postpartum, medication, mobility, edema, unreliable assay and illness flags gate their affected inputs. The current core demographics projection has no view of clinical eligibility flags. |
| Fibre and meals | A dated habitual fibre value in g/day, or seven consecutive confirmed complete days with identified, quantified meals. A partial food log cannot establish a complete day. The current meal summary does not certify this protocol. |
| Whole grains, vegetables, fruit, nuts, legumes, fish, red meat, processed meat, sugary drinks, overall diet pattern | Only method-verified habitual amounts in the published catalog units, or a validated diet-pattern quintile. A single short questionnaire response does not establish the full diet-pattern measure. |
| Verified lifting frequency, sedentary time, short vigorous bursts, sauna, usual gait | The published protocol, eligibility flags and habitual exposure must be projected explicitly. The 62-day activity-session view supplies session history only. The 183-day training projection can supply a completed strength-session count for the current week. Workout minutes and training text cannot establish these habitual doses. |
| HRV, push-ups, jumps and other context | No separate R2 mortality-age coefficient. Device HRV and self-reported exercise counts do not substitute for a calibrated performance test. |

## Reconstruction and cache

Each point uses only evidence dated on or before its local evaluation date. A supplied release date also limits eligibility. A publication may already include corrections to an older result. The history label identifies reconstruction under the current model and available evidence. The earliest point is bounded by the join date, the 365-day projection and the first eligible panel. A shorter source window limits which factors can contribute. Weekly change uses unrounded comparable contributions at a fixed reference age. New and stale panels never become a week of habit change. Reference-only periods carry their own basis.

One sealed row per member holds the entire bundle. The dependency digest binds the member, R2 and wire versions, immutable published input identity and data version, local calendar date and time zone. The conditional publish uses the previously observed row revision. A new canonical check-in becomes visible with replica publication. No invalidation write is needed. Private read authority is checked before and after every response, including a cache hit. Current game and working-on rows remain outside the age cache.

## Reproduction

Run `python3 -B apps/web/scripts/health-you/reference.py <R2-directory>` to regenerate the frozen aggregate model and synthetic Python expectations. The TypeScript parity suite checks all persona combinations and dated trajectories. Run `pnpm exec tsx apps/web/scripts/health-you/views.ts` to regenerate six main views and six details under `apps/web/test/fixtures/health-you/goldens/`. A schema or model change requires a corresponding version bump.
