# Private You views

The seven gated GET handlers under `health/you` serve the authenticated member. The TypeScript engine uses the frozen r4.0 mortality-equivalent age comparison. The precise estimate includes an interval for missing-panel spread. A probability is available in the research parity interface only.

## Dated inputs

The reader loads the published core, metric index, requested metric buckets and lab shard. Required read failures produce unavailable or a previously sealed, matching stale view. Optional fields on older publications remain unknown.

| Input | Replica evidence and limits |
| --- | --- |
| Demographics | Reference sex and birth year from canonical demographics. The internal age anchor uses July 1 with an explicit approximation flag. The member’s join date and the vault time zone bound replay. The private headline requires an unambiguous eligibility year. |
| Sleep, pulse, steps and VO₂ | Same-provider and same-method daily sleep minutes and resting pulse. Positive watch steps with unknown wear can supply reduced, favorable-only credit. Full wear enables the two-sided comparison. Phone and unqualified relay sources supply no wearable credit. A device VO₂ estimate needs two dates from the same supported watch. Daily sleep and pulse need three observations in nine days and a reading within the last seven days. Overnight RMSSD is descriptive. Other HRV, sleep stages, vendor scores, distance and floors supply context. Stable original device attribution, explicit wear and effort-qualified intensity are only usable where projected. |
| Bedtime | The optional nightly start, end and local zone support the bedtime target using a fourteen-night personal baseline. A dated provider map on newer publications identifies the selected instrument. Four comparable nights including a weekend support sleep-onset variability. Legacy windows remain usable for the bedtime target. |
| Workouts | The 62-day session projection supplies dated types and durations. New publications preserve additional recognized workout types alongside the legacy session list. Completed structured training with duration adds up to 183 days. R4 uses the largest daily dose per slot in a 28-day window. Cycling and swimming can supplement steps. Strength shares the conditioning budget. |
| Body | Dated height, weight, BMI, waist and body fat. Pair weight with an eligible height known at the time. Direct BMI is a fallback. Body fat remains descriptive. Weight-loss scoring needs a dated unintentional-loss answer, currently unavailable. Lean mass requires paired same-method readings. |
| Blood and urine | Normalized results, catalog units, comparator, pending status and identified draw. Same-draw total and HDL cholesterol can establish non-HDL. ApoB, LDL and non-HDL share one lipid slot. Lp(a) has separate mass and molar comparisons. Fasting markers require an explicit fasting status. HOMA-IR needs a common draw. Erythrocyte-specific omega-3 and insulin-use status are unavailable. The publication does not carry a complete history of result revisions, retractions or release timestamps. The normal 365-day read bounds long-lived markers too. |
| Blood pressure | Canonical upper/lower components share one measurement ID and timestamp. Event-grain relay readings require matching source, timestamp and setting. The office route needs paired readings on two days. Validated home readings need morning and evening pairs on three days. For member-entered home readings the policy assumes a validated cuff and retains the 0.75 home weight. A missing setting cannot establish a series. |
| Combine | Canonical version-one assessments supply protocol, session, date, station, variant and reported performance. Floor rise and a completed ten-second stance can receive reduced weight. The current grip station carries one result without separate left/right maxima. Skipped stations supply no score. |
| Food | A submitted version-one food week supplies its portion answers and eating days. R4 uses the weekly eating pattern and consistent alcohol totals for up to 42 days. Individual meals include nutrients. A confirmed whole-day flag is unavailable, so meal fibre stays absent. |
| Game habits | Only credited photo-backed entries of the member’s own joined games qualify. The task at the credited date must match a recognized habit. Corrections, withdrawals and departures change the input revision. A words post or an unverified wearable summary supplies no proof. |
| Other check-ins | Dated one-off assessments supply pregnancy or postpartum eligibility, smoking status, quit date, habitual weekly drinks, diabetes, serious disease, self-rated health and WHOOP zone confirmation. Corrections take effect on the reporting day. An unsure response closes the question and leaves the factor unknown. Core demographics also lack clinical eligibility, medication and mobility flags. |

## Views and history

Unsupported profiles pause. A reference-only profile, an out-of-range equivalent age, an ambiguous eligibility year, or a single proxy or published factor without independent corroboration yields a building headline. Observed factors carry their frozen definition and interpretation. Directional norms show favorable ranks of 60 or higher as Top N% and ranks from 40 to 59 as Average.

Each historical point uses dated evidence available by the evaluation day. Comparable factor changes at fixed chronological age determine weekly modeled days. A reference-only week shows 7.0 days, carries a partial flag and has empty helped and cost lists. Age events with kind `evidenceChange` carry the remaining change in years over the preceding week. They can include changed coverage, reference age or new measurements. An event does not assign that entire change to a lab draw or workout.

One sealed row per member holds the view bundle. Its dependency binds the member, model and schema versions, immutable publication, data version, verified game inputs, local date and zone. Conditional publication protects against stale writers. The next replica publication makes canonical check-ins visible. Private read authority is checked before and after serving the response. The challenge list and working-on rows load separately.

## Reproduction

Run `python3 -B apps/web/scripts/health-you/reference.py <R4-directory>` to import the frozen aggregates and all 168 invented research cases with history. The colocated parity suite compares complete outputs. The research Node verifier accepts an ES module exporting `fromRaw` from the same engine. Run `pnpm exec tsx apps/web/scripts/health-you/views.ts` to regenerate the six synthetic main states and six companion details in `apps/web/test/fixtures/health-you/goldens/`.
